Pollution MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Pollution questions to revise the UPSC Environment syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Consider the following statements regarding Particulate Matter (PM): 1. PM10 particles are smaller than PM2.5. 2. PM2.5 can penetrate deep into the lungs and enter the bloodstream. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: PM2.5 are finer particles (<2.5 µm) that can enter the bloodstream; PM10 are larger (2.5-10 µm). Statement 1 is false.

2. Which of the following pollutants is primarily responsible for the formation of photochemical smog?

  1. Carbon monoxide
  2. Nitrogen oxides
  3. Sulfur dioxide
  4. Particulate matter

Answer: Nitrogen oxides

Explanation: Photochemical smog is formed by the reaction of nitrogen oxides (NOx) and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in sunlight.

3. Consider the following statements about Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD): 1. High BOD indicates a high level of dissolved oxygen in water. 2. BOD is a measure of organic pollution in water bodies. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: High BOD indicates low dissolved oxygen as microbes consume oxygen to decompose organic matter. Statement 2 is correct.

4. Which of the following gases is not a primary pollutant but a secondary pollutant formed in the atmosphere?

  1. Sulfur dioxide
  2. Nitrogen dioxide
  3. Ozone
  4. Carbon monoxide

Answer: Ozone

Explanation: Ozone is a secondary pollutant formed from photochemical reactions of NOx and VOCs; others are directly emitted.

5. Consider the following statements regarding Eutrophication: 1. It is caused by enrichment of water bodies with nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus. 2. It leads to an increase in dissolved oxygen levels. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Eutrophication causes algal blooms, and subsequent decomposition depletes dissolved oxygen, not increase.
