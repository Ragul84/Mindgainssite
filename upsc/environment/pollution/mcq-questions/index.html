26. With reference to 'Eutrophication', consider the following: 1. It is caused by enrichment of water bodies with nitrates and phosphates. 2. It leads to oxygen depletion and fish kills. 3. It is a natural process, not accelerated by human activities. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Eutrophication is caused by nutrient enrichment (nitrates, phosphates) leading to oxygen depletion. It is accelerated by human activities like fertilizer runoff; hence statement 3 is false.

27. Consider the following pollutants: 1. Methane 2. Nitrous oxide 3. Black carbon 4. Sulphur hexafluoride Which of the above are considered 'Short-lived Climate Pollutants' (SLCPs)?

  1. 1, 2 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 4 only
  3. 2, 3 and 4 only
  4. 1, 2, 3 and 4

Answer: 1, 2 and 3 only

Explanation: SLCPs include methane, nitrous oxide, black carbon, and hydrofluorocarbons; sulphur hexafluoride is a long-lived greenhouse gas.

28. Which of the following is NOT a source of 'Ground-level Ozone' pollution?

  1. Vehicle exhaust
  2. Industrial emissions
  3. Stratospheric intrusion
  4. Photochemical reactions

Answer: Stratospheric intrusion

Explanation: Ground-level ozone is formed by photochemical reactions of NOx and VOCs from vehicles and industries; stratospheric ozone is beneficial and does not contribute to surface pollution.

29. Consider the following statements about 'Marine Plastic Pollution': 1. Microplastics are plastic particles larger than 5 mm. 2. Plastic waste in oceans can be ingested by marine organisms. 3. The Great Pacific Garbage Patch is a continuous solid mass of plastic. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1 and 2 only

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Microplastics are <5 mm; statement 1 is false. The Great Pacific Garbage Patch is a dispersed area of debris, not a solid mass; statement 3 is false. Ingestion is correct.

30. Which of the following is a proposed mechanism under the 'Montreal Protocol' to control emissions of substances that deplete the ozone layer?

  1. Kigali Amendment
  2. Paris Agreement
  3. Stockholm Convention
  4. Basel Convention

Answer: Kigali Amendment

Explanation: The Kigali Amendment (2016) to the Montreal Protocol phases down hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), which are not ozone depleting but potent greenhouse gases.

31. Consider the following statements regarding photochemical smog: 1. It is primarily formed in warm, sunny climates with high vehicular emissions. 2. It contains high concentrations of ozone and peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN). 3. It is a reducing smog, unlike London smog which is oxidizing. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Photochemical smog is oxidizing (not reducing), so statement 3 is false. Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

32. Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) is a measure of the amount of oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic waste in water. Which of the following water bodies is likely to have the highest BOD?

  1. A mountain stream
  2. A lake with high algal bloom
  3. An estuary with industrial discharge
  4. A deep ocean trench

Answer: An estuary with industrial discharge

Explanation: Industrial discharge contains high organic pollutants, leading to rapid microbial activity and higher BOD. Algal bloom can also cause high BOD but industrial discharge typically has more direct organic loading.

33. Which of the following pollutants is primarily responsible for the depletion of the stratospheric ozone layer?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Nitrous oxide
  3. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
  4. Sulfur hexafluoride

Answer: Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)

Explanation: CFCs release chlorine atoms in the stratosphere, which catalytically destroy ozone molecules. Nitrous oxide also contributes but is secondary.

34. Consider the following pairs: Pollutant : Associated Health Effect 1. Lead : Neurological damage 2. Mercury : Minamata disease 3. Asbestos : Asbestosis Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three are correctly matched: lead causes neurological issues, mercury causes Minamata disease, and asbestos causes asbestosis.

35. Which of the following is/are primary air pollutants? 1. Sulfur dioxide (SO2) 2. Ozone (O3) 3. Nitrogen oxides (NOx) Select the correct answer using the code given below.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Primary pollutants are emitted directly from sources. SO2 and NOx are primary; ozone is a secondary pollutant formed in the atmosphere.

36. Noise pollution is measured in decibels (dB). According to the World Health Organization, what is the recommended maximum noise level for residential areas during nighttime to avoid sleep disturbance?

  1. 30 dB
  2. 45 dB
  3. 55 dB
  4. 70 dB

Answer: 45 dB

Explanation: WHO recommends below 30 dB for sleep, but for residential areas nighttime limit is 45 dB to avoid disturbance.

37. Microplastics are a major concern in marine pollution. Which of the following statements is/are correct? 1. Microplastics can adsorb toxic chemicals from the surrounding water. 2. They are biodegradable and break down completely in ocean water. 3. They can enter the food chain through filter-feeding organisms. Select the correct answer using the code given below.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Microplastics are not biodegradable (persist for long) so statement 2 is false. Statements 1 and 3 are correct.

38. Thermal pollution is often caused by discharge of heated water from power plants. Which of the following is a likely consequence of thermal pollution in a river?

  1. Increase in dissolved oxygen levels
  2. Decrease in metabolic rate of aquatic organisms
  3. Thermal stratification leading to reduced mixing
  4. Increase in biodiversity due to warmer conditions

Answer: Thermal stratification leading to reduced mixing

Explanation: Heated water reduces dissolved oxygen and can cause thermal stratification, lowering vertical mixing. It does not increase biodiversity or decrease metabolic rate.

39. Indoor air pollution is a major health risk in rural areas of developing countries. Which of the following is the most significant contributor?

  1. Volatile organic compounds from paints
  2. Radon gas from building materials
  3. Smoke from burning biomass for cooking
  4. Mold and dampness

Answer: Smoke from burning biomass for cooking

Explanation: Burning biomass (wood, dung) in traditional stoves releases high levels of particulate matter and toxic gases, causing substantial indoor air pollution.

40. Which of the following statements about E-waste (Management) Rules, 2016 in India is correct?

  1. They apply only to manufacturers and not to consumers
  2. They mandate Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)
  3. They ban the import of all e-waste
  4. They exempt small-scale producers from compliance

Answer: They mandate Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)

Explanation: The rules establish EPR, making producers responsible for collection and recycling. They apply to producers, consumers, and dismantlers, and do not ban imports entirely.

41. Consider the following statements regarding the National Clean Air Programme (NCAP): 1. It aims to achieve a 40% reduction in PM2.5 concentration by 2026 compared to 2017 levels. 2. It covers both non-attainment cities and rural areas with high pollution. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: NCAP targets a 20-30% reduction by 2024, revised to 40% by 2026, but only for non-attainment cities, not rural areas.

42. Which of the following pollutants is primarily responsible for the formation of 'brown cloud' over the Indo-Gangetic Plain?

  1. Sulphur dioxide
  2. Black carbon
  3. Ozone
  4. Lead

Answer: Black carbon

Explanation: Black carbon from incomplete combustion absorbs sunlight and contributes to the brownish haze, especially in the Indo-Gangetic Plain.

43. Consider the following pairs: Pollutant - Major Source 1. Mercury - Coal-fired power plants 2. Arsenic - Pesticide runoff 3. Nitrous oxide - Agricultural fertilizers Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Mercury is emitted from coal combustion; nitrous oxide from fertilizers; arsenic in groundwater is geogenic, not primarily from pesticide runoff.

44. The term 'PM2.5' refers to particulate matter that can penetrate deep into the lungs. Which of the following is the most significant natural source of PM2.5 in India?

  1. Volcanic eruptions
  2. Sea salt spray
  3. Dust storms from arid regions
  4. Forest fires

Answer: Dust storms from arid regions

Explanation: Dust storms from the Thar Desert and other arid regions are a major natural source of PM2.5 in India, especially in northern states.

45. Which of the following is a secondary pollutant formed in the atmosphere through photochemical reactions?

  1. Carbon monoxide
  2. Sulphur dioxide
  3. Peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN)
  4. Ammonia

Answer: Peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN)

Explanation: PAN is a secondary pollutant formed when volatile organic compounds and nitrogen oxides react in sunlight.

46. Consider the following statements regarding the 'Polluter Pays Principle' in Indian environmental law: 1. It is explicitly mentioned in the Environment Protection Act, 1986. 2. It was applied by the Supreme Court in the Oleum Gas Leak case. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: The principle is not explicitly in the EPA 1986 but was applied by the Supreme Court in M.C. Mehta vs Union of India (Oleum Gas Leak case).

47. Which of the following is the most effective method to control emissions of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) from industrial sources?

  1. Electrostatic precipitators
  2. Carbon adsorption
  3. Cyclone separators
  4. Wet scrubbers

Answer: Carbon adsorption

Explanation: Carbon adsorption traps VOCs on activated carbon surfaces, making it highly effective for controlling these gaseous pollutants.

48. Consider the following: 1. Eutrophication 2. Biomagnification 3. Ocean acidification Which of the above is/are directly caused by nitrogen and phosphorus pollution from agricultural runoff?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Eutrophication is directly caused by nutrient runoff; biomagnification occurs for persistent pollutants like mercury, not typically N and P; ocean acidification is from CO2.

49. Which of the following is the primary reason for the high concentration of ground-level ozone in urban areas during summer?

  1. Increased vehicular emissions of nitrogen oxides
  2. Higher humidity levels
  3. Reduced wind speed
  4. Increased solar radiation

Answer: Increased solar radiation

Explanation: Ground-level ozone forms when NOx and VOCs react in the presence of sunlight; stronger solar radiation in summer accelerates this photochemical reaction.

50. Consider the following statements regarding the 'Bhopal Gas Tragedy' (1984): 1. The primary toxic gas released was methyl isocyanate (MIC). 2. The disaster led to the enactment of the Environment Protection Act, 1986. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: MIC was the main gas released, and the tragedy directly prompted the Indian government to pass the comprehensive Environment Protection Act in 1986.
