Protected Areas MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Protected Areas questions to revise the UPSC Environment syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Consider the following statements regarding Biosphere Reserves in India: 1. They are designated solely under the UNESCO Man and Biosphere Programme. 2. They consist of a legally protected core zone, a buffer zone, and a transition zone. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Biosphere Reserves are designated under UNESCO's MAB Programme, but India also has its own national designation. The core zone is legally protected, but the buffer and transition zones are not necessarily legally protected.

2. Which of the following is the primary legal basis for the establishment of 'Community Reserves' in India?

  1. Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972
  2. Forest Conservation Act, 1980
  3. Environment Protection Act, 1986
  4. Biological Diversity Act, 2002

Answer: Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972

Explanation: Community Reserves are a category of protected areas introduced by the 2002 amendment to the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, to involve local communities in conservation.

3. Consider the following pairs: Protected Area (State) 1. Kanger Ghati National Park (Chhattisgarh) 2. Mukurthi National Park (Tamil Nadu) 3. Ranthambore National Park (Rajasthan) Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Kanger Ghati is in Chhattisgarh, Mukurthi is in Tamil Nadu, and Ranthambore is in Rajasthan. All three pairs are correctly matched.

4. Which of the following is NOT a criterion for declaring an area as a 'Critical Wildlife Habitat' under the Forest Rights Act, 2006?

  1. It must be an inviolate area for wildlife conservation
  2. It must be part of a national park or wildlife sanctuary
  3. It must be determined by the State Government in consultation with the Gram Sabha
  4. It must be notified by the Central Government

Answer: It must be notified by the Central Government

Explanation: Critical Wildlife Habitats are determined by the State Government in consultation with the Gram Sabha, not notified by the Central Government. They are located within national parks and sanctuaries.

5. With reference to 'Conservation Reserves' in India, consider the following statements: 1. They are declared by the State Government. 2. They can be located on private or community lands. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Conservation Reserves are declared by the State Government and can be located on any land, including private and community lands, as per the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.

6. The 'Satkosia Tiger Reserve' is located in which of the following states?

  1. Odisha
  2. Madhya Pradesh
  3. Maharashtra
  4. Karnataka

Answer: Odisha

Explanation: Satkosia Tiger Reserve is located in Odisha, spanning across the Mahanadi River basin.

7. Consider the following statements regarding 'Elephant Reserves' in India: 1. They are notified under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972. 2. The first Elephant Reserve was established in Karnataka. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Elephant Reserves are notified under Project Elephant, not the Wildlife (Protection) Act. The first Elephant Reserve was the Mysore Elephant Reserve in Karnataka.

8. Which of the following protected areas is known for the conservation of the 'Great Indian Bustard'?

  1. Desert National Park
  2. Kaziranga National Park
  3. Keoladeo National Park
  4. Gir National Park

Answer: Desert National Park

Explanation: Desert National Park in Rajasthan is a key protected area for the critically endangered Great Indian Bustard.

9. Consider the following: 1. Gulf of Mannar Marine National Park 2. Sundarbans National Park 3. Rani Jhansi Marine National Park Which of the above is/are located in the Bay of Bengal?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 2 and 3 only

Explanation: Sundarbans National Park (West Bengal) and Rani Jhansi Marine National Park (Andaman & Nicobar) are in the Bay of Bengal. Gulf of Mannar is in the Arabian Sea.

10. Which of the following best describes the concept of 'One Health' in the context of protected area management?

  1. Integrating human, animal, and environmental health
  2. Focusing solely on wildlife disease control
  3. Promoting eco-tourism for health benefits
  4. Establishing health clinics in buffer zones

Answer: Integrating human, animal, and environmental health

Explanation: One Health is a collaborative approach recognizing the interconnection between human, animal, and environmental health, crucial for managing zoonotic diseases at the wildlife-livestock-human interface in protected areas.

11. Consider the following statements regarding Biosphere Reserves in India: 1. Biosphere Reserves are designated under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. 2. The core zone of a Biosphere Reserve is legally protected as a national park or wildlife sanctuary. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Biosphere Reserves are designated under UNESCO's Man and Biosphere Programme, not the Wildlife Protection Act. The core zone is legally protected as a national park or sanctuary.

12. Consider the following pairs: (Protected Area - State) 1. Koyna Wildlife Sanctuary - Maharashtra 2. Bhadra Wildlife Sanctuary - Karnataka 3. Pakhui Tiger Reserve - Arunachal Pradesh How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. All three
  4. None

Answer: All three

Explanation: Koyna in Maharashtra, Bhadra in Karnataka, and Pakhui in Arunachal Pradesh are all correctly matched.

13. Which of the following protected areas is NOT a tiger reserve?

  1. Nagarhole National Park
  2. Bandipur National Park
  3. Silent Valley National Park
  4. Periyar National Park

Answer: Silent Valley National Park

Explanation: Silent Valley National Park is not designated as a tiger reserve, while the others are part of Project Tiger.

14. Consider the following statements regarding Community Reserves under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972: 1. They are declared on private or community land. 2. They are aimed at protecting habitats outside the protected area network. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Community reserves are declared on private or community land to protect habitats and wildlife outside formal protected areas.

15. Which of the following is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and also a Biosphere Reserve?

  1. Sundarbans National Park
  2. Kaziranga National Park
  3. Both A and B
  4. Neither A nor B

Answer: Both A and B

Explanation: Both Sundarbans and Kaziranga are UNESCO World Heritage Sites and are part of designated Biosphere Reserves.

16. Consider the following: 1. Great Indian Bustard Sanctuary - Maharashtra 2. Desert National Park - Rajasthan 3. Rann of Kutch Wildlife Sanctuary - Gujarat Which of the above are located in the Thar Desert region?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 2 and 3 only

Explanation: Desert National Park (Rajasthan) and Rann of Kutch (Gujarat) are in the Thar Desert; Great Indian Bustard Sanctuary is in Maharashtra, not Thar.

17. Which of the following statements about Elephant Reserves in India is correct?

  1. They are established under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.
  2. The first elephant reserve was established in 2002.
  3. They are managed by the National Tiger Conservation Authority.
  4. None of the above

Answer: None of the above

Explanation: Elephant Reserves are established under Project Elephant (not a specific act), the first was Singhbhum in 2001, and they are managed by Project Elephant, not NTCA.

18. Consider the following pairs: (Ramsar Site - State) 1. Loktak Lake - Manipur 2. Chilika Lake - Odisha 3. Keoladeo National Park - Rajasthan 4. Wular Lake - Jammu & Kashmir How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. Only three
  4. All four

Answer: All four

Explanation: All four are correctly matched Ramsar sites in their respective states.

19. Which of the following is NOT a criterion for declaring a National Park under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972?

  1. It must have adequate ecological, faunal, floral, geomorphological, natural or zoological significance.
  2. It must be declared by the state government.
  3. It must be at least 500 square kilometers in area.
  4. It must be free from all forms of exploitation and habitation.

Answer: It must be at least 500 square kilometers in area.

Explanation: There is no minimum area requirement for a National Park; the other criteria are correct.

20. Consider the following statements regarding Conservation Reserves: 1. They are declared by the Central Government. 2. They are areas adjacent to national parks and sanctuaries to provide connectivity. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Conservation reserves are declared by state governments, not central. They serve as buffer zones or corridors adjacent to protected areas.

21. Consider the following statements about Conservation Reserves in India: 1. They are declared by the State Government under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. 2. They are established to protect landscapes, seascapes, and biodiversity outside national parks and sanctuaries. 3. They have a core zone where no human activity is allowed. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Conservation reserves are declared under Section 36A of WPA, 1972, and aim to protect biodiversity outside protected areas. They do not have a core zone; that concept applies to biosphere reserves.

22. With reference to Biosphere Reserves in India, consider the following statements: 1. They are recognized under the UNESCO Man and Biosphere Programme. 2. The core zone of a biosphere reserve is legally protected as a national park or sanctuary. 3. Buffer zone surrounds the core area and allows limited human activities. Which is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All statements are correct. Biosphere reserves are part of UNESCO MAB; core zone is legally protected as NP or sanctuary; buffer zone permits limited activities like research and education.

23. Consider the following pairs: 1. Ranthambore National Park – Rajasthan 2. Kaziranga National Park – Assam 3. Corbett National Park – Uttar Pradesh How many pairs are correctly matched?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. All three
  4. None

Answer: Only two

Explanation: Ranthambore (Rajasthan) and Kaziranga (Assam) are correct. Corbett is in Uttarakhand, not Uttar Pradesh. Hence two pairs are correctly matched.

24. With reference to the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972, which of the following categories of protected areas can be declared on private or community land? 1. Community Reserve 2. Conservation Reserve 3. Wildlife Sanctuary Select correct answer:

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Community and conservation reserves can be declared on private/community land. Wildlife sanctuaries are typically on government land.

25. Which of the following is/are UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India (Natural category)? 1. Sundarbans National Park 2. Nanda Devi and Valley of Flowers National Parks 3. Western Ghats

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1, 2 and 3
  4. None

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three are UNESCO Natural World Heritage Sites: Sundarbans (1987), Nanda Devi & Valley of Flowers (1988/2005), Western Ghats (2012).
