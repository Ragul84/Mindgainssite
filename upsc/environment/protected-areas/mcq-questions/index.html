26. Consider the following statements regarding Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZ) around protected areas: 1. They are declared under the Environment Protection Act, 1986. 2. Mining is completely prohibited in ESZs. 3. The National Board for Wildlife (NBWL) is the final authority on the notification of ESZs. Which of the above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: ESZs are notified under EPA, 1986. Mining is regulated, not completely prohibited. NBWL is the final approval authority for ESZ notification.

27. With reference to Tiger Reserves in India, consider the following: 1. They are created under Project Tiger, a centrally sponsored scheme. 2. The core area of a tiger reserve is legally constituted as a national park or sanctuary. 3. The buffer area is managed as a conservation reserve or community reserve. Which is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All statements are correct. Tiger reserves are part of Project Tiger; core is NP/sanctuary; buffer can be conservation or community reserve.

28. The 'Kuno National Park' recently in news is located in which state?

  1. Madhya Pradesh
  2. Rajasthan
  3. Gujarat
  4. Uttar Pradesh

Answer: Madhya Pradesh

Explanation: Kuno National Park is in Madhya Pradesh, known for cheetah reintroduction.

29. Consider the following statements about 'Critical Wildlife Habitats': 1. They are identified within Protected Areas under the Forest Rights Act, 2006. 2. They are areas where rights of forest dwellers can be modified or restricted with their consent. 3. The process of notification requires approval from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. Which is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All statements are correct. Critical wildlife habitats are defined under FRA 2006; rights can be modified with consent; notification requires MoEFCC approval.

30. Which of the following categories of protected areas in India have the highest level of legal protection?

  1. National Park
  2. Wildlife Sanctuary
  3. Biosphere Reserve
  4. Conservation Reserve

Answer: National Park

Explanation: National parks have the highest legal protection under the Wildlife Protection Act, with strict prohibitions on human activities and rights.

31. Consider the following statements regarding Biosphere Reserves in India: 1. They are designated solely under the UNESCO Man and Biosphere Programme. 2. They consist of a core zone, a buffer zone, and a transition zone. 3. They are legally protected under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 2 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Biosphere Reserves are designated under UNESCO's MAB Programme and have three zones, but they are not legally protected under the Wildlife Protection Act; that applies to national parks and sanctuaries.

32. With reference to ‘Community Reserves’ in India, which of the following statements is/are correct? 1. They are created under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. 2. They can be declared on private or community land. 3. They have a higher degree of protection than a National Park.

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Community Reserves are created under the Wildlife Protection Act (amended 2002) on private/community land, but they offer less protection than National Parks or Sanctuaries.

33. Which of the following is the primary legal basis for declaring a ‘Critical Wildlife Habitat’ in India?

  1. Wildlife Protection Act, 1972
  2. Forest Conservation Act, 1980
  3. Environment Protection Act, 1986
  4. Biological Diversity Act, 2002

Answer: Wildlife Protection Act, 1972

Explanation: Critical Wildlife Habitats are designated within National Parks and Sanctuaries under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972, particularly for inviolate areas for tribal communities.

34. Consider the following pairs: Protected Area – State: 1. Ranthambore Tiger Reserve – Rajasthan; 2. Periyar Tiger Reserve – Kerala; 3. Kaziranga National Park – Assam. How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

  1. Only one
  2. Only two
  3. All three
  4. None

Answer: All three

Explanation: All three pairs are correctly matched: Ranthambore in Rajasthan, Periyar in Kerala, and Kaziranga in Assam.

35. Which of the following is NOT a criterion for declaring a ‘Marine Protected Area’ (MPA) in India?

  1. Presence of coral reefs
  2. Breeding grounds for marine species
  3. High density of human settlements
  4. Unique or rare marine ecosystems

Answer: High density of human settlements

Explanation: MPAs are declared to conserve marine biodiversity, not based on human settlement density; high human density often conflicts with conservation goals.

36. In the context of Indian forest conservation, what is the primary difference between a ‘Conservation Reserve’ and a ‘Community Reserve’?

  1. Conservation Reserves are larger in area
  2. Community Reserves involve voluntary participation of local communities
  3. Conservation Reserves are only for tigers
  4. Community Reserves are declared by the Central Government

Answer: Community Reserves involve voluntary participation of local communities

Explanation: Community Reserves are declared on private or community land with community consent, while Conservation Reserves are on government land linking protected areas.

37. Consider the following statements: 1. A ‘Tiger Reserve’ in India always includes a core area that is a National Park or Sanctuary. 2. The buffer zone of a Tiger Reserve is managed as a multiple-use area. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Tiger Reserves have a core (National Park/Sanctuary) and a buffer zone allowing regulated human activities, as per Project Tiger guidelines.

38. Which of the following is the largest protected area (by area) in India that is a single continuous unit?

  1. Hemis National Park
  2. Great Himalayan National Park
  3. Kutch Desert Wildlife Sanctuary
  4. Gir National Park

Answer: Kutch Desert Wildlife Sanctuary

Explanation: Kutch Desert Wildlife Sanctuary in Gujarat is the largest wildlife sanctuary in India by area, covering over 7500 sq km.

39. With reference to ‘Elephant Reserves’ in India, which of the following statements is correct?

  1. They are legally notified under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972
  2. They are established under Project Elephant
  3. They have a core zone and a buffer zone like Tiger Reserves
  4. They are exclusively located in southern India

Answer: They are established under Project Elephant

Explanation: Elephant Reserves are established under Project Elephant, a centrally sponsored scheme, but they lack statutory backing under the Wildlife Protection Act.

40. Consider the following: 1. National Park; 2. Wildlife Sanctuary; 3. Biosphere Reserve. Which of the above is/are considered ‘Protected Areas’ under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 1 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries are legally defined under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972; Biosphere Reserves are not statutory under this Act.

41. Consider the following statements about Biosphere Reserves in India: 1. They are designated by UNESCO under the Man and Biosphere Programme. 2. They are legally protected under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. 3. The core zone is free from human interference. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Biosphere Reserves are designated by UNESCO, and core zones have minimal human activity, but they are not statutory under the Wildlife Protection Act.

42. Consider the following: 1. National Parks 2. Wildlife Sanctuaries 3. Conservation Reserves Which of the above are declared under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three categories are established under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972: National Parks (Section 35), Sanctuaries (Section 18), Conservation Reserves (Section 36A).

43. Which of the following pairs of Tiger Reserves and their states is/are correctly matched? 1. Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam – Andhra Pradesh 2. Satpura – Madhya Pradesh 3. Buxa – West Bengal Select the correct answer using the code below:

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam (Andhra Pradesh/Telangana), Satpura (Madhya Pradesh), Buxa (West Bengal) are all correctly matched.

44. Consider the following statements regarding Community Reserves: 1. They are declared under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. 2. They are declared over private or community lands. 3. They are aimed at protecting landscapes and seascapes outside protected areas. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1, 2 and 3
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Community Reserves are notified under WPA 1972 (Section 36C) on private/community land to protect areas outside national parks and sanctuaries.

45. Which of the following are internationally recognized designations for protected areas? 1. World Heritage Sites 2. Ramsar Sites 3. Biosphere Reserves 4. Important Bird Areas (IBAs) Select the correct answer using the code below:

  1. 1, 2, 3 only
  2. 1, 3, 4 only
  3. 2, 3, 4 only
  4. 1, 2, 3, 4

Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4

Explanation: All four are internationally recognized: World Heritage Sites (UNESCO), Ramsar Sites (Ramsar Convention), Biosphere Reserves (UNESCO), IBAs (BirdLife).

46. Consider the following: 1. Kaziranga National Park – Assam 2. Manas National Park – Assam 3. Dudhwa National Park – Uttar Pradesh 4. Valmiki National Park – Bihar Which of the above are Tiger Reserves?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1, 2, 3 only
  3. 1, 2, 4 only
  4. 1, 2, 3, 4

Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4

Explanation: All four are designated as Tiger Reserves under Project Tiger: Kaziranga, Manas (Assam), Dudhwa (UP), Valmiki (Bihar).

47. Which of the following statements about 'Critical Tiger Habitats' is/are correct? 1. They are declared under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972. 2. They are areas within National Parks where tigers breed. 3. They are inviolate for human activities except research and regulated tourism. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Critical Tiger Habitats (core areas) are declared under WPA, located within tiger reserves, and are off-limits for human activities except research and regulated tourism.

48. Consider the following pairs of Protected Area and its location (state): 1. Silent Valley National Park – Kerala 2. Gir National Park – Gujarat 3. Bandipur National Park – Karnataka 4. Kanger Ghati National Park – Chhattisgarh Which of the above are correctly matched?

  1. 1, 2, 3 only
  2. 1, 2, 4 only
  3. 1, 3, 4 only
  4. 1, 2, 3, 4

Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4

Explanation: All four national parks are correctly matched: Silent Valley (Kerala), Gir (Gujarat), Bandipur (Karnataka), Kanger Ghati (Chhattisgarh).

49. Consider the following statements about 'Sacred Groves': 1. They are patches of forest protected by local communities for religious reasons. 2. They are legally recognized as a category of protected areas under the Wildlife Protection Act. 3. They act as refugia for rare and endemic species. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Sacred Groves are community-protected forests for religious reasons and serve as biodiversity refugia, but are not a statutory category under the Wildlife Protection Act.

50. Consider the following statements regarding Conservation Reserves under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972: 1. They can be declared on any land, including private or community land. 2. The consent of the owner is required before declaration. 3. They are managed by the State Forest Department with an advisory committee. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Conservation Reserves under WPA 1972 can be on private/community land with owner consent, and are managed by the Forest Department with an advisory committee.
