26. Under the Forest Conservation Act, 1980, the state government requires prior approval from the central government for: 1. Leasing of forest land to a private entity. 2. Assignment of forest land to any person not under government control. 3. Clearance of forest land for mining purposes. Select the correct answer using the code below:

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Section 2 of the Forest Conservation Act requires central approval for any non-forest use including leasing, assigning, or clearing forest land for mining or any other purpose.

27. The 'Biological Diversity Act, 2002' established which of the following institutions? 1. National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) 2. State Biodiversity Boards (SBBs) 3. Biodiversity Management Committees (BMCs) Select the correct answer using the code below:

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: The Act creates a three-tier structure: NBA at national level, SBBs at state level, and BMCs at local level to manage biodiversity.

28. Consider the following statements about the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981: 1. It was enacted under Article 253 of the Constitution to implement decisions of the Stockholm Conference. 2. It establishes the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs). 3. The Act covers noise pollution as a form of air pollution. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: The Act was passed to implement the Stockholm Conference, creates CPCB/SPCBs, and defines air pollution to include noise under the definition of 'air pollutant'.

29. Which of the following Acts in India provides for the 'Eco-Sensitive Zones' (ESZs) around protected areas?

  1. The Wildlife Protection Act, 1972
  2. The Environment Protection Act, 1986
  3. The Forest Conservation Act, 1980
  4. The National Green Tribunal Act, 2010

Answer: The Environment Protection Act, 1986

Explanation: ESZs are notified under the Environment Protection Act, 1986 through guidelines and notifications, not under the Wildlife Protection Act or Forest Conservation Act.

30. Under the 'Public Liability Insurance Act, 1991', which of the following is mandatory for industries handling hazardous substances?

  1. Obtaining environmental clearance from the Ministry of Environment
  2. Taking out insurance policies to cover liability for accidents
  3. Conducting a social impact assessment before establishment
  4. Setting up a grievance redressal committee for local communities

Answer: Taking out insurance policies to cover liability for accidents

Explanation: The Act makes it compulsory for industries handling hazardous substances to take out insurance policies to provide immediate relief to victims of accidents.

31. Consider the following statements regarding the Environment Protection Act, 1986: 1. It empowers the central government to take measures to protect and improve the environment. 2. It provides for the establishment of environmental laboratories. 3. It was enacted in the wake of the Bhopal Gas Tragedy. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: The Act was enacted after the Bhopal disaster, empowers the central government, and allows establishment of environmental laboratories under Section 12.

32. Under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972, which schedule includes animals that may be hunted with permission if they become dangerous to human life or property?

  1. Schedule I
  2. Schedule II
  3. Schedule III
  4. Schedule IV

Answer: Schedule II

Explanation: Schedule II provides for conditional hunting, while Schedule I grants absolute protection.

33. The 'precautionary principle' was first explicitly recognized by the Supreme Court of India in which case?

  1. M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (Oleum Gas Leak)
  2. Vellore Citizens' Welfare Forum v. Union of India
  3. Indian Council for Enviro-Legal Action v. Union of India
  4. Rural Litigation and Entitlement Kendra v. State of U.P.

Answer: Vellore Citizens' Welfare Forum v. Union of India

Explanation: The Supreme Court in Vellore Citizens (1996) invoked the precautionary principle as part of environmental law.

34. Consider the following statements about the National Green Tribunal (NGT): 1. It was established under the NGT Act, 2010. 2. It has original jurisdiction over environmental disputes. 3. Its orders are binding but can be appealed only to the Supreme Court. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: NGT orders can be appealed to the High Court under Section 22 of the NGT Act, not solely to the Supreme Court.

35. The Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) notification, 2019 classifies coastal areas into how many categories?

  1. Three
  2. Four
  3. Five
  4. Six

Answer: Four

Explanation: CRZ 2019 has four categories: CRZ-I (ecologically sensitive), CRZ-II (built-up), CRZ-III (rural), and CRZ-IV (water areas).

36. Under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, which authority is established at the national level to regulate access to biological resources?

  1. National Biodiversity Authority
  2. Biodiversity Management Committee
  3. State Biodiversity Board
  4. National Green Tribunal

Answer: National Biodiversity Authority

Explanation: The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) is created under Section 8 of the Act.

37. Which of the following is NOT a principle recognized under Indian environmental jurisprudence?

  1. Polluter pays principle
  2. Sustainable development
  3. Strict liability
  4. Absolute liability

Answer: Strict liability

Explanation: Indian courts replaced strict liability with absolute liability in the M.C. Mehta (Oleum Gas) case, so strict liability is not followed.

38. Consider the following statements regarding the Forest Conservation Act, 1980: 1. It requires prior approval of the central government for diversion of forest land for non-forest purposes. 2. It applies to all forests, including those owned by private individuals. 3. The Act was amended in 2023 to include climate change mitigation provisions. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: The Act applies only to government and notified forests, not private forests; the 2023 amendment focused on compensatory afforestation, not climate change mitigation.

39. The 'Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981' was enacted primarily to implement the decisions of which international conference?

  1. Stockholm Conference, 1972
  2. Rio Earth Summit, 1992
  3. Kyoto Protocol, 1997
  4. Paris Agreement, 2015

Answer: Stockholm Conference, 1972

Explanation: The Air Act was passed after the UN Conference on Human Environment (Stockholm) in 1972.

40. Under the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974, which is the main regulatory body at the central level?

  1. Central Pollution Control Board
  2. Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change
  3. National Green Tribunal
  4. Central Water Commission

Answer: Central Pollution Control Board

Explanation: CPCB was established under the Water Act as the central pollution control authority.

41. Consider the following statements about the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972: 1. It provides for the constitution of the National Board for Wildlife. 2. It prohibits trade in all wild animals and their derivatives. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is correct as the National Board for Wildlife is a statutory body under the Act. Statement 2 is false because trade is prohibited only for species listed in Schedules I to IV.

42. Consider the following statements regarding the Forest Conservation Act, 1980: 1. It requires prior approval of the central government for diversion of forest land to non-forest purposes. 2. The Act applies to all forests, regardless of ownership. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements are correct; the Act mandates central approval for any forest land diversion and covers any land recorded as forest in government records.

43. Under the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981, which of the following is/are functions of the Central Pollution Control Board? 1. Advise the central government on matters concerning prevention of air pollution. 2. Establish ambient air quality standards. Select the correct answer using the code below.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: The CPCB advises the central government on air pollution matters and is empowered to set ambient air quality standards under the Act.

44. The National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 deals with which of the following? 1. Civil cases relating to environmental protection. 2. Criminal cases involving environmental offences. Select the correct answer using the code below.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: The NGT has jurisdiction over civil cases related to environmental issues; criminal cases remain with regular courts.

45. Consider the following about the Biological Diversity Act, 2002: 1. It provides for the establishment of National Biodiversity Authority. 2. It requires prior approval for accessing biological resources for commercial purposes. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements are correct; the NBA is a statutory body and prior approval is mandatory for commercial access to biological resources.

46. Which of the following notifications under the Environment Protection Act, 1986 primarily deals with coastal regulation?

  1. Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019
  2. Wetlands Conservation Rules
  3. Solid Waste Management Rules
  4. E-Waste Management Rules

Answer: Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019

Explanation: The CRZ Notification is issued under the EPA to regulate activities along coastal stretches.

47. Consider the following statements about the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974: 1. The Act applies to all streams, inland waters, and coastal waters. 2. The Act provides for the constitution of State Water Pollution Control Boards. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is false because the Act does not cover coastal waters; statement 2 is true as State Boards are established under the Act.

48. The concept of 'Public Trust Doctrine' has been applied by Indian courts in which of the following landmark environmental cases?

  1. M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath
  2. Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan
  3. Olga Tellis v. Bombay Municipal Corporation
  4. Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala

Answer: M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath

Explanation: In M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath, the Supreme Court invoked the public trust doctrine to protect natural resources from private exploitation.

49. Consider the following statements regarding the Environment Protection Act, 1986: 1. It was enacted in the aftermath of the Bhopal Gas Tragedy. 2. It is an umbrella legislation covering all aspects of environment. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements are correct; the EPA was passed after the Bhopal disaster and serves as framework legislation for environmental protection.

50. Under which of the following Acts are 'Ecologically Sensitive Areas' (ESAs) officially declared in India?

  1. Wildlife Protection Act, 1972
  2. Forest Conservation Act, 1980
  3. Environment Protection Act, 1986
  4. Biological Diversity Act, 2002

Answer: Environment Protection Act, 1986

Explanation: ESAs are notified under the Environment Protection Act, 1986 by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
