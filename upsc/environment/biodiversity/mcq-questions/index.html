Biodiversity MCQs with answers

26. Which of the following are examples of ex-situ conservation? 1. Botanical gardens, 2. National parks, 3. Gene banks. Select the correct answer using the code given below:

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: National parks are in-situ conservation; botanical gardens and gene banks are ex-situ.

27. Which of the following is considered a keystone species in Indian forests?

  1. Tiger
  2. Elephant
  3. Fig tree
  4. Lotus

Answer: Fig tree

Explanation: Fig trees provide year-round fruit, supporting many animals, and are classic keystone species.

28. Consider the following statements regarding the IUCN Red List: 1. Critically Endangered species have a very high risk of extinction in the wild. 2. Vulnerable species are considered less threatened than Near Threatened. 3. Data Deficient means not enough data to assess risk. Which of the statements given above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Vulnerable is more threatened than Near Threatened, so statement 2 is false; statements 1 and 3 are correct.

29. Which of the following is an invasive alien species in India?

  1. Neem
  2. Lantana camara
  3. Sandalwood
  4. Mango

Answer: Lantana camara

Explanation: Lantana camara is a highly invasive exotic shrub that disrupts native ecosystems in India.

30. As per the Convention on Biological Diversity, which of the following are components of biodiversity? 1. Genetic diversity, 2. Species diversity, 3. Ecosystem diversity. Select the correct answer using the code given below:

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: The CBD defines biodiversity at three levels: genetic, species, and ecosystem diversity.

31. Consider the following statements about 'Endemic Species': 1. They are found exclusively in a particular geographic region. 2. They always have a high tolerance to environmental changes. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Endemic species are restricted to a specific area; statement 2 is false as they often have low tolerance to change.

32. Which of the following best describes a 'Keystone Species'?

  1. Species with highest biomass in an ecosystem
  2. Species that has a disproportionately large effect on its environment relative to its abundance
  3. Species that is the most numerous in a community
  4. Species that is at the top of the food chain

Answer: Species that has a disproportionately large effect on its environment relative to its abundance

Explanation: A keystone species has a large impact on ecosystem structure and function despite low abundance.

33. Consider the following pairs: Biodiversity Hotspot - Location: 1. Indo-Burma - India and Myanmar 2. Western Ghats - India 3. Sundaland - India and Sri Lanka Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Indo-Burma and Western Ghats are correctly matched; Sundaland does not include India or Sri Lanka.

34. Which of the following is NOT a designated zone of a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve?

  1. Core zone
  2. Buffer zone
  3. Transition zone
  4. Exploitation zone

Answer: Exploitation zone

Explanation: Biosphere reserves consist of core, buffer, and transition zones; exploitation zone is not a standard zone.

35. With reference to 'Genetic diversity', consider the following statements: 1. It refers to variation among individuals of the same species. 2. It enables species to adapt to changing environmental conditions. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements are correct; genetic diversity is the variety of genes within a species and aids adaptation.

36. The term 'Anthropocene' is used to describe:

  1. A geological epoch characterized by significant human impact on biodiversity and ecosystems
  2. A period of mass extinction caused solely by natural climate changes
  3. The era when early hominins first appeared
  4. A prolonged ice age period

Answer: A geological epoch characterized by significant human impact on biodiversity and ecosystems

Explanation: The Anthropocene denotes the current epoch where human activities dominate global environmental change.

37. Which of the following definitions best captures an 'Alien Invasive Species'?

  1. A species introduced outside its natural range that causes ecological or economic harm
  2. A species that migrates seasonally between different habitats
  3. A species that is native and restricted to a particular region
  4. A species that is exclusively cultivated for agricultural purposes

Answer: A species introduced outside its natural range that causes ecological or economic harm

Explanation: Invasive alien species are non-native organisms whose introduction threatens ecosystems, habitats, or species.

38. Consider the following statements about 'Ecological Niche': 1. It refers to the functional role and position of a species within its environment. 2. Two different species can occupy the exact same niche indefinitely without competition. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is correct; statement 2 is false due to the competitive exclusion principle.

39. The 'Red List of Threatened Species' is officially published by which organization?

  1. IUCN
  2. UNEP
  3. WWF
  4. CITES

Answer: IUCN

Explanation: The International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) compiles and publishes the Red List.

40. Which of the following is NOT a component of biodiversity as defined by the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)?

  1. Genetic diversity
  2. Species diversity
  3. Ecosystem diversity
  4. Cultural diversity

Answer: Cultural diversity

Explanation: CBD defines biodiversity as variability among living organisms including genetic, species, and ecosystem diversity, but not cultural diversity.

41. Consider the following statements about Keystone species: 1. They have a disproportionately large effect on their environment relative to their abundance. 2. Their removal can cause a cascade of changes in the ecosystem. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements correctly describe keystone species; they exert strong ecological influence and their loss often triggers cascading effects.

42. Which of the following is a biodiversity hotspot in India?

  1. Sundarbans
  2. Western Ghats
  3. Chilika Lake
  4. Gulf of Mannar

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats is one of the four biodiversity hotspots in India; Sundarbans and Chilika are Ramsar sites, Gulf of Mannar is a marine biosphere reserve.

43. The concept of 'Endemic species' refers to species that are:

  1. Found only in a particular geographic area
  2. Introduced from other regions
  3. Highly migratory
  4. Invasive in nature

Answer: Found only in a particular geographic area

Explanation: Endemic species are those that are native to and restricted to a specific area and not found naturally elsewhere.

44. Which of the following factors is primarily responsible for high biodiversity in tropical regions?

  1. High temperature and high rainfall
  2. Low temperature and high rainfall
  3. High temperature and low rainfall
  4. Low temperature and low rainfall

Answer: High temperature and high rainfall

Explanation: Stable high temperatures and abundant rainfall in tropics promote rapid speciation and support diverse life forms.

45. Consider the following statements regarding Biosphere Reserves: 1. They are designated by UNESCO under the Man and the Biosphere Programme. 2. They have three zones: core, buffer, and transition. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: UNESCO's MAB Programme designates biosphere reserves, which consist of core, buffer, and transition zones for conservation and sustainable use.

46. Which of the following is a 'Critically Endangered' bird species found in India?

  1. Great Indian Bustard
  2. Indian Peafowl
  3. Black-necked Crane
  4. Sarus Crane

Answer: Great Indian Bustard

Explanation: The Great Indian Bustard is listed as Critically Endangered; peafowl, black-necked crane, and sarus crane are not critically endangered.

47. The term 'Alien Invasive Species' refers to a species that:

  1. Is native to the region
  2. Has been introduced outside its natural range and causes harm
  3. Is extinct in the wild
  4. Forms the base of a food chain

Answer: Has been introduced outside its natural range and causes harm

Explanation: Alien invasive species are non-native species that, when introduced, cause ecological or economic harm to the new environment.

48. Consider the following statements about the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA): 1. It is a statutory body under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. 2. It regulates access to biological resources by foreigners. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: NBA is a statutory body established under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, under MoEFCC, and it regulates access to biological resources and associated knowledge.

49. Which of the following best describes the 'Edge Effect' in ecology?

  1. Increased biodiversity at the boundary of two ecosystems
  2. Decreased species richness in core areas
  3. Uniform distribution of species across habitats
  4. Complete isolation of ecosystem boundaries

Answer: Increased biodiversity at the boundary of two ecosystems

Explanation: Edge effect refers to the phenomenon where ecotones (transition zones) often support greater species diversity than adjacent interiors.

50. Consider the following statements regarding Biodiversity Hotspots: 1. A region must have at least 1,500 species of endemic vascular plants to qualify. 2. It must have lost at least 70% of its original primary vegetation. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both criteria are correct: ≥1500 endemic vascular plants and ≥70% habitat loss.
