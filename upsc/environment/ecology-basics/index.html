Ecology Basics MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Ecology Basics questions to revise the UPSC Environment syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Consider the following statements regarding ecological pyramids: 1. The pyramid of numbers in a parasitic food chain is always inverted. 2. The pyramid of biomass in the sea is generally inverted. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: In a parasitic food chain, one host supports many parasites, causing an inverted pyramid of numbers. In marine ecosystems, phytoplankton biomass is less than zooplankton, leading to an inverted pyramid of biomass.

2. Which of the following is the correct sequence of trophic levels in a detritus food chain?

  1. Detritus → Herbivores → Carnivores
  2. Detritus → Decomposers → Carnivores
  3. Detritus → Microconsumers → Predators
  4. Detritus → Detritivores → Carnivores

Answer: Detritus → Detritivores → Carnivores

Explanation: Detritus food chain begins with dead organic matter consumed by detritivores (e.g., earthworms), which are then eaten by carnivores.

3. Consider the following: 1. Net primary productivity 2. Gross primary productivity 3. Secondary productivity Which of the above represents the energy available to herbivores?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 3 only
  4. 1 and 3

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Net primary productivity (NPP) is the biomass remaining after plant respiration, which is available to herbivores. Gross primary productivity includes respiration, and secondary productivity is by consumers.

4. Which of the following pairs is/are correctly matched? Biome : Characteristic vegetation 1. Taiga : Coniferous forests 2. Tundra : Mosses and lichens 3. Tropical rainforest : Evergreen broadleaf trees

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Taiga is boreal coniferous forest, tundra has mosses and lichens, and tropical rainforest features evergreen broadleaf trees. All pairs are correct.

5. Consider the following statements regarding the carbon cycle: 1. Photosynthesis and respiration are the main biological processes involved. 2. Carbon is released into the atmosphere by volcanic eruptions. Which is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Photosynthesis fixes CO2, respiration releases it; volcanic eruptions also release CO2 from Earth's interior. Both statements are correct.

6. In an ecosystem, the term 'ecotone' refers to:

  1. The transition zone between two ecosystems
  2. The area with highest biodiversity
  3. The boundary between land and water
  4. The zone of stress for organisms

Answer: The transition zone between two ecosystems

Explanation: Ecotone is the transition area between adjacent ecosystems, often showing edge effect with unique species.

7. Which of the following is an example of commensalism?

  1. Orchid growing on a mango tree
  2. Lion and deer
  3. Tick on a dog
  4. Fungi and algae in lichen

Answer: Orchid growing on a mango tree

Explanation: Commensalism benefits one species without harming the other; orchid uses tree for support (epiphyte) while tree is unaffected.

8. Consider the following statements about the phosphorus cycle: 1. It is a sedimentary cycle. 2. The main reservoir is the atmosphere. Which is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Phosphorus cycle is sedimentary with the main reservoir in rocks, not the atmosphere. Only statement 1 is correct.

9. The concept of 'ecological niche' refers to:

  1. The physical place where an organism lives
  2. The functional role and position of an organism in its environment
  3. The total number of species in an area
  4. The range of temperature an organism can tolerate

Answer: The functional role and position of an organism in its environment

Explanation: Ecological niche encompasses an organism's functional role, habitat, resource use, and interactions, not just physical location or tolerance limits.

10. Consider the following pairs: Type of species interaction : Example 1. Competition : Two species of Paramecium in same culture 2. Predation : Tiger eating deer 3. Mutualism : Rhizobium in root nodules of legumes Which pairs are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: Gause's principle demonstrated competition in Paramecium; tiger-deer is predation; Rhizobium-legume mutualism. All pairs are correct.

11. Consider the following statements regarding ecological succession: I. Primary succession occurs on previously unoccupied sites. II. Secondary succession is faster than primary succession. III. The final stable community in succession is called climax community. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. I only
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: I, II and III

Explanation: All three statements are correct: primary succession starts on bare rock, secondary on existing soil (faster), and climax is the stable endpoint.

12. Which of the following correctly represents the flow of energy in an ecosystem?

  1. Unidirectional
  2. Bidirectional
  3. Cyclic
  4. Random

Answer: Unidirectional

Explanation: Energy flows unidirectionally from sun to producers to consumers and is lost as heat; it cannot be recycled.

13. Consider the following pairs: 1. Tropical rainforest - Broadleaf evergreen trees 2. Taiga - Coniferous forests 3. Tundra - Mosses and lichens Which of the pairs above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three pairs are correct: tropical rainforest has broadleaf evergreens, taiga conifers, tundra mosses/lichen.

14. Which of the following statements about keystone species is/are correct? I. They have a disproportionately large effect on their environment. II. Their removal causes significant changes in ecosystem structure. III. They are always top predators. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. I only
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: I and II only

Explanation: Keystone species have a large effect and their removal causes major changes, but they are not always top predators (e.g., sea stars).

15. In an ecosystem, the term 'biomass' refers to:

  1. The total weight of living organisms at a trophic level
  2. The total number of organisms at a trophic level
  3. The rate of production of organic matter
  4. The energy content of organic matter

Answer: The total weight of living organisms at a trophic level

Explanation: Biomass is the mass of living organisms; number is population, rate is productivity, energy content is calorific value.

16. Consider the following statements about the carbon cycle: I. Photosynthesis is the primary process that removes CO2 from the atmosphere. II. Respiration releases CO2 back into the atmosphere. III. Burning of fossil fuels adds CO2 to the atmosphere. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. I only
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: I, II and III

Explanation: All three statements are correct: photosynthesis fixes CO2, respiration releases it, and fossil fuel combustion adds more.

17. Which of the following is an example of a pioneer species in primary succession?

  1. Lichens
  2. Grasses
  3. Shrubs
  4. Trees

Answer: Lichens

Explanation: Lichens colonize bare rock first, breaking it down to form soil, making them pioneer species in primary succession.

18. The '10% law' of energy transfer in a food chain is associated with:

  1. Lindeman
  2. Tansley
  3. Odum
  4. Elton

Answer: Lindeman

Explanation: Raymond Lindeman proposed the trophic efficiency concept, often referred to as the 10% law.

19. Consider the following statements regarding ecosystems: I. An ecosystem is a functional unit of nature. II. Ecosystems can be natural or artificial. III. All ecosystems are self-sustaining in terms of energy. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. I only
  2. I and II only
  3. II and III only
  4. I, II and III

Answer: I and II only

Explanation: Ecosystems are functional units and can be artificial (e.g., aquarium), but not all are self-sustaining in energy; most rely on sun.

20. In the context of ecological pyramids, which of the following statements is/are correct? 1. The pyramid of numbers in a parasitic food chain is always inverted. 2. The pyramid of biomass in a terrestrial ecosystem is generally upright. 3. The pyramid of energy is always upright. Select the correct answer using the code given below:

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 2 and 3 only

Explanation: Pyramid of numbers in a parasitic food chain is inverted; pyramid of biomass is upright in terrestrial ecosystems; pyramid of energy is always upright.

21. Consider the following statements regarding ecological succession: 1. Hydrosere is the succession that takes place in a water body. 2. The climax community in a xerosere is always a forest. 3. Pioneer species in a lithosere are lichens. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Hydrosere is aquatic succession; lithosere pioneers are lichens; climax community in xerosere depends on climate, not always forest.

22. Which of the following best describes the concept of 'Ecological Niche'?

  1. The physical space occupied by an organism
  2. The functional role and position of a species in its environment
  3. The total number of species in a habitat
  4. The sum of all abiotic factors affecting a species

Answer: The functional role and position of a species in its environment

Explanation: Ecological niche includes the role, resource use, and interactions of a species, not just its habitat.

23. Consider the following pairs: 1. Primary productivity : Rate of biomass production by producers 2. Standing crop : Total biomass at a given time 3. Net primary productivity : Gross primary productivity minus respiration Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three definitions are standard ecological terms: primary productivity, standing crop, and net primary productivity.

24. In a food chain, which of the following organisms will have the highest concentration of a non-biodegradable pollutant?

  1. Phytoplankton
  2. Zooplankton
  3. Small fish
  4. Large fish

Answer: Large fish

Explanation: Biomagnification leads to highest pollutant concentration at the highest trophic level, here large fish.

25. Consider the following statements about 'keystone species': 1. Their removal causes significant changes in ecosystem structure. 2. They always occupy the top trophic level. 3. They have a disproportionately large effect on their environment relative to their abundance. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Keystone species have large impact despite low abundance; they need not be top predators.
