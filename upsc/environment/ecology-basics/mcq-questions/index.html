26. The '10% law' of energy transfer in a food chain is associated with which of the following?

  1. Lindeman's trophic efficiency
  2. Odum's ecological energetics
  3. Elton's pyramid of numbers
  4. Hutchinson's niche concept

Answer: Lindeman's trophic efficiency

Explanation: Lindeman's trophic efficiency rule states only about 10% of energy is transferred to the next trophic level.

27. Consider the following statements regarding 'ecotone': 1. It is a transition zone between two ecosystems. 2. It often contains species from both adjacent ecosystems. 3. It always has lower biodiversity than the adjacent ecosystems. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Ecotone is a transition zone with edge effect, often higher biodiversity (edge species), not always lower.

28. Which of the following ecological pyramids is commonly inverted?

  1. Pyramid of biomass in grassland
  2. Pyramid of energy in any ecosystem
  3. Pyramid of number in tree ecosystem
  4. Pyramid of biomass in aquatic ecosystem

Answer: Pyramid of biomass in aquatic ecosystem

Explanation: In aquatic ecosystems, the biomass of phytoplankton is often less than that of consumers, leading to an inverted pyramid of biomass.

29. A keystone species is characterized by:

  1. Always being a top predator
  2. Having a disproportionately large effect on the ecosystem relative to its abundance
  3. Possessing the highest biomass in the community
  4. Being the most numerous species

Answer: Having a disproportionately large effect on the ecosystem relative to its abundance

Explanation: A keystone species exerts a strong influence on ecosystem structure and function that is much greater than expected from its abundance.

30. Which of the following is an example of primary ecological succession?

  1. Regrowth after a forest fire
  2. Development on newly formed volcanic rock
  3. Recolonization of abandoned farmland
  4. Recovery after a flood

Answer: Development on newly formed volcanic rock

Explanation: Primary succession begins in lifeless areas like bare rock or newly cooled lava, where no soil exists initially.

31. Consider the following statements about food chains: 1. The grazing food chain begins with dead organic matter. 2. The detritus food chain begins with autotrophs. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Neither 1 nor 2

Explanation: The grazing chain starts with living plants (autotrophs), while the detritus chain starts with dead organic matter; both are often linked in ecosystems.

32. The ecological niche of an organism refers to:

  1. Its physical habitat only
  2. Its trophic level in the food web
  3. Its role and position in the ecosystem including interactions
  4. The total number of individuals in its population

Answer: Its role and position in the ecosystem including interactions

Explanation: Niche encompasses the functional role of an organism, including its habitat, resource use, and relationships with other species.

33. Net Primary Productivity (NPP) is equal to:

  1. Gross Primary Productivity + Respiration
  2. Gross Primary Productivity - Respiration
  3. Secondary Productivity - Respiration
  4. Total biomass of producers

Answer: Gross Primary Productivity - Respiration

Explanation: NPP is the energy remaining after autotrophs meet their respiratory needs, representing the biomass available to consumers.

34. Which of the following biogeochemical cycles is classified as sedimentary?

  1. Carbon cycle
  2. Nitrogen cycle
  3. Phosphorus cycle
  4. Oxygen cycle

Answer: Phosphorus cycle

Explanation: The phosphorus cycle lacks a gaseous phase and its main reservoir is the Earth's crust, making it a sedimentary cycle.

35. A population at its carrying capacity exhibits:

  1. Exponential growth
  2. Zero net growth
  3. Maximum intrinsic rate of increase
  4. Declining birth rate only

Answer: Zero net growth

Explanation: At carrying capacity, birth and death rates are equal, resulting in zero population growth.

36. Consider the following statements: 1. Energy flow in an ecosystem is unidirectional. 2. Nutrient flow in an ecosystem is cyclic. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Energy flows linearly from sun to decomposers, while nutrients are recycled within the ecosystem.

37. Which of the following statements correctly distinguishes a food chain from a food web?

  1. A food chain represents a single pathway of energy flow, while a food web represents interconnected multiple pathways.
  2. A food web is a linear sequence of organisms, while a food chain is a complex network of trophic interactions.
  3. Both food chain and food web represent the same concept but differ in the number of organisms involved.
  4. A food chain exists only in aquatic ecosystems, whereas a food web exists only in terrestrial ecosystems.

Answer: A food chain represents a single pathway of energy flow, while a food web represents interconnected multiple pathways.

Explanation: A food chain is a linear sequence of energy transfer, while a food web consists of interconnected food chains, showing multiple feeding relationships.

38. Consider the following statements regarding ecological pyramids: 1. The pyramid of numbers in a forest ecosystem is always upright. 2. The pyramid of biomass in an aquatic ecosystem is often inverted. 3. The pyramid of energy is always upright. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 2 and 3 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is false (forest pyramid may be inverted, e.g., many insects on one tree); statement 2 is true (e.g., zooplankton biomass > phytoplankton); statement 3 is true (energy decreases at each trophic level).

39. Which of the following best describes the term 'ecological niche'?

  1. The physical space occupied by a species in its habitat
  2. The functional role and position of a species within its ecosystem
  3. The total number of individuals of a species in an area
  4. The geographical range of a species' distribution

Answer: The functional role and position of a species within its ecosystem

Explanation: Ecological niche refers to the role a species plays, including its habitat, resource use, and interactions with other species, not just its physical space.

40. In the context of biogeochemical cycles, which of the following processes is primarily responsible for returning nitrogen to the atmosphere?

  1. Nitrification
  2. Denitrification
  3. Ammonification
  4. Nitrogen fixation

Answer: Denitrification

Explanation: Denitrification converts nitrates/nitrites into gaseous nitrogen (N2), which is released into the atmosphere, completing the nitrogen cycle.

41. Consider the following pairs: 1. Pioneer community - Lichens 2. Climax community - Oak forest 3. Sere - Entire sequence of successional stages Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three are correct: lichens are typical pioneers in primary succession; oak forest can be a climax community in certain regions; sere refers to the full series of successional stages.

42. Which of the following statements about detritus food chain is correct?

  1. It begins with dead organic matter and involves decomposers and detritivores.
  2. It is the primary food chain in all terrestrial ecosystems.
  3. It does not involve any energy loss at trophic levels.
  4. It is independent of solar energy.

Answer: It begins with dead organic matter and involves decomposers and detritivores.

Explanation: Detritus food chain starts with dead organic matter (detritus) and is broken down by decomposers and detritivores; it is not the primary chain in all ecosystems and does lose energy.

43. What is the term for the total amount of organic matter produced by plants through photosynthesis per unit area per unit time?

  1. Net primary productivity
  2. Gross primary productivity
  3. Standing crop
  4. Biomass

Answer: Gross primary productivity

Explanation: Gross primary productivity (GPP) is the total rate of photosynthesis including energy used for respiration; net primary productivity (NPP) is GPP minus respiration.

44. Which of the following factors is LEAST likely to cause an increase in the carrying capacity of a habitat for a herbivore population?

  1. Increase in rainfall leading to more vegetation
  2. Introduction of a predator that controls herbivore numbers
  3. Reduction in competition from other herbivores
  4. Enrichment of soil nutrients increasing plant growth

Answer: Introduction of a predator that controls herbivore numbers

Explanation: Carrying capacity is the maximum population size an environment can sustain; introducing a predator reduces population but does not increase the habitat's ability to support more herbivores.

45. In an ecosystem, which trophic level typically has the highest amount of energy available?

  1. Secondary consumers
  2. Primary consumers
  3. Producers
  4. Tertiary consumers

Answer: Producers

Explanation: Energy decreases at each trophic level; producers (autotrophs) have the most energy because they capture solar energy directly.

46. Consider the following statements regarding ecological succession: 1. Secondary succession occurs on previously vegetated areas where soil is present. 2. Primary succession takes longer to reach climax stage than secondary succession. 3. Hydrarch succession occurs in dry areas with little water. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is correct; statement 2 is correct (primary starts from bare rock, no soil); statement 3 is false (hydrarch is in water bodies, xerarch is dry). So correct are 1 and 2.

47. Consider the following statements regarding ecological pyramids: 1. The pyramid of biomass in a terrestrial ecosystem is generally inverted. 2. The pyramid of numbers in a parasitic food chain is always upright. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Neither 1 nor 2

Explanation: The pyramid of biomass in terrestrial ecosystems is generally upright, not inverted. The pyramid of numbers in a parasitic food chain is typically inverted.

48. Consider the following statements about keystone species: 1. They have a disproportionately large effect on their environment relative to their abundance. 2. Their removal can cause a cascade of changes in the ecosystem. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Keystone species exert high influence despite low biomass, and their removal often triggers significant ecosystem shifts.

49. In the context of ecology, what does 'primary succession' refer to?

  1. Succession occurring in an area that previously had vegetation but was disturbed
  2. Succession starting on a bare rock or newly formed land without soil
  3. The annual cycle of growth and decay in a grassland
  4. The replacement of one forest type by another due to climate change

Answer: Succession starting on a bare rock or newly formed land without soil

Explanation: Primary succession begins on substrates devoid of soil, such as bare rock or volcanic lava, unlike secondary succession which starts on existing soil.

50. Which of the following is an example of a 'commensalism' relationship?

  1. A lion feeding on a zebra
  2. A remora fish attaching to a shark for transport
  3. A fungus and algae forming a lichen
  4. A tapeworm living in a human intestine

Answer: A remora fish attaching to a shark for transport

Explanation: Commensalism benefits one species (remora gets transport) while the other (shark) is neither helped nor harmed. The other options are predation, mutualism, and parasitism respectively.
