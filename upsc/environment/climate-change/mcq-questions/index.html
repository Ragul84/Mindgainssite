26. Consider the following pairs: (Climate feedback) – (Effect on warming) 1. Water vapor feedback – Positive 2. Ice-albedo feedback – Negative 3. Cloud feedback – Uncertain sign Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Water vapor feedback is positive; ice-albedo is positive (not negative); cloud feedback has uncertain sign. Hence only 1 and 3 are correct.

27. Which of the following greenhouse gases has the highest global warming potential (GWP) over a 100-year time horizon?

  1. Methane (CH4)
  2. Nitrous oxide (N2O)
  3. Sulfur hexafluoride (SF6)
  4. Carbon dioxide (CO2)

Answer: Sulfur hexafluoride (SF6)

Explanation: SF6 has an extremely high GWP (over 23,000 times that of CO2 over 100 years), much higher than CH4 and N2O.

28. Consider the following statements regarding ocean acidification: 1. It is caused by the absorption of excess CO2 from the atmosphere. 2. It reduces the availability of carbonate ions, which are essential for calcifying organisms. 3. It primarily affects the surface ocean and has negligible impact on deep-sea ecosystems. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Deep-sea ecosystems are also affected as acidification penetrates deeper over time, so statement 3 is incorrect.

29. The term 'albedo effect' in the context of climate change refers to:

  1. The cooling effect of volcanic aerosols in the stratosphere
  2. The ability of Earth's surface to reflect solar radiation
  3. The trapping of outgoing longwave radiation by greenhouse gases
  4. The reduction of surface temperature due to cloud cover

Answer: The ability of Earth's surface to reflect solar radiation

Explanation: Albedo is the fraction of sunlight reflected by a surface; changes in ice cover affect albedo and thus warming.

30. Consider the following statements regarding the 'carbon footprint' of products: 1. It accounts for all greenhouse gas emissions along the supply chain, from raw material extraction to disposal. 2. Carbon footprint is expressed in terms of CO2-equivalent units. 3. It includes only carbon dioxide emissions and ignores other greenhouse gases. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Carbon footprint includes all GHGs, not just CO2, so statement 3 is false.

31. Consider the following statements regarding the term 'Climate Sensitivity': 1. It refers to the equilibrium change in global mean surface temperature following a doubling of atmospheric CO2 concentration. 2. A higher climate sensitivity value implies a greater potential for warming for the same increase in greenhouse gas concentration. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Climate sensitivity is defined as the temperature rise from a doubling of CO2, and a higher value means more warming for the same forcing.

32. Which of the following best describes the 'Albedo Effect' in the context of climate change?

  1. Increased absorption of solar radiation due to melting of ice
  2. Release of methane from permafrost due to warming
  3. Reduction in ocean pH due to CO2 absorption
  4. Strengthening of the greenhouse effect by water vapor

Answer: Increased absorption of solar radiation due to melting of ice

Explanation: Melting ice reduces surface albedo, causing more solar radiation to be absorbed, which amplifies warming.

33. Consider the following pairs: 1. Black Carbon - Short-lived climate pollutant with warming effect 2. Sulfate Aerosols - Short-lived climate pollutant with cooling effect 3. Tropospheric Ozone - Long-lived greenhouse gas Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Black carbon warms, sulfate aerosols cool, but tropospheric ozone is a short-lived climate pollutant, not long-lived.

34. The term 'Ocean Acidification' is primarily caused by which of the following processes?

  1. Increased evaporation due to warming
  2. Absorption of excess CO2 by seawater
  3. Release of dissolved oxygen from warming waters
  4. Deposition of nitrogen from agricultural runoff

Answer: Absorption of excess CO2 by seawater

Explanation: Ocean acidification results from seawater absorbing atmospheric CO2, forming carbonic acid and lowering pH.

35. Which of the following is a key feature of the 'Kigali Amendment' to the Montreal Protocol?

  1. Phase down of hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)
  2. Phase out of chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
  3. Reduction of black carbon emissions
  4. Establishment of a loss and damage fund

Answer: Phase down of hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)

Explanation: The Kigali Amendment targets HFCs, potent greenhouse gases, for phasedown under the Montreal Protocol.

36. Consider the following statements regarding 'Carbon Sequestration': 1. It involves capturing and storing atmospheric carbon dioxide. 2. Afforestation is a form of biological carbon sequestration. 3. Carbon capture and storage (CCS) technology is currently the most cost-effective method globally. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Statements 1 and 2 are correct; CCS is not yet the most cost-effective method due to high costs and energy requirements.

37. Which of the following is the primary source of the 'El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO)' influence on global climate patterns?

  1. Changes in the Indian Ocean Dipole
  2. Variations in sea surface temperatures in the Pacific Ocean
  3. Shifts in the jet stream over the Atlantic
  4. Melting of Arctic sea ice

Answer: Variations in sea surface temperatures in the Pacific Ocean

Explanation: ENSO is driven by changes in sea surface temperatures and atmospheric pressure over the equatorial Pacific Ocean.

38. The term 'Climate Justice' in international environmental law primarily emphasizes:

  1. Equal per capita emission rights for all nations
  2. Historical responsibility of developed countries for emissions
  3. Technology transfer to developing countries only
  4. Uniform carbon tax across all countries

Answer: Historical responsibility of developed countries for emissions

Explanation: Climate justice focuses on the disproportionate historical emissions of developed nations and their responsibility to act.

39. Consider the following statements: 1. Methane has a higher global warming potential (GWP) than carbon dioxide over a 100-year period. 2. Methane has a shorter atmospheric lifetime than carbon dioxide. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Methane has a GWP about 28 times that of CO2 over 100 years, but its atmospheric lifetime is around 12 years, much shorter than CO2.

40. Which of the following is a likely consequence of a 'Permanent El Niño-like state' in the Pacific Ocean under global warming?

  1. Increased frequency of Atlantic hurricanes
  2. Strengthening of the Indian monsoon
  3. Reduced rainfall over the western Pacific and increased over the eastern Pacific
  4. Expansion of Antarctic sea ice

Answer: Reduced rainfall over the western Pacific and increased over the eastern Pacific

Explanation: A permanent El Niño-like state would shift rainfall patterns, causing drying in the western Pacific and wetter conditions in the eastern Pacific.

41. Consider the following statements regarding the 'Carbon Budget' approach in climate change mitigation: 1. It refers to the maximum amount of carbon dioxide that can be emitted while keeping global warming below a specific temperature limit. 2. The IPCC's Sixth Assessment Report revised the remaining carbon budget for 1.5°C to be larger than previously estimated. 3. Carbon budgets are typically expressed in gigatonnes of carbon (GtC) rather than CO2 equivalent. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is correct; statement 2 is false (IPCC AR6 reduced the remaining budget); statement 3 is false (budgets are in GtCO2, not GtC).

42. With reference to 'Blue Carbon' ecosystems, consider the following statements: 1. They include mangroves, seagrasses, and salt marshes. 2. They sequester carbon at rates higher than terrestrial forests on a per-hectare basis. 3. Carbon stored in these ecosystems is released only when they are disturbed. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three statements are correct: blue carbon ecosystems are highly efficient carbon sinks, and disturbance releases stored carbon.

43. Which of the following best describes 'Ocean Acidification' in the context of climate change?

  1. Increase in ocean pH due to absorption of carbon dioxide
  2. Decrease in ocean pH due to absorption of carbon dioxide
  3. Increase in ocean temperature causing thermal expansion
  4. Decrease in ocean salinity due to melting of polar ice

Answer: Decrease in ocean pH due to absorption of carbon dioxide

Explanation: Ocean acidification is the decrease in pH (increase in acidity) caused by increased CO2 absorption from the atmosphere.

44. Consider the following pairs: (Climate-related concept) – (Description): 1. Albedo effect – Reflectivity of Earth's surface; feedback loop in Arctic amplification. 2. Permafrost carbon feedback – Release of CO2 and methane from thawing permafrost. 3. Cloud condensation nuclei – Aerosols that affect cloud formation and radiative forcing. Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three pairs are correctly matched: they describe important climate feedback mechanisms.

45. The term 'Nationally Determined Contributions' (NDCs) under the Paris Agreement primarily refer to:

  1. Legally binding emission reduction targets for all countries
  2. Voluntary climate action plans submitted by each country
  3. Financial contributions to the Green Climate Fund
  4. Technology transfer commitments from developed to developing nations

Answer: Voluntary climate action plans submitted by each country

Explanation: NDCs are voluntary pledges; they are not legally binding, though there is a mechanism for global stocktake.

46. Consider the following statements regarding 'Climate Finance' in international negotiations: 1. The $100 billion per year goal by 2020 was set at the Copenhagen Accord. 2. The Green Climate Fund is the main multilateral fund for climate mitigation and adaptation. 3. Loss and Damage finance is a separate track under the UNFCCC. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three are correct: $100bn goal from Copenhagen, GCF is main fund, and Loss & Damage is a distinct pillar.

47. Which of the following is the most accurate description of 'Climate Sensitivity'?

  1. The increase in global temperature per unit increase in atmospheric CO2 concentration
  2. The equilibrium temperature change resulting from a doubling of CO2 concentration
  3. The rate at which glaciers recede due to rising temperatures
  4. The reduction in solar radiation reaching Earth due to volcanic aerosols

Answer: The equilibrium temperature change resulting from a doubling of CO2 concentration

Explanation: Climate sensitivity is defined as the long-term temperature change after a doubling of CO2, typically 1.5-4.5°C.

48. Consider the following statements about the 'Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change' (IPCC): 1. It was established by the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) and the World Meteorological Organization (WMO). 2. It conducts original research on climate change. 3. Its assessment reports are released every five to seven years. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Statement 1 and 3 are correct; IPCC does not conduct original research but reviews existing science.

49. Consider the following statements about 'Carbon Capture, Utilization and Storage' (CCUS): 1. It removes CO2 directly from the atmosphere and stores it underground. 2. Direct air capture (DAC) is a subcategory of CCUS. 3. Bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS) can result in negative emissions. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is false (CCUS captures from point sources, not directly from atmosphere; DAC does that); statements 2 and 3 are correct.

50. Which of the following is a negative climate forcing agent?

  1. Black carbon
  2. Methane
  3. Sulfate aerosols
  4. Water vapor

Answer: Sulfate aerosols

Explanation: Sulfate aerosols reflect incoming solar radiation back to space, causing a cooling effect (negative radiative forcing).
