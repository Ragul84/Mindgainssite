26. How does rupee depreciation affect the poor in India?

  1. Increases their purchasing power
  2. Reduces their purchasing power
  3. Has no impact on their purchasing power
  4. Leads to higher employment opportunities

Answer: Reduces their purchasing power

Explanation: Rupee depreciation increases import prices, reducing the purchasing power of the poor.

27. What is the impact of sterilized interventions by the RBI on poverty?

  1. Reduces poverty by increasing money supply
  2. Increases poverty by reducing money supply
  3. Has no direct impact on poverty
  4. Leads to higher economic growth

Answer: Has no direct impact on poverty

Explanation: Sterilized interventions have no direct impact on poverty.

28. Which component of the balance of payments is most relevant to poverty reduction in India?

  1. Current account
  2. Capital account
  3. Financial account
  4. Official reserve account

Answer: Current account

Explanation: Current account reflects trade and services, impacting employment and poverty.

29. How does inflation affect the poor in India?

  1. Increases their real income
  2. Reduces their real income
  3. Has no impact on their real income
  4. Leads to higher savings

Answer: Reduces their real income

Explanation: Inflation reduces the purchasing power of the poor.

30. What is the primary goal of agricultural reforms in the context of poverty and inclusion in India?

  1. Increase agricultural exports
  2. Reduce agricultural imports
  3. Improve farmers' income and reduce poverty
  4. Promote organic farming

Answer: Improve farmers' income and reduce poverty

Explanation: Agricultural reforms aim to improve farmers' income and reduce poverty.

31. Which social indicator is most closely related to poverty reduction in India?

  1. Literacy rate
  2. Infant mortality rate
  3. Human Development Index
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All these indicators are related to poverty reduction.

32. How does a trade deficit affect the poor in India?

  1. Increases their employment opportunities
  2. Reduces their employment opportunities
  3. Has no impact on their employment opportunities
  4. Leads to higher economic growth

Answer: Reduces their employment opportunities

Explanation: Trade deficit can lead to reduced employment opportunities.

33. What is the impact of fiscal policy on poverty reduction in India?

  1. Always reduces poverty
  2. Always increases poverty
  3. Can reduce poverty if targeted correctly
  4. Has no impact on poverty

Answer: Can reduce poverty if targeted correctly

Explanation: Fiscal policy can reduce poverty if targeted correctly.

34. What is the primary objective of monetary policy in the context of poverty reduction in India?

  1. Controlling inflation
  2. Promoting economic growth
  3. Reducing unemployment
  4. Increasing foreign exchange reserves

Answer: Controlling inflation

Explanation: Monetary policy aims to control inflation, which disproportionately affects the poor. Low inflation supports poverty reduction.

35. Which of the following fiscal indicators is most relevant to assessing poverty inclusion in India?

  1. Fiscal deficit
  2. Revenue deficit
  3. Primary deficit
  4. Social sector expenditure

Answer: Social sector expenditure

Explanation: Social sector expenditure directly impacts poverty inclusion by allocating funds to welfare schemes.

36. Agricultural reforms can contribute to poverty reduction by

  1. Increasing farm subsidies
  2. Enhancing market access for farmers
  3. Implementing price controls
  4. Reducing agricultural imports

Answer: Enhancing market access for farmers

Explanation: Better market access for farmers can increase their incomes, thus reducing poverty.

37. Inflation can exacerbate poverty in India because it

  1. Increases the purchasing power of the poor
  2. Reduces the cost of living
  3. Decreases the value of savings
  4. Erodes the real income of the poor

Answer: Erodes the real income of the poor

Explanation: Inflation reduces the purchasing power of the poor, making them poorer.

38. A trade deficit can impact poverty in India by

  1. Increasing foreign exchange reserves
  2. Enhancing domestic production
  3. Reducing imports of essential goods
  4. Decreasing the value of the rupee

Answer: Decreasing the value of the rupee

Explanation: A depreciating rupee increases import prices, affecting the poor who spend a larger portion of their income on essentials.

39. Sterilized interventions by the RBI are used to

  1. Increase money supply
  2. Reduce inflation
  3. Manage exchange rates
  4. Absorb excess liquidity

Answer: Absorb excess liquidity

Explanation: Sterilized interventions absorb excess liquidity to prevent inflation.

40. The balance of payments (BOP) is closely related to poverty because it affects

  1. Foreign investment in poverty alleviation programs
  2. The value of the rupee and thus import prices
  3. The government's ability to fund social welfare schemes
  4. The level of foreign aid received

Answer: The value of the rupee and thus import prices

Explanation: BOP affects the rupee's value, which in turn impacts import prices and the cost of living for the poor.

41. Rupee depreciation can affect poverty by

  1. Increasing the cost of imports
  2. Reducing the cost of exports
  3. Enhancing foreign investment
  4. Decreasing inflation

Answer: Increasing the cost of imports

Explanation: Rupee depreciation increases import costs, affecting the poor who rely on imported goods.

42. Social indicators such as the Human Development Index (HDI) are important for assessing poverty inclusion because they

  1. Measure economic growth
  2. Reflect the standard of living
  3. Capture multidimensional aspects of well-being
  4. Focus solely on income poverty

Answer: Capture multidimensional aspects of well-being

Explanation: HDI captures aspects of well-being beyond income, providing a comprehensive view of poverty.

43. Fiscal policy can directly address poverty through

  1. Taxation reforms
  2. Public expenditure on social sectors
  3. Monetary policy adjustments
  4. Trade policy reforms

Answer: Public expenditure on social sectors

Explanation: Public expenditure on social sectors directly benefits the poor and vulnerable populations.

44. What is the expected impact of rupee depreciation on poverty and inclusion in India?

  1. Increase in exports and reduction in poverty
  2. Decrease in imports and increase in poverty
  3. Increase in inflation and reduction in poverty
  4. Decrease in economic growth and increase in poverty

Answer: Increase in exports and reduction in poverty

Explanation: Rupee depreciation can lead to increase in exports, which can create jobs and reduce poverty.

45. Which of the following agricultural reforms is most likely to benefit small and marginal farmers in India?

  1. Minimum Support Price
  2. Agricultural Marketing Reforms
  3. Land Reforms
  4. Irrigation Reforms

Answer: Agricultural Marketing Reforms

Explanation: Agricultural marketing reforms can help small farmers sell their produce at better prices, reducing poverty.

46. What is the relationship between inflation and poverty in India?

  1. Inflation reduces poverty
  2. Inflation has no impact on poverty
  3. Inflation increases poverty
  4. Inflation is not related to poverty

Answer: Inflation increases poverty

Explanation: Inflation can reduce the purchasing power of the poor, increasing poverty.

47. Which of the following trade balances is most closely related to poverty and inclusion in India?

  1. Current Account Deficit
  2. Capital Account Surplus
  3. Trade Deficit
  4. Balance of Payments Surplus

Answer: Trade Deficit

Explanation: Trade deficit can lead to reduction in economic growth, increasing poverty.

48. What is the impact of sterilized interventions by the RBI on poverty and inclusion in India?

  1. Reduction in inflation and poverty
  2. Increase in economic growth and reduction in poverty
  3. No impact on poverty and inclusion
  4. Increase in inflation and poverty

Answer: Reduction in inflation and poverty

Explanation: Sterilized interventions can help reduce inflation, which can reduce poverty.

49. Which of the following social indicators is most closely related to poverty and inclusion in India?

  1. Human Development Index
  2. Gini Coefficient
  3. Poverty Headcount Ratio
  4. Life Expectancy at Birth

Answer: Poverty Headcount Ratio

Explanation: Poverty headcount ratio is a direct measure of poverty, making it closely related to poverty and inclusion.

50. What is the expected impact of increase in foreign direct investment on poverty and inclusion in India?

  1. Increase in economic growth and reduction in poverty
  2. Decrease in economic growth and increase in poverty
  3. No impact on poverty and inclusion
  4. Increase in inequality and poverty

Answer: Increase in economic growth and reduction in poverty

Explanation: Increase in FDI can lead to increase in economic growth, creating jobs and reducing poverty.
