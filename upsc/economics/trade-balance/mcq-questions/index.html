Trade Balance MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Trade Balance questions to revise the UPSC Economy syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 553 questions.

26. Which fiscal indicator can influence the trade balance by altering aggregate demand?

  1. Fiscal deficit
  2. Revenue deficit
  3. Primary deficit
  4. Trade deficit

Answer: Fiscal deficit

Explanation: A fiscal deficit can increase aggregate demand, thus affecting the trade balance by increasing imports.

27. How does a rise in global oil prices affect India's trade balance?

  1. Improves the trade balance
  2. Worsens the trade balance
  3. No impact on the trade balance
  4. Increases exports

Answer: Worsens the trade balance

Explanation: Higher oil prices increase India's import bill, thus worsening the trade balance.

28. What is the relationship between inflation and trade balance in the context of Indian economy?

  1. Higher inflation leads to improved trade balance
  2. Higher inflation leads to worsened trade balance
  3. No relationship between inflation and trade balance
  4. Lower inflation leads to worsened trade balance

Answer: Higher inflation leads to worsened trade balance

Explanation: Higher inflation can make exports less competitive, thus worsening the trade balance.

29. Which of the following monetary policy tools can influence the trade balance?

  1. Repo rate
  2. Reverse repo rate
  3. Cash Reserve Ratio
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All these tools can influence the trade balance by altering interest rates and money supply.

30. What is the impact of agricultural reforms on India's trade balance?

  1. Increases exports and improves trade balance
  2. Decreases exports and worsens trade balance
  3. No impact on trade balance
  4. Increases imports

Answer: Increases exports and improves trade balance

Explanation: Agricultural reforms can increase agricultural exports, thus improving the trade balance.

31. Which social indicator can be affected by the trade balance?

  1. Poverty rate
  2. Unemployment rate
  3. Income inequality
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The trade balance can affect employment, income, and poverty levels, thus influencing social indicators.

32. How does a trade deficit affect the exchange rate of the Indian rupee?

  1. Appreciates the rupee
  2. Depreciates the rupee
  3. No impact on the exchange rate
  4. Increases foreign exchange reserves

Answer: Depreciates the rupee

Explanation: A trade deficit can lead to a depreciation of the rupee as it increases the demand for foreign currency.

33. Which of the following is a component of the balance of payments?

  1. A: Trade balance
  2. B: Fiscal deficit
  3. C: Monetary policy
  4. D: Inflation rate

Answer: A: Trade balance

Explanation: Trade balance is a component of the balance of payments, which includes the current account and capital account.

34. What is the effect of sterilized interventions by the RBI on the money supply?

  1. A: Increase in money supply
  2. B: Decrease in money supply
  3. C: No impact on money supply
  4. D: Increase in foreign exchange reserves

Answer: B: Decrease in money supply

Explanation: Sterilized interventions by the RBI decrease the money supply by absorbing excess liquidity.

35. Which of the following factors can lead to a trade deficit?

  1. A: Increase in domestic production
  2. B: Increase in exports
  3. C: Increase in imports
  4. D: Decrease in inflation

Answer: C: Increase in imports

Explanation: An increase in imports can lead to a trade deficit if it exceeds the value of exports.

36. What is the relationship between trade balance and exchange rates?

  1. A: Trade balance has no impact on exchange rates
  2. B: Trade surplus leads to depreciation of the currency
  3. C: Trade deficit leads to appreciation of the currency
  4. D: Trade surplus leads to appreciation of the currency

Answer: D: Trade surplus leads to appreciation of the currency

Explanation: A trade surplus can lead to an appreciation of the currency as there is a high demand for it.

37. Which of the following is a consequence of a large trade deficit?

  1. A: Increase in foreign exchange reserves
  2. B: Decrease in foreign exchange reserves
  3. C: No impact on foreign exchange reserves
  4. D: Increase in inflation

Answer: B: Decrease in foreign exchange reserves

Explanation: A large trade deficit can lead to a decrease in foreign exchange reserves as more foreign currency is spent on imports.

38. What is the impact of protectionist policies on trade balance?

  1. A: Increase in trade deficit
  2. B: Decrease in trade deficit
  3. C: No impact on trade balance
  4. D: Increase in trade surplus

Answer: B: Decrease in trade deficit

Explanation: Protectionist policies can lead to a decrease in trade deficit by reducing imports and increasing domestic production.

39. Which of the following is a tool used by the government to correct a trade deficit?

  1. A: Monetary policy
  2. B: Fiscal policy
  3. C: Tariffs and quotas
  4. D: Inflation targeting

Answer: C: Tariffs and quotas

Explanation: Tariffs and quotas are tools used by the government to correct a trade deficit by reducing imports.

40. What is the relationship between economic growth and trade balance?

  1. A: High economic growth always leads to a trade surplus
  2. B: High economic growth always leads to a trade deficit
  3. C: Economic growth has no impact on trade balance
  4. D: High economic growth can lead to either a trade surplus or deficit

Answer: D: High economic growth can lead to either a trade surplus or deficit

Explanation: High economic growth can lead to either a trade surplus or deficit, depending on the factors driving growth.

41. Which of the following is a factor that can influence India's trade balance with other countries?

  1. A: Exchange rates
  2. B: Inflation rates
  3. C: Government policies
  4. D: All of the above

Answer: D: All of the above

Explanation: Exchange rates, inflation rates, and government policies can all influence India's trade balance with other countries.

42. Which factor can lead to a trade deficit in India?

  1. Increase in agricultural production
  2. Increase in industrial production
  3. Depreciation of the rupee
  4. Increase in imports of essential goods

Answer: Increase in imports of essential goods

Explanation: Increase in imports of essential goods can lead to a trade deficit.

43. Which of the following is a tool used by the government to influence trade balance?

  1. Monetary policy
  2. Fiscal policy
  3. Tariffs and subsidies
  4. Inflation targeting

Answer: Tariffs and subsidies

Explanation: Tariffs and subsidies are tools used by the government to influence trade balance.

44. What is the impact of a trade surplus on a country's foreign exchange reserves?

  1. Decrease in foreign exchange reserves
  2. Increase in foreign exchange reserves
  3. No impact on foreign exchange reserves
  4. Increase in fiscal deficit

Answer: Increase in foreign exchange reserves

Explanation: A trade surplus leads to an increase in foreign exchange reserves.

45. Which of the following can lead to a deterioration in trade balance?

  1. Increase in domestic production
  2. Increase in exports
  3. Increase in global demand
  4. Increase in imports due to domestic consumption

Answer: Increase in imports due to domestic consumption

Explanation: Increase in imports due to domestic consumption can lead to a deterioration in trade balance.

46. What is the relationship between inflation and trade balance?

  1. High inflation leads to an improvement in trade balance
  2. High inflation leads to a deterioration in trade balance
  3. Inflation has no impact on trade balance
  4. Low inflation leads to a deterioration in trade balance

Answer: High inflation leads to a deterioration in trade balance

Explanation: High inflation can lead to a deterioration in trade balance.

47. Which component of the balance of payments is most closely related to trade balance?

  1. A: Capital account
  2. B: Current account
  3. C: Financial account
  4. D: Official reserve account

Answer: B: Current account

Explanation: The current account includes the trade balance, which is the difference between exports and imports of goods and services.

48. Which of the following is a consequence of a trade deficit for the Indian economy?

  1. A: Increase in foreign exchange reserves
  2. B: Decrease in foreign exchange reserves
  3. C: Increase in domestic production
  4. D: Decrease in imports

Answer: B: Decrease in foreign exchange reserves

Explanation: A trade deficit leads to more money leaving the country than entering, thus decreasing foreign exchange reserves.

49. Which fiscal policy tool can help improve the trade balance?

  1. A: Increase in subsidies on exports
  2. B: Increase in import duties
  3. C: Decrease in corporate tax
  4. D: Increase in government spending

Answer: B: Increase in import duties

Explanation: Increasing import duties makes imports more expensive, thus reducing their demand and improving the trade balance.

50. How does inflation in India affect its trade balance?

  1. A: High inflation increases exports
  2. B: High inflation decreases exports
  3. C: Inflation has no impact on trade balance
  4. D: High inflation increases imports

Answer: B: High inflation decreases exports

Explanation: High inflation makes Indian products more expensive, thus decreasing exports and worsening the trade balance.
