26. The population of a town increases by 5% annually. If the present population is 1,00,000, what will it be after 2 years?

  1. 1,10,250
  2. 1,10,000
  3. 1,05,000
  4. 1,10,500

Answer: 1,10,250

Explanation: After 1 year: 1,05,000; after 2 years: 1,05,000 × 1.05 = 1,10,250.

27. In an examination, a student scored 40% marks and failed by 30 marks. Another student scored 50% and got 20 marks more than the passing marks. What is the maximum marks?

  1. 400
  2. 500
  3. 600
  4. 300

Answer: 500

Explanation: Let max = x; passing marks = 0.4x + 30 = 0.5x - 20 → 0.1x = 50 → x = 500.

28. A shopkeeper sells an item at a profit of 20%. If the cost price is increased by 10% and selling price remains same, what is the new profit percentage?

  1. 9.09%
  2. 10%
  3. 8.33%
  4. 12.5%

Answer: 9.09%

Explanation: Let CP=100, SP=120; new CP=110; profit=10; profit% = 10/110 ≈ 9.09%.

29. Which of the following statements is/are correct? 1. If a number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%, the net effect is no change. 2. If a number is decreased by 20% and then increased by 25%, the net effect is a 5% increase.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1: 100→125→100, no change. Statement 2: 100→80→100, no change, not 5% increase.

30. In a mixture of 80 litres, milk and water are in the ratio 3:2. How much water must be added to make the milk percentage 50%?

  1. 16 litres
  2. 20 litres
  3. 24 litres
  4. 32 litres

Answer: 16 litres

Explanation: Milk = 48 litres, water = 32 litres. Let added water = x; 48/(80+x) = 0.5 → x = 16 litres.

31. If the price of a commodity is increased by 20%, by what percentage must consumption be reduced so that expenditure remains unchanged?

  1. 16.67%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 33.33%

Answer: 16.67%

Explanation: Expenditure = Price × Consumption. To keep expenditure constant, if price increases by 20% (multiplier 1.2), consumption must be multiplied by 1/1.2 ≈ 0.8333, i.e., a reduction of 16.67%.

32. A number is first increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:

  1. 5% increase
  2. 5% decrease
  3. No change
  4. 10% increase

Answer: No change

Explanation: Net multiplier = 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.00, so the number remains unchanged.

33. In an election between two candidates, one candidate got 55% of the total valid votes. 10% of the total votes were invalid. If the total votes polled were 1,00,000, what was the number of valid votes the winning candidate received?

  1. 49,500
  2. 55,000
  3. 45,000
  4. 50,000

Answer: 49,500

Explanation: Valid votes = 90% of 1,00,000 = 90,000. Winning candidate got 55% of 90,000 = 49,500.

34. If A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, then B's salary is what percentage less than A's salary?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 33.33%
  4. 16.67%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Let B = 100, then A = 125. B is less by (25/125)×100 = 20%.

35. The population of a town increases by 10% annually. If its present population is 1,21,000, what was it two years ago?

  1. 1,00,000
  2. 1,10,000
  3. 1,05,000
  4. 90,000

Answer: 1,00,000

Explanation: Let population two years ago be P. Then P × 1.1 × 1.1 = 1,21,000 ⇒ P = 1,21,000 / 1.21 = 1,00,000.

36. In a mixture of milk and water, the ratio of milk to water is 3:2. What percentage of the mixture is water?

  1. 40%
  2. 60%
  3. 33.33%
  4. 66.67%

Answer: 40%

Explanation: Total parts = 5. Water = 2 parts, so percentage = (2/5)×100 = 40%.

37. If the side of a square is increased by 10%, by what percentage does its area increase?

  1. 10%
  2. 20%
  3. 21%
  4. 100%

Answer: 21%

Explanation: Area = side². New side = 1.1, new area = 1.21, increase = 21%.

38. A student scored 30% marks in an exam and failed by 20 marks. Another student scored 40% marks and got 30 marks more than the passing marks. What are the maximum marks?

  1. 500
  2. 400
  3. 300
  4. 600

Answer: 500

Explanation: Let max marks = M. Passing marks = 0.3M + 20 = 0.4M - 30 ⇒ 0.1M = 50 ⇒ M = 500.

39. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and denominator is decreased by 10%, the fraction becomes 16/21. What was the original fraction?

  1. 4/7
  2. 5/7
  3. 6/7
  4. 3/7

Answer: 4/7

Explanation: Let original fraction = x/y. Then (1.2x)/(0.9y) = 16/21 ⇒ x/y = (16/21)×(0.9/1.2) = 4/7.

40. In a class, 60% of the students are boys and the rest are girls. If 40% of the boys and 30% of the girls passed an exam, what percentage of the total students passed?

  1. 36%
  2. 35%
  3. 34%
  4. 40%

Answer: 36%

Explanation: Passed boys = 0.4×60 = 24% of total, passed girls = 0.3×40 = 12% of total. Total passed = 36%.

41. If the price of an item is increased by 20%, then decreased by 20%, what is the net change?

  1. 4% decrease
  2. 4% increase
  3. 0%
  4. 2% decrease

Answer: 4% decrease

Explanation: Net multiplier = 1.20 × 0.80 = 0.96, so a 4% decrease.

42. A number is increased by 25% then decreased by 10%. The resulting number is what percent of the original?

  1. 112.5%
  2. 115%
  3. 110%
  4. 108%

Answer: 112.5%

Explanation: Multiplier = 1.25 × 0.9 = 1.125, hence 112.5%.

43. If A's income is 20% more than B's, then B's income is what percentage less than A's?

  1. 16.67%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 33.33%

Answer: 16.67%

Explanation: Let B = 100, A = 120; difference 20; (20/120)×100 = 16.67%.

44. The population of a town increases by 10% annually. If the present population is 12100, what was it two years ago?

  1. 10000
  2. 11000
  3. 1000
  4. 1210

Answer: 10000

Explanation: Present = x × 1.1² = 1.21x = 12100, so x = 10000.

45. In an election, candidate A gets 40% of the total valid votes. If 15% of total votes are invalid, and total votes are 8000, how many valid votes did A get?

  1. 2720
  2. 3200
  3. 2800
  4. 3000

Answer: 2720

Explanation: Valid votes = 8000 × 0.85 = 6800; A's votes = 0.4 × 6800 = 2720.

46. If a number is increased by 50% and then decreased by 50%, the net change is:

  1. 25% decrease
  2. 0%
  3. 25% increase
  4. 50% decrease

Answer: 25% decrease

Explanation: Net multiplier = 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75, so a 25% decrease.

47. The price of a commodity increased by 20%. By what percent must a family reduce its consumption so that expenditure remains the same?

  1. 16.67%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 33.33%

Answer: 16.67%

Explanation: Required reduction = (20/120)×100 = 16.67% or 1 - (1/1.2) = 16.67%.

48. A shopkeeper marks his goods at 20% above cost price and gives a discount of 10%. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 8%
  2. 10%
  3. 12%
  4. 15%

Answer: 8%

Explanation: Let CP = 100; MP = 120; SP = 120 × 0.9 = 108; profit = 8%.

49. The value of a machine depreciates 10% annually. If its present value is Rs. 729, what was its value 3 years ago?

  1. Rs. 1000
  2. Rs. 900
  3. Rs. 810
  4. Rs. 7290

Answer: Rs. 1000

Explanation: Let x; x × 0.9³ = 729; x = 729 / 0.729 = 1000.

50. Consider the following statements about percentage change: 1. If a number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%, the net effect is 1% increase. 2. If a number is increased by 20% and then the new number is increased by 20%, the net effect is 44% increase. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Statement 1 gives 1% decrease (false); statement 2 gives 44% increase (true).
