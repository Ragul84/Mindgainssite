UPSC CSAT Quantitative Aptitude practice

Percentage MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Percentage questions to revise the UPSC CSAT Quantitative Aptitude syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. If the price of a commodity is increased by 25%, by what percentage must the consumption be reduced to keep the expenditure the same?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 16.67%
  4. 33.33%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Expenditure = Price × Consumption. To keep constant, reduction = (increase%)/(100+increase%) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%.

2. A student scored 72 marks in an exam and failed by 12 marks. If the passing percentage is 40%, what is the maximum marks?

  1. 210
  2. 180
  3. 200
  4. 240

Answer: 210

Explanation: Pass marks = 72 + 12 = 84, which is 40% of maximum. So maximum = 84 / 0.4 = 210.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. If A's salary is 25% more than B's, then B's salary is 20% less than A's. 2. If a number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%, the net change is a decrease of 4%. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Statement 1: B's less percentage = 25/125 × 100 = 20%. Statement 2: Net change = 20 - 20 + (20×-20)/100 = -4%. Both correct.

4. In an election, candidate A got 55% of the total valid votes. The invalid votes were 10% of the total votes polled. If total votes polled were 8000, how many valid votes did A get?

  1. 4400
  2. 3960
  3. 3600
  4. 4000

Answer: 3960

Explanation: Valid votes = 90% of 8000 = 7200. A's valid votes = 55% of 7200 = 3960.

5. The population of a town increased by 10% in the first year and 20% in the second year. The overall percentage increase is:

  1. 30%
  2. 32%
  3. 33%
  4. 31%

Answer: 32%

Explanation: Successive percentage change: 10 + 20 + (10×20)/100 = 32%.

6. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator is decreased by 10%, the fraction becomes 16/21. Find the original fraction.

  1. 4/7
  2. 5/7
  3. 3/7
  4. 6/7

Answer: 4/7

Explanation: Let original be x/y. Then (1.2x)/(0.9y) = 16/21 ⇒ (4/3)(x/y) = 16/21 ⇒ x/y = 4/7.

7. In an exam, 30% of the students failed in Mathematics, 20% failed in English, and 10% failed in both. What percentage of students passed in both subjects?

  1. 50%
  2. 60%
  3. 40%
  4. 70%

Answer: 60%

Explanation: Failed in at least one = 30 + 20 - 10 = 40%. Hence passed both = 100 - 40 = 60%.

8. A shopkeeper marks his goods 20% above cost price and gives a discount of 10%. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 8%
  2. 10%
  3. 12%
  4. 6%

Answer: 8%

Explanation: Let CP = 100, MP = 120, SP = 120 × 0.9 = 108, profit = 8%.

9. Consider the following: 1. 0.125% of 800 is 1. 2. 0.5% of 2000 is 100. Which is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both
  4. Neither

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: 1: 0.125% = 0.00125, 0.00125×800=1, correct. 2: 0.5% = 0.005, 0.005×2000=10, not 100.

10. If 60% of a number is added to 120, the result is the number itself. Find the number.

  1. 200
  2. 300
  3. 400
  4. 250

Answer: 300

Explanation: Let number = x. Then 0.6x + 120 = x ⇒ 0.4x = 120 ⇒ x = 300.

11. If the length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and the breadth is decreased by 20%, then the area changes by:

  1. 4% decrease
  2. 4% increase
  3. No change
  4. 1% decrease

Answer: 4% decrease

Explanation: New area = (1.2L)×(0.8B) = 0.96LB, so a 4% decrease.

12. A's income is 25% more than B's. B's income is what percent less than A's?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 16.67%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: If B = 100, A = 125. Difference = 25, which is 20% of 125.

13. In an election, 10% of votes were invalid. The winner got 60% of the valid votes and won by a margin of 5400 votes. The total number of votes polled is:

  1. 25000
  2. 30000
  3. 35000
  4. 40000

Answer: 30000

Explanation: Valid votes = 0.9T, winner = 0.54T, loser = 0.36T, margin 0.18T = 5400 => T = 30000.

14. The population of a town increases by 5% annually. If the present population is 1,00,000, what was it two years ago?

  1. 90,702
  2. 90,703
  3. 90,700
  4. 90,000

Answer: 90,703

Explanation: Let P be population 2 years ago. P × (1.05)^2 = 100000 => P = 100000/1.1025 ≈ 90703.

15. If the price of a commodity is reduced by 10% and the sales increase by 20%, what is the net effect on revenue?

  1. 8% increase
  2. 8% decrease
  3. 10% increase
  4. 10% decrease

Answer: 8% increase

Explanation: Revenue = price × quantity. Assume original 100 each, new price 90, quantity 120 => revenue 10800, an 8% increase.

16. The value of a machine depreciates by 10% each year. After 2 years, its value is Rs. 16,200. What was its original value?

  1. 18,000
  2. 20,000
  3. 22,000
  4. 24,000

Answer: 20,000

Explanation: Value after 2 years = P × (0.9)^2 = 0.81P = 16200 => P = 20000.

17. A number is increased by 20% and then the result is decreased by 25%. The net percentage change is:

  1. 10% decrease
  2. 10% increase
  3. 5% decrease
  4. 5% increase

Answer: 10% decrease

Explanation: Let number = 100. After increase = 120, after 25% decrease = 90, so 10% decrease.

18. Consider the following statements about percentage changes: 1. If a number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%, the net change is a 1% decrease. 2. If a number is decreased by 10% and then increased by 10%, the net change is a 1% increase. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both
  4. Neither

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1 correct (100→110→99, decrease 1%). Statement 2 incorrect (100→90→99, decrease 1%).

19. In a test, a student scored 40% and failed by 20 marks. If the passing percentage is 50%, the total marks are:

  1. 200
  2. 250
  3. 300
  4. 400

Answer: 200

Explanation: Pass marks - score = 0.5T - 0.4T = 0.1T = 20 => T = 200.

20. A shopkeeper marks an article at Rs. 500 and gives a discount of 10%. He still makes a profit of 20%. The cost price of the article is:

  1. 375
  2. 400
  3. 425
  4. 450

Answer: 375

Explanation: Selling price = 500 × 0.9 = 450. Profit 20% on CP, so CP = 450/1.2 = 375.

21. If the price of a commodity is increased by 20%, by what percentage should consumption be reduced so that expenditure remains the same?

  1. 16.67%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 33.33%

Answer: 16.67%

Explanation: Let original price = 100, new price = 120. To keep expenditure constant, consumption becomes 100/120 = 5/6, reduction = 1/6 ≈ 16.67%.

22. In an election, candidate A got 55% of the total votes and won by a margin of 2000 votes. What was the total number of votes?

  1. 10000
  2. 20000
  3. 15000
  4. 25000

Answer: 20000

Explanation: Margin = 55% - 45% = 10% = 2000 votes, so total votes = 20000.

23. A number is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net percentage change?

  1. 1% increase
  2. 1% decrease
  3. 0%
  4. 0.1% decrease

Answer: 1% decrease

Explanation: Let number = 100; after increase = 110; after decrease = 99; net decrease = 1%.

24. Which of the following statements is/are correct? 1. If A's income is 20% less than B's, then B's income is 25% more than A's. 2. If A's income is 20% more than B's, then B's income is 20% less than A's.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1: B=100, A=80, B is 25% more than A. Statement 2: B=100, A=120, B is 16.67% less, not 20%.

25. In a class, 60% of the students are boys. If 40% of the boys and 30% of the girls are scholarship holders, what percentage of the class are scholarship holders?

  1. 36%
  2. 34%
  3. 30%
  4. 40%

Answer: 36%

Explanation: Let total=100; boys=60, girls=40; scholarship holders: 40% of 60=24, 30% of 40=12; total=36%.
