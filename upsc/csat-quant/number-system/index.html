Number System MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Number System questions to revise the UPSC CSAT Quantitative Aptitude syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Which of the following numbers is divisible by 11?

  1. 1234321
  2. 1234567
  3. 9876543
  4. 1111111

Answer: 1234321

Explanation: Alternating sum of digits of 1234321 is 1-2+3-4+3-2+1=0, hence divisible by 11.

2. What is the remainder when 2^100 is divided by 5?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. 3

Answer: 1

Explanation: Cycle of 2^n mod 5 is 2,4,3,1; exponent 100 is multiple of 4, so remainder 1.

3. How many trailing zeroes are there in the decimal expansion of 100!?

  1. 22
  2. 24
  3. 20
  4. 25

Answer: 24

Explanation: Number of trailing zeroes = floor(100/5)+floor(100/25)=20+4=24.

4. Which of the following is a prime number?

  1. 91
  2. 97
  3. 99
  4. 111

Answer: 97

Explanation: 97 has no divisors other than 1 and itself; 91=7×13, 99=9×11, 111=3×37.

5. Consider the following statements: 1. The sum of two prime numbers is always even. 2. The product of two prime numbers is always odd. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Neither 1 nor 2

Explanation: 2+3=5 (odd) disproves statement 1; 2×3=6 (even) disproves statement 2.
