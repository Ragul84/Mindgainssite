26. Consider the following statements about the number 2^10: 1. It is a multiple of 8. 2. Its last digit is 4. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: 2^10 = 1024, divisible by 8 (1024/8=128), so statement 1 correct. Last digit is 4, so statement 2 also correct. Both correct.

27. What is the remainder when 7^3 + 7^4 + 7^5 is divided by 19?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 7
  4. 18

Answer: 0

Explanation: 7^3 = 343, 343 mod 19 = 1 (since 19×18=342). So sum mod 19 = 1 + 7 + 49 = 57, 57 mod 19 = 0.

28. If the sum of two numbers is 25 and their product is 144, what is the sum of their reciprocals?

  1. 25/144
  2. 144/25
  3. 25/12
  4. 12/25

Answer: 25/144

Explanation: Let numbers be a and b. a+b=25, ab=144. Sum of reciprocals = (a+b)/ab = 25/144.

29. Consider the following statements: 1. Every integer is a rational number. 2. Every rational number is an integer. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Any integer can be written as n/1, so it is rational. But rational numbers like 1/2 are not integers. Only statement 1 is correct.

30. Consider the following statements about prime numbers: 1. The product of any two prime numbers is always odd. 2. The sum of any two prime numbers is always even. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Neither 1 nor 2

Explanation: Statement 1 is false because 2×3=6 (even). Statement 2 is false because 2+3=5 (odd).

31. The number of distinct prime factors of 2310 is:

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 7

Answer: 5

Explanation: 2310 = 2 × 3 × 5 × 7 × 11, hence five distinct prime factors.

32. Consider the following statements: 1. Every integer that ends with 0 is divisible by 10. 2. Every integer that is divisible by 10 ends with 0. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both are true: divisibility by 10 requires last digit 0, and vice versa.

33. If a number N is divisible by both 3 and 4, then N must be divisible by:

  1. 6
  2. 12
  3. Both 6 and 12
  4. Neither 6 nor 12

Answer: Both 6 and 12

Explanation: LCM of 3 and 4 is 12, so N is a multiple of 12, hence also of 6.

34. The remainder when 7^105 is divided by 5 is:

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 1

Answer: 2

Explanation: 7 ≡ 2 mod 5. 2^4 ≡ 1 mod 5, and 105 = 4×26 + 1, so remainder = 2^1 = 2.

35. Consider the following statements about irrational numbers: 1. The sum of two irrational numbers is always irrational. 2. The product of two irrational numbers is always irrational. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Neither 1 nor 2

Explanation: Counterexample for sum: √2 + (-√2) = 0 (rational). For product: √2 × √2 = 2 (rational).

36. The unit digit of 3^100 + 2^100 is:

  1. 1
  2. 3
  3. 7
  4. 9

Answer: 7

Explanation: 3^100 ends with 1 (cycle of 4), 2^100 ends with 6 (cycle of 4). Sum unit digit = 1+6 = 7.

37. How many numbers between 1 and 100 (inclusive) are divisible by 5 but not by 10?

  1. 10
  2. 9
  3. 11
  4. 5

Answer: 10

Explanation: Multiples of 5: 20 numbers. Multiples of 10: 10 numbers. Subtract: 20-10=10.

38. Consider the following statements: 1. The LCM of two numbers is always a multiple of their HCF. 2. The product of two numbers is equal to the product of their LCM and HCF. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both are standard properties: LCM × HCF = product, and LCM is a multiple of HCF.

39. The smallest positive integer that is a multiple of all numbers from 1 to 10 is:

  1. 2520
  2. 5040
  3. 1260
  4. 7560

Answer: 2520

Explanation: LCM of 1 to 10 is 2520.

40. Consider the following statements regarding the number 4536: I. It is divisible by 4. II. It is divisible by 8. III. It is divisible by 9. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. I only
  2. I and II
  3. I and III
  4. All three

Answer: All three

Explanation: Last two digits 36 divisible by 4; last three 536 divisible by 8; sum of digits 18 divisible by 9.

41. The product of two consecutive positive integers is always divisible by which number?

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 2

Explanation: Among any two consecutive integers, one is always even, so product divisible by 2.

42. If the numbers 2, 3, 5, 7 are factors of a number N, then which of the following must be a factor of N?

  1. 6
  2. 15
  3. 70
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Since N is divisible by 2,3,5,7, it is divisible by any product of distinct subsets, so 6, 15, and 70 are all factors.

43. What is the least number which must be added to 3500 to make it divisible by 42?

  1. 8
  2. 16
  3. 20
  4. 28

Answer: 28

Explanation: 3500 divided by 42 leaves remainder 14; adding (42-14)=28 makes it divisible.

44. Consider the following statements about prime numbers: 1. Every prime number greater than 5 can be written in the form 6k+1 or 6k-1. 2. The product of two primes is always odd. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both
  4. None

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is true (except 2,3); statement 2 false because 2×3=6 is even.

45. Find the unit digit of (23)^56 + (47)^23.

  1. 0
  2. 2
  3. 4
  4. 6

Answer: 4

Explanation: Cycles: 3^4 ends with 1, so 23^56 ends with 1; 7^4 ends with 1, so 47^23 ends with 3; sum 1+3=4.

46. Find the smallest positive integer that leaves remainders 2, 3, 4 when divided by 3, 4, 5 respectively.

  1. 59
  2. 119
  3. 29
  4. 89

Answer: 59

Explanation: Adding 1 makes it divisible by 3,4,5; LCM is 60, so smallest number = 60-1 = 59.

47. The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is always divisible by:

  1. 3
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 9

Answer: 3

Explanation: Sum = 3 × (middle number), hence always divisible by 3.

48. How many prime numbers are there between 1 and 20?

  1. 7
  2. 8
  3. 9
  4. 10

Answer: 8

Explanation: Primes under 20: 2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19 (8 numbers).

49. Consider the following statements: 1. The number 34567 is divisible by 3. 2. The number 34567 is divisible by 7. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both
  4. None

Answer: None

Explanation: Sum of digits 25 not divisible by 3; 34567÷7 leaves remainder 1, so neither statement correct.

50. Consider the following statements about natural numbers: 1. The sum of the first n natural numbers is always divisible by n. 2. The sum of the first n natural numbers is always divisible by (n+1). Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Neither 1 nor 2

Explanation: Sum of first n natural numbers = n(n+1)/2, which is divisible by n only if (n+1)/2 is integer (n odd), and by n+1 only if n/2 is integer (n even); neither holds for all n.
