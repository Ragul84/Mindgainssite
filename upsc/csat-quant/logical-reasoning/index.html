Logical Reasoning MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Logical Reasoning questions to revise the UPSC CSAT Quantitative Aptitude syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Statements: All cats are mammals. Some mammals are dogs. Conclusions: I. All cats are dogs. II. Some dogs are cats. Which of the following is correct?

  1. Only I
  2. Only II
  3. Both I and II
  4. None

Answer: None

Explanation: Using Venn diagram, no definite relation between cats and dogs; both conclusions are invalid.

2. A man walks 5 km east, turns right, walks 10 km, turns right, walks 15 km, turns left, walks 10 km. How far is he from the starting point and in which direction?

  1. 5 km West
  2. 10 km West
  3. 15 km West
  4. 20 km West

Answer: 10 km West

Explanation: Net displacement: East-West: 5 - 15 = -10 (10 km West); North-South: -10 + 10 = 0, so he is 10 km West.

3. P is the father of Q. Q is the mother of R. R is the brother of S. How is P related to S?

  1. Grandfather
  2. Father
  3. Uncle
  4. Brother

Answer: Grandfather

Explanation: Q is mother of R and S, so P is father of Q, hence grandfather of S.

4. In a class, Rahul is 7th from the top and 18th from the bottom. How many students are there in the class?

  1. 24
  2. 25
  3. 26
  4. 27

Answer: 24

Explanation: Total students = (Rank from top) + (Rank from bottom) - 1 = 7 + 18 - 1 = 24.

5. At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be together?

  1. 4:21 9/11
  2. 4:22
  3. 4:23
  4. 4:24

Answer: 4:21 9/11

Explanation: Time = (30*H - 5.5*M) = 0; for H=4, M = (30*4)/5.5 = 120/5.5 = 21 9/11 minutes.

6. What is the next number in the series: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?

  1. 36
  2. 49
  3. 64
  4. 35

Answer: 36

Explanation: The pattern is squares of consecutive integers: 1^2, 2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2; next is 6^2 = 36.

7. Find the odd one out: 8, 27, 64, 100, 125

  1. 8
  2. 27
  3. 64
  4. 100

Answer: 100

Explanation: 8, 27, 64, 125 are perfect cubes (2^3, 3^3, 4^3, 5^3); 100 is not a perfect cube.

8. If 'CAT' is coded as 'DBU', what is 'DOG' coded as?

  1. EPH
  2. FQI
  3. CPH
  4. DPH

Answer: EPH

Explanation: Each letter shifted forward by 1 position: D→E, O→P, G→H gives EPH.

9. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?

  1. School
  2. College
  3. Classroom
  4. Education

Answer: School

Explanation: Doctor works in a hospital; similarly, a teacher typically works in a school.

10. Find the missing number: 3, 7, 13, 21, ?

  1. 31
  2. 33
  3. 35
  4. 37

Answer: 31

Explanation: Differences increase by 2 each time: 4, 6, 8, next difference 10, so 21+10 = 31.

11. Consider the following statements: 1. All squares are rectangles. 2. Some rectangles are not squares. Which of the conclusions logically follows?

  1. All rectangles are squares
  2. Some squares are not rectangles
  3. All squares are not rectangles
  4. Some rectangles are squares

Answer: Some rectangles are squares

Explanation: Statement 1 implies squares are a subset of rectangles, so some rectangles are necessarily squares.

12. In a code language, if 'APPLE' is coded as 'BQQMF', how is 'MANGO' coded?

  1. NBOHS
  2. NBPHP
  3. NCOIP
  4. NBOIP

Answer: NBPHP

Explanation: Each letter is shifted by +1 in the alphabet; M→N, A→B, N→O, G→H, O→P gives NBPHP.

13. If the day after tomorrow is Sunday, what day was the day before yesterday?

  1. Monday
  2. Tuesday
  3. Wednesday
  4. Thursday

Answer: Wednesday

Explanation: If day after tomorrow is Sunday, today is Friday. Day before yesterday is Wednesday.

14. Consider the statements: 1. Some politicians are honest. 2. No honest person is corrupt. Which conclusion is valid?

  1. Some politicians are corrupt
  2. No politician is corrupt
  3. Some politicians are not corrupt
  4. All politicians are honest

Answer: Some politicians are not corrupt

Explanation: From 1 and 2, the honest politicians (some) are not corrupt, so some politicians are not corrupt.

15. A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand?

  1. 7.5 degrees
  2. 0 degrees
  3. 15 degrees
  4. 30 degrees

Answer: 7.5 degrees

Explanation: At 3:15, hour hand is at 97.5°, minute hand at 90°, difference is 7.5°.

16. In a row of 40 students, Ravi is 15th from the left. What is his rank from the right?

  1. 25th
  2. 26th
  3. 24th
  4. 27th

Answer: 26th

Explanation: Total students = left rank + right rank - 1; 40 = 15 + right - 1, so right = 26.

17. Consider the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?. What is the next number?

  1. 40
  2. 42
  3. 44
  4. 46

Answer: 42

Explanation: Pattern: n*(n+1); 5*6=30, next 6*7=42.

18. If 'P' means '×', 'Q' means '÷', 'R' means '+', and 'S' means '−', then what is 8 P 4 Q 2 R 6 S 3?

  1. 19
  2. 17
  3. 15
  4. 13

Answer: 19

Explanation: Substitute: 8 × 4 ÷ 2 + 6 − 3 = 16 + 6 − 3 = 19.

19. A man walks 5 km east, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 3 km
  2. 5 km
  3. 8 km
  4. 2 km

Answer: 3 km

Explanation: He ends 3 km south of start; distance = 3 km.

20. Consider: 1. All birds can fly. 2. Penguins are birds. 3. Penguins cannot fly. Which statement is false?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Only 3
  4. Both 1 and 3

Answer: Only 1

Explanation: Statement 1 is false because not all birds (e.g., penguins) can fly.

21. Consider the following statements: 1. All cats are mammals. 2. Some mammals are not cats. Which of the above statements is/are logically correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Statement 1 is true by definition; statement 2 is also true as there exist mammals that are not cats.

22. In a certain code, 'UPSC' is written as 'VQTD'. What is the code for 'EXAM'?

  1. FYBN
  2. FZBN
  3. FYCO
  4. FZCO

Answer: FYBN

Explanation: Each letter is replaced by the next letter in the alphabet; thus E→F, X→Y, A→B, M→N gives FYBN.

23. Consider the following sequence: 2, 6, 18, 54, ? What is the next term?

  1. 108
  2. 162
  3. 216
  4. 270

Answer: 162

Explanation: Each term is triple the previous; 54 × 3 = 162.

24. Three friends A, B, C are sitting in a row facing north. A is to the left of B, and C is to the right of B. Who is at the extreme left?

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. Cannot be determined

Answer: A

Explanation: The order from left to right is A, B, C; thus A is at extreme left.

25. In a group of 100 persons, 60 like tea, 40 like coffee, and 20 like both. How many like neither?

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 30
  4. 40

Answer: 20

Explanation: Neither = total - (tea + coffee - both) = 100 - (60 + 40 - 20) = 20.
