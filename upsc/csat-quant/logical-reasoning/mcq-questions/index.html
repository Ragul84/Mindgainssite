26. If 'MANGO' is coded as 'NBPHP', then 'APPLE' will be coded as?

  1. BQQMF
  2. BQQNF
  3. CQQMF
  4. BQQME

Answer: BQQMF

Explanation: Each letter is replaced by the next letter; A→B, P→Q, P→Q, L→M, E→F gives BQQMF.

27. Consider the following statements: 1. Some squares are rectangles. 2. No rectangle is a polygon. Which of the following conclusions logically follows?

  1. Some squares are not polygons.
  2. No square is a polygon.
  3. Some polygons are squares.
  4. None of the above

Answer: Some squares are not polygons.

Explanation: Since some squares are rectangles and no rectangle is a polygon, those squares are not polygons.

28. A man walks 5 km east, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 0 km
  2. 2 km
  3. 3 km
  4. 5 km

Answer: 3 km

Explanation: The east-west displacement cancels, leaving 3 km south; distance = 3 km.

29. Consider the following series: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ? What is the next term?

  1. 10
  2. 11
  3. 12
  4. 13

Answer: 13

Explanation: This is the Fibonacci sequence where each term is the sum of the previous two; 5+8=13.

30. A person walks 10 m east, then turns left and walks 10 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?

  1. North
  2. South
  3. East
  4. West

Answer: North

Explanation: After walking east, left (north), left (west), net displacement is 10 m north from start.

31. Find the next term in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

  1. 40
  2. 42
  3. 44
  4. 46

Answer: 42

Explanation: Each term is n(n+1) for n=1,2,...; next term is 6×7=42.

32. A is the father of B. C is the daughter of A. D is the brother of B. How is D related to C?

  1. Brother
  2. Uncle
  3. Cousin
  4. Nephew

Answer: Brother

Explanation: A has children B, C, and D, so D is brother of C.

33. If 1st January 2020 is Wednesday, what day is 1st January 2021?

  1. Thursday
  2. Friday
  3. Saturday
  4. Sunday

Answer: Friday

Explanation: 2020 is a leap year (366 days), so day advances by 2; Wednesday + 2 = Friday.

34. In a code, LAMP is written as 121316. Following the same pattern (A=1, B=2, ...), what is the code for CAMP?

  1. 31316
  2. 311316
  3. 313116
  4. 31116

Answer: 31316

Explanation: Each letter is replaced by its alphabetical position: C=3, A=1, M=13, P=16, concatenated as 31316.

35. Statements: All apples are fruits. Some fruits are sweet. Which of the following conclusions logically follow? I. Some apples are sweet. II. Some sweets are apples.

  1. Only I
  2. Only II
  3. Both I and II
  4. Neither I nor II

Answer: Neither I nor II

Explanation: No direct relation; sweet fruits may not be apples, so neither conclusion is certain.

36. In a row of children, Ravi is 7th from the left and 10th from the right. How many children are there in the row?

  1. 16
  2. 17
  3. 18
  4. 15

Answer: 16

Explanation: Total = left rank + right rank - 1 = 7+10-1 = 16.

37. To find the age of A, which statement(s) is/are sufficient? (1) A is 5 years older than B. (2) B is 20 years old.

  1. Statement (1) alone is sufficient
  2. Statement (2) alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone nor together is sufficient

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient

Explanation: Both together give A's age = 20+5 = 25; each alone is insufficient.

38. Find the missing number: 3 : 9 :: 5 : ?

  1. 15
  2. 25
  3. 20
  4. 10

Answer: 25

Explanation: The pattern is first number squared; 5² = 25.

39. Four friends A, B, C, D sit in a row. A is to the left of B. C is to the right of B. D is to the left of C. Who is at the extreme left?

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: A

Explanation: Order from left: A, B, D, C; thus A is extreme left.

40. Consider the following statements: 1. All cats are animals. 2. Some animals are dogs. Which of the following conclusions logically follows from these statements?

  1. Some cats are dogs
  2. Some animals are cats
  3. All dogs are cats
  4. No conclusion follows

Answer: Some animals are cats

Explanation: From 'All cats are animals', we know cats are a subset of animals; thus some animals are cats is a valid inference.

41. In a certain code, 'ABCD' is written as 'BECF'. How is 'EFGH' written in that code?

  1. FHIJ
  2. FGIJ
  3. FHIK
  4. FGIK

Answer: FHIJ

Explanation: Each letter is shifted +1, +2, +3, +4 positions respectively: E→F, F→H, G→I, H→J gives 'FHIJ'.

42. A is the father of B. C is the sister of B. D is the mother of C. How is D related to A?

  1. Sister
  2. Wife
  3. Mother
  4. Daughter

Answer: Wife

Explanation: A is father of B and C; D is mother of C, so D is also mother of B, hence A's wife.

43. A person walks 10 metres north, turns right and walks 5 metres, turns right and walks 10 metres, then turns left and walks 5 metres. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 5 metres
  2. 10 metres
  3. 15 metres
  4. 20 metres

Answer: 10 metres

Explanation: Net east-west displacement: 5m east then 5m west? Actually after right turn from north (east) 5m, then right (south) 10m, left (east) 5m: net east 10m, north 0m, so 10m from start.

44. Five persons P, Q, R, S, T are sitting in a row facing north. P is second to the left of T. Q is to the immediate right of R. S is not at any end. Who is at the left end?

  1. P
  2. Q
  3. R
  4. S

Answer: R

Explanation: Arrangement: R Q S P T (from left to right) satisfies conditions; R is at left end.

45. Consider the statement: 'If it rains, then the ground is wet.' Which of the following conclusions is logically valid if the ground is not wet?

  1. It did not rain
  2. It rained
  3. The ground is dry
  4. No conclusion

Answer: It did not rain

Explanation: By modus tollens, if the consequent is false, the antecedent must be false; thus it did not rain.

46. What is the next number in the series: 2, 5, 10, 17, ?

  1. 24
  2. 25
  3. 26
  4. 27

Answer: 26

Explanation: Pattern: n²+1 (1²+1=2, 2²+1=5, 3²+1=10, 4²+1=17, 5²+1=26).

47. Doctor is to Hospital as Teacher is to?

  1. School
  2. Classroom
  3. Education
  4. Student

Answer: School

Explanation: A doctor typically works in a hospital; similarly, a teacher typically works in a school.

48. Statement: 'India should invest heavily in solar energy.' Which of the following is an implicit assumption in this statement?

  1. Solar energy is cheaper than fossil fuels
  2. Solar energy is available in abundance
  3. Investment in solar energy will solve all energy problems
  4. India currently has no solar power plants

Answer: Solar energy is available in abundance

Explanation: The statement assumes solar energy is sufficiently available to justify heavy investment; that is an implicit assumption.

49. Consider the following statements: 1. All musicians are artists. 2. Some artists are painters. 3. No painter is a sculptor. Which of the following is definitely true?

  1. Some musicians are painters
  2. No musician is a sculptor
  3. Some artists are not sculptors
  4. All sculptors are musicians

Answer: Some artists are not sculptors

Explanation: Some artists are painters, and no painter is a sculptor, so those artists who are painters are not sculptors; thus some artists are not sculptors.

50. Consider the following statements: 1. If all A are B and some B are C, then some A are definitely C. 2. If no A is B and some C are B, then some C are not A. Which of the above statements is/are logically valid?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both
  4. None

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Statement 1 is invalid as the middle term 'B' may not include A; statement 2 is valid by the rule of contraposition.
