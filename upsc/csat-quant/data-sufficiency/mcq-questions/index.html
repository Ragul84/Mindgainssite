26. What is the speed of a train? Statement 1: It crosses a 200 m platform in 20 seconds. Statement 2: It crosses a man standing on the platform in 10 seconds.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient.
  4. Neither statement alone nor together is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient.

Explanation: From statement 1: speed = (train length + 200)/20. From statement 2: speed = train length/10. Solving both gives speed = 20 m/s; each alone is insufficient.

27. Is x an integer? Statement 1: x/2 is an integer. Statement 2: 2x is an integer.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient.
  4. Neither statement alone nor together is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient.

Explanation: If x/2 is integer, then x = 2*(integer) is integer. Statement 2 alone: 2x integer does not guarantee x integer (e.g., x=0.5).

28. What is the remainder when N is divided by 5? Statement 1: N leaves remainder 2 when divided by 10. Statement 2: N leaves remainder 3 when divided by 15.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient.
  4. Neither statement alone nor together is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient.

Explanation: Remainder modulo 5 is same as remainder modulo 10; statement 1 gives remainder 2. Statement 2 alone is insufficient.

29. Is triangle ABC right-angled at B? Statement 1: AB^2 + BC^2 = AC^2. Statement 2: Angle ABC = 90 degrees.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient.
  4. Neither statement alone nor together is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 2 alone is sufficient.

Explanation: Statement 2 directly states angle at B is 90°, so triangle is right-angled at B. Statement 1 confirms right angle at B by Pythagoras, but alone it doesn't specify which vertex is right angle.

30. What is the value of p + q? Statement 1: p - q = 5. Statement 2: p^2 - q^2 = 25.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient.
  4. Neither statement alone nor together is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient.

Explanation: From statement 2: (p-q)(p+q)=25. Using statement 1: p-q=5, so p+q=5. Each alone is insufficient; together they give the answer.

31. Is x > y? Statements: 1) x^2 > y^2; 2) x > 0 and y < 0.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Statement 2 alone is sufficient

Explanation: Statement 1 alone is insufficient as squares ignore sign. Statement 2 alone ensures x > 0 > y, so x > y is always true.

32. What is the remainder when N is divided by 5? Statements: 1) N is divisible by 10; 2) N is odd.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient

Explanation: Divisible by 10 implies last digit 0, so remainder 0. Statement 2 alone gives remainder 1 or 3, not unique.

33. Is the average of three distinct integers greater than 10? Statements: 1) The smallest integer is 9; 2) The largest integer is 15.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Neither statement alone is sufficient

Explanation: Smallest 9 gives possible average ≤ 9+10+11/3=10, not >10. Largest 15 gives possible average ≤ 13+14+15/3=14, but could be lower. Together, e.g., 9,10,15 gives average 11.33 >10, but 9,14,15 gives 12.67 >10; however 9,10,11 gives 10 exactly, not >10. So insufficient.

34. What is the speed of a train? Statements: 1) It crosses a 200 m platform in 20 seconds; 2) It crosses a man standing on platform in 10 seconds.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient

Explanation: Statement 1 gives (L+200)/20 = speed; statement 2 gives L/10 = speed. Solving both gives L and speed uniquely.

35. Is a > b? Statements: 1) a^3 > b^3; 2) a^2 > b^2.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient

Explanation: Cubes preserve order for real numbers, so a^3 > b^3 implies a > b. Squares do not preserve sign, so statement 2 alone is insufficient.

36. What is the two-digit number? Statements: 1) Sum of digits is 9; 2) The number is divisible by 5.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Neither statement alone is sufficient

Explanation: Sum 9 gives many numbers (18,27,...). Divisible by 5 gives numbers ending in 0 or 5. Together: 45, 90, etc. Not unique.

37. Is x an integer? Statements: 1) x/2 is an integer; 2) 2x is an integer.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient

Explanation: x/2 integer implies x = 2k, so x is integer. 2x integer allows x = 0.5, so not sufficient alone.

38. What is the area of a circle? Statements: 1) Its circumference is 44 cm; 2) Its diameter is 14 cm.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Each statement alone is sufficient

Explanation: Circumference gives radius = 7, area = 154. Diameter gives radius = 7, same area. Each alone is sufficient.

39. Is the integer x divisible by 12? Statement 1: x is divisible by 4. Statement 2: x is divisible by 3.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.

Explanation: Divisibility by 12 requires divisibility by both 4 and 3. Each alone is insufficient, but together they guarantee divisibility by 12.

40. What is the value of the two-digit number ab (where a is tens digit, b is units digit)? Statement 1: a + b = 9. Statement 2: The number is divisible by 5.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.

Explanation: From statement 1, possible pairs (a,b) sum to 9. From statement 2, b is 0 or 5. Together, only (4,5) or (9,0) are possible, but not unique; hence both together are insufficient.

41. Is x > y? Statement 1: x^2 > y^2. Statement 2: x > 0 and y < 0.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.

Explanation: Statement 1 alone is insufficient (e.g., x=-3, y=2 gives x^2>y^2 but x<y). Statement 2 alone ensures x>0>y, so x>y is always true.

42. What is the average speed of a car for a 200 km journey? Statement 1: The car took 4 hours to complete the journey. Statement 2: The car's speed was constant throughout the journey.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.

Explanation: Average speed = total distance / total time. Statement 1 gives time but not constant speed; statement 2 alone gives no time. Together, distance and time yield average speed.

43. Is the triangle PQR right-angled at Q? Statement 1: PQ^2 + QR^2 = PR^2. Statement 2: Angle PQR = 90 degrees.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Each statement alone is sufficient.

Explanation: Statement 1 directly gives Pythagoras theorem for right angle at Q. Statement 2 explicitly states the angle is 90 degrees. Each alone is sufficient.

44. What is the remainder when integer n is divided by 5? Statement 1: n leaves remainder 2 when divided by 10. Statement 2: n leaves remainder 7 when divided by 15.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.

Explanation: Statement 1: n = 10k+2, so n mod 5 = 2. Statement 2: n = 15m+7, so n mod 5 = 2 (since 15m divisible by 5, 7 mod 5 = 2). Each alone is sufficient.

45. Is the integer p prime? Statement 1: p has exactly two distinct positive divisors. Statement 2: p is odd and greater than 2.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.

Explanation: Statement 1 is the definition of a prime number. Statement 2 alone is insufficient (e.g., 9 is odd >2 but composite).

46. What is the value of a + b? Statement 1: a - b = 5. Statement 2: a^2 - b^2 = 25.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.

Explanation: From statement 2, (a-b)(a+b)=25. Using statement 1 (a-b=5), we get a+b=5. Neither alone gives a+b uniquely.

47. How many students are in a class? Statement 1: If 5 students are added, the total becomes a multiple of 7. Statement 2: If 3 students leave, the total becomes a multiple of 5.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.

Explanation: Let n be number. Statement 1: n+5=7k. Statement 2: n-3=5m. Two equations with three unknowns; infinite solutions exist (e.g., n=23, 58). Together insufficient.

48. Is x an integer? Statement 1: x^2 is an integer. Statement 2: x^3 is an integer.

  1. Statement 1 alone is sufficient, but statement 2 alone is not sufficient.
  2. Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.
  3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient.

Answer: Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not sufficient.

Explanation: Statement 1 alone is insufficient (e.g., x=√2 gives x^2=2 integer but x not integer). Statement 2 alone: if x^3 integer, x must be integer (since cube root of integer is integer only if perfect cube, but e.g., x=∛2 not integer; actually statement 2 alone is also insufficient. Correct: Both together insufficient? Wait, x=∛2 gives x^3=2 integer but x not integer. So statement 2 alone insufficient. Both together: x^2 and x^3 integers imply x^6 integer, but x=∛2 still works. So both insufficient. Answer: Neither alone nor together sufficient.

49. Is x > y? I. x = y + 2 II. x^2 = y^2

  1. Only statement I is sufficient
  2. Only statement II is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Only statement I is sufficient

Explanation: Statement I directly says x = y + 2, so x > y is always true. Statement II implies |x| = |y|, not sufficient.

50. What is the value of integer n? I. n is a prime number. II. n + 1 is a perfect square.

  1. Statement I alone is sufficient
  2. Statement II alone is sufficient
  3. Both statements together are sufficient
  4. Neither statement alone is sufficient

Answer: Both statements together are sufficient

Explanation: Together, n=3 (prime) and n+1=4 (perfect square) satisfies. Other primes like 2 give 3 (not square) so only one value works.
