26. A person travels from A to B at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 50 km/h
  2. 45 km/h
  3. 48 km/h
  4. 52 km/h

Answer: 48 km/h

Explanation: Average speed = 2*40*60/(40+60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h.

27. A train of length 300 m passes a pole in 15 seconds. What is its speed?

  1. 80 km/h
  2. 60 km/h
  3. 72 km/h
  4. 90 km/h

Answer: 72 km/h

Explanation: Speed = 300/15 = 20 m/s = 72 km/h.

28. Two trains of equal length (200 m each) run in the same direction at 72 km/h and 54 km/h. How long does the faster take to overtake the slower completely?

  1. 40 s
  2. 60 s
  3. 100 s
  4. 80 s

Answer: 80 s

Explanation: Relative speed = 72-54 = 18 km/h = 5 m/s. Distance = 400 m. Time = 400/5 = 80 s.

29. A cyclist covers 750 m in 2 minutes 30 seconds. What is his speed in km/h?

  1. 15 km/h
  2. 20 km/h
  3. 12 km/h
  4. 18 km/h

Answer: 18 km/h

Explanation: Speed = 750/150 = 5 m/s = 18 km/h.

30. A man crosses a 600 m long bridge in 5 minutes. What is his speed?

  1. 6 km/h
  2. 8 km/h
  3. 5 km/h
  4. 7.2 km/h

Answer: 7.2 km/h

Explanation: Speed = 600/(5*60) = 2 m/s = 7.2 km/h.

31. Two trains of lengths 200 m and 300 m are running on parallel tracks in opposite directions at speeds 60 km/h and 40 km/h. What is the time taken to cross each other?

  1. 18 sec
  2. 20 sec
  3. 24 sec
  4. 30 sec

Answer: 18 sec

Explanation: Relative speed = 100 km/h = 250/9 m/s; total distance = 500 m; time = 500 × 9/250 = 18 sec.

32. A man travels from A to B at 30 km/h and returns at 20 km/h. What is his average speed for the entire journey?

  1. 24 km/h
  2. 25 km/h
  3. 26 km/h
  4. 22.5 km/h

Answer: 24 km/h

Explanation: Average speed = 2×30×20/(30+20) = 24 km/h (harmonic mean).

33. A boat goes 12 km upstream and 12 km downstream in a total of 3 hours. The speed of the stream is 2 km/h. Find the speed of the boat in still water.

  1. 6 km/h
  2. 8 km/h
  3. 10 km/h
  4. 12 km/h

Answer: 8 km/h

Explanation: Let boat speed = x; 12/(x-2) + 12/(x+2) = 3; solving gives x = 8 km/h.

34. A person walks at 4 km/h and reaches the office 5 minutes late. If he walks at 5 km/h, he reaches 10 minutes early. What is the distance to the office?

  1. 2 km
  2. 3 km
  3. 4 km
  4. 5 km

Answer: 5 km

Explanation: Difference in time = 15 min = 0.25 h; d/4 - d/5 = 0.25 ⇒ d = 5 km.

35. Two cars start from the same point at the same time in opposite directions with speeds 60 km/h and 40 km/h. What is the distance between them after 2 hours?

  1. 100 km
  2. 200 km
  3. 150 km
  4. 250 km

Answer: 200 km

Explanation: Relative speed = 100 km/h; distance = 100 × 2 = 200 km.

36. A train 150 m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. What is the speed of the train?

  1. 10 m/s
  2. 15 m/s
  3. 20 m/s
  4. 25 m/s

Answer: 10 m/s

Explanation: Speed = length/time = 150/15 = 10 m/s.

37. A man cycles at 12 km/h. How many meters does he cover in 1 minute?

  1. 200 m
  2. 150 m
  3. 180 m
  4. 250 m

Answer: 200 m

Explanation: 12 km/h = 12000 m/60 min = 200 m/min.

38. Two trains of equal length 100 m are moving on parallel tracks in the same direction at 50 km/h and 40 km/h. How long does the faster train take to overtake the slower?

  1. 36 sec
  2. 72 sec
  3. 18 sec
  4. 54 sec

Answer: 72 sec

Explanation: Relative speed = 10 km/h = 25/9 m/s; total distance = 200 m; time = 200 × 9/25 = 72 sec.

39. A person travels 60 km at 30 km/h and then another 60 km at 40 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 34.29 km/h
  2. 35 km/h
  3. 36 km/h
  4. 33.33 km/h

Answer: 34.29 km/h

Explanation: Total distance = 120 km; total time = 2 + 1.5 = 3.5 h; average = 120/3.5 ≈ 34.29 km/h.

40. A boat covers 24 km downstream in 2 hours. The speed of the stream is 2 km/h. What is the speed of the boat in still water?

  1. 10 km/h
  2. 12 km/h
  3. 8 km/h
  4. 14 km/h

Answer: 10 km/h

Explanation: Downstream speed = 24/2 = 12 km/h; boat speed = 12 - 2 = 10 km/h.

41. A car travels from X to Y at 40 km/h and returns from Y to X at 60 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire journey?

  1. 50 km/h
  2. 48 km/h
  3. 45 km/h
  4. 52 km/h

Answer: 48 km/h

Explanation: Average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = 2*40*60/(100) = 48 km/h.

42. Two trains, 150 m and 200 m long, are running on parallel tracks in opposite directions at 60 km/h and 40 km/h respectively. How long will they take to cross each other?

  1. 12.6 s
  2. 18.5 s
  3. 15.2 s
  4. 10.5 s

Answer: 12.6 s

Explanation: Relative speed = 100 km/h = 500/18 m/s; total length = 350 m; time = 350 * 18/500 = 12.6 s.

43. A boat takes 6 hours to go 30 km upstream and 4 hours to go 36 km downstream. What is the speed of the stream?

  1. 1.5 km/h
  2. 2 km/h
  3. 2.5 km/h
  4. 3 km/h

Answer: 2 km/h

Explanation: Upstream speed = 5 km/h, downstream = 9 km/h; stream speed = (9-5)/2 = 2 km/h.

44. A person covers a certain distance at a speed of 30 km/h and returns at a different speed. If the average speed for the whole journey is 40 km/h, what is the speed while returning?

  1. 48 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 60 km/h
  4. 45 km/h

Answer: 60 km/h

Explanation: Using 2*30*x/(30+x)=40 gives 60x=1200+40x → x=60 km/h.

45. Two persons start running from the same point on a circular track of length 400 m in the same direction at speeds of 8 m/s and 6 m/s. After how much time will they meet again for the first time?

  1. 200 s
  2. 100 s
  3. 50 s
  4. 400 s

Answer: 200 s

Explanation: Relative speed = 2 m/s; time = track length / relative speed = 400/2 = 200 s.

46. A man covers half of a journey at a speed of 30 km/h and the remaining half at 40 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 35 km/h
  2. 34.29 km/h
  3. 36.5 km/h
  4. 33.33 km/h

Answer: 34.29 km/h

Explanation: Average speed = 2*30*40/(30+40) = 2400/70 ≈ 34.29 km/h.

47. Two cars start from points A and B towards each other at speeds of 50 km/h and 40 km/h respectively. The distance between A and B is 270 km. At what time will they meet if they start at 8:00 AM?

  1. 10:00 AM
  2. 11:00 AM
  3. 12:00 Noon
  4. 9:00 AM

Answer: 11:00 AM

Explanation: Relative speed = 90 km/h; time = 270/90 = 3 hours; meeting time = 11:00 AM.

48. Consider the following statements: 1. If a person travels at two different speeds for equal time intervals, the average speed is the arithmetic mean of the two speeds. 2. If a person travels at two different speeds for equal distances, the average speed is the harmonic mean of the two speeds. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements are correct: equal time → arithmetic mean; equal distance → harmonic mean.

49. A train 200 m long passes a telegraph post in 10 seconds. How long will it take to cross a platform 300 m long?

  1. 15 s
  2. 20 s
  3. 25 s
  4. 30 s

Answer: 25 s

Explanation: Speed = 200/10 = 20 m/s; crossing platform: distance = 200+300 = 500 m; time = 500/20 = 25 s.

50. A man usually drives to work at 40 km/h and reaches on time. One day he starts 30 minutes late and increases his speed by 20 km/h to reach on time. What is the distance to his office?

  1. 60 km
  2. 80 km
  3. 100 km
  4. 120 km

Answer: 60 km

Explanation: Let distance d. Normal time = d/40. Late by 0.5 h, new speed 60 km/h. Equation: d/60 = d/40 - 0.5 → d = 60 km.
