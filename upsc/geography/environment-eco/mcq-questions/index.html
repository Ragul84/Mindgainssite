26. Which mountain range runs along the border between France and Spain?

  1. Pyrenees
  2. Alps
  3. Carpathian
  4. Apennine

Answer: Pyrenees

Explanation: The Pyrenees mountain range runs along the border between France and Spain.

27. What is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the leaves?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant.

28. What is the term for the study of the relationship between organisms and their environment?

  1. Ecology
  2. Biology
  3. Geology
  4. Meteorology

Answer: Ecology

Explanation: Ecology is the study of the relationship between organisms and their environment.

29. Which of the following rivers is the longest in South America?

  1. Amazon
  2. Parana
  3. Sao Francisco
  4. Magdalena

Answer: Amazon

Explanation: The Amazon River is the longest in South America.

30. What is the term for the movement of tectonic plates?

  1. Continental drift
  2. Plate tectonics
  3. Weathering
  4. Erosion

Answer: Plate tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics refers to the movement of tectonic plates.

31. What is the term for the movement of the Earth's crust resulting in the formation of mountains?

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Plate tectonics
  4. Denudation

Answer: Plate tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics is the process by which the Earth's crust moves, resulting in mountain formation.

32. Which strait separates the continents of Asia and Africa?

  1. Bab-el-Mandeb
  2. Bosphorus
  3. Malacca
  4. Sunda

Answer: Bab-el-Mandeb

Explanation: The Bab-el-Mandeb strait separates Asia and Africa, connecting the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden.

33. Which of the following is NOT a type of natural ecosystem?

  1. Desert
  2. Forest
  3. Grassland
  4. Atmosphere

Answer: Atmosphere

Explanation: Atmosphere is not a type of natural ecosystem, but rather the layer of gases surrounding the Earth.

34. What is the term for the process by which the Earth's surface is worn away by natural forces?

  1. Denudation
  2. Deposition
  3. Erosion
  4. Sedimentation

Answer: Denudation

Explanation: Denudation is the process of wearing away the Earth's surface by natural forces like erosion and weathering.

35. Which of the following rivers is known for its braided channel pattern?

  1. Ganga
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Indus
  4. Godavari

Answer: Brahmaputra

Explanation: The Brahmaputra river is known for its braided channel pattern due to its high sediment load.

36. What is the term for the movement of plants and animals from one region to another?

  1. Migration
  2. Dispersal
  3. Invasion
  4. Extinction

Answer: Dispersal

Explanation: Dispersal refers to the movement of plants and animals from one region to another.

37. Which river is the longest in South America?

  1. Amazon
  2. Parana
  3. Sao Francisco
  4. Magdalena

Answer: Amazon

Explanation: The Amazon river is the longest in South America.

38. What is the process by which the Earth's plates move and change?

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Plate tectonics
  4. Glaciation

Answer: Plate tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics is the process by which the Earth's plates move and change.

39. What is the primary factor responsible for the formation of the Indian monsoon?

  1. Temperature
  2. Pressure
  3. Wind
  4. Latitude

Answer: Pressure

Explanation: The primary factor responsible for the formation of the Indian monsoon is the difference in pressure between the Indian Ocean and the Indian subcontinent. This pressure difference drives the wind patterns.

40. What is the largest desert in the world by area?

  1. Sahara
  2. Gobi
  3. Mojave
  4. Antarctic

Answer: Antarctic

Explanation: The Antarctic Desert is the largest desert in the world, covering an area of about 14 million square kilometers.

41. Which of the following is NOT a type of rock?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Mineral

Answer: Mineral

Explanation: Mineral is not a type of rock, but rather a naturally occurring inorganic solid substance with a specific chemical composition.

42. What is the term for the study of the shape and features of the Earth's surface?

  1. Geology
  2. Geomorphology
  3. Cartography
  4. Meteorology

Answer: Geomorphology

Explanation: Geomorphology is the study of the shape and features of the Earth's surface, including landforms, landscapes, and processes that shape them.

43. Which of the following types of climate is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of precipitation?

  1. Tropical
  2. Desert
  3. Temperate
  4. Polar

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: A tropical climate is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of precipitation throughout the year.

44. What is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the leaves, and is then released into the air as water vapor?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant and is released into the air as water vapor.

45. What is the term for the movement of tectonic plates that results in the creation of mountains?

  1. Continental drift
  2. Plate tectonics
  3. Orogenesis
  4. Weathering

Answer: Orogenesis

Explanation: Orogenesis is the term for the movement of tectonic plates that results in the creation of mountains.

46. Which of the following is a factor that affects the climate of a region?

  1. Latitude
  2. Altitude
  3. Ocean currents
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All of the above are factors that affect the climate of a region.

47. What is the primary cause of the formation of the monsoon low-pressure system over the Indian subcontinent?

  1. Temperature gradient
  2. Pressure gradient
  3. Coriolis force
  4. Trade winds

Answer: Temperature gradient

Explanation: Temperature gradient between land and sea causes the formation of the monsoon low-pressure system.

48. Which of the following straits separates the continents of Asia and Africa?

  1. Bosphorus
  2. Bab-el-Mandeb
  3. Malacca
  4. Sunda

Explanation: The Bab-el-Mandeb strait separates the continents of Asia and Africa.

49. Which of the following cities is located at the mouth of the Ganges River?

  1. Kolkata
  2. Mumbai
  3. Chennai
  4. Bangkok

Answer: Kolkata

Explanation: Kolkata is located at the mouth of the Ganges River.

50. Which type of rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Foliated

Answer: Igneous

Explanation: Igneous rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma.
