26. The largest salt lake in India is:

  1. Sambhar Lake, Rajasthan
  2. Chilika Lake, Odisha
  3. Pulicat Lake, Tamil Nadu/AP
  4. Pangong Tso, Ladakh

Answer: Sambhar Lake, Rajasthan

Explanation: Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) is India's largest inland saltwater lake (about 230 sq km during monsoon, much smaller in summer). It supplies most of Rajasthan's salt. Chilika is brackish, not fully saline.

27. The 'Convergence Zone' where trade winds from both hemispheres meet is called:

  1. Polar Front
  2. Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ)
  3. Sub-tropical High Pressure Belt
  4. Ferrel Cell

Answer: Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ)

Explanation: The ITCZ (Intertropical Convergence Zone) is a band near the equator where northeast and southeast trade winds converge. It plays a major role in the Indian monsoon mechanism.

28. The Dzukou Valley is in:

  1. Nagaland and Manipur border
  2. Arunachal Pradesh
  3. Sikkim
  4. Meghalaya

Answer: Nagaland and Manipur border

Explanation: Dzukou Valley (2,452 m) on the Nagaland-Manipur border is known as the 'Valley of Flowers of the Northeast' — famous for its seasonal flowers, especially the Dzukou lily. It is a biodiversity-rich valley.

29. The 'Jet Stream' is:

  1. A narrow band of fast-moving air in the upper troposphere
  2. An ocean current
  3. A type of monsoon wind
  4. A wind near the equator

Answer: A narrow band of fast-moving air in the upper troposphere

Explanation: The Jet Stream is a narrow, fast-moving band of air (wind speed 100-300 km/h) in the upper troposphere/lower stratosphere at 7-16 km altitude. It influences weather patterns, aviation routes, and the Indian monsoon.

30. Tropical cyclones in the Bay of Bengal are most frequent in:

  1. October-November and May-June (post and pre-monsoon)
  2. July-August
  3. January-February
  4. Throughout the year equally

Answer: October-November and May-June (post and pre-monsoon)

Explanation: Tropical cyclones in the Bay of Bengal are most frequent in October-November (retreating monsoon) and May-June (pre-monsoon). Tamil Nadu coast (Coromandel Coast) is especially vulnerable during the northeast monsoon.

31. What is the primary factor responsible for the formation of tropical cyclones in the Indian Ocean?

  1. Temperature
  2. Humidity
  3. Wind shear
  4. Coriolis force

Answer: Temperature

Explanation: Temperature is the primary factor for tropical cyclone formation, as warm ocean waters heat the air, causing it to rise and create a low-pressure system.

32. Which of the following climate types is characterized by low humidity and high evaporation rates?

  1. Tropical
  2. Desert
  3. Temperate
  4. Polar

Answer: Desert

Explanation: Desert climates are known for low humidity and high evaporation rates due to intense sunlight and limited vegetation.

33. What is the term for the movement of water from the ground to the atmosphere through plants?

  1. Respiration
  2. Transpiration
  3. Evaporation
  4. Condensation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water is transported through plants, from the roots to the atmosphere.

34. Which mountain range acts as a barrier to the monsoon winds in India?

  1. Himalayas
  2. Aravallis
  3. Vindhyas
  4. Satpuras

Answer: Himalayas

Explanation: The Himalayas act as a barrier to the monsoon winds, forcing them to rise and cool, resulting in precipitation.

35. What is the name of the climate classification system developed by Wladimir Köppen?

  1. Köppen climate classification
  2. Thornthwaite climate classification
  3. Holdridge climate classification
  4. Trewartha climate classification

Answer: Köppen climate classification

Explanation: The Köppen climate classification is a widely used system for categorizing climates based on temperature and precipitation patterns.

36. Which of the following factors contributes to the formation of a microclimate?

  1. Latitude
  2. Altitude
  3. Topography
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All of the above factors can contribute to the formation of a microclimate, which is a localized climate that differs from the surrounding area.

37. What is the term for the study of the relationship between climate and living organisms?

  1. Biogeography
  2. Climatology
  3. Ecology
  4. Bioclimatology

Answer: Bioclimatology

Explanation: Bioclimatology is the study of the relationship between climate and living organisms, including the impact of climate on ecosystems and species distribution.

38. Which of the following climate types is characterized by high levels of rainfall throughout the year?

  1. Tropical rainforest
  2. Tropical savanna
  3. Temperate grassland
  4. Desert

Answer: Tropical rainforest

Explanation: Tropical rainforests are known for high levels of rainfall throughout the year, supporting dense vegetation and diverse ecosystems.

39. What is the name of the phenomenon where the rotation of the Earth causes moving objects to deflect to the right in the Northern Hemisphere?

  1. Coriolis effect
  2. Trade winds
  3. Westerlies
  4. Jet stream

Answer: Coriolis effect

Explanation: The Coriolis effect is the apparent deflection of moving objects, such as air masses and ocean currents, due to the rotation of the Earth.

40. Which of the following is a consequence of climate change?

  1. Rising sea levels
  2. Decreasing global temperatures
  3. Increasing biodiversity
  4. Stable weather patterns

Answer: Rising sea levels

Explanation: Rising sea levels are a consequence of climate change, resulting from the melting of glaciers and ice sheets, as well as the thermal expansion of seawater.

41. What is the primary factor influencing the climate of India?

  1. Latitude
  2. Altitude
  3. Ocean Currents
  4. Distance from the Equator

Answer: Distance from the Equator

Explanation: Distance from the equator influences the climate of India, with the tropics receiving more solar radiation.
