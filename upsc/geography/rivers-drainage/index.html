Rivers Drainage MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Rivers Drainage questions to revise the UPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. The Sundarbans delta is formed by which rivers?

  1. Ganga and Yamuna
  2. Ganga and Brahmaputra
  3. Indus and Chenab
  4. Krishna and Godavari

Answer: Ganga and Brahmaputra

Explanation: The Sundarbans delta — the world's largest mangrove forest — is formed by the Ganga and Brahmaputra rivers in West Bengal and Bangladesh.

2. The soil found in the Gangetic plains is:

  1. Red laterite soil
  2. Black regur soil
  3. Alluvial soil
  4. Desert soil

Answer: Alluvial soil

Explanation: Alluvial soil (brought by rivers) is found in the Gangetic plains. It is the most fertile soil type in India, ideal for wheat, rice, and sugarcane cultivation.

3. India's largest river island is:

  1. Majuli
  2. Wheeler Island
  3. Sriharikota
  4. Neil Island

Answer: Majuli

Explanation: Majuli in Assam is India's largest river island, located on the Brahmaputra river. It is also the world's largest inhabited river island.

4. The Chambal river is a tributary of:

  1. Yamuna
  2. Ganga
  3. Ghaghra
  4. Son

Answer: Yamuna

Explanation: The Chambal river (originating from Janapav hills, Vindhya Range, MP) is a major right-bank tributary of the Yamuna. It flows through MP and Rajasthan before joining the Yamuna in UP.

5. The Indus river originates from:

  1. Mansarovar Lake area, Tibet
  2. Gangotri glacier
  3. Amarkantak
  4. Trimbak, Nashik

Answer: Mansarovar Lake area, Tibet

Explanation: The Indus (Sindhu) originates from Sengge Zangbo springs near Mansarovar Lake in Tibet (China). It flows through Ladakh (India), Pakistan, and drains into the Arabian Sea.

6. Which river is called 'Sorrow of Bihar'?

  1. Kosi
  2. Son
  3. Gandak
  4. Bagmati

Answer: Kosi

Explanation: The Kosi river is called the 'Sorrow of Bihar' because of the devastating floods it causes annually. It frequently changes course and causes enormous loss of life and property.

7. The river Narmada flows into:

  1. Arabian Sea
  2. Bay of Bengal
  3. Indian Ocean
  4. Gulf of Mannar

Answer: Arabian Sea

Explanation: The Narmada (originating from Amarkantak, MP) flows westward through the Vindhya-Satpura rift valley and drains into the Arabian Sea near Bharuch in Gujarat.

8. The Kaveri river originates from:

  1. Talakaveri, Kodagu (Coorg), Karnataka
  2. Ghats near Ooty
  3. Brahmagiri hills, Kerala
  4. Coorg, near Mysore

Answer: Talakaveri, Kodagu (Coorg), Karnataka

Explanation: The Kaveri (Cauvery) originates from Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu (Coorg) district, Karnataka. It flows through Karnataka and Tamil Nadu and drains into the Bay of Bengal.

9. Mangrove forests in India are largest in:

  1. Sundarbans (West Bengal)
  2. Kerala
  3. Goa
  4. Odisha

Answer: Sundarbans (West Bengal)

Explanation: India has the world's largest mangrove — the Sundarbans in West Bengal. Other mangrove areas: Godavari-Krishna delta (AP), Mahanadi delta (Odisha), Gulf of Kutch (Gujarat), and Andamans.

10. The world's longest river is:

  1. Nile
  2. Amazon
  3. Yangtze
  4. Mississippi

Answer: Nile

Explanation: The Nile (6,650 km) is the world's longest river, flowing through northeastern Africa into the Mediterranean Sea. The Amazon is the largest by volume/discharge.

11. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is for protection of:

  1. Wetlands (marshes, lakes, rivers, peat bogs, coastal areas)
  2. Forests
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Mountain ecosystems

Answer: Wetlands (marshes, lakes, rivers, peat bogs, coastal areas)

Explanation: The Ramsar Convention on Wetlands (signed 1971, Ramsar, Iran) is an international treaty for conservation of wetlands. India has 75 Ramsar sites. Chilika Lake (Odisha) was India's first Ramsar site (1981).

12. India's first Ramsar site (wetland of international importance) was:

  1. Chilika Lake, Odisha (1981)
  2. Keoladeo Ghana, Rajasthan
  3. Wular Lake, J&K
  4. Loktak Lake, Manipur

Answer: Chilika Lake, Odisha (1981)

Explanation: Chilika Lake (Odisha) was designated as India's first Ramsar site in 1981, along with Keoladeo Ghana (Bharatpur, Rajasthan). Both were designated simultaneously. Chilika is Asia's largest brackish-water lake.

13. Shipki La Pass connects:

  1. India (Himachal Pradesh) and China (Tibet)
  2. India and Nepal
  3. India and Bhutan
  4. India and Pakistan

Answer: India (Himachal Pradesh) and China (Tibet)

Explanation: Shipki La Pass (5,669 m) in Kinnaur district, Himachal Pradesh, connects India with the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. The Sutlej river enters India through this pass.

14. The Bhakra Nangal Dam is on which river?

  1. Sutlej
  2. Ravi
  3. Beas
  4. Chenab

Answer: Sutlej

Explanation: Bhakra Nangal Dam (Bhakra Dam — 226 m high) is on the Sutlej river in Himachal Pradesh. The Nangal Dam is a downstream smaller dam. The reservoir is called Gobind Sagar Lake.

15. Hirakud Dam (world's longest earthen dam) is on:

  1. Mahanadi river, Odisha
  2. Godavari
  3. Brahmani
  4. Baitarani

Answer: Mahanadi river, Odisha

Explanation: Hirakud Dam on the Mahanadi river in Odisha is one of the world's longest earthen dams at 25.8 km. It forms Hirakud reservoir, one of India's largest artificial lakes.

16. Nagarjunasagar Dam is on:

  1. Krishna river, Telangana/Andhra Pradesh border
  2. Godavari
  3. Tungabhadra
  4. Mahanadi

Answer: Krishna river, Telangana/Andhra Pradesh border

Explanation: Nagarjunasagar Dam on the Krishna river (Telangana-Andhra Pradesh border) has one of the world's largest masonry dams. The reservoir provides irrigation and drinking water to both states.

17. The Tungabhadra Dam (supporting Vijayanagara area) is in:

  1. Karnataka (Hospet)
  2. Andhra Pradesh
  3. Telangana
  4. Tamil Nadu

Answer: Karnataka (Hospet)

Explanation: Tungabhadra Dam is in Hospet, Karnataka on the Tungabhadra river (a tributary of Krishna). It provides irrigation water to Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. The Hampi (Vijayanagara) ruins are nearby.

18. The Idukki Dam (arch dam) in Kerala is on:

  1. Periyar river
  2. Kabani
  3. Chaliyar
  4. Pamba

Answer: Periyar river

Explanation: Idukki Dam is a double curvature arch dam on the Periyar river in Kerala's Idukki district. It is India's largest arch dam. The nearby Cheruthoni and Kulamavu dams form the Idukki Hydroelectric Project.

19. Which city is called the 'Manchester of India'?

  1. Ahmedabad
  2. Mumbai
  3. Coimbatore
  4. Kanpur

Answer: Ahmedabad

Explanation: Ahmedabad (Gujarat) is called the 'Manchester of India' for its cotton textile industry. Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu) is called the 'Manchester of South India' for similar reasons.

20. A 'watershed' or 'water divide' is:

  1. A ridge separating two river basins/drainage systems
  2. A dam or barrier
  3. A water body
  4. Flood plain of a river

Answer: A ridge separating two river basins/drainage systems

Explanation: A watershed (water divide) is a ridge of high land that separates two adjacent river drainage basins. Rain falling on one side flows to one river system, and on the other side to a different system.

21. The 'Delta' of a river is formed at:

  1. Mouth of the river where it meets the sea
  2. Source of the river
  3. Confluence of two rivers
  4. Where a river bends

Answer: Mouth of the river where it meets the sea

Explanation: A river delta is a landform created by deposition of sediment carried by a river as it enters a slower-moving body of water (sea, ocean, lake). Named after the Greek letter Delta (Δ) due to its triangular shape.

22. 'Lagoons' are:

  1. Shallow water bodies separated from the sea by a narrow barrier (sandbar/coral reef)
  2. Deep sea trenches
  3. Mountain lakes
  4. River ox-bow lakes

Answer: Shallow water bodies separated from the sea by a narrow barrier (sandbar/coral reef)

Explanation: Lagoons are shallow water bodies (lakes) separated from the larger sea/ocean by a narrow barrier of land (sandbar, barrier island, or coral reef). Chilika (Odisha) and Pulicat (Tamil Nadu-AP) are famous Indian lagoons.

23. ISRO's satellite launch center (SDSC-SHAR) is at:

  1. Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh
  2. Chennai, Tamil Nadu
  3. Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
  4. Bengaluru, Karnataka

Answer: Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh

Explanation: ISRO's Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC-SHAR) is at Sriharikota island on Pulicat Lake, in Andhra Pradesh. It is India's main satellite launch center. ISRO's HQ and research centers are in Bengaluru.

24. Which of the following lakes is famous for rose-colored water?

  1. Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan)
  2. Chilika Lake (Odisha)
  3. Wular Lake (J&K)
  4. Loktak Lake (Manipur)

Answer: Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan)

Explanation: Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) — India's largest inland saltwater lake — sometimes appears pinkish due to salt-loving microorganisms (halophytes). It is the main source of salt in Rajasthan and a Ramsar wetland site.

25. The Ganga-Brahmaputra delta (Sundarbans) is the world's:

  1. Largest river delta in the world
  2. Second largest delta
  3. Largest mangrove forest only
  4. Largest lagoon

Answer: Largest river delta in the world

Explanation: The Ganga-Brahmaputra-Meghna delta (Sundarbans) is the world's largest river delta, covering about 100,000 sq km across Bangladesh and West Bengal (India). It is also the world's largest mangrove forest.
