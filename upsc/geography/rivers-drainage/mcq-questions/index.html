26. Which river is known as the 'Lifeline of Rajasthan'?

  1. Indira Gandhi Canal (Luni river is the main river)
  2. Chambal
  3. Banas
  4. Mahi

Answer: Indira Gandhi Canal (Luni river is the main river)

Explanation: The Luni river is the only significant river in Rajasthan (drains into Rann of Kutch). But the Indira Gandhi Canal is often called the 'Lifeline of Rajasthan' as it brings water from the Sutlej-Beas to the Thar Desert.

27. The river Beas originates from:

  1. Beas Kund, near Rohtang Pass, Himachal Pradesh
  2. Mansarovar
  3. Siachen glacier
  4. Gangotri

Answer: Beas Kund, near Rohtang Pass, Himachal Pradesh

Explanation: The Beas (Vipasa) originates from the Beas Kund near Rohtang Pass in Kullu, Himachal Pradesh. It flows through Kullu Valley and joins the Sutlej near Harike (Punjab). Part of the Indus system.

28. The Ravi river flows through:

  1. Himachal Pradesh and Punjab before entering Pakistan
  2. Only Punjab
  3. Only Himachal Pradesh
  4. Haryana and Punjab

Answer: Himachal Pradesh and Punjab before entering Pakistan

Explanation: Ravi (Parushni/Iravati) originates from Kullu, Himachal Pradesh, flows through Punjab (Pathankot area), enters Pakistan and joins the Chenab. Under Indus Waters Treaty (1960), Pakistan gets the Ravi, Beas, and Sutlej.

29. The Nile river flows through:

  1. Ethiopia, Sudan, and Egypt (primary course)
  2. South Africa
  3. Central Africa
  4. West Africa

Answer: Ethiopia, Sudan, and Egypt (primary course)

Explanation: The Nile flows north through eastern Africa — originating in the Ethiopian Highlands (Blue Nile source: Lake Tana) and central Africa (White Nile source: Lake Victoria area) before flowing through Sudan and Egypt into the Mediterranean.

30. The Congo river (Zaire) is the world's:

  1. Deepest river and second largest by volume
  2. Longest river in Africa
  3. Largest river in the world
  4. Widest river

Answer: Deepest river and second largest by volume

Explanation: The Congo river (Central Africa) is the world's deepest river (over 220 m) and second largest by volume/discharge (after Amazon). It is Africa's second longest river (after Nile) and crosses the equator twice.

31. The Mississippi-Missouri river system is in:

  1. North America (USA)
  2. South America
  3. Asia
  4. Africa

Answer: North America (USA)

Explanation: The Mississippi-Missouri river system (about 6,275 km total length) is in the USA. The Mississippi flows south from Minnesota to the Gulf of Mexico. The Missouri is its longest tributary.

32. Lake Victoria is significant for:

  1. Being the source of the White Nile and Africa's largest lake
  2. Being the deepest lake in Africa
  3. Having the greatest volume of water
  4. Being Africa's largest saltwater lake

Answer: Being the source of the White Nile and Africa's largest lake

Explanation: Lake Victoria (68,870 sq km) in East Africa (shared by Uganda, Tanzania, Kenya) is Africa's largest lake by area and the source of the White Nile. It is the world's second-largest freshwater lake by area.

33. Saline/alkaline soil (Usar/Reh) is formed in:

  1. Waterlogged and arid areas with high salt content
  2. High rainfall areas
  3. Mountain slopes
  4. River deltas

Answer: Waterlogged and arid areas with high salt content

Explanation: Saline/alkaline soils (Usar, Reh, Kallar) form in waterlogged and arid areas where salts accumulate due to high evaporation or poor drainage. Common in parts of UP, Punjab, Haryana, Bihar, and Rajasthan.

34. The National Waterway No. 1 (NW-1) is on:

  1. Ganga river (Allahabad to Haldia, 1,620 km)
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Godavari
  4. Sundarbans

Answer: Ganga river (Allahabad to Haldia, 1,620 km)

Explanation: National Waterway No. 1 (NW-1) is on the Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hooghly river system from Allahabad (Prayagraj) to Haldia (West Bengal), covering 1,620 km. India has 111 national waterways.

35. The Kaziranga National Park is on the banks of:

  1. Brahmaputra river, Assam
  2. Ganga
  3. Godavari
  4. Mahanadi

Answer: Brahmaputra river, Assam

Explanation: Kaziranga National Park is in Golaghat and Nagaon districts of Assam, on the banks of the Brahmaputra river. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for the one-horned rhinoceros and Bengal tiger.

36. Which is the only 'river island' national park in India?

  1. Majuli (not exactly) — Dibru-Saikhowa National Park, Assam is on a river island
  2. Majuli Island (Brahmaputra), Assam
  3. Bhavani Island, Telangana
  4. None exist

Answer: Majuli Island (Brahmaputra), Assam

Explanation: Majuli in the Brahmaputra river (Assam) is the world's largest river island (875 sq km). It is a major Vaishnavite cultural center. Dibru-Saikhowa National Park (Assam) is also in the Brahmaputra floodplain.

37. The Satpura Range is significant because:

  1. It runs east-west between the Narmada and Tapti rivers, dividing the Deccan Plateau from the Narmada plains
  2. It is the highest range in central India
  3. It is on the east coast
  4. It separates Rajasthan from MP

Answer: It runs east-west between the Narmada and Tapti rivers, dividing the Deccan Plateau from the Narmada plains

Explanation: The Satpura Range runs east-west between the Narmada (north) and Tapti/Tapi (south) rivers in central India (MP and Maharashtra). It runs roughly parallel to the Vindhya Range. Pachmarhi is the only hill station in MP.

38. The petrochemical hub of India is:

  1. Vadodara (Baroda) and Jamnagar, Gujarat
  2. Mumbai
  3. Kolkata
  4. Chennai

Answer: Vadodara (Baroda) and Jamnagar, Gujarat

Explanation: Gujarat (Vadodara with GAIL/IPCL and Jamnagar with Reliance Industries — world's largest refinery complex) is India's petrochemical hub. Vadodara hosts major GPCL/ONGC/IPCL facilities.

39. The longest river in Europe is:

  1. Volga (Russia)
  2. Danube
  3. Rhine
  4. Thames

Answer: Volga (Russia)

Explanation: Volga river (3,692 km) in Russia is Europe's longest river. It flows into the Caspian Sea (not the ocean). The Danube (2,860 km) is Europe's second-longest river and flows through 10 countries.

40. The twin cities of Hyderabad and Secunderabad are separated by:

  1. Hussain Sagar Lake
  2. Musi river
  3. A railway line
  4. A canal

Answer: Hussain Sagar Lake

Explanation: Hussain Sagar Lake (a man-made lake) separates Hyderabad and Secunderabad — the twin cities of Telangana. The lake was built in 1563 CE during Ibrahim Quli Qutub Shah's rule.

41. The newly created capital of Andhra Pradesh is:

  1. Amaravati (proposed/partial)
  2. Vijayawada
  3. Visakhapatnam
  4. Guntur

Answer: Amaravati (proposed/partial)

Explanation: Amaravati (on the Krishna river, between Vijayawada and Guntur) is the proposed new capital of Andhra Pradesh, after Hyderabad became the capital of Telangana. Its construction has been contested politically.

42. The Manasarovar Lake is sacred to which religions?

  1. Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Bon religion (all consider it holy)
  2. Only Hinduism
  3. Only Buddhism
  4. Hinduism and Buddhism only

Answer: Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Bon religion (all consider it holy)

Explanation: Lake Manasarovar (4,590 m, Tibet/China) is considered sacred in Hinduism (associated with Lord Shiva and Parvati, near Kailash), Buddhism (where Queen Maya Devi bathed before conceiving Buddha), Jainism, and Bon.

43. Which is India's highest waterfall?

  1. Kunchikal Falls, Karnataka (455 m)
  2. Jog Falls, Karnataka (253 m)
  3. Dudhsagar Falls, Goa
  4. Athirappilly Falls, Kerala

Answer: Kunchikal Falls, Karnataka (455 m)

Explanation: Kunchikal Falls (455 m) on the Varahi river in Shimoga/Shivamogga district, Karnataka, is India's highest waterfall. Jog Falls (253 m) on the Sharavathi river is the most famous. Dudhsagar Falls (310 m) is on the Goa-Karnataka border.

44. The Dal Lake is in:

  1. Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir
  2. Shimla, Himachal Pradesh
  3. Nainital, Uttarakhand
  4. Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

Answer: Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir

Explanation: Dal Lake is in Srinagar, J&K. Known for its houseboats and shikaras, it is called the 'Jewel in the Crown of Kashmir'. It has four basins and covers about 18 sq km.

45. The Jayakwadi (Nathsagar) reservoir in Maharashtra is on:

  1. Godavari river
  2. Krishna river
  3. Bhima river
  4. Tapi river

Answer: Godavari river

Explanation: Jayakwadi (Nathsagar) reservoir is on the Godavari river in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) district, Marathwada, Maharashtra. It is one of Maharashtra's largest reservoirs.

46. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of:

  1. Tunga and Bhadra rivers (Karnataka)
  2. Tungabhadra and Krishna
  3. Tunga and Krishna
  4. Two branches of Kaveri

Answer: Tunga and Bhadra rivers (Karnataka)

Explanation: The Tungabhadra is formed by the confluence of the Tunga and Bhadra rivers near Shimoga (Shivamogga), Karnataka. It flows northeast and joins the Krishna river in Andhra Pradesh.

47. The 'Bolan Pass' connects:

  1. Pakistan (Quetta) and Afghanistan — historically India's route to Central Asia
  2. India and Pakistan
  3. Afghanistan and Iran
  4. India and China

Answer: Pakistan (Quetta) and Afghanistan — historically India's route to Central Asia

Explanation: Bolan Pass in Pakistan's Balochistan connects the Indus plains (Sindh/Punjab) with Quetta and Afghanistan. Historically, it was one of the main invasion routes into the Indian subcontinent from Central Asia.

48. The Tapi (Tapti) river flows westward through:

  1. Maharashtra, MP, and Gujarat into Arabian Sea
  2. Gujarat into Arabian Sea directly
  3. Maharashtra and Gujarat
  4. Maharashtra only

Answer: Maharashtra, MP, and Gujarat into Arabian Sea

Explanation: Tapi (Tapti) river (724 km) originates from Satpura Range (MP), flows westward through Vidarbha (Maharashtra), Surat (Gujarat), and drains into the Arabian Sea. Ukai dam is on the Tapi in Gujarat.

49. The Luni river drains into:

  1. Rann of Kutch (endorheic/internal drainage)
  2. Arabian Sea
  3. Bay of Bengal
  4. Rajasthan desert (dry up before reaching sea)

Answer: Rann of Kutch (endorheic/internal drainage)

Explanation: Luni (320 km) originates from Aravalli Range near Ajmer (Rajasthan) and drains into the Rann of Kutch in Gujarat — making it an endorheic river (no outlet to ocean). Water becomes brackish/saline in lower reaches.

50. The Chenab river is a tributary of:

  1. Indus (through Pakistan)
  2. Beas
  3. Jhelum
  4. Sutlej

Answer: Indus (through Pakistan)

Explanation: Chenab is the largest of the five Punjab rivers. It originates from the Lahaul Valley (Himachal Pradesh), flows through J&K and Pakistan, and joins the Indus through the Panjnad (confluence of five rivers).
