26. Adam's Bridge (Ram Setu) connects:

  1. Pamban Island (India) and Mannar Island (Sri Lanka)
  2. India and Maldives
  3. Tamil Nadu and Kerala
  4. Sri Lanka and Indonesia

Answer: Pamban Island (India) and Mannar Island (Sri Lanka)

Explanation: Adam's Bridge (Ram Setu) is a chain of limestone shoals between Pamban Island (Rameshwaram, Tamil Nadu) and Mannar Island (Sri Lanka). It is about 50 km long and is visible in satellite imagery.

27. The Dead Sea (Israel/Jordan border) is famous for:

  1. Being the lowest point on Earth's land surface and extreme salinity
  2. Extreme cold
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Being the largest saltwater lake

Answer: Being the lowest point on Earth's land surface and extreme salinity

Explanation: The Dead Sea (430 m below sea level) is the lowest point on Earth's land surface. Its salinity is about 34% (10 times the ocean), making it impossible for most aquatic life to survive — hence 'Dead' Sea.

28. The Mariana Trench (deepest point on Earth, ~11,034 m) is in:

  1. Pacific Ocean (west of Mariana Islands)
  2. Atlantic Ocean
  3. Indian Ocean
  4. Arctic Ocean

Answer: Pacific Ocean (west of Mariana Islands)

Explanation: The Mariana Trench is in the western Pacific Ocean, east of the Mariana Islands. Challenger Deep (10,929 m in the Mariana Trench) is the deepest known point on Earth.

29. The Labrador Current (cold) causes dense fog along the coast of:

  1. Newfoundland, Canada (Grand Banks area)
  2. Norway
  3. Japan
  4. Alaska

Answer: Newfoundland, Canada (Grand Banks area)

Explanation: The Labrador Current (cold, from the Arctic) meets the Gulf Stream (warm) near the Grand Banks of Newfoundland, Canada, creating dense fog. This area is one of the world's richest fishing grounds.
