UPSC Geography practice

Map Based MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Map Based questions to revise the UPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. India shares its longest international border with which country?

  1. China
  2. Nepal
  3. Pakistan
  4. Bangladesh

Answer: Bangladesh

Explanation: India shares its longest international border with Bangladesh (4,096 km).

2. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are separated from the southeast Asian countries by:

  1. Strait of Malacca
  2. Ten Degree Channel
  3. Nine Degree Channel
  4. Duncan Passage

Answer: Strait of Malacca

Explanation: The Strait of Malacca separates the Andaman and Nicobar Islands from Malaysia and Indonesia. The Ten Degree Channel separates the Andamans from the Nicobars.

3. The Palk Strait separates:

  1. India and Sri Lanka
  2. India and Maldives
  3. Sri Lanka and Maldives
  4. Andaman and Nicobar Islands

Answer: India and Sri Lanka

Explanation: The Palk Strait is a narrow strip of water separating Tamil Nadu (India) from the Northern Province of Sri Lanka. Its width ranges from 53-80 km.

4. Duncan Passage separates:

  1. South Andaman and Little Andaman
  2. Andaman and Nicobar
  3. Great Nicobar and Little Nicobar
  4. North and South Andaman

Answer: South Andaman and Little Andaman

Explanation: Duncan Passage separates South Andaman from Little Andaman. The Ten Degree Channel separates Andaman Islands from Nicobar Islands. The Nine Degree Channel separates Lakshadweep Islands.

5. Mount Everest (8,849 m) is located in:

  1. Nepal (on Nepal-China border)
  2. India
  3. China (Tibet)
  4. Pakistan

Answer: Nepal (on Nepal-China border)

Explanation: Mount Everest sits on the Nepal-China (Tibet) border. First climbed by Edmund Hillary (New Zealand) and Tenzing Norgay Sherpa (India/Nepal) on 29 May 1953.

6. The southernmost point of the Indian mainland is:

  1. Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin)
  2. Indira Point
  3. Point Calimere
  4. Rameshwaram

Answer: Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin)

Explanation: Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin) is the southernmost tip of the Indian mainland. Indira Point on Great Nicobar Island (6.5°N) is the southernmost point of India's entire territory.

7. The state with the longest coastline in India is:

  1. Gujarat
  2. Andhra Pradesh
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. Maharashtra

Answer: Gujarat

Explanation: Gujarat has the longest coastline (1,600+ km) among Indian states, followed by Andhra Pradesh (~974 km) and Tamil Nadu (~906 km). India's total coastline is about 7,517 km (including islands).

8. India shares the longest border with which country?

  1. Bangladesh
  2. China
  3. Pakistan
  4. Nepal

Answer: Bangladesh

Explanation: India shares its longest land border with Bangladesh (4,156 km), followed by China (3,488 km), Pakistan (3,323 km), Nepal (1,751 km), Myanmar (1,643 km), Bhutan (699 km), and Afghanistan (106 km, disputed).

9. The Radcliffe Line is the border between:

  1. India and Pakistan
  2. India and Bangladesh
  3. India and Nepal
  4. India and China

Answer: India and Pakistan

Explanation: The Radcliffe Line (drawn by Sir Cyril Radcliffe in 1947) is the boundary between India and Pakistan, and between India and (East) Bangladesh. It was drawn in 5 weeks and announced on 17 August 1947.

10. The Andaman Islands are separated from Myanmar (Burma) by:

  1. Andaman Sea (and Ten Degree Channel from Nicobars)
  2. Bay of Bengal
  3. Palk Strait
  4. Indian Ocean

Answer: Andaman Sea (and Ten Degree Channel from Nicobars)

Explanation: The Andaman Islands lie in the Andaman Sea (part of the Bay of Bengal), east of the Indian subcontinent and west of Myanmar. They are closer to Myanmar and Thailand than to mainland India.

11. The Strait of Gibraltar separates:

  1. Spain (Europe) and Morocco (Africa)
  2. Italy and Tunisia
  3. Greece and Turkey
  4. UK and France

Answer: Spain (Europe) and Morocco (Africa)

Explanation: The Strait of Gibraltar (14 km wide at its narrowest) separates Spain (Europe) from Morocco (Africa). It connects the Atlantic Ocean to the Mediterranean Sea. Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory.

12. The English Channel separates:

  1. England (UK) and France
  2. England and Ireland
  3. Scotland and Norway
  4. UK and Belgium

Answer: England (UK) and France

Explanation: The English Channel (La Manche in French) separates southern England from northern France. The Channel Tunnel (Chunnel, 1994) connects the two countries under the sea. The strait is 33 km wide at its narrowest (Dover-Calais).

13. The Bering Strait separates:

  1. Russia (Asia) and USA (Alaska)
  2. Alaska and Canada
  3. USA and Canada
  4. Russia and Japan

Answer: Russia (Asia) and USA (Alaska)

Explanation: The Bering Strait (85 km wide) separates Russia (Siberia, Asia) from the USA (Alaska, North America), connecting the Pacific Ocean (Bering Sea) with the Arctic Ocean. A proposed land bridge existed here during ice ages.

14. India's highest road (motorable) is:

  1. Umling La Pass, Ladakh (19,024 ft / 5,799 m)
  2. Khardung La, Ladakh
  3. Rohtang Pass
  4. Chang La, Ladakh

Answer: Umling La Pass, Ladakh (19,024 ft / 5,799 m)

Explanation: Umling La Pass in Ladakh (5,799 m / 19,024 ft) is the world's highest motorable road, surpassing Khardung La. It was made motorable by the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) in 2021.

15. The country with the longest coastline in the world is:

  1. Canada
  2. Russia
  3. Australia
  4. Indonesia

Answer: Canada

Explanation: Canada has the world's longest coastline at 202,080 km (including islands). It is followed by Norway and Russia. Canada's coastline is about 27 times longer than India's.

16. Itanagar is the capital of:

  1. Arunachal Pradesh
  2. Meghalaya
  3. Nagaland
  4. Manipur

Answer: Arunachal Pradesh

Explanation: Itanagar is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh. Arunachal Pradesh is India's largest northeastern state by area and borders China, Bhutan, and Myanmar. Its capital Itanagar is named after Ita Fort.

17. 'Contour lines' on a topographic map connect places of:

  1. Equal elevation (height above sea level)
  2. Equal temperature
  3. Equal rainfall
  4. Equal pressure

Answer: Equal elevation (height above sea level)

Explanation: Contour lines connect points of equal elevation on a topographic map. Closely-spaced contours indicate steep slopes; widely-spaced contours indicate gentle slopes. Hills appear as closed concentric circles.

18. 'Great Circle' routes are used by aircraft because:

  1. They are the shortest paths between two points on a sphere
  2. They avoid turbulence
  3. They follow the equator
  4. They avoid mountains

Answer: They are the shortest paths between two points on a sphere

Explanation: Great Circle routes are the shortest paths between two points on a sphere (Earth). Aircraft flying long-distance routes follow great circle paths to save fuel and time, even though the routes may appear curved on flat maps.

19. The Equator passes through which continents?

  1. South America, Africa, and Asia (through the islands of Indonesia)
  2. Only Africa and South America
  3. Only Africa
  4. All continents

Answer: South America, Africa, and Asia (through the islands of Indonesia)

Explanation: The Equator (0° latitude) passes through 3 continents: South America (Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil), Africa (Gabon, Congo, DR Congo, Uganda, Kenya, Somalia), and Asia (Indonesian islands — Sumatra, Borneo, Sulawesi).

20. India's latitude extends approximately from:

  1. 8°4'N to 37°6'N
  2. 6°N to 40°N
  3. 10°N to 35°N
  4. 8°N to 36°N

Answer: 8°4'N to 37°6'N

Explanation: India's mainland extends from 8°4'N (Kanyakumari) to 37°6'N (Kashmir) in latitude, and 68°7'E to 97°25'E in longitude. With islands, it extends to about 6°45'N (Great Nicobar).

21. The latitude 23.5°N is called the Tropic of Cancer. India's southernmost mainland point Kanyakumari is at approximately:

  1. 8°4'N
  2. 10°N
  3. 6°N
  4. 12°N

Answer: 8°4'N

Explanation: Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin), India's southernmost mainland point, is at 8°4'N. The southernmost point of India's territory (including islands) is Indira Point on Great Nicobar Island at about 6°45'N.

22. India's coastline length (including islands) is approximately:

  1. 7,517 km
  2. 5,000 km
  3. 10,000 km
  4. 6,000 km

Answer: 7,517 km

Explanation: India's total coastline is 7,517 km — 5,423 km of mainland coast and about 2,094 km of island coastline (Andaman & Nicobar + Lakshadweep). Gujarat has the longest state coastline.

23. India's southernmost point is:

  1. Cape Comorin (Kanyakumari)
  2. Indira Point (Great Nicobar Island)
  3. Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram)
  4. Rameshwaram

Answer: Indira Point (Great Nicobar Island)

Explanation: Indira Point on Great Nicobar Island (6°45'N) is the southernmost point of India. Cape Comorin (Kanyakumari, 8°04'N) is the southernmost tip of the Indian mainland.

24. The Ten Degree Channel separates:

  1. Andaman Islands from Nicobar Islands
  2. South Andaman from Little Andaman
  3. Lakshadweep from Maldives
  4. India from Sri Lanka

Answer: Andaman Islands from Nicobar Islands

Explanation: The Ten Degree Channel (at 10°N latitude) separates the Andaman Islands from the Nicobar Islands. The Duncan Passage separates South Andaman from Little Andaman.

25. The Gulf of Mannar is between:

  1. Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka
  2. India and Maldives
  3. Bangladesh and Myanmar
  4. Lakshadweep and Maldives

Answer: Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka

Explanation: The Gulf of Mannar lies between southeastern India (Tamil Nadu coast) and the western coast of Sri Lanka. It is known for rich marine biodiversity and pearl fishing.
