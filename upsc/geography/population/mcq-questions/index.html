Population MCQs with answers

26. Which state in India has the highest population density?

  1. Bihar
  2. Uttar Pradesh
  3. West Bengal
  4. Kerala

Answer: Bihar

Explanation: Bihar has the highest population density among Indian states, due to its fertile plains and limited land area.

27. What is the primary cause of seasonal migration in India?

  1. Employment
  2. Education
  3. Agriculture
  4. Climate

Answer: Agriculture

Explanation: Agriculture is the primary cause of seasonal migration in India, as people move in search of work during harvest seasons.

28. Which strait separates the Indian subcontinent from Southeast Asia?

  1. Malacca
  2. Lombok
  3. Makassar
  4. Sunda

Answer: Malacca

Explanation: The Malacca strait separates the Indian subcontinent from Southeast Asia, connecting the Indian Ocean to the South China Sea.

29. What is the term for the study of the relationship between population and environment?

  1. Demography
  2. Ecology
  3. Geography
  4. Sociology

Answer: Ecology

Explanation: Ecology is the study of the relationship between organisms and their environment, including population dynamics.

30. Which monsoon wind brings rainfall to the Indian subcontinent?

  1. Southwest
  2. Northeast
  3. Southeast
  4. Northwest

Answer: Southwest

Explanation: The southwest monsoon wind brings rainfall to the Indian subcontinent during the summer months.

31. What is the term for the process of people moving from one place to another?

  1. Migration
  2. Immigration
  3. Emigration
  4. Transmigration

Answer: Migration

Explanation: Migration refers to the process of people moving from one place to another, often driven by economic or social factors.

32. Which of the following factors affects population growth rates?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Death rate
  3. Migration rate
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All of the above factors, including birth rate, death rate, and migration rate, affect population growth rates.

33. What is the process by which a population becomes more concentrated in a specific area?

  1. Agglomeration
  2. Dispersion
  3. Migration
  4. Diffusion

Answer: Agglomeration

Explanation: Agglomeration refers to the process by which a population becomes more concentrated in a specific area.

34. Which of the following factors is NOT a push factor for migration?

  1. Poverty
  2. Lack of job opportunities
  3. Natural disasters
  4. Better job opportunities

Answer: Better job opportunities

Explanation: Better job opportunities is a pull factor, not a push factor, as it attracts people to move to a new area.

35. Which of the following is a consequence of rapid population growth?

  1. Increased food production
  2. Improved healthcare
  3. Environmental degradation
  4. Reduced poverty

Answer: Environmental degradation

Explanation: Rapid population growth can lead to environmental degradation due to increased resource consumption and waste generation.

36. What is the term for the number of people living in a given area?

  1. Population density
  2. Population size
  3. Population growth rate
  4. Population distribution

Answer: Population size

Explanation: Population size refers to the number of people living in a given area.

37. Which of the following is a characteristic of a developed country?

  1. High population growth rate
  2. Low life expectancy
  3. High population density
  4. Low dependency ratio

Answer: Low dependency ratio

Explanation: A developed country typically has a low dependency ratio, indicating a high proportion of working-age population.

38. What is the process by which a population becomes more urban?

  1. Urbanization
  2. Ruralization
  3. Migration
  4. Displacement

Answer: Urbanization

Explanation: Urbanization is the process of population shift to urban areas.

39. Which of the following factors affects population growth rate?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Death rate
  3. Migration rate
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All the above factors affect population growth rate.

40. What is the concept of the minimum number of people required for a settlement to be considered urban?

  1. Town
  2. City
  3. Urban threshold
  4. Rural

Answer: Urban threshold

Explanation: Urban threshold is the minimum population for an urban settlement.

41. Which of the following is a characteristic of a prorupted population pyramid?

  1. High birth rate
  2. Low death rate
  3. Aging population
  4. Broad base

Answer: High birth rate

Explanation: A prorupted population pyramid has a high birth rate.

42. What is the mechanism that drives the monsoon winds in India?

  1. Temperature gradient
  2. Pressure gradient
  3. Wind gradient
  4. Humidity gradient

Answer: Pressure gradient

Explanation: The pressure gradient between the Indian Ocean and the Indian landmass drives the monsoon winds. This gradient is caused by the difference in temperature and pressure.

43. What is the term for the study of the relationship between humans and their environment?

  1. Ecology
  2. Geography
  3. Demography
  4. Anthropology

Answer: Geography

Explanation: Geography is the study of the relationship between humans and their environment. It encompasses the physical and human aspects of the environment.

44. Which mountain range runs along the border between India and Pakistan?

  1. Karakoram
  2. Himalaya
  3. Aravalli
  4. Vindhya

Answer: Karakoram

Explanation: The Karakoram range is located in the northwestern part of the Indian subcontinent. It forms part of the border between India and Pakistan.

45. What is the process by which water moves through a river system?

  1. Runoff
  2. Infiltration
  3. Evaporation
  4. Transpiration

Answer: Runoff

Explanation: Runoff is the process by which water moves through a river system. It involves the flow of water over the land and through streams and rivers.

46. Which of the following climate patterns is characterized by high temperatures and high humidity?

  1. Tropical
  2. Temperate
  3. Desert
  4. Polar

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: Tropical climate is characterized by high temperatures and high humidity. This type of climate is found near the equator and is typical of regions like India.

47. What is the main cause of urbanization in developing countries?

  1. Industrialization
  2. Agricultural development
  3. Rural-urban migration
  4. Government policies

Answer: Rural-urban migration

Explanation: Rural-urban migration is the primary cause of urbanization in developing countries.

48. What is the primary reason for the high population growth rate in India?

  1. High birth rate
  2. Low death rate
  3. Improved healthcare
  4. All of the above

Answer: High birth rate

Explanation: High birth rate is the primary reason for the high population growth rate in India.

49. Which monsoon mechanism affects population distribution in the Indian subcontinent?

  1. Trade Winds
  2. Westerlies
  3. Jet Stream
  4. Intertropical Convergence Zone

Answer: Intertropical Convergence Zone

Explanation: Intertropical Convergence Zone influences the Indian monsoon.

50. What type of climate is characterized by high population density due to fertile soil?

  1. Tropical
  2. Temperate
  3. Desert
  4. Continental

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: Tropical climates often have high population densities.
