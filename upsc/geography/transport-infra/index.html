Transport Infra MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Transport Infra questions to revise the UPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. The world's longest railway platform is in:

  1. Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh
  2. Kharagpur, West Bengal
  3. Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh
  4. Hubli, Karnataka

Answer: Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh

Explanation: Gorakhpur railway station in Uttar Pradesh has the world's longest railway platform at 1,366.33 m. Kharagpur (1,072 m) was previously the record holder. Hubli also has a very long platform.

2. India's busiest airport (by passenger traffic) is:

  1. Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi
  2. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, Mumbai
  3. Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru
  4. Chennai International Airport

Answer: Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi

Explanation: Indira Gandhi International Airport (Delhi) is India's busiest airport, handling over 70 million passengers annually. Mumbai's airport is the second busiest. Bengaluru is third.

3. The Golden Quadrilateral highway project connects:

  1. Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata
  2. Four metro cities via expressways
  3. Delhi, Jaipur, Mumbai, Bengaluru
  4. Srinagar, Kanyakumari, Porbandar, Silchar

Answer: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata

Explanation: The Golden Quadrilateral (GQ) connects Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata — India's four major cities — via 5,846 km of 4/6-lane National Highways. It is the largest highway project in India.

4. The North-South and East-West Corridor highway project:

  1. Connects Srinagar to Kanyakumari (N-S) and Porbandar to Silchar (E-W)
  2. Connects Delhi to Kolkata
  3. Links Northeast India to South India
  4. Part of Golden Quadrilateral

Answer: Connects Srinagar to Kanyakumari (N-S) and Porbandar to Silchar (E-W)

Explanation: North-South Corridor: Srinagar (J&K) to Kanyakumari (Tamil Nadu) — 4,076 km. East-West Corridor: Porbandar (Gujarat) to Silchar (Assam) — 3,640 km. Together forming the 7,142 km backbone.

5. Which Indian city has the largest natural harbour?

  1. Mumbai
  2. Vizag (Visakhapatnam)
  3. Kochi
  4. Chennai

Answer: Mumbai

Explanation: Mumbai (Bombay) has one of the best natural harbours in the world. This is a key reason why it became India's commercial capital — it allowed large ships to dock safely. Vizag is India's deepest port.

6. Which is India's longest road tunnel?

  1. Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Tunnel (Banihal-Qazigund, J&K)
  2. Atal Tunnel (Rohtang)
  3. Jawahar Tunnel (Banihal)
  4. Zoji La Tunnel

Answer: Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Tunnel (Banihal-Qazigund, J&K)

Explanation: Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Tunnel (Banihal-Qazigund Tunnel, 8.45 km rail tunnel) is part of the J&K railway. The Atal (Rohtang) Road Tunnel (9.02 km) is currently India's longest road tunnel.

7. National Highway 48 (old NH-8) connects:

  1. Delhi to Mumbai via Jaipur-Ahmedabad
  2. Delhi to Kolkata
  3. Mumbai to Chennai
  4. Delhi to Amritsar

Answer: Delhi to Mumbai via Jaipur-Ahmedabad

Explanation: NH-48 (formerly NH-8) is one of India's busiest highways, connecting Delhi to Mumbai (via Gurugram, Jaipur, Ajmer, Udaipur, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat). Part of the Golden Quadrilateral.

8. India's pharmaceutical hub (largest generic drug exporter) is:

  1. Hyderabad (Telangana) — called 'Genome Valley' and 'Pharma City'
  2. Mumbai
  3. Ahmedabad
  4. Bengaluru

Answer: Hyderabad (Telangana) — called 'Genome Valley' and 'Pharma City'

Explanation: Hyderabad is India's top pharmaceutical hub ('Pharma City'), home to Dr. Reddy's, Cipla, Hetero, Aurobindo, Natco. India is the world's largest exporter of generic medicines.

9. The Khyber Pass connects:

  1. Pakistan (Peshawar) and Afghanistan (Kabul) — historic invasion route into India
  2. India and Pakistan
  3. India and China
  4. Afghanistan and Iran

Answer: Pakistan (Peshawar) and Afghanistan (Kabul) — historic invasion route into India

Explanation: Khyber Pass (3.5 km wide at narrowest) connects Peshawar (Pakistan) with Kabul (Afghanistan). Historically the most important invasion route into the Indian subcontinent — used by Persians, Greeks, Mughals.

10. Which state in India has no railway network?

  1. Sikkim (no railway; Rangpo station being built to connect it)
  2. Meghalaya
  3. Nagaland
  4. Manipur

Answer: Sikkim (no railway; Rangpo station being built to connect it)

Explanation: Sikkim was the only state with no railway network until recently. A rail line to Rangpo from West Bengal is being constructed. Sikkim is connected to the rest of India by NH-10 (Siliguri-Gangtok highway).

11. India's largest port (by cargo handled) is:

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT), Mumbai
  2. Mundra Port, Gujarat
  3. Chennai Port
  4. Vizag Port

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT), Mumbai

Explanation: Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT/Nhava Sheva) near Mumbai was India's largest container port for many years. However, Mundra Port (Adani Ports, Gujarat) has recently surpassed JNPT in total cargo.

12. The Gulf of Kutch is famous for:

  1. Tidal energy potential, Kandla Port, and flamingos
  2. Coral reefs
  3. Pearl fishing
  4. Mangroves only

Answer: Tidal energy potential, Kandla Port, and flamingos

Explanation: Gulf of Kutch (Gujarat) has one of the highest tidal ranges in India (7-8 m), making it ideal for tidal energy. Kandla Port (Deendayal Port) is India's second-largest port by cargo. The gulf also has flamingos and corals.

13. What is the primary mode of transport in the Himalayan region?

  1. Road
  2. Rail
  3. Air
  4. Water

Answer: Road

Explanation: Roads are the primary mode of transport in the Himalayan region due to its rugged terrain.

14. Which of the following rivers is navigable and supports transport infrastructure?

  1. Brahmaputra
  2. Ganga
  3. Godavari
  4. Narmada

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga river is navigable and supports transport infrastructure.

15. What is the name of the strait separating India and Sri Lanka?

  1. Palk Strait
  2. Malacca Strait
  3. Bosphorus Strait
  4. Sunda Strait

Answer: Palk Strait

Explanation: The Palk Strait separates India and Sri Lanka.

16. Which of the following types of climate is characterized by high temperatures and high humidity?

  1. Tropical
  2. Temperate
  3. Desert
  4. Alpine

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: Tropical climate is characterized by high temperatures and high humidity.

17. What is the term for the movement of water from the ground to the atmosphere through plants?

  1. Evaporation
  2. Transpiration
  3. Condensation
  4. Infiltration

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the movement of water from the ground to the atmosphere through plants.

18. Which of the following mountain ranges runs along the border between India and Pakistan?

  1. Karakoram
  2. Himalaya
  3. Aravalli
  4. Vindhya

Answer: Karakoram

Explanation: The Karakoram mountain range runs along the border between India and Pakistan.

19. What is the name of the canal connecting the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea?

  1. Suez Canal
  2. Panama Canal
  3. Grand Canal
  4. Erie Canal

Answer: Suez Canal

Explanation: The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea.

20. Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon?

  1. Colorado
  2. Mississippi
  3. Rio Grande
  4. Yellowstone

Answer: Colorado

Explanation: The Colorado river flows through the Grand Canyon.

21. What is the term for the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the atmosphere?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evapotranspiration

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant.

22. Which of the following types of transport infrastructure is used for carrying goods and people over long distances?

  1. Highway
  2. Railway
  3. Waterway
  4. Airway

Answer: Railway

Explanation: Railway is used for carrying goods and people over long distances.

23. What is the primary purpose of the Golden Quadrilateral project in India?

  1. Reduce traffic congestion
  2. Improve rural connectivity
  3. Enhance industrial growth
  4. Increase national highway network

Answer: Increase national highway network

Explanation: The Golden Quadrilateral project aims to improve the national highway network by connecting major cities.

24. Which of the following rivers is not navigable?

  1. Ganga
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Narmada
  4. Yamuna

Answer: Narmada

Explanation: The Narmada river has a steep gradient and is not navigable.

25. What is the name of the strait separating the islands of Java and Sumatra?

  1. Sunda Strait
  2. Malacca Strait
  3. Lombok Strait
  4. Makassar Strait

Answer: Sunda Strait

Explanation: The Sunda Strait separates Java and Sumatra.
