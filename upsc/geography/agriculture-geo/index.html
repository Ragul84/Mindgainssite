Agriculture Geo MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Agriculture Geo questions to revise the UPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 460 questions.

1. The Green Revolution was primarily associated with which crop?

  1. Wheat
  2. Rice
  3. Cotton
  4. Pulses

Answer: Wheat

Explanation: The Green Revolution (mid-1960s) was primarily about wheat — using HYV seeds in Punjab, Haryana, and western UP. M.S. Swaminathan and Norman Borlaug were key figures. India became self-sufficient in wheat.

2. Lakshadweep is the smallest Union Territory of India. Its capital is:

  1. Kavaratti
  2. Agatti
  3. Minicoy
  4. Androth

Answer: Kavaratti

Explanation: Kavaratti is the capital of Lakshadweep, India's smallest Union Territory by area (32 sq km). Agatti island has the only airport. The islands are coral atolls in the Arabian Sea.

3. Pink Revolution is associated with:

  1. Meat and poultry production (also prawn/onion in some contexts)
  2. Rose farming
  3. Tomato production
  4. Strawberry farming

Answer: Meat and poultry production (also prawn/onion in some contexts)

Explanation: Pink Revolution primarily refers to the increased production of meat (mutton, poultry) in India. Some sources also use it for prawn/shrimp farming or onion production. The colour analogy follows other agricultural revolutions.

4. India is the world's largest producer of coffee in Asia. Major coffee-growing state is:

  1. Karnataka (70%+ of India's coffee)
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Kerala
  4. Andhra Pradesh

Answer: Karnataka (70%+ of India's coffee)

Explanation: Karnataka produces about 70% of India's coffee (primarily in Coorg/Kodagu, Chikmagalur, Hassan). Kerala (20%) and Tamil Nadu (5-6%) are other major producers. India grows both Arabica and Robusta.

5. Which Indian State/UT has no international borders?

  1. Maharashtra
  2. Rajasthan
  3. Arunachal Pradesh
  4. Mizoram

Answer: Maharashtra

Explanation: Maharashtra has no international borders — it is surrounded by Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Goa, plus the Arabian Sea coast. Border states touch Pakistan, China, Bangladesh, Myanmar, or Nepal/Bhutan.
