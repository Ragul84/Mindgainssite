26. What is the name of the monsoon wind that brings rain to India?

  1. Southwest Monsoon
  2. Northeast Monsoon
  3. Trade Winds
  4. Westerlies

Answer: Southwest Monsoon

Explanation: The Southwest Monsoon brings rain to India.

27. Which of the following states has the largest area under irrigation?

  1. Punjab
  2. Uttar Pradesh
  3. Bihar
  4. West Bengal

Answer: Punjab

Explanation: Punjab has the largest area under irrigation.

28. What is the term for the study of the relationship between organisms and their environment?

  1. Ecology
  2. Geography
  3. Biology
  4. Environmental Science

Answer: Ecology

Explanation: Ecology is the study of the relationship between organisms and their environment.

29. What is the primary factor influencing the distribution of rainfall in India?

  1. Temperature
  2. Pressure
  3. Relief
  4. Wind

Answer: Relief

Explanation: Relief is the primary factor influencing the distribution of rainfall in India, as mountain ranges force warm air to rise and cool, resulting in precipitation.

30. Which of the following rivers flows through a rift valley?

  1. Narmada
  2. Tapi
  3. Godavari
  4. Krishna

Answer: Narmada

Explanation: The Narmada river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges.

34. Which of the following is a consequence of deforestation?
