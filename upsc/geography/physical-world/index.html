Physical World MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Physical World questions to revise the UPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. The longest river in India is:

  1. Ganga
  2. Godavari
  3. Indus
  4. Brahmaputra

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga (Ganges) is the longest river in India at about 2,525 km. It originates from Gangotri glacier in Uttarakhand and drains into the Bay of Bengal through the Sundarbans delta.

2. The river Brahmaputra is known as 'Tsangpo' in:

  1. Tibet (China)
  2. Bhutan
  3. Bangladesh
  4. Arunachal Pradesh

Answer: Tibet (China)

Explanation: The Brahmaputra originates from Chemayungdung glacier in Tibet (China) as the Tsangpo. It enters India in Arunachal Pradesh (called Siang/Dihang), flows through Assam as Brahmaputra, and enters Bangladesh as Jamuna.

3. The river Yamuna originates from:

  1. Yamunotri glacier, Uttarakhand
  2. Gangotri glacier
  3. Kedarnath
  4. Badrinath

Answer: Yamunotri glacier, Uttarakhand

Explanation: The Yamuna originates from the Yamunotri glacier in Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand. It flows southward through Uttarakhand, Haryana, Delhi, and UP before joining the Ganga at Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam).

4. The Barren Island (Andaman) is India's only:

  1. Active volcano
  2. Dormant volcano
  3. Extinct volcano
  4. Underwater volcano

Answer: Active volcano

Explanation: Barren Island in the Andaman Sea is India's only active volcano and the only confirmed active volcano in South Asia. It last erupted in 2017 and has been periodically active since its major eruption in 1991.

5. The phenomenon of 'Aurora Borealis' (Northern Lights) is seen near:

  1. Arctic/polar regions due to solar wind interactions with Earth's magnetic field
  2. Tropical regions
  3. Near the equator
  4. Near the Tropic of Cancer

Answer: Arctic/polar regions due to solar wind interactions with Earth's magnetic field

Explanation: Aurora Borealis (Northern Lights) and Aurora Australis (Southern Lights) occur near the poles when charged particles from the sun (solar wind) interact with Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere, causing colored light displays.

6. 'Subduction zone' is where:

  1. A denser tectonic plate slides under a lighter plate (causes earthquakes and volcanoes)
  2. Two plates move apart
  3. Two continental plates collide
  4. A plate cracks open

Answer: A denser tectonic plate slides under a lighter plate (causes earthquakes and volcanoes)

Explanation: A subduction zone is where an oceanic plate (denser) slides under a continental or another oceanic plate. This causes deep-focus earthquakes, trenches (like Mariana Trench), and volcanic arcs (Ring of Fire).

7. The Three Sisters (Sibling Islands) of the Andaman group are known for:

  1. Being uninhabited volcanic rocks
  2. Being the largest islands
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Pearl farming

Answer: Being uninhabited volcanic rocks

Explanation: The Three Sisters (or siblings) are small uninhabited rocky outcrops in the Andaman archipelago. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands group has 572 islands, islets, rocks, etc., of which about 38 are inhabited.

8. The Himalayan range is classified as:

  1. Fold mountains
  2. Block mountains
  3. Volcanic mountains
  4. Residual mountains

Answer: Fold mountains

Explanation: The Himalayas are fold mountains, formed when the Indian tectonic plate collided with the Eurasian plate (~50 million years ago), folding sedimentary rocks upward. They are still rising about 5 mm per year.

9. The Siachen glacier is in:

  1. Karakoram Range
  2. Great Himalayan Range
  3. Zanskar Range
  4. Pir Panjal Range

Answer: Karakoram Range

Explanation: Siachen glacier (world's highest battlefield) is in the Karakoram Range. India controls most of it since Operation Meghdoot (1984). It is the world's second-longest non-polar glacier.

10. Which ocean current is responsible for the warm climate of Western Europe?

  1. North Atlantic Drift (Gulf Stream extension)
  2. Labrador Current
  3. Canary Current
  4. Humboldt Current

Answer: North Atlantic Drift (Gulf Stream extension)

Explanation: The North Atlantic Drift (an extension of the Gulf Stream) carries warm tropical water to Western Europe, giving it a mild climate despite its high latitude. Without it, Western Europe would be much colder.

11. The Ring of Fire is a zone of:

  1. Volcanoes and earthquakes surrounding the Pacific Ocean
  2. Volcanic activity in the Indian Ocean
  3. Typhoon activity
  4. Seismic activity in the Atlantic

Answer: Volcanoes and earthquakes surrounding the Pacific Ocean

Explanation: The Ring of Fire is a horseshoe-shaped zone of volcanic activity and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean rim. It has 75% of the world's active volcanoes and experiences 90% of the world's earthquakes.

12. The Humboldt (Peru) Current is a cold current flowing along:

  1. Western coast of South America (Chile and Peru)
  2. Eastern USA coast
  3. Western Africa
  4. Eastern Australia

Answer: Western coast of South America (Chile and Peru)

Explanation: The Humboldt (Peru) Current is a cold ocean current flowing northward along the western coast of South America (Chile and Peru). It causes aridity (Atacama Desert) and supports rich marine life (anchovy fisheries).

13. India's largest solar power plant (as of recent years) is:

  1. Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan
  2. Gujarat solar parks
  3. Kamuthi Solar Power Project, Tamil Nadu
  4. Rewa Solar Park, Madhya Pradesh

Answer: Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan

Explanation: Bhadla Solar Park in Jodhpur, Rajasthan (~2,245 MW) is one of the world's largest solar parks. Earlier, Kamuthi Solar Power Project in Tamil Nadu (648 MW) held this record when commissioned in 2016.

14. What is the primary factor that influences the climate of India?

  1. Latitude
  2. Altitude
  3. Ocean Currents
  4. Monsoon Winds

Answer: Monsoon Winds

Explanation: Monsoon winds play a crucial role in shaping India's climate, bringing significant rainfall and temperature variations.

15. Which mountain range runs along the border between India and Pakistan?

  1. Karakoram
  2. Himalayan
  3. Aravalli
  4. Sulaiman

Answer: Karakoram

Explanation: The Karakoram range is located in the northwestern part of the Indian subcontinent.

16. What is the name of the strait that separates India from Sri Lanka?

  1. Palk Strait
  2. Malacca Strait
  3. Lombok Strait
  4. Bosphorus Strait

Answer: Palk Strait

Explanation: The Palk Strait is a narrow waterway that connects the Bay of Bengal to the Gulf of Mannar.

17. Which river is the longest in India?

  1. Ganga
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Godavari
  4. Krishna

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga river stretches over 2,500 kilometers from its source to the Bay of Bengal.

18. What is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the atmosphere?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process of water movement through plants, playing a key role in the water cycle.

19. Which of the following types of rocks is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Foliated

Answer: Igneous

Explanation: Igneous rocks are created through the cooling and solidification of magma or lava.

20. What is the term for the movement of the Earth's crust that results in the creation of mountain ranges?

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Plate Tectonics
  4. Glaciation

Answer: Plate Tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics is the process that shapes the Earth's surface, resulting in the formation of mountains and volcanoes.

21. Which of the following is a characteristic of a tropical cyclone?

  1. Low Pressure
  2. High Temperature
  3. Low Humidity
  4. Strong Winds

Answer: Low Pressure

Explanation: Tropical cyclones are formed in low-pressure systems over warm ocean waters.

22. What is the name of the desert that covers much of western India?

  1. Thar Desert
  2. Gobi Desert
  3. Mojave Desert
  4. Atacama Desert

Answer: Thar Desert

Explanation: The Thar Desert is a large arid region in northwestern India and eastern Pakistan.

23. Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon?

  1. Colorado River
  2. Mississippi River
  3. Rio Grande
  4. Yellow River

Answer: Colorado River

Explanation: The Colorado River is the primary river that flows through the Grand Canyon in Arizona, USA.

24. What is the primary factor responsible for the formation of the Indian monsoon?

  1. Temperature
  2. Pressure
  3. Wind
  4. Latitude

Answer: Pressure

Explanation: The primary factor is the pressure difference between land and sea. Pressure

25. What is the name of the strait separating the islands of Java and Sumatra?

  1. Sunda
  2. Lombok
  3. Makassar
  4. Malacca

Answer: Sunda

Explanation: The Sunda Strait is the waterway between these islands.
