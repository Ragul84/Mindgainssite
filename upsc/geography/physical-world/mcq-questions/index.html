26. Which river is the longest in South India?

  1. Godavari
  2. Krishna
  3. Kaveri
  4. Tungabhadra

Answer: Godavari

Explanation: The Godavari River is the longest in South India.

27. What is the term for the movement of the Earth's crust resulting in the formation of mountains?

  1. Orogeny
  2. Weathering
  3. Erosion
  4. Deposition

Answer: Orogeny

Explanation: Orogeny is the process of mountain formation.

28. Which of the following climate types is characterized by high temperatures and high humidity?

  1. Tropical
  2. Temperate
  3. Desert
  4. Polar

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: Tropical climates have high temperatures and humidity.

29. What is the name of the plateau in India where the Narmada River flows?

  1. Deccan
  2. Chota Nagpur
  3. Malwa
  4. Satpura

Answer: Deccan

Explanation: The Deccan Plateau is where the Narmada River flows.

30. What is the term for the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the atmosphere?

  1. Transpiration
  2. Evaporation
  3. Condensation
  4. Infiltration

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process of water movement through plants.

31. Which of the following mountain ranges is located in northeastern India?

  1. Himalaya
  2. Aravalli
  3. Vindhya
  4. Purvachal

Answer: Himalaya

Explanation: The Himalayan range is located in northeastern India.

32. What is the primary factor influencing the climate of India?

  1. Latitude
  2. Altitude
  3. Ocean Currents
  4. Monsoon Winds

Answer: Monsoon Winds

Explanation: Monsoon winds play a crucial role in shaping India's climate, bringing significant rainfall and temperature variations.

33. Which of the following is NOT a type of rock?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Atmospheric

Answer: Atmospheric

Explanation: Atmospheric refers to the atmosphere, not a type of rock.

34. What is the term for the movement of the Earth's crust?

  1. Plate Tectonics
  2. Continental Drift
  3. Weathering
  4. Erosion

Answer: Plate Tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics describes the movement of the Earth's crust.

35. Which of the following deserts is located in Australia?

  1. Sahara
  2. Gobi
  3. Mojave
  4. Great Victoria

Answer: Great Victoria

Explanation: The Great Victoria Desert is located in Australia.

36. What is the largest island in the Mediterranean Sea?

  1. Sicily
  2. Sardinia
  3. Corsica
  4. Crete

Answer: Sicily

Explanation: Sicily is the largest island in the Mediterranean.

37. Which of the following rivers is the longest in South America?

  1. Amazon
  2. Parana
  3. Sao Francisco
  4. Magdalena

Answer: Amazon

Explanation: The Amazon River is the longest in South America.

38. What is the term for a body of water that is partially enclosed by land?

  1. Gulf
  2. Bay
  3. Fjord
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All the options are types of bodies of water partially enclosed by land.

39. What is the term for the study of the shape and features of the Earth's surface?

  1. Geomorphology
  2. Geology
  3. Geography
  4. Cartography

Answer: Geomorphology

Explanation: Geomorphology is the study of the Earth's surface shape and features.

40. Which of the following is a type of natural hazard?

  1. Drought
  2. Flood
  3. Landslide
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All of the above options are types of natural hazards.

41. Which river is the longest in South Asia?

  1. Ganges
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Indus
  4. Godavari

Answer: Brahmaputra

Explanation: The Brahmaputra river flows through India, China, and Bangladesh. It is approximately 2,900 kilometers long.

42. What is the name of the largest desert in India?

  1. Thar Desert
  2. Cholistan Desert
  3. Kutch Desert
  4. Rajasthan Desert

Answer: Thar Desert

Explanation: The Thar Desert is located in northwestern India. It covers an area of approximately 260,000 square kilometers.

43. What is the term for the process by which water moves through a plant?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration refers to the process of water movement through a plant. It occurs mainly through the leaves.

44. Which of the following is a type of volcanic landform?

  1. Plateau
  2. Delta
  3. Canyon
  4. Caldera

Answer: Caldera

Explanation: A caldera is a large depression formed by a volcanic eruption. It is a type of volcanic landform.

45. What is the name of the strait that separates the islands of Java and Sumatra in Indonesia?

  1. Sunda
  2. Lombok
  3. Makassar
  4. Malacca

Answer: Sunda

Explanation: The Sunda Strait is a strait that separates the islands of Java and Sumatra in Indonesia, connecting the Java Sea to the Indian Ocean.

46. Which river is the longest in South Asia and flows through India, Bangladesh, and Nepal?

  1. Ganga
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Indus
  4. Godavari

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga River is the longest in South Asia and flows through India, Bangladesh, and Nepal, stretching over 2,500 kilometers.

47. What is the term for the process by which the Earth's rotation causes moving objects to deflect to the right in the Northern Hemisphere?

  1. Coriolis
  2. Trade
  3. Westerly
  4. Jet

Answer: Coriolis

Explanation: The Coriolis effect is the term for the process by which the Earth's rotation causes moving objects to deflect to the right in the Northern Hemisphere and to the left in the Southern Hemisphere.

48. Which of the following climate types is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of precipitation throughout the year?

  1. Tropical
  2. Desert
  3. Temperate
  4. Polar

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: The tropical climate type is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of precipitation throughout the year, typically found near the equator.

49. What is the name of the largest island in the Mediterranean Sea?

  1. Sicily
  2. Sardinia
  3. Corsica
  4. Crete

Answer: Sicily

Explanation: Sicily is the largest island in the Mediterranean Sea, located off the coast of Italy.

50. Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon in Arizona, USA?

  1. Colorado
  2. Missouri
  3. Mississippi
  4. Rio

Answer: Colorado

Explanation: The Colorado River flows through the Grand Canyon in Arizona, USA, carving out one of the most iconic natural wonders in the world.
