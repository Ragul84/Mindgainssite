26. 'Jhum cultivation' (shifting cultivation) is mainly practiced in:

  1. Northeast India (Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Mizoram, Manipur, Arunachal Pradesh)
  2. Rajasthan
  3. Punjab
  4. Tamil Nadu

Answer: Northeast India (Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Mizoram, Manipur, Arunachal Pradesh)

Explanation: Jhum (shifting/slash-and-burn) cultivation is practiced in Northeast India — also called 'Bewar/Dahia/Penda' in central India. Forest is cleared, cultivated for 2-3 years, then abandoned to let it regenerate.

27. The 'Dokar La' Pass and 'Jelep La' Pass are on the border of:

  1. Sikkim (India) and Tibet/Bhutan
  2. Arunachal Pradesh and China
  3. Uttarakhand and China
  4. Himachal Pradesh and China

Answer: Sikkim (India) and Tibet/Bhutan

Explanation: Jelep La Pass (4,270 m) is on the Sikkim-China (Tibet) border and was a major trade route before 1962. Nathu La (4,310 m) is the currently open border crossing between Sikkim and Tibet.

28. India's total forest cover is approximately (as per FSI 2021):

  1. About 24% of India's total geographical area
  2. About 33% (Constitutional mandate)
  3. About 40%
  4. About 15%

Answer: About 24% of India's total geographical area

Explanation: India's forest and tree cover is about 24.62% of total geographical area as per Forest Survey of India (FSI) State of Forest Report 2021. The National Forest Policy (1988) mandates 33% forest cover — this target has not been achieved.

29. The 'Dongria Kondh' tribal community opposes mining in:

  1. Niyamgiri Hills, Odisha (Vedanta/Sterlite bauxite mining case)
  2. Jharkhand forests
  3. MP forests
  4. Assam forests

Answer: Niyamgiri Hills, Odisha (Vedanta/Sterlite bauxite mining case)

Explanation: Dongria Kondh tribe of Niyamgiri Hills (Odisha) successfully opposed Vedanta's bauxite mining project through a historic gram sabha vote in 2013, citing the hills as sacred. Supreme Court upheld their right under Forest Rights Act.

30. India's exclusive economic zone (EEZ) extends up to how many nautical miles from its coast?

  1. 12
  2. 24
  3. 200
  4. 350

Answer: 200

Explanation: India's EEZ extends 200 nautical miles from the coast. India has sovereign rights over the resources (fishing, minerals, oil) in this zone.

31. OPEC (Organisation of Petroleum Exporting Countries) was founded in:

  1. 1945
  2. 1960
  3. 1973
  4. 1980

Answer: 1960

Explanation: OPEC was founded in 1960 in Baghdad by Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and Venezuela. Today it has 13 members. OPEC controls ~40% of world oil production. Its secretariat is in Vienna, Austria.

32. The Amazon rainforest is primarily in:

  1. Brazil
  2. Peru
  3. Colombia
  4. Venezuela

Answer: Brazil

Explanation: The Amazon rainforest (world's largest tropical rainforest, ~5.5 million sq km) is primarily in Brazil (60%), plus Peru, Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Bolivia, and other South American countries.

33. India's longest national highway is:

  1. NH-44 (Srinagar to Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km)
  2. NH-1
  3. NH-7 (old)
  4. NH-27

Answer: NH-44 (Srinagar to Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km)

Explanation: NH-44 (3,745 km) from Srinagar to Kanyakumari via Delhi-Agra-Nagpur-Bangalore-Chennai is India's longest national highway, running the full north-south length of India.

34. The deepest port in India is:

  1. Visakhapatnam (Vizag) Port, Andhra Pradesh
  2. Chennai Port
  3. Kolkata Port
  4. Kochi Port

Answer: Visakhapatnam (Vizag) Port, Andhra Pradesh

Explanation: Visakhapatnam Port (Vizag) is India's deepest natural port. It has a natural draft of about 17.5 m and is a major port on the east coast. It handles iron ore, coal, and petroleum products.

35. The 'Terai' zone in northern India is:

  1. A marshy, forested, low-land zone at the base of the Himalayas (south of Bhabar)
  2. A desert zone
  3. A plateau
  4. A coastal zone

Answer: A marshy, forested, low-land zone at the base of the Himalayas (south of Bhabar)

Explanation: Terai (from Sanskrit 'low land') is a belt of marshy, forested, low-lying land in northern India and Nepal, south of the Bhabar zone and Shivalik Hills. Underground streams resurface here, creating swampy conditions.

36. The Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant (Tamil Nadu) was built with assistance from:

  1. Russia
  2. France
  3. USA
  4. UK

Answer: Russia

Explanation: Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu, was built with Russia's technical and financial assistance. It has two VVER-1000 reactors, each of 1,000 MW. More units are under construction.

37. What is the primary factor influencing the formation of monsoon patterns in the Indian subcontinent?

  1. Temperature
  2. Pressure
  3. Wind
  4. Ocean Currents

Answer: Pressure

Explanation: Pressure differences between land and sea drive monsoon patterns. Low pressure over land and high pressure over the ocean create a pressure gradient.

38. Which of the following rivers is known for flowing through the Grand Canyon?

  1. Colorado
  2. Missouri
  3. Rio Grande
  4. Arkansas

Answer: Colorado

Explanation: The Colorado River flows through the Grand Canyon. It is a major river in the southwestern United States.

39. What is the term for the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the atmosphere?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process of water movement through plants. It occurs mainly through stomata on leaves.

40. Which mountain range runs along the border between France and Spain?

  1. Pyrenees
  2. Alps
  3. Carpathian
  4. Apennine

Answer: Pyrenees

Explanation: The Pyrenees mountain range forms the border between France and Spain. It stretches for about 435 km.

41. What is the largest desert in the world, covering over 9 million square kilometers?

  1. Sahara
  2. Gobi
  3. Mojave
  4. Antarctic

Answer: Antarctic

Explanation: The Antarctic Desert is the largest desert, covering the entire continent of Antarctica. It is also the coldest desert.

42. Which strait separates the continents of Asia and Africa?

  1. Bab-el-Mandeb
  2. Bosphorus
  3. Malacca
  4. Sunda

Answer: Bab-el-Mandeb

Explanation: The Bab-el-Mandeb strait connects the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden, separating Asia from Africa.

43. What is the process by which the Earth's plates move and change, resulting in the creation of mountains and volcanoes?

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Plate Tectonics
  4. Glaciation

Answer: Plate Tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics is the process of plate movement. It shapes the Earth's surface, creating mountains and volcanoes.

44. Which river is the longest in South America, flowing through Brazil, Peru, and Colombia?

  1. Amazon
  2. Parana
  3. Sao Francisco
  4. Magdalena

Answer: Amazon

Explanation: The Amazon River is the longest in South America, stretching over 6,400 km. It flows through several countries.

45. What is the term for a body of water that is partially enclosed by land, with a restricted connection to the open ocean?

  1. Gulf
  2. Bay
  3. Sea
  4. Fjord

Answer: Sea

Explanation: A sea is a body of water partially enclosed by land. It has a restricted connection to the open ocean.

46. Which climate pattern is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of precipitation throughout the year?

  1. Tropical
  2. Desert
  3. Temperate
  4. Polar

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: Tropical climates are hot and humid, with high temperatures and precipitation year-round. They are found near the equator.

47. What is the primary cause of the formation of the Indian monsoon?

  1. Temperature difference
  2. Pressure gradient
  3. Latitude
  4. Ocean currents

Answer: Temperature difference

Explanation: Temperature difference between land and sea is the primary cause of the Indian monsoon.

48. Which of the following rivers flows through the Rift Valley?

  1. Nile
  2. Amazon
  3. Ganga
  4. Brahmaputra

Answer: Nile

Explanation: The Nile river flows through the Rift Valley.

49. Which of the following straits separates the continents of Asia and Africa?

  1. Bosphorus
  2. Malacca
  3. Bab-el-Mandeb
  4. Suez

Answer: Bab-el-Mandeb

Explanation: The Bab-el-Mandeb strait separates Asia and Africa.

50. Which river is the longest in South America, stretching over 6,800 kilometers?

  1. Amazon

Explanation: The Amazon river is the longest in South America.
