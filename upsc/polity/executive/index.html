Executive MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Executive questions to revise the UPSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Under which article can the President proclaim a National Emergency?

  1. Article 352
  2. Article 356
  3. Article 360
  4. Article 370

Answer: Article 352

Explanation: Article 352 empowers the President to proclaim a National Emergency if the security of India is threatened by war, external aggression, or armed rebellion.

2. Under which article can the President impose President's Rule in a State?

  1. Article 352
  2. Article 356
  3. Article 360
  4. Article 370

Answer: Article 356

Explanation: Article 356 (President's Rule) is invoked when the constitutional machinery of a State fails. It is also called State Emergency.

3. The President of India can be impeached for:

  1. Violation of Constitution
  2. Corruption
  3. Criminal offences
  4. Any misconduct

Answer: Violation of Constitution

Explanation: The President can be impeached only for violation of the Constitution (Article 61). The process starts in either House (special majority of 2/3rd) and is ratified by the other House.

4. The 'Kitchen Cabinet' refers to:

  1. Informal group of influential advisors to PM
  2. Cabinet committee on food
  3. Sub-committee of Cabinet
  4. Emergency cabinet

Answer: Informal group of influential advisors to PM

Explanation: Kitchen Cabinet is an informal term for a small group of close advisors (not necessarily ministers) who the PM consults informally on important decisions, bypassing the formal Cabinet.

5. The 'Union Cabinet' is different from 'Council of Ministers' in that:

  1. Union Cabinet is a smaller body of senior Cabinet Ministers
  2. They are the same
  3. Council of Ministers has only PM and senior ministers
  4. Union Cabinet includes all ministers

Answer: Union Cabinet is a smaller body of senior Cabinet Ministers

Explanation: Council of Ministers includes Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State (independent charge), and Ministers of State (no independent charge). Cabinet is a smaller sub-set of senior ministers who collectively decide policy.

6. The Governor's rule in a state (Article 356) was first imposed in:

  1. Punjab (1951)
  2. Bengal (1952)
  3. Travancore-Cochin (1956)
  4. UP (1950)

Answer: Punjab (1951)

Explanation: Article 356 (President's Rule) was first imposed in Punjab in 1951. It has been imposed over 100 times since independence — Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh have had it most often.

7. The Advocate General of a State is appointed by:

  1. Governor
  2. Chief Minister
  3. President
  4. High Court Chief Justice

Answer: Governor

Explanation: Article 165: Advocate General is appointed by the Governor. The Advocate General is the chief law officer of the state, analogous to the Attorney General of India at the Union level.

8. The Attorney General of India is appointed by:

  1. President
  2. Parliament
  3. Chief Justice of India
  4. Union Cabinet

Answer: President

Explanation: Article 76: Attorney General is appointed by the President (on advice of the Council of Ministers). The AG must be qualified to be a judge of the Supreme Court.

9. The Central Information Commission (CIC) was established under:

  1. RTI Act 2005
  2. IT Act 2000
  3. CIC Act 2003
  4. Constitutional provision

Answer: RTI Act 2005

Explanation: Central Information Commission was established under the Right to Information Act 2005 to hear appeals and complaints related to public information. Chief Information Commissioner and commissioners are appointed by President.

10. The Inter-State Council is chaired by:

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Home Minister
  4. Vice President

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The Inter-State Council (Article 263) is chaired by the Prime Minister. It consists of Chief Ministers of all states and 6 Cabinet Ministers of the Union as members.

11. Who administers the oath of office to the Vice President of India?

  1. President of India
  2. Chief Justice of India
  3. Speaker of Lok Sabha
  4. Chief Justice of High Court

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The President administers the oath of office to the Vice President (Art 69). Whereas, the Chief Justice of India administers the oath to the President.

12. Under which Article is the Prime Minister appointed?

  1. Article 75
  2. Article 74
  3. Article 76
  4. Article 77

Answer: Article 75

Explanation: Article 75(1) states the Prime Minister shall be appointed by the President. Article 74 states the Council of Ministers with the PM at head shall aid and advise the President.

13. Which case established that the President must act on the advice of the Council of Ministers?

  1. Shamsher Singh v. State of Punjab (1974)
  2. Kesavananda Bharati (1973)
  3. Maneka Gandhi (1978)
  4. Minerva Mills (1980)

Answer: Shamsher Singh v. State of Punjab (1974)

Explanation: Shamsher Singh v. State of Punjab (1974) — the Supreme Court held that the President and Governors are constitutional heads and must act on the advice of the Cabinet/Council of Ministers.

14. A Judge of a High Court can be transferred by:

  1. President on recommendation of CJI after consultation with collegium
  2. Governor on advice of CM
  3. Parliament by resolution
  4. State Public Service Commission

Answer: President on recommendation of CJI after consultation with collegium

Explanation: A Judge of a High Court can be transferred by the President after consultation with the Chief Justice of India (Art 222). The collegium system now governs such transfers.

15. The Governor of a State holds office:

  1. During the pleasure of the President
  2. For a fixed 5-year term
  3. Until removed by State Legislature
  4. For life tenure like a judge

Answer: During the pleasure of the President

Explanation: The Governor holds office during the pleasure of the President (Art 156). Though the normal term is 5 years, the President (on advice of Cabinet) can remove the Governor at any time.

16. Under Article 163, the Governor can act in his discretion regarding:

  1. Certain specific matters listed in the Constitution
  2. All matters
  3. Only during President's Rule
  4. None — Governor must always act on CM's advice

Answer: Certain specific matters listed in the Constitution

Explanation: Article 163 states Governor acts on the advice of the Council of Ministers except when the Constitution expressly requires him to act in his discretion (like sending report for President's Rule under Art 356).

17. Who appoints the Advocate General of a State?

  1. Governor
  2. President
  3. Chief Minister
  4. High Court Chief Justice

Answer: Governor

Explanation: The Advocate General is appointed by the Governor (Art 165). He/she must be qualified to be a judge of the High Court and holds office during the pleasure of the Governor.

18. The Finance Commission of India is constituted under:

  1. Article 280
  2. Article 281
  3. Article 266
  4. Article 284

Answer: Article 280

Explanation: The Finance Commission is constituted under Article 280 by the President every fifth year (or earlier). It recommends distribution of taxes between Centre and States and grants-in-aid to States.

19. The 'Doctrine of Pleasure' in the Indian Constitution means:

  1. Certain officers hold office at the pleasure of the President/Governor
  2. Citizens have the pleasure of removing officials
  3. Courts can remove officials at pleasure
  4. Parliament can dismiss officers at any time

Answer: Certain officers hold office at the pleasure of the President/Governor

Explanation: The Doctrine of Pleasure (Art 310) means certain government servants hold office during the pleasure of the President or Governor. However, Art 311 protects civil servants from arbitrary dismissal — they must be given a hearing.

20. Which Article provides for special provisions relating to language spoken by a section of a State's population?

  1. Article 347
  2. Article 343
  3. Article 350A
  4. Article 351

Answer: Article 347

Explanation: Article 347 states the President may, on demand, direct that a language spoken by a substantial proportion of the State's population be officially recognized for that State. Punjabi in Haryana is an example.

21. Under Article 72, the President's pardoning power includes:

  1. Pardon, Reprieve, Respite, Remission, Commutation
  2. Only pardon and commutation
  3. Only death sentence cases
  4. All except offences under State laws

Answer: Pardon, Reprieve, Respite, Remission, Commutation

Explanation: Article 72 gives the President power to grant pardon, reprieve, respite, remission of punishment or commute the sentence — in cases of court martial, offences against Union laws, and death sentences.

22. The power of the Governor to pardon under Article 161 differs from Art 72 (President) in that the Governor:

  1. Cannot pardon death sentences
  2. Can pardon all types of offences
  3. Cannot grant reprieve
  4. Has more powers than the President

Answer: Cannot pardon death sentences

Explanation: Under Art 161, the Governor can pardon, reprieve, respite, remit, or commute sentences for offences against State laws. But unlike the President, the Governor CANNOT pardon death sentences — only the President can under Art 72.

23. The Constitution of Jammu & Kashmir was abrogated by which action?

  1. Presidential Order abrogating Article 370 in August 2019
  2. J&K Reorganization Act 2019 replacing the State with two UTs
  3. Both Presidential Order (abrogating Art 370) and J&K Reorganization Act 2019
  4. Constitutional Amendment

Answer: Both Presidential Order (abrogating Art 370) and J&K Reorganization Act 2019

Explanation: Article 370 was abrogated by Presidential Order on 5 August 2019, and simultaneously the J&K Reorganization Act 2019 bifurcated the State into two Union Territories — J&K (with legislature) and Ladakh (without legislature).

24. The Constitutional provision for special officer for Linguistic Minorities is under:

  1. Article 350B
  2. Article 350A
  3. Article 347
  4. Article 29

Answer: Article 350B

Explanation: Article 350B provides for a Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities to investigate all matters relating to safeguards for linguistic minorities. The officer reports to the President annually.

25. The National Development Council (NDC) is chaired by:

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Finance Minister
  4. NITI Aayog CEO

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The National Development Council is chaired by the Prime Minister. It includes all Union Cabinet Ministers, Chief Ministers of all States, Lt. Governors/Administrators of UTs, and members of NITI Aayog.
