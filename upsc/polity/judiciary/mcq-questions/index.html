26. NOTA (None of the Above) option on EVMs was introduced in:

  1. 2013
  2. 2010
  3. 2004
  4. 2014

Answer: 2013

Explanation: NOTA was introduced following a Supreme Court order (PUCL case, 2013). Voters can press the NOTA button to reject all candidates while exercising their right to vote.

27. Statement 1: The Supreme Court of India has the power to review its own judgments under Article 137. Statement 2: The power of judicial review is implicit in Article 32 and Article 226 of the Constitution.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Both statements are correct as Article 137 gives the Supreme Court the power to review its judgments and Articles 32 and 226 imply judicial review.

28. Statement 1: The Chief Justice of India is appointed by the President without any consultation. Statement 2: The Second Judges Case (1993) established the Collegium system for appointing judges to the higher judiciary.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: B

Explanation: Only statement 2 is correct as the Second Judges Case did establish the Collegium system.

29. Statement 1: Article 124(2) of the Constitution lays down the eligibility criteria for a Supreme Court judge. Statement 2: A judge of the Supreme Court can be removed from office through impeachment under Article 124(4).

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Both statements are correct as Article 124(2) sets eligibility criteria and Article 124(4) provides for removal through impeachment.

30. Statement 1: The concept of 'Basic Structure' was introduced in the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973). Statement 2: The judgment in the Minerva Mills case (1980) reaffirmed the doctrine of 'Basic Structure'.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Both statements are correct as the Kesavananda Bharati case introduced the concept and the Minerva Mills case reaffirmed it.

31. Statement 1: The Supreme Court has original jurisdiction in disputes between the Centre and one or more states under Article 131. Statement 2: The Advisory Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court is provided under Article 143.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Both statements are correct as Article 131 provides for original jurisdiction and Article 143 for advisory jurisdiction.

32. Statement 1: The power to increase the number of judges in the Supreme Court is vested in the Parliament. Statement 2: The Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976, curtailed the judicial review power of the Supreme Court.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Both statements are correct as Parliament can increase the number of judges and the 42nd Amendment curtailed judicial review.

33. Statement 1: The Supreme Court can declare a law as unconstitutional if it violates the 'Basic Structure' of the Constitution. Statement 2: The 'Basic Structure' doctrine was first applied in the Golaknath case (1967).

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: A

Explanation: Only statement 1 is correct as the 'Basic Structure' doctrine was introduced later in Kesavananda Bharati case.

34. Statement 1: The President of India has the power to appoint ad-hoc judges to the Supreme Court under Article 127. Statement 2: The Collegium for appointment of Supreme Court judges includes the Chief Justice and four senior-most judges.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Both statements are correct as Article 127 allows for ad-hoc judges and the Collegium includes the Chief Justice and four senior-most judges.

35. Statement 1: The Supreme Court has the power of judicial review over the decisions of administrative authorities. Statement 2: The power of judicial review is limited to reviewing laws and not executive actions.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D
