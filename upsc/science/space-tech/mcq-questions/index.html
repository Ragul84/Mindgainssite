26. The main component of natural gas is:

  1. Methane (CH₄)
  2. Propane
  3. Butane
  4. Ethane

Answer: Methane (CH₄)

Explanation: Natural gas is primarily methane (CH₄) — about 70-90%. It also contains smaller amounts of ethane, propane, butane, and other hydrocarbons. Used as a cleaner fuel than coal or oil. CNG (for vehicles) is compressed natural gas.

27. Ethanol (ethyl alcohol, C₂H₅OH) is produced by:

  1. Fermentation of sugars by yeast
  2. Distillation of petroleum
  3. Electrolysis of water
  4. Synthesis from methane

Answer: Fermentation of sugars by yeast

Explanation: Ethanol is produced by fermentation — yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) converts sugars (glucose) to ethanol and CO₂: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂. Used in alcoholic beverages, antiseptics, and biofuels.

28. Brass is an alloy of:

  1. Copper and Zinc
  2. Copper and Tin
  3. Iron and Carbon
  4. Aluminum and Copper

Answer: Copper and Zinc

Explanation: Brass = Copper (60-90%) + Zinc (10-40%). It is a gold-colored alloy used in musical instruments, locks, plumbing fittings, and decorative items. Bronze = Copper + Tin (used in coins, statues, bells).

29. Which gas causes suffocation in coal mines (Blackdamp)?

  1. Carbon dioxide (CO₂) and Nitrogen mixture
  2. Carbon monoxide
  3. Methane
  4. Hydrogen sulfide

Answer: Carbon dioxide (CO₂) and Nitrogen mixture

Explanation: Blackdamp (choke damp) in coal mines is primarily CO₂ mixed with Nitrogen, which displaces oxygen and causes suffocation. Firedamp is methane (CH₄) which is explosive. Whitedamp is CO (toxic).

30. Osmosis is the movement of water:

  1. From high water concentration to low water concentration through a semi-permeable membrane
  2. From low to high concentration (active transport)
  3. Of solute particles only
  4. Through open pores in any direction

Answer: From high water concentration to low water concentration through a semi-permeable membrane

Explanation: Osmosis: movement of water molecules from a region of higher water concentration (dilute solution/pure water) to lower water concentration (concentrated solution) through a selectively permeable membrane.

31. The study of insects is called:

  1. Entomology
  2. Ornithology
  3. Ichthyology
  4. Herpetology

Answer: Entomology

Explanation: Entomology is the study of insects. Ornithology = birds. Ichthyology = fish. Herpetology = reptiles and amphibians. Mammology = mammals. Malacology = mollusks.

32. The main site of absorption of nutrients is:

  1. Small intestine (especially the jejunum)
  2. Stomach
  3. Large intestine
  4. Liver

Answer: Small intestine (especially the jejunum)

Explanation: The small intestine (particularly the jejunum) is the main site of nutrient absorption. Villi and microvilli (brush border) greatly increase surface area. Large intestine absorbs water and forms feces.

33. The telephone was invented by:

  1. Alexander Graham Bell (1876)
  2. Thomas Edison
  3. Guglielmo Marconi
  4. Nikola Tesla

Answer: Alexander Graham Bell (1876)

Explanation: Alexander Graham Bell patented the telephone on March 7, 1876. He made the first telephone call on March 10, 1876. However, Antonio Meucci had developed a similar device earlier (officially recognized by US Congress in 2002).

34. The World Wide Web (WWW) was invented by:

  1. Tim Berners-Lee (1989-91)
  2. Bill Gates
  3. Steve Jobs
  4. Al Gore

Answer: Tim Berners-Lee (1989-91)

Explanation: Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989 (proposal) and 1991 (public launch) at CERN, Switzerland. He created HTML, URL, and HTTP. He deliberately made it free and open, not proprietary.

35. The first artificial satellite was:

  1. Sputnik 1 (USSR, 4 October 1957)
  2. Explorer 1 (USA)
  3. Aryabhata (India)
  4. Vanguard (USA)

Answer: Sputnik 1 (USSR, 4 October 1957)

Explanation: Sputnik 1 (Soviet Union, 4 October 1957) was the world's first artificial satellite. This launched the Space Age and the Space Race between USA and USSR. India's first satellite: Aryabhata (1975).

36. Alternating current (AC) is preferred over DC for long-distance transmission because:

  1. Voltage can be stepped up (increasing voltage, reducing current) to reduce power loss in transmission lines
  2. AC is faster than DC
  3. DC cannot travel long distances
  4. AC generators are simpler

Answer: Voltage can be stepped up (increasing voltage, reducing current) to reduce power loss in transmission lines

Explanation: P_loss = I²R in transmission lines. By stepping up voltage (using transformers — only work with AC), current is reduced enormously, minimizing resistive heat loss. DC cannot be transformed easily.

37. In a series circuit, the total resistance is:

  1. Sum of individual resistances (R_total = R₁ + R₂ + R₃...)
  2. Less than the smallest resistance
  3. Reciprocal of sum of reciprocals
  4. Equal to the largest resistance

Answer: Sum of individual resistances (R_total = R₁ + R₂ + R₃...)

Explanation: In series circuits: R_total = R₁ + R₂ + R₃... (total resistance is sum of all). Same current flows through all. Voltage divides. In parallel: 1/R_total = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂... (total resistance less than smallest).

38. Superconductors are materials that:

  1. Have zero electrical resistance below a critical temperature
  2. Have infinite resistance
  3. Conduct electricity only in one direction
  4. Only conduct heat, not electricity

Answer: Have zero electrical resistance below a critical temperature

Explanation: Superconductors exhibit zero electrical resistance below their critical temperature (Tc). Discovered by Heike Kamerlingh Onnes (1911) in mercury at 4.2 K. Used in MRI magnets, particle accelerators, maglev trains.

39. Plaster of Paris is:

  1. Calcium sulphate hemihydrate (CaSO₄·½H₂O)
  2. Calcium carbonate
  3. Calcium chloride
  4. Calcium hydroxide

Answer: Calcium sulphate hemihydrate (CaSO₄·½H₂O)

Explanation: Plaster of Paris (POP) is calcium sulphate hemihydrate (CaSO₄·½H₂O). Made by heating gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) at ~120°C. On adding water, it sets into hard gypsum — used in sculpting, casting, and medical plaster.

40. Washing soda (Sodium carbonate) formula is:

  1. Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  2. NaHCO₃
  3. NaOH
  4. Na₂SO₄

Answer: Na₂CO₃·10H₂O

Explanation: Washing soda is hydrated sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O). Used in glass manufacturing, paper industry, laundry, and water softening. Baking soda is NaHCO₃. Caustic soda is NaOH.

41. Galvanizing is coating iron with:

  1. Zinc (to prevent corrosion/rusting)
  2. Tin
  3. Chromium
  4. Paint only

Answer: Zinc (to prevent corrosion/rusting)

Explanation: Galvanizing is coating iron/steel with zinc to prevent rusting. Even if the zinc coating is scratched, zinc (more reactive) corrodes preferentially (sacrificial protection), protecting iron. Used in buckets, roofing sheets, pipes.

42. Deficiency of Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) causes:

  1. Beriberi (affects nervous system and heart)
  2. Scurvy
  3. Rickets
  4. Night blindness

Answer: Beriberi (affects nervous system and heart)

Explanation: Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) deficiency causes Beriberi — 'Wet Beriberi' (affects cardiovascular system, oedema) and 'Dry Beriberi' (peripheral neuropathy). Common in populations whose diet is mainly polished white rice.

43. Iron deficiency causes:

  1. Anaemia (reduced haemoglobin — reduced oxygen-carrying capacity)
  2. Goitre
  3. Rickets
  4. Night blindness

Answer: Anaemia (reduced haemoglobin — reduced oxygen-carrying capacity)

Explanation: Iron deficiency anaemia is the most common nutritional deficiency worldwide. Iron is needed for haemoglobin synthesis. Symptoms: fatigue, pale skin, shortness of breath. Sources: red meat, leafy greens, lentils.

44. Fat-soluble vitamins are:

  1. Vitamins A, D, E, and K (stored in fatty tissue and liver)
  2. Vitamins B and C
  3. Vitamins A, B, C, and D
  4. Vitamins C and K

Answer: Vitamins A, D, E, and K (stored in fatty tissue and liver)

Explanation: Fat-soluble vitamins: A, D, E, K — absorbed with dietary fat and stored in body fat/liver (toxicity possible from excess). Water-soluble vitamins: B complex and C — not stored in body (excess excreted in urine, toxicity rare).

45. The 10% rule in ecology states:

  1. Only about 10% of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next
  2. 10% of food is wasted
  3. 10% of species survive
  4. Energy is transferred 100% but 10% is heat

Answer: Only about 10% of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next

Explanation: 10% Rule (Lindemann, 1942): only about 10% of energy at one trophic level is transferred to the next level (90% is lost as heat, excretion, respiration). This limits food chain length to about 4-5 levels.

46. The planet closest to the Sun is:

  1. Mercury
  2. Venus
  3. Earth
  4. Mars

Answer: Mercury

Explanation: Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun (average 57.9 million km). It is also the smallest planet. Despite being closest, Venus is hotter due to its thick CO₂ atmosphere (greenhouse effect) — Mercury has no atmosphere.

47. A compass needle points towards the geographic North Pole because:

  1. Earth's geographic North Pole is magnetically a south pole (attracts the compass's north-seeking pole)
  2. Earth's core is iron
  3. Magnetic monopoles exist
  4. Earth rotates

Answer: Earth's geographic North Pole is magnetically a south pole (attracts the compass's north-seeking pole)

Explanation: Earth's geographic North Pole is actually the magnetic south pole — that's why the north-seeking end of a compass needle (which is a magnetic north pole) points toward it. Earth's magnetic axis is tilted about 11° from its rotational axis.

48. The phenomenon of electromagnetic induction was discovered by:

  1. Michael Faraday (1831)
  2. James Watt
  3. Nikola Tesla
  4. André-Marie Ampère

Answer: Michael Faraday (1831)

Explanation: Michael Faraday discovered electromagnetic induction in 1831 — a changing magnetic field induces an EMF in a conductor. This principle is the basis for electric generators, transformers, and induction motors.

49. Which electromagnetic wave has the highest penetrating power?

  1. Gamma rays
  2. X-rays
  3. Ultraviolet rays
  4. Radio waves

Answer: Gamma rays

Explanation: Gamma rays have the highest energy and penetrating power in the EM spectrum. They can penetrate dense materials like lead (though thick lead shields them). Used in cancer treatment (radiotherapy) and sterilizing medical equipment.

50. Electrolysis of water produces:

  1. Hydrogen at cathode, Oxygen at anode (ratio 2:1 by volume)
  2. Oxygen at cathode, Hydrogen at anode
  3. Only hydrogen
  4. Only oxygen

Answer: Hydrogen at cathode, Oxygen at anode (ratio 2:1 by volume)

Explanation: In electrolysis of water: cathode (negative electrode) produces H₂, anode (positive electrode) produces O₂. Ratio H₂:O₂ = 2:1 by volume. Electrolyte (like H₂SO₄ or NaOH) is added to increase conductivity.
