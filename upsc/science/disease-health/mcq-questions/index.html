26. Which public health vaccine uses mRNA technology?

  1. Pfizer
  2. Moderna
  3. Covaxin
  4. Both A and B

Answer: Both A and B

Explanation: Both Pfizer and Moderna vaccines use mRNA technology to prevent COVID-19, which is a new approach to vaccine development and has shown high efficacy in clinical trials.

27. What is the name of the biotechnology used to develop personalized cancer vaccines?

  1. CRISPR
  2. Gene therapy
  3. Neoantigen therapy
  4. Immunotherapy

Answer: Neoantigen therapy

Explanation: Neoantigen therapy is a biotechnology used to develop personalized cancer vaccines, which involves identifying and targeting specific mutations in a patient's cancer cells.

Explanation: The International Space Station (ISS) is used to study the effects of space travel on human health, including the effects of microgravity and radiation on the human body.
