26. What is the term for the process of converting sunlight into electrical energy using semiconducting materials?

  1. Photovoltaic
  2. Thermoelectric
  3. Piezoelectric
  4. Electrochemical

Answer: Photovoltaic

Explanation: Photovoltaic effect is the process of converting sunlight into electrical energy using semiconducting materials.

27. Which of the following countries has developed a 6G wireless network technology?

  1. USA
  2. China
  3. Japan
  4. South Korea

Answer: China

Explanation: China has developed a 6G wireless network technology with faster data rates and lower latency than 5G.

28. What is the name of the vaccine technology used to develop the COVID-19 vaccine?

  1. mRNA
  2. DNA
  3. Protein
  4. Vector

Answer: mRNA

Explanation: mRNA vaccine technology was used to develop the COVID-19 vaccine, which uses a piece of genetic material to instruct cells to produce a protein.

29. Which of the following energy sources is used in nuclear power plants?

  1. Uranium
  2. Thorium
  3. Plutonium
  4. Deuterium

Answer: Uranium

Explanation: Uranium is the primary energy source used in nuclear power plants to generate electricity.

30. What is the term for the process of using microorganisms to produce biofuels?

  1. Fermentation
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Respiration
  4. Transcription

Answer: Fermentation

Explanation: Fermentation is the process of using microorganisms to produce biofuels, such as ethanol and butanol.

31. Which of the following satellite launch vehicles is used by the Indian Space Research Organisation?

  1. PSLV
  2. GSLV
  3. Ariane
  4. Falcon

Answer: PSLV

Explanation: PSLV is the satellite launch vehicle used by the Indian Space Research Organisation to launch satellites into orbit.

32. Which of the following technologies uses CRISPR gene editing for biofuel production?

  1. Synthetic biology
  2. Biotechnology
  3. Genetic engineering
  4. Microbiology

Answer: Synthetic biology

Explanation: Synthetic biology uses CRISPR for biofuel production. It involves designing new biological systems.

33. What is the name of India's first indigenous nuclear-powered attack submarine?

34. Which country has developed the 6G technology for high-speed data transmission?

36. Which of the following is a type of renewable energy source?

  1. Coal
  2. Petroleum
  3. Natural gas
  4. Solar

Answer: Solar

Explanation: Solar energy is a renewable energy source. It is sustainable and non-depletable.

40. Which type of energy storage is used in electric vehicles?

  1. Lead-Acid Batteries
  2. Lithium-Ion Batteries
  3. Fuel Cells
  4. Super Capacitors

Answer: Lithium-Ion Batteries

Explanation: Lithium-ion batteries are commonly used in electric vehicles due to their high energy density.

41. What is the term for the process of generating energy from the heat of the Earth's core?

  1. Geothermal Energy
  2. Solar Energy
  3. Wind Energy
  4. Hydro Energy

Answer: Geothermal Energy

Explanation: Geothermal energy harnesses heat from the Earth's core.

47. Which of the following technologies uses CRISPR gene editing to develop biofuels?

  1. Synthetic Biology
  2. Genetic Engineering
  3. Biotechnology
  4. Microbiology

Answer: Synthetic Biology

Explanation: Synthetic biology applies CRISPR gene editing to design microorganisms that produce biofuels. This field combines engineering and biology principles.

48. What type of energy storage is used in most electric vehicles?

  1. Lead-Acid Batteries
  2. Lithium-Ion Batteries
  3. Fuel Cells
  4. Supercapacitors

Answer: Lithium-Ion Batteries

Explanation: Lithium-ion batteries are widely used in electric vehicles due to their high energy density and long cycle life. They enable efficient and reliable energy storage.

49. Which space agency developed the reusable launch vehicle Falcon 9?

  1. NASA
  2. ESA
  3. SpaceX
  4. ISRO

Answer: SpaceX

Explanation: SpaceX developed the Falcon 9 launch vehicle, which can return to Earth after launching payloads into orbit. This reusable technology reduces launch costs.

50. What is the primary application of concentrated solar power systems?

  1. Electricity Generation
  2. Water Heating
  3. Space Cooling
  4. Industrial Process Heat

Answer: Electricity Generation

Explanation: Concentrated solar power systems generate electricity by focusing sunlight onto a heat exchanger. This technology produces electricity from solar energy.
