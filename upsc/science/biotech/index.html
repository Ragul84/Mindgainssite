Biotech MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Biotech questions to revise the UPSC Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. Which scientist discovered the structure of DNA (Double Helix)?

  1. Louis Pasteur and Robert Koch
  2. Watson and Crick (with Franklin's X-ray data)
  3. Mendel and Darwin
  4. Hershey and Chase

Answer: Watson and Crick (with Franklin's X-ray data)

Explanation: James Watson and Francis Crick discovered the double helix structure of DNA in 1953, using X-ray crystallography data from Rosalind Franklin. They received the Nobel Prize in 1962.

2. CRISPR-Cas9 is a technology used for:

  1. Growing bacteria in labs
  2. Gene editing — cutting and modifying specific DNA sequences
  3. Diagnosing cancer
  4. Producing antibiotics

Answer: Gene editing — cutting and modifying specific DNA sequences

Explanation: CRISPR-Cas9 is a revolutionary gene-editing tool that allows scientists to precisely edit DNA sequences. It has applications in genetic disease treatment, agriculture, and research.

3. The double helix structure of DNA was discovered by:

  1. Watson and Crick (1953)
  2. Gregor Mendel
  3. Rosalind Franklin alone
  4. Erwin Chargaff

Answer: Watson and Crick (1953)

Explanation: James Watson and Francis Crick described the double helix structure of DNA in 1953 (published in Nature journal). Rosalind Franklin's X-ray crystallography data was crucial to their discovery. They won Nobel Prize in 1962.

4. Bt cotton is a genetically modified crop that produces:

  1. Bt toxin (from Bacillus thuringiensis) in the plant — kills bollworm insects that eat the plant; reduces pesticide use
  2. More yield without any modification
  3. Nitrogen fertilizer
  4. Drought resistance

Answer: Bt toxin (from Bacillus thuringiensis) in the plant — kills bollworm insects that eat the plant; reduces pesticide use

Explanation: Bt cotton contains a gene from Bacillus thuringiensis bacteria that produces Cry proteins (Bt toxin) harmful to lepidopteran insects (bollworms). Bt crops have significantly reduced pesticide use in India. Bt cotton was first approved in India in 2002.

5. What is the primary application of CRISPR technology in biotech?

  1. Gene editing
  2. Vaccine development
  3. Stem cell research
  4. Cancer treatment

Answer: Gene editing

Explanation: CRISPR technology is primarily used for gene editing, allowing for precise modifications to the genome. This has significant implications for treating genetic diseases.

6. Which of the following biotech companies developed the first mRNA vaccine?

  1. Pfizer
  2. Moderna
  3. AstraZeneca
  4. Johnson & Johnson

Answer: Moderna

Explanation: Moderna developed the first mRNA vaccine, which has been used to combat COVID-19. This technology has revolutionized vaccine development.

7. What is the name of the gene editing tool that uses a bacterial defense system?

  1. CRISPR
  2. TALEN
  3. ZFN
  4. RNAi

Answer: CRISPR

Explanation: CRISPR is a gene editing tool that uses a bacterial defense system to make precise modifications to the genome. This technology has transformed the field of biotech.

8. Which of the following is a potential application of synthetic biology in biotech?

  1. Biofuel production
  2. Gene therapy
  3. Regenerative medicine
  4. Nanotechnology

Answer: Biofuel production

Explanation: Synthetic biology has the potential to produce biofuels, providing a sustainable alternative energy source. This application could significantly impact the environment.

9. What is the name of the international project that aims to map the human genome?

  1. Human Genome Project
  2. Genome Editing Project
  3. Gene Mapping Project
  4. DNA Sequencing Project

Answer: Human Genome Project

Explanation: The Human Genome Project is an international project that aims to map the human genome, providing insights into human biology and disease. This project has led to significant advances in biotech.

10. Which of the following biotech techniques uses high-throughput sequencing to analyze gene expression?

  1. RNA-seq
  2. ChIP-seq
  3. DNA microarray
  4. Protein sequencing

Answer: RNA-seq

Explanation: RNA-seq is a biotech technique that uses high-throughput sequencing to analyze gene expression, providing insights into cellular biology. This technique has transformed the field of genomics.

11. What is the name of the company that developed the first commercial insulin product using recombinant DNA technology?

  1. Eli Lilly
  2. Genentech
  3. Amgen
  4. Biogen

Answer: Genentech

Explanation: Genentech developed the first commercial insulin product using recombinant DNA technology, revolutionizing the treatment of diabetes. This application has had a significant impact on public health.

12. Which of the following is a potential risk associated with gene editing using CRISPR technology?

  1. Off-target effects
  2. Mosaicism
  3. Gene silencing
  4. Epigenetic modification

Answer: Off-target effects

Explanation: Off-target effects are a potential risk associated with gene editing using CRISPR technology, highlighting the need for careful regulation and safety protocols. This risk must be mitigated to ensure safe application.

13. What is the name of the biotech technique that uses RNA interference to silence gene expression?

  1. RNAi
  2. CRISPR
  3. TALEN
  4. ZFN

Answer: RNAi

Explanation: RNAi is a biotech technique that uses RNA interference to silence gene expression, providing a powerful tool for studying gene function. This technique has significant implications for treating diseases.

14. Which of the following is a potential application of regenerative medicine in biotech?

  1. Tissue engineering
  2. Stem cell therapy
  3. Gene therapy
  4. Organ transplantation

Answer: Stem cell therapy

Explanation: Stem cell therapy is a potential application of regenerative medicine in biotech, providing a promising approach for treating diseases and injuries. This application could significantly impact human health.

15. What is the name of the satellite launch vehicle used by India's space program?

  1. PSLV
  2. GSLV
  3. ISRO
  4. Agni

Answer: PSLV

Explanation: PSLV is the primary satellite launch vehicle used by India's space program.

16. Which gene editing tool uses a guide RNA to locate a specific sequence of DNA?

  1. CRISPR
  2. TALEN
  3. ZFN
  4. RNAi

Answer: CRISPR

Explanation: CRISPR uses a guide RNA to locate and edit specific DNA sequences.

17. What is the term for the use of living organisms to produce biofuels?

  1. Bioremediation
  2. Biofuel production
  3. Biomass conversion
  4. Biotechnology

Answer: Biomass conversion

Explanation: Biomass conversion refers to the use of living organisms to produce biofuels.

18. Which of the following is a benefit of using 5G technology in biotech?

  1. Faster data transfer
  2. Increased security
  3. Improved accuracy
  4. Reduced costs

Answer: Faster data transfer

Explanation: Faster data transfer is a key benefit of using 5G technology in biotech applications.

19. What is the name of the defense missile technology developed by India?

  1. Agni
  2. Prithvi
  3. Akash
  4. Nag

Answer: Agni

Explanation: Agni is a defense missile technology developed by India.

20. Which of the following biotech techniques is used to produce insulin?

  1. Recombinant DNA
  2. Gene editing
  3. Stem cell therapy
  4. Vaccine development

Answer: Recombinant DNA

Explanation: Recombinant DNA technology is used to produce insulin.

21. What is the term for the study of the structure and function of genomes?

  1. Genomics
  2. Proteomics
  3. Metagenomics
  4. Bioinformatics

Answer: Genomics

Explanation: Genomics is the study of the structure and function of genomes.

22. Which of the following public health vaccines uses a weakened form of the virus?

  1. Inactivated vaccine
  2. Live attenuated vaccine
  3. Subunit vaccine
  4. Conjugate vaccine

Answer: Live attenuated vaccine

Explanation: Live attenuated vaccines use a weakened form of the virus to stimulate an immune response.

23. What is the purpose of using satellite imaging in biotech?

  1. Crop monitoring
  2. Gene sequencing
  3. Protein synthesis
  4. Vaccine distribution

Answer: Crop monitoring

Explanation: Satellite imaging is used in biotech for crop monitoring, allowing for the assessment of crop health and yield prediction. This helps in optimizing agricultural practices.

24. Which gene editing tool is used for precise modifications to DNA sequences?

  1. CRISPR
  2. TALEN
  3. ZFN
  4. RNAi

Answer: CRISPR

Explanation: CRISPR is a gene editing tool used for precise modifications to DNA sequences. It has become a crucial tool in biotech research and applications.

25. What is the role of biotech in developing public health vaccines?

  1. Manufacturing
  2. Distribution
  3. Research
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Biotech plays a significant role in developing public health vaccines, encompassing research, manufacturing, and distribution. This has been evident in the rapid development of COVID-19 vaccines.
