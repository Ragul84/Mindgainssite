26. Which of the following is a biotech application of 5G technology?

  1. Telemedicine
  2. Remote surgery
  3. Gene sequencing
  4. Vaccine development

Answer: Telemedicine

Explanation: Telemedicine is a biotech application of 5G technology, enabling remote healthcare services and consultations. This has improved access to healthcare services.

27. What is the significance of biotech in space missions?

  1. Food production
  2. Air purification
  3. Water recycling
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Biotech has significance in space missions for food production, air purification, and water recycling, which are essential for sustaining life in space. These applications support long-duration space missions.

28. Which biotech technique is used for the production of therapeutic proteins?

  1. Gene editing
  2. Gene sequencing
  3. Recombinant DNA technology
  4. PCR

Answer: Recombinant DNA technology

Explanation: Recombinant DNA technology is used for the production of therapeutic proteins, such as insulin and vaccines. This technique has revolutionized the production of biopharmaceuticals.

29. What is the purpose of using defense missile technology in biotech?

  1. Vaccine delivery
  2. Gene editing
  3. Protein synthesis
  4. None of the above

Answer: Vaccine delivery

Explanation: Defense missile technology is being explored for vaccine delivery, using needle-free injection systems. This could improve vaccine administration and accessibility.

30. Which of the following is a biotech application of satellite launch vehicles?

  1. Space exploration
  2. Earth observation
  3. Telemedicine
  4. Vaccine development

Answer: Earth observation

Explanation: Earth observation is a biotech application of satellite launch vehicles, used for monitoring environmental changes and assessing the impact of climate change on ecosystems. This information is crucial for conservation efforts.

31. Which of the following biotech companies developed the first COVID-19 vaccine?

  1. Pfizer
  2. Moderna
  3. AstraZeneca
  4. Johnson & Johnson

Answer: Pfizer

Explanation: Pfizer developed the first COVID-19 vaccine approved for emergency use.

32. What is the name of the gene editing tool that uses a small RNA molecule to locate a specific sequence of DNA?

  1. CRISPR
  2. TALEN
  3. ZFN
  4. RNAi

Answer: CRISPR

Explanation: CRISPR uses a small RNA molecule to locate a specific sequence of DNA for editing.

33. Which biotech technique involves the use of living organisms or their enzymes to produce biofuels?

  1. Fermentation
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Respiration
  4. Transcription

Answer: Fermentation

Explanation: Fermentation is a biotech technique used to produce biofuels using living organisms or their enzymes.

34. What is the term for the study of the structure, function, and mapping of genomes?

  1. Genomics
  2. Proteomics
  3. Metabolomics
  4. Transcriptomics

Answer: Genomics

Explanation: Genomics is the study of the structure, function, and mapping of genomes.

35. Which of the following is a type of biotech crop that is resistant to a specific pest or disease?

  1. Bt crop
  2. GM crop
  3. Hybrid crop
  4. Organic crop

Answer: Bt crop

Explanation: Bt crop is a type of biotech crop that is resistant to a specific pest.

36. What is the name of the biotech tool that uses a combination of genetic engineering and gene editing to produce novel biological pathways?

  1. Synthetic biology
  2. Systems biology
  3. Genomics
  4. Proteomics

Answer: Synthetic biology

Explanation: Synthetic biology is a biotech tool that uses genetic engineering and gene editing to produce novel biological pathways.

37. Which of the following biotech companies is known for its work on gene editing and CRISPR technology?

  1. Editas Medicine
  2. CRISPR Therapeutics
  3. Intellia Therapeutics
  4. Sangamo Therapeutics

Answer: Editas Medicine

Explanation: Editas Medicine is a biotech company known for its work on gene editing and CRISPR technology.

38. What is the term for the use of biotech to improve human health and quality of life?

  1. Biomedical engineering
  2. Biotechnology
  3. Bioengineering
  4. Medical biotechnology

Answer: Medical biotechnology

Explanation: Medical biotechnology is the use of biotech to improve human health and quality of life.

39. Which of the following is a potential application of biotech in the field of environmental conservation?

  1. Bioremediation
  2. Biofuels
  3. Biomaterials
  4. Bioplastics

Answer: Bioremediation

Explanation: Bioremediation is a potential application of biotech in environmental conservation.

40. Which technology enables targeted genome editing?

  1. CRISPR
  2. Gene therapy
  3. Stem cell therapy
  4. RNA interference

Answer: CRISPR

Explanation: CRISPR enables targeted genome editing with high precision.

41. Which of the following is a biotech application of gene editing?

  1. Cancer treatment
  2. Regenerative medicine
  3. Synthetic biology
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Gene editing has various biotech applications, including cancer treatment, regenerative medicine, and synthetic biology.

42. What is the primary goal of using biotech in space missions?

  1. To develop new vaccines
  2. To study the effects of microgravity on living organisms
  3. To create new bioproducts
  4. To improve crop yields

Answer: To study the effects of microgravity on living organisms

Explanation: The primary goal of using biotech in space missions is to study the effects of microgravity on living organisms.

43. Which biotech tool is used for gene expression analysis?

  1. PCR
  2. Microarray
  3. Gene sequencing
  4. CRISPR

Answer: Microarray

Explanation: Microarray is a biotech tool used for gene expression analysis, allowing for the study of many genes simultaneously.

44. What is the purpose of biotech research on defense missile technology?

  1. To develop new propulsion systems
  2. To create new materials for missile construction
  3. To improve missile guidance systems
  4. To develop biosensors for detecting chemical agents

Answer: To develop biosensors for detecting chemical agents

Explanation: Biotech research on defense missile technology includes developing biosensors for detecting chemical agents.

45. What is the purpose of Golden Rice in biotech?

  1. To increase crop yield
  2. To improve nutritional content
  3. To develop pest-resistant crops
  4. To create drought-tolerant crops

Answer: To improve nutritional content

Explanation: Golden Rice is a type of rice that has been genetically modified to produce beta-carotene, improving its nutritional content. This aims to address vitamin A deficiency in developing countries.

46. What is the name of the first genetically modified organism to be approved for human consumption?

  1. Flavr Savr tomato
  2. Golden Rice
  3. Bt corn
  4. Roundup Ready soybean

Answer: Flavr Savr tomato

Explanation: The Flavr Savr tomato was the first genetically modified organism to be approved for human consumption. It was designed to have a longer shelf life.

47. Which of the following is a potential application of biotech in environmental conservation?

  1. Bioremediation
  2. Biofuels
  3. Biomimicry
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Biotech has various applications in environmental conservation, including bioremediation, biofuels, and biomimicry. These technologies can help address pollution and climate change.

48. What is the name of the international treaty that regulates the use of genetically modified organisms?

  1. Cartagena Protocol
  2. Kyoto Protocol
  3. Paris Agreement
  4. Nagoya Protocol

Answer: Cartagena Protocol

Explanation: The Cartagena Protocol is an international treaty that regulates the use of genetically modified organisms. It aims to ensure their safe handling and transfer.

49. Which of the following biotech techniques involves the use of microorganisms to clean up environmental pollutants?

  1. Bioremediation
  2. Bioaugmentation
  3. Biostimulation
  4. All of the above

Answer: Bioremediation

Explanation: Bioremediation involves the use of microorganisms to clean up environmental pollutants. This technique has been used to address soil and water contamination.

50. What is the purpose of synthetic biology in biotech?

  1. To develop new biofuels
  2. To create novel biomaterials
  3. To design new biological systems
  4. To improve crop yields

Answer: To design new biological systems

Explanation: Synthetic biology involves the design and construction of new biological systems, such as genetic circuits. This field has the potential to revolutionize various industries.
