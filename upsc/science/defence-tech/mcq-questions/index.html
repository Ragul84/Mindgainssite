26. Which space mission demonstrated the capability to destroy satellites in orbit?

  1. Mission Shakti
  2. Chandrayaan-1
  3. Mangalyaan
  4. GSLV Mk III

Answer: Mission Shakti

Explanation: Mission Shakti was a space mission that demonstrated India's capability to destroy satellites in orbit. It was a significant milestone in India's defence and space research.

27. What is the name of the defence missile technology developed by India?

  1. BrahMos
  2. Akash
  3. Nag
  4. Prithvi

Answer: BrahMos

Explanation: BrahMos is a defence missile technology developed by India in collaboration with Russia. It is a supersonic cruise missile with high precision and accuracy.

Answer: COVID-19

Explanation: COVID-19 vaccines are being developed using gene editing technology, such as mRNA and viral vector platforms. These vaccines have shown high efficacy and safety in clinical trials.
