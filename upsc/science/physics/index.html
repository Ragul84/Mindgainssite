Physics MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Physics questions to revise the UPSC Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. The 'Photoelectric Effect' was explained by:

  1. Isaac Newton
  2. Max Planck
  3. Albert Einstein
  4. Niels Bohr

Answer: Albert Einstein

Explanation: Albert Einstein explained the photoelectric effect in 1905 using the concept of light as photons (quanta of energy). This work won him the Nobel Prize in Physics (1921).

2. Newton's First Law of Motion states that:

  1. A body at rest or uniform motion continues unless acted upon by an external force
  2. Force equals mass times acceleration
  3. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction
  4. Momentum is always conserved

Answer: A body at rest or uniform motion continues unless acted upon by an external force

Explanation: Newton's First Law (Law of Inertia): A body continues in its state of rest or of uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force. This is why we feel a jerk when a bus suddenly brakes.

3. Newton's Second Law of Motion (F = ma) means:

  1. Force equals mass multiplied by acceleration
  2. Force is proportional to velocity
  3. Force is inversely proportional to mass
  4. All bodies accelerate at the same rate

Answer: Force equals mass multiplied by acceleration

Explanation: F = ma — the net force acting on a body equals the product of its mass and acceleration. A larger force produces greater acceleration; a larger mass requires more force to achieve the same acceleration.

4. Newton's Third Law of Motion states:

  1. For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction
  2. Objects fall at 9.8 m/s²
  3. F = ma
  4. Momentum is conserved

Answer: For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction

Explanation: Newton's Third Law: For every action (force), there is an equal and opposite reaction. Example: rocket engines push exhaust gas backward (action), gas pushes rocket forward (reaction).

5. The SI unit of force is:

  1. Newton (N)
  2. Dyne
  3. Joule
  4. Pascal

Answer: Newton (N)

Explanation: The SI unit of force is Newton (N). 1 N = 1 kg·m/s². 1 Newton is the force that gives a 1 kg mass an acceleration of 1 m/s². Named after Sir Isaac Newton.
