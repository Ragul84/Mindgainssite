UPSC · Polity · Judicial Review

The 'Basic Structure' doctrine of the Constitution was propounded by the Supreme Court in which case?

Kesavananda Bharati (1973) held that Parliament may amend the Constitution but cannot alter its basic structure. Minerva Mills later reinforced it; Golaknath came before it and was overruled by it.

UPSC FAQs

How is this different from a normal UPSC quiz app?

A quiz app gives you a score. This connects each question back to the concept it tested, shows you the source, and brings the miss back for revision before you forget it.

Does MindGains replace standard UPSC books?

No. It is built on them. Explanations cite NCERT and standard sources so you can go back to the book rather than trusting a paraphrase.

Can I use MindGains for free?

Yes. The quizzes, previous-year questions and the daily current-affairs page are free and need no sign-up.
