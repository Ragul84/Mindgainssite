26. What was the primary material used for making tools during the Neolithic period in India?

  1. Stone
  2. Copper
  3. Bronze
  4. Iron

Answer: Stone

Explanation: The primary material used for making tools during the Neolithic period in India was stone, with the use of polished stone tools.

27. Which of the following is a characteristic of the Neolithic economy in India?

  1. Pastoralism
  2. Agriculture
  3. Hunting
  4. Gathering

Answer: Agriculture

Explanation: The Neolithic economy in India was characterized by the practice of agriculture, with the domestication of plants and animals.

28. What was the name of the prehistoric people who lived in the Indus Valley region?

  1. Harappans
  2. Vedics
  3. Dravidians
  4. Aryans

Answer: Harappans

Explanation: The Harappans were the prehistoric people who lived in the Indus Valley region, known for their urban civilization.

29. In which year was the prehistoric site of Mohenjodaro discovered?

  1. 1921
  2. 1922
  3. 1923
  4. 1924

Answer: 1922

Explanation: The prehistoric site of Mohenjodaro was discovered in 1922 by Daya Ram Sahni.

30. What was the primary crop cultivated by the Neolithic people in India?

  1. Wheat
  2. Barley
  3. Rice
  4. Millets

Answer: Wheat

Explanation: The primary crop cultivated by the Neolithic people in India was wheat, along with other crops like barley.

31. Which of the following prehistoric sites is located in the state of Karnataka?

  1. Brahmagiri
  2. Maski
  3. Kodekal
  4. Paiyampalli

Answer: Brahmagiri

Explanation: Brahmagiri is a prehistoric site located in the state of Karnataka, known for its megalithic structures.

32. What was the primary source of food for the people of the Mesolithic period in India?

  1. Hunting
  2. Fishing
  3. Agriculture
  4. Gathering

Answer: Hunting

Explanation: The Mesolithic period in India was characterized by a primary source of food as hunting, with evidence of hunting tools found at various sites. This period saw the beginning of settled life.

33. Which of the following prehistoric sites is known for its cave paintings?

  1. Bhimbetka
  2. Dholavira
  3. Kalibangan
  4. Lothal

Answer: Bhimbetka

Explanation: Bhimbetka is a prehistoric site known for its cave paintings, depicting the lives of early humans. These paintings are a significant part of India's cultural heritage.

34. What was the name of the tool-making technique used by early humans during the Paleolithic period?

  1. Levallois
  2. Core
  3. Flake
  4. Blade

Answer: Levallois

Explanation: The Levallois technique was a method of tool-making used by early humans, characterized by the production of flake tools. This technique marked a significant development in human technology.

35. Which prehistoric period saw the emergence of settled agriculture in India?

  1. Neolithic
  2. Mesolithic
  3. Paleolithic
  4. Chalcolithic

Answer: Neolithic

Explanation: The Neolithic period saw the emergence of settled agriculture in India, with the domestication of plants and animals. This marked a significant shift in human subsistence strategies.

36. What was the name of the ancient Indian philosopher who lived during the prehistoric period?

  1. Mahavira
  2. Gautama
  3. Kanada
  4. None

Answer: None

Explanation: There is no recorded ancient Indian philosopher from the prehistoric period, as this period predates written records. The prehistoric period is characterized by a lack of written evidence.

37. Which of the following terms refers to a prehistoric administrative unit?

  1. Gram
  2. Janapada
  3. Mandal
  4. None

Answer: Janapada

Explanation: The term Janapada refers to a prehistoric administrative unit, characterized by a tribal or clan-based system of governance. This system was prevalent during the Vedic period.

38. What was the primary material used for tool-making during the Paleolithic period?

  1. Stone
  2. Wood
  3. Bone
  4. Metal

Answer: Stone

Explanation: The primary material used for tool-making during the Paleolithic period was stone, with tools made from flint, quartz, and other types of stone. This marked the beginning of human tool use.

39. Which prehistoric site is known for its evidence of early human habitation?

  1. Mehrgarh
  2. Harappa
  3. Mohenjo-Daro
  4. Dholavira

Answer: Mehrgarh

Explanation: Mehrgarh is a prehistoric site known for its evidence of early human habitation, with remains dating back to the Neolithic period. This site provides valuable insights into early human settlement patterns.

40. What was the name of the prehistoric treaty that marked the beginning of the end of the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Treaty of Westphalia
  2. Treaty of Versailles
  3. None
  4. Indus-Saraswati

Answer: None

Explanation: There is no recorded prehistoric treaty that marked the beginning of the end of the Indus Valley Civilization, as this period predates written records. The decline of the Indus Valley Civilization is still a topic of debate among scholars.

41. Which of the following prehistoric philosophies emphasized the importance of non-violence?

  1. Jainism
  2. Buddhism
  3. Hinduism
  4. None

Answer: Jainism

Explanation: Jainism is a prehistoric philosophy that emphasized the importance of non-violence, with a focus on ahimsa or non-harming. This philosophy emerged during the 6th century BCE.

42. Which of the following prehistoric sites in India is known for its cave paintings?

  1. Bhimbetka
  2. Dholavira
  3. Lothal
  4. Kalibangan

Answer: Bhimbetka

Explanation: Bhimbetka is a prehistoric site in India known for its cave paintings, which depict the lives of early humans. The site has over 700 rock shelters, with many containing ancient paintings.

43. What was the name of the tool-making technique used during the Paleolithic period in India?

  1. Levallois
  2. Core
  3. Flake
  4. Blade

Answer: Levallois

Explanation: The Levallois technique was a tool-making method used during the Paleolithic period, characterized by the removal of flakes from a core to create a desired shape. This technique was used to create a variety of stone tools.

44. Which prehistoric period in India saw the emergence of settled agriculture?

  1. Neolithic
  2. Mesolithic
  3. Paleolithic
  4. Chalcolithic

Answer: Neolithic

Explanation: The Neolithic period in India saw the emergence of settled agriculture, with people domesticating plants and animals. This period also saw the development of more complex societies and the use of pottery.

45. What was the name of the ancient Indian philosopher who wrote about the importance of non-violence during the prehistoric period?

  1. Mahavira
  2. Gautama
  3. Kanada
  4. Patanjali

Answer: Mahavira

Explanation: Mahavira was an ancient Indian philosopher who emphasized the importance of non-violence and compassion. He founded the Jain tradition, which emphasizes non-violence and self-control.

46. Which of the following prehistoric sites in India is known for its well-planned urban layout?

  1. Dholavira
  2. Lothal
  3. Kalibangan
  4. Rakhigarhi

Answer: Dholavira

Explanation: Dholavira is a prehistoric site in India known for its well-planned urban layout, with advanced drainage systems and architecture. The site was an important urban center during the Indus Valley Civilization.

47. What was the primary material used for tool-making during the Paleolithic period in India?

  1. Stone
  2. Wood
  3. Bone
  4. Metal

Answer: Stone

Explanation: Stone was the primary material used for tool-making during the Paleolithic period in India, with early humans using stones to create a variety of tools. The use of stone tools characterized this period.

48. Which prehistoric period in India saw the emergence of microliths?

  1. Mesolithic
  2. Neolithic
  3. Paleolithic
  4. Chalcolithic

Answer: Mesolithic

Explanation: The Mesolithic period in India saw the emergence of microliths, which were small, geometric stone tools. This period was characterized by a nomadic lifestyle and the use of microliths.

49. What was the name of the ancient Indian socio-religious reform association that emerged during the prehistoric period?

  1. Arya Samaj
  2. Brahmo Samaj
  3. Ramakrishna Mission
  4. Jain Sangh

Answer: Jain Sangh

Explanation: The Jain Sangh was an ancient Indian socio-religious reform association that emerged during the prehistoric period, emphasizing non-violence and self-control. It was founded by Mahavira and emphasized the importance of compassion and non-violence.

50. Which of the following prehistoric sites in India is known for its advanced irrigation systems?

  1. Dholavira
  2. Lothal
  3. Kalibangan
  4. Rakhigarhi

Answer: Kalibangan

Explanation: Kalibangan is a prehistoric site in India known for its advanced irrigation systems, with evidence of sophisticated water management. The site was an important urban center during the Indus Valley Civilization.
