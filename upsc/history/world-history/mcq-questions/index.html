26. The Mughal administrative unit 'suba' was headed by a

  1. Amil
  2. Muqaddam
  3. Subedar
  4. Diwan

Answer: Subedar

Explanation: Subedar was the head of a Mughal suba, Amil and Muqaddam were lower administrative officials, Diwan was a revenue official.

27. The 1905 partition of Bengal was annulled in

  1. 1911
  2. 1912
  3. 1915
  4. 1917

Answer: 1911

Explanation: The partition of Bengal was annulled in 1911 due to strong opposition.

28. The 'muqaddam' was a

  1. Village headman
  2. Revenue official
  3. Military officer
  4. Judicial officer

Answer: Village headman

Explanation: Muqaddam was the headman of a village in medieval India.

29. The 'Arya Samaj' was founded by

  1. Dayananda Saraswati
  2. Ram Mohan Roy
  3. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
  4. Keshab Chandra Sen

Answer: Dayananda Saraswati

Explanation: Arya Samaj was founded by Dayananda Saraswati in 1875.

30. The 'Regulating Act' of 1773 was passed to regulate the affairs of the

  1. East India Company
  2. British Crown
  3. Indian Princely States
  4. Mughal Empire

Answer: East India Company

Explanation: Regulating Act of 1773 was passed to regulate the East India Company.

31. The 'Khilafat Movement' was led by

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  3. Maulana Azad

32. The 'Treaty of Lahore' was signed in

  1. 1845
  2. 1846
  3. 1849
  4. 1850

Answer: 1846

Explanation: Treaty of Lahore was signed in 1846 after the First Anglo-Sikh War.

33. The 'Sufi' movement in India was influenced by

  1. Islam

  4. Christianity

Explanation: Sufi movement in India was influenced by Islamic mysticism.

34. The 'Jallianwala Bagh Massacre' occurred on

  1. 13 April 1918
  2. 13 April 1919
  3. 13 May 1919
  4. 13 June 1919

Answer: 13 April 1919

Explanation: Jallianwala Bagh Massacre occurred on 13 April 1919.

35. The term 'muqaddam' refers to a medieval administrative position in India, specifically a

  1. Village headman
  2. Provincial governor
  3. Revenue collector
  4. Military commander

Answer: Village headman

Explanation: Muqaddam was a village headman in medieval India, responsible for local administration.

36. The 'Bhakti movement' in India was characterized by

  1. Devotion to a personal deity
  2. Rejection of caste system
  3. Focus on ritualistic practices
  4. Emphasis on asceticism

Answer: Devotion to a personal deity

Explanation: Bhakti movement emphasized devotion to a personal deity and spiritual growth.

37. The 'Regulating Act of 1773' was a significant legislative act in Indian history, as it

  1. Established the British East India Company's monopoly
  2. Created the office of the Governor-General
  3. Abolished the Mughal Empire
  4. Introduced the concept of zemindari

Answer: Created the office of the Governor-General

Explanation: The Regulating Act of 1773 created the office of the Governor-General and established British parliamentary control over India.

38. The 'Swadeshi movement' was a phase of the Indian freedom struggle, marked by

  1. Non-cooperation and civil disobedience
  2. Boycott of British goods
  3. Armed rebellion against the British
  4. Negotiations with the British government

Answer: Boycott of British goods

Explanation: The Swadeshi movement involved the boycott of British goods and promotion of indigenous industries.

39. The 'Amil' was a medieval administrative term in India, referring to a

  1. Revenue collector
  2. Provincial governor
  3. Village headman
  4. Military commander

Answer: Revenue collector

Explanation: Amil was a revenue collector in medieval India, responsible for tax collection.

40. The 'Arya Samaj' was a socio-religious reform association in India, founded by

  1. Dayananda Saraswati
  2. Ram Mohan Roy
  3. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
  4. Keshab Chandra Sen

Answer: Dayananda Saraswati

Explanation: Arya Samaj was founded by Dayananda Saraswati to promote social and religious reform.

  4. 1784

Answer: 1765

Explanation: The Treaty of Allahabad was signed in 1765, marking a significant event in Indian history.

42. The 'Mughal architectural style' is characterized by

  1. Use of arches and domes
  2. Intricate carvings and sculptures
  3. Use of bright colors and ornate decorations
  4. Emphasis on simplicity and minimalism

Answer: Use of arches and domes

Explanation: Mughal architectural style is known for its use of arches and domes, as seen in iconic buildings like the Taj Mahal.

43. The 'Ryotwari system' was a land revenue system introduced in India by

  1. Lord Cornwallis
  2. Lord Wellesley
  3. Thomas Munro
  4. Warren Hastings

Answer: Thomas Munro

Explanation: The Ryotwari system was introduced by Thomas Munro, which fixed revenue directly with the cultivator.

  4. Salt Satyagraha

  1. Amil
  2. Muqaddam
  3. Faujdar
  4. Diwan

Explanation: Amil was the Mughal administrative unit head for revenue collection. This position played a crucial role in the Mughal Empire's financial administration.
