Post 1947 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Post 1947 questions to revise the UPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. The States Reorganization Act (1956) reorganized states primarily on the basis of:

  1. Language
  2. Religion
  3. Geography
  4. Population

Answer: Language

Explanation: States Reorganization Act 1956, based on the Fazl Ali Commission report, reorganized Indian states primarily on linguistic basis, creating 14 states and 6 Union Territories.

2. The First Five-Year Plan of India focused on:

  1. Agriculture and infrastructure
  2. Industrialization
  3. Defense
  4. Education

Answer: Agriculture and infrastructure

Explanation: India's First Five-Year Plan (1951-56) was based on the Harrod-Domar model and focused on agriculture, irrigation, power, and rehabilitation after partition.

3. The first Five-Year Plan in India was launched in which year?

  1. 1951
  2. 1956
  3. 1961
  4. 1966

Answer: 1951

Explanation: The first Five-Year Plan was launched in 1951, focusing on agricultural development.

4. The Indian National Congress split into two factions in which year?

  1. 1967
  2. 1969
  3. 1971
  4. 1975

Answer: 1969

Explanation: The Indian National Congress split in 1969 due to internal disagreements.

5. The 42nd Amendment to the Indian Constitution was enacted in which year?

  1. 1975
  2. 1976
  3. 1977
  4. 1978

Answer: 1976

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment was enacted in 1976, introducing significant changes.

6. The Janata Party came to power in which year?

  1. 1975
  2. 1977
  3. 1980
  4. 1984

Answer: 1977

Explanation: The Janata Party came to power in 1977, ending Congress rule.

7. The National Emergency in India was declared in which year?

  1. 1975
  2. 1976
  3. 1977
  4. 1980

Answer: 1975

Explanation: The National Emergency was declared in 1975, suspending civil liberties.

8. The Mandal Commission submitted its report in which year?

  1. 1979
  2. 1980
  3. 1981
  4. 1982

Answer: 1980

Explanation: The Mandal Commission submitted its report in 1980, recommending reservations.

9. The Indian government introduced the New Economic Policy in which year?

  1. 1990
  2. 1991
  3. 1992
  4. 1993

Answer: 1991

Explanation: The New Economic Policy was introduced in 1991, liberalizing the economy.

10. The Bharatiya Janata Party won the general election in which year?

  1. 1996
  2. 1998
  3. 1999
  4. 2000

Answer: 1998

Explanation: The BJP won the general election in 1998, forming a coalition government.

11. The National Democratic Alliance came to power in which year?

  1. 1998
  2. 1999
  3. 2000
  4. 2001

Answer: 1998

Explanation: The NDA came to power in 1998, led by the BJP.

12. The Indian government implemented the Right to Information Act in which year?

  1. 2004
  2. 2005
  3. 2006
  4. 2007

Answer: 2005

Explanation: The Right to Information Act was implemented in 2005, promoting transparency.

13. Who was the chief architect of the Indian Constitution?

  1. B.R. Ambedkar
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Subhas Chandra Bose

Answer: B.R. Ambedkar

Explanation: B.R. Ambedkar played a key role in drafting the Indian Constitution.

14. The Indian government adopted the policy of Non-Alignment during which period?

  1. 1947-1952
  2. 1952-1962
  3. 1962-1972
  4. 1972-1982

Answer: 1952-1962

Explanation: India adopted the policy of Non-Alignment during the Cold War era.

15. The Green Revolution in India was led by which scientist?

  1. M.S. Swaminathan
  2. J.C. Kumarappa
  3. Verrier Elwin
  4. Norman Borlaug

Answer: M.S. Swaminathan

Explanation: M.S. Swaminathan was a key figure in introducing high-yielding wheat varieties.

16. The Treaty of Peace and Friendship between India and Nepal was signed in which year?

  1. 1950
  2. 1955
  3. 1960
  4. 1965

Answer: 1950

Explanation: The treaty established a close relationship between the two countries.

17. The first nuclear test by India was conducted in which year?

  1. 1972
  2. 1974
  3. 1976
  4. 1978

Answer: 1974

Explanation: The test marked a significant milestone in India's nuclear program.

18. The Indian government launched the Integrated Rural Development Programme in which year?

  1. 1978
  2. 1980
  3. 1982
  4. 1985

Answer: 1978

Explanation: The program aimed to improve rural development and poverty alleviation.

19. The Indian government adopted the policy of Non-Alignment during the Cold War under the leadership of

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Indira Gandhi
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Subhas Chandra Bose

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru

Explanation: Jawaharlal Nehru played a key role in adopting the policy of Non-Alignment.

20. The Bharat Ratna award was instituted in which year?

  1. 1952
  2. 1954
  3. 1955
  4. 1956

Answer: 1955

Explanation: The Bharat Ratna award was instituted in 1954, as the highest civilian award in India.

21. The Green Revolution in India was led by

  1. M.S. Swaminathan
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Indira Gandhi
  4. C. Subramaniam

Explanation: M.S. Swaminathan played a key role in leading the Green Revolution in India.

  1. 1967
  2. 1969
  3. 1971
  4. 1973

  1. 1971
  2. 1972
  3. 1973
  4. 1974

Answer: 1972

  1. 1990
  2. 1991
  3. 1992
  4. 1993

  3. 1977
  4. 1978

Answer: 1975
