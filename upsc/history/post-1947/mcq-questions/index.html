26. The Indian government adopted the policy of Non-Alignment during the Cold War under which prime minister?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Lal Bahadur Shastri
  3. Indira Gandhi
  4. Morarji Desai

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru

Explanation: Jawaharlal Nehru played a key role in the Non-Alignment Movement.

27. The 42nd Amendment to the Indian Constitution was passed in which year?

  1. 1976
  2. 1975
  3. 1976
  4. 1974

Answer: 1976

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment was passed in 1976, making significant changes to the Constitution.

28. The Treaty of Peace, Friendship, and Cooperation was signed between India and which country in 1971?

  1. USSR
  2. USA
  3. China
  4. Pakistan

Answer: USSR

Explanation: The Treaty of Peace, Friendship, and Cooperation was signed between India and the USSR in 1971.

29. The Green Revolution in India began in which year?

  1. 1965
  2. 1966
  3. 1967
  4. 1968

Answer: 1965

Explanation: The Green Revolution started in 1965, introducing high-yielding wheat varieties.

30. The National Emergency was imposed in India in which year?

  1. 1975
  2. 1976
  3. 1977
  4. 1974

Answer: 1975

Explanation: The National Emergency was imposed in 1975, suspending civil liberties.

31. The Janata Party came to power in India in which year?

  1. 1977
  2. 1978
  3. 1979
  4. 1980

Answer: 1977

Explanation: The Janata Party formed the government in 1977, ending Congress rule.

32. The Indian government launched the Jawahar Rozgar Yojana in which year?

  1. 1989
  2. 1990
  3. 1988
  4. 1991

Answer: 1989

Explanation: The Jawahar Rozgar Yojana was launched in 1989, aiming at employment generation.

33. What was the main objective of the Zamindari Abolition Act of 1950?

  1. Land Reform
  2. Taxation
  3. Industrialization
  4. Urbanization

Answer: Land Reform

Explanation: The Zamindari Abolition Act aimed to reform land ownership and reduce poverty among farmers by abolishing the zamindari system. It was a key step in post-1947 land reform.

34. Who was the key figure behind the establishment of the Indian National Congress's socio-religious reform association, the Servants of India Society?

  1. Gopal Krishna Gokhale
  2. Lala Lajpat Rai
  3. Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  4. Dadabhai Naoroji

Answer: Gopal Krishna Gokhale

Explanation: Gopal Krishna Gokhale founded the Servants of India Society in 1905 to promote social and political reform. The society played a significant role in India's freedom struggle.

35. What was the significance of the Five-Year Plans introduced in 1951?

  1. Rapid Industrialization
  2. Agricultural Development
  3. Social Reform
  4. Economic Growth

Answer: Economic Growth

Explanation: The Five-Year Plans were a series of nation-wide centralized exercises in economic planning introduced in 1951 to promote economic growth and development. They played a crucial role in shaping India's economy.

36. Which architectural feature is characteristic of the post-1947 planning and development of Chandigarh?

  1. Mughal Style
  2. Art Deco
  3. Brutalist Architecture
  4. Gothic Style

Answer: Brutalist Architecture

Explanation: Chandigarh's architecture is characterized by Brutalist Architecture, which emphasizes functionality and simplicity. The city was designed by Le Corbusier and Pierre Jeanneret.

37. What was the primary goal of the Community Development Programme launched in 1952?

  1. Rural Development
  2. Urban Planning
  3. Industrial Growth
  4. Social Welfare

Answer: Rural Development

Explanation: The Community Development Programme aimed to promote rural development and improve the standard of living of rural communities. It was a key initiative in post-1947 India.

38. Who was the key proponent of the philosophy of Integral Humanism, which influenced India's post-1947 development?

  1. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya
  2. Jayaprakash Narayan
  3. Ram Manohar Lohia
  4. Nehru

Answer: Deen Dayal Upadhyaya

Explanation: Deen Dayal Upadhyaya was the key proponent of Integral Humanism, which emphasized the integration of India's social, economic, and cultural development. His philosophy influenced India's post-1947 development.

39. What was the significance of the Hindu Code Bills passed between 1955 and 1961?

  1. Social Reform
  2. Economic Growth
  3. Political Stability
  4. Cultural Development

Answer: Social Reform

Explanation: The Hindu Code Bills aimed to reform Hindu personal law and promote social reform, particularly women's rights. They were a significant step in post-1947 India's social development.

40. Which administrative term refers to the district-level revenue officer in post-1947 India?

  1. Tehsildar
  2. Collector
  3. Magistrate
  4. SP

Answer: Tehsildar

Explanation: The Tehsildar is the district-level revenue officer responsible for collecting revenue and maintaining land records. The term is still used in India today.

43. What was the main objective of the Community Development Programme launched in 1952?

  1. Poverty Alleviation
  2. Rural Development
  3. Industrial Growth
  4. Urban Planning

Answer: Rural Development

Explanation: The Community Development Programme aimed at rural development through community participation. It focused on improving rural infrastructure and services.

44. Which act abolished the zamindari system in India?

  2. Land Reform Act
  3. Agricultural Produce Market Act
  4. Tenancy Act

Explanation: The Zamindari Abolition Act abolished the zamindari system, aiming to reduce exploitation of farmers.

  2. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya

  2. Second Five-Year Plan

  4. Fourth Five-Year Plan

Explanation: The First Five-Year Plan focused on agricultural development and rehabilitation of refugees.

47. Which of the following was a key feature of the Indian Constitution enacted in 1950?

  2. Parliamentary System
  3. Socialist Principles
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Indian Constitution adopted a federal system, parliamentary system, and incorporated socialist principles.

48. Who was the first Chairman of the Planning Commission of India?

49. What was the main goal of the Bhoodan Movement started by Vinoba Bhave in 1951?

  2. Social Equality
  3. Economic Empowerment
  4. Caste Abolition

Explanation: The Bhoodan Movement aimed at land reform through voluntary donation of land to the poor.
