  2. Civil Disobedience Movement

Answer: None

17. Which of the following was a notable feature of Indus Valley architecture?

  1. Use of arches
  2. Use of domes
  3. Standardized bricks
  4. Minarets

Answer: Standardized bricks

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is known for its well-planned cities with advanced drainage systems and standardized bricks.

18. What was the name of the major city in the Indus Valley Civilization known for its sophisticated drainage system?

  1. Mohenjo-Daro
  2. Harappa
  3. Lothal
  4. Kalibangan

Answer: Mohenjo-Daro

Explanation: Mohenjo-Daro is one of the most famous cities of the Indus Valley Civilization, known for its advanced urban planning and drainage system.

19. Which of the following terms is associated with the administrative system of the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Amil
  2. Muqaddam
  3. Granary
  4. Guild

Answer: Granary

Explanation: The granary was an important administrative feature in the Indus Valley Civilization, used for storing grains.

20. What was the primary material used for making seals in the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Copper
  2. Steatite
  3. Gold
  4. Silver

Answer: Steatite

Explanation: Steatite was the primary material used for making seals in the Indus Valley Civilization, which were used for trade and identification purposes.

21. Which of the following was a major crop cultivated in the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Wheat
  2. Barley
  3. Rice
  4. Sugarcane

Answer: Wheat

Explanation: Wheat was one of the major crops cultivated in the Indus Valley Civilization, which supported the civilization's food needs.

22. What was the name of the famous Indus Valley site known for its dockyard?

  1. Lothal
  2. Mohenjo-Daro
  3. Harappa
  4. Kalibangan

Answer: Lothal

Explanation: Lothal is a major site of the Indus Valley Civilization, known for its dockyard and maritime trade.

23. Which of the following animals was revered in the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Bull
  2. Elephant
  3. Tiger
  4. Rhino

Answer: Bull

Explanation: The bull was an important animal in the Indus Valley Civilization, often depicted in seals and artwork.

24. What was the approximate time period of the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. 2500-1900 BCE
  2. 2000-1500 BCE
  3. 1500-1000 BCE
  4. 1000-500 BCE

Answer: 2500-1900 BCE

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization flourished around 2500-1900 BCE, which is considered one of the earliest urban civilizations.

  2. Class system
  3. Matriarchal society
  4. Patriarchal society
