26. What was the primary occupation of the people in the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Agriculture
  2. Trade
  3. Pastoralism
  4. Hunting

Answer: Agriculture

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization was primarily an agricultural society, with evidence of sophisticated irrigation systems and crop management.

27. Which of the following was a key feature of the Indus Valley Civilization's urban planning?

  1. Fortified cities
  2. Public baths
  3. Marketplaces
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is known for its well-planned cities, which included features like fortified cities, public baths, and marketplaces.

28. What was the name of the famous Indus Valley Civilization site discovered in 1921?

  1. Mohenjo-Daro
  2. Harappa
  3. Lothal
  4. Kalibangan

Answer: Mohenjo-Daro

Explanation: Mohenjo-Daro was one of the first Indus Valley Civilization sites to be discovered in 1921.

29. Which of the following administrative terms was used in the medieval period to refer to a revenue official?

  1. Amil
  2. Muqaddam
  3. Qanungo
  4. Patwari

Answer: Amil

Explanation: Amil was a medieval administrative term used to refer to a revenue official.

30. The Indus Valley Civilization is believed to have flourished around which of the following time periods?

  1. 2500 BCE - 1900 BCE
  2. 1500 BCE - 1000 BCE
  3. 1000 BCE - 500 BCE
  4. 500 BCE - 100 CE

Answer: 2500 BCE - 1900 BCE

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is believed to have flourished around 2500 BCE - 1900 BCE.

31. Which of the following philosophies is believed to have originated in the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Hinduism
  2. Buddhism
  3. Jainism
  4. None of the above

Answer: Jainism

Explanation: Jainism is believed to have originated in the Indus Valley Civilization, with evidence of Jain symbols and motifs found at various sites.

32. What was the name of the treaty signed between the British East India Company and the Indus Valley region's rulers in 1832?

  1. Treaty of Lahore
  2. Treaty of Amritsar
  3. Treaty of Indus
  4. No treaty was signed

Answer: Treaty of Lahore

Explanation: The Treaty of Lahore was signed in 1832 between the British East India Company and the rulers of the Indus Valley region.

33. Which of the following architectural features is characteristic of the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Use of arches
  2. Use of domes
  3. Use of columns
  4. Use of drains

Answer: Use of drains

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is known for its sophisticated drainage system, with evidence of well-planned drains and sewage systems.

34. The Indus Valley Civilization is believed to have been influenced by which of the following socio-religious reform associations?

  4. None of the above

Answer: None of the above

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is not believed to have been directly influenced by any of the above socio-religious reform associations, as they emerged much later.
