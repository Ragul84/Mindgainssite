26. What was the significance of the Gandhi-Irwin Pact of 1931?

  1. End of British Rule
  2. Release of Prisoners
  3. Abolition of Salt Tax
  4. Communal Award

Answer: Release of Prisoners

Explanation: The Gandhi-Irwin Pact led to the release of prisoners. It was a key event in the Indian freedom struggle.

27. Which of the following was a key feature of the Gandhian philosophy?

  1. Non-Violence
  2. Civil Disobedience
  3. Socialism
  4. Nationalism

Answer: Non-Violence

Explanation: Non-violence was a key feature. It was central to Gandhi's philosophy and movement.

28. Who was the leader of the Khilafat Movement that was closely associated with the Non-Cooperation Movement?

  1. Maulana Azad
  2. Maulana Shaukat Ali
  3. Maulana Muhammad Ali
  4. Hakim Ajmal Khan

Answer: Maulana Muhammad Ali

Explanation: Maulana Muhammad Ali was a key leader. He played a significant role in the Khilafat Movement.

29. What was the main goal of the Quit India Movement launched by Gandhi in 1942?

  1. Complete Independence
  2. Dominion Status
  3. Constitutional Reforms
  4. Economic Development

Answer: Complete Independence

Explanation: The Quit India Movement aimed at complete independence. It was a key phase in the Indian freedom struggle.

30. Which of the following was a key consequence of the Quit India Movement?

  1. Immediate Independence
  2. Mass Arrests
  3. Widespread Violence
  4. Constitutional Reforms

Answer: Mass Arrests

Explanation: The Quit India Movement led to mass arrests. It was a key event in the Indian freedom struggle.

31. Which of the following acts was passed by the British government in response to the growing nationalist movement in India during the Gandhi era?

  1. Rowlatt Act
  2. Government of India Act
  3. Indian Councils Act
  4. Public Safety Act

Answer: Rowlatt Act

Explanation: The Rowlatt Act was a repressive legislation that allowed for the detention of individuals without trial, prompting widespread protests.

32. What was the name of the ashram founded by Gandhi in 1915 in Ahmedabad?

  1. Sabarmati Ashram
  2. Sevagram Ashram
  3. Wardha Ashram
  4. Phoenix Settlement

Answer: Sabarmati Ashram

Explanation: Sabarmati Ashram was a key center for Gandhi's activities and a symbol of his commitment to simple living and self-sufficiency.

33. Who was the British Viceroy who negotiated the Gandhi-Irwin Pact in 1931?

  1. Lord Irwin
  2. Lord Linlithgow
  3. Lord Wavell
  4. Lord Mountbatten

Answer: Lord Irwin

Explanation: The Gandhi-Irwin Pact marked a significant turning point in the Indian freedom struggle, with Gandhi agreeing to call off the Civil Disobedience Movement.

34. What was the term used by Gandhi to describe the poverty and backwardness of Indian villages?

  1. Daridranarayan
  2. Garibi Hatao
  3. Swadeshi
  4. Sarvodaya

Answer: Daridranarayan

Explanation: Daridranarayan referred to the impoverished and marginalized sections of Indian society, whom Gandhi sought to uplift through his constructive programs.

35. Which of the following was a key feature of the Quit India Movement launched by Gandhi in 1942?

  1. Non-violent resistance
  2. Armed struggle
  3. Civil disobedience
  4. Constitutional reforms

Answer: Non-violent resistance

Explanation: The Quit India Movement was marked by widespread violence and unrest, but Gandhi's commitment to non-violent resistance remained unwavering.

36. What was the name of the organization founded by Gandhi in 1921 to promote Hindu-Muslim unity?

  1. Khilafat Committee
  2. Hindu-Muslim Unity Board
  3. Servants of India Society
  4. Indian National Congress

Answer: Khilafat Committee

Explanation: The Khilafat Committee was a key platform for Gandhi's efforts to promote inter-communal harmony and cooperation.

37. Who was the British politician who met Gandhi in London in 1931 to discuss Indian constitutional reforms?

  1. Winston Churchill
  2. Ramsay MacDonald
  3. David Lloyd George
  4. Stanley Baldwin

Answer: Ramsay MacDonald

Explanation: The meeting between Gandhi and MacDonald marked a significant moment in the Indian freedom struggle, with Gandhi advocating for greater autonomy and self-rule.

38. What was the term used by Gandhi to describe his vision of a self-sufficient and decentralized Indian society?

  1. Ram Rajya
  2. Sarvodaya
  3. Swadeshi
  4. Panchayati Raj

Answer: Sarvodaya

Explanation: Sarvodaya referred to Gandhi's ideal of a society in which everyone had the opportunity to develop their full potential and contribute to the common good.

39. Which of the following was a key aspect of Gandhi's constructive programs during the 1920s and 1930s?

  1. Spinning and weaving
  2. Agricultural reform
  3. Education and literacy
  4. Health and sanitation

Answer: Spinning and weaving

Explanation: Gandhi's emphasis on spinning and weaving was part of his broader effort to promote self-sufficiency and economic development in Indian villages.

40. Who was the founder of the Servants of India Society, an organization that influenced Gandhi's thoughts on social service?

  1. Gopal Krishna Gokhale
  2. Lala Lajpat Rai
  3. Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  4. Bipin Chandra Pal

41. What was the term used by Gandhi to describe his vision of a self-sufficient Indian village?

  1. Ram Rajya

  4. Hind Swaraj

Answer: Gram Swaraj

Explanation: Gram Swaraj referred to Gandhi's ideal of village self-governance. It was a key concept in his vision for India's future.

44. Which of the following was a major consequence of the Quit India Movement launched by Gandhi in 1942?

  1. Immediate independence for India
  2. Mass arrests of nationalist leaders
  3. Widespread communal riots
  4. Economic crisis in India

Answer: Mass arrests of nationalist leaders

Explanation: The Quit India Movement led to mass arrests. It was a major challenge to British authority.

45. What was the term used by Gandhi to describe his method of non-violent resistance?

  1. Satyagraha
  2. Ahimsa
  3. Civil Disobedience
  4. Non-Cooperation

Answer: Satyagraha

Explanation: Satyagraha was a key concept in Gandhi's philosophy. It referred to non-violent resistance.

46. Who was the founder of the Servants of India Society, an organization that aimed to promote social and economic reform in India?

  2. Delhi Pact
  3. Lucknow Pact
  4. Simla Agreement

Answer: Gandhi-Irwin Pact

Explanation: The Gandhi-Irwin Pact was a treaty that granted some concessions to the Congress, including the release of political prisoners.

48. Which of the following philosophies was a key influence on Gandhi's thought and activism?

  1. Ahimsa
  2. Satyagraha
  3. Swaraj
  4. Socialism

Answer: Ahimsa

Explanation: Ahimsa, or non-violence, was a core principle of Gandhi's philosophy and guided his approach to social and political change.

49. What was the name of the movement launched by Gandhi in 1942, which called for an end to British rule in India?

  1. Quit India Movement
  2. Non-Cooperation Movement
  3. Civil Disobedience Movement
  4. Salt Satyagraha

Answer: Quit India Movement

Explanation: The Quit India Movement was a mass movement that called for an end to British rule in India and marked a significant turning point in the freedom struggle.
