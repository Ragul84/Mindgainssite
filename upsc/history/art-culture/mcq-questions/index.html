Art Culture MCQs with answers

26. The 'khutba' was a

  1. Friday sermon
  2. Royal decree
  3. Administrative order
  4. Military command

Answer: Friday sermon

Explanation: Khutba was a Friday sermon in a mosque. It was an important part of Islamic worship in medieval India.

27. The 'qanungo' was a

  1. Revenue official
  2. Military officer
  3. Administrative officer
  4. Judicial officer

Answer: Revenue official

Explanation: Qanungo was a revenue official in medieval India. The qanungo was responsible for maintaining revenue records.

28. The 'sufi' movement in India was influenced by

  1. Islam
  2. Sikhism
  3. Hinduism
  4. Buddhism

Answer: Islam

Explanation: The Sufi movement in India was influenced by Islam. It emphasized spiritual devotion and personal connection with God.

29. The Mughal administrative unit 'suba' was headed by a

  1. Amil
  2. Muqaddam
  3. Subedar
  4. Diwan

Answer: Subedar

Explanation: Subedar was the head of a Mughal suba, responsible for its administration and governance. This position was crucial in the Mughal Empire's administrative structure.

30. The Bhakti movement in India emphasized

  1. Orthodox Hinduism
  2. Bhakti or devotion
  3. Sufism
  4. Tantrism

Answer: Bhakti or devotion

Explanation: The Bhakti movement focused on personal devotion and love for a deity, rejecting ritualistic and orthodox practices. It played a significant role in Indian religious and social history.

31. The 'muqaddam' in medieval India referred to a

  1. Village headman
  2. Provincial governor
  3. Revenue official
  4. Military commander

Answer: Village headman

Explanation: A muqaddam was the headman of a village in medieval India, responsible for local administration and revenue collection. This position was vital in the rural administrative setup.

32. The Vijayanagara Empire's architectural style is characterized by

  1. Dravidian
  2. Nagara
  3. Vesara
  4. Indo-Saracenic

Answer: Dravidian

Explanation: The Dravidian style is a prominent feature of the Vijayanagara Empire's architecture, marked by large temples and intricate carvings. This style reflects the empire's rich cultural heritage.

33. The 'amil' in the Mughal administration was responsible for

  1. Revenue collection
  2. Military affairs
  3. Judicial matters
  4. Foreign policy

Answer: Revenue collection

Explanation: An amil was a revenue official in the Mughal Empire, tasked with collecting taxes and managing local revenue administration. This role was essential for the empire's financial stability.

34. The Rama Janmabhoomi movement is associated with the

  1. Babri Masjid
  2. Somnath Temple
  3. Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  4. Ayodhya Temple

Answer: Babri Masjid

Explanation: The Rama Janmabhoomi movement revolves around the disputed site of the Babri Masjid in Ayodhya, which is believed to be the birthplace of Lord Rama. This movement has been a significant factor in Indian politics and social discourse.

35. The 'Diwan' in the Mughal administration referred to a

  1. Finance minister
  2. Revenue official
  3. Military commander
  4. Provincial governor

Answer: Finance minister

Explanation: A Diwan was the finance minister or chief revenue officer in the Mughal Empire, responsible for managing the empire's finances and revenue policies. This position played a crucial role in the empire's economic administration.

36. The 'Sufi' movement in India emphasized

  1. Devotion and love
  2. Orthodox Islam
  3. Mysticism and asceticism
  4. Social reform

Answer: Devotion and love

Explanation: The Sufi movement in India focused on personal devotion, love, and spiritual growth, often through mystical practices and poetry. It has contributed significantly to Indian cultural and literary heritage.

37. The 'Red Fort' in Delhi was built by

  1. Shah Jahan
  2. Aurangzeb
  3. Akbar
  4. Jahangir

Answer: Shah Jahan

Explanation: The Red Fort in Delhi was constructed by Shah Jahan in the 17th century, serving as the imperial palace of the Mughal Empire. It is a testament to Mughal architectural grandeur and a symbol of Indian history.

38. The Bhakti movement in medieval India was characterized by which of the following features?

  1. Emphasis on rituals
  2. Rejection of caste system
  3. Focus on temple worship
  4. Acceptance of image worship

Answer: Rejection of caste system

Explanation: The Bhakti movement rejected the caste system and emphasized personal devotion to a deity. It emerged as a reaction to the formalized religious practices.

39. The administrative term 'muqaddam' refers to which of the following positions in medieval India?

  1. Village headman
  2. Provincial governor
  3. District administrator
  4. Revenue collector

Answer: Village headman

Explanation: A muqaddam was the headman of a village in medieval India, responsible for collecting revenue and maintaining law and order.

40. The socio-religious reform association 'Arya Samaj' was founded by which of the following individuals?

  1. Dayananda Saraswati
  2. Ram Mohan Roy
  3. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
  4. Keshab Chandra Sen

Answer: Dayananda Saraswati

Explanation: Arya Samaj was founded by Dayananda Saraswati in 1875 to reform Hindu society and promote Vedic values.

41. The architectural feature 'shikhara' is characteristic of which of the following styles of temple architecture in India?

  1. Dravidian
  2. Nagara
  3. Vesara
  4. Maru-Gurjara

Answer: Nagara

Explanation: The shikhara is a towering spire that is a characteristic feature of the Nagara style of temple architecture in India.

42. The 'amil' was an administrative term used in medieval India to refer to which of the following positions?

  1. Revenue collector
  2. Provincial governor
  3. District administrator
  4. Village headman

Answer: Revenue collector

Explanation: An amil was a revenue collector in medieval India, responsible for collecting taxes and revenue from the peasants.

43. The freedom struggle phase 'Non-Cooperation Movement' was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in which of the following years?

  1. 1919
  2. 1920
  3. 1921
  4. 1922

Answer: 1920

Explanation: The Non-Cooperation Movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in 1920 to protest the Rowlatt Act and the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.

44. The 'Treaty of Allahabad' was signed in which of the following years between the East India Company and the Nawab of Awadh?

  1. 1759
  2. 1762
  3. 1764
  4. 1765

Answer: 1764

Explanation: The Treaty of Allahabad was signed in 1764 between the East India Company and the Nawab of Awadh, marking the beginning of British expansion in India.

45. The philosophical concept of 'Advaita Vedanta' was propounded by which of the following philosophers?

  1. Adi Shankara
  2. Ramanuja
  3. Madhva
  4. Nimbarka

Answer: Adi Shankara

Explanation: Advaita Vedanta is a philosophical concept that was propounded by Adi Shankara, emphasizing the non-dual nature of reality.

46. The 'Regulating Act of 1773' was enacted by the British Parliament to regulate the affairs of the East India Company in which of the following ways?

  1. Establishing a board of control
  2. Appointing a governor-general
  3. Creating a supreme court
  4. Imposing a tax on Indian goods

Answer: Establishing a board of control

Explanation: The Regulating Act of 1773 established a board of control to regulate the affairs of the East India Company and marked the beginning of British parliamentary control over India.

47. The 'Pitt's India Act of 1784' was enacted by the British Parliament to which of the following purposes?

  1. Establishing a permanent settlement
  2. Creating a supreme court
  3. Appointing a governor-general
  4. Regulating the affairs of the East India Company

Answer: Regulating the affairs of the East India Company

Explanation: The Pitt's India Act of 1784 was enacted to regulate the affairs of the East India Company and to establish a system of government in India.

48. The term 'muqaddam' refers to a medieval administrative position in India, specifically a

  1. Village headman
  2. Revenue collector
  3. Military commander
  4. Provincial governor

Answer: Village headman

Explanation: Muqaddam was a village headman in medieval India, responsible for local administration.

49. The Bhakti movement in India was characterized by a focus on

  1. Ritualistic worship
  2. Devotional singing
  3. Yogic practices
  4. Tantric rituals

Answer: Devotional singing

Explanation: Bhakti movement emphasized devotional singing and personal devotion to a deity.

50. The architectural feature of the 'shikhara' is commonly found in

  1. Buddhist stupas
  2. Jain temples
  3. Hindu temples
  4. Mughal mosques

Answer: Hindu temples

Explanation: Shikhara is a tower-like structure typically found in Hindu temples.
