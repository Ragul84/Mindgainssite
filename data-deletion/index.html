Data Deletion

Last updated: 25 September 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data MindGains holds about you at any time. This page covers both your MindGains account and data from our Instagram account @mindgains_ai.

Delete your MindGains account and data

  1. Email contact@mindgains.ai with the subject "Delete my MindGains data".
  2. Send it from the email address you signed in with, or include the mobile number linked to your account.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account, study progress, AI tutor history and linked Instagram data within 30 days.

Delete only your Instagram data

If you interacted with @mindgains_ai on Instagram (a comment mention or a direct message):

  1. Email contact@mindgains.ai with the subject "Delete my Instagram data" and your Instagram username.
  2. We will delete your Instagram user ID, username, the comments and messages you sent us, our replies, and any link to a MindGains account, within 30 days.

If you only want to disconnect Instagram from your MindGains account, open your Profile in the MindGains app and tap Disconnect under Instagram.

Removing MindGains from your Meta account

You can also remove app access in Instagram or Facebook under Settings → Apps and websites. This stops future access; to delete data we already hold, use one of the options above.

What we may keep

We may keep limited records where the law requires it (for example, payment and tax records) or to prevent fraud and abuse. These are kept only as long as needed and are not used for anything else.

Questions? Contact MindGains Labs Private Limited at contact@mindgains.ai. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
