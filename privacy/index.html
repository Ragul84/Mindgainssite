Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains how MindGains Labs Private Limited ("MindGains", "we", "us") handles personal data when you use the MindGains app, the mindgains.ai website, and our Instagram account @mindgains_ai (together, the "Services"). MindGains helps learners prepare for exams such as UPSC, TNPSC, SSC and RRB.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address or mobile number, and profile picture when you sign in with Google or with a phone one-time password.
  • Study data: your target exam, syllabus preferences, quiz and test answers, scores, streaks, weak topics, study plans and saved lessons.
  • AI tutor conversations: the questions, images or voice input you send to our AI tutor (MIGA) and the answers it gives, so you can see your history and we can improve answer quality.
  • Subscription data: your plan and purchase status. Payments are processed by Google Play or Apple; we never receive your full card details.
  • Device and usage data: app version, device type, notification tokens, crash information and basic usage events.
  • Instagram data: see section 4.

2. How we use data

  • To provide the Services: personalised lessons, quizzes, study plans, revision and AI answers.
  • To manage your account, subscription and daily usage limits.
  • To keep the Services secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To improve content accuracy and product quality.
  • To send notifications you have allowed, such as streak and revision reminders.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who help us run the Services, under contracts that restrict their use of it:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and server functions.
  • AI model providers (such as Google Gemini, accessed directly or through OpenRouter): to generate answers to your questions. We send only the content needed to answer.
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and Apple: to manage subscriptions.
  • Google: for Google sign-in.
  • Meta: to receive and reply to Instagram comments and messages (section 4).

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Instagram (@mindgains_ai)

When you mention @mindgains_ai in a comment, or send us a direct message, our AI replies with study help. To do this we use Meta's official Instagram APIs and process:

  • your Instagram-scoped user ID and username;
  • the text of the comment or message you sent us, and our reply;
  • when your request is about a post or Reel, publicly accessible information about that post (such as its caption and a short AI-generated summary of its content). We do not store the downloaded video or image itself.

We only process content you send to or tag us in. We never access private accounts, private posts or messages that were not sent to us.

If you choose to link your Instagram account to your MindGains account (by sending us "LINK" and confirming in the app), we connect the two so replies can use your study progress. You can unlink at any time from your MindGains profile.

Instagram data is used only to answer you and to apply daily usage limits. It is not used for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, study and AI tutor data: while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete your account.
  • Instagram interaction records: up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Temporary data such as one-time link codes and cached post summaries: deleted automatically when they expire.
  • We may keep limited records longer where the law requires it, for example payment records.

6. Your choices and rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, unlink Instagram, and turn off notifications. To delete your data, follow the steps on our Data Deletion page. Where applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) gives you further rights, you can exercise them by contacting us.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, stored with access controls, and access to sensitive tables is restricted to our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

8. Children

The Services are meant for exam aspirants and students. If you are under 18, please use MindGains with a parent or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has given us data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
