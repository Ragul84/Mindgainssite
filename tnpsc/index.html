Common questions about TNPSC preparation

Is the content based on Samacheer Kalvi books?

Yes. Answers and explanations are drawn from the Samacheer Kalvi textbooks that the TNPSC syllabus is based on, and the app shows the class and chapter for each one.

Can I study in Tamil?

Yes. Miga explains topics in Tamil or English, and you can switch language at any time.

Is it free?

Every TNPSC topic quiz on the website is free and needs no sign up. The Android app adds mock tests, answer writing and Quiz Rooms.
