TNPSC FAQs

How is this different from a normal TNPSC quiz app?

MindGains connects the whole loop: a textbook explanation leads into a previous-year question, a wrong answer becomes spaced revision, revision earns XP, and XP feeds live Quiz Rooms with friends. Nothing you study is thrown away.

Does MindGains support Tamil preparation?

Tamil is a first-class TNPSC track. Thirukkural, Sangam literature, grammar and modern Tamil all have dedicated topic quizzes, and you can ask Miga to explain in Tamil.

Can I use MindGains for free?

Yes. Every TNPSC topic quiz on the website is free with no sign-up. The Android app adds the Daily Dose, connected revision, Miga and Quiz Rooms.
