Tn Districts MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Tn Districts questions to revise the TNPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which is the capital of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Coimbatore
  2. Madurai
  3. Chennai
  4. Trichy

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai (formerly Madras) is the capital of Tamil Nadu and the fourth largest metropolitan city in India.

2. Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary (Tamil Nadu) is in:

  1. Chengalpattu district
  2. Thanjavur district
  3. Coimbatore district
  4. Vellore district

Answer: Chengalpattu district

Explanation: Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary in Chengalpattu district, Tamil Nadu, is one of India's oldest bird sanctuaries (1936). It is a winter habitat for migratory birds like painted storks and open-billed storks.

3. Point Calimere Wildlife Sanctuary (Tamil Nadu) is famous for:

  1. Flamingos and migratory birds
  2. Tigers
  3. Elephants
  4. Mangroves and turtles

Answer: Flamingos and migratory birds

Explanation: Point Calimere (Kodikkarai) in Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu, is famous for Greater Flamingos and migratory birds from Central Asia. Rare blackbucks are also found here.

4. How many districts does Tamil Nadu have currently?

  1. 38 districts
  2. 32 districts
  3. 33 districts
  4. 36 districts

Answer: 38 districts

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has 38 districts after the creation of 6 new districts in 2019: Chengalpattu, Kallakurichi, Tenkasi, Ranipet, Tirupathur, and Mayiladuthurai were carved out from existing districts.

5. Rameswaram island is in which district of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Ramanathapuram district
  2. Madurai district
  3. Dindigul district
  4. Tuticorin district

Answer: Ramanathapuram district

Explanation: Rameswaram island is in Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu. It is connected to mainland India by the Pamban Bridge (rail) and Annai Indira Gandhi Road Bridge. It is a major Hindu pilgrimage site.

6. The Chennai Metro was inaugurated in:

  1. 2015
  2. 2012
  3. 2010
  4. 2017

Answer: 2015

Explanation: Chennai Metro Rail was inaugurated on 29 June 2015 by PM Narendra Modi. Phase 1 covers about 45 km. Phase 2 is under construction to expand the network to about 118 km.

7. Chennai Port (Madras Port) was developed as a:

  1. Major artificial (man-made) harbour on the Coromandel Coast
  2. Natural harbour
  3. River port
  4. Inland port

Answer: Major artificial (man-made) harbour on the Coromandel Coast

Explanation: Chennai Port (established 1881) is the second largest artificial port in India (after Jawaharlal Nehru Port, Mumbai) and the largest in the Bay of Bengal. It is one of India's premier ports for container traffic.

8. Lipulekh Pass is used for:

  1. Kailash Mansarovar Yatra — border between India (Uttarakhand) and China (Tibet)
  2. India-Nepal trade
  3. India-Bhutan border
  4. Ladakh access

Answer: Kailash Mansarovar Yatra — border between India (Uttarakhand) and China (Tibet)

Explanation: Lipulekh Pass (5,334 m) in Uttarakhand (Pithoragarh district) connects India with Tibet (China). It is used for the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra. It is also disputed between India and Nepal.

9. The Golden Quadrilateral highway project connects:

  1. Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata
  2. Four metro cities via expressways
  3. Delhi, Jaipur, Mumbai, Bengaluru
  4. Srinagar, Kanyakumari, Porbandar, Silchar

Answer: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata

Explanation: The Golden Quadrilateral (GQ) connects Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata — India's four major cities — via 5,846 km of 4/6-lane National Highways. It is the largest highway project in India.

10. Tirupur is famous for:

  1. Knitwear and hosiery (called the 'Knit City' or 'Dollar City')
  2. Silk sarees
  3. Steel industry
  4. IT sector

Answer: Knitwear and hosiery (called the 'Knit City' or 'Dollar City')

Explanation: Tirupur (Tiruppur) in Coimbatore district is India's largest knitwear export hub — called the 'Knit City', 'Knitwear Capital of India', and 'Dollar City' (dollar=forex earnings). It exports cotton knitwear globally.

11. Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) is in which district?

  1. Chengalpattu district
  2. Kancheepuram district
  3. Chennai district
  4. Villupuram district

Answer: Chengalpattu district

Explanation: Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) is in Chengalpattu district, Tamil Nadu. It was a major Pallava port city and is UNESCO World Heritage Site for its 7th-8th century rock-cut temples, Shore Temple, and Five Rathas.

12. Tirunelveli is famous for:

  1. Halwa (sweet) and banana (Nendran variety)
  2. Steel
  3. Knitwear
  4. Diamond industry

Answer: Halwa (sweet) and banana (Nendran variety)

Explanation: Tirunelveli is famous for its unique 'Tirunelveli Halwa' (made from wheat/maida), Nendran banana, and its proximity to Courtallam (Kutralam) waterfalls. The NTPC Kudankulam plant is in Tirunelveli district.

13. The Sathankulam-Koonthankulam Bird Sanctuary in Tamil Nadu is famous for:

  1. Spot-billed pelicans and migratory water birds
  2. Elephants
  3. Tigers
  4. Flamingos only

Answer: Spot-billed pelicans and migratory water birds

Explanation: Koonthankulam Bird Sanctuary in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu is one of the largest bird sanctuaries in Asia, famous for the spot-billed pelican (Pelecanus philippensis) and other migratory water birds.

14. Which Tamil Nadu district was created by bifurcating South Arcot district?

  1. Cuddalore and Villupuram (bifurcated in 1993)
  2. Thanjavur and Tiruvarur
  3. Salem and Namakkal
  4. Coimbatore and Tiruppur

Answer: Cuddalore and Villupuram (bifurcated in 1993)

Explanation: South Arcot district was bifurcated into Cuddalore and Villupuram districts in 1993. Similarly, North Arcot was bifurcated into Vellore and Tiruvannamalai. Many Tamil Nadu district bifurcations happened in 1993.

15. The Kanyakumari district (southernmost in Tamil Nadu) borders:

  1. Kerala (north and west) and Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, Indian Ocean (south and east)
  2. Kerala and Karnataka
  3. Kerala only
  4. Tirunelveli and Kerala

Answer: Kerala (north and west) and Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, Indian Ocean (south and east)

Explanation: Kanyakumari district borders Kerala (Thiruvananthapuram and Kollam districts) on the north-northwest, Tirunelveli district on the north-east, and is surrounded by the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean to the south.

16. Tiruvannamalai district in Tamil Nadu is famous for:

  1. Annamalai Hill (Arunachala) and Annamalayar Temple
  2. Silk weaving
  3. Steel industry
  4. IT sector

Answer: Annamalai Hill (Arunachala) and Annamalayar Temple

Explanation: Tiruvannamalai is famous for the Annamalai Hill (Arunachala) and the Annamalayar Temple — a major Shaivite pilgrimage site. The hill is sacred as a manifestation of Agni (fire). Ramana Maharshi ashram is also here.

17. Salem district (Tamil Nadu) is famous for:

  1. Steel plant (Salem Steel Plant), mangoes (Banginapalli/Bangalora variety), and marble
  2. Cotton textiles
  3. IT sector
  4. Silk weaving

Answer: Steel plant (Salem Steel Plant), mangoes (Banginapalli/Bangalora variety), and marble

Explanation: Salem district is known for the Salem Steel Plant (SAIL plant, 1981), Salem mangoes, silk weaving (Kancheepuram-type in Salem), and marble/granite quarrying. Salem is the 5th largest city in Tamil Nadu.

18. What is the capital of Tamil Nadu's Nilgiri district?

  1. Coonoor
  2. Kotagiri
  3. Ooty (Udhagamandalam)
  4. Gudalur

Answer: Ooty (Udhagamandalam)

Explanation: Ooty (Udhagamandalam) is the district headquarters of the Nilgiri district in Tamil Nadu, and a popular hill station.

19. The longest National Highway in India is:

  1. NH-1 (Delhi-Amritsar)
  2. NH-44 (Srinagar to Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km)
  3. NH-7
  4. NH-2

Answer: NH-44 (Srinagar to Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km)

Explanation: NH-44 (formerly NH-7) from Srinagar (J&K) to Kanyakumari (Tamil Nadu) is the longest National Highway in India at approximately 3,745 km. It passes through Pathankot, Jalandhar, Delhi, Agra, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Madurai.

20. India's longest national highway is:

  1. NH-44 (Srinagar to Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km)
  2. NH-1
  3. NH-7 (old)
  4. NH-27

Answer: NH-44 (Srinagar to Kanyakumari, ~3,745 km)

Explanation: NH-44 (3,745 km) from Srinagar to Kanyakumari via Delhi-Agra-Nagpur-Bangalore-Chennai is India's longest national highway, running the full north-south length of India.

21. The largest district (by area) in Tamil Nadu is:

  1. Dindigul
  2. Coimbatore
  3. Vellore
  4. Cuddalore

Answer: Dindigul

Explanation: Dindigul is the largest district by area in Tamil Nadu (6,266 sq km). Coimbatore is the second largest. Tamil Nadu has 38 districts (after reorganization adding Chengalpattu, Kallakurichi, Tenkasi, Ranipet, Tirupathur, Mayiladuthurai in 2019).

22. The Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant (Tamil Nadu) was built with assistance from:

  1. Russia
  2. France
  3. USA
  4. UK

Answer: Russia

Explanation: Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu, was built with Russia's technical and financial assistance. It has two VVER-1000 reactors, each of 1,000 MW. More units are under construction.

23. Which river is the longest in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Cauvery
  2. Vaigai
  3. Tamiraparani
  4. Palar

Answer: Cauvery

Explanation: The Cauvery river is approximately 800 km long and flows through Tamil Nadu. It is considered the lifeblood of the state.

24. What type of soil is predominantly found in the Cauvery delta region?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Laterite

Answer: Alluvial

Explanation: Alluvial soil is rich in nutrients and suitable for agriculture, making the Cauvery delta a fertile region.

25. Which mountain range runs along the western border of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Western Ghats
  2. Eastern Ghats
  3. Nilgiri
  4. Anamalai

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats mountain range is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to diverse flora and fauna.
