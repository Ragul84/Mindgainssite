26. What is the primary factor that influences the formation of different types of soil in India?

  1. Climate
  2. Relief
  3. Vegetation
  4. Parent rock

Answer: Climate

Explanation: Climate is the primary factor that influences the formation of different types of soil in India. It affects the weathering and erosion of rocks, leading to the formation of various soil types.

27. Which type of soil is commonly found in the delta regions of the Cauvery river?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Laterite

Answer: Alluvial

Explanation: Alluvial soil is commonly found in the delta regions of the Cauvery river, which is deposited by the river. This type of soil is fertile and suitable for agriculture.

28. What is the name of the soil type that is formed due to intense weathering and is commonly found in the Western Ghats region?

  1. Laterite
  2. Alluvial
  3. Black
  4. Red

Answer: Laterite

Explanation: Laterite soil is formed due to intense weathering and is commonly found in the Western Ghats region. It is rich in iron and aluminum.

29. Which of the following factors affects the fertility of soil in India?

  1. Monsoon
  2. Relief
  3. Vegetation
  4. All

Answer: All

Explanation: All the factors, including monsoon, relief, and vegetation, affect the fertility of soil in India. They influence the formation and degradation of soil.

30. What is the primary use of red soil in India?

  1. Agriculture
  2. Construction
  3. Industry
  4. Transportation

Answer: Agriculture

Explanation: Red soil is primarily used for agriculture in India, as it is rich in iron and aluminum. It is suitable for growing various crops.

31. Which type of soil is commonly found in the arid regions of India?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Arid

Answer: Arid

Explanation: Arid soil is commonly found in the arid regions of India, which has low water-holding capacity. It is formed due to low rainfall and high evaporation.
