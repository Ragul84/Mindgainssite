Earth Universe MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Earth Universe questions to revise the TNPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which state in India receives the highest rainfall?

  1. Assam
  2. Meghalaya
  3. Kerala
  4. West Bengal

Answer: Meghalaya

Explanation: Meghalaya receives the highest rainfall in India. Mawsynram in Meghalaya is the wettest place on Earth.

2. The Sundarbans delta is formed by which rivers?

  1. Ganga and Yamuna
  2. Ganga and Brahmaputra
  3. Indus and Chenab
  4. Krishna and Godavari

Answer: Ganga and Brahmaputra

Explanation: The Sundarbans delta — the world's largest mangrove forest — is formed by the Ganga and Brahmaputra rivers in West Bengal and Bangladesh.

3. The Atacama Desert — the driest place on Earth — is in which continent?

  1. Africa
  2. Asia
  3. South America
  4. Australia

Answer: South America

Explanation: The Atacama Desert, the driest non-polar desert on Earth, is in northern Chile (South America).

4. The latitude that divides India roughly into northern and southern halves passes through:

  1. Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N)
  2. 30°N latitude
  3. Standard Meridian (82.5°E)
  4. Equator (0°)

Answer: Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N)

Explanation: The Tropic of Cancer (23°30'N) passes through the middle of India, dividing it into tropical south and subtropical north.

5. The 'Roaring Forties', 'Furious Fifties', and 'Screaming Sixties' are associated with:

  1. Monsoon winds
  2. Polar easterlies
  3. Strong westerly winds in southern hemisphere
  4. Tropical cyclones

Answer: Strong westerly winds in southern hemisphere

Explanation: These are strong westerly winds that blow in the southern hemisphere between 40°-70°S latitude. They are unimpeded by land masses and are very strong.
