26. 'Subduction zone' is where:

  1. A denser tectonic plate slides under a lighter plate (causes earthquakes and volcanoes)
  2. Two plates move apart
  3. Two continental plates collide
  4. A plate cracks open

Answer: A denser tectonic plate slides under a lighter plate (causes earthquakes and volcanoes)

Explanation: A subduction zone is where an oceanic plate (denser) slides under a continental or another oceanic plate. This causes deep-focus earthquakes, trenches (like Mariana Trench), and volcanic arcs (Ring of Fire).

27. Which is the deepest point in the Indian Ocean?

  1. Java Trench (Sunda Trench) — about 7,290 m
  2. Mariana Trench
  3. Puerto Rico Trench
  4. Philippine Trench

Answer: Java Trench (Sunda Trench) — about 7,290 m

Explanation: The Java Trench (Sunda Trench) at approximately 7,290 m depth is the deepest point in the Indian Ocean, located south of Java (Indonesia). The Mariana Trench is in the Pacific Ocean.

28. India's latitude extends approximately from:

  1. 8°4'N to 37°6'N
  2. 6°N to 40°N
  3. 10°N to 35°N
  4. 8°N to 36°N

Answer: 8°4'N to 37°6'N

Explanation: India's mainland extends from 8°4'N (Kanyakumari) to 37°6'N (Kashmir) in latitude, and 68°7'E to 97°25'E in longitude. With islands, it extends to about 6°45'N (Great Nicobar).

29. The famous Pongal festival in Tamil Nadu celebrates:

  1. Harvest festival — thanksgiving for a good harvest (Thai Pongal)
  2. New Year
  3. Religious festival
  4. Monsoon arrival

Answer: Harvest festival — thanksgiving for a good harvest (Thai Pongal)

Explanation: Pongal is Tamil Nadu's most important harvest festival (4-day festival in Thai month, mid-January). Thai Pongal (1st day) — thanksgiving to Sun god; Mattu Pongal (2nd day) — for cattle; Kaanum Pongal (3rd day) — family outing.

30. The latitude 23.5°N is called the Tropic of Cancer. India's southernmost mainland point Kanyakumari is at approximately:

  1. 8°4'N
  2. 10°N
  3. 6°N
  4. 12°N

Answer: 8°4'N

Explanation: Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin), India's southernmost mainland point, is at 8°4'N. The southernmost point of India's territory (including islands) is Indira Point on Great Nicobar Island at about 6°45'N.

31. Which direction does the sun rise?

  1. North
  2. South
  3. East
  4. West

Answer: East

Explanation: The sun rises in the East due to Earth's west-to-east rotation.

32. The largest continent in the world is:

  1. Africa
  2. North America
  3. Asia
  4. Australia

Answer: Asia

Explanation: Asia is the world's largest continent (44.6 million km², ~29.5% of Earth's land area). It has the largest population (4.7 billion, ~60% of world). Asia has the highest peak (Everest), lowest point (Dead Sea), and largest lake (Caspian Sea).

33. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and Indian Standard Time (IST) differ by:

  1. 5 hours
  2. 5 hours 30 minutes (IST = GMT + 5:30)
  3. 4 hours 30 minutes
  4. 6 hours

Answer: 5 hours 30 minutes (IST = GMT + 5:30)

Explanation: Indian Standard Time (IST) = GMT + 5 hours 30 minutes (+5:30). India does not observe Daylight Saving Time. IST is based on 82.5°E longitude passing through Mirzapur, UP.

34. The 'Ring of Fire' refers to:

  1. A volcanic ring around Africa
  2. The zone of earthquakes and volcanoes around the Pacific Ocean basin
  3. A meteorological phenomenon
  4. Desert fires in Australia

Answer: The zone of earthquakes and volcanoes around the Pacific Ocean basin

Explanation: The Ring of Fire is a 40,000 km horseshoe-shaped zone around the Pacific Ocean, containing 75% of the world's volcanoes and ~90% of its earthquakes. It follows tectonic plate boundaries — Pacific Plate, Philippine Plate, Juan de Fuca Plate, etc.

35. The Ozone layer protects Earth from:

  1. Cosmic rays
  2. Ultraviolet (UV) radiation from the Sun — particularly UV-B and UV-C
  3. Infrared radiation
  4. Sound waves

Answer: Ultraviolet (UV) radiation from the Sun — particularly UV-B and UV-C

Explanation: The ozone layer (stratosphere, 15–35 km altitude) absorbs 93–99% of the Sun's UV radiation (especially harmful UV-B and UV-C), protecting life on Earth. CFCs deplete ozone. Montreal Protocol (1987) banned CFCs.

36. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is related to:

  1. Protecting endangered species
  2. Conservation of wetlands of international importance
  3. Combating desertification
  4. Reducing carbon emissions

Answer: Conservation of wetlands of international importance

Explanation: The Ramsar Convention on Wetlands (signed in Ramsar, Iran, 1971) is an international treaty for the conservation and sustainable use of wetlands. India has 75 Ramsar sites (as of 2023). Largest: Sundarbans, West Bengal.

37. The Sundarbans is located in:

  1. West Bengal (India) and Bangladesh
  2. Odisha
  3. Andaman Islands
  4. Assam

Answer: West Bengal (India) and Bangladesh

Explanation: The Sundarbans (world's largest mangrove forest) straddles the India-Bangladesh border in the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to the Royal Bengal Tiger.

38. The Ten Degree Channel separates:

  1. Andaman Islands from Nicobar Islands
  2. South Andaman from Little Andaman
  3. Lakshadweep from Maldives
  4. India from Sri Lanka

Answer: Andaman Islands from Nicobar Islands

Explanation: The Ten Degree Channel (at 10°N latitude) separates the Andaman Islands from the Nicobar Islands. The Duncan Passage separates South Andaman from Little Andaman.

39. Ozone layer is found in which layer of the atmosphere?

  1. Stratosphere
  2. Troposphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Stratosphere

Explanation: The ozone layer is found in the stratosphere (15-50 km altitude), specifically between 20-30 km. It absorbs harmful UV radiation from the sun. CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons) deplete the ozone layer.

40. Which ocean current is responsible for the warm climate of Western Europe?

  1. North Atlantic Drift (Gulf Stream extension)
  2. Labrador Current
  3. Canary Current
  4. Humboldt Current

Answer: North Atlantic Drift (Gulf Stream extension)

Explanation: The North Atlantic Drift (an extension of the Gulf Stream) carries warm tropical water to Western Europe, giving it a mild climate despite its high latitude. Without it, Western Europe would be much colder.

41. The Dead Sea (Israel/Jordan border) is famous for:

  1. Being the lowest point on Earth's land surface and extreme salinity
  2. Extreme cold
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Being the largest saltwater lake

Answer: Being the lowest point on Earth's land surface and extreme salinity

Explanation: The Dead Sea (430 m below sea level) is the lowest point on Earth's land surface. Its salinity is about 34% (10 times the ocean), making it impossible for most aquatic life to survive — hence 'Dead' Sea.

42. The Ring of Fire is a zone of:

  1. Volcanoes and earthquakes surrounding the Pacific Ocean
  2. Volcanic activity in the Indian Ocean
  3. Typhoon activity
  4. Seismic activity in the Atlantic

Answer: Volcanoes and earthquakes surrounding the Pacific Ocean

Explanation: The Ring of Fire is a horseshoe-shaped zone of volcanic activity and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean rim. It has 75% of the world's active volcanoes and experiences 90% of the world's earthquakes.

43. The Mariana Trench (deepest point on Earth, ~11,034 m) is in:

  1. Pacific Ocean (west of Mariana Islands)
  2. Atlantic Ocean
  3. Indian Ocean
  4. Arctic Ocean

Answer: Pacific Ocean (west of Mariana Islands)

Explanation: The Mariana Trench is in the western Pacific Ocean, east of the Mariana Islands. Challenger Deep (10,929 m in the Mariana Trench) is the deepest known point on Earth.

44. The Khasi Hills and Jaintia Hills are in:

  1. Meghalaya
  2. Nagaland
  3. Assam
  4. Manipur

Answer: Meghalaya

Explanation: Khasi Hills and Jaintia Hills are in Meghalaya. Cherrapunji (Sohra) and Mawsynram are in the Khasi Hills — the wettest places on Earth. Shillong is in the East Khasi Hills.

45. India's largest solar power plant (as of recent years) is:

  1. Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan
  2. Gujarat solar parks
  3. Kamuthi Solar Power Project, Tamil Nadu
  4. Rewa Solar Park, Madhya Pradesh

Answer: Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan

Explanation: Bhadla Solar Park in Jodhpur, Rajasthan (~2,245 MW) is one of the world's largest solar parks. Earlier, Kamuthi Solar Power Project in Tamil Nadu (648 MW) held this record when commissioned in 2016.

46. What is the largest river in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Cauvery
  2. Vaigai
  3. Tamiraparani
  4. Palar

Answer: Cauvery

Explanation: The Cauvery River is the largest river in Tamil Nadu, flowing through several districts.

47. Which mountain range runs along the western edge of India?

  1. Himalayas
  2. Aravallis
  3. Western Ghats
  4. Eastern Ghats

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats mountain range runs along the western edge of India, parallel to the Arabian Sea.

48. What type of soil is commonly found in the delta regions of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Laterite

Answer: Alluvial

Explanation: Alluvial soil is commonly found in the delta regions of Tamil Nadu, particularly in the Cauvery Delta.

49. Which city is the major port in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Mumbai
  3. Kolkata
  4. Cochin

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai is the major port city in Tamil Nadu, handling a significant volume of trade.

50. What is the name of the monsoon that affects India?

  1. Southwest Monsoon
  2. Northeast Monsoon
  3. Northwest Monsoon
  4. Southeast Monsoon

Answer: Southwest Monsoon

Explanation: The Southwest Monsoon is the primary monsoon that affects India, bringing significant rainfall.
