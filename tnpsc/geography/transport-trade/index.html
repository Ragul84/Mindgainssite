1. The Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve in Tamil Nadu includes which famous wildlife sanctuary?

  1. Vedanthangal
  2. Mudumalai Wildlife Sanctuary (and Anamalai/Indira Gandhi WS, Mundanthurai)
  3. Point Calimere
  4. Guindy National Park

Answer: Mudumalai Wildlife Sanctuary (and Anamalai/Indira Gandhi WS, Mundanthurai)

Explanation: The Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve (UNESCO listed) includes: Mudumalai WS (Tamil Nadu), Wayanad WS (Kerala), Bandipur NP (Karnataka), Nagarhole NP (Karnataka), and Silent Valley NP (Kerala). It supports tigers, elephants, leopards.

2. The world's longest railway platform is in:

  1. Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh
  2. Kharagpur, West Bengal
  3. Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh
  4. Hubli, Karnataka

Answer: Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh

Explanation: Gorakhpur railway station in Uttar Pradesh has the world's longest railway platform at 1,366.33 m. Kharagpur (1,072 m) was previously the record holder. Hubli also has a very long platform.

3. India's busiest airport (by passenger traffic) is:

  1. Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi
  2. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, Mumbai
  3. Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru
  4. Chennai International Airport

Answer: Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi

Explanation: Indira Gandhi International Airport (Delhi) is India's busiest airport, handling over 70 million passengers annually. Mumbai's airport is the second busiest. Bengaluru is third.

4. The North-South and East-West Corridor highway project:

  1. Connects Srinagar to Kanyakumari (N-S) and Porbandar to Silchar (E-W)
  2. Connects Delhi to Kolkata
  3. Links Northeast India to South India
  4. Part of Golden Quadrilateral

Answer: Connects Srinagar to Kanyakumari (N-S) and Porbandar to Silchar (E-W)

Explanation: North-South Corridor: Srinagar (J&K) to Kanyakumari (Tamil Nadu) — 4,076 km. East-West Corridor: Porbandar (Gujarat) to Silchar (Assam) — 3,640 km. Together forming the 7,142 km backbone.

5. National Highway 48 (old NH-8) connects:

  1. Delhi to Mumbai via Jaipur-Ahmedabad
  2. Delhi to Kolkata
  3. Mumbai to Chennai
  4. Delhi to Amritsar

Answer: Delhi to Mumbai via Jaipur-Ahmedabad

Explanation: NH-48 (formerly NH-8) is one of India's busiest highways, connecting Delhi to Mumbai (via Gurugram, Jaipur, Ajmer, Udaipur, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat). Part of the Golden Quadrilateral.

6. India's pharmaceutical hub (largest generic drug exporter) is:

  1. Hyderabad (Telangana) — called 'Genome Valley' and 'Pharma City'
  2. Mumbai
  3. Ahmedabad
  4. Bengaluru

Answer: Hyderabad (Telangana) — called 'Genome Valley' and 'Pharma City'

Explanation: Hyderabad is India's top pharmaceutical hub ('Pharma City'), home to Dr. Reddy's, Cipla, Hetero, Aurobindo, Natco. India is the world's largest exporter of generic medicines.

7. Which state in India has no railway network?

  1. Sikkim (no railway; Rangpo station being built to connect it)
  2. Meghalaya
  3. Nagaland
  4. Manipur

Answer: Sikkim (no railway; Rangpo station being built to connect it)

Explanation: Sikkim was the only state with no railway network until recently. A rail line to Rangpo from West Bengal is being constructed. Sikkim is connected to the rest of India by NH-10 (Siliguri-Gangtok highway).

8. The Nathula Pass connects:

  1. Sikkim (India) and Tibet (China)
  2. India and Bhutan
  3. India and Nepal
  4. Himachal Pradesh and Tibet

Answer: Sikkim (India) and Tibet (China)

Explanation: Nathula Pass (4,310 m) connects Sikkim (India) with the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. It was reopened in 2006 for limited bilateral trade after being closed since the 1962 Sino-Indian War.

9. India's largest port (by cargo handled) is:

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT), Mumbai
  2. Mundra Port, Gujarat
  3. Chennai Port
  4. Vizag Port

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT), Mumbai

Explanation: Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT/Nhava Sheva) near Mumbai was India's largest container port for many years. However, Mundra Port (Adani Ports, Gujarat) has recently surpassed JNPT in total cargo.

10. Trade winds blow from:

  1. Sub-tropical high pressure belts towards the equator (from NE in N hemisphere, SE in S hemisphere)
  2. Equator towards poles
  3. Polar regions towards equator
  4. West to east everywhere

Answer: Sub-tropical high pressure belts towards the equator (from NE in N hemisphere, SE in S hemisphere)

Explanation: Trade winds blow from the sub-tropical high pressure belts (around 30°N and 30°S) towards the equatorial low pressure belt. In the N hemisphere, they blow from NE; in the S hemisphere, from SE.

11. Which river is the main source of water for the state of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Cauvery
  2. Vaigai
  3. Godavari
  4. Krishna

Answer: Cauvery

Explanation: The Cauvery river is the main source of water for Tamil Nadu, flowing through major districts.

12. What is the primary mode of transport for international trade in India?

  1. Road
  2. Rail
  3. Sea
  4. Air

Answer: Sea

Explanation: Sea transport is the primary mode for international trade in India due to its vast coastline.

13. Which mountain range runs along the western coast of India?

  1. Himalayas
  2. Aravallis
  3. Western Ghats
  4. Eastern Ghats

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats mountain range runs along the western coast of India, parallel to the Arabian Sea.

14. Which of the following ports is located in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Mumbai
  3. Kolkata
  4. Cochin

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: The Chennai port is one of the major ports in Tamil Nadu, handling significant cargo traffic.

15. What is the name of the monsoon that affects India?

  1. Southwest Monsoon
  2. Northeast Monsoon
  3. Northwest Monsoon
  4. Southeast Monsoon

Answer: Southwest Monsoon

Explanation: The Southwest Monsoon brings significant rainfall to India during the summer months.

16. Which river flows through the city of Madurai in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Vaigai
  2. Cauvery
  3. Tamiraparani
  4. Palar

Answer: Vaigai

Explanation: The Vaigai river flows through the city of Madurai, a major city in Tamil Nadu.

17. What is the main type of soil found in the delta regions of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Laterite

Answer: Alluvial

Explanation: Alluvial soil is found in the delta regions of Tamil Nadu, which is fertile and suitable for agriculture.

18. Which city is the major trade center in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Coimbatore
  3. Madurai
  4. Tiruchirappalli

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai is the major trade center in Tamil Nadu, with a well-developed port and infrastructure.

19. What is the name of the strait separating India and Sri Lanka?

  1. Palk Strait
  2. Manor Strait
  3. Adam Strait
  4. Laccadive Strait

Answer: Palk Strait

Explanation: The Palk Strait separates India and Sri Lanka, connecting the Bay of Bengal to the Gulf of Mannar.

20. Which of the following is a major resource found in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Iron Ore
  2. Coal
  3. Lignite
  4. Bauxite

Answer: Lignite

Explanation: Lignite is a major resource found in Tamil Nadu, with significant deposits in the Neyveli region.

21. What is the primary mode of transport in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Road
  2. Rail
  3. Air
  4. Sea

Answer: Road

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a well-developed road network, making road the primary mode of transport.

22. Which river in Tamil Nadu is known for its agricultural significance?

  1. Cauvery
  2. Vaigai
  3. Tamiraparani
  4. Palar

Answer: Cauvery

Explanation: The Cauvery river is the primary source of water for irrigation in Tamil Nadu.

23. What is the name of the major port in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Tuticorin
  3. Ennore
  4. Kattupalli

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: The Chennai port is one of the major ports in Tamil Nadu.

24. Which type of soil is predominantly found in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Laterite

Answer: Red

Explanation: Red soil is predominantly found in Tamil Nadu due to the state's geological characteristics.

25. What is the significance of the monsoon in India's transport and trade?

  1. Increases trade
  2. Disrupts trade
  3. No impact
  4. Enhances transport

Answer: Disrupts trade

Explanation: The monsoon can disrupt trade and transport in India due to flooding and landslides.
