26. What is the name of the largest lake in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Pulicat Lake
  2. Vedaranyam Lake
  3. Kodaikanal Lake
  4. Berijam Lake

Answer: Pulicat Lake

Explanation: Pulicat Lake is the largest lake in Tamil Nadu, a brackish water lagoon located on the border with Andhra Pradesh.

27. Which mountain range runs along the western edge of India?

  1. Himalayas
  2. Aravallis
  3. Western Ghats
  4. Eastern Ghats

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats mountain range runs along the western edge of India, parallel to the Arabian Sea.

28. Which monsoon brings rainfall to Tamil Nadu during October and November?

  2. Northeast
  3. Northwest
  4. Southeast

Explanation: The Northeast monsoon brings rainfall to Tamil Nadu during October and November, providing relief to the state after a hot summer.
