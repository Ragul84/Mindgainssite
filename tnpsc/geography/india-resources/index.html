India Resources MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked India Resources questions to revise the TNPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. The standard meridian of India is:

  1. 72°30'E
  2. 80°30'E
  3. 82°30'E
  4. 90°30'E

Answer: 82°30'E

Explanation: The standard meridian of India is 82°30'E (passing through Mirzapur, UP), which is used to determine Indian Standard Time (IST = UTC+5:30).

2. The Neyveli Lignite Corporation (NLC) mines lignite for power generation in:

  1. Erode
  2. Cuddalore (Neyveli, South Arcot)
  3. Tirunelveli
  4. Salem

Answer: Cuddalore (Neyveli, South Arcot)

Explanation: Neyveli Lignite Corporation (NLC India Ltd) is located in Cuddalore district (formerly South Arcot), Tamil Nadu. It mines lignite (brown coal) and operates thermal power plants. Neyveli has Asia's largest opencast lignite mines.

3. India's largest iron ore reserves are in:

  1. Rajasthan
  2. Jharkhand and Odisha (Jharkhand-Odisha iron ore belt)
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. Kerala

Answer: Jharkhand and Odisha (Jharkhand-Odisha iron ore belt)

Explanation: The Jharkhand-Odisha iron ore belt (Singhbhum, Keonjhar, Mayurbhanj) has India's largest iron ore reserves. Other important areas: Karnataka (Bellary, Sandur), Chhattisgarh (Bastar), Goa. India has ~5.5 billion tonnes of iron ore reserves.

4. The 'Oil and Natural Gas Corporation' (ONGC) produces most of India's petroleum from:

  1. Assam oilfields only
  2. Mumbai High (offshore, Arabian Sea) — about 60% of production
  3. Rajasthan (Barmer) fields
  4. Gujarat coast

Answer: Mumbai High (offshore, Arabian Sea) — about 60% of production

Explanation: Mumbai High (Bombay High) offshore oilfields in the Arabian Sea produce about 60% of India's total crude oil. Other producing areas: Gujarat (Ankleshwar, Kalol), Assam (Digboi — oldest oilfield in Asia, 1889), Rajasthan (Barmer/Mangala).

5. Neyveli Lignite Corporation is in:

  1. Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu
  2. Vellore district
  3. Tirunelveli district
  4. Salem district

Answer: Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu

Explanation: Neyveli Lignite Corporation (now NLC India Limited) is in Neyveli, Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu. It mines lignite (brown coal) and operates thermal power stations generating electricity primarily for Tamil Nadu.

6. The Kolar Gold Fields (KGF) are in:

  1. Karnataka
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Andhra Pradesh
  4. Kerala

Answer: Karnataka

Explanation: Kolar Gold Fields (KGF) are in Kolar district, Karnataka. Once the world's deepest gold mines, they produced gold for over 120 years before being closed in 2001 by the government due to non-viability.

7. Singareni Coalfields are in:

  1. Telangana
  2. Chhattisgarh
  3. Jharkhand
  4. Odisha

Answer: Telangana

Explanation: Singareni Collieries Company Limited (SCCL) operates coal mines in Telangana (formerly Andhra Pradesh). Singareni is the only government-owned coal company owned jointly by the State (51%) and Centre (49%).

8. Neyveli Lignite (brown coal) is found in:

  1. Tamil Nadu (Cuddalore district)
  2. Rajasthan
  3. Jharkhand
  4. Goa

Answer: Tamil Nadu (Cuddalore district)

Explanation: Neyveli in Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu has the largest deposits of lignite (brown coal) in India. NLC India Limited (formerly Neyveli Lignite Corporation) mines it and generates electricity.

9. Which state is the leading producer of petroleum (crude oil) in India?

  1. Gujarat (offshore and onshore)
  2. Assam
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Andhra Pradesh

Answer: Gujarat (offshore and onshore)

Explanation: Gujarat is the leading producer of crude oil in India, with major offshore fields in the Arabian Sea (Bombay High, operated by ONGC) and onshore fields in Ankleshwar. Rajasthan (Barmer) has emerged as a major onshore producer.

10. The Montreal Protocol (1987) aimed to phase out:

  1. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) that deplete the ozone layer
  2. Carbon dioxide emissions
  3. Plastic pollution
  4. Nuclear waste

Answer: Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) that deplete the ozone layer

Explanation: The Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer (1987) is a landmark treaty phasing out CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons) and other ozone-depleting substances. It is considered the most successful environmental treaty.

11. Evergreen Revolution aims at:

  1. Sustainable increase in agricultural productivity without environmental damage
  2. Growing only green vegetables
  3. Forest conservation
  4. Using solar energy in agriculture

Answer: Sustainable increase in agricultural productivity without environmental damage

Explanation: Evergreen Revolution (concept by M.S. Swaminathan) aims at sustainable increase in agricultural productivity without ecological harm — combining the productivity of the Green Revolution with environmental sustainability.

12. OPEC (Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries) headquarters is in:

  1. Vienna, Austria
  2. Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
  3. Dubai, UAE
  4. Geneva, Switzerland

Answer: Vienna, Austria

Explanation: OPEC is headquartered in Vienna, Austria. Founded in Baghdad in 1960, it currently has 13 member countries. Saudi Arabia is the de facto leader. OPEC controls about 40% of world oil production.

13. OPEC (Organisation of Petroleum Exporting Countries) was founded in:

  1. 1945
  2. 1960
  3. 1973
  4. 1980

Answer: 1960

Explanation: OPEC was founded in 1960 in Baghdad by Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and Venezuela. Today it has 13 members. OPEC controls ~40% of world oil production. Its secretariat is in Vienna, Austria.

14. The Gulf of Kutch is famous for:

  1. Tidal energy potential, Kandla Port, and flamingos
  2. Coral reefs
  3. Pearl fishing
  4. Mangroves only

Answer: Tidal energy potential, Kandla Port, and flamingos

Explanation: Gulf of Kutch (Gujarat) has one of the highest tidal ranges in India (7-8 m), making it ideal for tidal energy. Kandla Port (Deendayal Port) is India's second-largest port by cargo. The gulf also has flamingos and corals.

15. What is the largest river in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Cauvery
  2. Vaigai
  3. Tamiraparani
  4. Palar

Answer: Cauvery

Explanation: The Cauvery River is the largest river in Tamil Nadu, flowing through several districts.

16. Which mountain range runs along the western coast of India?

  1. Himalayas
  2. Western Ghats
  3. Eastern Ghats
  4. Aravallis

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats mountain range runs along the western coast of India.

17. What type of soil is commonly found in the delta regions of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Alluvial
  2. Black
  3. Red
  4. Laterite

Answer: Alluvial

Explanation: Alluvial soil is commonly found in the delta regions of Tamil Nadu.

18. Which city is the major port in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Tiruchirappalli
  3. Madurai
  4. Coimbatore

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai is the major port city in Tamil Nadu.

19. What is the name of the monsoon that affects India?

  1. Southwest
  2. Northeast
  3. Northwest
  4. Southeast

Answer: Southwest

Explanation: The Southwest monsoon affects India.

20. Which river flows through the city of Madurai?

  1. Vaigai
  2. Cauvery
  3. Tamiraparani
  4. Palar

Answer: Vaigai

Explanation: The Vaigai River flows through the city of Madurai.

21. What is the name of the plateau in southern India?

  1. Deccan
  2. Chota Nagpur
  3. Malwa
  4. Chhattisgarh

Answer: Deccan

Explanation: The Deccan Plateau is located in southern India.

22. Which type of forest is commonly found in the Western Ghats?

  1. Tropical
  2. Temperate
  3. Montane
  4. Alpine

Explanation: Tropical forests are commonly found in the Western Ghats.

Explanation: The Palk Strait is between India and Sri Lanka.

24. Which city is the capital of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Madurai

  4. Tiruchirappalli

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai is the capital of Tamil Nadu.

25. What is the name of the strait separating India and Sri Lanka?

  1. Palk Strait
  2. Adam's Bridge
  3. Mannar Strait
  4. Laccadive Strait

Answer: Palk Strait

Explanation: The Palk Strait is the narrow waterway separating India and Sri Lanka, connecting the Bay of Bengal to the Gulf of Mannar.
