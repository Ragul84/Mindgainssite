World Oceans MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked World Oceans questions to revise the TNPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which ocean is to the south of India?

  1. Pacific Ocean
  2. Atlantic Ocean
  3. Indian Ocean
  4. Arctic Ocean

Answer: Indian Ocean

Explanation: India is bounded by the Indian Ocean to the south, the Bay of Bengal to the southeast, and the Arabian Sea to the southwest.

2. The Palk Strait separates India from:

  1. Bangladesh
  2. Pakistan
  3. Sri Lanka
  4. Maldives

Answer: Sri Lanka

Explanation: The Palk Strait separates Tamil Nadu in India from the Northern Province of Sri Lanka.

3. The Palk Strait separates:

  1. India and Pakistan
  2. India and Sri Lanka (between Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka)
  3. India and Bangladesh
  4. Andaman and Nicobar Islands

Answer: India and Sri Lanka (between Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka)

Explanation: The Palk Strait separates India (Tamil Nadu) from Sri Lanka. It is about 64–137 km wide. The Gulf of Mannar lies to the south and Palk Bay to the north of the strait.

4. The Suez Canal connects:

  1. Red Sea and Mediterranean Sea
  2. Red Sea and Indian Ocean
  3. Persian Gulf and Red Sea
  4. Caspian Sea and Black Sea

Answer: Red Sea and Mediterranean Sea

Explanation: The Suez Canal (193 km, Egypt) connects the Red Sea to the Mediterranean Sea. Opened in 1869, it allows ships to travel between Europe and Asia without going around Africa, reducing the journey by ~7,000 km.

5. The Panama Canal connects:

  1. Atlantic Ocean and Pacific Ocean
  2. Gulf of Mexico and Caribbean Sea
  3. South Atlantic and North Atlantic
  4. Caribbean Sea and Amazon River

Answer: Atlantic Ocean and Pacific Ocean

Explanation: The Panama Canal (82 km, Panama) connects the Atlantic (via Caribbean Sea) and Pacific Ocean. Opened in 1914, ships avoid going around South America (Cape Horn), saving about 12,000 km.

6. The Pamban Island (Rameswaram) is connected to mainland by:

  1. Pamban Bridge (railway) and Annai Indira Gandhi Road Bridge
  2. Only road bridge
  3. Only rail bridge
  4. Causeway

Answer: Pamban Bridge (railway) and Annai Indira Gandhi Road Bridge

Explanation: Pamban (Rameswaram) island is connected by Pamban Railway Bridge (India's first sea bridge, 1914) and Annai Indira Gandhi Road Bridge (1988). The new cable-stayed rail bridge is under construction.

7. India's first Metro rail was established in:

  1. Kolkata (1984)
  2. Delhi (2002)
  3. Mumbai (2014)
  4. Chennai (2015)

Answer: Kolkata (1984)

Explanation: Kolkata Metro was India's first metro rail system, inaugurated on 24 October 1984 (though partial opening was in 1983). Delhi Metro (2002) is currently the largest metro network in India.

8. The Strait of Gibraltar separates:

  1. Spain (Europe) and Morocco (Africa)
  2. Italy and Tunisia
  3. Greece and Turkey
  4. UK and France

Answer: Spain (Europe) and Morocco (Africa)

Explanation: The Strait of Gibraltar (14 km wide at its narrowest) separates Spain (Europe) from Morocco (Africa). It connects the Atlantic Ocean to the Mediterranean Sea. Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory.

9. The English Channel separates:

  1. England (UK) and France
  2. England and Ireland
  3. Scotland and Norway
  4. UK and Belgium

Answer: England (UK) and France

Explanation: The English Channel (La Manche in French) separates southern England from northern France. The Channel Tunnel (Chunnel, 1994) connects the two countries under the sea. The strait is 33 km wide at its narrowest (Dover-Calais).

10. The Strait of Malacca connects:

  1. Indian Ocean and South China Sea (between Malaysia and Indonesia)
  2. Pacific and Indian Ocean
  3. China Sea and Japan Sea
  4. Bay of Bengal and South China Sea

Answer: Indian Ocean and South China Sea (between Malaysia and Indonesia)

Explanation: The Strait of Malacca (between Malaysia/Singapore and Sumatra/Indonesia) is one of the world's most important shipping lanes, connecting the Indian Ocean with the South China Sea and Pacific Ocean.

11. The Bering Strait separates:

  1. Russia (Asia) and USA (Alaska)
  2. Alaska and Canada
  3. USA and Canada
  4. Russia and Japan

Answer: Russia (Asia) and USA (Alaska)

Explanation: The Bering Strait (85 km wide) separates Russia (Siberia, Asia) from the USA (Alaska, North America), connecting the Pacific Ocean (Bering Sea) with the Arctic Ocean. A proposed land bridge existed here during ice ages.

12. Which is India's longest road tunnel?

  1. Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Tunnel (Banihal-Qazigund, J&K)
  2. Atal Tunnel (Rohtang)
  3. Jawahar Tunnel (Banihal)
  4. Zoji La Tunnel

Answer: Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Tunnel (Banihal-Qazigund, J&K)

Explanation: Dr. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Tunnel (Banihal-Qazigund Tunnel, 8.45 km rail tunnel) is part of the J&K railway. The Atal (Rohtang) Road Tunnel (9.02 km) is currently India's longest road tunnel.

13. The Gulf of Mannar Marine National Park (Tamil Nadu) is famous for:

  1. Coral reefs, dugongs, sea turtles, and diverse marine life
  2. Mangroves only
  3. Dolphins
  4. Whale watching

Answer: Coral reefs, dugongs, sea turtles, and diverse marine life

Explanation: Gulf of Mannar Marine National Park (21 islands between Rameswaram and Tuticorin) is the first marine national park in South and Southeast Asia. Famous for coral reefs, dugongs, sea turtles, sea cucumbers, and 3,600+ species.

14. The 'Dokar La' Pass and 'Jelep La' Pass are on the border of:

  1. Sikkim (India) and Tibet/Bhutan
  2. Arunachal Pradesh and China
  3. Uttarakhand and China
  4. Himachal Pradesh and China

Answer: Sikkim (India) and Tibet/Bhutan

Explanation: Jelep La Pass (4,270 m) is on the Sikkim-China (Tibet) border and was a major trade route before 1962. Nathu La (4,310 m) is the currently open border crossing between Sikkim and Tibet.

15. The International Date Line roughly follows:

  1. The Prime Meridian (0°)
  2. The Equator
  3. The 180° meridian (with deviations for island nations)
  4. The Tropic of Cancer

Answer: The 180° meridian (with deviations for island nations)

Explanation: The International Date Line (IDL) roughly follows the 180° meridian in the Pacific Ocean, but deviates to avoid splitting countries. Going west across the IDL, the date advances by one day. Going east, the date goes back by one day.

16. The deepest ocean trench in the world is:

  1. Java Trench
  2. Puerto Rico Trench
  3. Mariana Trench (Pacific Ocean, ~11,034m deep)
  4. Tonga Trench

Answer: Mariana Trench (Pacific Ocean, ~11,034m deep)

Explanation: The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific Ocean is the world's deepest point at ~11,034m (Challenger Deep). James Cameron reached the bottom in 2012 in a submersible.

17. The International Date Line runs approximately along:

  1. 180° Meridian (with deviations)
  2. Prime Meridian (0°)
  3. Equator
  4. 23.5°N

Answer: 180° Meridian (with deviations)

Explanation: The International Date Line runs approximately along the 180° meridian in the Pacific Ocean, deviating to avoid splitting countries. Crossing it westward gains a day; eastward loses a day.

18. The Palk Bay lies between:

  1. Tamil Nadu coast and the Palk Strait to the east
  2. India and Sri Lanka on all sides
  3. Sri Lanka and Maldives
  4. Tamil Nadu and Kerala

Answer: Tamil Nadu coast and the Palk Strait to the east

Explanation: The Palk Bay is a shallow bay bounded by Tamil Nadu on the west, the Palk Strait on the east (between India and Sri Lanka), and Rameshwaram/Pamban Island on the south. Fishing is important here.

19. What is the largest ocean that borders the eastern coast of India?

  1. Pacific Ocean
  2. Indian Ocean
  3. Atlantic Ocean
  4. Arctic Ocean

Answer: Indian Ocean

Explanation: The Indian Ocean is the largest ocean bordering the eastern coast of India. It plays a significant role in India's climate and trade.

20. Which river in Tamil Nadu is known for its significant contribution to agriculture and is a major tributary of the Cauvery River?

  1. Vaigai River
  2. Tamiraparani River
  3. Palar River
  4. Bhavani River

Answer: Bhavani River

Explanation: The Bhavani River is a major tributary of the Cauvery River and contributes significantly to agriculture in Tamil Nadu.

21. What type of soil is predominantly found in the coastal regions of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Alluvial Soil
  2. Black Soil
  3. Red Soil
  4. Laterite Soil

Answer: Alluvial Soil

Explanation: Alluvial soil is predominantly found in the coastal regions of Tamil Nadu, which is rich in nutrients and supports agriculture.

22. Which port city in Tamil Nadu is one of the major ports in India and handles a significant amount of trade?

  1. Chennai
  2. Tuticorin
  3. Ennore
  4. Kattupalli

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai is one of the major ports in India and handles a significant amount of trade, contributing to the economy of Tamil Nadu.

23. What is the name of the monsoon that brings rainfall to Tamil Nadu during the month of October and November?

  1. Southwest Monsoon
  2. Northeast Monsoon
  3. Northwest Monsoon
  4. Southeast Monsoon

Answer: Northeast Monsoon

Explanation: The Northeast Monsoon brings rainfall to Tamil Nadu during the month of October and November, providing relief to the state.

24. Which mountain range runs along the western coast of India and affects the climate of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Western Ghats
  2. Eastern Ghats
  3. Himalayan Range
  4. Aravalli Range

Answer: Western Ghats

Explanation: The Western Ghats mountain range runs along the western coast of India and affects the climate of Tamil Nadu by blocking the southwest monsoon winds.

25. What is the name of the strait that separates the Indian subcontinent from Sri Lanka?

  1. Palk Strait
  2. Adam's Bridge
  3. Mannar Strait
  4. Laccadive Strait

Answer: Palk Strait

Explanation: The Palk Strait separates the Indian subcontinent from Sri Lanka and is a significant geographical feature.
