India Rivers MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked India Rivers questions to revise the TNPSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which is the longest river in India?

  1. Brahmaputra
  2. Yamuna
  3. Ganga
  4. Godavari

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga (Ganges), approximately 2,525 km long, is the longest river in India.

2. Which river is known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar'?

  1. Ganga
  2. Son
  3. Kosi
  4. Mahanadi

Answer: Kosi

Explanation: The Kosi river is known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar' due to devastating floods it causes by frequently changing its course.

3. Which is the longest river entirely within India?

  1. Ganga
  2. Godavari
  3. Mahanadi
  4. Narmada

Answer: Godavari

Explanation: The Godavari (1,465 km) is the longest river that flows entirely within India. The Ganga's source is in India but it also flows through Bangladesh.

4. The river Brahmaputra enters India from:

  1. China (Tibet)
  2. Nepal
  3. Bangladesh
  4. Bhutan

Answer: China (Tibet)

Explanation: The Brahmaputra (called Tsangpo in Tibet) originates in Tibet, China, and enters India through Arunachal Pradesh.

5. Which is the national river of India?

  1. Yamuna
  2. Ganga
  3. Brahmaputra
  4. Godavari

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: Ganga was declared the National River of India in 2008. It has deep religious, cultural, and economic significance.
