India Rivers MCQs with answers

26. Mettur Dam is built on which river?

  1. Kaveri (Cauvery)
  2. Bhavani
  3. Palar
  4. Vaigai

Answer: Kaveri (Cauvery)

Explanation: Mettur Dam (Stanley Reservoir) is built on the Kaveri river in Salem district, Tamil Nadu. Opened in 1934, it is one of India's largest dams and supplies water for irrigation in the Kaveri delta.

27. The Bhavani river is a tributary of:

  1. Kaveri (Cauvery)
  2. Palar
  3. Vaigai
  4. Ponnaiyar

Answer: Kaveri (Cauvery)

Explanation: The Bhavani river is a major left-bank tributary of the Kaveri (Cauvery). Bhavani Sagar dam provides water for Erode and Coimbatore districts. The river flows through the Nilgiris and Erode districts.

28. The Palar river flows through:

  1. Vellore and Kancheepuram districts into Bay of Bengal
  2. Coimbatore and Salem
  3. Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi
  4. Madurai and Dindigul

Answer: Vellore and Kancheepuram districts into Bay of Bengal

Explanation: The Palar river flows through Vellore and Kancheepuram districts of Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh before draining into the Bay of Bengal near Mahabalipuram. Vellore and Kancheepuram are major cities on its banks.

29. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is for protection of:

  1. Wetlands (marshes, lakes, rivers, peat bogs, coastal areas)
  2. Forests
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Mountain ecosystems

Answer: Wetlands (marshes, lakes, rivers, peat bogs, coastal areas)

Explanation: The Ramsar Convention on Wetlands (signed 1971, Ramsar, Iran) is an international treaty for conservation of wetlands. India has 75 Ramsar sites. Chilika Lake (Odisha) was India's first Ramsar site (1981).

30. India's first Ramsar site (wetland of international importance) was:

  1. Chilika Lake, Odisha (1981)
  2. Keoladeo Ghana, Rajasthan
  3. Wular Lake, J&K
  4. Loktak Lake, Manipur

Answer: Chilika Lake, Odisha (1981)

Explanation: Chilika Lake (Odisha) was designated as India's first Ramsar site in 1981, along with Keoladeo Ghana (Bharatpur, Rajasthan). Both were designated simultaneously. Chilika is Asia's largest brackish-water lake.

31. Shipki La Pass connects:

  1. India (Himachal Pradesh) and China (Tibet)
  2. India and Nepal
  3. India and Bhutan
  4. India and Pakistan

Answer: India (Himachal Pradesh) and China (Tibet)

Explanation: Shipki La Pass (5,669 m) in Kinnaur district, Himachal Pradesh, connects India with the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. The Sutlej river enters India through this pass.

32. The Bhakra Nangal Dam is on which river?

  1. Sutlej
  2. Ravi
  3. Beas
  4. Chenab

Answer: Sutlej

Explanation: Bhakra Nangal Dam (Bhakra Dam — 226 m high) is on the Sutlej river in Himachal Pradesh. The Nangal Dam is a downstream smaller dam. The reservoir is called Gobind Sagar Lake.

33. Nagarjunasagar Dam is on:

  1. Krishna river, Telangana/Andhra Pradesh border
  2. Godavari
  3. Tungabhadra
  4. Mahanadi

Answer: Krishna river, Telangana/Andhra Pradesh border

Explanation: Nagarjunasagar Dam on the Krishna river (Telangana-Andhra Pradesh border) has one of the world's largest masonry dams. The reservoir provides irrigation and drinking water to both states.

34. The Tungabhadra Dam (supporting Vijayanagara area) is in:

  1. Karnataka (Hospet)
  2. Andhra Pradesh
  3. Telangana
  4. Tamil Nadu

Answer: Karnataka (Hospet)

Explanation: Tungabhadra Dam is in Hospet, Karnataka on the Tungabhadra river (a tributary of Krishna). It provides irrigation water to Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. The Hampi (Vijayanagara) ruins are nearby.

35. The Idukki Dam (arch dam) in Kerala is on:

  1. Periyar river
  2. Kabani
  3. Chaliyar
  4. Pamba

Answer: Periyar river

Explanation: Idukki Dam is a double curvature arch dam on the Periyar river in Kerala's Idukki district. It is India's largest arch dam. The nearby Cheruthoni and Kulamavu dams form the Idukki Hydroelectric Project.

36. The Papanasam (Aazhiyar) dam in Tamil Nadu is on:

  1. Tamiraparani river
  2. Kaveri
  3. Bhavani
  4. Palar

Answer: Tamiraparani river

Explanation: Papanasam (Aazhiyar) dam is on the Tamiraparani river in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu. Shengottai-Papanasam area is known for the Courtallam (Kutralam) waterfalls fed by the Chittar river.

37. Which city is called the 'Manchester of India'?

  1. Ahmedabad
  2. Mumbai
  3. Coimbatore
  4. Kanpur

Answer: Ahmedabad

Explanation: Ahmedabad (Gujarat) is called the 'Manchester of India' for its cotton textile industry. Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu) is called the 'Manchester of South India' for similar reasons.

38. Coimbatore is called the 'Manchester of South India' for:

  1. Its cotton textile and spinning machinery industry
  2. Tea plantations
  3. IT industry
  4. Automobile industry

Answer: Its cotton textile and spinning machinery industry

Explanation: Coimbatore is Tamil Nadu's second-largest city, known as 'Manchester of South India' for its textile (cotton spinning) industry. It is also a major producer of wet grinders, motors, and pumps.

39. A 'watershed' or 'water divide' is:

  1. A ridge separating two river basins/drainage systems
  2. A dam or barrier
  3. A water body
  4. Flood plain of a river

Answer: A ridge separating two river basins/drainage systems

Explanation: A watershed (water divide) is a ridge of high land that separates two adjacent river drainage basins. Rain falling on one side flows to one river system, and on the other side to a different system.

40. The 'Delta' of a river is formed at:

  1. Mouth of the river where it meets the sea
  2. Source of the river
  3. Confluence of two rivers
  4. Where a river bends

Answer: Mouth of the river where it meets the sea

Explanation: A river delta is a landform created by deposition of sediment carried by a river as it enters a slower-moving body of water (sea, ocean, lake). Named after the Greek letter Delta (Δ) due to its triangular shape.

41. 'Lagoons' are:

  1. Shallow water bodies separated from the sea by a narrow barrier (sandbar/coral reef)
  2. Deep sea trenches
  3. Mountain lakes
  4. River ox-bow lakes

Answer: Shallow water bodies separated from the sea by a narrow barrier (sandbar/coral reef)

Explanation: Lagoons are shallow water bodies (lakes) separated from the larger sea/ocean by a narrow barrier of land (sandbar, barrier island, or coral reef). Chilika (Odisha) and Pulicat (Tamil Nadu-AP) are famous Indian lagoons.

42. Pulicat Lake (lagoon) is located on the border of:

  1. Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh
  2. Tamil Nadu and Kerala
  3. Odisha and West Bengal
  4. Andhra Pradesh and Odisha

Answer: Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh

Explanation: Pulicat Lake (India's second largest brackish water lagoon after Chilika) is on the Tamil Nadu-Andhra Pradesh border, north of Chennai. Sriharikota (ISRO launch center) is on the barrier island of this lagoon.

43. ISRO's satellite launch center (SDSC-SHAR) is at:

  1. Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh
  2. Chennai, Tamil Nadu
  3. Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
  4. Bengaluru, Karnataka

Answer: Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh

Explanation: ISRO's Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC-SHAR) is at Sriharikota island on Pulicat Lake, in Andhra Pradesh. It is India's main satellite launch center. ISRO's HQ and research centers are in Bengaluru.

44. The Andaman Islands are separated from Myanmar (Burma) by:

  1. Andaman Sea (and Ten Degree Channel from Nicobars)
  2. Bay of Bengal
  3. Palk Strait
  4. Indian Ocean

Answer: Andaman Sea (and Ten Degree Channel from Nicobars)

Explanation: The Andaman Islands lie in the Andaman Sea (part of the Bay of Bengal), east of the Indian subcontinent and west of Myanmar. They are closer to Myanmar and Thailand than to mainland India.

45. Which of the following lakes is famous for rose-colored water?

  1. Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan)
  2. Chilika Lake (Odisha)
  3. Wular Lake (J&K)
  4. Loktak Lake (Manipur)

Answer: Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan)

Explanation: Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) — India's largest inland saltwater lake — sometimes appears pinkish due to salt-loving microorganisms (halophytes). It is the main source of salt in Rajasthan and a Ramsar wetland site.

46. The Vellore district is known for:

  1. Tanning and leather industry, and the Vellore Fort
  2. Cotton textiles
  3. Silk weaving
  4. IT industry

Answer: Tanning and leather industry, and the Vellore Fort

Explanation: Vellore is famous for its leather and tanning industry — one of the largest leather clusters in India. Vellore Fort (16th century, Vijayanagara style) is the oldest fort in Tamil Nadu. CMC Hospital is also here.

47. Tuticorin (Thoothukudi) is known for:

  1. Pearl diving, salt industry, and V.O. Chidambaranar Port
  2. Cotton textiles
  3. Tea production
  4. IT sector

Answer: Pearl diving, salt industry, and V.O. Chidambaranar Port

Explanation: Tuticorin (Thoothukudi) is known for pearl diving (Gulf of Mannar), salt production (India's largest salt-producing area), V.O. Chidambaranar Port (major port), and thermal power plant.

48. Which district has Hogenakkal Falls (the 'Niagara of India')?

  1. Dharmapuri district
  2. Salem district
  3. Krishnagiri district
  4. Erode district

Answer: Dharmapuri district

Explanation: Hogenakkal Falls (on the Kaveri river) is in Dharmapuri district, Tamil Nadu. The falls are on the Tamil Nadu-Karnataka border. Hogenakkal means 'smoky rocks' in Kannada. It is called the Niagara of India.

49. The river Yamuna originates from:

  1. Yamunotri glacier, Uttarakhand
  2. Gangotri glacier
  3. Kedarnath
  4. Badrinath

Answer: Yamunotri glacier, Uttarakhand

Explanation: The Yamuna originates from the Yamunotri glacier in Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand. It flows southward through Uttarakhand, Haryana, Delhi, and UP before joining the Ganga at Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam).

50. Which river is known as the 'Lifeline of Rajasthan'?

  1. Indira Gandhi Canal (Luni river is the main river)
  2. Chambal
  3. Banas
  4. Mahi

Answer: Indira Gandhi Canal (Luni river is the main river)

Explanation: The Luni river is the only significant river in Rajasthan (drains into Rann of Kutch). But the Indira Gandhi Canal is often called the 'Lifeline of Rajasthan' as it brings water from the Sutlej-Beas to the Thar Desert.
