Environment MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Environment questions to revise the TNPSC Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 80 questions.

1. The study of living organisms and their interactions with the environment is called:

  1. Ecology
  2. Biology
  3. Zoology
  4. Entomology

Answer: Ecology

Explanation: Ecology studies the interactions between organisms and their environment (biotic and abiotic factors). Ernst Haeckel coined the term 'Ecology' in 1866. Ecosystem = community of organisms + their environment.

2. The term 'ecology' was coined by:

  1. Ernst Haeckel (1866) — from Greek 'oikos' (household) + 'logos' (study) — study of interactions between organisms and their environment
  2. Charles Darwin
  3. Gregor Mendel
  4. Aristotle

Answer: Ernst Haeckel (1866) — from Greek 'oikos' (household) + 'logos' (study) — study of interactions between organisms and their environment

Explanation: Ernst Haeckel (1866) coined 'ecology' (Ökologie in German) from Greek oikos (house/habitat) + logos (study). Ecology studies interactions between organisms and their abiotic environment. Key concepts: ecosystem, food web, energy flow, nutrient cycles, population dynamics, and biodiversity.

3. India's Samudrayaan project aims to:

  1. Send Indian astronauts (manned submersible 'Matsya 6000') to 6,000 m ocean depth to explore deep-sea resources
  2. Send astronauts to the Moon
  3. Develop ocean-based solar power
  4. Map ocean currents

Answer: Send Indian astronauts (manned submersible 'Matsya 6000') to 6,000 m ocean depth to explore deep-sea resources

Explanation: Samudrayaan (2021) is India's first manned deep-ocean mission: the 'Matsya 6000' submersible will carry 3 people to 6,000 m depth in the Indian Ocean to study polymetallic nodules (manganese, nickel, copper, cobalt) and deep-sea biodiversity. Operated by NIOT, Chennai.

4. Which greenhouse gas is primarily responsible for global warming?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Oxygen
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Hydrogen

Answer: Carbon dioxide

Explanation: Carbon dioxide is the most abundant anthropogenic greenhouse gas, trapping heat in the atmosphere.

5. The pH of acid rain is typically less than:

  1. 5.6
  2. 7.0
  3. 6.5
  4. 8.0

Answer: 5.6

Explanation: Normal rainwater has a pH of about 5.6 due to dissolved CO2; values below 5.6 indicate acid rain.
