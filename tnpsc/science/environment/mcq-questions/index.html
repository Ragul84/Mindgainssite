Environment MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Environment questions to revise the TNPSC Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 80 questions.

26. Which national park in Tamil Nadu is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a Biosphere Reserve?

  1. Mudumalai National Park
  2. Indira Gandhi (Annamalai) National Park
  3. Gulf of Mannar Marine National Park
  4. Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve

Answer: Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve

Explanation: The Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the first biosphere reserve in India, located in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Karnataka.

27. Which gas is primarily responsible for the greenhouse effect?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Oxygen
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Hydrogen

Answer: Carbon dioxide

Explanation: Carbon dioxide traps infrared radiation, making it the main greenhouse gas from human activities.

28. The layer of the atmosphere that protects Earth from harmful ultraviolet radiation is the

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Stratosphere

Explanation: The ozone layer in the stratosphere absorbs most of the Sun's ultraviolet radiation.

29. Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) is a measure of

  1. Oxygen required to decompose organic matter in water
  2. Dissolved oxygen in water
  3. Acidity of water
  4. Turbidity of water

Answer: Oxygen required to decompose organic matter in water

Explanation: High BOD indicates high organic pollution, as more oxygen is needed for microbial decomposition.

30. Which of the following is a non-renewable energy source?

  1. Solar
  2. Wind
  3. Natural gas
  4. Tidal

Answer: Natural gas

Explanation: Natural gas is a fossil fuel formed over millions of years and is finite in supply.

31. The term 'Eutrophication' is associated with

  1. Nutrient enrichment in water bodies
  2. Soil erosion
  3. Air pollution
  4. Deforestation

Answer: Nutrient enrichment in water bodies

Explanation: Excess nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus cause algal blooms, leading to oxygen depletion.

32. The main cause of acid rain is

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides
  3. Chlorofluorocarbons
  4. Methane

Answer: Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides

Explanation: SO₂ and NOx react with water vapour to form sulphuric and nitric acids, lowering pH of rain.

33. Which of the following is a bioindicator of air pollution?

  1. Lichens
  2. Ferns
  3. Algae
  4. Mosses

Answer: Lichens

Explanation: Lichens are highly sensitive to sulphur dioxide and their absence indicates poor air quality.

34. The 'Montreal Protocol' is related to

  1. Climate change
  2. Ozone depletion
  3. Biodiversity
  4. Desertification

Answer: Ozone depletion

Explanation: The Montreal Protocol aims to phase out substances that deplete the stratospheric ozone layer.

35. The 'Red Data Book' contains a list of

  1. Endangered species
  2. Extinct species
  3. Invasive species
  4. Medicinal plants

Answer: Endangered species

Explanation: The IUCN Red List categorises species at risk of extinction, helping conservation efforts.

36. Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD) is a measure of:

  1. Oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic waste
  2. Oxygen dissolved in water
  3. Carbon dioxide produced by aquatic plants
  4. Oxygen released by phytoplankton

Answer: Oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic waste

Explanation: BOD measures the amount of oxygen needed by aerobic microorganisms to break down organic matter in water, indicating pollution level.

37. Which of the following gases is NOT a greenhouse gas?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Methane
  3. Nitrous oxide
  4. Oxygen

Answer: Oxygen

Explanation: Oxygen (O2) does not absorb infrared radiation and hence is not a greenhouse gas; the others are potent greenhouse gases.

38. The phenomenon of 'biomagnification' is most commonly associated with which pollutant?

  1. DDT
  2. Sulfur dioxide
  3. Carbon monoxide
  4. Lead

Answer: DDT

Explanation: DDT is a persistent organic pollutant that increases in concentration up the food chain through biomagnification.

39. Acid rain is primarily caused by emissions of:

  1. CO2 and CH4
  2. SO2 and NOx
  3. CFCs and O3
  4. CO and PM

Answer: SO2 and NOx

Explanation: Oxides of sulfur (SO2) and nitrogen (NOx) react with water vapour to form sulfuric and nitric acids, leading to acid rain.

40. The ozone layer is located in which atmospheric layer?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Stratosphere

Explanation: The ozone layer lies in the stratosphere, approximately 15–35 km above Earth’s surface, absorbing harmful UV radiation.

41. Which of the following is a renewable energy source?

  1. Natural gas
  2. Nuclear power
  3. Solar energy
  4. Coal

Answer: Solar energy

Explanation: Solar energy is inexhaustible and naturally replenished, unlike fossil fuels and nuclear fuels which are non-renewable.

42. The enrichment of water bodies with nutrients leading to algal blooms is called:

  1. Eutrophication
  2. Biomagnification
  3. Bioaccumulation
  4. Denitrification

Answer: Eutrophication

Explanation: Eutrophication is the process where excess nutrients (nitrates, phosphates) cause rapid algal growth, depleting oxygen in water.

43. Which of the following is an example of a non-point source of water pollution?

  1. Industrial discharge pipe
  2. Agricultural runoff
  3. Sewage treatment plant outfall
  4. Oil spill from a tanker

Answer: Agricultural runoff

Explanation: Non-point source pollution comes from diffuse sources like agricultural runoff, while the others are point sources from identifiable outlets.

44. The Montreal Protocol is an international treaty aimed at protecting the environment by phasing out substances that deplete:

  1. Ozone layer
  2. Carbon dioxide
  3. Biodiversity
  4. Water resources

Answer: Ozone layer

Explanation: The Montreal Protocol targets ozone-depleting substances such as CFCs, halons, and other chemicals that destroy stratospheric ozone.

45. Which type of pollution is most directly linked to the use of refrigerants like CFCs?

  1. Air pollution
  2. Water pollution
  3. Ozone depletion
  4. Noise pollution

Answer: Ozone depletion

Explanation: Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) release chlorine atoms in the stratosphere, catalytically destroying ozone molecules.

46. Which of the following gases is primarily responsible for the depletion of the ozone layer in the Earth's stratosphere?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Chlorofluorocarbons
  3. Methane
  4. Nitrous oxide

Answer: Chlorofluorocarbons

Explanation: Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) release chlorine atoms that catalytically destroy ozone molecules in the stratosphere.

47. The term 'Biochemical Oxygen Demand' (BOD) is a measure of which of the following in a water body?

  1. Dissolved oxygen required by microorganisms
  2. Total dissolved solids
  3. Acidity of water
  4. Concentration of heavy metals

Answer: Dissolved oxygen required by microorganisms

Explanation: BOD measures the amount of dissolved oxygen needed by aerobic microorganisms to decompose organic matter in water.

48. Which of the following is a renewable source of energy derived from organic matter, such as plant and animal waste?

  1. Natural gas
  2. Nuclear energy
  3. Biomass
  4. Coal

Answer: Biomass

Explanation: Biomass energy is obtained from organic materials like wood, agricultural residues, and animal dung, and is renewable.

49. The 'Greenhouse Effect' is primarily caused by the increased concentration of which gas in the atmosphere?

  1. Oxygen
  2. Nitrogen
  3. Carbon dioxide
  4. Hydrogen

Answer: Carbon dioxide

Explanation: Carbon dioxide traps infrared radiation from the Earth's surface, leading to the greenhouse effect and global warming.

50. Which of the following is a primary pollutant that forms 'photochemical smog' in the presence of sunlight?

  1. Sulfur dioxide
  2. Nitrogen oxides
  3. Carbon monoxide
  4. Lead particles

Answer: Nitrogen oxides

Explanation: Nitrogen oxides (NOx) react with volatile organic compounds in sunlight to form photochemical smog.
