26. Which part of the human brain is primarily responsible for maintaining balance and coordinating voluntary movements?

  1. Cerebellum
  2. Cerebrum
  3. Medulla oblongata
  4. Hypothalamus

Answer: Cerebellum

Explanation: The cerebellum coordinates muscle movements and maintains posture and balance.

27. In the human respiratory system, the process of gas exchange between alveoli and blood occurs through which mechanism?

  1. Diffusion
  2. Osmosis
  3. Active transport
  4. Facilitated diffusion

Answer: Diffusion

Explanation: Oxygen and carbon dioxide move across alveolar and capillary walls by simple diffusion due to concentration gradients.

28. Which blood vessel carries deoxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs?

  1. Pulmonary artery
  2. Pulmonary vein
  3. Aorta
  4. Vena cava

Answer: Pulmonary artery

Explanation: The pulmonary artery transports deoxygenated blood from the right ventricle to the lungs for oxygenation.

29. The enzyme pepsin, which digests proteins in the stomach, is secreted in an inactive form called:

  1. Pepsinogen
  2. Trypsinogen
  3. Rennin
  4. Gastrin

Answer: Pepsinogen

Explanation: Pepsinogen is activated by hydrochloric acid to form active pepsin, preventing self-digestion of stomach lining.

30. Which hormone is secreted by the beta cells of the pancreas and helps lower blood glucose levels?

  1. Insulin
  2. Glucagon
  3. Somatostatin
  4. Cortisol

Answer: Insulin

Explanation: Insulin promotes cellular uptake of glucose and glycogenesis, reducing blood sugar.

31. The structural and functional unit of the human kidney responsible for filtering blood is called:

  1. Nephron
  2. Neuron
  3. Alveolus
  4. Glomerulus

Answer: Nephron

Explanation: Each nephron consists of a glomerulus and tubule, performing filtration, reabsorption, and secretion.

32. Which of the following is a long bone in the human body?

  1. Femur
  2. Rib
  3. Skull bone
  4. Vertebra

Answer: Femur

Explanation: The femur (thigh bone) is a typical long bone with a shaft and two ends.

33. The sense organ responsible for detecting sound vibrations and transmitting them as nerve impulses is the:

  1. Cochlea
  2. Retina
  3. Olfactory epithelium
  4. Taste buds

Answer: Cochlea

Explanation: The cochlea in the inner ear converts mechanical vibrations into electrical signals sent to the brain.

34. Which type of muscle is involuntary and found in the walls of internal organs like the stomach and intestines?

  1. Smooth muscle
  2. Skeletal muscle
  3. Cardiac muscle
  4. Striated muscle

Answer: Smooth muscle

Explanation: Smooth muscle lacks striations and is controlled by the autonomic nervous system for peristalsis.

35. In the human reproductive system, the primary male sex hormone produced by the testes is:

  1. Testosterone
  2. Estrogen
  3. Progesterone
  4. LH (luteinizing hormone)

Answer: Testosterone

Explanation: Testosterone is responsible for development of male secondary sexual characteristics and sperm production.

36. Which of the following is the primary function of the sinoatrial (SA) node in the human heart?

  1. Pumping blood into the ventricles
  2. Generating electrical impulses to initiate heartbeat
  3. Preventing backflow of blood into the atria
  4. Supplying oxygenated blood to the heart muscle

Answer: Generating electrical impulses to initiate heartbeat

Explanation: The SA node acts as the natural pacemaker, generating electrical impulses that set the rhythm for the heartbeat.

37. In which part of the human nephron does the maximum reabsorption of glucose and amino acids occur?

  1. Distal convoluted tubule
  2. Loop of Henle
  3. Proximal convoluted tubule
  4. Collecting duct

Answer: Proximal convoluted tubule

Explanation: The proximal convoluted tubule is responsible for reabsorbing nearly all glucose and amino acids from the filtrate back into the blood.

38. The hormone responsible for regulating the calcium level in human blood is secreted by which gland?

  1. Thyroid
  2. Parathyroid
  3. Adrenal
  4. Pituitary

Answer: Parathyroid

Explanation: Parathyroid hormone (PTH), secreted by the parathyroid glands, increases blood calcium levels by acting on bones, kidneys, and intestines.

39. Which of the following blood vessels carries deoxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs?

  1. Pulmonary vein
  2. Aorta
  3. Pulmonary artery
  4. Vena cava

Answer: Pulmonary artery

Explanation: The pulmonary artery is the only artery that carries deoxygenated blood, transporting it from the right ventricle to the lungs for oxygenation.

40. In human digestion, the enzyme pepsin acts on which nutrient in the stomach?

  1. Carbohydrates
  2. Fats
  3. Proteins
  4. Vitamins

Answer: Proteins

Explanation: Pepsin is a proteolytic enzyme that breaks down proteins into smaller peptides in the acidic environment of the stomach.

41. Which type of white blood cell is primarily involved in the production of antibodies?

  1. Neutrophils
  2. Eosinophils
  3. Monocytes
  4. Lymphocytes

Answer: Lymphocytes

Explanation: Lymphocytes, specifically B-cells, are responsible for producing antibodies as part of the adaptive immune response.

42. The condition 'hypoxia' in humans refers to a deficiency of oxygen at the tissue level. Which of the following is a common cause?

  1. Excess carbon dioxide in blood
  2. Reduced red blood cell count
  3. High blood pressure
  4. Increased platelet count

Answer: Reduced red blood cell count

Explanation: Reduced red blood cell count (anemia) decreases the oxygen-carrying capacity of blood, leading to tissue hypoxia.

43. Which of the following is the correct sequence of air passage during inhalation in humans?

  1. Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli
  2. Bronchi → Trachea → Alveoli → Bronchioles
  3. Alveoli → Bronchioles → Bronchi → Trachea
  4. Trachea → Alveoli → Bronchioles → Bronchi

Answer: Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli

Explanation: Air enters the trachea, then passes into the bronchi, further into bronchioles, and finally reaches the alveoli for gas exchange.

44. Which vitamin is synthesized by the human skin when exposed to sunlight?

  1. Vitamin A
  2. Vitamin B12
  3. Vitamin D
  4. Vitamin K

Answer: Vitamin D

Explanation: Ultraviolet B (UVB) rays from sunlight trigger the conversion of 7-dehydrocholesterol in the skin into vitamin D3.

45. Which blood vessel carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart?

  1. Pulmonary artery
  2. Pulmonary vein
  3. Aorta
  4. Vena cava

Answer: Pulmonary vein

Explanation: The pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the left atrium of the heart.

46. Which part of the human brain is primarily responsible for balance and coordination?

  1. Cerebrum
  2. Cerebellum
  3. Medulla oblongata
  4. Hypothalamus

Answer: Cerebellum

Explanation: The cerebellum coordinates voluntary movements and maintains balance and posture.

47. The hormone insulin is secreted by which gland?

  1. Pituitary
  2. Thyroid
  3. Pancreas
  4. Adrenal

Answer: Pancreas

Explanation: Insulin is produced by the beta cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas.

48. Which vitamin is synthesized by human skin upon exposure to sunlight?

  1. Vitamin A
  2. Vitamin B
  3. Vitamin C
  4. Vitamin D

Answer: Vitamin D

Explanation: Ultraviolet B rays from sunlight trigger vitamin D synthesis in the skin.

49. In human blood, which component is primarily responsible for clotting?

  1. Red blood cells
  2. White blood cells
  3. Platelets
  4. Plasma

Answer: Platelets

Explanation: Platelets (thrombocytes) aggregate at injury sites and release clotting factors.

50. Which part of the human digestive system is primarily responsible for water absorption?

  1. Stomach
  2. Small intestine
  3. Large intestine
  4. Esophagus

Answer: Large intestine

Explanation: The large intestine absorbs water and electrolytes from undigested food, forming feces.
