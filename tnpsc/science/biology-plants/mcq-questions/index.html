Biology Plants MCQs with answers

26. The plant hormone that induces stomatal closure during water stress is

  1. Auxin
  2. Gibberellin
  3. Cytokinin
  4. Abscisic acid

Answer: Abscisic acid

Explanation: Abscisic acid (ABA) promotes stomatal closure under drought conditions to reduce water loss.

27. In C4 plants, the first stable product of carbon dioxide fixation is

  1. 3-Phosphoglycerate
  2. Oxaloacetate
  3. Malate
  4. Ribulose bisphosphate

Answer: Oxaloacetate

Explanation: C4 plants initially fix CO2 into oxaloacetate in mesophyll cells before transferring it to bundle sheath cells.

28. The part of the root that increases the surface area for water absorption is

  1. Root hair
  2. Xylem
  3. Phloem
  4. Stomata

Answer: Root hair

Explanation: Root hairs are epidermal extensions that greatly enhance water and mineral uptake from the soil.

29. The type of vascular bundle found in dicot stems that allows secondary growth is

  1. Open
  2. Closed
  3. Concentric
  4. Radial

Answer: Open

Explanation: Open vascular bundles have cambium between xylem and phloem, enabling secondary growth in dicots.

30. An example of a modified stem is

  1. Carrot
  2. Potato
  3. Radish
  4. Sweet potato

Answer: Potato

Explanation: Potato is a tuber, a modified stem for storage; carrot, radish, and sweet potato are modified roots.

31. The process of water loss from aerial parts of plants in the form of water vapor is called

  1. Transpiration
  2. Evaporation
  3. Guttation
  4. Respiration

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the evaporation of water from plant leaves and stems through stomata and lenticels.

32. The central metal atom in the chlorophyll molecule is

  1. Magnesium
  2. Iron
  3. Cobalt
  4. Zinc

Answer: Magnesium

Explanation: Chlorophyll contains a porphyrin ring with a magnesium ion at its center, essential for photosynthesis.

33. The primary site of photosynthesis in plants is the

  1. Leaf
  2. Root
  3. Stem
  4. Flower

Answer: Leaf

Explanation: Leaves contain abundant chloroplasts in mesophyll tissues where photosynthesis mainly occurs.

34. The process by which ATP is synthesized using light energy in chloroplasts is known as

  1. Photophosphorylation
  2. Glycolysis
  3. Krebs cycle
  4. Calvin cycle

Answer: Photophosphorylation

Explanation: Photophosphorylation uses light energy to convert ADP and inorganic phosphate into ATP in the thylakoid membranes.

35. Which of the following pigments is primarily responsible for capturing light energy during photosynthesis?

  1. Chlorophyll a
  2. Chlorophyll b
  3. Xanthophyll
  4. Carotene

Answer: Chlorophyll a

Explanation: Chlorophyll a is the primary photosynthetic pigment that directly participates in the light reactions.

36. The process of loss of water in the form of water vapour from the aerial parts of a plant is called:

  1. Transpiration
  2. Guttation
  3. Evaporation
  4. Exudation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the loss of water vapour mainly through stomata, lenticels, and cuticle.

37. Which plant hormone is responsible for promoting cell division and delaying senescence?

  1. Cytokinin
  2. Auxin
  3. Gibberellin
  4. Abscisic acid

Answer: Cytokinin

Explanation: Cytokinins promote cell division, delay leaf senescence, and stimulate lateral bud growth.

38. In which part of the plant does the Calvin cycle of photosynthesis occur?

  1. Stroma of chloroplast
  2. Thylakoid membrane
  3. Granum
  4. Cytoplasm

Answer: Stroma of chloroplast

Explanation: The Calvin cycle (dark reaction) takes place in the stroma of chloroplasts.

39. The tissue responsible for the secondary growth in dicot stems is:

  1. Vascular cambium
  2. Cork cambium
  3. Interfascicular cambium
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Both vascular cambium and cork cambium (phellogen) contribute to secondary growth; interfascicular cambium is part of vascular cambium.

40. Which of the following is a characteristic feature of C4 plants?

  1. Kranz anatomy
  2. Single cell type in bundle sheath
  3. Photorespiration is high
  4. Calvin cycle occurs in mesophyll cells

Answer: Kranz anatomy

Explanation: C4 plants exhibit Kranz anatomy with distinct bundle sheath cells surrounding vascular bundles.

41. The male gametophyte in angiosperms is represented by:

  1. Pollen grain
  2. Anther
  3. Stamen
  4. Microspore mother cell

Answer: Pollen grain

Explanation: The pollen grain is the male gametophyte that develops from a microspore.

42. Which of the following is a modified stem used for food storage?

  1. Potato
  2. Carrot
  3. Sweet potato
  4. Radish

Answer: Potato

Explanation: Potato is a modified stem (tuber) for storage; carrot, sweet potato, and radish are modified roots.

43. The phenomenon of opening and closing of stomata is regulated by:

  1. Guard cells
  2. Epidermal cells
  3. Subsidiary cells
  4. Mesophyll cells

Answer: Guard cells

Explanation: Guard cells control stomatal movement by changing turgor pressure due to ion and water movement.

44. Consider the following statements regarding transpiration pull: 1. It is a consequence of cohesion and adhesion of water molecules. 2. It is driven by root pressure. 3. It is responsible for ascent of sap in tall trees. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Transpiration pull is based on cohesion-tension theory; root pressure is not the main driver for tall trees.

45. Which plant hormone is primarily responsible for stomatal closure during water stress?

  1. Auxin
  2. Gibberellin
  3. Abscisic acid
  4. Ethylene

Answer: Abscisic acid

Explanation: Abscisic acid (ABA) triggers stomatal closure to conserve water under drought conditions.

46. Consider the following statements about photorespiration: 1. It occurs in C3 plants when rubisco fixes oxygen instead of CO2. 2. It consumes ATP and releases CO2. 3. It is enhanced under high temperature and low CO2 conditions. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All three statements accurately describe photorespiration, a wasteful process in C3 plants.

47. The primary function of lenticels in woody stems is:

  1. Transpiration
  2. Gas exchange
  3. Absorption of water
  4. Storage of food

Answer: Gas exchange

Explanation: Lenticels are pores in the bark that allow gas exchange between internal tissues and the atmosphere.

48. Assertion (A): In a typical dicotyledonous root, the vascular bundles are radial in arrangement. Reason (R): The root has reduced pith and no endodermis. Choose the correct code.

  1. Both A and R are true and R explains A
  2. Both A and R are true but R does not explain A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: A is true but R is false

Explanation: Dicot root has radial vascular bundles (true), but endodermis is present; thus R is false.

49. Which of the following is an example of a modified stem for food storage?

  1. Carrot
  2. Potato
  3. Sweet potato
  4. Radish

Answer: Potato

Explanation: Potato is a stem tuber; carrot, sweet potato, and radish are modified roots.

50. Consider the following statements regarding double fertilization in angiosperms: 1. One sperm fuses with egg to form zygote. 2. The other sperm fuses with secondary nucleus to form triploid endosperm. 3. Double fertilization results in a diploid zygote and triploid endosperm. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Explanation: All statements correctly describe the process and products of double fertilization.
