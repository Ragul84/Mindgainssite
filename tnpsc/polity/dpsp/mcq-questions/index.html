DPSP MCQs with answers

26. The Swaran Singh Committee (1976) recommended the inclusion of:

  1. Fundamental Duties in the Constitution
  2. Directive Principles relating to environment
  3. OBC reservations
  4. Anti-defection law

Answer: Fundamental Duties in the Constitution

Explanation: The Swaran Singh Committee recommended the inclusion of Fundamental Duties in the Constitution. Acting on this, the 42nd Amendment (1976) added Part IV-A with Article 51A containing 10 Fundamental Duties.

27. The concept of 'Welfare State' in the Indian Constitution is reflected in:

  1. Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV)
  2. Fundamental Rights (Part III)
  3. Preamble only
  4. Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A)

Answer: Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV)

Explanation: The Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV, Arts 36-51) embody the concept of a Welfare State — they direct the State to promote welfare of the people by securing social, economic, and political justice.

28. Which DPSP directs the State to secure a Uniform Civil Code for all citizens?

  1. Article 40
  2. Article 44
  3. Article 45
  4. Article 48

Answer: Article 44

Explanation: Article 44 directs the State to endeavour to secure a Uniform Civil Code for all citizens throughout India.

29. Article 368 of the Constitution deals with the power of Parliament to:

  1. Dissolve the Lok Sabha
  2. Amend the Constitution
  3. Impeach the President
  4. Remove the Chief Justice

Answer: Amend the Constitution

Explanation: Article 368 provides the procedure for amendment of the Constitution. Some provisions require simple majority, others a special majority, and some also require ratification by State legislatures.

30. The procedure for amending the Constitution is in:

  1. Article 360
  2. Article 368
  3. Article 356
  4. Article 370

Answer: Article 368

Explanation: Article 368 deals with the power of Parliament to amend the Constitution. There are three methods: simple majority (certain provisions), special majority (2/3 of members present and voting + majority of total membership), and special majority + ratification by half the state legislatures.

31. Financial Emergency (Article 360) has been invoked in India:

  1. Never
  2. Once (1975)
  3. Twice
  4. Three times

Answer: Never

Explanation: Financial Emergency under Article 360 has NEVER been proclaimed in India since independence. It can be proclaimed when the financial stability or credit of India or any state is threatened.

32. Which DPSP article directs the State to take steps to separate the judiciary from the executive?

  1. Article 39A
  2. Article 43A
  3. Article 50
  4. Article 51

Answer: Article 50

Explanation: Article 50 directs the State to take steps to separate the judiciary from the executive in the public services of the State.

33. The Directive Principles of State Policy are:

  1. Enforceable by courts
  2. Non-enforceable but fundamental in governance
  3. Enforceable only in emergencies
  4. Applicable only to the Central Government

Answer: Non-enforceable but fundamental in governance

Explanation: DPSPs (Part IV) are non-justiciable — courts cannot enforce them — but they are 'fundamental in the governance of the country' and guide State policy.

34. Part IV-A of the Constitution deals with:

  1. Fundamental Duties
  2. Directive Principles
  3. Fundamental Rights
  4. Union Territories

Answer: Fundamental Duties

Explanation: Part IV-A (Article 51A) contains Fundamental Duties, added by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment (1976) on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.

35. Article 368 deals with:

  1. Power to amend the Constitution
  2. Emergency provisions
  3. Citizenship
  4. Election of President

Answer: Power to amend the Constitution

Explanation: Article 368 (Part XX) provides for the amendment of the Constitution. Parliament can amend any provision of the Constitution (subject to the Basic Structure doctrine established in Kesavananda Bharati case).

36. Under which part of the Constitution of India are the Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) enshrined?

  1. Part III
  2. Part IV
  3. Part IV-A
  4. Part V

Answer: Part IV

Explanation: DPSP are contained in Part IV (Articles 36-51) of the Indian Constitution.

37. The concept of Directive Principles of State Policy was borrowed from the Constitution of which country?

  1. USA
  2. Ireland
  3. Canada
  4. Australia

Answer: Ireland

Explanation: The DPSP were inspired by the Irish Constitution, which itself drew from the Spanish Constitution.

38. Which of the following statements about Directive Principles of State Policy is correct?

  1. They are enforceable by courts
  2. They are fundamental for governance but non-justiciable
  3. They can be amended only by a special majority
  4. They are superior to Fundamental Rights

Answer: They are fundamental for governance but non-justiciable

Explanation: DPSP are non-justiciable, meaning they cannot be enforced by courts, but are fundamental in the governance of the country.

39. Which article of the Constitution of India directs the State to organise village panchayats?

  1. Article 38
  2. Article 39
  3. Article 40
  4. Article 41

Answer: Article 40

Explanation: Article 40 directs the State to take steps to organise village panchayats and endow them with necessary powers.

40. Which of the following Directive Principles is based on Gandhian philosophy?

  1. Promotion of international peace and security
  2. Provision of free legal aid
  3. Prohibition of cow slaughter
  4. Separation of judiciary from executive

Answer: Prohibition of cow slaughter

Explanation: Prohibition of cow slaughter (Article 48) reflects Gandhian ideals of cow protection and rural economy.

41. Which Constitutional Amendment added Article 48A (Protection of environment) to the Directive Principles?

  1. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
  2. 44th Amendment Act, 1978
  3. 73rd Amendment Act, 1992
  4. 86th Amendment Act, 2002

Answer: 42nd Amendment Act, 1976

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Article 48A to protect and improve the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife.

42. Which article of DPSP deals with the Uniform Civil Code for citizens?

  1. Article 42
  2. Article 43
  3. Article 44
  4. Article 45

Answer: Article 44

Explanation: Article 44 directs the State to endeavour to secure a Uniform Civil Code throughout the territory of India.

43. The Directive Principle under Article 39A is related to:

  1. Equal pay for equal work
  2. Free legal aid
  3. Right to education
  4. Living wage for workers

Answer: Free legal aid

Explanation: Article 39A mandates the State to provide free legal aid to ensure equal justice and opportunities.

44. Which of the following pairs correctly matches a Directive Principle with its classification?

  1. Article 43 – Liberal Intellectual
  2. Article 39 – Gandhian
  3. Article 38 – Socialistic
  4. Article 46 – Liberal Intellectual

Answer: Article 38 – Socialistic

Explanation: Article 38 (promote welfare of the people) is a socialistic principle, aiming to reduce inequalities.

45. In the case of Minerva Mills v. Union of India (1980), the Supreme Court held that:

  1. Fundamental Rights are subordinate to DPSP
  2. DPSP are beyond judicial review
  3. Harmony between Fundamental Rights and DPSP is the basic structure
  4. DPSP can override Fundamental Rights during emergency

Answer: Harmony between Fundamental Rights and DPSP is the basic structure

Explanation: The Court ruled that the balance and harmony between Fundamental Rights and DPSP is part of the basic structure of the Constitution.

46. Which part of the Indian Constitution contains the Directive Principles of State Policy?

  1. Part III
  2. Part IV
  3. Part IV-A
  4. Part IX

Answer: Part IV

Explanation: Directive Principles of State Policy are enshrined in Part IV (Articles 36-51) of the Indian Constitution.

47. The Directive Principles of State Policy are borrowed from the Constitution of which country?

  1. USA
  2. UK
  3. Ireland
  4. Canada

Answer: Ireland

Explanation: The DPSP were borrowed from the Irish Constitution, which itself drew from the Spanish Constitution.

48. Which of the following is NOT a Directive Principle of State Policy?

  1. Right to work
  2. Uniform civil code
  3. Right to constitutional remedies
  4. Free legal aid

Answer: Right to constitutional remedies

Explanation: Right to constitutional remedies (Article 32) is a Fundamental Right, not a Directive Principle.

49. The aim of Directive Principles is to establish a ______ in India.

  1. welfare state
  2. sovereign state
  3. police state
  4. theocratic state

Answer: welfare state

Explanation: Directive Principles aim to create a welfare state by providing social and economic justice.

50. Which article of the Constitution directs the state to promote cottage industries in rural areas?

  1. Article 40
  2. Article 43
  3. Article 46
  4. Article 47

Answer: Article 43

Explanation: Article 43 directs the state to promote cottage industries on an individual or cooperative basis in rural areas.
