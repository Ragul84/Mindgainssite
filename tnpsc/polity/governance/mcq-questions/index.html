26. UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) conducts examinations for:

  1. State government jobs
  2. All India Services and Central Services Group A & B (civil services exam, IAS, IPS, IFS, etc.)
  3. Only IAS examination
  4. Defence recruitment only

Answer: All India Services and Central Services Group A & B (civil services exam, IAS, IPS, IFS, etc.)

Explanation: UPSC (Article 315) conducts Civil Services Examination (IAS, IPS, IFS), combined defence services, engineering services, NDA, etc. for Group A and B Central Services and All India Services. State PSCs conduct state service exams.

27. Under Article 352, National Emergency can be proclaimed on grounds of:

  1. War, External Aggression, or Armed Rebellion
  2. Natural Disaster only
  3. Financial crisis only
  4. Constitutional breakdown only

Answer: War, External Aggression, or Armed Rebellion

Explanation: National Emergency (Article 352) can be proclaimed on grounds of: (i) War, (ii) External Aggression, or (iii) Armed Rebellion (formerly 'internal disturbance', changed by 44th Amendment).

28. The 44th Constitutional Amendment (1978) changed the term 'Internal Disturbance' in Article 352 to:

  1. Armed Rebellion
  2. Civil War
  3. Insurgency
  4. Revolution

Answer: Armed Rebellion

Explanation: 44th Amendment (1978) changed 'internal disturbance' to 'armed rebellion' in Article 352, making it harder to impose National Emergency on minor internal disturbances.

29. The National Commission for Scheduled Castes is a:

  1. Constitutional body under Article 338
  2. Statutory body under SC/ST Act
  3. Advisory body under Planning Commission
  4. Executive body under Home Ministry

Answer: Constitutional body under Article 338

Explanation: Article 338 establishes the National Commission for Scheduled Castes as a constitutional body. It investigates matters relating to constitutional and statutory safeguards for SCs.

30. The concept of 'Inter-State Council' is provided in:

  1. Article 263
  2. Article 264
  3. Article 280
  4. Article 262

Answer: Article 263

Explanation: Article 263 provides for an Inter-State Council for coordinating relations between states and between states and the Centre. The Inter-State Council was set up in 1990.

31. How many types of majority are needed for Constitutional Amendments?

  1. Three types
  2. Two types
  3. One type
  4. Four types

Answer: Three types

Explanation: Constitutional amendments require: (1) Simple majority (for some provisions like Article 5), (2) Special majority: 2/3rd present and voting + majority of total membership (most amendments), (3) Special majority + ratification by half the states.

32. Part II of the Constitution (Articles 5-11) deals with:

  1. Citizenship
  2. Fundamental Rights
  3. Directive Principles
  4. Fundamental Duties

Answer: Citizenship

Explanation: Part II (Articles 5-11) deals with Citizenship — at the commencement of the Constitution. The details of citizenship are governed by the Citizenship Act 1955.

33. Article 16(4) allows:

  1. Reservation in government jobs for backward classes
  2. Reservation in education
  3. Gender-based reservation
  4. Geographic-based reservation

Answer: Reservation in government jobs for backward classes

Explanation: Article 16(4): Nothing in Article 16 shall prevent the State from making provision for reservation of appointments for any backward class of citizens not adequately represented in services.

34. The EWS (Economically Weaker Sections) 10% reservation was introduced by:

  1. 103rd Constitutional Amendment (2019)
  2. 102nd Amendment
  3. 101st Amendment
  4. 105th Amendment

Answer: 103rd Constitutional Amendment (2019)

Explanation: 103rd Constitutional Amendment (January 2019) introduced 10% reservation for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) among general category — through new Articles 15(6) and 16(6).

35. GST Council is chaired by:

  1. Union Finance Minister
  2. Prime Minister
  3. RBI Governor
  4. Niti Aayog CEO

Answer: Union Finance Minister

Explanation: GST Council (Article 279A, inserted by 101st Amendment 2016) is chaired by the Union Finance Minister. Members include Finance Ministers of all states.

36. Reservation of seats for women in Panchayats is mandated by:

  1. Article 243D — not less than 1/3rd
  2. Article 243C — not less than 1/4th
  3. Article 243E — 50%
  4. Article 243B — 40%

Answer: Article 243D — not less than 1/3rd

Explanation: Article 243D mandates reservation of not less than one-third of seats for women in Panchayats at all levels. Many States (including Tamil Nadu) have increased this to 50% by State law.

37. Article 15(4) allows the State to make special provisions for:

  1. Socially and educationally backward classes and SC/STs
  2. Economic backward classes
  3. Women only
  4. Linguistic minorities

Answer: Socially and educationally backward classes and SC/STs

Explanation: Article 15(4) — inserted by 1st Amendment (1951) — allows the State to make special provisions for the advancement of socially and educationally backward classes of citizens, SCs and STs.

38. Which Article deals with the official language of the Union?

  1. Article 343
  2. Article 350
  3. Article 351
  4. Article 348

Answer: Article 343

Explanation: Article 343 states Hindi in Devanagari script shall be the official language of the Union. English was also continued as an official language by the Official Languages Act, 1963.

39. The Goods and Services Tax (GST) was introduced by the:

  1. 101st Amendment, 2016
  2. 100th Amendment, 2015
  3. 99th Amendment, 2014
  4. 102nd Amendment, 2018

Answer: 101st Amendment, 2016

Explanation: The 101st Constitutional Amendment (2016) introduced GST — inserting Art 246A (concurrent power to levy GST), Art 269A (IGST), Art 279A (GST Council), and amending Schedule 6/7.

40. The two-party system in Tamil Nadu politics refers to:

  1. DMK and AIADMK alternating power
  2. Congress and BJP
  3. INC and DMK
  4. AIADMK and BJP

Answer: DMK and AIADMK alternating power

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a unique two-Dravidian party system where DMK and AIADMK have alternated in power since 1967. National parties like INC and BJP have been junior alliance partners rather than independent power centers.

41. Which article of the Constitution protects persons from double jeopardy?

  1. Article 20(1)
  2. Article 20(2)
  3. Article 20(3)
  4. Article 22

Answer: Article 20(2)

Explanation: Article 20(2) provides that no person shall be prosecuted and punished for the same offence more than once — protection against double jeopardy.

42. Which article of the Constitution prohibits child labour (below 14 years) in factories and hazardous employment?

  1. Article 21A
  2. Article 23
  3. Article 24
  4. Article 39

Answer: Article 24

Explanation: Article 24 prohibits employment of children below 14 years in factories, mines, or other hazardous employment.

43. The official language of the Union of India is:

  1. Hindi in Devanagari script
  2. English
  3. Both Hindi and English
  4. Sanskrit

Answer: Hindi in Devanagari script

Explanation: Article 343: Hindi in Devanagari script is the official language of the Union. English continues to be used for official purposes under Article 343(2). India has no 'national language'.

44. The official language Hindi was to replace English by:

  1. 1965 (but not implemented)
  2. 1950
  3. 1952
  4. 1960

Answer: 1965 (but not implemented)

Explanation: Article 343(2) provided that English would continue for 15 years until 1965. But due to protests (especially in Tamil Nadu), the Official Languages Act 1963 made English continue indefinitely.

45. The Right to Information Act was passed in:

  1. 2005
  2. 2000
  3. 2003
  4. 2007

Answer: 2005

Explanation: The Right to Information (RTI) Act 2005 was passed to provide citizens access to information held by public authorities, making governance more transparent and accountable.

46. The Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act was passed in:

  1. 2013
  2. 2011
  3. 2015
  4. 2010

Answer: 2013

Explanation: The Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013 established the Lokpal at the Union level and Lokayuktas at the state level for investigation of corruption charges against public servants.

47. Which of the following is a feature of the Preamble to the Indian Constitution?

  1. It is enforceable in a court of law
  2. It declares India as a Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic
  3. It was adopted on 26th January 1950
  4. It lists the Fundamental Duties of citizens

Answer: It declares India as a Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic

Explanation: The Preamble declares India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic. It is not enforceable in court.

48. Under the Indian Constitution, which article guarantees the Right to Equality?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32

Answer: Article 14

Explanation: Article 14 guarantees equality before the law and equal protection of the laws.

49. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act is associated with which level of governance?

  1. Urban Local Bodies
  2. Panchayati Raj Institutions
  3. State Legislature
  4. High Courts

Answer: Panchayati Raj Institutions

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions (rural local self-government).

50. Which of the following is a Directive Principle of State Policy under the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Freedom of Speech
  2. Right to Constitutional Remedies
  3. Equal pay for equal work for both men and women
  4. Right against Exploitation

Answer: Equal pay for equal work for both men and women

Explanation: Equal pay for equal work is a Directive Principle under Article 39(d), guiding state policy.
