Governance MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Governance questions to revise the TNPSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which article of the Constitution defines India as a Union of States?

  1. Article 1
  2. Article 2
  3. Article 3
  4. Article 4

Answer: Article 1

Explanation: Article 1 states that 'India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States.'

2. Which article of the Constitution abolishes untouchability?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 15
  3. Article 17
  4. Article 21

Answer: Article 17

Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability in any form and makes its practice a punishable offence.

3. Which article of the Constitution provides for the right against self-incrimination?

  1. Article 20(1)
  2. Article 20(2)
  3. Article 20(3)
  4. Article 22

Answer: Article 20(3)

Explanation: Article 20(3) provides that no person accused of an offence shall be compelled to be a witness against himself.

4. Untouchability is abolished and its practice is forbidden under:

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 17
  3. Article 15
  4. Article 16

Answer: Article 17

Explanation: Article 17: 'Untouchability is abolished and its practice in any form is forbidden. The enforcement of any disability arising out of untouchability shall be an offence punishable by law.' The Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955 penalises untouchability.

5. The Constitution describes India as:

  1. Union of States
  2. Federation of States
  3. Confederation
  4. Republic of India

Answer: Union of States

Explanation: Article 1 describes India as a 'Union of States' — not federation. This implies the union is indestructible and states cannot secede. India is not a true federation.

6. Article 17 abolishes:

  1. Untouchability
  2. Bonded labor
  3. Child labor
  4. Discrimination based on religion

Answer: Untouchability

Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability and its practice in any form is an offense punishable by law. The Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955 and SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989 implement this.

7. Articles 23 and 24 deal with:

  1. Right against Exploitation
  2. Cultural and Educational Rights
  3. Right to Freedom
  4. Right to Religion

Answer: Right against Exploitation

Explanation: Articles 23-24 (Right against Exploitation): Art. 23 prohibits human trafficking and forced labor (begar). Art. 24 prohibits employment of children below 14 years in factories/mines/hazardous work.

8. Article 15 prohibits discrimination on grounds of:

  1. Religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth
  2. Religion, race, caste, sex only
  3. Religion only
  4. Caste only

Answer: Religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth

Explanation: Article 15 prohibits discrimination by the State on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, OR place of birth. However, special provisions for women, children, SCs, STs, OBCs are allowed.

9. The National Commission for Backward Classes is established under:

  1. Article 338B (102nd Amendment 2018)
  2. Article 340
  3. Article 338
  4. Constitutional from 1950

Answer: Article 338B (102nd Amendment 2018)

Explanation: 102nd Constitutional Amendment (2018) inserted Article 338B, giving constitutional status to the National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC). Previously it was a statutory body under 1993 Act.

10. The Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) was established by:

  1. CVC Act 2003
  2. Executive order in 1964 and later CVC Act 2003
  3. Constitutional Amendment
  4. Parliament Act 1990

Answer: CVC Act 2003

Explanation: CVC was established by executive order in 1964 (on recommendation of Santhanam Committee on Corruption Prevention). It was given statutory status by the CVC Act 2003.

11. Jammu & Kashmir was reorganized into two Union Territories in:

  1. 2019 (via J&K Reorganization Act)
  2. 2017
  3. 2021
  4. 2020

Answer: 2019 (via J&K Reorganization Act)

Explanation: J&K Reorganization Act 2019 bifurcated the state of J&K into: (1) J&K UT (with legislature), (2) Ladakh UT (without legislature), effective 31 October 2019. Article 370 was also abrogated.

12. Article 371 deals with:

  1. Special provisions for certain states (like NE states)
  2. Panchayati Raj
  3. Minority rights
  4. Language policies

Answer: Special provisions for certain states (like NE states)

Explanation: Articles 371-371J provide special provisions for certain states: 371A (Nagaland), 371B (Assam), 371C (Manipur), 371D-F (Andhra Pradesh/Telangana), 371G (Mizoram), 371H (Arunachal), 371I (Goa), 371J (Karnataka).

13. Which Article prohibits traffic in human beings and forced labour?

  1. Article 23
  2. Article 24
  3. Article 17
  4. Article 19

Answer: Article 23

Explanation: Article 23 prohibits trafficking in human beings, begar (forced labour) and other similar forms of forced labour. Any contravention is an offence punishable by law.

14. Which Article prohibits employment of children below 14 years in hazardous work?

  1. Article 24
  2. Article 23
  3. Article 39(f)
  4. Article 45

Answer: Article 24

Explanation: Article 24 prohibits employment of children below 14 years in factories, mines or other hazardous employment. The Child Labour (Prohibition & Regulation) Act implements this.

15. Protection against self-incrimination is guaranteed by:

  1. Article 20(3)
  2. Article 20(2)
  3. Article 20(1)
  4. Article 22

Answer: Article 20(3)

Explanation: Article 20(3) states no person accused of any offence shall be compelled to be a witness against himself. This is the right against self-incrimination (nemo tenetur seipsum accusare).

16. Under which Articles are protection against arbitrary arrest and detention provided?

  1. Article 22
  2. Article 21
  3. Article 19
  4. Article 20

Answer: Article 22

Explanation: Article 22 provides protection against arbitrary arrest: right to be informed of grounds, right to consult a lawyer, production before magistrate within 24 hours, and preventive detention limits.

17. The Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) was given statutory status by:

  1. CVC Act, 2003
  2. CVC Ordinance, 1998
  3. Constitutional Amendment
  4. Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988

Answer: CVC Act, 2003

Explanation: The Central Vigilance Commission was established in 1964 by executive resolution based on Santhanam Committee recommendations. It was given statutory status by the CVC Act, 2003.

18. The Lokpal Act was passed in:

  1. 2013
  2. 2010
  3. 2015
  4. 2016

Answer: 2013

Explanation: The Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act was passed in December 2013. India's first Lokpal (Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose) was appointed in March 2019 — nearly 6 years after the Act was passed.

19. Article 1 of the Indian Constitution describes India as:

  1. A Union of States
  2. A Federation of States
  3. A Confederation
  4. A Republic of States

Answer: A Union of States

Explanation: Article 1 describes India as a 'Union of States' — not a federation. This term was deliberately chosen to indicate that States have no right to secede from the Union (unlike a federation or confederation).

20. Article 17 of the Constitution abolishes:

  1. Untouchability
  2. Titles
  3. Forced labour
  4. Child labour

Answer: Untouchability

Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form. Enforcement of any disability arising out of untouchability shall be an offence. The Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 implements this.

21. The 6th Schedule of the Constitution provides for:

  1. Administration of Tribal Areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram
  2. Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in general
  3. All tribal areas in India
  4. Forest rights of tribals

Answer: Administration of Tribal Areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram

Explanation: The 6th Schedule provides for autonomous District Councils and Regional Councils for tribal areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram — giving tribal communities substantial self-governance powers.

22. The maximum number of members in a State Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad) cannot exceed:

  1. One-third of the total members of the State Legislative Assembly
  2. One-fourth of the total members of the State Assembly
  3. 50 members
  4. 100 members

Answer: One-third of the total members of the State Legislative Assembly

Explanation: Article 171 states that the total number of members in a Vidhan Parishad shall not exceed one-third of the total members of the Vidhan Sabha, and shall not be less than 40 members.

23. Which constitutional amendment introduced the Goods and Services Tax (GST)?

  1. 99th Amendment
  2. 100th Amendment
  3. 101st Amendment
  4. 102nd Amendment

Answer: 101st Amendment

Explanation: The 101st Constitutional Amendment Act (2016) introduced GST in India by amending Article 246A and others.

24. Which Finance Commission is constituted to recommend devolution of taxes between Centre and States under Article 280?

  1. Every 3 years
  2. Every 5 years
  3. Every 7 years
  4. Every 10 years

Answer: Every 5 years

Explanation: Article 280 provides for the constitution of a Finance Commission every 5 years (or earlier if needed) to recommend the distribution of net proceeds of taxes between the Centre and States.

25. The total number of Articles in the original Indian Constitution was:

  1. 315
  2. 368
  3. 395
  4. 448

Answer: 395

Explanation: The original Constitution had 395 Articles, 8 Schedules, and 22 Parts. Currently it has around 448 Articles and 12 Schedules due to amendments.
