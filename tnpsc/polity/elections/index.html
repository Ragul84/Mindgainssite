Elections MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Elections questions to revise the TNPSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. What is the minimum age to vote in Indian elections?

  1. 16 years
  2. 18 years
  3. 21 years
  4. 25 years

Answer: 18 years

Explanation: The 61st Constitutional Amendment (1988) reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

2. Which body conducts elections in India?

  1. Parliament
  2. Supreme Court
  3. Election Commission of India
  4. UPSC

Answer: Election Commission of India

Explanation: The Election Commission of India (ECI) is an autonomous constitutional body that administers elections at the national and state level.

3. The Model Code of Conduct comes into effect from:

  1. Date of announcement of elections by Election Commission
  2. Date of filing of nominations
  3. Date of election notification
  4. Date of voting

Answer: Date of announcement of elections by Election Commission

Explanation: The Model Code of Conduct comes into force from the date of announcement of election schedule by the Election Commission. It remains in force till the completion of the election process.

4. Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) were first used in India in:

  1. 1982 Kerala State elections
  2. 1989 General Elections
  3. 1984 State elections
  4. 1991 General Elections

Answer: 1982 Kerala State elections

Explanation: EVMs were first used on an experimental basis in 50 polling stations in the Parur Assembly constituency, Kerala in 1982. They were used nationwide from 2004 General Elections.

5. The Gram Sabha consists of:

  1. All persons registered as voters in the Panchayat area
  2. Only elected Panchayat members
  3. All adult residents (whether registered voters or not)
  4. All taxpayers in the village

Answer: All persons registered as voters in the Panchayat area

Explanation: The Gram Sabha (Art 243A) consists of all persons registered in the electoral rolls of a village — i.e., all adult voters of that village. It is the foundation of democratic decentralization and can exercise powers as the State Legislature decides.

6. The principle 'One Vote, One Value' in Indian elections means:

  1. Each vote has equal value — no plural voting
  2. Only one person can vote per family
  3. Property-based voting is allowed
  4. Only citizens can vote

Answer: Each vote has equal value — no plural voting

Explanation: The principle of 'One Person, One Vote, One Value' ensures universal adult franchise — each citizen above 18 years has one vote of equal value, regardless of wealth, gender, religion, or caste.

7. The Election Commission of India was established on:

  1. 26 January 1950
  2. 25 January 1950
  3. 15 August 1947
  4. 26 November 1949

Answer: 25 January 1950

Explanation: The Election Commission of India was established on 25 January 1950 (one day before the Constitution came into force). 25 January is celebrated as National Voters' Day.

8. The minimum age for voting in India is:

  1. 21 years
  2. 18 years (changed from 21 by 61st Amendment, 1988)
  3. 20 years
  4. 25 years

Answer: 18 years (changed from 21 by 61st Amendment, 1988)

Explanation: The voting age was reduced from 21 to 18 years by the 61st Constitutional Amendment Act (1988). This was done to ensure greater youth participation in democracy. India has ~950 million eligible voters (2024).

9. EVM (Electronic Voting Machine) was first used in a general election in India in:

  1. 1989
  2. 1999
  3. 2004
  4. 1984

Answer: 1999

Explanation: EVMs were first used in the Goa state assembly elections (1989) on a trial basis. The 1999 General Elections used EVMs across India for the first time. Full nationwide use since 2004 General Elections.

10. Minimum age to vote in India was reduced to 18 years by:

  1. 61st Amendment (1988)
  2. 52nd Amendment
  3. 42nd Amendment
  4. 44th Amendment

Answer: 61st Amendment (1988)

Explanation: 61st Constitutional Amendment (1988) reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years. The first election where 18-year-olds could vote was in 1989.

11. Model Code of Conduct comes into force:

  1. From the date of announcement of election schedule
  2. After filing of nominations
  3. On election day
  4. After declaration of results

Answer: From the date of announcement of election schedule

Explanation: Model Code of Conduct (MCC) comes into operation from the date of announcement of election schedule (by Election Commission) and remains in force until the completion of the election process.

12. The State Election Commission conducts elections for:

  1. Panchayats and Municipalities
  2. State Legislatures
  3. Both State Legislatures and Local Bodies
  4. Lok Sabha seats in the State

Answer: Panchayats and Municipalities

Explanation: The State Election Commission (Art 243K/243ZA) superintends, directs and controls the preparation of electoral rolls and the conduct of all elections to Panchayats and Municipalities.

13. Under which Article of the Constitution of India is the Election Commission established?

  1. Article 324
  2. Article 326
  3. Article 324A
  4. Article 329

Answer: Article 324

Explanation: Article 324 provides for the Election Commission of India as a constitutional body to supervise elections.

14. What is the minimum age for voting in India as per the Constitution?

  1. 21 years
  2. 18 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 16 years

Answer: 18 years

Explanation: The 61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988 reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

15. Which type of electoral system is followed for Lok Sabha elections in India?

  1. Proportional Representation
  2. First-Past-the-Post
  3. Mixed Member Proportional
  4. Single Transferable Vote

Answer: First-Past-the-Post

Explanation: India uses the First-Past-the-Post system where the candidate with the highest votes wins.

16. Who appoints the Chief Election Commissioner of India?

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Chief Justice of India
  4. Vice President

Answer: President

Explanation: The President appoints the Chief Election Commissioner and other Election Commissioners under Article 324.

17. Which body is responsible for the preparation of electoral rolls in India?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. Registrar General of India
  3. State Governments
  4. Ministry of Law

Answer: Election Commission of India

Explanation: The Election Commission of India prepares and updates electoral rolls for all constituencies.

18. What is the maximum expenditure limit for a Lok Sabha candidate in a large state as per recent EC guidelines?

  1. ₹50 lakh
  2. ₹70 lakh
  3. ₹95 lakh
  4. ₹1 crore

Answer: ₹95 lakh

Explanation: For larger states, the expenditure limit for Lok Sabha candidates is ₹95 lakh (as of 2022).

19. Which of the following is a ground for disqualification of a person from voting in India?

  1. Being a government employee
  2. Being convicted of certain electoral offences
  3. Being illiterate
  4. Being a member of a political party

Answer: Being convicted of certain electoral offences

Explanation: Conviction for electoral offences like corrupt practices can disqualify a person from voting.

20. Which constitutional amendment introduced the provision for reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the Lok Sabha?

  1. 1st Amendment
  2. 12th Amendment
  3. 23rd Amendment
  4. 45th Amendment

Answer: 23rd Amendment

Explanation: The 23rd Amendment (1969) extended the reservation of seats for SC/ST in the Lok Sabha and state assemblies.

21. What is the term of office of the Election Commissioner of India?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. Until the age of 65
  4. Both B and C

Answer: Both B and C

Explanation: Election Commissioners hold office for 6 years or until the age of 65, whichever is earlier.

22. Which of the following is a function of the Election Commission of India?

  1. Conducting census
  2. Delimitation of constituencies
  3. Settling election disputes
  4. Issuing voter IDs

Answer: Issuing voter IDs

Explanation: The Election Commission issues voter identity cards (EPIC) to registered voters.

23. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the Election Commission?

  1. Article 324
  2. Article 326
  3. Article 329
  4. Article 356

Answer: Article 324

Explanation: Article 324 establishes the Election Commission to supervise elections.

24. Which constitutional amendment lowered the voting age from 21 to 18?

  1. 61st Amendment
  2. 73rd Amendment
  3. 86th Amendment
  4. 44th Amendment

Answer: 61st Amendment

Explanation: The 61st Amendment Act, 1988, changed the voting age to 18.

25. The Election Commission of India is a/an ________ body.

  1. independent constitutional
  2. statutory
  3. executive
  4. judicial

Answer: independent constitutional

Explanation: The Election Commission is a constitutional body established under Article 324.
