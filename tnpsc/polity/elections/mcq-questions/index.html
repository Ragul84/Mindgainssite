Elections MCQs with answers

26. Which of the following is a function of the Election Commission?

  1. Conducting free and fair elections
  2. Forming government
  3. Enacting laws
  4. Interpreting constitution

Answer: Conducting free and fair elections

Explanation: The Election Commission is responsible for conducting free and fair elections.

27. The concept of 'One Nation One Election' refers to:

  1. Simultaneous elections for Lok Sabha and state assemblies
  2. Single voter list
  3. Uniform polling date across India
  4. None of the above

Answer: Simultaneous elections for Lok Sabha and state assemblies

Explanation: It proposes holding elections for Lok Sabha and all state assemblies together.

28. Who was the first Chief Election Commissioner of India?

  1. Sukumar Sen
  2. T.N. Seshan
  3. S.Y. Quraishi
  4. Sunil Arora

Answer: Sukumar Sen

Explanation: Sukumar Sen served as the first CEC from 1950 to 1958.

29. Which article prohibits discrimination in voting on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, etc.?

  1. Article 325
  2. Article 326
  3. Article 324
  4. Article 329

Answer: Article 325

Explanation: Article 325 ensures no person is ineligible for inclusion in electoral rolls on these grounds.

30. In Tamil Nadu, the State Election Commission is responsible for conducting elections to which bodies?

  1. Local bodies (Panchayats and Municipalities)
  2. State Legislative Assembly
  3. Lok Sabha
  4. Rajya Sabha

Answer: Local bodies (Panchayats and Municipalities)

Explanation: The State Election Commission conducts elections to local self-government bodies under Article 243K.

31. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the superintendence, direction, and control of elections by the Election Commission of India?

  1. Article 324
  2. Article 326
  3. Article 327
  4. Article 329

Answer: Article 324

Explanation: Article 324 vests the Election Commission with the power of superintendence, direction, and control of elections.

32. Elections to the Panchayats are conducted by which body?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. State Election Commission
  3. State Legislature
  4. District Collector

Answer: State Election Commission

Explanation: Article 243K of the Constitution mandates the creation of a State Election Commission to conduct Panchayat elections.

33. Which of the following is a ground for disqualification of a candidate from contesting elections in India?

  1. Being a government employee
  2. Having a criminal record
  3. Being insolvent
  4. All of the above

Answer: Being insolvent

Explanation: Under the Representation of the People Act, 1951, being an undischarged insolvent is a valid ground for disqualification.

34. The first-past-the-post electoral system is used for elections to which body in India?

  1. Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies
  2. Rajya Sabha
  3. President of India
  4. Vice-President of India

Answer: Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies

Explanation: India uses the first-past-the-post system for direct elections to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies.

35. What is the term of office for a member of the Election Commission of India?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. 7 years
  4. Until age 65

Answer: 6 years

Explanation: The term of an Election Commissioner is six years, subject to a maximum age limit of 65 years.

36. According to the Representation of the People Act, 1951, what is the maximum expenditure allowed for a Lok Sabha candidate from a large state?

  1. ₹50 lakh
  2. ₹70 lakh
  3. ₹95 lakh
  4. ₹1 crore

Answer: ₹95 lakh

Explanation: For Lok Sabha elections, the expenditure limit for a candidate in a large state is ₹95 lakh as per recent amendments.

37. The concept of 'one person, one vote' is enshrined in which part of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Fundamental Rights
  2. Directive Principles
  3. Preamble
  4. Election Laws

Answer: Preamble

Explanation: The Preamble of the Constitution ensures political equality through the principle of 'one person, one vote'.

38. Elections to local bodies in Tamil Nadu are conducted under which Act?

  1. Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act, 1994
  2. Tamil Nadu Municipalities Act, 1920
  3. Representation of the People Act, 1951
  4. Constitution (73rd Amendment) Act, 1992

Answer: Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act, 1994

Explanation: The Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act, 1994 governs the conduct of elections to rural local bodies in the state.

39. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the superintendence, direction, and control of elections by the Election Commission?

  1. Article 324
  2. Article 326
  3. Article 327
  4. Article 329

Answer: Article 324

Explanation: Article 324 vests the Election Commission with the power of superintendence, direction, and control of elections.

40. What is the minimum age for a person to be eligible to vote in India?

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 16 years

Answer: 18 years

Explanation: The 61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988, reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

41. Which of the following is NOT a ground for disqualification of a candidate from contesting elections under the Representation of the People Act, 1951?

  1. Conviction for an offence
  2. Being of unsound mind
  3. Holding an office of profit under the government
  4. Being a member of a political party

Answer: Being a member of a political party

Explanation: Being a member of a political party is not a disqualification; disqualifications include conviction, unsound mind, and holding an office of profit.

42. The Election Commission of India is a:

  1. Constitutional body
  2. Statutory body
  3. Executive body
  4. Quasi-judicial body

Answer: Constitutional body

Explanation: The Election Commission is a constitutional body established under Article 324 of the Constitution.

43. Which type of election is conducted to fill a casual vacancy in the office of a Member of Parliament?

  1. By-election
  2. Mid-term election
  3. General election
  4. Special election

Answer: By-election

Explanation: A by-election is held to fill a vacancy caused by the death, resignation, or disqualification of a sitting member.

44. Under the Constitution, who has the power to decide disputes relating to the election of the President of India?

  1. Supreme Court
  2. Election Commission
  3. Parliament
  4. High Court

Answer: Supreme Court

Explanation: Article 71 vests the authority to decide disputes regarding the election of the President in the Supreme Court.

45. What is the maximum duration for which the Election Commission can postpone an election in a constituency?

  1. No fixed limit
  2. One month
  3. Six months
  4. One year

Answer: No fixed limit

Explanation: The Constitution does not prescribe a fixed maximum duration; the Election Commission can postpone elections based on circumstances.

46. Which of the following is a feature of the First Past the Post (FPTP) electoral system used in India?

  1. Candidate with highest votes wins
  2. Proportional representation
  3. Preferential voting
  4. Single transferable vote

Answer: Candidate with highest votes wins

Explanation: In FPTP, the candidate who secures the highest number of votes in a constituency is declared the winner.

47. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the establishment of the Election Commission of India?

  1. Article 324
  2. Article 326
  3. Article 329
  4. Article 325

Answer: Article 324

Explanation: Article 324 vests the superintendence, direction, and control of elections in the Election Commission of India.

48. What is the minimum voting age in India as per the Constitution?

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 16 years
  4. 25 years

Answer: 18 years

Explanation: The 61st Amendment Act, 1988 reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

49. Which body conducts elections to Panchayati Raj institutions in a state?

  1. State Election Commission
  2. Election Commission of India
  3. State Legislature
  4. District Magistrate

Answer: State Election Commission

Explanation: The State Election Commission is responsible for conducting elections to local bodies like Panchayats and Municipalities.

50. The principle of 'one person, one vote' is based on which concept?

  1. Political equality
  2. Social justice
  3. Economic equality
  4. Federalism

Answer: Political equality

Explanation: It ensures every citizen has an equal right to vote, reflecting political equality.
