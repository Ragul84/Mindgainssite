26. What does 'Republic' mean in Indian context (as in 'Sovereign Democratic Republic')?

  1. India has a king
  2. The head of state is elected, not hereditary
  3. India has no head of state
  4. India's PM is elected by people

Answer: The head of state is elected, not hereditary

Explanation: Republic means the head of state (President) is elected by the people's representatives, not hereditary like a monarch.

27. The Preamble calls India a 'Republic' which means:

  1. Head of state is elected, not hereditary
  2. States have equal powers
  3. No federal system
  4. Military rule

Answer: Head of state is elected, not hereditary

Explanation: 'Republic' in the Preamble means the head of state (President) is elected for a fixed term, not hereditary. This distinguishes India from constitutional monarchies like UK.

28. India is a 'Sovereign' state which means:

  1. India is free from external control and internal control is with the people
  2. India has nuclear weapons
  3. India is a UN member
  4. India has no debts

Answer: India is free from external control and internal control is with the people

Explanation: Sovereign means India is an independent state — externally it is free from foreign domination, internally the people are the ultimate authority through their elected representatives.

29. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution declares India to be a:

  1. Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic
  2. Sovereign Democratic Republic
  3. Federal Republic
  4. Union of States

Answer: Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic

Explanation: The Preamble declares India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic.

30. Which word was added to the Preamble by the 42nd Amendment?

  1. Socialist
  2. Secular
  3. Both Socialist and Secular
  4. Integrity

Answer: Both Socialist and Secular

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment added 'Socialist' and 'Secular' to the Preamble.

31. The term 'Justice' in the Preamble includes:

  1. Social, Economic and Political
  2. Social and Economic only
  3. Economic and Political only
  4. Social and Political only

Answer: Social, Economic and Political

Explanation: The Preamble mentions Social, Economic and Political justice.

32. The Preamble is considered the:

  1. Soul of the Constitution
  2. Heart of the Constitution
  3. Key to the Constitution
  4. Both A and B

Answer: Soul of the Constitution

Explanation: The Preamble is often called the soul of the Constitution.

33. The Preamble of the Constitution was adopted on:

  1. 26 November 1949
  2. 26 January 1950
  3. 15 August 1947
  4. 26 January 1949

Answer: 26 November 1949

Explanation: The Preamble was adopted on 26 November 1949 along with the Constitution.

34. Which of the following is NOT a feature mentioned in the Preamble?

  1. Federal
  2. Democratic
  3. Republic
  4. Sovereign

Answer: Federal

Explanation: The Preamble does not use the word 'Federal'; it says 'Union of States'.

35. The Preamble secures to all citizens:

  1. Liberty of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship
  2. Liberty of speech and expression only
  3. Liberty of movement and residence
  4. Liberty of profession

Answer: Liberty of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship

Explanation: The Preamble mentions liberty of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.

36. The term 'Fraternity' in the Preamble assures:

  1. Dignity of the individual and unity and integrity of the nation
  2. Only unity of the nation
  3. Only dignity of the individual
  4. Equality of status

Answer: Dignity of the individual and unity and integrity of the nation

Explanation: Fraternity assures dignity of the individual and unity and integrity of the nation.

37. The Preamble is a part of the Constitution as held by the Supreme Court in which case?

  1. Kesavananda Bharati case
  2. Berubari Union case
  3. Golaknath case
  4. Minerva Mills case

Answer: Kesavananda Bharati case

Explanation: In Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), the Supreme Court held that the Preamble is a part of the Constitution.

38. The Preamble declares India as a 'Republic' which means:

  1. The head of the state is elected
  2. The head of the state is hereditary
  3. The head of the state is appointed
  4. The head of the state is a monarch

Answer: The head of the state is elected

Explanation: Republic means the head of the state is elected, not hereditary.

39. The term 'We the People of India' in the Preamble signifies that the Constitution derives its authority from:

  1. The Parliament
  2. The people of India
  3. The Constituent Assembly
  4. The President

Answer: The people of India

Explanation: It signifies that the ultimate source of the Constitution is the people of India.

40. Which of the following words was added to the Preamble by the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976?

  1. Socialist
  2. Secular
  3. Integrity
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment added 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble.

41. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution is based on the 'Objective Resolution' moved by:

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. B.R. Ambedkar
  3. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
  4. Rajendra Prasad

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru

Explanation: The Objective Resolution was moved by Jawaharlal Nehru in 1946, which later became the Preamble.

42. Which of the following is NOT a key word in the Preamble?

  1. Sovereign
  2. Democratic
  3. Federal
  4. Republic

Answer: Federal

Explanation: The Preamble does not contain the word 'Federal'; it says 'Union of States'.

43. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution ensures 'Justice' in which forms?

  1. Social, Economic and Political
  2. Social, Economic and Cultural
  3. Political, Economic and Legal
  4. Social, Political and Legal

Answer: Social, Economic and Political

Explanation: The Preamble mentions Social, Economic and Political justice.

44. The Preamble begins with the phrase:

  1. We, the people of India, having solemnly resolved...
  2. We the people of India...
  3. In the name of God...
  4. We the citizens of India...

Answer: We the people of India...

Explanation: The exact opening is 'We, the people of India' (though commas may vary), making option B the correct choice.

45. The concept of 'Fraternity' in the Preamble ensures:

  1. Unity and integrity of the nation
  2. Equality of status and opportunity
  3. Justice for all
  4. Liberty of thought and expression

Answer: Unity and integrity of the nation

Explanation: Fraternity assures the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the nation.

46. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution has been amended only once by which Amendment?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 86th Amendment

Answer: 42nd Amendment

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) amended the Preamble by adding words.

47. The Preamble is considered the 'key' to the Constitution. This was stated in which Supreme Court case?

  1. Kesavananda Bharati case
  2. Berubari Union case
  3. Golaknath case
  4. Minerva Mills case

Answer: Berubari Union case

Explanation: In the Berubari Union case (1960), the Supreme Court said the Preamble is the key to the Constitution.

48. The word 'Socialist' was added to the Preamble by which Amendment?

  1. 42nd
  2. 44th
  3. 73rd
  4. 86th

Answer: 42nd

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble.

49. Who proposed the Preamble in the Constituent Assembly?

  1. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Sardar Patel
  4. Rajendra Prasad

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru

Explanation: Jawaharlal Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution which later became the Preamble.

50. The Preamble is considered the 'soul' of the Constitution by:

  1. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
  2. Sir Alladi Krishnaswami Iyer
  3. K.M. Munshi
  4. Thakurdas Bhargava

Answer: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar referred to the Preamble as the 'soul' of the Constitution.
