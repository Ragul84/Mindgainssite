Local Bodies MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Local Bodies questions to revise the TNPSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which amendment gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 86th Amendment

Answer: 73rd Amendment

Explanation: The 73rd Constitutional Amendment (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions (local self-government in rural areas).

2. The Eleventh Schedule of the Constitution lists subjects relating to:

  1. Powers of Union
  2. Powers of States
  3. Panchayati Raj
  4. Municipalities

Answer: Panchayati Raj

Explanation: The Eleventh Schedule (added by 73rd Amendment) lists 29 subjects that may be entrusted to Panchayati Raj institutions.

3. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act (1992) related to:

  1. Parliament elections
  2. Panchayati Raj institutions — gave constitutional status to 3-tier system
  3. Urban local bodies
  4. Financial relations between states and centre

Answer: Panchayati Raj institutions — gave constitutional status to 3-tier system

Explanation: 73rd Amendment (1992) added Part IX (Articles 243-243O) and 11th Schedule to the Constitution, giving constitutional status to Panchayati Raj. Mandated 3-tier system (Village, Intermediate, District Panchayats), elections, reservation for SC/ST/women (33%), 5-year term.

4. The 74th Constitutional Amendment Act (1992) related to:

  1. Panchayati Raj
  2. Urban local bodies (Municipalities) — added Part IXA, Articles 243P-243ZG, and 12th Schedule
  3. Elections to state assemblies
  4. Financial emergency

Answer: Urban local bodies (Municipalities) — added Part IXA, Articles 243P-243ZG, and 12th Schedule

Explanation: 74th Amendment (1992) added Part IXA and the 12th Schedule, giving constitutional status to urban local bodies (Municipal Corporations, Municipal Councils, Nagar Panchayats). 12th Schedule lists 18 functions for municipalities.

5. How many subjects are listed in the 11th Schedule (Panchayati Raj functions)?

  1. 18
  2. 29
  3. 47
  4. 66

Answer: 29

Explanation: The 11th Schedule (73rd Amendment) lists 29 subjects that may be transferred to Panchayats, including agriculture, animal husbandry, fisheries, education, markets, public health, libraries, etc. The 12th Schedule (74th Amendment) has 18 subjects for Municipalities.

6. The PESA Act (1996) extended Panchayati Raj to:

  1. Scheduled (Tribal) Areas of 5th Schedule
  2. All urban areas
  3. Himalayan states
  4. All UTs

Answer: Scheduled (Tribal) Areas of 5th Schedule

Explanation: Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act 1996 (PESA): Extended modified Panchayati Raj provisions to Scheduled Tribal Areas in 10 states with provisions to protect tribal customs and traditions.

7. Which schedule of the Constitution lists the powers and functions of Gram Panchayats?

  1. 11th Schedule
  2. 7th Schedule
  3. 8th Schedule
  4. 10th Schedule

Answer: 11th Schedule

Explanation: The 11th Schedule (added by 73rd Amendment, 1992) contains 29 subjects/functions of Gram Panchayats, like agriculture, rural housing, drinking water, education, etc.

8. The 12th Schedule of the Constitution relates to:

  1. Municipalities (urban local bodies)
  2. Panchayati Raj
  3. Languages
  4. Anti-defection

Answer: Municipalities (urban local bodies)

Explanation: The 12th Schedule (added by 74th Amendment, 1992) contains 18 functions of Municipalities/Urban Local Bodies, like urban planning, regulation of land use, public health, etc.

9. Tamil Nadu's Panchayati Raj has how many tiers?

  1. 3 tiers — Village Panchayat, Panchayat Union, District Panchayat
  2. 2 tiers
  3. 4 tiers
  4. 5 tiers

Answer: 3 tiers — Village Panchayat, Panchayat Union, District Panchayat

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a 3-tier Panchayati Raj structure: Village Panchayat (lowest), Panchayat Union/Block Panchayat (middle), and District Panchayat/Zila Parishad (top). This conforms to the 73rd Amendment mandate.

10. PESA Act (Panchayats Extension to Scheduled Areas Act) was passed in:

  1. 1996
  2. 1992
  3. 2006
  4. 2000

Answer: 1996

Explanation: The PESA Act, 1996 extended Panchayati Raj to tribal (scheduled) areas under the 5th Schedule. It recognizes customary law, traditions, and cultural identity of tribal communities and gives them self-governance powers.

11. The 74th Constitutional Amendment (1992) is related to:

  1. Panchayati Raj
  2. Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities)
  3. Environment Protection
  4. Land Reform

Answer: Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities)

Explanation: The 74th Amendment gave constitutional status to Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities), Nagar Panchayats, Municipal Councils, and Municipal Corporations.

12. The State Election Commission (for local body elections) was established by:

  1. 73rd and 74th Amendments (Articles 243K and 243ZA)
  2. An ordinary state law
  3. The Election Commission of India
  4. Parliament

Answer: 73rd and 74th Amendments (Articles 243K and 243ZA)

Explanation: The 73rd and 74th Amendments made it mandatory for states to establish State Election Commissions (Article 243K for Panchayats, 243ZA for Municipalities) to superintend, direct, and control elections to local bodies.

13. The Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957) recommended:

  1. 3-tier Panchayati Raj system
  2. 2-tier system
  3. 4-tier system
  4. Zila Parishad only

Answer: 3-tier Panchayati Raj system

Explanation: Balwant Rai Mehta Committee recommended a three-tier Panchayati Raj structure: (1) Gram Panchayat at village level, (2) Panchayat Samiti at block level, (3) Zila Parishad at district level.

14. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment (1992) relates to:

  1. Panchayati Raj
  2. Urban Local Bodies
  3. Education
  4. Environmental Protection

Answer: Panchayati Raj

Explanation: 73rd Constitutional Amendment (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions, adding Part IX (Articles 243-243O) and 11th Schedule (29 functions transferred to Panchayats).

15. The 11th Schedule of the Constitution relates to:

  1. Functions of Panchayats (29 subjects)
  2. Functions of Municipalities
  3. Official Languages
  4. Anti-Defection

Answer: Functions of Panchayats (29 subjects)

Explanation: 11th Schedule (added by 73rd Amendment, 1992) contains 29 subjects/functions to be assigned to Panchayats — including agriculture, land improvement, rural roads, health, education, etc.

16. 74th Constitutional Amendment (1992) relates to:

  1. Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities)
  2. Panchayati Raj
  3. Tribal Areas
  4. Forest Rights

Answer: Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities)

Explanation: 74th Constitutional Amendment (1992) gave constitutional status to Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities), adding Part IX-A (Articles 243P-243ZG) and 12th Schedule (18 municipal functions).

17. 'Gram Sabha' is a body consisting of:

  1. All registered voters in a village
  2. Elected members of Gram Panchayat
  3. Appointed officials
  4. Village heads only

Answer: All registered voters in a village

Explanation: Gram Sabha is a body consisting of all persons registered in the electoral rolls of the village — the primary democratic unit at grassroots level under the 73rd Amendment.

18. The 74th Constitutional Amendment relates to:

  1. Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities)
  2. Gram Panchayats
  3. Tribal Areas
  4. Cooperative Societies

Answer: Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities)

Explanation: The 74th Amendment (1992) constitutionalized Urban Local Bodies — Nagar Panchayats, Municipal Councils, Municipal Corporations. It added Part IX-A (Arts 243P-243ZG) and the 12th Schedule.

19. What are the three types of Government in India?

  1. Federal, State, District
  2. National, Regional, Local
  3. Central, State, Local self-government
  4. Supreme, High, Lower courts

Answer: Central, State, Local self-government

Explanation: India has three tiers of government: Central Government, State Government, and Local Self-Government (Panchayats and Municipalities).

20. Which part of the Indian Constitution contains the provisions related to Panchayati Raj Institutions?

  1. Part IX
  2. Part IX-A
  3. Part X
  4. Part VIII

Answer: Part IX

Explanation: Part IX of the Constitution deals with Panchayats, added by the 73rd Amendment Act of 1992.

21. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act added which new schedule to the Indian Constitution?

  1. Tenth Schedule
  2. Eleventh Schedule
  3. Twelfth Schedule
  4. Ninth Schedule

Answer: Eleventh Schedule

Explanation: The Eleventh Schedule lists 29 functional items for Panchayats, added by the 73rd Amendment.

22. Under the Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act, what is the minimum age for a person to be eligible for election as a member of a Panchayat?

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 30 years

Answer: 21 years

Explanation: The minimum age for contesting Panchayat elections in Tamil Nadu is 21 years.

23. Which authority conducts elections for Panchayati Raj Institutions in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. State Election Commission
  3. Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission
  4. District Collector

Answer: State Election Commission

Explanation: The State Election Commission of Tamil Nadu is responsible for conducting local body elections.

24. What is the term of office for a Panchayat in Tamil Nadu, unless dissolved earlier?

  1. 3 years
  2. 4 years
  3. 5 years
  4. 6 years

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: The term of every Panchayat is five years from the date of its first meeting.

25. Which of the following is a mandatory provision under the 73rd Amendment for all Panchayats?

  1. Reservation for OBCs
  2. Reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes
  3. Direct election of the Chairperson of the District Panchayat
  4. Fixed tenure of 6 years

Answer: Reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes

Explanation: Reservation of seats for SCs and STs in proportion to their population is mandatory for all Panchayats.
