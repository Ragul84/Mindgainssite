26. In the three-tier Panchayati Raj system of Tamil Nadu, which is the apex body at the district level?

  1. District Planning Committee
  2. District Panchayat
  3. Zilla Parishad
  4. District Rural Development Agency

Answer: District Panchayat

Explanation: The District Panchayat (formerly called District Council) is the top tier of the Panchayati Raj system in Tamil Nadu.

27. The 74th Constitutional Amendment Act deals with which type of local bodies?

  1. Panchayats
  2. Municipalities
  3. Cooperative Societies
  4. District Planning Committees

Answer: Municipalities

Explanation: The 74th Amendment (1992) added Part IX-A to the Constitution, dealing with Municipalities (urban local bodies).

28. What is the minimum reservation for women in Panchayati Raj institutions as per the 73rd Amendment?

  1. One-fourth of the seats
  2. One-third of the seats
  3. Half of the seats
  4. No fixed reservation

Answer: One-third of the seats

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment mandates that not less than one-third of the total seats be reserved for women.

29. Which Article of the Indian Constitution defines a 'Gram Sabha'?

  1. Article 243
  2. Article 243A
  3. Article 243B
  4. Article 243C

Answer: Article 243A

Explanation: Article 243A declares that a Gram Sabha may exercise such powers as the Legislature of a State may provide by law.

30. Under which part of the Indian Constitution are Panchayats given constitutional status?

  1. Part IX
  2. Part IX-A
  3. Part X
  4. Part XI

Answer: Part IX

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment added Part IX (Articles 243 to 243O) for Panchayats.

31. What is the minimum age for a person to become a member of a Panchayat?

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 30 years

Answer: 21 years

Explanation: Article 243F prescribes the minimum age for Panchayat membership as 21 years.

32. Which Schedule of the Constitution contains the list of subjects for Panchayats?

  1. Tenth Schedule
  2. Eleventh Schedule
  3. Twelfth Schedule
  4. Ninth Schedule

Answer: Eleventh Schedule

Explanation: The Eleventh Schedule lists 29 functional items for Panchayats (added by the 73rd Amendment).

33. What is the minimum reservation for women in Panchayats?

  1. One-third
  2. One-half
  3. Two-thirds
  4. One-fourth

Answer: One-third

Explanation: Article 243D mandates that not less than one-third of total seats be reserved for women.

34. Who appoints the State Finance Commission for local bodies?

  1. President of India
  2. Governor of the State
  3. Chief Minister
  4. State Legislature

Answer: Governor of the State

Explanation: Under Article 243I, the Governor appoints the State Finance Commission to review local body finances.

35. What is the normal term of a Panchayat?

  1. 3 years
  2. 4 years
  3. 5 years
  4. 6 years

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: Article 243E states every Panchayat continues for five years from its first meeting.

36. Which Constitutional Amendment gave constitutional status to Municipalities?

  1. 72nd Amendment
  2. 73rd Amendment
  3. 74th Amendment
  4. 75th Amendment

Answer: 74th Amendment

Explanation: The 74th Amendment (1992) introduced Part IX-A for Municipalities.

37. Who conducts elections to Panchayats?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. State Election Commission
  3. State Government
  4. District Magistrate

Answer: State Election Commission

Explanation: Article 243K vests the State Election Commission with superintendence of Panchayat elections.

38. In Tamil Nadu's three-tier Panchayati Raj system, the intermediate level is called?

  1. Village Panchayat
  2. Panchayat Union
  3. District Panchayat
  4. Block Development Office

Answer: Panchayat Union

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has Village Panchayat (lower), Panchayat Union (intermediate), and District Panchayat (upper).

39. Which body is the general assembly of all registered voters of a village panchayat?

  1. Gram Sabha
  2. Gram Panchayat
  3. Nyaya Panchayat
  4. Ward Sabha

Answer: Gram Sabha

Explanation: Article 243A defines Gram Sabha as consisting of all persons registered as voters in the panchayat area.

40. Which part of the Indian Constitution contains provisions related to Panchayati Raj Institutions?

  1. Part IX
  2. Part IX-A
  3. Part X
  4. Part VIII

Answer: Part IX

Explanation: Part IX of the Constitution, added by the 73rd Amendment, deals with Panchayats.

41. What is the minimum age prescribed for a person to contest elections to a Panchayat in Tamil Nadu?

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 30 years

Answer: 21 years

Explanation: As per the Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act, the minimum age to contest Panchayat elections is 21 years.

42. Under which Article of the Indian Constitution is the 'District Planning Committee' constituted?

  1. Article 243 ZD
  2. Article 243 ZE
  3. Article 243 ZA
  4. Article 243 ZC

Answer: Article 243 ZD

Explanation: Article 243 ZD provides for the constitution of a District Planning Committee to consolidate plans for the district.

43. Which authority conducts elections to the local bodies in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. Tamil Nadu State Election Commission
  3. Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission
  4. State Finance Commission

Answer: Tamil Nadu State Election Commission

Explanation: The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission is the constitutional authority responsible for conducting local body elections.

44. What is the term of office for a Mayor of a Municipal Corporation in Tamil Nadu?

  1. 3 years
  2. 5 years
  3. 6 years
  4. 4 years

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: The term of a Mayor in Tamil Nadu Municipal Corporations is five years from the date of election.

45. Which of the following is a mandatory function of a Gram Panchayat under the Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act?

  1. Maintenance of public streets
  2. Establishment of colleges
  3. Running of state transport
  4. Maintenance of state highways

Answer: Maintenance of public streets

Explanation: Maintenance of public streets, drains, and sanitation are mandatory functions of a Gram Panchayat.

46. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act added a new schedule to the Indian Constitution. Which one?

  1. Eleventh Schedule
  2. Twelfth Schedule
  3. Tenth Schedule
  4. Ninth Schedule

Answer: Eleventh Schedule

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment added the Eleventh Schedule, listing 29 subjects for Panchayats.

47. In Tamil Nadu, who appoints the Village Panchayat Secretary?

  1. President of the Panchayat
  2. District Collector
  3. State Government
  4. Panchayat Union Commissioner

Answer: State Government

Explanation: The Village Panchayat Secretary is appointed by the State Government as per the Tamil Nadu Panchayats Act.

48. What is the composition of the State Finance Commission in Tamil Nadu?

  1. One member
  2. Three members
  3. Five members
  4. Seven members

Answer: Three members

Explanation: The Tamil Nadu State Finance Commission consists of a Chairperson and two other members.

49. Which level of Panchayati Raj is the intermediate tier in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Panchayat Union (Block) Council
  3. District Panchayat
  4. Village Council

Answer: Panchayat Union (Block) Council

Explanation: The Panchayat Union Council (Block level) is the intermediate tier between Gram Panchayat and District Panchayat.

50. Which part of the Indian Constitution contains provisions related to Panchayati Raj?

  1. Part IX
  2. Part IX-A
  3. Part X
  4. Part VIII

Answer: Part IX

Explanation: Part IX of the Constitution deals with Panchayats, added by the 73rd Amendment Act, 1992.
