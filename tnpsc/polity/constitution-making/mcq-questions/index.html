26. Who was the Chairman of the Constituent Assembly that drafted the Indian Constitution?

  1. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
  2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
  3. Jawaharlal Nehru
  4. Mahatma Gandhi

Answer: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

Explanation: Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the President (Chairman) of the Constituent Assembly. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

27. Which of the following is a feature borrowed from the Irish Constitution in the Indian Constitution?

  1. Directive Principles of State Policy
  2. Federal System
  3. Judicial Review
  4. Fundamental Rights

Answer: Directive Principles of State Policy

Explanation: The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) were borrowed from the Irish Constitution.

28. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution declares India to be a:

  1. Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic
  2. Federal, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic
  3. Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic State
  4. Sovereign, Democratic, Republic with a Unitary bias

Answer: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic

Explanation: The Preamble declares India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic.

29. Under which Article of the Indian Constitution are Fundamental Rights guaranteed?

  1. Article 12 to 35
  2. Article 14 to 32
  3. Article 19 to 22
  4. Article 36 to 51

Answer: Article 12 to 35

Explanation: Fundamental Rights are enshrined in Part III of the Constitution, covering Articles 12 to 35.

30. The concept of 'Panchayati Raj' was constitutionalized through which Amendment Act?

  1. 73rd Amendment Act, 1992
  2. 74th Amendment Act, 1992
  3. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
  4. 44th Amendment Act, 1978

Answer: 73rd Amendment Act, 1992

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment Act (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions.

31. Which of the following is NOT a Directive Principle of State Policy?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Equal pay for equal work
  3. Free legal aid
  4. Protection of the environment

Answer: Right to Equality

Explanation: Right to Equality is a Fundamental Right (Article 14), not a Directive Principle.

32. Who appoints the Chief Election Commissioner of India?

  1. President of India
  2. Prime Minister of India
  3. Chief Justice of India
  4. Parliament

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The President of India appoints the Chief Election Commissioner and other Election Commissioners.

33. The Supreme Court of India was established under which Article of the Constitution?

  1. Article 124
  2. Article 32
  3. Article 136
  4. Article 141

Answer: Article 124

Explanation: Article 124 provides for the establishment and constitution of the Supreme Court of India.

34. Which part of the Indian Constitution deals with 'Local Self-Government' (Panchayats and Municipalities)?

  1. Part IX and Part IXA
  2. Part III and Part IV
  3. Part V and Part VI
  4. Part X and Part XI

Answer: Part IX and Part IXA

Explanation: Part IX deals with Panchayats and Part IXA deals with Municipalities.

35. The idea of 'Judicial Review' in the Indian Constitution is borrowed from which country?

  1. United States of America
  2. United Kingdom
  3. Canada
  4. Australia

Answer: United States of America

Explanation: The concept of Judicial Review was borrowed from the Constitution of the United States of America.

36. Which of the following is a feature of the Tamil Nadu Panchayati Raj system?

  1. Three-tier system with direct elections
  2. Two-tier system with nominated members
  3. Single-tier system for rural areas
  4. Indirect elections for all levels

Answer: Three-tier system with direct elections

Explanation: Tamil Nadu follows a three-tier Panchayati Raj system with direct elections at all levels.

37. Which of the following describes the nature of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Unitary
  2. Federal
  3. Federal with unitary bias
  4. Confederation

Answer: Federal with unitary bias

Explanation: The Indian Constitution is federal in form but unitary in spirit, often called quasi-federal.

38. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution declares India as a ...

  1. Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic
  2. Sovereign, Socialist, Democratic, Republic
  3. Sovereign, Secular, Democratic Republic
  4. Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic

Answer: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic

Explanation: The Preamble declares India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic.

39. Which Fundamental Right is guaranteed only to citizens of India?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right to Freedom of Speech and Expression
  3. Right to Freedom of Religion
  4. Right to Constitutional Remedies

Answer: Right to Freedom of Speech and Expression

Explanation: Article 19 (Right to Freedom) is available only to citizens, not to foreigners.

40. Directive Principles of State Policy are ...

  1. Justiciable
  2. Non-justiciable
  3. Enforceable by courts
  4. Part of Fundamental Rights

Answer: Non-justiciable

Explanation: DPSPs are non-justiciable guidelines for the state, not enforceable by courts.

41. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 is related to ...

  1. Urban Local Bodies
  2. Panchayati Raj
  3. Election Commission
  4. Judiciary

Answer: Panchayati Raj

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions.

42. The judges of the Supreme Court of India are appointed by the ...

  1. President
  2. Prime Minister
  3. Chief Justice of India
  4. Law Minister

Answer: President

Explanation: The President appoints Supreme Court judges after consulting the Chief Justice of India.

43. In Tamil Nadu, the intermediate tier of Panchayati Raj is known as ...

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Panchayat Union
  3. District Panchayat
  4. Zilla Parishad

Answer: Panchayat Union

Explanation: The three tiers are Gram Panchayat (village), Panchayat Union (block), and District Panchayat.

44. The Fundamental Duties were added to the Indian Constitution by which amendment?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 86th Amendment

Answer: 42nd Amendment

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added Part IVA with Fundamental Duties.

45. The concept of a 'Welfare State' in the Indian Constitution is primarily reflected in which part?

  1. Fundamental Rights
  2. Directive Principles
  3. Preamble
  4. Fundamental Duties

Answer: Directive Principles

Explanation: The Directive Principles aim to establish social and economic democracy, reflecting the welfare state ideal.

46. The concept of 'Directive Principles of State Policy' in the Indian Constitution was borrowed from which country?

  1. Ireland
  2. USA
  3. Canada
  4. Australia

Answer: Ireland

Explanation: The Directive Principles of State Policy were borrowed from the Irish Constitution.

47. Who was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Sardar Patel
  3. B. R. Ambedkar
  4. Rajendra Prasad

Answer: B. R. Ambedkar

Explanation: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

48. The Indian Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on which date?

  1. 15 August 1947
  2. 26 January 1950
  3. 26 November 1949
  4. 9 December 1946

Answer: 26 November 1949

Explanation: The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949.

49. Which of the following is NOT a feature of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Federal system
  2. Unitary bias
  3. Presidential system
  4. Parliamentary system

Answer: Presidential system

Explanation: India follows a parliamentary system, not a presidential system.

50. The word 'Socialist' was added to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution by which amendment?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 86th Amendment

Answer: 42nd Amendment

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble.
