26. Which Article of the Indian Constitution prohibits the practice of untouchability?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 15
  3. Article 16
  4. Article 17

Answer: Article 17

Explanation: Article 17 abolishes 'untouchability' and forbids its practice in any form.

27. Who appoints the Governor of a state in India?

  1. Prime Minister
  2. Chief Minister
  3. President
  4. Vice President

Answer: President

Explanation: The Governor of a state is appointed by the President of India under Article 155.

28. What is the term of office for members of Panchayats (village-level local bodies)?

  1. 3 years
  2. 4 years
  3. 5 years
  4. 6 years

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: Article 243E provides that every Panchayat shall continue for five years from its first meeting.

29. Which word was added to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution by the 42nd Amendment?

  1. Sovereign
  2. Democratic
  3. Socialist
  4. Republic

Answer: Socialist

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added the words 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble.

30. Judges of a High Court are appointed by whom?

  1. President
  2. Governor
  3. Chief Justice of High Court
  4. Chief Minister

Answer: President

Explanation: Under Article 217, the President appoints High Court judges after consultation with the Chief Justice of India and the Governor.

31. The State Election Commission is responsible for conducting elections to which bodies?

  1. Lok Sabha
  2. State Assembly
  3. Panchayats and Municipalities
  4. Rajya Sabha

Answer: Panchayats and Municipalities

Explanation: The State Election Commission conducts elections to Panchayats and Municipalities as per Article 243K and 243ZA.

32. Which article provides for reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in Panchayats?

  1. 243C
  2. 243D
  3. 243E
  4. 243F

Answer: 243D

Explanation: Article 243D provides for reservation of seats for SCs/STs in every Panchayat.

33. Fundamental Duties were added to the Indian Constitution by which Amendment?

  1. 44th Amendment
  2. 42nd Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 74th Amendment

Answer: 42nd Amendment

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Part IVA containing Fundamental Duties under Article 51A.

34. Under which part of the Indian Constitution are the provisions related to the State Executive and Legislature found?

  1. Part V
  2. Part VI
  3. Part VII
  4. Part VIII

Answer: Part VI

Explanation: Part VI (Articles 152-237) of the Constitution deals with the State Government, including the Executive and Legislature.

35. Who appoints the Governor of a state?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. President of India
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Chief Justice of High Court

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The Governor is appointed by the President of India under Article 155 of the Constitution.

36. What is the minimum age required to become a member of the State Legislative Assembly?

  1. 21 years
  2. 25 years
  3. 30 years
  4. 35 years

Answer: 25 years

Explanation: Article 173 prescribes the minimum age of 25 years for membership in the State Legislative Assembly.

37. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act is related to which of the following?

  1. Municipalities
  2. Panchayati Raj
  3. Cooperative Societies
  4. Scheduled Castes

Answer: Panchayati Raj

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment (1992) constitutionalized Panchayati Raj institutions in India.

38. The term of office of a member of the State Legislative Council (where it exists) is?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. 7 years
  4. 4 years

Answer: 6 years

Explanation: Members of the Legislative Council have a term of 6 years, with one-third retiring every two years.

39. Which schedule of the Constitution contains provisions for the administration of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in states?

  1. Fifth Schedule
  2. Sixth Schedule
  3. Seventh Schedule
  4. Eighth Schedule

Answer: Fifth Schedule

Explanation: The Fifth Schedule deals with the administration of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in states (except northeastern states).

40. The concept of 'District Planning Committee' is mentioned in which article of the Constitution?

  1. Article 243W
  2. Article 243ZD
  3. Article 243ZE
  4. Article 243Z

Answer: Article 243ZD

Explanation: Article 243ZD provides for the constitution of District Planning Committees to consolidate plans from Panchayats and Municipalities.

41. The 'Amma Unavagam' scheme, providing subsidized meals, is a flagship initiative of which state government?

  1. Kerala
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Karnataka
  4. Andhra Pradesh

Answer: Tamil Nadu

Explanation: Amma Unavagam (canteens) were launched by the Tamil Nadu government to provide affordable meals to the public.

42. The power to decide disqualification of a member of the State Legislature under the anti-defection law lies with?

  1. Governor
  2. Chief Minister
  3. Speaker/Chairman
  4. Election Commission

Answer: Speaker/Chairman

Explanation: Under the Tenth Schedule, the Speaker (or Chairman) of the House decides on disqualification for defection.

43. Which article of the Constitution provides for the establishment of a State Finance Commission?

  1. Article 243I
  2. Article 243J
  3. Article 280
  4. Article 243Y

Answer: Article 243I

Explanation: Article 243I mandates the formation of a State Finance Commission for Panchayats; Article 243Y does the same for Municipalities.

44. According to Article 164, who appoints the Chief Minister of a state?

  1. President
  2. Governor
  3. Chief Minister himself
  4. Legislative Assembly

Answer: Governor

Explanation: The Governor appoints the Chief Minister under Article 164.

45. What is the maximum strength of a state's Legislative Council as per Article 171?

  1. One-fourth of the Assembly
  2. One-third of the Assembly
  3. One-half of the Assembly
  4. Two-thirds of the Assembly

Answer: One-third of the Assembly

Explanation: Article 171 states that the total number of members in the Legislative Council shall not exceed one-third of the total number of members in the Legislative Assembly.

46. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act is related to which local self-government?

  1. Municipalities
  2. Panchayats
  3. Cooperative Societies
  4. District Planning Committees

Answer: Panchayats

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment Act, 1992, provides a constitutional framework for Panchayati Raj institutions.

47. Who conducts elections to Panchayats in a state?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. State Election Commission
  3. Governor
  4. State Legislature

Answer: State Election Commission

Explanation: Under Article 243K, the State Election Commission is responsible for conducting Panchayat elections.

48. In which year was the state of Madras officially renamed as Tamil Nadu?

  1. 1965
  2. 1967
  3. 1969
  4. 1971

Answer: 1969

Explanation: Madras State was renamed Tamil Nadu on January 14, 1969, under the Madras State (Alteration of Name) Act.

49. Under Article 200, the Governor may reserve a bill passed by the state legislature for the consideration of whom?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. Supreme Court
  3. President
  4. Union Cabinet

Answer: President

Explanation: Article 200 allows the Governor to reserve a bill for the President's consideration.

50. The Governor holds office during the pleasure of whom?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. President
  3. Prime Minister
  4. State Legislature

Answer: President

Explanation: Under Article 156, the Governor serves at the pleasure of the President.
