State Govt MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked State Govt questions to revise the TNPSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 211 questions.

1. Who appoints the Chief Minister of a State?

  1. President of India
  2. Governor of the State
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Speaker of Lok Sabha

Answer: Governor of the State

Explanation: The Governor appoints the Chief Minister, who must enjoy the confidence of the Legislative Assembly.

2. A State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) member must be at least:

  1. 21 years old
  2. 25 years old
  3. 30 years old
  4. 18 years old

Answer: 25 years old

Explanation: To be a member of a State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha), a person must be at least 25 years old (Article 173). To vote, the minimum age is 18 years (reduced from 21 by the 61st Amendment, 1988).

3. Tamil Nadu's Legislative Assembly is called:

  1. Vidhan Sabha
  2. Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly (it has unicameral legislature — only one house)
  3. Vidhan Parishad
  4. Tamil Nadu Rajya Sabha

Answer: Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly (it has unicameral legislature — only one house)

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a unicameral legislature — only a Legislative Assembly (not a Vidhan Parishad/Legislative Council). It has 234 constituencies (234 MLAs). Tamil Nadu abolished the Legislative Council in 1986.

4. The Advocate General of a State is appointed by:

  1. Governor
  2. Chief Minister
  3. President
  4. High Court Chief Justice

Answer: Governor

Explanation: Article 165: Advocate General is appointed by the Governor. The Advocate General is the chief law officer of the state, analogous to the Attorney General of India at the Union level.

5. The status of Delhi as National Capital Territory was given by:

  1. 69th Constitutional Amendment (1991)
  2. Constitutional from 1950
  3. An Act of Parliament in 1950
  4. Presidential Order

Answer: 69th Constitutional Amendment (1991)

Explanation: 69th Constitutional Amendment (1991) gave Delhi the status of National Capital Territory (NCT) with its own Legislative Assembly of 70 seats.

6. Chief Minister is appointed by:

  1. Governor
  2. President
  3. State Legislature
  4. Lok Sabha

Answer: Governor

Explanation: Chief Minister is appointed by the Governor. Conventionally, the leader of the majority party/coalition in the State Legislative Assembly is appointed CM. CM need not be a sitting MLA.

7. The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly has how many seats?

  1. 234
  2. 235
  3. 240
  4. 250

Answer: 234

Explanation: The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) has 234 seats (232 general + 2 Anglo-Indian nominated seats, though the 104th Amendment abolished Anglo-Indian nomination from 2020). Currently 234 elected seats.

8. When was the State of Tamil Nadu officially renamed from Madras State?

  1. 1969
  2. 1956
  3. 1950
  4. 1976

Answer: 1969

Explanation: Madras State was renamed Tamil Nadu on 14 January 1969 under Chief Minister C.N. Annadurai. 'Tamil Nadu' means 'Land of Tamils' in Tamil.

9. The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly is:

  1. Unicameral (only one House)
  2. Bicameral (two Houses)
  3. Consists of Rajya Sabha members
  4. Part of bicameral Parliament

Answer: Unicameral (only one House)

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a unicameral legislature — only the Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha). It had a Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad) but it was abolished in 1986.

10. The first Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu was:

  1. C.N. Annadurai
  2. O.P. Ramaswamy Reddiyar
  3. K. Kamaraj
  4. Chakravarti Rajagopalachari (Rajaji)

Answer: C.N. Annadurai

Explanation: O.P. Ramaswamy Reddiyar was the first Chief Minister of Madras State (1947-1949) after independence. Chakravarti Rajagopalachari became the first CM under the new Constitution in 1952.

11. Jayalalithaa served as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu how many times?

  1. 6 times
  2. 4 times
  3. 5 times
  4. 3 times

Answer: 6 times

Explanation: J. Jayalalithaa served as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu 6 times: 1991-96, 2001, 2002-06, 2011-14, 2015, 2016-17. She was the leader of AIADMK from 1989 till her death in December 2016.

12. The maximum strength of a State Legislative Assembly is:

  1. 500 members
  2. 600 members
  3. 400 members
  4. 250 members

Answer: 500 members

Explanation: Article 170: State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) can have not more than 500 members and not less than 60 members (except Goa, Sikkim, Mizoram, Nagaland have less than 60).

13. Tamil Nadu State Legislature is:

  1. Unicameral (only Vidhan Sabha)
  2. Bicameral
  3. Tricameral
  4. Has no Legislature

Answer: Unicameral (only Vidhan Sabha)

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a unicameral legislature — only the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) with 234 members. The Tamil Nadu Legislative Council was abolished in 1986.

14. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the establishment of Panchayats?

  1. Article 243
  2. Article 243A
  3. Article 243B
  4. Article 243C

Answer: Article 243

Explanation: Article 243 of the Constitution provides for the establishment of Panchayats in India.

15. The Governor of a State is appointed by whom?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. President of India
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Chief Justice of India

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The Governor of a State is appointed by the President of India under Article 155.

16. Which part of the Indian Constitution deals with the Directive Principles of State Policy?

  1. Part III
  2. Part IV
  3. Part IV-A
  4. Part V

Answer: Part IV

Explanation: Part IV of the Constitution (Articles 36-51) contains the Directive Principles of State Policy.

17. The minimum age for becoming a member of the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly is:

  1. 21 years
  2. 25 years
  3. 30 years
  4. 35 years

Answer: 25 years

Explanation: A person must be at least 25 years old to be a member of the State Legislative Assembly.

18. Which of the following is a Fundamental Right under the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Property
  2. Right to Work
  3. Right to Education
  4. Right to Adequate Livelihood

Answer: Right to Education

Explanation: Right to Education (Article 21A) is a Fundamental Right; others are not.

19. The term 'Secular' in the Preamble of the Indian Constitution means:

  1. State has an official religion
  2. State treats all religions equally
  3. State promotes atheism
  4. State controls all religions

Answer: State treats all religions equally

Explanation: Secular means the State treats all religions equally and has no official religion.

20. Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Tamil Nadu State Planning Commission?

  1. Governor
  2. Chief Minister
  3. Finance Minister
  4. Chief Secretary

Answer: Chief Minister

Explanation: The Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu is the ex-officio Chairman of the State Planning Commission.

21. Under the Panchayati Raj system, the Gram Sabha consists of:

  1. Elected Panchayat members
  2. All adult residents of the village
  3. Appointed officials
  4. Village elders only

Answer: All adult residents of the village

Explanation: Gram Sabha comprises all adult persons registered as voters in the village.

22. The power to promulgate an Ordinance in a State rests with the:

  1. Chief Minister
  2. Governor
  3. Speaker of Legislative Assembly
  4. High Court

Answer: Governor

Explanation: The Governor can promulgate Ordinances under Article 213 when the legislature is not in session.

23. Which Tamil Nadu government scheme provides free breakfast to students in government schools?

  1. Amma Unavagam
  2. Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme
  3. Pudhumai Penn Scheme
  4. Kalaignar Kanavu Illam

Answer: Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme

Explanation: The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme provides free breakfast to students in government primary schools.

24. Which article of the Indian Constitution defines the Gram Sabha?

  1. 243A
  2. 243B
  3. 243C
  4. 243D

Answer: 243A

Explanation: Article 243A defines the Gram Sabha as a body consisting of persons registered in the electoral rolls of a village.

25. The Directive Principles of State Policy are contained in which Part of the Constitution?

  1. Part III
  2. Part IV
  3. Part IVA
  4. Part V

Answer: Part IV

Explanation: Part IV (Articles 36-51) contains the Directive Principles of State Policy.
