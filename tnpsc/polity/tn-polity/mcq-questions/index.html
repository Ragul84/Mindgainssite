26. Under which Article of the Indian Constitution are Fundamental Rights guaranteed?

  1. Article 12 to 35
  2. Article 14 to 32
  3. Article 12 to 32
  4. Article 14 to 35

Answer: Article 12 to 35

Explanation: Fundamental Rights are enshrined in Part III of the Constitution, covering Articles 12 to 35.

27. Which of the following Directive Principles is based on Gandhian philosophy?

  1. Uniform civil code
  2. Free legal aid
  3. Promotion of cottage industries
  4. Separation of judiciary from executive

Answer: Promotion of cottage industries

Explanation: Promotion of cottage industries on a cooperative basis (Article 43) reflects Gandhian economic thought.

28. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 added which part to the Indian Constitution?

  1. Part IX
  2. Part IX-A
  3. Part X
  4. Part XI

Answer: Part IX

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment added Part IX (Panchayats) to the Constitution, containing Articles 243 to 243-O.

29. Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Tamil Nadu State Planning Commission?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. Governor
  3. Finance Minister
  4. Chief Secretary

Answer: Chief Minister

Explanation: The Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu is the ex-officio Chairman of the State Planning Commission.

30. The Election Commission of India is a body consisting of how many members?

  1. One Chief Election Commissioner and two Election Commissioners
  2. One Chief Election Commissioner and one Election Commissioner
  3. Three Election Commissioners
  4. One Chief Election Commissioner only

Answer: One Chief Election Commissioner and two Election Commissioners

Explanation: Since 1993, the Election Commission has been a multi-member body with one CEC and two ECs.

31. The High Court of Madras was established in which year?

  1. 1862
  2. 1858
  3. 1861
  4. 1870

Answer: 1862

Explanation: The Madras High Court was established on 15 August 1862, making it one of the oldest High Courts in India.

32. Which Tamil Nadu government scheme provides free breakfast to primary school students?

  1. Pudhumai Penn
  2. Kalaignar Kanavu Illam
  3. Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme
  4. Amma Unavagam

Answer: Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme

Explanation: The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme was launched in 2022 to provide free breakfast to students in government primary schools.

33. The Governor of a state is appointed by whom?

  1. President of India
  2. Prime Minister
  3. Chief Minister
  4. Chief Justice of India

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The Governor of a state is appointed by the President of India under Article 155 of the Constitution.

34. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the establishment of Panchayats?

  1. Article 243
  2. Article 246
  3. Article 280
  4. Article 324

Answer: Article 243

Explanation: Article 243 of the Indian Constitution provides for the establishment of Panchayats at the village, intermediate, and district levels.

35. Which of the following is a Directive Principle of State Policy?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right to Freedom of Religion
  3. Right to Work
  4. Right to Constitutional Remedies

Answer: Right to Work

Explanation: Directive Principles, like the Right to Work (Article 41), are non-justiciable guidelines for the state, unlike Fundamental Rights which are justiciable.

36. The State Election Commission in Tamil Nadu is responsible for:

  1. Conducting elections to the State Legislature
  2. Conducting elections to Panchayats and Municipalities
  3. Delimitation of Parliamentary constituencies
  4. Appointment of the Governor

Answer: Conducting elections to Panchayats and Municipalities

Explanation: The State Election Commission conducts elections to local bodies (Panchayats and Municipalities) in Tamil Nadu, as per Article 243K.

37. The minimum age for voting in India is:

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 16 years

Answer: 18 years

Explanation: The 61st Constitutional Amendment Act (1988) reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

38. The Governor of a state is appointed by the:

  1. Chief Minister
  2. President of India
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Chief Justice of the High Court

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The Governor is the constitutional head of a state and is appointed by the President of India under Article 155.

39. Which of the following is a Fundamental Duty under the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Education
  2. To safeguard public property
  3. Right to Freedom of Speech
  4. Right to Life

Answer: To safeguard public property

Explanation: Fundamental Duties (Article 51A) include 'to safeguard public property and to abjure violence', added by the 42nd Amendment.

40. The highest judicial authority in a state is the:

  1. Supreme Court
  2. High Court
  3. District Court
  4. Lok Adalat

Answer: High Court

Explanation: The High Court is the highest judicial body in a state, with appellate jurisdiction over lower courts.

41. The concept of 'Panchayati Raj' in India was constitutionalised through which amendment?

  1. 73rd Amendment
  2. 74th Amendment
  3. 42nd Amendment
  4. 44th Amendment

Answer: 73rd Amendment

Explanation: The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions.

42. The Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu is appointed by the:

  1. President of India
  2. Governor of Tamil Nadu
  3. Chief Justice of Madras High Court
  4. Speaker of the Legislative Assembly

Answer: Governor of Tamil Nadu

Explanation: The Governor appoints the Chief Minister, who is the leader of the majority party in the state legislative assembly.

43. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the establishment of Panchayati Raj institutions?

  1. Article 243
  2. Article 243A
  3. Article 243B
  4. Article 243C

Answer: Article 243

Explanation: Article 243 of the Indian Constitution deals with the definition and establishment of Panchayats.

44. In which year was the Tamil Nadu Panchayati Raj Act enacted?

  1. 1992
  2. 1994
  3. 1998
  4. 2000

Answer: 1994

Explanation: The Tamil Nadu Panchayati Raj Act was enacted in 1994 to implement the 73rd Constitutional Amendment.

45. Who appoints the Governor of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. President of India
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Chief Justice of India

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The Governor of a state is appointed by the President of India under Article 155 of the Constitution.

46. What is the tenure of a member of the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. 4 years
  4. 3 years

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: The normal term of a State Legislative Assembly is five years from the date of its first meeting.

47. Which of the following Fundamental Rights under the Indian Constitution is available only to citizens of India?

  1. Right to Life
  2. Right to Equality
  3. Right to Freedom of Speech and Expression
  4. Right against Exploitation

Answer: Right to Freedom of Speech and Expression

Explanation: Article 19 (Right to Freedom of Speech and Expression) is available only to citizens, not to foreigners.

48. Which of the following is a flagship social welfare scheme of the Tamil Nadu government?

  1. Mid-Day Meal Scheme
  2. Amma Unavagam
  3. Ayushman Bharat
  4. Jan Dhan Yojana

Answer: Amma Unavagam

Explanation: Amma Unavagam is a subsidized meal scheme launched by the Tamil Nadu government in 2013.

49. In Tamil Nadu, the local self-government at the urban level is called:

  1. Panchayat
  2. Municipality
  3. Zilla Parishad
  4. District Board

Answer: Municipality

Explanation: Urban local self-government in Tamil Nadu is governed by municipalities and municipal corporations.

50. The Election Commission of India is responsible for conducting elections to which of the following in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Lok Sabha only
  2. State Legislative Assembly only
  3. Both Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assembly
  4. Only Local Bodies

Answer: Both Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assembly

Explanation: The Election Commission conducts elections to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies, including in Tamil Nadu.
