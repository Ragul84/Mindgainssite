Tn Polity MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Tn Polity questions to revise the TNPSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 137 questions.

1. Which States currently have a Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad)?

  1. Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh
  2. All States have legislative councils
  3. Only 4 states — UP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka
  4. Only 3 States — Maharashtra, UP, Karnataka

Answer: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh

Explanation: Currently 6 States have Vidhan Parishad: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Bihar, and Uttar Pradesh. Tamil Nadu abolished its Vidhan Parishad in 1986.

2. Which party ruled Tamil Nadu for the longest period after 1967?

  1. DMK and AIADMK alternately
  2. INC
  3. BJP
  4. PMK

Answer: DMK and AIADMK alternately

Explanation: Since 1967, Tamil Nadu has been ruled alternately by DMK (founded by M. Karunanidhi) and AIADMK (founded by M.G. Ramachandran). Congress has not won independently since 1967. No other party has formed a government.

3. Periyar E.V. Ramasamy founded which organization?

  1. Self-Respect Movement (1925) and Dravidar Kazhagam (1944)
  2. Justice Party
  3. DMK
  4. Tamil Nadu Congress

Answer: Self-Respect Movement (1925) and Dravidar Kazhagam (1944)

Explanation: Periyar founded the Self-Respect Movement in 1925 and the Dravidar Kazhagam (DK) in 1944. His disciple C.N. Annadurai broke away in 1949 to form the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK).

4. Which Article of the Constitution of India deals with the appointment of the Governor of a state?

  1. Article 155
  2. Article 156
  3. Article 157
  4. Article 158

Answer: Article 155

Explanation: Article 155 states that the Governor of a state shall be appointed by the President by warrant under his hand and seal.

5. How many members are there in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly?

  1. 234
  2. 235
  3. 240
  4. 243

Answer: 234

Explanation: The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly consists of 234 members elected from single-member constituencies.

6. Who was the first Chief Minister of Madras State (later Tamil Nadu) after India's independence?

  1. O. P. Ramaswamy Reddiar
  2. C. Rajagopalachari
  3. K. Kamaraj
  4. M. Bhaktavatsalam

Answer: O. P. Ramaswamy Reddiar

Explanation: O. P. Ramaswamy Reddiar became the first Chief Minister of Madras State in 1947.

7. The three-tier Panchayati Raj system in Tamil Nadu is based on the recommendations of which committee?

  1. Balwant Rai Mehta Committee
  2. Ashok Mehta Committee
  3. L. M. Singhvi Committee
  4. G. V. K. Rao Committee

Answer: Balwant Rai Mehta Committee

Explanation: The Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957) recommended the establishment of a three-tier Panchayati Raj system, adopted by Tamil Nadu.

8. Under which Article can the Governor of Tamil Nadu reserve a state bill for the consideration of the President?

  1. Article 200
  2. Article 201
  3. Article 202
  4. Article 203

Answer: Article 200

Explanation: Article 200 empowers the Governor to reserve a bill for the President's consideration.

9. How many seats in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly are reserved for Scheduled Castes?

  1. 44
  2. 54
  3. 34
  4. 24

Answer: 44

Explanation: As per the latest delimitation, 44 seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes in the Tamil Nadu Assembly.

10. Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for the establishment of a State Public Service Commission?

  1. Article 315
  2. Article 316
  3. Article 317
  4. Article 318

Answer: Article 315

Explanation: Article 315 provides for the establishment of a Public Service Commission for the state.

11. The 'Amma Unavagam' scheme of the Tamil Nadu government primarily aims to provide:

  1. Subsidized meals
  2. Free education
  3. Health insurance
  4. Housing

Answer: Subsidized meals

Explanation: Amma Unavagam (canteens) provide subsidized nutritious meals at low cost to the public.

12. Which of the following is NOT a feature of the Panchayati Raj system in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Direct election of all members
  2. Reservation for women
  3. Three-tier structure
  4. Appointment of members by the Governor

Answer: Appointment of members by the Governor

Explanation: Panchayati Raj members are elected, not appointed by the Governor; the Governor does not appoint them.

13. The Preamble of the Indian Constitution declares India to be a:

  1. Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic
  2. Sovereign, Democratic, Republic
  3. Socialist, Democratic, Republic
  4. Sovereign, Secular, Democratic

Answer: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic

Explanation: The Preamble, as amended by the 42nd Amendment, describes India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic.

14. Which term was added to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution by the 42nd Amendment?

  1. Socialist
  2. Secular
  3. Federal
  4. Both A and B

Answer: Both A and B

Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble.

15. Which Article of the Indian Constitution abolishes untouchability?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 15
  3. Article 17
  4. Article 18

Answer: Article 17

Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form.

16. Which Directive Principle is based on the Gandhian principle?

  1. Uniform civil code
  2. Prohibition of cow slaughter
  3. Free legal aid
  4. Right to work

Answer: Prohibition of cow slaughter

Explanation: Article 48 (prohibition of cow slaughter) is a Gandhian directive principle.

17. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act is related to which level of government?

  1. Urban Local Bodies
  2. Panchayati Raj
  3. Municipalities
  4. District Planning

Answer: Panchayati Raj

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment (1992) deals with Panchayati Raj institutions in rural areas.

18. Who conducts elections to the Panchayats in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Election Commission of India
  2. State Election Commission
  3. Tamil Nadu State Election Commission
  4. Chief Electoral Officer

Answer: State Election Commission

Explanation: The State Election Commission is responsible for conducting Panchayat elections in Tamil Nadu.

19. The High Court of Tamil Nadu is located in which city?

  1. Chennai
  2. Madurai
  3. Coimbatore
  4. Trichy

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: The principal seat of the Madras High Court is in Chennai, with a bench in Madurai.

20. Which scheme provides free breakfast to school children in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Mid-Day Meal Scheme
  2. Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme
  3. Nutritious Meal Programme
  4. Smart Breakfast Scheme

Answer: Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme

Explanation: The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme was launched in 2022 to provide free breakfast to government school students.

21. The Constitution of India is described as?

  1. Unitary
  2. Federal
  3. Quasi-federal
  4. Confederal

Answer: Quasi-federal

Explanation: India is a quasi-federal state with a strong unitary bias, as per K.C. Wheare.

22. Right to Freedom of Religion is guaranteed under which Articles?

  1. 25-28
  2. 14-18
  3. 19-22
  4. 23-24

Answer: 25-28

Explanation: Articles 25 to 28 of the Constitution guarantee the Right to Freedom of Religion.

23. Which tier of Panchayati Raj is optional for states with a population below 20 lakhs?

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Block Panchayat
  3. District Panchayat
  4. All are mandatory

Answer: Block Panchayat

Explanation: The intermediate level (Block Panchayat) is not mandatory for states with less than 20 lakh population.

24. Which of the following is a feature of the Indian Constitution borrowed from the Government of India Act, 1935?

  1. Directive Principles of State Policy
  2. Federal Scheme
  3. Fundamental Rights
  4. Preamble

Answer: Federal Scheme

Explanation: The federal scheme, including the office of Governor and the judiciary structure, was largely borrowed from the Government of India Act, 1935.

25. The concept of 'Justice, social, economic and political' in the Preamble of the Indian Constitution is taken from which revolution?

  1. American Revolution
  2. French Revolution
  3. Russian Revolution
  4. Glorious Revolution

Answer: Russian Revolution

Explanation: The ideals of social, economic and political justice were inspired by the Russian Revolution (1917).
