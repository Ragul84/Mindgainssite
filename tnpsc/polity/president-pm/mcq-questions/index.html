26. The Constitutional provision for special officer for Linguistic Minorities is under:

  1. Article 350B
  2. Article 350A
  3. Article 347
  4. Article 29

Answer: Article 350B

Explanation: Article 350B provides for a Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities to investigate all matters relating to safeguards for linguistic minorities. The officer reports to the President annually.

27. The National Development Council (NDC) is chaired by:

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Finance Minister
  4. NITI Aayog CEO

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The National Development Council is chaired by the Prime Minister. It includes all Union Cabinet Ministers, Chief Ministers of all States, Lt. Governors/Administrators of UTs, and members of NITI Aayog.

28. Which Article deals with the composition of the UPSC?

  1. Article 316
  2. Article 315
  3. Article 317
  4. Article 320

Answer: Article 316

Explanation: Article 316 deals with the appointment and term of office of UPSC members. The Chairman and members are appointed by the President. They can serve for 6 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier.

29. Which article gives the President the power to grant pardon?

  1. Article 71
  2. Article 72
  3. Article 73
  4. Article 74

Answer: Article 72

Explanation: Article 72 empowers the President to grant pardon, reprieve, respite, or remission of punishment to persons convicted of offences.

30. Who was the first President of India?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
  3. Dr. S. Radhakrishnan
  4. Dr. Zakir Hussain

Answer: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

Explanation: Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the first President of India (1950-1962), serving for two consecutive terms.

31. Who was the first Prime Minister of India?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Sardar Patel
  3. Jawaharlal Nehru
  4. Dr. Ambedkar

Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru

Explanation: Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru was India's first Prime Minister, serving from 15 August 1947 until his death on 27 May 1964.

32. The Finance Commission of India is constituted by:

  1. Parliament through a law
  2. President of India every 5 years (Article 280) to recommend how taxes are divided between Centre and States
  3. Prime Minister
  4. RBI and Finance Ministry jointly

Answer: President of India every 5 years (Article 280) to recommend how taxes are divided between Centre and States

Explanation: The Finance Commission (Article 280) is constituted by the President every 5 years. It recommends: how central taxes are shared with states, grants-in-aid to states, and any other matter referred. The 15th Finance Commission (2021-26) recommended 41% tax devolution to states.

33. Who administers the oath to the President?

  1. Chief Justice of India
  2. Vice President
  3. Speaker of Lok Sabha
  4. Attorney General

Answer: Chief Justice of India

Explanation: The Chief Justice of India administers the oath to the President (Article 60). In CJI's absence, the senior-most judge of the Supreme Court administers it.

34. The President acts on the aid and advice of:

  1. Council of Ministers headed by PM
  2. Prime Minister alone
  3. Parliament
  4. Council of State

Answer: Council of Ministers headed by PM

Explanation: Article 74: Council of Ministers with PM as head shall aid and advise the President, who shall act in accordance with such advice (after 42nd and 44th Amendments, this is binding).

35. The Finance Commission is constituted under:

  1. Article 280
  2. Article 263
  3. Article 315
  4. Article 324

Answer: Article 280

Explanation: Article 280: Finance Commission is constituted by the President every 5 years to recommend distribution of tax revenues between Centre and States and grants-in-aid to states.

36. The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) is established by:

  1. Article 315
  2. Article 316
  3. Article 280
  4. Article 324

Answer: Article 315

Explanation: Article 315 establishes the UPSC. It conducts examinations for appointments to the services of the Union. Its chairman is appointed by the President.

37. The National SC/ST Commission is chaired by:

  1. A member from SC/ST community appointed by President
  2. Chief Justice of India
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Minister of Social Justice

Answer: A member from SC/ST community appointed by President

Explanation: National Commission for Scheduled Castes (Art. 338) and SC/ST has a Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson and other members appointed by the President.

38. The Chairman of NITI Aayog is:

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Finance Minister
  4. Home Minister

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The Prime Minister is the ex-officio Chairman of NITI Aayog. It also has a CEO (appointed by PM), Vice Chairman, and Governing Council (all CMs and LGs of states/UTs).

39. Which article of the Constitution talks about abolition of titles?

  1. Article 18
  2. Article 17
  3. Article 16
  4. Article 15

Answer: Article 18

Explanation: Article 18 abolishes titles: No title shall be conferred by the State. Citizens cannot accept titles from foreign governments without President's consent. Exceptions: Military/academic distinctions.

40. The Inter-State Council (Art 263) is chaired by:

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Vice President
  4. Home Minister

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The Inter-State Council is chaired by the Prime Minister and includes Chief Ministers of all States and Administrators of UTs with legislatures, plus 6 Cabinet Ministers nominated by PM.

41. The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) is a:

  1. Constitutional body under Article 315
  2. Statutory body
  3. Executive body
  4. Advisory body to State governments

Answer: Constitutional body under Article 315

Explanation: UPSC is a Constitutional body established under Article 315. It recruits civil servants for All India Services and Central Services. Its Chairperson can be removed only by Presidential order.

42. The National Commission for Backward Classes was given constitutional status by which Amendment?

  1. 102nd Amendment, 2018
  2. 101st Amendment, 2016
  3. 103rd Amendment, 2019
  4. 97th Amendment, 2011

Answer: 102nd Amendment, 2018

Explanation: The 102nd Amendment (2018) gave constitutional status to the National Commission for Backward Classes under Art 338B. It also inserted Art 342A empowering President to specify Other Backward Classes.

43. The Governor of Tamil Nadu is appointed by:

  1. President of India
  2. Prime Minister
  3. Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu
  4. Parliament

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The Governor of a State is appointed by the President of India under Art 155. By convention, this is done on the advice of the Union Cabinet (PM). The Governor holds office during the President's pleasure.

44. Union Territories are administered by:

  1. President through Lieutenant Governor/Administrator
  2. Governor
  3. Chief Minister
  4. Home Ministry directly

Answer: President through Lieutenant Governor/Administrator

Explanation: Union Territories are administered by the President of India (Article 239) through a Lieutenant Governor (LG) or Administrator appointed by the President.

45. Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?

  1. President of India
  2. Prime Minister of India
  3. Vice President of India
  4. Speaker of Lok Sabha

Answer: Vice President of India

Explanation: The Vice President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

46. Which part of the Indian Constitution deals with the Directive Principles of State Policy?

  1. Part III
  2. Part IV
  3. Part IVA
  4. Part V

Answer: Part IV

Explanation: Directive Principles of State Policy are covered in Part IV of the Indian Constitution.

47. The President of India can nominate how many members to the Lok Sabha from the Anglo-Indian community?

  1. Two
  2. One
  3. Twelve
  4. Ten

Answer: Two

Explanation: The President nominates two members from the Anglo-Indian community to Lok Sabha if the community is not adequately represented.

48. Which article of the Indian Constitution empowers the President to promulgate ordinances when Parliament is not in session?

  1. Article 123
  2. Article 213
  3. Article 352
  4. Article 356

Answer: Article 123

Explanation: Article 123 gives the President the power to promulgate ordinances during recess of Parliament.

49. The Prime Minister of India is appointed by the President. Under which article is this provision?

  1. Article 74
  2. Article 75
  3. Article 78
  4. Article 79

Answer: Article 75

Explanation: Article 75 states that the Prime Minister is appointed by the President and other ministers on PM's advice.

50. Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Right under Part III of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right to Freedom
  3. Right to Property
  4. Right against Exploitation

Answer: Right to Property

Explanation: Right to Property is a legal right under Article 300A, not a Fundamental Right.
