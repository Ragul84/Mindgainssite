26. Which of the following is a feature of GDP?

  1. It includes only goods produced
  2. It includes only services produced
  3. It includes both goods and services produced
  4. It excludes income from abroad

Answer: It includes both goods and services produced

Explanation: GDP includes both goods and services produced.

27. What is the name of the national income concept that includes income from abroad?

  1. Gross National Income
  2. Net National Income
  3. Gross Domestic Product
  4. National Income

Answer: Gross National Income

Explanation: Gross National Income includes income from abroad.

28. Which of the following is a goal of the Planning Commission/NITI Aayog?

  1. To reduce poverty and inequality
  2. To increase foreign investment
  3. To promote economic growth
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Planning Commission/NITI Aayog aims to promote economic growth and reduce poverty.

29. Which of the following is a key feature of the Tamil Nadu government's 'Uzhavan' app?

  1. To provide farm equipment
  2. To offer crop insurance
  3. To give farmers market information
  4. To provide farm labor

Answer: To give farmers market information

Explanation: The Uzhavan app provides market information to farmers. It helps them make informed decisions.

30. What does the National Income account for?

  1. Only consumer spending
  2. Only government spending
  3. All goods and services produced
  4. Only foreign trade

Answer: All goods and services produced

Explanation: National Income accounts for all goods and services produced. It is a measure of a country's economic activity.

31. Which scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers in Tamil Nadu?

  3. Uzhavan Scheme
  4. Kisan Credit Card

Explanation: The Uzhavan Scheme provides financial assistance to farmers. It is a Tamil Nadu government initiative.

32. What is the primary goal of the budgeting process in India?

  1. To reduce taxes
  2. To increase spending
  3. To allocate resources efficiently
  4. To reduce national debt

Answer: To allocate resources efficiently

Explanation: The primary goal of budgeting is to allocate resources efficiently. It helps in achieving economic objectives.
