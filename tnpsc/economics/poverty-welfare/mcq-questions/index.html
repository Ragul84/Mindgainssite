26. The Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) in India is conducted by:

  1. NITI Aayog
  2. Ministry of Labour
  3. National Statistical Office (NSO)
  4. NSSO under MOSPI

Answer: National Statistical Office (NSO)

Explanation: PLFS is conducted by NSO (National Statistical Office) under MoSPI. Launched 2017-18, provides quarterly urban + annual rural employment/unemployment estimates.

27. India's Urban Employment Guarantee Scheme (UGEGS) for urban poor was introduced in:

  1. 2020 during COVID-19 pandemic as 'PM Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyan' for migrant workers — some states like Rajasthan have their own urban employment guarantee schemes
  2. 2005 (same time as MGNREGA)
  3. 2010
  4. 2015

Answer: 2020 during COVID-19 pandemic as 'PM Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyan' for migrant workers — some states like Rajasthan have their own urban employment guarantee schemes

Explanation: Urban employment guarantee: the PM Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyan (June 2020) provided 125 days of work to returning migrant workers in 116 districts. Rajasthan's Indira Gandhi Shahri Rozgar Guarantee Yojana (2022): 100 days urban employment. Kerala has similar scheme. No national-level urban employment guarantee act yet.

28. POSHAN 2.0 (2021) merged which earlier schemes?

  1. ICDS and MGNREGA
  2. POSHAN Abhiyaan and Supplementary Nutrition Programme and other nutrition schemes
  3. NHM and ICDS
  4. Anganwadi and PM POSHAN

Answer: POSHAN Abhiyaan and Supplementary Nutrition Programme and other nutrition schemes

Explanation: POSHAN 2.0: merged POSHAN Abhiyaan, ICDS (Supplementary Nutrition), National Creche Scheme, Kishori Shakti Yojana into an integrated nutrition support programme under Mission Saksham Anganwadi.

29. PM POSHAN scheme (renamed from Mid-Day Meal in 2021) covers students up to:

  1. Class V
  2. Class VIII
  3. Class X
  4. Class XII

Answer: Class VIII

Explanation: PM POSHAN: covers students from pre-primary (Balvatika) to Class VIII in government and government-aided schools. ~11.8 crore students across ~11.2 lakh schools.

30. Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is the flagship scheme for:

  1. Agricultural skilling
  2. Short-term skill training and certification for youth
  3. Entrepreneurship development for women
  4. Digital literacy in rural areas

Answer: Short-term skill training and certification for youth

Explanation: PMKVY (2015): short-term skill training by NSDC through Training Partners. Provides free training, assessment, certification, and monetary reward. Target: skill 1 crore people.

31. The Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme provides incentive as a percentage of:

  1. Fixed capital investment
  2. Incremental sales over a base year
  3. Employment generated
  4. Export value

Answer: Incremental sales over a base year

Explanation: PLI: companies receive incentive (4-20% depending on sector) on incremental sales above their base year (2019-20). Covers 14 sectors. Aims to attract investment, enhance manufacturing, and boost exports.

32. Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (Jan Arogya Yojana) provides health cover of how much per family per year?

  1. Rs 1 lakh
  2. Rs 3 lakh
  3. Rs 5 lakh
  4. Rs 10 lakh

Answer: Rs 5 lakh

Explanation: PM-JAY (launched September 23, 2018): Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. World's largest government health insurance scheme. ~55 crore beneficiaries (50 crore poor).

33. Which committee recommended the rationalization of centrally sponsored schemes (CSS) in India?

  1. Rangarajan Committee
  2. Kelkar Committee
  3. B.K. Chaturvedi Committee
  4. Shivraj Patil Committee

Answer: B.K. Chaturvedi Committee

Explanation: B.K. Chaturvedi Committee (2011): recommended rationalizing and restructuring CSS. Later, Sub-Group of CMs under CM Shivraj Singh Chouhan (2015) recommended reducing CSS from 66 to 28 for more flexibility.
