Banking Finance MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Banking Finance questions to revise the TNPSC Economy syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 644 questions.

1. Which is the apex bank of India?

  1. State Bank of India
  2. Bank of India
  3. Reserve Bank of India
  4. Punjab National Bank

Answer: Reserve Bank of India

Explanation: RBI is the apex bank (central bank) of India, established in 1935. It regulates all commercial banks and controls monetary policy.

2. The headquarters of RBI is in:

  1. New Delhi
  2. Kolkata
  3. Mumbai
  4. Chennai

Answer: Mumbai

Explanation: The Reserve Bank of India's headquarters is in Mumbai (Maharashtra). It was originally established in Kolkata in 1935 and moved to Mumbai in 1937.

3. The 'Reverse Repo Rate' is:

  1. Rate at which RBI borrows from commercial banks
  2. Rate at which banks borrow from public
  3. Rate at which RBI lends to banks
  4. Rate at which banks lend to each other

Answer: Rate at which RBI borrows from commercial banks

Explanation: Reverse Repo Rate is the rate at which the RBI borrows money from commercial banks. It is always lower than the Repo Rate.

4. The CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio) means:

  1. The percentage of deposits banks must lend to industry
  2. The minimum percentage of deposits banks must maintain as cash with RBI
  3. The ratio of gold to currency
  4. The minimum capital banks must hold

Answer: The minimum percentage of deposits banks must maintain as cash with RBI

Explanation: CRR is the percentage of a bank's total deposits that must be maintained as cash reserves with the RBI. It controls money supply — higher CRR = less money available to lend.

5. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was established in:

  1. 1935 (April 1) — under the RBI Act 1934; nationalized in 1949; headquartered in Mumbai
  2. 1947
  3. 1969
  4. 1950

Answer: 1935 (April 1) — under the RBI Act 1934; nationalized in 1949; headquartered in Mumbai

Explanation: RBI: established April 1, 1935, as a private shareholders' bank under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (based on Hilton-Young Commission recommendations). Nationalized January 1, 1949. Governor: Shaktikanta Das (26th Governor). HQ: Mumbai. Key functions: monetary policy, currency issue, banking regulation, forex management.
