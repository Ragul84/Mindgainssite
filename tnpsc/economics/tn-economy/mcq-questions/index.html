26. Which of the following is a type of national income?

  2. GNP
  3. NDP
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All of the above are types of national income.

27. Which organization is responsible for estimating national income in India?

  1. CSO
  2. RBI
  3. NSE
  4. BSE

Explanation: The Central Statistical Office (CSO) is responsible for estimating national income in India.
