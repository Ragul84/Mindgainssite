Current Schemes MCQs with answers

1. The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) aims at:

  1. Financial inclusion — opening zero-balance bank accounts for unbanked sections with RuPay debit card, overdraft facility, and insurance
  2. Free LPG connections
  3. Healthcare for poor
  4. Rural electrification

Answer: Financial inclusion — opening zero-balance bank accounts for unbanked sections with RuPay debit card, overdraft facility, and insurance

Explanation: PMJDY (August 28, 2014): world's largest financial inclusion initiative. Features: zero-balance savings account (Jan Dhan account), RuPay debit card with Rs 2 lakh accident insurance, Rs 30,000 life insurance, Rs 10,000 overdraft facility. By 2024: 52+ crore accounts opened, mostly in rural areas.

2. The National Food Security Act (NFSA) 2013 provides:

  1. Subsidized food grains to ~81 crore beneficiaries (75% rural, 50% urban population) — rice at Rs 3/kg, wheat at Rs 2/kg, coarse grains at Rs 1/kg
  2. Free food to all citizens
  3. Food to BPL families only
  4. Export of food grains

Answer: Subsidized food grains to ~81 crore beneficiaries (75% rural, 50% urban population) — rice at Rs 3/kg, wheat at Rs 2/kg, coarse grains at Rs 1/kg

Explanation: NFSA 2013: legal entitlement to subsidized food. Priority households: 5 kg/person/month. Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY — poorest of poor): 35 kg/household/month. Prices: rice Rs 3, wheat Rs 2, coarse cereals Rs 1 per kg. Implemented through PDS. About 81 crore people covered.

3. Pension reforms in India were initiated with the establishment of:

  1. PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority, 2003) — regulates NPS, APY, and other pension schemes; statutory status 2014 under PFRDA Act
  2. LIC
  3. RBI
  4. SEBI

Answer: PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority, 2003) — regulates NPS, APY, and other pension schemes; statutory status 2014 under PFRDA Act

Explanation: PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority): interim PFRDA set up 2003 to manage NPS rollout for new central government employees. PFRDA Act 2013 (in force 2014) gave it statutory status. Regulates: NPS (National Pension System — for government and private sector), APY (Atal Pension Yojana — for unorganized sector). Fund managers: SBI, LIC, HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Kotak, UTI, Aditya Birla.

4. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) was launched to:

  1. Provide LPG connections to BPL (Below Poverty Line) households — 8 crore connections given; reduces indoor air pollution from burning solid fuels; launched 2016 from Ballia, UP
  2. Provide electricity connections
  3. Supply drinking water
  4. Build toilets (that's Swachh Bharat)

Answer: Provide LPG connections to BPL (Below Poverty Line) households — 8 crore connections given; reduces indoor air pollution from burning solid fuels; launched 2016 from Ballia, UP

Explanation: PMUY (May 2016): provided free LPG connections (deposit waived, EMI for stove/first cylinder) to BPL women. Phase 1: 5 crore connections. Phase 2: 3 crore more. PMUY 2.0 (2021): 1 crore more, including migrants. Beneficiary: women in BPL household. Benefits: reduction in indoor air pollution, time saved from collecting biomass fuel, empowerment of women.

5. The Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY) is:

  1. Skill training and placement program for rural poor youth (15-35 years, BPL families) — provides free training in 250+ trades, placement guarantee; part of MGNREGA complementary scheme
  2. Scholarship for rural students
  3. Employment guarantee (MGNREGA is that)
  4. Urban skill development

Answer: Skill training and placement program for rural poor youth (15-35 years, BPL families) — provides free training in 250+ trades, placement guarantee; part of MGNREGA complementary scheme

Explanation: DDU-GKY (2014, renamed Aajeevika Skills/NRLM Skills): targets rural BPL youth. Free skill training, boarding, lodging during training, and placement assistance. Coverage: 15-35 years (extended to 45 for some groups). Target: place 75%+ trained youth in paid jobs. Implemented by Ministry of Rural Development through State Rural Livelihoods Missions.
