26. What is the name of the organization responsible for promoting and regulating foreign trade in India?

  1. Directorate General of Foreign Trade
  2. Ministry of Commerce and Industry
  3. Ministry of Finance
  4. Reserve Bank of India

Answer: Directorate General of Foreign Trade

Explanation: The Directorate General of Foreign Trade is responsible for promoting and regulating foreign trade in India. It is an attached office of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

27. What is the primary aim of the Export-Import Bank of India in the context of international trade?

  1. Promote exports
  2. Regulate imports
  3. Stabilize currency
  4. Manage foreign debt

Answer: Promote exports

Explanation: The Export-Import Bank of India aims to promote exports by providing financial assistance to exporters and importers.

28. Which of the following is a key component of India's international trade policy?

  1. Make in India
  2. Digital India
  3. Export-oriented manufacturing
  4. Import substitution

Answer: Make in India

Explanation: Make in India is a key component of India's international trade policy, aiming to promote manufacturing and exports.

30. Which organization is responsible for formulating India's Five-Year Plans, including those related to international trade?

  4. Ministry of Finance

Explanation: NITI Aayog is responsible for formulating India's development plans, including those related to international trade.

31. What is the term for the difference between the value of a country's exports and imports?

  1. Balance of trade
  2. Balance of payments
  3. Current account deficit
  4. Trade surplus

Answer: Balance of trade

Explanation: The balance of trade refers to the difference between the value of a country's exports and imports.

32. What is the name of the free trade agreement between India and ASEAN countries?

  2. India-Singapore FTA
  3. India-Malaysia FTA

Answer: India-ASEAN FTA

Explanation: The India-ASEAN FTA is a free trade agreement between India and ASEAN countries, aiming to promote trade and investment.

33. Which of the following is a benefit of international trade for the Indian economy?

  1. Increased competition
  2. Improved productivity
  3. Higher employment
  4. Reduced inflation

Answer: Improved productivity

Explanation: International trade can lead to improved productivity in the Indian economy by promoting competition and innovation.

34. What is the term for the value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders?

  1. GDP
  2. GNP
  3. NDP
  4. NNP

Answer: GDP

Explanation: GDP refers to the value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders.

35. Which of the following is a major challenge faced by Indian exporters in international trade?

  1. Competition from China

  3. High logistics costs
  4. Stringent quality standards

Answer: Fluctuating exchange rates

Explanation: Fluctuating exchange rates can be a major challenge for Indian exporters, affecting their competitiveness in international markets.
