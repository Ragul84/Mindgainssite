National Income MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked National Income questions to revise the TNPSC Economy syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. GDP stands for:

  1. Gross Domestic Product
  2. General Domestic Price
  3. Gross Development Plan
  4. Government Domestic Policy

Answer: Gross Domestic Product

Explanation: GDP (Gross Domestic Product) is the total monetary value of all goods and services produced within a country's borders in a specific period.

2. Which body formulates India's monetary policy?

  1. SEBI
  2. Ministry of Finance
  3. Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
  4. NITI Aayog

Answer: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Explanation: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is India's central bank and formulates monetary policy to control inflation, money supply, and credit.

3. Inflation means:

  1. Fall in general price level
  2. Rise in general price level
  3. Stable prices
  4. Fall in interest rates

Answer: Rise in general price level

Explanation: Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises, eroding purchasing power.

4. The 'Repo Rate' is the rate at which:

  1. Banks borrow from each other
  2. RBI lends money to commercial banks (short-term)
  3. Commercial banks lend to customers
  4. Government borrows from RBI

Answer: RBI lends money to commercial banks (short-term)

Explanation: Repo Rate is the rate at which the RBI lends money to commercial banks for short periods (usually overnight) against government securities. Increasing repo rate reduces money supply and controls inflation.

5. The 'Human Development Index' (HDI) is published by:

  1. World Bank
  2. IMF
  3. UNDP (United Nations Development Programme)
  4. WHO

Answer: UNDP (United Nations Development Programme)

Explanation: The HDI is published by UNDP in the Human Development Report. It measures countries on three dimensions: life expectancy, education, and per capita income.
