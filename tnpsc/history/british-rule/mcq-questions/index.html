British Rule MCQs with answers

26. The British government divided Bengal into two provinces in:

  1. 1903
  2. 1905
  3. 1911
  4. 1915

Answer: 1905

Explanation: The British government divided Bengal into two provinces in 1905. This decision was met with strong opposition from the Indian nationalist movement.

27. India gained independence from British rule on:

  1. 14 August 1947
  2. 15 August 1947
  3. 16 August 1947
  4. 17 August 1947

Answer: 15 August 1947

Explanation: India gained independence from British rule on 15 August 1947. This marked the end of British rule in India and the beginning of a new era for the country.

28. Who was the first Governor-General of India under the British East India Company?

  1. Warren Hastings
  2. Lord Cornwallis
  3. Lord Wellesley
  4. Lord Curzon

Answer: Warren Hastings

Explanation: Warren Hastings was the first Governor-General of India, serving from 1773 to 1785. He played a key role in establishing British rule in India.

29. The British East India Company's monopoly on Indian trade was granted by which royal charter?

  1. 1600
  2. 1612
  3. 1658
  4. 1708

Answer: 1600

Explanation: The British East India Company was granted a royal charter in 1600, giving it a monopoly on Indian trade. This charter marked the beginning of British involvement in India.

30. The Permanent Settlement of 1793 was implemented by which British official?

  1. Lord Cornwallis
  2. Lord Wellesley
  3. Warren Hastings
  4. Lord Bentinck

Answer: Lord Cornwallis

Explanation: The Permanent Settlement of 1793 was implemented by Lord Cornwallis, which had a significant impact on Indian agriculture and land ownership. It introduced a new system of land revenue collection.

31. The Indian Rebellion of 1857 was also known as?

  1. Sepoy Mutiny
  2. Indian War of Independence
  3. Great Rebellion
  4. National Uprising

Answer: Sepoy Mutiny

Explanation: The Indian Rebellion of 1857 is also known as the Sepoy Mutiny, which was a major uprising against British rule in India. It marked a significant turning point in Indian history.

32. The British government passed the Government of India Act in which year?

  1. 1833
  2. 1858
  3. 1909
  4. 1935

Answer: 1858

Explanation: The British government passed the Government of India Act in 1858, which marked a significant shift in British policy towards India. It established the British Raj in India.

33. The Quit India Movement was launched in which year?

  1. 1940
  2. 1942
  3. 1945
  4. 1947

Answer: 1942

Explanation: The Quit India Movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in 1942, which was a major campaign demanding immediate independence for India. It was a key moment in the Indian freedom struggle.

34. The Indian National Congress was founded in which year?

  1. 1884
  2. 1885
  3. 1887
  4. 1890

Answer: 1885

Explanation: The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885, which became a major platform for the Indian independence movement. It played a key role in the struggle for Indian freedom.

35. The British East India Company's rule in India was marked by the Battle of?

  1. Plassey
  2. Buxar
  3. Panipat
  4. Wandiwash

Answer: Plassey

Explanation: The Battle of Plassey in 1757 marked the beginning of British rule in India, as the British East India Company defeated the ruler of Bengal. It was a decisive victory for the British.

36. The Regulating Act of 1773 was passed by the?

  1. British Parliament
  2. British East India Company
  3. Indian National Congress
  4. Mughal Empire

Answer: British Parliament

Explanation: The Regulating Act of 1773 was passed by the British Parliament, which was the first attempt to regulate the British East India Company's affairs in India. It marked a significant shift in British policy towards India.

37. The British East India Company's monopoly over Indian trade was abolished by the:

  1. Charter Act of 1813
  2. Charter Act of 1833
  3. Charter Act of 1853
  4. Government of India Act 1858

Answer: Charter Act of 1813

Explanation: The British East India Company's monopoly over Indian trade was abolished by the Charter Act of 1813. This act marked a significant change in British policy towards India.

38. The British East India Company was founded in which year?

  1. 1600
  2. 1650
  3. 1700
  4. 1750

Answer: 1600

Explanation: The British East India Company was founded in 1600. It was granted a royal charter by Queen Elizabeth I.

39. The British government divided Bengal into two parts in?

  1. 1903
  2. 1905
  3. 1911
  4. 1915

Answer: 1905

Explanation: The British government divided Bengal into two parts in 1905. It was a policy of divide and rule.

40. The Indian Rebellion of 1857 started at?

  1. Delhi
  2. Meerut
  3. Kanpur
  4. Lucknow

Answer: Meerut

Explanation: The Indian Rebellion of 1857 started at Meerut. It was a major uprising against British rule in India.

41. The Government of India Act of 1935 was passed to?

  1. Establish provincial autonomy
  2. Abolish the Indian princely states
  3. Introduce federalism in India
  4. Make English the official language

Answer: Establish provincial autonomy

Explanation: The Government of India Act of 1935 was passed to establish provincial autonomy. It marked a significant step towards self-government in India.

42. The Quit India Movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in?

  1. 1940
  2. 1942
  3. 1945
  4. 1947

Answer: 1942

Explanation: The Quit India Movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in 1942. It was a major movement demanding an end to British rule in India.

43. The Rowlatt Act was passed in?

  1. 1917
  2. 1918
  3. 1919
  4. 1920

Answer: 1919

Explanation: The Rowlatt Act was passed in 1919. It was a repressive legislation that sparked widespread protests in India.

44. Which act abolished the East India Company's monopoly on trade in India?

  1. Regulating Act
  2. Pitt's India Act
  3. Charter Act
  4. Government of India Act

Answer: Charter Act

Explanation: The Charter Act of 1813 abolished the East India Company's monopoly on trade in India, opening up the Indian market to other British traders.

45. The British East India Company's rule in India began with the Battle of?

  1. Plassey
  2. Buxar
  3. Wandiwash
  4. Arcot

Answer: Plassey

Explanation: The Battle of Plassey in 1757 marked the beginning of British East India Company's rule in India.

46. Who led the Sepoy Mutiny of 1857 against British rule?

  1. Mangal Pandey
  2. Tantia Tope
  3. Rani Lakshmibai
  4. Bahadur Shah II

Answer: Mangal Pandey

Explanation: Mangal Pandey is often credited as the first person to revolt against British rule, sparking the Sepoy Mutiny of 1857.

47. The permanent settlement of land revenue was introduced by?

  1. Lord Cornwallis
  2. Lord Wellesley
  3. Lord Curzon
  4. Lord Dalhousie

Answer: Lord Cornwallis

Explanation: Lord Cornwallis introduced the permanent settlement of land revenue in 1793, which had a significant impact on Indian agriculture and society.

48. Which British policy led to the exploitation of Indian resources and the decline of Indian industries?

  1. Drain of Wealth
  2. Divide and Rule
  3. Laissez Faire
  4. Free Trade

Answer: Drain of Wealth

Explanation: The Drain of Wealth policy led to the exploitation of Indian resources and the decline of Indian industries, as Britain extracted vast amounts of wealth and resources from India.

49. Who is known as the 'Father of the Indian National Movement'?

  1. Raja Ram Mohan Roy
  2. Mahatma Gandhi
  3. Bipin Chandra Pal
  4. A.O. Hume

Answer: Raja Ram Mohan Roy

Explanation: Raja Ram Mohan Roy is often referred to as the 'Father of the Indian National Movement' due to his pioneering work in social and political reform.

  1. 1905
  2. 1910
  3. 1915
  4. 1919

Answer: 1919

Explanation: The Rowlett Act was passed in 1919, giving the British government vast powers to suppress Indian nationalism and dissent.
