26. Who was the leader of the Indian independence movement who advocated for non-violent resistance?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Subhas Chandra Bose
  3. Jawaharlal Nehru
  4. Bhagat Singh

Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi led the Indian independence movement using non-violent resistance.

27. The Maratha Empire was founded by which leader in 1674?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Sambhaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Tarabai

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji founded the Maratha Empire.

28. The British East India Company established its first factory in India at which location in 1612?

  1. Surat
  2. Madras
  3. Bombay
  4. Calcutta

Answer: Surat

Explanation: The British East India Company set up its first factory in Surat.

29. Which social reform movement in India aimed to uplift the status of women and promote female education?

  1. Arya Samaj
  2. Ramakrishna Mission
  3. Brahmo Samaj
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Several social reform movements, including the Arya Samaj, Ramakrishna Mission, and Brahmo Samaj, worked towards improving women's status and promoting female education.

30. The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885 by which British civil servant?

  4. Lord Dalhousie

Answer: Allan Octavian Hume

Explanation: Allan Octavian Hume founded the Indian National Congress.

31. Which of the following Indus Valley cities is known for its well-planned drainage system?

  1. Mohenjo-Daro
  2. Harappa
  3. Lothal
  4. Kalibangan

Answer: Mohenjo-Daro

Explanation: Mohenjo-Daro is known for its well-planned drainage system, a notable feature of Indus Valley civilization.

32. Who is the author of the ancient Indian text 'Rigveda'?

  1. Vyasa
  2. Valmiki
  3. Vedavyasa
  4. Unknown

Answer: Unknown

Explanation: The author of the ancient Indian text 'Rigveda' is unknown, as it is a compiled work of various sages.

  4. 1947
