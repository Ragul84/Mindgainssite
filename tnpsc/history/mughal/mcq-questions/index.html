26. Who was the founder of the Mughal Empire in India?

  1. Babur
  2. Akbar
  3. Humayun
  4. Shah Jahan

Answer: Babur

Explanation: Babur founded the Mughal Empire in 1526. He was a Central Asian ruler.

27. Which Mughal emperor built the Taj Mahal?

  1. Shah Jahan
  2. Akbar
  3. Jahangir
  4. Aurangzeb

Answer: Shah Jahan

Explanation: Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

28. What was the name of the Mughal emperor who introduced the mansabdari system?

  1. Akbar
  2. Babur
  3. Humayun
  4. Shah Jahan

Answer: Akbar

Explanation: Akbar introduced the mansabdari system to organize the Mughal army and administration.

29. Which of the following was a key feature of the Mughal administrative system?

  1. Zamindari
  2. Mansabdari
  3. Jagirdari
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Mughal administrative system featured zamindari, mansabdari, and jagirdari.

30. Who was the last Mughal emperor?

  1. Bahadur Shah II
  2. Aurangzeb
  3. Shah Jahan
  4. Akbar II

Answer: Bahadur Shah II

Explanation: Bahadur Shah II was the last Mughal emperor, exiled by the British in 1857.

31. What was the name of the Mughal emperor who issued the Din-i-Ilahi?

  1. Akbar
  2. Jahangir
  3. Shah Jahan
  4. Humayun

Answer: Akbar

Explanation: Akbar introduced the Din-i-Ilahi, a syncretic religion, in 1581.

32. Which of the following Mughal emperors was known for his tolerance of other religions?

  1. Akbar
  2. Aurangzeb
  3. Shah Jahan
  4. Jahangir

Answer: Akbar

Explanation: Akbar was known for his tolerance of other religions, including Hinduism and Christianity.
