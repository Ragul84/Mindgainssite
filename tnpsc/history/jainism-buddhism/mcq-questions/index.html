Jainism Buddhism MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Jainism Buddhism questions to revise the TNPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

26. Which Jain Tirthankara is associated with the symbol of a lion?

  1. Rishabhanatha
  2. Mahavira
  3. Parshvanatha
  4. Neminatha

Answer: Mahavira

Explanation: Mahavira, the 24th Tirthankara, is symbolized by a lion.

27. The first Buddhist Council was held at which location?

  1. Vaishali
  2. Rajgriha
  3. Pataliputra
  4. Kundagrama

Answer: Rajgriha

Explanation: The first Buddhist Council was convened at Rajgriha (Rajgir) shortly after Buddha's death.

28. Which Mauryan ruler embraced Jainism and is said to have starved himself to death?

  1. Bindusara
  2. Chandragupta Maurya
  3. Ashoka
  4. Dasharatha

Answer: Chandragupta Maurya

Explanation: Chandragupta Maurya adopted Jainism and performed Sallekhana (fast unto death) at Shravanabelagola.

29. The term 'Tripitaka' is associated with which religious tradition?

  1. Jainism
  2. Buddhism
  3. Hinduism
  4. Ajivika

Answer: Buddhism

Explanation: Tripitaka (Three Baskets) are the canonical texts of Buddhism, comprising Vinaya, Sutta, and Abhidhamma Pitakas.

30. Which Chola king is known for his patronage of Jainism and built the Jain temple at Tirumalai?

  1. Rajaraja I
  2. Rajendra I
  3. Kulottunga I
  4. Vijayalaya

Answer: Kulottunga I

Explanation: Kulottunga I (later Chola) was a patron of Jainism and constructed the Jain cave temple at Tirumalai.

31. The concept of 'Syadvada' or 'Anekantavada' is a core doctrine of which religion?

  1. Buddhism
  2. Jainism
  3. Charvaka
  4. Nyaya

Answer: Jainism

Explanation: Syadvada (theory of conditional predication) and Anekantavada (non-absolutism) are key Jain philosophical concepts.

32. Which Pallava king was a noted patron of Jainism and authored the work 'Mattavilasa Prahasana'?

  1. Mahendravarman I
  2. Narasimhavarman I
  3. Simhavishnu
  4. Nandivarman II

Answer: Mahendravarman I

Explanation: Mahendravarman I was initially a Jain patron but later converted to Shaivism; he wrote Mattavilasa Prahasana.

33. The 'Maha Bodhi Temple' at Bodh Gaya was built during the reign of which ruler?

  1. Ashoka
  2. Kanishka
  3. Harsha
  4. Chandragupta II

Answer: Ashoka

Explanation: Emperor Ashoka built the first temple at Bodh Gaya to mark the site of Buddha's enlightenment.

34. Which Tamil epic is considered a classic of Jain literature and was composed by the Jain monk Ilango Adigal?

  1. Silappadikaram
  2. Manimekalai
  3. Tirukkural
  4. Jivaka Chintamani

Answer: Silappadikaram

Explanation: Silappadikaram, though a secular epic, was authored by the Jain prince-turned-monk Ilango Adigal.

35. The 'Fourth Buddhist Council' was held during the reign of which ruler?

  1. Ashoka
  2. Kanishka
  3. Harsha
  4. Menander

Answer: Kanishka

Explanation: The Fourth Buddhist Council was convened under the patronage of Kushana king Kanishka in Kashmir.

36. Which of the following is considered the founder of Jainism in its present form?

  1. Rishabhanatha
  2. Mahavira
  3. Parsvanatha
  4. Gautama Buddha

Answer: Mahavira

Explanation: Mahavira is the 24th Tirthankara and is credited with systematizing Jain doctrines.

37. The concept of 'Triratnas' (Three Jewels) in Jainism includes right faith, right knowledge, and which of the following?

  1. Right conduct
  2. Right livelihood
  3. Right speech
  4. Right effort

Answer: Right conduct

Explanation: The Triratnas of Jainism are Samyag Darshan (right faith), Samyag Jnana (right knowledge), and Samyag Charitra (right conduct).

38. Which Buddhist council was held during the reign of King Kanishka?

  1. First
  2. Second
  3. Third
  4. Fourth

Answer: Fourth

Explanation: The Fourth Buddhist Council was convened under the patronage of Kanishka in Kashmir, leading to the split into Hinayana and Mahayana.

39. Which Chola king is known for patronizing Buddhism and building a vihara at Nagapattinam?

  1. Rajaraja I
  2. Rajendra I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Aditya I

Answer: Rajendra I

Explanation: Rajendra I built the Kadaram (Sri Vijaya) vihara at Nagapattinam, reflecting his patronage of Buddhism.

40. The Jain text 'Kalpa Sutra' was composed by which scholar?

  1. Bhadrabahu
  2. Sthulabhadra
  3. Hemachandra
  4. Acharya Kundakunda

Answer: Bhadrabahu

Explanation: Bhadrabahu is traditionally credited with composing the Kalpa Sutra, a key Jain text detailing the lives of Tirthankaras.

41. Which Mauryan emperor is credited with spreading Buddhism to Sri Lanka?

  1. Chandragupta Maurya
  2. Bindusara
  3. Ashoka
  4. Dasharatha

Answer: Ashoka

Explanation: Ashoka sent his son Mahinda and daughter Sanghamitta to Sri Lanka to propagate Buddhism.

42. The Pallava king Mahendravarman I was initially a patron of which religion before converting to Jainism?

  1. Buddhism
  2. Hinduism
  3. Ajivika
  4. Christianity

Answer: Hinduism

Explanation: Mahendravarman I was initially a Jain but later converted to Hinduism (Shaivism) under the influence of Appar.

43. Which of the following is a key difference between Jainism and Buddhism?

  1. Belief in soul
  2. Rejection of caste
  3. Use of Prakrit
  4. Concept of ahimsa

Answer: Belief in soul

Explanation: Jainism believes in the eternal soul (jiva), while Buddhism rejects the concept of a permanent soul (anatta).

44. The famous Jain site of Sittannavasal is located in which modern-day Indian state?

  1. Karnataka
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Maharashtra
  4. Gujarat

Answer: Tamil Nadu

Explanation: Sittannavasal, known for its Jain cave temple and frescoes, is located in Tamil Nadu.

45. Who is the founder of Buddhism?

  1. Mahavira
  2. Gautama Buddha
  3. Ashoka
  4. Kanishka

Answer: Gautama Buddha

Explanation: Siddhartha Gautama, known as Gautama Buddha, founded Buddhism in the 6th century BCE.

46. Which Jain council was held at Vallabhi in the 5th century CE?

  1. First Jain Council
  2. Second Jain Council
  3. Third Jain Council
  4. Fourth Jain Council

Answer: Second Jain Council

Explanation: The Second Jain Council at Vallabhi (453 CE) compiled and codified the Jain canonical texts (Siddhanta).

47. What is the collective name of the three main Buddhist canonical texts (Vinaya, Sutta, Abhidhamma)?

  1. Jatakas
  2. Tripitaka
  3. Dhammapada
  4. Milindapanha

Answer: Tripitaka

Explanation: The Tripitaka ('Three Baskets') are the foundational scriptures of Buddhism, comprising Vinaya, Sutta, and Abhidhamma Pitakas.

48. Which Tamil Nadu region is particularly associated with the spread of Buddhism during the Sangam age?

  1. Kaveripoompattinam
  2. Madurai
  3. Kanchipuram
  4. Thanjavur

Answer: Kaveripoompattinam

Explanation: Kaveripoompattinam (Puhar) was a major centre of Buddhism in early Tamilakam, with Buddhist viharas and missionary activity.

49. Which Jain monk is said to have influenced Chandragupta Maurya to adopt Jainism?

  1. Bhadrabahu
  2. Haribhadra
  3. Sthulabhadra
  4. Hemachandra

Answer: Bhadrabahu

Explanation: According to Jain tradition, Bhadrabahu was a revered Jain monk who guided Chandragupta Maurya towards Jain asceticism.

50. Under which Kushan ruler was the Fourth Buddhist Council held?

  1. Kujula Kadphises
  2. Vima Kadphises
  3. Kanishka
  4. Huvishka

Answer: Kanishka

Explanation: The Fourth Buddhist Council was convened under Kanishka in the 1st–2nd century CE, likely in Kashmir, leading to the division of Buddhism into Hinayana and Mahayana.
