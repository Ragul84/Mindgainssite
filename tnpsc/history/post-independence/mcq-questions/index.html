26. The founder of the Mauryan dynasty was-

  1. Chandragupta Maurya
  2. Ashoka
  3. Samudragupta
  4. Bimbisara

Answer: Chandragupta Maurya

Explanation: Chandragupta Maurya was the founder of the Mauryan dynasty. He played a crucial role in Indian history.

27. The freedom fighter who adopted the tactic of non-cooperation was-

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Subhas Chandra Bose
  3. Bhagat Singh
  4. Jawaharlal Nehru

Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi adopted the tactic of non-cooperation. It was a significant strategy in the Indian freedom struggle.

28. The Chola kingdom was located in-

  1. North India
  2. South India
  3. East India
  4. West India

Answer: South India

Explanation: The Chola kingdom was located in South India. It was a prominent kingdom in the region.

29. The Gupta period is known as the-

  1. Golden Age
  2. Iron Age
  3. Bronze Age
  4. Dark Age

Answer: Golden Age

Explanation: The Gupta period is known as the Golden Age of India. It was a period of significant cultural and scientific advancements.

30. The Pallavas were known for their-

  1. Military conquests
  2. Cultural achievements
  3. Economic prosperity
  4. Administrative skills

Answer: Cultural achievements

Explanation: The Pallavas were known for their cultural achievements. They made significant contributions to art, architecture, and literature.

31. The socio-reform movement in India was led by-

  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Jawaharlal Nehru

Answer: Raja Ram Mohan Roy

Explanation: The socio-reform movement in India was led by Raja Ram Mohan Roy. He played a crucial role in reforming Indian society.

32. The Indus Valley Civilization is known for its sophisticated:

  1. Agriculture

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is known for its sophisticated architecture, with well-planned cities and advanced drainage systems.

Explanation: All of the above leaders played a significant role in the socio-reform movements in India.

43. The Pandyas were a powerful kingdom in?

  1. North India
  2. South India
  3. East India
  4. West India

Answer: South India

Explanation: The Pandyas were a powerful kingdom in South India, specifically in the region of Tamil Nadu.

44. The Indus Valley Civilization is notable for its:

  1. Urban planning
  2. Agricultural practices
  3. Trade networks
  4. Artistic achievements

Answer: Urban planning

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is known for its sophisticated urban planning, with well-organized cities like Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa.

45. The British conquest of India began with the:

  1. Battle of Plassey
  2. Battle of Buxar
  3. Battle of Panipat
  4. Battle of Wandiwash

Answer: Battle of Plassey

Explanation: The British conquest of India is often dated to the Battle of Plassey in 1757, where the British East India Company defeated the ruler of Bengal.

46. The Chola dynasty is famous for its:
