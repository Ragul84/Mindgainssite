26. Kautilya's Arthashastra was rediscovered by:

  1. R. Shamasastri in 1905
  2. Max Mueller in 1880
  3. William Jones in 1790
  4. James Prinsep in 1830

Answer: R. Shamasastri in 1905

Explanation: R. Shamasastri (a librarian at Mysore Government Oriental Library) discovered a manuscript of Arthashastra in 1905 and published the first translation in 1909.

  1. Chandragupta Maurya

  3. Samudragupta
  4. Bimbisara

Explanation: Chandragupta Maurya founded the Mauryan dynasty with the help of Chanakya.
