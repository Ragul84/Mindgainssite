Vijayanagara MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Vijayanagara questions to revise the TNPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Hampi was the capital of:

  1. Vijayanagara Empire
  2. Chalukyas
  3. Rashtrakutas
  4. Kakatiya Kingdom

Answer: Vijayanagara Empire

Explanation: Hampi (Karnataka) was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire. It is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site with magnificent ruins including Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple, and palace structures.

2. The Vijayanagara Empire was founded by:

  1. Deva Raya I
  2. Harihara I and Bukka Raya I (with guidance of sage Vidyaranya)
  3. Krishna Deva Raya
  4. Achyuta Deva Raya

Answer: Harihara I and Bukka Raya I (with guidance of sage Vidyaranya)

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire was founded in 1336 CE by brothers Harihara I and Bukka Raya I (formerly commanders of the Kampili kingdom) with the spiritual guidance of sage Vidyaranya (Madhava). Capital: Hampi (Vijayanagara).

3. The greatest ruler of the Vijayanagara Empire was:

  1. Harihara I
  2. Deva Raya II
  3. Krishna Deva Raya
  4. Rama Raya

Answer: Krishna Deva Raya

Explanation: Krishna Deva Raya (1509–1529 CE) was the greatest Vijayanagara ruler. He defeated Bijapur, Bidar, Golconda, and the Zamorin of Calicut. He was a poet (wrote Amuktamalyada in Telugu) and patron of Ashtadiggajas (eight court poets).

4. Portuguese traveller Domingo Paes visited Vijayanagara during the reign of:

  1. Deva Raya II
  2. Krishna Deva Raya
  3. Achyuta Raya
  4. Sadashiva Raya

Answer: Krishna Deva Raya

Explanation: Domingo Paes (Portuguese traveller, c. 1520–1522) visited during Krishna Deva Raya's reign and described Vijayanagara as 'the best provided city in the world' with a population of 500,000. Other visitors: Abdur Razzak (1443), Nuniz (1535–36).

5. The greatest ruler of Vijayanagara Empire was:

  1. Krishnadevaraya
  2. Devaraya II
  3. Harihara II
  4. Saluva Narasimha

Answer: Krishnadevaraya

Explanation: Krishnadevaraya (1509-1529 CE) was the greatest ruler — a brilliant military commander, poet (wrote Amuktamalyada in Telugu), and generous patron of arts. Known as Kannada Rajya Rama Ramana.

6. Abdur Razzaq, the Persian ambassador, visited which Vijayanagara king's court?

  1. Devaraya II
  2. Krishnadevaraya
  3. Harihara I
  4. Bukka I

Answer: Devaraya II

Explanation: Abdur Razzaq (Persian envoy, 1443) visited the court of Devaraya II and gave a detailed description of Vijayanagara city and the empire's grandeur.

7. Battle of Talikota (1565) ended:

  1. Vijayanagara Empire
  2. Mughal dominance
  3. Delhi Sultanate
  4. Bahmani Kingdom

Answer: Vijayanagara Empire

Explanation: Battle of Talikota (Jan 1565): Vijayanagara defeated by the combined forces of Bijapur, Bidar, Ahmadnagar, and Golconda. The city of Vijayanagara was sacked and destroyed.

8. The Hazara Rama Temple in Hampi was built by:

  1. Krishnadevaraya
  2. Devaraya I
  3. Harihara I
  4. Bukka I

Answer: Krishnadevaraya

Explanation: Hazara Rama Temple (Temple of 1000 Ramas) in Hampi was built during the reign of Krishnadevaraya, known for its magnificent bas-reliefs depicting scenes from the Ramayana.

9. Who founded the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Harihara
  2. Bukka
  3. Krishna Deva Raya
  4. Ashoka

Answer: Harihara

Explanation: Harihara and Bukka founded the Vijayanagara Empire, but Harihara is often credited as the main founder.

10. What was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Vijayanagara
  2. Hampi
  3. Mysore
  4. Bangalore

Answer: Hampi

Explanation: Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire, also known as Vijayanagara.

11. Which dynasty ruled the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Sangama
  2. Tuluva
  3. Aravidu
  4. Hoysala

Answer: Sangama

Explanation: The Sangama dynasty was the first to rule the Vijayanagara Empire.

12. Who was the most famous king of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Krishna Deva Raya
  2. Achyuta Deva Raya
  3. Aliya Rama Raya
  4. Venkatapati Raya

Answer: Krishna Deva Raya

Explanation: Krishna Deva Raya was the most famous king of the Vijayanagara Empire, known for his military campaigns and administrative skills.

13. What was the main reason for the decline of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Internal conflicts
  2. External invasions
  3. Economic troubles
  4. Environmental disasters

Answer: External invasions

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire declined mainly due to external invasions by the Deccan Sultanates.

14. Which battle marked the end of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Battle of Talikota
  2. Battle of Raichur
  3. Battle of Vijayanagara
  4. Battle of Hampi

Answer: Battle of Talikota

Explanation: The Battle of Talikota marked the end of the Vijayanagara Empire in 1565.

15. Who was the last king of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Aliya Rama Raya
  2. Venkatapati Raya
  3. Sriranga Raya
  4. Achyuta Deva Raya

Answer: Aliya Rama Raya

Explanation: Aliya Rama Raya was the last king of the Vijayanagara Empire, although some sources consider Venkatapati Raya as the last.

16. What was the main contribution of the Vijayanagara Empire to Indian culture?

  1. Development of Telugu literature
  2. Spread of Vaishnavism
  3. Construction of temples
  4. Patronage of arts

Answer: Construction of temples

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire made significant contributions to Indian culture, including the construction of temples like the Virupaksha Temple.

17. Which of the following was a notable feature of the Vijayanagara Empire's administration?

  1. Centralized bureaucracy
  2. Decentralized administration
  3. Feudal system
  4. Matrilineal system

Answer: Decentralized administration

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire had a decentralized administration, with governors and nobles holding significant power.

18. What was the economic base of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Agriculture
  2. Trade
  3. Industry
  4. Tribute

Answer: Agriculture

Explanation: The economic base of the Vijayanagara Empire was agriculture, with the empire being a major producer of crops like rice and cotton.

19. Which of the following was a notable feature of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Administration
  2. Architecture
  3. Economy
  4. All

Answer: All

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire was notable for its administration, architecture, and economy.

20. Who was the founder of the Tuluva dynasty?

  1. Saluva Narasimha
  2. Tuluva Narasa Nayaka
  3. Vira Narasimha
  4. Krishna Deva Raya

Answer: Tuluva Narasa Nayaka

Explanation: Tuluva Narasa Nayaka was the founder of the Tuluva dynasty.

21. What was the name of the famous temple built during the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Virupaksha Temple
  2. Meenakshi Temple
  3. Brihadeeswara Temple
  4. Vitthala Temple

Answer: Vitthala Temple

Explanation: The Vitthala Temple was a famous temple built during the Vijayanagara Empire.

22. Which of the following kingdoms was a rival of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Bahmani Kingdom
  2. Gajapati Kingdom
  3. Pandya Kingdom
  4. All

Answer: All

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire had rival kingdoms including the Bahmani Kingdom, Gajapati Kingdom, and others.

23. In which year did the Vijayanagara Empire decline?

  1. 1542
  2. 1565
  3. 1646
  4. 1675

Answer: 1565

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire began to decline after the Battle of Talikota in 1565.

24. What was the main crop cultivated in the Vijayanagara Empire?

  1. Rice
  2. Wheat
  3. Sugarcane
  4. Cotton

Answer: Rice

Explanation: Rice was the main crop cultivated in the Vijayanagara Empire, which was the staple food of the region.

25. Which of the following was a notable achievement of the Vijayanagara Empire in the field of architecture?

  1. Development of Dravidian style
  2. Introduction of Islamic architecture
  3. Construction of temples
  4. All

Answer: Construction of temples

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire was known for its magnificent temples, such as the Virupaksha Temple and the Vitthala Temple.
