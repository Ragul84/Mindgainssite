26. Who was the founder of the Tuluva dynasty of the Vijayanagara Empire?

  2. Tuluva Narasa Nayaka
  3. Vira Narasimha
  4. Krishna Deva Raya

Answer: Tuluva Narasa Nayaka

Explanation: Tuluva Narasa Nayaka was the founder of the Tuluva dynasty of the Vijayanagara Empire.

27. In which year did the Vijayanagara Empire come to an end?

  2. 1646

Explanation: The Vijayanagara Empire came to an end in 1646, when the last king, Sriranga III, was defeated by the Bijapur Sultanate.
