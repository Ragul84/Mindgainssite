1. Who wrote the Indian national anthem 'Jana Gana Mana'?

  1. Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay
  2. Rabindranath Tagore
  3. Sarojini Naidu
  4. Subramanya Bharati

Answer: Rabindranath Tagore

Explanation: Jana Gana Mana was written by Rabindranath Tagore in Bengali. It was adopted as the National Anthem of India on 24 January 1950.

2. The Chola Empire was a famous empire from which part of India?

  1. North India
  2. Western India
  3. South India (Tamil Nadu)
  4. Eastern India

Answer: South India (Tamil Nadu)

Explanation: The Chola Empire was one of the longest-ruling dynasties in South India (Tamil Nadu), known for maritime power, temple architecture, and administration.

3. Who discovered the sea route to India?

  1. Christopher Columbus
  2. Vasco da Gama
  3. Ferdinand Magellan
  4. Amerigo Vespucci

Answer: Vasco da Gama

Explanation: Portuguese explorer Vasco da Gama discovered the sea route to India in 1498, arriving at Calicut (Kozhikode) on the Malabar Coast.

4. The Great Wall of China was built primarily to protect against:

  1. Mongol invasions
  2. Japanese invasions
  3. Korean invasions
  4. Persian invasions

Answer: Mongol invasions

Explanation: The Great Wall of China was built over centuries, primarily to protect Chinese states and empires against Xiongnu nomadic tribes from the north — later associated with Mongol invasions.

5. Which Chola king sent a naval expedition to Southeast Asia?

  1. Rajaraja I
  2. Rajendra Chola I
  3. Kulottunga I
  4. Parantaka I

Answer: Rajendra Chola I

Explanation: Rajendra Chola I (1014–1044 CE) sent a famous naval expedition to Southeast Asia (Srivijaya Empire in Sumatra and Malay Peninsula) around 1025 CE.
