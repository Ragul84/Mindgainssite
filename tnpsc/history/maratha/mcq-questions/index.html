Maratha MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Maratha questions to revise the TNPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

26. Which treaty ended the Third Anglo-Maratha War?

  1. Treaty of Bassein
  2. Treaty of Salbai
  3. Treaty of Purandar
  4. Treaty of Mandasor

Answer: Treaty of Mandasor

Explanation: The Treaty of Mandasor (1818) ended the Third Anglo-Maratha War, leading to British supremacy in India.

27. The Peshwas were originally the prime ministers of which Maratha ruler?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Sambhaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Shahu

Answer: Shahu

Explanation: Shahu I appointed Balaji Vishwanath as Peshwa, making the position hereditary and powerful.

28. Which Maratha ruler introduced the 'Ryotwari' system in Malwa?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Balaji Baji Rao
  3. Mahadji Shinde
  4. Ahilyabai Holkar

Answer: Ahilyabai Holkar

Explanation: Ahilyabai Holkar, the queen of Malwa, implemented the Ryotwari system for direct revenue collection from peasants.

29. The Battle of Panipat (1761) was fought between the Marathas and which invader?

  1. Nadir Shah
  2. Ahmad Shah Abdali
  3. Babur
  4. Humayun

Answer: Ahmad Shah Abdali

Explanation: The Third Battle of Panipat (1761) saw the Marathas defeated by Ahmad Shah Abdali, an Afghan ruler.

30. Which Maratha leader signed the Treaty of Bassein (1802) with the British?

  1. Bajirao II
  2. Daulat Rao Shinde
  3. Yashwantrao Holkar
  4. Mahadji Shinde

Answer: Bajirao II

Explanation: Bajirao II signed the Treaty of Bassein (1802), accepting British protection and triggering the Second Anglo-Maratha War.

31. The Maratha navy was primarily based at which port?

  1. Surat
  2. Bombay
  3. Vijaydurg
  4. Calicut

Answer: Vijaydurg

Explanation: Vijaydurg (Sindhudurg) served as the main base for Shivaji's navy under Kanhoji Angre.

32. Which Maratha queen famously defended her kingdom against the Mughals in the 18th century?

  1. Ahilyabai Holkar
  2. Tarabai
  3. Jijabai
  4. Soyarabai

Answer: Tarabai

Explanation: Tarabai, wife of Rajaram, led Maratha resistance against the Mughals after Rajaram's death.

33. The 'Ashtapradhan' council was established by which Maratha ruler?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Sambhaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Shahu

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji created the Ashtapradhan (eight ministers) council to administer his kingdom efficiently.

34. Which treaty ended the Third Anglo-Maratha War in 1818?

  1. Treaty of Bassein
  2. Treaty of Salbai
  3. Treaty of Purandar
  4. Treaty of Mandasor

Answer: Treaty of Mandasor

Explanation: The Treaty of Mandasor (1818) ended the Third Anglo-Maratha War, leading to British supremacy over the Marathas.

35. Which Maratha ruler introduced the 'Ryotwari' system in the territories under his control?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Sambhaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Shahu

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji introduced the Ryotwari system, where revenue was directly collected from peasants without intermediaries.

36. Which Maratha queen fought against the Mughals and is known for her military leadership?

  1. Tarabai
  2. Ahilyabai Holkar
  3. Jijabai
  4. Mastani

Answer: Tarabai

Explanation: Tarabai, wife of Rajaram, led the Maratha resistance against the Mughals after her husband's death.

37. The Maratha kingdom of Tanjore was founded by which Maratha ruler?

  1. Ekoji I
  2. Shivaji
  3. Sambhaji
  4. Rajaram

Answer: Ekoji I

Explanation: Ekoji I, half-brother of Shivaji, founded the Maratha kingdom of Tanjore in 1674.

38. Which Maratha ruler signed the Treaty of Salbai (1782) with the British?

  1. Madhav Rao I
  2. Narayan Rao
  3. Raghunath Rao
  4. Shahu II

Answer: Raghunath Rao

Explanation: Raghunath Rao (Raghoba) signed the Treaty of Salbai with the British, ending the First Anglo-Maratha War.

39. What was the council of ministers under Shivaji called?

  1. Ashtapradhan
  2. Navratna
  3. Diwan
  4. Peshwa

Answer: Ashtapradhan

Explanation: Ashtapradhan was the council of eight ministers that assisted Shivaji in administration.

40. In which battle did Shivaji defeat Afzal Khan to gain control over major forts?

  1. Battle of Pratapgarh
  2. Battle of Panipat
  3. Battle of Plassey
  4. Battle of Buxar

Answer: Battle of Pratapgarh

Explanation: The Battle of Pratapgarh (1659) saw Shivaji kill Afzal Khan and capture key forts.

41. The Treaty of Bassein (1802) was signed between the Marathas and which British Governor-General?

  1. Wellesley
  2. Hastings
  3. Clive
  4. Dalhousie

Answer: Wellesley

Explanation: Lord Wellesley signed the Treaty of Bassein with the Peshwa, leading to Maratha subjugation.

42. Who founded the Maratha kingdom in Tanjore?

  1. Ekoji
  2. Serfoji
  3. Tuljaji
  4. Rajaram

Answer: Ekoji

Explanation: Ekoji, half-brother of Shivaji, established the Maratha kingdom in Tanjore in the 1670s.

43. What was the revenue system introduced by Shivaji for collecting tribute from neighbouring territories?

  1. Ryotwari
  2. Mahalwari
  3. Chauth and Sardeshmukhi
  4. Zabti

Answer: Chauth and Sardeshmukhi

Explanation: Chauth (one-fourth) and Sardeshmukhi (one-tenth) were taxes levied outside Maratha territory.

44. The Third Battle of Panipat (1761) was fought between the Marathas and which force?

  1. Mughals
  2. Afghans
  3. British
  4. Nizam

Answer: Afghans

Explanation: Marathas were defeated by Ahmad Shah Abdali's Afghan forces at Panipat in 1761.

45. Who is considered the greatest Peshwa of the Maratha Empire?

  1. Baji Rao I
  2. Balaji Baji Rao
  3. Madhav Rao
  4. Nana Fadnavis

Answer: Baji Rao I

Explanation: Baji Rao I (1720-1740) expanded the Maratha Empire across northern India and is regarded as the greatest Peshwa.

46. Which Maratha ruler built a strong navy and constructed several coastal forts to protect the western coast?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Sambhaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Tarabai

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji developed a naval fleet and built forts like Suvarnadurg and Vijaydurg to counter foreign powers.

47. Which Maratha ruler introduced the system of 'Ashtapradhan' (Council of Eight Ministers)?

  1. Sambhaji
  2. Rajaram
  3. Shivaji
  4. Shahu

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji established the Ashtapradhan, a council of eight ministers, to administer the Maratha Empire.

48. The Third Battle of Panipat (1761) was fought between the Marathas and which Afghan ruler?

  1. Ahmad Shah Abdali
  2. Nadir Shah
  3. Babur
  4. Sher Shah Suri

Answer: Ahmad Shah Abdali

Explanation: The Third Battle of Panipat in 1761 was between the Marathas and Ahmad Shah Abdali, resulting in a Maratha defeat.

49. Who was the first hereditary Peshwa of the Maratha Empire?

  1. Balaji Vishwanath
  2. Baji Rao I
  3. Madhav Rao I
  4. Nana Sahib

Answer: Balaji Vishwanath

Explanation: Balaji Vishwanath became the first hereditary Peshwa in 1713, strengthening the Maratha Empire.

50. Which Maratha king was known as 'Chhatrapati' and established the capital at Raigad?

  1. Sambhaji
  2. Shivaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Shahu

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad in 1674, making it the capital of the Maratha Empire.
