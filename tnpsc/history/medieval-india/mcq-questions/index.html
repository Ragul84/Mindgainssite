26. Who was the founder of the Maratha empire?

  1. Shivaji
  2. Sambhaji
  3. Rajaram
  4. Tarabai

Answer: Shivaji

Explanation: Shivaji was the founder of the Maratha empire. He was a skilled military leader.

27. What was the name of the socio-reform movement started by Rammohan Roy?

  1. Brahmo Samaj
  2. Arya Samaj
  3. Ramakrishna Mission
  4. Theosophical Society

Answer: Brahmo Samaj

Explanation: The Brahmo Samaj was a socio-reform movement started by Rammohan Roy. It aimed to reform Hindu society.

28. Which British governor-general introduced the Permanent Settlement in India?

  1. Warren Hastings
  2. Lord Cornwallis
  3. Lord Curzon
  4. Lord Dalhousie

Answer: Lord Cornwallis

Explanation: The Permanent Settlement was introduced by Lord Cornwallis. It was a land revenue system.

29. Who led the Indian Rebellion of 1857?

  1. Mangal Pandey
  2. Bahadur Shah
  3. Rani Lakshmibai
  4. Tantia Tope

Answer: Bahadur Shah

Explanation: Bahadur Shah was the last Mughal emperor and led the Indian Rebellion of 1857. He was exiled to Burma.

30. What was the name of the movement started by Mahatma Gandhi to quit India?

  1. Non-Cooperation Movement
  2. Civil Disobedience Movement
  3. Quit India Movement
  4. Swadeshi Movement

Answer: Quit India Movement

Explanation: The Quit India Movement was started by Mahatma Gandhi. It was a call for immediate independence from British rule.
