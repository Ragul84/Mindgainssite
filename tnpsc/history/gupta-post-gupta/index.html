Gupta Post Gupta MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Gupta Post Gupta questions to revise the TNPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Harshavardhana's empire was described by the Chinese pilgrim:

  1. Fa Hien
  2. Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang)
  3. I-tsing
  4. Sung Yun

Answer: Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang)

Explanation: Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang) visited India during Harsha's reign (7th century CE) and described Harsha's empire and character in detail in his 'Si-Yu-Ki' (Record of Western Countries).

2. Nalanda University was established during:

  1. Gupta period
  2. Mauryan period
  3. Kushan period
  4. Pala period

Answer: Gupta period

Explanation: Nalanda University was established during the Gupta period (5th century CE, under Kumaragupta I) and flourished until it was destroyed by Bakhtiyar Khilji in 1193 CE.

3. Chandragupta II was also known as:

  1. Vikramaditya
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Skandagupta
  4. Ramagupta

Answer: Vikramaditya

Explanation: Chandragupta II (375-415 CE) was known as Vikramaditya (Sun of Valor). His reign is considered the golden age of Gupta empire — Kalidasa, Varahamihira flourished in his court.

4. 'Allahabad Pillar Inscription' (Prayag Prashasti) eulogizes:

  1. Samudragupta
  2. Chandragupta I
  3. Ashoka
  4. Kanishka

Answer: Samudragupta

Explanation: Prayag Prashasti (composed by court poet Harisena) on the Allahabad Pillar describes the military conquests and character of Samudragupta — called 'Napoleon of India'.

5. The decimal system of numeration was developed in:

  1. Gupta period
  2. Mauryan period
  3. Vedic period
  4. Colonial period

Answer: Gupta period

Explanation: The decimal system with place value notation (including zero) was developed in India during the Gupta period, later transmitted to Arabs and then to Europe.

6. The Huna invasion of India weakened the:

  1. Gupta Empire
  2. Mauryan Empire
  3. Vijayanagara Empire
  4. Delhi Sultanate

Answer: Gupta Empire

Explanation: The Huna (Hephthalite) invasions in the late 5th and 6th centuries significantly weakened the Gupta Empire, contributing to its decline.

7. Fa-Hien (Faxian) visited India during whose reign?

  1. Chandragupta II
  2. Ashoka
  3. Kanishka
  4. Samudragupta

Answer: Chandragupta II

Explanation: Fa-Hien (399-414 CE), the Chinese Buddhist pilgrim, visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya) to collect Buddhist scriptures and study Buddhism.

8. Kalidasa's famous works include:

  1. Meghaduta, Abhijnana Shakuntalam, Raghuvamsa
  2. Arthashastra, Mudrarakshasa
  3. Ashtadhyayi, Raghuvamsa
  4. Amarakosa, Nitisara

Answer: Meghaduta, Abhijnana Shakuntalam, Raghuvamsa

Explanation: Kalidasa (Gupta period) wrote: Plays — Abhijnana Shakuntalam, Vikramorvasiyam, Malavikagnimitram; Epic poems — Raghuvamsa, Kumarasambhavam; Lyric poems — Meghaduta, Ritusamhara.

9. Harshavardhana's Chinese visitor was:

  1. Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang)
  2. Fa-Hien
  3. I-tsing
  4. Marco Polo

Answer: Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang)

Explanation: Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang), the Chinese Buddhist pilgrim, visited India during Harsha's reign (630-644 CE). His 'Si-Yu-Ki' gives valuable information about 7th century India.

10. The Banabhatta was the court poet of:

  1. Harshavardhana
  2. Chandragupta II
  3. Ashoka
  4. Samudragupta

Answer: Harshavardhana

Explanation: Banabhatta was the court poet of King Harshavardhana (606-647 CE) and wrote Harshacharita (biography of Harsha) and Kadambari (prose romance).

11. The Gupta Empire is called the 'Golden Age' of India because:

  1. It had the largest territory
  2. It saw great achievements in science, mathematics, astronomy, literature, and art; Aryabhata, Kalidasa, iron pillar, decimal system
  3. It defeated all foreign invaders
  4. It had the most gold reserves

Answer: It saw great achievements in science, mathematics, astronomy, literature, and art; Aryabhata, Kalidasa, iron pillar, decimal system

Explanation: The Gupta period (320–550 CE) is called the Golden Age: Aryabhata discovered zero and calculated pi; Kalidasa wrote Shakuntala, Meghaduta; Varahamihira was an astronomer; Sushruta was a physician; the Nalanda university flourished.

12. Samudragupta is often called the 'Napoleon of India' because:

  1. He lost many battles
  2. He conquered virtually all of northern India and received tribute from southern kingdoms through military campaigns
  3. He was exiled like Napoleon
  4. He introduced a new law code

Answer: He conquered virtually all of northern India and received tribute from southern kingdoms through military campaigns

Explanation: Samudragupta (c. 335–375 CE) is called the Napoleon of India by historian V.A. Smith for his extensive military conquests across North India and the Deccan. The Allahabad Pillar inscription (by Harishena) records his campaigns.

13. Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya) defeated the:

  1. Huns
  2. Shakas (Western Kshatrapas) of Malwa and Gujarat
  3. Kushanas
  4. Persians

Answer: Shakas (Western Kshatrapas) of Malwa and Gujarat

Explanation: Chandragupta II (c. 375–415 CE) defeated the Shakas (Western Kshatrapas) of western India, annexing Malwa, Gujarat, and Kathiawar. This extended Gupta empire to the Arabian Sea. He took the title 'Vikramaditya'.

14. The Chinese pilgrim Fa-Hien visited India during the reign of:

  1. Ashoka
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Chandragupta II
  4. Harshavardhana

Answer: Chandragupta II

Explanation: Fa-Hien (Faxian) visited India (399–414 CE) during Chandragupta II's reign. He described a prosperous, peaceful society, absence of capital punishment, and abundant food. He came to collect Buddhist scriptures.

15. The Gupta period mathematician who wrote 'Aryabhatiya' was:

  1. Brahmagupta
  2. Varahamihira
  3. Aryabhata
  4. Bhaskara I

Answer: Aryabhata

Explanation: Aryabhata (476–550 CE) wrote Aryabhatiya at age 23, containing mathematics and astronomy. He calculated pi ≈ 3.1416, explained solar eclipses, calculated Earth's rotation, and may have used a place-value system.

16. The Pallava king who defeated the Chalukya king Pulakesi II was:

  1. Mahendravarman I
  2. Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla)
  3. Paramesvaravarman
  4. Narasimhavarman II

Answer: Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla)

Explanation: Narasimhavarman I defeated Chalukya king Pulakesi II (who had previously defeated Harshavardhana) in 642 CE. He captured the Chalukya capital Vatapi and took the title 'Vatapikonda' (conqueror of Vatapi).

17. The Gupta Empire was founded by which ruler?

  1. Chandragupta Maurya
  2. Chandragupta I
  3. Samudragupta
  4. Skandagupta

Answer: Chandragupta I

Explanation: Chandragupta I is considered the founder of the Gupta Empire, establishing its rule in Magadha around 320 CE.

18. Which Chinese pilgrim visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II?

  1. Hiuen Tsang
  2. Fa-Hien
  3. I-Tsing
  4. Marco Polo

Answer: Fa-Hien

Explanation: Fa-Hien (Faxian) visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II and recorded his observations on Gupta society and Buddhism.

19. The famous Iron Pillar of Delhi belongs to the period of which Gupta ruler?

  1. Ashoka
  2. Chandragupta II
  3. Samudragupta
  4. Harsha

Answer: Chandragupta II

Explanation: The Iron Pillar of Delhi was erected during the reign of Chandragupta II, known for its rust-resistant composition.

20. Harshavardhana shifted his capital from Thanesar to which city?

  1. Pataliputra
  2. Kannauj
  3. Ujjain
  4. Mathura

Answer: Kannauj

Explanation: Harshavardhana moved his capital to Kannauj, which became a major political and cultural center under his rule.

21. Which Pallava king built the Shore Temple at Mamallapuram?

  1. Narasimhavarman I
  2. Mahendravarman I
  3. Narasimhavarman II
  4. Simhavishnu

Answer: Narasimhavarman II

Explanation: The Shore Temple was built by Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha), a prominent Pallava ruler known for his architectural contributions.

22. What was the capital of the Gupta Empire?

  1. Pataliputra
  2. Ujjain
  3. Mathura
  4. Ayodhya

Answer: Pataliputra

Explanation: Pataliputra (modern Patna) served as the capital of the Gupta Empire, continuing the Mauryan tradition.

23. The Chinese traveller Hiuen Tsang visited India during the reign of which ruler?

  1. Harsha
  2. Chandragupta II
  3. Samudragupta
  4. Pulakesin II

Answer: Harsha

Explanation: Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang) visited India during Harsha's reign and wrote detailed accounts of the political and religious conditions.

24. Which Gupta ruler is known as the 'Napoleon of India' for his military conquests?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Chandragupta II
  4. Kumaragupta I

Answer: Samudragupta

Explanation: Samudragupta earned the title 'Napoleon of India' due to his extensive military campaigns and expansion of the Gupta Empire.

25. Which post-Gupta kingdom in Tamil Nadu was prominent during the 6th-7th centuries?

  1. Chola
  2. Pandya
  3. Pallava
  4. Vijayanagara

Answer: Pallava

Explanation: The Pallava kingdom rose in the post-Gupta period and became a major power in Tamil Nadu, known for its art and architecture.
