26. The famous Kailasanatha temple at Kanchipuram was built by which Pallava ruler?

  1. Narasimhavarman I
  2. Mahendravarman I
  3. Narasimhavarman II
  4. Simhavishnu

Answer: Narasimhavarman II

Explanation: The Kailasanatha temple was built by Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha), showcasing early Dravidian architecture.

27. The Gupta Empire is known for a 'Golden Age' in which field?

  1. Literature and Science
  2. Architecture
  3. Military conquests
  4. Trade

Answer: Literature and Science

Explanation: The Gupta period saw advancements in literature (Kalidasa) and science (Aryabhata), earning it the title of Golden Age.

28. Which Gupta ruler is known as 'Napoleon of India'?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Chandragupta II
  4. Skandagupta

Answer: Samudragupta

Explanation: Samudragupta was a great conqueror, often called the Napoleon of India by historians.

29. The famous Iron Pillar at Mehrauli belongs to which period?

  1. Maurya
  2. Gupta
  3. Post-Gupta
  4. Vedic

Answer: Gupta

Explanation: The Iron Pillar was erected during the reign of Chandragupta II of the Gupta period.

30. The famous Nalanda University flourished during which period?

  1. Gupta
  2. Post-Gupta
  3. Both Gupta and Post-Gupta
  4. Only Maurya

Answer: Both Gupta and Post-Gupta

Explanation: Nalanda was established in the Gupta period and continued to flourish in the post-Gupta (Pala) period.

31. Who was the founder of the Gupta dynasty?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Sri Gupta
  4. Ghatotkacha

Answer: Sri Gupta

Explanation: Sri Gupta was the first known ruler and founder of the Gupta dynasty.

32. The Pallava dynasty is associated with which region?

  1. Northern India
  2. Deccan
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. Gujarat

Answer: Tamil Nadu

Explanation: The Pallavas ruled in Tamil Nadu with their capital at Kanchipuram.

33. Which post-Gupta ruler wrote the play 'Nagananda'?

  1. Harsha
  2. Kalidasa
  3. Bhavabhuti
  4. Banabhatta

Answer: Harsha

Explanation: Harshavardhana (Harsha) wrote plays including Nagananda, Ratnavali, and Priyadarshika.

34. The Badami Chalukyas were contemporaries of which dynasty?

  1. Pallavas
  2. Guptas
  3. Cholas
  4. Pandyas

Answer: Pallavas

Explanation: The Badami Chalukyas and the Pallavas were contemporary powers in the Deccan and Tamil region.

35. The 'Dashavatara Temple' at Deogarh belongs to which period?

  1. Gupta
  2. Post-Gupta
  3. Maurya
  4. Chola

Answer: Gupta

Explanation: The Dashavatara Temple at Deogarh is a Gupta period temple, an early example of Nagara architecture.

36. Which Gupta ruler is known as the 'Napoleon of India'?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Chandragupta II
  4. Skandagupta

Answer: Samudragupta

Explanation: Samudragupta is called the Napoleon of India for his extensive military conquests.

37. The Nalanda University was a major center of learning during which period?

  1. Maurya
  2. Gupta
  3. Sangam
  4. Vijayanagara

Answer: Gupta

Explanation: Nalanda University flourished as a premier Buddhist learning center during the Gupta period.

38. Who was the Chinese pilgrim who visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II?

  1. Hiuen Tsang
  2. Fa-Hien
  3. I-Tsing
  4. Marco Polo

Answer: Fa-Hien

Explanation: Fa-Hien visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II and wrote about Gupta society and culture.

39. Which dynasty succeeded the Guptas in North India?

  1. Chola
  2. Vardhana
  3. Pallava
  4. Rashtrakuta

Answer: Vardhana

Explanation: The Vardhana dynasty, under Harshavardhana, rose after the decline of the Guptas.

40. The Ajanta cave paintings are associated with which period?

  1. Maurya
  2. Gupta
  3. Chola
  4. Pallava

Answer: Gupta

Explanation: Ajanta cave paintings reached their peak during the Gupta period, depicting Buddhist themes.

41. Which Gupta ruler issued the 'Allahabad Pillar Inscription'?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Chandragupta II
  4. Kumaragupta

Answer: Samudragupta

Explanation: The Allahabad Pillar Inscription, composed by Harisena, describes Samudragupta's achievements.

42. The 'Pallava' kingdom was centered in which region?

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Karnataka
  3. Andhra Pradesh
  4. Odisha

Answer: Karnataka

Explanation: The Pallava kingdom was primarily centered in northern Tamil Nadu with its capital at Kanchipuram.

43. Which Sangam-age kingdom is associated with the post-Gupta period in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chera
  2. Chola
  3. Pandya
  4. Pallava

Answer: Pandya

Explanation: The Pandya kingdom continued to flourish in the post-Gupta period, with Madurai as its capital.

44. The 'Harsha Charita' was written by which author?

  1. Kalidasa
  2. Banabhatta
  3. Harisena
  4. Aryabhata

Answer: Banabhatta

Explanation: Banabhatta, a court poet of Harshavardhana, wrote the 'Harsha Charita', a biography of the king.

45. Which Gupta ruler is known for his military campaigns against the Hunas and is considered the last great Gupta emperor?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Skandagupta
  4. Kumaragupta I

Answer: Skandagupta

Explanation: Skandagupta repelled the Hunas and was the last powerful Gupta emperor.

46. The famous Iron Pillar at Mehrauli, known for its rust-resistant composition, belongs to which period?

  1. Mauryan
  2. Gupta
  3. Post-Gupta
  4. Vedic

Answer: Gupta

Explanation: The Iron Pillar of Mehrauli is attributed to the Gupta period, specifically to Chandragupta II.

47. Which Chinese pilgrim visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II and left detailed accounts?

  1. Hiuen Tsang
  2. Fa Hien
  3. I-tsing
  4. Megasthenes

Answer: Fa Hien

Explanation: Fa Hien visited India during the Gupta period (Chandragupta II) and wrote about the condition of the empire.

48. The Nalanda University, a major center of learning, flourished during which period?

  1. Mauryan
  2. Gupta and Post-Gupta
  3. Vijayanagara
  4. Chola

Answer: Gupta and Post-Gupta

Explanation: Nalanda rose to prominence during the Gupta period and continued under later dynasties like the Palas.

49. The famous Gupta emperor who assumed the title 'Vikramaditya' and is known for his patronage of literature and art?

  1. Chandragupta I
  2. Samudragupta
  3. Chandragupta II
  4. Skandagupta

Answer: Chandragupta II

Explanation: Chandragupta II adopted the title Vikramaditya and his court included the Navaratnas like Kalidasa.

50. The post-Gupta period saw the rise of which dynasty in the Kanchipuram region of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chalukyas
  2. Pallavas
  3. Rashtrakutas
  4. Pandyas

Answer: Pallavas

Explanation: The Pallavas established their rule in Tondaimandalam with Kanchi as capital, flourishing in the post-Gupta era.
