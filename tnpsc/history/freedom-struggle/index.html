Freedom Struggle MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Freedom Struggle questions to revise the TNPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. India gained independence from British rule on:

  1. 26 January 1950
  2. 15 August 1947
  3. 26 November 1949
  4. 30 January 1948

Answer: 15 August 1947

Explanation: India gained independence on 15 August 1947. Jawaharlal Nehru became the first Prime Minister.

2. Who is called the 'Father of the Nation' of India?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Subhas Chandra Bose
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Sardar Patel

Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi (Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi) is called the Father of the Nation. He led India's non-violent independence movement.

3. The 'Salt March' (Dandi March) was led by:

  1. Subhas Chandra Bose
  2. Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Jawaharlal Nehru

Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi led the Salt March (Dandi March) from 12 March to 6 April 1930, marching 241 miles to Dandi to make salt from sea water to protest the British salt tax.

4. The non-cooperation movement was launched in which year?

  1. 1905
  2. 1915
  3. 1920
  4. 1930

Answer: 1920

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi launched the Non-Cooperation Movement in 1920. It called for boycott of British goods, institutions, and courts.

5. Who founded the Indian National Congress in 1885?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  3. A.O. Hume
  4. Gopal Krishna Gokhale

Answer: A.O. Hume

Explanation: Allan Octavian Hume, a retired British civil servant, founded the Indian National Congress in 1885 in Bombay.
