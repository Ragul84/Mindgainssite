Chola Pandya MCQs with answers

26. Who was the most famous Chola king?

  1. Raja Raja Chola
  2. Rajendra Chola
  3. Karikala Chola
  4. Aditya Chola

Answer: Raja Raja Chola

Explanation: Raja Raja Chola was a renowned king of the Chola dynasty. He expanded the empire and built the Brihadeeswara temple.

27. Which kingdom rivaled the Cholas in South India?

  1. Pandyas
  2. Pallavas
  3. Vijayanagara
  4. Hoysalas

Answer: Pandyas

Explanation: The Pandyas were a powerful kingdom that rivaled the Cholas in South India. They ruled from Madurai and had a distinct culture.

28. What was the name of the famous Chola temple?

  1. Brihadeeswara
  2. Meenakshi
  3. Kailasanathar
  4. Ekambareswarar

Answer: Brihadeeswara

Explanation: The Brihadeeswara temple was built by Raja Raja Chola. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a testament to Chola architecture.

29. In which century did the Chola-Pandya conflict occur?

  1. 8th
  2. 9th
  3. 10th
  4. 11th

Answer: 10th

Explanation: The Chola-Pandya conflict occurred in the 10th century. It was a period of intense rivalry between the two kingdoms.

30. Who built the Grand Anicut dam?

  1. Karikala Chola
  2. Raja Raja Chola
  3. Rajendra Chola
  4. Narasimhavarman

Answer: Karikala Chola

Explanation: Karikala Chola built the Grand Anicut dam. It is an ancient irrigation dam that still functions today.

31. What was the name of the Chola naval expedition?

  1. Kadaram
  2. Sri Vijaya
  3. Maldives
  4. Laccadives

Answer: Kadaram

Explanation: The Chola naval expedition to Kadaram was a significant event. It marked the expansion of Chola power into Southeast Asia.

32. Which Chola king conquered Sri Lanka?

  1. Raja Raja Chola
  2. Rajendra Chola
  3. Aditya Chola
  4. Parantaka Chola

Answer: Rajendra Chola

Explanation: Rajendra Chola conquered Sri Lanka. He expanded the Chola empire and established trade relations with neighboring kingdoms.

33. What was the name of the Pandya king who resisted the Chola invasion?

  1. Vira Pandya
  2. Sundara Pandya
  3. Maravarman
  4. Jatavarman

Answer: Vira Pandya

Explanation: Vira Pandya resisted the Chola invasion. He was a brave king who fought against the Chola empire.

34. Which Chola ruler is credited with the construction of the Brihadeeswarar Temple at Thanjavur?

  1. Rajaraja I
  2. Rajendra I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Vikrama Chola

Answer: Rajaraja I

Explanation: Rajaraja I built the Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur, a masterpiece of Chola architecture.

35. The Pandya kingdom, known for its maritime trade, had its capital at which city?

  1. Madurai
  2. Kanchipuram
  3. Thanjavur
  4. Kaveripattinam

Answer: Madurai

Explanation: Madurai was the capital of the Pandya kingdom, famous for its trade with the Roman Empire.

36. Which Chola king is known for his naval expedition to Kadaram (present-day Malaysia/Indonesia)?

  1. Rajendra I
  2. Rajaraja I
  3. Aditya I
  4. Parantaka I

Answer: Rajendra I

Explanation: Rajendra I led a successful naval expedition to Kadaram, showcasing Chola maritime power.

37. The Pandya king who is mentioned in the 'Maduraikanchi' and known for his patronage of Tamil literature was?

  1. Nedunjeliyan I
  2. Maran Sendan
  3. Kadungon
  4. Arikesari Maravarman

Answer: Nedunjeliyan I

Explanation: Nedunjeliyan I, a Pandya king, is celebrated in the Sangam poem 'Maduraikanchi' for his patronage.

38. The Chola empire reached its zenith under which ruler, who also built the new capital at Gangaikonda Cholapuram?

  1. Rajendra I
  2. Rajaraja I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Rajadhiraja I

Answer: Rajendra I

Explanation: Rajendra I built Gangaikonda Cholapuram and expanded the Chola empire to its peak.

39. Which Pandya ruler revived the kingdom's power in the 6th century CE after the Kalabhra interregnum?

  1. Kadungon
  2. Nedunjeliyan I
  3. Maran Sendan
  4. Srimara Srivallabha

Answer: Kadungon

Explanation: Kadungon is credited with reviving the Pandya dynasty and ending the Kalabhra rule.

40. The famous 'Thiruvalluvar' statue at Kanyakumari is associated with which ancient Tamil kingdom's cultural heritage?

  1. Pandya
  2. Chola
  3. Chera
  4. Pallava

Answer: Pandya

Explanation: Thiruvalluvar, author of the Thirukkural, is traditionally associated with the Pandya kingdom's patronage.

41. Which Chola king defeated the Pandya king Maravarman Sundara Pandya and established Chola supremacy over the Pandya region?

  1. Rajaraja I
  2. Rajendra I
  3. Aditya I
  4. Parantaka I

Answer: Rajaraja I

Explanation: Rajaraja I defeated the Pandya ruler and brought the Pandya territory under Chola control.

42. The Pandya kingdom's decline in the 14th century was primarily due to invasions by which sultanate?

  1. Delhi Sultanate
  2. Bahmani Sultanate
  3. Vijayanagara Empire
  4. Madurai Sultanate

Answer: Delhi Sultanate

Explanation: The Delhi Sultanate under Malik Kafur invaded the Pandya kingdom, leading to its decline.

43. Which Chola ruler is known for his administrative reforms, including the creation of the 'Valanadu' administrative division?

  1. Rajaraja I
  2. Rajendra I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Vikrama Chola

Answer: Rajaraja I

Explanation: Rajaraja I implemented administrative reforms, dividing the empire into Valanadus for efficient governance.

44. Who was the founder of the Chola dynasty that captured Thanjavur in 850 CE?

  1. Vijayalaya
  2. Rajaraja I
  3. Karikala
  4. Aditya I

Answer: Vijayalaya

Explanation: Vijayalaya captured Thanjavur from the Muttaraiyar around 850 CE and founded the Chola dynasty.

45. Which Chola ruler constructed the Brihadeeswarar Temple at Thanjavur?

  1. Rajendra I
  2. Rajaraja I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Rajadhiraja I

Answer: Rajaraja I

Explanation: Rajaraja I built the Brihadeeswarar Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site, completed in 1010 CE.

46. Which Pandya king is credited with expanding the Meenakshi Amman Temple complex?

  1. Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan
  2. Maravarman Kulasekara Pandyan
  3. Nedum Chezhian
  4. Arikesari Parankusa

Answer: Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan

Explanation: Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan (13th century) was a prominent Pandya ruler who patronized the temple and Tamil literature.

47. What title did Rajendra I assume after his victory over the Ganges region?

  1. Mummudi Chola
  2. Gangaikonda Chola
  3. Rajakesari
  4. Parakesari

Answer: Gangaikonda Chola

Explanation: Rajendra I assumed the title Gangaikonda Chola and founded the city Gangaikonda Cholapuram.

48. The Pandya kingdom was a major exporter of which item to the Roman Empire?

  1. Pepper
  2. Silk
  3. Gold
  4. Cotton

Answer: Pepper

Explanation: The Pandyas were famous for exporting pepper and other spices to the Roman Empire.

49. Which early Chola king is mentioned in the Sangam poem 'Pattinappalai'?

  1. Karikala
  2. Vijayalaya
  3. Rajaraja I
  4. Aditya I

Answer: Karikala

Explanation: The Sangam poem Pattinappalai describes Karikala Chola's reign and his capital Puhar.

50. The Battle of Venni, fought between the Cholas and the Cheras and Pandyas, is associated with which Chola king?

  1. Karikala
  2. Rajendra I
  3. Rajadhiraja I
  4. Kulothunga I

Answer: Karikala

Explanation: Karikala Chola defeated the Cheras and Pandyas at the Battle of Venni, establishing Chola supremacy.
