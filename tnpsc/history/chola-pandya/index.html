Chola Pandya MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Chola Pandya questions to revise the TNPSC History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. The Constituent Assembly of India was set up under the:

  1. Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946
  2. Cripps Mission, 1942
  3. Mountbatten Plan, 1947
  4. Wavell Plan, 1945

Answer: Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946

Explanation: The Constituent Assembly of India was set up under the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946). It first met on 9 December 1946 with Dr. Rajendra Prasad as its permanent chairman from 11 December 1946.

2. Who was the first President of Indian Republic?

  1. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
  2. Dr. S. Radhakrishnan
  3. Dr. Zakir Husain
  4. Dr. V.V. Giri

Answer: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

Explanation: Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the first President of the Republic of India (1950-1962), serving two terms. He was also the President of the Constituent Assembly.

3. The Brihadeeswara Temple at Thanjavur was built in the year:

  1. 985 CE
  2. 1003–1010 CE
  3. 1014 CE
  4. 1025 CE

Answer: 1003–1010 CE

Explanation: The Brihadeeswara Temple (also called Raja Raja Peruvudaiyar Kovil or Tanjore Big Temple) was completed c. 1010 CE during the reign of Rajaraja I. The 66m (216 ft) vimana is one of the tallest in South India.

4. The Constituent Assembly of India was set up based on the recommendations of:

  1. Simon Commission
  2. Cripps Mission
  3. Cabinet Mission Plan (1946)
  4. Mountbatten Plan

Answer: Cabinet Mission Plan (1946)

Explanation: The Cabinet Mission Plan (1946, by Pethick-Lawrence, Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander) created the Constituent Assembly. It had 389 members (296 from British India + 93 from Princely States). First meeting: 9 December 1946; Dr. Rajendra Prasad was president.

5. Which dynasty built the Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur?

  1. Pallavas
  2. Pandyas
  3. Cholas
  4. Vijayanagara

Answer: Cholas

Explanation: The Brihadeeswarar Temple was built by the Chola dynasty in the 11th century. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

6. Who was the founder of the Chola dynasty?

  1. Vijayalaya
  2. Aditya I
  3. Parantaka I
  4. Rajendra I

Answer: Vijayalaya

Explanation: Vijayalaya was the founder of the Chola dynasty. He ruled in the 9th century.

7. What was the capital of the Pandya kingdom?

  1. Madurai
  2. Thanjavur
  3. Tirunelveli
  4. Kanchipuram

Answer: Madurai

Explanation: Madurai was the capital of the Pandya kingdom. It was an important cultural and trade center.

8. Which Chola king built the Gangaikonda Cholapuram temple?

  1. Rajendra I
  2. Rajadhiraja I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Vikrama Chola

Answer: Rajendra I

Explanation: Rajendra I built the Gangaikonda Cholapuram temple in the 11th century. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

9. Who was the most famous Pandya king?

  1. Arikesari Parankusa Maravarman
  2. Srivallabha
  3. Varaguna
  4. Jatavarman Sundara Pandya

Answer: Jatavarman Sundara Pandya

Explanation: Jatavarman Sundara Pandya was the most famous Pandya king. He ruled in the 13th century.

10. What was the name of the Chola-Pandya war that took place in the 13th century?

  1. War of the Three Kingdoms
  2. Battle of Koppam
  3. Battle of Singalanthakka
  4. Battle of Manapad

Answer: Battle of Singalanthakka

Explanation: The Battle of Singalanthakka was a war between the Cholas and Pandyas. It marked the decline of the Chola dynasty.

11. Which of the following was a notable feature of the Chola administration?

  1. Centralized bureaucracy
  2. Decentralized administration
  3. Feudal system
  4. Tribal councils

Answer: Centralized bureaucracy

Explanation: The Chola administration was known for its centralized bureaucracy. It had a well-organized system of governance.

12. Who was the Chola king that conquered Sri Lanka?

  1. Rajendra I
  2. Rajadhiraja I
  3. Kulothunga I
  4. Parantaka I

Answer: Rajendra I

Explanation: Rajendra I conquered Sri Lanka in the 11th century. He was a powerful and expansionist king.

13. What was the name of the Pandya king who built the Meenakshi Amman Temple?

  1. Arikesari Parankusa Maravarman
  2. Srivallabha
  3. Varaguna
  4. Kulasekara Pandya

Answer: Kulasekara Pandya

Explanation: Kulasekara Pandya built the Meenakshi Amman Temple in Madurai. It is a famous Hindu temple.

14. Which of the following was a major trade center of the Chola kingdom?

  1. Kanchipuram
  2. Madurai
  3. Thanjavur
  4. Nagapattinam

Answer: Nagapattinam

Explanation: Nagapattinam was a major trade center of the Chola kingdom. It was an important port city.

15. What was the name of the Chola-Pandya war?

  1. Tiruvannamalai war
  2. Kurukshetra war
  3. Vellar war
  4. Kudamalai war

Answer: Vellar war

Explanation: The Vellar war was a series of conflicts between the Chola and Pandya kingdoms. It lasted for several centuries.

16. What was the name of the Pandya king who defeated the Cholas?

  1. Maravarman Sundara Pandya
  2. Jatavarman Sundara Pandya
  3. Arikesari Parankusa Maravarman
  4. Vira Pandya

Answer: Maravarman Sundara Pandya

Explanation: Maravarman Sundara Pandya defeated the Cholas in the 13th century. He was a powerful Pandya king.

17. Which dynasty built the Meenakshi Amman Temple in Madurai?

  1. Pallavas
  2. Cholas
  3. Pandyas
  4. Vijayanagara

Answer: Pandyas

Explanation: The Meenakshi Amman Temple was built by the Pandya dynasty. It is a famous Hindu temple in India.

18. Who was the last Chola king?

  1. Rajendra III
  2. Rajendra II
  3. Kulottunga III
  4. Kulottunga II

Answer: Rajendra III

Explanation: Rajendra III was the last Chola king. He ruled from 1246 to 1279 AD.

19. What was the name of the Chola king who defeated the Pandyas?

  1. Aditya I
  2. Parantaka I
  3. Rajendra I
  4. Kulottunga I

Answer: Parantaka I

Explanation: Parantaka I defeated the Pandyas and expanded the Chola empire. He ruled from 907 to 955 AD.

20. Which of the following was a major port city of the Chola kingdom?

  1. Kaveripattinam
  2. Muziris
  3. Nagapattinam
  4. Poompuhar

Answer: Nagapattinam

Explanation: Nagapattinam was a major port city of the Chola kingdom. It was an important center for trade and commerce.

21. Who was the Chola king who built the Airavatesvara Temple?

  1. Rajendra II
  2. Kulottunga II
  3. Rajaraja II
  4. Rajendra I

Answer: Rajendra II

Explanation: Rajendra II built the Airavatesvara Temple in 1160 AD. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

22. What was the name of the Pandya king who fought against the Cholas?

  1. Vira Pandya
  2. Sundara Pandya
  3. Maravarman
  4. Jatavarman

Answer: Vira Pandya

Explanation: Vira Pandya fought against the Cholas and tried to expand the Pandya kingdom. He ruled from 1251 to 1281 AD.

23. Which of the following was a major achievement of the Chola dynasty?

  1. Development of Tamil literature
  2. Construction of temples
  3. Expansion of trade
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Chola dynasty achieved all of the above, including development of Tamil literature, construction of temples, and expansion of trade. They were a major power in South India.

24. Which dynasty ruled Tamil Nadu during the 9th to 13th centuries?

  1. Pallavas
  2. Cholas
  3. Pandyas
  4. Vijayanagara

Answer: Cholas

Explanation: The Chola dynasty ruled Tamil Nadu from the 9th to 13th centuries. They were known for their administrative and military prowess.

25. What was the capital of the Chola kingdom?

  1. Tanjavur
  2. Madurai
  3. Kanchipuram
  4. Trichy

Answer: Tanjavur

Explanation: Tanjavur was the capital of the Chola kingdom during its peak. It was an important center of learning and culture.
