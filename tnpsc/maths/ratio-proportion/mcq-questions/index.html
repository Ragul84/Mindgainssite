26. The mean proportional between 16 and 25 is:

  1. 20
  2. 22
  3. 18
  4. 21

Answer: 20

Explanation: Mean proportional = √(16×25) = √400 = 20.

27. 4 boys can complete a task in 10 days. 5 girls can complete same task in 8 days. In what ratio is 1 boy's work to 1 girl's work?

  1. 1:1
  2. 5:4
  3. 4:5
  4. 2:1

Answer: 1:1

Explanation: Boys total work=4×10=40 boy-days. Girls total work=5×8=40 girl-days. Both equal, so 1 boy-day = 1 girl-day. Ratio=1:1.

28. Two supplementary angles are in ratio 2:3. The angles are:

  1. 72° and 108°
  2. 60° and 120°
  3. 80° and 100°
  4. 90° and 90°

Answer: 72° and 108°

Explanation: 2x+3x=180°. 5x=180°. x=36°. Angles: 72° and 108°.

29. A and B invest ₹4,000 and ₹6,000 in a business. Profit of ₹5,000 is divided in ratio:

  1. 2:3
  2. 4:6 (same)
  3. 3:2
  4. 1:2

Answer: 2:3

Explanation: Profit ratio = Investment ratio = 4000:6000 = 2:3.

30. A invests ₹5,000 for 12 months and B invests ₹8,000 for 9 months. Profit ratio:

  1. 5:6
  2. 8:9
  3. 12:9
  4. 3:4

Answer: 5:6

Explanation: A's capital×time = 5000×12=60000. B's = 8000×9=72000. Ratio=60000:72000=5:6.

31. A, B, C invest ₹2000, ₹3000, ₹5000. Total profit ₹4000. C's share:

  1. ₹2,000
  2. ₹1,200
  3. ₹800
  4. ₹1,500

Answer: ₹2,000

Explanation: Ratio=2:3:5. Total parts=10. C's share=(5/10)×4000=₹2,000.

32. A starts business with ₹10,000. After 3 months B joins with ₹20,000. After 1 year, profit ratio A:B:

  1. 2:3
  2. 3:2
  3. 1:2
  4. 3:4

Answer: 2:3

Explanation: A: 10000×12=120000. B: 20000×9=180000. Ratio=120000:180000=2:3.

33. A sum of money is divided among three people in the ratio 2:3:5. If the total amount is Rs. 1000, what is the share of the person with the highest ratio?

  1. 200
  2. 300
  3. 500
  4. 400

Answer: 500

Explanation: The total parts are 2+3+5 = 10, the person with the highest ratio gets 5 parts, which is 5/10 of 1000, i.e., 500.

34. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 7:5. If there are 280 boys, how many girls are there?

  1. 200
  2. 180
  3. 160
  4. 150

Answer: 200

Explanation: Let the number of boys be 7x and girls be 5x, given 7x = 280, x = 40, so girls = 5x = 200.

35. A mixture of milk and water is in the ratio 4:1. If 5 liters of water is added to it, the ratio becomes 2:1. Find the quantity of milk in the mixture.

  1. 20
  2. 16
  3. 24
  4. 30

Answer: 16

Explanation: Let the initial quantity of milk be 4x and water be x, after adding 5 liters, the ratio becomes 4x:(x+5) = 2:1, solving gives x = 5, so milk = 4x = 20.

  1. 25
  2. 30
  3. 28
  4. 26

Answer: 30

Explanation: Let the ages be 3x and 5x, given 3x = 18, x = 6, so the older person's age is 5x = 30.

37. A bag contains Re. 1, 50 paisa and 25 paisa coins in the ratio 3:4:5. If the total amount is Rs. 140, find the number of 50 paisa coins.

  1. 160
  2. 140
  3. 120
  4. 100

Answer: 120

Explanation: Let the number of coins be 3x, 4x, 5x, total amount = (3x*1) + (4x*0.5) + (5x*0.25) = 140, solving gives x = 20, so 50 paisa coins = 4x = 80, but since the value is asked in terms of number, we need to consider the value of 50 paisa coins which will be 80*0.5 = 40, and total amount is 140, hence 140/1.2 = 116.67, hence 4x = 120.

38. The ratio of the number of boys and girls in a class is 7:3. If the number of boys is increased by 50% and the number of girls is increased by 20%, what is the new ratio?

  1. 7:3
  2. 14:5
  3. 21:7
  4. 7:5

Answer: 14:5

Explanation: New ratio = (7*1.5):(3*1.2) = 10.5:3.6, simplifying gives 14:5 (after multiplying both parts by 10/3*2 to get whole numbers).

42. The ratio of the areas of two similar figures is 4:9. What is the ratio of their corresponding sides?

  1. 2:3
  2. 3:4
  3. 4:5
  4. 2:5

Answer: 2:3

Explanation: The ratio of areas is the square of the ratio of the corresponding sides, so the ratio of the sides is the square root of 4:9, which is 2:3.
