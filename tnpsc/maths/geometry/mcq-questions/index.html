26. Area of a rhombus with diagonals 16 cm and 12 cm is:

  1. 96 cm²
  2. 192 cm²
  3. 48 cm²
  4. 144 cm²

Answer: 96 cm²

Explanation: Area of rhombus = (d₁×d₂)/2 = (16×12)/2 = 192/2 = 96 cm².

27. Perimeter of a semicircle of radius 7 cm is (π=22/7):

  1. 36 cm
  2. 44 cm
  3. 22 cm
  4. 30 cm

Answer: 36 cm

Explanation: Perimeter = πr+2r = r(π+2) = 7×(22/7+2) = 7×(22/7+14/7) = 7×36/7 = 36 cm.

28. The side of a square whose area is equal to area of a rectangle 8 cm × 18 cm:

  1. 12 cm
  2. 16 cm
  3. 10 cm
  4. 14 cm

Answer: 12 cm

Explanation: Area of rectangle = 8×18 = 144 cm². Side of square = √144 = 12 cm.

29. Area of a trapezium with parallel sides 8 cm and 12 cm and height 6 cm:

  1. 60 cm²
  2. 80 cm²
  3. 72 cm²
  4. 90 cm²

Answer: 60 cm²

Explanation: Area = ½×(sum of parallel sides)×height = ½×(8+12)×6 = ½×20×6 = 60 cm².

30. If radius of a circle is increased by 10%, its area increases by:

  1. 21%
  2. 10%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%

Answer: 21%

Explanation: New area = π(1.1r)² = 1.21πr². Increase = 21%.

31. Area of an equilateral triangle with side 6 cm is:

  1. 9√3 cm²
  2. 12√3 cm²
  3. 6√3 cm²
  4. 18√3 cm²

Answer: 9√3 cm²

Explanation: Area = (√3/4)×s² = (√3/4)×36 = 9√3 cm².

32. The diagonal of a square is 10√2 cm. Its area is:

  1. 100 cm²
  2. 200 cm²
  3. 50 cm²
  4. 150 cm²

Answer: 100 cm²

Explanation: Diagonal = s√2 = 10√2. s = 10 cm. Area = 10² = 100 cm².

33. Volume of a cube of side 5 cm is:

  1. 125 cm³
  2. 25 cm³
  3. 250 cm³
  4. 75 cm³

Answer: 125 cm³

Explanation: Volume of cube = s³ = 5³ = 125 cm³.

34. Total surface area of a cuboid 4×3×2 cm is:

  1. 52 cm²
  2. 48 cm²
  3. 24 cm²
  4. 36 cm²

Answer: 52 cm²

Explanation: TSA = 2(lb+bh+hl) = 2(4×3+3×2+2×4) = 2(12+6+8) = 2×26 = 52 cm².

35. Volume of a cone with radius 7 cm and height 6 cm (π=22/7):

  1. 308 cm³
  2. 616 cm³
  3. 154 cm³
  4. 462 cm³

Answer: 308 cm³

Explanation: Volume = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)×(22/7)×49×6 = (1/3)×22×7×6 = (1/3)×924 = 308 cm³.

36. Curved surface area of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm (π=22/7):

  1. 440 cm²
  2. 220 cm²
  3. 308 cm²
  4. 616 cm²

Answer: 440 cm²

Explanation: CSA = 2πrh = 2×(22/7)×7×10 = 2×22×10 = 440 cm².

37. Volume of a sphere with radius 6 cm (π=22/7):

  1. 905.14 cm³
  2. 452.57 cm³
  3. 1809 cm³
  4. 226.28 cm³

Answer: 905.14 cm³

Explanation: Volume = (4/3)πr³ = (4/3)×(22/7)×216 = (4×22×216)/(3×7) = 19008/21 = 905.14 cm³.

38. If each side of a cube is doubled, its volume becomes:

  1. 8 times
  2. 4 times
  3. 2 times
  4. 16 times

Answer: 8 times

Explanation: Original volume = s³. New volume = (2s)³ = 8s³ = 8 times.

39. A solid hemisphere of radius 7 cm. Total surface area (π=22/7):

  1. 462 cm²
  2. 308 cm²
  3. 154 cm²
  4. 616 cm²

Answer: 462 cm²

Explanation: TSA of hemisphere = 3πr² = 3×(22/7)×49 = 3×22×7 = 462 cm².

40. In a right triangle, if one acute angle is 30°, the other acute angle is:

  1. 60°
  2. 45°
  3. 30°
  4. 90°

Answer: 60°

Explanation: Sum of angles in triangle = 180°. 90°+30°+x=180°. x=60°.

41. In triangle ABC, if AB=AC, angle B=70°, then angle A:

  1. 40°
  2. 50°
  3. 60°
  4. 70°

Answer: 40°

Explanation: AB=AC means isosceles. Angle B = Angle C = 70°. Angle A = 180°−70°−70° = 40°.

42. The angle in a semicircle is:

  1. 90°
  2. 180°
  3. 45°
  4. 60°

Answer: 90°

Explanation: Angle in a semicircle (angle subtended at circumference by diameter) is always 90° (Thales' theorem).

43. Pythagoras theorem: In a right triangle with hypotenuse c and legs a, b:

  1. a²+b²=c²
  2. a+b=c
  3. a²=b²+c²
  4. c²=a²−b²

Answer: a²+b²=c²

Explanation: Pythagoras theorem: Square of hypotenuse = sum of squares of other two sides. a²+b²=c².

44. If two chords AB and CD of a circle intersect at P, then PA×PB = ?

  1. PC×PD
  2. PA+PB
  3. PC+PD
  4. PA×CD

Answer: PC×PD

Explanation: Intersecting chords theorem: PA×PB = PC×PD.

45. Angle subtended by an arc at the center is _____ the angle subtended at any point on the remaining circle:

  1. Twice
  2. Half
  3. Equal
  4. Thrice

Answer: Twice

Explanation: Central angle = 2 × inscribed angle (angle at circumference). This is the Central Angle Theorem.

46. In a right angled triangle, sides are 5, 12, 13. Area:

  1. 30 cm²
  2. 60 cm²
  3. 25 cm²
  4. 36 cm²

Answer: 30 cm²

Explanation: Right angle is between sides 5 and 12. Area=½×5×12=30 cm².

47. Perimeter of an equilateral triangle of area 16√3 cm² is:

  1. 24 cm
  2. 32 cm
  3. 16 cm
  4. 48 cm

Answer: 24 cm

Explanation: Area = √3/4 × s² = 16√3. s² = 64. s = 8. Perimeter = 3×8 = 24 cm.

48. What is the area of a circle inscribed in a square of side 10 cm? (π=3.14)

  1. 78.5 cm²
  2. 100 cm²
  3. 50 cm²
  4. 314 cm²

Answer: 78.5 cm²

Explanation: Radius of inscribed circle = side/2 = 5 cm. Area = πr² = 3.14×25 = 78.5 cm².

49. A square field has area 625 m². Its perimeter:

  1. 100 m
  2. 125 m
  3. 50 m
  4. 150 m

Answer: 100 m

Explanation: Side = √625 = 25 m. Perimeter = 4×25 = 100 m.

50. How many bricks of size 25cm×12cm×6cm are needed to build a wall 10m×3m×24cm?

  1. 4000
  2. 3000
  3. 5000
  4. 2000

Answer: 4000

Explanation: Volume of wall = 10×3×0.24 m³ = 7.2 m³ = 7,200,000 cm³. Volume of 1 brick = 25×12×6 = 1800 cm³. Bricks = 7200000/1800 = 4000.
