26. A bike travels 240 km in 4 hours. What is its average speed?

  1. 50 km/h
  2. 55 km/h
  3. 60 km/h

Explanation: To find the average speed, divide the distance by the time. The average speed is 60 km/h.

45. A shopkeeper buys an item for 100 and sells it for 120. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 10%
  2. 15%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Profit percentage is (selling price - cost price) / cost price * 100, so (120 - 100) / 100 * 100 = 20%.

46. A person invests 1000 at a 5% interest rate. What is the simple interest after 1 year?

  1. 45
  2. 50
  3. 55
  4. 60

Answer: 50

Explanation: Simple interest is principal * rate * time, so 1000 * 0.05 * 1 = 50.

47. A bat and a ball together cost 110. The bat costs 100 more than the ball. What is the cost of the ball?

  1. 5
  2. 6
  3. 7
  4. 8

Answer: 5

Explanation: Let ball cost be x, then bat cost is x + 100, so x + x + 100 = 110, 2x = 10, x = 5.

48. A man can do a job in 10 days. How many days will it take for 2 men to do the job?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 5

Explanation: If one man can do the job in 10 days, two men can do it in 10 / 2 = 5 days.
