26. What is the simple interest on a principal amount of Rs. 1200 at 6% interest for 2 years?

  1. 120
  2. 140
  3. 160
  4. 180

Answer: 140

Explanation: Simple interest is calculated as principal * rate * time, so 1200 * 0.06 * 2 = 144, which is closest to option 140.

34. A sum of money amounts to Rs. 800 in 3 years and Rs. 1000 in 6 years at simple interest, what is the rate of interest?

  1. 6%
  2. 8%
  3. 10%
  4. 12%

Answer: 8%

Explanation: Rate of interest can be found by calculating the difference in amount and dividing by principal * time, so (1000 - 800) / (800 * 3) = 0.08 or 8%.
