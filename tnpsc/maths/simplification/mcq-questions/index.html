26. A number when divided by 6 leaves remainder 5. What is the remainder when the same number is divided by 3?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. 5

Answer: 2

Explanation: Number = 6k+5 = 3(2k+1)+2. So remainder when divided by 3 is 2.

27. The unit digit of 7^34 is:

  1. 1
  2. 3
  3. 7
  4. 9

Answer: 9

Explanation: Powers of 7: 7¹=7, 7²=49(unit:9), 7³=343(unit:3), 7⁴=2401(unit:1), cycle of 4. 34=4×8+2, unit digit of 7^34 = unit digit of 7² = 9.

28. √(0.0064) = ?

  1. 0.08
  2. 0.8
  3. 0.008
  4. 8

Answer: 0.08

Explanation: √(0.0064) = √(64/10000) = 8/100 = 0.08.

29. The value of (−3)³ is:

  1. 9
  2. −9
  3. 27
  4. −27

Answer: −27

Explanation: (−3)³ = (−3)×(−3)×(−3) = 9×(−3) = −27. Odd power of negative number is negative.

30. 20% of 350 + 35% of 200 = ?

  1. 140
  2. 135
  3. 130
  4. 145

Answer: 140

Explanation: 20% of 350 = 70. 35% of 200 = 70. Total = 70+70 = 140.

31. A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. The net change is:

  1. 0%
  2. 5% increase
  3. 5% decrease
  4. 10% increase

Answer: 0%

Explanation: Let number = 100. After 25% increase = 125. After 20% decrease = 125×0.8 = 100. Net change = 0%.

32. 60% of a number is 96. What is 85% of that number?

  1. 136
  2. 128
  3. 140
  4. 120

Answer: 136

Explanation: Number = 96/0.6 = 160. 85% of 160 = 0.85×160 = 136.

33. In an election, candidate A gets 55% of total votes. If total votes are 80,000, how many votes did candidate B get?

  1. 36,000
  2. 44,000
  3. 40,000
  4. 38,000

Answer: 36,000

Explanation: A gets 55% = 44,000. B gets 45% = 80,000×0.45 = 36,000.

34. Population of a city is 5,00,000. If it increases at 4% annually, population after 2 years is:

  1. 5,40,800
  2. 5,20,000
  3. 5,41,200
  4. 5,40,000

Answer: 5,40,800

Explanation: P(1+r/100)² = 500000×(1.04)² = 500000×1.0816 = 540800.

35. If 15% of a number is 45, what is 30% of that number?

  1. 90
  2. 75
  3. 60
  4. 120

Answer: 90

Explanation: Number = 45/0.15 = 300. 30% of 300 = 90.

36. SI on ₹2,000 at 5% per annum for 3 years is:

  1. ₹250
  2. ₹300
  3. ₹350
  4. ₹400

Answer: ₹300

Explanation: SI = PRT/100 = 2000×5×3/100 = 30000/100 = ₹300.

37. A vessel has 40 litres of milk. 10 litres is replaced by water. This is done again. Milk remaining:

  1. 22.5 L
  2. 25 L
  3. 20 L
  4. 27.5 L

Answer: 22.5 L

Explanation: After 1st: milk = 40×(30/40) = 30 L. After 2nd: milk = 30×(30/40) = 22.5 L.

38. How much water must be added to 60 L of milk at ₹3.50/L so that cost price is ₹2.80/L?

  1. 15 L
  2. 12 L
  3. 10 L
  4. 18 L

Answer: 15 L

Explanation: By alligation: (3.50−2.80):(2.80−0) = 0.70:2.80 = 1:4. Milk:Water = 4:1. Water = 60/4 = 15 L.

39. A grocer mixes 30 kg of sugar at ₹12/kg with 20 kg at ₹15/kg. Average price:

  1. ₹13.20/kg
  2. ₹13.50/kg
  3. ₹13/kg
  4. ₹14/kg

Answer: ₹13.20/kg

Explanation: Total cost=(30×12)+(20×15)=360+300=660. Total weight=50kg. Average=660/50=₹13.20/kg.

40. A's age 4 years ago was twice B's age. After 4 years, A will be 1.5 times B's age. Present ages:

  1. A=20, B=12
  2. A=24, B=14
  3. A=16, B=10
  4. A=28, B=16

Answer: A=20, B=12

Explanation: Let present ages: A and B. A−4=2(B−4)→A=2B−4. A+4=1.5(B+4)→A=1.5B+2. 2B−4=1.5B+2→0.5B=6→B=12. A=2(12)−4=20.

41. Sum of ages of A and B is 30. A is 4 years older than B. A's age:

  1. 17
  2. 19
  3. 16
  4. 18

Answer: 17

Explanation: A+B=30, A=B+4. B+4+B=30. 2B=26. B=13. A=17.

42. Present age of mother is 3 times her daughter's age. 12 years hence, mother will be twice daughter's age. Daughter's present age:

  1. 12 years
  2. 10 years
  3. 15 years
  4. 18 years

Answer: 12 years

Explanation: M=3D. M+12=2(D+12)=2D+24. 3D+12=2D+24. D=12 years.

43. At 3:30 PM, the angle between clock hands is:

  1. 75°
  2. 90°
  3. 60°
  4. 45°

Answer: 75°

Explanation: At 3:30, hour hand is at 3.5 hours = 3.5×30°=105°. Minute hand at 30×6°=180°. Angle=180°−105°=75°.

44. How many times do clock hands coincide in 12 hours?

  1. 11 times
  2. 12 times
  3. 10 times
  4. 22 times

Answer: 11 times

Explanation: Clock hands coincide 11 times in every 12 hours (not 12, because at 12:00 they coincide and it's counted once).

45. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. If set correctly at 9 AM, what time does it show at 5 PM?

  1. 5:40 PM
  2. 5:30 PM
  3. 5:45 PM
  4. 6:00 PM

Answer: 5:40 PM

Explanation: 8 hours elapsed. Gain = 8×5 = 40 minutes extra. Clock shows 5 PM + 40 min = 5:40 PM.

46. The minute hand of a clock covers 360° in:

  1. 60 min
  2. 30 min
  3. 12 hours
  4. 24 hours

Answer: 60 min

Explanation: The minute hand completes one full revolution (360°) every 60 minutes.

47. At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock do the hands of a clock coincide?

  1. 21 9/11 min past 4
  2. 20 min past 4
  3. 22 min past 4
  4. 21 min past 4

Answer: 21 9/11 min past 4

Explanation: Minute hand catches hour hand: M = 5H×12/11. At 4, H=4. M=60×12/11=720/11=65 5/11. This is past the hour, so coincidence at 21 9/11 minutes past 4.

48. If January 1, 2020 is a Wednesday, what day is January 1, 2021?

  1. Friday
  2. Thursday
  3. Saturday
  4. Wednesday

Answer: Friday

Explanation: 2020 is a leap year (366 days = 52 weeks + 2 days). So Jan 1, 2021 is Wednesday + 2 = Friday.

49. What day of the week was August 15, 1947?

  1. Friday
  2. Saturday
  3. Thursday
  4. Sunday

Answer: Friday

Explanation: August 15, 1947 (India's Independence Day) was a Friday.

50. Odd days in a century (100 years) are:

  1. 5
  2. 4
  3. 3
  4. 6

Answer: 5

Explanation: 100 years = 76 ordinary + 24 leap = 76×365+24×366 = 36524 days = 5217 weeks + 5 days. Odd days = 5.
