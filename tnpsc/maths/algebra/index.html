1. The roots of the equation x² - 5x + 6 = 0 are:

  1. 2 and 4
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 6
  4. 3 and 4

Answer: 2 and 3

Explanation: x² - 5x + 6 = 0 → (x-2)(x-3) = 0 → x = 2 or x = 3.

2. If 2x−y=4 and x+y=5, then x = ?

  1. 3
  2. 2
  3. 4
  4. 1

Answer: 3

Explanation: Adding equations: 3x=9. x=3.

3. Equation of x-axis is:

  1. y=0
  2. x=0
  3. y=x
  4. x=y

Answer: y=0

Explanation: The x-axis is the horizontal axis where y-coordinate = 0 for all points. Equation: y=0.

4. If a = 2 and b = 3, then the value of a^2 + b^2 is

  1. 10
  2. 13
  3. 12
  4. 7

Answer: 10

Explanation: Calculate a^2 + b^2 = 2^2 + 3^2 = 4 + 9 = 13, but since a is 2 and b is 3, a^2 is 4 and b^2 is 9, so a^2 + b^2 equals 13.

  2. 3

Answer: 6

Explanation: Highest common factor of 12 and 18 is 6.

6. A person travels 30 km in 2 hours, his speed is

  1. 10 km/hr
  2. 15 km/hr
  3. 12 km/hr
  4. 20 km/hr

Answer: 12 km/hr

Explanation: Speed equals distance divided by time, so 30 km / 2 hr = 15 km/hr.

7. The value of x in the equation 2x + 5 = 11 is

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 3

Explanation: Solving for x, 2x = 11 - 5, so 2x = 6 and x = 3.

8. A shopkeeper sells a product at a 10% profit, if the cost price is 200, the selling price is

  1. 210
  2. 220
  3. 200
  4. 180

Answer: 220

Explanation: Selling price equals cost price plus 10% of cost price, so 200 + 0.1 * 200 = 200 + 20 = 220.

9. The ratio of two numbers is 2:3, if the smaller number is 8, the larger number is

  1. 10
  2. 12
  3. 8
  4. 6

Answer: 12

Explanation: Since the ratio of the smaller number to the larger number is 2:3 and the smaller number is 8, the larger number is 8 * (3/2) = 12.

11. A sum of money becomes triple in 5 years, the rate of interest is

  1. 20%
  2. 60%
  3. 40%
  4. 100%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: If a sum of money becomes triple, it means the interest is 2 times the principal, so rate of interest is 100% / 5 years = 20% per year.

12. A person deposits 1000 at a 5% interest, the simple interest after 2 years is

  1. 100
  2. 50
  3. 75
  4. 200

Answer: 100

Explanation: Simple interest equals principal times rate times time, so 1000 * 0.05 * 2 = 100.

13. The value of (a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2 is

  1. 4ab
  2. 4a
  3. 4b
  4. 4ab + 4a

Answer: 4ab

Explanation: Expanding the squares, (a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2 equals a^2 + 2ab + b^2 - (a^2 - 2ab + b^2), which simplifies to 4ab.
