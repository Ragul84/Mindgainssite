26. The LCM of two numbers is 180 and their HCF is 6, what is their product?

  1. 600
  2. 720
  3. 1080
  4. 1200

Answer: 1080

Explanation: The product of two numbers is equal to the product of their LCM and HCF, so the product of the two numbers is 180 * 6 = 1080.
