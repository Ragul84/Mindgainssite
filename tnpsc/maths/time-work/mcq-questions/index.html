27. 4 men and 6 women can complete a work in 8 days. If 1 man is as efficient as 2 women, in how many days will 8 men complete the work?

  1. 6 days
  2. 7 days
  3. 8 days
  4. 9 days

Answer: 7 days

Explanation: 6 women = 3 men, so total 7 men. Work = 7*8 = 56 man-days. 8 men take 56/8 = 7 days.

28. A can do a work in 15 days. B is 25% more efficient than A. How many days will B take to do the same work?

  1. 10 days
  2. 12 days
  3. 18 days
  4. 20 days

Answer: 12 days

Explanation: B's efficiency = 125% of A, so time = 15 / 1.25 = 12 days.

29. A alone can complete a work in 15 days. B alone can complete the same work in 30 days. In how many days will A and B together complete the work?

  1. 8 days
  2. 10 days
  3. 12 days
  4. 9 days

Answer: 10 days

Explanation: A's 1 day work = 1/15, B's 1 day work = 1/30. Combined = 1/15 + 1/30 = 1/10, so 10 days.

35. P and Q together can do a piece of work in 8 days. P alone can do it in 12 days. In how many days can Q alone do it?

  1. 20 days
  2. 24 days
  3. 18 days
  4. 16 days

Answer: 24 days

Explanation: P+Q 1 day = 1/8, P 1 day = 1/12. Q 1 day = 1/8 - 1/12 = 1/24, so 24 days.

44. A and B together can complete a work in 30 days. They work together for 10 days, then A leaves. B completes the remaining work in 20 days. In how many days can B alone complete the whole work?

  1. 60 days
  2. 45 days
  3. 30 days
  4. 50 days

Answer: 30 days

Explanation: Combined rate = 1/30; work done in 10 days = 10/30=1/3; remaining = 2/3 done by B in 20 days, so B's rate = (2/3)/20 = 1/30; B alone takes 30 days.
