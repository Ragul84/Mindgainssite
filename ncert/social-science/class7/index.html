Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 249 questions.

1. Which of the following is a classical dance form originating from the state of Kerala?

  1. Kathakali
  2. Bharatanatyam
  3. Kuchipudi
  4. Odissi

Answer: Kathakali

Explanation: Kathakali is a highly stylized classical dance-drama from Kerala known for its elaborate makeup and costumes.

2. The Bhakti movement emphasized which of the following principles?

  1. Devotion to a personal god
  2. Strict adherence to caste hierarchies
  3. Ritualistic animal sacrifices
  4. Exclusive use of Sanskrit in worship

Answer: Devotion to a personal god

Explanation: The Bhakti movement focused on a personal, loving bond between the devotee and God, regardless of caste or creed.

3. Which language was primarily used by the Alvars and Nayanars to spread their message of devotion in South India?

  1. Tamil
  2. Kannada
  3. Telugu
  4. Malayalam

Answer: Tamil

Explanation: The Alvars and Nayanars composed hymns in Tamil to make spiritual teachings accessible to the common people.

4. The 'Baul' singers are associated with the folk culture of which Indian state?

  1. West Bengal
  2. Punjab
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Assam

Answer: West Bengal

Explanation: Bauls are mystic minstrels from Bengal known for their unique spiritual songs.

5. Which of the following is a characteristic of the Sufi tradition?

  1. Emphasis on austerity and love for God
  2. Strict adherence to formal legalism
  3. Rejection of music in worship
  4. Promotion of social stratification

Answer: Emphasis on austerity and love for God

Explanation: Sufism emphasizes the inner search for God through love, meditation, and austerity.

6. The 'Yakshagana' is a traditional theatre form from which state?

  1. Karnataka
  2. Maharashtra
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. Kerala

Answer: Karnataka

Explanation: Yakshagana is a traditional folk theatre form that combines dance, music, and dialogue from Karnataka.

7. Which of these is a primary factor that contributed to the evolution of regional cultures in India?

  1. Geographical isolation and local traditions
  2. Uniformity in language across the subcontinent
  3. Complete absence of foreign influence
  4. The banning of folk arts

Answer: Geographical isolation and local traditions

Explanation: Regional cultures evolved due to the interaction between local traditions and the specific geography of different areas.

8. The 'Bihu' dance is a celebration of harvest and nature in which state?

  1. Assam
  2. Manipur
  3. Meghalaya
  4. Sikkim

Answer: Assam

Explanation: Bihu is the most popular folk dance of Assam, performed during the Bihu festivals.

9. Which saint is associated with the 'Saguna' form of Bhakti, focusing on the worship of God with attributes?

  1. Tulsidas
  2. Kabir
  3. Guru Nanak
  4. Basavanna

Answer: Tulsidas

Explanation: Tulsidas worshipped Rama, a form of God with specific attributes, which is known as Saguna Bhakti.

10. The 'Lavani' folk dance is most prominent in which state?

  1. Maharashtra
  2. Gujarat
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Madhya Pradesh

Answer: Maharashtra

Explanation: Lavani is a traditional dance form of Maharashtra performed to the beats of the Dholki.

11. Which of the following is a characteristic of 'Nirguna' Bhakti?

  1. Worship of an abstract, formless God
  2. Use of idols for worship
  3. Focus on temple rituals
  4. Emphasis on royal patronage

Answer: Worship of an abstract, formless God

Explanation: Nirguna Bhakti believes in a formless God who cannot be represented by an image or idol.

12. The 'Pandavani' is a folk singing style that narrates stories from the Mahabharata in which state?

  1. Chhattisgarh
  2. Bihar
  3. Odisha
  4. Uttar Pradesh

Answer: Chhattisgarh

Explanation: Pandavani is a traditional storytelling art form from Chhattisgarh.

13. Which of the following is a key element of the 'Ghoomar' dance?

  1. Pirouettes by women in flowing skirts
  2. Fast-paced footwork with bells
  3. Use of hand puppets
  4. Silent mime acting

Answer: Pirouettes by women in flowing skirts

Explanation: Ghoomar is a Rajasthani folk dance where women perform graceful pirouettes in colorful ghagras.

14. The 'Virashaiva' movement, led by Basavanna, challenged which of the following?

  1. The caste system and ritualism
  2. The use of regional languages
  3. The practice of agriculture
  4. The concept of monotheism

Answer: The caste system and ritualism

Explanation: Basavanna and the Virashaivas campaigned against the caste system and the dominance of rituals.

15. Which of these is a traditional craft associated with the regional culture of Kashmir?

  1. Pashmina shawls
  2. Phulkari embroidery
  3. Madhubani painting
  4. Kanjeevaram silk

Answer: Pashmina shawls

Explanation: Kashmir is world-famous for its high-quality Pashmina wool and shawl weaving.

16. True or False: The Bhakti and Sufi movements encouraged the use of local languages to reach the masses.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Both movements shifted from Sanskrit/Arabic to regional languages to make spirituality accessible to common people.

17. True or False: Classical dances like Bharatanatyam are purely improvised and have no formal rules or texts.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Classical dances are based on strict rules and texts, such as the Natya Shastra.

18. Which of the following factors contributed to the development of regional cultures in India?

  1. Geographical isolation and distinct environments
  2. Trade and interaction with other regions
  3. The patronage of local rulers
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Regional cultures evolved due to a combination of geographical factors, interactions with other societies, and support from local rulers.

19. The Chera Kingdom was located in which part of ancient India?

  1. Northern India
  2. Eastern India
  3. Southern India
  4. Western India

Answer: Southern India

Explanation: The Chera Kingdom was a prominent kingdom in the southern part of ancient India, particularly in the region of present-day Kerala.

20. Malayalam is a Dravidian language primarily spoken in which Indian state?

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Karnataka
  3. Andhra Pradesh
  4. Kerala

Answer: Kerala

Explanation: Malayalam is the principal language of the Indian state of Kerala and is part of the Dravidian language family.

21. The development of Malayalam is closely associated with the history of which ancient kingdom?

  1. Chola Kingdom
  2. Pandya Kingdom
  3. Chera Kingdom
  4. Vijayanagara Empire

Answer: Chera Kingdom

Explanation: The Chera Kingdom played a significant role in the formation and development of the Malayalam language.

22. Which of the following is a characteristic of regional cultures?

  1. Uniformity across the country
  2. Unique traditions, customs, and languages
  3. Lack of historical development
  4. Exclusion of external influences

Answer: Unique traditions, customs, and languages

Explanation: Regional cultures are distinguished by their unique sets of traditions, customs, languages, and art forms.

23. The Cheras were known for their control over which important trade route?

  1. The Grand Trunk Road
  2. The Silk Road
  3. The spice routes of the Indian Ocean
  4. The Deccan Plateau routes

Answer: The spice routes of the Indian Ocean

Explanation: The Chera Kingdom was strategically located and controlled important spice routes, facilitating trade with foreign lands.

24. True or False: Regional cultures in India have remained static throughout history.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Regional cultures are dynamic and have continuously evolved due to various social, economic, and political influences.

25. The earliest available literary work in Malayalam is believed to be from which period?

  1. 3rd Century CE
  2. 8th Century CE
  3. 12th Century CE
  4. 15th Century CE

Answer: 8th Century CE

Explanation: Early works in Malayalam, such as the 'Ramacharitam', are generally dated to around the 8th century CE.
