Class7 MCQs with answers

26. The Chera rulers were often in conflict with which other prominent South Indian dynasties?

  1. The Guptas and the Satavahanas
  2. The Cholas and the Pandyas
  3. The Pallavas and the Chalukyas
  4. The Rashtrakutas and the Palas

Answer: The Cholas and the Pandyas

Explanation: The Cheras, Cholas, and Pandyas were the three major dynasties that dominated South India for centuries, often engaging in conflicts.

27. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of the Chera Kingdom?

  1. Prominent in South India
  2. Controlled spice trade
  3. Capital at Pataliputra
  4. Associated with the development of Malayalam

Answer: Capital at Pataliputra

Explanation: Pataliputra was the capital of the Mauryan Empire and later other North Indian empires, not the Chera Kingdom.

28. True or False: The Malayalam script has always been identical to the Tamil script.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: While related, the Malayalam script evolved from the Grantha script and has distinct features from the Tamil script.

29. Which of the following is a significant aspect of regional cultural identity?

  1. Adoption of a single national language
  2. Preservation of unique linguistic heritage
  3. Uniformity in religious practices
  4. Rejection of local art forms

Answer: Preservation of unique linguistic heritage

Explanation: The preservation and evolution of a region's unique linguistic heritage are crucial to its cultural identity.

30. The development of Malayalam language was influenced by Sanskrit due to:

  1. Religious texts and scholarly works
  2. Trade with North India
  3. Political dominance of Sanskrit speakers
  4. All of the above

Answer: Religious texts and scholarly works

Explanation: Sanskrit had a significant influence on Malayalam, particularly through religious texts, scholarly writings, and the adoption of vocabulary.

31. True or False: The evolution of regional cultures is a continuous process of change and adaptation.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Regional cultures are not static; they constantly evolve and adapt to new social, economic, and political conditions.

32. Which of the following statements best describes the relationship between language and regional culture?

  1. Language is independent of regional culture.
  2. Language is a key component and carrier of regional culture.
  3. Regional culture influences language only through borrowed words.
  4. Language has no impact on the development of regional culture.

Answer: Language is a key component and carrier of regional culture.

Explanation: Language is fundamental to regional culture, as it transmits traditions, stories, values, and the unique worldview of a region.

33. Which Indian state is primarily associated with the Malayalam language?

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Kerala
  3. Karnataka
  4. Andhra Pradesh

Answer: Kerala

Explanation: Malayalam is the principal language of the Indian state of Kerala and the union territories of Lakshadweep and Puducherry.

34. The script used to write Malayalam is derived from which ancient Indian script?

  1. Devanagari
  2. Bengali
  3. Brahmi
  4. Gurmukhi

Answer: Brahmi

Explanation: The Malayalam script evolved from the Brahmi script, which is the ancestor of many South Indian scripts.

35. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of the Malayalam script?

  1. Abugida system
  2. Vowel signs attached to consonants
  3. Primarily uses horizontal strokes
  4. Developed from Brahmi

Answer: Primarily uses horizontal strokes

Explanation: Malayalam script, like other Indic scripts, uses an abugida system where vowels are attached to consonants, and it does not primarily use horizontal strokes.

36. The early form of Malayalam was known as:

  1. Tamil
  2. Sanskrit
  3. Manipravalam
  4. Keralabhasha

Answer: Manipravalam

Explanation: Manipravalam was an early literary style that blended Sanskrit and Malayalam, influencing the development of the language.

37. Which classical Dravidian language is considered a major influence on the vocabulary and grammar of Malayalam?

  1. Telugu
  2. Kannada
  3. Tamil
  4. Tulu

Answer: Tamil

Explanation: Tamil, another major Dravidian language, has had a significant historical and linguistic influence on Malayalam.

38. The influence of Sanskrit on Malayalam is evident in its:

  1. Grammatical structure only
  2. Vocabulary and literary style
  3. Phonology only
  4. Script development only

Answer: Vocabulary and literary style

Explanation: Sanskrit contributed extensively to Malayalam's vocabulary, particularly in religious, philosophical, and literary contexts, and also influenced its literary styles.

39. The Malayalam script has undergone changes over centuries, with its modern form largely standardized in the:

  1. 10th century
  2. 15th century
  3. 19th century
  4. 21st century

Answer: 19th century

Explanation: The modern Malayalam script was significantly standardized and reformed during the 19th century, making it more accessible.

40. The term 'Dravidian languages' refers to a language family spoken primarily in:

  1. North India
  2. South India
  3. East India
  4. West India

Answer: South India

Explanation: The Dravidian language family is predominantly spoken in Southern India.

41. Which of the following is a famous early literary work in Malayalam, often associated with the Manipravalam style?

  1. Ramayanam
  2. Mahabharatam
  3. Unnuneeli Sandesham
  4. Bhagavad Gita

Answer: Unnuneeli Sandesham

Explanation: Unnuneeli Sandesham is a well-known example of Manipravalam literature, showcasing the blend of Sanskrit and Malayalam.

42. The development of Malayalam literature was significantly enriched by the translation and adaptation of epics from which language?

  1. Pali
  2. Prakrit
  3. Sanskrit
  4. Persian

Answer: Sanskrit

Explanation: Major Sanskrit epics like the Ramayana and Mahabharata were translated and adapted into Malayalam, greatly influencing its literary tradition.

43. The script used for Malayalam is known as the Malayalam script.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The script used to write the Malayalam language is indeed called the Malayalam script.

44. Malayalam is a language primarily spoken in the state of Tamil Nadu.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Malayalam is primarily spoken in Kerala, not Tamil Nadu.

45. The evolution of Malayalam was solely influenced by Sanskrit.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: While Sanskrit was a major influence, Malayalam's evolution was also shaped by other Dravidian languages and local traditions.

46. The Malayalam script is an alphabet where each letter represents a distinct sound.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Malayalam script is an abugida, not a pure alphabet, meaning consonants have an inherent vowel sound.

47. Which of the following is a characteristic of the Malayalam script?

  1. It is written from right to left.
  2. It has unique vowel diacritics.
  3. It lacks any connection to Brahmi.
  4. It is an alphabet with independent vowels and consonants.

Answer: It has unique vowel diacritics.

Explanation: Malayalam script features unique vowel diacritics that are attached to consonants, and it is derived from Brahmi.

48. In which Indian state is the Jagannath Temple located?

  1. West Bengal
  2. Odisha
  3. Andhra Pradesh
  4. Tamil Nadu

Answer: Odisha

Explanation: The Jagannath Temple is located in the coastal town of Puri in Odisha.

49. Which of the following deities is NOT part of the primary triad worshipped at the Puri temple?

  1. Jagannath
  2. Balabhadra
  3. Subhadra
  4. Ganesha

Answer: Ganesha

Explanation: The temple is dedicated to Lord Jagannath, his brother Balabhadra, and sister Subhadra.

50. The famous annual festival where the deities travel to the Gundicha Temple is known as:

  1. Ratha Yatra
  2. Kumbh Mela
  3. Diwali
  4. Holi

Answer: Ratha Yatra

Explanation: The Ratha Yatra is the chariot festival where the deities visit their aunt's temple.
