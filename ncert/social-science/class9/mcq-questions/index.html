Class9 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class9 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 287 questions.

26. True or False: Population density is calculated by dividing the total population by the total area.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Population density is indeed the measure of population per unit area.

27. True or False: All regions in India have the same population density.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: India exhibits significant variations in population density across its diverse geographical regions.

28. True or False: Economic factors, such as industrial development, do not influence population distribution.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Economic opportunities, like jobs in industries, are major pull factors that attract people and influence population distribution.

29. True or False: Population is a static entity and does not change over time.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Population is dynamic and constantly changes due to births, deaths, and migration.

30. Which of the following is a factor that contributes to the high population density in certain parts of India?

  1. Lack of water resources
  2. Rugged mountainous terrain
  3. Availability of fertile land and water
  4. Extreme climatic conditions

Answer: Availability of fertile land and water

Explanation: Fertile land for agriculture and ample water resources are key factors that support large populations and lead to high population density.

31. Which state in India has the highest population density according to the 2011 Census?

  1. West Bengal
  2. Bihar
  3. Uttar Pradesh
  4. Kerala

Answer: Bihar

Explanation: Bihar is the most densely populated state in India due to its fertile plains and agricultural productivity.

32. Which of the following factors most significantly influences the high population density in the Northern Plains?

  1. Rugged terrain
  2. Availability of water and fertile soil
  3. Extreme cold climate
  4. Lack of transport facilities

Answer: Availability of water and fertile soil

Explanation: The fertile alluvial soil and abundance of water from the Himalayan rivers make the Northern Plains ideal for settlement.

33. Which region in India is characterized by very low population density?

  1. The Indo-Gangetic Plains
  2. The Coastal Plains
  3. The Himalayan Mountains
  4. The Deccan Plateau

Answer: The Himalayan Mountains

Explanation: Harsh climatic conditions and rugged mountainous terrain make the Himalayas sparsely populated.

34. What is the term used to describe the number of persons per unit area of land?

  1. Population Growth
  2. Population Density
  3. Population Distribution
  4. Demography

Answer: Population Density

Explanation: Population density is the measurement of population per square kilometer.

35. Which of these is a social factor affecting the distribution of population?

  1. Climate
  2. Availability of minerals
  3. Religious or cultural significance
  4. Soil fertility

Answer: Religious or cultural significance

Explanation: Cities like Varanasi or Amritsar attract people due to their religious and cultural importance.

36. Which state has one of the lowest population densities in India?

  1. Arunachal Pradesh
  2. Bihar
  3. West Bengal
  4. Kerala

Answer: Arunachal Pradesh

Explanation: Arunachal Pradesh has a very low density due to its mountainous terrain and dense forests.

37. Which of the following is an economic factor influencing population distribution?

  1. Rainfall
  2. Industrialization
  3. Altitude
  4. Forest cover

Answer: Industrialization

Explanation: Industrial hubs provide employment opportunities, attracting a larger population to urban areas.

38. The population of India is unevenly distributed primarily because of:

  1. Uniform climate across the country
  2. Variations in physical and socio-economic factors
  3. Equal distribution of minerals
  4. Lack of urban centers

Answer: Variations in physical and socio-economic factors

Explanation: Differences in terrain, climate, and economic opportunities lead to uneven population distribution.

39. Which of the following is a pull factor for migration to cities?

  1. Lack of healthcare
  2. Better job opportunities
  3. Natural disasters
  4. Poor soil quality

Answer: Better job opportunities

Explanation: Better employment and higher wages act as pull factors that attract people to urban centers.

40. Which of these areas is likely to have a higher population density?

  1. Desert regions
  2. High altitude plateaus
  3. River valleys
  4. Dense forest areas

Answer: River valleys

Explanation: River valleys provide water and fertile land, supporting larger populations.

41. Which of the following is a push factor for migration from rural areas?

  1. Quality education
  2. Lack of employment
  3. Modern healthcare
  4. Entertainment facilities

Answer: Lack of employment

Explanation: Lack of jobs in villages pushes people to migrate to cities in search of work.

42. Which state is known for having a high population density due to its coastal location and trade?

  1. Rajasthan
  2. Kerala
  3. Madhya Pradesh
  4. Chhattisgarh

Answer: Kerala

Explanation: Kerala's coastal geography and trade history have contributed to its high population density.

43. The Thar Desert has a low population density mainly due to:

  1. Excessive rainfall
  2. Lack of water and extreme temperatures
  3. High industrialization
  4. Lack of land

Answer: Lack of water and extreme temperatures

Explanation: Arid conditions and scarcity of water make the desert region difficult for large-scale settlement.

44. Which of the following is a physical factor affecting population distribution?

  1. Political stability
  2. Topography
  3. Market availability
  4. Educational facilities

Answer: Topography

Explanation: Topography refers to the physical features of the land, which determines habitability.

45. What happens to population density in areas with extreme cold climates?

  1. It increases
  2. It remains constant
  3. It decreases
  4. It becomes zero

Answer: It decreases

Explanation: Extreme cold makes living conditions difficult, leading to lower population densities.

46. The Northern Plains of India are the most densely populated region of the country.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Northern Plains are the most densely populated due to fertile soil and water availability.

47. Mountainous regions generally have a higher population density than plains.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Plains are easier to build on and farm, making them more densely populated than mountains.

48. What is the official process of collecting and compiling information about the population of a country at a specific time called?

  1. Census
  2. Survey
  3. Sampling
  4. Demography

Answer: Census

Explanation: A census is a complete enumeration of a population, providing comprehensive data.

49. Which of the following is NOT a primary objective of conducting a census?

  1. To determine the total population size
  2. To collect data on age, sex, and literacy
  3. To forecast the weather for the next decade
  4. To understand the distribution of population

Answer: To forecast the weather for the next decade

Explanation: Weather forecasting is unrelated to the objectives of a population census.

50. In India, the census is typically conducted every:

  1. 5 years
  2. 10 years
  3. 7 years
  4. 20 years

Answer: 10 years

Explanation: The Indian census is a decennial event, meaning it is conducted every ten years.
