Class9 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class9 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 287 questions.

1. Which of the following is a key factor in determining the size and composition of a population?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Average rainfall
  3. Geographical location
  4. Cultural traditions

Answer: Birth rate

Explanation: Birth rate directly influences the increase in population. While other factors can indirectly affect population, birth rate is a primary determinant of its size.

2. A high birth rate and a low death rate generally lead to:

  1. Population decline
  2. Population growth
  3. Zero population growth
  4. Population stabilization

Answer: Population growth

Explanation: When more people are born than die, the population naturally increases. This is the fundamental principle of population growth.

3. Which demographic characteristic refers to the number of deaths per thousand people in a year?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Fertility rate
  3. Mortality rate
  4. Migration rate

Answer: Mortality rate

Explanation: Mortality rate, also known as death rate, is the measure of deaths in a population over a specific period, usually expressed per thousand.

4. The movement of people from one place to another is called:

  1. Urbanization
  2. Industrialization
  3. Migration
  4. Globalization

Answer: Migration

Explanation: Migration is the act of moving from one country, region, or place to another. It can be internal or international.

5. A population pyramid that is broad at the base and tapers towards the top indicates:

  1. A declining population
  2. A stable population
  3. A growing population
  4. An aging population

Answer: A growing population

Explanation: A broad base signifies a large proportion of young people, indicating a high birth rate and a growing population.

6. The age structure of a population significantly impacts its:

  1. Geographical features
  2. Economic needs and social services
  3. Climate patterns
  4. Natural resources

Answer: Economic needs and social services

Explanation: The age distribution of a population determines the demand for education, healthcare, employment, and retirement benefits.

7. Population density is calculated by dividing the total population by the:

  1. Average income
  2. Total land area
  3. Number of cities
  4. Literacy rate

Answer: Total land area

Explanation: Population density measures how crowded a place is, and it is determined by the number of people living in a unit of area.

8. A large working-age population can contribute to economic growth by providing a larger:

  1. Consumer base
  2. Labor force
  3. Unemployment rate
  4. Government debt

Answer: Labor force

Explanation: A larger workforce means more people are available to produce goods and services, which is a key driver of economic activity.

9. True or False: A country with a high dependency ratio often has a larger proportion of elderly people.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: A high dependency ratio means there are more dependents (children and elderly) relative to the working-age population. A larger proportion of elderly people contributes to this.

10. True or False: Population distribution refers to the total number of people living in a region.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Population distribution describes how people are spread across a geographical area, not the total number of people.

11. True or False: Urbanization is the process of people moving from rural areas to cities.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Urbanization is characterized by the increasing proportion of a population living in urban areas, often due to migration from rural settings.

12. How does a large population influence the demand for public services like healthcare and education?

  1. It decreases the demand.
  2. It increases the demand.
  3. It has no impact on the demand.
  4. It stabilizes the demand.

Answer: It increases the demand.

Explanation: A larger population requires more healthcare facilities, doctors, schools, and teachers to cater to the needs of more people.

13. In social studies, understanding population dynamics is crucial for:

  1. Predicting weather patterns
  2. Analyzing social and economic trends
  3. Studying geological formations
  4. Understanding astronomical events

Answer: Analyzing social and economic trends

Explanation: Population data helps in understanding societal changes, economic development, resource allocation, and policy-making.

14. The study of population characteristics and their changes is known as:

  1. Geology
  2. Sociology
  3. Demography
  4. Anthropology

Answer: Demography

Explanation: Demography is the scientific study of human populations, including their size, structure, distribution, and changes.

15. True or False: The age structure of a population can influence the types of political issues that are prominent in a society.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: For example, a young population might prioritize issues related to education and youth employment, while an aging population might focus on healthcare and pensions.

16. Which of the following is a significant role of population in social studies?

  1. It determines the availability of natural resources.
  2. It is a key factor in economic development and social change.
  3. It dictates the climate of a region.
  4. It influences the geological formations of a country.

Answer: It is a key factor in economic development and social change.

Explanation: Population is a fundamental element in social studies as it drives economic activities, shapes societies, and influences social structures and changes.

17. What does 'population distribution' refer to?

  1. The total number of people in a region.
  2. The rate at which a population grows.
  3. The pattern of how people are spread across a geographical area.
  4. The movement of people from one place to another.

Answer: The pattern of how people are spread across a geographical area.

Explanation: Population distribution describes the spatial arrangement of people within a given area, indicating where people live and in what densities.

18. Which Indian state has the highest population density?

  1. Uttar Pradesh
  2. West Bengal
  3. Bihar
  4. Delhi

Answer: Delhi

Explanation: Delhi, being a Union Territory and a major metropolitan area, has the highest population density among Indian states and UTs.

19. The uneven distribution of population in India is primarily influenced by:

  1. Religious beliefs and practices.
  2. Physical, economic, and socio-cultural factors.
  3. The number of historical monuments.
  4. The popularity of certain sports.

Answer: Physical, economic, and socio-cultural factors.

Explanation: Physical factors like terrain and climate, economic opportunities, and socio-cultural preferences significantly impact where people choose to settle.

20. Which of the following is NOT a factor influencing population distribution?

  1. Topography
  2. Urbanization
  3. Literacy Rate
  4. Climate

Answer: Literacy Rate

Explanation: While literacy rate is an important demographic indicator, it is a consequence of population characteristics rather than a primary driver of its distribution.

21. The Northern Plains of India are densely populated due to:

  1. Extreme cold climate and mountainous terrain.
  2. Fertile soil, flat land, and abundant water resources.
  3. Scarcity of water and arid conditions.
  4. Limited agricultural opportunities.

Answer: Fertile soil, flat land, and abundant water resources.

Explanation: The fertile alluvial soil, availability of water from rivers, and the flat terrain make the Northern Plains ideal for agriculture and human settlement.

22. Which of the following is a characteristic of a sparsely populated region in India?

  1. High industrial development and job opportunities.
  2. Flat terrain and fertile river valleys.
  3. Difficult terrain, extreme climate, and low economic opportunities.
  4. High rainfall and abundant water resources.

Answer: Difficult terrain, extreme climate, and low economic opportunities.

Explanation: Regions with challenging physical conditions like mountains or deserts, coupled with limited economic prospects, tend to have lower population densities.

23. The term 'population size' refers to:

  1. The average age of people in a country.
  2. The total number of individuals in a specific geographical area at a given time.
  3. The difference between birth rates and death rates.
  4. The rate of migration within a country.

Answer: The total number of individuals in a specific geographical area at a given time.

Explanation: Population size is a measure of the total number of people residing in a particular region or country.

24. India's population is characterized by its ______.

  1. Uniform distribution across all regions.
  2. Significant variations in density across different states and regions.
  3. Declining population over the past few decades.
  4. Low birth rates and high death rates.

Answer: Significant variations in density across different states and regions.

Explanation: India's population is unevenly distributed, with some areas being highly dense and others sparsely populated.

25. Which of the following is a socio-cultural factor influencing population distribution?

  1. Availability of mineral resources.
  2. Proximity to ports.
  3. Religious and cultural significance of a place.
  4. Presence of large river systems.

Answer: Religious and cultural significance of a place.

Explanation: Places with religious or cultural importance often attract people, influencing settlement patterns.
