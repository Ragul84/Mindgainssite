Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 321 questions.

26. Which of the following is a community-owned resource?

  1. Private house
  2. Village grazing grounds
  3. National highways
  4. Mining sites owned by a company

Answer: Village grazing grounds

Explanation: Community resources are accessible to all members of a specific community.

27. The process of using resources carefully to avoid depletion is called:

  1. Over-exploitation
  2. Conservation
  3. Industrialization
  4. Urbanization

Answer: Conservation

Explanation: Conservation involves the planned and judicious use of resources to prevent waste and depletion.

28. Which of the following is an abiotic resource?

  1. Human beings
  2. Livestock
  3. Iron ore
  4. Forests

Answer: Iron ore

Explanation: Abiotic resources are composed of non-living things, such as minerals and rocks.

29. Agenda 21 is a declaration signed at which summit?

  1. Kyoto Protocol
  2. Earth Summit 1992
  3. Paris Agreement
  4. Montreal Protocol

Answer: Earth Summit 1992

Explanation: Agenda 21 was adopted at the 1992 Earth Summit to achieve global sustainable development.

30. Which of the following is an example of a national resource?

  1. A private farm
  2. Roads and canals
  3. Air
  4. The deep seabed beyond 200 nautical miles

Answer: Roads and canals

Explanation: National resources are those within the political boundaries and territorial waters of a nation.

31. What is the primary cause of land degradation due to mining?

  1. Afforestation
  2. Leaving deep scars and debris
  3. Crop rotation
  4. Organic farming

Answer: Leaving deep scars and debris

Explanation: Mining leaves deep scars and creates waste dumps, leading to land degradation.

32. Resources that are a subset of the stock, which can be put into use with existing technology, are called:

  1. Potential resources
  2. Reserves
  3. Ubiquitous resources
  4. Biotic resources

Answer: Reserves

Explanation: Reserves are the part of a stock that can be utilized with current technology but are kept for future needs.

33. The use of organic manure and compost helps in:

  1. Increasing soil salinity
  2. Reducing soil fertility
  3. Maintaining soil health
  4. Increasing chemical runoff

Answer: Maintaining soil health

Explanation: Organic manure improves soil structure and adds essential nutrients naturally.

34. Sustainable development focuses on the needs of the present without compromising the needs of future generations.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: This is the standard definition of sustainable development.

35. Solar energy is a non-renewable resource.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Solar energy is renewable as it is derived from the sun and is virtually inexhaustible.

36. Sustainable development refers to development that:

  1. Focuses only on economic growth
  2. Meets present needs without compromising future generations
  3. Prioritizes industrialization over environment
  4. Uses all available resources quickly

Answer: Meets present needs without compromising future generations

Explanation: Sustainability ensures that resources remain available for future generations while meeting current needs.

37. The Rio de Janeiro Earth Summit 1992 was primarily focused on:

  1. Global trade laws
  2. Environmental protection and socio-economic development
  3. Nuclear disarmament
  4. Space exploration

Answer: Environmental protection and socio-economic development

Explanation: The Earth Summit addressed urgent problems of environmental protection and sustainable development.

38. Which state in India has the highest potential for wind energy?

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Bihar
  3. Punjab
  4. Uttar Pradesh

Answer: Tamil Nadu

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has significant coastal wind potential, making it a leader in wind energy production.

39. What is the primary cause of land degradation in Punjab and Haryana?

  1. Deforestation
  2. Over-irrigation
  3. Mining
  4. Overgrazing

Answer: Over-irrigation

Explanation: Over-irrigation leads to waterlogging and increased salinity in the soil.

40. Which of the following is a method of soil conservation in hilly areas?

  1. Strip cropping
  2. Terrace farming
  3. Overgrazing
  4. Monoculture

Answer: Terrace farming

Explanation: Terrace farming reduces the speed of water runoff and prevents soil erosion on slopes.

41. Resource planning is essential because:

  1. Resources are evenly distributed
  2. Resources are limited and unevenly distributed
  3. Resources are infinite
  4. Planning increases resource depletion

Answer: Resources are limited and unevenly distributed

Explanation: Uneven distribution makes planning necessary to ensure equitable use and avoid wastage.

42. The 'Agenda 21' was adopted at which international conference?

  1. Kyoto Protocol
  2. Montreal Protocol
  3. Earth Summit 1992
  4. Paris Agreement

Answer: Earth Summit 1992

Explanation: Agenda 21 is a non-binding action plan of the UN with regard to sustainable development adopted at the 1992 Earth Summit.

43. Which type of soil is most suitable for growing cotton?

  1. Alluvial soil
  2. Red soil
  3. Black soil
  4. Laterite soil

Answer: Black soil

Explanation: Black soil, also known as regur soil, is ideal for cotton cultivation due to its moisture-holding capacity.

44. What is the main reason for the depletion of groundwater in many Indian cities?

  1. Rainwater harvesting
  2. Over-extraction for agriculture and industry
  3. Planting more trees
  4. Reduced pollution

Answer: Over-extraction for agriculture and industry

Explanation: Excessive pumping of water for irrigation and urban use depletes the water table.

45. Which of the following is a method to prevent gully erosion?

  1. Deforestation
  2. Overgrazing
  3. Construction of check dams
  4. Burning of crop residue

Answer: Construction of check dams

Explanation: Check dams slow down the flow of water, preventing the formation and deepening of gullies.

46. Which of these is a potential resource?

  1. Coal in Jharkhand
  2. Wind energy in Rajasthan
  3. Iron ore in Odisha
  4. Petroleum in Assam

Answer: Wind energy in Rajasthan

Explanation: Potential resources are those found in a region but not yet fully utilized.

47. What is the primary goal of the '3Rs' in waste management?

  1. Read, Write, Repeat
  2. Reduce, Reuse, Recycle
  3. Recover, Refine, Replace
  4. Remove, Retain, Restore

Answer: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Explanation: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle aim to minimize waste and conserve resources.

48. Overgrazing is a major cause of land degradation in Gujarat and Rajasthan.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Overgrazing by livestock removes vegetation cover, leading to soil erosion in these arid regions.

49. All renewable resources are completely pollution-free.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: While cleaner, some renewable energy setups (like large dams) can cause environmental displacement or habitat loss.

50. Which of the following best defines sustainable development?

  1. Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
  2. Rapid industrialization to achieve economic growth at any cost.
  3. Exploitation of natural resources for immediate economic benefits.
  4. Focusing solely on environmental protection without considering economic progress.

Answer: Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

Explanation: Sustainable development balances economic growth, social equity, and environmental protection for present and future generations.
