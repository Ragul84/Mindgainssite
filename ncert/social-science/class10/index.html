Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 321 questions.

1. Which of the following is an example of a biotic resource?

  1. Coal
  2. Iron ore
  3. Forests
  4. Water

Answer: Forests

Explanation: Biotic resources are derived from living organisms. Forests are composed of trees and other living plants.

2. Resources that are found in a region and are expected to be used in the future are called:

  1. Actual resources
  2. Potential resources
  3. Stock resources
  4. Reserve resources

Answer: Potential resources

Explanation: Potential resources are those that exist in a region and could be utilized in the future, but their development has not yet begun.

3. Which of the following is a non-renewable resource?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Hydroelectric power

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that take millions of years to form and are consumed much faster than they can be replenished.

4. The process of transforming available resources into usable forms is known as:

  1. Conservation
  2. Development
  3. Utilization
  4. Exploitation

Answer: Utilization

Explanation: Utilization refers to the act of using resources for a specific purpose or benefit.

5. Which type of resource is replenished by natural processes at a rate comparable to their consumption?

  1. Non-renewable
  2. Renewable
  3. Potential
  4. Stock

Answer: Renewable

Explanation: Renewable resources can be replenished naturally over a relatively short period.
