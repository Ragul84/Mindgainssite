Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 321 questions.

1. Which of the following is an example of a biotic resource?

  1. Coal
  2. Iron ore
  3. Forests
  4. Water

Answer: Forests

Explanation: Biotic resources are derived from living organisms. Forests are composed of trees and other living plants.

2. Resources that are found in a region and are expected to be used in the future are called:

  1. Actual resources
  2. Potential resources
  3. Stock resources
  4. Reserve resources

Answer: Potential resources

Explanation: Potential resources are those that exist in a region and could be utilized in the future, but their development has not yet begun.

3. Which of the following is a non-renewable resource?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Hydroelectric power

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that take millions of years to form and are consumed much faster than they can be replenished.

4. The process of transforming available resources into usable forms is known as:

  1. Conservation
  2. Development
  3. Utilization
  4. Exploitation

Answer: Utilization

Explanation: Utilization refers to the act of using resources for a specific purpose or benefit.

5. Which type of resource is replenished by natural processes at a rate comparable to their consumption?

  1. Non-renewable
  2. Renewable
  3. Potential
  4. Stock

Answer: Renewable

Explanation: Renewable resources can be replenished naturally over a relatively short period.

6. The uneven distribution of resources across the globe is primarily due to differences in:

  1. Population density
  2. Technological advancement
  3. Geographical factors
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Geographical factors like climate and terrain, along with population distribution and technological capabilities, influence resource availability and distribution.

7. Over-utilization of resources can lead to:

  1. Sustainable development
  2. Environmental degradation
  3. Resource regeneration
  4. Economic prosperity

Answer: Environmental degradation

Explanation: Excessive use of resources depletes them and can cause significant damage to the environment.

8. Which of the following is a man-made resource?

  1. Rivers
  2. Minerals
  3. Buildings
  4. Air

Answer: Buildings

Explanation: Buildings are constructed by humans and are therefore man-made resources.

9. The stock of resources that are technically feasible to use but their use has not started is called:

  1. Potential resources
  2. Actual resources
  3. Reserve resources
  4. Stock resources

Answer: Reserve resources

Explanation: Reserve resources are a subset of potential resources that can be brought into use with the help of existing technology.

10. Sustainable development aims to meet the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: This is the fundamental definition of sustainable development, emphasizing balance between present and future needs.

11. Which of the following is a renewable resource?

  1. Petroleum
  2. Natural gas
  3. Sunlight
  4. Coal

Answer: Sunlight

Explanation: Sunlight is a continuously available and inexhaustible source of energy.

12. The conservation of resources is essential for:

  1. Rapid industrialization
  2. Economic growth at any cost
  3. Long-term survival and well-being
  4. Maximizing immediate profits

Answer: Long-term survival and well-being

Explanation: Resource conservation ensures that resources are available for future generations and maintains ecological balance.

13. Resources that are found everywhere are called ubiquitous resources.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Ubiquitous resources are those that are found in abundance everywhere, such as air and sunlight.

14. The development of resources should be balanced with:

  1. Population growth
  2. Technological advancement
  3. Environmental protection
  4. Economic liberalization

Answer: Environmental protection

Explanation: Sustainable resource development requires careful consideration of environmental impacts to ensure long-term viability.

15. Which of the following is an example of a potential resource that is currently being utilized?

  1. Wind energy in Rajasthan
  2. Coal reserves in Jharkhand
  3. Petroleum reserves in the Arabian Sea
  4. Uranium deposits in India

Answer: Wind energy in Rajasthan

Explanation: While wind energy has potential, its utilization in regions like Rajasthan is an example of a potential resource being actively used.

16. The study of resources and their utilization is crucial for understanding economic development and environmental sustainability.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Understanding how resources are used and managed is fundamental to both economic planning and ensuring a healthy environment.

17. Which of the following is an example of a non-renewable resource?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Water

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Coal is a fossil fuel that takes millions of years to form and cannot be replaced quickly once consumed.

18. Resources which are found everywhere are known as:

  1. Localized resources
  2. Ubiquitous resources
  3. Biotic resources
  4. Potential resources

Answer: Ubiquitous resources

Explanation: Ubiquitous resources, such as air, are found everywhere on Earth.

19. What is the main objective of sustainable development?

  1. Rapid industrialization
  2. Maximum exploitation of resources
  3. Balancing the need to develop with the need to conserve
  4. Stopping all resource use

Answer: Balancing the need to develop with the need to conserve

Explanation: Sustainable development ensures that development meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs.

20. Which of the following is a biotic resource?

  1. Rocks
  2. Metals
  3. Flora
  4. Minerals

Answer: Flora

Explanation: Biotic resources are derived from living organisms, such as plants (flora) and animals (fauna).

21. Resources that are found in a region but have not been utilized yet are called:

  1. Developed resources
  2. Stock
  3. Potential resources
  4. Reserve

Answer: Potential resources

Explanation: Potential resources exist in a region but are not currently being used due to lack of technology or infrastructure.

22. The Rio de Janeiro Earth Summit of 1992 was primarily focused on:

  1. Nuclear disarmament
  2. Global environmental protection and socio-economic development
  3. Trade agreements
  4. Space exploration

Answer: Global environmental protection and socio-economic development

Explanation: The Earth Summit addressed urgent problems of environmental protection and sustainable development.

23. Which of the following is an example of an individual resource?

  1. Public parks
  2. Buried treasure in a person's backyard
  3. Oceanic minerals
  4. Forests

Answer: Buried treasure in a person's backyard

Explanation: Individual resources are owned privately by individuals.

24. Which of these is a method of soil conservation in hilly areas?

  1. Overgrazing
  2. Deforestation
  3. Terrace farming
  4. Monoculture

Answer: Terrace farming

Explanation: Terrace farming reduces soil erosion by creating steps on slopes to slow down water runoff.

25. Resources that can be renewed or reproduced are called:

  1. Non-renewable resources
  2. Renewable resources
  3. Abiotic resources
  4. Exhaustible resources

Answer: Renewable resources

Explanation: Renewable resources can be replenished naturally over a short period of time.
