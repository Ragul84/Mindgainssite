Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the NCERT Social Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 291 questions.

26. What was the main problem faced by planters in Nij cultivation?

  1. Lack of seeds
  2. Difficulty in procuring large compact blocks of land
  3. Lack of water
  4. Low demand for indigo

Answer: Difficulty in procuring large compact blocks of land

Explanation: Planters found it difficult to acquire large, contiguous pieces of land for Nij cultivation.

27. The 'Blue Rebellion' of 1859 was a revolt by which group of people?

  1. Zamindars
  2. Sepoys
  3. Indigo farmers
  4. Tribal leaders

Answer: Indigo farmers

Explanation: The Blue Rebellion was a massive uprising by indigo farmers against the oppressive system of indigo cultivation.

28. Under the Mahalwari system, the unit of revenue collection was the:

  1. Individual farmer
  2. Village or Mahal
  3. Zamindar's estate
  4. District

Answer: Village or Mahal

Explanation: The village or 'Mahal' was the unit of revenue settlement in the Mahalwari system.

29. Why did the Permanent Settlement often lead to the eviction of peasants?

  1. Peasants refused to farm
  2. High revenue demands by Zamindars
  3. Lack of seeds
  4. Government banned farming

Answer: High revenue demands by Zamindars

Explanation: Zamindars increased rents to meet the fixed revenue demands of the Company, leading to peasant distress.

30. Which plant was used to produce a blue dye that was more vibrant than woad?

  1. Cotton
  2. Indigo
  3. Opium
  4. Tea

Answer: Indigo

Explanation: Indigo produced a rich blue color, whereas woad produced a pale and dull blue.

31. The East India Company's role changed from a trading company to a territorial colonial power after the:

  1. Battle of Plassey
  2. Battle of Buxar
  3. Grant of Diwani
  4. Revolt of 1857

Answer: Grant of Diwani

Explanation: The Grant of Diwani made the Company the chief financial administrator of Bengal.

32. The Indigo Commission was set up to investigate the grievances of the farmers after the:

  1. Battle of Buxar
  2. Blue Rebellion
  3. Permanent Settlement
  4. Ryotwari System

Answer: Blue Rebellion

Explanation: The Indigo Commission was appointed following the Blue Rebellion to look into the system of indigo production.

33. The Ryotwari system was introduced because the Company felt there were no traditional landlords in the south.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Munro believed that in the south, there were no traditional zamindars, so settlement should be made with ryots.

34. Under the Permanent Settlement, the revenue amount was revised every five years.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The revenue was fixed permanently and was not revised, which is why it is called 'Permanent Settlement'.

35. Which system of revenue collection was introduced by the East India Company that made peasants responsible for paying revenue directly to the company?

  1. Permanent Settlement
  2. Ryotwari System
  3. Mahalwari System
  4. Village System

Answer: Ryotwari System

Explanation: The Ryotwari System made individual peasants responsible for paying revenue directly to the company, unlike other systems that involved intermediaries.

36. The Permanent Settlement was primarily introduced in which regions of India?

  1. Bengal and Bihar
  2. Madras and Bombay
  3. North-Western Provinces
  4. Punjab

Answer: Bengal and Bihar

Explanation: The Permanent Settlement, introduced by Lord Cornwallis, was first implemented in Bengal and Bihar.

37. Under the Permanent Settlement, who was recognized as the owner of the land and responsible for paying revenue to the company?

  1. The peasant cultivator
  2. The village headman
  3. The Zamindar
  4. The moneylender

Answer: The Zamindar

Explanation: The Zamindars were recognized as landowners and were made responsible for collecting revenue from the peasants and paying it to the company.

38. What was the main objective of the Mahalwari System of revenue settlement?

  1. To collect revenue from individual peasants
  2. To collect revenue from the entire village community
  3. To collect revenue from Zamindars
  4. To collect revenue from moneylenders

Answer: To collect revenue from the entire village community

Explanation: The Mahalwari System involved collecting revenue from the entire village community, which was collectively responsible for the payment.

39. The East India Company's economic policies were primarily aimed at:

  1. Developing Indian industries
  2. Maximizing profit for the company
  3. Improving the living standards of Indian peasants
  4. Promoting free trade for Indian merchants

Answer: Maximizing profit for the company

Explanation: The primary goal of the East India Company's economic policies was to extract resources and maximize profits for the company.

40. Which of the following was a consequence of the East India Company's revenue policies on Indian agriculture?

  1. Increased agricultural productivity
  2. Diversification of crops
  3. Increased burden on peasants and frequent famines
  4. Development of modern farming techniques

Answer: Increased burden on peasants and frequent famines

Explanation: The high revenue demands and rigid collection methods often led to the impoverishment of peasants and contributed to famines.

41. The introduction of new revenue systems by the East India Company led to:

  1. Greater autonomy for village communities
  2. Increased power of traditional village leaders
  3. Disruption of traditional landholding patterns
  4. A decline in the importance of moneylenders

Answer: Disruption of traditional landholding patterns

Explanation: The new revenue systems often disregarded existing land rights and disrupted traditional social and economic structures.

42. The East India Company's policy of de-industrialization in India led to:

  1. Growth of Indian handicraft industries
  2. Increased demand for Indian manufactured goods
  3. Decline of Indian textile industries
  4. Development of new Indian factories

Answer: Decline of Indian textile industries

Explanation: The company promoted the import of British manufactured goods and imposed policies that harmed Indian textile and handicraft industries.

43. True or False: The East India Company's economic policies generally benefited the Indian economy.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The East India Company's policies were designed to benefit Britain, often at the expense of the Indian economy and its people.

44. True or False: The Mahalwari System involved individual land ownership and revenue payment.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Mahalwari System focused on collective responsibility of the village community for revenue payment, not individual ownership.

45. Which of the following was NOT a direct consequence of the East India Company's economic policies in India?

  1. Decline of traditional crafts
  2. Increased agricultural output
  3. Drain of wealth from India
  4. Rise of a new class of landlords

Answer: Increased agricultural output

Explanation: While revenue policies impacted agriculture, the overall economic policies led to a decline in output and increased poverty, not increased agricultural output.

46. The introduction of cash crops like indigo and cotton by the East India Company was primarily to meet the demands of:

  1. Indian consumers
  2. British industries
  3. Local markets
  4. European traders

Answer: British industries

Explanation: The company encouraged the cultivation of cash crops to supply raw materials for British industries, particularly the textile industry.

47. True or False: The East India Company's revenue policies were designed to be flexible and considerate of the peasants' ability to pay.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The revenue demands were often rigid and high, with little consideration for the peasants' economic conditions.

48. Which of the following revenue systems was introduced in the North-Western Provinces (modern Uttar Pradesh)?

  1. Permanent Settlement
  2. Ryotwari System
  3. Mahalwari System
  4. Village System

Answer: Mahalwari System

Explanation: The Mahalwari System was implemented in the North-Western Provinces and parts of Punjab.

49. Which battle in 1757 established the British East India Company's political influence in Bengal?

  1. Battle of Buxar
  2. Battle of Plassey
  3. Battle of Panipat
  4. Battle of Mysore

Answer: Battle of Plassey

Explanation: The Battle of Plassey in 1757 gave the Company a foothold in Bengal after defeating Siraj-ud-Daulah.

50. The 'Diwani' rights granted to the East India Company allowed them to:

  1. Collect land revenue
  2. Command the army
  3. Appoint the Nawab
  4. Build forts in Delhi

Answer: Collect land revenue

Explanation: Diwani rights gave the Company the authority to collect revenues from the provinces of Bengal, Bihar, and Odisha.
