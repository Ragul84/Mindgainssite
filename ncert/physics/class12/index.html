1. A parallel plate capacitor has capacitance C. If the distance between the plates is halved and a dielectric medium of dielectric constant K is introduced between the plates, then the new capacitance becomes:

  1. 2KC
  2. KC/2
  3. C/(2K)
  4. C/(K)

Answer: 2KC

Explanation: The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is given by C = ε₀A/d, where A is the area of the plates and d is the distance between them. When the distance is halved, the new capacitance C' = ε₀A/(d/2) = 2(ε₀A/d) = 2C. When a dielectric medium of dielectric constant K is introduced, the capacitance becomes C'' = KC'. Therefore, the new capacitance is C'' = K(2C) = 2KC.

2. Two point charges +q and -q are placed at a distance 'r' from each other. The electric potential at the midpoint of the line joining the two charges is:

  1. 0
  2. kq/r
  3. 2kq/r
  4. -kq/r

Answer: 0

Explanation: The electric potential due to a point charge Q at a distance r is V = kQ/r. At the midpoint, the distance from each charge is r/2. The potential due to +q is V₁ = k(+q)/(r/2) = 2kq/r. The potential due to -q is V₂ = k(-q)/(r/2) = -2kq/r. The total potential at the midpoint is the algebraic sum of the individual potentials: V_total = V₁ + V₂ = (2kq/r) + (-2kq/r) = 0.

3. A current of 10 A flows through a conductor for 2 minutes. How much charge passes through any cross-section of the conductor during this time?

  1. 1200 C
  2. 20 C
  3. 120 C
  4. 200 C

Answer: 1200 C

Explanation: The relationship between current (I), charge (Q), and time (t) is given by I = Q/t. Therefore, Q = I × t. Given current I = 10 A and time t = 2 minutes. First, convert time to seconds: t = 2 × 60 = 120 seconds. Now, calculate the charge: Q = 10 A × 120 s = 1200 C.

4. Which of the following statements is INCORRECT regarding magnetic field lines?

  1. Magnetic field lines form closed loops.
  2. Magnetic field lines do not intersect each other.
  3. The tangent to a magnetic field line at any point gives the direction of the magnetic field at that point.
  4. Magnetic field lines originate from the south pole and terminate at the north pole.

Answer: Magnetic field lines originate from the south pole and terminate at the north pole.

Explanation: Magnetic field lines originate from the North pole and terminate at the South pole outside the magnet, and inside the magnet, they go from South to North, thus forming continuous closed loops. Options A, B, and C are correct properties of magnetic field lines.

5. A metallic rod of length 'L' is rotated with an angular frequency 'ω' with one end hinged at the centre and the other end at the circumference of a circular metallic ring of radius 'R', about an axis passing through the centre and perpendicular to the plane of the ring. A uniform magnetic field 'B' parallel to the axis is present everywhere. The emf induced between the centre and the metallic ring is:

  1. BωR²/2
  2. BωR
  3. BωR²/4
  4. BωL²/2

Answer: BωR²/2

Explanation: The induced emf in a rotating rod in a uniform magnetic field is given by ε = (1/2)BωR², where R is the length of the rod. In this case, the length of the rod is R (radius of the ring). So, the induced emf is ε = (1/2)BωR².
