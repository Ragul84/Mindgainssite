26. Which of the following types of resources can be replenished by nature over a short period of time?

  1. Renewable Resources
  2. Non-renewable Resources
  3. Individual Resources
  4. Community-owned Resources

Answer: Renewable Resources

Explanation: Renewable resources are those that can be renewed or reproduced by physical, chemical, or mechanical processes. Examples include solar energy, wind energy, water, forests, and wildlife. Non-renewable resources, on the other hand, take millions of years to form and cannot be replenished within a human lifespan.

27. What is the primary purpose of rainwater harvesting in arid and semi-arid regions of India?

  1. To generate hydroelectricity
  2. To increase groundwater recharge
  3. To control floods
  4. To provide water for industrial use

Answer: To increase groundwater recharge

Explanation: In arid and semi-arid regions, rainwater harvesting is crucial for increasing groundwater recharge. By collecting and storing rainwater, it allows water to percolate into the ground, replenishing aquifers and making water available for future use, especially for drinking and irrigation, during dry periods.

28. Which of the following is a 'kharif' crop?

  1. Wheat
  2. Mustard
  3. Rice
  4. Barley

Answer: Rice

Explanation: Kharif crops are sown with the onset of monsoon in different parts of the country and harvested in September-October. Rice (paddy) is a prominent kharif crop. Wheat, mustard, and barley are examples of rabi crops, which are sown in winter and harvested in spring.

29. Which of the following minerals is a non-ferrous metallic mineral?

  1. Iron ore
  2. Manganese
  3. Bauxite
  4. Chromite

Answer: Bauxite

Explanation: Non-ferrous metallic minerals do not contain iron. Bauxite is the ore from which aluminium is extracted, and it does not contain iron. Iron ore, manganese, and chromite are all ferrous minerals, meaning they contain iron.

30. Which of the following industries is known as the 'basic industry' because it provides raw materials to other industries?

  1. Textile industry
  2. Sugar industry
  3. Iron and Steel industry
  4. Cement industry

Answer: Iron and Steel industry

Explanation: The Iron and Steel industry is considered a basic industry because its products (steel) are used as raw materials for a wide range of other industries, including machinery, automobiles, construction, and defence equipment. Without steel, many other industries cannot function.

31. Which mode of transport is most suitable for transporting heavy and bulky goods over long distances within the country?

  1. Airways
  2. Roadways
  3. Railways
  4. Pipelines

Answer: Railways

Explanation: Railways are the most suitable and economical mode of transport for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances within a country. They have a large carrying capacity and are efficient for mass transportation. Airways are expensive, roadways are good for shorter distances and door-to-door service, and pipelines are specific to liquids and gases.

32. Which of the following is a major advantage of international trade?

  1. Increased domestic production only
  2. Limited choice for consumers
  3. Exchange of goods and services between countries
  4. Reduced competition among producers

Answer: Exchange of goods and services between countries

Explanation: International trade primarily involves the exchange of goods and services between two or more countries. This allows countries to specialize in producing what they are most efficient at, leading to a wider variety of goods for consumers, increased competition, and often, economic growth.

33. Which of the following types of resources can be classified as 'Biotic Resources'?

  1. Rocks and Minerals
  2. Flora and Fauna
  3. Water and Air
  4. Solar and Wind Energy

Answer: Flora and Fauna

Explanation: Biotic resources are obtained from the biosphere and have life. Examples include human beings, flora (plants), fauna (animals), fisheries, and livestock. Rocks and minerals are abiotic, water and air are abiotic, and solar and wind energy are renewable but abiotic sources.

34. Which of the following is a major cause of water scarcity in India, even in regions with ample rainfall?

  1. Lack of proper irrigation facilities
  2. Over-exploitation and mismanagement of water resources
  3. Absence of large rivers
  4. High evaporation rates due to desert climate

Answer: Over-exploitation and mismanagement of water resources

Explanation: While other options might contribute to water issues, over-exploitation and mismanagement of water resources, including excessive groundwater pumping for agriculture and industrial use, along with pollution, are the primary reasons for water scarcity in many parts of India, even where rainfall is sufficient. Lack of proper irrigation facilities is a consequence, not the root cause of scarcity in general. India has many large rivers, and high evaporation rates are specific to certain regions, not a nationwide primary cause of scarcity despite ample rainfall.

35. Which of the following is a non-renewable resource?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Forests

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that take millions of years to form and cannot be replenished within a human lifespan. Coal is a fossil fuel formed over geological periods and is therefore a non-renewable resource. Solar energy, wind energy, and forests are examples of renewable resources as they can be replenished naturally or through human management within a relatively short period.

36. What is the largest planet in our solar system?

  1. Earth
  2. Saturn
  3. Jupiter
  4. Uranus

Answer: Jupiter

Explanation: Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant.

37. What is the term for the study of the Earth's physical features?

  1. Geology
  2. Geography
  3. Meteorology
  4. Astronomy

Answer: Geography

Explanation: Geography is the study of the Earth's physical features. It includes the study of land, water, and climate.

38. What is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the leaves, and is then released into the air as water vapor?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant. It is an important part of the water cycle.

39. What is the largest river in India?

  1. Ganga
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Godavari
  4. Krishna

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga is the largest river in India. It is considered sacred by Hindus.

40. What type of climate is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of rainfall throughout the year?

  1. Tropical
  2. Temperate
  3. Desert
  4. Polar

Answer: Tropical

Explanation: Tropical climate is characterized by high temperatures and high levels of rainfall. It is found near the equator.

41. What is the term for the movement of people from rural areas to cities?

  1. Urbanization
  2. Ruralization
  3. Migration
  4. Emigration

Answer: Urbanization

Explanation: Urbanization is the movement of people from rural areas to cities. It is a common phenomenon in developing countries.

42. What is the process by which the Earth's plates move and change, resulting in the creation of mountains and volcanoes?

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Plate Tectonics
  4. Glaciation

Answer: Plate Tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics is the process by which the Earth's plates move and change. It shapes the Earth's surface.

43. What is the term for the area of land that drains water into a particular river or sea?

  1. Watershed
  2. Drainage Basin
  3. Catchment Area
  4. Water Table

Answer: Drainage Basin

Explanation: Drainage basin is the area of land that drains water into a particular river or sea. It is an important concept in geography.

44. What is the type of farming that involves growing crops and raising livestock on a small scale?

  1. Subsistence Farming
  2. Commercial Farming
  3. Organic Farming
  4. Intensive Farming

Answer: Subsistence Farming

Explanation: Subsistence farming is the type of farming that involves growing crops and raising livestock on a small scale. It is practiced by many farmers in developing countries.

45. What is the term for the process by which the Earth's atmosphere becomes warmer due to the increase in greenhouse gases?

  1. Global Warming
  2. Climate Change
  3. Ozone Depletion
  4. Acid Rain

Answer: Global Warming

Explanation: Global warming is the process by which the Earth's atmosphere becomes warmer. It is a major environmental concern.

46. Which of the following rivers is the longest in India?

  1. Ganga
  2. Brahmaputra
  3. Godavari
  4. Narmada

Answer: Ganga

Explanation: The Ganga river is approximately 2525 km long. It is the longest river in India.

47. What is the term for the movement of the Earth's plates?

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Plate Tectonics
  4. Glaciation

Answer: Plate Tectonics

Explanation: Plate tectonics is the theory of the Earth's plates moving. It explains the formation of mountains and volcanoes.

48. Which type of rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Foliated

Answer: Igneous

Explanation: Igneous rocks are formed from the cooling of magma. They can be either intrusive or extrusive.

49. What is the process by which the Earth's surface is worn away?

  1. Erosion
  2. Deposition
  3. Weathering
  4. Plate Tectonics

Answer: Erosion

Explanation: Erosion is the process of wearing away the Earth's surface. It can occur through water, wind, or ice.

50. Which of the following is a type of natural resource?

  1. Water
  2. Soil
  3. Minerals
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Natural resources include water, soil, and minerals. They are essential for human survival.
