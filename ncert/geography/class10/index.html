Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 305 questions.

1. Which of the following types of resources are non-renewable but recyclable?

  1. Biotic resources
  2. Metallic minerals
  3. Flow resources
  4. Non-metallic minerals

Answer: Metallic minerals

Explanation: Metallic minerals are non-renewable because they take millions of years to form, but they are recyclable, meaning they can be reused after processing, unlike fossil fuels which are non-recyclable non-renewable resources.

2. Which of the following is a major cause of the depletion of forest and wildlife resources in India?

  1. Afforestation programs
  2. Expansion of agriculture
  3. Conservation projects
  4. Ecotourism development

Answer: Expansion of agriculture

Explanation: The expansion of agriculture, especially during the colonial period and post-independence, has been a significant factor leading to the clearing of forests and the subsequent depletion of wildlife habitats in India.

3. What is the primary objective of rainwater harvesting in arid and semi-arid regions of India?

  1. To increase industrial water supply
  2. To generate hydroelectricity
  3. To provide drinking water and irrigation
  4. To control floods

Answer: To provide drinking water and irrigation

Explanation: In arid and semi-arid regions, rainwater harvesting is crucial for collecting and storing water for domestic use, especially drinking, and for irrigation purposes, as these areas face severe water scarcity.

4. Which of the following is a characteristic feature of 'shifting cultivation'?

  1. High use of fertilizers and pesticides
  2. Cultivation on permanent plots of land
  3. Short periods of cultivation followed by long fallow periods
  4. Commercial farming for export

Answer: Short periods of cultivation followed by long fallow periods

Explanation: Shifting cultivation, also known as 'slash and burn' agriculture, involves clearing a patch of forest, cultivating it for a few years until soil fertility declines, and then abandoning it for a new patch, allowing the old one to regenerate over a long fallow period.

5. Which of the following minerals is the basic raw material for the cement industry?

  1. Bauxite
  2. Mica
  3. Limestone
  4. Copper

Answer: Limestone

Explanation: Limestone is the primary and most essential raw material used in the manufacturing of cement. It is calcined (heated) with other materials like clay to produce clinker, which is then ground to make cement.

6. Which of the following factors is NOT typically considered an 'agglomeration economy' for the location of industries?

  1. Proximity to markets
  2. Availability of skilled labor
  3. Access to banking and insurance services
  4. Government subsidies for remote areas

Answer: Government subsidies for remote areas

Explanation: Agglomeration economies refer to the benefits that firms obtain by locating near each other, such as shared infrastructure, skilled labor pools, and access to services like banking. Government subsidies for remote areas are incentives to decentralize industries, not a benefit of agglomeration.

7. Which mode of transport is most suitable for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances within a country?

  1. Airways
  2. Roadways
  3. Railways
  4. Pipelines

Answer: Railways

Explanation: Railways are highly efficient and cost-effective for transporting heavy and bulky goods over long distances, especially within a country, due to their large carrying capacity and relatively lower fuel consumption per ton-mile compared to roadways.

8. Which of the following is an example of a 'tertiary activity' in the Indian economy?

  1. Fishing
  2. Manufacturing of textiles
  3. Banking services
  4. Mining of coal

Answer: Banking services

Explanation: Tertiary activities, also known as service sector activities, provide services rather than producing goods. Banking, along with transport, communication, trade, and education, falls under this category. Fishing and mining are primary activities, while manufacturing is a secondary activity.

9. Which of the following is a major cause of depletion of forest and wildlife resources in India?

  1. Afforestation programs
  2. Expansion of agricultural land
  3. Conservation projects
  4. Sustainable development practices

Answer: Expansion of agricultural land

Explanation: The expansion of agricultural land to meet the growing food demands of a large population has led to the clearing of vast forest areas, directly contributing to the depletion of forest and wildlife resources. Afforestation, conservation, and sustainable development are measures to protect, not deplete, these resources.

10. Which of the following is a traditional method of rainwater harvesting in Rajasthan?

  1. Canals
  2. Tube wells
  3. Rooftop rainwater harvesting
  4. Guls and Kuls

Answer: Rooftop rainwater harvesting

Explanation: Rooftop rainwater harvesting is a common traditional practice in Rajasthan, particularly in arid and semi-arid regions, where rainwater is collected on rooftops and stored in underground tanks (tankas) for later use. Canals and tube wells are modern irrigation methods, while Guls and Kuls are diversion channels used in mountainous regions for agriculture.

11. Which type of agriculture is characterized by 'slash and burn' cultivation?

  1. Intensive subsistence farming
  2. Commercial farming
  3. Primitive subsistence farming
  4. Plantation agriculture

Answer: Primitive subsistence farming

Explanation: Primitive subsistence farming, also known as 'slash and burn' or 'jhum' cultivation, is a traditional method where farmers clear a patch of land by felling trees and burning the vegetation. The ashes provide nutrients to the soil, and crops are grown for a few years until soil fertility declines, after which the farmers move to a new patch. Intensive subsistence farming involves high input on small plots, commercial farming is for market sale, and plantation agriculture is a type of commercial farming for a single crop.

12. Which of the following industries is often referred to as the 'basic industry' because it provides raw materials to many other industries?

  1. Textile industry
  2. Sugar industry
  3. Iron and Steel industry
  4. Information Technology industry

Answer: Iron and Steel industry

Explanation: The Iron and Steel industry is considered a basic industry because its products (steel, iron) are used as raw materials for a wide range of other industries, including machinery, automobiles, construction, railways, and defence equipment. Without steel, many other manufacturing sectors would not be able to function. Textile and sugar industries are consumer goods industries, and IT is a service industry.

13. Which mode of transport is most suitable for transporting heavy and bulky goods over long distances within a country?

  1. Airways
  2. Roadways
  3. Railways
  4. Pipelines

Answer: Railways

Explanation: Railways are the most efficient and economical mode of transport for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances within a country. They have a large carrying capacity and are less affected by weather conditions compared to roadways or airways. Airways are for fast, light, and expensive goods, roadways for shorter distances and door-to-door service, and pipelines for liquids and gases.

14. Which of the following is a major advantage of using pipelines as a mode of transport?

  1. Suitable for all types of goods
  2. High initial cost but subsequent running costs are minimal
  3. Flexible routes and easy to construct
  4. Fastest mode of transport for all commodities

Answer: High initial cost but subsequent running costs are minimal

Explanation: Pipelines have a high initial cost for construction, but once laid, their subsequent running costs are minimal. They are efficient for transporting liquids (like crude oil, petroleum products, water) and gases (natural gas) continuously without trans-shipment losses or delays. They are not suitable for all types of goods, have fixed routes, and are not the fastest for all commodities (airways are faster for certain goods).

15. Which of the following types of resources can be renewed or reproduced by physical, chemical, or mechanical processes?

  1. Renewable Resources
  2. Non-renewable Resources
  3. Individual Resources
  4. Community-owned Resources

Answer: Renewable Resources

Explanation: Renewable resources are those that can be renewed or reproduced over a period of time by natural processes or human actions. Examples include solar energy, wind energy, water, forests, and wildlife. Non-renewable resources, on the other hand, take millions of years to form and cannot be replenished once depleted.

16. Which of the following is a key reason for the decline in India's biodiversity?

  1. Afforestation programs
  2. Conservation efforts by the government
  3. Habitat destruction and hunting
  4. Increased awareness about wildlife protection

Answer: Habitat destruction and hunting

Explanation: Habitat destruction, hunting, poaching, over-exploitation of resources, environmental pollution, and forest fires are major factors contributing to the decline in India's biodiversity. Afforestation and conservation efforts aim to protect biodiversity, while increased awareness, though positive, has not fully counteracted the negative impacts of other factors.

17. Which of the following is a traditional method of rainwater harvesting practiced in Rajasthan?

  1. Bamboodrip irrigation
  2. Guls and Kuls
  3. Tankas
  4. Rooftop rainwater harvesting

Answer: Tankas

Explanation: Tankas are underground tanks, typically built in the main house or courtyard, for storing potable rainwater, especially common in Rajasthan. Bamboodrip irrigation is practiced in Meghalaya, and Guls and Kuls are diversion channels used in the Western Himalayas for agriculture. Rooftop rainwater harvesting is a broader term, but Tankas specifically refer to the traditional underground storage in Rajasthan.

18. Which of the following crops is a rabi crop?

  1. Rice
  2. Wheat
  3. Maize
  4. Jowar

Answer: Wheat

Explanation: Rabi crops are sown in winter (October to December) and harvested in summer (April to June). Wheat is a prime example of a rabi crop. Rice, maize, and jowar are examples of kharif crops, which are sown with the onset of monsoon and harvested in September-October.

19. Which mode of transport is most suitable for transporting heavy and bulky goods over long distances within India?

  1. Airways
  2. Roadways
  3. Railways
  4. Pipelines

Answer: Railways

Explanation: Railways are the most efficient and economical mode of transport for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances in India. While roadways are good for shorter distances and door-to-door service, and airways for speed, railways offer the best combination of capacity and cost-effectiveness for bulk cargo. Pipelines are specific to liquids and gases.

20. Which one of the following types of resources is iron ore?

  1. Renewable
  2. Biotic
  3. Flow
  4. Non-renewable

Answer: Non-renewable

Explanation: Iron ore is a non-renewable resource because it takes millions of years to form and cannot be replenished within a human lifespan once consumed. Renewable resources can be replenished, biotic resources are living things, and flow resources are continuous like wind or water.

21. Which type of farming is practiced in areas of high population pressure on land, where all available land is used to grow crops for self-consumption?

  1. Commercial farming
  2. Plantation farming
  3. Intensive subsistence farming
  4. Extensive commercial farming

Answer: Intensive subsistence farming

Explanation: Intensive subsistence farming is characterized by high population density, small landholdings, and the use of simple tools and more labour to produce enough food for the farmer's family. Commercial farming aims for market sale, and plantation farming is a type of commercial farming for a single crop.

22. Which of the following is a non-metallic mineral?

  1. Iron ore
  2. Bauxite
  3. Mica
  4. Copper

Answer: Mica

Explanation: Mica is a non-metallic mineral known for its excellent insulating properties and resistance to high voltage. Iron ore, bauxite (from which aluminium is extracted), and copper are all metallic minerals.

23. Which of the following industries is often referred to as the 'basic industry' because it provides raw materials to numerous other industries?

  1. Textile industry
  2. Sugar industry
  3. Iron and Steel industry
  4. Cement industry

Answer: Iron and Steel industry

Explanation: The Iron and Steel industry is considered the basic industry because its products (steel) are used as raw materials for manufacturing a wide range of other goods, from machinery and tools to construction materials and automobiles. Without steel, many other industries would not be able to function.

24. Which mode of transport is most suitable for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances within the country?

  1. Airways
  2. Roadways
  3. Railways
  4. Pipelines

Answer: Railways

Explanation: Railways are the most suitable and economical mode of transport for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances within a country. Airways are expensive, roadways are good for shorter distances and door-to-door service, and pipelines are specifically for liquids and gases.

25. Which of the following is a major advantage of pipelines as a mode of transport?

  1. Suitable for carrying passengers
  2. High initial cost but subsequent running costs are minimal
  3. Flexible in terms of routes and destinations
  4. Can transport all types of goods

Answer: High initial cost but subsequent running costs are minimal

Explanation: Pipelines have a high initial cost for construction, but their subsequent running costs are minimal, making them an efficient mode for transporting liquids (like crude oil, petroleum products) and gases over long distances. They are not suitable for passengers or all types of goods, and their routes are fixed once laid.
