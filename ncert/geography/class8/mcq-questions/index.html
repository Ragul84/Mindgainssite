26. What is the term for a group of people living in a particular area, sharing common characteristics, and interacting with each other?

  1. Population
  2. Community
  3. Nation
  4. State

Answer: Community

Explanation: A community refers to a group of people living in the same place or having a particular characteristic in common. While 'population' refers to the total number of people, 'community' emphasizes their interaction and shared characteristics within a specific area. 'Nation' and 'state' refer to larger political or geographical entities.

27. Which of the following factors is NOT a major influence on the distribution of population?

  1. Climate
  2. Availability of water
  3. Soil fertility
  4. Type of government

Answer: Type of government

Explanation: The distribution of population is primarily influenced by geographical factors like climate, availability of water, soil fertility, topography, and mineral resources, as well as social, cultural, and economic factors. While the type of government can indirectly influence policies that affect population movement, it is not a direct or primary factor in determining where people choose to settle compared to the fundamental geographical and economic attractions.

28. Which of the following is an example of a non-renewable resource?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Forests

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that have a limited stock and, once exhausted, may take thousands of years to be renewed or replenished. Coal is formed over millions of years from the remains of plants and animals and is therefore a non-renewable resource. Solar energy, wind energy, and forests are examples of renewable resources.

29. Which of the following is a major characteristic of shifting cultivation?

  1. Intensive use of fertilizers and pesticides
  2. Permanent fields and advanced machinery
  3. Clearing a patch of land by felling trees and burning them
  4. Growing a single crop over a large area

Answer: Clearing a patch of land by felling trees and burning them

Explanation: Shifting cultivation, also known as 'slash and burn' agriculture, involves clearing a patch of land by felling trees and burning them. The ashes are then mixed with the soil, and crops are grown. After a few years, the soil loses its fertility, and the cultivators move to a new patch of land. This is a traditional form of agriculture, unlike intensive farming or plantation agriculture.

30. What is the primary purpose of rainwater harvesting?

  1. To increase industrial water supply
  2. To generate hydroelectricity
  3. To conserve water for future use
  4. To prevent soil erosion

Answer: To conserve water for future use

Explanation: Rainwater harvesting is a method of collecting and storing rainwater for future use. Its primary purpose is to conserve water, especially in areas facing water scarcity, by recharging groundwater or storing it in tanks for domestic, agricultural, or industrial purposes. While it can indirectly help in other aspects, water conservation is the main goal.

31. Which of the following is a major threat to wildlife resources?

  1. Establishment of national parks
  2. Afforestation programs
  3. Habitat destruction and poaching
  4. Controlled grazing

Answer: Habitat destruction and poaching

Explanation: Habitat destruction (due to deforestation, urbanization, pollution) and poaching (illegal hunting) are two of the most significant threats to wildlife resources, leading to a decline in species populations and even extinction. National parks and afforestation programs are conservation efforts, and controlled grazing, if managed properly, can be part of sustainable land use.

32. Which of the following factors is NOT a major cause of soil degradation?

  1. Deforestation
  2. Overgrazing
  3. Afforestation
  4. Overuse of chemical fertilisers

Answer: Afforestation

Explanation: Soil degradation refers to the decline in soil quality. Deforestation (cutting down trees), overgrazing (excessive grazing by livestock), and overuse of chemical fertilisers all contribute to soil degradation by removing protective cover, compacting soil, or altering its chemical composition. Afforestation, which is the planting of trees, helps in soil conservation by preventing erosion and improving soil structure, thus it is not a cause of soil degradation.

33. Which type of agriculture is primarily practised to meet the needs of the farmer's family?

  1. Commercial farming
  2. Plantation agriculture
  3. Shifting cultivation
  4. Subsistence farming

Answer: Subsistence farming

Explanation: Subsistence farming is a type of agriculture where the produce is primarily consumed by the farmer and their family, with little or no surplus for sale. Commercial farming involves growing crops for sale in the market. Plantation agriculture is a type of commercial farming where a single crop is grown on a large estate. Shifting cultivation is a form of subsistence agriculture, but the primary characteristic described is meeting family needs, which is the core of subsistence farming.

34. Which of the following is a key factor influencing the location of an industry?

  1. Availability of raw materials
  2. Distance from the sea
  3. Colour of the soil
  4. Number of historical monuments nearby

Answer: Availability of raw materials

Explanation: The availability of raw materials is a crucial factor for the location of an industry, as it directly impacts production costs and efficiency. Industries often prefer to be located close to their raw material sources to reduce transportation costs. While other factors like proximity to markets, labour, capital, power, and water are also important, the availability of raw materials is fundamental. Distance from the sea, soil colour, and historical monuments are generally not primary factors for industrial location.

35. Which of the following is an example of a biotic resource?

  1. Minerals
  2. Water
  3. Forests
  4. Sunlight

Answer: Forests

Explanation: Biotic resources are derived from living organisms. Forests consist of trees and other living organisms, making them a biotic resource. Minerals, water, and sunlight are abiotic resources as they are non-living.
