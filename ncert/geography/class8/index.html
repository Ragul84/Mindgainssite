Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the NCERT Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which of the following is an example of a potential resource?

  1. Petroleum deposits in the Middle East
  2. Uranium in Ladakh
  3. Coal mines in Jharkhand
  4. Water in a river used for irrigation

Answer: Uranium in Ladakh

Explanation: Potential resources are those whose entire quantity may not be known and which are not being used at present but could be used in the future. Uranium in Ladakh is an example because it is known to exist but is not currently being extensively developed or used. Petroleum deposits, coal mines, and river water for irrigation are examples of actual resources, as their quantity is known and they are being used.

2. Which of the following factors is NOT responsible for soil formation?

  1. Parent rock
  2. Climate
  3. Time
  4. Distance from the sea

Answer: Distance from the sea

Explanation: Soil formation is a complex process influenced by several factors. Parent rock determines the chemical composition and texture of the soil. Climate (temperature, rainfall) affects weathering and decomposition. Time allows for the development of soil profiles. Organisms (flora, fauna, microorganisms) and topography (relief) are also crucial. Distance from the sea, while influencing climate, is not a direct factor in the chemical and physical breakdown of parent material into soil.

3. Shifting cultivation is also known as 'Jhum' cultivation in which part of India?

  1. Southern India
  2. Western India
  3. North-Eastern India
  4. Central India

Answer: North-Eastern India

Explanation: Shifting cultivation, a type of primitive subsistence agriculture, is known by various local names. In North-Eastern India, it is commonly referred to as 'Jhum' cultivation. This practice involves clearing a patch of land, cultivating it for a few years, and then abandoning it to allow the soil to regain fertility, moving to a new patch.

4. Which of the following is a primary activity?

  1. Manufacturing steel
  2. Banking services
  3. Fishing
  4. Teaching

Answer: Fishing

Explanation: Primary activities involve the extraction and production of natural resources. Fishing is a primary activity as it involves harvesting resources directly from nature. Manufacturing steel is a secondary activity (processing raw materials). Banking services and teaching are tertiary activities (providing services).

5. Which of these is a major input for the cotton textile industry?

  1. Iron ore
  2. Bauxite
  3. Cotton
  4. Petroleum

Answer: Cotton

Explanation: The cotton textile industry processes raw cotton into fabric and garments. Therefore, cotton is the primary raw material or input for this industry. Iron ore is used in the iron and steel industry, bauxite in the aluminium industry, and petroleum in the petrochemical industry.

6. What is the main reason for the uneven distribution of population in the world?

  1. Availability of advanced technology
  2. Differences in cultural practices
  3. Variations in geographical and economic factors
  4. Political stability of regions

Answer: Variations in geographical and economic factors

Explanation: The uneven distribution of population across the globe is primarily due to variations in geographical factors (like climate, topography, soil, water, minerals) and economic factors (like availability of jobs, industrial development, infrastructure). While technology, culture, and political stability can influence population distribution, geographical and economic factors are the most fundamental and widespread determinants.

7. Which of the following is a non-renewable resource?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Forests

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that have a limited stock. Once the stocks are exhausted, they may take millions of years to be renewed or replenished. Coal is a fossil fuel formed over millions of years and is therefore a non-renewable resource. Solar energy and wind energy are renewable resources, and forests, if managed sustainably, are also considered renewable.

8. What is the term for the number of people living per unit area of land?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Death rate
  3. Population density
  4. Life expectancy

Answer: Population density

Explanation: Population density refers to the number of people living in a specific unit area of land, typically expressed as persons per square kilometre. Birth rate is the number of live births per 1000 people, death rate is the number of deaths per 1000 people, and life expectancy is the average number of years a person is expected to live.

9. Which of the following factors is NOT a major determinant of soil formation?

  1. Parent rock
  2. Climate
  3. Time
  4. Distance from the sea

Answer: Distance from the sea

Explanation: Soil formation is a complex process influenced by several factors. The parent rock determines the chemical composition and texture of the soil. Climate (temperature, rainfall) influences the rate of weathering and humus formation. Time determines the maturity of the soil profile. Relief (altitude, slope) and flora, fauna, and microorganisms also play crucial roles. The distance from the sea, while influencing climate, is not a direct, primary determinant of soil formation itself in the same way as the other factors.

10. Which of the following is a major input for the cotton textile industry?

  1. Iron ore
  2. Bauxite
  3. Cotton
  4. Petroleum

Answer: Cotton

Explanation: The cotton textile industry processes raw cotton into fabric and garments. Therefore, cotton is the primary raw material or input for this industry. Iron ore is used in the iron and steel industry, bauxite in the aluminium industry, and petroleum in the petrochemical industry.

11. What is the term for the average number of people living per unit area, usually per square kilometre?

  1. Population growth rate
  2. Population density
  3. Birth rate
  4. Death rate

Answer: Population density

Explanation: Population density refers to the number of people living in a unit area of the earth's surface, typically expressed as persons per square kilometre. It helps us understand how crowded or sparsely populated a region is. Population growth rate, birth rate, and death rate are measures related to population change, not its distribution per unit area.

12. Which of the following is an example of a renewable resource?

  1. Coal
  2. Petroleum
  3. Solar energy
  4. Natural gas

Answer: Solar energy

Explanation: Renewable resources are those that can be replenished or renewed naturally over a period of time. Solar energy is continuously available and inexhaustible, making it a renewable resource. Coal, petroleum, and natural gas are fossil fuels, which are non-renewable resources as their formation takes millions of years.

13. Which of the following is a major characteristic of a developed industry?

  1. Low capital investment
  2. Use of traditional methods
  3. High level of technology and automation
  4. Small-scale production

Answer: High level of technology and automation

Explanation: Developed industries are characterized by significant capital investment, advanced technology, automation, and large-scale production to achieve efficiency and high output. Low capital investment, traditional methods, and small-scale production are more typical of cottage or small-scale industries.

14. Which of these is a non-metallic mineral?

  1. Iron ore
  2. Bauxite
  3. Limestone
  4. Copper

Answer: Limestone

Explanation: Minerals are broadly classified into metallic and non-metallic. Metallic minerals contain metal in their raw form (e.g., iron ore, bauxite, copper). Non-metallic minerals do not contain metals. Limestone is a non-metallic mineral primarily composed of calcium carbonate, used in construction and as a raw material for cement.

15. What is the term used for the number of people living per unit area of land?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Death rate
  3. Population density
  4. Migration rate

Answer: Population density

Explanation: Population density is a measure of the number of people living in a specific area, usually expressed as persons per square kilometre. Birth rate and death rate refer to the number of births and deaths per 1000 people, respectively, while migration rate refers to the movement of people into or out of an area.

16. Which of the following practices is most effective in preventing soil erosion on steep slopes?

  1. Ploughing parallel to the contours of a hill slope
  2. Leaving the land fallow for several years
  3. Overgrazing by livestock
  4. Growing a single crop year after year

Answer: Ploughing parallel to the contours of a hill slope

Explanation: Ploughing parallel to the contours of a hill slope, known as contour ploughing, creates a natural barrier for water to flow down the slope, thus reducing soil erosion. Leaving land fallow might help soil fertility but doesn't directly prevent erosion on slopes. Overgrazing and growing a single crop (monoculture) can actually increase soil erosion.

17. Which type of agriculture involves growing food and fodder crops and rearing livestock for sale?

  1. Shifting cultivation
  2. Nomadic herding
  3. Mixed farming
  4. Plantation agriculture

Answer: Mixed farming

Explanation: Mixed farming is a type of agriculture where the land is used for growing food and fodder crops and rearing livestock. This combination provides a more stable income and efficient use of resources. Shifting cultivation and nomadic herding are subsistence forms, while plantation agriculture focuses on a single cash crop.

18. Which of the following is a characteristic of a secondary activity or manufacturing industry?

  1. Extraction of raw materials directly from the earth
  2. Processing raw materials into finished goods
  3. Providing services to consumers
  4. Trading goods between different regions

Answer: Processing raw materials into finished goods

Explanation: Secondary activities or manufacturing industries are involved in processing raw materials obtained from primary activities into finished products. Extraction of raw materials is a primary activity, providing services is a tertiary activity, and trading is also a tertiary activity.

19. Which of the following factors is LEAST likely to influence the location of an industry?

  1. Availability of raw materials
  2. Proximity to markets
  3. Availability of capital
  4. Colour of the local soil

Answer: Colour of the local soil

Explanation: The colour of the local soil generally has no direct influence on the location of most industries, unless it's a very specific industry like pottery that uses a particular type of clay. Availability of raw materials, proximity to markets, and availability of capital (along with labour, power, land, and transport) are crucial factors influencing industrial location.

20. What is the term used to describe the number of people living per unit area of land?

  1. Population growth
  2. Population distribution
  3. Population density
  4. Population composition

Answer: Population density

Explanation: Population density refers to the number of people living in a specific area, usually expressed as persons per square kilometre. Population growth is the change in population over time, distribution refers to how people are spread out, and composition refers to the structure of the population (age, sex, etc.).

21. Which of the following is a major reason for the uneven distribution of population across the world?

  1. Availability of advanced technology
  2. Presence of flat plains and fertile soils
  3. High levels of education
  4. Abundance of mineral resources

Answer: Presence of flat plains and fertile soils

Explanation: People prefer to live in areas with favourable geographical conditions. Flat plains and fertile soils are ideal for agriculture, making these regions densely populated. While advanced technology, education, and mineral resources can attract population, the fundamental geographical factors like landforms and soil fertility are primary reasons for uneven distribution.

22. What is the main reason for the uneven distribution of population across the world?

  1. Availability of advanced technology
  2. Political stability
  3. Geographical and economic factors
  4. Cultural diversity

Answer: Geographical and economic factors

Explanation: The uneven distribution of population is primarily due to geographical factors (like climate, topography, soil, water availability, mineral resources) and economic factors (like industrialization, urbanization, employment opportunities). While political stability and technology can influence population distribution, they are not the main overarching reasons for the global unevenness.

23. What is the term for a narrow zone of contact between the land, water, and air that supports life?

  1. Atmosphere
  2. Hydrosphere
  3. Lithosphere
  4. Biosphere

Answer: Biosphere

Explanation: The biosphere is the narrow zone of contact between the land (lithosphere), water (hydrosphere), and air (atmosphere) where life exists. It is the part of the Earth where living organisms are found. The other options represent individual components of the Earth's systems.

24. Which of the following is a major input in the agricultural system?

  1. Crop yield
  2. Storage
  3. Fertilizers
  4. Marketing

Answer: Fertilizers

Explanation: Inputs are the items or resources required to carry out an activity. Fertilizers are essential inputs in agriculture, along with seeds, machinery, labour, etc. Crop yield is an output, while storage and marketing are processes or outputs related to the agricultural system.

25. Which of the following is a characteristic of a cottage industry?

  1. Large-scale production
  2. Use of heavy machinery
  3. Products made by hand by artisans
  4. High capital investment

Answer: Products made by hand by artisans

Explanation: Cottage industries are small-scale industries where products are made by hand by artisans, often within their homes, using simple tools. They typically involve low capital investment and produce goods for local markets or specific demands, unlike large-scale industries that use heavy machinery and have high capital investment.
