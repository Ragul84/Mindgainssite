Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the NCERT Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

26. What is the primary focus of large scale manufacturing?

  1. Customization
  2. Mass production
  3. Manual crafting
  4. Small scale output

Answer: Mass production

Explanation: Large scale manufacturing focuses on mass production rather than custom-made items.

27. Materials from which of the following sources are transformed into useful products through manufacturing?

  1. Farms and forests
  2. Mines and the sea
  3. Both A and B
  4. None of the above

Answer: Both A and B

Explanation: The text states materials from farms, forests, mines, and the sea are transformed through manufacturing.

28. Which of the following is a key characteristic of manufacturing?

  1. The use of power
  2. Avoidance of tools
  3. Production of unique items
  4. Lack of standardization

Answer: The use of power

Explanation: The use of power is listed as a key characteristic of manufacturing.

29. Traditional craft methods produce items that are usually:

  1. Mass produced
  2. Low cost per unit
  3. Custom-made and high cost
  4. Standardized

Answer: Custom-made and high cost

Explanation: Traditional craft methods produce a few custom-made items at high costs.

30. Traditional craft methods are more efficient than modern large scale manufacturing.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Large scale manufacturing is more efficient and has lower costs per unit than craft methods.

31. Manufacturing can be done using primitive methods depending on available resources.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text states manufacturing can be done using modern technology or primitive methods.

32. Secondary activities only include the manufacturing of steel.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Secondary activities include manufacturing, processing, and construction industries broadly.

33. Standardized goods are produced using specialized labor in factory settings.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text mentions specialized labor in factory settings is used to produce standardized goods.

34. What is the primary goal of modern large scale manufacturing?

  1. Mass production of goods
  2. Producing custom-made items
  3. Reducing the use of machinery
  4. Increasing the cost per unit

Answer: Mass production of goods

Explanation: Modern large scale manufacturing focuses on mass production to produce goods in large quantities.

35. Manufacturing is defined as the process of transforming raw materials into:

  1. Raw materials of lower value
  2. Finished goods of higher value
  3. Handmade items only
  4. Agricultural products

Answer: Finished goods of higher value

Explanation: Manufacturing transforms raw materials into finished goods that possess a higher value.

36. Which of the following is a key characteristic of modern large scale manufacturing?

  1. High cost per unit
  2. Traditional craft methods
  3. Division of labor
  4. Lack of standardization

Answer: Division of labor

Explanation: Division of labor is a key characteristic that contributes to increased productivity.

37. How does modern large scale manufacturing differ from traditional craft methods?

  1. It produces few custom items
  2. It has higher costs per unit
  3. It focuses on mass production
  4. It avoids the use of machinery

Answer: It focuses on mass production

Explanation: Unlike traditional craft methods that produce a few custom items, modern manufacturing focuses on mass production.

38. What allows for lower costs per unit in modern manufacturing?

  1. Standardized and efficient production
  2. Using traditional craft methods
  3. Producing custom-made items
  4. Reducing the scale of production

Answer: Standardized and efficient production

Explanation: Standardized and efficient production processes allow for lower costs per unit.

39. Originally, the term 'manufacturing' referred to making things by:

  1. Machines
  2. Hand
  3. Robots
  4. Chemical processes

Answer: Hand

Explanation: Originally, manufacturing meant making things by hand before the introduction of machines.

40. Which of these contributes to increased productivity and reduced costs in modern manufacturing?

  1. Economies of scale
  2. Custom-made production
  3. High unit costs
  4. Traditional skills

Answer: Economies of scale

Explanation: Economies of scale, along with advanced machinery and division of labor, reduce costs and increase productivity.

41. In a manufacturing industry, workers often perform which type of tasks?

  1. Unique and varied tasks
  2. Repetitive tasks
  3. Non-standardized tasks
  4. Agricultural tasks

Answer: Repetitive tasks

Explanation: Workers often perform repetitive tasks to produce large quantities of standardized parts.

42. Where are the finished goods from manufacturing industries sold?

  1. Only in local markets
  2. Only in distant markets
  3. In local or distant markets
  4. They are not sold

Answer: In local or distant markets

Explanation: Finished goods are sold in both local and distant markets.

43. What is the result of using advanced machinery and division of labor in manufacturing?

  1. Increased costs per unit
  2. Decreased productivity
  3. Increased productivity and reduced costs
  4. Production of custom-made items

Answer: Increased productivity and reduced costs

Explanation: Advanced machinery and division of labor contribute to higher productivity and lower costs.

44. Modern large scale manufacturing uses which of the following to produce goods?

  1. Specialized skills and methods
  2. Only manual labor
  3. Random production methods
  4. Low-scale production

Answer: Specialized skills and methods

Explanation: Modern manufacturing involves producing goods using specialized skills and methods.

45. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of modern large scale manufacturing?

  1. Economies of scale
  2. Advanced machinery
  3. Custom-made items at high costs
  4. Division of labor

Answer: Custom-made items at high costs

Explanation: Custom-made items at high costs are characteristic of traditional craft methods, not modern large scale manufacturing.

46. What is the primary focus of the production process in modern manufacturing?

  1. Standardization and efficiency
  2. Uniqueness of each product
  3. High cost per unit
  4. Small scale production

Answer: Standardization and efficiency

Explanation: The production process is standardized and efficient to enable mass production.

47. Modern manufacturing now includes goods made by:

  1. Only hands
  2. Only traditional tools
  3. Machines
  4. No tools

Answer: Machines

Explanation: While it originally meant making things by hand, it now includes goods made by machines.

48. What is produced in large quantities in a manufacturing industry?

  1. Custom-made parts
  2. Standardized parts
  3. Raw materials
  4. Unique prototypes

Answer: Standardized parts

Explanation: Manufacturing industries produce large quantities of standardized parts.

49. Modern large scale manufacturing focuses on producing goods in:

  1. Small quantities
  2. Large quantities
  3. Limited quantities
  4. Custom quantities

Answer: Large quantities

Explanation: Modern large scale manufacturing involves producing goods in large quantities.

50. Traditional craft methods produce items that are:

  1. Mass produced
  2. Standardized
  3. Custom-made at high costs
  4. Low cost per unit

Answer: Custom-made at high costs

Explanation: Traditional craft methods produce a few custom-made items at high costs.
