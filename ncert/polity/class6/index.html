Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the NCERT Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 335 questions.

1. Which of the following is NOT an example of diversity in India?

  1. People speaking different languages
  2. People eating different types of food
  3. People having different religions
  4. All people having the same height

Answer: All people having the same height

Explanation: Diversity refers to the variety and differences among people, such as language, food, religion, and culture. Having the same height for everyone would mean a lack of diversity in physical appearance, not an example of it.

2. When people are treated unfairly because of their differences, it is called:

  1. Equality
  2. Diversity
  3. Discrimination
  4. Unity

Answer: Discrimination

Explanation: Discrimination is the act of treating a person or group differently and unfairly, often based on prejudices related to their religion, caste, gender, or other characteristics. Equality means treating everyone the same, diversity is about differences, and unity is about coming together despite differences.

3. Which of these is a function of the government?

  1. Playing games
  2. Making laws
  3. Watching movies
  4. Eating food

Answer: Making laws

Explanation: The government is responsible for making laws, enforcing them, providing public services like roads and schools, and ensuring the welfare of its citizens. Playing games, watching movies, and eating food are personal activities, not functions of the government.

4. The Gram Sabha is a meeting of all adult members who live in the area covered by a:

  1. District
  2. State
  3. Panchayat
  4. Country

Answer: Panchayat

Explanation: The Gram Sabha is a key institution in the Panchayati Raj system. It consists of all adult citizens (18 years or older) who are registered as voters in a particular Gram Panchayat area. It discusses and approves the plans for the village.

5. Who is responsible for maintaining land records in a village?

  1. Police Officer
  2. Patwari
  3. Doctor
  4. Teacher

Answer: Patwari

Explanation: The Patwari (also known as Lekhpal, Kanungo, or Village Officer in different regions) is a government official responsible for maintaining land records, measuring land, and collecting land revenue in a village. The Police Officer maintains law and order, a doctor provides medical care, and a teacher educates students.

6. Which of the following is a function of the Municipal Corporation?

  1. Building national highways
  2. Maintaining law and order in the entire country
  3. Providing water supply and sanitation in cities
  4. Managing international relations

Answer: Providing water supply and sanitation in cities

Explanation: Municipal Corporations are local self-governing bodies in urban areas. Their primary functions include providing essential services like water supply, sanitation, street lighting, road construction, and maintaining public health within the city limits. Building national highways and managing international relations are functions of the central government, and maintaining law and order in the entire country is a broader function involving both state and central police forces.

7. Which of these is a common activity for people living in rural areas?

  1. Working in a large factory
  2. Farming
  3. Working in a multinational company office
  4. Driving a city bus

Answer: Farming

Explanation: Farming (agriculture) is the primary livelihood for a large majority of people living in rural areas. Working in large factories, multinational company offices, or driving city buses are more common in urban settings.

8. People who sell goods on the streets or in temporary stalls in a city are often called:

  1. Factory workers
  2. Office employees
  3. Street vendors
  4. Bank managers

Answer: Street vendors

Explanation: Street vendors are individuals who sell goods or services directly to the public on the streets, often from temporary stalls or carts. This is a common form of urban livelihood. Factory workers, office employees, and bank managers have different types of employment.

9. The Gram Sabha is a meeting of all adult members living in the area covered by a:

  1. City
  2. State
  3. Panchayat
  4. Country

Answer: Panchayat

Explanation: The Gram Sabha is a key part of the Panchayati Raj system. It is a meeting of all adult citizens (18 years or older) who live in the area covered by a Gram Panchayat. They discuss and decide on development plans for their village.

10. Which of these is a function of the Municipal Corporation?

  1. Building national highways
  2. Providing electricity to rural areas
  3. Collecting garbage in cities
  4. Managing international relations

Answer: Collecting garbage in cities

Explanation: Municipal Corporations are local self-governing bodies in urban areas. Their functions include collecting garbage, providing water supply, maintaining roads, street lighting, and public health services within the city limits. Building national highways and managing international relations are functions of the central government, while providing electricity to rural areas is often handled by state electricity boards.

11. Which of the following is a common livelihood in rural areas?

  1. Working in a large factory
  2. Farming
  3. Working in a multinational company
  4. Being a software engineer

Answer: Farming

Explanation: Rural areas are primarily characterized by agriculture and related activities. Farming, livestock rearing, fishing, and small-scale crafts are common livelihoods. Working in large factories, multinational companies, or as a software engineer are more typical of urban livelihoods.

12. People who sell goods on the streets or in temporary stalls in cities are often called:

  1. Farmers
  2. Shopkeepers
  3. Hawkers or Street Vendors
  4. Factory workers

Answer: Hawkers or Street Vendors

Explanation: Hawkers or street vendors are common in urban areas. They sell various goods like vegetables, fruits, clothes, or snacks from temporary stalls or by moving around on the streets. Farmers work in rural areas, shopkeepers have permanent shops, and factory workers are employed in industries.

13. Which of the following is an example of diversity in India?

  1. People speaking different languages
  2. Everyone eating the same food
  3. All houses looking identical
  4. Everyone wearing the same clothes

Answer: People speaking different languages

Explanation: Diversity refers to the presence of a variety of differences among people. In India, people speak many different languages, follow various religions, and have diverse food habits and clothing styles, which are all examples of its rich diversity. The other options describe uniformity, not diversity.

14. When people are treated unfairly because of their religion, caste, or gender, it is called:

  1. Equality
  2. Diversity
  3. Discrimination
  4. Unity

Answer: Discrimination

Explanation: Discrimination occurs when people are treated differently or unfairly based on their group, category, or other characteristics like religion, caste, gender, or economic status. Equality means treating everyone the same, diversity is about differences, and unity is about coming together despite differences.

15. What is the main role of a government?

  1. To make laws and ensure justice
  2. To only build roads
  3. To only provide entertainment
  4. To only collect taxes

Answer: To make laws and ensure justice

Explanation: A government is responsible for making laws for the country, ensuring that these laws are followed, providing public services like healthcare and education, maintaining law and order, and working towards the welfare and justice of its citizens. While building roads and collecting taxes are functions, they are not the main, overarching role.

16. Who is responsible for electing the members of the Gram Panchayat?

  1. The District Collector
  2. All adults living in the Gram Sabha area
  3. Only children in the village
  4. The Chief Minister

Answer: All adults living in the Gram Sabha area

Explanation: The Gram Panchayat is a local self-government body at the village level. Its members (Panchs) and the Sarpanch (President) are elected by all adult members (those 18 years or older) of the Gram Sabha, which includes all registered voters in the village or group of villages.

17. Which of the following is a common rural livelihood in India?

  1. Working in a large software company
  2. Farming
  3. Working as a stockbroker
  4. Managing a multinational bank

Answer: Farming

Explanation: Farming (agriculture) is the most common and primary livelihood for a large majority of people living in rural areas of India. Other rural livelihoods include fishing, animal rearing, and working as artisans. The other options are typically urban livelihoods.

18. Which of the following is a common livelihood activity in rural areas?

  1. Working in a large software company
  2. Farming
  3. Managing a big shopping mall
  4. Working as an airline pilot

Answer: Farming

Explanation: Farming (agriculture) is the most common and primary livelihood activity in rural areas, where people depend on land for growing crops and raising livestock. The other options are typically urban livelihoods.

19. Which body is responsible for providing public facilities like water supply and street lights in a large city?

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Municipal Corporation
  3. District Collector
  4. State Government

Answer: Municipal Corporation

Explanation: In urban areas, the Municipal Corporation (or Municipality in smaller towns) is the local self-governing body responsible for providing essential public services like water supply, sanitation, street lighting, and maintaining roads. Gram Panchayat works in rural areas, and the District Collector is an administrative head, while the State Government operates at a higher level.

20. Which of these is NOT a function of the Municipal Corporation?

  1. Collecting garbage
  2. Providing water supply
  3. Maintaining law and order
  4. Building and maintaining roads

Answer: Maintaining law and order

Explanation: The Municipal Corporation is responsible for urban administration, including services like garbage collection, water supply, building roads, and maintaining streetlights. Maintaining law and order is primarily the responsibility of the police department, which falls under the state government, not directly the Municipal Corporation.

21. Which of the following is an example of a rural livelihood?

  1. Working in a call center
  2. Farming
  3. Working in a factory in a big city
  4. Selling clothes in a shopping mall

Answer: Farming

Explanation: Rural livelihoods are ways people earn a living in rural areas. Farming (agriculture) is the most common and primary livelihood in villages. Working in a call center, factory, or shopping mall are typically urban livelihoods.

22. Which body is responsible for making decisions at the village level in India?

  1. State Assembly
  2. Parliament
  3. Gram Panchayat
  4. Municipal Corporation

Answer: Gram Panchayat

Explanation: The Gram Panchayat is the local self-government body at the village level in India. It is responsible for making decisions and implementing development programs for the village. The State Assembly and Parliament are higher levels of government, and the Municipal Corporation is for urban areas.

23. People who sell goods on the streets or in temporary stalls in cities are called:

  1. Factory workers
  2. Office employees
  3. Street vendors
  4. Farmers

Answer: Street vendors

Explanation: Street vendors are people who sell goods or services on the streets, often from temporary stalls or carts. They form a significant part of urban livelihoods, providing various goods and services to city dwellers. Factory workers and office employees have different types of urban jobs, and farmers are involved in rural livelihoods.

24. Which of these is the lowest level of the Panchayati Raj system?

  1. Zila Parishad
  2. Gram Panchayat
  3. Block Samiti
  4. State Assembly

Answer: Gram Panchayat

Explanation: The Panchayati Raj system in India has three levels: the Gram Panchayat at the village level (lowest), the Block Samiti (or Janpad Panchayat) at the block level, and the Zila Parishad at the district level (highest). The State Assembly is part of the state government, not the Panchayati Raj.

25. Which body is responsible for providing basic services like water supply and street lighting in a large city?

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Municipal Corporation
  3. Zila Parishad
  4. State Government

Answer: Municipal Corporation

Explanation: In large cities, the Municipal Corporation (or Nagar Nigam) is the local self-government body responsible for providing essential services like water supply, sanitation, street lighting, road construction, and public health. Gram Panchayat is for villages, Zila Parishad for districts, and the State Government operates at a higher level.
