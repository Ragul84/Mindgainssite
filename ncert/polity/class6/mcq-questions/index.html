26. Which of the following is a common source of livelihood in rural areas?

  1. Working in a large factory
  2. Farming and agriculture
  3. Working in an IT company
  4. Running a multinational business

Answer: Farming and agriculture

Explanation: In rural areas, the primary source of livelihood for most people is agriculture, including farming, animal rearing, and related activities. Working in large factories, IT companies, or running multinational businesses are more common in urban or industrial settings.

27. When people are treated unfairly or badly because of their differences, it is called:

  1. Equality
  2. Diversity
  3. Discrimination
  4. Unity

Answer: Discrimination

Explanation: Discrimination is the unfair treatment of a person or group of people based on their differences, such as caste, religion, gender, or economic status. Equality means treating everyone the same, diversity is about differences, and unity is about coming together despite differences.

28. Which of these is the lowest tier of the Panchayati Raj system in India?

  1. Zila Parishad
  2. Block Samiti
  3. Gram Panchayat
  4. State Assembly

Answer: Gram Panchayat

Explanation: The Panchayati Raj system has three tiers: the Gram Panchayat at the village level (lowest), the Block Samiti (or Janpad Panchayat) at the block level, and the Zila Parishad at the district level (highest). The State Assembly is part of the state government, not the Panchayati Raj.

29. Which of the following is NOT a function of a Municipal Corporation?

  1. Collecting garbage
  2. Providing electricity to homes
  3. Building and maintaining roads
  4. Running schools and hospitals

Answer: Providing electricity to homes

Explanation: Municipal Corporations are responsible for urban services like garbage collection, road maintenance, water supply, public health (hospitals), and education (schools). Providing electricity to homes is typically the responsibility of state electricity boards or private power companies, not directly the Municipal Corporation.

30. Which of these is a common livelihood activity in rural areas?

  1. Working in a large software company
  2. Farming and agriculture
  3. Managing a big shopping mall
  4. Working as a stockbroker

Answer: Farming and agriculture

Explanation: Rural livelihoods are predominantly based on agriculture and related activities like farming, animal husbandry, and fishing. Working in software companies, managing shopping malls, or being a stockbroker are typically urban livelihood activities.

31. What is the main role of a Gram Panchayat?

  1. To elect the Chief Minister of the state
  2. To make laws for the entire country
  3. To look after the welfare of the village and solve local problems
  4. To manage the finances of the central government

Answer: To look after the welfare of the village and solve local problems

Explanation: The Gram Panchayat is a local self-government body at the village level. Its main role is to work for the welfare of the village, including solving local problems like water supply, sanitation, and road maintenance. It does not elect the Chief Minister, make laws for the country, or manage central government finances.

32. What is the term used for treating someone unfairly because of their caste, religion, or gender?

  1. Equality
  2. Justice
  3. Discrimination
  4. Cooperation

Answer: Discrimination

Explanation: Discrimination refers to the act of treating a person or a group of people differently or unfairly based on characteristics like their caste, religion, gender, or economic background. Equality means treating everyone the same, justice means fairness, and cooperation means working together.

33. Which of the following is NOT a level of government in India?

  1. Local level
  2. State level
  3. National level
  4. International level

Answer: International level

Explanation: The Indian government operates at three main levels: Local (like Panchayats and Municipalities), State (governing individual states), and National (governing the entire country). An International level of government does not exist within India's administrative structure.

34. What is the main role of a Gram Sabha in a village?

  1. To elect the Chief Minister of the state
  2. To make laws for the entire country
  3. To discuss and approve the plans for the village
  4. To manage the police force in the district

Answer: To discuss and approve the plans for the village

Explanation: The Gram Sabha is a meeting of all adult members of a village who are registered voters. Its main role is to discuss and approve the plans for the village's development, oversee the work of the Gram Panchayat, and ensure transparency in its functioning. It does not elect the Chief Minister, make national laws, or manage the police force.

35. Which of the following is NOT a level of the Panchayati Raj system in India?

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Zila Parishad
  3. State Legislature
  4. Block Samiti

Answer: State Legislature

Explanation: The Panchayati Raj system in India has three main levels: the Gram Panchayat at the village level, the Block Samiti (or Janpad Panchayat) at the block level, and the Zila Parishad at the district level. The State Legislature is a part of the state government, not a level within the Panchayati Raj system itself.
