26. The UN agencies work on which of the following aspects?

  1. Human rights and economic development
  2. Only military strategy
  3. Only national borders
  4. Only religious laws

Answer: Human rights and economic development

Explanation: UN agencies work on human rights, economic development, and environmental protection.

27. What does the UN aim to promote alongside peace and security?

  1. Social progress and better living standards
  2. Global warfare
  3. Economic isolation
  4. Nationalist agendas

Answer: Social progress and better living standards

Explanation: The UN promotes social progress, better living standards, and human rights.

28. International organisations are established by ________.

  1. Oral agreements
  2. Treaties
  3. Military force
  4. Private companies

Answer: Treaties

Explanation: International organisations are groups established by treaties.

29. The UN coordinates global efforts to tackle challenges that affect ________.

  1. Only wealthy nations
  2. Only developed cities
  3. All of humanity
  4. Only the Security Council

Answer: All of humanity

Explanation: The UN coordinates efforts to tackle challenges that affect all of humanity.

30. The UN acts as a center for ________ the actions of nations.

  1. Dividing
  2. Harmonizing
  3. Ignoring
  4. Opposing

Answer: Harmonizing

Explanation: The UN is a center for harmonizing the actions of nations.

  2. Future conflicts
  3. Diplomatic dialogue

38. What is the primary purpose for which the United Nations (UN) was established?

  1. To foster global cooperation and maintain peace and security

Explanation: The UN was founded to foster global cooperation and maintain peace and security, preventing future conflicts.

  2. Before World War I

Explanation: The UN was established after World War II with the aim of preventing future conflicts.

42. The UN serves as a platform for dialogue and diplomacy among nations to address global challenges.

44. What is a key benefit of using dialogue through international organizations for conflict resolution?

Explanation: The text highlights that dialogue through international organizations is a more effective and less destructive approach than military action.
