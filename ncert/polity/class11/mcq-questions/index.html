Class11 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class11 questions to revise the NCERT Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 410 questions.

26. The Parliament of India does not regulate itself.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The text explicitly mentions that regulating itself is one of the Parliament's roles.

27. What is the primary role of the legislature in a representative democracy?

  1. To implement laws independently
  2. To hold elected representatives accountable
  3. To bypass the will of the people
  4. To act as the sole judicial authority

Answer: To hold elected representatives accountable

Explanation: The legislature ensures that elected representatives are held accountable and government actions align with the people's will.

28. Which body is responsible for initiating policies and setting the governance agenda?

  1. The Judiciary
  2. The Cabinet
  3. The Electorate
  4. The Local Government

Answer: The Cabinet

Explanation: The Cabinet, as part of the executive branch, initiates policies and sets the governance agenda.

29. Why is Parliament considered one of the most democratic forums for debate?

  1. Because it is the only body that makes laws
  2. Because it represents the people
  3. Because it is independent of the executive
  4. Because it is not subject to elections

Answer: Because it represents the people

Explanation: Parliament is seen as a democratic forum because it represents the people and allows for open debate.

30. What power does Parliament hold regarding the government?

  1. The power to appoint judges
  2. The power to choose and remove the government
  3. The power to ignore the Cabinet
  4. The power to rewrite the constitution alone

Answer: The power to choose and remove the government

Explanation: Parliament has the critical power to both choose and remove the government.

31. What is necessary for even a strong Cabinet to function effectively?

  1. Absolute power over the people
  2. A majority in the legislature
  3. The removal of the legislature
  4. Complete independence from the Parliament

Answer: A majority in the legislature

Explanation: Even strong Cabinets must maintain a majority in the legislature to function effectively.

32. What ensures that Parliament reflects the diversity of the population?

  1. Its judicial powers
  2. Its composition
  3. Its relationship with the Cabinet
  4. The governance agenda

Answer: Its composition

Explanation: The composition of Parliament ensures it is the most representative organ, reflecting population diversity.

33. Which branch of government has gained more influence in many democracies recently?

  1. The Judiciary
  2. The Executive branch
  3. The Local Government
  4. The Electoral Commission

Answer: The Executive branch

Explanation: The executive branch, including the Cabinet, has gained more influence in many democracies.

34. What is the primary function of elected representatives within Parliament?

  1. To implement policies without debate
  2. To discuss and make laws
  3. To manage the judiciary
  4. To avoid accountability

Answer: To discuss and make laws

Explanation: Parliament is the place where elected representatives discuss and make laws.

35. The legislature ensures that government actions align with:

  1. The will of the people
  2. The will of the judiciary
  3. The will of the bureaucracy
  4. The will of the military

Answer: The will of the people

Explanation: The legislature's role is to ensure government actions align with the will of the people.

36. Which of the following is a function of the Cabinet?

  1. Holding the Cabinet accountable
  2. Implementing policies
  3. Representing the diversity of the population
  4. Removing the legislature

Answer: Implementing policies

Explanation: The Cabinet is responsible for initiating and implementing policies.

37. Parliament is described as the most ______ organ of the government.

  1. Secretive
  2. Representative
  3. Limited
  4. Ineffective

Answer: Representative

Explanation: Due to its composition and representation of the people, it is the most representative organ.

38. What has led some to believe the legislature's power has diminished?

  1. The removal of the Cabinet
  2. The increased influence of the executive branch
  3. The lack of debate in Parliament
  4. The loss of the power to make laws

Answer: The increased influence of the executive branch

Explanation: The shift of influence toward the executive branch has led to beliefs that legislative power has diminished.

39. Which of these is a key characteristic of Parliament as a forum?

  1. Closed and restrictive
  2. Democratic and open for debate
  3. Solely focused on implementation
  4. Independent of the people's will

Answer: Democratic and open for debate

Explanation: Parliament is considered one of the most democratic and open forums for debate.

40. The Cabinet is part of which branch of government?

  1. Legislative
  2. Executive
  3. Judicial
  4. Administrative

Answer: Executive

Explanation: The Cabinet is explicitly mentioned as part of the executive branch.

41. What is the result of Parliament's power to choose and remove the government?

  1. It makes Parliament a weak institution
  2. It makes Parliament a very important institution
  3. It eliminates the need for a Cabinet
  4. It removes the need for elections

Answer: It makes Parliament a very important institution

Explanation: The power to choose and remove the government makes Parliament a very important institution.

42. The executive branch has gained more influence in many democracies.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text states that in many democracies, the executive branch has gained more influence.

43. Parliament is a closed forum for debate.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The text describes Parliament as one of the most open forums for debate.

44. Strong Cabinets can function effectively without a majority in the legislature.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The text states that even strong Cabinets need to maintain a majority in the legislature to function effectively.

45. Parliament is the most representative organ of the government.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text explicitly states that Parliament is the most representative organ of the government.

46. What is a primary function of the legislature in relation to the executive branch?

  1. To implement and enforce laws
  2. To oversee and control the executive
  3. To appoint judges to the judiciary
  4. To conduct foreign policy negotiations

Answer: To oversee and control the executive

Explanation: The legislature's key function is to oversee and control the executive branch, ensuring it acts within legal bounds and remains accountable.

47. Which of the following is a method by which the legislature can control the executive?

  1. Issuing presidential pardons
  2. Approving budgets
  3. Declaring war
  4. Establishing diplomatic relations

Answer: Approving budgets

Explanation: The legislature can control the executive through means such as approving budgets, conducting inquiries, and passing laws.

48. What is the main purpose of the legislature's control over the executive?

  1. To increase executive power
  2. To ensure the executive branch acts within the law and is accountable
  3. To bypass the judicial branch
  4. To reduce legislative oversight

Answer: To ensure the executive branch acts within the law and is accountable

Explanation: Legislative oversight ensures that the executive branch acts within the bounds of the law and is accountable to the people.

49. Effective legislative control over the executive helps to maintain a balance of power and prevent:

  1. Legislative gridlock
  2. Judicial review
  3. Misuse of authority
  4. Executive elections

Answer: Misuse of authority

Explanation: Effective control helps maintain a balance of power and prevents the misuse of authority by the executive branch.

50. India's Parliament is composed of how many houses?

  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Three
  4. Four

Answer: Two

Explanation: India has a bicameral legislature, meaning its Parliament consists of two houses.
