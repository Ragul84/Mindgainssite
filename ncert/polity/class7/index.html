Class7 MCQs with answers

1. Which of the following is NOT an example of discrimination based on caste in India?

  1. Denying a person entry to a temple.
  2. Refusing to serve food to someone in a restaurant.
  3. Providing equal opportunities for education to all children.
  4. Preventing someone from drawing water from a common well.

Answer: Providing equal opportunities for education to all children.

Explanation: Discrimination involves treating someone unfairly based on their caste, religion, gender, etc. Denying entry to a temple, refusing food, or preventing access to a common well are all examples of discrimination. Providing equal opportunities for education, however, promotes equality and is the opposite of discrimination.

2. What is the primary role of the government in providing healthcare services to its citizens?

  1. To earn profit from medical treatments.
  2. To ensure that everyone has access to quality healthcare facilities.
  3. To promote private hospitals only.
  4. To provide healthcare only to the rich.

Answer: To ensure that everyone has access to quality healthcare facilities.

Explanation: According to the Constitution, it is the primary duty of the government to ensure the welfare of the people and provide healthcare facilities for all. This means ensuring that everyone, regardless of their economic status, has access to quality medical care. Earning profit, promoting only private hospitals, or serving only the rich are not the primary roles of a democratic government in healthcare.

3. Who is the head of the state government in India?

  1. The Prime Minister
  2. The President
  3. The Chief Minister
  4. The Governor

Answer: The Chief Minister

Explanation: In India, the Chief Minister is the head of the state government and leads the council of ministers. The Governor is the constitutional head of the state, appointed by the President, but the Chief Minister holds the executive power. The Prime Minister is the head of the central government, and the President is the head of the country.

4. Which of the following statements best describes the concept of 'gender roles'?

  1. Biological differences between boys and girls.
  2. The roles and responsibilities that society expects from boys and girls.
  3. The personal preferences of individuals regarding their work.
  4. The legal rights granted to men and women.

Answer: The roles and responsibilities that society expects from boys and girls.

Explanation: Gender roles are socially constructed expectations about how boys and girls, and men and women, should behave, think, and feel. These are not based on biological differences but on societal norms and traditions. Personal preferences and legal rights are distinct concepts from gender roles.

5. What is the main objective of the 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' campaign in India?

  1. To promote sports among girls.
  2. To encourage girls to pursue higher education abroad.
  3. To address the declining child sex ratio and promote girls' education.
  4. To provide financial assistance for girls' marriages.

Answer: To address the declining child sex ratio and promote girls' education.

Explanation: The 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' (Save the Daughter, Educate the Daughter) campaign was launched to address the issue of the declining child sex ratio and to promote the education and empowerment of girls in India. It aims to challenge gender discrimination and ensure girls' rights to survival, protection, and education.
