Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the NCERT Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 299 questions.

1. Which of the following is NOT an example of discrimination based on caste in India?

  1. Denying a person entry to a temple.
  2. Refusing to serve food to someone in a restaurant.
  3. Providing equal opportunities for education to all children.
  4. Preventing someone from drawing water from a common well.

Answer: Providing equal opportunities for education to all children.

Explanation: Discrimination involves treating someone unfairly based on their caste, religion, gender, etc. Denying entry to a temple, refusing food, or preventing access to a common well are all examples of discrimination. Providing equal opportunities for education, however, promotes equality and is the opposite of discrimination.

2. What is the primary role of the government in providing healthcare services to its citizens?

  1. To earn profit from medical treatments.
  2. To ensure that everyone has access to quality healthcare facilities.
  3. To promote private hospitals only.
  4. To provide healthcare only to the rich.

Answer: To ensure that everyone has access to quality healthcare facilities.

Explanation: According to the Constitution, it is the primary duty of the government to ensure the welfare of the people and provide healthcare facilities for all. This means ensuring that everyone, regardless of their economic status, has access to quality medical care. Earning profit, promoting only private hospitals, or serving only the rich are not the primary roles of a democratic government in healthcare.

3. Who is the head of the state government in India?

  1. The Prime Minister
  2. The President
  3. The Chief Minister
  4. The Governor

Answer: The Chief Minister

Explanation: In India, the Chief Minister is the head of the state government and leads the council of ministers. The Governor is the constitutional head of the state, appointed by the President, but the Chief Minister holds the executive power. The Prime Minister is the head of the central government, and the President is the head of the country.

4. Which of the following statements best describes the concept of 'gender roles'?

  1. Biological differences between boys and girls.
  2. The roles and responsibilities that society expects from boys and girls.
  3. The personal preferences of individuals regarding their work.
  4. The legal rights granted to men and women.

Answer: The roles and responsibilities that society expects from boys and girls.

Explanation: Gender roles are socially constructed expectations about how boys and girls, and men and women, should behave, think, and feel. These are not based on biological differences but on societal norms and traditions. Personal preferences and legal rights are distinct concepts from gender roles.

5. What is the main objective of the 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' campaign in India?

  1. To promote sports among girls.
  2. To encourage girls to pursue higher education abroad.
  3. To address the declining child sex ratio and promote girls' education.
  4. To provide financial assistance for girls' marriages.

Answer: To address the declining child sex ratio and promote girls' education.

Explanation: The 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' (Save the Daughter, Educate the Daughter) campaign was launched to address the issue of the declining child sex ratio and to promote the education and empowerment of girls in India. It aims to challenge gender discrimination and ensure girls' rights to survival, protection, and education.

6. Which of the following is an example of 'paid media'?

  1. A news report on a television channel.
  2. An advertisement in a newspaper.
  3. A social media post by a friend.
  4. A public service announcement by the government.

Answer: An advertisement in a newspaper.

Explanation: Paid media refers to content that an organization or individual pays to place in media outlets. An advertisement in a newspaper is a classic example of paid media, as the advertiser pays the newspaper for the space. News reports, social media posts by friends, and public service announcements are generally not considered paid media in the same way.

7. What is the primary function of a 'wholesale market'?

  1. To sell goods directly to individual consumers.
  2. To buy goods in large quantities from producers and sell them to retailers.
  3. To sell handmade crafts and unique items.
  4. To provide services like haircuts and repairs.

Answer: To buy goods in large quantities from producers and sell them to retailers.

Explanation: A wholesale market is where goods are bought in bulk from producers or manufacturers and then sold in smaller quantities to retailers, who then sell them to individual consumers. It acts as an intermediary stage in the supply chain, not directly serving individual consumers or providing services.

8. Who benefits the most when a shirt is sold at a very high price in a fancy showroom?

  1. The cotton farmer who grew the cotton.
  2. The workers who stitched the shirt.
  3. The showroom owner and the brand company.
  4. The transporter who carried the shirt.

Answer: The showroom owner and the brand company.

Explanation: In the 'Shirt in the Market' chapter, it is highlighted that when a shirt is sold at a high price in a fancy showroom, the largest share of the profit goes to the showroom owner and the brand company. The cotton farmer and the workers who stitched the shirt often receive very little in comparison, despite their significant contributions to the product.

9. Which of the following is an example of discrimination based on caste, 'On Equality'?

  1. Being denied a job because of your religion.
  2. Being denied access to a public well because of your caste.
  3. Being denied admission to a school because of your gender.
  4. Being denied a promotion because of your age.

Answer: Being denied access to a public well because of your caste.

Explanation: The chapter 'On Equality' specifically discusses how the caste system has historically led to discrimination, where people from certain castes were denied access to public resources like wells. While other options represent discrimination, denying access to a public well based on caste is a direct example from the chapter's context.

10. In the chapter 'Role of the Government in Health', what is the primary responsibility of the government regarding healthcare?

  1. To provide private healthcare facilities for all citizens.
  2. To ensure that all citizens have access to quality healthcare services.
  3. To encourage citizens to seek healthcare abroad.
  4. To only provide healthcare for the wealthy.

Answer: To ensure that all citizens have access to quality healthcare services.

Explanation: The chapter 'Role of the Government in Health' emphasizes that a democratic government's primary responsibility is to ensure the welfare of its people, which includes providing accessible and quality healthcare services for all, regardless of their economic status. Private facilities are not the government's primary responsibility, and encouraging healthcare abroad or only for the wealthy contradicts the principle of universal access.

11. Who is the head of the State Government and represents the state at the national level?

  1. The Chief Minister
  2. The Governor
  3. The Prime Minister
  4. The President

Answer: The Governor

Explanation: As discussed in 'How the State Government Works', the Governor is the constitutional head of the state and acts as a link between the state and the central government. The Chief Minister is the head of the elected government, but the Governor is the representative of the President and the Union government in the state.

12. 'Women Change the World', what is a common stereotype associated with girls regarding their career choices?

  1. They are expected to pursue careers in engineering and science.
  2. They are encouraged to take up leadership roles in politics.
  3. They are often encouraged to choose professions like teaching or nursing.
  4. They are expected to excel in sports and athletics.

Answer: They are often encouraged to choose professions like teaching or nursing.

Explanation: The chapter 'Women Change the World' highlights how societal expectations and stereotypes often channel girls towards certain professions like teaching or nursing, which are traditionally seen as 'feminine' and caring roles, limiting their choices in other fields.

13. What is the main purpose of a 'press conference' as discussed in 'Understanding Media'?

  1. To entertain the public with celebrity news.
  2. To allow media persons to ask questions and get information from officials.
  3. To broadcast live sports events.
  4. To advertise new products to consumers.

Answer: To allow media persons to ask questions and get information from officials.

Explanation: In 'Understanding Media', a press conference is explained as a formal meeting where officials or organizations provide information to the media and answer their questions, allowing the media to gather news and report it to the public.

14. Which of the following is an example of a 'weekly market' as described in 'Markets Around Us'?

  1. A large shopping mall with multiple stores.
  2. A small shop selling groceries every day.
  3. A market that sets up on a specific day of the week in different locations.
  4. An online store selling products globally.

Answer: A market that sets up on a specific day of the week in different locations.

Explanation: The chapter 'Markets Around Us' defines a weekly market as a temporary market that operates on a specific day of the week, often moving to different locations. This allows vendors to sell their goods to a wider range of customers without the overhead of a permanent shop.

15. In the story 'A Shirt in the Market', what is the role of the 'ginning mill'?

  1. To weave cotton into fabric.
  2. To separate cotton fibres from seeds.
  3. To dye the fabric different colours.
  4. To stitch the fabric into shirts.

Answer: To separate cotton fibres from seeds.

Explanation: The chapter 'A Shirt in the Market' explains that the ginning mill is where the raw cotton, after being picked, is processed to separate the cotton fibres from the seeds, a crucial step before it can be spun into yarn.

16. What is the main objective of 'Struggles for Equality'?

  1. To promote individual wealth accumulation.
  2. To ensure that all people are treated with dignity and have equal rights.
  3. To establish a single dominant culture in society.
  4. To encourage competition among different social groups.

Answer: To ensure that all people are treated with dignity and have equal rights.

Explanation: The chapter 'Struggles for Equality' focuses on the various movements and efforts made by different groups to achieve a society where everyone is treated with dignity, respect, and has equal opportunities and rights, regardless of their background.

17. Which of the following statements best describes the concept of 'gender stereotypes'?

  1. The biological differences between boys and girls
  2. The roles and responsibilities that society expects from boys and girls
  3. The legal rights granted to boys and girls
  4. The personal preferences of individuals regarding their work

Answer: The roles and responsibilities that society expects from boys and girls

Explanation: Gender stereotypes are oversimplified and often unfair beliefs about what men and women, or boys and girls, are like or what they should do. They are not about biological differences or legal rights, but rather about the societal expectations and roles assigned to each gender, which can limit individual choices and opportunities.

18. What is a 'public protest' often used for in a democracy?

  1. To cause chaos and disorder
  2. To express dissatisfaction and demand change from the government
  3. To promote a political party
  4. To entertain the public

Answer: To express dissatisfaction and demand change from the government

Explanation: In a democracy, public protests are a legitimate way for citizens to express their dissatisfaction with government policies or actions and to demand change. They are a form of collective action to make their voices heard and influence decision-making, not to cause chaos, promote a political party, or entertain.

19. Which of the following is an example of a 'local market'?

  1. An online shopping website
  2. A weekly haat or bazaar in a village
  3. A large shopping mall in a metropolitan city
  4. An international trade fair

Answer: A weekly haat or bazaar in a village

Explanation: A local market refers to a place where goods and services are exchanged within a specific, often smaller, geographical area. A weekly haat or bazaar in a village is a classic example of a local market, serving the immediate community. Online shopping, large malls, and international trade fairs represent broader or different types of markets.

20. What is the main purpose of a 'Minimum Wage Act'?

  1. To ensure workers are paid less than a certain amount
  2. To set the highest possible wages for all workers
  3. To protect workers from exploitation by ensuring they receive a fair basic wage
  4. To allow employers to pay any wage they deem fit

Answer: To protect workers from exploitation by ensuring they receive a fair basic wage

Explanation: The Minimum Wage Act is a crucial piece of legislation designed to protect workers, especially those in unorganized sectors, from exploitation. Its main purpose is to ensure that no worker is paid below a certain basic amount, thereby guaranteeing a fair and living wage and preventing employers from paying extremely low wages.

21. Who is responsible for making laws for a particular state in India?

  1. The Prime Minister
  2. The President
  3. The Members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs)
  4. The Chief Justice of India

Answer: The Members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs)

Explanation: In India, each state has its own Legislative Assembly. The Members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs) are elected by the people of that state and are responsible for discussing, debating, and passing laws that apply specifically to that state. The Prime Minister and President are part of the central government, and the Chief Justice is part of the judiciary.

22. Which of the following is a common stereotype associated with 'growing up as boys' in many societies?

  1. Expected to be emotional and sensitive.
  2. Encouraged to pursue careers in nursing or teaching.
  3. Taught to be strong, independent, and not show weakness.
  4. Primarily responsible for household chores.

Answer: Taught to be strong, independent, and not show weakness.

Explanation: Societies often have gender stereotypes that influence how boys and girls are raised. A common stereotype for boys is that they should be strong, tough, independent, and suppress emotions like crying or fear. This contrasts with stereotypes often associated with girls, such as being emotional or primarily responsible for domestic work.

23. Which of the following is an example of women 'changing the world' by breaking traditional gender roles?

  1. Women choosing to be homemakers.
  2. Women pursuing careers as pilots or engineers.
  3. Women participating in religious ceremonies.
  4. Women wearing traditional attire.

Answer: Women pursuing careers as pilots or engineers.

Explanation: Women 'changing the world' often refers to their efforts to challenge and break free from traditional gender roles and expectations. Pursuing careers in fields historically dominated by men, such as pilots or engineers, is a significant example of women expanding their opportunities and demonstrating their capabilities beyond societal norms. The other options, while valid choices, do not inherently represent a challenge to traditional gender roles.

24. What is the main purpose of advertising in media?

  1. To provide unbiased news and information.
  2. To entertain the audience with stories.
  3. To persuade consumers to buy certain products or services.
  4. To educate people about social issues.

Answer: To persuade consumers to buy certain products or services.

Explanation: Advertising is a key component of media, and its primary purpose is commercial. Advertisers pay media outlets to display messages designed to persuade consumers to purchase specific products or services, thereby generating revenue for both the advertisers and the media. While media can inform and entertain, advertising specifically aims at influencing consumer behavior.

25. Which type of market allows you to buy goods directly from the producer, often at a lower price?

  1. Shopping malls
  2. Weekly markets/Haats
  3. Online stores
  4. Departmental stores

Answer: Weekly markets/Haats

Explanation: Weekly markets, also known as 'haats' in India, are places where vendors, often producers themselves or small traders, set up stalls for a day or two each week. This direct interaction between producers/small traders and consumers often results in lower prices because there are fewer intermediaries involved in the supply chain compared to shopping malls, online stores, or departmental stores.
