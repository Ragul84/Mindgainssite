Class11 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class11 questions to revise the NCERT History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 455 questions.

1. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a reason for the displacement of indigenous peoples?

  1. Development projects
  2. Resource extraction
  3. Environmental changes
  4. Cultural festivals

Answer: Cultural festivals

Explanation: The text lists development projects, resource extraction, and environmental changes as reasons for displacement, but not cultural festivals.

2. What kind of disruptions can occur for indigenous communities due to displacement?

  1. Significant social, cultural, and economic disruptions.
  2. Minor inconveniences and temporary relocation.
  3. Increased political representation and autonomy.
  4. Enhanced preservation of traditional lifestyles.

Answer: Significant social, cultural, and economic disruptions.

Explanation: The text states that displacement often leads to significant social, cultural, and economic disruptions for these communities.

3. Modernisation efforts, aimed at improving infrastructure and economic growth, can sometimes result in the displacement of indigenous peoples. This statement is:

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The provided text explicitly states that modernization efforts can lead to the displacement of indigenous peoples.

4. Which of the following activities is mentioned as a potential cause for the displacement of indigenous peoples due to modernization?

  1. Construction of dams
  2. Establishment of libraries
  3. Organization of art exhibitions
  4. Development of educational programs

Answer: Construction of dams

Explanation: The text specifically mentions the construction of dams as an example of modernization that can lead to displacement.

5. What was the age of Charles Lindbergh when he made his historic transatlantic flight?

  1. 25 years old
  2. 30 years old
  3. 20 years old
  4. 35 years old

Answer: 25 years old

Explanation: The text mentions that Charles Lindbergh was 25 years old when he made his historic flight.

6. The event of Lindbergh's flight symbolized advancements in technology and the growing interconnectedness of the world. This statement is:

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text explicitly states that Lindbergh's flight symbolized advancements in technology and global interconnectedness.

7. Which periods are contrasted with the era symbolized by Lindbergh's flight?

  1. Medieval and early modern periods
  2. Ancient and classical periods
  3. Future and futuristic periods
  4. Industrial and post-industrial periods

Answer: Medieval and early modern periods

Explanation: The text mentions that Lindbergh's flight moved away from the medieval and early modern periods.

8. The European Renaissance is mentioned in relation to which historical periods?

  1. Medieval and early modern periods
  2. Ancient and classical periods
  3. Industrial and post-industrial periods
  4. Renaissance and Baroque periods

Answer: Medieval and early modern periods

Explanation: The text states that the medieval and early modern periods were characterized by feudalism and the European Renaissance.

9. What type of airplane did Charles Lindbergh use for his solo transatlantic flight?

  1. Single-engine airplane
  2. Biplane
  3. Jet airplane
  4. Seaplane

Answer: Single-engine airplane

Explanation: The text specifies that Lindbergh flew a single-engine airplane.

10. Which of the following is an example of a development project that can lead to the displacement of indigenous peoples?

  1. Construction of highways
  2. Building of museums
  3. Creation of national parks
  4. Establishment of universities

Answer: Construction of highways

Explanation: The text lists the construction of highways as an example of a development project that can cause displacement.

11. Lindbergh's flight contributed to the growing interactions between Europeans and the peoples of which continents?

  1. Asia, Africa, and the Americas
  2. Europe, Asia, and Africa
  3. North America, South America, and Europe
  4. Africa, Australia, and Asia

Answer: Asia, Africa, and the Americas

Explanation: The text mentions that Lindbergh's flight highlighted increasing interactions between Europeans and the peoples of Asia, Africa, and the Americas.

12. The text implies that the era before Lindbergh's flight was characterized by less global interconnectedness. This statement is:

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: By stating Lindbergh's flight symbolized growing interconnectedness and moved away from earlier periods, the text implies less interconnectedness before.

13. In which year did Charles Lindbergh complete his solo flight across the Atlantic Ocean?

  1. 1927
  2. 1917
  3. 1937
  4. 1907

Answer: 1927

Explanation: Charles Lindbergh made history in 1927 by flying solo from New York to Paris.

14. Charles Lindbergh flew from New York to which city?

  1. London
  2. Paris
  3. Berlin
  4. Madrid

Answer: Paris

Explanation: The flight was a solo journey from New York to Paris.

15. What did Lindbergh's flight symbolize in the context of global history?

  1. The return to feudalism
  2. The end of the Renaissance
  3. Advancements in technology and global connectivity
  4. The decline of aviation

Answer: Advancements in technology and global connectivity

Explanation: The flight symbolized technological advancements and the growing interconnectedness of the world.

16. What was the age of Charles Lindbergh when he made his historic flight?

  1. 20
  2. 25
  3. 30
  4. 35

Answer: 25

Explanation: The text states Charles Lindbergh was 25 years old during his flight.

17. What type of airplane did Charles Lindbergh use for his transatlantic flight?

  1. Double-engine airplane
  2. Jet engine airplane
  3. Single-engine airplane
  4. Glider

Answer: Single-engine airplane

Explanation: Lindbergh used a single-engine airplane for his journey.

18. Modernisation is defined as the process of societal transformation from traditional societies to:

  1. Feudal societies
  2. Modern societies
  3. Medieval societies
  4. Renaissance societies

Answer: Modern societies

Explanation: Modernisation is the transformation from traditional or pre-modern societies to modern societies.

19. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a sector marked by significant changes during modernisation?

  1. Technology
  2. Governance
  3. Agriculture
  4. Artistic painting

Answer: Artistic painting

Explanation: The text lists technology, industry, agriculture, and governance, but not artistic painting.

20. Where did the Industrial Revolution begin in the late 18th century?

  1. France
  2. USA
  3. Britain
  4. Germany

Answer: Britain

Explanation: The Industrial Revolution began in Britain in the late 18th century.

21. Which political revolutions are mentioned as crucial roles in transforming subjects?

  1. Russian and Chinese Revolutions
  2. American and French Revolutions
  3. Industrial and Agricultural Revolutions
  4. English and Belgian Revolutions

Answer: American and French Revolutions

Explanation: The American and French Revolutions are cited as crucial in transforming subjects.

22. What was the primary focus of the first phase of industrialization in Britain?

  1. Railways and Steel
  2. Coal and cotton textile industries
  3. Electronics and Chemicals
  4. Automobiles and Aviation

Answer: Coal and cotton textile industries

Explanation: The first phase focused on coal and cotton textile industries.

23. What marked the second phase of industrialization in Britain?

  1. The invention of the steam engine
  2. The development of railways
  3. The rise of cotton textiles
  4. The discovery of coal

Answer: The development of railways

Explanation: The second phase was marked by the development of railways.

24. According to recent historical research, how did other countries' industrialization relate to the British model?

  1. They replicated it exactly
  2. They ignored it completely
  3. They were influenced by it but were unique
  4. They developed before Britain

Answer: They were influenced by it but were unique

Explanation: Research suggests each country's industrialization was unique, influenced by but not replicating the British model.

25. The Industrial Revolution brought about mechanised manufacturing and increased productivity in which sector?

  1. Agriculture
  2. Fishing
  3. Hunting
  4. Mining

Answer: Agriculture

Explanation: The Industrial Revolution increased agricultural productivity.
