Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the NCERT History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 466 questions.

1. The Harappan Civilisation is also known by which other name?

  1. Indus Valley Civilisation
  2. Mesopotamian Civilisation
  3. Egyptian Civilisation
  4. Sumerian Civilisation

Answer: Indus Valley Civilisation

Explanation: The Harappan Civilisation is also referred to as the Indus Valley Civilisation.

2. Around what period did the Harappan Civilisation flourish?

  1. 2500-1900 BCE
  2. 3000-2000 BCE
  3. 2000-1500 BCE
  4. 1500-1000 BCE

Answer: 2500-1900 BCE

Explanation: The Harappan Civilisation flourished around 2500-1900 BCE, and in some sources, 2600-1900 BCE.

3. In which modern-day regions was the Harappan Civilisation located?

  1. Pakistan and Western India
  2. Eastern India and Bangladesh
  3. Central Asia
  4. Middle East

Answer: Pakistan and Western India

Explanation: The Harappan Civilisation was located in what is now Pakistan and Western India.

4. What is a key characteristic of the Harappan Civilisation's urban planning?

  1. Well-planned cities with brick houses
  2. Nomadic settlements
  3. Underground cave dwellings
  4. Wooden structures

Answer: Well-planned cities with brick houses

Explanation: The Harappan Civilisation is renowned for its advanced urban planning, including well-planned cities with brick houses.

5. Which of the following was a feature of the sophisticated drainage systems in Harappan cities?

  1. Public baths
  2. Open canals
  3. Wooden pipes
  4. No drainage system

Answer: Public baths

Explanation: Sophisticated drainage systems, including public baths, were a hallmark of Harappan urban planning.

6. Harappans used tools and ornaments made from which materials?

  1. Stone, metal, and clay
  2. Gold and silver only
  3. Wood and bone
  4. Bronze and iron

Answer: Stone, metal, and clay

Explanation: Tools and ornaments from the Harappan Civilisation were crafted from various materials like stone, metal, and clay.

7. What significant artefacts did the Harappans create featuring animal motifs and undeciphered script?

  1. Seals
  2. Pottery
  3. Sculptures
  4. Weapons

Answer: Seals

Explanation: Seals featuring animal motifs and undeciphered script are significant artefacts of the Harappan Civilisation.

8. The Harappan Civilisation flourished around 2600-1900 BCE.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The provided text states that the Harappan Civilisation flourished around 2600-1900 BCE.

9. The Harappan Civilisation was named after the modern-day country of India.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Harappan Civilisation was named after Harappa, one of the first cities excavated, which is located in Pakistan and India.

10. Harappan cities had rudimentary drainage systems.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The text describes the drainage systems as 'sophisticated', not rudimentary.

11. The Harappan Civilisation is known for its advanced urban planning.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The source material explicitly mentions the advanced urban planning of the Harappan Civilisation.

12. Harappans primarily used wooden tools.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Harappans used tools and ornaments made from stone, metal, and clay, not primarily wood.

13. What is the approximate timeframe for the flourishing of the Harappan Civilisation?

  1. 2600-1900 BCE
  2. 3000-2500 BCE
  3. 1900-1000 BCE
  4. 4000-3000 BCE

Answer: 2600-1900 BCE

Explanation: The text mentions the Harappan Civilisation flourishing around 2600-1900 BCE.

14. The Harappan Civilisation spanned parts of modern-day Pakistan and which other country?

  1. India
  2. Afghanistan
  3. Iran
  4. China

Answer: India

Explanation: The Harappan Civilisation's area spans parts of modern-day Pakistan and India.

15. Besides brick houses and drainage systems, what other urban feature is mentioned for Harappan cities?

  1. Granaries
  2. Temples
  3. Amphitheatres
  4. Palaces

Answer: Granaries

Explanation: The text mentions well-organized cities with brick houses, drainage systems, and granaries.

16. What do the artefacts like seals, pottery, and tools provide insights into?

  1. Social, economic, and cultural life
  2. Military strategies
  3. Religious rituals only
  4. Agricultural techniques

Answer: Social, economic, and cultural life

Explanation: Archaeologists have uncovered numerous artifacts that provide insights into the social, economic, and cultural aspects of the Harappan Civilisation.

17. What is another name for the Harappan Civilisation?

  1. Indus Valley Civilisation
  2. Mesopotamian Civilisation
  3. Egyptian Civilisation
  4. Chinese Civilisation

Answer: Indus Valley Civilisation

Explanation: The text states the Harappan Civilisation is also known as the Indus Valley Civilisation.

18. The Mature Harappan phase is characterized as the most:

  1. Prosperous and urbanized period
  2. Formative period
  3. Decline period
  4. Agricultural period

Answer: Prosperous and urbanized period

Explanation: The Mature Harappan phase (2600 BCE-1900 BCE) is described as the most prosperous and urbanized period.

19. Which of the following was one of the first cities to be excavated in the Indus Valley?

  1. Harappa
  2. Mohenjo-daro
  3. Lothal
  4. Kalibangan

Answer: Harappa

Explanation: The civilisation was named after Harappa, one of the first cities to be excavated.

20. The Early Harappan phase lasted from which period?

  1. 6000 BCE-2600 BCE
  2. 2600 BCE-1900 BCE
  3. 1900 BCE-1300 BCE
  4. 3000 BCE-1500 BCE

Answer: 6000 BCE-2600 BCE

Explanation: The Early Harappan phase (6000 BCE-2600 BCE) is considered the formative period.

21. Which of these is NOT mentioned as a feature of Harappan urban planning?

  1. Pyramids
  2. Brick houses
  3. Drainage systems
  4. Granaries

Answer: Pyramids

Explanation: The text mentions brick houses, drainage systems, and granaries, but not pyramids.

22. The Late Harappan phase lasted until which year?

  1. 1300 BCE
  2. 1900 BCE
  3. 2600 BCE
  4. 6000 BCE

Answer: 1300 BCE

Explanation: The Late Harappan phase lasted until 1300 BCE.

23. Which of the following materials was used for Harappan articles?

  1. Copper and bronze
  2. Iron and steel
  3. Plastic and glass
  4. Aluminum

Answer: Copper and bronze

Explanation: The text lists copper and bronze articles as distinctive artifacts.

24. In which of these modern-day countries did the Harappan Civilisation span?

  1. Pakistan and India
  2. Iran and Iraq
  3. Egypt and Sudan
  4. China and Vietnam

Answer: Pakistan and India

Explanation: The civilisation spans parts of modern-day Pakistan and India.

25. Which phase is considered the 'formative period' of the civilization?

  1. Early Harappan
  2. Mature Harappan
  3. Late Harappan
  4. Post-Harappan

Answer: Early Harappan

Explanation: The Early Harappan phase is considered the formative period of the civilization.
