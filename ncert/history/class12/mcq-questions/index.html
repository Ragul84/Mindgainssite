Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the NCERT History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 466 questions.

26. Which of these artifacts provides insights into the social and economic life of the Harappans?

  1. Seals
  2. Iron swords
  3. Paper scrolls
  4. Oil paintings

Answer: Seals

Explanation: Archaeologists uncovered seals, pottery, and tools to gain these insights.

27. The decline of the civilization began around which year?

  1. 1900 BCE
  2. 2600 BCE
  3. 1300 BCE
  4. 6000 BCE

Answer: 1900 BCE

Explanation: The decline of the civilization began around 1900 BCE.

28. Which of the following Indian states is mentioned as a site for Harappan finds?

  1. Gujarat
  2. Kerala
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. West Bengal

Answer: Gujarat

Explanation: Gujarat is listed among the Indian states where artifacts were discovered.

29. The Mature Harappan phase flourished between which years?

  1. 2600-1900 BCE
  2. 6000-2600 BCE
  3. 1900-1300 BCE
  4. 3000-2000 BCE

Answer: 2600-1900 BCE

Explanation: The Mature Harappan phase occurred from 2600 BCE to 1900 BCE.

30. Which of these is listed as a distinctive artifact of the Harappan culture?

  1. Beads
  2. Silk fabrics
  3. Marble statues
  4. Gold coins

Answer: Beads

Explanation: Beads are specifically mentioned as distinctive artifacts.

31. The Harappan Civilisation is described as one of the world's earliest:

  1. Urban settlements
  2. Nomadic tribes
  3. Mountain cultures
  4. Island societies

Answer: Urban settlements

Explanation: The text describes it as one of the world's earliest urban settlements.

32. The Harappan Civilization flourished from around 6000 BCE to 1300 BCE.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text explicitly states the timeframe from 6000 BCE to 1300 BCE.

33. The Late Harappan phase was the most prosperous period of the civilization.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Mature Harappan phase, not the Late phase, was the most prosperous.

34. Harappan artifacts have been discovered in Afghanistan.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text mentions artifacts were discovered in Afghanistan, Pakistan, and several Indian states.

35. The Harappan civilization had no trade or cultural influence on other regions.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The finds highlight the extensive trade and cultural influence of the Harappan people.

36. During which period did the Harappan Civilization flourish?

  1. 2600-1900 BCE
  2. 3000-2000 BCE
  3. 2500-1500 BCE
  4. 1900-1200 BCE

Answer: 2600-1900 BCE

Explanation: The text states the civilization flourished around 2600-1900 BCE.

37. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a distinctive artifact of the Harappan Civilization?

  1. Iron tools
  2. Seals
  3. Beads
  4. Pottery

Answer: Iron tools

Explanation: The text mentions copper and bronze articles, but not iron tools.

38. In which region did the Harappan Civilization flourish?

  1. Ganges Valley
  2. Indus Valley
  3. Narmada Valley
  4. Kaveri Valley

Answer: Indus Valley

Explanation: The civilization flourished in the Indus Valley region.

39. Which of these modern-day countries is mentioned as a location where Harappan artifacts were discovered?

  1. Afghanistan
  2. Sri Lanka
  3. Nepal
  4. Myanmar

Answer: Afghanistan

Explanation: Artifacts were discovered in parts of modern-day Afghanistan, Pakistan, and India.

40. What do the wide geographical discoveries of Harappan artifacts highlight?

  1. Isolation of the people
  2. Extensive trade and cultural influence
  3. Lack of urban planning
  4. Agricultural decline

Answer: Extensive trade and cultural influence

Explanation: The finds highlight the extensive trade and cultural influence of the Harappan people.

41. What materials were used for making articles in the Harappan Civilization?

  1. Copper and Bronze
  2. Iron and Steel
  3. Gold and Silver
  4. Aluminum and Zinc

Answer: Copper and Bronze

Explanation: The text specifically mentions copper and bronze articles.

42. What characterized the early archaeological cultures before the Mature Harappan period?

  1. Industrial machinery
  2. Unique pottery styles and artifacts
  3. Written scriptures
  4. Iron smelting

Answer: Unique pottery styles and artifacts

Explanation: Early cultures are identified by their unique pottery styles and other artifacts.

43. What did the early archaeological cultures lay the foundation for?

  1. Agricultural decline
  2. Urbanization in the Mature Harappan period
  3. Nomadic lifestyles
  4. The Vedic period

Answer: Urbanization in the Mature Harappan period

Explanation: These early cultures laid the foundation for the urbanization that followed in the Mature Harappan period.

44. Which state is mentioned alongside Punjab and Haryana?

  1. Bihar
  2. Rajasthan
  3. West Bengal
  4. Assam

Answer: Rajasthan

Explanation: Rajasthan is listed as one of the Indian states where artifacts were found.

45. The Harappan Civilization is described as one of the earliest ______ cultures.

  1. Rural
  2. Urban
  3. Nomadic
  4. Forest

Answer: Urban

Explanation: The text describes it as one of the earliest urban cultures.

46. Which of these is mentioned as a location of discovery?

  1. Maharashtra
  2. Karnataka
  3. Andhra Pradesh
  4. Telangana

Answer: Maharashtra

Explanation: Maharashtra is listed as one of the Indian states where artifacts were discovered.

47. What was the primary identifier for early archaeological cultures in the Indus Valley?

  1. Coinage
  2. Pottery styles
  3. Stone monuments
  4. Written laws

Answer: Pottery styles

Explanation: These cultures are identified by their unique pottery styles and other artifacts.

48. Which of the following is mentioned as a location for Harappan finds?

  1. Uttar Pradesh
  2. Bihar
  3. Jharkhand
  4. Chhattisgarh

Answer: Uttar Pradesh

Explanation: Uttar Pradesh is mentioned as one of the Indian states where artifacts were found.

49. Early archaeological cultures flourished before the rise of the Mature Harappan civilization.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The text explicitly states that several cultures flourished before the rise of the Mature Harappan civilization.

50. Harappan artifacts were only discovered within the borders of modern-day India.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Artifacts were also found in Afghanistan and Pakistan.
