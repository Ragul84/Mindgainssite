26. If 3x + 7 = 19, what is the value of x?

  1. 4
  2. 6
  3. 12
  4. 2

Answer: 4

Explanation: To solve the equation 3x + 7 = 19 for x, we need to isolate x. First, subtract 7 from both sides of the equation: 3x + 7 - 7 = 19 - 7. This simplifies to 3x = 12. Next, divide both sides by 3 to find the value of x: 3x / 3 = 12 / 3. This gives x = 4. Therefore, the value of x is 4.

27. Two angles are complementary if their sum is 90°. If one angle is 35°, what is its complementary angle?

  1. 145°
  2. 55°
  3. 65°
  4. 90°

Answer: 55°

Explanation: Complementary angles add up to 90°. Let the unknown complementary angle be 'y'. We are given one angle as 35°. So, 35° + y = 90°. To find y, subtract 35° from both sides: y = 90° - 35°. Therefore, y = 55°. The complementary angle is 55°.

28. In a triangle, if two angles are 60° and 70°, what is the measure of the third angle?

  1. 50°
  2. 60°
  3. 70°
  4. 130°

Answer: 50°

Explanation: The sum of the angles in any triangle is always 180°. We are given two angles: 60° and 70°. Let the third angle be 'z'. So, 60° + 70° + z = 180°. First, add the given angles: 60° + 70° = 130°. Now, the equation becomes 130° + z = 180°. To find z, subtract 130° from both sides: z = 180° - 130°. Therefore, z = 50°. The measure of the third angle is 50°.

29. A shop offers a 10% discount on a shirt priced at ₹800. What is the discount amount?

  1. ₹80
  2. ₹720
  3. ₹100
  4. ₹800

Answer: ₹80

Explanation: To find the discount amount, you need to calculate 10% of the original price. The original price is ₹800. To calculate 10% of 800, you can multiply 800 by 10/100 or 0.10. So, Discount = 10/100 × 800 = (10 × 800) / 100 = 8000 / 100 = 80. Therefore, the discount amount is ₹80.

30. Which of the following is a rational number equivalent to -3/5?

  1. -6/10
  2. 3/5
  3. -3/-5
  4. 6/10

Answer: -6/10

Explanation: A rational number equivalent to -3/5 can be obtained by multiplying both the numerator and the denominator by the same non-zero integer. Let's check the options:
1. -6/10: If we multiply the numerator (-3) by 2 and the denominator (5) by 2, we get -6/10. This is equivalent.
2. 3/5: This is the positive counterpart, not equivalent.
3. -3/-5: This simplifies to 3/5 (negative divided by negative is positive), which is not equivalent to -3/5.
4. 6/10: This simplifies to 3/5, which is not equivalent to -3/5.
Therefore, -6/10 is equivalent to -3/5.

31. The perimeter of a square field is 36 meters. What is the length of one side of the field?

  1. 9 meters
  2. 18 meters
  3. 6 meters
  4. 12 meters

Answer: 9 meters

Explanation: The perimeter of a square is the sum of the lengths of all its four equal sides. The formula for the perimeter of a square is P = 4 × side. We are given that the perimeter (P) is 36 meters. So, 36 = 4 × side. To find the length of one side, divide the perimeter by 4: side = 36 / 4. Therefore, the length of one side of the field is 9 meters.

32. If 2/3 of a number is 18, what is the number?

  1. 12
  2. 27
  3. 36
  4. 9

Answer: 27

Explanation: Let the unknown number be 'x'.
The problem states that 2/3 of the number is 18. This can be written as an equation:
(2/3) * x = 18
To find 'x', we need to isolate it. We can do this by multiplying both sides of the equation by the reciprocal of 2/3, which is 3/2.
x = 18 * (3/2)
x = (18 * 3) / 2
x = 54 / 2
x = 27
So, the number is 27.

33. Two angles are complementary. If one angle is 35°, what is the measure of the other angle?

  1. 145°
  2. 55°
  3. 90°
  4. 65°

Answer: 55°

Explanation: Complementary angles are two angles whose sum is 90°.
Let the two complementary angles be A and B.
We are given that A + B = 90°.
One angle is given as 35°. Let's say A = 35°.
So, 35° + B = 90°.
To find B, subtract 35° from both sides of the equation:
B = 90° - 35°
B = 55°.
Therefore, the measure of the other angle is 55°.

34. Which of the following expressions is equivalent to 3x + 5y - x + 2y?

  1. 2x + 7y
  2. 4x + 7y
  3. 2x + 3y
  4. 4x + 3y

Answer: 2x + 7y

Explanation: To simplify the expression 3x + 5y - x + 2y, we need to combine like terms. Like terms are terms that have the same variables raised to the same powers.
Step 1: Identify the 'x' terms: 3x and -x.
Step 2: Combine the 'x' terms: 3x - x = (3 - 1)x = 2x.
Step 3: Identify the 'y' terms: 5y and 2y.
Step 4: Combine the 'y' terms: 5y + 2y = (5 + 2)y = 7y.
Step 5: Combine the simplified 'x' and 'y' terms: 2x + 7y.
Therefore, the equivalent expression is 2x + 7y.

35. A rectangular park has a length of 45 meters and a width of 20 meters. What is the area of the park?

  1. 130 square meters
  2. 900 square meters
  3. 65 square meters
  4. 90 square meters

Answer: 900 square meters

Explanation: The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width.
Given:
Length (L) = 45 meters
Width (W) = 20 meters
Formula for the area of a rectangle (A) = L × W
Substitute the given values into the formula:
A = 45 meters × 20 meters
A = 900 square meters
Therefore, the area of the park is 900 square meters.
