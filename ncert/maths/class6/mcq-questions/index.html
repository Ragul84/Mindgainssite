26. How is 0.75 written as a fraction in its simplest form?

  1. 75/100
  2. 3/4
  3. 7/5
  4. 1/4

Answer: 3/4

Explanation: The decimal 0.75 means 75 hundredths, which can be written as the fraction 75/100. To simplify this fraction, we find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 75 and 100. The GCD of 75 and 100 is 25. Dividing both the numerator and the denominator by 25, we get 75 ÷ 25 = 3 and 100 ÷ 25 = 4. So, the simplest form of the fraction is 3/4.

27. What is the equivalent fraction of 3/5 with denominator 20?

  1. 6/20
  2. 9/20
  3. 12/20
  4. 15/20

Answer: 12/20

Explanation: To find an equivalent fraction, we multiply both the numerator and the denominator by the same non-zero number. We want the denominator to be 20. Since 5 multiplied by 4 gives 20, we multiply both the numerator and the denominator of 3/5 by 4. So, (3 × 4) / (5 × 4) = 12/20.

28. If 'x' represents a number, which of the following expressions represents '5 more than twice the number x'?

  1. 5x + 2
  2. 2x + 5
  3. x + 2 + 5
  4. 2(x + 5)

Answer: 2x + 5

Explanation: Let's break down the phrase:
- 'twice the number x' means 2 multiplied by x, which is 2x.
- '5 more than' means we add 5 to the previous expression. So, it becomes 2x + 5.

29. The perimeter of a square with side length 5 cm is:

  1. 10 cm
  2. 20 cm
  3. 25 cm
  4. 15 cm

Answer: 20 cm

Explanation: The perimeter of a square is the total length of its boundary. Since all four sides of a square are equal, the formula for the perimeter of a square is 4 × side length. Given the side length is 5 cm, the perimeter is 4 × 5 cm = 20 cm.

30. What is the successor of the largest 4-digit number?

  1. 9999
  2. 10000
  3. 9998
  4. 1000

Answer: 10000

Explanation: The largest 4-digit number is 9999. The successor of a number is obtained by adding 1 to it. So, the successor of 9999 is 9999 + 1 = 10000.

31. What is the value of (-5) + 7?

  1. 12
  2. -12
  3. 2
  4. -2

Answer: 2

Explanation: When adding integers with different signs, we subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger absolute value and keep the sign of the integer with the larger absolute value. Here, the absolute value of -5 is 5 and the absolute value of 7 is 7. Subtracting 5 from 7 gives 2. Since 7 has a larger absolute value and is positive, the answer is +2.

'5 more than twice the number' means we add 5 to 2x. So, the expression is 2x + 5.

34. Which of the following is the smallest 4-digit number that can be formed using the digits 9, 0, 2, 5 exactly once?

  1. 2059
  2. 0259
  3. 9520
  4. 2095

Answer: 2059

Explanation: To form the smallest 4-digit number, we need to arrange the given digits in ascending order. The digits are 9, 0, 2, 5. Arranging them in ascending order gives 0, 2, 5, 9. However, a 4-digit number cannot start with 0. So, we place the next smallest digit (2) at the thousands place, followed by 0, and then the remaining digits in ascending order. Thus, the smallest 4-digit number formed is 2059.

35. What is the perimeter of a rectangle whose length is 10 cm and breadth is 5 cm?

  1. 15 cm
  2. 50 cm
  3. 30 cm
  4. 25 cm

Answer: 30 cm

Explanation: The perimeter of a rectangle is calculated using the formula: Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth). Given length = 10 cm and breadth = 5 cm. So, Perimeter = 2 × (10 cm + 5 cm) = 2 × (15 cm) = 30 cm.
