26. A quadrilateral has diagonals that bisect each other at right angles. The quadrilateral is a:

  1. Rectangle
  2. Rhombus
  3. Parallelogram
  4. Trapezium

Answer: Rhombus

Explanation: Properties of quadrilaterals:
- In a rectangle, diagonals bisect each other but not necessarily at right angles.
- In a rhombus, diagonals bisect each other at right angles.
- In a parallelogram, diagonals bisect each other but not necessarily at right angles.
- In a trapezium, diagonals do not necessarily bisect each other.
Therefore, a quadrilateral whose diagonals bisect each other at right angles is a rhombus.

27. What is the degree of the polynomial p(x) = 5x³ - 2x² + 7x - 9?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4

Answer: 3

Explanation: The degree of a polynomial is the highest power of the variable in the polynomial. In the given polynomial p(x) = 5x³ - 2x² + 7x - 9, the highest power of x is 3 (from the term 5x³). Therefore, the degree of the polynomial is 3.

28. The coordinates of a point are (3, -5). In which quadrant does this point lie?

  1. First Quadrant
  2. Second Quadrant
  3. Third Quadrant
  4. Fourth Quadrant

Answer: Fourth Quadrant

Explanation: In the Cartesian coordinate system:
- First Quadrant: x > 0, y > 0
- Second Quadrant: x < 0, y > 0
- Third Quadrant: x < 0, y < 0
- Fourth Quadrant: x > 0, y < 0
For the point (3, -5), the x-coordinate is positive (3 > 0) and the y-coordinate is negative (-5 < 0). Therefore, the point lies in the Fourth Quadrant.

29. Which of the following is a solution to the equation 2x + y = 7?

  1. (1, 4)
  2. (2, 2)
  3. (3, 1)
  4. (0, 6)

Answer: (3, 1)

Explanation: To find the solution, substitute the x and y values from each option into the equation 2x + y = 7:
- For (1, 4): 2(1) + 4 = 2 + 4 = 6 ≠ 7
- For (2, 2): 2(2) + 2 = 4 + 2 = 6 ≠ 7
- For (3, 1): 2(3) + 1 = 6 + 1 = 7. This is a solution.
- For (0, 6): 2(0) + 6 = 0 + 6 = 6 ≠ 7
Thus, (3, 1) is the correct solution.

31. In ΔABC, if AB = AC and ∠B = 50°, then ∠A is equal to:

  1. 50°
  2. 80°
  3. 100°
  4. 130°

Answer: 80°

Explanation: In ΔABC, if AB = AC, then the angles opposite to these equal sides are also equal. So, ∠C = ∠B = 50°.
The sum of angles in a triangle is 180°. Therefore, ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°.
∠A + 50° + 50° = 180°
∠A + 100° = 180°
∠A = 180° - 100°
∠A = 80°.

32. The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. What is the perimeter of the rhombus?

  1. 10 cm
  2. 20 cm
  3. 40 cm
  4. 80 cm

Answer: 40 cm

Explanation: In a rhombus, the diagonals bisect each other at right angles. Let the diagonals be d₁ = 16 cm and d₂ = 12 cm. When they bisect, they form four right-angled triangles. The half-diagonals will be d₁/2 = 16/2 = 8 cm and d₂/2 = 12/2 = 6 cm. These half-diagonals are the legs of the right-angled triangles, and the side of the rhombus is the hypotenuse.
Using the Pythagorean theorem: side² = (d₁/2)² + (d₂/2)²
side² = 8² + 6²
side² = 64 + 36
side² = 100
side = √100 = 10 cm.
The perimeter of a rhombus is 4 × side.
Perimeter = 4 × 10 cm = 40 cm.

33. The area of a triangle whose sides are 5 cm, 12 cm, and 13 cm is:

  1. 30 cm²
  2. 60 cm²
  3. 15 cm²
  4. 78 cm²

Answer: 30 cm²

Explanation: First, check if it's a right-angled triangle using the Pythagorean theorem: 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169. And 13² = 169. Since 5² + 12² = 13², it is a right-angled triangle with legs 5 cm and 12 cm.
The area of a right-angled triangle = (1/2) × base × height.
Area = (1/2) × 5 cm × 12 cm = (1/2) × 60 cm² = 30 cm².
Alternatively, using Heron's formula:
Semi-perimeter (s) = (5 + 12 + 13) / 2 = 30 / 2 = 15 cm.
Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]
Area = √[15(15-5)(15-12)(15-13)]
Area = √[15 × 10 × 3 × 2]
Area = √[900]
Area = 30 cm².
