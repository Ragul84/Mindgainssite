1. Which of the following rational numbers lies between -2/3 and 1/2?

  1. -1/6
  2. 5/6
  3. -5/6
  4. 7/6

Answer: -1/6

Explanation: To find a rational number between -2/3 and 1/2, we can find a common denominator. The LCM of 3 and 2 is 6. So, -2/3 = -4/6 and 1/2 = 3/6. The rational numbers between -4/6 and 3/6 are -3/6, -2/6, -1/6, 0/6, 1/6, 2/6. Among the given options, -1/6 is the only one that lies between -2/3 and 1/2.

2. If 3x - 5 = x + 7, what is the value of x?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 6
  4. 12

Answer: 6

Explanation: To solve the equation 3x - 5 = x + 7, we need to isolate x.
Step 1: Subtract x from both sides: 3x - x - 5 = 7 => 2x - 5 = 7.
Step 2: Add 5 to both sides: 2x = 7 + 5 => 2x = 12.
Step 3: Divide both sides by 2: x = 12 / 2 => x = 6.
Therefore, the value of x is 6.

3. The sum of the interior angles of a regular polygon is 1080°. How many sides does the polygon have?

  1. 6
  2. 7
  3. 8
  4. 9

Answer: 8

Explanation: The formula for the sum of the interior angles of a polygon with 'n' sides is (n - 2) × 180°.
Given that the sum is 1080°, we have (n - 2) × 180° = 1080°.
Divide both sides by 180°: n - 2 = 1080 / 180 => n - 2 = 6.
Add 2 to both sides: n = 6 + 2 => n = 8.
So, the polygon has 8 sides (it's an octagon).

5. What is the cube root of 1728?

  1. 10
  2. 12
  3. 14
  4. 16

Answer: 12

Explanation: To find the cube root of 1728, we can use prime factorization or estimation.
Using prime factorization:
1728 = 2 × 864 = 2 × 2 × 432 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 216 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 108 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 54 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 27 = 2⁶ × 3³.
For the cube root, we divide the exponents by 3: (2⁶ × 3³)¹/³ = 2^(6/3) × 3^(3/3) = 2² × 3¹ = 4 × 3 = 12.
Alternatively, by estimation: We know 10³ = 1000 and 20³ = 8000. So the cube root of 1728 is between 10 and 20. The last digit of 1728 is 8, and the only digit whose cube ends in 8 is 2 (since 2³ = 8). So, the cube root must be 12.

6. A shopkeeper buys an article for ₹400 and sells it for ₹480. What is the profit percentage?

  1. 10%
  2. 15%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Cost Price (CP) = ₹400
Selling Price (SP) = ₹480
Profit = SP - CP = ₹480 - ₹400 = ₹80.
Profit percentage = (Profit / CP) × 100%
Profit percentage = (80 / 400) × 100% = (1/5) × 100% = 20%.
Therefore, the profit percentage is 20%.

7. What is the product of (2x + 3y) and (2x - 3y)?

  1. 4x² + 9y²
  2. 4x² - 9y²
  3. 4x² + 12xy + 9y²
  4. 4x² - 12xy + 9y²

Answer: 4x² - 9y²

Explanation: This is an application of the algebraic identity (a + b)(a - b) = a² - b².
Here, a = 2x and b = 3y.
So, (2x + 3y)(2x - 3y) = (2x)² - (3y)²
= 4x² - 9y².
Therefore, the product is 4x² - 9y².

9. Which of the following is the additive inverse of -7/19?

  1. 7/19
  2. -7/19
  3. 19/7
  4. -19/7

Answer: 7/19

Explanation: The additive inverse of a number 'a' is a number 'b' such that a + b = 0. For -7/19, its additive inverse is 7/19 because (-7/19) + (7/19) = 0.

10. If 3x - 5 = 10, what is the value of x?

  1. 3
  2. 5
  3. 15
  4. 10

Answer: 5

Explanation: To solve the equation 3x - 5 = 10, we first add 5 to both sides: 3x - 5 + 5 = 10 + 5, which simplifies to 3x = 15. Then, we divide both sides by 3: 3x/3 = 15/3, which gives x = 5.

11. The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is:

  1. 180 degrees
  2. 360 degrees
  3. 90 degrees
  4. 270 degrees

Answer: 360 degrees

Explanation: A quadrilateral can be divided into two triangles by drawing one diagonal. Since the sum of angles in a triangle is 180 degrees, the sum of angles in a quadrilateral is 2 * 180 degrees = 360 degrees.

12. What is the square root of 144?

  1. 10
  2. 12
  3. 14
  4. 16

Answer: 12

Explanation: The square root of a number 'n' is a number 'x' such that x * x = n. For 144, 12 * 12 = 144, so the square root of 144 is 12.

13. What is the cube of 4?

  1. 8
  2. 16
  3. 64
  4. 32

Answer: 64

Explanation: The cube of a number 'n' is n multiplied by itself three times, i.e., n * n * n. So, the cube of 4 is 4 * 4 * 4 = 16 * 4 = 64.

14. If the price of an item is increased by 20% and its original price was ₹500, what is the new price?

  1. ₹520
  2. ₹600
  3. ₹400
  4. ₹550

Answer: ₹600

Explanation: An increase of 20% on ₹500 means the increase amount is (20/100) * 500 = ₹100. The new price will be the original price plus the increase: ₹500 + ₹100 = ₹600.

15. Simplify the expression: (a + b)²

  1. a² + b²
  2. a² - b²
  3. a² + 2ab + b²
  4. a² - 2ab + b²

Answer: a² + 2ab + b²

Explanation: The expression (a + b)² is an algebraic identity which expands to (a + b) * (a + b). Using the distributive property, this becomes a(a + b) + b(a + b) = a² + ab + ba + b² = a² + 2ab + b².

16. The area of a rectangle with length 10 cm and width 5 cm is:

  1. 15 cm²
  2. 50 cm²
  3. 25 cm²
  4. 30 cm²

Answer: 50 cm²

Explanation: The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width. So, Area = Length × Width = 10 cm × 5 cm = 50 cm².

17. Solve for x: 3x - 5 = x + 7

  1. x = 1
  2. x = 2
  3. x = 6
  4. x = 12

Answer: x = 6

Explanation: To solve the equation 3x - 5 = x + 7, we need to isolate 'x'.
Step 1: Subtract 'x' from both sides: 3x - x - 5 = x - x + 7 => 2x - 5 = 7
Step 2: Add 5 to both sides: 2x - 5 + 5 = 7 + 5 => 2x = 12
Step 3: Divide both sides by 2: 2x / 2 = 12 / 2 => x = 6.

18. The sum of the measures of the exterior angles of any convex polygon is:

  1. 180 degrees
  2. 360 degrees
  3. 540 degrees
  4. 720 degrees

Answer: 360 degrees

Explanation: For any convex polygon, regardless of the number of sides, the sum of the measures of its exterior angles (one at each vertex) is always 360 degrees. This is a fundamental property of polygons.

19. What is the cube of 5?

  1. 15
  2. 25
  3. 125
  4. 625

Answer: 125

Explanation: The cube of a number 'n' is 'n' multiplied by itself three times, i.e., n * n * n. For the number 5, its cube is 5 * 5 * 5.
5 * 5 = 25
25 * 5 = 125. So, the cube of 5 is 125.

20. Simplify the expression: (2x + 3y) - (x - 2y)

  1. x + y
  2. x + 5y
  3. 3x + y
  4. 3x + 5y

Answer: x + 5y

Explanation: To simplify the expression (2x + 3y) - (x - 2y), we first distribute the negative sign to the terms inside the second parenthesis:
(2x + 3y) - x + 2y
Now, combine like terms:
(2x - x) + (3y + 2y)
x + 5y

21. The area of a rectangle is 48 cm². If its length is 8 cm, what is its breadth?

  1. 4 cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 8 cm
  4. 12 cm

Answer: 6 cm

Explanation: The formula for the area of a rectangle is Area = Length × Breadth.
Given: Area = 48 cm², Length = 8 cm.
Let Breadth be 'b'.
So, 48 = 8 × b
To find 'b', divide both sides by 8:
b = 48 / 8
b = 6 cm. Therefore, the breadth of the rectangle is 6 cm.

22. The sum of the measures of the exterior angles of any polygon is always:

  1. 180°
  2. 360°
  3. 90°
  4. 540°

Answer: 360°

Explanation: A fundamental property of polygons is that the sum of the measures of their exterior angles (one at each vertex) is always 360°, regardless of the number of sides the polygon has.

23. A shop offers a 20% discount on all items. If a shirt costs ₹800, what is its selling price after the discount?

  1. ₹640
  2. ₹160
  3. ₹780
  4. ₹820

Answer: ₹640

Explanation: First, calculate the discount amount: 20% of ₹800 = (20/100) * 800 = 0.20 * 800 = ₹160. Then, subtract the discount from the original price to find the selling price: ₹800 - ₹160 = ₹640.

24. What is the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?

  1. 180°
  2. 360°
  3. 540°
  4. 720°

Answer: 360°

Explanation: The sum of the interior angles of a polygon with 'n' sides is given by the formula (n - 2) × 180°. For a quadrilateral, n = 4. So, the sum of interior angles = (4 - 2) × 180° = 2 × 180° = 360°.
