26. What is the value of (2x + 3y)²?

  1. 4x² + 9y²
  2. 4x² + 6xy + 9y²
  3. 4x² + 12xy + 9y²
  4. 2x² + 12xy + 3y²

Answer: 4x² + 12xy + 9y²

Explanation: This is an application of the algebraic identity (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b². Here, a = 2x and b = 3y.
So, (2x + 3y)² = (2x)² + 2(2x)(3y) + (3y)²
= 4x² + 12xy + 9y².

28. The volume of a cube is 216 cm³. What is the length of its side?

  1. 4 cm
  2. 5 cm
  3. 6 cm
  4. 7 cm

Answer: 6 cm

Explanation: The volume of a cube is given by the formula V = s³, where 's' is the length of its side. We are given V = 216 cm³.
So, s³ = 216.
To find 's', we need to find the cube root of 216.
We know that 6 × 6 × 6 = 36 × 6 = 216.
Therefore, s = ³√216 = 6 cm.

29. Factorise: 6xy - 4y + 6 - 9x

  1. (2y - 3)(3x - 2)
  2. (2y + 3)(3x - 2)
  3. (2y - 3)(3x + 2)
  4. (2y + 3)(3x + 2)

Answer: (2y - 3)(3x - 2)

Explanation: To factorise 6xy - 4y + 6 - 9x, we can group terms:
Group 1: 6xy - 4y. Take out the common factor 2y: 2y(3x - 2).
Group 2: 6 - 9x. Take out the common factor -3: -3(3x - 2).
Now, the expression becomes 2y(3x - 2) - 3(3x - 2).
Take out the common binomial factor (3x - 2):
(3x - 2)(2y - 3).
So, the factorised form is (2y - 3)(3x - 2).

30. The sum of the interior angles of a polygon with 'n' sides is given by the formula (n-2) × 180°. What is the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon?

  1. 180°
  2. 360°
  3. 540°
  4. 720°

Answer: 720°

Explanation: A hexagon is a polygon with 6 sides, so n = 6.
Using the formula for the sum of interior angles: (n-2) × 180°
Substitute n = 6: (6-2) × 180°
Calculate: 4 × 180° = 720°.
So, the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon is 720°.

31. What is the value of (2⁻¹)⁻²?

  1. 1/4
  2. 4
  3. -4
  4. 1/2

Answer: 4

Explanation: We use the exponent rule (a^m)^n = a^(m*n).
Here, a = 2, m = -1, and n = -2.
So, (2⁻¹)⁻² = 2^((-1) × (-2)) = 2^(2).
2² = 2 × 2 = 4.
Therefore, the value of (2⁻¹)⁻² is 4.

  3. ₹490
  4. ₹510

Answer: ₹450

Explanation: The marked price of the item is ₹500.
The discount offered is 10%.

The selling price is the marked price minus the discount amount.
Selling Price = Marked Price - Discount Amount = ₹500 - ₹50 = ₹450.

33. The sum of three consecutive multiples of 8 is 888. Find the smallest multiple.

  1. 288
  2. 296
  3. 304
  4. 280

Answer: 288

Explanation: Let the three consecutive multiples of 8 be 8x, 8(x+1), and 8(x+2).
According to the problem, their sum is 888.
So, 8x + 8(x+1) + 8(x+2) = 888
8x + 8x + 8 + 8x + 16 = 888
24x + 24 = 888
24x = 888 - 24
24x = 864
x = 864 / 24
x = 36
The smallest multiple is 8x = 8 * 36 = 288.
The other multiples are 8(36+1) = 8 * 37 = 296 and 8(36+2) = 8 * 38 = 304.
Check: 288 + 296 + 304 = 888.

34. Which of the following is a perfect square?

  1. 1024
  2. 1025
  3. 1026
  4. 1027

Answer: 1024

Explanation: To find if a number is a perfect square, we can try to find its square root or look for properties of perfect squares.
Let's check the options:
1. 1024: We know that 30^2 = 900 and 32^2 = 1024. So, 1024 is a perfect square (32 x 32).
2. 1025: Ends with 5, so its square root must end with 5. However, 30^2 = 900, 35^2 = 1225. 1025 is not a perfect square.
3. 1026: Ends with 6. A perfect square ending in 6 must have a unit digit of 4 or 6 in its square root. We already know 32^2 = 1024. 34^2 = 1156. 1026 is not a perfect square.
4. 1027: Ends with 7. No perfect square can end with the digit 2, 3, 7, or 8. So, 1027 is not a perfect square.
Therefore, 1024 is the only perfect square among the given options.

35. The interior angle of a regular polygon is 108°. How many sides does the polygon have?

  1. 5
  2. 6
  3. 7
  4. 8

Answer: 5

Explanation: For a regular polygon with 'n' sides, the formula for each interior angle is given by:
Interior Angle = (n - 2) × 180° / n
We are given that the interior angle is 108°.
So, 108 = (n - 2) × 180 / n
108n = 180n - 360
360 = 180n - 108n
360 = 72n
n = 360 / 72
n = 5
Alternatively, we can find the exterior angle first. The sum of an interior angle and its corresponding exterior angle is 180°.
Exterior Angle = 180° - Interior Angle = 180° - 108° = 72°.
For any regular polygon, the sum of all exterior angles is 360°.
If 'n' is the number of sides, then n × (Exterior Angle) = 360°.
n × 72° = 360°
n = 360° / 72°
n = 5
Thus, the polygon has 5 sides, which means it is a pentagon.
