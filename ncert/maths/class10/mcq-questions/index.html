26. The sum of the first 15 multiples of 8 is:

  1. 960
  2. 1000
  3. 1020
  4. 1080

Answer: 960

Explanation: The first 15 multiples of 8 form an Arithmetic Progression (AP):
8, 16, 24, ..., (8 × 15)
Here, the first term (a) = 8
The common difference (d) = 8
The number of terms (n) = 15
The last term (l) = 8 × 15 = 120
The sum of an AP is given by the formula S_n = n/2 * (a + l)
S_15 = 15/2 * (8 + 120)
S_15 = 15/2 * (128)
S_15 = 15 * 64
S_15 = 960.

27. A tangent PQ at a point P of a circle of radius 5 cm meets a line through the centre O at a point Q such that OQ = 12 cm. The length of PQ is:

  1. 13 cm
  2. 8.5 cm
  3. √119 cm
  4. 12 cm

Answer: √119 cm

Explanation: According to the theorem, the tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact. Therefore, ∠OPQ = 90°.
We have a right-angled triangle ΔOPQ, where:
OP = radius = 5 cm
OQ = 12 cm
PQ is the tangent.
Using the Pythagorean theorem: OP² + PQ² = OQ²
5² + PQ² = 12²
25 + PQ² = 144
PQ² = 144 - 25
PQ² = 119
PQ = √119 cm.

28. A bag contains 3 red balls and 5 black balls. A ball is drawn at random from the bag. What is the probability that the ball drawn is red?

  1. 3/8
  2. 5/8
  3. 1/2
  4. 3/5

Answer: 3/8

Explanation: The total number of balls in the bag is the sum of red balls and black balls.
Number of red balls = 3
Number of black balls = 5
Total number of balls = 3 + 5 = 8
The number of favorable outcomes (drawing a red ball) = 3
The probability of an event is given by the formula: P(E) = (Number of favorable outcomes) / (Total number of possible outcomes).
Probability of drawing a red ball = (Number of red balls) / (Total number of balls)
P(Red) = 3 / 8.

29. If α and β are the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial p(x) = x² - 5x + 6, then the value of α + β is:

  1. -5
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. -6

Answer: 5

Explanation: For a quadratic polynomial ax² + bx + c, the sum of the zeroes (α + β) is given by -b/a.
In the given polynomial p(x) = x² - 5x + 6, we have a = 1, b = -5, and c = 6.
Therefore, α + β = -(-5)/1 = 5/1 = 5.

30. If the 17th term of an AP exceeds its 10th term by 7, then the common difference is:

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 7

Answer: 1

Explanation: Let the first term of the AP be 'a' and the common difference be 'd'.
The nth term of an AP is given by a_n = a + (n-1)d.
According to the problem, the 17th term exceeds the 10th term by 7.
So, a_17 = a_10 + 7
(a + (17-1)d) = (a + (10-1)d) + 7
a + 16d = a + 9d + 7
16d - 9d = 7
7d = 7
d = 1
Therefore, the common difference is 1.

31. If the HCF of 65 and 117 is expressible in the form 65m - 117, then the value of m is:

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4

Answer: 2

Explanation: First, we find the HCF of 65 and 117 using the Euclidean Algorithm:
117 = 65 × 1 + 52
65 = 52 × 1 + 13
52 = 13 × 4 + 0
The HCF of 65 and 117 is 13.
Given that the HCF is expressible in the form 65m - 117.
So, 65m - 117 = 13
65m = 13 + 117
65m = 130
m = 130 / 65
m = 2
Thus, the value of m is 2.

32. A card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52 playing cards. The probability of getting a non-face card is:

  1. 10/13
  2. 3/13
  3. 1/13
  4. 4/13

Answer: 10/13

Explanation: Total number of cards in a well-shuffled deck = 52.
Number of face cards in a deck:
Jacks = 4
Queens = 4
Kings = 4
Total number of face cards = 4 + 4 + 4 = 12.
Number of non-face cards = Total cards - Number of face cards
Number of non-face cards = 52 - 12 = 40.
Probability of getting a non-face card = (Number of non-face cards) / (Total number of cards)
Probability = 40 / 52
Simplifying the fraction by dividing both numerator and denominator by their HCF (which is 4):
Probability = 40 ÷ 4 / 52 ÷ 4 = 10 / 13.
Thus, the probability of getting a non-face card is 10/13.

33. For what value of 'k' will the pair of linear equations 3x + y = 1 and (2k - 1)x + (k - 1)y = 2k + 1 have no solution?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. -1

Answer: 2

Explanation: For a pair of linear equations a₁x + b₁y = c₁ and a₂x + b₂y = c₂ to have no solution, the condition is a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂.
Given equations are:
1) 3x + y = 1 (Here, a₁=3, b₁=1, c₁=1)
2) (2k - 1)x + (k - 1)y = 2k + 1 (Here, a₂=2k-1, b₂=k-1, c₂=2k+1)
Applying the condition a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂:
3 / (2k - 1) = 1 / (k - 1)
Cross-multiply:
3(k - 1) = 1(2k - 1)
3k - 3 = 2k - 1
3k - 2k = -1 + 3
k = 2
Now, we must also check if b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ for k=2.
b₁/b₂ = 1 / (k - 1) = 1 / (2 - 1) = 1/1 = 1
c₁/c₂ = 1 / (2k + 1) = 1 / (2(2) + 1) = 1 / (4 + 1) = 1/5
Since 1 ≠ 1/5, the condition b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ is satisfied.
Thus, for k = 2, the pair of linear equations has no solution.

34. If the sum of the first 'n' terms of an AP is given by S_n = 3n² + 5n, then its common difference is:

  1. 6
  2. 3
  3. 8
  4. 2

Answer: 6

Explanation: We are given the sum of the first 'n' terms of an AP as S_n = 3n² + 5n.
To find the common difference (d), we can find the first two terms of the AP.
First term (a₁): S₁ = 3(1)² + 5(1) = 3 + 5 = 8
So, a₁ = 8.
Sum of the first two terms (S₂): S₂ = 3(2)² + 5(2) = 3(4) + 10 = 12 + 10 = 22
We know that S₂ = a₁ + a₂.
So, 22 = 8 + a₂
a₂ = 22 - 8
a₂ = 14
The common difference (d) is the difference between consecutive terms:
d = a₂ - a₁
d = 14 - 8
d = 6
Alternatively, for an AP where S_n = An² + Bn, the common difference d = 2A.
In this case, A = 3, so d = 2 * 3 = 6.

35. If the sum of the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial kx² + 2x + 3k is equal to the product of its zeroes, then the value of k is:

  1. -2/3
  2. 2/3
  3. 1/3
  4. -1/3

Answer: -2/3

Explanation: Let the quadratic polynomial be P(x) = kx² + 2x + 3k.
Comparing this with the standard form ax² + bx + c, we have a = k, b = 2, and c = 3k.
Sum of the zeroes (α + β) = -b/a = -2/k.
Product of the zeroes (αβ) = c/a = 3k/k = 3.
According to the problem, the sum of the zeroes is equal to the product of its zeroes.
So, -2/k = 3.
To find the value of k, we can cross-multiply:
-2 = 3k
k = -2/3.
Therefore, the value of k is -2/3.
