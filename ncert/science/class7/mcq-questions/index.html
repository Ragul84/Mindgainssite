Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 358 questions.

26. Which of the following components is essential for an electric circuit to be complete and allow current to flow?

  1. Switch
  2. Bulb
  3. Connecting wires
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: For an electric circuit to be complete and allow current to flow, it needs a power source (like a cell/battery), connecting wires, a load (like a bulb), and often a switch to control the flow of current. All these components are essential for a functional circuit.

27. Which of these materials is a good conductor of heat?

  1. Wood
  2. Plastic
  3. Air
  4. Copper

Answer: Copper

Explanation: Copper is a metal and metals are generally good conductors of heat, meaning heat can easily pass through them. Wood, plastic, and air are poor conductors of heat, also known as insulators.

28. Which of the following substances turns blue litmus paper red?

  1. Baking soda solution
  2. Lemon juice
  3. Soap solution
  4. Distilled water

Answer: Lemon juice

Explanation: Acids turn blue litmus paper red. Lemon juice is acidic due to the presence of citric acid. Baking soda solution and soap solution are basic and would turn red litmus blue. Distilled water is neutral and would not change the color of litmus paper.

29. Which of the following is a physical change?

  1. Burning of wood
  2. Rusting of iron
  3. Melting of ice
  4. Digestion of food

Answer: Melting of ice

Explanation: Melting of ice is a physical change because only the state of water changes from solid to liquid, but its chemical composition remains the same. Burning of wood, rusting of iron, and digestion of food are chemical changes as new substances are formed.

30. During heavy exercise, the muscle cells respire anaerobically. What is the end product of this respiration?

  1. Carbon dioxide and water
  2. Lactic acid
  3. Ethanol and carbon dioxide
  4. Oxygen

Answer: Lactic acid

Explanation: When muscle cells respire anaerobically (without oxygen) during heavy exercise, they produce lactic acid. This accumulation of lactic acid can cause muscle cramps. Aerobic respiration produces carbon dioxide and water. Ethanol and carbon dioxide are products of anaerobic respiration in yeast.

31. Which part of a flower contains the pollen grains?

  1. Sepal
  2. Petal
  3. Anther
  4. Ovary

Answer: Anther

Explanation: The anther is part of the stamen (male reproductive organ) of a flower and produces pollen grains, which contain the male gametes. Sepals protect the bud, petals attract pollinators, and the ovary contains ovules.

32. A car travels a distance of 100 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

  1. 200 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 100 km/h
  4. 2 km/h

Answer: 50 km/h

Explanation: Speed is calculated by dividing the distance traveled by the time taken. In this case, Speed = Distance / Time = 100 km / 2 hours = 50 km/h.

33. Which of the following devices works on the heating effect of electric current?

  1. Electric bell
  2. Electric fan
  3. Electric heater
  4. Electric motor

Answer: Electric heater

Explanation: An electric heater works on the principle that when electric current passes through a high-resistance wire, it generates heat. Electric bells and motors work on the magnetic effect of electric current, and electric fans use a motor to rotate blades.

34. Where does the complete digestion of food primarily occur in humans?

  1. Stomach
  2. Large intestine
  3. Small intestine
  4. Oesophagus

Answer: Small intestine

Explanation: The small intestine is the primary site for the complete digestion and absorption of food in humans. While digestion begins in the mouth and continues in the stomach, most of the chemical digestion and nutrient absorption takes place in the small intestine with the help of digestive juices from the liver and pancreas.

35. Which of these is a good conductor of heat?

  1. Wood
  2. Plastic
  3. Air
  4. Copper

Answer: Copper

Explanation: Copper is a metal and metals are generally good conductors of heat, meaning they allow heat to pass through them easily. Wood, plastic, and air are insulators, which means they do not conduct heat well.

36. Which substance is used as an indicator to test for the presence of an acid or a base?

  1. Salt
  2. Sugar
  3. Litmus paper
  4. Water

Answer: Litmus paper

Explanation: Litmus paper is a common indicator used to test whether a solution is acidic or basic. Blue litmus paper turns red in acidic solutions, and red litmus paper turns blue in basic solutions. Salt, sugar, and water are not indicators.

37. During heavy exercise, muscle cells respire anaerobically. What is the end product of this anaerobic respiration?

  1. Carbon dioxide and water
  2. Lactic acid
  3. Ethanol and carbon dioxide
  4. Oxygen

Answer: Lactic acid

Explanation: When muscle cells respire anaerobically (without oxygen) during heavy exercise, they produce lactic acid. This accumulation of lactic acid can cause muscle cramps. Aerobic respiration produces carbon dioxide and water, while anaerobic respiration in yeast produces ethanol and carbon dioxide.

38. When a blue litmus paper is dipped into a solution of baking soda, what change will be observed?

  1. It will turn red.
  2. It will remain blue.
  3. It will turn green.
  4. It will turn yellow.

Answer: It will remain blue.

Explanation: Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) is a basic substance. Bases turn red litmus paper blue, but they do not change the color of blue litmus paper. Therefore, when blue litmus paper is dipped into a baking soda solution, it will remain blue.

39. Which of the following processes involves the absorption of heat from the surroundings?

  1. Burning of wood
  2. Condensation of water vapour
  3. Melting of ice
  4. Freezing of water

Answer: Melting of ice

Explanation: Melting of ice is an endothermic process, meaning it absorbs heat from its surroundings to change from a solid to a liquid state. Burning of wood (combustion) releases heat. Condensation of water vapour and freezing of water are exothermic processes, meaning they release heat to the surroundings.

40. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a physical change?

  1. No new substance is formed.
  2. It is usually reversible.
  3. The chemical composition of the substance changes.
  4. Changes in state, shape, or size can occur.

Answer: The chemical composition of the substance changes.

Explanation: A physical change involves a change in the physical properties of a substance, such as its state, shape, or size, but its chemical composition remains the same. No new substance is formed, and these changes are often reversible. For example, melting ice into water is a physical change because water (H2O) is still water, just in a different state. The option 'The chemical composition of the substance changes' describes a chemical change, not a physical change.

41. The SI unit of speed is:

  1. Kilometer per hour (km/h)
  2. Meter per second (m/s)
  3. Centimeter per second (cm/s)
  4. Kilometer per minute (km/min)

Answer: Meter per second (m/s)

Explanation: The SI (International System of Units) unit for speed is meter per second (m/s). While other units like km/h are commonly used, m/s is the standard unit in scientific contexts.

42. Which of the following is a device that converts electrical energy into light energy?

  1. Electric motor
  2. Electric bell
  3. Electric bulb
  4. Electric heater

Answer: Electric bulb

Explanation: An electric bulb converts electrical energy primarily into light energy (and some heat). An electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. An electric bell converts electrical energy into sound energy. An electric heater converts electrical energy into heat energy.

43. A substance that turns red litmus paper blue is a/an:

  1. Acid
  2. Base
  3. Salt
  4. Neutral substance

Answer: Base

Explanation: Bases are substances that turn red litmus paper blue. Acids turn blue litmus paper red. Salts and neutral substances generally do not change the colour of litmus paper.

44. During heavy exercise, the muscle cells respire anaerobically. What is the byproduct of this process?

  1. Carbon dioxide and water
  2. Lactic acid
  3. Alcohol
  4. Oxygen

Answer: Lactic acid

Explanation: When muscle cells respire anaerobically (without oxygen) during heavy exercise, they produce lactic acid. This accumulation of lactic acid can cause muscle cramps. Carbon dioxide and water are products of aerobic respiration, and alcohol is produced during anaerobic respiration in yeast.

45. During heavy exercise, muscle cells respire anaerobically. What is the end product of this process?

  1. Carbon dioxide and water
  2. Lactic acid
  3. Ethanol and carbon dioxide
  4. Oxygen

Answer: Lactic acid

Explanation: When muscle cells respire anaerobically (without oxygen) during heavy exercise, glucose is partially broken down to produce lactic acid. This accumulation of lactic acid can cause muscle cramps. Carbon dioxide and water are products of aerobic respiration. Ethanol and carbon dioxide are products of anaerobic respiration in yeast.

46. Which of the following is a non-biodegradable waste?

  1. Vegetable peels
  2. Paper
  3. Plastic bottle
  4. Cotton cloth

Answer: Plastic bottle

Explanation: A plastic bottle is a non-biodegradable waste, meaning it cannot be broken down by natural processes and microorganisms. Vegetable peels, paper, and cotton cloth are biodegradable and can decompose over time.

47. The tiny pores on the surface of leaves through which gaseous exchange occurs are called:

  1. Xylem
  2. Phloem
  3. Stomata
  4. Chloroplasts

Answer: Stomata

Explanation: Stomata are tiny pores present on the surface of leaves, primarily on the underside, that allow for the exchange of gases like carbon dioxide and oxygen during photosynthesis and respiration. Xylem and phloem are vascular tissues for transport, and chloroplasts are organelles where photosynthesis takes place.

48. Which blood vessels carry oxygen-rich blood from the heart to all parts of the body?

  1. Veins
  2. Arteries
  3. Capillaries
  4. Lymph vessels

Answer: Arteries

Explanation: Arteries are blood vessels that carry oxygen-rich blood (except pulmonary artery) away from the heart to various parts of the body. Veins carry carbon dioxide-rich blood back to the heart. Capillaries are tiny blood vessels that connect arteries and veins, facilitating exchange of substances. Lymph vessels are part of the lymphatic system.

49. Which of the following is a component of wastewater?

  1. Clean drinking water
  2. Rainwater collected in a tank
  3. Sewage from homes and industries
  4. Water used for irrigation in fields

Answer: Sewage from homes and industries

Explanation: Wastewater is any water that has been affected by human use. Sewage from homes and industries contains human waste, chemicals, and other pollutants, making it a primary component of wastewater. Clean drinking water, rainwater, and irrigation water are generally not considered wastewater unless they become contaminated.

50. Which of the following substances would turn blue litmus paper red?

  1. Baking soda solution
  2. Lemon juice
  3. Soap solution
  4. Distilled water

Answer: Lemon juice

Explanation: Acids turn blue litmus paper red. Lemon juice is acidic due to the presence of citric acid. Baking soda solution and soap solution are basic and would turn red litmus blue. Distilled water is neutral and would not change the color of litmus paper.
