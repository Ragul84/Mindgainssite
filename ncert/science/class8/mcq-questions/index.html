Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 284 questions.

26. Which gland is responsible for secreting hormones that control the development of secondary sexual characteristics in humans?

  1. Thyroid gland
  2. Adrenal gland
  3. Pituitary gland
  4. Pancreas

Answer: Pituitary gland

Explanation: The pituitary gland, often called the 'master gland', secretes hormones that stimulate other endocrine glands, including the testes in males and ovaries in females, to produce their respective hormones (testosterone and estrogen) which are responsible for secondary sexual characteristics.

27. Which of the following is an example of a non-contact force?

  1. Muscular force
  2. Frictional force
  3. Magnetic force
  4. Tension force

Answer: Magnetic force

Explanation: Non-contact forces are forces that act on an object without coming physically in contact with it. Magnetic force is an example, as magnets can attract or repel objects from a distance. Muscular, frictional, and tension forces all require direct contact.

28. What is the main purpose of establishing a wildlife sanctuary?

  1. To grow crops
  2. To protect animals in their natural habitat
  3. To build factories
  4. To conduct mining activities

Answer: To protect animals in their natural habitat

Explanation: Wildlife sanctuaries are protected areas where animals are conserved in their natural habitats, free from human disturbance and hunting. The primary goal is to ensure the survival and well-being of various animal species. Growing crops, building factories, or mining activities are detrimental to wildlife and are not permitted in sanctuaries.

29. Which of the following forces is responsible for the apple falling from a tree?

  1. Muscular force
  2. Frictional force
  3. Gravitational force
  4. Magnetic force

Answer: Gravitational force

Explanation: Gravitational force is the attractive force exerted by the Earth on all objects, pulling them towards its center. This is why an apple falls from a tree. Muscular force is exerted by muscles. Frictional force opposes motion. Magnetic force is exerted by magnets.

30. The loudness of sound is determined by its:

  1. Frequency
  2. Amplitude
  3. Pitch
  4. Wavelength

Answer: Amplitude

Explanation: The loudness of sound is directly proportional to the amplitude of the vibration. A larger amplitude means a louder sound. Frequency determines the pitch of the sound (how high or low it is). Wavelength is related to both frequency and speed of sound.

31. Which microorganism is responsible for converting milk into curd?

  1. Yeast
  2. Lactobacillus
  3. Penicillium
  4. Rhizobium

Answer: Lactobacillus

Explanation: Lactobacillus is a bacterium that converts the lactose sugar in milk into lactic acid, which causes the milk to coagulate and form curd. Yeast is used in baking and fermentation, Penicillium produces antibiotics, and Rhizobium fixes nitrogen in leguminous plants.

32. Which of the following forces is responsible for the falling of an apple from a tree?

  1. Muscular force
  2. Frictional force
  3. Magnetic force
  4. Gravitational force

Answer: Gravitational force

Explanation: Gravitational force is the attractive force exerted by the Earth on all objects, pulling them towards its center. This is why an apple falls from a tree. Muscular force is applied by muscles, frictional force opposes motion, and magnetic force acts between magnets.

33. Which of the following is NOT a rabi crop?

  1. Wheat
  2. Gram
  3. Pea
  4. Paddy

Answer: Paddy

Explanation: Rabi crops are sown in winter and harvested in spring. Examples include wheat, gram, and pea. Paddy (rice) is a kharif crop, sown in the rainy season.

34. Which microorganism is responsible for the fermentation of milk into curd?

  1. Yeast
  2. Lactobacillus
  3. Penicillium
  4. Rhizobium

Answer: Lactobacillus

Explanation: Lactobacillus is a bacterium that converts the lactose sugar in milk into lactic acid, which causes the milk to curdle and form curd. Yeast is used in bread making, Penicillium produces penicillin, and Rhizobium helps in nitrogen fixation.

35. Which of the following is a major cause of desertification?

  1. Afforestation
  2. Overgrazing
  3. Rainwater harvesting
  4. Wildlife sanctuaries

Answer: Overgrazing

Explanation: Desertification is the process by which fertile land becomes desert, typically as a result of drought, deforestation, or inappropriate agriculture. Overgrazing by livestock removes vegetation, exposing the soil to erosion and leading to desertification. Afforestation and wildlife sanctuaries help conserve nature, and rainwater harvesting helps manage water resources.

36. Which hormone is responsible for the development of secondary sexual characteristics in males during adolescence?

  1. Estrogen
  2. Progesterone
  3. Testosterone
  4. Insulin

Answer: Testosterone

Explanation: Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone responsible for the development of male secondary sexual characteristics such as deepening of voice, growth of facial hair, and muscle development. Estrogen and progesterone are female sex hormones, and insulin regulates blood sugar.

37. Which of the following forces is responsible for the wear and tear of surfaces in contact?

  1. Gravitational force
  2. Magnetic force
  3. Frictional force
  4. Electrostatic force

Answer: Frictional force

Explanation: Frictional force is the force that opposes the relative motion or tendency of motion between two surfaces in contact. This constant rubbing and resistance cause wear and tear on the surfaces. Gravitational, magnetic, and electrostatic forces are different types of forces.

38. Which of the following microorganisms is responsible for the conversion of milk into curd?

  1. Yeast
  2. Lactobacillus
  3. Rhizobium
  4. Penicillium

Answer: Lactobacillus

Explanation: Lactobacillus is a bacterium that converts the lactose sugar in milk into lactic acid, which causes the milk to coagulate and form curd. Yeast is used in baking and fermentation, Rhizobium fixes nitrogen, and Penicillium is a fungus used to produce antibiotics.

39. Which of the following practices is NOT considered a part of crop production and management?

  1. Sowing seeds
  2. Irrigation
  3. Weeding
  4. Cooking food

Answer: Cooking food

Explanation: Crop production and management involves various agricultural practices carried out by farmers over a period of time. These include preparation of soil, sowing, adding manure and fertilisers, irrigation, protection from weeds, harvesting, and storage. Cooking food is a post-harvest activity related to food preparation, not directly a part of crop production and management.

40. Which of the following is a non-renewable resource?

  1. Sunlight
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Water

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that are present in limited quantities and cannot be replenished easily once consumed. Sunlight, wind energy, and water are renewable resources as they are either inexhaustible or can be replenished naturally. Coal, on the other hand, is formed over millions of years from dead vegetation and its reserves are finite, making it a non-renewable resource.

41. Which hormone is responsible for the development of secondary sexual characteristics in males?

  1. Estrogen
  2. Thyroxine
  3. Insulin
  4. Testosterone

Answer: Testosterone

Explanation: Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone, responsible for the development of male secondary sexual characteristics such as facial hair, deepening of voice, and muscle development. Estrogen is the primary female sex hormone, Thyroxine regulates metabolism, and Insulin regulates blood sugar.

42. Which of the following materials is a good conductor of electricity?

  1. Rubber
  2. Plastic
  3. Wood
  4. Copper

Answer: Copper

Explanation: A good conductor of electricity allows electric current to pass through it easily. Copper is a metal and an excellent conductor of electricity, widely used in electrical wiring. Rubber, plastic, and wood are insulators, meaning they do not conduct electricity well.

43. Which phenomenon is responsible for the twinkling of stars?

  1. Reflection of light
  2. Dispersion of light
  3. Refraction of light
  4. Scattering of light

Answer: Refraction of light

Explanation: The twinkling of stars is caused by the atmospheric refraction of starlight. As starlight passes through different layers of the Earth's atmosphere with varying densities and temperatures, it bends continuously, causing the apparent position and brightness of the star to fluctuate, making it appear to twinkle.

44. What is the term used for the areas reserved for wildlife where they can live freely in their natural habitat?

  1. Botanical gardens
  2. Zoological parks
  3. Wildlife sanctuaries
  4. National parks

Answer: Wildlife sanctuaries

Explanation: Wildlife sanctuaries are protected areas where animals are protected from any disturbance to them and their habitat. National parks are also protected areas but are generally larger and aim to protect entire ecosystems. Botanical gardens are for plants, and zoological parks (zoos) are for animals in captivity.

45. Which gland is responsible for secreting hormones that control the activity of other endocrine glands?

  1. Thyroid gland
  2. Adrenal gland
  3. Pituitary gland
  4. Pancreas

Answer: Pituitary gland

Explanation: The pituitary gland is often called the 'master gland' because it produces hormones that regulate the functions of other endocrine glands, such as the thyroid, adrenal glands, and gonads. The thyroid gland produces thyroid hormones, adrenal glands produce adrenaline, and the pancreas produces insulin and glucagon.

46. Which part of the human ear vibrates when sound waves strike it?

  1. Cochlea
  2. Auditory nerve
  3. Eardrum
  4. Ossicles

Answer: Eardrum

Explanation: The eardrum (tympanic membrane) is a thin, taut membrane that vibrates when sound waves strike it. These vibrations are then transmitted to the ossicles (malleus, incus, stapes), which amplify the vibrations and pass them to the cochlea, where they are converted into electrical signals for the auditory nerve.

47. Which of the following forces is responsible for objects falling towards the Earth?

  1. Muscular force
  2. Frictional force
  3. Gravitational force
  4. Magnetic force

Answer: Gravitational force

Explanation: Gravitational force is the attractive force exerted by the Earth on all objects, pulling them towards its center. Muscular force is exerted by muscles, frictional force opposes motion, and magnetic force acts between magnets or magnetic materials.

48. Which of the following is an endangered animal species?

  1. Dog
  2. Cow
  3. Tiger
  4. Goat

Answer: Tiger

Explanation: Endangered species are those that are at a very high risk of extinction in the wild. Among the given options, the Tiger is an endangered animal due to habitat loss and poaching. Dogs, cows, and goats are domesticated animals and are not endangered.

49. The SI unit of pressure is:

  1. Newton
  2. Joule
  3. Pascal
  4. Watt

Answer: Pascal

Explanation: The SI unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa), which is defined as one Newton per square meter (N/m²). Newton is the unit of force, Joule is the unit of energy, and Watt is the unit of power.

50. The unit of frequency is:

  1. Metre
  2. Second
  3. Hertz
  4. Decibel

Answer: Hertz

Explanation: Frequency is the number of oscillations or cycles per unit time, and its SI unit is Hertz (Hz). Metre is the unit of length, second is the unit of time, and decibel is the unit for measuring the intensity of sound.
