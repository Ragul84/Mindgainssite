Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 284 questions.

1. Which of the following is a rabi crop?

  1. Paddy
  2. Maize
  3. Wheat
  4. Groundnut

Answer: Wheat

Explanation: Rabi crops are sown in winter and harvested in spring. Wheat is a classic example of a rabi crop. Paddy (rice), maize, and groundnut are kharif crops, sown in the monsoon season.

2. Which microorganism is responsible for the conversion of milk into curd?

  1. Yeast
  2. Lactobacillus
  3. Penicillium
  4. Rhizobium

Answer: Lactobacillus

Explanation: Lactobacillus is a bacterium that converts the lactose sugar in milk into lactic acid, which causes the milk proteins to coagulate and form curd. Yeast is used in baking and fermentation, Penicillium produces antibiotics, and Rhizobium fixes nitrogen in leguminous plants.

3. Which of the following is a non-renewable source of energy?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Hydroelectric energy

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that cannot be replenished in a short period of time once they are used up. Coal is a fossil fuel formed over millions of years and is therefore non-renewable. Solar, wind, and hydroelectric energy are renewable sources.

4. The lowest temperature at which a substance catches fire is called its:

  1. Melting point
  2. Boiling point
  3. Ignition temperature
  4. Flash point

Answer: Ignition temperature

Explanation: Ignition temperature is the minimum temperature to which a combustible substance must be heated before it catches fire and starts burning. Melting point is when a solid turns to liquid, boiling point is when a liquid turns to gas, and flash point is the lowest temperature at which a liquid gives off enough vapor to form an ignitable mixture with air.

5. What is the term for a species whose population has been reduced to a critical level and is facing a very high risk of extinction?

  1. Endemic species
  2. Extinct species
  3. Endangered species
  4. Migratory species

Answer: Endangered species

Explanation: An endangered species is a species that is very likely to become extinct in the near future, either worldwide or in a particular political jurisdiction. Endemic species are found only in a particular geographical area, extinct species no longer exist, and migratory species move from one region to another seasonally.

6. Which of the following is an example of internal fertilization?

  1. Frog
  2. Fish
  3. Hen
  4. Starfish

Answer: Hen

Explanation: Internal fertilization is the fusion of male and female gametes inside the body of the female. Hens exhibit internal fertilization, where the egg is fertilized inside the female's body before being laid. Frogs, fish, and starfish typically show external fertilization.

7. The period of life when the body undergoes changes, leading to reproductive maturity, is called:

  1. Childhood
  2. Adulthood
  3. Adolescence
  4. Infancy

Answer: Adolescence

Explanation: Adolescence is the transitional stage from childhood to adulthood, typically occurring between puberty and legal adulthood. During this period, significant physical, psychological, and social changes occur, including the development of reproductive maturity.

8. Which of the following forces is responsible for the upward push experienced by objects immersed in a fluid?

  1. Muscular force
  2. Gravitational force
  3. Frictional force
  4. Buoyant force

Answer: Buoyant force

Explanation: Buoyant force (or buoyancy) is the upward force exerted by a fluid that opposes the weight of an immersed object. Muscular force is exerted by muscles, gravitational force pulls objects towards the Earth, and frictional force opposes motion.

9. Friction always:

  1. Helps in motion
  2. Opposes motion
  3. Increases speed
  4. Decreases weight

Answer: Opposes motion

Explanation: Friction is a force that opposes the relative motion or tendency of such motion of two surfaces in contact. It acts in the opposite direction to the motion or attempted motion.

10. The unit of frequency of sound is:

  1. Metre
  2. Second
  3. Hertz
  4. Decibel

Answer: Hertz

Explanation: Hertz (Hz) is the SI unit of frequency, representing cycles per second. Metre is a unit of length, second is a unit of time, and decibel (dB) is a unit used to measure the intensity or loudness of sound.

11. What is the main purpose of establishing a biosphere reserve?

  1. To promote tourism
  2. To conserve biodiversity and traditional life of tribals
  3. To conduct scientific experiments only
  4. To grow exotic plant species

Answer: To conserve biodiversity and traditional life of tribals

Explanation: Biosphere reserves are large protected areas of land for the conservation of wildlife, plant and animal resources, and the traditional life of the tribals living in the area. They aim to balance conservation with sustainable use.

12. The process of fusion of male and female gametes is called:

  1. Budding
  2. Fragmentation
  3. Fertilization
  4. Regeneration

Answer: Fertilization

Explanation: Fertilization is the process where the male gamete (sperm) fuses with the female gamete (egg) to form a zygote, which then develops into a new individual. Budding, fragmentation, and regeneration are types of asexual reproduction.

13. Which of the following is a hormone secreted by the adrenal glands?

  1. Insulin
  2. Thyroxine
  3. Adrenaline
  4. Estrogen

Answer: Adrenaline

Explanation: Adrenaline is a hormone secreted by the adrenal glands, often referred to as the 'fight or flight' hormone, preparing the body for stressful situations. Insulin is from the pancreas, thyroxine from the thyroid, and estrogen from the ovaries.

14. Which of the following forces is responsible for the upward push experienced by an object immersed in a liquid?

  1. Muscular force
  2. Gravitational force
  3. Frictional force
  4. Buoyant force

Answer: Buoyant force

Explanation: Buoyant force (or upthrust) is the upward force exerted by a fluid that opposes the weight of an immersed object. Muscular force is from muscles, gravitational force pulls objects down, and frictional force opposes motion.

15. Which of the following surfaces would offer the least friction?

  1. Rough concrete
  2. Sandpaper
  3. Polished marble
  4. Carpet

Answer: Polished marble

Explanation: Friction is a force that opposes motion. Smoother surfaces have fewer irregularities and thus offer less friction. Polished marble is the smoothest among the given options, hence it would offer the least friction.

16. Sound cannot travel through which of the following medium?

  1. Air
  2. Water
  3. Steel
  4. Vacuum

Answer: Vacuum

Explanation: Sound is a mechanical wave, meaning it requires a medium (like solids, liquids, or gases) to propagate. It travels by vibrations of particles in the medium. A vacuum has no particles, so sound cannot travel through it. Air, water, and steel are all mediums through which sound can travel.

17. Which of these is a non-renewable source of energy?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Coal
  4. Hydropower

Answer: Coal

Explanation: Non-renewable resources are those that cannot be replenished in a short period of time once they are used up. Coal is a fossil fuel formed over millions of years and is therefore non-renewable. Solar, wind, and hydropower are all renewable sources of energy.

18. Which of the following is a major cause of deforestation?

  1. Afforestation
  2. Reforestation
  3. Construction of dams
  4. Establishment of national parks

Answer: Construction of dams

Explanation: Deforestation is the clearing of forests for other land uses. The construction of large dams often requires clearing vast areas of forest for the reservoir and associated infrastructure. Afforestation and reforestation are processes of planting trees, while national parks are established for conservation.

19. The process of fusion of the male gamete with the female gamete is called:

  1. Ovulation
  2. Gestation
  3. Fertilization
  4. Implantation

Answer: Fertilization

Explanation: Fertilization is the process where the male gamete (sperm) fuses with the female gamete (egg) to form a zygote. Ovulation is the release of an egg from the ovary, gestation is the period of development in the womb, and implantation is the attachment of the embryo to the uterine wall.

20. Which gland is responsible for the secretion of growth hormones in humans?

  1. Thyroid gland
  2. Adrenal gland
  3. Pituitary gland
  4. Pancreas

Answer: Pituitary gland

Explanation: The pituitary gland, often called the 'master gland', secretes several hormones, including growth hormone, which regulates growth and development. The thyroid gland produces thyroid hormones, adrenal glands produce adrenaline, and the pancreas produces insulin and glucagon.

21. The SI unit of force is:

  1. Joule
  2. Watt
  3. Newton
  4. Pascal

Answer: Newton

Explanation: The SI unit of force is the Newton (N). Joule is the unit of energy, Watt is the unit of power, and Pascal is the unit of pressure.

22. The part of the eye that controls the amount of light entering it is the:

  1. Retina
  2. Cornea
  3. Iris
  4. Lens

Answer: Iris

Explanation: The iris is the colored part of the eye that surrounds the pupil. It contains muscles that contract or expand to control the size of the pupil, thereby regulating the amount of light that enters the eye. The retina is the light-sensitive layer, the cornea is the transparent outer layer, and the lens focuses light onto the retina.

23. Which zone of a candle flame is the hottest?

  1. Innermost dark zone
  2. Middle luminous zone
  3. Outermost non-luminous zone
  4. Wick zone

Answer: Outermost non-luminous zone

Explanation: The outermost non-luminous zone of a candle flame is the hottest because it has complete combustion due to ample supply of oxygen. The innermost dark zone has unburnt wax vapours and is the coolest, while the middle luminous zone has partial combustion.

24. What is the term used for a place where animals are protected in their natural habitat?

  1. Botanical garden
  2. Zoological park
  3. Wildlife sanctuary
  4. National park

Answer: Wildlife sanctuary

Explanation: A wildlife sanctuary is an area where animals are protected from any disturbance to them and their habitat. A national park is also a protected area but is generally larger and protects entire ecosystems. Botanical gardens are for plants, and zoological parks (zoos) keep animals in artificial enclosures.

25. The process of fusion of the male and female gametes is called:

  1. Budding
  2. Fragmentation
  3. Fertilization
  4. Regeneration

Answer: Fertilization

Explanation: Fertilization is the process where the male gamete (sperm) fuses with the female gamete (ovum) to form a zygote. Budding, fragmentation, and regeneration are types of asexual reproduction.
