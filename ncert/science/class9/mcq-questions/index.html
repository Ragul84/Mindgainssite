26. Which type of tissue is responsible for the movement of our body?

  1. Epithelial tissue
  2. Connective tissue
  3. Muscular tissue
  4. Nervous tissue

Answer: Muscular tissue

Explanation: Muscular tissue is composed of specialized cells called muscle fibers that can contract and relax, enabling movement of various body parts, including limbs, heart, and internal organs.

27. The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) on the surface of the Earth is approximately:

  1. 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ Nm²/kg²
  2. 9.8 m/s²
  3. 3.7 m/s²
  4. 1.6 m/s²

Answer: 9.8 m/s²

Explanation: The acceleration due to gravity (g) on the surface of the Earth is approximately 9.8 m/s². This value can vary slightly depending on location, but 9.8 m/s² is the standard approximate value used in calculations. 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ Nm²/kg² is the gravitational constant (G).

28. Which of the following is the unit of work in the SI system?

  1. Watt
  2. Joule
  3. Newton
  4. Pascal

Answer: Joule

Explanation: The SI unit of work is the Joule (J). Watt is the unit of power, Newton is the unit of force, and Pascal is the unit of pressure.

29. Which type of plant tissue is responsible for increasing the girth (width) of the plant stem and root?

  1. Apical meristem
  2. Intercalary meristem
  3. Lateral meristem
  4. Parenchyma

Answer: Lateral meristem

Explanation: Lateral meristem, also known as cambium, is responsible for increasing the diameter or girth of the plant stem and root. Apical meristem is responsible for growth in length, and intercalary meristem is found at the base of leaves or internodes and causes growth in length in those regions. Parenchyma is a type of permanent tissue involved in storage, photosynthesis, and other functions.

30. When a body is lifted to a certain height, its energy increases. What type of energy is this?

  1. Kinetic energy
  2. Chemical energy
  3. Potential energy
  4. Heat energy

Answer: Potential energy

Explanation: When a body is lifted to a certain height, it gains gravitational potential energy due to its position in the Earth's gravitational field. Kinetic energy is associated with motion, chemical energy is stored in chemical bonds, and heat energy is related to the internal energy of a system.

31. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of sound waves?

  1. They are longitudinal waves.
  2. They require a medium for propagation.
  3. They can travel through a vacuum.
  4. They are mechanical waves.

Answer: They can travel through a vacuum.

Explanation: Sound waves are mechanical waves, meaning they require a material medium (solid, liquid, or gas) for their propagation. They cannot travel through a vacuum because there are no particles to transmit the vibrations. They are also longitudinal waves, where the particles of the medium oscillate parallel to the direction of wave propagation.

32. Which separation technique would be most suitable for separating a mixture of salt and ammonium chloride?

  1. Evaporation
  2. Filtration
  3. Sublimation
  4. Distillation

Answer: Sublimation

Explanation: Ammonium chloride is a substance that sublimes, meaning it changes directly from a solid to a gas upon heating without passing through the liquid state. Salt (sodium chloride) does not sublime. Therefore, sublimation is the most effective method to separate ammonium chloride from salt, as the ammonium chloride will vaporize and can be collected, leaving the salt behind.

33. Which of the following discoveries is NOT correctly matched with the scientist?

  1. Electron - J.J. Thomson
  2. Proton - E. Rutherford
  3. Neutron - J. Chadwick
  4. Nucleus - N. Bohr

Answer: Nucleus - N. Bohr

Explanation: J.J. Thomson discovered the electron. Ernest Rutherford discovered the proton and the atomic nucleus through his gold foil experiment. James Chadwick discovered the neutron. Niels Bohr proposed the planetary model of the atom, explaining electron orbits and energy levels, but he did not discover the nucleus itself. Rutherford is credited with the discovery of the nucleus.

34. Which cell organelle is responsible for synthesizing lipids and detoxifying drugs and poisons?

  1. Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum
  2. Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum
  3. Golgi Apparatus
  4. Mitochondria

Answer: Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum

Explanation: The Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER) plays a crucial role in the synthesis of lipids, including steroids and phospholipids. It is also involved in the detoxification of various drugs and poisons, particularly in liver cells. The Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER) is involved in protein synthesis and modification, Golgi apparatus in packaging and modifying proteins and lipids, and mitochondria in cellular respiration.

35. Which type of plant tissue is responsible for increasing the girth (diameter) of the stem and root?

  1. Apical meristem
  2. Intercalary meristem
  3. Lateral meristem
  4. Parenchyma

Answer: Lateral meristem

Explanation: Lateral meristems, such as vascular cambium and cork cambium, are responsible for increasing the girth or diameter of the plant stem and root. Apical meristems are found at the tips of roots and shoots and are responsible for primary growth (increase in length). Intercalary meristems are found at the base of leaves or internodes and contribute to the elongation of organs. Parenchyma is a type of simple permanent tissue involved in storage, photosynthesis, and secretion.

36. A stone is dropped from a height. As it falls, what happens to its potential energy and kinetic energy?

  1. Potential energy increases, kinetic energy decreases.
  2. Potential energy decreases, kinetic energy increases.
  3. Both potential energy and kinetic energy increase.
  4. Both potential energy and kinetic energy decrease.

Answer: Potential energy decreases, kinetic energy increases.

Explanation: As a stone falls from a height, its height above the ground decreases, causing its gravitational potential energy (PE = mgh) to decrease. Simultaneously, its speed increases due to gravity, leading to an increase in its kinetic energy (KE = ½mv²). This is an example of the conservation of mechanical energy, where potential energy is converted into kinetic energy.

37. Which of the following statements is INCORRECT regarding the properties of a colloid?

  1. Colloids are heterogeneous mixtures.
  2. The particles of a colloid can be seen with the naked eye.
  3. Colloids show the Tyndall effect.
  4. The particles of a colloid do not settle down when left undisturbed.

Answer: The particles of a colloid can be seen with the naked eye.

Explanation: Colloids are indeed heterogeneous mixtures, and their particles are large enough to scatter light (Tyndall effect) but small enough not to be seen with the naked eye. They are also stable, meaning their particles do not settle down. Therefore, the statement that 'The particles of a colloid can be seen with the naked eye' is incorrect, as colloidal particles are generally too small to be individually visible without a microscope.

38. An object of mass 'm' is moving with a uniform velocity 'v'. What is the net force acting on the object?

  1. mv
  2. mv²
  3. 0
  4. mg

Answer: 0

Explanation: According to Newton's First Law of Motion, an object in uniform motion (constant velocity) will continue in that state unless acted upon by an external net force. If an object is moving with uniform velocity, it means its acceleration is zero. From Newton's Second Law of Motion, F = ma. Since a = 0, the net force (F) acting on the object must also be 0.

39. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of meristematic tissues in plants?

  1. Cells are actively dividing.
  2. Cells have dense cytoplasm and prominent nuclei.
  3. Cells have large vacuoles.
  4. Cells are compactly arranged without intercellular spaces.

Answer: Cells have large vacuoles.

Explanation: Meristematic tissues are responsible for plant growth and consist of actively dividing cells. These cells typically have dense cytoplasm, prominent nuclei, and are compactly arranged with little to no intercellular spaces. They generally lack large vacuoles because large vacuoles would hinder the rapid cell division process by taking up too much space and making the cell less flexible. Large vacuoles are characteristic of mature, differentiated plant cells, especially parenchyma.

40. Which of the following elements has the highest number of valence electrons?

  1. Sodium (Na)
  2. Magnesium (Mg)
  3. Aluminium (Al)
  4. Chlorine (Cl)

Answer: Chlorine (Cl)

Explanation: To determine the number of valence electrons, we need to look at the electronic configuration of each element: Sodium (Na) has atomic number 11, configuration 2, 8, 1 (1 valence electron). Magnesium (Mg) has atomic number 12, configuration 2, 8, 2 (2 valence electrons). Aluminium (Al) has atomic number 13, configuration 2, 8, 3 (3 valence electrons). Chlorine (Cl) has atomic number 17, configuration 2, 8, 7 (7 valence electrons). Therefore, Chlorine has the highest number of valence electrons among the given options.

41. What is the mass of 0.5 mole of nitrogen gas (N₂)? (Atomic mass of N = 14 u)

  1. 7 g
  2. 14 g
  3. 28 g
  4. 1.4 g

Answer: 14 g

Explanation: The atomic mass of nitrogen (N) is 14 u. Nitrogen gas is diatomic (N₂), so its molecular mass is 2 × 14 u = 28 u. The molar mass of N₂ is 28 g/mol. Therefore, the mass of 0.5 mole of N₂ is 0.5 mol × 28 g/mol = 14 g.

42. Which subatomic particle was discovered by J.J. Thomson?

  1. Proton
  2. Neutron
  3. Electron
  4. Nucleus

Answer: Electron

Explanation: J.J. Thomson is credited with the discovery of the electron through his cathode ray tube experiments. Protons were discovered by E. Goldstein and characterized by Rutherford, and neutrons were discovered by James Chadwick. The nucleus was discovered by Rutherford.

43. Which type of animal tissue is responsible for connecting muscles to bones?

  1. Ligament
  2. Tendon
  3. Cartilage
  4. Adipose tissue

Answer: Tendon

Explanation: Tendons are fibrous connective tissues that connect muscles to bones. Ligaments connect bones to other bones. Cartilage is a flexible connective tissue found in joints, and adipose tissue is a fat-storing connective tissue.

44. When a body is lifted to a certain height, its potential energy increases. This is because:

  1. Its kinetic energy increases.
  2. Work is done against the force of gravity.
  3. Its mass increases.
  4. Its velocity increases.

Answer: Work is done against the force of gravity.

Explanation: Potential energy is the energy stored in an object due to its position or state. When a body is lifted, work is done against the force of gravity to change its position. This work done is stored as gravitational potential energy in the body. Its kinetic energy might change, but that's not the direct reason for the increase in potential energy. Mass remains constant, and velocity is not directly related to potential energy increase due to height.

45. Which of the following is responsible for the synthesis of lipids in an animal cell?

  1. Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum
  2. Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum
  3. Golgi apparatus
  4. Ribosomes

Answer: Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum

Explanation: The Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER) plays a crucial role in the synthesis of lipids, steroids, and detoxification of drugs and poisons. The Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER) is involved in protein synthesis and modification due to the presence of ribosomes. The Golgi apparatus is involved in packaging and modifying proteins and lipids. Ribosomes are the sites of protein synthesis.

46. A car accelerates uniformly from 10 m/s to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What is the acceleration of the car?

  1. 1 m/s²
  2. 2 m/s²
  3. 3 m/s²
  4. 4 m/s²

Answer: 2 m/s²

Explanation: Acceleration (a) is calculated using the formula: a = (v - u) / t, where v is the final velocity, u is the initial velocity, and t is the time taken. Given u = 10 m/s, v = 20 m/s, and t = 5 s. So, a = (20 - 10) / 5 = 10 / 5 = 2 m/s².

47. Which of the following factors does NOT affect the speed of sound in a medium?

  1. Temperature of the medium
  2. Density of the medium
  3. Pressure of the medium
  4. Elasticity of the medium

Answer: Pressure of the medium

Explanation: The speed of sound is primarily affected by the temperature, density, and elasticity of the medium. While pressure changes can affect density, a change in pressure alone, without a change in density or temperature, does not significantly affect the speed of sound in gases. For example, in an ideal gas, if temperature is constant, pressure changes do not affect the speed of sound because density changes proportionally, keeping the ratio constant.

48. Which of the following is a macronutrient required by plants in relatively large quantities?

  1. Iron
  2. Zinc
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Manganese

Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Macronutrients are required by plants in large quantities. Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium (N, P, K) are primary macronutrients. Calcium, Magnesium, and Sulfur are secondary macronutrients. Iron, Zinc, and Manganese are micronutrients, required in smaller amounts.

49. According to the Law of Conservation of Mass, if 10g of calcium carbonate (CaCO₃) is heated, it decomposes to produce 5.6g of calcium oxide (CaO) and Xg of carbon dioxide (CO₂). What is the value of X?

  1. 4.4g
  2. 5.6g
  3. 10g
  4. 15.6g

Answer: 4.4g

Explanation: The Law of Conservation of Mass states that mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Therefore, the total mass of reactants must equal the total mass of products. Here, Mass of reactant (CaCO₃) = 10g. Mass of products = Mass of CaO + Mass of CO₂. So, 10g = 5.6g + Xg. Solving for X, X = 10g - 5.6g = 4.4g.

50. Which of the following is an example of potential energy?

  1. A car moving at a high speed.
  2. A ball rolling down a hill.
  3. Water stored in a dam.
  4. A person running a marathon.

Answer: Water stored in a dam.

Explanation: Potential energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its position or state. Water stored in a dam is at a certain height, giving it gravitational potential energy. The other options describe kinetic energy, which is the energy of motion.
