Class9 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class9 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which of the following statements is INCORRECT regarding the properties of solids?

  1. Solids have a definite shape and distinct boundaries.
  2. Solids have negligible compressibility.
  3. Solids have a high rate of diffusion.
  4. Solids have a definite volume.

Answer: Solids have a high rate of diffusion.

Explanation: Solids have a very low rate of diffusion compared to liquids and gases because their particles are tightly packed and have very little kinetic energy to move past each other. The other options correctly describe properties of solids.

2. Which of the following methods is most suitable for separating a mixture of salt and ammonium chloride?

  1. Filtration
  2. Evaporation
  3. Sublimation
  4. Distillation

Answer: Sublimation

Explanation: Ammonium chloride is a sublimable substance, meaning it changes directly from solid to gas upon heating without passing through the liquid state. Salt (sodium chloride) does not sublime. Therefore, sublimation can be used to separate ammonium chloride from salt.

3. According to the Law of Conservation of Mass, if 10 g of calcium carbonate (CaCO₃) is heated, it decomposes to produce 5.6 g of calcium oxide (CaO) and carbon dioxide (CO₂). What is the mass of carbon dioxide produced?

  1. 4.4 g
  2. 5.6 g
  3. 10 g
  4. 15.6 g

Answer: 4.4 g

Explanation: The Law of Conservation of Mass states that mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Therefore, the total mass of reactants must equal the total mass of products. Mass of CaCO₃ = Mass of CaO + Mass of CO₂. So, 10 g = 5.6 g + Mass of CO₂. Mass of CO₂ = 10 g - 5.6 g = 4.4 g.

4. Which subatomic particle is responsible for the chemical properties of an atom?

  1. Proton
  2. Neutron
  3. Electron
  4. Nucleus

Answer: Electron

Explanation: Electrons, specifically the valence electrons (electrons in the outermost shell), determine how an atom interacts with other atoms to form chemical bonds. Protons determine the atomic number and identity of the element, while neutrons contribute to the mass but do not directly influence chemical properties.

5. Which of the following cell organelles is known as the 'powerhouse of the cell'?

  1. Ribosome
  2. Endoplasmic Reticulum
  3. Mitochondria
  4. Golgi Apparatus

Answer: Mitochondria

Explanation: Mitochondria are responsible for cellular respiration, a process that generates adenosine triphosphate (ATP), which is the main energy currency of the cell. Hence, they are called the 'powerhouse of the cell'.

6. Which type of plant tissue is responsible for the transport of water and minerals from the roots to the rest of the plant?

  1. Phloem
  2. Parenchyma
  3. Xylem
  4. Sclerenchyma

Answer: Xylem

Explanation: Xylem is a complex permanent tissue in plants that forms a network of tubes throughout the plant body, primarily responsible for the upward transport of water and dissolved minerals from the roots to the leaves and other parts.

7. A car accelerates uniformly from 10 m/s to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What is its acceleration?

  1. 1 m/s²
  2. 2 m/s²
  3. 3 m/s²
  4. 4 m/s²

Answer: 2 m/s²

Explanation: Acceleration (a) is calculated using the formula: a = (v - u) / t, where v is the final velocity, u is the initial velocity, and t is the time taken. Given u = 10 m/s, v = 20 m/s, and t = 5 s. So, a = (20 - 10) / 5 = 10 / 5 = 2 m/s².

8. According to Newton's third law of motion, for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Which of the following is an example of this law?

  1. A ball rolling down an inclined plane.
  2. A book resting on a table.
  3. A rocket propelling upwards by expelling gases downwards.
  4. A car stopping due to friction.

Answer: A rocket propelling upwards by expelling gases downwards.

Explanation: In rocket propulsion, the rocket expels hot gases downwards (action force), and in response, the gases exert an equal and opposite force upwards on the rocket, causing it to move upwards (reaction force). This is a classic example of Newton's third law.

9. The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) at the Earth's surface is approximately:

  1. 6.67 x 10⁻¹¹ Nm²/kg²
  2. 9.8 m/s²
  3. 3.7 m/s²
  4. 1.62 m/s²

Answer: 9.8 m/s²

Explanation: The acceleration due to gravity (g) on the surface of the Earth is approximately 9.8 m/s². 6.67 x 10⁻¹¹ Nm²/kg² is the value of the universal gravitational constant (G). 3.7 m/s² is approximately the acceleration due to gravity on Mars, and 1.62 m/s² is on the Moon.

10. Which of the following is NOT a good practice for improving food resources?

  1. Using high-yielding crop varieties.
  2. Practicing crop rotation.
  3. Excessive use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides.
  4. Implementing proper irrigation systems.

Answer: Excessive use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides.

Explanation: Excessive use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides can lead to soil degradation, water pollution, and harm to beneficial organisms, which are detrimental to long-term food resource improvement. The other options are beneficial practices for sustainable agriculture and improving food resources.

11. What is the chemical formula for calcium carbonate?

  1. CaCO
  2. CaCO₂
  3. CaCO₃
  4. Ca₂CO₃

Answer: CaCO₃

Explanation: Calcium has a valency of +2 (Ca²⁺) and the carbonate ion has a valency of -2 (CO₃²⁻). To balance the charges, one calcium ion combines with one carbonate ion, resulting in the chemical formula CaCO₃.

12. Which cell organelle is known as the 'powerhouse of the cell'?

  1. Ribosome
  2. Mitochondria
  3. Endoplasmic Reticulum
  4. Golgi apparatus

Answer: Mitochondria

Explanation: Mitochondria are responsible for cellular respiration, a process that generates adenosine triphosphate (ATP), the main energy currency of the cell. Therefore, they are called the 'powerhouse of the cell'. Ribosomes are involved in protein synthesis, the endoplasmic reticulum in synthesis and transport of proteins and lipids, and the Golgi apparatus in modifying, sorting, and packaging proteins and lipids.

13. Which type of tissue forms the lining of the mouth and the skin?

  1. Connective tissue
  2. Muscular tissue
  3. Nervous tissue
  4. Epithelial tissue

Answer: Epithelial tissue

Explanation: Epithelial tissue forms the covering or protective lining of the body and internal organs. The lining of the mouth and the outer layer of the skin are examples of epithelial tissue. Connective tissue supports and connects other tissues, muscular tissue is responsible for movement, and nervous tissue transmits electrical signals.

14. A car travels a distance of 100 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

  1. 200 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 100 km/h
  4. 0.02 km/h

Answer: 50 km/h

Explanation: Average speed is calculated by dividing the total distance traveled by the total time taken. In this case, Average Speed = Distance / Time = 100 km / 2 hours = 50 km/h.

15. According to Newton's first law of motion, an object at rest tends to stay at rest, and an object in motion tends to stay in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force. This law is also known as the law of:

  1. Action and Reaction
  2. Momentum
  3. Inertia
  4. Conservation of Energy

Answer: Inertia

Explanation: Newton's first law of motion describes the property of inertia, which is the resistance of any physical object to any change in its state of motion, including changes to its speed, direction, or state of rest. The law of action and reaction is Newton's third law, and momentum is related to Newton's second law.

16. The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) is maximum at the:

  1. Equator
  2. Poles
  3. Center of the Earth
  4. Same everywhere on Earth's surface

Answer: Poles

Explanation: The Earth is not a perfect sphere; it is slightly flattened at the poles and bulges at the equator. This means the radius of the Earth is smaller at the poles than at the equator. Since acceleration due to gravity (g) is inversely proportional to the square of the radius (g = GM/R²), g is maximum at the poles where the radius is minimum.

17. Which of the following is NOT a unit of energy?

  1. Joule
  2. Calorie
  3. Watt
  4. Kilowatt-hour

Answer: Watt

Explanation: Joule, calorie, and kilowatt-hour are all units of energy. Watt is a unit of power, which is the rate at which energy is transferred or used (Energy/Time). 1 Watt = 1 Joule/second.

18. Which of the following is a Rabi crop?

  1. Paddy
  2. Maize
  3. Wheat
  4. Soybean

Answer: Wheat

Explanation: Rabi crops are sown in winter (October to December) and harvested in spring (April to June). Wheat is a prime example of a Rabi crop. Paddy (rice), maize, and soybean are Kharif crops, which are sown with the onset of monsoon and harvested in September-October.

19. What is the mass of 0.5 moles of nitrogen gas (N₂)? (Atomic mass of N = 14 u)

  1. 7 g
  2. 14 g
  3. 28 g
  4. 1 g

Answer: 14 g

Explanation: The molecular mass of nitrogen gas (N₂) is 2 × 14 u = 28 u. This means 1 mole of N₂ has a mass of 28 g. Therefore, 0.5 moles of N₂ will have a mass of 0.5 × 28 g = 14 g.

20. Which subatomic particle is responsible for determining the chemical properties of an element?

  1. Proton
  2. Neutron
  3. Electron
  4. Nucleus

Answer: Electron

Explanation: Electrons, specifically the valence electrons in the outermost shell, are involved in chemical bonding and reactions. The number and arrangement of these electrons determine how an atom interacts with other atoms, thus defining its chemical properties. Protons determine the atomic number and identity of the element, while neutrons contribute to mass but not chemical properties.

21. According to Newton's third law of motion, for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Which of the following statements is true about action and reaction forces?

  1. They act on the same body.
  2. They always cancel each other out.
  3. They act on two different bodies.
  4. They are always in the same direction.

Answer: They act on two different bodies.

Explanation: Newton's third law states that action and reaction forces always act on two different bodies. For example, when you push a wall, you exert a force on the wall (action), and the wall exerts an equal and opposite force on you (reaction). Because they act on different bodies, they do not cancel each other out.

22. If the distance between two objects is doubled, how does the gravitational force between them change?

  1. It becomes half.
  2. It becomes one-fourth.
  3. It becomes double.
  4. It remains the same.

Answer: It becomes one-fourth.

Explanation: According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, the gravitational force (F) between two objects is inversely proportional to the square of the distance (r) between their centers (F ∝ 1/r²). If the distance is doubled (2r), the new force will be proportional to 1/(2r)² = 1/(4r²). Thus, the gravitational force becomes one-fourth of its original value.

23. Which of the following methods would be most suitable to separate a mixture of salt and ammonium chloride?

  1. Filtration
  2. Evaporation
  3. Sublimation
  4. Distillation

Answer: Sublimation

Explanation: Ammonium chloride is a sublimable substance, meaning it changes directly from solid to gas upon heating without passing through the liquid state. Salt (sodium chloride) does not sublime. Therefore, sublimation is the most effective method to separate these two components. Filtration separates insoluble solids from liquids, evaporation separates a soluble solid from a liquid, and distillation separates liquids with different boiling points or a soluble solid from a liquid where the liquid is to be recovered.

24. According to Newton's first law of motion, an object at rest will remain at rest, and an object in motion will remain in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by a/an:

  1. Internal force
  2. Balanced force
  3. Unbalanced force
  4. Gravitational force

Answer: Unbalanced force

Explanation: Newton's first law of motion, also known as the law of inertia, states that an object's state of motion (at rest or in uniform motion) changes only when an external unbalanced force acts upon it. Balanced forces result in no change in motion.

25. Which of the following practices is NOT a part of sustainable agriculture for improving food resources?

  1. Crop rotation
  2. Excessive use of chemical fertilizers
  3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
  4. Mixed cropping

Answer: Excessive use of chemical fertilizers

Explanation: Excessive use of chemical fertilizers can lead to soil degradation, water pollution, and harm to beneficial microorganisms, making it an unsustainable practice. Crop rotation, Integrated Pest Management (IPM), and mixed cropping are all sustainable agricultural practices that help maintain soil fertility, reduce pest outbreaks, and increase biodiversity without causing significant environmental harm.
