Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 359 questions.

1. Which of the following food items is a rich source of carbohydrates, providing quick energy to our body?

  1. Rice
  2. Spinach
  3. Fish
  4. Butter

Answer: Rice

Explanation: Carbohydrates are the primary source of energy for our body. Rice, wheat, potatoes, and bread are common examples of carbohydrate-rich foods. Spinach is rich in vitamins and minerals, fish in protein, and butter in fats.

2. Which of these materials is generally transparent, allowing light to pass through it completely?

  1. Wood
  2. Glass
  3. Cardboard
  4. Metal

Answer: Glass

Explanation: Transparent materials allow light to pass through them completely, enabling us to see clearly through them. Glass is a common example of a transparent material. Wood, cardboard, and metal are opaque materials, meaning they do not allow light to pass through.

3. Which method is used to separate insoluble solid particles from a liquid by allowing them to settle down due to gravity?

  1. Evaporation
  2. Filtration
  3. Sedimentation
  4. Distillation

Answer: Sedimentation

Explanation: Sedimentation is the process where heavier, insoluble particles in a liquid settle down at the bottom of the container due to gravity. This is often followed by decantation to separate the liquid from the settled solids. Filtration uses a filter to separate solids from liquids, evaporation separates a soluble solid from a liquid, and distillation separates liquids with different boiling points.

4. Which part of a plant absorbs water and minerals from the soil?

  1. Flower
  2. Stem
  3. Leaf
  4. Root

Answer: Root

Explanation: The roots of a plant are responsible for anchoring the plant to the soil and absorbing water and essential minerals from it. The stem supports the plant, leaves perform photosynthesis, and flowers are involved in reproduction.

5. Which of the following joints allows movement in all directions, like the shoulder joint?

  1. Hinge joint
  2. Pivot joint
  3. Fixed joint
  4. Ball and socket joint

Answer: Ball and socket joint

Explanation: A ball and socket joint allows movement in all directions. The shoulder and hip joints are examples of ball and socket joints. A hinge joint allows movement in one plane (like the elbow), a pivot joint allows rotation (like the neck), and fixed joints do not allow any movement (like in the skull).

6. Which of these is a characteristic feature of living organisms?

  1. They do not need food
  2. They do not grow
  3. They reproduce
  4. They are always stationary

Answer: They reproduce

Explanation: Reproduction is a fundamental characteristic of living organisms, allowing them to produce offspring and ensure the continuation of their species. Living organisms also need food, grow, and can move (though not all are motile in the same way).

7. What is the standard unit for measuring length in the International System of Units (SI)?

  1. Kilogram
  2. Second
  3. Meter
  4. Liter

Answer: Meter

Explanation: The meter is the SI unit for measuring length. Kilogram is for mass, second for time, and liter is a unit for volume (though not the SI base unit, which is cubic meter).

8. What is formed when an opaque object blocks the path of light?

  1. A reflection
  2. A shadow
  3. A rainbow
  4. A light source

Answer: A shadow

Explanation: A shadow is formed when an opaque object comes in the path of light, blocking the light rays and creating a dark area behind the object where light cannot reach. Reflection is the bouncing back of light, a rainbow is formed by dispersion of light, and a light source emits light.

9. Which component is essential for an electric circuit to be complete and allow current to flow?

  1. A switch
  2. An insulator
  3. A closed path
  4. A fuse

Answer: A closed path

Explanation: For an electric current to flow, the circuit must be a closed path, meaning there are no breaks in the connection from the positive terminal of the power source, through the components, and back to the negative terminal. A switch is used to open or close the circuit, an insulator prevents current flow, and a fuse protects the circuit.

10. Which of the following materials is attracted by a magnet?

  1. Wood
  2. Plastic
  3. Iron
  4. Copper

Answer: Iron

Explanation: Magnets attract certain materials, primarily those containing iron, nickel, or cobalt. Iron is a ferromagnetic material and is strongly attracted by magnets. Wood, plastic, and copper are non-magnetic materials.

11. Which of these materials is generally used to make the body of a cooking utensil due to its good heat conductivity?

  1. Wood
  2. Plastic
  3. Metal
  4. Cotton

Answer: Metal

Explanation: Metals like aluminum and steel are good conductors of heat, meaning they allow heat to pass through them easily, which is essential for cooking. Wood, plastic, and cotton are poor conductors of heat (insulators).

12. Which method would you use to separate small pieces of stone from rice?

  1. Filtration
  2. Decantation
  3. Handpicking
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Handpicking

Explanation: Handpicking is a suitable method for separating slightly larger impurities like stones from rice, where the components are different in size, shape, or color and can be picked out manually. Filtration is for liquids and insoluble solids, decantation for separating liquids from settled solids, and evaporation for separating a soluble solid from a liquid.

13. Which part of a plant helps to absorb water and minerals from the soil?

  1. Flower
  2. Stem
  3. Leaf
  4. Root

Answer: Root

Explanation: Roots are the underground parts of a plant that anchor it to the soil and are primarily responsible for absorbing water and dissolved minerals from the soil. Flowers are for reproduction, stems support the plant, and leaves perform photosynthesis.

14. Which of these is an example of a terrestrial habitat?

  1. Pond
  2. Ocean
  3. Desert
  4. River

Answer: Desert

Explanation: Terrestrial habitats are land-based environments. A desert is a land habitat characterized by very little rainfall. Ponds, oceans, and rivers are examples of aquatic (water-based) habitats.

15. When light falls on an opaque object, what is formed behind the object?

  1. A rainbow
  2. A shadow
  3. A reflection
  4. A spectrum

Answer: A shadow

Explanation: An opaque object blocks light from passing through it. When light is blocked, a dark area called a shadow is formed behind the object. Rainbows and spectrums are formed by the dispersion of light, and reflection is when light bounces off a surface.

16. Which method is best suited to separate small pieces of stone from rice?

  1. Filtration
  2. Decantation
  3. Handpicking
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Handpicking

Explanation: Handpicking is a suitable method for separating slightly larger impurities like small stones from rice, where the components are different in size and can be easily picked out by hand. Filtration is for liquids and insoluble solids, decantation for immiscible liquids or liquid-solid mixtures where solid settles, and evaporation for separating a soluble solid from a liquid.

17. Which of the following joints allows movement in all directions?

  1. Hinge joint
  2. Fixed joint
  3. Ball and socket joint
  4. Pivot joint

Answer: Ball and socket joint

Explanation: The ball and socket joint, found in the shoulder and hip, allows for movement in all directions (rotation, flexion, extension, abduction, adduction). A hinge joint allows movement in one plane, a fixed joint allows no movement, and a pivot joint allows rotation.

18. Which of these is a non-living component of the environment?

  1. Plants
  2. Animals
  3. Sunlight
  4. Bacteria

Answer: Sunlight

Explanation: Non-living components of the environment are called abiotic factors. Sunlight is an abiotic factor, while plants, animals, and bacteria are all living organisms (biotic factors).

19. If you measure the length of a table using a measuring tape, what is the standard unit of length you would most likely use?

  1. Kilogram
  2. Litre
  3. Metre
  4. Second

Answer: Metre

Explanation: The standard unit of length in the International System of Units (SI) is the metre. Kilogram is for mass, litre for volume, and second for time.

20. Which of these materials is a good conductor of electricity?

  1. Rubber
  2. Plastic
  3. Wood
  4. Copper

Answer: Copper

Explanation: Copper is a metal and an excellent conductor of electricity, meaning it allows electric current to pass through it easily. Rubber, plastic, and wood are insulators, meaning they do not allow electricity to flow through them easily.

21. Which part of a magnet has the strongest magnetic force?

  1. The middle
  2. The ends (poles)
  3. The sides
  4. It is uniform throughout

Answer: The ends (poles)

Explanation: The magnetic force is strongest at the ends of a magnet, which are called the poles (North and South poles). The magnetic field lines are most concentrated at these points.

22. Which of these materials is generally opaque, hard, and a good conductor of heat and electricity?

  1. Plastic
  2. Wood
  3. Glass
  4. Iron

Answer: Iron

Explanation: Iron is a metal, which is typically opaque (light cannot pass through it), hard, and an excellent conductor of both heat and electricity. Plastic, wood, and glass have different properties; for example, glass is transparent, and plastic and wood are generally poor conductors.

23. Which method is used to separate husk from grains by allowing the wind to carry away the lighter husk particles?

  1. Filtration
  2. Decantation
  3. Winnowing
  4. Sedimentation

Answer: Winnowing

Explanation: Winnowing is a method used to separate heavier and lighter components of a mixture by wind or by blowing air. The lighter husk particles are carried away by the wind, while the heavier grains fall down. Filtration separates insoluble solids from liquids, decantation separates immiscible liquids or a liquid from a settled solid, and sedimentation is the process where heavier particles settle down.

24. Which type of joint allows movement in all directions, like the shoulder joint?

  1. Hinge joint
  2. Fixed joint
  3. Ball and socket joint
  4. Pivot joint

Answer: Ball and socket joint

Explanation: A ball and socket joint allows movement in all directions. The shoulder joint and hip joint are examples of ball and socket joints. A hinge joint allows movement in one plane (like the elbow), a fixed joint allows no movement (like in the skull), and a pivot joint allows rotation (like in the neck).

25. Which of the following is a characteristic feature of a desert animal, helping it survive in hot and dry conditions?

  1. Thick fur
  2. Webbed feet
  3. Long legs and humps
  4. Gills

Answer: Long legs and humps

Explanation: Desert animals like camels have long legs to keep their bodies away from the hot sand and humps to store fat, which can be converted into water and energy when needed. Thick fur is for cold climates, webbed feet for aquatic environments, and gills for breathing underwater.
