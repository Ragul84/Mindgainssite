Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 359 questions.

26. Which component is essential for an electric circuit to allow current to flow and light up a bulb?

  1. A plastic wire
  2. An open switch
  3. A complete path from one terminal of the cell to the other
  4. A rubber band

Answer: A complete path from one terminal of the cell to the other

Explanation: For an electric circuit to work and light up a bulb, there must be a complete and closed path for the electric current to flow from one terminal of the electric cell, through the bulb, and back to the other terminal. A plastic wire is an insulator, an open switch breaks the circuit, and a rubber band is an insulator and not part of the circuit.

27. Which of these body parts helps in bending our arm at the elbow?

  1. Skull
  2. Rib cage
  3. Knee joint
  4. Elbow joint

Answer: Elbow joint

Explanation: Joints are places where two or more bones meet, allowing movement. The elbow joint specifically allows the bending and straightening of the arm. The skull protects the brain, the rib cage protects internal organs, and the knee joint is in the leg.

28. Which of the following is an example of a terrestrial habitat?

  1. Pond
  2. Ocean
  3. Desert
  4. River

Answer: Desert

Explanation: Terrestrial habitats are land-based environments. A desert is a terrestrial habitat characterized by very low rainfall and extreme temperatures. Ponds, oceans, and rivers are aquatic habitats.

29. If you measure the length of a table with a ruler, what type of motion is the ruler undergoing relative to the table?

  1. Rotational motion
  2. Circular motion
  3. Translational motion
  4. Oscillatory motion

Answer: Translational motion

Explanation: Translational motion is motion along a straight or curved path where all parts of the object move in the same direction. When measuring with a ruler, the ruler moves from one point to another along the length of the table, which is a form of translational motion. Rotational motion is spinning, circular motion is moving in a circle, and oscillatory motion is back and forth.

30. Which of these materials is attracted by a magnet?

  1. Copper
  2. Aluminium
  3. Iron
  4. Plastic

Answer: Iron

Explanation: Magnets attract certain materials called magnetic materials. Iron is a common magnetic material that is strongly attracted by magnets. Copper, aluminium, and plastic are non-magnetic materials.

31. Which property helps us to sort materials like wood, plastic, and metal into different groups?

  1. Colour
  2. Hardness
  3. Shape
  4. Smell

Answer: Hardness

Explanation: Hardness is a key property used to sort materials. Wood is generally softer than metal, and plastic can vary in hardness. While colour, shape, and smell can also be properties, hardness is a more fundamental characteristic for grouping materials based on their physical resistance to scratching or indentation.

32. Which method is used to separate small stones from rice or pulses at home?

  1. Filtration
  2. Decantation
  3. Handpicking
  4. Threshing

Answer: Handpicking

Explanation: Handpicking is a simple method used to separate slightly larger impurities like small stones, dirt, or husks from food grains. Filtration is for separating insoluble solids from liquids, decantation for separating immiscible liquids or a liquid from a settled solid, and threshing for separating grain from stalks.

33. Which of these joints allows movement in all directions?

  1. Hinge joint
  2. Fixed joint
  3. Ball and socket joint
  4. Pivot joint

Answer: Ball and socket joint

Explanation: The ball and socket joint, found in the shoulder and hip, allows for movement in all directions (rotation, flexion, extension, abduction, adduction). A hinge joint allows movement in one plane (like the elbow), a fixed joint allows no movement (like in the skull), and a pivot joint allows rotation (like in the neck).

34. If you measure the length of a table using a measuring tape, what unit would be most appropriate for the measurement?

  1. Millimeter
  2. Centimeter
  3. Meter
  4. Kilometer

Answer: Meter

Explanation: For measuring the length of a table, a meter is the most appropriate unit. Millimeters are too small, centimeters are suitable for smaller objects, and kilometers are used for very long distances like between cities.

35. Which method is best suited to separate sand from water?

  1. Handpicking
  2. Threshing
  3. Filtration
  4. Winnowing

Answer: Filtration

Explanation: Filtration is a process used to separate insoluble solids from a liquid. Sand is insoluble in water, so passing the mixture through a filter paper or cloth will separate the sand from the water. Handpicking is for larger impurities, threshing for separating grain from stalks, and winnowing for separating lighter husk from heavier grains.

36. Which of these is a characteristic feature of a desert animal?

  1. Thick fur to keep warm
  2. Webbed feet for swimming
  3. Ability to store water for long periods
  4. Large leaves for photosynthesis

Answer: Ability to store water for long periods

Explanation: Desert animals, like camels, have adaptations to survive in hot and dry conditions. Storing water for long periods is a crucial adaptation. Thick fur is for cold climates, webbed feet for aquatic environments, and large leaves are for plants, not animals.

37. Which of these materials is generally opaque, meaning light cannot pass through it completely?

  1. Clear glass
  2. Tracing paper
  3. Wooden door
  4. Water

Answer: Wooden door

Explanation: Opaque materials do not allow light to pass through them at all. A wooden door is opaque. Clear glass is transparent (light passes through completely), tracing paper is translucent (light passes through partially), and water is transparent.

38. Which method would you use to separate sand from water?

  1. Handpicking
  2. Winnowing
  3. Filtration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Filtration

Explanation: Filtration is the process used to separate insoluble solids (like sand) from a liquid (like water) using a filter paper or cloth. Handpicking is for larger impurities, winnowing for lighter and heavier components using wind, and evaporation is for separating a soluble solid from a liquid.

39. Which of the following food items is primarily a source of carbohydrates, providing quick energy to our body?

  1. Rice
  2. Ghee
  3. Pulses
  4. Spinach

Answer: Rice

Explanation: Carbohydrates are the main source of energy for our body. Rice is rich in carbohydrates like starch, which are easily broken down to provide quick energy. Ghee is a fat, pulses are rich in proteins, and spinach is a source of vitamins and minerals.

40. Which of these materials would you choose to make the transparent window of a house?

  1. Wood
  2. Metal
  3. Glass
  4. Cardboard

Answer: Glass

Explanation: Windows need to be transparent so that light can pass through them, allowing us to see outside. Glass is a transparent material, meaning light can pass through it clearly. Wood, metal, and cardboard are opaque materials, which do not allow light to pass through them.

41. Which of these is a characteristic of living organisms?

  1. They do not grow
  2. They do not reproduce
  3. They respond to stimuli
  4. They do not need food

Answer: They respond to stimuli

Explanation: Living organisms exhibit several characteristics, including growth, reproduction, respiration, excretion, and the ability to respond to stimuli (changes in their surroundings). They also require food for energy.

42. Which of the following is a good conductor of electricity?

  1. Plastic
  2. Wood
  3. Copper wire
  4. Rubber

Answer: Copper wire

Explanation: A good conductor of electricity allows electric current to pass through it easily. Metals like copper are excellent conductors. Plastic, wood, and rubber are insulators, meaning they do not allow electricity to pass through them easily.

43. Which of the following food items is a rich source of carbohydrates, providing energy to our body?

  1. Spinach
  2. Butter
  3. Rice
  4. Eggs

Answer: Rice

Explanation: Carbohydrates are the primary source of energy for our body. Rice, wheat, and potatoes are rich in carbohydrates. Spinach is rich in vitamins and minerals, butter in fats, and eggs in proteins.

44. Which of these body parts helps in the movement of our arm at the shoulder?

  1. Fixed joint
  2. Hinge joint
  3. Ball and socket joint
  4. Pivot joint

Answer: Ball and socket joint

Explanation: The shoulder joint is a ball and socket joint, which allows for movement in all directions (rotation, flexion, extension, abduction, adduction). A hinge joint allows movement in one plane (like the elbow), a pivot joint allows rotation (like the neck), and fixed joints do not allow movement (like in the skull).

45. Which of the following is a characteristic of living organisms?

  1. They do not grow
  2. They do not need food
  3. They reproduce
  4. They are always stationary

Answer: They reproduce

Explanation: Reproduction is a fundamental characteristic of living organisms, allowing them to produce offspring and continue their species. Living organisms grow, need food for energy, and can move (though not all are motile in the same way).

46. Which component is essential for an electric circuit to allow current to flow?

  1. A switch
  2. A bulb
  3. A cell (battery)
  4. A wire

Answer: A cell (battery)

Explanation: A cell (or battery) is the source of electric current in a circuit. Without a power source, there would be no current to flow. A switch controls the flow, a bulb shows the effect of current, and wires conduct the current, but the cell provides the energy.

47. If you measure the length of a curved line using a thread, what is the next step after placing the thread along the curve?

  1. Measure the thread with a ruler
  2. Cut the thread into small pieces
  3. Tie knots in the thread
  4. Stretch the thread tightly

Answer: Measure the thread with a ruler

Explanation: To measure a curved line using a thread, you first place the thread carefully along the curve. Once the thread covers the entire curve, you mark the start and end points on the thread. The next step is to straighten the thread and measure its length using a ruler or measuring tape.

48. Which component is essential for a simple electric circuit to allow current to flow?

  1. A plastic rod
  2. An open switch
  3. A source of electricity (like a cell)
  4. A rubber band

Answer: A source of electricity (like a cell)

Explanation: For an electric circuit to work and allow current to flow, it needs a source of electricity (like a cell or battery) to provide the energy. An open switch breaks the circuit, a plastic rod is an insulator, and a rubber band is also an insulator and not part of the circuit's electrical path.

49. Which of these is an example of a living component in a pond ecosystem?

  1. Water
  2. Sunlight
  3. Fish
  4. Rocks

Answer: Fish

Explanation: Living components of an ecosystem are called biotic components. Fish are living organisms that breathe, grow, and reproduce. Water, sunlight, and rocks are non-living or abiotic components.

50. Which of the following materials is a good conductor of electricity?

  1. Wood
  2. Plastic
  3. Copper
  4. Rubber

Answer: Copper

Explanation: Copper is a metal and metals are generally good conductors of electricity, meaning they allow electric current to flow through them easily. Wood, plastic, and rubber are insulators, which do not allow electricity to pass through them.
