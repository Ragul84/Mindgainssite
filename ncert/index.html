NCERT · Class 11 Biology · Photosynthesis

In which part of the chloroplast does the Calvin cycle (dark reaction) take place?

The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma, the fluid around the thylakoids. The light reactions happen on the thylakoid membranes.
