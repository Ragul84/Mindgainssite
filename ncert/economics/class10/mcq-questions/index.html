26. If a consumer is not satisfied with the decision of a District Consumer Court, they can appeal to the:

  1. Supreme Court
  2. High Court
  3. State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
  4. National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission

Answer: State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission

Explanation: The Consumer Protection Act establishes a three-tier quasi-judicial machinery for consumer grievance redressal. If a consumer is dissatisfied with the verdict of the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (District Forum), they can appeal to the State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (State Commission). Further appeals can be made to the National Commission and then to the Supreme Court.

27. What does HDI stand for?

  1. Human Development Index
  2. High Development Indicator
  3. Human Development Indicator
  4. High Development Index

Answer: Human Development Index

Explanation: HDI stands for Human Development Index. It is a composite statistic of life expectancy, education, and per capita income indicators, which are used to rank countries into four tiers of human development. It is a key measure discussed in the 'Development' chapter to compare the development of different countries.

28. Which sector is also known as the service sector?

  1. Primary sector
  2. Secondary sector
  3. Tertiary sector
  4. Quaternary sector

Answer: Tertiary sector

Explanation: The tertiary sector is also known as the service sector because it provides services rather than producing goods. Examples include transport, banking, education, and healthcare. The primary sector extracts raw materials, and the secondary sector processes them into finished goods.

29. Which of the following activities is an example of the primary sector?

  1. Manufacturing of cars
  2. Banking services
  3. Fishing
  4. Teaching

Answer: Fishing

Explanation: The primary sector involves activities that directly use natural resources. Fishing is an activity that directly extracts resources from nature. Manufacturing of cars belongs to the secondary sector, while banking and teaching are part of the tertiary (service) sector.

30. What is the main function of money in an economy?

  1. To store wealth only
  2. To facilitate exchange
  3. To produce goods and services
  4. To regulate government policies

Answer: To facilitate exchange

Explanation: The main function of money is to act as a medium of exchange. It eliminates the need for a double coincidence of wants, making transactions much easier and more efficient than the barter system. While money can store wealth, its primary role is facilitating exchange.

31. What is the primary aim of foreign trade?

  1. To increase domestic production only
  2. To restrict competition among countries
  3. To connect markets and facilitate the exchange of goods and services between countries
  4. To promote self-sufficiency in all goods

Answer: To connect markets and facilitate the exchange of goods and services between countries

Explanation: Foreign trade primarily aims to connect markets of different countries, allowing for the exchange of goods, services, and investments. This enables countries to specialize in what they produce best and access a wider variety of products, leading to greater choice and often lower prices for consumers.

32. Which of the following is a right of a consumer?

  1. Right to produce
  2. Right to sell
  3. Right to information
  4. Right to advertise

Answer: Right to information

Explanation: The Right to Information is a fundamental consumer right, allowing consumers to obtain information about the quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard, and price of goods or services. The other options are not recognized consumer rights. The Consumer Protection Act (COPRA) outlines several such rights.

33. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of the unorganised sector in India?

  1. Employment is regular and secure.
  2. Workers often face low wages.
  3. There are no provisions for paid leave or provident fund.
  4. Government rules and regulations are not strictly followed.

Answer: Employment is regular and secure.

Explanation: The unorganised sector is characterized by irregular and insecure employment, low wages, lack of benefits like paid leave or provident fund, and non-adherence to government rules. Therefore, 'Employment is regular and secure' is a characteristic of the organised sector, not the unorganised sector.

34. What is the primary function of a Self-Help Group (SHG) in the context of rural credit?

  1. To provide large-scale industrial loans to entrepreneurs.
  2. To act as an intermediary between the government and large corporations.
  3. To pool savings of members and provide small loans at reasonable interest rates.
  4. To regulate the stock market and foreign exchange rates.

Answer: To pool savings of members and provide small loans at reasonable interest rates.

Explanation: Self-Help Groups (SHGs) are crucial in rural areas for pooling the small savings of their members. This collected money is then used to provide small loans to members for various purposes, often at reasonable interest rates, thereby reducing their dependence on informal lenders and promoting financial inclusion.

35. Which of the following is a major benefit of globalisation for consumers in India?

  1. Increased prices of goods and services.
  2. Limited choice of products due to foreign competition.
  3. Greater choice of goods and services at competitive prices.
  4. Reduced employment opportunities in domestic industries.

Answer: Greater choice of goods and services at competitive prices.

Explanation: Globalisation leads to increased competition among producers, both domestic and foreign. This competition often results in a wider variety of goods and services being available to consumers, often at more competitive prices, as companies strive to attract customers.
