Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the NCERT Economy syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 330 questions.

26. The ability of money to be divided into smaller units to facilitate low-value transactions is called:

  1. Portability
  2. Divisibility
  3. Durability
  4. Stability

Answer: Divisibility

Explanation: Divisibility allows for the purchase of goods of varying values without needing to split the physical asset.

27. Which function of money removes the need for the double coincidence of wants?

  1. Store of value
  2. Unit of account
  3. Medium of exchange
  4. Standard of deferred payments

Answer: Medium of exchange

Explanation: By acting as an intermediary, money allows a seller to sell to one person and buy from another.

28. If a person saves a portion of their income for a rainy day, money is performing which function?

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Store of value
  3. Unit of account
  4. Standard of deferred payments

Answer: Store of value

Explanation: Storing wealth for future consumption is the store of value function.

29. Which of the following is a primary function of money?

  1. Store of value
  2. Medium of exchange
  3. Standard of deferred payments
  4. Investment tool

Answer: Medium of exchange

Explanation: The primary functions are medium of exchange and unit of account; others are secondary/subsidiary.

30. What happens to the efficiency of trade when moving from a barter system to a monetary system?

  1. Trade becomes slower
  2. Trade becomes more efficient
  3. Trade stops
  4. No change in efficiency

Answer: Trade becomes more efficient

Explanation: Money reduces transaction costs and search costs, making trade significantly more efficient.

31. Which of the following is a disadvantage of using cattle as money in a barter-like system?

  1. High value
  2. Lack of divisibility
  3. General acceptance
  4. Intrinsic value

Answer: Lack of divisibility

Explanation: Cattle cannot be easily divided into smaller units to buy small items without killing the animal.

32. In a barter system, the search for a trading partner who has what you want and wants what you have is called the search for double coincidence of wants.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: This is the definition of double coincidence of wants.

33. Money as a store of value is only effective if the purchasing power of money remains stable over time.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: High inflation erodes the store of value function because the money buys fewer goods in the future.

34. Which function of money allows it to act as a bridge between the time of earning and the time of spending?

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Store of value
  3. Unit of account
  4. Standard of deferred payments

Answer: Store of value

Explanation: Store of value allows purchasing power to be transferred from the present to the future.

35. Money serves as a unit of account by providing a common measure for:

  1. Only the value of gold
  2. The value of goods and services
  3. The quantity of labor
  4. The speed of transactions

Answer: The value of goods and services

Explanation: Unit of account allows the value of different goods to be expressed in a single common denominator.

36. The 'double coincidence of wants' is a problem associated with which system?

  1. Monetary economy
  2. Barter system
  3. Digital economy
  4. Credit system

Answer: Barter system

Explanation: Barter requires both parties to simultaneously want what the other has, which money eliminates.

37. When money is used to settle debts or make future payments, it is performing which function?

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Unit of account
  3. Standard of deferred payments
  4. Store of value

Answer: Standard of deferred payments

Explanation: Standard of deferred payments facilitates loans and future contractual obligations.

38. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of money as a unit of account?

  1. It simplifies accounting
  2. It enables price comparison
  3. It requires physical exchange of goods
  4. It provides a common measure of value

Answer: It requires physical exchange of goods

Explanation: Unit of account is a measurement function and does not require the physical exchange of goods.

39. Money solves the problem of 'lack of a common measure of value' by acting as a:

  1. Store of value
  2. Unit of account
  3. Medium of exchange
  4. Standard of deferred payment

Answer: Unit of account

Explanation: Unit of account provides a numerical scale to measure the value of all goods and services.

40. Which function of money is most critical for the functioning of modern capital markets and loans?

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Unit of account
  3. Standard of deferred payments
  4. Store of value

Answer: Standard of deferred payments

Explanation: Deferred payments allow for the borrowing and lending of money over time.

41. If a person saves money in a bank account for future use, money is acting as a:

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Unit of account
  3. Store of value
  4. Standard of deferred payment

Answer: Store of value

Explanation: Saving money preserves purchasing power for future consumption.

42. The ability to compare the price of a pen and a notebook using a single currency is an example of:

  1. Store of value
  2. Unit of account
  3. Medium of exchange
  4. Deferred payment

Answer: Unit of account

Explanation: Unit of account allows for the comparison of values across different types of goods.

43. Which of the following is a secondary function of money?

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Unit of account
  3. Store of value
  4. None of the above

Answer: Store of value

Explanation: Store of value and standard of deferred payments are secondary functions.

44. Money acts as a medium of exchange by:

  1. Increasing the cost of goods
  2. Eliminating the need for double coincidence of wants
  3. Preventing inflation
  4. Reducing the total money supply

Answer: Eliminating the need for double coincidence of wants

Explanation: Money allows buyers to purchase goods without needing to find someone who wants their specific asset.

45. Which function of money is most affected by high inflation?

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Unit of account
  3. Store of value
  4. Standard of deferred payment

Answer: Store of value

Explanation: Inflation erodes the purchasing power of money, making it a poor store of value.

46. The use of money to record profits and losses in a company's balance sheet is an application of:

  1. Medium of exchange
  2. Unit of account
  3. Store of value
  4. Standard of deferred payment

Answer: Unit of account

Explanation: Accounting requires a common unit of measurement to aggregate different assets and liabilities.

47. Which of the following is an essential quality for money to function as a store of value?

  1. High volatility
  2. Stability of value
  3. Bulkiness
  4. Lack of portability

Answer: Stability of value

Explanation: For money to store value, its purchasing power must remain relatively stable over time.

48. Money as a unit of account removes the need for:

  1. Banks
  2. Price lists in terms of other goods
  3. Central banks
  4. Currency notes

Answer: Price lists in terms of other goods

Explanation: Without a unit of account, every good would need a price relative to every other good.

49. The primary purpose of money as a medium of exchange is to:

  1. Store wealth
  2. Facilitate trade
  3. Measure value
  4. Regulate interest rates

Answer: Facilitate trade

Explanation: Medium of exchange facilitates the smooth transfer of goods and services between parties.

50. Money as a medium of exchange eliminates the need for the barter system.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Money replaces the inefficient barter system by acting as an intermediary.
