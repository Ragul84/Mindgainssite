Class11 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class11 questions to revise the NCERT Economy syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 337 questions.

26. Which of the following is NOT a tool used for Import Substitution?

  1. Tariffs
  2. Quotas
  3. Lowering import duties
  4. Licensing

Answer: Lowering import duties

Explanation: Lowering import duties encourages imports, which contradicts the goal of import substitution.

27. The 'License Raj' refers to the system of:

  1. Free entry of firms
  2. Government permits required to start or expand businesses
  3. Licensing of foreign tourists
  4. Agricultural subsidies

Answer: Government permits required to start or expand businesses

Explanation: The permit raj required firms to obtain licenses for production and capacity expansion.

28. In a socialist economy, the distribution of goods is based on:

  1. Ability to pay
  2. Market demand
  3. Social need and equity
  4. Auction prices

Answer: Social need and equity

Explanation: Socialist systems prioritize equitable distribution based on need rather than purchasing power.

29. The Small Scale Industries (SSI) were encouraged in India because they are:

  1. Capital intensive
  2. Labor intensive
  3. Only focused on exports
  4. Owned by the government

Answer: Labor intensive

Explanation: SSIs generate more employment per unit of capital compared to large industries.

30. The main objective of the 'Planning Commission' (established in 1950) was:

  1. To manage the stock market
  2. To formulate Five Year Plans
  3. To regulate foreign exchange
  4. To manage the judiciary

Answer: To formulate Five Year Plans

Explanation: The Planning Commission was responsible for assessing resources and formulating plans for economic development.

31. The Green Revolution led to a significant increase in the production of food grains.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The use of HYV seeds and irrigation drastically increased food grain output.

32. In a centrally planned economy, the price mechanism is the primary tool for resource allocation.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: In a planned economy, the central authority allocates resources, not the price mechanism.

33. Which economic system is characterized by the private ownership of the means of production and the operation of markets?

  1. Capitalist Economy
  2. Socialist Economy
  3. Mixed Economy
  4. Traditional Economy

Answer: Capitalist Economy

Explanation: Capitalism relies on private ownership and the price mechanism to allocate resources.

34. In a socialist economy, the central problem of 'What to produce' is decided by:

  1. The Price Mechanism
  2. The Central Planning Authority
  3. Individual Consumers
  4. Private Entrepreneurs

Answer: The Central Planning Authority

Explanation: A central planning authority determines production based on social needs rather than profit.

35. Which of the following is a primary characteristic of a Mixed Economy?

  1. Complete state control of all resources
  2. Absence of private property
  3. Co-existence of public and private sectors
  4. Reliance solely on barter system

Answer: Co-existence of public and private sectors

Explanation: A mixed economy combines elements of both capitalism and socialism.

36. The 'Price Mechanism' is the primary tool for resource allocation in which system?

  1. Market Economy
  2. Planned Economy
  3. Command Economy
  4. Socialist Economy

Answer: Market Economy

Explanation: Market economies use supply and demand to determine prices and allocate resources.

37. What is the main objective of production in a socialist economic system?

  1. Profit Maximization
  2. Social Welfare
  3. Market Expansion
  4. Individual Wealth Accumulation

Answer: Social Welfare

Explanation: Socialism aims to distribute resources to maximize the welfare of society as a whole.

38. In a mixed economy, the public sector is primarily responsible for providing:

  1. Luxury goods for the wealthy
  2. Essential public services and infrastructure
  3. All consumer electronics
  4. Only agricultural tools

Answer: Essential public services and infrastructure

Explanation: The public sector focuses on social welfare and infrastructure that private firms may ignore.

39. Which of the following is NOT a feature of a Capitalist economy?

  1. Consumer Sovereignty
  2. Profit Motive
  3. Central Planning
  4. Private Property Rights

Answer: Central Planning

Explanation: Central planning is a feature of socialist/command economies, not capitalist ones.

40. Who owns the factors of production in a purely socialist economy?

  1. Private individuals
  2. The State/Community
  3. Foreign investors
  4. Shareholders

Answer: The State/Community

Explanation: Socialism advocates for collective or state ownership of the means of production.

41. Which system attempts to balance economic efficiency with social equity?

  1. Pure Capitalism
  2. Pure Socialism
  3. Mixed Economy
  4. Barter Economy

Answer: Mixed Economy

Explanation: Mixed economies use market efficiency while implementing state regulations for equity.

42. In a command economy, the allocation of resources is determined by:

  1. Market forces
  2. Government directives
  3. Consumer demand
  4. International trade

Answer: Government directives

Explanation: Command economies follow government-mandated plans for resource distribution.

43. Which of these is a disadvantage of a purely capitalist system?

  1. Lack of innovation
  2. Inefficient resource use
  3. High income inequality
  4. Lack of competition

Answer: High income inequality

Explanation: Unregulated capitalism often leads to a wide gap between the rich and the poor.

44. Which of the following is a characteristic of a planned economy?

  1. Laissez-faire approach
  2. Absence of government intervention
  3. Fixed prices set by the state
  4. Competition between firms

Answer: Fixed prices set by the state

Explanation: Planned economies often set prices administratively rather than through market forces.

45. The term 'Laissez-faire' refers to:

  1. Heavy government regulation
  2. Minimum government interference
  3. Complete state ownership
  4. A mixed economic approach

Answer: Minimum government interference

Explanation: Laissez-faire is the policy of leaving the market to operate without state interference.

46. Which system is most likely to experience 'Market Failure' without government intervention?

  1. Socialist Economy
  2. Capitalist Economy
  3. Command Economy
  4. Planned Economy

Answer: Capitalist Economy

Explanation: Market failures occur when the private market fails to allocate resources efficiently.

47. India's economic system since independence is an example of a:

  1. Purely Socialist Economy
  2. Purely Capitalist Economy
  3. Mixed Economy
  4. Traditional Economy

Answer: Mixed Economy

Explanation: India combines private enterprise with state-led planning and public sector undertakings.

48. A mixed economy allows for the coexistence of both public and private sectors.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: This is the defining characteristic of a mixed economic system.

49. In a socialist economy, there is intense competition between producers to attract consumers.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Competition is absent in socialism as the state controls production and distribution.

50. Which of the following is a characteristic of a mixed economy?

  1. Private ownership of all means of production
  2. Central planning by the government
  3. Coexistence of private and public sectors
  4. Absence of government intervention

Answer: Coexistence of private and public sectors

Explanation: A mixed economy combines elements of both capitalism and socialism, allowing for both private ownership and government intervention and ownership.
