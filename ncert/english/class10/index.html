Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Choose the correct sentence: Neither of the students ______ submitted their project yet.

  1. has
  2. have
  3. are
  4. is

Answer: has

Explanation: The subject 'Neither of the students' is singular because 'neither' refers to one student at a time. Therefore, the singular verb 'has' should be used to maintain subject-verb concord.

2. The teacher said to the students, 'Complete your homework by tomorrow.' Convert this into reported speech.

  1. The teacher told the students that they should complete their homework by tomorrow.
  2. The teacher asked the students to complete their homework by the next day.
  3. The teacher ordered the students to complete their homework by tomorrow.
  4. The teacher requested the students to complete their homework by the next day.

Answer: The teacher asked the students to complete their homework by the next day.

Explanation: When reporting a command or request, we generally use an infinitive phrase (to + verb). 'By tomorrow' changes to 'by the next day' in reported speech. 'Asked' is a suitable reporting verb for a general instruction or command.

3. Which of the following is a complex sentence?

  1. She sings beautifully, and she plays the guitar well.
  2. Although it was raining, we decided to go for a walk.
  3. He ran fast but missed the bus.
  4. The sun rose, and the birds began to sing.

Answer: Although it was raining, we decided to go for a walk.

Explanation: A complex sentence contains an independent clause and at least one dependent clause. 'Although it was raining' is a dependent clause (subordinate conjunction 'although'), and 'we decided to go for a walk' is an independent clause. The other options are compound sentences (joined by coordinating conjunctions like 'and', 'but').

4. Identify the antonym of 'benevolent'.

  1. kind
  2. generous
  3. malevolent
  4. compassionate

Answer: malevolent

Explanation: 'Benevolent' means well-meaning and kindly. Its antonym, 'malevolent', means having or showing a wish to do evil to others. The other options are synonyms or related positive traits.

5. Choose the correct determiner: There isn't ______ milk left in the fridge.

  1. many
  2. much
  3. a few
  4. some

Answer: much

Explanation: 'Milk' is an uncountable noun. 'Much' is used with uncountable nouns in negative sentences and questions. 'Many' is for countable nouns, 'a few' is for countable nouns, and 'some' is typically used in affirmative sentences or offers/requests.
