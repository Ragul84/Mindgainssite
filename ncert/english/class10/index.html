Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Choose the correct sentence: Neither of the students ______ submitted their project yet.

  1. has
  2. have
  3. are
  4. is

Answer: has

Explanation: The subject 'Neither of the students' is singular because 'neither' refers to one student at a time. Therefore, the singular verb 'has' should be used to maintain subject-verb concord.

2. The teacher said to the students, 'Complete your homework by tomorrow.' Convert this into reported speech.

  1. The teacher told the students that they should complete their homework by tomorrow.
  2. The teacher asked the students to complete their homework by the next day.
  3. The teacher ordered the students to complete their homework by tomorrow.
  4. The teacher requested the students to complete their homework by the next day.

Answer: The teacher asked the students to complete their homework by the next day.

Explanation: When reporting a command or request, we generally use an infinitive phrase (to + verb). 'By tomorrow' changes to 'by the next day' in reported speech. 'Asked' is a suitable reporting verb for a general instruction or command.

3. Which of the following is a complex sentence?

  1. She sings beautifully, and she plays the guitar well.
  2. Although it was raining, we decided to go for a walk.
  3. He ran fast but missed the bus.
  4. The sun rose, and the birds began to sing.

Answer: Although it was raining, we decided to go for a walk.

Explanation: A complex sentence contains an independent clause and at least one dependent clause. 'Although it was raining' is a dependent clause (subordinate conjunction 'although'), and 'we decided to go for a walk' is an independent clause. The other options are compound sentences (joined by coordinating conjunctions like 'and', 'but').

4. Identify the antonym of 'benevolent'.

  1. kind
  2. generous
  3. malevolent
  4. compassionate

Answer: malevolent

Explanation: 'Benevolent' means well-meaning and kindly. Its antonym, 'malevolent', means having or showing a wish to do evil to others. The other options are synonyms or related positive traits.

5. Choose the correct determiner: There isn't ______ milk left in the fridge.

  1. many
  2. much
  3. a few
  4. some

Answer: much

Explanation: 'Milk' is an uncountable noun. 'Much' is used with uncountable nouns in negative sentences and questions. 'Many' is for countable nouns, 'a few' is for countable nouns, and 'some' is typically used in affirmative sentences or offers/requests.

6. The new manager decided to ______ the old policies.

  1. do away with
  2. put up with
  3. look up to
  4. break down

Answer: do away with

Explanation: The phrasal verb 'do away with' means to abolish or get rid of. This fits the context of a new manager changing old policies. 'Put up with' means to tolerate, 'look up to' means to admire, and 'break down' means to stop functioning.

7. Which of the following is a direct question?

  1. He asked if I was coming.
  2. I wonder where she went.
  3. Could you tell me the time?
  4. She wanted to know what I was doing.

Answer: Could you tell me the time?

Explanation: A direct question is a question asked directly, usually ending with a question mark. 'Could you tell me the time?' is a direct question. The other options are indirect questions or statements.

8. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The old house stood on a hill, overlooking the valley. Its windows, like vacant eyes, stared out at the world, reflecting the setting sun in hues of orange and purple. A lone, gnarled oak tree guarded its entrance, its branches reaching out like skeletal arms.' What is the mood conveyed by the description of the house and its surroundings?

  1. Joyful and vibrant
  2. Mysterious and melancholic
  3. Lively and bustling
  4. Peaceful and serene

Answer: Mysterious and melancholic

Explanation: The description uses words like 'vacant eyes,' 'gnarled oak tree,' and 'skeletal arms,' which evoke a sense of mystery, age, and a touch of sadness or gloom. This combination points towards a mysterious and melancholic mood, rather than joy, liveliness, or simple peace.

9. Choose the correct sentence: Neither the students nor the teacher _______ present at the meeting.

  1. was
  2. were
  3. are
  4. have been

Answer: was

Explanation: This question tests Subject-Verb Concord. When 'neither...nor' connects two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject closer to it. Here, 'the teacher' is singular, so the singular verb 'was' is correct.

10. The teacher said to the students, 'Always speak the truth.' Convert this into reported speech.

  1. The teacher told the students that always speak the truth.
  2. The teacher advised the students to always speak the truth.
  3. The teacher asked the students if they always spoke the truth.
  4. The teacher ordered the students always speak the truth.

Answer: The teacher advised the students to always speak the truth.

Explanation: This is a command/advice in direct speech. When converting commands or advice to reported speech, we use an infinitive construction (to + verb). 'Advised' is the most appropriate reporting verb here, followed by 'to always speak the truth'.

11. Which of the following is a statement?

  1. What a beautiful day!
  2. Please close the door.
  3. The sun rises in the east.
  4. Are you coming to the party?

Answer: The sun rises in the east.

Explanation: A statement is a declarative sentence that conveys information and ends with a full stop. 'What a beautiful day!' is an exclamation. 'Please close the door.' is a request/command. 'Are you coming to the party?' is a question. 'The sun rises in the east.' is a factual statement.

12. There isn't _______ milk left in the fridge. We need to buy _______ more.

  1. many, a few
  2. much, some
  3. a lot, any
  4. few, much

Answer: much, some

Explanation: This question tests Determiners. 'Milk' is an uncountable noun, so 'much' is used with it in negative sentences. 'Some' is used for an unspecified quantity of uncountable nouns in affirmative sentences or requests/offers. Therefore, 'much' and 'some' are the correct determiners.

13. Identify the complex sentence among the following:

  1. He ran fast, but he missed the train.
  2. She sings well, and she plays the guitar.
  3. Although it was raining, we went for a walk.
  4. The dog barked loudly.

Answer: Although it was raining, we went for a walk.

Explanation: A complex sentence contains an independent clause and at least one dependent clause. 'Although it was raining' is a dependent clause (subordinate conjunction 'although'), and 'we went for a walk' is an independent clause. The other options are a compound sentence (joined by 'but' or 'and') and a simple sentence.

14. Choose the antonym of 'benevolent'.

  1. kind
  2. generous
  3. malevolent
  4. compassionate

Answer: malevolent

Explanation: 'Benevolent' means well-meaning and kindly. Its antonym would be a word meaning ill-willed or malicious. 'Malevolent' means having or showing a wish to do evil to others, making it the direct opposite.

15. The new manager always tries to _______ his authority over the team.

  1. put up with
  2. lay down
  3. boss around
  4. throw his weight around

Answer: throw his weight around

Explanation: This question tests idioms and phrasal verbs. 'To throw one's weight around' means to use one's position or authority in an aggressive or overbearing way. 'Put up with' means to tolerate. 'Lay down' means to establish rules. 'Boss around' means to tell someone what to do in an overbearing way, but 'throw his weight around' more accurately captures the idea of asserting authority in an unpleasant manner.

16. Read the following passage: 'The old house stood on a hill, overlooking the sleepy town. Its windows, like vacant eyes, stared out at the world, holding secrets within its crumbling walls. A lone oak tree, gnarled and ancient, guarded the entrance, its branches swaying rhythmically in the wind.' What can be inferred about the house?

  1. It is a new and modern house.
  2. It is likely abandoned or very old.
  3. It is a lively and welcoming place.
  4. It is located in the center of the town.

Answer: It is likely abandoned or very old.

Explanation: This is an inference-based reading comprehension question. Phrases like 'old house', 'vacant eyes', 'crumbling walls', and 'gnarled and ancient' suggest that the house is either abandoned, very old, or in a state of disrepair, implying it's not new, modern, lively, or welcoming. The description of it 'overlooking the sleepy town' and 'on a hill' indicates it's not in the center.

17. There is ______ milk left in the bottle, so we can't make tea.

  1. a little
  2. little
  3. much
  4. many

Answer: little

Explanation: This question tests determiners. 'Milk' is an uncountable noun. 'Little' (without 'a') means 'hardly any' or 'not much', implying a scarcity that prevents making tea. 'A little' would mean 'some', which contradicts the second part of the sentence. 'Much' would imply a large quantity, and 'many' is used for countable nouns.

18. The manager decided to ______ the new employee for her excellent performance.

  1. call off
  2. call on
  3. call up
  4. call for

Answer: call for

Explanation: This question tests phrasal verbs. 'Call for' means to demand or require something, or to deserve something. In this context, the new employee's excellent performance 'calls for' recognition or praise. 'Call off' means to cancel. 'Call on' means to visit or ask someone to do something. 'Call up' means to telephone or summon.

19. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The old woman sat by the window, her gaze fixed on the bustling street below. A faint smile played on her lips as she watched the children chase a stray dog. The aroma of freshly baked bread wafted from the bakery across the road, reminding her of her own childhood.' What can be inferred about the old woman's mood?

  1. She is feeling lonely and sad.
  2. She is angry at the children.
  3. She is nostalgic and content.
  4. She is bored and indifferent.

Answer: She is nostalgic and content.

Explanation: The passage describes her with 'a faint smile' and being 'reminded her of her own childhood' by a pleasant aroma. These details suggest a positive, reflective, and peaceful state, indicating she is nostalgic and content, rather than lonely, angry, or bored.

20. Choose the correct sentence: The team ______ playing well since the beginning of the tournament.

  1. has been
  2. have been
  3. is
  4. are

Answer: has been

Explanation: The subject 'The team' is singular, even though it refers to multiple individuals. When a collective noun acts as a single unit, it takes a singular verb. 'Since the beginning' indicates an action that started in the past and continues to the present, requiring the present perfect continuous tense. Therefore, 'has been' is the correct choice.

21. There isn't ______ milk left in the fridge. We need to buy ______.

  1. many, some
  2. much, some
  3. few, any
  4. a lot, a few

Answer: much, some

Explanation: 'Milk' is an uncountable noun. For uncountable nouns, we use 'much' in negative sentences and questions, and 'some' in affirmative sentences or offers/requests. Therefore, 'much' is appropriate for 'There isn't ______ milk' and 'some' is appropriate for 'We need to buy ______'.

22. Identify the complex sentence:

  1. He ran fast, but he missed the train.
  2. She sings beautifully and plays the guitar well.
  3. Although it was raining, they went for a walk.
  4. The dog barked loudly; the cat ran away.

Answer: Although it was raining, they went for a walk.

Explanation: A complex sentence contains one independent clause and at least one dependent clause. 'Although it was raining' is a dependent clause (subordinate conjunction 'although'), and 'they went for a walk' is an independent clause. Option A is a compound sentence (two independent clauses joined by 'but'). Option B is a simple sentence with a compound predicate. Option D is a compound sentence (two independent clauses joined by a semicolon).

23. Choose the antonym for 'benevolent'.

  1. kind
  2. generous
  3. malevolent
  4. compassionate

Answer: malevolent

Explanation: 'Benevolent' means well-meaning and kindly. 'Kind,' 'generous,' and 'compassionate' are synonyms or related positive traits. 'Malevolent' means wishing evil or harm to others, making it the direct opposite (antonym) of 'benevolent'.

24. The manager decided to ______ the new employee for her outstanding performance.

  1. call off
  2. call on
  3. call for
  4. call up

Answer: call for

Explanation: The phrasal verb 'call for' means to require or deserve something. In this context, the employee's outstanding performance 'calls for' recognition or praise. 'Call off' means to cancel. 'Call on' means to visit or ask someone to do something. 'Call up' means to telephone or summon.

25. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The old house stood on a hill, overlooking the valley. Its windows, like vacant eyes, stared out at the world, reflecting the setting sun in a melancholic glow. No one had lived there for decades, and the garden had long since surrendered to the wild embrace of nature.' What can be inferred about the house?

  1. It is a new construction.
  2. It is well-maintained.
  3. It is abandoned and neglected.
  4. It is a popular tourist attraction.

Answer: It is abandoned and neglected.

Explanation: The passage describes the house as 'old,' with 'vacant eyes' and a 'melancholic glow.' It explicitly states 'No one had lived there for decades' and 'the garden had long since surrendered to the wild embrace of nature.' These details strongly infer that the house is abandoned and neglected, not new, well-maintained, or a tourist attraction.
