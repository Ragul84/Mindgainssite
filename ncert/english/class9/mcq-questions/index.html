26. What is the antonym of 'benevolent'?

  1. Kind
  2. Generous
  3. Malevolent
  4. Compassionate

Answer: Malevolent

Explanation: This question tests Synonyms and Antonyms. 'Benevolent' means well-meaning and kindly. 'Kind', 'generous', and 'compassionate' are synonyms or related positive traits. 'Malevolent' means having or showing a wish to do evil to others, making it the direct opposite or antonym of 'benevolent'.

27. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The old house stood on a hill overlooking the sea. Its windows were like vacant eyes, staring out at the endless horizon. A lone cypress tree, gnarled and twisted by years of wind, stood guard beside the crumbling porch.' What does the phrase 'vacant eyes' suggest about the windows?

  1. They were sparkling clean.
  2. They were empty and lifeless.
  3. They were reflecting the sea.
  4. They were brightly painted.

Answer: They were empty and lifeless.

Explanation: This question tests Reading Comprehension and Analysis. The phrase 'vacant eyes' is a metaphor, comparing the windows to eyes that are empty or lacking expression. This suggests a sense of emptiness, abandonment, or lifelessness in the house, rather than cleanliness, reflection, or bright paint.

28. In the sentence, 'The ancient ruins were a testament to the ingenuity of the civilization,' what does 'ingenuity' mean?

  1. Lack of skill
  2. Cleverness and originality
  3. Destruction and decay
  4. Simplicity and plainness

Answer: Cleverness and originality

Explanation: This question tests Vocabulary and Contextual Meanings. The word 'ingenuity' refers to the quality of being clever, original, and inventive. In the context of 'ancient ruins' being a 'testament' (evidence or proof) to this quality, it implies that the civilization was skilled and creative in its construction. 'Lack of skill', 'destruction and decay', and 'simplicity and plainness' are all contrary to this meaning.

29. Choose the correct determiner to complete the sentence: _________ students were present at the annual function.

  1. Much
  2. Little
  3. Many
  4. Any

Answer: Many

Explanation: The noun 'students' is a countable plural noun. 'Much' and 'little' are used with uncountable nouns. 'Any' is typically used in negative sentences or questions. 'Many' is the correct determiner for countable plural nouns.

30. Identify the correct sentence in the Simple Past Tense: 'She _______ a beautiful song yesterday.'

  1. sings
  2. will sing
  3. sang
  4. is singing

Answer: sang

Explanation: The phrase 'yesterday' indicates an action completed in the past. The Simple Past Tense form of the verb 'to sing' is 'sang'. 'Sings' is Simple Present, 'will sing' is Simple Future, and 'is singing' is Present Continuous.

31. Choose the correct preposition to complete the sentence: 'She is good _________ mathematics.'

  1. at
  2. in
  3. on
  4. with

Answer: at

Explanation: The idiom 'good at' is used to express proficiency or skill in a particular subject or activity. Therefore, 'at' is the correct preposition.

32. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The sun dipped below the horizon, painting the sky in hues of orange and purple. A gentle breeze rustled through the leaves, carrying the scent of jasmine. The day's heat slowly receded, replaced by the cool embrace of twilight.' What is the primary mood conveyed by the passage?

  1. Excitement
  2. Melancholy
  3. Serenity
  4. Suspense

Answer: Serenity

Explanation: The passage describes a peaceful and calming scene: the sunset, gentle breeze, scent of jasmine, and the cool embrace of twilight. These elements evoke a feeling of tranquility and calmness, which aligns with 'serenity'. There are no elements suggesting excitement, sadness (melancholy), or tension (suspense).

33. Choose the correct option to complete the sentence: 'Neither the students nor the teacher ______ present at the meeting.'

  1. was
  2. were
  3. is
  4. are

Answer: was

Explanation: This question tests Subject-Verb Concord. When 'neither...nor' is used, the verb agrees with the noun or pronoun closest to it. In this case, 'teacher' is singular, so the singular verb 'was' is appropriate. 'Were' and 'are' are plural, and 'is' is present tense, while the context implies a past event ('present at the meeting').

34. Identify the sentence that is in the Passive Voice:

  1. The gardener waters the plants daily.
  2. The plants are watered by the gardener daily.
  3. The gardener is watering the plants.
  4. The plants grow well in this soil.

Answer: The plants are watered by the gardener daily.

Explanation: This question tests understanding of Passive Voice. In the passive voice, the subject of the sentence receives the action. The structure typically involves a form of 'to be' + past participle. Option B, 'The plants are watered by the gardener daily,' clearly shows 'plants' (the subject) receiving the action 'watered', performed by 'the gardener'. Options A and C are in the active voice, and Option D is an intransitive sentence.

35. Read the following sentence and choose the most appropriate synonym for the underlined word: 'The ancient ruins were a magnificent sight.'

  1. ordinary
  2. splendid
  3. dull
  4. common

Answer: splendid

Explanation: This question tests Vocabulary and Synonyms. The word 'magnificent' means extremely beautiful, elaborate, or impressive. Among the given options, 'splendid' is the closest in meaning, also implying grandeur and impressiveness. 'Ordinary', 'dull', and 'common' are antonyms or words with opposite meanings.
