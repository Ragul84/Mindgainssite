Class9 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class9 questions to revise the NCERT English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Choose the correct option to complete the sentence: 'Neither of the students ______ submitted their project yet.'

  1. have
  2. has
  3. are
  4. were

Answer: has

Explanation: The phrase 'Neither of the students' indicates a singular subject because 'neither' refers to 'not one' or 'not the other' of two. Therefore, the verb must be singular. 'Has' is the correct singular verb form that agrees with 'neither'.

2. Identify the correct sentence in the simple past tense: 'She ______ to the market yesterday.'

  1. goes
  2. will go
  3. went
  4. is going

Answer: went

Explanation: The simple past tense is used to describe actions that happened at a specific time in the past. The word 'yesterday' clearly indicates a past action. 'Went' is the simple past form of the verb 'to go'.

3. Convert the following sentence into passive voice: 'The children are playing cricket.'

  1. Cricket is played by the children.
  2. Cricket was played by the children.
  3. Cricket is being played by the children.
  4. Cricket has been played by the children.

Answer: Cricket is being played by the children.

Explanation: To convert an active voice sentence in the present continuous tense (subject + is/am/are + verb-ing + object) to passive voice, the structure is: object + is/am/are + being + past participle of the verb + by + subject. Here, 'cricket' is the object, 'is' is the auxiliary verb, 'being' is added, and 'played' is the past participle of 'playing'.

4. Identify the type of clause underlined in the sentence: 'I know the boy who won the race.'

  1. Noun clause
  2. Adverb clause
  3. Relative clause
  4. Independent clause

Answer: Relative clause

Explanation: The underlined part 'who won the race' is a dependent clause that modifies the noun 'boy'. It provides more information about the boy. Clauses that modify nouns or pronouns are called relative clauses (also known as adjective clauses) and are often introduced by relative pronouns like 'who', 'whom', 'whose', 'which', or 'that'.

5. Choose the correct preposition to complete the sentence: 'She is fond ______ classical music.'

  1. of
  2. with
  3. for
  4. about

Answer: of

Explanation: The adjective 'fond' is always followed by the preposition 'of' when expressing a liking or affection for something. So, 'fond of' is the correct idiomatic expression.

6. Which pair of words has an analogous relationship to 'Doctor : Hospital'?

  1. Teacher : School
  2. Chef : Restaurant
  3. Lawyer : Court
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The analogy 'Doctor : Hospital' signifies a professional and their primary workplace. All the given options follow the same pattern: 'Teacher : School' (teacher works in a school), 'Chef : Restaurant' (chef works in a restaurant), and 'Lawyer : Court' (lawyer works in a court). Therefore, 'All of the above' is the correct answer.

7. Read the following passage: 'The old house stood on a hill, overlooking the quiet town. Its windows were like vacant eyes, staring out at the world. A gnarled oak tree, centuries old, guarded its entrance, its branches twisted like ancient arms.' What is the primary mood conveyed by the description of the house?

  1. Joyful
  2. Mysterious
  3. Lively
  4. Modern

Answer: Mysterious

Explanation: The description uses words like 'old house', 'vacant eyes', 'gnarled oak tree', and 'twisted like ancient arms'. These words evoke a sense of age, emptiness, and a slightly eerie or intriguing atmosphere, which collectively contribute to a mysterious mood. There's no indication of joy, liveliness, or modernity.

8. In the sentence, 'The politician's speech was full of platitudes, offering no concrete solutions,' what does 'platitudes' most likely mean?

  1. Original ideas
  2. Sincere promises
  3. Meaningless clichés
  4. Detailed plans

Answer: Meaningless clichés

Explanation: The context clue 'offering no concrete solutions' suggests that the speech lacked substance or originality. 'Platitudes' refers to a remark or statement, especially one with a moral content, that has been used too often to be interesting or thoughtful; a cliché. Therefore, 'meaningless clichés' is the most appropriate contextual meaning.

9. Choose the correct determiner to complete the sentence: 'There isn't ______ milk left in the refrigerator.'

  1. many
  2. much
  3. a few
  4. several

Answer: much

Explanation: The noun 'milk' is an uncountable noun. 'Much' is used with uncountable nouns in negative sentences and questions. 'Many', 'a few', and 'several' are used with countable nouns.

10. Identify the correct sentence in the simple past tense: 'She ______ her homework yesterday.'

  1. has done
  2. will do
  3. did
  4. is doing

Answer: did

Explanation: The simple past tense is used to describe actions completed in the past. 'Did' is the simple past form of the verb 'do'. 'Has done' is present perfect, 'will do' is simple future, and 'is doing' is present continuous.

11. Convert the following sentence into passive voice: 'The students are writing an essay.'

  1. An essay is written by the students.
  2. An essay was being written by the students.
  3. An essay is being written by the students.
  4. The students have written an essay.

Answer: An essay is being written by the students.

Explanation: The original sentence is in the present continuous tense (subject + is/are + verb-ing + object). To convert it to passive voice, the structure becomes: object + is/are + being + past participle of the verb + by + subject. Therefore, 'An essay is being written by the students' is the correct passive form.

12. Identify the type of clause in the underlined part of the sentence: 'I know the boy who won the first prize.'

  1. Noun Clause
  2. Adverb Clause
  3. Relative Clause
  4. Independent Clause

Answer: Relative Clause

Explanation: The underlined part 'who won the first prize' modifies the noun 'boy' by providing more information about him. Clauses that modify nouns or pronouns are called Relative Clauses (also known as Adjective Clauses). They usually begin with relative pronouns like who, whom, whose, which, or that.

13. Choose the correct preposition to complete the sentence: 'The cat jumped ______ the table.'

  1. in
  2. on
  3. over
  4. at

Answer: over

Explanation: The preposition 'over' is used to indicate movement from one side to another, often implying an arc or a leap, which fits the action of a cat jumping. 'In' implies being inside, 'on' implies being on the surface, and 'at' indicates a specific point or direction.

14. What is the antonym of 'ancient'?

  1. old
  2. modern
  3. historic
  4. aged

Answer: modern

Explanation: 'Ancient' means belonging to the very distant past and no longer in existence. 'Modern' means relating to the present or recent times, making it the direct opposite. 'Old', 'historic', and 'aged' are synonyms or related terms.

15. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The sun dipped below the horizon, painting the sky in hues of orange and purple. A gentle breeze rustled through the leaves, carrying the scent of jasmine. Soon, the first stars began to twinkle, signaling the end of a beautiful day.' What time of day is being described?

  1. Morning
  2. Noon
  3. Evening
  4. Midnight

Answer: Evening

Explanation: The passage mentions 'the sun dipped below the horizon', 'painting the sky in hues of orange and purple', and 'the first stars began to twinkle'. These are all characteristic descriptions of evening or twilight, just after sunset.

16. In the sentence, 'The old mansion stood desolate on the hill,' what does 'desolate' most closely mean?

  1. crowded
  2. well-maintained
  3. empty and bleak
  4. bright and cheerful

Answer: empty and bleak

Explanation: 'Desolate' means feeling or showing misery, unhappiness, or loneliness. When describing a place, it means uninhabited and giving an impression of bleak emptiness. Therefore, 'empty and bleak' is the closest contextual meaning. 'Crowded', 'well-maintained', and 'bright and cheerful' are antonyms or unrelated.

17. Choose the correct determiner to complete the sentence: 'There isn't ______ milk left in the fridge.'

  1. many
  2. much
  3. few
  4. a lot

Answer: much

Explanation: The noun 'milk' is an uncountable noun. 'Much' is used with uncountable nouns in negative sentences and questions. 'Many' is used with countable nouns. 'Few' is also used with countable nouns. 'A lot' can be used with both, but 'much' is more appropriate for a negative statement about an uncountable quantity.

19. Choose the correct preposition to complete the sentence: 'The book is ______ the table.'

  1. in
  2. at
  3. on
  4. under

Answer: on

Explanation: 'On' is the most appropriate preposition to indicate that something is resting on the surface of another object. 'In' implies being enclosed, 'at' indicates a specific point, and 'under' means beneath.

20. In the sentence, 'The old house had a peculiar aroma,' what does 'peculiar' most likely mean?

  1. pleasant
  2. strong
  3. unusual
  4. faint

Answer: unusual

Explanation: 'Peculiar' means strange or odd, or unusual. While an aroma can be strong or faint, 'peculiar' specifically describes its unusual nature, not its intensity or pleasantness. Therefore, 'unusual' is the best contextual meaning.

22. In the sentence, 'The old house had a dilapidated roof,' what does 'dilapidated' most closely mean?

  1. new
  2. well-maintained
  3. in a state of disrepair
  4. beautifully designed

Answer: in a state of disrepair

Explanation: 'Dilapidated' means in a state of disrepair or ruin as a result of age or neglect. Therefore, 'in a state of disrepair' is the closest contextual meaning. 'New', 'well-maintained', and 'beautifully designed' are antonyms or unrelated.

23. Identify the correct sentence: If I had known about the party, I ______ definitely have come.

  1. would
  2. will
  3. shall
  4. can

Answer: would

Explanation: This question tests Tenses and Modals, specifically conditional sentences (Type 3). The structure for Type 3 conditional sentences is 'If + past perfect, would/could/might + have + past participle'. Here, 'If I had known' requires 'would have come' to complete the conditional meaning of a past unreal condition and its consequence.

24. Convert the following sentence into Passive Voice: The children are drawing beautiful pictures.

  1. Beautiful pictures are being drawn by the children.
  2. Beautiful pictures were drawn by the children.
  3. The children have drawn beautiful pictures.
  4. Beautiful pictures are drawn by the children.

Answer: Beautiful pictures are being drawn by the children.

Explanation: This question tests Passive Voice. The original sentence is in the Present Continuous Tense (Subject + is/am/are + V-ing + Object). To convert it to passive voice, the structure is Object + is/am/are + being + V3 (past participle) + by + Subject. Thus, 'Beautiful pictures are being drawn by the children' is the correct passive form.
