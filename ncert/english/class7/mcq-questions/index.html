26. Convert the following into indirect speech: 'She said, 'I am going to the market today.''

  1. She said that she was going to the market that day.
  2. She said that she is going to the market today.
  3. She said that I was going to the market that day.
  4. She said that she had gone to the market that day.

Answer: She said that she was going to the market that day.

Explanation: When converting direct speech to indirect speech, several changes occur. 'Said' remains 'said'. The comma and quotation marks are removed, and 'that' is often added. The pronoun 'I' changes to 'she' to match the subject. The present continuous tense 'am going' changes to past continuous 'was going'. Finally, time expressions like 'today' change to 'that day'.

27. Choose the correct verb to complete the sentence: 'Neither the students nor the teacher _______ present.'

  1. was
  2. were
  3. are
  4. have been

Answer: was

Explanation: When 'neither...nor' is used, the verb agrees with the noun or pronoun closest to it. In this sentence, 'teacher' is singular and is closest to the blank, so the singular verb 'was' should be used.

28. The students ______ playing in the park right now.

  1. is
  2. are
  3. was
  4. were

Answer: are

Explanation: This question tests Subject-Verb Agreement. The subject 'students' is plural, so the plural verb 'are' must be used. 'Is' is for singular subjects, and 'was'/'were' are past tense forms.

29. Choose the correct passive voice form of the sentence: 'The gardener waters the plants daily.'

  1. The plants are watered daily by the gardener.
  2. The plants were watered daily by the gardener.
  3. The plants is watered daily by the gardener.
  4. The gardener is watering the plants daily.

Answer: The plants are watered daily by the gardener.

Explanation: This question tests Active and Passive Voice. In passive voice, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject. The tense remains the same (Simple Present). So, 'The plants' (new subject) + 'are' (to be verb for plural in present) + 'watered' (past participle) + 'daily by the gardener'.

30. She said, 'I am going to the market.' (Change into Indirect Speech)

  1. She said that she was going to the market.
  2. She said that I was going to the market.
  3. She said that she is going to the market.
  4. She said that she had gone to the market.

Answer: She said that she was going to the market.

Explanation: This question tests Direct and Indirect Speech. When converting to indirect speech, the reporting verb 'said' is followed by 'that'. The pronoun 'I' changes to 'she' (referring to the speaker). The present continuous tense 'am going' changes to past continuous 'was going' as the reporting verb is in the past tense.

31. You ______ submit your project by Friday, or you will lose marks.

  1. can
  2. might
  3. must
  4. could

Answer: must

Explanation: This question tests Modals. The sentence implies a strong obligation or necessity, as there's a consequence for not submitting. 'Must' is the appropriate modal verb to express strong obligation or compulsion. 'Can' expresses ability, 'might' expresses possibility, and 'could' expresses past ability or possibility.

32. By next year, I ______ my graduation.

  1. will complete
  2. will have completed
  3. have completed
  4. am completing

Answer: will have completed

Explanation: This question tests Tenses and Verbs. The phrase 'By next year' indicates a future action that will be completed by a specific time in the future. This requires the Future Perfect Tense, which is formed with 'will have' + past participle. So, 'will have completed' is the correct option.

33. The cat jumped ______ the table.

  1. in
  2. on
  3. over
  4. into

Answer: over

Explanation: This question tests Prepositions. 'Over' is the most appropriate preposition to describe the cat's movement from one side to another, clearing the table. 'On' implies being stationary on the surface, 'in' implies being inside, and 'into' implies entering something.

34. Choose the synonym for 'ancient'.

  1. Modern
  2. Old
  3. New
  4. Recent

Answer: Old

Explanation: This question tests Synonyms and Antonyms. A synonym is a word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word. 'Ancient' means belonging to the very distant past, so 'old' is its closest synonym. 'Modern', 'new', and 'recent' are antonyms or related to the opposite meaning.

35. Choose the correct sentence in passive voice for: 'The students are reading books.'

  1. Books are being read by the students.
  2. Books were read by the students.
  3. Books are read by the students.
  4. The students read books.

Answer: Books are being read by the students.

Explanation: The original sentence 'The students are reading books' is in the present continuous tense (Subject + is/are/am + V-ing + Object). To change it to passive voice, the structure becomes Object + is/are/am + being + V3 (past participle) + by + Subject. Here, 'Books' is the object, 'are' is the helping verb, 'being' is added for continuous tense, and 'read' is the past participle of 'reading'. Therefore, 'Books are being read by the students' is the correct passive voice conversion.
