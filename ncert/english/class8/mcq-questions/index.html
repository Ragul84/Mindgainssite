26. Identify the adverbial clause in the sentence: 'He will succeed if he works hard.'

  1. He will succeed
  2. if he works hard
  3. he works hard
  4. will succeed

Answer: if he works hard

Explanation: An adverbial clause modifies a verb, an adjective, or another adverb, and it usually begins with a subordinating conjunction. Here, 'if he works hard' is an adverbial clause of condition, telling us under what condition he will succeed.

27. Choose the correct conjunction to complete the sentence: 'She studied hard, ______ she failed the exam.'

  1. but
  2. and
  3. so
  4. for

Answer: but

Explanation: The sentence presents a contrast: studying hard usually leads to success, but here it led to failure. The conjunction 'but' is used to show contrast between two clauses.

28. Identify the relative clause in the following sentence: 'The boy who won the race is my cousin.'

  1. The boy
  2. who won the race
  3. is my cousin
  4. The boy is my cousin

Answer: who won the race

Explanation: A relative clause (also called an adjective clause) begins with a relative pronoun (who, whom, whose, which, that) and modifies a noun or pronoun. Here, 'who won the race' describes 'the boy'.

29. Choose the correct verb to complete the sentence: 'Neither of the students ______ present today.'

  1. is
  2. are
  3. were
  4. have been

Answer: is

Explanation: When 'neither of' is used, the verb should agree with the singular pronoun 'neither', even if the noun following 'of' is plural. Therefore, 'is' is the correct singular verb.

30. Read the passage and answer the question: 'The ancient city of Pompeii was buried under volcanic ash and pumice after the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD. For centuries, it remained hidden, preserving a snapshot of Roman life. When it was rediscovered, archaeologists found well-preserved buildings, frescoes, and even human remains, offering invaluable insights into the daily lives of its inhabitants.' What caused the destruction of Pompeii?

  1. An earthquake
  2. A flood
  3. A volcanic eruption
  4. A war

Answer: A volcanic eruption

Explanation: The passage clearly states: 'The ancient city of Pompeii was buried under volcanic ash and pumice after the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD.' This indicates that a volcanic eruption was the cause of its destruction.

31. Identify the sentence with the correct subject-verb agreement:

  1. Each of the students have submitted their assignments.
  2. Neither the principal nor the teachers is present.
  3. The committee has decided on the new rules.
  4. A number of books is missing from the library.

Answer: The committee has decided on the new rules.

Explanation: Subject-verb agreement means that the verb must agree in number with its subject. In option A, 'Each' is singular, so the verb should be 'has' (Each of the students has submitted...). In option B, when 'neither...nor' connects two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject closer to it. 'Teachers' is plural, so the verb should be 'are' (Neither the principal nor the teachers are present). In option C, 'committee' is a collective noun. When it acts as a single unit, it takes a singular verb, which 'has decided' is. In option D, 'A number of' is followed by a plural noun and takes a plural verb, so it should be 'are missing' (A number of books are missing...). Therefore, option C is the only grammatically correct sentence.

32. Complete the sentence with the most appropriate conjunction: He studied hard, ______ he failed the exam.

  1. and
  2. but
  3. so
  4. for

Answer: but

Explanation: Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. The sentence presents a contrast: studying hard usually leads to success, but here it led to failure. 'But' is a coordinating conjunction used to show contrast or opposition between two clauses. 'And' is used for addition, 'so' for consequence, and 'for' for reason. Therefore, 'but' is the most appropriate conjunction to express this contrasting idea.

33. Choose the correct one-word substitution for: 'A person who is skilled in many different fields.'

  1. Novice
  2. Dilettante
  3. Versatile
  4. Specialist

Answer: Versatile

Explanation: One-word substitution involves replacing a phrase or a clause with a single word that conveys the same meaning. A 'novice' is a beginner. A 'dilettante' is a person who cultivates an area of interest, such as the arts, without real commitment or knowledge. A 'specialist' is a person who concentrates primarily on a particular subject or activity. A 'versatile' person is able to adapt or be adapted to many different functions or activities. Therefore, 'versatile' accurately describes a person skilled in many different fields.

34. Identify the sentence that uses an adverbial clause correctly:

  1. She sings beautifully, which is why everyone loves her.
  2. Although it was raining, they went for a walk.
  3. He is the boy who won the first prize.
  4. The book on the table is mine.

Answer: Although it was raining, they went for a walk.

Explanation: An adverbial clause is a dependent clause that functions as an adverb, modifying a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It typically answers questions like 'when?', 'where?', 'why?', 'how?', 'under what condition?', or 'to what extent?'. In option A, 'which is why everyone loves her' is a relative clause modifying the entire preceding clause. In option B, 'Although it was raining' is an adverbial clause of concession, indicating a contrast to the main clause 'they went for a walk'. It tells us 'under what condition' they went for a walk. In option C, 'who won the first prize' is a relative clause modifying 'boy'. In option D, 'on the table' is a prepositional phrase, not a clause. Therefore, option B correctly uses an adverbial clause.

35. Choose the correct sentence in Indirect Speech for: "He said, 'I am going to the market now.'"

  1. He said that he was going to the market then.
  2. He said that I was going to the market now.
  3. He said that he is going to the market then.
  4. He said that he went to the market now.

Answer: He said that he was going to the market then.

Explanation: When converting Direct Speech to Indirect Speech, several changes occur. The reporting verb 'said' remains. The comma and inverted commas are replaced by 'that'. The pronoun 'I' changes to 'he' to match the subject of the reporting verb. The present continuous tense 'am going' changes to the past continuous tense 'was going'. The adverb of time 'now' changes to 'then'. Therefore, option A is the correct transformation.
