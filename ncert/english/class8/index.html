Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the NCERT English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Choose the correct indirect speech for the following sentence: She said, 'I am going to the market now.'

  1. She said that she was going to the market then.
  2. She said that she is going to the market now.
  3. She said that I was going to the market then.
  4. She said that she had gone to the market then.

Answer: She said that she was going to the market then.

Explanation: When converting direct speech to indirect speech, the tense usually changes (present continuous 'am going' becomes past continuous 'was going'). The pronoun 'I' changes to 'she' to match the subject of the reporting verb. Also, time expressions like 'now' change to 'then'.

2. Convert the following sentence into passive voice: The students are writing an essay.

  1. An essay is being written by the students.
  2. An essay was written by the students.
  3. The students have written an essay.
  4. An essay will be written by the students.

Answer: An essay is being written by the students.

Explanation: In active voice, the subject performs the action. In passive voice, the action is performed on the subject. For present continuous tense (are writing), the passive structure is 'is/are being + past participle'. So, 'An essay is being written by the students' is the correct passive form.

3. Identify the conjunction in the following sentence: He is intelligent, but he is lazy.

  1. He
  2. is
  3. intelligent
  4. but

Answer: but

Explanation: A conjunction is a word used to connect clauses or sentences or to coordinate words in the same clause. In this sentence, 'but' connects two contrasting ideas: 'He is intelligent' and 'he is lazy'.

4. Complete the sentence with a suitable relative pronoun: This is the boy ______ won the first prize.

  1. which
  2. whose
  3. who
  4. whom

Answer: who

Explanation: A relative pronoun introduces a relative clause. 'Who' is used to refer to people when it is the subject of the relative clause. Here, 'who' refers to 'the boy' and acts as the subject of the verb 'won'.

5. Choose the correct form of the verb to complete the sentence: Neither of the students ______ submitted their homework.

  1. have
  2. has
  3. are
  4. were

Answer: has

Explanation: When 'neither of' is used, the verb should agree with the singular noun implied, even if 'students' is plural. 'Neither of' takes a singular verb. Therefore, 'has' is the correct choice.

6. Identify the adverbial clause in the following sentence: We will go for a picnic if the weather is good.

  1. We will go for a picnic
  2. if the weather is good
  3. the weather is good
  4. go for a picnic

Answer: if the weather is good

Explanation: An adverbial clause is a dependent clause that functions as an adverb, modifying a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It often begins with a subordinating conjunction. Here, 'if the weather is good' tells us the condition under which 'we will go for a picnic', making it an adverbial clause of condition.

7. What does the idiom 'to bite the bullet' mean?

  1. To eat something very quickly.
  2. To face a difficult situation with courage.
  3. To complain loudly about a problem.
  4. To avoid responsibility for an action.

Answer: To face a difficult situation with courage.

Explanation: The idiom 'to bite the bullet' means to endure a difficult or unpleasant situation with courage and fortitude, often because there is no other option.

8. Read the following passage and answer the question: 'The old woman lived in a small cottage by the river. Every morning, she would sit on her porch and watch the boats pass by. She loved the peaceful atmosphere and the gentle sound of the water. One day, a young boy approached her, asking for directions to the nearest village.' What did the old woman love about her home?

  1. The young boy who visited her.
  2. The bustling market nearby.
  3. The peaceful atmosphere and the gentle sound of the water.
  4. The large garden she maintained.

Answer: The peaceful atmosphere and the gentle sound of the water.

Explanation: The passage explicitly states: 'She loved the peaceful atmosphere and the gentle sound of the water.' The other options are either not mentioned or contradict the information given in the passage.

9. Transform the following sentence into passive voice: The students are writing an essay.

  1. An essay is being written by the students.
  2. An essay was written by the students.
  3. The students have written an essay.
  4. An essay is written by the students.

Answer: An essay is being written by the students.

Explanation: In passive voice, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject. The verb form changes to 'be + past participle'. Since the original sentence is in present continuous tense ('are writing'), the passive form will be 'is being written'.

10. Complete the sentence with the correct tense and conjunction: He ______ (study) hard ______ he wants to pass the exam.

  1. is studying, because
  2. studied, but
  3. will study, so
  4. studies, although

Answer: is studying, because

Explanation: The first blank requires a present continuous tense ('is studying') to indicate an ongoing action. The second blank requires a conjunction that shows cause or reason, which is 'because'. He is studying hard because he wants to pass the exam makes logical sense.

11. Identify the relative clause in the following sentence: The girl who is wearing a red dress is my sister.

  1. The girl
  2. who is wearing a red dress
  3. is my sister
  4. wearing a red dress

Answer: who is wearing a red dress

Explanation: A relative clause (also called an adjective clause) is a dependent clause that modifies a noun or pronoun. It usually begins with a relative pronoun like 'who', 'whom', 'whose', 'which', or 'that'. In this sentence, 'who is wearing a red dress' describes 'the girl'.

12. Choose the correct form of the verb to complete the sentence: Neither John nor his friends ______ (like) to play cricket.

  1. likes
  2. like
  3. is liking
  4. has liked

Answer: like

Explanation: When 'neither...nor' connects two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject closest to it. In this case, 'his friends' is plural, so the verb should be 'like'.

13. Identify the adverbial clause in the following sentence: We will go to the park when the rain stops.

  1. We will go to the park
  2. when the rain stops
  3. to the park
  4. the rain stops

Answer: when the rain stops

Explanation: An adverbial clause is a dependent clause that functions as an adverb, modifying a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It tells us 'when', 'where', 'why', 'how', 'to what extent', or 'under what condition' something happens. Here, 'when the rain stops' tells us when we will go to the park.

14. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom 'to bite the bullet'.

  1. To accept a difficult situation.
  2. To eat something quickly.
  3. To argue with someone.
  4. To be very angry.

Answer: To accept a difficult situation.

Explanation: The idiom 'to bite the bullet' means to endure a difficult or unpleasant situation with courage and fortitude, often because there is no other option.

15. Read the passage and answer the question: 'The old woman lived in a small cottage by the river. She spent her days tending to her garden, which was full of colourful flowers. Every evening, she would sit on her porch and watch the boats pass by.' What did the old woman do every evening?

  1. She tended to her garden.
  2. She watched the boats pass by.
  3. She cooked dinner.
  4. She went for a walk.

Answer: She watched the boats pass by.

Explanation: The passage explicitly states: 'Every evening, she would sit on her porch and watch the boats pass by.' This directly answers the question.

16. The teacher said, 'The Earth revolves around the Sun.' Convert this into indirect speech.

  1. The teacher said that the Earth revolves around the Sun.
  2. The teacher said that the Earth revolved around the Sun.
  3. The teacher said that the Earth had revolved around the Sun.
  4. The teacher said that the Earth would revolve around the Sun.

Answer: The teacher said that the Earth revolves around the Sun.

Explanation: This is a universal truth. When a universal truth or a habitual fact is reported, the tense of the reported speech does not change, even if the reporting verb is in the past tense. Therefore, 'revolves' remains 'revolves'.

17. Change the following sentence into passive voice: 'The students are writing an essay.'

  1. An essay is being written by the students.
  2. An essay was being written by the students.
  3. An essay has been written by the students.
  4. An essay will be written by the students.

Answer: An essay is being written by the students.

Explanation: The original sentence is in the present continuous tense (are writing). To change it to passive voice, we use 'is/are + being + past participle'. So, 'an essay' (object) becomes the subject, and 'is being written' is the correct passive form.

18. Identify the correct conjunction to complete the sentence: 'She studied hard, _______ she failed the exam.'

  1. but
  2. and
  3. so
  4. for

Answer: but

Explanation: The sentence shows a contrast between studying hard and failing the exam. 'But' is used to introduce a statement that contrasts with something that has just been said. 'And' adds information, 'so' indicates a result, and 'for' indicates a reason.

19. Choose the correct relative pronoun to complete the sentence: 'This is the book _______ I borrowed from the library.'

  1. which
  2. who
  3. whom
  4. whose

Answer: which

Explanation: The relative pronoun 'which' is used to refer to things or animals. In this sentence, 'book' is a thing, so 'which' is the appropriate choice. 'Who' and 'whom' are for people, and 'whose' shows possession.

20. Choose the correct verb to complete the sentence: 'Neither the students nor the teacher _______ present in the class.'

  1. was
  2. were
  3. are
  4. have been

Answer: was

Explanation: When 'neither...nor' connects two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject closer to it. In this case, 'the teacher' is singular and is closer to the verb, so the singular verb 'was' is correct.

21. Identify the adverbial clause in the following sentence: 'He will succeed if he works hard.'

  1. He will succeed
  2. if he works hard
  3. he works hard
  4. will succeed

Answer: if he works hard

Explanation: An adverbial clause is a dependent clause that functions as an adverb, modifying a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It tells us 'how', 'when', 'where', 'why', 'to what extent', or 'under what condition' something happens. Here, 'if he works hard' tells us the condition for his success.

22. Read the passage and answer the question: 'The old woman lived in a small cottage by the river. Every morning, she would wake up early, tend to her garden, and then sit by the window, watching the boats pass by. She loved the peaceful rhythm of her life.' What did the old woman do every morning after tending to her garden?

  1. She went for a walk.
  2. She cooked breakfast.
  3. She sat by the window, watching the boats.
  4. She read a book.

Answer: She sat by the window, watching the boats.

Explanation: According to the passage, 'Every morning, she would wake up early, tend to her garden, and then sit by the window, watching the boats pass by.' This directly states her activity after gardening.
