26. Choose the correct article to complete the sentence: 'I saw ______ elephant at the zoo.'

  1. a
  2. an
  3. the
  4. no article

Answer: an

Explanation: We use 'an' before words that start with a vowel sound. The word 'elephant' begins with a vowel sound (e). 'A' is used before consonant sounds, and 'the' is used for specific nouns.

27. Read the passage and answer the question: 'Rohan loves to read books. His favorite genre is adventure. He often spends hours in the library exploring new stories. He dreams of traveling the world one day.' What does Rohan love to do?

  1. Play games
  2. Watch movies
  3. Read books
  4. Sing songs

Answer: Read books

Explanation: The first sentence of the passage clearly states, 'Rohan loves to read books.' The rest of the passage elaborates on his love for reading, mentioning his favorite genre and time spent in the library.

28. Read the passage and answer the question: 'Rohan loves to play football. Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice for an hour and then play a match. Rohan is a good player and often scores goals.' What does Rohan do every evening?

  1. He reads books.
  2. He watches TV.
  3. He goes to the park to play football.
  4. He sleeps.

Answer: He goes to the park to play football.

Explanation: The passage clearly states, 'Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice for an hour and then play a match.' This indicates he goes to the park to play football.

29. Which of the following words is a pronoun?

  1. table
  2. she
  3. quickly
  4. beautiful

Answer: she

Explanation: A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun. 'She' replaces a female noun (like a girl's name or 'woman'). 'Table' is a noun, 'quickly' is an adverb, and 'beautiful' is an adjective.

30. Identify the verb in the sentence: 'The birds sing sweetly in the morning.'

  1. birds
  2. sweetly
  3. sing
  4. morning

Answer: sing

Explanation: A verb is a word that describes an action, state, or occurrence. In this sentence, 'sing' is the action performed by the birds. 'Birds' is a noun, 'sweetly' is an adverb, and 'morning' is a noun.

31. Which preposition correctly completes the sentence: 'The book is ______ the table.'

  1. and
  2. but
  3. on
  4. or

Answer: on

Explanation: A preposition shows the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence, often indicating position or direction. 'On' indicates the position of the book relative to the table. 'And', 'but', 'or' are conjunctions.

32. Fill in the blank with the correct article: 'She saw ______ elephant at the zoo.'

  1. a
  2. an
  3. the
  4. no article

Answer: an

Explanation: We use 'an' before words that start with a vowel sound. 'Elephant' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is the correct article.

33. Which of the following sentences is grammatically correct?

  1. Ran he fast.
  2. Fast he ran.
  3. He ran fast.
  4. Fast ran he.

Answer: He ran fast.

Explanation: In a basic English sentence, the usual word order is Subject + Verb + Object/Adverb. 'He' is the subject, 'ran' is the verb, and 'fast' is an adverb describing how he ran. So, 'He ran fast' is the correct structure.

34. Identify the pronoun in the following sentence: 'She loves to read books in her free time.'

  1. loves
  2. books
  3. She
  4. read

Answer: She

Explanation: A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun. In this sentence, 'She' is used instead of a person's name (like 'Ria' or 'Meena'), making it a pronoun. 'Loves' is a verb, 'books' is a noun, and 'read' is a verb.

35. Choose the correct article to complete the sentence: 'My mother bought ______ new dress for me.'

  1. a
  2. an
  3. the
  4. no article

Answer: a

Explanation: We use 'a' before words that start with a consonant sound. 'New' starts with the consonant sound /n/, so 'a' is the correct article. 'An' is used before vowel sounds, and 'the' is used for specific or already mentioned nouns.
