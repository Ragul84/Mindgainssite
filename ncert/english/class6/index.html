Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the NCERT English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Identify the pronoun in the following sentence: 'She loves to read books.'

  1. loves
  2. She
  3. read
  4. books

Answer: She

Explanation: A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun. In this sentence, 'She' is used instead of a person's name, making it a pronoun.

2. Which of the following sentences uses the simple past tense correctly?

  1. He is playing cricket now.
  2. They will go to the market tomorrow.
  3. She ate an apple yesterday.
  4. We are watching a movie.

Answer: She ate an apple yesterday.

Explanation: The simple past tense describes an action that happened in the past. 'Ate' is the past tense of 'eat', and 'yesterday' indicates a past action. Options A and D are present continuous, and option B is future tense.

3. Choose the correct adjective to complete the sentence: 'The tortoise is a ______ animal.'

  1. slowly
  2. slow
  3. slowness
  4. slowest

Answer: slow

Explanation: An adjective describes a noun. 'Slow' describes the tortoise. 'Slowly' is an adverb, 'slowness' is a noun, and 'slowest' is a superlative adjective (used for comparison among three or more).

4. Which preposition correctly completes the sentence: 'The cat is hiding ______ the table.'

  1. on
  2. in
  3. under
  4. at

Answer: under

Explanation: A preposition shows the relationship between a noun/pronoun and other words in the sentence, often indicating position. 'Under' correctly describes the cat's hiding place relative to the table.

5. Fill in the blank with the correct article: 'I saw ______ elephant at the zoo.'

  1. a
  2. an
  3. the
  4. no article

Answer: an

Explanation: We use 'an' before words that start with a vowel sound. 'Elephant' starts with the vowel sound /e/, so 'an' is the correct article.

6. Which of the following is a grammatically correct simple sentence?

  1. Because it was raining, we stayed indoors.
  2. The dog barked loudly.
  3. She likes to sing, and he likes to dance.
  4. Although he was tired, he continued working.

Answer: The dog barked loudly.

Explanation: A simple sentence contains one independent clause (a subject and a verb that expresses a complete thought). 'The dog barked loudly' has 'dog' as the subject and 'barked' as the verb, forming a complete thought. The other options are complex or compound sentences.

7. What is a synonym for the word 'happy'?

  1. sad
  2. joyful
  3. angry
  4. tired

Answer: joyful

Explanation: A synonym is a word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word. 'Joyful' means very happy, making it a synonym for 'happy'.

8. Read the passage and answer the question: 'Rohan loves to play football. Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice for an hour and then play a match. Rohan is the captain of his team.' What does Rohan do every evening?

  1. He watches TV.
  2. He goes to the library.
  3. He plays football at the park.
  4. He studies for an hour.

Answer: He plays football at the park.

Explanation: The passage clearly states: 'Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice for an hour and then play a match.' This indicates he plays football at the park.

9. Choose the correct adjective to complete the sentence: 'The ______ cat chased the mouse.'

  1. quickly
  2. softly
  3. fluffy
  4. happily

Answer: fluffy

Explanation: An adjective describes a noun. 'Fluffy' describes the cat (a noun). 'Quickly', 'softly', and 'happily' are adverbs, which describe verbs or other adverbs, not nouns.

10. Complete the sentence with the correct preposition: 'The book is ______ the table.'

  1. under
  2. on
  3. in
  4. from

Answer: on

Explanation: A preposition shows the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence, often indicating position. 'On' correctly indicates that the book is resting on the surface of the table. 'Under' means below, 'in' means inside, and 'from' indicates origin.

11. Which article should be used before the word 'umbrella'?

  1. A
  2. An
  3. The
  4. No article

Answer: An

Explanation: We use 'an' before words that start with a vowel sound. 'Umbrella' starts with a vowel sound (u-m-bre-lla), so 'an' is the correct article.

12. Read the passage and answer the question: 'Rohan loves to play cricket. Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice batting and bowling for an hour. Rohan dreams of becoming a famous cricketer one day.' What does Rohan do every evening?

  1. He watches television.
  2. He studies for school.
  3. He plays cricket in the park.
  4. He visits his grandparents.

Answer: He plays cricket in the park.

Explanation: The passage clearly states: 'Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice batting and bowling for an hour.' This indicates that Rohan plays cricket in the park every evening.

13. Choose the correct adjective to complete the sentence: 'The tortoise is a very ______ animal.'

  1. slowly
  2. slow
  3. slowness
  4. slowed

Answer: slow

Explanation: An adjective describes a noun. Here, we need a word to describe the 'animal'. 'Slow' is an adjective that describes the speed of the tortoise. 'Slowly' is an adverb, 'slowness' is a noun, and 'slowed' is a verb in past tense.

14. Fill in the blank with the correct preposition: 'The cat jumped ______ the table.'

  1. in
  2. on
  3. over
  4. under

Answer: over

Explanation: A preposition shows the relationship between a noun/pronoun and other words in the sentence. 'Over' correctly indicates the action of jumping from one side to another, clearing the table. 'In' suggests being inside, 'on' suggests being on top, and 'under' suggests being below, which do not fit the action of jumping across.

15. Choose the synonym for the word 'happy'.

  1. sad
  2. joyful
  3. angry
  4. tired

Answer: joyful

Explanation: A synonym is a word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word. 'Joyful' means full of joy, which is very similar in meaning to 'happy'. 'Sad', 'angry', and 'tired' are antonyms or unrelated emotions.

16. Read the passage and answer the question: 'The sun was shining brightly. Birds were chirping in the trees. A gentle breeze blew through the garden. It was a perfect day for a picnic.' What kind of day was it?

  1. A rainy day
  2. A cloudy day
  3. A perfect day for a picnic
  4. A stormy day

Answer: A perfect day for a picnic

Explanation: The last sentence of the passage explicitly states, 'It was a perfect day for a picnic.' The preceding sentences also describe pleasant weather conditions (sun shining, gentle breeze), supporting this conclusion.

17. Which preposition correctly completes the sentence: 'The cat jumped ______ the table.'

  1. in
  2. on
  3. under
  4. over

Answer: over

Explanation: The preposition 'over' indicates movement from one side to another, often with a leap or jump, which fits the action of a cat jumping. 'In' and 'on' suggest being stationary, and 'under' suggests being beneath.

18. Read the passage and answer the question: 'Rohan loves to play football. Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice for an hour and then play a match. Rohan is a very good player and often scores goals.' What does Rohan do every evening?

  1. He watches TV.
  2. He goes to the library.
  3. He plays football at the park.
  4. He studies for an hour.

Answer: He plays football at the park.

Explanation: The passage clearly states, 'Every evening, he goes to the park with his friends. They practice for an hour and then play a match.' This indicates he plays football at the park.

19. Choose the correct adjective to complete the sentence: 'The ______ elephant walked slowly.'

  1. quickly
  2. beautifully
  3. enormous
  4. softly

Answer: enormous

Explanation: An adjective describes a noun. 'Enormous' describes the size of the elephant, making it the correct adjective. 'Quickly', 'beautifully', and 'softly' are adverbs, describing how an action is done.

20. Fill in the blank with the correct preposition: 'The book is ______ the table.'

  1. under
  2. in
  3. on
  4. at

Answer: on

Explanation: A preposition shows the relationship between a noun/pronoun and other words in a sentence. 'On' is the most appropriate preposition to indicate that the book is resting on the surface of the table.

21. Which article would you use before the word 'umbrella'?

  1. A
  2. An
  3. The
  4. No article

Answer: An

Explanation: We use 'an' before words that start with a vowel sound. 'Umbrella' starts with the vowel sound 'uh', so 'an' is the correct article.

22. Which of the following is a complete and grammatically correct sentence?

  1. Running fast.
  2. The cat sat on the mat.
  3. Happy children playing.
  4. Books and pens.

Answer: The cat sat on the mat.

Explanation: A complete sentence must have a subject and a verb, and express a complete thought. 'The cat sat on the mat' has a subject ('The cat') and a verb ('sat'), and makes complete sense. The other options are fragments.

23. Identify the pronoun in the following sentence: 'She went to the market to buy some fruits.'

  1. went
  2. market
  3. She
  4. fruits

Answer: She

Explanation: A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun. In this sentence, 'She' is used instead of a person's name, making it a pronoun. 'Went' is a verb, 'market' is a noun, and 'fruits' is also a noun.

24. Choose the correct form of the verb to complete the sentence: 'The birds ______ sweetly every morning.'

  1. sing
  2. sings
  3. sang
  4. singing

Answer: sing

Explanation: The subject 'birds' is plural. For a plural subject in the simple present tense, we use the base form of the verb without 's' or 'es'. Therefore, 'sing' is the correct form. 'Sings' would be for a singular subject, 'sang' is past tense, and 'singing' requires a helping verb (e.g., 'are singing').

25. Which word in the sentence 'The little girl ran quickly to her mother.' is an adverb?

  1. little
  2. girl
  3. quickly
  4. mother

Answer: quickly

Explanation: An adverb describes a verb, an adjective, or another adverb, often telling us how, when, where, or to what extent something happens. In this sentence, 'quickly' describes how the girl ran (the verb 'ran'), making it an adverb. 'Little' is an adjective describing 'girl', 'girl' is a noun, and 'mother' is a noun.
