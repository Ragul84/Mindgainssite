101. How many electrons are there in one coulomb charge?

Answer: B. 6.25×10^18 electrons

102. A bullet of mass 15 g is horizontally fired with velocity 100 ms^-1 from a pistol of mass 2 kg. Total momentum of the pistol and bullet before firing is equal to

Answer: A. zero

103. What is the chemical name of DDT?

Answer: A. Dichloro diphenyl trichloro ethane

104. What are Microsporum, Epidermophyton?

Answer: B. Disease causing fungi in man

105. Ovulation is stimulated by

Answer: A. LH

106. Which gymnospermic plant cures rheumatism?

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: B. Ephedra / C. Gnetum

107. A special root-like structure in cuscuta and viscum is called

Answer: B. Haustoria

108. Which of the following is observed as "National Girl Child Day"?

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

109. Which of the following was discovered by ONGC in the Indian Ocean on 26th July 2016?

Answer: A. Hydrated Methane

110. Which Bill was passed in the Parliament of India in 2016 to adopt Uniform Tax of States?

Answer: B. Goods and Service Tax

111. Which State Governor was appointed as Tamilnadu Governor additional-charge in September, 2016?

Answer: C. Maharashtra

112. Which Musician was honoured in August 2016, by United Nations Organization for her contribution to music?

Answer: A. M.S. Subbulakshmi

113. Identify the 2016 Dhronacharya Awardee

Answer: A. Bishweshwar Nandi

114. The Asian Junior Chess Championships, May 2016 was held at

Answer: D. New Delhi

115. Which country's Badminton player was defeated by P.V. Sindhu in the semi-finals at the Rio 2016 Olympic games?

Answer: D. Japan

116. In July 2016, Theresa May was appointed as the ______ Woman Prime Minister of UK (Britain).

Answer: B. second

117. The Third Asian Ministerial Conference on Tiger Conservation 2016 was held

Answer: C. From April 11th to 14th 2016 at New-Delhi

118. Indian Constitution Drafting Committee's Chairman

Answer: C. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

119. Which year the Constitution of India (61st Amendment Act) lowered the voting age from 21 years to 18 years?

Answer: A. 1988

120. Article 63 of the Indian Constitution refers to

Answer: A. Vice-President

121. Who is the chairperson of Niti Aayog?

Answer: B. Prime Minister

122. The writ of Habeas Corpus is

Answer: A. To safeguard people from illegal arrest

123. Match the following: (a) To eradicate the inequality in the society (b) Women depend on male companions (c) To stop the customs of child marriage (d) Cancer institute at Adyar | 1. Beliefs passed through the generation 2. It proclaims the fame of Dr. Muthu Lakshmi 3. Social justice 4. Prohibition

Answer: A. (a)-3 (b)-1 (c)-4 (d)-2

124. Which is known as the "Murmering Auditorium"?

Answer: B. Golkumbas

125. Which Mughal King killed the Sikh Guru Tegh Bahadur?

Answer: B. Aurangazeb

126. The Pallava inscription at ______ refers to a great musician Rudracharya.

Answer: A. Kudumiyan Malai

127. During the age of Kalabhras, a Jain monk who established Dravida Sangha at Madurai was

Answer: A. Vajranandhi

128. The leading producers of jute in the world is

Answer: A. India and Bangladesh

129. Asteroids are found between

Answer: A. Mars and Jupiter

130. Match the following: (a) Kandla (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Paradip (d) Haldia | 1. Maharashtra 2. Gujarat 3. West Bengal 4. Orissa

Answer: D. (a)-2 (b)-1 (c)-4 (d)-3

131. Match: (a) Sathya Sodhak Samaj (b) Jeeva Karunyam (c) Dharma Paribalanam (d) Jeeva is Siva | 1. Ramalinga Adigal 2. Jyotiba Phule 3. Swami Vivekanandha 4. Sri Narayana Guru

Answer: A. (a)-2 (b)-1 (c)-4 (d)-3

132. Which year has a special significance in Indian Economy as far as Economic Reforms are concerned?

Answer: C. 1991

133. Where is the diamond cutting industry located in India?

Answer: B. Surat

134. Net National Product is

Answer: B. Gross National Product – Depreciation

135. When a person lives below the minimum subsistence level he is said to live in

Answer: A. Absolute poverty

136. (-1 2/7) + (-3 5/7) + (6 4/7) is ______.

Answer: C. 11/7

137. The population of a city in the year 2014 is 1,80,000 and increases at a rate of 20% per year. Find the population of the city in the year 2016?

Answer: B. 2,59,200

138. 3 - 25 here, which of the following number is suitable in -, so that the number will be a perfect square?

Answer: B. 0

139. The average mark of 10 children is 80 then their total mark is

Answer: C. 800

140. A man and woman are engaged in a work. A man can do a piece of work in 4 days and the woman can do in 12 days. Find how many days will they take to finish it together?

Answer: D. 3 days

141. Which of the following set of measurements will form a right angle triangle?

Answer: C. 5, 5, 5√2

142. The value of sqrt(609 + sqrt(248 + sqrt(60 + sqrt(7 + sqrt(81)))))

Answer: B. 25

143. The price of a house is decreased from rupees fifteen lakhs to rupees twelve lakhs. The percentage of decrease is

Answer: B. 20%

144. If 3(t-3) = 5(2t+1) then t = ?

Answer: A. -2

145. The value of (1.75×1.75+2×1.75×0.75+0.75×0.75)/(1.75×1.75-0.75×0.75)

Answer: D. 2.5

146. The value of 16^3 + 7^3 - 23^3 is

Answer: A. -7728

147. Find the next term of 4 in the series is 1, 1, 2, 8, 3, 27, 4, ...

Answer: D. 64

148. Three angles of a triangle are x-30°, x-45°, x+15°, find the value of x.

Answer: C. 80°

149. The difference between simple interest and compound interest for a sum of Rs. 12,000 lent at 10% per annum in 2 years, is

Answer: C. Rs. 120

150. If you stand in a rectangular room, where two adjacent walls are covered with plane mirrors, the total number of your images will be

Answer: C. 3

151. Audible range of rabbit is

Answer: C. 1,000 - 1,00,000 Hz

152. Why vegetables and fruits turn brown on cutting?

Answer: A. Due to the reaction between phenolic compound and oxygen in air

153. Among the following which one is not a biofertilizer?

Answer: C. Lindane

154. Consider the statements: (a) Sterility is caused by the deficiency of Vitamin K. (b) Night blindness is caused by the deficiency of Vitamin D.

Answer: A. Both (a) and (b) are false

155. The valve which regulates the blood flow from right ventricle to the pulmonary artery

Answer: A. Semi-lunar valve

156. Match List I and List II: List I (a) Glycolysis (b) Kreb's cycle (c) Electron transport chain (d) Fermentation | List II 1. O2 is not utilised 2. ATP are produced 3. Oxidation of pyruvic acid 4. Occurs in cytoplasm

Answer: B. (a)-4 (b)-3 (c)-2 (d)-1

157. Who among the Indian-born American scientist bagged the prestigious award Lemelson-MIT prize worth 5,00,000 dollars?

Answer: C. Ramesh Raskar

158. Who won the GD Birla Award for Scientific Research for the year 2016?

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

159. Who is the Chief Information Commissioner of India in September 2016?

Answer: C. R.K. Mathoor

160. "Operation calm down" is associated with which state?

Answer: C. Jammu Kashmir

161. Name the Battery that shuts down on over heating and restarts when the temperature becomes cool

Answer: B. Lithium ion

162. Name the person who was appointed as the ambassador on behalf of India to work for the cause of gender equality and progress of women in UNO for the year 2016

Answer: C. Aishwarya Dhanush

163. What is the name of Ganga river cleaning scheme?

Answer: A. Namami Ganga scheme

164. The INSAT-3DR was launched through rocket for Metereological Research on 8th September, 2016 by which rocket?

Answer: A. GSLV-F05

165. Who won the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Rathna Award for Badminton in the year 2016?

Answer: D. P.V. Sindhu

166. Which year has been declared by UNO as the "International Year of Pulses"?

Answer: C. 2016

167. Match the following: Part-I (a) Raj Bhavan (b) Rastrapathy Bhavan (c) Impeachment (d) Lt. Governor | Part-II 1. President 2. Governor 3. Union Territories 4. Violation of the Constitution

Answer: D. (a)-2 (b)-1 (c)-4 (d)-3

168. The election process at the state level is supervised by

Answer: C. Chief Electoral Officer

169. Find out the correct statement. The Indian Constitution contains

Answer: A. XXII parts, 449 Articles and 12 Schedules

170. Which of the following is incorrectly matched?

Answer: D. Constituent Assembly adopted National Anthem – January 23, 1950

171. Which of the following is correctly matched?

Answer: B. The Chief Minister – Appointed by the Governor

172. Consumer Courts are set up in ______ tier system.

Answer: B. Three

173. The Tanjore king who helped Vijayanagar king in the battle of Thalaikottai was

Answer: B. Achutappa Nayak

174. Which of these describes the conquest and victories of Samudra Gupta?

Answer: B. Allahabad Pillar inscription

175. Who was the last Sultan of Delhi Sulthanate?

Answer: A. Ibrahim Lodi

176. Who built the Nalanda university?

Answer: A. Kumara Gupta

177. Ox-bow lakes are formed in the

Answer: C. Plain region

178. The shortest National Highway is

Answer: D. NH 47 A

Answer: D. Annadurai

181. Where was Dr. B.R. Ambedkar born?

Answer: A. Mhow

182. How many years V.O.C. was sentenced to imprisonment?

Answer: C. 40

183. Who remarked "The light has gone out of our lives and there is darkness everywhere"?

Answer: D. Jawaharlal Nehru

184. Who chaired the first session of the Indian National Congress held at Bombay?

Answer: B. W.C. Banerjee

185. In India the organisation which was responsible for self-sufficiency in food production was

Answer: B. ICAR

186. As explained by Robert Malthus, Population increases in the

Answer: B. Geometric ratio

187. Which plan was implemented after the Annual Plans of 1966-69?

Answer: D. Fourth Five Year Plan

188. "Bachpan Bachao Andolan" is an organisation that fights against

Answer: A. Child labour

189. The base of a triangle is four times its height and its area is 50 m^2. The length of the base is

Answer: C. 20 m

190. A can do a piece of work in 20 days and B can do it in 25 days. Both of them finished the work and earned Rs. 3,600. Then A's share is

Answer: B. Rs. 2,000

191. If (7/12)^-4 × (7/12)^3x = (7/12)^5; then the value of x is

Answer: D. 3

192. If 22 men can build a wall of 110 meters in 10 days. The length of a similar wall built by 30 men in 6 days is

Answer: B. 90 mts

193. If the ratio of length and breadth of a rectangle is 4:7. Find the length while its breadth is 77 cm.

Answer: C. 44 cm

194. A room is 5 m 40 cm long and 4 m 50 cm broad. Its Area is

Answer: B. 24.3 m^2

Answer: C. 62 1/2%

197. The breadth, height and volume of a cuboid are 10 cm, 11 cm and 3080 cm^3 respectively. Find the length of the cuboid.

Answer: B. 28 cm

198. Which medicine is known as "THE QUEEN OF DRUGS"?

Answer: D. PENICILLIN

Answer: A. 10%
