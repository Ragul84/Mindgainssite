101. Consider the following statements: (i) The closure of Atrio ventricular valves result in "lubb" sound. (ii) The closure of semilunar valves result in "dubb" sound. Choose the right answer.

Answer: D. Both (i) and (ii) are correct

102. Name the vitamin and mineral necessary for blood clotting

Answer: C. Vitamin K and Calcium

103. Choose the correct order of the parts marked in the diagram (a tRNA cloverleaf structure with 3 arms labeled 1, 2, 3).

Answer: A. (1) TUC loop (2) Anticodon loop (3) DHU loop

104. Name the gas which is evolved during photosynthesis

Answer: D. Oxygen

105. Match the following: (a) August Offer (b) C.R. Formula (c) Wavell Plan (d) Interim Government | 1. 1944 2. 1945 3. 1946 4. 1940

Answer: C. (a)-4 (b)-1 (c)-2 (d)-3

106. Match the following: (a) Nehru Report (b) Second Round Table Conference (c) Individual Satyagraha (d) Lord Attlee's Announcement | 1. 1940 2. 1928 3. 1946 4. 1931

Answer: A. (A) 2 4 1 3

107. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A): V.O. Chidambaram Pillai established Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company in 1910. Reason (R) : V.O. Chidambaram Pillai is called as Kappalottiya Tamilan.

Answer: D. (A) is false (R) is true

108. Point out the wrong statement/statements in the following: (i) V.O.C. born at Ettayapuram. (ii) Annie Besant started Theosophical Society. (iii) Bharathiyar died at Pondicherry. (iv) E.V.R. started Self-Respect Movement.

Answer: D. (i), (ii) and (iii)

109. Which of the following is incorrectly matched?

Answer: B. Eggs and poultry - White Revolution

110. Consider the following and select the false ones: I. India's food problem is concerned with demand and supply of food grains. II. India's food problem is concerned with quality, distribution and economic reasons. III. India's food problem is concerned with procurement of food grains. IV. India's food problem is concerned with improper distribution.

Answer: C. III and IV false

111. The year in which number of poor in India a sharp increase over that of the preceding year is

Answer: C. 1993-94

112. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A): After the completion of the third five year plan 'Plan Holiday' was given. Reason (R): The third five year plan was affected by India-China war and India-Pakistan wars. Select your answer according to the coding scheme given below :

Answer: D. Both (A) and (R) are true

113. Assertion (A): To reduce the high tides of sea during storm oil is poured around the ship. Reason (R): Surface tension of oil is less than sea water, reduces the wrath of waves.

Answer: B. Both A and R are correct

114. The property of attraction or repulsion between charged bodies is used in

Answer: B. Electrostatic paint spraying

115. The time periods of two simple pendulum are in the ratio 2 : 1. The corresponding ratio of their lengths is

Answer: A. 4:1

116. Arrange the Remote Sensing Satellites as per year of launching, in ascending order. I. IRS II. SPOT III. TRIOS IV. LANDSAT

Answer: B. III, IV, II, I

117. Identify the incorrect pair. Biosphere States I. Kanha - Madhya Pradesh II. Nandadevi - Uttranchal III. Manas - Assam IV. Namdhapa - Arunachala Pradesh

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

118. Choose the correct answer from the following: These occur as a result of continuous thunder storming.

Answer: A. Landslides and floods

119. Which of the following statements about 'Fertilizer' is TRUE? I. It should be available in low cost. II. It should be easily assimilated by plants. III. It should not dissolve readily in water.

Answer: A. I and II

120. Identify the odd one from the group of chemicals given below : BHC, DDT, 2,4-D, Urea

Answer: D. Urea

121. Who was the first woman judge of the Supreme Court?

Answer: D. Fathima Beevi

122. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A): The (ARC) recommended that the institution of Lokpal and Lokayakta. Reason (R) : 1. should be demonstratively Independent and Impartial. 2. their appointment should be as far as possible non - Political. Select your answer according to the coding scheme given below.

Answer: B. Both (A) and (R) is correct

123. Arrange the Presidents of India in chronological order of their term in Office. I. R. Venkatraman II. Dr. Sankar Dayal Sharma III. Dr. K.R. Narayanan IV. Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Answer: A. I, II, III, IV

124. The Bhopal Gas Tragedy happened in

Answer: D. 1984

125. The books 'Wings of Fire' was written by

Answer: B. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

126. In the products 2a×1a = 41a; 2b×1b=37b, a and b represent positive integers. Then a+b=

Answer: B. 11

127. Find the odd man out in the following: 1, 144, 16, 25, 49, 81, 121, 36, 62

Answer: D. 62

128. The World-Wide Tea product of 5 countries for the year 2011-2012. The angle subtended by E at the centre of the circle is

Answer: C. 54°

129. In an examination 30% of total students failed in English, 40% of students failed in Hindi and 20% in both. Find the percentage of students who passed in both the subjects.

Answer: A. 50%

130. Three positions of the same die are given. Find the number opposite to the side represented by 2.

Answer: B. 3

131. If RIVER is coded as OFSBO then WATER is decoded as

Answer: B. ZDWHU

132. A can complete 2/3 part of a work in 10 days. A can complete 1/3 part of the same work in

Answer: C. 5 days

133. Rs. 800 becomes Rs. 956 in 3 years at certain simple rate of interest. If the rate of interest is increased by 4%, what amount will Rs. 800 become in 3 years?

Answer: C. Rs. 1,052

134. Amala invests Rs. 6,000 in a bond which gives interest at 4% per annum during the first year, 5% during the second year, 10% during the third year. How much does she get at the end of the third year?

Answer: D. Rs. 7,207.2

135. What will come in the place of a question mark?

Answer: A. 9

136. Find out the wrong number in the sequence. 623, 251, 543, 356

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

137. Find out the wrong number in the sequence. 121, 264, 312, 462, 583

Answer: D. 312

138. Match: List I (a) Andhra (b) Kerala (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Punjab | List II 1. Bharathanatyam 2. Kuchipudi 3. Bangra 4. Kathakali

Answer: C. (C) 2 4 1 3

139. Anna changed, the official name of the state from 'Madras' to Tamil Nadu in

Answer: C. 1969

140. Match the following: (a) Larkana District (b) Kalibangan (c) Lothal (d) Harappa | 1. Gujarat 2. Punjab 3. Rajasthan 4. Sind

Answer: B. 4 3 1 2

141. Match the following with suitable options: List I (Works) List II (Authors) (a) Kondrai Venthan (b) Neethineri Vilakkam (c) Nanneri (d) Neethi Nool

Answer: B. (B) 3 4 2 1

142. Match the following with suitable options: List I (Age) List II (Art style) (a) Kushanas (b) Guptas (c) Chalukyas (d) Cholas | 1. Dravidian style 2. Vesara style 3. Nagara style 4. Gandhara Art style

Answer: A. (A) 4 3 2 1

143. Match the authors with their books and choose the right option: (a) Malgudi Days (b) Harsha Charita (c) Arthasasthra (d) Satyarth Prakash | 1. Kautilya 2. Swami Dayanand 3. R.K. Narayan 4. Bana Bhatta

Answer: A. 3 4 1 2

144. Match the following and choose the right answer: (a) Recent Military camp took place in | 1. Brazil (b) Snowden's plea for assylum was rejected by | 2. India (c) Serial Bomb-Blast took place at Mahabhodhi temple is in | 3. England (d) After 10 years Andy Murry brought laurels to his country by becoming Wimbledon Champion | 4. Egypt

145. Point out the wrong statement in the following: I. National Animal - Tiger II. National Bird - Peacock III. National Flower - Rose IV. National Fruit - Mango

Answer: C. III

146. Who is the Whistle Blower, who brought the secret of American Intelligence, doing the Global Electronic Surveillance on other nations, to the world?

Answer: C. Edward Snowden

147. Which article empowers the President to dissolve the Lok Sabha at any time before its tenure?

Answer: A. Art 85

148. Which is not the member of G-20?

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: B. North Africa / C. Bolivia / D. Egypt

149. Match the List I with List II and select the correctly matched answer: List I (a) Rheumatic arthritis (b) Pellagra (c) Cholera (d) Sickle cell anaemia List II 1. Contageous disease 2. Auto immune disease 3. Hereditary disease 4. Vitamin deficiency disease

Answer: B. (B) 2 4 1 3

150. Match the Wild Life Sanctuaries in List I with their location in List II: List I (a) Viralimalai (b) Mukkurthi National Park (c) Indira Gandhi Wild Life Sanctuary (d) Vedanthangal | List II 1. Western ghats 2. Kancheepuram district 3. The Nilgiris 4. Trichy district

Answer: B. (B) 4 3 1 2

151. Which of the following is referred to as EMP pathway?

Answer: A. Glycolysis

152. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A): Insectivorous plants digest proteins of insect body. Reason (R) : Insectivorous plants grown in Nitrogen (N2) deficient soil. Select your answer according to the coding scheme given below :

Answer: B. Both (A) and (R) are true, (R) explains (A)

153. Which of the following is/are wrong? I. Aligarh movement was led by Sir Syed Ahmed Khan. II. Sir Syed Ahmed Khan supported Shuddi movement. III. Sir Syed Ahmed Khan started Anglo-oriental college.

Answer: A. II only

154. Which one of the following is correctly matched? (A) Ettayapuram - Collector Ashe (B) Jallian Walah Bagh Tragedy - Hunter Committee (C) Swaraj Party - B.G. Tilak (D) Prohibition - V.O. Chidambaranar

Answer: B. Jallian Walah Bagh Tragedy - Hunter Committee

155. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below : List I (a) Tattvabodhini Sabha (b) Young Bengal Movement (c) Widow Remarriage Society (d) Hitakarini Samajam | List II 1. Henry Vivian Derozio 2. Vishnu Sashtri Pandit 3. Veeresalingam 4. Devendranath Tagore

Answer: A. (A) 4 1 2 3

156. Consider the following statements with reference to the nature of the Indian National awakening: (i) It was basically started by intellectuals. (ii) It was a movement in which, all sections of society became united against the English. (iii) After 1920, national movement was led by Gandhiji. (iv) Gandhiji started the Khilafat movement. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Answer: B. (i), (ii) and (iii)

157. The organisation which introduced Green Revolution in India is

Answer: A. ICAR

158. Framing of 5 year plans:

Answer: D. Planning Commission

159. Match the following: (a) Jawahar Gram Samriddhi Yojana | 1. 2000-2001 (b) National Social Assistance Programme | 2. 1993 (c) Employment Assurance Scheme | 3. 1999 (d) Pradhanmantri Gramodaya Yojana | 4. 1995

Answer: C. (a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 1

160. Take the odd man out: India's Navaratna Companies

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: A. BHEL / D. IPCL

161. Who is the Father of Consumer Movement?

Answer: D. Ralph Radar

162. Who frames the monetary policy of India?

Answer: B. Reserve Bank of India

163. Consider the following statements. Choose the correct answer. "Comets have no tail". I. when they are far away from the sun. II. when they are close to the sun. III. when they are close to the earth. IV. when they are in between the earth and the sun.

Answer: B. I is correct but II, III and IV are wrong

164. Identify the Seaports and name them

Answer: C. Mangalore, Cochin, Chennai, Paradwip

165. I. Moon is called a Meteoroids. II. Moon is called a Satellite. III. Moon is called a Asteroids. IV. Moon is called a Comet. (A) I and III are correct (B) II is only correct (C) II and IV are correct (D) IV is only correct

Answer: B. II is only correct

166. Choose incorrectly matched.

Answer: C. Grey Revolution - Oil seeds

167. Choose the correct answer: In which District Grizzled Squirrel Wild Life Sanctuary is found?

Answer: C. Virdhunagar

168. The mixture present in the pencil lead is

Answer: C. Carbon and Clay

169. Which of the following statement(s) about 'acid' is TRUE? I. It turns blue litmus paper to red. II. It has pH value greater than 7. III. It reacts with bases to give salt and water.

Answer: B. I and III

170. Which country became a member of the U.N.O. in the year 2011?

Answer: C. South Sudan

171. The first meeting of the Constituent Assembly of India was held in

Answer: B. 1946

172. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A): The CVC has to present annually to the President a report on its performance. Reason (R) : The President places this report before both House of Parliament. Select your answer according to the coding scheme given below.

Answer: A. Both (A) and (R) are correct

173. Who presides over the joint sessions of the Parliament?

Answer: C. The Speaker

174. The National Development Council was established in the year

Answer: A. 1952

175. If 2+3=89, 3÷4=2716, 4+3=649 then 1+2=?

Answer: B. 14

176. Five distinct items A, B, C, D and E are to be placed in distinct boxes numbered as 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Items B and E cannot be grouped together. Maximum number of boxes can be left out without items is

Answer: C. 3

177. In how many years did the sales of science books exceed the sales of arts books from 2000 to 2009?

Answer: C. 6 years

178. Five persons A, K, L, R and U are sitting around a circular table. K is immediate left to U and R is between A and U, then immediate neighbours of L are

Answer: A. K and A

179. A circular ground of radius 7 m has a path of width 7 m around it on its outside. The area of the path is approximately equal to ___________ (π = 22/7)

Answer: C. 462 sq.m.

180. A can do a work in 12 days; B in 6 days and C in 3 days. A and B start working together and after a day, C joins them. The total number of days required to complete the work is

Answer: A. 2 2/7 days

181. At what rate percent compound interest does a sum of money become nine fold in 2 years?

Answer: B. 200%

182. Simplify: $\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x^2}}$

Answer: A. $x^{1/6}$

183. Find the missing block.

Answer: B. A block with a line starting from the bottom left, going up, then right, then up, then right, then down.

184. Find the greatest number that will divide 43, 91, 183 so as to leave the same remainder in each case.

Answer: A. 4

185. The number of number-pairs lying between 40 and 100 with their H.C.F. as 15 is

Answer: B. 4

186. A, B, C, D start at the same time to run around a circular garden in the same direction. A completes a round in 30 minutes, B in 60 minutes, C in 90 minutes and D in 105 minutes. After what time will they meet again at the starting point?

Answer: C. 21 hours

187. Find the greatest number of 4 digits which is divisible by 15, 25, 40 and 75

Answer: A. 9600

188. Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy started an orphanage known as

Answer: B. Avvai Illam

189. Match the following: List I (a) Rajendra Chola I (b) Karavela (c) Ashoka (d) Samudra Gupta | List II 1. Brahma Giri 2. Allahabad 3. Hathi Kumbha 4. Malpadi

Answer: C. (a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 2

190. Match List I with List II and select the correct answer using the codes given below : List I (a) Brahmo Samaj (b) Arya Samaj (c) Ramakrishna Mission (d) Theosophical society | List II 1. Swami Vivekananda 2. Madame Blavatsky 3. Rajaram Mohan Roy 4. Swami Dayanand Saraswathi

Answer: A. (A) 3 4 1 2

191. Match the following river names with suitable options: List I (Ancient Name) | List II (New Name) (a) Shutudri (b) Vipas (c) Parushini (d) Asikini | 1. Chenab 2. Sutlej 3. Beas 4. Ravi

Answer: D. (D) 2 3 4 1

192. Arrange the following literary works in Chronological order: I. Mudra rakshasa II. Mattavilasa Prakasana III. Milindapanho IV. Arthasastra

Answer: D. IV, III, I, II

193. What is the study of Algae called?

Answer: B. Phycology

194. Which of these is not among the BRIC Nations?

Answer: A. Chile

195. Match List I with List II and choose the correct option using the coding scheme given below : List I (a) National Rural Employment Programme (b) Jawahar Rozgar Yojana (c) Indira Awaz Yojana (d) Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana | List II 1. 1985 2. 1993 3. 1980 4. 1989

Answer: C. (C) 3 4 1 2

196. The name of World's fastest Super Computer, announced on June 19, 2012 is

Answer: B. Sequdia

197. Who gave the name "Hindu rate of growth"?

Answer: B. Raj Krishnan

198. Arrange the following countries based on the number of medals won in London Olympics- 2012 in descending order. I. Britain II. Russia III. China IV. America

Answer: B. IV, III, I, II

199. Which article of the constitution provides for the imposition of President's rule in the states?

Answer: C. Article 356
