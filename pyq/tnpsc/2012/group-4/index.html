TNPSC 2012 Group 4 · General Studies: Questions with Answers

All 100 questions from the TNPSC 2012 Group 4 · General Studies paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

100 questionsOfficial answer key

1. Arrange in chronological order : I. Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam II. Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma III. K. R. Narayanan IV. P. Venkataraman. Of these

Answer: A. IV, II, III and I

2. Among the following which is not related to Industrial Development and Finance?

Answer: D. ITC.

3. Which of the following oil companies is not a public sector company ?

Answer: D. Reliance Petroleum Ltd.

4. Which of the following fund(s) is/are operated directly from the Prime Minster's Office? I. The Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF ). II. National Defence Fund (NDF). Of these

Answer: C. Both I & II

5. Haemoglobin is the pigment useful to transport gases. They are present in Red Blood cells. Identify the animal which has blood without pigments.

Answer: D. Cockroach.

6. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: List I {Rivers} a) Mahanadi b) Periyar c) Godavari d) Krishna List II {Origin} 1. Nasik Hills 2. Mahabaleswar hills 3. Amarkantak 4. Cardamon hills.

Answer: A. A) 3 4 1 2

7. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: List I a) Cholera b) African sleeping sickness c) Phlegm d) Dandruff List II 1. Fungi 2. Virus 3. Bacteria 4. Protozoa. Codes: a b c d

Answer: A. A) 3 4 2 1

8. In Tamil Nadu the most windforms are with districts of

Answer: B. Tuticorin, Kanyakumari, Tirunelveli

9. The name of the organisations which are associated in the Chandrayan I mission are given below. Identify the wrong one:

Answer: D. ONGC.

10. Observe the following statements: Assertion (A): Saturn floats when put in water. Reason (R): The density of Saturn is less than one. Choose the correct answer from the code given below:

Answer: A. (A) and (R) are both correct, and (R) is the correct explanation for (A)

11. How much current is consumed for a tubelight which has 40W for domestic use?

Answer: A. 0-2 A

12. Thumba equatorial rocket launching station was established at which place in India? I. Bangalore II. Sriharikota III. Mahendragiri IV. Thiruvananthapuram. Of these

Answer: C. IV only

13. Among the following taxes which is not considered as a direct tax?

Answer: B. Sales tax

14. Match the following: பட்டியல் I a) தங்க கழுத்துப்பட்டை பணியாளர்கள் b) வெள்ளை கழுத்துப்பட்டை பணியாளர்கள் c) சிவப்பு கழுத்துப்பட்டை பணியாளர்கள் d) நீல கழுத்துப்பட்டை பணியாளர்கள் பட்டியல் II 1. நான்காம் நிலைத் தொழில் நடவடிக்கைகளில் ஈடுபட்டுள்ளவர்கள் 2. இரண்டாம் நிலைத்தொழில் நடவடிக்கையில் ஈடுபட்டுள்ளவர்கள் 3. ஐந்தாம் நிலைத் தொழில் நடவடிக்கையில் ஈடுபட்டுள்ளவர்கள் 4. அடிப்படை தொழில் நடவடிக்கையில் ஈடுபட்டுள்ளவர்கள்.

Answer: C. C) 3 1 4 2

15. In India, the first Agriculture Research Centre was started by

Answer: B. B) Lord Curzon

16. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A) : Lord Curzon's partition of Bengal in 1905 was an immediate cause for the rise of extremism in the Indian National Movement. Reason (R) : Curzon's real intention was to divide Hindus and Muslims in Bengal and to destroy the national unity of Bengal. Choose the correct answer from the codes given below:

Answer: A. A) Both (A) and (R) are true, and (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)

17. In which tax the incidence and impact is on one and the same person?

Answer: A. Income tax

18. Arrange the following in chronological order : I. Sama Veda II. Rig Veda III. Yajur Veda IV. Atharva Veda. Of these

Answer: D. II, III, I & IV.

19. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: List I a) First Five-Year Plan b) Second Five-Year Plan c) Third Five-Year Plan d) Fourth Five-Year Plan List II 1. Self-sufficiency 2. Agriculture and Industrial development 3. Agriculture development 4. Heavy industries development. Codes: a b c d

Answer: D. D) 3 4 1 2

20. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: List I a) H. J. Bhaba b) S. S. Bhatnagar c) D. S. Kothari d) Vikram Sarabhai List II 1. Scientific and industrial research 2. Atomic energy 3. Indian Space Research Organisation 4. Defence Science Organisation.

Answer: A. A) 2 1 4 3

21. Arrange the following periods in a chronological order : I. Neolithic period II. Mesolithic period III. Chalcolithic period IV. Palaeolithic period. Of these

Answer: B. IV. II, I & III

22. Consider the following statements: I. The Atomic Energy Commission was set up under the Chairmanship of Homi J. Bhaba to formulate a policy for all atomic energy activities in the country. II. In 1956 India's first nuclear reactor in Trombay near Bombay began to function. Which of the statements is/are correct?

Answer: C. Both I & II

23. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: List I a) Justice Party b) Devadasi system c) Vaikam Hero d) Veera Tamilannai List II 1. E. V. Ramasamy Periyar 2. Dr. S. Dharmambal 3. Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy 4. Thiyagaraja Chetty.

Answer: A. A) 4 3 1 2

24. Which one of the following is incorrectly matched ? A) Bhonsle — Nagpur B) Holkar — Indore C) Peshwa — Delhi D) Scindia — Gwalior.

Answer: C. Peshwa — Delhi

25. Which one of the following is incorrectly matched ? A) Swaraj Party — C. R. Das B) Forward Bloc — Subhash Chandra Bose C) Muslim League — Nawab Salimullah Khan D) Justice Party — E. V. R. Periyar.

Answer: D. Justice Party — E. V. R. Periyar.

26. Which one of the following is correctly matched ?

Answer: B. Abhinava Bharat — Savarkar Brothers

27. Which one of the following is incorrectly matched ? A) New army regulations — Vellore mutiny B) Sir John Cradock — Commander-in-Chief C) Lord William Bentinck — Governor of Madras D) 4th Regiment rose in revolt against — Colonel Forbes.

Answer: D. 4th Regiment rose in revolt against — Colonel Forbes.

28. Match the following List I a) Assam b) Orissa c) Andhra Pradesh d) Kerala List II 1. Ponnam 2. Mason 3. Jhum 4. Podu. Match the following codes: a b c d

Answer: C. 3 4 2 1

29. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: (Computer Generations) List I a) Present Generation b) First c) Second d) Third List II 1. Valves 2. Integrated Circuit 3. Fifth Generation 4. Transistor.

Answer: C. C) 3 1 4 2

30. Which is the correct statement ? 'Ratooning' is the process followed in the cultivation of I. Sugarcane II. Paddy III. Cotton IV. Jute. Of these

Answer: A. A) I only

31. Match the deserts in List I with their respective countries in List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below : List I a) Thar b) Atacama c) Sahel d) Gobi List II 1. Africa 2. China 3. Chile 4. India. Codes: a b c d

Answer: C. C) 4 3 1 2

32. Consider the following statements: I. Mercury has the fastest orbital motion II. Mars requires 687 days to complete one revolution III. Saturn is the fifth planet from the sun. Which of the statement(s) is/are correct?

Answer: B. I and II

33. Match the following: பட்டியல் I (List I) a) வேலூர் (Vellore) b) நாமக்கல் (Namakkal) c) விழுப்புரம் (Villupuram) d) திருவண்ணாமலை (Tiruvannamalai) பட்டியல் II (List II) 1. கொல்லிமலை (Kolli Hills) 2. கல்வராயன் மலை (Kalvarayan Hills) 3. செஞ்சி மலை (Gingee Hills) 4. ஏலகிரி மலை (Yelagiri Hills)

Answer: A. A) 2 3 4 1

34. Which of the following statements is/are correct? I. In India, the winds that blow from north get deflected and come from the north west. These winds are known as the 'retreating monsoon'. II. In India, the winds that blow from the north get deflected and come from the north east. These winds are known as the 'retreating monsoon'. Of these

Answer: B. B) II only

35. Consider the following statements: I. Himachal lies between Himadri and Siwalik ranges. II. Passes are the natural gaps across the mountains. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Answer: C. Both I and II

36. Match the following: List I a) Australia b) South Africa c) Eurasia d) North America List II 1. Wild Horse 2. Bison 3. Kangaroo 4. Zebra. Codes: a b c d

Answer: C. C) 3 4 1 2

37. The harmonic mean and geometric mean of two values are 6.4 and 8 respectively. The values are

Answer: C. C) 4 and 16

38. If the sum of 3 consecutive integers is 540, then integers are

Answer: D. D) 179, 180, 181.

39. From the group of 5 men and 5 women, two persons are chosen at random. The probability that one of them is man and the other woman is

Answer: C. 5/9

40. A box contains 4 red. 5 blue and 6 green balls of identical size. Two balls are drawn at random. The probability that one is blue and the other is green is

Answer: B. 5 x 6 x 2 / 15 x 14

41. Which endocrine gland is attached to kidney ?

Answer: D. Adrenal gland.

42. The opaqueness of the lens of the eye is known as

Answer: B. Cataract

43. Find out the correct composition of Honey:

Answer: A. Sugar, Mineral, Pollen, Vitamin

44. Inheritance of genetic defects to children can be prevented by

Answer: B. Germ cell gene therapy

45. Consider the following statements: I. Cutting down trees affects the ecosystem (water and land habitats). II. More food will be available for living organisms. Among these:

Answer: A. I is correct but II is wrong

46. The bottle lid made of cork is viewed with the help of lens. There are many chambers in it. This observation by .................... led to the discovery of cell.

Answer: C. Robert Hooke

Answer: C. I & II

Answer: A. A) 2 4 1 3

49. Respiratory substrates are I. Carbohydrates II. Fats III. Proteins IV. Vitamins. Of these

Answer: B. I, II and III

50. Five boys A, B, C, D & E are sitting in a park in a circle. A is facing south west. D is facing south east. B and E are right opposite to A and D respectively and C is equidistant between D and B. Which direction is C facing to?

Answer: D. East.

51. A man earns Rs. 28,000 as monthly salary. His expenditure and savings are given in the diagram. How much did he spend on entertainment?

Answer: B. Rs. 1.960

52. The trader marks his goods at 20% above cost price and allows 10% discount. Then the gain per cent is

Answer: B. 8%

53. In a division sum the divisor is 12 times the quotient and 5 times the remainder. If the remainder is 24. then the dividend is

Answer: A. 1224

54. The role of aluminium in aluminothermic process is

Answer: C. reducing agent

55. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answer using the codes given below: List I a) Finely divided nickel b) Acidified potassium dichromate c) Loss of electrons d) Gain of electrons List II 1. Oxidation 2. Reducing agent 3. Reduction 4. Oxidising agent.

Answer: C. C) 2 4 1 3

56. Green manures can be obtained from which of the following?

Answer: B. Sun-hemp and cluster bean

57. Electroplating is a process of coating one metal over the other metal by electrolysis. In the electroplating of silver spoon with gold. the silver spoon must be taken as

Answer: A. cathode

58. Which of the following statements is/are correct? I. During oxidation, addition of hydrogen takes place. II. During oxidation, loss of electrons takes place. III. During oxidation, gain of electrons takes place. IV. During oxidation, addition of electropositive ion takes place. Of these

Answer: C. II alone is correct

59. Which one of the following is correctly matched ? A) pH value — S. P. L. Sorenson B) Acid — Faraday C) Battery — Arrhenius D) Germanium — Conductor.

Answer: A. pH value — S. P. L. Sorenson

60. Which one of the following is correctly matched ? A) Fifth Five-Year Plan – Generation of Employment B) Seventh Five-Year Plan – Poverty eradication and self-reliance. C) Eighth Five-Year Plan – Employment generation leading to achieve full employment by 2000 D) Tenth Five-Year Plan – Growth with social justice and equality.

Answer: C. Eighth Five-Year Plan – Employment generation leading to achieve full employment by 2000

Answer: A. A) the President

66. Match the following A) இந்திய திட்டமிடலின் தந்தை - காந்திஜி B) தமிழ்நாட்டின் முதல் ஆளுநர் - P. C. அலெக்சாண்டர் C) இந்தியாவின் முதல் பெண் பிரதமர் - இந்திரா காந்தி D) இந்தியாவின் முதல் ஜனாதிபதி - நேரு
