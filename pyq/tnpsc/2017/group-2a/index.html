101. Projectile motion is a combination of horizontal motion with constant __________ and vertical motion with constant __________.

Answer: B. Velocity, acceleration

102. Nuclear force is

Answer: D. spin dependent but charge independent

103. The area of a a semi-circle of radius 7 c.m. is

Answer: C. 77 cm²

104. The oxidation of SO₃²⁻ to SO₄²⁻ ion by oxygen SO₃²⁻ + :Ö: → SO₄²⁻, in this reaction which is Lewis acid and Lewis base?

Answer: B. Oxygen is Lewis acid and SO₃²⁻ is Lewis base

105. Cyanide resistant respiration is found in

Answer: D. Plants and Microbes

106. Male sex hormone 'testosterone' is produced by

Answer: A. Leydig cells

107. Which one of the following is correctly matched? I. Gregor Mendel - Neurospora II. T.H. Morgan - Drosophila III. C.B. Bridges - Garden pea IV. J.H. Muller - Mice

Answer: B. II

108. Tax is charged on some items which are manufactured in the country. This is collected by the Government of India. This Tax is

Answer: C. Excise Duty

109. The value of x²-2x / x² + 2x × 3x+6 / x-2

Answer: B. 3

110. A boy is now twice as old as his sister, four years ago, he was thrice as old as her what are their ages now?

Answer: C. 16, 8

111. x³/² : 9 = 16 : √x . What is the value of x?

Answer: D. ±12

112. Total surface area of hollow hemisphere is equal to

Answer: C. π(3R² + r²) sq. units

113. The probability that a leap year will have 53 Fridays or 53 Saturdays is

Answer: C. 3/7

114. Factorization of (x + y)² +9(x + y)+8 is

Answer: B. (x+y+1) (x + y +8)

115. What is the difference between the sum of first 'n' odd natural numbers and that of first 'n' natural numbers?

Answer: B. n(n-1)/2

116. Which of the following is arranged in correct ascending order?

Answer: A. 25% of 8, 40% of 6, 30% of 9, 20% of 15

117. Which of the following is larger in area?

Answer: C. An equilateral triangle whose sides are 10 cm each

118. The arithmetic mean of a group of 75 observations was calculated as 27. It was later found that one observation was wrongly read as 43 instead of the correct value 53. Obtain the correct arithmetic mean of the data.

Answer: B. 27.13

119. A sum of money triples itself at 8% per annum over certain time. The number of years is equal to

Answer: B. 25

120. Match the following -: (a) Annie Besant 1. Kesari (b) Bipin Chandrapal 2. Common weal (c) Boopendra Nath Dutt 3. The New Asia (d) Tilak 4. Yuganthar

Answer: D. (D) 2 3 4 1

121. Before E.V. Ramaswamy Vaikom Satyagraha was first started by three leaders. They were

Answer: B. T.K. Madhavan, K.P. Kesava Menon and George Joseph

122. Who was called 'The Father of Modern India'?

Answer: D. Rajaram Mohan Roy

123. Tambapanni is the old name of

Answer: C. Srilanka

124. Name the journal which declared 'Swami Vivekanand the greatest figure in the Parliament'.

Answer: D. New York Herald

125. Cultivable lands which has not been ploughed for between one and five years are called as

Answer: C. Other fallows

126. Tidal forests of Ganga-Brahmaputra delta, mainly consists of

Answer: B. Hardwood palms

127. Which is wrongly matched? (A) Indo - European Family - Arya (B) Austric Family - Nishada (C) Dravidian Family - Dravidian (D) Sino - Tibetan Family – Munda

Answer: D. Sino - Tibetan Family – Munda

128. ___________ marked the beginning of census taking in India.

Answer: B. The year 1872

129. The MRTP Act, 1969 has been repealed and replaced by the competition Act, 2002 with effect from

Answer: B. 2009

130. The basic objective of Gandhian plan brought out by S.N. Agarwala was

Answer: D. To raise the material as well as the cultural level of Indian masses

131. The last Deputy Chairman of Planning Commission of India was

Answer: D. Montek Singh Ahluwalia

132. The first stage of the strategy related to Intensive Agricultural District Programme was started in the year

Answer: B. 1960-61

133. Which of the following statements are correct with regard to Tamil Nadu 'Pudhu Vaazhvu Project'?. (i) It was launched in 2005 (ii) It is aided by World Bank (iii) The total outlay is Rs. 1,667 crores (iv) It is being implemented in 26 districts

Answer: A. All are correct statement

134. Whether governor of a state has the power to dissolve the state legislature?

Answer: A. Yes

135. In the rules of business of Indian Parliament, if the speaker admits, notice of a motion but no date is fixed for its introduction - then it is called

Answer: D. No day - Yet – named motion

136. Constitutional status to the Panchayat Raj Institutions was initiated based on the recommendation of

Answer: B. L.M. Singhvi Committee

137. Which Article of the Constitution of India conferred special leave petitions power to the Supreme Court?

Answer: A. Art. 136

138. In India, power of Judicial Review is restricted because the

Answer: C. Constitution is supreme

139. The government of India instituted Bharat Ratna and Padma Shri awards under

Answer: B. Article 18 of the Indian constitution

140. A scheme to provide Support to Training and Employment of Women (STEP) was launched in the year

Answer: A. 1986-87

141. Prime Minister of India inaugurated India's longest road bridge on May 26, 2017. Which states are connected by this bridge?

Answer: A. Assam and Arunachal Pradesh

142. In India which organisation's scientists recently produced electricity from water without using energy?

Answer: A. NPL, New Delhi

143. In which place Prime Minister of India inaugurated the country's first Indian Institute of Skills [IIS] on Dec. 20, 2016?

Answer: C. Kanpur

144. Which one of the organisation organised the Gyan Sangam [Knowledge Summit] in New Delhi on March, 2017?

Answer: A. Prajna Parvah

145. Which of the following aim comes under Prime Minister Krishan Sai Yojana?

Answer: B. To increase ground water

146. Iron Lady of Manipur, Irom Sharmila got how many votes in the 2017 Manipur Assembly election?

Answer: D. 90 votes

147. Which district of Tamilnadu, Chief Minister announced new dental college from the year 2017?

Answer: C. Virudhunagar

148. Which one of the following state government has shifted its financial year from [April 1 to March 31] to calendar year [January 1 to December 31]?

Answer: B. Madhya Pradesh

149. What percentage of total cultivation area in Tamilnadu is rainfed (Manavari)?

Answer: B. 57%

150. Indian Neutrino Observatory is situated in which district of Tamilnadu?

Answer: B. Theni

151. A supernova is an exploding star. In which years the last two supernova are visible to the naked eye?

Answer: C. 1604, 1987

152. Calculate the dimensions of Planck's constant 'h', using the equation E=hγ, where E → energy and γ→ frequency

Answer: B. ML²T⁻¹

153. Which one of the organic pesticides that contain phosphorous?

Answer: D. Parathion

154. Match the following: (a) Mixed fertilizer. (b) Complex fertilizer (c) Bio fertilizer (d) Organic nitrogen fertilizer 1. DAP 2. NPK 3. Oil cake 4. Algae

Answer: B. (a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 3

155. In Transmission Electron Microscope (TEM) _________ is used to magnify the objects.

Answer: C. Electrons

156. The Respiratory quotient value for glucose is

Answer: B. 1.0

157. Which one is wrongly matched pair in their characters?

Answer: C. Testis - Estrogen

158. Which of the following hormone stimulates contraction of uterine smooth muscle?

Answer: C. Oxytocin

159. The cylinder whose base is not in circular form is called

Answer: C. Oblique cylinder

160. 10 sq. m. is equal to

Answer: D. 100000 sq. cm.

161. A ____________ is often drawn as a guide, so that a frequency polygon can be drawn over the top.

Answer: B. Histogram

162. If 100 persons can finish a work in 7 days, how many persons can finish the same work 35 days?

Answer: A. 20 persons

163. What percent of an hour is 36 seconds?

Answer: B. 1%

164. $\left(\frac{x^{m}}{x^{n}}\right)^{m+n} \cdot\left(\frac{x^{n}}{x^{l}}\right)^{n+l} \cdot\left(\frac{x^{l}}{x^{m}}\right)^{l+m} =\ldots$

Answer: A. 1

165. If A=+, B=÷, C =x and D= - then what is the value of 1/5 C2B 1/5 A 1/5 D 1/10 =?

Answer: A. 2.1

166. The value of : x³-1/x+3 ÷ x²+x+1/3x+9 =?

Answer: C. 3(x-1)

167. What is the 85th term of the sequence x, x^(3/2), x^2, x^(5/2),...?

Answer: C. x^43

168. The radius and the slant height of a cone are respectively 'r' and 'l'. What is the volume of the cone?

Answer: A. 1/3 πr^2√(l^2 - r^2)

169. What is the 21st term of the sequence 100, 95, 90, 85.....?

Answer: C. 0

170. Simplify : 2x⁴-162 / (x²+9) (2x-6)

Answer: B. x+3

171. If the LCM of x and y is z, what is the H.C.F of x and y?

Answer: A. xy/z

172. The number of delegates who attended the first session of the Indian National Congress was

Answer: C. 72

173. The revolutionary nationalist Nelakanda Brahmachari was born at

Answer: C. Sirkali

174. Arrange the following in chronological order: 1. Senkuttuvan 2. Emayavaramban 3. Kanaikal Irumporai 4. Kuttuvan Cheral

Answer: A. 2, 1, 4, 3

175. In Chola kingdom the revenue department was called as

Answer: C. Puravuvari

176. Where the All India Muslim League was formed in 1906?

Answer: D. Dhaka

177. When 'Swadeshi day' was celebrated in India?

Answer: C. 29 May, 1932

178. Who was the first ruler in the Chola-Chalukya line?

Answer: C. Kulothunga I

179. The Vaikam Satyagraha arose in Kerala in the year

Answer: B. 1924

180. Periyar incorporated the ideas of the justice party and formed a new party called

Answer: A. Dravida Kazhagam

181. Mercury the smallest planet has only 88 earth days to orbit the sun, because

Answer: C. Distance from the sun

182. Match the disasters with its type and pick the correct answers code: (a) Blizzards 1. Hydrological disaster (b) Tsunamis 2. Health disaster (c) Nuclear accidents 3. Atmospheric disaster (d) Pandemic 4. Anthropogenic disaster

Answer: D. (a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 2

183. Despite the substantial improvement in health conditions of people in India, there has been certain negative trends due to I. High incidence of HIV/AIDS in rural areas II. High incidence of HIV/AIDS in urban areas III. Growing incidence of Heart diseases and cancer in rural areas IV. Growing incidence of Heart diseases and cancer in urban areas

Answer: B. I and III correct

184. Which one of the following matches is correct? (a) Thyroid hormone 1. Cushing's disease (b) Insulin 2. Myxedema (c) Corticosteroids 3. Acromegaly (d) Growth hormone 4. Diabetes

Answer: D. (D) 2 4 1 3

185. Which one of the following changes was not called by the Gandhian model and growth in the Indian Planning System?

Answer: B. To replace employment oriented planning with the production oriented planning

186. The word 'Secularism' was inserted in the preamble of the constitution after supreme court of India observed 'Secularism' was a basic feature of constitution in

Answer: B. 1973

187. The special status provided to Jammu and Kashmir under Art. 370 limits parliament to enact laws relating to

Answer: A. List-I and List-III of the Seventh Schedule

188. The ideals of liberty, equality and fraternity enshrined in the preamble of the Constitution of India were adopted under inspiration from the

Answer: B. French Revolution

189. Which one of the following is not correct related to the distinctions between the grants under Article 275 and Article 282 of the Constitution of India?

Answer: D. Allocation of Grants under Article 282 is based on the recommendation of Cabinet Committee

190. Match List I with List II and select the correct answer using the code given below : List I (a) P.A. Sangma (b) G.M.C. Balayogi (c) Manohar Joshi (d) Somnath Chatterjee List II 1. Telugu Desam party 2. CPI (m) 3. Shiv Sena 4. Congress - I

Answer: B. (B) 4 1 3 2

191. Kaviko Arakkattalai Award given to _________ during the year 2017.

Answer: A. Poet Muthulingam

192. What percentage of downtrodden students enrolment in private schools as per Right to Education Act?

Answer: A. 25

193. Find out the total votes of the electoral college for the Indian Presidential Election, 2017.

Answer: B. 11,04,546 votes

194. Which one of the following is the objective of perspective plan 2023?

Answer: A. Create Employment Opportunity

195. Which of the following objectives regarding Skill India Mission is incorrect? (A) Its objective is to achieve skilling of the youth across the country (B) Its aim is to remove financial constraints in accessing skill training programmes (C) Under the mission, loans from Rs. 50,000 to 1.5 lakh will be available to more the 34 lakh Youth of India (D) The tagline of this mission is Kaushal Bharat

Answer: C. Under the mission, loans from Rs. 50,000 to 1.5 lakh will be available to more the 34 lakh Youth of India

196. Which country physicists created a new form of Hydrogen? (Negatively charged hydrogen cluster)

Answer: B. Austria

197. Rajiv Gandhi School of Intellectual Property Rights Law is located in ____________ (city) of ____________ state.

Answer: D. Khargpur – West Bengal

198. Which one of the following is not correctly matched? (A) Varishtha Pension Bima Yojana – Social security for old age (B) Pravasi Karshal Vikas Yojana – Seeking employment in abroad (C) PM Fasal Bima Yojana – Insurance coverage to farmers (D) PM Gram Sadak Yojana – Empowerment of rural women

Answer: D. PM Gram Sadak Yojana – Empowerment of rural women

199. When BSE's sensex has reached its maximum 30,000 mark for the first time in the history?

Answer: C. 26 April 2017

200. The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully tested indigenously developed cryogenic engine on

Answer: A. January 25, 2017
