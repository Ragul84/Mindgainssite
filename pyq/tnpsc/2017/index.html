TNPSC 2017 Previous Year Questions with Answers

37 questions from the TNPSC 2017 papers, grouped by paper. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

37 questionsGroup 1 Prelims · General Studies (19 Feb 2017) · official TNPSC key

Group 1 Prelims · General Studies (19 Feb 2017) · official TNPSC key

37 questions

1. Which of the following is a wrong statement?

Answer: B. All ores are not minerals

2. Assertion (A): The pyramid of a grassland ecosystem is upright. Reason (R): Producers are maximum in number and tertiary consumers are least in number.

Answer: A. (A) and (R) are correct

3. Assertion (A): The temple was not merely a place of worship; it filled a large place in the cultural and economic life of the Tamil people. Reason (R): Its construction and maintenance offered employment to a number of people.

Answer: A. Both correct and (R) explains (A)

4. Ramsay MacDonald, the British Prime Minister, announced the Communal Award on

Answer: A. 16 August 1932

5. Who was the first woman President of the Indian National Congress?

Answer: B. Annie Besant

6. In which schedule of the Constitution are the 22 recognised languages included?

Answer: C. 8th Schedule

7. The idea that Indians should have a Constituent Assembly to frame a Constitution was initiated by

Answer: B. M.N. Roy

8. Which statement about Executive Magistrates is not correct?

Answer: C. An order of an Executive Magistrate is not subject to the revisional jurisdiction of the High Court

9. The institution of Lokayukta was established first in

Answer: D. Maharashtra

10. Match the committees with their purpose: (a) Balwant Rai Mehta (b) Ashok Mehta (c) G.V.K. Rao (d) L.M. Singhvi - 1. Revitalisation of Panchayati Raj institutions 2. Rural development and poverty alleviation 3. Examine the working of the Community Development Programme 4. Strengthening Panchayati Raj institutions

Answer: C. 3 4 2 1

11. The Chairman and members of the UPSC hold office for six years or till the age of

Answer: A. 65 years

12. A fort had provisions for 300 men for 90 days. After 20 days, 50 men left. How long will the food last at the same rate?

Answer: C. 84 days

13. What comes in place of the question mark? 24, 536, 487, 703, 678, ?

Answer: C. 742

14. The sum of n terms of an AP is 2n² + n. The eighth term is

Answer: D. 31

15. If the radii of two cylinders are in the ratio 5:3 and their heights in the ratio 3:5, the ratio of their volumes is

Answer: D. 5:3

16. Which option arranges the states in descending order of population?

Answer: C. Uttar Pradesh - Maharashtra - Bihar - West Bengal

17. In 2016, Adani Green Energy set up India's largest solar power plant in

Answer: A. Tamil Nadu

18. A convex lens of focal length 40 cm is in contact with a concave lens of focal length 25 cm. The power of the combination is

Answer: A. −1.5 D

19. Setting up village panchayats is associated with which part of the Constitution?

Answer: C. Directive Principles of State Policy

20. Which is an indirect tax?

Answer: B. Service tax

21. Under Article 170, the strength of a Legislative Assembly is

Answer: A. Not more than 500 and not less than 60

22. The Sarkaria Commission on Centre-State relations was appointed in

Answer: C. 1983

23. Which statement about public interest litigation is not correct?

Answer: B. A PIL filed before the Supreme Court may not be transferred to an appropriate High Court

24. Who was the ruler of Jammu and Kashmir in 1940?

Answer: B. Maharaja Hari Singh

25. The Maternity Benefit Act was enacted in

Answer: A. 1961

26. Article 132 of the Constitution deals with

Answer: A. Appellate jurisdiction of the Supreme Court in constitutional cases

27. Which Amendment enabled Delhi and Puducherry to vote in Presidential elections?

Answer: A. 70th

28. A wall 60 m long, 3 m wide and 5 m high is built with bricks 30 cm × 15 cm × 20 cm. How many bricks are needed?

Answer: C. 1,00,000

29. How many prime numbers lie between 1 and 100?

Answer: B. 25

30. The median of 12, 17, 5, 8, 13, 6, 9 is

Answer: B. 9

31. A gardener gave a girl 2 apples on day 1, 4 on day 2, 8 on day 3 and so on for ten days. How many apples did she get in total?

Answer: D. 2046

32. A cone, a hemisphere and a cylinder have equal bases, and the cone and cylinder have height equal to the common radius. The ratio of their volumes is

Answer: A. 1:2:3

33. The present ages of A and B are in the ratio 4:5; after five years it will be 5:6. The sum of their present ages is

Answer: B. 45 years

34. The longest cycle highway in India is in

Answer: A. Uttar Pradesh

35. Which state did NOT go to assembly polls in February 2017?

Answer: A. Haryana

36. The Digital Financial Literacy Project was launched on 1 December 2016 by the

Answer: A. Union HRD Ministry

37. The first digital village (Akodara) in India is in

Answer: A. Gujarat
