TNPSC 2024 Group 4 · General Studies: Questions with Answers

All 69 questions from the TNPSC 2024 Group 4 · General Studies paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

69 questionsUnofficial answers
⚠ Unofficial · 69/100

The official key for this paper was published for a different booklet version, so these answers were worked out by MindGains. Questions we could not read clearly from the scan, or could not answer with certainty, are left out.

1. Who is the Chief Executive Authority of the Municipal Corporation?

Answer: B. Municipal Commissioner

2. One of history's great oration, "I have a dream" was given by

Answer: C. Martin Luther King

3. In which year did the Govt. of India declare Tamil as the first Classical Language?

Answer: C. 2004

4. Human Rights Day is celebrated on

Answer: C. 10th December

5. A person invested Rs.12,000 in a fixed deposit scheme for 2 years at the rate of 5% compound interest. How much amount will be got on maturity of the fixed deposit?

Answer: C. Rs.13,230

6. Identify the teeth absent in milk dentition in mammals.

Answer: A. Premolars

7. Which of the following food material is translocated by phloem loading?

Answer: D. Sucrose

8. A fungus causing disease in plants is reported recently to cause infection in man is

Answer: B. Chondrostereum purpureum

9. A woman reformer who was instrumental in Dr. Muthulakshmi's introduction of the Devadasi Abolition Act

Answer: A. Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar

10. Pagoda was a ____ coin of Vijayanagar descent.

Answer: D. Gold

11. Tamil Nadu Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) has grown at the rate of ____% for the year 2022-2023 as against India's GDP growth rate of 7.24% during the same period.

Answer: D. 8.19

12. If Rameela says, "Ragavi's father Raman is the only son of my father-in-law Ramanathan", then how is Ramani, who is the sister of Ragavi, related to Ramanathan?

Answer: D. Grand Daughter

13. 3 men and 4 women can earn Rs. 3,780 in 7 days. 11 men and 13 women earn Rs. 15,040 in 8 days. In what time will 7 men and 9 women earn Rs. 12,400?

Answer: C. 10 days

14. The acids present in Grape and Tomato are ____ respectively.

Answer: C. Tartaric acid, Oxalic acid

15. Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan Scheme aims to

Answer: A. Integrate vocational education with General Academic Education

16. Who is the author of the book "Ambedkar : A Life"?

Answer: A. Shashi Tharoor

17. Which animal was not known to the Harappans?

Answer: C. Horse

18. Arrange the following Committees in the correct chronological order: I. G.V.K. Rao Committee, II. Balwant Rai Mehta Committee, III. L.M. Singhvi Committee, IV. Ashok Mehta Committee

Answer: D. II, IV, I, III

19. "The Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company" started with capital amount of

Answer: D. Rs. 10 lakh

20. Ram Prasad Bismil and Ashfaqulla Khan were arrested and tried in the ____ case.

Answer: B. Kakori conspiracy case

21. The Roman writer Pliny the Elder writes of ____ in his Natural History as the first emporium of India.

Answer: C. Musiri

22. Iyothee Thassar founded the Dravida Mahajana Sabha in 1891 and organised the First Conference of the association at

Answer: C. Nilgiris

23. How many great circles can a hemisphere have?

Answer: A. 1

24. Find the volume of a cylinder whose height is 4 m and whose base area is 500 m2.

Answer: A. 2000 m3

25. In which country was the 49th G7 summit (2023) held?

Answer: D. Japan

26. ____ started a mass Raksha Bandhan festival during the Partition of Bengal.

Answer: D. Rabindranath Tagore

27. A king who emerged victorious in all his expeditions and engraved the title Second Alexander on his coins

Answer: A. Bahman Shah

28. Which day is celebrated as overseas Indian's day?

Answer: C. January-9

29. Who is the author of the book "Why I am an Atheist"?

Answer: C. Bhagat Singh

30. Name the author of book "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India".

Answer: C. Dadabhai Naoroji

31. The term "Satyaputra" found in the Ashokan Inscription refers to

Answer: A. Adiyaman

32. What is the meaning of the word "Extinguish" when Valluvar tries to compare lust to "Pouring Ghee to Fire"?

Answer: A. Put out

33. Kayal bought 5 dozen eggs. Out of that 45 eggs are good eggs. Express the number of rotten eggs in percentage.

Answer: C. 25%

34. Match the following: (a) Cerebral cortex – 1. Temperature control; (b) Hypothalamus – 2. Sensory perception; (c) Cerebellum – 3. Role in sleep-awake cycle; (d) Pons – 4. Maintenance of posture and balance. Choose (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: D. 2 1 4 3

35. Who appeared in the court in defence of revolutionary nationalists in the Alipur blast case?

Answer: A. Chittaranjan Dass

36. What are the two things that one should keep in mind when setting out to conquer the world in the Kural? ~ "Gnalam Karuthinum Kaikoodum"

Answer: B. Time and Place

37. The radius of a conical tent is 700 cm and the height is 24 m. Calculate the length of the canvas used to make the tent if the width of the rectangular canvas is 4 m.

Answer: A. 137.5m

38. Find the next number : 1, 2, 6, 24, 120, ?

Answer: C. 720

39. If 3x+4y: 4x + 6y = 22:32 then x: y is

Answer: A. 1:2

40. If two numbers a and b are in the ratio m:n, then the LCM of a and b is

Answer: C. mb

41. The first modernised jute industrial unit was established at ____ in West Bengal in 1855.

Answer: D. Rishra

42. Which among the following inscriptions mention the trade contacts between Mesopotamia and Harappan civilisation?

Answer: C. Cuneiform

43. Aksai Chin remains a disputed area between China and

Answer: B. India

44. The H.C.F. of two numbers is 11 and their L.C.M is 693. If one of the number is 77, find the other

Answer: C. 99

45. A merchant has 120 Itrs and 180 ltrs of two kinds of oil. He wants to sell oil by filling the two kinds of oil in tins of equal volumes. What is the greatest capacity of such a tin?

Answer: C. 60 ltrs

46. Vignesh borrows Rs.10,000 for 2 years at 4% p.a. simple interest. He lends it to Sanjay at 6% p.a. for 2 years. Find his gain in this transaction per year.

Answer: A. 200

47. Find the ratio between HCF and LCM of the numbers 7 and 13.

Answer: A. 1:91

48. Consider the following statement (Reason and Assertion type) : Assertion [A]: Make in India program was launched in 2014. Reason [R] : Intended to boost the domestic manufacturing sector and also augment investment into the country.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true, and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

49. Which of the following states have a Common High Court?

Answer: C. Punjab and Haryana

50. The Industrial Policy of India, which abolished the MRTP Act in 1969 was

Answer: A. 1991 Industrial Policy

51. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) was launched in 1998 by

Answer: D. Reserve Bank of India and NABARD Bank

52. What removes the inner darkness of mankind like a lamp, according to Valluvar?

Answer: D. Lamp with truth's pure radiance

53. Anemia Mukt Bharat was implemented in Tamil Nadu in the year

Answer: C. 2018

54. India observed 2018 as the national year of which crop?

Answer: A. Millets

55. Kumaran bought a raincoat and saved Rs. 25 with discount of 20%, What was the original price of the raincoat?

Answer: A. Rs. 125

56. An intelligent man carries 3 sacks with 30 mangoes each. As he crosses each toll, he has to give 1 mango for each sack. He crosses 30 tolls. How many mangoes he will have at the end?

Answer: D. 25

57. Find the length of the longest pole that can be placed in a room 24 m long, 18 m broad and 16 m high.

Answer: C. 34m

58. Choose the correct pairs: 1. Phenolphthalein – colourless in acidic medium and pink in basic medium; 2. Methyl orange – pink in basic medium and yellow in acidic medium; 3. Red litmus paper – turns blue in basic medium; 4. Blue litmus paper – turns red in basic medium

Answer: B. 1 and 3 are correct

59. Indian Railways has introduced a cutting-edge AI based technology called —— to prevent elephant accidents on railway tracks.

Answer: C. Gajraj Suraksha

60. Arrange the three-tier Panchayat Raj system as per the constitutional provisions.

Answer: A. Village Panchayat – Intermediate Panchayat – District Panchayat

61. Which of the following are incorrectly paired? (i) Taxes on agricultural income – State; (ii) Taxes on land and buildings – State; (iii) Taxes on the consumption or sale of electricity – Centre; (iv) Land revenue – Centre

Answer: C. (iii) and (iv)

62. The functional head of NITI Aayog

Answer: C. Vice-Chairman

63. Who published the Tamil newspaper Thozhilalan (worker)?

Answer: A. M. Singaravelar

64. The LCM of two numbers is 6 times their HCF. If the HCF is 12 and one of the numbers is 36, then find the other number.

Answer: A. 24

65. The Tamil Nadu Government declares ____ as Education Development Day.

Answer: C. 15th July

66. For the principal Rs.10,000 the rate of interest is 15 p.a. for 3 years Find the difference between C.I and S.1.

Answer: C. 708.75

67. A person takes a loan of Rs.2,000 at 6% simple interest. He returns Rs.1,000 at the end of one year. In order to clear his dues at the end of 2 years how much he would pay?

Answer: B. Rs. 1,180

68. If 1³ + 2³ + 3³ + ... + K³ = 72900, then find 1 + 2 + 3 + ... + K.

Answer: C. 270

69. In the series 2, 3, 5, 9, x, 33, y, ..... what is the value of x+y?

Answer: D. 82
