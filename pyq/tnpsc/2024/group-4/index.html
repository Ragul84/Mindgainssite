TNPSC · 2024

TNPSC 2024 Group 4 · General Studies: Questions with Answers

All 99 questions from the TNPSC 2024 Group 4 · General Studies paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

99 questionsUnofficial answers
⚠ Unofficial · 99/100

The official key for this paper was published for a different booklet version, so these answers were worked out by MindGains. Q136 is left out: none of its four options is correct (the swamp deer is the state animal of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh).

101. Who is the Chief Executive Authority of the Municipal Corporation?

Answer: B. Municipal Commissioner

102. One of history's great orations, "I have a dream", was given by

Answer: C. Martin Luther King

103. Which of the following statements are true about the Himalayas? (i) Zaskar, Ladakh, Kailash and Karakoram ranges are found in the Trans-Himalayas. (ii) The Greater Himalayas receive less rainfall as compared to the Lesser Himalayas and the Siwaliks. (iii) The Outer Himalayas is the most continuous range.

Answer: B. (i) and (ii) only

104. In which year did the Govt. of India declare Tamil as the first Classical Language?

Answer: C. 2004

105. Human Rights Day is celebrated on

Answer: C. 10th December

106. Assertion [A]: The Ministry of Education (formerly MHRD) in India formulates education policy and also undertakes education programmes. Reason [R]: The centre, represented by the Ministry of Education, decides India's education budget.

Answer: A. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] explains [A]

107. A person invested Rs. 12,000 in a fixed deposit scheme for 2 years at the rate of 5% compound interest. How much amount will be got on maturity of the fixed deposit?

Answer: C. Rs. 13,230

108. Assertion [A]: "Thiranari Thaervu Thittam" was launched in the year 2023. Reason [R]: To encourage government school students to pursue higher education in premier institutions.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true, [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

109. If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, …, Z = 26, then the L.C.M. of H, C, F is not equal to

Answer: C. A + C + H

110. Find the value of √(5√(5√(5…)))

Answer: D. 5¹

111. Identify the teeth absent in milk dentition in mammals.

Answer: B. Molars

112. Which of the following food materials is translocated by phloem loading?

Answer: D. Sucrose

113. A fungus causing disease in plants is reported recently to cause infection in man. It is

Answer: B. Chondrostereum purpureum

114. Which one of the following is the wrong option for the rock-cut temples of the Gupta period?

Answer: C. Elephanta Cave (Maharashtra)

115. Assertion [A]: Article 249 of the Constitution enables Parliament to transfer a subject from the State List to the Union List. Reason [R]: It is for the purpose of legislation by Parliament on grounds of national interest.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true; and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

116. A woman reformer who was instrumental in Dr. Muthulakshmi's introduction of the Devadasi Abolition Act

Answer: A. Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar

117. Pagoda was a ______ coin of Vijayanagar descent.

Answer: D. Gold

118. Which of the following statements are true about V.O. Chidambaram Pillai? (i) He was a follower of Gopalakrishna Gokhale. (ii) He was known as Chekkiluththa Chemmal. (iii) He founded the Servants of India Society. (iv) He was a lawyer.

Answer: C. (ii) and (iv) only

119. Tamil Nadu Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) has grown at the rate of ______% for the year 2022-2023 as against India's GDP growth rate of 7.24% during the same period.

Answer: D. 8.19

120. Assertion [A]: "Mangala Malai" scheme was introduced in 2012-2013. Reason [R]: To facilitate the orphan girls to get a suitable bridegroom.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true, [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

121. If Rameela says, "Ragavi's father Raman is the only son of my father-in-law Ramanathan", then how is Ramani, who is the sister of Ragavi, related to Ramanathan?

Answer: D. Grand Daughter

122. 3 men and 4 women can earn Rs. 3,780 in 7 days. 11 men and 13 women earn Rs. 15,040 in 8 days. In what time will 7 men and 9 women earn Rs. 12,400?

Answer: C. 10 days

123. The acids present in grape and tomato are ______ and ______.

Answer: C. Tartaric acid, Oxalic acid

124. Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan Scheme aims to

Answer: A. Integrate vocational education with general academic education

125. Who is the author of the book "Ambedkar: A Life"?

Answer: A. Shashi Tharoor

126. Which animal was not known to the Harappans?

Answer: C. Horse

127. Arrange the following committees in the correct chronological order: I. G.V.K. Rao Committee II. Balwant Rai Mehta Committee III. L.M. Singhvi Committee IV. Ashok Mehta Committee

Answer: D. II, IV, I, III

128. "The Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company" started with a capital amount of

Answer: D. Rs. 10 lakh

129. Ram Prasad Bismil and Ashfaqulla Khan were arrested and tried in the ______ case.

Answer: B. Kakori conspiracy case

130. Various welfare schemes to enhance the overall development of Adi Dravidar and Scheduled Tribe people: a sum of Rs. ______ crores has been allocated in the Budget Estimate for 2023-2024.

Answer: C. Rs. 3512.85

131. The Roman writer Pliny the Elder writes of ______ in his Natural History as the first emporium of India.

Answer: C. Musiri

132. Iyothee Thassar founded the Dravida Mahajana Sabha in 1891 and organised the first conference of the association at

Answer: C. Nilgiris

133. How many great circles can a hemisphere have?

Answer: A. 1

134. Find the volume of a cylinder whose height is 4 m and whose base area is 500 m².

Answer: A. 2000 m³

135. Which of the following statements are false about the "conditions for no Doppler effect"? 1. Listener at rest and source moves towards the listener 2. S and L move in such a way that the distance between them remains constant 3. S and L are moving in mutually perpendicular directions 4. The source is situated at the centre of the circle along which the listener is moving

Answer: A. 1 only

137. In which country was the 49th G7 summit (2023) held?

Answer: D. Japan

138. ______ started a mass Raksha Bandhan festival during the Partition of Bengal.

Answer: D. Rabindranath Tagore

139. A king who emerged victorious in all his expeditions and engraved the title 'Second Alexander' on his coins

Answer: A. Bahman Shah

140. Which day is celebrated as Overseas Indians' Day?

Answer: C. January 9

141. Who is considered to be the Architect of the Constitution of India?

Answer: A. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

142. The author of the book "Why I am an Atheist"?

Answer: C. Bhagat Singh

143. Name the author of the book "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India".

Answer: C. Dadabhai Naoroji

144. The term "Satyaputra" found in the Ashokan inscription refers to

Answer: A. Adiyaman

145. What is the meaning of the word "Extinguish" (நுதுப்பேம்) when Valluvar compares lust to "pouring ghee to fire"?

Answer: A. Put out

146. Who said "Better bullock carts and freedom than a train de luxe with subjection"?

Answer: D. Annie Besant

147. Kayal bought 5 dozen eggs. Out of that, 45 eggs are good eggs. Express the number of rotten eggs as a percentage.

Answer: C. 25%

148. If Sunday = 2, Tuesday = 3, Thursday = 2, what is the value of Monday?

Answer: B. 2

149. Match the following: (a) Cerebral cortex (b) Hypothalamus (c) Cerebellum (d) Pons — 1. Temperature control 2. Sensory perception 3. Role in sleep-awake cycle 4. Maintenance of posture and balance. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: D. 2 1 4 3

150. Match the religion and place of worship: (a) Jainism (b) Judaism (c) Buddhism (d) Zoroastrianism — 1. Synagogue 2. Agiyari 3. Basadi 4. Vihara. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: D. 3 1 4 2

151. Match the following: (a) Peshwa (b) Shurnavis (c) Nyayadhish (d) Pandit Rao — 1. Secretary 2. Chief Justice 3. High Priest 4. Prime Minister. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 4 1 2 3

152. Which of the following statements are true about the "Anti-defection law"? (i) Provided by the 52nd Amendment Act of 1985 (ii) Added in the 9th Schedule to the Constitution (iii) Made some changes in the 91st Amendment Act of 2003

Answer: B. (i) and (iii) only

153. Who appeared in court in defence of the revolutionary nationalists in the Alipur blast case?

Answer: A. Chittaranjan Dass

154. Who was honoured with titles such as Rao Sahib (1926), Rao Bahadur (1930) and Divan Bahadur (1936) for his selfless social services?

Answer: A. Rettaimalai Srinivasan

155. Who eulogised Thirukkural as given below? "Will any other didactic book be needed if one knows Thirukkural".

Answer: B. Prof. Sundarampillai

156. What are the two things that one should keep in mind when setting out to conquer the world, according to the Kural "Gnalam Karuthinum Kaikoodum"?

Answer: B. Time and place

157. The radius of a conical tent is 700 cm and the height is 24 m. Calculate the length of the canvas used to make the tent if the width of the rectangular canvas is 4 m.

Answer: A. 137.5 m

158. Find the next number: 1, 2, 6, 24, 120, ?

Answer: C. 720

159. If 3x + 4y : 4x + 6y = 22 : 32, then x : y is

Answer: A. 1 : 2

160. If two numbers a and b are in the ratio m : n, then the LCM of a and b is

Answer: C. mb

161. Identify the correctly matched fossil:

More than one option is correct: B. Bryophyte – Naiadita / C. Pteridophyte – Lepidocarpon

162. Choose the correct statements: (i) The Government of India enacted the Wildlife Protection Act in 1972. (ii) Project Tiger was launched in April 1974. (iii) The Indian Government has established 16 Biosphere Reserves.

Answer: A. (i) only

163. The first modernised jute industrial unit was established at ______ in West Bengal in 1855.

Answer: D. Rishra

164. Arrange the following countries in descending order of their Human Development Index score (as of 2016): 1. Norway 2. Switzerland 3. India 4. Australia

Answer: A. 1 4 2 3

165. Which among the following inscriptions mention the trade contacts between Mesopotamia and the Harappan civilisation?

Answer: C. Cuneiform

166. Which of the following statements are true about the Directive Principles of State Policy? (i) Articles 36 to 51 deal with the Directive Principles of State Policy. (ii) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar described them as "novel features" of the Indian Constitution. (iii) These principles are enforceable by the courts.

Answer: C. (i) and (ii) only

167. Which of the following statements are true about Theeran Chinnamalai? (i) Theeran Chinnamalai was a Palayakkarar of Kongu country who fought against the British East India Company. (ii) He was trained by the French and Tipu. (iii) From 1800 to July 31, 1805 (the day of his hanging), Theeran Chinnamalai ceaselessly fought against the French.

Answer: C. (i) and (ii) only

168. Which of the traits differentiates all living things from human beings according to Thiruvalluvar?

Answer: A. Actions

169. Aksai Chin remains a disputed area between China and ______.

Answer: B. India

170. The H.C.F. of two numbers is 11 and their L.C.M. is 693. If one of the numbers is 77, find the other.

Answer: C. 99

171. A merchant has 120 litres and 180 litres of two kinds of oil. He wants to sell the oil by filling the two kinds of oil in tins of equal volumes. What is the greatest capacity of such a tin?

Answer: C. 60 litres

172. Vignesh borrows Rs. 10,000 for 2 years at 4% p.a. simple interest. He lends it to Sanjay at 6% p.a. for 2 years. Find his gain in this transaction per year.

Answer: A. 200

173. Find the ratio between the HCF and LCM of the numbers 7 and 13.

Answer: A. 1 : 91

174. Match the following: (a) Ganymede (b) Titan (c) Neptune (d) Triton — 1. Moves in the opposite direction to the direction of the spin of its planet 2. Greenish star-like appearance 3. Moon with clouds in our solar system 4. Largest moon of our solar system. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: A. 4 3 2 1

175. Assertion [A]: The Make in India programme was launched in 2014. Reason [R]: It is intended to boost the domestic manufacturing sector and also augment investment into the country.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true, and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

176. Which of the following states have a common High Court?

Answer: C. Punjab and Haryana

177. The Industrial Policy of India which abolished the MRTP Act of 1969 was

Answer: A. 1991 Industrial Policy

178. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) was launched in 1998 by

Answer: D. Reserve Bank of India and NABARD Bank

179. What removes the inner darkness of mankind like a lamp, according to Valluvar?

Answer: D. Lamp with truth's pure radiance

180. Anemia Mukt Bharat was implemented in Tamil Nadu in the year

Answer: C. 2018

181. Implementation of the Tamil Nadu Urban Healthcare Project funded by the Japan International Cooperation Agency is for ______ years.

Answer: C. 7

182. India observed 2018 as the national year of which crop?

Answer: A. Millets

183. If x : y = 2 : 1, then (x² − y²) : (x² + y²) is

Answer: D. 3 : 5

184. Kumaran bought a raincoat and saved Rs. 25 with a discount of 20%. What was the original price of the raincoat?

Answer: A. Rs. 125

185. An intelligent man carries 3 sacks with 30 mangoes each. As he crosses each toll, he has to give 1 mango for each sack. He crosses 30 tolls. How many mangoes will he have at the end?

Answer: D. 25

186. Find the length of the longest pole that can be placed in a room 24 m long, 18 m broad and 16 m high.

Answer: C. 34 m

187. Choose the correct pairs: 1. Phenolphthalein – Colourless in acidic medium and pink in basic medium 2. Methyl orange – Pink in basic medium and yellow in acidic medium 3. Red litmus paper – Turns blue in basic medium 4. Blue litmus paper – Turns red in basic medium

Answer: B. 1 and 3 are correct

188. Indian Railways has introduced a cutting-edge AI-based technology called ______ to prevent elephant accidents on railway tracks.

Answer: C. Gajraj Suraksha

189. Arrange the three-tier Panchayat Raj system as per the constitutional provisions.

Answer: A. Village Panchayat – Intermediate Panchayat – District Panchayat

190. Which of the following are incorrectly paired? (i) Taxes on agricultural income – State (ii) Taxes on land and buildings – State (iii) Taxes on the consumption or sale of electricity – Centre (iv) Land revenue – Centre

Answer: C. (iii) and (iv)

191. The functional head of NITI Aayog

Answer: C. Vice-Chairman

192. Who published the Tamil newspaper Thozhilalan (Worker)?

Answer: A. M. Singaravelar

193. The LCM of two numbers is 6 times their HCF. If the HCF is 12 and one of the numbers is 36, then find the other number.

Answer: A. 24

194. Assertion [A]: Cement production and consumption continue to grow despite the general recession in the economy. Reason [R]: India is one of the largest cement producers and ranked first in the world.

Answer: A. [A] is true but [R] is false

195. The Tamil Nadu Government declares ______ as Education Development Day.

Answer: C. 15th July

196. For a principal of Rs. 10,000, the rate of interest is 15% p.a. for 3 years. Find the difference between C.I. and S.I.

Answer: C. 708.75

197. A person takes a loan of Rs. 2,000 at 6% simple interest. He returns Rs. 1,000 at the end of one year. In order to clear his dues at the end of 2 years, how much would he pay?

Answer: B. Rs. 1,180

198. If 1³ + 2³ + 3³ + … + K³ = 72900, then find 1 + 2 + 3 + … + K.

Answer: C. 270

199. In the series 2, 3, 5, 9, x, 33, y, …, what is the value of x + y?

Answer: D. 82

200. When we write AZBYCX ….. YBZA, how many consonants will appear immediately to the left of the vowels?

Answer: C. 9
