TNPSC 2024 Group 2 Prelims · General English: Questions with Answers

All 67 questions from the TNPSC 2024 Group 2 Prelims · General English paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

67 questionsUnofficial answers
⚠ Unofficial · 67/100

The official key for this paper was published for a different booklet version, so these answers were worked out by MindGains. Questions we could not read clearly from the scan, or could not answer with certainty, are left out.

1. What was the name of the system that Nelson Mandela fought against?

Answer: B. Apartheid

2. Abraham Lincoln proclaimed the abolition of slavery on

Answer: B. January 1, 1863

3. "I must go down to the seas again, to the vagrant gypsy life" - 'Gypsy life' means

Answer: B. A wandering man's life

4. "But we ants never borrow; We ants never lend" - Why do you think ants neither borrow nor lend?

Answer: C. Ants are wise and hard working

5. Choose the correct tense form: If she works hard, she ____ a scholarship.

Answer: C. will get

6. Select the correct passive form: She had already sent the parcel.

Answer: B. The parcel had already been sent by her

7. Choose the correct active voice: The rules should not be violated.

Answer: D. Nobody should violate the rules.

8. "The woods are lovely, dark and deep / But I have promises to keep" - Who wrote these lines?

Answer: A. Robert Frost

9. "But the gladdest sort of people, when the busy day is done, / Are the brothers and the sisters who together share their fun." - Who wrote these lines?

Answer: B. Edgar Albert Guest

10. The word 'Plaintively' means

Answer: B. Sadly

11. Prospero's daughter's name was

Answer: D. Miranda

12. When was Subhas Chandra Bose born?

Answer: A. January 23, 1897

13. Helen Keller's autobiography was named

Answer: B. The Story of My Life

14. How did Kalpana Chawla die?

Answer: A. Space Shuttle disaster

15. "But of late, my computer has been behaving badly too, / It is so absent-minded, that I don't know what to do." - Mention the figure of speech.

Answer: B. Personification

16. "But I know no better spectacle / Than a comet in full flight." - What does 'spectacle' mean here?

Answer: A. unusual event that attracts attention

17. Choose the correct question tag: This is the bus that I have to travel, ____?

Answer: D. isn't it?

18. Select the suitable tag: He lives in Europe, ____?

Answer: D. doesn't he?

19. (Passage) Raj was upset as he had done ____ in his English test.

Answer: B. poorly

20. (Passage) His grandmother gave him a ____.

Answer: C. pencil

21. (Passage) Granny compared ____ with the pencil.

Answer: B. Raj

22. (Passage) Raj's pain of not doing well in his test was compared with ____ of pencils.

Answer: D. sharpening

23. Choose the correct meaning of the phrase 'Reconcile to'

Answer: B. Accept half-heartedly

24. Pick out the complex sentence.

Answer: B. One who does not love one's country is a wretched person

25. What kind of sentence is this? 'If he is at home, I shall see him.'

Answer: C. Complex

26. Combine: I saw a tiger. It was wounded.

Answer: B. I saw a wounded tiger

27. What is the landform formed by silt called?

Answer: A. Alluvial

28. Which historical character inspired young Indira?

Answer: B. Joan of Arc

29. Why is Dr. Salim Ali called the "Birdman of India"?

Answer: B. His immense contribution to the field of ornithology

30. "Courage isn't a brilliant dash, / A daring deed in a moment's flash; / It isn't an instantaneous thing / Born of despair with a sudden spring" - Rhyme scheme?

Answer: C. AABB

31. "Speak gently to the young, for they / Will have enough to bear - / Pass through this life as best they may, / 'Tis full of anxious care!" - Rhyme scheme?

Answer: A. ABAB

32. "And all I ask is a windy day with the white clouds flying, / And the flung spray and the blown spume, and the sea-gulls crying." - The rhyming words are

Answer: D. flying, crying

33. Choose the correct antonym for 'Concentrate'.

Answer: C. Distract

34. Select the correct prefix for 'Violet'.

Answer: A. ultra

35. "For simple sheep; and such are daffodils / With the green world they live in; and clear rills..." - These lines were written by

Answer: C. John Keats

36. "Come you back unto the fireside and be comrade with your kin" - This line is taken from the poem

Answer: C. The Stick-Together Families

37. Select the grammatically correct sentence: (a) My son is fond of music (b) My son is fond to music (c) My son is fond in music (d) My son is fond with music

Answer: D. Only (a) is correct

38. Which of the following is a noun?

Answer: C. Sun

39. A ____ of birds were flying in the sky.

Answer: C. flock of

40. Identify the correct statement: (a) Crises (b) Media

Answer: D. Both (a) and (b) are plural words

41. Who brought up Sir Isaac Newton?

Answer: B. Grandmother

42. Beauty lies in the beholder's ____.

Answer: B. Eyes

43. "My Reminiscences" was written by

Answer: D. Rabindranath Tagore

44. Identify the figure of speech: "The bleeding bark will heal."

Answer: A. Personification

45. Foreign + Exchange = ? Find the blended word.

Answer: D. Forex

46. Which two words blend together to form the new word 'Camcorder'?

Answer: B. Camera + recorder

47. Which of the following is correct for 'Overcoat'?

Answer: D. Preposition + Noun

48. Identify the compound word that is Gerund + Noun.

Answer: C. Bleaching powder

49. Replace with its American word: We should use the bin for the garbage.

Answer: B. Trash can

50. "And all I ask is a merry yarn from a laughing fellow-rover, / And quiet sleep and a sweet dream when the long trick's over" - These lines are from the poem

Answer: B. Sea Fever

51. Find the error part: I found an one rupee coin / in the playground / while playing

Answer: A. I found an one rupee coin

52. 'Practice hard and see what magic can transpire' was the advice given by ____ to Sachin Tendulkar.

Answer: A. Ramakant Achrekar

53. The Adventures of Tom Sawyer is a

Answer: B. Novel

54. "And I sunned it with smiles" - Identify the alliteration.

Answer: C. Sunned - smiles

55. "To win, run as fast as the wind" - Identify the figure of speech.

Answer: C. Simile

56. Which of the following is a metaphor?

Answer: C. His words are pearls of wisdom

57. "Autumn is English in red, yellow and brown / Autumn is Indian whenever leaves fall down" - Who wrote this poem?

Answer: A. Nisha Dyrene

58. "And one day, my naughty computer actually gobbled a worm, / And behaved so erratically that it made me squirm." - The poet was made to squirm because of the ____ behaviour of the computer.

Answer: C. erratic

59. "Every soul that touches yours / Be it the slightest contact" - Who wrote these lines?

Answer: C. George Eliot

60. "Persistence is the key to everything", says she. What does 'Persistence' mean?

Answer: C. determination

61. "It's helping your fellowman to score / When his chances hopeless seem" - The word 'it' refers to

Answer: C. team work

62. Identify the sentence pattern: I painted the car blue.

Answer: A. SVOC

63. Identify the sentence pattern: In 1998, Frank moved to London.

Answer: A. ASVA

64. They rejoined the group as Sarah passed out ____ and coffee.

Answer: C. dessert

65. Who was chirping at the edge of the woods?

Answer: D. Birds

66. The setting of the play 'The Dying Detective' is in

Answer: D. Sherlock Holmes' bedroom

67. Sir Arthur Ignatius Conan Doyle was a ____ writer.

Answer: C. British
