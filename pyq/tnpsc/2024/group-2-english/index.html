TNPSC 2024 Group 2 Prelims · General English: Questions with Answers

All 99 questions from the TNPSC 2024 Group 2 Prelims · General English paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

99 questionsUnofficial answers
⚠ Unofficial · 99/100

The official key for this paper was published for a different booklet version, so these answers were worked out by MindGains. Questions we could not read clearly from the scan, or could not answer with certainty, are left out.

101. Dr. Salim Ali was introduced to the serious study of birds by

Answer: A. W.S. Millard

102. "Elephant Rides are for the Royals. Forget this Dream" – Who said this to Rani of Jhansi?

Answer: B. Moropant Tambe

103. What was the name of the system that Nelson Mandela fought against?

Answer: B. Apartheid

104. Abraham Lincoln proclaimed the abolition of slavery on

Answer: B. January 1, 1863

105. "And the wheel's kick and the wind's song and the white sail's shaking" – Pick out the figure of speech given in the above poetic line.

Answer: C. Alliteration

106. "I must go down to the seas again, to the vagrant gypsy life" – Gypsy life means ______.

Answer: B. A Wandering Man's Life

107. "But we ants never borrow; We ants never lend" – Why do you think ants neither borrow nor lend?

Answer: C. Ants are wise and hard working

108. Choose the correct tense form: If she works hard, she ______ a scholarship.

Answer: C. will get

109. Select the correct tense: If you ______ hard you would have won the relay match.

Answer: D. had worked

110. Select the correct passive form: She had already sent the parcel.

Answer: B. The parcel had already been sent by her

111. Choose the correct active voice: The rules should not be violated.

Answer: D. Nobody should violate the rules.

112. Which of the following is suitable to complete the meaning? I stopped ______ to him.

Answer: B. talking

113. "The woods are lovely, dark and deep / But I have promises to keep" – Who wrote the above lines?

Answer: A. Robert Frost

114. "But the gladdest sort of people, when the busy day is done, / Are the brothers and the sisters who together share their fun." – Who wrote the above poem?

Answer: B. Edgar Albert Guest

115. The word 'Plaintively' means

Answer: B. Sadly

116. Prospero's daughter's name was

Answer: D. Miranda

118. Choose the correct synonym for the underlined word: Fill all the bowls, tubs, pails and pitchers with fresh water.

Answer: B. jugs

119. Choose the correct synonym by matching Column A and B: (a) Affluent (b) Dilate (c) Smacks (d) Summit — 1. Widen 2. Spire 3. Wealthy 4. Drive

Answer: C. 3 1 4 2

120. "Great Leaders have something in them which inspires a whole people and makes them do great deeds". Who quotes this?

Answer: C. Jawaharlal Nehru

121. When was Subash Chandra Bose born?

Answer: A. January 23, 1897

122. Helen Keller's Autobiography was named ______.

Answer: B. The Story of my Life

123. How did Kalpana Chawla die?

Answer: A. Space Shuttle Disaster

124. "But of late, my computer has been behaving badly too, / It is so absent-minded, that I don't know what to do". Mention the figure of speech used in the above lines.

Answer: B. Personification

125. "But I know no better spectacle / Than a comet in full flight." What do you mean by 'spectacle' here?

Answer: A. unusual event that attracts attention

126. "It's written in victory and defeat / And every trial a man may meet." What does 'it' refer to?

Answer: C. Courage

127. Add a prefix to the word 'Natural' to complete the sentence: His job is to investigate ______ natural phenomena.

Answer: C. super

128. Fill in the blank with a suitable article: India is ______ largest democracy in the world.

Answer: B. the

129. Identify the correct preposition: I will have finished this essay ______ Friday.

Answer: D. by

130. Choose the correct question tag: This is the bus that I have to travel, ______?

Answer: D. isn't it?

131. Select the suitable tag: He lives in Europe, ______?

Answer: D. doesn't he?

132. Passage (Raj and the pencil): Raj was upset as he had done ______ in his English test.

Answer: B. poorly

133. Passage (Raj and the pencil): His grandmother gave him a ______.

Answer: C. pencil

134. Passage (Raj and the pencil): Granny compared ______ with the pencil.

Answer: B. Raj

135. Passage (Raj and the pencil): Raj's pain of not doing well in his test was compared with ______ of pencils.

Answer: D. sharpening

136. Passage (Raj and the pencil): Raj understood that failures are stepping stones to

Answer: A. success

137. Choose the correct meaning of the phrase: Reconcile to

Answer: B. Accept half heartedly

138. Pick out the complex sentence from the given options.

Answer: B. One who does not love one's country is a wretched person

139. Find out what kind of sentence is given below: 'If he is at home, I shall see him'.

Answer: C. Complex

140. Combine the pair of sentences: I saw a Tiger. It was wounded.

Answer: B. I saw a wounded Tiger

141. Identify the correct Degree of Comparison: (1) Very few people in this town are as generous as Mr. Mohan. (2) I cannot do anything better for you than this.

Answer: B. (1) Positive (2) Comparative

142. What is the landform formed by silt called?

Answer: A. Alluvial

143. Which historical character inspired young Indira?

Answer: B. Joan of Arc

144. Why do we call Dr. Salim Ali the "Birdman of India"?

Answer: B. His immense contribution to the field of Ornithology

145. In whose biography do you find this statement? "I would sweep them out of existence if I had the power."

Answer: C. Mahatma Gandhi

146. "Courage isn't a brilliant dash, / A daring deed in a moment's flash; / It isn't an instantaneous thing / Born of despair with a sudden spring" – Write the rhyme scheme of the poem.

Answer: C. AABB

147. "Speak gently to the young, for they / Will have enough to bear – / Pass through this life as best they may, / 'T is full of anxious care!" – Write the rhyme scheme of the stanza.

Answer: A. ABAB

148. "And all I ask is a windy day with the white clouds flying, / And the flung spray and the blown spume, and the sea-gulls crying." The rhyming words in the above lines are

Answer: D. flying, crying

149. Choose the correct antonym for the word 'Concentrate'.

Answer: C. Distract

150. Match with correct antonyms: (a) brisk (b) desperate (c) plaintive (d) praise — 1. happy 2. criticize 3. hopeful 4. dull

Answer: B. 4 3 1 2

151. Select the correct prefix for 'Violet' from the options given:

Answer: A. ultra

152. Pick a suitable prefix for the word: Understand

Answer: C. mis

153. Match the poets with their poems: (a) Mary Botham Howitt (b) Caroline Ann Bowles (c) Robert Frost (d) Edgar Albert Guest — 1. The River 2. The Stick-together Families 3. The Spider and the Fly 4. Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening

Answer: A. 3 1 4 2

154. "For simple sheep; and such are Daffodils / With the green world they live in; and clear rills…" The above lines were written by

Answer: C. John Keats

155. "Come you back unto the fireside and be comrade with your kin" – This line is taken from the poem

Answer: C. The Stick-Together Families

156. "Still seeking what I sought when but a boy, / New friendship, high adventure and a crown" – Who wrote the above lines?

Answer: B. Henry Van Dyke

157. Select the grammatically correct sentence: (a) My son is fond of music (b) My son is fond to music (c) My son is fond in music (d) My son is fond with music

Answer: D. Only (a) is correct

158. Which of the following is a noun?

Answer: C. Sun

159. A ______ of birds were flying in the sky.

Answer: C. flock of

160. Identify which is the correct singular word: (a) Crises (b) Media

Answer: D. Both (a) and (b) are plural words

161. State which statement is correct: (1) Little Czar was a European brown bear cub. (2) Little Czar was an Indian brown bear cub. (3) Little Czar was an American brown bear cub. (4) Little Czar was a Germanian brown bear cub.

Answer: A. 1

162. Who brought up Sir Isaac Newton?

Answer: B. Grandmother

163. Beauty lies in the beholder's ______

Answer: B. Eyes

164. "My Reminiscences" was written by ______.

Answer: D. Rabindranath Tagore

165. Identify the figure of speech employed in: "The bleeding bark will heal".

Answer: A. Personification

166. "Lights flicker on and off / But fear takes over me." Identify the onomatopoeic word in the given stanza.

Answer: C. flicker

167. "We were taken from the ore-bed and the mine, / We were melted in the furnace and the pit – / We were cast and wrought and hammered to design, / We were cut and filed and tooled and gauged to fit". What is the poetic device used here?

Answer: D. Anaphora

168. Foreign + Exchange = ______. Find the blended word.

Answer: D. Forex

169. Which two words blend together to form the new word 'Camcorder'?

Answer: B. Camera + recorder

170. Which of the following is correct for 'Overcoat'?

Answer: D. Preposition + Noun

171. Identify the compound word that is Gerund + Noun:

Answer: C. Bleaching powder

172. Replace the underlined word with its American word: We should use the Bin for the garbage.

Answer: B. Trash Can

173. "You were the dad chosen for me" – The author's dad was chosen by ______.

Answer: C. God

174. "Then my computer caught a virus, and fell very sick, / So I had to call in a doctor, double quick." Why did the poet call the doctor?

Answer: A. as the computer fell very sick

175. "And all I ask is a merry yarn from a laughing fellow-rover, / And quiet sleep and a sweet dream when the long trick's over" – The above lines are in the poem

Answer: B. Sea Fever

176. Someday people will ______ of our great exploits.

Answer: C. write

177. Find out the error part: I found an one rupee coin (A) / in the playground (B) / while playing (C)

Answer: A. I found an one rupee coin

178. Choose the correct articles: "This has got to stop immediately", Justice Mathematics said in ______ stern voice, rapping ______ hammer for silence in ______ court.

Answer: C. a, the, the

179. Choose the correct preposition: Miranda hung ______ her father saying, "Why are you so ungentle"?

Answer: A. upon

180. Select the correct meaningful sentence from the following.

Answer: D. News never comes too late.

181. 'Practice hard and see what magic can transpire', was the advice given by ______ to Sachin Tendulkar.

Answer: A. Ramakant Achrekar

182. Tom used the pain killer to

Answer: B. mend the crack on the sitting room floor

183. The Adventures of Tom Sawyer is a ______.

Answer: B. Novel

184. "And I sunned it with smiles" – Identify the alliteration in the given line.

Answer: C. Sunned – smile

185. "To win, run as fast as the wind" – Identify the figure of speech.

Answer: C. Simile

186. Which of the given options is a Metaphor?

Answer: C. His words are pearls of wisdom

187. "Autumn is English / in red, yellow and brown / Autumn is Indian / Whenever leaves fall down" – Who wrote the above poem?

Answer: A. Nisha Dyrene

188. "And one day, my naughty computer actually gobbled a worm, / And behaved so erratically that it made me squirm". The poet was made to squirm because of the ______ behaviour of the computer.

Answer: C. erratic

189. "Every soul that touches yours / Be it the slightest contact" – Who wrote the above lines?

Answer: C. George Eliot

190. "She puts her life at Stake" – Give the meaning of the word 'Stake'.

Answer: D. risk

191. "Strong is she in her faith and beliefs. 'Persistence is the key to everything', says she." What does the word 'Persistence' mean?

Answer: C. determination

192. "It's helping your fellowman to score / When his chances hopeless seem;" The word 'it' in the above lines refers to

Answer: C. team work

193. Match the non-finite verbs: (a) Walking is a healthy habit (b) I like to walk early in the morning (c) Having walked a long distance I felt tired (d) These are my walking shoes — 1. Infinitive 2. Present Participle 3. Gerund 4. Past Participle

Answer: B. 3 1 4 2

194. Identify the sentence pattern: I painted the car blue.

Answer: A. SVOC

195. Identify the sentence pattern: In 1998, Frank moved to London.

Answer: A. ASVA

196. They rejoined the group as Sarah passed out ______ and coffee.

Answer: C. dessert

197. Who was chirping at the edge of the woods?

Answer: D. Birds

198. On the way to his home, Peter stopped to pick ______ or to listen to the ______.

Answer: B. Blue flowers, the rabbits' soft tread

199. The setting of the play 'The Dying Detective' is in

Answer: D. Sherlock Holmes' bedroom

200. Sir Arthur Ignatius Conan Doyle was a ______ writer.

Answer: C. British
