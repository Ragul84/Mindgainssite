TNPSC · 2024

TNPSC 2024 Group 2 Prelims · General Studies: Questions with Answers

All 100 questions from the TNPSC 2024 Group 2 Prelims · General Studies paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

100 questionsOfficial answer key

1. Which one of the following pairs is wrongly matched? Revolt - Area: (I) Ramosis - Poona, (II) Kuka - Punjab, (III) Santhals - Orissa, (IV) Kittur - Malabar

Answer: B. (III) and (IV) only

2. "Problem of the Rupee" is the thesis submitted by

Answer: A. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

3. Who wrote a series of articles entitled 'New Lamps for Old'?

Answer: D. Aurobindo Ghosh

4. Match the following correct Institutions with its location: (a) NIFPHATT - 1. Bangalore, (b) CICEF - 2. Kochi, (c) NFDB - 3. Chennai, (d) CAA - 4. Hyderabad

Answer: B. 2 1 4 3

5. Assertion [A]: In India, the traditional small scale industries have greater employment potential than the modern small scale industries. Reason [R]: The value of capital per worker in traditional small scale industries is smaller than the value of capital per worker in the modern small scale industries.

Answer: A. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

6. Which of the following statement is correct? (a) Equitable development is the main target of Eleventh Five Year Plan (XI FYP). (b) Rapid, sustainable and more inclusive growth are main targets of Eleventh Five Year Plan.

Answer: B. (b) alone is correct

7. Which among the following is correct regarding the target of literacy in Tenth Plan?

Answer: A. to increase of literacy to 75% within the plan period

8. Consider the following statements and identify the correct answer:

Answer: D. Both are not eligible for reappointment

9. Find out the incorrect statement about the Sarkaria Commission: (i) The Commission was appointed on 1983, (ii) The Commission made 247 recommendations, (iii) The Commission recommended to scrap the Article 356, (iv) The Commission recommended that the residuary powers of taxation should be given to the states

Answer: A. (iii) and (iv) only

10. Choose the correct pair: Name of the President - Elected Year: (1) Dr. S. Radha Krishnan - 1961, (2) Dr. Zakir Hussain - 1966, (3) Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed - 1974, (4) N. Sanjeeva Reddy - 1977

Answer: C. (3) and (4) are correct

11. Find out the incorrectly matched. (i) Article 18 - Abolition of Titles, (ii) Article 21 - Protection of life and personal liberty, (iii) Article 23 - Protection against arrest, (iv) Article 20 - Right to free movement

Answer: A. (iii) and (iv) are incorrectly matched

12. Which of the Vedic Gods is described as the "Destroyer of cities"?

Answer: B. Indra

13. Which one of the following is not correct about Harappan culture?

Answer: A. All Harappan sites are urban character

14. The Gupta emperors before Chandragupta-II issued only ______ coins.

Answer: A. Gold

15. Haque-i-Sharb (or) Hasil-i-Sharb means

Answer: B. Irrigation Tax

16. Which of the following is correctly paired? (i) Hematite Ore - Oxide of iron, (ii) Magnetite Ore - Black ore, (iii) Limonite - Dolomite, (iv) Siderite - Iron Carbonate

Answer: C. (i), (ii) and (iv) only

17. Densification and compaction are unit operations that are used to increase the density of recovered materials to reduce transportation costs and simplify storage. This method is used in

Answer: B. Transportation and Transfer station storage of MSW

18. Recently in Dubai, cloud seeding technology is used to get artificial rain. The most commonly used seeding agents among the following are (1) Silver iodide, (2) Dry ice, (3) Sodium chloride, (4) Lead iodide, (5) Ammonium sulphate

Answer: A. (1), (2) and (3) only

19. 'Tamil Nadu Day' celebrated every year on

Answer: D. July 18

20. Which of the following is correctly paired? (1) 2014 - Target Olympic Podium Scheme, (2) 2023 - Mera Yuva Bharat (MY Bharat), (3) 1982 - Sports Authority of India (SAI), (4) 1984 - Dronacharya Award was instituted

Answer: B. (1) and (2) are correct

21. A current of 0.5 A passes through a lamp for two minutes, then the number of electrons pass through the lamp is

Answer: D. 3.8 x 10^20 electrons

22. ISRO prefer to launch Human space flight mission during 75th independence, the (crew member) explorer is named as

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

23. Select the chronological order of mentor - student chain in the establishment of scientific methods

Answer: C. Socrates - Plato - Aristotle - Alexander

24. A force of 1N acts on a body of mass 10 kg. As a result, the body covers 100 cm in 4 seconds moving along a straight line. Find the initial velocity of the body.

Answer: B. 5 cm/s

25. Green light shines on a rose flower. Then, the colour of its petals seems to be

Answer: C. Black

26. The Neyveli Lignite Corporation was established by the Government of India in the year

Answer: A. 1956

27. Arrange the following universities in chronological order based on the year of establishment : (1) Manonmaniam Sundaranar University (2) Bharathidasan University (3) Thiruvalluvar University (4) Periyar University

Answer: B. (2), (1), (4), (3)

28. Assertion [A] : Backward class commission under chairmanship of B.P. Mandal was appointed by the President in exercise of the powers confessed by Article 240. Reason [R] : The commission has developed eleven indicators to determine backwardness.

Answer: C. [A] is false, [R] is true

29. Match the following : (a) Non-Brahmin Movement (b) Self-Respect Movement (c) Shuddhi Movement (d) Sathya Shodhak Samaj Movement - 1. Jyothi Rao Phule 2. E.V. Ramasamy 3. Dayananda Saraswathi 4. Sri Sahu Maharaj

Answer: D. (a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4

30. Arrange the following events in chronological order about E.V.R. (1) He became the President of Justice Party (2) He joined Congress (3) He became President of Tamil Nadu Congress Committee (4) He organised Vaikom Satyagraha

Answer: C. (2), (3), (4), (1)

31. "Vaanalum Selvamum Mannarasum Yaan Venden" [I don't want rule the sky, wealth and monarch] Who said these words?

Answer: D. Kulasekharar

32. Match correctly the Newspapers associated with its Founders : (a) Richard Johnston (b) G.P. Pillai (c) Charles Lawson and Henry Cornish (d) G. Subramania Iyer - 1. Madras Courier 2. Madras Mail 3. Madras Standard 4. The Hindu

Answer: A. (a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 4

33. "Number four and two are strengthening your words" Number 'two' refers to what?

Answer: B. Thirukkural

34. According to Valluvar, which will be considered with utmost gratitude?

Answer: B. As Millet as Benefit

35. "Kallaarai Kaanungal Kalvi Nalga Kasadarikku thookkumaram angae undaam" Who wrote these lines?

Answer: A. Bharathidasan

36. The Pakistan resolution was passed by the All India Muslim League at its Lahore Session on

Answer: C. March 23rd, 1940

37. If ABCDE = P1Q2R3S4T5 then which one of the following is equal to Q2BFV7A.

Answer: D. BQ2U6GP1

38. Gopi sold a laptop at 12% gain. If it had been sold for Rs. 1,200 more, the gain would have been 20%. Find the cost price of the laptop.

Answer: A. Rs. 15,000

39. What is 20% of 30% in 500?

Answer: A. 30

40. Find the missing number in the series. 4, 18, ?, 100, 180, 294, 448

Answer: A. 48

41. The sum of first n natural numbers is 253, then find the value of n.

Answer: A. 22

42. If fox=cat then xerox may be equal to

Answer: D. terat

43. Three dice thrown at once. Find the probability of getting same numbers in all dice

Answer: C. 1/36

44. The curved surface area of a right circular cylinder of height 7 cm is 66 cm^2. Find the diameter of the cylinder.

Answer: D. 3 cm

45. The value of a motor cycle 2 years ago was Rs. 80,000. It depreciates at the rate of 4% p.a. in first year. In second year it increases by 4% p.a. Find the present value.

Answer: D. Rs. 79,872

46. There are four cell phones in a house. At 5 a.m, all the four phones will ring together. Thereafter, the first one rings every 20 mins, the second one rings every 30 mins, the third one rings every 40 mins and the fourth one rings every 50 mins. At what time, will the four cell phones ring together again?

Answer: C. 3 P.M.

47. If HCF of x^3-8y^3 and x^2-k^2 y^2 is x+ky then value of k

Answer: C. -2

48. In how many months the principal of Rs. 2,000 will double at 8% per annum in simple interest?

Answer: B. 150 months

49. The L.C.M. of 3, 2.7 and 0.09 is

Answer: D. 27

50. Which one of the following lies between 7^(1/3) and 5^(1/4)?

Answer: B. 3^(1/2)

51. Arrange the following Books of Jawaharlal Nehru in Chronological order considering their year of publication. (1) An Autobiography : Toward Freedom (2) Glimpses of world History (3) The Discovery of India (4) Before Freedom

Answer: B. (2), (1), (3), (4)

52. Who among the following had started 'Mitra Mela' Association?

Answer: B. Vinayak Damodar Savarkar

53. Who founded the All-India Muslim League in 1906?

Answer: B. Khwaja Salimullah

54. Assertion [A] : Swami Vivekananda founded Ramakrishna Mission to popularise the teachings of his master. Reason [R] : Ramakrishna Mission was a monastic order and devoted itself to the spiritual development of Man.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

55. Arrange the following studies of Poverty in India in chronological order: (1) Ojha's study (2) Bardhan's study (3) Minhas study (4) Dandekar and Rath study

Answer: B. (1), (4), (3), (2)

56. Inorder to double the Farmer's income through digital technologies, action should be taken from (i) Technology generation and dissemination, policies and reforms and development initiatives (ii) Policies and Reforms (iii) Reforms and Development (iv) Artificial Intelligence

Answer: A. (i) only

57. Match the following: (a) Tax Revenue (b) Non-tax revenue (c) Capital receipts -- 1. Interest receipts 2. Net market borrowings 3. Taxes on Income and Expenditure

Answer: A. (a) 3, (b) 1, (c) 2

58. Match the following: Enacted Year: (a) 1963 (b) 1991 (c) 1971 (d) 1951 -- Name of the Act: 1. Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act 2. Representation of People Act 3. Government of Union Territory Act 4. Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi Act

Answer: A. (a) 3, (b) 4, (c) 1, (d) 2

59. Assertion [A] : Governor in the Indian Federal System is assigned dual role, as the constitutional Head of the State as well as the representative of the Centre. Reason [R] : The Office of Governor is against the Federal structure.

Answer: A. [A] is true, but [R] is false

60. The framers of the Constitution incorporated the duties of the State in the constitution in the form of the

Answer: C. Directive principle of state policy

61. Which of the following bodies doesn't/don't find mention in the Constitution? (1) National Development Council (2) NITI Aayog (3) Election Commission (4) Finance Commission

Answer: A. (1) and (2) only

62. Who among the following is the only woman historian to have written a historical account of Mughal period?

Answer: A. Gulbadan Begum

63. Which one of the following is false with regard to the reign of Ahmad Shah?

Answer: D. He annexed the kingdom of Gujarat to his Empire

64. Who called India as "Ethnological Museum"?

Answer: D. V.A. Smith

65. Which of the following Harappan sites are not in India?

Answer: A. Mohenjodara and Harappa

66. Around 25 districts in the east coast; 10 in Tamil Nadu, 7 in Andhra Pradesh, 6 in Odisha and 2 in West Bengal - are vulnerable to cyclonic storms. Among these, which area accounts to 70 percent of the cyclones of severe categories?

Answer: D. Sundarbans Area

67. A tribe of India is (1) Connected with Eklavya in the epic Mahabharatha (2) Lives in Aravallis, Vindhyan and Satpura Ranges (3) Hutment on a small Hillock (4) Maize is their staple food. Which tribal group of the following suits for the above conditions?

Answer: C. Bhils

68. The northern upland of the Karnataka Plateau is known as

Answer: A. Malnad

69. Which Ministry launched the Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP) and 'Chakshu' facility on Sanchar Saathi portal?

Answer: C. Ministry of Communications and Information

70. Match the following: Part I: (a) India (b) Japan (c) United States (d) China -- Part II: 1. Bald Eagle 2. Dolphin 3. Peony 4. Cherry blossom 5. Royal Bengal Tiger

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

71. Who had been given the "Shilp Awards" by the Ministry of Textiles and Industry?

Answer: D. to legendary crafts person

72. Some parts of the moon never see sunlight. Because the axis of the moon is ____ to the direction of the Sun's light.

Answer: A. Perpendicular

73. Match the following: (a) CO2 (b) NOx (c) Lead (Pb) (d) Hydrocarbons (HC) | 1. Ground water pollution and particulate in air 2. Global warming 3. Acid rain 4. An ingredient for the formation of photochemical smog

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: A. (a)-2 (b)-3 (c)-1 (d)-4 / C. (a)-3 (b)-2 (c)-1 (d)-4

74. Assertion [A] : 2 - 4 - D is used as herbicides. Reason [R] : 2 - 4 - D induces production of fruits without fertilization.

Answer: D. [A] is true, [R] is not the correct explanation

75. Objectivity, Predictability and Generality are the features of ____

Answer: A. Scientific method

76. Identify the correct expansion/meaning of "CEDAW"

Answer: C. Convention on the elimination of all forms of discrimination against women

77. Which one of the following is a one-stop source for all the geospatial data for the state of Tamil Nadu?

Answer: B. Tamil Nadu Geographical Information System (TNGIS)

78. The United Nation has declared 1987 as

Answer: B. International year of Shelter for the homeless

79. In which year Flipkart signed a memorandum of understanding with the Tamil Nadu MSME Trade and Investment Promotion Bureau?

Answer: A. 2021

80. Match correctly the following foundations with their corresponding names: (a) South Indian Liberal Federation (b) Kudiyarasu Journal (c) Emergence of Labour Oragnisations (d) Swatantra Party; 1. A. Singaravelar 2. C. Rajagopalachari 3. Thanthai Periyar 4. C. Natesan

Answer: C. (a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 2

81. According to 2011 census, the density of population in Tamil Nadu is

Answer: B. 555 per square km

82. Tamil women who were competent in literature, music and drama were called as

Answer: A. Mudukkuravai

83. Choose the right matches among the following. (1) Thiru.V.Ka - Desabakthan (2) Subramania Bharathiyar - Hindu (3) Annie Besant - New India (4) Kasthuri Ranga Ayyangar - India

Answer: A. (1) and (3) only

84. __________ original name was Kumarasamy Nayak.

Answer: B. Oomaithurai

85. According to Valluvar, what is the weapon to destroy the arrogance of foes?

Answer: D. Wealth

86. "__________ that man desire it is plain whom others wealth Delights not, feeling envious pain". Whom does Thiruvalluvar refers to in the given above Thirukkural?

Answer: A. Nor wealth nor virtue does that man

87. What kind of Dhoti worn by maternal uncle while singing lullaby you identified?

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

88. "Utruzhi uthaviyum uruporul koduthum" - What field is eulogised in this Purananootru Paadal?

Answer: A. Education (Kalvi)

89. Choose the Sangam period in which the poet Nakkirar was considered the most predominant poet: (a) First Sangam (b) Third Sangam (c) Second Sangam (d) Fourth Sangam

Answer: B. (b) only

90. One side of a rectangular field is 15 m and one of its diagonals is 17 m. Find the area of the field.

Answer: D. 120 m^2

91. If 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3 are the faces of a die, 2 such dice are rolled. In how many cases 2nd die face is bigger than 1st die.

Answer: D. 11

92. If Rs. 10,890 is divided among A and B groups in the ratio 4:5 then the group B's share is

Answer: C. Rs. 6,050

93. If cat=abc, dog=def then which one of the following may be equal to tan/cot

Answer: D. cbn/acc

94. E & G B D M 4 N K H 2 A C Z S V 3 F 1 J L O Q 5 P R. What will come in place of question mark in the following sequence? GDR BMP D45 ?

Answer: A. MNQ

95. A can finish a job in 6 days whereas B finishes it in 12 days. The time taken to complete the job working together is __________?

Answer: B. 4 days

96. A swimming bath is 24 m long and 15 m broad. When a number of men dive into the bath, the height of water rises by 1 cm. If the average volume of water displaced by each man be 0.1 m^3, how many men are there in the bath?

Answer: B. 36

97. Find the simple interest on Rs. 68,000, at 16 2/3% per annum for 9 months.

Answer: B. Rs. 8,500

98. If A:B:C = 2:3:4 then A/B : B/C : C/A is equal to

Answer: C. 8:9:24
