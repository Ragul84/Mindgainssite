Answer: D. 4 hours
Let's assume the distance between the two stations is D. The time taken by the first train to cover the distance D is D/50. The time taken by the second train to cover the distance D is D/70. When the two trains meet, the sum of the distances covered by each train will be equal to the distance between the two stations. Therefore, D/50 + D/70 = D, which gives 7D + 5D = 12D, and D = 60. Therefore, the time taken by the two trains to meet is D/50 = 60/50 = 6/5 = 1.2 hours.

32. A pump can fill a tank in 2 hours. However, due to a leak, it takes 3 hours to fill the tank. How long will it take to fill the tank if the leak is plugged?

Answer: B. 2 hours
Let's assume the rate at which the pump fills the tank is 1/2 per hour and the leak drains the tank at a rate of x per hour. When the leak is plugged, the combined rate of filling the tank is 1/2. We can set up the equation: 1/2 - x = 1/6. Solving for x, we get x = 1/12. When the leak is plugged, the combined rate of filling the tank is 1/2 + 1/12 = 1/3. Therefore, it will take 3 hours to fill the tank.

74. A bus travels from City A to City B at a speed of 40 km/h and returns from City B to City A at a speed of 60 km/h. What is the average speed of the bus for the entire trip?

Answer: C. 48 km/h
The total distance traveled by the bus is the sum of the distances between City A and City B and between City B and City A. Let's call this distance D. The time taken by the bus to travel from City A to City B is D/40 and the time taken by the bus to travel from City B to City A is D/60. The total time taken by the bus is D/40 + D/60 = (3D + 2D)/120 = 5D/120 = D/24. Therefore, the average speed of the bus for the entire trip is D/(D/24) = 24 km/h.
