TNPSC 2021 Previous Year Questions with Answers

172 questions from the TNPSC 2021 papers, grouped by paper. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

172 questionsGroup 1 Prelims · General Studies (3 Jan 2021) · official TNPSC key

Group 1 Prelims · General Studies (3 Jan 2021) · official TNPSC key

172 questions

1. Which objectives are true of the Tamil Nadu Social Welfare Board? I. Upliftment of people below the poverty line through voluntary institutions II. Promote setting up of NGO social welfare organisations III. Technical and financial assistance to voluntary institutions for better services IV. Strive for equality between men and women

Answer: D. I, II and III

2. Choose the correct statements on 'black money': (a) assets not reported to the government when generated (b) estimated black money in India for 1983-84 was 95% of GDP (c) tax havens are big countries imposing high tax on foreigners who settle there

Answer: A. (a) only

3. Statement 1: Census 2011 showed around 50 lakh fewer children aged 0-6 than Census 2001. Statement 2: Population growth between 2001 and 2011 was lower than between 1991 and 2001.

Answer: A. S1 and S2 are correct but S2 does not explain S1

4. Which global trends led to the RBI 'sell-buy' swap of USD/INR? (a) Financial markets worldwide facing selling pressure due to Covid-19 (b) Slump in international crude prices (c) Emerging market currencies under upward pressure

Answer: C. (a) and (b) only

5. Name the first Backward Classes Commission, set up in 1953.

Answer: B. Kalelkar Commission

6. Which statements about Arignar Anna are correct? (a) He was sub-editor of 'Justice' (b) He became editor of 'Viduthalai' (c) He was associated with the Tamil weekly 'Kudi Arasu'

Answer: B. (a) and (b)

7. At which district conference in 1944 was the Justice Party converted into the Dravidar Kazhagam?

Answer: A. Salem

8. Which is NOT an objective of the 'Cradle Baby Scheme'?

Answer: A. To ensure adoption of family planning

9. Which statements are true of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961? I. Transfer substantive provisions under the IPC II. Dowry givers should be excluded from punishment III. Appointment of Dowry Prohibition Officers IV. Marriage expenses limited to 20% of the annual income of the girl's parents

Answer: C. I and III only

10. The Samarasa Suddha Sanmarga Sangam was founded by

Answer: D. Ramalinga Adigal

11. Which facts relate to defining GDP? (a) It is not for a specific period of time (b) It measures only goods and not services (c) It is a non-monetary measure

Answer: C. (a), (b), (c) are wrong

12. Which district occupies the last rank in the Human Development Index in Tamil Nadu?

Answer: D. Ariyalur

13. Match author and book: (a) Vannadasan (b) Inquilab (c) S. Ramakrishnan (d) Cho. Dharman - 1. Sancharam 2. Sool 3. Kaandal Naatkal 4. Oru Siru Isai

Answer: C. 4 3 1 2

14. The Child Development Index takes into account I. Education II. Income III. Health IV. Nutritional status

Answer: B. I, III and IV

15. Which article of the Constitution abolishes untouchability?

Answer: B. Article 17

16. Which ideology threatens Indian unity?

Answer: A. Communalism

17. This place, 12 km south-east of Madurai, proves an urban civilisation existed on the Vaigai in the Sangam era; R. Balakrishnan points to its similarities with the Indus civilisation. Which place is it?

Answer: C. Keeladi

18. Which book explains the four noble truths of Buddhism?

Answer: B. Petakopadesa

19. Who first brought the Christian faith to South India?

Answer: D. St. Thomas

20. This philosophy was founded in the 5th century BC by Makkhali Gosala, rivalled Vedic religion, peaked under Bindusara and survived in Karnataka and Tamil Nadu till the 14th century CE. Which is it?

Answer: B. Ajivika

21. How was the coronation of the crown prince described under Vijayanagar rule?

Answer: D. Yuvaraja Pattabhishekam

22. A simple pendulum with bob of mass M has time period T. If the bob is replaced with one of mass 2M (massless string), the time period is

Answer: C. T

23. Alamgir Nama was written by

Answer: B. Muhammad Kazim

24. Arrange in chronological order: 1. Death of Humayun 2. Second Battle of Panipat 3. Building of Fatehpur Sikri 4. Proclamation of Din-i-Ilahi

Answer: B. 1-2-3-4

25. Which land distribution system was adopted by the Delhi Sultans?

Answer: A. Iqtadari

26. Match: (a) Mahabaladhikrita (b) Mahashvapati (c) Mahapilupati (d) Mahadandanayaka - 1. Commander of cavalry 2. Commander-in-chief 3. Chief justice 4. Commander of elephant force

Answer: B. 2 1 4 3

27. Who was Director General of the Archaeological Survey of India at the time of the Indus Valley excavations?

Answer: B. Sir John Marshall

28. More than 500 seals discovered in the Indus Valley civilisation are all made of

Answer: C. Terracotta

29. The source of dendrothermal energy is

Answer: A. Biomass

30. Which is a consequence of climate-change-related warming of rivers?

Answer: B. Changes in fish migration patterns

31. The major cause of ozone depletion is

Answer: A. Aerosols

32. The pollutant that impairs plant metabolism is

Answer: C. Sulphur dioxide

33. The Chairperson of the National Disaster Management Authority is

Answer: A. Prime Minister

34. The most important scheduled tribe of Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand is

Answer: D. Tharus

35. The most populous state of India is

Answer: C. Uttar Pradesh

36. The total number of railway zones in India is

Answer: D. Seventeen

37. Identify the rivers in north-to-south sequence.

Answer: B. Mahanadi - Godavari - Krishna - Kaveri

38. A supercomputer that lets researchers model energy technologies using AI is

Answer: A. Joule 2.0

39. Which facts about the Covid-19 virus are wrong? (a) Infected people without symptoms do not spread it (b) Reinfection is not possible (c) Anosmia (loss of smell) is a symptom (d) Babies do not get infected

Answer: B. (a), (b) and (d)

40. "The one who gave false judgement... a woman who tore off her breast to burn the city" - the Kannagi story is hinted at in which Sangam text?

Answer: B. Natrinai

41. Consider: (1) India introduced family planning in the 1950s (2) It adopted a neo-Marxian approach to birth control (3) The end of the National Emergency ruled out compulsion in family planning (4) Planning and implementation were by the central government

Answer: B. 1 and 3 only correct

42. The Kalpana Chawla Award for Courage and Daring Enterprise in 2020 was given to

Answer: D. Tmt. Senthamil Selvi, Tmt. Muthammal and Tmt. Ananthavalli

43. Which statements about political parties in India are correct? (a) They function within a federal institutional framework and are bound by electoral laws (b) Strong institutionalised parties are vital for healthy democracies

Answer: C. Both (a) and (b)

44. Which book-author pair is wrongly matched?

Answer: B. My Country My Life - Dalai Lama

45. Which of the following are not in Rajasthan? (a) Dilwara Temples (b) Mehrangarh Fort (c) Bara Imambara (d) Purana Qila

Answer: B. (c) and (d)

46. Where was the 3rd Global Ministerial Conference on Road Safety (WHO and World Bank) held in 2020?

Answer: A. Stockholm

47. Kidney stones form by precipitation of I. Cystine II. Oxalate III. Uric acid IV. Sodium

Answer: B. I, II and III

48. The colour of the skin of ripe apple, tomato and grape is due to

Answer: C. Anthocyanin

49. Single-celled eukaryotes are included in

Answer: C. Protista

50. In Haber's process for the manufacture of ammonia

Answer: A. Finely divided iron is the catalyst

51. What are isotones?

Answer: B. Atoms with the same neutrons but different protons

52. Silicon becomes a better conductor as temperature rises, but it is permanently damaged at about

Answer: B. 150°C

53. Common table salt is a

Answer: A. Compound

54. Which statement is true about COVID-19? (a) Adding pepper to soup prevents it (b) It spreads through houseflies (c) 5G networks spread it (d) Sunlight does not protect you from it

Answer: D. (d) only

55. With whom do we associate: 'Be educated, be organised and be agitated', 'I like the religion that teaches liberty, equality and fraternity', 'Life should be great rather than long'?

Answer: A. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

56. Name the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu during the 1965 anti-Hindi agitation.

Answer: C. Bhaktavatsalam

57. At which Self-Respect conference did Periyar pass resolutions for women's welfare?

Answer: D. Chengalpattu

58. Who presided over the first Madras Provincial Self-Respect Conference?

Answer: A. W.P.A. Soundara Pandian

59. Match: (a) Swarajya (b) Justice (c) Dravidan (d) Navasakthi - 1. T.M. Nair 2. T. Prakasam 3. Thiru. Vi. Ka 4. Bakthavachalam

Answer: C. 2 1 4 3

60. In 1916 the 'South Indian People's Association' was renamed as

Answer: C. Justice Party

61. Which political association became known as the Justice Party?

Answer: C. South Indian Liberal Federation

62. Who first introduced cartoons in a Tamil magazine?

Answer: C. C. Subramania Bharathi

63. Which idea does not fall within Periyar's definition of civilisation?

Answer: A. Accepting every religion

64. Name the woman leader who took part in the Neill Statue Satyagraha in Tamil Nadu in 1927.

Answer: B. Padmasani Ammal

65. Which statements about Chenbagaraman Pillai are correct? 1. He hailed from Kanyakumari district of Travancore 2. He started the Bharatha Matha Valibar Sangam 3. He married Lakshmibai in 1931 4. He joined the German side against the English in the world war

Answer: D. 1, 2, 3, 4 correct

66. Which woman freedom fighter took part in the Madras salt satyagraha?

Answer: C. Durgabai

67. Which factors caused the South Indian Rebellion? (i) Company reforms in the palayams (ii) Economic exploitation and heavy taxation by the Company (iii) The famine of 1798 (iv) Frequent wars of the Company

Answer: D. (i), (ii), (iii), (iv)

68. "____, ____, ____ - these three for aye to rulers of the land belong." In this Kural, 'these three' means

Answer: C. Sleepless promptitude, knowledge, decision

69. Whom does Valluvar call a fool though he is learned?

Answer: B. One who does not get along with the world

70. "These four a light of dreaded kings reveal" - the four virtues of a king according to Valluvar are

Answer: C. Beneficence, benevolence, rectitude and care for his people

71. What does the Kural "Vanja manaththaan padiruozhukkam boothangal ainthum agaththe nagum" reiterate?

Answer: C. False behaviour

72. Facts about hand sanitizers: (a) most are acid-based, not alcohol-based (b) they are flammable (c) they are effective against spores (d) wipe them off before they dry

Answer: B. (b) only

73. How does Valluvar guide us to overcome trouble?

Answer: C. Giving trouble to the trouble

74. "____ irandum thangaa viyanulagam vaanam vazhangaadhu enin" - which two will not happen if rain fails?

Answer: B. Gifts, austerities (Dhaanam, Thavam)

75. What are the symptoms of COVID-19? (a) Fever, dry cough, fatigue (b) Loss of taste (c) Sore throat (d) Breathing difficulty

Answer: D. (a), (b), (c) and (d)

76. Pick out the effect not related to earthquakes.

Answer: D. Depression zone formation

77. "Arampolum koormaiyar renu marampolvar" - whom does Valluvar call blocks of wood?

Answer: B. Those void of humane courtesy

78. According to Valluvar, friendship with the good is like

Answer: B. Having books that give perpetual delight

79. Thirukkural is said to have a balanced religious quality because 1. People of all religions claim it 2. Both believers and non-believers accept it 3. It is beyond caste, creed, religion and class 4. It writes about ethics and knowledge needed by humanity

Answer: D. All the above are right

80. Who is the author of the novel 'Manalveedu'?

Answer: A. Anuthama

81. Which book by Melanmai Ponnusamy won the Sahitya Akademi award?

Answer: A. Minsarapoo

82. Who wrote the poem opening "Anbu Sivam irandenbar arivilar"?

Answer: D. Thirumoolar

83. Which epic describes the significance of Tamil music?

Answer: A. Silappathikaram

84. Which Nayanmar made his heart the temple of Lord Siva?

Answer: B. Poosalar

85. What are the characteristics of a classical language? (a) An independent literary tradition (b) A large ancient body of oral literature only (c) A large ancient body of written literature

Answer: D. (a) and (c) only

86. Which quality is expressed in the Perumpanatruppadai verse "Mahamurai mahamurai nokki..."?

Answer: D. Hospitality (Virundhombal)

87. According to NITI Aayog's evaluation of MSP, which are correct? (a) MSP is declared before the sowing season (b) MSP gives guaranteed prices and saves farmers from price fluctuations (c) With free trade, protecting farmers from price swings is not important

Answer: B. (a), (b) only

88. According to Abhijit Banerjee, the only short-run solution to Covid-induced economic pain is

Answer: C. More money in the hands of the poor

89. Which reflect the unprecedented fall in Q1 (April-June) due to Covid? (a) ~50.3% contraction in construction (b) ~39.3% contraction in manufacturing (c) ~10% contraction in agriculture, forestry and fishing (d) ~5% contraction in trade, hotels, transport

Answer: A. (a) and (b) only

90. Choose the correct statements on recent bank mergers in India: (a) Large banks can borrow more from the market and help the economy revive (b) Mergers will increase operational efficiency

Answer: B. (b) only

91. Who said "Man is born free, self-respect is his birthright, social justice ensures social equality"?

Answer: A. Thanthai Periyar

92. Regarding 'Fraternity' in the Preamble: (a) It leaves no room for communalism (b) It leaves room for communalism (c) It leaves no room for casteism

Answer: A. (a) and (c)

93. Arrange in chronological order: 1. Champaran Satyagraha 2. Kheda Satyagraha 3. Ahmedabad struggle 4. Baisakhi day (Jallianwala)

Answer: A. 1-2-3-4

94. Name Abul Kalam Azad's biographical work written during his internment in 1916.

Answer: A. Tazkira

95. Who produced Tarjuman-ul-Quran, a translation of the Quran from Arabic to Urdu?

Answer: C. Maulana Abul Kalam Azad

96. Which statement about Maulana Abul Kalam Azad is incorrect? 1. He was born in Calcutta 2. He edited 'Lisan-ul-Sidq' 3. He published 'Al-Hilal' 4. He was Congress President in 1923 and 1940

Answer: D. 2, 3, 4 correct; 1 incorrect

97. Which day was celebrated by Tamil Swadeshi leaders for the release of Bipin Chandra Pal?

Answer: C. Swarajya Day

98. Which is correct about Bharathiyar? 1. He attended the Congress session at Kasi in 1905 2. In 1906 he started the Bala Bharata Sabha in Chennai 3. He attended the Surat Congress in 1907 with VOC 4. He wrote 'Vangame Vaazhiya'

Answer: B. 1, 2, 3, 4 correct

99. The editor of the Tamil paper Kudi Arasu was

Answer: A. Thanthai Periyar

100. Who threw a bomb with Bhagat Singh in the Central Assembly in Delhi?

Answer: D. Batukeshwar Dutt

101. Rabindranath Tagore renounced his knighthood in protest against

Answer: C. Jallianwala Bagh massacre

102. Which is correct about Subhas Chandra Bose? 1. He joined the freedom struggle under C.R. Das 2. In 1921 he organised a protest in Calcutta against the Prince of Wales's visit 3. He boycotted the Simon Commission 4. He served as Secretary and President of the Bengal Congress

Answer: B. 1, 2, 3, 4 correct

103. Regarding the Poona Sarvajanik Sabha: 1. M.G. Ranade was its brain 2. It awakened people to their political rights 3. It protested against the Vernacular Press Act, 1878 4. It published 'Amrita Bazar Patrika'

Answer: C. 1, 2 and 3

104. Which is incorrect about Gokhale? 1. He started the Servants of India Society in 1905 2. He went to South Africa and helped Gandhi against the colour bar 3. Gandhi accepted him as political guru 4. Gandhi called him the diamond of India, jewel of Maharashtra and prince of workers

Answer: B. 1, 2, 3 correct; only 4 incorrect

105. Who served as British ambassador to the Mughal court?

Answer: C. Thomas Roe

106. Who was called the 'King of the Two Worlds'?

Answer: C. Dhondaji Waugh

107. What is density of population?

Answer: A. Number of people per sq. km

108. Periyar passed a resolution on this at the 1929 Chengalpattu Self-Respect conference; Tamil Nadu made a law on it in 1989; the Supreme Court ruled on it on 11 August 2020. What is it?

Answer: B. Equal rights for women in family property

109. As per Su. Venkatesan's 'Vel Pari': (a) Paari was a man of nature who knew birds, insects and plants well (b) The story is the conflict between man's greed and nature

Answer: C. (a) and (b)

110. About the Tamil film 'Pariyerum Perumal': (a) It shows the ill effects of caste (b) It won the Filmfare award for best film (c) It was directed by Mari Selvaraj and produced by Neelam Productions

Answer: C. (a), (b) and (c)

111. Why has India not become self-reliant in edible oil? I. High cultivation cost II. Low productivity III. Lack of government support IV. Deficient domestic demand

Answer: A. II only

112. Which agricultural practice is eco-friendly?

Answer: A. Organic farming

113. "Every registered landholder is its proprietor." This system is called

Answer: A. Ryotwari system

114. Which sectors of the Indian economy were most affected by the Covid pandemic? (a) MSME (b) Aviation (c) Tourism and hospitality (d) Telecommunication

Answer: B. (a), (b) and (c)

115. Which states showed a declining trend in GST collection in 2019-20?

Answer: D. Maharashtra, West Bengal and Tamil Nadu

116. The objectives of fiscal policy are 1. Capital formation 2. Equitable distribution 3. Unemployment 4. Regional balance

Answer: B. 1, 2 and 4

117. Which is not a direct tax revenue of the central government?

Answer: C. Taxes on commodities and services

118. The first blueprint for Indian planning came from an engineer-administrator:

Answer: A. M. Visvesvaraya

119. Which statements about Treasury Bills are correct? (a) Issued by RBI (b) Issued by the Government of India (c) They pay no interest (d) They are long-term debt instruments

Answer: B. (b) and (c) only

120. Periyar wanted people to treat which as equal to their lives?

Answer: C. Self-respect

121. Consider: 1. The term of the Attorney General is not fixed by the Constitution 2. The remuneration of the Attorney General is not fixed by the Constitution

Answer: C. Both 1 and 2

122. A Chief Minister supported by Periyar challenged: "Show me a person who died because a lower-caste doctor injected wrongly, or a building that collapsed because a lower-caste engineer built it." Who?

Answer: B. Kamarajar

123. A High Court has jurisdiction to (1) punish for its contempt (2) advise on a legal question referred by the President (3) advise on a legal question referred by the Governor (4) issue writs to protect fundamental rights

Answer: A. (1) and (4) only

124. Which statement is not true? (a) In 1969 Tamil Nadu appointed the two-member Rajamannar Committee on Centre-State relations (b) It recommended abolishing All-India services (c) It recommended making the Finance Commission permanent

Answer: A. (a) is not true

125. Which schedule of the Constitution specifies the powers and responsibilities of Panchayats?

Answer: D. Eleventh

126. Consider: I. The Governor is appointed by the President II. He is the nominee of the Central Government III. The Draft Constitution provided for indirect election of the Governor IV. The Constituent Assembly opted for indirect appointment of the Governor

Answer: C. (I), (II) and (IV) only correct

127. Which institution has the final power to decide the composition of a state Legislative Council?

Answer: A. Parliament

128. The President has a veto over I. Ordinary bills II. Money bills III. Constitutional amendment bills IV. State legislation bills

Answer: A. (I) and (IV) only

129. I. Money bills can be introduced in Parliament without the President's approval II. The Finance Commission is appointed by the President

Answer: B. II only

130. The Council of Ministers holds office during the pleasure of the

Answer: B. President

131. Which articles broadly govern the relationship between the Prime Minister and the President?

Answer: A. Articles 74, 75 and 78

132. The Directive Principles were described as 'life-giving provisions of the Constitution' by

Answer: A. L.M. Singhvi

133. On Fundamental Duties: 1. Parliament cannot enforce them by legislation 2. They apply only to Indian citizens 3. All are either moral or civic duties 4. Paying taxes is a fundamental duty

Answer: C. 2 and 3 are correct

134. Which fundamental right was converted into a legal right?

Answer: D. Right to property

135. Identify the fundamental duty added in 2002 by the 86th Amendment.

Answer: D. Provide opportunities for education to children aged 6-14

136. Consider: I. The Constitution establishes a parliamentary form of government II. The Prime Minister is the head of government III. The President is the head of government IV. Real executive power rests with the President

Answer: A. (I) and (II) only true

137. By what name(s) is our country mentioned in the Constitution?

Answer: B. India and Bharat

138. Find k if 1³ + 2³ + 3³ + ... + k³ = 44100.

Answer: A. 20

139. If A = 2^65 and B = 2^64 + 2^63 + ... + 2^0, which is true?

Answer: B. A is larger than B by 1

140. Find the missing number: 3, 12, 27, 48, 75, 108, ___

Answer: A. 147

141. If HUMBLE is coded as EHLUBM, how is EDUCATION coded?

Answer: D. NEODIUTCA

142. The present ages of Arun and Suresh are 24 and 36. What was the ratio of Suresh's age to Arun's 8 years ago?

Answer: D. 3:2

143. A and B together can do a work in 16 days and A alone in 48 days. How long will B alone take?

Answer: B. 24 days

144. 210 men working 12 hours a day finish a job in 18 days. How many men working 14 hours a day finish it in 20 days?

Answer: B. 162

145. If 5 persons complete 5 projects in 5 days, 50 persons complete 50 projects in

Answer: A. 5 days

146. A mason represents the area of a rectangular floor by 2x³ + 16 and the length by 2(x + 2). The width is

Answer: C. (x² - 2x + 4)

147. A solid hemisphere has volume 29106 cm³. Another hemisphere with two-thirds of that volume is carved out. Find its radius.

Answer: C. 21 cm

148. The difference between simple and compound interest on a sum for 2 years at 2% p.a. is Rs. 4. Find the sum.

Answer: C. Rs. 10,000

149. The difference between CI and SI on Rs. 18,000 for two years is Rs. 405. The rate of interest is

Answer: B. 15%

150. The difference between CI and SI on Rs. 8,000 for 2 years (compounded annually) is Rs. 20. Find the rate.

Answer: A. 5%

151. Find the principal if the amount is Rs. 11,800 at 6% simple interest for 3 years.

Answer: C. Rs. 10,000

152. A machine depreciates at 10% per year. If its present value is Rs. 1,62,000, what is it worth after two years?

Answer: D. Rs. 1,31,220

153. Rs. 48,000 lent at simple interest amounts to Rs. 55,560 after 2 years 3 months. Find the rate per year.

Answer: A. 7%

154. Raghul's height to shadow ratio is 4:1. A tree's shadow is 15 feet. What is the tree's height?

Answer: C. 60 feet

155. A shopkeeper marks an article 15% above cost and then gives a 15% discount. He gets

Answer: B. Loss

156. A hemispherical tank of radius 1.75 m is full of water and is emptied by a pipe at 7 litres per second. How long will it take to empty?

Answer: A. 27 minutes

157. Find the LCM of (x⁴ − 1) and (x² − 2x + 1).

Answer: D. (x² + 1)(x + 1)(x − 1)²

158. Find the HCF of (x²y + xy²) and (x² + xy).

Answer: C. x(x + y)

159. According to Darwin, speciation is effected by

Answer: A. Small continuous heritable variation

160. AGRISNET was launched to provide I. Rainfall II. Fertilizer position III. Minimum support price IV. Sister departments of the farm community

Answer: B. I, II and IV

161. Which incident led Periyar to agitate for communal reservation?

Answer: D. The High Court and Supreme Court struck down communal reservation for professional courses

162. In NITI Aayog's SDG India Index 2019, Tamil Nadu ranked

Answer: C. Third

163. A water drop in space is spherical because, with reduced gravity, the forces on it are equal and so the drop

Answer: A. Attains a shape with the least surface area

164. The Athikadavu-Avinashi scheme is related to

Answer: D. Drinking water and groundwater recharge

165. Why is Chennai called the 'Detroit of Asia'?

Answer: B. Automobile industry

166. According to AISHE 2019, which state produced the most Ph.D.s in 2018?

Answer: A. Tamil Nadu

167. Assertion (A): Reducing the influence of the rich and powerful in government decisions is the challenge in 'expansion of democracy'. Reason (R): People's participation is meaningful only when the influence of the rich is reduced.

Answer: A. (A) is wrong but (R) is correct

168. Assertion (A): Access to primary health centres helps identify, care for and prevent communicable diseases in rural areas. Reason (R): Better use of rural health infrastructure is expected to yield results.

Answer: A. Both true and (R) explains (A)

169. Which was the first state to employ differently-abled persons under MGNREGS?

Answer: B. Tamil Nadu

170. Identify: (a) He was titled Rao Sahib, Rao Bahadur and Diwan Bahadur for service to the marginalised (b) His autobiography is 'Jeeviya Saritha Surukkam' (c) He founded the Adi Dravida Mahajana Sabha

Answer: B. Rettaimalai Srinivasan

171. In January 2019 the government approved a merger of three banks. Which was not part of it?

Answer: D. Lakshmi Vilas Bank

172. The 'Post-Tsunami Sustainable Livelihood Programme for the coastal communities of Tamil Nadu' is assisted by

Answer: B. International Fund for Agricultural Development
