TNPSC 2019 Previous Year Questions with Answers

171 questions from the TNPSC 2019 papers, grouped by paper. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

Group 1 Prelims · General Studies (3 Mar 2019) · official TNPSC key

171 questions

1. In vacuum all objects fall with

Answer: B. Same acceleration, and reach the ground at the same time

2. The reverberation time of a hall can be decreased by

Answer: B. Opening all windows and doors

3. Indian saltpetre is

Answer: C. Potassium nitrate

4. The main constituents of steel in India are

Answer: B. Fe and Cr

5. When did the Prime Minister inaugurate India's longest rail-cum-road bridge over the Brahmaputra at Bogibeel, Assam?

Answer: B. 25 December 2018

6. The end product of anaerobic respiration is

Answer: B. Ethyl alcohol

7. Golden rice is a genetically modified crop. The incorporated gene is meant for biosynthesis of

Answer: A. Vitamin A

8. The most widely used bioweapon is

Answer: C. Bacillus anthracis

9. Which plant has oil-repelling property?

Answer: D. Aloe vera

10. What happens in human thalassaemias? 1. Reduced α-globin synthesis 2. Reduced β-globin synthesis 3. Enhanced α and β-globin synthesis

Answer: C. 1 and 2 are correct

11. Which plant boosts the production of red blood cells?

Answer: C. Lemon grass

12. Consider: 1. 21 June - summer solstice, more radiation in the northern hemisphere 2. 23 September - autumn equinox, unequal radiation in both hemispheres 3. 22 December - winter solstice, more radiation in the southern hemisphere

Answer: B. 1 and 3

13. How many seismic zones are there in India?

Answer: B. 5

14. The Sabarmati river flows through

Answer: B. Gujarat and Rajasthan

15. About black soil: 1. It is generally clayey, deep and impermeable 2. It swells and becomes sticky when wet and shrinks when dry 3. It retains moisture for a long time

Answer: D. 1, 2 and 3

16. The creamy layer concept was first introduced in the case

Answer: B. Indra Sawhney vs Union of India

17. Agricultural crop insurance was introduced by the Government of India in

Answer: C. 1985

18. The Golden Quadrilateral distance between Chennai and Kolkata is

Answer: A. 1684 km

19. About the First Amendment: I. It was enacted in 1952 II. It was enacted before the first general elections III. It was enacted by the provisional Parliament

Answer: B. II and III are true

20. Which stage of man's progress is not properly matched?

Answer: C. Food production - Chalcolithic Age

21. The most outstanding Buddhist writer in Sanskrit was

Answer: B. Asvaghosa

22. Arrange in chronological order: 1. Battle of Chausa 2. Death of Babur 3. Battle of Kannauj 4. Death of Akbar

Answer: C. 2-1-3-4

23. Which are true of the commercialisation of agriculture under British rule? 1. Money lending and exploitation of poor peasants 2. Scarcity of food 3. Increased demand for cash crops 4. Commercial agriculture went into bankruptcy

Answer: D. 1, 2, 3, 4 are true

24. Name the famous dancer crowned 'Kathak King of India'.

Answer: B. Shambhu Maharaj

25. What was the primary reason E.V. Ramasamy left the Indian National Congress?

Answer: B. Crisis in the Cheranmahadevi gurukulam

26. What strengthened the National Movement?

Answer: D. Secular ideology

27. Parliament's financial control over the executive is exercised through the

Answer: A. Comptroller and Auditor General of India

28. The Comptroller and Auditor General is appointed by the President under Article

Answer: B. 148

29. Consider: 1. Articles 308 to 314 deal with All India Services 2. Article 308 applies exclusively to Jammu and Kashmir 3. Parliament enacted the All India Services Act in 1952 4. Article 312 empowers Parliament to create new All India Services

Answer: A. 1 and 4

30. Article ____ was inserted by the 73rd Constitutional Amendment.

Answer: C. 243B

31. Which is not among the powers of Parliament?

Answer: B. It cannot abolish a State Legislative Council

32. Jallikattu is protected under which article of the Constitution?

Answer: A. 29(1)

33. Who headed the Provincial Constitution Committee of the Constituent Assembly?

Answer: D. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

34. Who was not a member of the Drafting Committee?

Answer: C. Pattabhi Sitaramayya

35. Which committee was appointed in 1986 on 'Revitalisation of Panchayati Raj institutions for democracy and development'?

Answer: C. L.M. Singhvi Committee

36. Match: (a) Special Economic Zones policy (b) New Foreign Trade Policy (c) GST Act (d) Narasimham Committee report - 1. 1991 2. 2000 3. 2015 4. 2017

Answer: A. 2 3 4 1

37. The father of the Green Revolution in India was

Answer: A. M.S. Swaminathan

38. Which is a direct tax?

Answer: A. Gift tax

39. The sum of the deficit on revenue account and the deficit on capital account is

Answer: A. Budgetary deficit

40. Removal or relaxation of government restrictions at all stages of industry is known as

Answer: B. Liberalisation

41. Rural development in India means I. Poverty alleviation and welfare of the people II. Urbanisation of rural areas

Answer: A. I only

42. Who is eligible for the Kisan Credit Card? I. All farmers II. Share croppers III. Tenants

Answer: C. II and III

43. Match: (a) Kol uprising (b) Santhal uprising (c) Khond uprising (d) Ahom uprising - 1. Bhagalpur 2. Orissa 3. Assam 4. Chotanagpur

Answer: A. 4 1 2 3

44. Which decision of Gandhiji was opposed by Ambedkar?

Answer: B. Calling the lower castes 'Harijans'

45. Match with the year of the Act: (a) Communal representation introduced (b) Right to discuss the budget (c) Separation of Burma (d) August Declaration - 1. 1935 2. 1919 3. 1909 4. 1892

Answer: B. 3 4 1 2

46. The sequence of terms in the Preamble is

Answer: A. Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic

47. In a right-triangular ground the sides adjacent to the right angle are 50 m and 80 m. Find the cost of cementing it at Rs. 5 per sq. m.

Answer: C. Rs. 10,000

48. The average of 5 numbers is 180. If one number is removed the average becomes 155. Find the removed number.

Answer: B. 280

49. A person crosses a 600 m street in 5 minutes. His speed in km/h is

Answer: B. 7.2

50. A ramp for unloading a truck has an angle of elevation of 30°. If its top is 0.9 m above the ground, find its length.

Answer: C. 1.8 m

51. The standard deviation of 10 values is 3. If each value is increased by 4, the variance of the new set is

Answer: C. 9

52. Find the mean of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.

Answer: B. 9

53. Shyam's monthly income is Rs. 12,000 and he saves Rs. 1,200. Find his savings and expenditure percentages.

Answer: B. 10%, 90%

54. A colour TV's price rises 30% and sales fall 40%. The percentage change in income is

Answer: D. 22% decrease

55. Find the least number which, divided by 24, 32 and 36, leaves remainders 19, 27 and 31.

Answer: C. 283

56. If the LCM of a and b is a and the LCM of b and c is b, the LCM of c and a is

Answer: A. a

57. The exterior angles of a pentagon are in the ratio 6:3:4:3:2. Find its interior angles.

Answer: D. 60°, 120°, 100°, 120°, 140°

58. Which cannot be the sides of a triangle?

Answer: D. 1, 2, 3

59. Without log tables, find the approximate value of log₁₀ 2.

Answer: B. 0.3

60. Levelling and turfing a square lawn at Rs. 2.50 per m² costs Rs. 13,322.50. Find the cost of fencing it at Rs. 5 per metre.

Answer: D. Rs. 1460

61. The diameter of a semicircular plot is 70 m. Find the cost of fencing it at Rs. 12 per metre.

Answer: C. Rs. 2160

62. A circus tent is a cone on a cylinder: total height 49 m, base diameter 42 m, cylinder height 21 m. Find the cost of canvas at Rs. 12.50 per m².

Answer: C. Rs. 63525

63. Find the compound interest on Rs. 9,000 for 2 years at 10% and 12% for successive years.

Answer: B. Rs. 2,088

64. A sum at simple interest amounts to Rs. 8,880 in 6 years and Rs. 7,920 in 4 years. Find the principal and rate.

Answer: A. Rs. 6,000, 8%

65. A fair die is rolled. Find the probability of getting a prime factor of 6.

Answer: B. 1/3

66. The country's first transgender clinic was established in

Answer: C. Kerala

67. India's first indigenous microprocessor is known as

Answer: A. Shakti

68. Which taxes were subsumed under GST?

Answer: B. Excise duty, entertainment tax, sales tax

69. When was Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin launched?

Answer: A. April 2016

70. Who said "District administration is the total management of public affairs"?

Answer: A. S.S. Khera

71. Which is recognised as the Regional Tsunami Service Provider for the Indian Ocean?

Answer: C. Hyderabad

72. Consider: 1. The National Cadet Corps was established in 1948 2. Sainik schools were set up as a joint venture of the Central and State governments

Answer: C. Both 1 and 2 correct

73. Kargil Vijay Diwas was observed on

Answer: C. 26 July 2018

74. How many islands in Andaman and Nicobar were renamed by the Prime Minister on 30 December 2018?

Answer: C. 3

75. 'SAMPRITI 2017' is a joint military exercise between India and

Answer: B. Bangladesh

76. The Indian short film 'The School Bag' won best short film at the South Asian film festival in Montreal (Nov 2017). Its director is

Answer: B. Dheeraj Jindal

77. China's spacecraft that probed the far side of the moon is

Answer: B. Chang'e 4

78. Match: (a) First Schedule (b) Eleventh Schedule (c) Seventh Schedule (d) Eighth Schedule - 1. Division of powers between Union and states 2. Languages 3. Names of states and UTs 4. Panchayats

Answer: A. 3 4 1 2

79. Match: (a) Abid Hussain (b) K. Parasaran (c) Subhash Kashyap (d) K. Punnaiah - 1. Former Attorney General 2. Former Ambassador to the USA 3. Former Judge of AP High Court 4. Former Secretary General, Lok Sabha

Answer: D. 2 1 4 3

80. In the first stage of demographic transition: I. Death rates are high II. Birth rate is low III. Medical aid is absent

Answer: A. I and III only

81. Find the wrong one: 1. Section 377 IPC relates to LGBTQ rights 2. Joseph Shine vs Union of India relates to Section 497 IPC 3. Indian Young Lawyers Association vs State of Kerala relates to women's rights 4. Yashwant vs State of Maharashtra relates to the right to education

Answer: A. 4 only

82. If the earth's diameter doubled with mass unchanged, the weight of an object on its surface would

Answer: B. Become one-fourth

83. Nuclear forces are

Answer: A. Strongly attractive

84. By Moseley's law, the frequency of a spectral line in the X-ray spectrum varies as

Answer: B. Square of the atomic number

85. A cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO) is used to measure ____ of a signal.

Answer: D. All the above

86. The Prime Minister announced that Havelock Island in Andaman would be renamed as

Answer: C. Swaraj Dweep

87. Which is not a protonic acid?

Answer: C. B(OH)₃

88. SO₂ acts as

Answer: C. Both reducing and oxidising agent

89. Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) is used in medicine as

Answer: D. Anaesthetic

90. Which is a strong acid - strong base reaction?

Answer: D. HCl + NaOH

91. Find the wrong pairs: 1. Metabolism - sum total of enzymatic reactions 2. Auxin - photosynthesis 3. Photolysis of water - growth 4. Oxidative phosphorylation - respiration

Answer: C. 2 and 3

92. Which is a non-conventional raw material for paper production?

Answer: C. Sugarcane bagasse

93. Which is correctly matched? 1. Islets of Langerhans - insulin 2. Thyroid gland - diabetes 3. Adrenal gland - goitre

Answer: A. 1 only

94. The development and maturation of erythrocytes from haemocytoblasts is called

Answer: A. Erythropoiesis

95. The combination drug used to cure malaria is

Answer: B. Fansidar

96. Mammary glands in mammals are modified forms of 1. Salivary glands 2. Sebaceous glands 3. Pituitary gland

Answer: B. 2 only

97. Which ecological pyramid is always upright?

Answer: A. Pyramid of energy

98. Match rivers with towns: (a) Ganga (b) Brahmaputra (c) Yamuna (d) Tapti - 1. Surat 2. Agra 3. Guwahati 4. Benares

Answer: D. 4 3 2 1

99. What is the covalency of nitrogen in the ammonia molecule?

Answer: C. 3

100. Which forest grows where rainfall exceeds 250 cm and temperature is 25-27°C?

Answer: B. Tropical wet evergreen

101. 1. Project Tiger is a tiger conservation scheme 2. It was launched on 1 April 1983 3. 27 tiger reserves were set up in 17 states under it

Answer: A. 1 only

102. Which type of rainfall is associated with the equatorial region?

Answer: C. Convectional

103. Inversion of temperature occurs during

Answer: C. Long winter nights and clear sky

104. The Indus script was written

Answer: D. Right to left and left to right

105. With reference to the Sangam period, which pair is not correct?

Answer: D. Naura - Tondi

106. The Battle of Talikota was fought in

Answer: B. 1565

107. Which are correctly matched? 1. City of Jaunpur - Firuz Tughluq 2. City of Ahmedabad - Ahmad Shah 3. Zain-ul-Abidin - the Akbar of Kashmir 4. Founder of the Khalji dynasty in Malwa - Husain Shah

Answer: A. 1, 2, 3 only

108. The Northcote-Trevelyan Commission of 1853 is linked with

Answer: C. ICS

109. In which field did the colonial regime follow the 'Downward Filtration' theory?

Answer: A. Educational policy

110. Which concept is not part of the Rule of Law?

Answer: A. Natural justice

111. Under which article was the judiciary separated from the executive?

Answer: A. Article 50

112. The Government of India issued the Citizenship (Pondicherry) Order in

Answer: A. 1962

113. The Balwant Rai Mehta Committee suggested a

Answer: A. Three-tier system

114. Who was Prime Minister when a no-confidence motion was moved in Parliament for the first time?

Answer: A. Jawaharlal Nehru

115. Which statements on fundamental duties are correct? I. They were part of the Constitution since its adoption II. They apply only to citizens III. They were added on the Swaran Singh Committee's recommendation IV. They can be enforced through writs

Answer: C. II, III

116. The mandate of the Official Language Commission under Article 344: 1. Progressive use of Hindi for official purposes of the Union 2. Restriction on the use of English for official purposes of the Union

Answer: C. Both 1 and 2

117. The words 'Socialist' and 'Secular' were added to the Preamble by the

Answer: D. Forty-second Amendment Act, 1976

118. Who is associated with the 'PURA' model?

Answer: B. Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

119. Which Industrial Policy Resolution adopted the socialistic pattern of society?

Answer: B. 1956

120. Inflation adversely affects

Answer: B. Fixed income earners

121. The objectives of monetary policy are I. Price stability II. Economic growth III. Social justice IV. Reducing budgetary deficit

Answer: A. I only

122. About NITI Aayog: (i) Transforming the development agenda of India (ii) Promoting growth and development

Answer: A. (i) only

123. The 'Social Protection Index' was coined by

Answer: A. Asian Development Bank

124. Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) is primarily a

Answer: C. Rural roads development programme

125. A closed economy is one which I. does not trade with other countries II. imposes no restrictions on international trade III. has no coastline IV. is not a UN member

Answer: A. I only

126. According to R.C. Majumdar, the Revolt of 1857 failed because

Answer: D. There was no cordiality between Hindus and Muslims

127. 1. C.R. Das was known as Deenbandhu 2. He was the uncrowned king of Bengal

Answer: D. Neither 1 nor 2

128. Which song is called 'the Bible of modern Bengali patriotism'?

Answer: B. Vande Mataram

129. Who said "I am an Indian tom-tom, waking up all the sleepers so that they may work for their motherland"?

Answer: A. Annie Besant

130. What form of struggle did Congress adopt against the British in 1940?

Answer: B. Individual satyagraha

131. Arrange chronologically: 1. Chauri Chaura incident 2. Russo-Japanese war 3. Gandhiji's return from South Africa

Answer: D. 2, 3, 1

132. In which year did E.V. Ramasamy become Chairman of the Erode Municipality?

Answer: C. 1917

133. Pocket money of 7 students: Rs. 42, 22, 40, 26, 23, 28, 43. Find the median.

Answer: C. Rs. 28

134. The range of the first 10 prime numbers is

Answer: D. 27

135. Students of a class donated Rs. 4,624, each giving as many rupees as there are students. How many students are there?

Answer: B. 68

136. If ax² − 28x + 49 = 0 has equal roots, the value of a is

Answer: D. 4

137. Find the median of 12, 14, 25, 23, 18, 17, 24, 20.

Answer: D. 19

138. In a two-digit number the units digit is twice the tens digit. Reversing the digits gives a number 27 more. Find the number.

Answer: B. 36

139. A village has 32,000 people: 40% men, 25% women, the rest children. Find the number of men and children.

Answer: B. 12800 men, 11200 children

140. Find the greatest four-digit number exactly divisible by 15, 21 and 27.

Answer: B. 9450

141. If 3a + 4b = 22 and 8a − 5b = −4, then (a + b)² =

Answer: A. 36

142. A box has 3 green, 9 blue, 4 yellow and 8 orange cubes. (a) ratio of orange to yellow (b) ratio of green to blue (c) how many different colour-to-colour ratios can be formed?

Answer: D. 2:1, 1:3, 12 ratios

143. A troop has provisions for 276 soldiers for 20 days. How many soldiers must leave for the provisions to last 46 days?

Answer: B. 156

144. A clay cone of height 48 cm and base radius 12 cm is reshaped into a sphere. Find its radius.

Answer: A. 12 cm

145. A balloon's radius increases from 3 cm to 9 cm. Find the ratio of its volumes.

Answer: C. 1:27

146. A circle of diameter 4 cm lies inside a circle of radius 10 cm. The maximum possible distance between their centres is

Answer: C. 8

147. What is half the area of the triangle with vertices (1, 1), (3, 1), (1, 3)?

Answer: A. 1

148. A wire bent into a square encloses 484 m². Bent into a circle, its diameter is (π = 22/7)

Answer: B. 28 m

149. In how many years will Rs. 1,600 amount to Rs. 1,852.20 at 5% compound interest?

Answer: B. 3 years

150. If 123 represents GOD and 456 represents CAT, then 'DOG EAT EGG' may be represented by

Answer: D. 321 756 711

151. Find the number of 3-digit natural numbers divisible by 6.

Answer: B. 150

152. What is the probability that a leap year chosen at random has 53 Sundays?

Answer: A. 2/7

153. Which are major reasons driving outsourcing? 1. Technological innovation 2. Economic emergence of developing nations 3. Knowledge-based economy 4. Favourable balance of payments

Answer: C. 1, 2 and 3

154. Which factors influence digitalisation in banking? 1. Changing consumer behaviour 2. Financial inclusion and government initiatives 3. Increased smartphone use

Answer: D. 1, 2 and 3

155. Cord blood banks in Tamil Nadu are located at

Answer: A. Chennai

156. 50% of India's poor live in which states? 1. Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh and Jharkhand 2. Goa, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Jharkhand 3. Bihar, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh and Jammu 4. Kerala, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh and Bihar

Answer: A. 1 only

157. The urban heat island effect is due to 1. Concrete surfaces in urban areas 2. Low soil moisture 3. High wind velocity 4. High rainfall

Answer: B. 1 and 2

158. The first language declared a classical language in India is

Answer: D. Tamil

159. The headquarters of Interpol is in

Answer: B. Lyon

160. 'My Presidential Years' was written by

Answer: D. R. Venkataraman

161. In which year was the National Award to Teachers instituted?

Answer: B. 1958

162. The Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay National Welfare Fund assists

Answer: C. Outstanding sportspersons

163. What is the purpose of ISRO's Gaganyaan project?

Answer: A. To send three Indians to space

164. The States Reorganisation Commission comprised

Answer: B. Fazl Ali, Panikkar, Kunzru

165. How many states had achieved 100% household electrification (2019)?

Answer: A. 10

166. Who headed the three-member Lokayukta search committee?

Answer: C. Thiru. M.K. Krishnamoorthy

167. For which country did the Prime Minister announce Rs. 4,500 crore for hydropower projects in 2018?

Answer: C. Bhutan

168. Which statue is called the 'Statue of Unity'?

Answer: C. Statue of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

169. Which scheme is a placement-linked skill training programme for rural poor youth?

Answer: C. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana

170. The Doklam standoff was between

Answer: A. India - China

171. Jagjit Singh vs State of Haryana (2007) relates to

Answer: C. Members (MLAs, MPs) supporting from outside
