TNPSC 2025 Group 4 · General Studies: Questions with Answers

All 100 questions from the TNPSC 2025 Group 4 · General Studies paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

100 questionsOfficial answer key

101. Match the following: (a) Financial Assistance to Dependent children (b) Maintenance of Orphan and Destitute Girls (c) Foster Care Services Scheme (d) State After-Care Home for Boys — 1. Between the age group of 18 and 21 years 2. Providing destitute, orphan and unattached children 3. Children below the age of 16 years 4. Children between the age of 6 and 16 years. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: C. 3 4 2 1

102. As per the Tamil Nadu Economic Survey of 2024-2025, arrange the following in ascending order based on productivity. (1) Paddy productivity (2) Maize productivity (3) Oil seeds, Groundnut and Sugarcane productivity

Answer: A. (1) (2) (3)

103. Find out the incorrect statement(s) about the Tamil Nadu Health System Reform Program (TNHSRP): (i) This program was supported by the World Bank. (ii) This program is sponsored by the Central and State Government in the ratio of 60 : 40. (iii) It is implemented by the Department of Health and Family Welfare of Tamil Nadu on Program for Results basis. (iv) This program aims to achieve the goal of SDG 9.

Answer: C. (ii) and (iv) only

104. Assertion [A]: In 1892, Tremenheere report was accepted by the Government for increasing the income of the Government, to allot the poramboke land to down trodden community. Reason [R]: In Chengalpet, the Panchamar Schools were run by philanthropic individuals in Panchami Land for promoting education for downtrodden community people.

Answer: A. [A] is true [R] is false

105. Identify the incorrectly paired matches: (1) Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act came into force – January 16th, 2014 (2) Tamil declared as Classical Language – Oct 10, 2004 (3) Election Commission of India – Article 324 (4) National Integration Council in India – 1960

Answer: B. (2) and (4)

106. The purpose of "Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana" is

Answer: A. Installing roof top solar

107. Assertion [A]: Zamindari system was created by the East India Company in 1793 when Lord Cornwallis entered into the permanent settlement. Reason [R]: Under the settlement, the landlords were declared full proprietors of large areas of land.

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

108. Assertion [A]: Goods and Services Tax in India is a comprehensive, multistage, destination-based tax that is levied on every value addition. Reason [R]: The motto is 'One Tax, One Market, One Nation'.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true; and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

109. Arrange the following Finance Ministers of Tamil Nadu according to the Budget Year (chronological order): 1. Mr. Paneerselvam 2. Dr. Nedunchezhiyan 3. Mr. Palanivel Thiagarajan 4. Mr. Thangam Thennarasu

Answer: C. 2 1 3 4

110. Assertion [A]: The plan period 1966 – 69 was called "Annual plans or plan holiday". Reason [R]: Because of the two major conflicts with China and Pakistan.

Answer: C. Both [A] and [R] are true, and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

111. Statement: Thiruvalluvar has captured certain features like pride and administration of the Three Great Kings (Moovendhar) in his couplets. Reason: Thiruvalluvar wanted to portray the administrative skills of Kings.

Answer: D. Statement is incorrect but Reason is correct

112. Give the chapter No. ______ in Thirukkural that talks about Ascetic Virtues.

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

113. Who is the author of "Valluvarum Kuralum"?

Answer: B. Ki.Aa.Pe. Vishwanatham

114. Who was the first to draw the portrait of Thiruvalluvar?

Answer: B. Venugopala Sharma

115. Choose the right matches: (1) New India – Bharathi (2) Desabakthan – V.V. Subramanian (3) Nava Sakthi – Thiru. V. Kalyanasundaram (4) The Hindu – G. Subramaniam

Answer: C. (3) and (4) are correct

116. Which of the following statements are true about the Theosophical Movement? (i) The Theosophical Society was founded by Madam Blavatsky and Col. H.S. Olcott in New York. (ii) In 1888 Mrs. Annie Besant joined the society in India. (iii) The headquarters of the society is in Adyar.

Answer: C. (i) and (iii)

117. Assertion [A]: E.V.R. Periyar started the Self-Respect Movement in 1925. Reason [R]: New India was the official newspaper of the Self-Respect Movement.

Answer: A. [A] is true, but [R] is false

118. Consider the following statements and identify the wrong answers: (1) On 1st September 1798 Major Bannerman served an ultimatum directing Kattabomman to see him at Palayamkottai. (2) Kattabomman's Fort, 500 feet long and 300 feet broad, was constructed entirely of mud. (3) On September 16, 1799 Veera Pandiya Kattabomman was tried before an assembly of Palayakkarars at Kayatar. (4) On 1st June, 1799 Kattabomman, attended by 500 men, proceeded to Sivagangai.

Answer: C. (1) and (3) are wrong

119. Which of the following statements are true? (1) Congress boycotted the election which was held in 1923 in Chennai. (2) Swaraj Party won 11 seats. (3) Justice Party formed Ministry in Chennai. (4) Kamaraj led the Non-Co-operation movement in Chennai.

Answer: D. (1), (2), (3) are correct

120. Match the following: (a) Karaikkal Ammaiyar (b) Andal (c) Aranganadha Mudaliyar (d) Seraman Perumal Nayanar — 1. Thiruvantadi 2. Kachhikalambagam 3. Pavaippattu 4. Moothathiruppathigangal. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: C. 4 3 2 1

121. Which of the following statements are true about the Sivakalai excavation? (i) Sivakalai excavation is located on the north shore of the river Thamiraparani. (ii) Tamil society lived in Sivakalai is considered very ancient. (iii) Copper and gold goods are found.

Answer: C. (i) and (ii) only

122. What is the crucial role played by noncoding RNAs?

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: B. replications / C. gene regulation

123. Choose the right matches among the following: (1) Five Kingdom System – R.H. Whittaker (2) Six Kingdom System – Carl Woese, et al (3) Four Kingdom System – Carl Linnaeus (4) Three Kingdom System – Ernst Haeckel

Answer: B. (1), (2) and (4) are correct

124. 0.10 M of weak acid is found to be dissociated to the extent of 1.20% at 25°C. Its dissociation constant is

Answer: C. 1.44 × 10⁻⁵

125. The GSLV-F15 was launched on ______ by ISRO.

Answer: A. January 29, 2025

126. Which of the following is incorrectly matched? (1) Trans Siberian Railway line – It connects Baltic Sea and Pacific Ocean (2) Trans Caspian Railway line – It connects Central Asia and Japan (3) Canadian Pacific Railway line – It connects Atlantic Ocean and Indian Ocean (4) Trans-Continental Railway line – It connects Buenos Aires with Valparaiso

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: B. (2) only / C. (3) only

127. Who among the following was the Indian Prime Minister when the 42nd Amendment Act was passed?

Answer: C. Indira Gandhi

128. Assertion [A]: The Pallavas constructed the cave temples without brick, wood, metal and sudhai. Reason [R]: The stones kept one upon another and not bound with plaster is known as "Katraligal".

Answer: A. [A] true, but [R] false

129. Choose the wrong pair: (1) South Dravidian Language – Malayalam (2) East Dravidian Language – Munda (3) Central Dravidian Language – Telugu (4) North Dravidian Language – Kuruk

Answer: B. (2)

130. Assertion [A]: Rabindranath Tagore surrendered his knighthood. Reason [R]: On 13th April 1919 the Jallianwala Bagh Massacre occurred in Amritsar.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

131. Assertion [A]: The Indian National Congress first conference convened on 28th December 1885 in Bombay. Reason [R]: The Tamil Nadu region was represented by 21 members who attended.

Answer: A. [A] is true but [R] is false

132. Which of the following statements are true about the Indus Civilisation? (i) In the 1920s Mortimer Wheeler excavated the Harappan sites. (ii) The longest text has about twenty six signs. (iii) The term "Meluhha" in the Prakrit inscriptions refers to the Indus region. (iv) The "Priest King" of steatite was discovered in Mohenjo-Daro.

Answer: D. (ii) and (iv) only

133. The correct statements on the new Chief Election Commissioner of India are: (i) Gyanesh Kumar took charge as the 26th Chief Election Commissioner of India on February 19th, 2025. (ii) He is a Kerala cadre officer of the Indian Administrative Service of the 1989 batch. (iii) He has been responsible for handling various departments as the secretary to the government of Kerala.

Answer: B. (i) and (iii) only

134. Match the following (Act – enacted year): (a) Consumer Protection Rules (b) Drug Control Act (c) The Monopolies and Restrictive Trade Practices Act (d) Goods and Services Sales Act — 1. 1930 2. 1987 3. 1950 4. 1969. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: A. 2 3 4 1

135. Assertion [A]: Supreme Court judges are to be appointed by the President. Reason [R]: In the case of appointment of the Judges, the Law Minister of India shall always be consulted.

Answer: C. [A] is true but [R] is false

136. Arrange in chronological order (commissions related to Centre–State relations / Inter-State Council): (1) Punchhi Commission (2) Rajamannar Commission (3) Sarkaria Commission (4) Venkatachaliah Commission

Answer: D. (2), (3), (4), (1)

137. Choose the right match: (1) Retirement age of a Supreme Court Judge – 60 years (2) Minimum age for contesting for the President of India – 35 years (3) Minimum age for becoming a Rajya Sabha member – 30 years (4) Minimum age for becoming a Lok Sabha member – 21 years

Answer: C. (2) and (3) are correct

138. Arrange the following in chronological order: 1. Bharat Ratna 2. Padma Vibhushan 3. Padma Sri 4. Padma Bhushan

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

139. Match the following (original 1950 classification of States): (a) Himachal Pradesh (b) Rajasthan (c) West Bengal (d) The Andaman and Nicobar Islands — 1. Part A 2. Part D 3. Part B 4. Part C. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: A. 4 3 1 2

140. P and Q can do a piece of work in 20 days and 40 days respectively. They started the work together and Q left after some days of work and P finished the remaining work in 8 days. After how many days from the start did Q leave?

Answer: C. 8 days

141. Divide Rs. 2,602 between A and B, so that the amount of A at the end of 6 years is equal to the amount of B at the end of 8 years, compound interest being at 4% per annum.

Answer: D. Rs. 1,352, Rs. 1,250

142. Find the simple interest on Rs. 68,000 at 16⅔% per annum for 9 months.

Answer: A. Rs. 8,500

143. If a : b = 3 : 7 and b : c = 7 : 6, then find the ratio between c and a.

Answer: D. 2 : 1

144. The ratio of two numbers is 3 : 4 and their H.C.F. is 4. Their L.C.M. is ______.

Answer: A. 48

145. A number x is divisible by 13. When this number is divided by 16, 24, 32 it leaves a remainder 6 in each case. The least value of x is ______.

Answer: A. 390

146. There are 80 students in a class. If 25% are absent on a particular day, find the number of students present in the class.

Answer: B. 60

147. The ratio of boys and girls in a class is 5 : 3. If 16% of boys and 8% of girls failed in an examination, then find the percentage of passed students.

Answer: A. 87%

148. Find the sum of 1 + 3 + 5 + … + 55

Answer: B. 784

149. The next term in the given sequence is 0, 2, 24, 252, 3120, ______.

Answer: B. 46650

150. In the series 6 4 1 2 2 8 7 4 2 1 5 3 8, how many pairs of successive numbers have a difference of 2 each?

Answer: C. Three

151. A dice is thrown four times and its four different positions show (top / left-front / right-front): 2 / 3 / 5; 1 / 2 / 3; 3 / 4 / 5; 6 / 2 / 5. Find the number on the face opposite the face showing 2.

Answer: A. 4

152. Find the missing term of the sequence: 7, 12, 19, 24, 43, ______, 91

Answer: C. 48

153. Assertion [A]: Space MAITRI is a collaborative space mission between India and Australia. Reason [R]: India aims to lower the cost of access to space through this mission.

Answer: C. [A] is correct but [R] is wrong

154. Which of the following statement(s) is/are true about agricultural cultivation in 2021-2022 in Tamil Nadu? (i) Mayiladuthurai District had the most paddy cultivation in Tamil Nadu. (ii) Perambalur District had the most maize cultivation in Tamil Nadu. (iii) Kallakurichi had the most sugarcane cultivation in Tamil Nadu.

Answer: D. (ii) and (iii) only

155. Assertion [A]: As per the Economic Survey of Tamil Nadu 2024-2025, urban inflation in Tamil Nadu has come down from 6% in 2019-2020 to 4.5% in 2024-2025, while rural inflation is static at 5.4%. Reason [R]: It is mainly due to the Government's policy on the Tamil Nadu Public Distribution System and other income support schemes like Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thittam and hundred days employment guarantee schemes.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

156. Assertion [A]: The "Illam Thedi Kalvi" scheme in Tamil Nadu aims to bridge learning gaps among school students caused by the Covid-19 pandemic. Reason [R]: The scheme involves volunteers providing remedial education to students at their homes.

Answer: A. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

157. Which of the following statement(s) is/are true about "The Mandal Commission Report"? (i) The commission used three indicators, i.e. Social, Educational and Economic. (ii) In the social indicator there were five criteria and in the educational indicator there were two criteria. (iii) In the economic indicator there were four criteria. Thus there were 11 criteria used in the Mandal Commission Report.

Answer: B. (i) and (iii) only

158. What was the cause for high inflation in India?

Answer: B. Weakening rupee against the dollar

159. Arrange the poverty eradication schemes in chronological order: 1. National Rural Livelihood Mission 2. Rajiv Awas Yojana 3. Bharat Nirman Yojana 4. National Food Security Scheme

Answer: C. 3 2 1 4

160. Which of the following is incorrectly paired? (1) MFAL – Marginal Farmers and Agricultural Labourers Development Agency (2) DPAP – Drought Prone Area Programme (3) DDP – District Development Programme (4) HDA – Hills Development Agencies

Answer: C. (3) and (4)

161. Identify the incorrect acronym: (1) TSC – Total Sanitation Campaign (2) NIC – National Information Centre (3) MIS – Management Information System (4) IMIS – Integrated Management Infrastructure System

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

162. Choose the correct pair: 1. Bombay Plan – 1940 2. Gandhian Plan – 1945 3. People's Plan – 1944 4. Sarvodaya Plan – 1950

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

163. Kokkokka Koompum Paruvaththu Matradhan / Kuththokka Seerththa Itaththu — "As heron with folded wing, so wait in waiting hour; as heron snaps its prey, when fortune smiles, put forth your power." Evaluate: (1) To act like a heron is incorrect. (2) You must wait till the time ripens. (3) This Kural appears in the chapter titled "Therinthu Thelithal". (4) "Vaadiyirukkumaam Kokku" is the phrase best suited to this Thirukkural.

Answer: C. (1) Wrong (2) Right (3) Wrong (4) Right

164. "Manaththodu vaiymai mozhiyin thavaththodu / Thaananj Seiyvaarin Thalai". Statement 1: Those who speak the TRUTH are always superior to those who practise charity or penance. Statement 2: Those who practise charity and penance are far better than those who speak the TRUTH.

Answer: A. Statement 1 – Correct

165. Match the following: (a) Iyotheethass Pandithar (b) Ramalinga Adigal (c) Jotiba Phule (d) Syed Ahmed Khan — 1. Tahdhib-ul-Akhlaq 2. Gulamgiri 3. Thiruvarutpa 4. Oru Paisa Tamilan. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 4 3 2 1

166. Assertion [A]: K.M. Balasubramanian, author of South Indian Celebrities, compared Dr. C. Natesa Mudaliar to Mahatma Gandhi. Reason [R]: He was the epitome of love, character, tolerance and compromise, and he was the heart of the Justice Party.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

167. Which of the following statements are true about Ramalinga Adigal? (i) He said that apart from musical instruments the mind should unite. (ii) He started night schools for farmers. (iii) He said that through human charity one should get full benefit in life and be one with God.

Answer: B. (i) and (iii) only

168. Assertion [A]: In 1930 Muthulakshmi Ammaiyar introduced in the Madras Legislative Council a Bill on the prevention of the dedication of women to Hindu temples in the 'Presidency of Madras'. Reason [R]: The Bill, which later became the Devadasi Abolition Act, declared the "Pottukattu Ceremony" illegal.

Answer: C. Both [A] and [R] are true and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

169. Assertion [A]: Under the leadership of Rajaji, Congress decided to boycott the Simon Commission in Chennai. Reason [R]: The Simon Commission had only white men as members, that is, no Indians.

Answer: C. [A] is false, [R] is true

170. Match the works/books with the authors: (a) Vetriverkai (b) Nanneri (c) Neethinerivilakam (d) Nalvazhi — 1. Avvaiyar 2. Kumara Kurubarar 3. Athiveerarama Pandiyar 4. Duraimangalam Sivaprakasar. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: C. 3 4 2 1

171. Match the excavated places with their districts: (a) Azhagankulam (b) Vasavasamudram (c) Kodumanal (d) Keezhadi — 1. Sivagangai 2. Erode 3. Kanchipuram 4. Ramanathapuram. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: D. 4 3 2 1

172. Match the diseases in Column I with the site of infection in Column II: (a) Dengue (b) Poliomyelitis (c) Measles (d) Chicken Pox — 1. Intestine, Brain 2. Skin and Blood 3. Respiratory tract and Nervous system 4. Skin and Respiratory tract. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: A. 2 1 4 3

173. Assertion [A]: Cobalt-60 is useful in cancer therapy. Reason [R]: Cobalt-60 is a source of γ-radiation capable of killing cancerous cells.

Answer: A. Both [A] and [R] are correct and [R] is a correct explanation of [A]

174. In Tamil Nadu, the Vidiyal Payanam Scheme was introduced in the month of

Answer: A. May, 2021

175. Assertion [A]: Climate, relief, soil and natural resources influence the distribution of population. Reason [R]: Birth and death rates do not affect the distribution of population.

Answer: A. [A] is true, but [R] is false

176. Match the following (shifting cultivation – region): (a) Ladang (b) Milpa (c) Roca (d) Ray — 1. Malaysia 2. Vietnam 3. Brazil 4. Mexico. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: A. 1 4 3 2

177. Match the following (author – work): (a) Amoghavarsha (b) Jinasena (c) Gunabhadra (d) Bana — 1. Mahapurana 2. Kavirajamarga 3. Kadambari 4. Adipurana. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 2 4 1 3

178. Which of the following statements are true about Velu Nachiyar? (i) Velu Nachiyar was the daughter of Chellamuthu Sethupathi, King of Sivagangai. (ii) Velu Nachiyar's husband was assassinated by the Nawab's army, and to attack the Nawab and the British she joined with Gopal Naicker and Hyder Ali. (iii) In 1790, she died due to illness.

Answer: A. (ii) only

179. Which of the following points were accepted in the Gandhi-Irwin Pact? (i) People allowed to make salt for their consumption. (ii) Not releasing the political prisoners who had not indulged in violence. (iii) Permitted picketing of foreign cloth shops. (iv) Reduced the death sentence of Bhagat Singh and his comrades.

Answer: B. (i) and (iii) only

180. Consider the following statements and identify the correct answer: (1) In 1918, the Madurai Harvey Mill labourers' strike happened. (2) B.B. Wadia was the labour union leader of Madurai Harvey Mill. (3) Dr. P. Varadharajulu's speech was an eye opener to the government. (4) Dr. P. Varadharajulu was the author of the "Prabanja Mitran" magazine.

Answer: C. (1), (3) and (4) only

181. False statements about Subhas Chandra Bose are: (i) Subhas Chandra Bose was born on 23rd January 1897, in Calcutta, West Bengal. (ii) The Government of India has declared his birthday, 23rd January, as "Parakram Diwas". (iii) To honour Subhas Chandra Bose's legacy, 23rd January is a National holiday.

Answer: D. (i) and (iii) only

182. Match the Presidents with the distinguished guests who attended Republic Day celebrations held under their leadership: (a) Droupadi Murmu (b) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (c) Pranab Mukherjee (d) Ram Nath Kovind — 1. Cyril Ramaphosa 2. Prabowo Subianto 3. Vladimir Putin 4. Barack Obama. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 2 3 4 1

183. The toll-free number of the Directorate of Vigilance and Anti-corruption, Tamil Nadu is: (1) 1062 and 1963 (2) 1064 and 1965 (3) 1066 and 1967 (4) 1068 and 1969

Answer: B. (2)

184. Find out the correct statements on elections: (i) In all the states' elections, EVMs were used in 2003. (ii) The commission took a historic decision to use only EVMs for the Lok Sabha election in 2004. (iii) Two million EVMs were used in these elections.

Answer: C. (i) and (ii) only

185. Choose the right matches (committee – chairman): (1) Committee on Panchayat Raj Election – K. Shanthanam (2) Committee on Rationalisation of Panchayat Statistics – G. Ramachandran (3) Committee on Panchayat Raj Training Centres – V.K. Rao (4) Study Team on Panchayat Raj Administration – V. Iswaran

Answer: B. (1) and (4) are correct

186. The correct statements on the Directive Principles of State Policy are: (i) The Directive Principles of State Policy are provided in Articles 36 to 51 of the Constitution. (ii) The framers of the Constitution borrowed this idea from the Spanish constitution. (iii) The Directive Principles form the soul of the Constitution.

Answer: A. (i) only

187. Assertion [A]: The Indian Constitution is federal in nature. Reason [R]: The Indian Constitution provides dual citizenship like the United States of America.

Answer: C. [A] is true [R] is false

188. Assertion [A]: The Indian Constitution has the qualities of being both rigid and flexible. Reason [R]: The Constitution was amended more than 100 times without changing its foundations.

Answer: B. Both [A] and [R] are true; and [R] is the correct explanation of [A]

189. The readings in a field book are as given below. The area of the field is

Answer: A. 6550 m²

190. A person distributes his pens among four friends A, B, C and D in the ratio 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5 : 1/6. Then the minimum number of pens that the person should have is ______.

Answer: C. 57

191. If the ratios formed using the numbers 2, 5, x, 20 are in the same order of proportion, then the value of x is

Answer: C. 8

192. If the HCF of 108 and 234 is expressible in the form 23x − 51, then what is the value of x?

Answer: A. 3

193. Find the HCF of the polynomials x³ + x² − x + 2 and 2x³ − 5x² + 5x − 3.

Answer: A. x² − x + 1

194. Simplify: (4⁻¹ + 4⁻² + 4⁻³)⁰ × (4⁻¹ + 4⁻² + 4⁻³)¹

Answer: C. 21/64

195. The average mark of 25 students was found to be 78.4. Later on, it was found that a score of 96 was misread as 69. Find the correct mean of the marks.

Answer: B. 79.48

196. D, F, G, H, I and M are sitting in a row. I and M are in the centre, and F and D are at the beginning and at the end. G is sitting on the left of D. Then who is sitting on the right of F?

Answer: A. H

197. If A = 26, SUN = 27, then CAT = ?

Answer: C. 57

198. If a ≠ b ≠ c is given, which one of the following is correct?

Answer: D. a ≠ c or a = c

199. The missing term in the given alpha-numeric series is 2 X 2, 7 T 4, 14 P 12, 23 L 48, ______.

Answer: A. 34 H 240

200. Two unbiased dice are rolled once. Find the probability of getting a product of face values of 4 or a difference of face values of 5.

Answer: B. 5/36
