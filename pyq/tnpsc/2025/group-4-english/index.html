TNPSC 2025 Group 4 · General English: Questions with Answers

All 99 questions from the TNPSC 2025 Group 4 · General English paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

1. Direct to Indirect Speech : He said to Mr. Y, “Mr. Z will leave for his native place tomorrow”.

Answer: D. He told Mr. Y that Mr. Z would leave for his native place the next day

2. The positive form of "Ramesh is the strongest man in the village" is

Answer: D. No other man in the village is so strong as Ramesh

3. Fill in with the correct phrase: The airhostess instructed the passengers to fasten the seat belts during the ______.

Answer: C. take off

4. Which of the following sentences have gerunds? (a) They began playing (b) They began to play (c) We prefer to take tea (d) We prefer taking tea

Answer: D. (a), (d)

5. Fill in the blank with a suitable infinitive: He is supposed ______ for his examinations now, but he is wasting his time relentlessly.

Answer: B. to be studying

6. Fill in with the correct regular verb: "Both the officer and the worker ______ the affected areas".

Answer: B. visited

7. Transform the following exclamatory sentence into an assertive sentence: "What a spectacular goal!" (a) Was it a spectacular goal? (b) The goal was spectacular (c) It was a spectacular goal. (d) Why the goal was spectacular?

Answer: C. (c)

8. The negative form of 'The Mission is accomplished' is

Answer: D. The mission is not accomplished

9. "Cannot you find me some place convenient for laying my eggs" – the statement form of this interrogative sentence is

Answer: D. Search a convenient place for laying my eggs

10. Choose the correct type of sentence: You leave today.

Answer: A. Imperative

11. Select correct active form for the sentence: "Our army has been defeated by the enemy".

Answer: C. The enemy has defeated our army

12. Find out the correct tense form of the verb from the options given below: I got up early and ______ a shower.

Answer: C. had

13. Which of the following British English words are correctly paired with the American English words? (1) Spanner – Wrench (2) Hoover – Nonsense (3) Bollocks – Vacuum (4) Hairslide – Barrette

Answer: B. (1) and (4)

14. PDA stands for

Answer: A. Personal Digital Assistant

15. Match the following: (a) Careful (b) Teach (c) Writer (d) Kindness — 1. Verb formed from a noun 2. Noun formed from a verb 3. Noun formed from an adjective 4. Adjective formed from a verb. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: C. 4 1 2 3

16. Choose the correct meaning of the given phrasal verb in the context of time: Run out

Answer: C. Exceed the allotted time

17. Match the following idioms with their meanings: (a) Fall out (b) Fall for (c) Fall flat (d) Fall foul of — 1. to collapse 2. to come into collision 3. yield to its charms 4. to quarrel. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: A. 4 3 1 2

18. Find out the suitable word for the underlined collocation in the following sentence: It rains cats and dogs.

Answer: B. Heavily

19. Replace the underlined words with their antonyms: Ram is a (1) robust person born to (2) affluent parents. His (3) odious behaviour and habits had an effect on his physical and mental (4) agility which made him to look (5) gaunt. Words: (a) Pleasant (b) Awkwardness (c) Portly (d) Weak (e) Poor

Answer: C. (1)-(d) (2)-(e) (3)-(a) (4)-(b) (5)-(c)

20. Which of the following synonyms are incorrectly paired? (1) Gregarious – A person who walks unsteadily (2) Glutton – A person who eats too much (3) Groggy – A person who likes to be with others (4) Gourmet – A person who enjoys food

Answer: B. (1) and (3)

21. An interchange of two elements in a phrase or sentence from a more logical to a less logical relationship ______.

Answer: A. Hypallage

22. Text: E-governance can empower citizens to participate in climate action by providing platforms for feedback, collaboration and reporting environmental concerns. Question: How can e-governance enhance citizen engagement in climate change initiatives? (a) Providing online forums for citizens to share ideas. (b) Enabling citizens to report illegal dumping or pollution incidents. (c) Offering online tools for citizens to calculate their carbon footprint. (d) Replacing all traditional in-person meetings with online surveys.

Answer: B. (a), (b) and (c)

23. Maria always carries an umbrella in her bag. Today, despite clear skies, she brought her umbrella to work. What can be inferred about Maria?

Answer: C. Maria expects it to rain later

24. Rearrange the jumbled sentences: (a) Taking a few steps back, the fox jumped and just missed the hanging grapes. (b) Finally, giving up, the fox turned up his nose and said "They are probably sour anyway" and proceeded to walk away. (c) One afternoon a fox was walking through the forest and spotted a bunch of grapes hanging from over a lofty branch. (d) Again the fox took a few paces back and tried to reach them but still failed. (e) "Just the thing to quench my thirst", he thought.

Answer: B. (c) (e) (a) (d) (b)

25. What type of letter is used to request information about a product or service? (a) Inquiry letter (b) Sales letter (c) Personal letter (d) Resignation letter

Answer: A. (a)

26. Scenario: A government agency is updating its website to include downloadable forms. They want to ensure the instructions for completing the forms are clear and professional. Question: Which of the following instructions best reflects the principles of official correspondence? (a) Just fill this out and send it in, no big deal. (b) You gotta put your info here, then sign there and you are good to go. (c) Please complete all sections of the form using legible hand writing or typed text. Submit the completed form along with the required supporting documentation. (d) Write your name and address, then do the rest however you want.

Answer: B. only (c)

27. Find the suitable words: The ______ clouds signaled an approaching storm.

Answer: B. Menacing

28. Scenario: Ms. Priyanka is seeking assistance and information regarding services for differently-abled individuals in the state. Match her needs with the appropriate department: (a) obtain a disability certificate and access related benefits (b) information about educational scholarships and vocational training programs for her differently-abled child (c) report accessibility issues in public buildings and transport (d) information on self-employment schemes and financial assistance for differently-abled entrepreneurs — 1. Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment 2. Directorate for Welfare of the Differently-abled 3. Department of Labour and Employment 4. Department of Municipal Administration and Water Supply. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 2 1 4 3

29. Fill in the blank: ______ refer to a situation where borrowing money from banks and other financial institutions becomes difficult, often due to rising interest rates or a general economic downturn.

Answer: A. Credit squeeze

30. The process of hiring and managing employees is called

Answer: C. Human Resources Management

31. Passage: Smart home technologies aim to simplify daily life. They offer increased convenience and efficiency. User-friendly interfaces are essential for widespread adoption. The goal is to empower individuals through technology. Question: Which of the following statements best describe the author's purpose in the passage? (a) to criticize the complexity of smart home technologies (b) to highlight the benefits of smart home technologies (c) to emphasize the importance of user-friendly interfaces (d) to advocate for the empowerment of individuals through technology

Answer: B. (b), (c), (d)

32. Passage: A solar eclipse happens when the moon blocks the sun. This causes temporary darkness. It requires the Sun, Moon and Earth to align. The result is a shadow on Earth. Question: Which of the following statements accurately define or explain terms from the passage? (a) A solar eclipse is when the Earth blocks the Sun. (b) A solar eclipse is when the Moon blocks the Sun. (c) Alignment of the Sun, Moon and Earth is required (d) A shadow on Earth is a result of a solar eclipse

Answer: B. (b), (c), (d)

33. Complete the following sentence, choosing the appropriate word from the options given. I can ______ the presence of something rotten in the room.

Answer: A. Nose out

34. Match the words in Column A with their suitable synonyms in Column B: (a) dissonance (b) esoteric (c) lampoon (d) idiosyncrasy — 1. abnormality 2. mockery 3. abstruse 4. discord. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 4 3 2 1

35. In the given dialogue, some questions are underlined. Mark 'A' if it is a strong question and 'B' if it is a weak question. Anu: Dear friends, how can we celebrate women's day? Malar: Please clarify. Where and how are we going to celebrate? Anu: It must be celebrated in a grand manner. Malar: Shall we identify the venue? Anu: JKM Palace. Malar: Will it be available? Anu: Yes.

Answer: A. A A B B

36. Passage: A new social media platform gained rapid popularity among teenagers. Concerns arose about data privacy and the spread of misinformation. Despite these issues, the platform provided a space for creative expression and fostered online communities. Question: Which of the following statements are most likely to be true? (a) The platform is universally considered harmful. (b) The platform has both positive and negative impacts. (c) The platform is primarily used for educational purposes. (d) There are significant concerns about the platform's ethical implications.

Answer: B. both (b) and (d)

37. Passage: The city's rapid expansion led to increased traffic congestion and air pollution. Public transport systems were overwhelmed, and green spaces were replaced by concrete structures for the growing population. Residents complained of rising stress levels and a loss of community feeling. Question: Which of the following statements is most likely to be true? (a) The city is focused on preserving its historical landmarks. (b) The city is experiencing negative consequences of unchecked growth. (c) Residents are actively involved in urban planning decisions. (d) The city's infrastructure is struggling to keep pace with its population.

Answer: C. both (b) and (d)

38. Passage: The advent of remote work changed employment. Seamless collaboration rose with technology. Flexibility increased, yet isolation concerns grew. Question: Which of the following statements accurately reflect the meaning of the underlined word(s) as used in the passage? (a) 'advent' means the gradual decline of (b) 'seamless' means smooth and without interruption (c) 'flexibility' refers to the ability to adapt (d) 'isolation' means the state of being alone

Answer: C. (b), (c), (d)

39. Passage: The relentless surge in global temperatures paints a stark picture of our planet's future. Melting glaciers, raging wildfires and unprecedented floods serve as grim reminders of the escalating crisis. A sense of urgency pervades scientific reports, echoing the desperate need for immediate and decisive action. The window of opportunity to mitigate the catastrophic effects of climate change is rapidly closing, leaving behind a legacy of irreversible damage. Question: Which of the following words and phrases contribute to the mood of the passage?

Answer: A. Relentless surge

40. நீங்கள் அவருடன் கிரிக்கெட் விளையாடிக் கொண்டிருந்தீர்களா? Choose the correct English sentence from the options given, which best conveys the meaning of the sentence above.

Answer: C. Were you playing cricket with him?

41. Sentence: The meeting will have been scheduled by the time we arrive. Question: Which of the following Tamil sentences best conveys the meaning of the given sentence? (a) நாம் வரும்போது கூட்டம் திட்டமிடப்படும். (b) நாம் வரும்போது கூட்டம் திட்டமிட்டிருப்பார்கள். (c) நாம் வரும்போது கூட்டம் திட்டமிடப்பட்டு இருக்கும். (d) நாம் வரும்போது கூட்டம் திட்டமிடுகிறார்கள்.

Answer: B. only (c)

42. Passage: The company needs to 'develop' a new strategy. They want to 'develop' the land for a new factory. Question: Which of the following Tamil translations are appropriate for the word 'develop' in the given sentences? (a) வளர்க்க (b) உருவாக்க (c) மேம்படுத்த (d) கட்டியெழுப்ப

Answer: A. Both (b) and (d)

43. Which of the following statements are incorrect? (1) The play "The Tempest" was written between 1610 and 1611. (2) "The Tempest" was not based on any actual incident. (3) Shakespeare had penned 10 sonnets. (4) Prospero dismissed Ariel from his service before he left the island.

Answer: A. (2) and (3) are incorrect

44. Match the categories in Column A with the words in Column B: (a) Abbreviations (b) Acronyms (c) Homonyms (d) Homophones — 1. Bat, Bat 2. Aloud, Allowed 3. WHO, PAN 4. ATM, CEO. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 4 3 1 2

45. Which of the following statements are true about "Sir C.V. Raman"? (1) Sir C.V. Raman was an Indian physicist. (2) Sir C.V. Raman was honoured with the Bharat Ratna Award. (3) Sir C.V. Raman's birthday is celebrated as "National Science Day".

Answer: C. (1) and (2) only

46. Choose the correct option: What did the young seagull do, when his parents beckoned to him? (1) He began to swoop (2) He screamed with fright and attempted to rise again (3) He answered with another scream (4) He sank his feet into the green sea

Answer: D. (2) and (4)

47. Identify the figure of speech in the following poetic line: 'They growl at the rain and they growl at the sun'.

Answer: C. Anaphora

48. Which is the largest planet in the Solar system?

Answer: D. Jupiter

49. Choose the correct 'Synonym' by matching Column 'A' and Column 'B': (a) strife (b) endure (c) vain (d) accents — 1. futile 2. disagreement 3. undergo 4. emphasise. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: C. 2 3 1 4

50. Identify the figure of speech employed in the following lines: Born of despair with a sudden spring.

Answer: B. Alliteration

51. Choose the correct answers. R.L. Stevenson's famous works are ______. (i) Treasure Island (ii) 'The Lamb' and 'The Tiger' (iii) Kidnapped (iv) The Jungle Book

Answer: C. (i) and (iii) only

52. Choose the suitable answer: If a writer uses a word or phrase in a special way, he/she can write it in (a) Parentheses (b) Quotation marks (c) Square brackets (d) Dash

Answer: B. (b)

53. Which of the following sentences is a correct synthesis using an appropriate conjunctive adverb? Sentence 1: The assignment was due last week. Sentence 2: We did not submit it on time. (a) The assignment was due last week; consequently, we did not submit it on time (b) The assignment was due last week, so we did not submit it on time (c) The assignment was due last week, because we did not submit it on time (d) The assignment was due last week and we did not submit it on time

Answer: A. (a)

54. Identify the Complex Sentence:

Answer: C. He was so tired that he could not walk

55. Which of the following statements has the correct question tag? (a) I needn't play, needn't I? (b) Let's play, shall we? (c) I am older than you, am I? (d) He was asleep, did he?

Answer: B. (b)

56. Identify the correct option for the given question: A participle is a/an ______ formed from a verb.

Answer: A. Adjective

57. Which of the following sentences uses both a main verb and an auxiliary verb? (a) She dances beautifully (b) They are singing (c) She reads fast (d) Students eat dinner together.

Answer: B. (b)

58. Choose the correct appreciative statement for the following imperative: "Take time to review your work".

Answer: D. I value the effort you put into reviewing your work

59. The interrogative form of 'Shut the door' is

Answer: A. Who will shut the door?

60. The imperative form of the following statement 'I am lonely' is

Answer: A. Have pity on my loneliness

61. What an ugly picture! is an

Answer: A. Exclamatory sentence

62. 'Don't be angry with us God' - is an ______ sentence.

Answer: B. Imperative

63. Which of the following does not exhibit subject – verb agreement?

Answer: A. I wonder if either of the boys were present

64. Which of the following sentences uses the conjunctions to connect the clauses correctly?

Answer: B. She wanted to go for the concert yet she had to finish her homework fast

65. Identify the figure of speech of the following phrase: He has just got some new wheel.

Answer: C. Synecdoche

66. Which of the following is an action-based and sport related compound word that involves movement? (a) Foot print (b) Skateboard (c) Back pack (d) Lifeguard

Answer: B. Only (b)

67. Choose the correct plurals given in the options and fill in the blanks. (1) The __(1)__ is for more people to work part-time in the future. (2) We are having __(2)__ casserole tonight. (3) Smoking is not allowed on the __(3)__ (4) We discussed the success __(4)__ together. Options — (1) A Prognosis, B Prognoses, C Prognosus, D Prognos; (2) A Tuna, B Tunas, C Tunae, D Tunii; (3) A Premisis, B Premisus, C Premises, D Premisas; (4) A Criterion, B Criteria, C Criterius, D Criterian

Answer: D. (1)–A (2)–A (3)–C (4)–B

68. Which of the following words is incorrect in the context of the sentence? "After the team's success, the coach felt a great sense of ______ for his player's performance".

Answer: C. Exasperation

69. Fill in the blank with correct spelling: "The restaurant offers a wide variety of delicious ______".

Answer: A. Victuals

70. Sathyan knew he had ______ the trail sometime ago, but couldn't remember exactly when he had ______ it. (a) Passed (b) Past (c) Pause (d) Paws

Answer: A. (a) and (a)

71. What is the plural form of 'Cactus' in English? (Not the Latin form)

Answer: B. Cactuses

72. Match the one word substitute in Column A with a suitable explanation in Column B: (a) Amnesia (b) Insomnia (c) Loquacious (d) Pathology — 1. A study of disease 2. A continuous talker 3. Loss of sleep 4. Loss of memory. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 4 3 2 1

73. Fill in the blanks with suitable words: I ______ my puppy in the ______

Answer: C. bathe, tub

74. A student wishes to suggest an improvement to the college library resources. The best way to phrase the request to the librarian is: (a) The library has no good books (b) You should buy more books (c) The library is terrible (d) I've noticed that the library's collection lacks recent publications in the field of Artificial Intelligence. Would it be possible to expand the collection with my researched list of latest publications?

Answer: D. (d)

75. In the following sentence ordering, the first (S1) and the sixth (S6) sentences are given; rearrange P, Q, R and S. S1: Once, a renowned writer decided to unravel the secrets of an ancient manuscript. P: This however, exposed that the writer had intentionally modified a few passages, leaving room for multiple interpretations. Q: Apparently, the discovery sparked a heated debate among experts. R: The manuscript, filled with enigmatic knowledge, had perplexed generations of researchers. S: Moreover, the writer delved deeper to unravel the cryptic meaning and symbols. S6: Eventually, the writer's quest for knowledge resonates with the multiple interpretations of experts.

Answer: D. S1, R, S, P, Q, S6

76. Write a letter to the editor of a journal about the stagnant rain water issue in your area. Write the format of the letter. (a) Salutation (b) Date (c) Receiver's address (d) Signature (e) Sender's address (f) Subject (g) Body of the letter (h) Complimentary close

Answer: B. (e), (b), (c), (a), (f), (g), (h), (d)

77. Scenario: A citizen has sent an email to a government agency requesting information about a public program. The agency's policy is to respond with official letters. Question: Which of the following actions best demonstrates adherence to official correspondence procedures? (a) Replying to the email with a brief, formal response. (b) Calling the citizen to provide the information over the phone. (c) Drafting a formal letter on letter head, including all necessary elements and mailing it to the citizen. (d) Forwarding the citizen's email to another department without a response

Answer: C. (a) and (c)

78. Money set aside by a legislative body for some government expenditure is a/an ______.

Answer: A. Appropriation

79. Scenario: A citizen has contacted a Government office seeking clarification on a new policy. Match the general term used by the citizen with the appropriate official term: (a) Can you tell me how to fix this form? (b) I need to get a copy of my records (c) What are the rules for this program? (d) Is there a way to appeal this decision? — 1. Is there a procedure to appeal this decision? 2. What are the regulations for this program? 3. Can you tell me how to rectify this form? 4. I need to obtain a copy of my records. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: D. 3 4 2 1

80. The term “Compliance” means

Answer: A. Adhering to laws, regulations and policies

81. Match the term in Column A with its correct definition in Column B: (a) Appropriation (b) Bureaucracy (c) Jurisdiction (d) Public servant — 1. a system of government where decisions are made by state officials 2. a person employed in the public sector 3. the official power to make legal decisions and judgments 4. money set aside for a specific purpose. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

Answer: B. 4 1 3 2

82. Passage: E-governance reduces physical paper work. Online data storage enhances security. This facilitates faster service delivery. Increased digital literacy is essential for widespread adoption. Question: Which of the following conclusions can be drawn from the passage? (a) E-governance increases physical paper work (b) Online data storage improves security. (c) Increased digital literacy is crucial for e-governance success. (d) E-governance slows down service delivery

Answer: C. (b), (c)

83. Passage: NASSCOM and similar global associations both represent tech sectors. NASSCOM focuses on India, others on regional or international markets. Both advocate the growth of the industry. NASSCOM's influence is primarily within India, others have a broader reach. Question: Which of the following statements accurately compare NASSCOM and similar associations? (a) NASSCOM and global associations represent different sectors (b) NASSCOM and global associations represent tech sectors (c) NASSCOM's influence is primarily within India (d) Global associations have limited reach

84. Complete the sentence below, choosing the suitable pair from the given options: The ______ are insufficient to implement the new education policy in rural areas.

Answer: C. Ways and Means

85. Passage: The bustling market place was a symphony of sounds and smells, vendors called out their wares, a mix of spices filled the air and the chatter of customers created a lively hum. Colourful textiles hung from stalls and fresh produce was piled high. Match the aspects in Column A with their purpose in Column B: (a) Vendors calling out their wares (b) Spices filling the air with their smell (c) Chatter of customers (d) Colourful textiles hanging from stalls — 1. creates a lively hum 2. appeals to the sense of sight 3. appeals to the sense of smell 4. indicates commerce and trade. Codes in order (a) (b) (c) (d):

86. The vibrant city streets were alive with the sounds of laughter and music as people celebrated the annual festival. Colourful decorations adorned the buildings and food stalls offered a variety of delicious treats from different cultures. Which question is most suitable based on the given passage?

Answer: D. What type of food is being sold at the food stalls in the festival?

87. Which of the following questions cannot be categorised under the type "weak questions"? (1) Are you sure you are okay? (2) What is your biggest weakness? (3) Ever had a crush? (4) Does she have a qualifying degree for this job? (5) How will you repay your loan, without a job?

Answer: B. (2) and (5)

88. The ocean is a vast mysterious place, home to countless species of marine life. From colourful coral reefs to deep sea creatures, the underwater world is full of wonders waiting to be explored. As we learn more about the ocean, it becomes clear that protecting this vital eco system is essential for the health of our planet. Which question is related to the given passage?

Answer: B. Why is protecting the ocean ecosystem vital for the health of our planet?

89. A small town of Brooksville is famous for its annual harvest festival, which celebrates the local farming community. The festival features pumpkin carving contests, hayrides and a petting zoo. Which of the following activities is NOT mentioned as part of the festival?

Answer: C. A talent show

90. Passage: Vitamin C is an essential ingredient for human beings. It is required for the synthesis of intercellular cement "collagen" which is responsible for keeping various cells of the human body together. As an oxidant, it acts like a cop in the body, apprehending the rowdy molecules called free radicals. The richest source of Vitamin C is amla. Question: Vitamin C is important for us because it is

Answer: A. Required for intercellular cement collagen

91. Passage: Vitamin C ... acts like a cop in the body, apprehending the rowdy molecules called free radicals. What does the word 'Cop' mean in the given context?

Answer: D. Police

92. Passage: The richest source of Vitamin C is amla. Question: What is amla rich in?

Answer: C. Vitamin C

93. நாளை மாலை 6 மணி முதல் 7 மணி வரை நான் கிரிக்கெட் விளையாடிக் கொண்டிருப்பேன். Choose the correct translation of the sentence given from the options listed.

Answer: A. I will be playing cricket between 6 pm and 7 pm tomorrow evening.

95. Fill in the blanks using the suitable words given in the options. The old man had been suffering with a severe nervous disorder. He __(1)__ in pain and __(2)__. However __(3)__ while walking. On seeing his old friend he was __(4)__ and __(5)__ him with hope to get some help. Words: (1) tottered over (2) startled (3) grunted (4) approached (5) stumbled

96. Which of the following statements are true about 'Turtles'? (1) Sea turtles live their life entirely in the ocean. (2) There are six species of turtles in the world. (3) Turtles come ashore to lay eggs.
