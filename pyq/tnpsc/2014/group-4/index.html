101. If power of a lens is -0.5 dioptre focal length and type of lens is

Answer: A. 2 m, concave

102. Find the odd one out: Related with heat in one action.

Answer: C. Absorption

103. Match the following: (a) Potassium chlorate (b) Epsum salt (c) Copper sulphate salt (d) Potassium nitrate | 1. Constipation 2. Match industry 3. Crackers 4. Fungicide

Answer: C. (a)-2 (b)-1 (c)-4 (d)-3

104. Find the correct statement/s: I. After the release of the ovum the ruptured graffian follicle is transformed into corpus luteum. II. Corpus luteum a transitory endocrine gland.

Answer: D. Both I and II are correct

105. Pseudomonas putida a genetically engineered bacterium was invented by

Answer: D. Dr. Ananda Mohan Chakraborty

106. Point out the wrong statement/statements in the following: (i) Organisms which are able to synthesize their own food are called autotrophs (ii) Organisms which are unable to synthesize their own food are called heterotrophs (iii) Organisms which are unable to synthesize their own food are called autotrophs (iv) Organisms which are able to prepare their own food are called heterotrophs

Answer: D. (iii) and (iv)

107. What is the genome of AIDS virus made up of?

Answer: B. RNA

108. Which authority conducts the Local Bodies Elections?

Answer: A. State Election Commission

109. During the period of which Indian Prime Minister was there hung Parliament?

Answer: C. I.K. Gujral

110. The Period of Tenth Five Year plan is

Answer: A. 2002-2007

111. Choose the answer: ___________ Article of the Constitution prohibits the state from arbitrarily arresting any person.

Answer: A. Article 22

112. In which part of the Constitution of India is dealt with Fundamental Rights?

Answer: B. Part III

113. 'Glimpses of World History' was written by

Answer: C. Jawaharlal Nehru

114. Forty-Second Amendment Act came into force in the year

Answer: B. 1976

115. From the fig, find the value of 'x'

Answer: A. 30°

116. Find the height of a parallelogram whose area is 300 cm² and base 15 cm is

Answer: C. 20 cm

117. Ram is 7 times as old as his daughter Nanthini. After 5 years he will be 5 times as old as his daughter. What are their present ages?

Answer: D. 10, 70

118. If a/b = 4/3 then the value of 6a + 4b / 6a - 5b is

Answer: C. 4

119. Find the missing number in the place '?'.

Answer: D. 14

120. Simplify √214 + √112 + √74 + √49

Answer: A. 15

121. Divide Rs. 700 among A, B, C so that A gets 2 times more than to B and B gets 2 times to C how much of amount they are getting?

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

122. A cone, hemisphere, and cylinder have equal bases. If the heights of the cone and a cylinder are equal and are same as the common radius then find the ratio of their respective volumes

Answer: B. 1:2:3

123. Using Identities x - y = -6; xy= 4, Find the value of x³ - y³

Answer: A. -288

124. Two taps can fill a tank in 30 minutes and 40 minutes. Another tap can empty it in 24 min. If the tank is empty and all the three taps are kept open, in how much time the tank will be filled

Answer: C. one hour

125. The radius of a wheel is 1.75. How many revolutions will it make in travelling 11 km?

TNPSC dropped this question and gave every candidate a grace mark, so all options count.

126. Monthly expenditure of a person is shown in the figure. Find the amount has in his savings?

Answer: D. Rs. 750

127. When was the uniform pattern of education policy introduced in our country?

Answer: B. 1968

128. FERA is ____________ Exchange Regulation Act.

Answer: A. Foreign

129. The first five year plan of India was launched in

Answer: B. 1951

130. Choose the correct answer: I. Nimbus clouds are vertical clouds II. Nimbus clouds are high clouds III. Nimbus clouds are low clouds IV. Nimbus clouds are cotton-like clouds

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: A. I only / C. III only

131. Unequal distribution of the rate of growth of population due to two reasons

Answer: C. Nature of terrain and climate

132. Which statement is correct? I. Eutrophication means natural nutrient enrichment of streams and lakes II. Water pollution mainly affects the water based ecosystem. III. The unwanted sound cannot damage physiological and psychological health

Answer: C. I and II

133. The Poet known as the "Parrot of India" was

Answer: C. Amirkhusrau

134. Shivaji was born at

Answer: C. Shivner

135. Language is the means of

Answer: C. Communication

136. Which one of the following is correctly matched? I. Kudavola System - Cheras II. Variya perumakkal - Pandyas III. Varipothagam - Cholas IV. Boomi Puthras - Kalapiras

Answer: C. III

137. Which one of the following is correctly matched?

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: B. Jallian Walah Bagh Tragedy - Hunter Committee / C. Swaraj Party - C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru

138. Which British commander captured the place Nerkkattum Seval?

Answer: B. Colonel Campel

139. The First Indian Governor General of Independent India was

Answer: C. Rajagopalachari

140. Rabindranath Tagore renounced his Knighthood in protest against ___________ incident.

Answer: A. The Jallian Walla Bagh Massacre

141. Choose the incorrect pair:

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: C. Sebastian Vettel - Tennis / D. Dale Steyn - Hockey

142. Match the following: (a) David Wilkie 1. Dance (b) Padma Subramaniyam 2. Music (c) Abdul Walid Khan 3. Sculpture (d) Auguste Rodin 4. Painting

Answer: C. 4 1 2 3

143. Match the following: (a) Afghanistan (b) Argentina (c) Zimbabwe (d) Singapore | 1. National Congress 2. National Assembly 3. Parliament 4. House of Assembly

Answer: D. 2 1 4 3

144. Highest Polling percentage of 16th Lok Sabha election in Tamil Nadu constituency is

Answer: C. Dharmapuri

145. Doordarshan provides free-to-air DTH service as I. Ku - band II. DD direct + III. S band IV. K band

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: A. I only / B. I and II only

146. Which country does Crimea belongs to?

Answer: C. Russia

147. National Sports for Indonesia is

Answer: D. Badminton

148. Choose the right answer: Which of the four metropolitan cities connecting the six lanes super highways?

Answer: D. Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi and Kolkata

149. Which lake is located in the eastern part of Great lakes?

Answer: C. Lake Ontario

150. Match the following: (a) Force (b) Momentum (c) Power (d) Energy | 1. Watt 2. Joule 3. Kg.m.s⁻¹ 4. Newton

Answer: D. 4 3 1 2

151. Choose the correct one: If kerosene, water and mercury is taken in a same glass tumbler, arrange the position of them from the top to the bottom of the glass tumbler.

Answer: B. Kerosene, Water, Mercury

152. The percentage of Gold in 22 Carat is

Answer: A. 91.67%

153. Find out the calcium compound found on bones and teeth

Answer: B. Calcium phosphate

154. Among the following which one is not an Insecticide?

Answer: D. Zinc sulphide

155. Identify the incorrect pair: I. Albumin - Water balance II. Globulin - Immunity III. Fibrinogen - Blood clotting IV. Plasma - Solid component

Answer: D. IV

156. Which among the following food chain is an example for Grassland ecosystem?

Answer: B. Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle

157. Which gas is release the refrigerator?

Answer: D. CFC

158. The Governor is appointed by

Answer: D. President

159. How long can a money bill be delayed by the Rajya Sabha?

Answer: D. 14 days

160. Which article permits Separate Constitution for the State of Jammu-Kashmir?

Answer: A. Article 370

161. Arrange the Prime Minister's of India in chronological order of their term in office I. Thiru. Jawaharlal Nehru II. Tmt. Indira Gandhi III. Thiru. Morarji Desai IV. Thiru. Lal Bahadur Shastri

Answer: A. I, IV, II, III

162. The word citizen is derived from

Answer: B. Latin

163. The Multi-Functionary at the district level is

Answer: D. District Collector

164. Consider the following statements: Assertion (A) : Governor can promulgate ordinance when the state legislative is not in session under Article 213. Reason (R) : These ordinance must be approved by the legislative within six months. Select your answer according to the coding scheme given below :

Answer: C. (A) is true and (R) is false

165. Usually the population of a Corporation is

Answer: D. 10 lakhs

166. Find the missing number in the place '?'

Answer: B. 19

167. Number of prime numbers between 20 and 30

Answer: B. 2

168. If the area of the AABC is 68 sq. units and the vertices are A (6,7), B(-4,1) and C (a,-9) taken in order. Find the value of a.

Answer: D. 2

169. If the mean of x, x + 2, x + 4, x+6, x + 8 is 20 then find the value of x

Answer: B. 16

170. By selling a car for Rs. 1,40,000 a man suffered a loss of 20%. What was the cost price of the car?

Answer: D. Rs. 1,75,000

171. In the given figure, B : C = 2 : 3. Find B

Answer: B. 52°

172. Find the point of intersection of the straight line 9x - y - 2 = 0 and 2x + y - 9 = 0.

Answer: C. (1, 7)

173. The cost of a dining table is Rs. 8,400. A wants to buy it in 10 instalments. His E.M.I. is Rs. 875. Find the rate of interest?

Answer: B. 5%

174. If (x + m) (x + n) = x² + 6x + 5 find m² + n²

Answer: B. 26

Answer: C. 37

176. Find the missing number.

Answer: D. 189

Answer: C. 405

178. Three numbers are in the ratio 3:4:5 and their L.C.M is 2400. Their H.C.F is

Answer: A. 40

179. Match List I with List II correctly and select your answers using the codes given below : List I (a) Rice production (b) Wheat production (c) Jute production (d) Coffee plants List II 1. Punjab and Haryana 2. Coorg and Nilgiris 3. Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal 4. West Bengal and Assam

Answer: D. (D) 3 1 4 2

180. Registration of Partnership is

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: B. Optional / D. Not compulsory

181. The Hindustan Shipyard is located at

Answer: D. Visakapatnam

Answer: C. 2 4 1 3

183. The last king of the Mauryan Dynasty was

Answer: B. Brihadratha

Answer: B. 4 3 1 2

185. Which king introduced "Chain of Justice" in the field of justice?

Answer: A. Jahangir

186. According to Quran "Mamaluk" means I. The poor II. The slave III. The rich IV. The king

Answer: B. II

187. The Vijayanagar Empire founded in the year

Answer: B. 1336 A.D.

Answer: B. 4 3 1 2

The Policy of 'Drain of Wealth' by British leads to National Movement IV. Lyton's Delhi Darbar and Vernacular Press Act leads to national movement

Answer: C. II alone False

Answer: C. 11th May
