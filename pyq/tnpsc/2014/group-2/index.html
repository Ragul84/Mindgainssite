TNPSC · 2014

TNPSC 2014 Group 2 Prelims · General Studies: Questions with Answers

All 78 questions from the TNPSC 2014 Group 2 Prelims · General Studies paper, in paper order. Tap an option to check your answer instantly.

78 questionsVerified answers

1. The period of William Wordsworth is

Answer: A. 1770 - 1850

2. Which one of the following poems has not been written by the poet "H.W. Longfellow"?

Answer: A. Be The Best

3. "'Ahimsa' means infinite love, which again means infinite capacity for suffering". The above is a quote by

Answer: D. Mahatma Gandhiji

4. Identify the poem in which this line occurs. "I often contemplate my Childhood Mom"

Answer: B. A Sonnet for my Incomparable Mother

18. The following lines are taken from the poem 'The music in my heart I bore Long after it was heard no more'.

Answer: D. 'The Solitary Reaper'

19. Who said this? 'Woman is the companion of man, gifted with equal mental capacities'.

Answer: B. Mahatma Gandhi

20. 'Sonnet No.: 116' is written by

Answer: C. William Shakespeare

22. 'Failure is an accepted procedure in experimenting, research, testing and all scientific forms of "finding out". Failure is simply the means of finding out what will not work so that it can be eliminated in the search for what will work'. What can be eliminated?

Answer: A. things that will not work

23. Choose the appropriate prefix for the word: 'Fortune'

Answer: B. mis-

24. "Knowledge is an exacting mistress; she needs devotion, whole-hearted, on the part of the person who seeks her". What requires whole-hearted devotion?

Answer: D. Knowledge

25. "Any organisation, society or even a nation without a vision is like a ship cruising on the high seas without any aim or direction: It is clarity of national vision which constantly drives the people towards the goal". What drives the people towards the goal?

Answer: B. a clear national vision

26. A Boat beneath a sunny sky Lingering onward dreamily What is lingering onward?

Answer: C. a boat

27. 'In solit'ry confinement as complete as any goal'. Solit'ry refers to

Answer: A. being alone

28. "We ran as if to meet the moon That slowly dawned behind the trees" These lines are taken from the poem

Answer: C. Going for Water

29. The man who posed as a beggar in the story 'The Model Millionaire' was

Answer: D. Baron Hausberg

30. I like to eat Maize. Replace the underlined word with its American equivalent

Answer: C. Corn

31. Identify the sentence with the verb in its passive form.

Answer: A. The symposium was inaugurated by the Director.

32. Identify the sentence that contains a verb in its active form.

Answer: B. The old man completed his work.

33. Choose the correct verb: Tendulkar ---- (bat) since the match started this morning.

Answer: C. has been batting

34. Fill in the blanks with a suitable tense form: I ---- (talk) to my mother, when the guests arrived.

Answer: D. was talking

36. Match the word child with the correct suffix.

Answer: B. ish

37. Match the following synonym from Column A with Column B and select the correct answer from the given codes. Column A: (a) Logic, (b) Electron, (c) Concentration, (d) Impatience. Column B: 1. Focus, 2. Inability to wait, 3. Reasoning, 4. Particle in a cell.

Answer: D. 3 4 1 2

38. Fill in the blank with a suitable gerund. ----well is an art.

Answer: C. Cooking

40. He lives in a flat. Replace the underlined word with its American equivalent.

Answer: C. apartment

41. She hired a taxi to the airport. Replace the underlined word with its American equivalent.

Answer: B. cab

42. Identify the author of the short story 'The Selfish Giant'.

Answer: C. Oscar Wilde

43. Shylock was envious of Antonio, a

Answer: A. merchant

44. The Selfish Giant stayed with his friend for

Answer: D. seven years

45. Match the following poems under Column A with the poets under Column B and select the correct code. Column A: (a) The Cry of the Children, (b) Be Glad Your Nose is on Your Face, (c) The Flying Wonder, (d) Snake. Column B: 1. Jack Prelutsky, 2. Stephen Vincent Benet, 3. D.H. Lawrence, 4. Elizabeth Barrett Browning.

Answer: B. 4 1 2 3

46. Choose the option that completes the given sentence. Rabindranath Tagore was awarded the Nobel prize for his work

Answer: D. Gitanjali

47. In which story do you come across the character 'Saruli'?

Answer: A. After the Storm

48. 'Ingratitude, more strong than traitor's arms'. Who utters these words?

Answer: C. Mark Antony in 'Julius Caesar'

49. Fill in the blank with a suitable article. Where there is -----will there is a way.

Answer: B. a

50. Pick out the rhyming words in the given lines: 'Through the coal-dark underground- Or, all day, we drive the wheels of iron In the factories round and round'

Answer: D. underground, round

51. To which nationality does the poet Robert Frost belong?

Answer: C. America

54. Which one of the following literary works is concerned with 'Global Warming'?

Answer: D. Gaia Tells Her Tale

56. "For the soul is dead that slumbers" Identify the option with words in alliteration.

Answer: C. soul, slumbers

57. Select the correct plural form of the word "Story".

Answer: D. Stories

59. Identify the correct sentence.

Answer: C. The hospital has three lady doctors.

60. The post came late. Replace the underlined word with its American equivalent.

Answer: D. mail

61. 'Tell me not in mournful numbers, Life is but an empty dream.' These lines are from the poem

Answer: A. A Psalm of Life

62. 'Listen to the sounds of nature ..... Take goodcare of our fragile planet'. Who said this?

Answer: C. Kalpana Chawla

63. Identify the part containing an error : Raghu prefers milk than tea. a b c d

Answer: D. than tea

64. Fill in the blanks with the correct homophones. I paid the ------ to go to the ------

Answer: A. fare, fair

65. Identify the author of the short story 'The Refugee'.

Answer: B. Pearl S Buck

66. Who is the author of the short story "Swept Away"?

Answer: D. Susannah Hickling

67. In which country does the story "Two Friends" take place?

Answer: B. France

68. How many plays have been written by the famous Playwright William Shakespeare?

Answer: A. 37

69. Identify the correct degree. Usha Riran is not the tallest of all the buildings in the city.

Answer: D. Superlative

70. Identify the figure of speech in the line: '0 winging words! Like homing bees you borrow'.

Answer: A. Simile

71. 'And the grave is not its goal' The word 'grave' refers to

Answer: B. Death

72. 'For I'm off to outer space tomorrow morning'. Where is the poet going?

Answer: C. outer space

73. 'Or, all day, we drive the wheels of iron' What do the children do all day?

Answer: C. drive the wheels

74. 'Where the mind is without fear And the head is held high' The given lines are written by

Answer: B. Rabindranath Tagore

75. Where did Antony address the public while displaying Caesar's body to the Romans?

Answer: B. at the market place

76. Alan Trevor was a

Answer: B. painter

77. In the story 'The Selfish Giant', the Giant was covered with - blossoms when he lay dead under the tree in his garden.

Answer: A. White

78. 'The Twin Towers of the World Trade Centre' is situated in

Answer: A. New York

79. 'It is heartening to meet Laxmi, our porter, the following morning. He is a sturdy young man'. Sturdy means

Answer: D. Strong

80. Match the British English words under Column A with their American English equivalents under Column B : Column A (a) Autumn (b) Holiday (c) Lorry (d) Goods Column B 1. Truck 2. Freight 3. Fall 4. Vacation

Answer: C. 3 4 1 2

81. What is the American English word for the British English word 'Underground'?

Answer: C. Subway

82. What is the American English word for the British English word, 'Terminus'?

Answer: B. Depot

83. Baron Hausberg was one of the ---- in Europe.

Answer: C. richest Men

84. Use a torch when there is no power supply. Replace the underlined word with its American equivalent.

Answer: A. flash light

86. Fill in the blanks with a gerund. -----is his favourite pastime. (A) To read ~ Reading (C) The read (D) Read

Answer: B. Reading

87. Which of the following words is formed with a prefix attached to it? ~ International (B) . Interest (C) Interior (D) Interim

Answer: A. International

88. Choose the correct antonym of the underlined word from the options given: Nowadays, the cciteria to avail Educational Loan is flexible. (A) Supple ~ Stiff (C) Stupid (D) Expandable

Answer: B. Stiff

89. Choose the correct antonym of the underlined word from the options given: Napoleon faced a catastrophic defeat. ~ Success (B) Failure (C) Excitement (D) Danger

Answer: A. Success

90. Choose the correct synonym of the underlined word: Most of its inhabitants were still in the arms of sleep. (A) Habitats ~ Residents (C) Truants (D) Tyrants

Answer: B. Residents

91. Match the following poems under Column A with the poets under Column B and select the correct code: Column A: (a) A Psalm of Life (b) The Man He Killed (c) Be The Best (d) Earth. Column B: 1. Khalil Gibran 2. Douglas Malloch 3. Thomas Hardy 4. H.W. Longfellow. Options: (A) 2 1 3 4 (B) 3 4 1 2 (C) 4 3 2 1 (D) 2 4 3 1

Answer: C. 4 3 2 1

92. Which one of the following options is not a famous tragedy of William Shakespeare? (A) Hamlet (B) King Lear (C) Othello (D) A Midsummer Night's Dream

Answer: D. A Midsummer Night's Dream

93. Acquiring knowledge is one's primary duty - not only for one's success in the examination but also for one's life. What is one's primary duty? (A) Playing well (B) Succeeding in the examination (C) Working earnestly (D) Acquiring knowledge

Answer: D. Acquiring knowledge

94. Kalpana Chawla was a famous (A) astrologer (B) astronaut (C) teacher (D) doctor

Answer: B. astronaut

95. Who is the 'Bird Man' of India? (A) Dr. Abdul Kalam (B) Dr. Salim Ali (C) R.K. Narayanan (D) Thakazhi Sivasankaran Pillai

Answer: B. Dr. Salim Ali

96. "Where the clear stream of reason has not lost its way into the dreary desert sand of dead habit" These lines are taken from the poem (A) A Psalm of Life (B) Be The Best (C) Where the Mind is Without Fear (D) The Solitary Reaper

Answer: C. Where the Mind is Without Fear

97. 'The 16th century palace complex was built by the N(iyaks and later renovated by the Marathas'. Who renovated the palace complex? (A) Nayaks (B) Marathas (C) Cholas (D) Pallavas

Answer: B. Marathas

98. Match the authors given under Column A with the characters given under Column B and choose the correct answer from the codes given: Column A: (a) Sundara Ramasamy (b) Oscar Wilde (c) William Shakespeare (d) Kushwant Singh. Column B: 1. Hughie 2. Mark Antony 3. Gunga Ram 4. Rowther. Options: (A) 4 3 2 1 (B) 4 2 1 3 (C) 3 1 2 4 (D) 4 1 2 3

Answer: D. 4 1 2 3

99. "I come to bury Caesar, not to praise him" Whose words are these? (A) Marcus Brutus (B) Mark Antony (C) Casca (D) Cassius

Answer: B. Mark Antony
