101. Match the following: Technology (a) Aeroplane (b) Air Balloon (c) Rocket (d) Steam Engine | Scientific Principles 1. Newton's Law 2. Bernoulli's Principle 3. Law of thermodynamics 4. Buoyant Force

Answer: D. (a)-2 (b)-4 (c)-1 (d)-3

102. Auto ionisation of H2O leads to the formation of

Answer: D. H3O+ and OH-

103. Arrange the following in increasing order of their pH values: I. Human Blood II. Stomach acid III. Pure water IV. Milk

Answer: C. II, IV, III and I

104. Which one of the following bacterium has extensive usage in Genetic Engineering work in plants?

Answer: D. Agrobacterium tumefaciens

105. Match the following: (a) Riboflavin | 1. Vitamin B6 (b) Thiamine | 2. Vitamin B2 (c) Pyridoxine | 3. Vitamin B12 (d) Cyanocobalamine | 4. Vitamin B1

Answer: C. (C) 2 4 1 3

106. Which of the following is common to both Aerobic and Anaerobic respiration?

Answer: A. Glycolysis

107. Name the hormone secreted by the pineal body

Answer: A. Melatonin

108. Which state has introduced the scheme Aahar for the poor people?

Answer: B. Odissa

109. Name the anti submarine warfare ship launched in 19th May 2015 at Kolkatta

Answer: B. INS - Kavaratti

110. The 'World Congress of Information Technology' in 2018 will be held in the Indian city of

Answer: C. Hyderabad

111. The Akraman-II exercise was conducted by Indian Army in the state of

Answer: D. Rajasthan

112. On January 10 2015, Vikram Sarabhai Memorial award was given to

Answer: A. M.Y.S. Prasad

113. The train which has been named as Yoga Express is

Answer: C. Haridwar Mail

114. What is the name given to India's relief and rescue mission in Quake-hit Nepal?

Answer: D. Operation Maitri

115. Madan Mohan Malviya National Mission is about

Answer: B. Teachers and Teaching

116. Choose the new savings scheme introduced for girl child from the following:

Answer: B. Sukanya Samridhi Yojana

117. _______ metal is found in the controversial product of Maggie Noodles.

Answer: C. Lead

118. Arrange the following peaks of the Himalayas in descending order of height: I. Everest II. Nanda Devi III. Dhaulagiri IV. Nanga Parbat

Answer: B. I, III, IV, II

119. Match the following revolutions with the respective fields: Revolution (a) Silver (b) Pink (c) Yellow (d) Grey | Field 1. Oil seeds 2. Fertilizers 3. Eggs 4. Shrimp

Answer: B. (B) 3 4 1 2

120. Identify the original states before its bifurcation and choose the correct answer from the codes given below. A (a) Haryana (b) Jharkhand (c) Uttarakhand (d) Chattisgarh | B i. Uttar Pradesh ii. Madhya Pradesh iii. Punjab iv. Bihar

Answer: C. (C) iii iv i ii

121. The Samjhauta Express runs between India and Pakistan. Identify its destination in both countries.

Answer: D. Delhi in India to Lahore in Pakistan

122. Who was the first well-known exponent of Algebra among Indian Mathematicians?

Answer: C. Aryabhatta

123. Who was the author of the Natya Shastra?

Answer: C. Bharatamuni

124. National Commission for Scheduled Tribes recommends that a National Education Commission must be setup for every

Answer: D. Five years

125. Who was the first recipient of Bharat Ratna Award?

TNPSC accepted more than one answer: A. Dr. S. Radhakrishnan / B. Sri. Rajagopalachari

126. Consider the following statements and find out the correct ones: 1. A democratic government may be liberal one 2. A liberal government may be democratic 3. A liberal democratic government is collectivistic 4. A liberal democratic government is a welfare state

Answer: A. 1, 2, 4

127. Which one of the following provisions can be amended by a simple majority in the parliament?

Answer: D. Provisions relating to composition of the legislative councils of the state

128. The Lokpal bill was first introduced in the Lok Sabha in which year?

Answer: A. 1968

129. The Verma Committee on fundamental duties was set-up in the year

Answer: B. 1998

130. Which one of the following articles says, "The Vice President presides over the meetings of the council of states"?

Answer: A. 89

131. Zamindari system was introduced by

Answer: B. Lord Cornwallis

Answer: C. Incentives and encouragement to the tillers

134. Who among the following has made the first systematic attempt of planning in India?

Answer: C. M. Viswesvarayya

135. Who was the leader of Satyagraha Committee, organised for the removal of Neel Statue in Chennai?

Answer: A. N. Somayajulu

136. When did Nehru become the President of Indian National Congress?

Answer: A. On 6 July 1946

137. From which novel was the National song of Vande Mataram taken?

Answer: D. Anand Math

138. During the freedom struggle on which day the Indian National Congress celebrated the Poorna Swaraj?

Answer: A. January 26, 1930

139. If "HYDROGEN" is represented as JCJZYSSD, how "ANTIMONY" will be represented as

Answer: B. CRZQWABO

140. Arrange in descending order: ∛12, √20, √25, √80, ∛112

Answer: A. √80, ∛12, √20, √25, ∛112

141. Find the range of the following data: 25, 67, 78, 43, 21, 17, 49, 54, 76, 92, 20, 45, 86, 37, 35.

Answer: B. 75

142. A is B's sister, C is B's mother, D is C's father, E is D's mother. Then, A is the ________ of D.

Answer: D. Grand daughter

143. A is richer than B, C is richer than A, D is richer than C. E is the richest of all. If they are made to sit in the above degree of richness, who will have the central position?

Answer: C. C

144. A hollow cylindrical iron pipe is of length 35 cm. Its outer and inner diameters are 10 cm and 8 cm respectively. Find the weight of the pipe if 1 cu.cm of iron weighs 7 gm.

Answer: A. 6.93 kg

145. Find out the missing term in the given alphabet series. AZ, GT, MN, _____, YB

Answer: B. SH

146. 7 men can complete a work in 52 days. In how many days will 13 men finish the same work?

Answer: D. 28 days

147. If a/b = 9/5 then a+b/a-b = ?

Answer: D. 7/2

148. Find the LCM of 4⁵, 4⁻⁸¹, 4¹² and 4⁷

Answer: A. 4¹²

149. Find the missing number in the following: 4242, 4254, 4230, 4266, 4218, 4278, _________

Answer: D. 4206

150. The average of 4 values is 20 and when a quantity is added to each value the average is 22. Find the quantity.

Answer: B. 2

151. Which of the following are correct? (i) Work = Force x Displacement (ii) Power = Work/Time (iii) Force = Mass x Velocity (iv) Acceleration = Velocity/Time

Answer: D. (i), (ii) and (iv)

152. Why the wings of an aeroplane are shaped with lower surface being flat and the upper surface being curved?

Answer: B. To make difference in pressure to lift the plane vertically

153. Match the following: Famous Indian Scientist Their Contribution (a) C.V. Raman (b) J.C. Bose (c) M.N. Saha (d) S.N. Bose | 1. Ultra Short Radio Waves 2. Thermal Ionization 3. Quantum Statistics 4. Inelastic Scattering of Light

Answer: D. (D) 4 1 2 3

154. Which one of the following is not an allotrope of carbon?

Answer: D. Butane

Pheromones 2. Juvenile Hormones 3. Pyrethrins 4. Organo phosphates

Answer: C. 3 4 1 2

156. Match the following: (a) Castor oil (b) Gingley oil (c) Neem oil (d) Groundnut oil | 1. Sesamum indicum 2. Azadirachta indica 3. Arachis hypogea 4. Ricinus communis

Answer: C. 4 1 2 3

Answer: A. Colour Blindness

Answer: A. Mr. Dadasaheb Phalke

Answer: B. 2

190. A survey in which information is collected from each and every individual of the population is known as

Answer: A. Census

191. Find the principal that yield a a compound interest of Rs. 1,632 in 2 years at 4% rate of interest per annum.

192. Mohan started from point P and walked 2 m towards west. He then took a right turn and walked 3 m before taking a left turn and walked 5 m. He finally took a left turn, walked 3 m and stopped at a point Q. How far is point Q from point P?

Answer: C. 7 m
