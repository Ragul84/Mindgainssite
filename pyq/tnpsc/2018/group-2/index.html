101. What is the basic concept of Raman effect?

Answer: C. coherent scattering

102. The Epsom salt which is used as laxative is

Answer: A. MgSO4.7H2O

103. Which one of the following is the correct composition of Brass?

Answer: B. Cu-60%; Zn-40%

104. What is phototropism?

Answer: B. movement of plants towards light

105. Match the following: (a) Ranthambore (b) Manas (c) Nagarjuna sagar (d) Palamala (e) Indravathi 1. Assam 2. Jharkhand 3. Andhra 4. Rajasthan 5. Chattisgarh

Answer: A. (A) 4 1 3 2 5

106. Match: Tribe (a) Masai (b) Saami (c) Kalinga (d) Bush men Region 1. Angola 2. Kenya 3. Philippines 4. Siberia

Answer: A. (A) 2 4 3 1

107. Humboldt ocean current is otherwise known as

Answer: C. Peru current

108. 'Sea Smoke' is common in

Answer: D. Arctic region

109. Who started Home Rule Movement in India?

Answer: A. Annie Besant

110. Tamil Books were printed firstly by whom?

Answer: A. Rev. Zieganbalg

111. Who converted Mahendra Varma I from Jainism to Saivism?

Answer: B. Appar

112. Name the chieftain who patronised Kabilar

Answer: C. Pari

113. The Portuguese Sailor who reached Calicut in 1498 A.D. was?

Answer: B. Vasco da Gama

114. On behalf of the Justice Party, who won and formed the ministry in 1923 election?

Answer: A. T.N. Sivagnanam Pillai

115. Where was the first Indian National Congress met?

Answer: B. Bombay

116. The Forward Block Party was started by whom?

Answer: A. Subhash Chandra Bose

117. The institution of Lok-Ayukta was established first in the state of

Answer: B. Maharashtra

118. Who said "The Directive Principle of State Policy is a Novel feature" of the Indian constitution?

Answer: B. B.R. Ambedkar

119. The idea of concurrent list was borrowed from

Answer: D. Australian Constitution

120. In which year the Parliament enacted the Official Language Act?

Answer: C. 1963

121. The members of the Central Administrative Tribunal are given status of Judges of High Court from an Amendment in the year:

Answer: D. 2006

122. What is the aim of Pradhan Mantri Gram-Sadak Yojana?

Answer: A. Provide all Weather road connectivity

123. When was the "Industrial Finance Corporation of India" established in India?

Answer: B. July 1, 1948

124. Which is the biggest nationalised enterprise?

Answer: C. Indian Railways

125. Identify the mismatch: (A) White revolution - Milk (B) Green revolution - Food grains (C) Blue revolution - Fish (D) Yellow revolution - Flowers

Answer: D. Yellow revolution - Flowers

126. National Agricultural policy aims at a growth rate of -% per year.

Answer: B. 4%

127. In how many years will a sum of ₹ 1,600 amount to ₹ 1,852.20 at 5% per annum compound interest?

Answer: A. 3

128. Simplify: (-1/3) - {1 ÷ (2/3 × 5/7) + 8 - [5 - (1/2 - 1/4)]}

Answer: B. -5 41/60

129. Find the area of the iron sheet required to prepare a cone 24 cm high with base radius 7 cm

Answer: A. 704 cm²

130. The sum of the series 31+33+....+53 is

Answer: C. 504

131. Find the G.C.D. of a³-1 and a²-1

Answer: D. a-1

132. What percent is 5 grams of 1 Kg?

Answer: C. 0.5%

133. The value of 9² x 18⁴ / 3¹⁶ is

Answer: C. 16/81

134. The cube root of 0.027 is

Answer: D. 0.3

135. If a+b/c = b+c/a = c+a/b = k, Then the value of k is

Answer: C. 2

136. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 4:5. If the number of boys is 20, then the number of girls is

Answer: C. 25

137. Given the pie chart. Obtain the pass percentage of student who passed in 1st class.

Answer: D. 25%

138. India's first bio-fuel flight was operated between

Answer: A. Dehradun to Delhi

139. In which of the following states, more number of people were affected by 2018 monsoon?

Answer: A. Kerala

140. The crop which gets affected by pink ball worm is

Answer: D. Cotton

141. World's first Block chain Bond was launched by

Answer: B. World Bank

142. Which among the following industries generates invisible exports?

Answer: B. Tourism

143. Which of the following agricultural commodity of India gives largest interms of export value?

Answer: B. Basmathi Rice

144. Which of the following programme aims at the promotion of savings among rural women?

Answer: D. Mahila Samridhi

145. Which of the following policy brings 5 lakh acres under organic farming?

Answer: B. Pramaparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana

146. According to 2018-19 budget report, India is at third rank in

Answer: A. Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)

147. Which currency is recently added in the currency basket of IMF?

Answer: B. Chinese Yuan

148. 1 Femto is equal to ____________ m.

Answer: B. 10⁻¹⁵

149. What is the value of gravitational field at the centre of the sphere?

Answer: A. zero

150. Which of the following is sensitive to ultrasonic waves?

Answer: B. Bat

151. What happens in the reduction process?

Answer: B. gain of electrons

152. Galena is the ore of which of the following metals?

Answer: B. Lead

153. In higher plants the sporophytic phase is formed after growth and development of the zygote. Point out the type of cell division involved in this growth.

Answer: A. Mitosis

154. Kuhne's tube can demonstrate the process of

Answer: A. Fermentation

155. A preparation of living (or) killed micro-organism (or) viruses used in prevention of diseases through immunization is called

Answer: B. Vaccine

156. The source of energy in any eco system are

Answer: B. Autotrophs

157. The Acid Rain destroys the vegetation, because it contains

Answer: D. Sulphuric acid

158. Which of the following statements regarding soil are correct? I. Soil is a dynamic natural element II. It is composed of both mineral and organic matter III. The characteristics of soils depend on parent rock IV. All the soils having a well developed soil profile

Answer: B. I, II and III are correct

159. Name the place where the River Ganga and Yamuna joins?

Answer: A. Allahabad

160. Name the dam constructed across the River Sutlej

Answer: B. Bakra Nangal dam

161. In which year Madras Mahajana Sabha was started?

Answer: D. 1884

162. Who was the founder of the Thiruchenkodu Ashram?

Answer: B. Rajaji

163. Annamalai University was started in the year

Answer: B. 1929

164. Where was the first nuclear reactor of Asia established?

Answer: D. India

165. Who assumed the title "Chittrakara Puli"?

Answer: C. Mahendra Varman I

166. Which congress conference was attended by Bharatiyar?

Answer: A. Surat Conference

167. Who extended co-operation to PuliTevar?

Answer: D. The Dutch

168. Which Act provided Provincial Autonomy to India?

Answer: C. The Government of India Act - 1935

169. Whom of the following was the Chief Organiser of the Revolt of 1857 in Bihar?

Answer: B. Kunwar Singh

170. Which one of the following Parliamentary Committee is Semi-Judicial in nature?

Answer: C. Committee on Privileges

171. The Constituent Assembly of India was set up according to the proposals of

Answer: B. The Cabinet Mission Plan

172. Which one is not part of the Panchayat Raj?

Answer: C. Township

173. Which part of the Indian Constitution deals with Fundamental Duties?

Answer: C. Part IV A

174. Metro Rail Service is available in I. Chennai II. Coimbatore III. Trichy IV. Madurai

Answer: A. I only

175. As per 2011 census the total urban population in the state of Tamil Nadu is

Answer: B. 34.95 million

176. Which district of Tamil Nadu produces the highest output of food grains?

Answer: C. Thanjavur

177. At what rate of interest compound interest per annum will ₹ 640 amount to ₹ 774.40 in 2 years

Answer: C. 10%

178. A circus tent is cylindrical to a height of 3 m and conical above it. If the base radius is 52.5 m and slant height of the cone is 53 m, find the area of canvas required to make the tent.

Answer: B. 3097.5 πm²

179. Area of a square is 1/2 hectare. The diagonal of the square is

Answer: B. 100 metres

180. The surface areas of two spheres are in the ratio 9: 25. Then their volumes are in the ratio

Answer: B. 27:125

181. Complete the series 1 ZA, 3 YB, 6 XC, 10 WD, ?

Answer: D. 15 VE

182. The product of two numbers is 1600 and their H.C.F. is 5, the L.C.M. of the number is

Answer: A. 320

183. A man sells two wrist watches at Rs. 594 each. On one he gains 10% and on the other he loses 10%. Find his gain or loss percent on the whole

Answer: C. Loss % = 1%

184. Simplify: (3³)² × (2²)⁻³ / (2⁴)⁻² × 3⁻⁴ × 4⁻²

Answer: A. 7 1/9

185. The principal amount triples itself at 8% per annum over a certain time. Find the number of years.

Answer: B. 25 years

186. What number should be subtracted from each of the numbers 54, 71, 75 and 99 so that the remainders may be proportional?

Answer: C. 3

187. Six men working 10 hours a day can do a piece of work in 24 days. In how many days will 9 men working for 8 hours a day do the same work?

Answer: C. 20 days

188. A can finish a job in 20 days and B can complete it in 30 days. They work together and finish the job. If Rs. 600 is paid as wages, then the share of A and B

Answer: C. 360, 240

189. If the average of the values 18, 41, x, 36, 31, 24, 37, 35, 27, 36 is 31. Find the value of x.

Answer: A. 25

190. Find the difference between simple interest and compound interest for a sum of Rs.8,000 lent at 10% p.a. in 2 years.

Answer: B. Rs.80

191. The national fruit which is common for Phillipines, Pakistan and India is

Answer: C. Mango

192. In which city the first Nepal – India “Think Tank summit” was held?

Answer: D. Kolkata

193. The campaign to create awareness among children to identify sexual abuse is

Answer: B. Our safety, Our rights

194. NREGA gives guaranteed employment for

Answer: B. 100 days

195. The concept of Goods and Services Tax (GST) was originated from

Answer: A. Canada

196. Black revolution is related to

Answer: C. Crude oil production

197. Budget 2017 has proposed _________ as the front office for issuing passport in far flung areas.

Answer: C. Post office

198. The facility announced for Senior Citizens in Budget 2017-2018 is

Answer: A. Aadhaar enabled smart card

199. Golden Handshake scheme is associated with

Answer: D. Voluntary retirement

200. Which of the following scheme has the objective to skill 1 crore youth in the next three years?

Answer: B. PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana
