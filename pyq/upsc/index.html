UPSC Prelims Previous Year Questions (GS Paper I)

117 UPSC prelims General Studies questions from 2015–2023, by year. Tap an option to check your answer.

117 questions2015–2023GS Paper I

UPSC 2023

8 questions

1. Which of the following organisms perform waggle dance for others of their kin to indicate the direction and the distance to a source of their food?

Answer: C. Honeybees

2. Which one of the following makes a tool with a stick to scrape insects from a hole in a tree or a log of wood?

Answer: B. Orangutan

3. Which one of the following best describes the concept of 'Small Farmer Large Field'?

Answer: B. Many marginal farmers in an area organize themselves into groups and synchronize and harmonize selected agricultural operations

4. In essence, what does 'Due Process of Law' mean?

Answer: A. The principle of natural justice

5. Which one of the following statements best reflects the chief purpose of the 'Constitution' of a country?

Answer: C. It defines and limits the powers of government

6. In India, which one of the following Constitutional Amendments was widely believed to be enacted to overcome the judicial interpretations of the Fundamental Rights?

Answer: A. 1st Amendment

7. With reference to ancient South India, Korkai, Poompuhar and Muchiri were well known as

Answer: B. ports

8. "Souls are not only the property of animal and plant life, but also of rocks, running water and many other natural objects not looked on as living by other religious sects." This reflects a core belief of

Answer: B. Jainism

UPSC 2022

14 questions

9. 'Rapid Financing Instrument' and 'Rapid Credit Facility' are related to the provisions of lending by which one of the following?

Answer: B. International Monetary Fund

10. Gandikota canyon of South India was created by which one of the following rivers?

Answer: C. Pennar

11. The term 'Levant' often heard in the news roughly corresponds to which of the following regions?

Answer: A. Region along the eastern Mediterranean shores

12. In the northern hemisphere, the longest day of the year normally occurs in the

Answer: B. Second half of the month of June

13. Which one of the following is the context in which the term 'qubit' is mentioned?

Answer: B. Quantum Computing

14. 'Climate Action Tracker' which monitors the emission reduction pledges of different countries is a

Answer: A. Database created by coalition of research organisations

15. Which of the following is not a bird?

Answer: A. Golden Mahseer

16. The 'Miyawaki method' is well known for the

Answer: C. Creation of mini forests in urban areas

17. In medieval India, the term 'Fanam' referred to

Answer: B. Coins

18. In India, which one of the following is responsible for maintaining price stability by controlling inflation?

Answer: D. Reserve Bank of India

19. In India, which one of the following compiles information on industrial disputes, closures, retrenchments and lay-offs in factories employing workers?

Answer: C. Labour Bureau

20. If a particular area is brought under the Fifth Schedule of the Constitution of India, which one of the following statements best reflects the consequence of it?

Answer: A. This would prevent the transfer of land of tribal people to non-tribal people

21. Which one of the following has been constituted under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986?

Answer: C. Central Ground Water Authority

22. Consider: 1. Carbon monoxide 2. Nitrogen oxide 3. Ozone 4. Sulphur dioxide. Excess of which of the above in the environment is/are cause(s) of acid rain?

Answer: B. 2 and 4 only

UPSC 2021

23 questions

23. Which among the following steps is most likely to be taken at the time of an economic recession?

Answer: B. Increase in expenditure on public projects

24. Which one of the following effects of creation of black money in India has been the main cause of worry to the Government of India?

Answer: D. Loss of revenue to the State Exchequer due to tax evasion

25. Which one of the following is likely to be the most inflationary in its effects?

Answer: D. Creation of new money to finance a budget deficit

26. The money multiplier in an economy increases with which one of the following?

Answer: C. Increase in the banking habit of the people

27. What is blue carbon?

Answer: A. Carbon captured by oceans and coastal ecosystems

28. Which one of the following is used in preparing a natural mosquito repellent?

Answer: C. Lemon grass

29. With reference to the history of ancient India, Bhavabhuti, Hastimalla and Kshemeshvara were famous

Answer: B. playwrights

30. Consider: 1. The Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms of 1919 recommended granting voting rights to all women above 21. 2. The Government of India Act of 1935 gave women reserved seats in legislature. Which is/are correct?

Answer: B. 2 only

31. With reference to 8th August, 1942 in Indian history, which one of the following statements is correct?

Answer: A. The Quit India Resolution was adopted by the AICC

32. With reference to the Indus river system, three of the following four rivers pour into one of them which joins the Indus direct. Which one joins the Indus direct?

Answer: D. Sutlej

33. Among the following, which one is the least water-efficient crop?

Answer: A. Sugarcane

34. With reference to water on Earth: 1. The amount of water in rivers and lakes is more than the amount of groundwater. 2. The amount of water in polar ice caps and glaciers is more than the amount of groundwater. Which is/are correct?

Answer: B. 2 only

35. The black cotton soil of India has been formed due to the weathering of

Answer: B. fissure volcanic rock

36. Which one of the following is a reason why astronomical distances are measured in light-years?

Answer: D. Speed of light is always same

37. 'Right to Privacy' is protected under which Article of the Constitution of India?

Answer: C. Article 21

38. At the national level, which ministry is the nodal agency for the Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006?

Answer: D. Ministry of Tribal Affairs

39. A legislation which confers on the executive or administrative authority an unguided and uncontrolled discretionary power in the application of law violates which Article?

Answer: A. Article 14

40. Which one of the following in Indian polity is an essential feature that indicates that it is federal in character?

Answer: A. The independence of judiciary is safeguarded

41. Which one of the following factors constitutes the best safeguard of liberty in a liberal democracy?

Answer: D. Separation of powers

42. Under the Indian Constitution, concentration of wealth violates

Answer: B. the Directive Principles of State Policy

43. What is the position of the Right to Property in India?

Answer: B. Legal right available to any person

44. What was the exact constitutional status of India on 26th January, 1950?

Answer: B. A Sovereign Democratic Republic

45. Constitutional government means

Answer: D. a government limited by the terms of the Constitution

UPSC 2020

9 questions

46. The responsibility of preparing a State List (the Third List) of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution rests with the Parliament.

Answer: B. The Constitution grants this responsibility to the State Legislature.

47. Which of the following is a judicial power vested in the Speaker of the Lok Sabha?

Answer: B. To admit petitions in Hindi

48. Which of the following is a feature of a Constitutional Monarchy?

Answer: C. The Head of State has absolute powers and is not accountable to anyone

49. Which of the following is the primary source of power of the President of India?

Answer: B. Article 74 of the Constitution

50. The power of the President to grant pardons, reprieves and remissions of punishment is derived from the

Answer: A. Article 72

51. Article 19 (1)(a) of the Constitution of India gives the right of equality and freedom of speech etc. Which of the following exceptions is/are NOT provided under it?

Answer: C. Exception 6

52. Which of the following is a feature of parliamentary system of government in India?

Answer: A. The Speaker is elected by the members of Lok Sabha

53. Which of the following is the primary purpose of the Parliamentary Committee system in India?

Answer: D. To prepare reports on important issues related to the Parliament

54. A proclamation of emergency can be issued by the President only after approval by both the Houses of Parliament if it is

Answer: D. a Financial Emergency

UPSC 2019

9 questions

55. In the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution of India, the powers exercisable by the Parliament with respect to a matter fall under which list?

Answer: A. List I

56. The Secrecy of Ballot Box is due to?

Answer: B. Article 103 of the Constitution of India

57. Which of the following is a type of emergency declared under Article 352 of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: B. National Emergency

58. Which of the following are not the features of the Panchayat system as defined in the 73rd Constitutional Amendment?

Answer: A. c) An elected mayor.

59. Which of the following is the correct order of the Writs issued by a High Court in India? amendment, prohibition, mandamus, injunction, habeas corpus

Answer: C. mandamus, injunction, habeas corpus, amendment, prohibition

60. The 'Qutb Minar' is made up of five succeeding superstructures of which the last one is made of 'pink sandstone' and is only a part of that series in terms of the totality of architectural grandeur.

Answer: B. Qutab Minar

61. Which of the following is a fundamental right under Article 19 of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: C. Right to Form Associations

62. Which of the following is a type of state subject under the Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: B. Public Order

63. Which of the following provisions of the Indian Constitution can be amended by a simple majority in Parliament?

Answer: A. Article 368

UPSC 2018

20 questions

64. 'Rule of Law Index' is released by which of the following?

Answer: D. World Justice Project

65. Which one of the following links all the ATMs in India?

Answer: C. National Payments Corporation of India

66. With reference to the Parliament of India, which Parliamentary Committee scrutinizes and reports whether the powers to make regulations, rules, sub-rules, by-laws, etc. conferred by the Constitution or delegated by Parliament are being properly exercised by the Executive?

Answer: B. Committee on Subordinate Legislation

67. International Labour Organization's Conventions 138 and 182 are related to

Answer: A. Child labour

68. Regarding Money Bill, which of the following statements is not correct?

Answer: C. A Money Bill is concerned with the appropriation of moneys out of the Contingency Fund of India

69. Which one of the following reflects the most appropriate relationship between law and liberty?

Answer: B. If there are no laws, there is no liberty

70. Consider: 1. No criminal proceedings shall be instituted against the Governor of a State in any court during his term of office. 2. The emoluments and allowances of the Governor of a State shall not be diminished during his term of office. Which is/are correct?

Answer: C. Both 1 and 2

71. The well-known painting 'Bani Thani' belongs to the

Answer: D. Kishangarh school

72. What is 'Terminal High Altitude Area Defense (THAAD)', sometimes seen in the news?

Answer: C. An American anti-missile system

73. Which one of the following statements correctly describes the meaning of legal tender money?

Answer: B. The money which a creditor is under compulsion to accept in settlement of his claims

74. If a commodity is provided free to the public by the Government, then

Answer: C. the opportunity cost is transferred from the consumers of the product to the tax-paying public

75. Increase in absolute and per capita real GNP do not connote a higher level of economic development, if

Answer: C. poverty and unemployment increase

76. Despite being a high saving economy, capital formation may not result in significant increase in output due to

Answer: D. high capital-output ratio

77. Economically, one of the results of the British rule in India in the 19th century was the

Answer: C. commercialization of Indian agriculture

78. If the President of India exercises his power under Article 356 in respect of a particular State, then

Answer: B. the powers of the Legislature of that State shall be exercisable by or under the authority of the Parliament

79. Which of the following statements best describes 'carbon fertilization'?

Answer: A. Increased plant growth due to increased concentration of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere

80. Who among the following were the founders of the 'Hind Mazdoor Sabha' established in 1948?

Answer: D. Ashok Mehta, T.S. Ramanujam and G.G. Mehta

81. With reference to religious practices in India, the 'Sthanakvasi' sect belongs to

Answer: B. Jainism

82. Right to Privacy is protected as an intrinsic part of Right to Life and Personal Liberty. Which of the following in the Constitution correctly and appropriately imply the above statement?

Answer: C. Article 21 and the freedoms guaranteed in Part III

83. In which one of the following States is Pakhui Wildlife Sanctuary located?

Answer: A. Arunachal Pradesh

UPSC 2017

25 questions

84. From the ecological point of view, which one of the following assumes importance in being a good link between the Eastern Ghats and the Western Ghats?

Answer: A. Sathyamangalam Tiger Reserve

85. Which principle was added to the Directive Principles of State Policy by the 42nd Amendment to the Constitution?

Answer: B. Participation of workers in the management of industries

86. Which one of the following statements is correct?

Answer: C. Rights are claims of the citizens against the State

87. Which of the following gives 'Global Gender Gap Index' ranking to the countries of the world?

Answer: A. World Economic Forum

88. The Trade Disputes Act of 1929 provided for

Answer: D. a system of tribunals and a ban on strikes

89. The object of the Butler Committee of 1927 was to

Answer: D. Improve the relationship between the Government of India and the Indian States

90. The term 'Domestic Content Requirement' is sometimes seen in the news with reference to

Answer: A. Developing solar power production in our country

91. Who among the following can join the National Pension System (NPS)?

Answer: C. All State Government employees joining the services after the date of notification by the respective State Governments

92. Which one of the following objectives is not embodied in the Preamble to the Constitution of India?

Answer: B. Economic liberty

93. Democracy's superior virtue lies in the fact that it calls into activity

Answer: A. the intelligence and character of ordinary men and women

94. The terms 'Event Horizon', 'Singularity', 'String Theory' and 'Standard Model' are sometimes seen in the news in the context of

Answer: A. Observation and understanding of the Universe

95. The main advantage of the parliamentary form of government is that

Answer: C. the executive remains responsible to the legislature

96. In the context of India, which one of the following is the correct relationship between Rights and Duties?

Answer: A. Rights are correlative with Duties

97. For election to the Lok Sabha, a nomination paper can be filed by

Answer: C. any citizen of India whose name appears in the electoral roll of a constituency

98. Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD) is a standard criterion for

Answer: C. Pollution assay in aquatic ecosystems

99. In the context of Indian history, the principle of 'Dyarchy (diarchy)' refers to

Answer: D. Division of the subjects delegated to the provinces into two categories

100. Which of the following statements best describes the term 'Scheme for Sustainable Structuring of Stressed Assets (S4A)'?

Answer: B. It is a scheme of RBI for reworking the financial structure of big corporate entities facing genuine difficulties

101. The painting of Bodhisattva Padmapani is one of the most famous and oft-illustrated paintings at

Answer: A. Ajanta

102. What is the importance of developing Chabahar Port by India?

Answer: C. India will not depend on Pakistan for access to Afghanistan and Central Asia

103. Right to vote and to be elected in India is a

Answer: C. Constitutional Right

104. What is the purpose of 'evolved Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (eLISA)' project?

Answer: B. To detect gravitational waves

105. What is the aim of the programme 'Unnat Bharat Abhiyan'?

Answer: B. Connecting institutions of higher education with local communities to address development challenges through appropriate technologies

106. In India, Judicial Review implies

Answer: A. the power of the Judiciary to pronounce upon the constitutionality of laws and executive orders

107. With reference to Indian freedom struggle, arrange chronologically: 1. Mutiny in Royal Indian Navy 2. Quit India Movement launched 3. Second Round Table Conference

Answer: C. 3-2-1

108. Out of the following statements, choose the one that brings out the principle underlying the Cabinet form of Government:

Answer: C. A mechanism of parliamentary democracy for ensuring collective responsibility of the Government to the people

UPSC 2016

5 questions

109. The Preamble to the Constitution of India is a part of the Constitution because it has been incorporated through the

Answer: C. Article 394(1)

110. Which one of the following best describes the Panchayats in the Indian Constitution?

Answer: A. Elected bodies at the village level to which extends to Urban Area

111. Which of the following is/are a fundamental duties under Article 51A of the Constitution?

Answer: C. c) and d)

112. Which of the following is the constitutional basis for the power of Parliament to regulate the rights of citizens, including fundamental rights, through laws?

Answer: C. Article 77

113. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the power to summon and enforce attendance of witnesses in the Indian Parliament?

Answer: C. Article 109

UPSC 2015

4 questions

114. Which of the following provisions is/are a limitation on the discretion of the President in exercising his executive power?

Answer: C. Article 123 (4)

115. The President of India has the power to appoint the Prime Minister under Article ______ of the Constitution.

Answer: B. 75

116. If there is a contravention of the provisions of Part III of the Constitution

Answer: C. the limits of the state can be withdrawn for 6 months by the President.

117. Which of the following provisions of the Indian Constitution is primarily aimed at addressing the socio-economic and political inequalities in rural India?

Answer: A. The Fifth and Sixth Schedules of the Constitution
