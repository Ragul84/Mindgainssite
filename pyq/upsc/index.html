84 questions2015–2023GS Paper I

UPSC 2023

3 questions

1. The term 'Panchayat' refers to a governing body elected by villagers and responsible for local self-government and administration of affairs of villages.

Answer: A. Article 243-A

2. The Chairman of the Planning Commission and the heads of the State Planning Boards cannot be removed from their offices on the recommendation of the Parliament and the Legislatures respectively, under

Answer: A. Article 312

3. The Economic and Social Council is set up by

Answer: C. Article 340 of the Constitution

UPSC 2022

4 questions

4. The provision for free and compulsory education for children between 6-14 years age group has been made under?

Answer: C. Article 39 of the Constitution

5. The 73rd Amendinent to The Constitution set-up three-tiured Panchayat a politics : P/

Answer: C. Zilla Parishad

6. The recent example of New Member States of european Union is

Answer: A. Itahary ,Malta and Czech

7. Which of the following is not a basic structure of the Indian Polity as enshrined in Article 370 of the Constitution?

Answer: B. Unitary Type of Government

UPSC 2021

23 questions

8. Which among the following steps is most likely to be taken at the time of an economic recession?

Answer: B. Increase in expenditure on public projects

9. Which one of the following effects of creation of black money in India has been the main cause of worry to the Government of India?

Answer: D. Loss of revenue to the State Exchequer due to tax evasion

10. Which one of the following is likely to be the most inflationary in its effects?

Answer: D. Creation of new money to finance a budget deficit

11. The money multiplier in an economy increases with which one of the following?

Answer: C. Increase in the banking habit of the people

12. What is blue carbon?

Answer: A. Carbon captured by oceans and coastal ecosystems

13. Which one of the following is used in preparing a natural mosquito repellent?

Answer: C. Lemon grass

14. With reference to the history of ancient India, Bhavabhuti, Hastimalla and Kshemeshvara were famous

Answer: B. playwrights

15. Consider: 1. The Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms of 1919 recommended granting voting rights to all women above 21. 2. The Government of India Act of 1935 gave women reserved seats in legislature. Which is/are correct?

Answer: B. 2 only

16. With reference to 8th August, 1942 in Indian history, which one of the following statements is correct?

Answer: A. The Quit India Resolution was adopted by the AICC

17. With reference to the Indus river system, three of the following four rivers pour into one of them which joins the Indus direct. Which one joins the Indus direct?

Answer: D. Sutlej

18. Among the following, which one is the least water-efficient crop?

Answer: A. Sugarcane

19. With reference to water on Earth: 1. The amount of water in rivers and lakes is more than the amount of groundwater. 2. The amount of water in polar ice caps and glaciers is more than the amount of groundwater. Which is/are correct?

Answer: B. 2 only

20. The black cotton soil of India has been formed due to the weathering of

Answer: B. fissure volcanic rock

21. Which one of the following is a reason why astronomical distances are measured in light-years?

Answer: D. Speed of light is always same

22. 'Right to Privacy' is protected under which Article of the Constitution of India?

Answer: C. Article 21

23. At the national level, which ministry is the nodal agency for the Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006?

Answer: D. Ministry of Tribal Affairs

24. A legislation which confers on the executive or administrative authority an unguided and uncontrolled discretionary power in the application of law violates which Article?

Answer: A. Article 14

25. Which one of the following in Indian polity is an essential feature that indicates that it is federal in character?

Answer: A. The independence of judiciary is safeguarded

26. Which one of the following factors constitutes the best safeguard of liberty in a liberal democracy?

Answer: D. Separation of powers

27. Under the Indian Constitution, concentration of wealth violates

Answer: B. the Directive Principles of State Policy

28. What is the position of the Right to Property in India?

Answer: B. Legal right available to any person

29. What was the exact constitutional status of India on 26th January, 1950?

Answer: B. A Sovereign Democratic Republic

30. Constitutional government means

Answer: D. a government limited by the terms of the Constitution

UPSC 2020

9 questions

31. The responsibility of preparing a State List (the Third List) of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution rests with the Parliament.

Answer: B. The Constitution grants this responsibility to the State Legislature.

32. Which of the following is a judicial power vested in the Speaker of the Lok Sabha?

Answer: B. To admit petitions in Hindi

33. Which of the following is a feature of a Constitutional Monarchy?

Answer: C. The Head of State has absolute powers and is not accountable to anyone

34. Which of the following is the primary source of power of the President of India?

Answer: B. Article 74 of the Constitution

35. The power of the President to grant pardons, reprieves and remissions of punishment is derived from the

Answer: A. Article 72

36. Article 19 (1)(a) of the Constitution of India gives the right of equality and freedom of speech etc. Which of the following exceptions is/are NOT provided under it?

Answer: C. Exception 6

37. Which of the following is a feature of parliamentary system of government in India?

Answer: A. The Speaker is elected by the members of Lok Sabha

38. Which of the following is the primary purpose of the Parliamentary Committee system in India?

Answer: D. To prepare reports on important issues related to the Parliament

39. A proclamation of emergency can be issued by the President only after approval by both the Houses of Parliament if it is

Answer: D. a Financial Emergency

UPSC 2019

9 questions

40. In the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution of India, the powers exercisable by the Parliament with respect to a matter fall under which list?

Answer: A. List I

41. The Secrecy of Ballot Box is due to?

Answer: B. Article 103 of the Constitution of India

42. Which of the following is a type of emergency declared under Article 352 of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: B. National Emergency

43. Which of the following are not the features of the Panchayat system as defined in the 73rd Constitutional Amendment?

Answer: A. c) An elected mayor.

44. Which of the following is the correct order of the Writs issued by a High Court in India? amendment, prohibition, mandamus, injunction, habeas corpus

Answer: C. mandamus, injunction, habeas corpus, amendment, prohibition

45. The 'Qutb Minar' is made up of five succeeding superstructures of which the last one is made of 'pink sandstone' and is only a part of that series in terms of the totality of architectural grandeur.

Answer: B. Qutab Minar

46. Which of the following is a fundamental right under Article 19 of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: C. Right to Form Associations

47. Which of the following is a type of state subject under the Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: B. Public Order

48. Which of the following provisions of the Indian Constitution can be amended by a simple majority in Parliament?

Answer: A. Article 368

UPSC 2018

20 questions

49. 'Rule of Law Index' is released by which of the following?

Answer: D. World Justice Project

50. Which one of the following links all the ATMs in India?

Answer: C. National Payments Corporation of India

51. With reference to the Parliament of India, which Parliamentary Committee scrutinizes and reports whether the powers to make regulations, rules, sub-rules, by-laws, etc. conferred by the Constitution or delegated by Parliament are being properly exercised by the Executive?

Answer: B. Committee on Subordinate Legislation

52. International Labour Organization's Conventions 138 and 182 are related to

Answer: A. Child labour

53. Regarding Money Bill, which of the following statements is not correct?

Answer: C. A Money Bill is concerned with the appropriation of moneys out of the Contingency Fund of India

54. Which one of the following reflects the most appropriate relationship between law and liberty?

Answer: B. If there are no laws, there is no liberty

55. Consider: 1. No criminal proceedings shall be instituted against the Governor of a State in any court during his term of office. 2. The emoluments and allowances of the Governor of a State shall not be diminished during his term of office. Which is/are correct?

Answer: C. Both 1 and 2

56. The well-known painting 'Bani Thani' belongs to the

Answer: D. Kishangarh school

57. What is 'Terminal High Altitude Area Defense (THAAD)', sometimes seen in the news?

Answer: C. An American anti-missile system

58. Which one of the following statements correctly describes the meaning of legal tender money?

Answer: B. The money which a creditor is under compulsion to accept in settlement of his claims

59. If a commodity is provided free to the public by the Government, then

Answer: C. the opportunity cost is transferred from the consumers of the product to the tax-paying public

60. Increase in absolute and per capita real GNP do not connote a higher level of economic development, if

Answer: C. poverty and unemployment increase

61. Despite being a high saving economy, capital formation may not result in significant increase in output due to

Answer: D. high capital-output ratio

62. Economically, one of the results of the British rule in India in the 19th century was the

Answer: C. commercialization of Indian agriculture

63. If the President of India exercises his power under Article 356 in respect of a particular State, then

Answer: B. the powers of the Legislature of that State shall be exercisable by or under the authority of the Parliament

64. Which of the following statements best describes 'carbon fertilization'?

Answer: A. Increased plant growth due to increased concentration of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere

65. Who among the following were the founders of the 'Hind Mazdoor Sabha' established in 1948?

Answer: D. Ashok Mehta, T.S. Ramanujam and G.G. Mehta

66. With reference to religious practices in India, the 'Sthanakvasi' sect belongs to

Answer: B. Jainism

67. Right to Privacy is protected as an intrinsic part of Right to Life and Personal Liberty. Which of the following in the Constitution correctly and appropriately imply the above statement?

Answer: C. Article 21 and the freedoms guaranteed in Part III

68. In which one of the following States is Pakhui Wildlife Sanctuary located?

Answer: A. Arunachal Pradesh

UPSC 2017

7 questions

69. Under the Constitution of India, which one of the following is a specic power of the Governor of a State?

Answer: D. he has no discretionary powers in judicial appointments

70. Which of the following Commissions is not a part of the structure of the Indian Union as mandated under Article 324 of the Constitution?

Answer: D. Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission

71. State items falling within an entry in List II of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution, but not covered by any of the items in List III, can be regulated by

Answer: B. Centre in case of terra multipliers and State in case of terra singularis

72. Which one of the following is NOT a fundamental right?

Answer: A. Right to Property

73. In the context of electoral reforms, which of the following statements is incorrect?

Answer: A. Electronic Voting Machine should be used

74. Which of the following is/are a valid reason for the postponement of the holding of polls under Article 172(1) of the Constitution?

Answer: B. Dissolution of the Lok Sabha.

75. What is the main purpose of the Advocate-General in a State?

Answer: A. To act as the legal advisor to the State Government

UPSC 2016

5 questions

76. The Preamble to the Constitution of India is a part of the Constitution because it has been incorporated through the

Answer: C. Article 394(1)

77. Which one of the following best describes the Panchayats in the Indian Constitution?

Answer: A. Elected bodies at the village level to which extends to Urban Area

78. Which of the following is/are a fundamental duties under Article 51A of the Constitution?

Answer: C. c) and d)

79. Which of the following is the constitutional basis for the power of Parliament to regulate the rights of citizens, including fundamental rights, through laws?

Answer: C. Article 77

80. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the power to summon and enforce attendance of witnesses in the Indian Parliament?

Answer: C. Article 109

UPSC 2015

4 questions

81. Which of the following provisions is/are a limitation on the discretion of the President in exercising his executive power?

Answer: C. Article 123 (4)

82. The President of India has the power to appoint the Prime Minister under Article ______ of the Constitution.

Answer: B. 75

83. If there is a contravention of the provisions of Part III of the Constitution

Answer: C. the limits of the state can be withdrawn for 6 months by the President.

84. Which of the following provisions of the Indian Constitution is primarily aimed at addressing the socio-economic and political inequalities in rural India?

Answer: A. The Fifth and Sixth Schedules of the Constitution
