UPSC 2023

3 questions

1. The term 'Panchayat' refers to a governing body elected by villagers and responsible for local self-government and administration of affairs of villages.

Answer: A. Article 243-A

2. The Chairman of the Planning Commission and the heads of the State Planning Boards cannot be removed from their offices on the recommendation of the Parliament and the Legislatures respectively, under

Answer: A. Article 312

3. The Economic and Social Council is set up by

Answer: C. Article 340 of the Constitution

UPSC 2022

4 questions

4. The provision for free and compulsory education for children between 6-14 years age group has been made under?

Answer: C. Article 39 of the Constitution

5. The 73rd Amendinent to The Constitution set-up three-tiured Panchayat a politics : P/

Answer: C. Zilla Parishad

6. The recent example of New Member States of european Union is

Answer: A. Itahary ,Malta and Czech

7. Which of the following is not a basic structure of the Indian Polity as enshrined in Article 370 of the Constitution?

Answer: B. Unitary Type of Government

UPSC 2021

5 questions

8. Which of the following is a constitutional provision for the protection of the basic human rights of citizens?

Answer: A. The Right to Equality

9. The principal purpose of Reservation of seats by reservation for Scheduled Castes/ ScheduledÞtrribes and women in legislative bodies in India is

Answer: D. To breakdown of any and all deprivations that are harmful and discriminatory in nature

10. What is the procedure to add a new Schedule to the Indian Constitution?

Answer: C. Schedule is added by the Lok Sabha by a two-thirds majority vote

11. Which of the following is an essential feature of a union territory in the Indian Constitution?

Answer: A. It has its own High Court

12. Which of the following is an essential feature of parliamentary democracy in India?

Answer: A. Separation of Powers

UPSC 2020

9 questions

13. The responsibility of preparing a State List (the Third List) of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution rests with the Parliament.

Answer: B. The Constitution grants this responsibility to the State Legislature.

14. Which of the following is a judicial power vested in the Speaker of the Lok Sabha?

Answer: B. To admit petitions in Hindi

15. Which of the following is a feature of a Constitutional Monarchy?

Answer: C. The Head of State has absolute powers and is not accountable to anyone

16. Which of the following is the primary source of power of the President of India?

Answer: B. Article 74 of the Constitution

17. The power of the President to grant pardons, reprieves and remissions of punishment is derived from the

Answer: A. Article 72

18. Article 19 (1)(a) of the Constitution of India gives the right of equality and freedom of speech etc. Which of the following exceptions is/are NOT provided under it?

Answer: C. Exception 6

19. Which of the following is a feature of parliamentary system of government in India?

Answer: A. The Speaker is elected by the members of Lok Sabha

20. Which of the following is the primary purpose of the Parliamentary Committee system in India?

Answer: D. To prepare reports on important issues related to the Parliament

21. A proclamation of emergency can be issued by the President only after approval by both the Houses of Parliament if it is

Answer: D. a Financial Emergency

UPSC 2019

9 questions

22. In the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution of India, the powers exercisable by the Parliament with respect to a matter fall under which list?

Answer: A. List I

23. The Secrecy of Ballot Box is due to?

Answer: B. Article 103 of the Constitution of India

24. Which of the following is a type of emergency declared under Article 352 of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: B. National Emergency

25. Which of the following are not the features of the Panchayat system as defined in the 73rd Constitutional Amendment?

Answer: A. c) An elected mayor.

26. Which of the following is the correct order of the Writs issued by a High Court in India? amendment, prohibition, mandamus, injunction, habeas corpus

Answer: C. mandamus, injunction, habeas corpus, amendment, prohibition

27. The 'Qutb Minar' is made up of five succeeding superstructures of which the last one is made of 'pink sandstone' and is only a part of that series in terms of the totality of architectural grandeur.

Answer: B. Qutab Minar

28. Which of the following is a fundamental right under Article 19 of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: C. Right to Form Associations

29. Which of the following is a type of state subject under the Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution?

Answer: B. Public Order

30. Which of the following provisions of the Indian Constitution can be amended by a simple majority in Parliament?

Answer: A. Article 368

UPSC 2018

10 questions

31. Not withstanding that the Indian Parliament is a sovereign legislative body

Answer: B. it has given power to the Supreme Court to inspect its own proceedings and to decide whether these have been in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution

32. Article 300A of the Indian Constitution talks about which of the following?

Answer: D. Abolition of Titles and Honours

33. The principle of inertia is formulated in the Constitution of India as?

Answer: A. In the Preamble to the Constitution

34. The Indian Constitution has incorporated certain provisions to ensure that the interests of the marginalized sections of society are protected. Which of the following is/are the features of these provisions?

Answer: D. Equal pay for equal work for women

35. Which of the following is not a power of the Union Parliament?

Answer: A. Article 87(1)

36. Which of the following is NOT a part of the Preamble to the Constitution of India?

Answer: D. Socialism

37. What is the primary purpose of the Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946?

Answer: A. To appoint a Lok Ayukta for Punjab and Haryana High Courts

38. Which of the following is the purpose of the Interstate Council (ISC)?

Answer: A. To coordinate and promote economic development between states

39. The official language of the Union Parliament and the official language or languages of a State or Union territory of India are

Answer: B. Both Hindi and English but both shall have equal honour and status.

40. Which of the following is the relationship between the President and the Council of Ministers?

Answer: D. The President is the head of the Council of Ministers

UPSC 2017

7 questions

41. Under the Constitution of India, which one of the following is a specic power of the Governor of a State?

Answer: D. he has no discretionary powers in judicial appointments

42. Which of the following Commissions is not a part of the structure of the Indian Union as mandated under Article 324 of the Constitution?

Answer: D. Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission

43. State items falling within an entry in List II of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution, but not covered by any of the items in List III, can be regulated by

Answer: B. Centre in case of terra multipliers and State in case of terra singularis

44. Which one of the following is NOT a fundamental right?

Answer: A. Right to Property

45. In the context of electoral reforms, which of the following statements is incorrect?

Answer: A. Electronic Voting Machine should be used

46. Which of the following is/are a valid reason for the postponement of the holding of polls under Article 172(1) of the Constitution?

Answer: B. Dissolution of the Lok Sabha.

47. What is the main purpose of the Advocate-General in a State?

Answer: A. To act as the legal advisor to the State Government

UPSC 2016

5 questions

48. The Preamble to the Constitution of India is a part of the Constitution because it has been incorporated through the

Answer: C. Article 394(1)

49. Which one of the following best describes the Panchayats in the Indian Constitution?

Answer: A. Elected bodies at the village level to which extends to Urban Area

50. Which of the following is/are a fundamental duties under Article 51A of the Constitution?

Answer: C. c) and d)

51. Which of the following is the constitutional basis for the power of Parliament to regulate the rights of citizens, including fundamental rights, through laws?

Answer: C. Article 77

52. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the power to summon and enforce attendance of witnesses in the Indian Parliament?

Answer: C. Article 109

UPSC 2015

4 questions

53. Which of the following provisions is/are a limitation on the discretion of the President in exercising his executive power?

Answer: C. Article 123 (4)

54. The President of India has the power to appoint the Prime Minister under Article ______ of the Constitution.

Answer: B. 75

55. If there is a contravention of the provisions of Part III of the Constitution

Answer: C. the limits of the state can be withdrawn for 6 months by the President.
