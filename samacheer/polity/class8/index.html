Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Class8 questions to revise the Samacheer Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 178 questions.

1. Which article of the Indian Constitution deals with the Right to Equality?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32

Answer: Article 14

Explanation: Article 14 of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Right to Equality to all citizens.

2. Who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. B.R. Ambedkar
  4. Sardar Patel

Answer: B.R. Ambedkar

Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is widely regarded as the Father of the Indian Constitution for his pivotal role in its drafting.

3. Which of the following is NOT a fundamental right under the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Freedom of Speech
  2. Right to Education
  3. Right to Property
  4. Right to Equality

Answer: Right to Property

Explanation: The Right to Property was removed from the list of fundamental rights and made a legal right under the 44th Amendment.

4. The Indian Constitution was adopted on which date?

  1. 26 January 1950
  2. 15 August 1947
  3. 26 November 1949
  4. 2 October 1949

Answer: 26 November 1949

Explanation: The Indian Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into effect on 26 January 1950.

5. Which of the following is a feature of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Unitary System
  2. Federal System
  3. Monarchy
  4. Theocracy

Answer: Federal System

Explanation: The Indian Constitution establishes a federal system of government with a strong central authority.
