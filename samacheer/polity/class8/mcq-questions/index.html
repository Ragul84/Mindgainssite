26. Match List I with List II: List I (Method) A. Naturalisation B. Birth C. Registration List II (Description) 1. Applying after residing for a period 2. Born within the territory 3. Applying through a specific legal process

  1. A-1, B-3, C-2
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1
  4. A-2, B-1, C-3

Answer: A-1, B-2, C-3

Explanation: Citizenship by birth comes from being born in India, naturalisation after residing for the required period and registration through a set legal application.

27. Match List I with List II: List I (Term) A. Deprivation B. Renunciation C. Termination List II (Meaning) 1. Forced loss due to fraud 2. Loss due to acquiring another citizenship 3. Giving up citizenship voluntarily

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3
  2. A-2, B-3, C-1
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2

Answer: A-1, B-3, C-2

Explanation: Renunciation is giving up citizenship voluntarily, termination is automatic on taking another citizenship and deprivation is forced loss, for example for fraud.

28. Match the following duties with their categories.

  1. Fundamental Duty
  2. Political Duty
  3. Civic Duty

Answer: Fundamental Duty

Explanation: Taxes are civic, environment is fundamental, and voting is political.

29. Which of the following is a fundamental principle of democracy?

  1. Rule by a single dictator
  2. Equality and justice for all citizens
  3. Hereditary succession of power
  4. Limited participation of the public

Answer: Equality and justice for all citizens

Explanation: Democracy is based on the principles of equality, justice, and universal suffrage.

30. Assertion: Universal Adult Franchise is a cornerstone of democracy. Reason: It ensures that all eligible citizens have the right to vote, irrespective of their social or economic status.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: Universal Adult Franchise is essential for democratic representation, as it allows all adults to participate in electing their representatives.

31. In a democracy, the ultimate power rests with the:

  1. President
  2. Prime Minister
  3. People
  4. Military

Answer: People

Explanation: In a democracy, the sovereignty lies with the citizens who elect their representatives.

32. Which of the following statements about the Preamble to the Indian Constitution is INCORRECT?

  1. It declares India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic.
  2. It guarantees justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity to all its citizens.
  3. It states that the Constitution is adopted and enacted by the Parliament alone.
  4. It emphasizes the unity and integrity of the nation.

Answer: It states that the Constitution is adopted and enacted by the Parliament alone.

Explanation: The Preamble states that the Constitution is adopted, enacted, and given to themselves by the people of India.

33. Consider the following statements about the role of the opposition in a democracy:

  1. The opposition plays a crucial role in holding the government accountable.
  2. It provides constructive criticism and alternative policies.
  3. The opposition's primary function is to obstruct all government actions.
  4. It ensures that diverse viewpoints are represented in policy-making.

Answer: The opposition's primary function is to obstruct all government actions.

Explanation: While the opposition criticizes, its role is constructive, not just obstructionist.

34. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a democratic government?

  1. Free and fair elections
  2. Protection of fundamental rights
  3. Accountability of the government to the people
  4. Suppression of dissent

Answer: Suppression of dissent

Explanation: Democracy thrives on the freedom to express dissent and is accountable to the people.

35. The concept of 'Rule of Law' in a democracy implies that:

  1. Everyone is equal before the law, including the rulers.
  2. Laws are made and enforced arbitrarily by the government.
  3. The judiciary has no independent power.
  4. The rights of citizens can be easily violated.

Answer: Everyone is equal before the law, including the rulers.

Explanation: Rule of Law means that no one is above the law, and all are subject to it equally.

36. Assertion: A democratic government is accountable to its citizens. Reason: Citizens can question the government and hold it responsible for its actions through various mechanisms.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: Accountability in a democracy is achieved through citizen participation, elections, and public scrutiny.

37. Which of the following is a fundamental right guaranteed by the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to property
  2. Right to education
  3. Right to work
  4. Right to information

Answer: Right to education

Explanation: The Right to Education is a fundamental right in India, ensuring free and compulsory education for children aged 6-14.

38. Consider the following statements about the importance of participation in a democracy:

  1. Citizen participation ensures that the government is responsive to people's needs.
  2. It legitimizes the decisions taken by the government.
  3. Participation is limited to casting a vote during elections.
  4. Active participation helps in strengthening democratic institutions.

Answer: Participation is limited to casting a vote during elections.

Explanation: Participation in a democracy extends beyond voting to include engagement in public discourse and decision-making processes.

39. The term 'Sovereignty' in the context of a nation means:

  1. The country is ruled by a king.
  2. The country is free from external control.
  3. The country has a strong military.
  4. The country is a member of the United Nations.

Answer: The country is free from external control.

Explanation: Sovereignty implies that a state has supreme power or authority within its territory and is independent of external control.

40. Which of the following is a feature of a democratic society?

  1. Respect for diversity and different opinions.
  2. Absence of any form of discrimination.
  3. Centralized control over all aspects of life.
  4. Limited freedom of speech and expression.

Answer: Respect for diversity and different opinions.

Explanation: Democratic societies value diversity, equality, and freedom of expression.

41. Consider the following statements about the concept of 'Liberty' in a democracy:

  1. Liberty means complete freedom without any restrictions.
  2. It includes freedom of thought, expression, belief, faith, and worship.
  3. Liberty is always accompanied by responsibility.
  4. In a democracy, liberty is guaranteed by the constitution.

Answer: It includes freedom of thought, expression, belief, faith, and worship.

Explanation: Liberty in a democracy is not absolute; it comes with responsibilities and is subject to reasonable restrictions.

42. The principle of 'Equality' in a democracy means:

  1. Everyone is equal in terms of wealth and income.
  2. All citizens are equal before the law and are entitled to equal protection.
  3. There should be no special privileges for anyone.
  4. Equality of opportunity is guaranteed to all.

Answer: Everyone is equal in terms of wealth and income.

Explanation: Equality in a democracy primarily refers to legal and social equality, not necessarily economic equality.

43. The Constitution of India was adopted on:

  1. 26 January 1950
  2. 15 August 1947
  3. 26 November 1949
  4. 02 October 1950

Answer: 26 November 1949

Explanation: The Constitution was adopted on November 26, 1949, and came into effect on January 26, 1950.

44. Which of the following is a mechanism for citizen participation in a democracy?

  1. Voting in elections
  2. Participating in public debates and discussions
  3. Writing letters to the editor
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All these activities are valid ways for citizens to participate in a democracy.

45. True or False: In a democracy, the government can make laws that violate fundamental rights.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Democratic constitutions protect fundamental rights, and laws cannot violate them.

46. True or False: A monarchy is a form of democratic government.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Monarchy is a system of hereditary rule, which is not democratic.

47. Which of the following is the highest judicial body in India?

  1. High Court
  2. District Court
  3. Supreme Court
  4. Lok Adalat

Answer: Supreme Court

Explanation: The Supreme Court of India is the apex court and the final court of appeal.

48. Who appoints the judges of the Supreme Court of India?

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President of India
  3. Chief Justice of India
  4. Law Minister

Answer: President of India

Explanation: The President of India appoints the judges of the Supreme Court.

49. What is the minimum age for retirement of a Supreme Court judge?

  1. 60 years
  2. 62 years
  3. 65 years
  4. 70 years

Answer: 65 years

Explanation: Supreme Court judges retire at the age of 65.

50. Which court has the power to issue writs for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

  1. Only Supreme Court
  2. Only High Court
  3. Both Supreme Court and High Court
  4. District Court

Answer: Both Supreme Court and High Court

Explanation: Both the Supreme Court (Art 32) and High Courts (Art 226) can issue writs.
