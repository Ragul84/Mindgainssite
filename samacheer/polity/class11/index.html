1. What is the Tamil translation of 'Absolute Monarchy' as per the glossary?

  1. முடியாட்சி
  2. நிருவாகச் ெட்்ம்
  3. செயற்ாட்்ாளர்
  4. ொர்ந்திருத்தல்

Answer: முடியாட்சி

Explanation: The glossary provides 'முடியாட்சி' as the Tamil translation for 'Absolute Monarchy'.

2. What does 'Administrative Machinery' refer to?

  1. Government's operational structure
  2. A type of law
  3. A political activist
  4. A temporary measure

Answer: Government's operational structure

Explanation: 'Administrative Machinery' refers to the government's operational structure as per the glossary.

3. Which of the following is considered inhuman and a crime according to the Government of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Untouchability
  2. Debate
  3. Case Study
  4. Glossary

Answer: Untouchability

Explanation: Untouchability is explicitly mentioned as inhuman and a crime in the textbook excerpts provided by the Government of Tamil Nadu.

4. What is the primary source of authority for democracy?

  1. Public opinion
  2. Government officials
  3. Military
  4. Religious leaders

Answer: Public opinion

Explanation: Democracy derives its authority from the people, primarily through public opinion.

5. Which of the following is NOT considered public opinion?

  1. Opinion of a respected individual
  2. Organized opinion of a section of people
  3. Expert opinion
  4. Considered opinion of many

Answer: Expert opinion

Explanation: Public opinion is not necessarily expert opinion; it is a collective view.
