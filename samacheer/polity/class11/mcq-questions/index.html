26. The importance of free and fair elections is primarily to ensure ________.

  1. Democratic legitimacy
  2. Political stability
  3. Economic growth
  4. Military control

Answer: Democratic legitimacy

Explanation: Free and fair elections ensure the legitimacy of democracy.

27. The role of youth in politics is important because ________.

  2. They are the future voters
  3. They can influence policies

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Youth contribute in multiple ways including bringing new ideas and influencing policies.

29. The core foundation of democracy is ________.
