26. Assertion: The Prime Minister is the most powerful political office in India. Reason: The Prime Minister leads the Council of Ministers and is the principal advisor to the President.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and reason is the correct explanation of assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but reason is not the correct explanation of assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and reason is the correct explanation of assertion.

Explanation: The Prime Minister's leadership of the government and advisory role to the President make it the most powerful political office.

27. The process of impeaching the President of India can be initiated in:

  1. Lok Sabha only
  2. Rajya Sabha only
  3. Either Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha
  4. Supreme Court

Answer: Either Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha

Explanation: Impeachment proceedings against the President can be started in either house of Parliament.

28. The number of members nominated by the President to the Rajya Sabha is:

  1. 10
  2. 12
  3. 15
  4. 20

Answer: 12

Explanation: The President nominates 12 members to the Rajya Sabha from persons having special knowledge or practical experience in literature, science, art, and social services.

29. The Union Government is responsible for matters listed in the:

  1. State List
  2. Concurrent List
  3. Union List
  4. Residuary List

Answer: Union List

Explanation: The Union List contains subjects on which only the Union Parliament can legislate.

30. Which of the following is a key feature of a democratic government?

  1. Rule by a single dictator
  2. Equality and justice for all citizens
  3. Limited participation of people
  4. Suppression of dissent

Answer: Equality and justice for all citizens

Explanation: Democratic governments are characterized by equality, justice, and the participation of all citizens.

31. In a democracy, who holds the ultimate power?

  1. The President
  2. The Prime Minister
  3. The Parliament
  4. The People

Answer: The People

Explanation: In a democracy, sovereignty rests with the people, who exercise their power through elected representatives.

32. The Universal Adult Franchise means:

  1. Only educated adults can vote
  2. Only wealthy adults can vote
  3. All adults above a certain age have the right to vote
  4. Only men can vote

Answer: All adults above a certain age have the right to vote

Explanation: Universal Adult Franchise grants voting rights to all adult citizens, irrespective of their caste, creed, colour, sex, or economic status.

33. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a democratic government?

  1. Accountability of the government to the people
  2. Freedom of speech and expression
  3. Absence of elections
  4. Rule of law

Answer: Absence of elections

Explanation: Absence of elections is a characteristic of non-democratic governments, not democracies.

34. Statement 1: In a democracy, the government is accountable to its citizens. Statement 2: Citizens can question the government and demand explanations for its decisions.

  1. Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  2. Both statements are true but Statement 2 is not the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  3. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.

Answer: Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.

Explanation: Accountability in a democracy implies that citizens have the right to question and seek explanations from the government.

35. Statement 1: Monarchy is a form of government where power is inherited. Statement 2: In a monarchy, citizens have the right to choose their leaders through elections.

  1. Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  2. Both statements are true but Statement 2 is not the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  3. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.

Explanation: In a monarchy, power is inherited, and citizens do not elect their leaders.

36. Statement 1: A democratic government is formed by the people. Statement 2: The people elect their representatives to form the government.

  1. Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  2. Both statements are true but Statement 2 is not the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  3. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.

Answer: Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.

Explanation: The formation of a democratic government is a direct result of the people electing their representatives.

37. Statement 1: In a democracy, the majority rule is important. Statement 2: The majority's decisions are always right and should not be questioned.

  1. Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  2. Both statements are true but Statement 2 is not the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  3. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.

Explanation: While majority rule is a principle, it does not mean the majority's decisions are always right or unquestionable. Minority rights must also be protected.

38. Statement 1: A government that is not democratic may still hold elections. Statement 2: Elections in non-democratic governments are usually free and fair.

  1. Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  2. Both statements are true but Statement 2 is not the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  3. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.

Explanation: Non-democratic governments might hold elections, but these are typically not free and fair, lacking genuine choice for the people.

39. Statement 1: In a democracy, the government is responsible for protecting the rights of its citizens. Statement 2: Citizens have the right to protest against unjust laws or government actions.

  1. Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  2. Both statements are true but Statement 2 is not the correct explanation of Statement 1.
  3. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.

Answer: Both statements are true and Statement 2 is the correct explanation of Statement 1.

Explanation: Protecting citizens' rights includes allowing them to express dissent and protest against perceived injustices.

40. Assertion: Democracy is considered the best form of government. Reason: It ensures the participation of people in decision-making.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: The participatory nature of democracy is a primary reason why it is often considered the best form of government.

41. Assertion: In an autocratic government, the ruler has absolute power. Reason: The people have no say in the governance of the country.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: Absolute power in autocracy means the absence of public participation in governance.

42. True or False: In a democracy, the government can make decisions without consulting the people.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Democratic governments are accountable to the people and often consult them or their representatives in decision-making.

43. True or False: Universal Adult Franchise ensures that every citizen, regardless of their background, has a voice in electing their government.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Universal Adult Franchise is a cornerstone of democracy, promoting equality in political participation.

44. True or False: A country with a hereditary monarch is a democracy.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: A hereditary monarchy is a form of government where power is inherited, not elected, and thus not a democracy.

45. Match List I with List II: List I (Term) A. Dictatorship B. Democracy C. Monarchy D. Universal adult franchise List II (Definition) 1. Rule by a king or queen 2. Government by the people 3. Rule by a single person with absolute power 4. Right to vote for all adults

  1. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  3. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  4. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1

Answer: A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Explanation: A dictatorship is rule by one person with absolute power, democracy government by the people, monarchy rule by a king or queen and universal adult franchise the right of every adult to vote.

46. Match List I with List II: List I (Concept) A. Sovereignty B. Justice C. Equality D. Accountability List II (Meaning) 1. Being responsible for one's actions 2. All individuals are treated the same 3. Supreme power or authority 4. Fairness and impartiality

  1. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  2. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  3. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Explanation: Sovereignty is supreme authority, justice fairness, equality equal treatment of all and accountability being answerable for one's actions.

47. Match List I with List II: List I (Government) A. Oligarchy B. Theocracy C. Dictatorship D. Representative democracy List II (Characteristic) 1. Religious leader 2. Absolute ruler 3. Rule by a small group 4. Elected representatives

  1. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  4. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Answer: A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4

Explanation: An oligarchy is rule by a small group, a theocracy rule by religious leaders, a dictatorship rule by one absolute ruler and a representative democracy rule through elected representatives.

48. Which of the following is the head of the State Government in India?

  1. President
  2. Prime Minister
  3. Governor
  4. Chief Minister

Answer: Governor

Explanation: The Governor is the constitutional head of a state in India, appointed by the President.

49. The Chief Minister is the leader of which house in the State Legislature?

  1. Legislative Council
  2. Legislative Assembly
  3. Both Legislative Council and Assembly
  4. None of the above

Answer: Legislative Assembly

Explanation: The Chief Minister is the leader of the majority party or coalition in the Legislative Assembly.

50. Assertion: The Governor has discretionary powers. Reason: The Governor acts on the advice of the Chief Minister and the Council of Ministers.

  1. Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason is the correct explanation of Assertion.
  2. Both Assertion and Reason are true, but Reason is not the correct explanation of Assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but Reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but Reason is true.

Answer: Assertion is true, but Reason is false.

Explanation: While the Governor generally acts on the advice of the Council of Ministers, they do possess certain discretionary powers, making the assertion true. However, the reason contradicts the existence of discretionary powers, making it false.
