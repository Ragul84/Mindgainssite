Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the Samacheer Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 109 questions.

1. Who is known as the 'Father of the Indian Constitution'?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
  4. Sardar Patel

Answer: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is known as the 'Father of the Indian Constitution' because he played a pivotal role in drafting it.

2. Which article of the Indian Constitution deals with the Right to Equality?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32

Answer: Article 14

Explanation: Article 14 of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Right to Equality to all citizens.

3. The Indian Parliament consists of how many houses?

  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Three
  4. Four

Answer: Two

Explanation: The Indian Parliament consists of two houses: the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha.

4. Which of the following is a fundamental duty of Indian citizens?

  1. To pay taxes
  2. To vote in elections
  3. To protect public property
  4. To join the armed forces

Answer: To protect public property

Explanation: Protecting public property is one of the fundamental duties listed in the Indian Constitution.

5. Which article of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Right to Equality?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32

Answer: Article 14

Explanation: Article 14 of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Right to Equality to all citizens.

6. Which of the following is NOT a fundamental duty mentioned in the Indian Constitution?

  1. To uphold the sovereignty of India
  2. To protect public property
  3. To vote in elections
  4. To cherish the noble ideals of the freedom struggle

Answer: To vote in elections

Explanation: Voting in elections is not listed as a fundamental duty in the Indian Constitution.

7. The term 'Secular' was added to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution by which amendment?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 86th Amendment

Answer: 42nd Amendment

Explanation: The term 'Secular' was added to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution by the 42nd Amendment in 1976.

8. The Indian Constitution was adopted on which date?

  1. 26th January 1950
  2. 15th August 1947
  3. 26th November 1949
  4. 2nd October 1947

Answer: 26th November 1949

Explanation: The Indian Constitution was adopted on 26th November 1949, though it came into effect on 26th January 1950.

9. Which of the following is NOT a feature of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Federal System
  2. Parliamentary Democracy
  3. Unitary System
  4. Fundamental Rights

Answer: Unitary System

Explanation: The Indian Constitution adopts a federal system with unitary features, but it is not purely unitary.

10. Which of the following is NOT a part of the Union Legislature?

  1. President
  2. Lok Sabha
  3. Rajya Sabha
  4. Prime Minister

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The Prime Minister is the head of the executive branch, not a part of the legislature.

11. The Lok Sabha is also known as the House of the People.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Lok Sabha, being directly elected by the people, is referred to as the House of the People.

12. The President of India is elected by:

  1. Members of Parliament
  2. Members of State Legislatures
  3. Members of Parliament and State Legislatures
  4. Direct election by the citizens

Answer: Members of Parliament and State Legislatures

Explanation: The President is elected by an electoral college consisting of elected members of both the Parliament and the State Legislatures.

13. The Prime Minister is appointed by the President.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The President appoints the Prime Minister, usually the leader of the majority party in the Lok Sabha.

14. Consider the following statements about the Rajya Sabha: 1. It is the upper house of the Parliament. 2. It is a permanent body and not subject to dissolution. 3. Its members are directly elected by the people. 4. It has a maximum strength of 250 members.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1, 2, and 4 only
  3. 2, 3, and 4 only
  4. 1, 2, 3, and 4

Answer: 1, 2, and 4 only

Explanation: Statement 3 is incorrect as members of the Rajya Sabha are indirectly elected by the elected members of the State Legislative Assemblies.

15. Assertion: The Parliament of India consists of the President, the Lok Sabha, and the Rajya Sabha. Reason: The President is an integral part of the Parliament, even though he does not sit in either house.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and reason is the correct explanation of assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but reason is not the correct explanation of assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and reason is the correct explanation of assertion.

Explanation: The President's assent is required for a bill to become a law, making him an essential part of the legislative process.

16. The term of office for a Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) is:

  1. 4 years
  2. 5 years
  3. 6 years
  4. Until the President dissolves the house

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: Members of the Lok Sabha are elected for a term of five years, unless dissolved earlier.

17. Which of the following is a key function of the Union Executive?

  1. Making laws
  2. Implementing laws
  3. Interpreting laws
  4. Appointing judges

Answer: Implementing laws

Explanation: The executive's primary role is to implement and enforce the laws made by the legislature.

18. The Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the:

  1. Lok Sabha
  2. Rajya Sabha
  3. Supreme Court
  4. NITI Aayog

Answer: Rajya Sabha

Explanation: The Vice-President automatically becomes the Chairman of the Rajya Sabha upon assuming office.

19. The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the:

  1. President
  2. Prime Minister
  3. Lok Sabha
  4. Rajya Sabha

Answer: Lok Sabha

Explanation: The Council of Ministers must maintain the confidence of the Lok Sabha to remain in power.

20. The power to summon and prorogue the sessions of Parliament lies with the:

  1. Prime Minister
  2. Speaker of Lok Sabha
  3. President
  4. Chief Justice of India

Answer: President

Explanation: The President has the constitutional authority to summon and prorogue sessions of Parliament.

21. The Attorney General of India is the chief legal advisor to the:

  1. Supreme Court
  2. Parliament
  3. President
  4. Prime Minister

Answer: President

Explanation: The Attorney General advises the Union Government on legal matters.

22. The Lok Sabha can be dissolved before the completion of its term by the:

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The President can dissolve the Lok Sabha on the advice of the Prime Minister.

23. Match the following terms with their definitions: A. Speaker B. Prime Minister C. President D. Attorney General

  1. Presides over the Lok Sabha
  2. Head of the Government
  3. Head of the State
  4. Chief Legal Advisor to the Government

Answer: Presides over the Lok Sabha

Explanation: Correct answer is Presides over the Lok Sabha

24. The minimum age to be a Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) is:

  1. 18 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 25 years
  4. 30 years

Answer: 25 years

Explanation: A person must be at least 25 years old to be eligible for election to the Lok Sabha.

25. Which of the following is a non-legislative function of the Parliament?

  1. Passing the budget
  2. Debating national issues
  3. Approving treaties
  4. Electing the President

Answer: Electing the President

Explanation: While Parliament discusses national issues and approves budgets, the election of the President is done by an electoral college.
