26. What is the main characteristic of a Monarchy?

  1. Elections every 5 years
  2. Rule by a single hereditary head
  3. Decision making by the public
  4. Rule by a committee of experts

Answer: Rule by a single hereditary head

Explanation: A monarchy is a form of government where a king or queen rules, and power is usually passed down hereditarily.

27. Consider the following statements: 1. The legislature makes the laws. 2. The executive implements the laws. 3. The judiciary interprets the laws. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: These are the three primary organs of government with distinct roles.

28. Consider the following statements about the Indian Government: 1. India follows a democratic system. 2. India has a unitary system of government. 3. The government works at three levels: Central, State, and Local. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: India is a democratic republic with a federal system (not unitary), operating at three levels.

29. Regarding the Local Government, consider these statements: 1. It is the government closest to the people. 2. It handles issues like street lighting and sanitation. 3. It is not a part of the overall government structure. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Local government is an integral part of the government structure and handles grassroots issues.

30. Consider the following statements about the Judiciary: 1. It settles disputes between citizens. 2. It ensures that laws are followed correctly. 3. It is responsible for collecting taxes. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Tax collection is the responsibility of the executive/finance department, not the judiciary.

31. Consider the following statements about the Executive: 1. It includes the Prime Minister and the Cabinet. 2. Its role is to implement laws passed by the legislature. 3. It is the body that writes the Constitution. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: The Constitution is written by a Constituent Assembly, not the executive branch.

32. Consider the following statements about the Legislature: 1. It is the law-making body. 2. In India, the Parliament is the central legislature. 3. It is responsible for interpreting the law. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Interpreting the law is the function of the Judiciary.

33. Consider the following statements regarding the State Government: 1. It manages affairs of a specific state. 2. It is subordinate to the local government. 3. It handles state-level issues like education and health. Which of the statements above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: The local government is subordinate to the state government, not vice versa.

34. Assertion: India is called a democratic country. Reason: The people of India elect their representatives through elections.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: Democracy is defined by the people's right to elect their leaders.

35. Assertion: The Judiciary is independent of the Executive. Reason: This ensures that justice is delivered without political pressure.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: Independence of the judiciary is essential for fair and impartial justice.

36. The government is not necessary for the smooth functioning of a society.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Government is essential for maintaining order, security, and providing public services.

37. In a monarchy, the king's decision is final and cannot be challenged by the people.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: In absolute monarchies, the monarch holds supreme authority.

38. The Central Government is responsible for the administration of a single village.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Central Government manages the entire country; the Local Government manages villages.

39. Match List I with List II: List I (Organ) A. Judiciary B. Legislature C. Executive List II (Function) 1. Makes Laws 2. Interprets Laws 3. Implements Laws

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-2, B-1, C-3
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2

Answer: A-2, B-1, C-3

Explanation: The legislature makes laws, the executive implements them and the judiciary interprets them.

40. Match List I with List II: List I (Level of government) A. Local government B. Central (Union) government C. State government List II (Scope) 1. Village/Town 2. Whole Country 3. Specific State

  1. A-3, B-2, C-1
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3

Answer: A-1, B-2, C-3

Explanation: Local government serves a village or town, the Union government the whole country and the state government one state.

41. Which of the following is the lowest tier of the Panchayati Raj system in India?

  1. Gram Panchayat
  2. Panchayat Samiti
  3. Zila Parishad
  4. Nagar Panchayat

Answer: Gram Panchayat

Explanation: The Gram Panchayat is the village-level local self-government body, forming the base of the Panchayati Raj system.

42. The primary function of a Gram Panchayat is to:

  1. Formulate national policies
  2. Manage defence and foreign affairs
  3. Provide basic amenities and services at the village level
  4. Regulate inter-state trade

Answer: Provide basic amenities and services at the village level

Explanation: Gram Panchayats are responsible for local development and providing essential services like sanitation, water supply, and roads in villages.

43. Which article of the Indian Constitution deals with the promotion of cooperative societies?

  1. Article 40
  2. Article 43B
  3. Article 243
  4. Article 370

Answer: Article 43B

Explanation: Article 43B was added by the 97th Amendment Act, 2011, to promote cooperative societies.

44. Assertion: The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992, gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions. Reason: This amendment aimed to strengthen democracy at the grassroots level by empowering local self-governments.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: The 73rd Amendment indeed constitutionalized Panchayati Raj and aimed at grassroots democracy.

45. Assertion: Municipal Corporations are responsible for urban local governance. Reason: They deal with issues like waste management, street lighting, and public health in cities.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: Municipal Corporations are the governing bodies for large urban areas and their functions include the services mentioned.

46. Statement 1: The Gram Sabha is the general body of the village. Statement 2: All adults residing in the village are members of the Gram Sabha.

  1. Both statements are true.
  2. Statement 1 is true, but Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false, but Statement 2 is true.
  4. Both statements are false.

Answer: Both statements are true.

Explanation: The Gram Sabha comprises all adult members of the village, making both statements correct.

47. Statement 1: The Panchayat Samiti is the intermediate tier of the Panchayati Raj system. Statement 2: It is responsible for implementing development schemes in a block.

  1. Both statements are true.
  2. Statement 1 is true, but Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false, but Statement 2 is true.
  4. Both statements are false.

Answer: Both statements are true.

Explanation: The Panchayat Samiti functions at the block level and oversees development activities within its jurisdiction.

48. Statement 1: A Nagar Panchayat is constituted for transitional areas, i.e., areas in transition from rural to urban. Statement 2: It is the lowest tier of urban local government.

  1. Both statements are true.
  2. Statement 1 is true, but Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false, but Statement 2 is true.
  4. Both statements are false.

Answer: Both statements are true.

Explanation: Nagar Panchayats serve areas undergoing the process of urbanization and are considered the entry-level urban local body.

49. Match the following urban local bodies with their corresponding areas: Column A: (1) Municipal Corporation (2) Municipality (3) Nagar Panchayat Column B: (a) Urban areas (b) Transitional areas (c) Large urban areas

  1. Large urban areas
  2. Urban areas
  3. Transitional areas

Answer: Large urban areas

Explanation: Municipal Corporations govern large cities, Municipalities manage smaller urban areas, and Nagar Panchayats cater to transitional areas.

50. True or False: The Zila Parishad is the apex body of the Panchayati Raj system at the district level.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Zila Parishad is indeed the highest body within the Panchayati Raj system at the district level.
