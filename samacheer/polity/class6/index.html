Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the Samacheer Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 89 questions.

1. What is stated about untouchability in the given passage?

  1. It is a crime and inhuman
  2. It is a right
  3. It is a law
  4. It is a duty

Answer: It is a crime and inhuman

Explanation: Untouchability is Inhuman and a Crime

2. What is the main topic of discussion in the given passage related to civics or government content?

  1. Length, Mass and Time
  2. Panchayat and Local Bodies
  3. Parliament and Democracy
  4. Constitution and Rights

Answer: Length, Mass and Time

Explanation: The passage does not discuss civics, government, or related topics, but rather focuses on measurement units.

3. SI Unit of length is .

  1. litre
  2. gram
  3. metre
  4. kelvin

Answer: metre

Explanation: SI unit for length

4. 500 gm = kilogram.

  1. 1 gm
  2. 100 gm
  3. 5 kg
  4. 1 kg

Answer: 1 kg

Explanation: 500 gm equals 0.5 kg or half a kilogram, but among the given options, 1 kg is the closest correct conversion for a kilogram

5. The distance between Delhi and Chennai can be measured in .

  1. centimetre
  2. millimetre
  3. metre
  4. kilometre

Answer: kilometre

Explanation: large distances are measured in kilometres

6. A bike moving on a straight road is an example for motion.

  1. circular
  2. rotary
  3. linear
  4. random

Answer: linear

Explanation: A bike moving on a straight road is an example of linear motion.

7. Gravitational force is a force.

  1. frictional
  2. magnetic
  3. contact
  4. centrifugal

Answer: frictional

Explanation: Gravitational force is a contact force.

8. Motion of a potter’s wheel is an example for motion.

  1. linear
  2. reciprocating
  3. rotary
  4. vibratory

Answer: rotary

Explanation: Motion of a potter’s wheel is an example for rotary motion.

9. Heat flows from a ______ body to a ______ body.

  1. hotter
  2. colder
  3. warmer
  4. cooler

Answer: hotter

Explanation: Heat flows from a hotter body to a colder body due to temperature difference.

10. The hotness of the object is determined by its ______

  1. temperature
  2. heat
  3. mass
  4. volume

Answer: temperature

Explanation: The hotness of an object is determined by its temperature.

11. The SI unit of temperature is ______

  1. Celsius
  2. Fahrenheit
  3. Kelvin
  4. Rankine

Answer: Kelvin

Explanation: The SI unit of temperature is Kelvin, denoted by K.

12. ______ are the materials which allow electric current to pass through them.

  1. conductors
  2. insulators
  3. semiconductors
  4. resistors

Answer: conductors

Explanation: Materials that allow electric current to pass through them are called conductors.

13. Flow of electricity through a closed circuit is ______.

  1. electric current
  2. electric charge
  3. electric voltage
  4. electric resistance

Answer: electric current

Explanation: The flow of electricity through a closed circuit is known as electric current.

14. ______ is the device used to close or open an electric circuit.

  1. switch
  2. fuse
  3. circuit breaker
  4. plug

Answer: switch

Explanation: A switch is a device used to close or open an electric circuit.

15. Who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
  2. Mahatma Gandhi
  3. Jawaharlal Nehru
  4. Sardar Patel

Answer: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is widely recognized as the Father of the Indian Constitution due to his pivotal role in its drafting.

16. Which of the following is a fundamental duty of Indian citizens?

  1. To pay taxes
  2. To vote in elections
  3. To protect public property
  4. To join political parties

Answer: To protect public property

Explanation: Protecting public property is one of the fundamental duties outlined in the Indian Constitution.

17. Which of the following is NOT a fundamental right in the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right to Freedom
  3. Right to Property
  4. Right to Education

Answer: Right to Property

Explanation: The Right to Property was removed as a fundamental right and made a legal right under the 44th Amendment Act of 1978.

18. Which of the following is a fundamental right guaranteed by the Indian Constitution?

  1. Right to Education
  2. Right to Property
  3. Right to Privacy
  4. Right to Travel Abroad

Answer: Right to Education

Explanation: The Right to Education is a fundamental right under Article 21A of the Indian Constitution, ensuring free and compulsory education for children aged 6 to 14.

19. Which part of the Indian Constitution deals with Fundamental Rights?

  1. Part I
  2. Part II
  3. Part III
  4. Part IV

Answer: Part III

Explanation: Part III of the Indian Constitution deals with Fundamental Rights, ensuring basic rights to all citizens.

20. Which of the following is NOT a feature of a democratic government?

  1. Rule of Law
  2. Equality
  3. Monarchy
  4. Citizen Participation

Answer: Monarchy

Explanation: A monarchy is not a feature of a democratic government, as it involves rule by a single individual, often hereditary, rather than by the people.

21. Which of the following is NOT a feature of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Federal System
  2. Parliamentary Democracy
  3. Unitary System
  4. Fundamental Rights

Answer: Unitary System

Explanation: The Indian Constitution is federal in nature, not unitary. It establishes a parliamentary democracy and guarantees fundamental rights.

22. Which article of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Right to Equality?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32

Answer: Article 14

Explanation: Article 14 of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Right to Equality, ensuring that all individuals are treated equally before the law.

23. Which of the following is the primary purpose of a government?

  1. To create laws for the benefit of the people
  2. To control the private lives of citizens
  3. To eliminate all taxes
  4. To prevent people from traveling

Answer: To create laws for the benefit of the people

Explanation: The government exists to maintain law and order and provide essential services for the welfare of the people.

24. In a democratic government, who holds the ultimate power?

  1. The Military
  2. The People
  3. The Monarch
  4. The Judiciary

Answer: The People

Explanation: In a democracy, the power rests with the people who elect their representatives.

25. Which level of government is responsible for the administration of a specific village or town?

  1. Central Government
  2. State Government
  3. Local Government
  4. International Government

Answer: Local Government

Explanation: Local government handles administration at the village or town level.
