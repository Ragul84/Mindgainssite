26. Regarding Federalism, identify the correct statements: 1. Power is divided between central and regional governments. 2. It usually involves a written constitution. 3. The central government can unilaterally change the division of power. 4. It provides for a dual system of government.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 1, 2, and 4
  3. 2, 3, and 4
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 4

Explanation: In a true federation, the division of power is usually constitutionally guaranteed and cannot be changed unilaterally.

27. Which of the following statements regarding the Presidential system are true? 1. The President is the head of state and government. 2. The executive is not responsible to the legislature. 3. The President is elected by the people. 4. The legislature can easily remove the President through a vote of no confidence.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 2, 3, and 4
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: The President is not removed by a simple vote of no confidence, but through a complex impeachment process.

28. Analyze the following statements about the Parliamentary system: 1. There is a distinction between head of state and head of government. 2. The executive is part of the legislature. 3. The Prime Minister is the real executive head. 4. The system is characterized by a rigid separation of powers.

  1. 1, 2, and 4
  2. 1, 2, and 3
  3. 2, 3, and 4
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: Parliamentary systems feature a fusion of powers, not a rigid separation.

29. Which statements are true regarding a Republic? 1. The head of state is an elected person. 2. It is the opposite of a monarchy. 3. It must always be a democracy. 4. India is a Republic.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 1, 2, and 4
  3. 2, 3, and 4
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 4

Explanation: A republic is defined by an elected head of state; while most are democracies, the term technically refers to the absence of a monarch.

30. Identify the correct statements about Unitary systems: 1. It is suitable for small countries. 2. It has a single set of laws for the whole country. 3. Local governments are subordinate to the center. 4. It promotes regional autonomy over central control.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 2, 3, and 4
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: Unitary systems prioritize central control over regional autonomy.

31. Assertion (A): India has a Parliamentary form of government. Reason (R): The Indian executive is responsible to the Lok Sabha.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: The accountability of the executive to the legislature is the defining feature of the parliamentary system.

32. Assertion (A): In a Presidential system, there is a strict separation of powers. Reason (R): The President is elected independently of the legislature.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: Independent election of the executive ensures that the executive is not dependent on the legislature, enabling separation of powers.

33. Assertion (A): A Unitary government is more flexible than a Federal government. Reason (R): In a Unitary system, the central government can make decisions without consulting regional governments.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: The lack of need for regional consensus makes the unitary system more flexible in decision-making.

34. In a democracy, the ultimate power rests with the people.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Popular sovereignty is the core of democracy.

35. A Monarchy is always a democratic form of government.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Monarchies are typically hereditary and not based on democratic elections.

36. In a Federal system, the central government and state governments share power.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Federalism is the division of power between different levels of government.

37. The USA follows a Parliamentary system of government.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The USA follows a Presidential system.

38. Match List I with List II: List I (Form of government) A. Parliamentary B. Unitary C. Presidential List II (Feature) 1. Collective Responsibility 2. Centralized Authority 3. Separation of Powers

  1. A-1, B-3, C-2
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-2, B-1, C-3
  4. A-3, B-2, C-1

Answer: A-1, B-2, C-3

Explanation: Parliamentary government rests on collective responsibility, unitary government on centralised authority and presidential government on separation of powers.

39. Match List I with List II: List I (Country) A. India B. France C. USA List II (System) 1. Semi-Presidential 2. Parliamentary 3. Presidential

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-2, B-1, C-3
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2

Answer: A-2, B-1, C-3

Explanation: India has a parliamentary system, France a semi-presidential one and the USA a presidential system.

40. Match List I with List II: List I (Term) A. Monarchy B. Oligarchy C. Republic List II (Meaning) 1. Hereditary Head of State 2. Rule by a few 3. Elected Head of State

  1. A-3, B-2, C-1
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3

Answer: A-1, B-2, C-3

Explanation: A monarchy has a hereditary head of state, an oligarchy is rule by a few and a republic has an elected head of state.

41. What is the main difference between a Republic and a Monarchy?

  1. Republics have elections, Monarchies do not
  2. Republics have an elected head of state, Monarchies have a hereditary head
  3. Republics are always unitary, Monarchies are always federal
  4. There is no difference

Answer: Republics have an elected head of state, Monarchies have a hereditary head

Explanation: The primary distinction is whether the head of state is elected or hereditary.

42. Which of the following is a characteristic of a Semi-Presidential system?

  1. No President
  2. No Prime Minister
  3. Both a President and a Prime Minister with shared powers
  4. Absolute power to the Monarch

Answer: Both a President and a Prime Minister with shared powers

Explanation: Semi-presidential systems combine features of both parliamentary and presidential systems.

43. In a federal system, who typically handles matters of national importance like defense?

  1. State Government
  2. Local Government
  3. Central Government
  4. Judiciary

Answer: Central Government

Explanation: National security and defense are typically the exclusive jurisdiction of the central government in a federation.

44. Which of these is a disadvantage of a Unitary system?

  1. Slow decision making
  2. Neglect of local needs
  3. Too many laws
  4. Conflict between center and state

Answer: Neglect of local needs

Explanation: Centralized power can lead to the neglect of regional or local requirements.

45. Which of the following is a characteristic of a Constitutional Monarchy?

  1. Monarch has absolute power
  2. Monarch's power is limited by a constitution
  3. There is no monarch
  4. The monarch is elected every 4 years

Answer: Monarch's power is limited by a constitution

Explanation: In a constitutional monarchy, the monarch serves as a ceremonial head while the constitution limits their power.

46. Which part of the Indian Constitution contains the Fundamental Rights?

  1. Part II
  2. Part III
  3. Part IV
  4. Part V

Answer: Part III

Explanation: Fundamental Rights are enshrined in Part III of the Indian Constitution.

47. Which right was described by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the 'Heart and Soul of the Constitution'?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right against Exploitation
  3. Right to Constitutional Remedies
  4. Right to Freedom of Religion

Answer: Right to Constitutional Remedies

Explanation: Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies) allows citizens to move the court for enforcement of rights.

48. The 'Right to Education' was made a fundamental right by which amendment?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 86th Amendment
  4. 92nd Amendment

Answer: 86th Amendment

Explanation: The 86th Amendment Act of 2002 inserted Article 21A.

49. Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Right?

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right to Property
  3. Right to Freedom
  4. Right against Exploitation

Answer: Right to Property

Explanation: Right to Property was removed from Fundamental Rights by the 44th Amendment.

50. Untouchability is abolished under which Article of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 15
  3. Article 17
  4. Article 18

Answer: Article 17

Explanation: Article 17 prohibits the practice of untouchability in any form.
