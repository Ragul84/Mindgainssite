26. Consider the following: 1. The government can deprive a person of citizenship if they show disloyalty to the Constitution. 2. Deprivation can happen if citizenship was obtained by fraud. Which is correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both disloyalty and fraud are grounds for deprivation of citizenship.

27. Consider these statements: 1. A person who acquires another country's citizenship automatically loses Indian citizenship. 2. This is a feature of the Indian Citizenship Act. Which is correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: India does not allow dual citizenship; acquiring another citizenship leads to automatic termination.

28. Assertion: India has a single citizenship system. Reason: This is intended to promote a feeling of unity and fraternity among all citizens. Which is correct?

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: Single citizenship ensures that all citizens are equal regardless of state of origin, fostering unity.

29. Assertion: The Parliament of India can change citizenship laws. Reason: Article 11 of the Constitution gives this power to the Parliament. Which is correct?

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: Article 11 explicitly grants the Parliament the authority to make laws regarding citizenship.

30. Assertion: An OCI card holder is a citizen of India. Reason: OCI card holders can vote in the Lok Sabha elections. Which is correct?

  1. Both A and R are true
  2. Both A and R are false
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are false

Explanation: OCI holders are not citizens and cannot vote; they are foreign citizens of Indian origin.

31. India allows dual citizenship for its citizens.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: India only allows single citizenship.

32. The Citizenship Act was enacted in 1955.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Act was passed in 1955 to provide for the acquisition and termination of citizenship.

33. Naturalisation is a method of acquiring citizenship by birth.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Naturalisation is a legal process for foreigners, not birth.

34. The President of India decides who becomes a citizen by registration.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Central Government (Ministry of Home Affairs) handles registration, not the President personally.

35. Match List I with List II: List I (Method) A. Birth B. Registration C. Naturalisation D. Incorporation of territory List II (Description) 1. Applied by PIO or spouse of a citizen 2. Acquired by a foreigner after residing for 12 years 3. Born within the territory of India 4. Acquired when a new territory becomes part of India

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  4. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Answer: A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4

Explanation: Citizenship comes by birth in India, by registration for PIOs or spouses of citizens, by naturalisation after long residence and by incorporation when new territory joins India.

36. Match the terms with their meanings: List I A. Renunciation B. Termination C. Deprivation D. OCI List II 1. Compulsory loss due to fraud or disloyalty 2. Overseas Citizen of India 3. Voluntary giving up of citizenship 4. Automatic loss upon acquiring another citizenship

  1. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  2. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Explanation: Renunciation – Voluntary giving up of citizenship; Termination – Automatic loss upon acquiring another citizenship; Deprivation – Compulsory loss due to fraud or disloyalty; OCI – Overseas Citizen of India.

37. Match the legal basis: List I A. Part II B. 1955 Act C. Article 11 D. OCI Scheme List II 1. Statutory law for citizenship 2. Benefits for Indian diaspora 3. Parliament's power to legislate 4. Constitutional provisions

  1. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  2. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  3. A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2
  4. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Answer: A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2

Explanation: Part II – Constitutional provisions; 1955 Act – Statutory law for citizenship; Article 11 – Parliament's power to legislate; OCI Scheme – Benefits for Indian diaspora.

38. Match the requirement with the method: List I A. 12 years residence B. Application to Govt C. Born in India D. Territory Annexation List II 1. Incorporation 2. Registration 3. Birth 4. Naturalisation

  1. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  2. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
  3. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1

Explanation: 12 years residence – Naturalisation; Application to Govt – Registration; Born in India – Birth; Territory Annexation – Incorporation.

39. Who is the head of the Tamil Nadu State Government?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. Governor
  3. Chief Justice
  4. Speaker

Answer: Chief Minister

Explanation: The Chief Minister is the head of the Tamil Nadu State Government.

40. The Governor of Tamil Nadu is appointed by the President of India. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Governor is appointed by the President of India.

41. Match List I with List II: List I (Position / body (State)) A. High Court B. Chief Minister C. State Legislature D. Governor List II (Role) 1. Makes laws 2. Heads the State Government 3. Interprets laws 4. Represents the President

  1. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  2. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  3. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4

Answer: A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Explanation: The High Court interprets laws, the Chief Minister heads the state government, the legislature makes laws and the Governor represents the President.

42. The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly has how many members?

  1. 234
  2. 200
  3. 250
  4. 300

Answer: 234

Explanation: Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly has 234 members.

43. The Tamil Nadu Legislative Council is a bicameral legislature. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has a unicameral legislature; hence, no Legislative Council.

44. The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission is responsible for conducting elections to local bodies. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The State Election Commission conducts local body elections.

45. The Tamil Nadu High Court is the highest court in the state. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The High Court is the highest judicial authority in Tamil Nadu.

46. Match List I with List II: List I (Body) A. Human Rights Commission B. Election Commission C. Finance Commission D. Public Service Commission List II (Function) 1. Recruitment of government employees 2. Distribution of financial resources 3. Protects human rights 4. Conducts free and fair elections

  2. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

47. The Governor has the power to dissolve the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Explanation: The Governor can dissolve the Assembly on the advice of the Chief Minister.

48. The Tamil Nadu Chief Minister is appointed by the Governor. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

49. The Tamil Nadu State Assembly passes the budget. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Assembly approves the state budget.

50. The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly is elected for a term of how many years?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. 4 years
  4. 3 years

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: Members of the Legislative Assembly are elected for 5-year terms.
