26. Statements on PR System: 1. It is used in Israel. 2. It leads to more multi-party systems. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: PR systems often result in coalition governments and multi-party systems.

27. Statements on FPTP: 1. It is simple for voters. 2. It ensures a stable government. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: FPTP is easy to understand and usually provides a clear majority to one party.

28. Statements on the CEC: 1. He has a fixed tenure. 2. He can be removed like a judge of the SC. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: The CEC has security of tenure and removal follows the SC judge process.

29. Statements on Delimitation: 1. It determines boundary of constituencies. 2. It is done by the Delimitation Commission. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Delimitation is the redrawing of boundaries by a specialized commission.

30. Statements on Voting: 1. EVMs are used for recording votes. 2. VVPAT provides a paper trail. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: EVMs record votes and VVPATs allow voters to verify their choice.

31. Statements on Election Expenses: 1. There is a legal limit on spending. 2. Candidates must file expense reports. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: The EC sets limits and requires accounting to prevent money power influence.

32. Statements on Political Parties: 1. They contest elections. 2. They provide a platform for policy debate. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Parties are essential for organizing political competition.

33. Statements on the Rajya Sabha: 1. It is a permanent house. 2. Members are elected by FPTP. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Only 1

Explanation: Rajya Sabha is permanent, but its members are elected via PR (STV), not FPTP.

34. Assertion: India adopted the FPTP system for Lok Sabha. Reason: It is simple and provides a clear winner.

  1. Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation
  2. Both A and R true, R is NOT correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation

Explanation: Simplicity and stability were key reasons for choosing FPTP.

35. Assertion: The EC is an independent body. Reason: The CEC cannot be removed easily by the government.

  1. Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation
  2. Both A and R true, R is NOT correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation

Explanation: Independence is ensured by the difficulty of removal.

36. Assertion: PR system is more representative. Reason: Every vote contributes to the seat count.

  1. Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation
  2. Both A and R true, R is NOT correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation

Explanation: PR reduces 'wasted votes' compared to FPTP.

37. Assertion: Reserved seats are necessary. Reason: They ensure representation for marginalized groups.

  1. Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation
  2. Both A and R true, R is NOT correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation

Explanation: Reservation prevents the exclusion of SC/ST from the legislative process.

38. The Election Commission of India is a constitutional body.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: It is established under Article 324 of the Constitution.

39. In FPTP, a candidate must get more than 50% of the total votes to win.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: In FPTP, the candidate with the most votes wins, even if it's less than 50%.

40. The Rajya Sabha members are elected for a term of 6 years.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Rajya Sabha members serve 6-year terms, with 1/3 retiring every 2 years.

41. The Model Code of Conduct is a legally binding law passed by Parliament.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: MCC is a set of guidelines agreed upon by parties, not a statutory law.

42. The 61st Amendment Act lowered the voting age to 18.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: This amendment was passed in 1988 and came into effect in 1989.

43. The President of India is elected directly by the people.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The President is elected indirectly by an electoral college.

44. Match the following: List I A. FPTP B. PR C. Article 324 D. 61st Amendment E. VVPAT List II 1. Voting Age 18 2. Paper Audit Trail 3. Proportional Representation 4. Plurality System 5. Election Commission

  1. A-2, B-3, C-5, D-1, E-4
  2. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1, E-5
  3. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-5, E-2
  4. A-4, B-3, C-5, D-1, E-2

Answer: A-4, B-3, C-5, D-1, E-2

Explanation: FPTP – Plurality System; PR – Proportional Representation; Article 324 – Election Commission; 61st Amendment – Voting Age 18; VVPAT – Paper Audit Trail.

45. Who is the formal head of the state in the Indian Parliamentary system?

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Chief Justice
  4. Speaker of Lok Sabha

Answer: President

Explanation: The President is the nominal/formal head of the state, while the PM is the real executive.

46. The President of India is elected by an electoral college consisting of:

  1. Only members of Lok Sabha
  2. Only members of Rajya Sabha
  3. Elected members of both houses of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies
  4. All citizens above 18 years

Answer: Elected members of both houses of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies

Explanation: The electoral college includes elected members of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies.

47. Which article of the Indian Constitution deals with the appointment of the Prime Minister?

  1. Article 74
  2. Article 75
  3. Article 78
  4. Article 80

Answer: Article 75

Explanation: Article 75 states that the Prime Minister shall be appointed by the President.

48. The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the:

  1. President
  2. Rajya Sabha
  3. Lok Sabha
  4. Supreme Court

Answer: Lok Sabha

Explanation: Collective responsibility to the Lok Sabha is a fundamental feature of the parliamentary system.

49. Who presides over the meetings of the Cabinet?

  1. President
  2. Vice President
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Home Minister

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The Prime Minister chairs the Cabinet meetings.

50. The tenure of the President of India is:

  1. 4 years
  2. 5 years
  3. 6 years
  4. Life term

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: The President holds office for a term of five years.
