Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the Samacheer Physics syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 392 questions.

26. The process of splitting a heavy nucleus into two lighter nuclei is called:

  1. Fusion
  2. Fission
  3. Decay
  4. Ionization

Answer: Fission

Explanation: Nuclear fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus.

27. The uncertainty principle was proposed by:

  1. Bohr
  2. Einstein
  3. Heisenberg
  4. Schrodinger

Answer: Heisenberg

Explanation: Werner Heisenberg proposed the uncertainty principle.

28. The unit of radioactivity is:

  1. Curie
  2. Tesla
  3. Newton
  4. Pascal

Answer: Curie

Explanation: Curie (Ci) or Becquerel (Bq) are units of radioactivity.

29. Which series of hydrogen spectrum is in the ultraviolet region?

  1. Lyman
  2. Balmer
  3. Paschen
  4. Pfund

Answer: Lyman

Explanation: Lyman series transitions to n=1 and are in the UV range.

30. Consider the following statements: 1. Alpha particles are helium nuclei. 2. Beta particles are fast moving electrons. 3. Gamma rays are electromagnetic waves. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All three statements correctly describe the types of radiation.

31. Regarding nuclear forces: 1. They are short-range. 2. They are charge-independent. 3. They are the strongest forces in nature. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Nuclear forces are short-range, charge-independent, and extremely strong.

32. About the photoelectric effect: 1. Kinetic energy depends on intensity. 2. Stopping potential depends on frequency. 3. Photoelectrons are emitted instantly. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 2 and 3 only

Explanation: KE depends on frequency, not intensity. Stopping potential depends on frequency.

33. Regarding the Bohr model: 1. Electrons revolve in circular orbits. 2. Angular momentum is quantized. 3. Energy is emitted when moving to a higher orbit. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Energy is absorbed (not emitted) when moving to a higher orbit.

34. About radioactive decay: 1. It is a random process. 2. Rate depends on temperature. 3. Rate depends on pressure. Which are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 only

Explanation: Radioactive decay is spontaneous and independent of external factors like T and P.

35. Regarding isotopes: 1. Same atomic number. 2. Same mass number. 3. Same chemical properties. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Isotopes have the same atomic number and chemical properties but different mass numbers.

36. About the nucleus: 1. It contains protons and neutrons. 2. It is positively charged. 3. Its size is much smaller than the atom. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All statements correctly describe the atomic nucleus.

37. Assertion: The Balmer series lines are visible. Reason: Transitions occur to the n=2 energy level.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: Transitions to n=2 result in wavelengths in the visible spectrum.

38. Assertion: Binding energy per nucleon is higher for Fe than for U. Reason: Fe is more stable than U.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: Higher binding energy per nucleon implies greater stability.

39. Assertion: Photoelectric effect proves the particle nature of light. Reason: Light consists of discrete packets called photons.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: The particle nature (photons) explains the immediate emission of electrons.

40. Assertion: Alpha decay reduces the atomic number by 2. Reason: An alpha particle consists of 2 protons and 2 neutrons.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: Loss of 2 protons reduces the atomic number by 2.

41. Assertion: The mass of a nucleus is always less than the sum of its constituents. Reason: This difference is called mass defect.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both true, R doesn't explain A

Explanation: Both are true, but the definition of mass defect doesn't 'explain' why the mass is less (binding energy does).

42. The Bohr model is applicable only to hydrogen-like atoms.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Bohr's model works for H, He+, Li2+, etc.

43. Gamma rays have the longest wavelength among alpha, beta, and gamma radiation.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Gamma rays have the shortest wavelength and highest frequency.

44. The mass of a proton is approximately 1836 times the mass of an electron.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: This is the standard mass ratio.

45. Nuclear fusion occurs at very low temperatures.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Fusion requires extremely high temperatures to overcome electrostatic repulsion.

46. Match List I with List II: List I (Radiation) A. Beta particle B. Gamma ray C. Alpha particle List II (Is) 1. Electron 2. Helium Nucleus 3. High energy photon

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1
  2. A-1, B-3, C-2
  3. A-1, B-2, C-3
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2

Answer: A-1, B-3, C-2

Explanation: A beta particle is an electron, a gamma ray a high-energy photon and an alpha particle a helium nucleus.

47. Match List I with List II: List I (Hydrogen series) A. Balmer series B. Paschen series C. Lyman series List II (Region) 1. Infrared 2. Visible 3. Ultraviolet

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2
  3. A-2, B-1, C-3
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3

Answer: A-2, B-1, C-3

Explanation: The Balmer series is visible, Paschen infrared and Lyman ultraviolet.

48. Match List I with List II: List I (Scientist) A. Niels Bohr B. Albert Einstein C. Ernest Rutherford List II (Known for) 1. Nucleus 2. Quantized orbits 3. Photoelectric effect

  1. A-1, B-3, C-2
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: Bohr proposed quantised orbits, Einstein explained the photoelectric effect and Rutherford discovered the nucleus.

49. Match List I with List II: List I (Decay) A. Gamma decay B. Alpha decay C. Beta decay List II (Emits) 1. Electron 2. Photon 3. He nucleus

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1
  4. A-1, B-3, C-2

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: Gamma decay emits a photon, alpha decay a helium nucleus and beta decay an electron.

50. Match List I with List II: List I (Quantity) A. Activity B. Wavelength C. Energy List II (Unit) 1. eV 2. Becquerel 3. Angstrom

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3
  2. A-2, B-3, C-1
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1
  4. A-1, B-3, C-2

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: Radioactivity is measured in becquerels, wavelength often in ångströms and particle energy in electron volts.
