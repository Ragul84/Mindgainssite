Class11 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class11 questions to revise the Samacheer Physics syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 359 questions.

26. Which of the following is an intensive property?

  1. Mass
  2. Volume
  3. Temperature
  4. Internal Energy

Answer: Temperature

Explanation: Intensive properties do not depend on the amount of matter present.

27. The molar heat capacity at constant pressure (Cp) and constant volume (Cv) are related by:

  1. Cp - Cv = R
  2. Cv - Cp = R
  3. Cp + Cv = R
  4. Cp/Cv = R

Answer: Cp - Cv = R

Explanation: Mayer's relation states Cp - Cv = R.

28. The process in which the volume remains constant is called:

  1. Isothermal
  2. Isobaric
  3. Isochoric
  4. Adiabatic

Answer: Isochoric

Explanation: Isochoric process occurs at constant volume.

29. The temperature at which the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales coincide is:

  1. 0
  2. 100
  3. -40
  4. -273

Answer: -40

Explanation: At -40 degrees, both scales show the same value.

30. The internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on its:

  1. Pressure
  2. Volume
  3. Temperature
  4. Density

Answer: Temperature

Explanation: For an ideal gas, internal energy is a function of temperature only.

31. The second law of thermodynamics introduces the concept of:

  1. Energy
  2. Entropy
  3. Enthalpy
  4. Work

Answer: Entropy

Explanation: Entropy is the measure of disorder introduced by the second law.

32. In an isothermal expansion of an ideal gas, the work done is:

  1. Zero
  2. Positive
  3. Negative
  4. Infinite

Answer: Positive

Explanation: Work is done by the gas during expansion, making it positive.

33. The root mean square speed of gas molecules is proportional to:

  1. T
  2. T^2
  3. sqrt(T)
  4. 1/T

Answer: sqrt(T)

Explanation: Vrms = sqrt(3RT/M), so it is proportional to the square root of temperature.

34. Consider the following statements: 1. Heat flows from hot to cold. 2. Entropy of the universe always increases. 3. A heat engine can be 100% efficient.

  1. 1 and 2 are correct
  2. 2 and 3 are correct
  3. 1 and 3 are correct
  4. All are correct

Answer: 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Statement 3 is false because Kelvin-Planck statement forbids 100% efficiency.

35. Regarding an adiabatic process: 1. dQ = 0. 2. Temperature changes. 3. It is a slow process.

  1. 1 and 2 are correct
  2. 2 and 3 are correct
  3. 1 and 3 are correct
  4. All are correct

Answer: 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Adiabatic processes are typically fast to prevent heat exchange.

36. Which of these are state functions? 1. Internal Energy 2. Work 3. Entropy 4. Heat

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 1 and 3
  3. 2 and 4
  4. 3 and 4

Answer: 1 and 3

Explanation: Internal energy and entropy are state functions; work and heat are path functions.

37. About the Carnot cycle: 1. Two isothermal processes. 2. Two adiabatic processes. 3. It is a reversible cycle.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All are correct

Answer: All are correct

Explanation: A Carnot cycle consists of two isothermal and two adiabatic reversible processes.

38. Properties of an ideal gas: 1. No intermolecular forces. 2. Molecules are point masses. 3. Follows PV=nRT.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All are correct

Answer: All are correct

Explanation: All three are fundamental assumptions of the kinetic theory of ideal gases.

39. Regarding heat transfer: 1. Conduction occurs in solids. 2. Convection occurs in fluids. 3. Radiation requires a medium.

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All are correct

Answer: 1 and 2 only

Explanation: Radiation does not require a medium to propagate.

40. Assertion: A Carnot engine is the most efficient heat engine. Reason: It operates on a completely reversible cycle.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: Reversibility ensures maximum efficiency as there are no dissipative losses.

41. Assertion: The internal energy of an ideal gas depends on pressure. Reason: Ideal gas molecules have no intermolecular forces.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: A false R true

Explanation: Internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on temperature, not pressure.

42. Assertion: In an isothermal process, dU = 0. Reason: Temperature remains constant during an isothermal process.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: Since internal energy depends on T, if T is constant, dU is zero.

43. Assertion: Heat cannot flow from a cold body to a hot body spontaneously. Reason: This is the Clausius statement of the second law.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: The Clausius statement explicitly defines this direction of heat flow.

44. Assertion: The molar heat capacity of a monatomic gas at constant volume is 3/2 R. Reason: Monatomic gases have only translational degrees of freedom.

  1. Both A and R true, R explains A
  2. Both true, R doesn't explain A
  3. A true R false
  4. A false R true

Answer: Both A and R true, R explains A

Explanation: Three translational degrees of freedom lead to Cv = 3/2 R.

45. The first law of thermodynamics is a statement of conservation of energy.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: It states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.

46. In an isobaric process, the work done is zero.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Work done in an isobaric process is P(V2 - V1), which is not zero.

47. Absolute zero is the temperature at which molecular motion ceases.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: At 0 K, kinetic energy of molecules becomes minimum/zero.

48. The efficiency of a Carnot engine is always less than 1.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Efficiency cannot be 1 (100%) as T2 cannot be 0 K.

49. Match List I with List II: List I (Process) A. Isobaric B. Isothermal C. Adiabatic D. Isochoric List II (Constant) 1. Temperature 2. Pressure 3. Volume 4. No heat exchange

  1. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  2. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
  3. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  4. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Answer: A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

Explanation: Isobaric: constant pressure; isothermal: constant temperature; adiabatic: no heat exchange; isochoric: constant volume.

50. Match List I with List II: List I (Law) A. Second law of thermodynamics B. Zeroth law C. Gay-Lussac's law D. First law of thermodynamics List II (About) 1. Energy Conservation 2. Thermal Equilibrium 3. P proportional to T 4. Entropy

  1. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  2. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
  3. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  4. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1

Answer: A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1

Explanation: The second law brings in entropy, the zeroth law defines thermal equilibrium, Gay-Lussac's law gives P ∝ T and the first law is energy conservation.
