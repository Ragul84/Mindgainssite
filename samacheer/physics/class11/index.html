Class11 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class11 questions to revise the Samacheer Physics syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 359 questions.

1. Which of the following is a fundamental unit in the SI system?

  1. Kilogram
  2. Newton
  3. Joule
  4. Watt

Answer: Kilogram

Explanation: The kilogram is a fundamental unit of mass in the SI system, while Newton, Joule, and Watt are derived units.

2. What is the primary focus of the chapter 'Kinematics'?

  1. Forces
  2. Motion of objects
  3. Energy transfer
  4. Thermodynamics

Answer: Motion of objects

Explanation: Kinematics deals with the motion of objects without considering the forces causing the motion.

3. Which law of motion is also known as the law of inertia?

  1. First law
  2. Second law
  3. Third law
  4. Law of gravitation

Answer: First law

Explanation: Newton's First Law of Motion is also known as the law of inertia, which states that an object will remain at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force.

4. What is the SI unit of work?

  1. Joule
  2. Watt
  3. Newton
  4. Pascal

Answer: Joule

Explanation: The SI unit of work is the Joule, which is defined as the work done when a force of one Newton moves an object by one meter.

5. What is the primary focus of the chapter 'Laws of Motion'?

  1. Thermodynamics
  2. Kinematics
  3. Newton's Laws
  4. Gravitation

Answer: Newton's Laws

Explanation: The chapter 'Laws of Motion' primarily focuses on Newton's Laws, which describe the relationship between a body and the forces acting upon it.

6. Which law of motion is primarily concerned with the concept of inertia?

  1. First Law
  2. Second Law
  3. Third Law
  4. Law of Gravitation

Answer: First Law

Explanation: Newton's First Law of Motion, also known as the Law of Inertia, states that an object will remain at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force.

7. What is the universal gravitational constant G?

  1. 6.674 × 10^-11 Nm^2/kg^2
  2. 9.8 m/s^2
  3. 3 × 10^8 m/s
  4. 1.6 × 10^-19 C

Answer: 6.674 × 10^-11 Nm^2/kg^2

Explanation: G is the constant in Newton's law of gravitation, approximately 6.674 × 10^-11 Nm^2/kg^2.

8. Which of the following is true about gravitational force?

  1. It acts only on planets
  2. It acts only on objects with mass
  3. It acts only at short distances
  4. It acts only on celestial bodies

Answer: It acts only on objects with mass

Explanation: Gravitational force acts on all objects with mass, regardless of size or distance.

9. The acceleration due to gravity on the Moon is approximately 1.6 m/s^2. If the mass of the Moon is doubled, what happens to the gravity?

  1. It doubles
  2. It halves
  3. It remains same
  4. It becomes zero

Answer: It remains same

Explanation: Gravity depends on mass; doubling mass doubles gravity, but in this case, the question implies the gravity remains proportional to mass.

10. The value of g at the Earth's surface is approximately 9.8 m/s^2. What is the primary reason for this value?

  1. Earth's mass and radius
  2. Earth's atmosphere
  3. Earth's rotation
  4. Earth's magnetic field

Answer: Earth's mass and radius

Explanation: g depends on Earth's mass and radius as per g = GM/R^2.

11. Which of the following is a correct statement about gravitational potential energy?

  1. It is zero at infinite distance
  2. It is maximum at Earth's surface
  3. It is independent of mass
  4. It is negative near Earth

Answer: It is negative near Earth

Explanation: Gravitational potential energy is negative near Earth due to binding energy.

12. The gravitational force between two objects is 100 N. If the distance between them is doubled, what is the new force?

  1. 25 N
  2. 50 N
  3. 100 N
  4. 200 N

Answer: 25 N

Explanation: Force varies as 1/r^2; doubling r reduces force to 1/4.

13. The acceleration due to gravity at a height h above Earth's surface is given by g' = g(1 - h/R). Is this statement true or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Incorrect; the correct relation is g' = g(1 - 2h/R) for small h.

14. If the radius of Earth decreases, what happens to the acceleration due to gravity on its surface?

  1. Increases
  2. Decreases
  3. Remains same
  4. Becomes zero

Answer: Increases

Explanation: g increases as radius decreases, since g ∝ 1/R^2.

15. The gravitational potential energy of a satellite in orbit is zero. True or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Potential energy is negative; zero at infinity.

16. The gravitational force between two objects is 50 N when separated by 10 m. What will be the force if the separation is 20 m?

  1. 12.5 N
  2. 25 N
  3. 50 N
  4. 100 N

Answer: 12.5 N

Explanation: Force varies as 1/r^2; doubling r reduces force to 1/4.

17. The gravitational constant G is measured in which units?

  1. Nm^2/kg^2
  2. N/m^2
  3. J
  4. kg·m^2/s^2

Answer: Nm^2/kg^2

Explanation: G's units are Nm^2/kg^2.

18. The gravitational force between two objects is 10 N. If the mass of one object is doubled and the other remains same, what is the new force?

  1. 20 N
  2. 10 N
  3. 40 N
  4. 5 N

Answer: 20 N

Explanation: Force is proportional to product of masses; doubling one doubles the force.

19. What is the effect on gravitational force if the distance between two masses is tripled?

  1. Force becomes one-ninth
  2. Force becomes three times
  3. Force remains same
  4. Force doubles

Answer: Force becomes one-ninth

Explanation: Force varies as 1/r^2; tripling r reduces force to 1/9.

20. The gravitational potential energy of an object near Earth's surface is negative. True or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Potential energy is negative relative to infinity.

21. The gravitational force between two objects is 50 N. If the masses are halved, what is the new force?

  1. 12.5 N
  2. 25 N
  3. 50 N
  4. 100 N

Answer: 12.5 N

Explanation: Force is proportional to product of masses; halving both reduces force to one-quarter.

22. Which of the following is the correct relation for the first law of thermodynamics?

  1. dQ = dU + dW
  2. dQ = dU - dW
  3. dU = dQ + dW
  4. dW = dQ + dU

Answer: dQ = dU + dW

Explanation: The first law states that heat supplied equals change in internal energy plus work done by the system.

23. In an adiabatic process, which of the following remains constant?

  1. Temperature
  2. Pressure
  3. Heat
  4. Volume

Answer: Heat

Explanation: An adiabatic process is one in which no heat enters or leaves the system.

24. The efficiency of a Carnot engine depends on:

  1. Working substance
  2. Temperature of source and sink
  3. Volume of cylinder
  4. Pressure of gas

Answer: Temperature of source and sink

Explanation: Efficiency is given by 1 - (T2/T1), where T1 is source and T2 is sink temperature.

25. The value of the universal gas constant R is:

  1. 8.314 J/mol K
  2. 1.38 J/mol K
  3. 6.022 J/mol K
  4. 9.81 J/mol K

Answer: 8.314 J/mol K

Explanation: R is the universal gas constant equal to 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹.
