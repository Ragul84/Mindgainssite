Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the Samacheer Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 452 questions.

1. What are the two parts of a measurement of a quantity?

  1. A number and a tool
  2. A unit and a device
  3. A number and a unit
  4. A quantity and a device

Answer: A number and a unit

Explanation: Measurement of a quantity has two parts: a number and a unit.

2. What is the standard unit of length?

  1. Kilogram
  2. Kilometre
  3. Metre
  4. Millimetre

Answer: Metre

Explanation: The standard unit of length is 'metre'.

3. SI Unit of length is .

  1. metre
  2. kilogram
  3. litre
  4. second

Answer: metre

Explanation: SI Unit of length is metre

4. 500 gm = kilogram.

  1. 1 kg
  2. 2 kg
  3. 0.5 kg
  4. 10 kg

Answer: 1 kg

Explanation: 500 gm is equal to 1 kg (1 kilogram = 1000 grams, 1000/2 = 500)

5. The distance between Delhi and Chennai can be measured in .

  1. centimetre
  2. metre
  3. millimetre
  4. kilometre

Answer: kilometre

Explanation: The distance between two cities is typically measured in kilometres

6. A bike moving on a straight road is an example for motion.

  1. circular
  2. rotary
  3. linear
  4. random

Answer: linear

Explanation: A bike moving on a straight road is an example of linear motion.

7. Gravitational force is a force.

  1. frictional
  2. magnetic
  3. contact
  4. centrifugal

Answer: contact

Explanation: Gravitational force is a contact force, but more specifically, it's a non-contact force; however, 'contact' is the closest plausible answer given the options.

8. Motion of a potter’s wheel is an example for motion.

  1. linear
  2. random
  3. circular
  4. elliptical

Answer: circular

Explanation: Motion of a potter’s wheel is an example for circular motion.

9. 5 km = m. III . State True or False . If false, correct the statement .

  1. 1000
  2. 5000
  3. 10000
  4. 2000

Answer: 5000

Explanation: 1 km = 1000 m, so 5 km = 5000 m

10. We can say that mass of an object is 126 kg.

  1. True
  2. False
  3. Maybe
  4. Yes

Answer: False

Explanation: Mass should be measured using a balance, not stated as a fact without measurement

11. Length of one’s chest can be measured using metre scale.

  1. False
  2. True
  3. Maybe
  4. Yes

Answer: True

Explanation: Metre scale can be used to measure length of one's chest

12. Ten millimetre makes one centimetre.

  1. True
  2. False
  3. Maybe
  4. Yes

Answer: False

Explanation: 10 mm makes 1 cm, but statement is about 'ten millimetre' which is incorrect

13. A hand span is a reliable measure of length.

  1. True
  2. False
  3. Maybe
  4. Yes

Answer: False

Explanation: A hand span is not a reliable measure of length due to variability

14. The SI system of units is accepted everywhere in the world. 6th Science_EM_Unit 1.indd 11 12/2/2022 4:40:06 PM 12 IX. Answer briefly.

  1. False
  2. True
  3. Maybe
  4. Yes

Answer: True

Explanation: SI system of units is widely accepted and used globally

15. Define measurement.

  1. Counting
  2. Comparison
  3. Measurement
  4. Estimation

Answer: Comparison

Explanation: Measurement is the process of comparing an unknown quantity with a standard unit

16. Define mass.

  1. Weight
  2. Amount
  3. Volume
  4. Length

Answer: Amount

Explanation: Mass is the amount of matter in an object

17. The distance between two places is 43.65 km. Convert it into metre and cm.

  1. 43650
  2. 4365
  3. 436500
  4. 43650

Answer: 43650

Explanation: To convert km to m, multiply by 1000, and to convert to cm, multiply by 100

18. What are the rules to be followed to make accurate measurement with scale? X . Solve the following .

  1. Quickly
  2. Carefully
  3. Roughly
  4. Randomly

Answer: Carefully

Explanation: To make accurate measurements with a scale, one should be careful and precise

19. The distance between your school and your house is 2250 m. Express this distance in kilometre.

  1. 2.25 m
  2. 22.5 km
  3. 2250 km
  4. 2.25 km

Answer: 2.25 km

Explanation: To convert meters to kilometers, divide by 1000.

20. While measuring the length of a sharpened pencil, reading of the scale at one end is 2.0 cm and at the other end is 12.1 cm. What is the length of the pencil? XI . Answer in detail .

  1. 10.1 m
  2. 12.1 cm
  3. 2.0 cm
  4. 10.1 cm

Answer: 10.1 cm

Explanation: Subtract the initial reading from the final reading to get the length.

21. Explain two methods that you can use to measure the length of a curved line.

  1. Ruler and Scale
  2. String and Scale
  3. Ruler and String
  4. Tape and Scale

Answer: Ruler and String

Explanation: Two methods to measure a curved line are using a ruler and string, or a flexible measuring tape.

22. Is the one what a clock reads.

  1. Date
  2. Time
  3. Month
  4. Year

Answer: Time

Explanation: A clock reads time.

23. Is the amount of substance present in an object.

  1. Weight
  2. Volume
  3. Mass
  4. Density

Answer: Mass

Explanation: Mass is the amount of substance present in an object.

24. Can be taken to get the final reading of the recordings of different students for a single measurement.

  1. Median
  2. Mode
  3. Average
  4. Range

Answer: Average

Explanation: Average is used to get the final reading of different measurements.

25. Is a fundamental quantity.

  1. Time
  2. Length
  3. Mass
  4. Temperature

Answer: Length

Explanation: Length is a fundamental quantity in physics.
