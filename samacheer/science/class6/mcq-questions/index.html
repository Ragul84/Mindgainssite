26. A tailor uses to take measurements to stitch the cloth.

  1. Ruler
  2. Scale
  3. Tape
  4. Meter

Answer: Tape

Explanation: A tailor uses a tape to take measurements.

27. When an object covers equal distances in equal interval of time, it is said to be in motion.

  1. random
  2. circular
  3. uniform
  4. irregular

Answer: uniform

Explanation: Uniform motion implies equal distances in equal time intervals.

28. Chillies are removed from ‘Upma’ by method.

  1. sieving
  2. sedimentation
  3. filtration
  4. decantation

Answer: sieving

Explanation: Chillies are removed from Upma by sieving method.

29. Water obtained from tube wells is usually water.

Explanation: Water obtained from tube wells is usually hard water.
