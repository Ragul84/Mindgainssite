Class7 MCQs with answers

26. What is the process by which plants make their own food from sunlight?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Decomposition
  4. Fermentation

Answer: Photosynthesis

Explanation: Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water into glucose and oxygen. It is a vital process that supports life on Earth.

27. Which human system is responsible for breaking down food into nutrients?

  1. Nervous system
  2. Circulatory system
  3. Digestive system
  4. Respiratory system

Answer: Digestive system

Explanation: The digestive system is responsible for breaking down food into nutrients that can be absorbed and utilized by the body. It includes organs such as the mouth, esophagus, stomach, and intestines.

28. What type of lens is used to correct nearsightedness?

  1. Concave lens
  2. Convex lens
  3. Plano lens
  4. Cylindrical lens

Answer: Concave lens

Explanation: A concave lens is used to correct nearsightedness, also known as myopia, by spreading out light rays before they enter the eye. This helps to focus the image on the retina.

29. Which part of the human eye is sensitive to light?

  1. Cornea
  2. Iris
  3. Retina
  4. Lens

Answer: Retina

Explanation: The retina is the light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye that converts light into electrical signals. These signals are transmitted to the brain, where they are interpreted as visual information.

30. What is the byproduct of photosynthesis that is released into the air?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Oxygen
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Water vapor

Answer: Oxygen

Explanation: Oxygen is the byproduct of photosynthesis that is released into the air as a result of the conversion of carbon dioxide and water into glucose. It is essential for the survival of most living organisms.

31. What is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the leaves?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the leaves, and is then released into the air as water vapor. It is an important part of the water cycle.

32. Which human system is responsible for transporting oxygen and nutrients to cells?

  1. Nervous system
  2. Circulatory system
  3. Digestive system
  4. Respiratory system

Answer: Circulatory system

Explanation: The circulatory system is responsible for transporting oxygen and nutrients to cells and removing waste products. It includes the heart, blood vessels, and blood.

33. Which sense organ is responsible for detecting light?

  1. Eye
  2. Ear
  3. Nose
  4. Tongue

Answer: Eye

Explanation: The eye is the sense organ responsible for detecting light and transmitting visual information to the brain. The eye contains specialized cells called photoreceptors that convert light into electrical signals.

34. What is the process by which the human body gets rid of waste?

  1. Respiration
  2. Digestion
  3. Excretion
  4. Circulation

Answer: Excretion

Explanation: Excretion is the process by which the human body gets rid of waste products, such as urine and feces. The kidneys and liver play important roles in this process.

35. Which part of the human skeleton protects the brain?

  1. Ribcage
  2. Skull
  3. Vertebral column
  4. Pelvis

Answer: Skull

Explanation: The skull is the bony structure that protects the brain, which is the control center of the body. The skull is made up of several bones that fuse together during childhood.

36. What is the main function of the human circulatory system?

  1. To digest food
  2. To transport oxygen
  3. To filter waste
  4. To circulate blood

Answer: To circulate blood

Explanation: The human circulatory system is responsible for circulating blood throughout the body, which delivers oxygen and nutrients to cells and removes waste products. The heart is the central organ of the circulatory system.

37. What is the role of chlorophyll in plant nutrition?

  1. To absorb water
  2. To produce flowers
  3. To make food
  4. To transport nutrients

Answer: To make food

Explanation: Chlorophyll is the green pigment found in plant cells that plays a crucial role in photosynthesis, which is the process by which plants make their own food. Chlorophyll absorbs light energy from the sun, which is then used to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.

38. Which part of the human brain controls breathing and heartbeat?

  1. Cerebrum
  2. Cerebellum
  3. Medulla oblongata
  4. Brainstem

Answer: Medulla oblongata

Explanation: The medulla oblongata is the lowest part of the brainstem and regulates automatic functions like breathing and heartbeat. It is a critical part of the central nervous system.

39. What is the role of the roots in plant nutrition?

  1. To absorb sunlight
  2. To absorb water and minerals
  3. To release oxygen
  4. To produce flowers

Answer: To absorb water and minerals

Explanation: The roots of a plant absorb water and minerals from the soil, which are then transported to the rest of the plant. This process is essential for plant growth and development.

40. Which sense organ is responsible for detecting light and color?

  1. Eye
  2. Ear
  3. Nose
  4. Tongue

Answer: Eye

Explanation: The eye is the sense organ responsible for detecting light and color, and it contains specialized cells called photoreceptors that convert light into electrical signals. These signals are then interpreted by the brain as visual information.

41. Which part of the human eye changes shape to focus on near or far objects?

  1. Cornea
  2. Iris
  3. Lens
  4. Retina

Answer: Lens

Explanation: The lens in the human eye changes shape to focus on near or far objects, a process known as accommodation. This is achieved through the contraction or relaxation of the ciliary muscles that surround the lens.

42. Which part of the human body helps in breathing?

  1. Heart
  2. Lungs
  3. Brain
  4. Liver

Answer: Lungs

Explanation: Lungs are the organs that help in breathing, taking in oxygen and expelling carbon dioxide.

43. What is the largest planet in our solar system?

  1. Earth
  2. Saturn
  3. Jupiter
  4. Uranus

Answer: Jupiter

Explanation: Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, with a massive size and stormy atmosphere.

44. Which sense organ helps us to see?

  1. Eyes
  2. Ears
  3. Nose
  4. Tongue

Answer: Eyes

Explanation: Eyes are the sense organs that help us to see and perceive the world around us.

45. What is the scientific term for the study of the structure of the Earth?

  1. Geology
  2. Biology
  3. Chemistry
  4. Physics

Answer: Geology

Explanation: Geology is the scientific term for the study of the structure, composition, and processes of the Earth.

46. Which element is essential for plant growth and development?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Carbon
  4. Hydrogen

Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen is an essential element for plant growth and development, necessary for photosynthesis and nutrient uptake.

47. Which type of rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of molten lava?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Foliated

Answer: Igneous

Explanation: Igneous rocks are formed from the cooling and solidification of molten lava, either below the Earth's surface or above as a result of volcanic activity.

48. What is the term for the movement of animals from one place to another at certain times of the year?

  1. Migration
  2. Hibernation
  3. Camouflage
  4. Adaptation

Answer: Migration

Explanation: Migration is the term for the movement of animals from one place to another at certain times of the year, often in search of food or shelter.

49. Which part of the human body helps in filtering waste and excess fluids?

  1. Kidneys
  2. Liver
  3. Heart
  4. Lungs

Answer: Kidneys

Explanation: Kidneys are the organs that help in filtering waste and excess fluids from the blood, regulating electrolyte balance and blood pressure.

50. What is the scientific term for the study of the weather and atmosphere?

  1. Meteorology
  2. Geology
  3. Biology
  4. Chemistry

Answer: Meteorology

Explanation: Meteorology is the scientific term for the study of the weather and atmosphere, including temperature, humidity, clouds, and precipitation.
