Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the Samacheer Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 589 questions.

1. What is the main function of the human respiratory system?

  1. To digest food
  2. To transport oxygen
  3. To circulate blood
  4. To filter waste

Answer: To transport oxygen

Explanation: The main function of the human respiratory system is to transport oxygen to the cells and remove carbon dioxide from the body. This process is essential for the survival of living organisms.

2. Which part of the human eye helps to change the shape of the lens?

  1. Iris
  2. Pupil
  3. Cornea
  4. Ciliary muscles

Answer: Ciliary muscles

Explanation: The ciliary muscles help to change the shape of the lens, allowing the eye to focus on objects at different distances. This process is known as accommodation.

3. What is the process by which plants make their own food?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Decomposition
  4. Fermentation

Answer: Photosynthesis

Explanation: Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria make their own food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. This process is essential for the survival of most living organisms.

4. Which element is a noble gas in the periodic table?

  1. Hydrogen
  2. Helium
  3. Oxygen
  4. Nitrogen

Answer: Helium

Explanation: Helium is a noble gas, which means it is unreactive and does not readily form compounds with other elements. Noble gases are found in group 18 of the periodic table.

5. What type of wave is sound?

  1. Mechanical wave
  2. Electromagnetic wave
  3. Longitudinal wave
  4. Transverse wave

Answer: Mechanical wave

Explanation: Sound is a type of mechanical wave, which means it requires a medium to propagate. Mechanical waves can be either longitudinal or transverse, but sound is a longitudinal wave.

6. What is the function of the roots in a plant?

  1. To make food
  2. To transport water
  3. To absorb nutrients
  4. To produce flowers

Answer: To absorb nutrients

Explanation: The roots of a plant absorb nutrients and water from the soil, which are then transported to the rest of the plant. This process is essential for the growth and survival of the plant.

7. Which sense organ is responsible for maintaining balance and equilibrium?

  1. Eye
  2. Ear
  3. Nose
  4. Tongue

Answer: Ear

Explanation: The ear is responsible for maintaining balance and equilibrium, as well as hearing. The vestibular system in the inner ear helps to detect changes in position and movement.

8. What is the process by which water moves through a plant?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Osmosis

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant, from the roots to the leaves, and is then released into the air as water vapor. This process is an important part of the water cycle.

9. Which type of rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma?

  1. Igneous rock
  2. Sedimentary rock
  3. Metamorphic rock
  4. Foliated rock

Answer: Igneous rock

Explanation: Igneous rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma, which can occur either below the surface of the Earth or as a result of volcanic activity. This type of rock can be either intrusive or extrusive.

10. What is the largest part of the human brain?

  1. Cerebrum
  2. Cerebellum
  3. Brainstem
  4. Frontal lobe

Answer: Cerebrum

Explanation: The cerebrum is the largest part of the human brain, accounting for about 85% of its total weight. It is responsible for processing sensory information, controlling movement, and managing higher-level cognitive functions.

11. Which nutrient is essential for the growth and development of plants?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Phosphorus
  3. Potassium
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium are all essential nutrients for the growth and development of plants. These nutrients are often referred to as NPK and are commonly found in fertilizers.

12. What is the term for the bending of light as it passes from one medium to another?

  1. Reflection
  2. Refraction
  3. Diffraction
  4. Dispersion

Answer: Refraction

Explanation: Refraction is the term for the bending of light as it passes from one medium to another, such as from air into water or glass. This phenomenon is responsible for a variety of optical effects, including the formation of rainbows.

13. What is the main function of the roots in a plant?

  1. To make food
  2. To breathe
  3. To absorb water
  4. To produce flowers

Answer: To absorb water

Explanation: The main function of roots is to absorb water and minerals from the soil, which is essential for plant growth. This process is a part of plant nutrition.

14. Which part of the human eye helps in changing the shape of the lens?

  1. Iris
  2. Pupil
  3. Cornea
  4. Ciliary muscles

Answer: Ciliary muscles

Explanation: The ciliary muscles help in changing the shape of the lens, which enables the eye to focus on objects at different distances. This is an important aspect of the human eye and its functioning.

15. Which of the following is a characteristic of a sound wave?

  1. It can travel through a vacuum
  2. It is a form of electromagnetic radiation
  3. It requires a medium to travel
  4. It has a very high speed

Answer: It requires a medium to travel

Explanation: Sound waves require a medium, such as air, water, or solids, to travel, as they are mechanical waves that transfer energy through the vibration of particles. This is a fundamental property of sound waves.

16. Which element is in the first period and first group of the periodic table?

  1. Hydrogen
  2. Helium
  3. Lithium
  4. Beryllium

Answer: Hydrogen

Explanation: Hydrogen is the lightest and most abundant chemical element in the universe, located in the first period and first group of the periodic table. It is highly reactive and forms compounds with most elements.

17. What is the function of the pancreas in the human body?

  1. To filter waste from the blood
  2. To produce digestive enzymes
  3. To regulate body temperature
  4. To store urine

Answer: To produce digestive enzymes

Explanation: The pancreas produces digestive enzymes that help break down food into smaller molecules, which can then be absorbed and utilized by the body. It also regulates blood sugar levels by producing insulin and glucagon.

18. Which type of lens is used to correct nearsightedness?

  1. Convex lens
  2. Concave lens
  3. Plano lens
  4. Cylindrical lens

Answer: Concave lens

Explanation: A concave lens is used to correct nearsightedness, also known as myopia, by spreading out light rays before they enter the eye, which helps to focus them properly on the retina. This type of lens is thinner in the middle than at the edges.

19. Which part of the human ear helps in maintaining balance?

  1. Cochlea
  2. Eustachian tube
  3. Malleus
  4. Vestibule

Answer: Vestibule

Explanation: The vestibule, along with the semicircular canals, helps in maintaining balance and equilibrium by detecting changes in the position and movement of the head. This is an important function of the inner ear.

20. What is the byproduct of photosynthesis?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Glucose
  3. Oxygen
  4. Water

Answer: Oxygen

Explanation: Oxygen is the byproduct of photosynthesis, released into the air as a result of the conversion of light energy into chemical energy. This process is essential for the survival of most living organisms.

21. Which of the following is a type of nutrient that plants require?

  1. Carbohydrates
  2. Proteins
  3. Fats
  4. Minerals

Answer: Minerals

Explanation: Minerals, such as nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium, are essential nutrients that plants require for growth and development. These minerals are obtained from the soil and are vital for various plant functions.

22. What is the main function of the human skeletal system?

  1. To produce blood cells
  2. To protect internal organs
  3. To facilitate movement
  4. To regulate body temperature

Answer: To protect internal organs

Explanation: The human skeletal system provides protection to internal organs such as the brain, heart, and lungs. It also provides a framework for the attachment of muscles, allowing for movement.

23. Which part of the plant is responsible for absorbing water and minerals from the soil?

  1. Roots
  2. Stem
  3. Leaves
  4. Flowers

Answer: Roots

Explanation: Roots are the underground structures of a plant that absorb water and minerals from the soil. They play a crucial role in plant nutrition and growth.

24. What type of energy is transferred through vibrations in a medium?

  1. Light energy
  2. Sound energy
  3. Heat energy
  4. Electrical energy

Answer: Sound energy

Explanation: Sound energy is transferred through vibrations in a medium, such as air, water, or solids. It is a form of mechanical energy that is perceived by the ear.

25. Which element is the lightest in the periodic table?

  1. Hydrogen
  2. Helium
  3. Oxygen
  4. Nitrogen

Answer: Hydrogen

Explanation: Hydrogen is the lightest element in the periodic table, with an atomic number of 1. It is a highly reactive gas that is abundant in the universe.
