Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the Samacheer English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 823 questions.

1. Which of the following is an example of inversion in a sentence?

  1. The dog ran quickly
  2. The sun rises in the east
  3. Up went the balloon
  4. The baby laughed loudly

Answer: Up went the balloon

Explanation: Inversion occurs when the normal subject-verb order is reversed. 'Up went the balloon' is an example of inversion.

2. What is the reported speech of 'I will go to the store,' said John?

  1. John said he will go to the store
  2. John said he would go to the store
  3. John said I will go to the store
  4. John said I would go to the store

Answer: John said he would go to the store

Explanation: In reported speech, the verb tense changes from present to past. 'John said he would go to the store' is the correct reported speech.

3. What is the meaning of the compound word 'bookshelf'?

  1. A shelf for books
  2. A book about shelves
  3. A shelf made of books
  4. A book on the shelf

Answer: A shelf for books

Explanation: A compound word is a word made up of two or more words. 'Bookshelf' refers to a shelf for books.

4. Which prepositional phrase indicates location?

  1. In the morning
  2. On the table
  3. At the store
  4. Under the bridge

Answer: On the table

Explanation: Prepositional phrases indicate location, time, or direction. 'On the table' indicates location.

5. What is the meaning of the cliche 'break a leg'?

  1. To wish someone good luck
  2. To wish someone bad luck
  3. To tell someone to be careful
  4. To tell someone to take a break

Answer: To wish someone good luck

Explanation: A cliche is an overused expression. 'Break a leg' means to wish someone good luck.

6. Which of the following is an example of an idiom?

  1. It is raining cats and dogs
  2. The sun is shining brightly
  3. The baby is sleeping soundly
  4. The dog is barking loudly

Answer: It is raining cats and dogs

Explanation: An idiom is a phrase with a non-literal meaning. 'It is raining cats and dogs' is an example of an idiom.

7. What is the difference between 'lift' and 'elevator'?

  1. Lift is British, elevator is American
  2. Lift is American, elevator is British
  3. Lift is a noun, elevator is a verb
  4. Lift is a verb, elevator is a noun

Answer: Lift is British, elevator is American

Explanation: British and American English have different vocabulary. 'Lift' is British, while 'elevator' is American.

8. Which of the following words is a synonym for 'big'?

  1. Large
  2. Small
  3. Happy
  4. Sad

Answer: Large

Explanation: A synonym is a word with the same meaning. 'Large' is a synonym for 'big'.

9. What is the meaning of the phrase 'in the nick of time'?

  1. Just in time
  2. Too late
  3. Very early
  4. Very late

Answer: Just in time

Explanation: The phrase 'in the nick of time' means just in time. It is an idiom.

10. Which of the following is an example of a prepositional phrase?

  1. The dog ran quickly
  2. The baby slept soundly
  3. The book is on the table
  4. The sun rises in the east

Answer: The book is on the table

Explanation: A prepositional phrase indicates location, time, or direction. 'The book is on the table' contains the prepositional phrase 'on the table'.

11. What is the meaning of the word 'nuance'?

  1. A subtle difference
  2. A big difference
  3. A happy feeling
  4. A sad feeling

Answer: A subtle difference

Explanation: The word 'nuance' refers to a subtle difference. It is a vocabulary word from the Samacheer Kalvi textbook.

12. Which of the following is an example of a compound word?

  1. Bookshelf
  2. Happy
  3. Big
  4. Run

Answer: Bookshelf

Explanation: A compound word is a word made up of two or more words. 'Bookshelf' is an example of a compound word.

13. What is the meaning of the phrase 'on the same page'?

  1. To be in agreement
  2. To be in disagreement
  3. To be happy
  4. To be sad

Answer: To be in agreement

Explanation: The phrase 'on the same page' means to be in agreement. It is an idiom.

14. Which of the following words is a synonym for 'happy'?

  1. Joyful
  2. Sad
  3. Big
  4. Small

Answer: Joyful

Explanation: A synonym is a word with the same meaning. 'Joyful' is a synonym for 'happy'.

15. What is the meaning of the word 'cliche'?

  1. An overused expression
  2. A new expression
  3. A happy feeling
  4. A sad feeling

Answer: An overused expression

Explanation: A cliche is an overused expression. It is a vocabulary word from the Samacheer Kalvi textbook.

16. Choose the correct form of inversion in the sentence: 'Not only did he...

  1. did he come
  2. he came
  3. came he
  4. he did come

Answer: did he come

Explanation: Inversion occurs after 'not only'.

17. Identify the reported speech for 'I will go'

  1. He said that he would go
  2. He said that he will go
  3. He said he would go
  4. He said he will go

Answer: He said that he would go

Explanation: Reported speech changes 'will' to 'would'.

18. What is the compound word for 'book' and 'shelf'

  1. bookshelf
  2. bookcase
  3. bookrack
  4. bookstore

Answer: bookshelf

Explanation: Compound word combines two words.

19. Choose the correct prepositional phrase for 'at the end of'

  1. by
  2. at
  3. on
  4. in

Answer: at

Explanation: Prepositional phrase shows location.

20. Identify the cliche for 'a lot of work'

  1. a piece of cake
  2. a breeze
  3. an uphill task
  4. a walk in the park

Answer: an uphill task

Explanation: Cliche is an overused expression.

21. Choose the idiom for 'to stop something'

  1. call it a day
  2. call off
  3. put off
  4. give up

Answer: call off

Explanation: Idiom is a fixed expression.

22. What is the American English variation for 'lift'

  1. elevator
  2. staircase
  3. escalator
  4. ramp

Answer: elevator

Explanation: American English differs from British English.

  1. mobile phone
  2. cell phone
  3. telephone
  4. phone

Answer: mobile phone

Explanation: British English differs from American English.

  1. who
  2. whom
  3. that
  4. which

Answer: that

Explanation: Reported speech changes 'who' to 'that'.

  1. tourist
  2. traveler
