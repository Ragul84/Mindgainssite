Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class10 questions to revise the Samacheer English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 913 questions.

1. What is the main theme of 'The Nose-Jewel' by C. Rajagopalachari?

  1. Love
  2. Friendship
  3. Self-discovery
  4. Morality

Answer: Morality

Explanation: The story revolves around moral values and principles.

2. In the poem 'I am Every Woman' by Rakhi Nariani Shirke, what is the central idea?

  1. Empowerment
  2. Nature
  3. Freedom
  4. Equality

Answer: Empowerment

Explanation: The poem celebrates women's empowerment and individuality.

3. Which tense is used in the sentence 'The bird will fly tomorrow'?

  1. Past
  2. Present
  3. Future
  4. Past Perfect

Answer: Future

Explanation: The sentence describes an action that will happen in the future.

4. What is the meaning of the phrase 'at the helm' in the context of 'The Tempest' adaptation?

  1. In control
  2. In danger
  3. In love
  4. In trouble

Answer: In control

Explanation: The phrase means being in charge or having control.

5. Identify the relative clause in the sentence 'The book, which is on the table, is mine'?

  1. The book
  2. which is on the table
  3. is mine
  4. The table

Answer: which is on the table

Explanation: The relative clause describes the noun 'book'.

6. What is the author's tone in the poem 'The Crocodile'?

  1. Serious
  2. Humorous
  3. Sad
  4. Angry

Answer: Humorous

Explanation: The poem has a humorous tone.

7. In 'His First Flight' by Liam O'Flaherty, what is the main character's struggle?

  1. Fear of flying
  2. Fear of water
  3. Fear of heights
  4. Fear of failure

Answer: Fear of flying

Explanation: The main character, a seagull, struggles with the fear of flying.

8. Which preposition is used to indicate movement from one place to another?

  1. In
  2. On
  3. At
  4. From

Answer: From

Explanation: The preposition 'from' indicates movement away from a place.

9. What is the meaning of the word 'solitary' in the poem 'Special Hero'?

  1. Alone
  2. Happy
  3. Brave
  4. Strong

Answer: Alone

Explanation: The word 'solitary' means being alone or isolated.

10. Identify the active voice in the sentence 'The ball was thrown by John'?

  1. The ball was thrown
  2. The ball threw
  3. John threw the ball
  4. John throws the ball

Answer: John threw the ball

Explanation: The active voice is 'John threw the ball'.

11. What is the author's purpose in writing 'The Nose-Jewel'?

  1. To entertain
  2. To educate
  3. To persuade
  4. To inform

Answer: To educate

Explanation: The author's purpose is to educate readers about moral values.

12. In the poem 'I am Every Woman' by Rakhi Nariani Shirke, what is the poet's attitude towards women?

  1. Respectful
  2. Disrespectful
  3. Indifferent
  4. Angry

Answer: Respectful

Explanation: The poet has a respectful attitude towards women.

13. Which verb form is used in the sentence 'If I had studied, I would have passed'?

  1. Past Perfect
  2. Past Simple
  3. Present Perfect
  4. Future Perfect

Answer: Past Perfect

Explanation: The sentence uses the past perfect tense.

14. What is the central idea of the poem 'The Crocodile'?

  1. Nature
  2. Wildlife
  3. Conservation
  4. Humor

Answer: Humor

Explanation: The poem has a humorous tone and central idea.

15. In 'His First Flight' by Liam O'Flaherty, what is the main character's final realization?

  1. Fear is necessary
  2. Fear is useless
  3. Fear is helpful
  4. Fear is dangerous

Answer: Fear is useless

Explanation: The main character realizes that fear is useless.

16. Which word is a synonym for 'courage' in the poem 'Special Hero'?

  1. Bravery
  2. Strength
  3. Happiness
  4. Sadness

Answer: Bravery

Explanation: The word 'bravery' is a synonym for 'courage'.

17. What is the main theme of the story 'The Nose-Jewel' by C. Rajagopalachari?

  1. Love
  2. Friendship
  3. Sacrifice
  4. Loyalty

Answer: Sacrifice

Explanation: The story 'The Nose-Jewel' revolves around the sacrifice of a wife for her husband's well-being.

18. Which poem is written by Rakhi Nariani Shirke?

  1. The Crocodile
  2. Special Hero
  3. I am Every Woman
  4. The Tempest

Answer: I am Every Woman

Explanation: Rakhi Nariani Shirke is the author of the poem 'I am Every Woman'.

19. What is the tense of the sentence 'I will study for the exam'?

  1. Present Simple
  2. Past Simple
  3. Future Simple
  4. Present Continuous

Answer: Future Simple

Explanation: The sentence 'I will study for the exam' is in the Future Simple tense.

20. Who wrote the short story 'His First Flight'?

  1. Liam O'Flaherty
  2. C. Rajagopalachari
  3. Rakhi Nariani Shirke
  4. William Shakespeare

Answer: Liam O'Flaherty

Explanation: Liam O'Flaherty is the author of the short story 'His First Flight'.

21. What is the meaning of the word 'tempest'?

  1. Storm
  2. Calm
  3. Peace
  4. War

Answer: Storm

Explanation: The word 'tempest' means a violent storm.

22. Which of the following is an example of a relative clause?

  1. I study English
  2. The book is mine
  3. The boy who studies English
  4. The teacher is strict

Answer: The boy who studies English

Explanation: The phrase 'The boy who studies English' is an example of a relative clause.

23. What is the passive voice of the sentence 'The teacher teaches the students'?

  1. The students teach the teacher
  2. The students are taught by the teacher
  3. The teacher is taught by the students
  4. The teacher teaches the students

Answer: The students are taught by the teacher

Explanation: The passive voice of the sentence 'The teacher teaches the students' is 'The students are taught by the teacher'.

24. What is the preposition in the sentence 'The book is on the table'?

  1. On
  2. In
  3. At
  4. Under

Answer: On

Explanation: The preposition in the sentence 'The book is on the table' is 'on'.

25. Which of the following is an example of a verb in the past tense?

  1. I study
  2. I studied
  3. I will study
  4. I am studying

Answer: I studied

Explanation: The verb 'studied' is an example of a verb in the past tense.
