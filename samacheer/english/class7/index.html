Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the Samacheer English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 877 questions.

1. What is the main theme of the story 'The Nose-Jewel' by C. Rajagopalachari?

  1. Love
  2. Friendship
  3. Sacrifice
  4. Loyalty

Answer: Sacrifice

Explanation: The story 'The Nose-Jewel' revolves around the theme of sacrifice, as the protagonist gives up her precious nose-jewel for her husband's sake.

2. Which tense is used in the sentence 'I am going to school'?

  1. Present Simple
  2. Present Continuous
  3. Past Simple
  4. Future Simple

Answer: Future Simple

Explanation: The sentence 'I am going to school' is in the Future Simple tense.

3. What is the meaning of the phrase 'I am every woman' in the poem 'I am Every Woman'?

  1. Independent
  2. Strong
  3. Confident
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The phrase 'I am every woman' means that the speaker is confident, independent, and strong.

4. Who wrote the story 'His First Flight'?

  1. Liam O'Flaherty
  2. C. Rajagopalachari
  3. William Shakespeare
  4. R.K. Narayan

Answer: Liam O'Flaherty

Explanation: The story 'His First Flight' was written by Liam O'Flaherty.

5. What is the preposition used in the sentence 'The book is under the table'?

  1. In
  2. On
  3. Under
  4. Above

Answer: Under

Explanation: The preposition 'under' is used in the sentence.

6. Which poem is about a crocodile?

  1. The Crocodile
  2. The Snake
  3. The Elephant
  4. The Lion

Answer: The Crocodile

Explanation: The poem 'The Crocodile' is about a crocodile.

7. What is the relative clause in the sentence 'The boy who is standing is my brother'?

  1. who is standing
  2. is standing
  3. my brother
  4. the boy

Answer: who is standing

Explanation: The relative clause in the sentence is 'who is standing'.

8. Who is the hero of the poem 'Special Hero'?

  1. A soldier
  2. A doctor
  3. A teacher
  4. A firefighter

Answer: A soldier

Explanation: The hero of the poem 'Special Hero' is a soldier.

9. What is the passive voice of the sentence 'The dog bites the man'?

  1. The man bites the dog
  2. The dog is bitten by the man
  3. The man is bitten by the dog
  4. The dog bites the man

Answer: The man is bitten by the dog

Explanation: The passive voice of the sentence is 'The man is bitten by the dog'.

10. Which adaptation is studied in Class 7?

  1. The Tempest
  2. Romeo and Juliet
  3. Hamlet
  4. Macbeth

Answer: The Tempest

Explanation: The adaptation 'The Tempest' is studied in Class 7.

  2. Rain

  4. Cloud

  1. Present Simple
  2. Present Continuous
  3. Past Simple
  4. Future Simple

Answer: Future Simple

Explanation: The sentence 'I will go to school' is in the Future Simple tense.

14. Which poem is about a woman's empowerment?

  3. The Snake

Answer: I am Every Woman

Explanation: The poem 'I am Every Woman' is about a woman's empowerment.

15. What is the preposition used in the sentence 'The book is on the table'?

  1. In
  2. On
  3. Under
  4. Above

Answer: On

Explanation: The preposition 'on' is used in the sentence.

16. Which tense is used to describe an action that started in the past and continues up to the present?

  1. Past Simple
  2. Present Perfect
  3. Past Perfect
  4. Future Simple

Answer: Present Perfect

Explanation: Present Perfect tense is used for actions starting in the past and continuing up to the present.

17. What is the preposition used to indicate movement from one place to another?

  1. From
  2. To
  3. At
  4. With

Answer: From

Explanation: From is used to indicate movement from one place.

18. Which relative clause is used to give essential information about a noun?

  1. Restrictive
  2. Non-Restrictive
  3. Defining
  4. Non-Defining

Answer: Restrictive

Explanation: Restrictive relative clauses give essential information.

19. What is the term for changing a sentence from active to passive voice?

  1. Active Voice
  2. Passive Voice
  3. Voice Change
  4. Tense Change

Answer: Voice Change

Explanation: Voice Change refers to changing from active to passive voice.

  1. Future Simple
  2. Future Perfect
  3. Past Simple
  4. Present Simple

Answer: Future Simple

Explanation: Future Simple tense is used for future actions.
