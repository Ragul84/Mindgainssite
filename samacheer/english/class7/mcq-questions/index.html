26. Which relative clause is used to give non-essential information about a noun?

  2. Non-Restrictive

  4. Non-Defining

Answer: Non-Restrictive

Explanation: Non-Restrictive relative clauses give non-essential information.

Explanation: Tense Change refers to changing from one tense to another.
