26. What is the meaning of the word 'nemesis'?

  1. a hero
  2. a villain
  3. an enemy
  4. a friend

Answer: an enemy

Explanation: A nemesis is an archenemy or a person's worst enemy.

27. Choose the correct form of the verb in the sentence: If I ______ more time, I would finish the project.

  1. have
  2. has
  3. had
  4. having

Answer: had

Explanation: The correct form is 'had' as it is used in the second conditional to talk about impossible situations.

28. Identify the type of sentence: What a beautiful day it is!

  1. Exclamatory
  2. Interrogative
  3. Declarative
  4. Imperative

Answer: Exclamatory

Explanation: The sentence is an example of an exclamatory sentence as it expresses strong emotions.

29. Choose the correct sentence using American English: The ______ of the company is very successful.

  1. CEO
  2. MD
  3. President
  4. Chairman

Answer: CEO

Explanation: In American English, 'CEO' is commonly used to refer to the chief executive officer of a company.

30. What is the meaning of the idiom 'bend over backwards'?

  1. to be lazy
  2. to be helpful
  3. to be angry
  4. to be sad

Answer: to be helpful

Explanation: The idiom 'bend over backwards' means to go out of one's way to help or please someone.

31. Which prepositional phrase indicates movement from one place to another?

  1. In the room
  2. On the table
  3. From home to school
  4. Under the bridge

Answer: From home to school

Explanation: The phrase indicates movement.

32. What is the difference between 'lift' and 'elevator'?

  1. Lift is American

  3. Lift is British
  4. Elevator is American

Answer: Lift is British

Explanation: Lift is used in British English.

33. Which of the following is a characteristic of American English?

  3. Using 'cookies' instead of 'biscuits'

Explanation: American English uses 'cookies'.

35. What is the difference between 'practise' and 'practice'?

Explanation: Practise is a verb in British English.

36. Which of the following is an example of inversion in a conditional sentence?

Explanation: Inversion occurs in conditional sentences.
