26. What is the primary function of chromosomes in inheritance?

  1. Transmit genetic information
  2. Produce proteins
  3. Regulate cell division
  4. Store energy

Answer: Transmit genetic information

Explanation: Chromosomes transmit genetic information from one generation to the next, ensuring the continuity of genetic traits.

27. Which scientist is credited with the discovery of the chromosomal theory of inheritance?

  1. Walter Sutton
  2. Gregor Mendel
  3. Thomas Hunt Morgan
  4. Charles Darwin

Answer: Walter Sutton

Explanation: Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri independently proposed the chromosomal theory of inheritance, which states that chromosomes carry the units of heredity.

28. Which of the following best describes the role of chromosomes in inheritance?

  1. Chromosomes carry genetic information
  2. Chromosomes are responsible for energy production
  3. Chromosomes regulate cell division
  4. Chromosomes synthesize proteins

Answer: Chromosomes carry genetic information

Explanation: Chromosomes carry genetic information in the form of genes, which are passed from parents to offspring.

29. Which of the following scientists is credited with the discovery of the chromosomal theory of inheritance?

  1. Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri
  2. Gregor Mendel
  3. Thomas Hunt Morgan
  4. Charles Darwin

Answer: Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri

Explanation: Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri independently proposed the chromosomal theory of inheritance, which links chromosomes to Mendelian inheritance.

30. Which scientist is credited with proposing the Chromosomal Theory of Inheritance?

  1. Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri
  2. Gregor Mendel
  3. Thomas Hunt Morgan
  4. Charles Darwin

Answer: Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri

Explanation: Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri independently proposed the Chromosomal Theory of Inheritance, which states that chromosomes are the carriers of genetic material.

31. Which of the following best describes the chromosomal theory of inheritance?

  1. Genes are located on chromosomes and their behavior during meiosis accounts for inheritance patterns.
  2. Chromosomes are made up of DNA and proteins, which determine traits.
  3. Inheritance is solely determined by the cytoplasm of the cell.
  4. Chromosomes are responsible for the synthesis of proteins.

Answer: Genes are located on chromosomes and their behavior during meiosis accounts for inheritance patterns.

Explanation: The chromosomal theory of inheritance states that genes are located on chromosomes, and the behavior of chromosomes during meiosis explains inheritance patterns.

Explanation: Genetic engineering is a fundamental principle of biotechnology, involving the manipulation of an organism's genes.

50. Which of the following statements is/are correct about apomixis? (i) It is a type of asexual reproduction. (ii) Seeds are formed without fertilization. (iii) It results in genetic variation. (iv) Examples include Citrus and Mango.

  1. i and ii only
  2. ii and iv only
  3. i, ii and iv
  4. i, ii, iii and iv

Answer: i, ii and iv

Explanation: Apomixis is asexual seed formation without fertilization; it does not create genetic variation because embryos are clones. Citrus and Mango show apomixis.
