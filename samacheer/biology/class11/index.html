Class11 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class11 questions to revise the Samacheer Biology syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 1,063 questions.

1. Which of the following is the basic unit of life?

  1. Cell
  2. Tissue
  3. Organ
  4. Organism

Answer: Cell

Explanation: The cell is the fundamental structural and functional unit of all living organisms.

2. Which of the following is NOT a part of the plant reproductive morphology?

  1. Stamen
  2. Pistil
  3. Leaf
  4. Ovary

Answer: Leaf

Explanation: The leaf is part of the vegetative morphology, not the reproductive morphology.

3. What is the main function of the cell cycle?

  1. Energy production
  2. Cell division
  3. Protein synthesis
  4. Waste elimination

Answer: Cell division

Explanation: The cell cycle is responsible for the growth and division of cells.

4. Which of the following is a characteristic of secondary growth in plants?

  1. Increase in height
  2. Increase in girth
  3. Formation of leaves
  4. Development of flowers

Answer: Increase in girth

Explanation: Secondary growth in plants results in an increase in the girth of the plant, primarily due to the activity of the vascular cambium.

5. What is the term for the process of water movement through a plant and its evaporation from aerial parts?

  1. Transpiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Respiration
  4. Osmosis

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which moisture is carried through plants from roots to small pores on the underside of leaves, where it changes to vapor and is released to the atmosphere.

6. Which of the following is a component of plant secondary growth?

  1. Phloem
  2. Xylem
  3. Cambium
  4. Epidermis

Answer: Cambium

Explanation: The cambium is responsible for secondary growth in plants, producing new xylem and phloem.

7. Which of the following is a characteristic of the Plant Kingdom?

  1. Autotrophic nutrition
  2. Heterotrophic nutrition
  3. Lack of cell walls
  4. Motility

Answer: Autotrophic nutrition

Explanation: Plants are primarily autotrophic, meaning they produce their own food through photosynthesis.

8. Which of the following is NOT a part of the Plant Kingdom?

  1. Bryophytes
  2. Pteridophytes
  3. Fungi
  4. Gymnosperms

Answer: Fungi

Explanation: Fungi belong to a separate kingdom called Fungi, distinct from the Plant Kingdom.

9. Which phase of the cell cycle involves the division of the nucleus?

  1. G1 phase
  2. S phase
  3. M phase
  4. G2 phase

Answer: M phase

Explanation: The M phase, or mitotic phase, involves the division of the nucleus through mitosis.

10. Which unit in the Bio-Botany syllabus covers 'Cell: The Unit of Life'?

  1. Unit I: Diversity of Living World
  2. Unit II: Plant Morphology and Taxonomy of Angiosperm
  3. Unit III: Cell Biology and Biomolecules
  4. Unit IV: Plant Anatomy (Structural Organisation)

Answer: Unit III: Cell Biology and Biomolecules

Explanation: Unit III: Cell Biology and Biomolecules includes the chapter 'Cell: The Unit of Life'.

11. Which chapter is studied in November according to the Bio-Botany syllabus?

  1. Photosynthesis
  2. Respiration
  3. Plant Growth and Development
  4. Mineral Nutrition

Answer: Photosynthesis

Explanation: The chapter 'Photosynthesis' is scheduled for November in the Bio-Botany syllabus.

12. What is the primary focus of Unit V in the Bio-Botany syllabus?

  1. Diversity of Living World
  2. Plant Morphology and Taxonomy of Angiosperm
  3. Cell Biology and Biomolecules
  4. Plant Physiology (Functional Organisation)

Answer: Plant Physiology (Functional Organisation)

Explanation: Unit V focuses on 'Plant Physiology (Functional Organisation)' in the Bio-Botany syllabus.

13. Which of the following is a promising solution for high nutrient pollution and eutrophication in surface waters?

  1. Hydroponics
  2. Floating Treatment Wetlands (FTWs)
  3. Aeroponics
  4. Nitrogen Fixation

Answer: Floating Treatment Wetlands (FTWs)

Explanation: Floating Treatment Wetlands (FTWs) offer a promising solution to nutrient pollution and eutrophication.

14. What is the approximate measurement of a built FTW structure?

  1. 1,000 sq.ft
  2. 2,000 sq.ft
  3. 3,000 sq.ft
  4. 4,000 sq.ft

Answer: 3,000 sq.ft

Explanation: The text states the built structure measures around 3,000 sq.ft.

15. Which of the following is NOT a layer in the construction of FTWs?

  1. Floatable bamboo
  2. Styrofoam
  3. Gunny bags with gravels
  4. Plastic sheets

Answer: Plastic sheets

Explanation: The four layers are bamboo, styrofoam, gunny bags with gravels, and the cleaning agent layer.

16. FTWs work on the principle of which of the following techniques?

  1. Aeroponics
  2. Hydroponics
  3. Nitrogen Metabolism
  4. Mineral Classification

Answer: Hydroponics

Explanation: FTW works on the principle of Hydroponics.

17. Which of these plants is being researched as a cleaning agent for FTWs?

  1. Rose
  2. Sunflower
  3. Vetivers
  4. Mango

Answer: Vetivers

Explanation: Native plants including Vetivers, Citronella, Tulsi and Withania are being researched.

18. What role do microbes play in Floating Treatment Wetlands?

  1. They produce oxygen
  2. They break down and consume organic matter in water
  3. They provide structural support
  4. They absorb sunlight

Answer: They break down and consume organic matter in water

Explanation: Microbes grown on the roots break down and consume organic matter in water to reduce pollution.

19. Which symptom is mentioned as an indicator of unhealthy plant growth?

  1. Rapid growth
  2. Chlorosis
  3. Increased leaf size
  4. Deep green color

Answer: Chlorosis

Explanation: Chlorosis is listed as a symptom of unhealthy growth.

20. Which of the following is a symptom of unhealthy plant growth?

  1. Hypertrophy
  2. Necrosis
  3. Photosynthesis
  4. Transpiration

Answer: Necrosis

Explanation: Necrosis is listed as a symptom of unhealthy growth.

21. Where do the microbes that reduce pollution in FTWs grow?

  1. On the bamboo base
  2. On the styrofoam
  3. On the roots of the plants
  4. In the gravels

Answer: On the roots of the plants

Explanation: Microbes grown on the roots of these plants break down organic matter.

22. Which of the following is a native plant used in FTWs?

  1. Withania
  2. Neem
  3. Peepal
  4. Banyan

Answer: Withania

Explanation: Withania is one of the native plants researched for use as cleaning agents.

23. What is the second layer of an FTW structure?

  1. Bamboo
  2. Styrofoam
  3. Gunny bags
  4. Plants

Answer: Styrofoam

Explanation: The second layer is styrofoam.

24. What is the final layer of the FTW structure designed to support?

  1. Gravels
  2. Styrofoam
  3. Cleaning agents (plants)
  4. Bamboo

Answer: Cleaning agents (plants)

Explanation: The final layer is to support cleaning agents (plants).

25. Consider the following statements: 1. FTWs reduce eutrophication. 2. FTWs use Aeroponics. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Only 1

Explanation: FTWs reduce eutrophication but work on the principle of Hydroponics, not Aeroponics.
