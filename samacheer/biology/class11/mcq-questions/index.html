26. Identify the correct statements: 1. Citronella is used in FTWs. 2. FTWs use gunny bags with gravels. 3. FTWs are used for air pollution.

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2

Explanation: Citronella and gunny bags with gravels are used; FTWs are for water pollution, not air pollution.

27. Which of the following are symptoms of unhealthy plants? 1. Chlorosis 2. Necrosis 3. Texture changes 4. Rapid growth

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 1, 2, and 4
  3. 2, 3, and 4
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: Chlorosis, necrosis, and texture changes are symptoms; rapid growth is healthy.
