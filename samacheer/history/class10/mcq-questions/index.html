26. Consider the following statements: 1. Restorative rebellions are spontaneous and leaderless. 2. Mass Insurrections are spontaneous and leaderless. Which of the above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: 2 only

Explanation: Mass Insurrections are leaderless and spontaneous; Restorative rebellions are about restoring old order.

  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both statements are correct as per the text on subletting of land.

28. Consider the following statements: 1. Haji Shariatullah died in 1839. 2. The Farazi movement was revived after 1862. Which of the above is/are correct?
