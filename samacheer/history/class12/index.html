Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the Samacheer History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 540 questions.

1. Which movement marked the rise of nationalism in India?

  1. Swadeshi Movement
  2. Quit India Movement
  3. Non-Cooperation Movement
  4. Civil Disobedience Movement

Answer: Swadeshi Movement

Explanation: The Swadeshi Movement marked the rise of nationalism in India as it encouraged the use of Indian-made goods and opposed British goods.

2. What was the primary goal of the British economic centralization in India?

  1. Promote Indian industries
  2. Better exploitation and control
  3. Develop infrastructure
  4. Encourage trade with other countries

Answer: Better exploitation and control

Explanation: The British aimed for better exploitation and control of India through economic centralization.

3. Which event significantly impacted the Indian Freedom Movement during World War I?

  1. Jallianwala Bagh Massacre
  2. Partition of Bengal
  3. Champaran Satyagraha
  4. Rowlatt Act

Answer: Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

Explanation: The Jallianwala Bagh Massacre significantly impacted the Indian Freedom Movement during World War I.

4. Who played a crucial role in the mass mobilization during the Indian Freedom Movement?

  1. Bhagat Singh
  2. Subhas Chandra Bose
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. Bal Gangadhar Tilak

Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi played a crucial role in the mass mobilization during the Indian Freedom Movement.

5. What was the main focus of the Reconstruction of Post-colonial India?

  1. Industrialization
  2. Agricultural reforms
  3. Building infrastructure
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Reconstruction of Post-colonial India focused on industrialization, agricultural reforms, and building infrastructure.

6. Who is known as the 'Father of the Nation' in India?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Subhas Chandra Bose
  4. Bhagat Singh

Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

Explanation: Mahatma Gandhi is known as the 'Father of the Nation' for his pivotal role in India's struggle for independence.

7. What was the main objective of the Swadeshi Movement?

  1. Promote British goods
  2. Encourage indigenous products
  3. Support colonial rule
  4. Demand partition of Bengal

Answer: Encourage indigenous products

Explanation: The main objective of the Swadeshi Movement was to encourage the use of indigenous products and boycott British goods.

8. Which phase of the Indian National Movement is known for mass mobilization?

  1. Radicalism
  2. Moderates
  3. Advent of Gandhi
  4. Communalism

Answer: Advent of Gandhi

Explanation: The Advent of Gandhi phase is known for mass mobilization, as Gandhi introduced non-violent civil disobedience to the freedom struggle.

9. Which movement marked the beginning of mass mobilisation in the Indian freedom struggle?

  1. Swadeshi Movement
  2. Quit India Movement
  3. Non-Cooperation Movement
  4. Civil Disobedience Movement

Answer: Swadeshi Movement

Explanation: The Swadeshi Movement, initiated in response to the partition of Bengal in 1905, marked the beginning of mass mobilisation in the Indian freedom struggle.

10. What was the primary objective of the British economic centralisation in India?

  1. Promote Indian industries
  2. Better exploitation and control
  3. Develop infrastructure
  4. Encourage trade

Answer: Better exploitation and control

Explanation: The British economic centralisation aimed at better exploitation and control of India's resources.

11. Which event is considered the advent of Gandhi in the Indian freedom movement?

  1. Champaran Satyagraha
  2. Jallianwala Bagh Massacre
  3. Dandi March
  4. Quit India Movement

Answer: Champaran Satyagraha

Explanation: The Champaran Satyagraha in 1917 is considered the advent of Gandhi in the Indian freedom movement.

12. Which period is associated with radicalism in the anti-imperialist struggles in India?

  1. 1905-1915
  2. 1919-1929
  3. 1930-1940
  4. 1942-1947

Answer: 1919-1929

Explanation: The period from 1919 to 1929 is associated with radicalism in the anti-imperialist struggles in India.

13. Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was born in which coastal town?

  1. Porbandar
  2. Ahmedabad
  3. Champaran
  4. Delhi

Answer: Porbandar

Explanation: Gandhi was born in Porbandar.

14. In which year did Gandhi return to India?

  1. 1915
  2. 1905
  3. 1920
  4. 1947

Answer: 1915

Explanation: Gandhi returned to India in 1915.

15. Which leader did Gandhi acknowledge as his political Guru?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Gopala Krishna Gokhale
  3. Subhas Chandra Bose
  4. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Answer: Gopala Krishna Gokhale

Explanation: Gandhi acknowledged Gopala Krishna Gokhale as his political Guru.

16. What was the technique evolved by Gandhi to fight against injustice?

  1. Satyagraha
  2. Ahimsa
  3. Civil Disobedience
  4. Non-Cooperation

Answer: Satyagraha

Explanation: Gandhi evolved the technique of 'Satyagraha' to resist injustice.

17. The Champaran Movement took place in which year?

  1. 1917
  2. 1918
  3. 1919
  4. 1920

Answer: 1917

Explanation: The Champaran Movement occurred in 1917.

18. Assertion: Gandhi did not take active part in the Home Rule movement. Reason: He was busy establishing his Sabarmati Ashram.

  1. Both Assertion and Reason are true
  2. Assertion is true, Reason is false
  3. Assertion is false, Reason is true
  4. Both Assertion and Reason are false

Answer: Both Assertion and Reason are true

Explanation: Both the assertion and reason are true.

19. Assertion: A Satyagrahi must accept suffering during resistance. Reason: Hatred has no place in Satyagraha.

  1. Both Assertion and Reason are true
  2. Assertion is true, Reason is false
  3. Assertion is false, Reason is true
  4. Both Assertion and Reason are false

Answer: Both Assertion and Reason are true

Explanation: Both the assertion and reason are true.

20. Assertion: Gandhi's philosophy was based on truth and non-violence. Reason: He believed in the use of force to achieve goals.

  1. Both Assertion and Reason are true
  2. Assertion is true, Reason is false
  3. Assertion is false, Reason is true
  4. Both Assertion and Reason are false

Answer: Assertion is true, Reason is false

Explanation: The assertion is true, but the reason is false.

21. Assertion: Gandhi's Satyagraha was meant for the elite class. Reason: It aimed to mobilize the masses against oppression.

  1. Both Assertion and Reason are true
  2. Assertion is true, Reason is false
  3. Assertion is false, Reason is true
  4. Both Assertion and Reason are false

Answer: Assertion is false, Reason is true

Explanation: The assertion is false, but the reason is true.

22. The indigo cultivators in Champaran were exploited by whom?

  1. Indian landlords
  2. European planters
  3. British government
  4. Local merchants

Answer: European planters

Explanation: The indigo cultivators were exploited by European planters.

23. Gandhi established his Sabarmati Ashram in which city?

  1. Ahmedabad
  2. Porbandar
  3. Bihar
  4. Delhi

Answer: Ahmedabad

Explanation: Gandhi established his Sabarmati Ashram in Ahmedabad.

24. Gandhi's principle of 'Ahimsa' refers to which of the following?

  1. Non-violence
  2. Truth
  3. Resistance
  4. Suffering

Answer: Non-violence

Explanation: 'Ahimsa' refers to non-violence.

25. Gandhi's Satyagraha was based on which two principles?

  1. Truth and Non-violence
  2. Resistance and Hatred
  3. Fear and Cowardice
  4. Power and Control

Answer: Truth and Non-violence

Explanation: Satyagraha is based on truth and non-violence.
