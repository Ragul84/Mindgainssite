Class9 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class9 questions to revise the Samacheer History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 561 questions.

1. Which period is known as the Old Stone Age?

  1. Neolithic
  2. Mesolithic
  3. Palaeolithic
  4. Chalcolithic

Answer: Palaeolithic

Explanation: The Palaeolithic period is the earliest Stone Age, also called the Old Stone Age.

2. Which river valley civilization is associated with Mesopotamia?

  1. Nile Valley
  2. Indus Valley
  3. Tigris-Euphrates Valley
  4. Yellow River Valley

Answer: Tigris-Euphrates Valley

Explanation: Mesopotamia developed between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers.

3. Buddha was born in Lumbini.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: According to Buddhist tradition, Siddhartha Gautama was born in Lumbini (in present-day Nepal).

4. Which of the following statements about feudalism is correct?

  1. Lords gave land to vassals in exchange for military service.
  2. Serfs were free to leave the manor.
  3. The king had absolute power over all land.
  4. Feudalism ended with the Renaissance.

Answer: Lords gave land to vassals in exchange for military service.

Explanation: Feudalism was based on a hierarchy where lords granted land (fiefs) to vassals in return for loyalty and military service.

5. The term 'Renaissance' means?

  1. revival
  2. rebirth
  3. reformation
  4. revolution

Answer: rebirth

Explanation: Renaissance is a French word meaning 'rebirth', referring to the cultural revival after the Middle Ages.

6. Who invented the spinning jenny?

  1. James Hargreaves
  2. Richard Arkwright
  3. Samuel Crompton
  4. Edmund Cartwright

Answer: James Hargreaves

Explanation: James Hargreaves invented the spinning jenny around 1764, a key invention of the Industrial Revolution.

7. The Berlin Conference of 1884-85 partitioned Africa.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Berlin Conference (1884–85) regulated European colonisation and partition of Africa.

8. Which form of democracy allows citizens to vote directly on laws?

  1. Representative democracy
  2. Direct democracy
  3. Parliamentary democracy
  4. Presidential democracy

Answer: Direct democracy

Explanation: In direct democracy, citizens participate directly in decision-making, such as voting on laws.

9. The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted by the United Nations in which year?

  1. 1945
  2. 1948
  3. 1950
  4. 1952

Answer: 1948

Explanation: The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted by the UN General Assembly on 10 December 1948.

10. Identify the statement that is true about a federal government.

  1. Power is concentrated in central government.
  2. Power is divided between central and state governments.
  3. Local governments have no power.
  4. The central government can revoke state powers at will.

Answer: Power is divided between central and state governments.

Explanation: In a federal system, power is constitutionally divided between a central authority and constituent states.

11. The Indus Valley Civilisation is also known as the Harappan Civilisation.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilisation is commonly called Harappan after the first site discovered.

12. Who was the founder of Buddhism?

  1. Mahavira
  2. Buddha
  3. Ashoka
  4. Kanishka

Answer: Buddha

Explanation: Siddhartha Gautama, known as Buddha, founded Buddhism in the 6th century BCE.

13. Which Delhi Sultanate ruler introduced token currency?

  1. Alauddin Khalji
  2. Muhammad bin Tughluq
  3. Firoz Shah Tughluq
  4. Iltutmish

Answer: Muhammad bin Tughluq

Explanation: Muhammad bin Tughluq introduced token currency using brass and copper coins.

14. The Renaissance began in Italy.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Renaissance, a cultural rebirth, started in Italy in the 14th century.

15. Which invention is credited with revolutionizing the textile industry during the Industrial Revolution?

  1. Spinning Jenny
  2. Steam engine
  3. Power loom
  4. Cotton gin

Answer: Spinning Jenny

Explanation: James Hargreaves' Spinning Jenny (1764) greatly increased thread production.

16. Which of the following statements about European colonialism is correct?

  1. It only aimed at spreading Christianity.
  2. It was driven by the need for raw materials and new markets.
  3. It had no impact on colonised societies.
  4. It began only in the 20th century.

Answer: It was driven by the need for raw materials and new markets.

Explanation: European colonialism was primarily motivated by economic factors like raw materials and markets.

17. The Sangam literature is associated with which period of Tamil history?

  1. Prehistoric
  2. Ancient
  3. Medieval
  4. Modern

Answer: Ancient

Explanation: Sangam literature flourished in ancient Tamilakam, roughly 300 BCE to 300 CE.

18. What is the period of the Prehistoric Age called when humans used stone tools?

  1. Neolithic Age
  2. Palaeolithic Age
  3. Mesolithic Age
  4. Chalcolithic Age

Answer: Palaeolithic Age

Explanation: The Palaeolithic Age or Old Stone Age is the earliest period when humans used stone tools.

19. The Indus Valley Civilization is also known as the Harappan Civilization.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is called Harappan Civilization because Harappa was the first site discovered.

20. Which of the following is NOT a feature of democracy?

  1. Free and fair elections
  2. Rule of law
  3. One-party rule
  4. Protection of rights

Answer: One-party rule

Explanation: One-party rule is a feature of authoritarian systems, not democracy.

21. The Sangam Age is associated with the history of which region?

  1. North India
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Kerala
  4. Andhra Pradesh

Answer: Tamil Nadu

Explanation: The Sangam Age is a period in the early history of Tamil Nadu, known for Sangam literature.

22. Which of the following statements about the Middle Ages is true?

  1. A: Feudalism was a key feature.
  2. B: The period saw the rise of the Roman Empire.
  3. C: The Renaissance began in this period.
  4. D: Both A and C

Answer: D: Both A and C

Explanation: Feudalism was a key feature of the Middle Ages, and the Renaissance began in the late Middle Ages.

23. What is the main source of water on Earth?

  1. Rivers
  2. Oceans
  3. Glaciers
  4. Groundwater

Answer: Oceans

Explanation: Oceans cover about 71% of Earth's surface and are the main source of water.

24. The concept of 'Human Rights' is covered in which Civics unit?

  1. Unit 1
  2. Unit 2
  3. Unit 3
  4. Unit 4

Answer: Unit 3

Explanation: Human Rights is Unit 3 in the Civics section of Class 9 Samacheer Kalvi.

25. Which of the following is a type of exogenetic process?

  1. Volcanism
  2. Weathering
  3. Earthquake
  4. Plate movement

Answer: Weathering

Explanation: Weathering is an exogenetic process that occurs on Earth's surface.
