Class9 MCQs with answers

26. Which of the following is a feature of a federal government?

  1. Centralized power
  2. Division of power
  3. Unitary system
  4. Single government

Answer: Division of power

Explanation: Federal government divides power between central and state governments.

27. True or False: The Earth's atmosphere is composed mainly of oxygen.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The atmosphere is mainly nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%).

28. The earliest urban civilization in the world is considered to be the _____.

  1. Indus Valley Civilization
  2. Mesopotamian Civilization
  3. Egyptian Civilization
  4. Chinese Civilization

Answer: Mesopotamian Civilization

Explanation: The Mesopotamian civilization (c. 3500 BCE) is widely regarded as the earliest urban civilization, with city-states like Ur and Uruk.

29. The period before the invention of writing is called ______.

  1. Neolithic
  2. Prehistoric
  3. Chalcolithic
  4. Historic

Answer: Prehistoric

Explanation: Prehistory refers to the time before recorded history and the invention of writing systems.

30. Which of the following statements about the Indus Valley Civilization is/are correct? (i) It had a highly developed drainage system. (ii) It was a rural civilization. (iii) The seals were used for trade.

  1. i only
  2. i and ii
  3. i and iii
  4. ii and iii

Answer: i and iii

Explanation: The Indus cities had advanced drainage (i) and seals were likely used in trade (iii), but it was an urban, not rural, civilization.

31. The slogan 'Liberty, Equality, Fraternity' is associated with which revolution?

  1. American Revolution
  2. French Revolution
  3. Russian Revolution
  4. Industrial Revolution

Answer: French Revolution

Explanation: The French Revolution (1789) popularised the motto 'Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité' as a core demand for democratic rights.

32. The term 'Feudalism' is associated with which period?

  1. Ancient Greece
  2. Middle Ages
  3. Renaissance
  4. Modern Age

Answer: Middle Ages

Explanation: Feudalism was the dominant social and political system in medieval Europe (Middle Ages), based on land ownership and obligations.

33. Which of the following are features of democracy? (i) Rule of law (ii) One-party system (iii) Periodic elections (iv) Censorship of media

  1. i and ii
  2. i and iii
  3. ii and iv
  4. iii and iv

Answer: i and iii

Explanation: Democracy is characterized by rule of law (i) and periodic elections (iii); one-party rule and censorship are non-democratic.

34. Which ancient civilization is known as the 'Cradle of Civilization'?

  1. Indus Valley
  2. Egyptian
  3. Mesopotamian
  4. Chinese

Answer: Mesopotamian

Explanation: Mesopotamia, located between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, is often called the 'Cradle of Civilization'.

35. The Indus Valley Civilization was a rural civilization.

  1. False
  2. True

Answer: False

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization was urban, with well-planned cities like Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa.

36. Who founded Buddhism?

  1. Mahavira
  2. Gautama Buddha
  3. Ashoka
  4. Chandragupta Maurya

Answer: Gautama Buddha

Explanation: Siddhartha Gautama, known as the Buddha, founded Buddhism in the 6th century BCE.

37. The feudal system was based on the exchange of land for __________.

  1. money
  2. military service
  3. goods
  4. taxes

Answer: military service

Explanation: Under feudalism, lords granted land (fiefs) to vassals in return for military service and loyalty.

38. The invention of the spinning jenny is attributed to __________.

  1. James Watt
  2. Richard Arkwright
  3. John Kay
  4. James Hargreaves

Answer: James Hargreaves

Explanation: James Hargreaves invented the spinning jenny in 1764, a key invention of the Industrial Revolution.

39. Which of the following statements about the Industrial Revolution are correct? (i) It started in England. (ii) It led to urbanization. (iii) It decreased pollution. (iv) It increased child labour.

  1. (i) and (ii) only
  2. (i), (ii) and (iv)
  3. All of the above
  4. None of the above

Answer: (i), (ii) and (iv)

Explanation: The Industrial Revolution started in England, led to urbanization and increased child labour, but increased pollution (so iii is false).

40. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of the Stone Age?

  1. Palaeolithic - Mesolithic - Neolithic
  2. Mesolithic - Palaeolithic - Neolithic
  3. Neolithic - Palaeolithic - Mesolithic
  4. Palaeolithic - Neolithic - Mesolithic

Answer: Palaeolithic - Mesolithic - Neolithic

Explanation: The Stone Age is divided into Palaeolithic (Old Stone), Mesolithic (Middle Stone), and Neolithic (New Stone) in that order.

41. Assertion (A): The Indus Valley Civilization is also known as Harappan civilization. Reason (R): The first site discovered was Harappa.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: Harappa was the first excavated site of the Indus Valley Civilization, hence the name Harappan civilization.

42. The French Revolution began in the year 1789.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The French Revolution started with the storming of the Bastille on July 14, 1789.

43. Which of the following statements about the Renaissance is correct? (i) It began in Italy. (ii) It was a revival of art and learning. (iii) It led to the Reformation. (iv) It started in the 15th century.

  1. (i) and (ii) only
  2. (i), (ii) and (iii) only
  3. (i), (ii), (iii) and (iv)
  4. (ii) and (iv) only

Answer: (i), (ii) and (iii) only

Explanation: The Renaissance began in Italy in the 14th century (not 15th), revived art and learning, and led to the Reformation.

44. The founder of Buddhism was ________.

  1. Mahavira
  2. Gautama Buddha
  3. Ajivika
  4. Charvaka

Answer: Gautama Buddha

Explanation: Siddhartha Gautama, known as the Buddha, founded Buddhism in the 6th century BCE.

45. Which Sangam age port was famous for trade with Rome?

  1. Puhar
  2. Madurai
  3. Kanchi
  4. Vanji

Answer: Puhar

Explanation: Puhar (Kaveripoompattinam) was a major port that traded with the Roman Empire during the Sangam period.

46. Who is known as the 'Father of Humanism' during the Renaissance?

  1. Petrarch
  2. Leonardo da Vinci
  3. Michelangelo
  4. Erasmus

Answer: Petrarch

Explanation: Petrarch, an Italian scholar and poet, is considered the father of Humanism.

47. State whether the following statement is True or False: The Industrial Revolution first began in France.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Industrial Revolution first began in England, not France.

48. Consider the following statements about the French Revolution: (i) The Estates General was called in 1789. (ii) The Third Estate declared itself the National Assembly. (iii) The Bastille was stormed on July 14, 1789. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. (i) only
  2. (ii) only
  3. (i) and (ii) only
  4. (i), (ii) and (iii)

Answer: (i), (ii) and (iii)

Explanation: All three statements are correct: the Estates General met in 1789, the Third Estate formed the National Assembly, and the Bastille fell on July 14, 1789.

49. Which ancient civilisation is known for the construction of ziggurats?

  1. Egyptian
  2. Mesopotamian
  3. Indus Valley
  4. Chinese

Answer: Mesopotamian

Explanation: Ziggurats were massive temple towers built in ancient Mesopotamia (Sumer, Babylon, etc.).

50. Jainism emphasizes non-violence and asceticism.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Ahimsa (non-violence) and ascetic life are core teachings of Jainism.
