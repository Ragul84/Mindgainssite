Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the Samacheer History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 429 questions.

26. In which year did Vasco da Gama land at Calicut?

  1. 1492
  2. 1498
  3. 1500
  4. 1510

Answer: 1498

Explanation: Vasco da Gama landed at Calicut on 20 May 1498.

27. The English East India Company was formed in 1600.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The English East India Company was granted a royal charter by Queen Elizabeth I in 1600.

28. The system of 'Subsidiary Alliance' was introduced by

  1. Robert Clive
  2. Lord Dalhousie
  3. Lord Wellesley
  4. Warren Hastings

Answer: Lord Wellesley

Explanation: Lord Wellesley introduced the Subsidiary Alliance system in 1798 to bring Indian states under British control.

29. Who was the last ruler of the Mughal Empire?

  1. Shah Alam II
  2. Bahadur Shah Zafar
  3. Akbar II
  4. Aurangzeb

Answer: Bahadur Shah Zafar

Explanation: Bahadur Shah Zafar was the last Mughal emperor, exiled after the Revolt of 1857.

30. The Revolt of 1857 began at Meerut.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The rebellion started on 10 May 1857 when sepoys at Meerut revolted and marched to Delhi.

31. Which of the following cities was NOT a Presidency during British rule?

  1. Madras
  2. Bombay
  3. Calcutta
  4. Delhi

Answer: Delhi

Explanation: The three Presidency cities were Madras, Bombay, and Calcutta. Delhi was not a Presidency.

32. Who authored the book 'Poverty and Un-British Rule in India'?

  1. R.C. Dutt
  2. Dadabhai Naoroji
  3. M.G. Ranade
  4. Gopal Krishna Gokhale

Answer: Dadabhai Naoroji

Explanation: Dadabhai Naoroji wrote 'Poverty and Un-British Rule in India' (1901) highlighting economic drain under British rule.

33. Wood's Dispatch of 1854 is called the 'Magna Carta of Indian Education'.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Wood's Dispatch laid the foundation for modern education in India, recommending a system from primary to university level.

34. The social reformer who led the campaign against the Sati system was

  1. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
  2. Dayanand Saraswati
  3. Raja Rammohan Roy
  4. Swami Vivekananda

Answer: Raja Rammohan Roy

Explanation: Raja Rammohan Roy worked tirelessly to abolish Sati, and the practice was legally banned in 1829.

35. Who was the first European power to establish a trading centre in India?

  1. French
  2. Dutch
  3. Portuguese
  4. English

Answer: Portuguese

Explanation: The Portuguese were the first European power to establish a trading centre in India, with Vasco da Gama arriving in Calicut in 1498.

36. Statement: The Vellore Mutiny of 1806 was one of the earliest uprisings against the British in India. Is this statement true or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: True. The Vellore Mutiny of 1806 was indeed one of the earliest uprisings against the British East India Company.

37. Who among the following was associated with the development of the jute industry in India?

  1. Jamsetji Tata
  2. George Acland
  3. Dwarkanath Tagore
  4. Henry Ford

Answer: George Acland

Explanation: George Acland was associated with the development of the jute industry in India.

38. The Permanent Settlement of Bengal was introduced by Lord Cornwallis in 1793. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: True. The Permanent Settlement was introduced by Lord Cornwallis in 1793 in Bengal.

39. Which of the following was a major reason for the Revolt of 1857?

  1. Introduction of railways
  2. Doctrine of Lapse
  3. English education
  4. Postal system

Answer: Doctrine of Lapse

Explanation: The Doctrine of Lapse, introduced by Lord Dalhousie, was a major political cause of the Revolt of 1857.

40. Statement: The Charter Act of 1813 allowed Christian missionaries to enter British India. Is this statement true or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: True. The Charter Act of 1813 permitted Christian missionaries to enter and work in British India.

41. Who was the first European to reach India by sea route?

  1. Vasco da Gama
  2. Christopher Columbus
  3. Ferdinand Magellan
  4. Captain James Cook

Answer: Vasco da Gama

Explanation: Vasco da Gama, a Portuguese explorer, reached Calicut (Kozhikode) in 1498, establishing the first direct sea route from Europe to India.

42. The system of 'Ryotwari' was introduced by the British in which region?

  1. Bengal
  2. Bombay and Madras
  3. Punjab
  4. Delhi

Answer: Bombay and Madras

Explanation: The Ryotwari system was introduced in the Bombay and Madras Presidencies, where the British directly settled revenue with the cultivator (ryot).

43. Statement: The Revolt of 1857 was the first war of Indian Independence. Which of the following was a major centre of this revolt?

  1. Mysore
  2. Delhi
  3. Hyderabad
  4. Pune

Answer: Delhi

Explanation: Delhi was a major centre of the 1857 Revolt, where the Mughal emperor Bahadur Shah Zafar was declared the leader.

44. True or False: The Permanent Settlement was introduced by Lord Cornwallis in Bengal in 1793.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Permanent Settlement was indeed introduced by Lord Cornwallis in 1793 in Bengal, fixing land revenue permanently.

45. Which of the following was a social reformer who worked for the upliftment of women in India?

  1. Raja Rammohan Roy
  2. Bal Gangadhar Tilak
  3. Lala Lajpat Rai
  4. Bhagat Singh

Answer: Raja Rammohan Roy

Explanation: Raja Rammohan Roy worked for women's rights, including the abolition of Sati and promotion of widow remarriage.

46. The 'Subsidiary Alliance' system was introduced by which British Governor-General?

  1. Warren Hastings
  2. Lord Wellesley
  3. Lord Dalhousie
  4. Lord Cornwallis

Answer: Lord Wellesley

Explanation: Lord Wellesley introduced the Subsidiary Alliance system to bring Indian states under British control without direct annexation.

47. Statement-based: Assertion (A): The British introduced railways in India. Reason (R): The main aim was to improve the economic condition of Indians.

  1. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true, but R is false
  4. A is false, but R is true

Answer: A is true, but R is false

Explanation: The British introduced railways mainly for administrative and military purposes and to exploit resources, not to improve Indian economy.

48. The 'Doctrine of Lapse' policy was implemented by which Governor-General?

  1. Lord Dalhousie
  2. Lord Canning
  3. Lord Ripon
  4. Lord Curzon

Answer: Lord Dalhousie

Explanation: Lord Dalhousie implemented the Doctrine of Lapse, which annexed states like Jhansi and Satara when rulers died without heirs.

49. True or False: The Hunter Commission of 1882 recommended the promotion of primary education in India.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Hunter Commission (1882) focused on improving primary education and recommended expanding vernacular schools.

50. Which European power was the first to establish a trading post in India?

  1. Portuguese
  2. Dutch
  3. English
  4. French

Answer: Portuguese

Explanation: The Portuguese, led by Vasco da Gama, arrived in 1498 and established the first European trading post in India.
