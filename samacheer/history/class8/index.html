Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the Samacheer History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 429 questions.

1. Who was the first European to arrive in India for trade?

  1. Portuguese
  2. Dutch
  3. English
  4. French

Answer: Portuguese

Explanation: The Portuguese were the first Europeans to arrive in India for trade, led by Vasco da Gama in 1498.

2. Statement: The British introduced the Permanent Settlement in Bengal. Is this statement true or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The British introduced the Permanent Settlement in Bengal in 1793 under Lord Cornwallis.

3. Who introduced the 'Doctrine of Lapse' policy?

  1. Lord Dalhousie
  2. Lord Wellesley
  3. Lord Cornwallis
  4. Warren Hastings

Answer: Lord Dalhousie

Explanation: Lord Dalhousie introduced the Doctrine of Lapse, which annexed states without a natural heir.

4. Which of the following is NOT a reason for the decline of Indian industries under British rule?

  1. Heavy import duties on Indian goods
  2. Free trade policy of Britain
  3. Introduction of railways
  4. Encouragement of Indian artisans

Answer: Encouragement of Indian artisans

Explanation: The British discouraged Indian artisans, not encouraged them, leading to the decline of industries.

5. Statement: The Wood's Despatch of 1854 is called the 'Magna Carta of English Education in India'. Is this true or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Wood's Despatch of 1854 laid the foundation for modern education in India and is known as the Magna Carta of English Education.

6. Which of the following was a major revolt against British rule in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Vellore Mutiny
  2. Santhal Rebellion
  3. Munda Rebellion
  4. Sepoy Mutiny

Answer: Vellore Mutiny

Explanation: The Vellore Mutiny of 1806 was a major revolt against British rule in Tamil Nadu.

7. Statement: The British introduced the Ryotwari system in Madras Presidency. Is this true or false?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Ryotwari system was introduced by Thomas Munro in the Madras Presidency in 1820.

8. Who was the last Mughal emperor of India?

  1. Bahadur Shah Zafar
  2. Shah Alam II
  3. Akbar II
  4. Aurangzeb

Answer: Bahadur Shah Zafar

Explanation: Bahadur Shah Zafar was the last Mughal emperor, who was exiled after the 1857 revolt.

9. The Ryotwari system was introduced by the British in which region?

  1. Bengal
  2. Madras Presidency
  3. Bombay Presidency
  4. Punjab

Answer: Madras Presidency

Explanation: The Ryotwari system was introduced in the Madras Presidency by Sir Thomas Munro.

10. The Vellore Mutiny of 1806 was a revolt by Indian soldiers against the British.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Vellore Mutiny of 1806 was indeed a revolt by Indian sepoys against the British East India Company.

11. Which of the following statements about the Permanent Settlement is correct?

  1. It was introduced in Madras
  2. It fixed the land revenue permanently
  3. It benefited the peasants
  4. It was introduced by Lord Cornwallis in Bengal

Answer: It was introduced by Lord Cornwallis in Bengal

Explanation: The Permanent Settlement was introduced by Lord Cornwallis in Bengal in 1793, fixing land revenue permanently.

12. Which of the following was a major urban change during the British period?

  1. Growth of hill stations
  2. Decline of cities
  3. Construction of temples
  4. Development of village industries

Answer: Growth of hill stations

Explanation: The British developed hill stations like Ooty and Shimla as summer retreats and administrative centers.

13. The practice of Sati was abolished by Lord William Bentinck in the year 1829.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Lord William Bentinck abolished the practice of Sati in 1829 with the help of social reformers like Raja Ram Mohan Roy.

14. Who was the first woman to graduate from a university in India?

  1. Kadambini Ganguly
  2. Anandibai Joshi
  3. Sarojini Naidu
  4. Pandita Ramabai

Answer: Kadambini Ganguly

Explanation: Kadambini Ganguly was the first woman to graduate from a university in India, graduating from Calcutta University in 1883.

15. Which of the following was a reason for the decline of handicrafts in India during British rule?

  1. Introduction of machine-made goods
  2. Increase in demand
  3. Government support
  4. Improved technology

Answer: Introduction of machine-made goods

Explanation: British machine-made goods flooded Indian markets, leading to the decline of traditional handicrafts.

16. Who was the first European to reach India by sea?

  1. Vasco da Gama
  2. Christopher Columbus
  3. Ferdinand Magellan
  4. John Cabot

Answer: Vasco da Gama

Explanation: Vasco da Gama, a Portuguese explorer, reached Calicut, India, in 1498, opening the sea route from Europe to India.

17. The Ryotwari system of land revenue was introduced by whom?

  1. Lord Cornwallis
  2. Thomas Munro
  3. Lord Wellesley
  4. Warren Hastings

Answer: Thomas Munro

Explanation: Thomas Munro, the Governor of Madras, introduced the Ryotwari system under which the government directly dealt with the cultivators (ryots).

18. The Revolt of 1857 is also known as the First War of Indian Independence. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Many Indian historians and textbooks refer to the 1857 Revolt as the First War of Indian Independence, although British historians often call it the Sepoy Mutiny.

19. The first cotton mill in India was established in which city?

  1. Calcutta
  2. Bombay
  3. Madras
  4. Delhi

Answer: Bombay

Explanation: The first cotton mill in India was set up in Bombay (Mumbai) in 1854 by Cowasji Nanabhai Davar.

20. Who was the first Governor General of Bengal under the Regulating Act of 1773?

  1. Robert Clive
  2. Warren Hastings
  3. Lord Cornwallis
  4. Lord Dalhousie

Answer: Warren Hastings

Explanation: Warren Hastings became the first Governor General of Bengal in 1774 under the Regulating Act of 1773.

21. The Permanent Settlement of land revenue was introduced in Bengal by Lord Cornwallis in 1793. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Lord Cornwallis introduced the Permanent Settlement (Zamindari system) in Bengal, Bihar, and Orissa in 1793, fixing land revenue permanently.

22. Which of the following statements about the 'Doctrine of Lapse' is correct?

  1. It was introduced by Lord Dalhousie.
  2. It allowed the British to annex states without a natural heir.
  3. It was applied to states like Satara and Jhansi.
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: The Doctrine of Lapse, introduced by Lord Dalhousie, permitted annexation of princely states that lacked a male heir, leading to the annexation of Satara, Jhansi, and other states.

23. The first railway line in India was opened between Bombay and Thane in 1853. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The first passenger railway line in India was inaugurated on 16 April 1853, connecting Bombay (Bori Bunder) and Thane over a distance of 34 km.

24. Who was the leader of the Vellore Mutiny of 1806?

  1. Tipu Sultan
  2. Velunachiyar
  3. Marthanda Varma
  4. The mutinous sepoys were led by the sons of Tipu Sultan

Answer: The mutinous sepoys were led by the sons of Tipu Sultan

Explanation: The Vellore Mutiny (1806) was led by Indian sepoys, inspired by the family of Tipu Sultan, who were imprisoned in the fort.

25. The system of 'Diarchy' was introduced in the provinces by which act?

  1. Government of India Act 1858
  2. Indian Councils Act 1892
  3. Government of India Act 1919
  4. Government of India Act 1935

Answer: Government of India Act 1919

Explanation: The Government of India Act 1919 introduced diarchy (dual government) in the provinces, dividing subjects into reserved and transferred lists.
