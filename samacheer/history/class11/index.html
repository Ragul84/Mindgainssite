Class11 MCQs with answers

1. Which period marks the beginning of early Indian cultures and civilisations?

  1. Old Stone Age
  2. Iron Age
  3. Vedic Period
  4. Gupta Period

Answer: Old Stone Age

Explanation: The Old Stone Age marks the beginning of early Indian cultures and civilisations, as mentioned in the textbook introduction.

2. Which unit discusses the emergence of state and empire in India?

  1. Unit 3
  2. Unit 4
  3. Unit 5
  4. Unit 6

Answer: Unit 4

Explanation: Unit 4 focuses on the emergence of state and empire in India, as per the table of contents.

3. Which kingdom is NOT discussed in the unit on cultural development in South India?

  1. Cholas
  2. Pandyas
  3. Guptas
  4. Vijayanagar

Answer: Guptas

Explanation: The Guptas are not discussed in the unit on cultural development in South India, as they are covered in Unit 7.

4. Which movement is associated with cultural syncretism in India?

  1. Bhakti Movement
  2. Buddhist Movement
  3. Jain Movement
  4. Sufi Movement

Answer: Bhakti Movement

Explanation: The Bhakti Movement is associated with cultural syncretism in India, as highlighted in Unit 13.

5. Which unit covers the effects of British rule in India?

  1. Unit 16
  2. Unit 17
  3. Unit 18
  4. Unit 19

Answer: Unit 17

Explanation: Unit 17 specifically discusses the effects of British rule in India, as per the table of contents.

6. Which kingdom is NOT discussed in the unit 'Later Cholas and Pandyas'?

  1. Cholas
  2. Pandyas
  3. Vijayanagar
  4. Pallavas

Answer: Pallavas

Explanation: The unit 'Later Cholas and Pandyas' focuses on the Cholas and Pandyas, while the Pallavas are not discussed in this unit.

7. Which unit covers the advent of Arabs and Turks in India?

  1. Unit 9
  2. Unit 10
  3. Unit 11
  4. Unit 12

Answer: Unit 10

Explanation: Unit 10 in the textbook discusses the advent of Arabs and Turks in India.

8. Which period is known as the Old Stone Age in Indian history?

  1. Paleolithic
  2. Neolithic
  3. Chalcolithic
  4. Iron Age

Answer: Paleolithic

Explanation: The Old Stone Age is referred to as the Paleolithic period, characterized by the use of stone tools.

9. Which age followed the Chalcolithic period in India?

  1. Iron Age
  2. Bronze Age
  3. Stone Age
  4. Vedic Age

Answer: Iron Age

Explanation: The Iron Age succeeded the Chalcolithic period, marked by the use of iron tools and weapons.

10. Which kingdom emerged prominently after the Mauryan Empire?

  1. Gupta
  2. Chola
  3. Pandya
  4. Bahmani

Answer: Gupta

Explanation: The Gupta Empire rose to prominence after the decline of the Mauryan Empire, known as the Golden Age of India.

11. Which movement emphasized devotion and cultural syncretism in India?

  1. Bhakti Movement
  2. Sufi Movement
  3. Buddhist Movement
  4. Jain Movement

Answer: Bhakti Movement

Explanation: The Bhakti Movement focused on devotion to God and promoted cultural syncretism across India.

12. Which period in Indian history is known for the emergence of territorial kingdoms and new religious sects?

  1. Chalcolithic Age
  2. Vedic Age
  3. Mauryan Period
  4. Post-Mauryan Period

Answer: Post-Mauryan Period

Explanation: The Post-Mauryan Period is characterized by the rise of territorial kingdoms and the emergence of new religious sects such as Buddhism and Jainism.
