Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the Samacheer History syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 375 questions.

26. Which of the following is NOT a source of medieval Indian history?

  1. Coins
  2. Temples
  3. Computer records
  4. Travelogues

Answer: Computer records

Explanation: Computer records are modern sources and not available for medieval history. Coins, temples, and travelogues are valuable primary or secondary sources.

27. Who founded the Vijayanagar empire?

  1. Krishnadevaraya
  2. Harihara and Bukka
  3. Devaraya II
  4. Rama Raya

Answer: Harihara and Bukka

Explanation: Harihara and Bukka, two brothers, founded the Vijayanagar empire in 1336.

28. The Bahmani kingdom was founded by Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah founded the Bahmani kingdom in 1347.

29. Which of the following statements about Krishnadevaraya is/are correct? (a) He belonged to the Tuluva dynasty. (b) He wrote Amuktamalyada. (c) He built the Hazara Rama temple. (d) He was defeated at the Battle of Talikota.

  1. a, b, c
  2. a, b, d
  3. b, c, d
  4. a, c, d

Answer: a, b, c

Explanation: Statements a, b, c are correct. Krishnadevaraya was not defeated at Talikota; it was Rama Raya.

30. The Bahmani kingdom was divided into how many provinces?

  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 5
  4. 6

Answer: 4

Explanation: The Bahmani kingdom was divided into four provinces (tarafs).

31. Krishnadevaraya belonged to the Sangama dynasty.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: False. Krishnadevaraya belonged to the Tuluva dynasty, the third dynasty of Vijayanagar.

32. Who was the first ruler of the Bahmani kingdom?

  1. Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah
  2. Mahmud Gawan
  3. Firoz Shah
  4. Ahmad Shah

Answer: Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah

Explanation: Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah founded the Bahmani kingdom in 1347.

33. Which was the chief port of the Vijayanagar empire?

  1. Calicut
  2. Pulicat
  3. Goa
  4. Surat

Answer: Pulicat

Explanation: Pulicat was an important port on the Coromandel coast under the Vijayanagar empire.

34. Which of the following statements about the Vijayanagar administration are correct? (a) The king was the head of the administration. (b) The empire was divided into mandalams. (c) The provincial governors were called Nayaks. (d) Agriculture was the main occupation.

  1. a, b, d
  2. a, c, d
  3. b, c, d
  4. a, b, c, d

Answer: a, b, c, d

Explanation: All statements a, b, c, d are correct regarding the Vijayanagar administration.

35. What is the study of coins called?

  1. Epigraphy
  2. Numismatics
  3. Archaeology
  4. Chronology

Answer: Numismatics

Explanation: Numismatics is the study of coins and currency.

36. The Pallavas built the famous rock-cut temples at Mamallapuram.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Mamallapuram rock-cut temples were built by the Pallava rulers, especially Narasimhavarman I.

37. Who wrote the famous Tamil epic 'Silappadikaram'?

  1. Ilango Adigal
  2. Thiruvalluvar
  3. Kamban
  4. Avvaiyar

Answer: Ilango Adigal

Explanation: Silappadikaram was composed by the Jain monk Ilango Adigal.

38. The Cholas maintained a strong navy.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Chola navy was powerful and helped control trade routes and expand influence to Southeast Asia.

39. What was the main source of revenue for the Chola empire?

  1. Land tax
  2. Trade tax
  3. Forest produce
  4. Tribute

Answer: Land tax

Explanation: Land tax was the primary source of income, collected as a share of the agricultural produce.

40. Which of the following statements about the Bhakti movement are correct? 1) It emphasized devotion to a personal god. 2) It rejected caste distinctions. 3) It was limited to Tamil Nadu. 4) It used local languages for preaching.

  1. 1,2,4 only
  2. 1,2,3
  3. 2,3,4
  4. All

Answer: 1,2,4 only

Explanation: The Bhakti movement spread across India, not limited to Tamil Nadu, so statement 3 is incorrect.

41. The famous 'Kailasanathar Temple' at Kanchipuram was built by:

  1. Rajasimha Pallava
  2. Mahendravarman I
  3. Narasimhavarman I
  4. Nandivarman II

Answer: Rajasimha Pallava

Explanation: Kailasanathar Temple was built by the Pallava king Rajasimha (also known as Narasimhavarman II).

42. The Delhi Sultanate was established by Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Delhi Sultanate was established by Qutb-ud-din Aibak, not Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

43. Which of the following is a primary source of history?

  1. Textbooks
  2. Coins and inscriptions
  3. Novels
  4. Newspapers

Answer: Coins and inscriptions

Explanation: Coins and inscriptions are primary sources as they are contemporary to the period, whereas textbooks and novels are secondary sources.

44. The Delhi Sultanate was established in which year?

  1. 1206 CE
  2. 1192 CE
  3. 1290 CE
  4. 1320 CE

Answer: 1206 CE

Explanation: The Delhi Sultanate began in 1206 CE with Qutb-ud-din Aibak.

45. Statement A: The Bhakti movement stressed on devotion to a personal god. Statement B: The Sufi movement was only popular in South India. Which of the following is correct?

  1. A is true, B is false
  2. A is false, B is true
  3. Both A and B are true
  4. Both A and B are false

Answer: A is true, B is false

Explanation: Bhakti movement emphasized personal devotion; Sufi movement was popular in both North and South India, so B is false.

46. The Mughal emperor who introduced the Din-i-Ilahi was Akbar.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Akbar founded the Din-i-Ilahi in 1582, aiming at religious syncretism.

47. Which of the following is not a lesson from the Samacheer Class 7 History syllabus?

  1. Vijayanagara and Bahmani Kingdoms
  2. The Delhi Sultanate
  3. The Mughal Empire
  4. The French Revolution

Answer: The French Revolution

Explanation: The French Revolution is part of higher classes, not Class 7 Samacheer History.

48. Statement: The Bhakti saint who preached in Tamil and is known for his devotional songs to Lord Vishnu is: 1. Meera Bai 2. Tulsidas 3. Andal 4. Surdas

  1. Meera Bai
  2. Tulsidas
  3. Andal
  4. Surdas

Answer: Andal

Explanation: Andal was a Tamil Bhakti saint and poetess, known for her devotion to Vishnu.

49. Which of the following statements about the Vijayanagara empire is true? A) It was located in the Deccan region. B) The capital was Hampi. C) It fought against the Bahmani sultanates. D) All of the above.

  1. Only A and B
  2. Only B and C
  3. A, B, and C
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All three statements are correct about the Vijayanagara empire.

50. The term 'Sufi' comes from the Arabic word meaning 'wool', referring to the simple woolen clothes worn by early mystics.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Sufi is derived from 'suf' (wool), indicating their simple lifestyle.
