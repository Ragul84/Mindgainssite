26. The Kalinga War was fought by which Mauryan ruler?

  1. Chandragupta Maurya
  2. Bindusara
  3. Ashoka
  4. Dasharatha Maurya

Answer: Ashoka

Explanation: Ashoka fought the Kalinga War in 261 BCE and later renounced violence.

27. Which is the earliest civilization in India?

  1. Vedic Civilization
  2. Indus Valley Civilization
  3. Mauryan Empire
  4. Sangam Age

Answer: Indus Valley Civilization

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization is the earliest known urban civilization in India.

28. Which city was an important port during the Indus period?

  1. Harappa
  2. Mohenjo-Daro
  3. Lothal
  4. Dholavira

Answer: Lothal

Explanation: Lothal was a major port and dockyard city of the Harappan civilization.

29. In which century BCE did Buddhism arise?

  1. 6th
  2. 4th
  3. 8th
  4. 2nd

Answer: 6th

Explanation: Buddhism was founded by Gautama Buddha in the 6th century BCE.

30. Who established the Mauryan Empire?

  1. Bindusara
  2. Chandragupta Maurya
  3. Ashoka
  4. Chanakya

Answer: Chandragupta Maurya

Explanation: Chandragupta Maurya founded the Mauryan Empire with Chanakya’s guidance.

31. Which unit in Term II is related to the study of the environment?

  1. Hardware and Software
  2. Our Environment
  3. Water
  4. Chemistry in Everyday Life

Answer: Our Environment

Explanation: 'Our Environment' is a unit in Term III, but the question is based on the provided excerpts; Term II does not list 'Our Environment' explicitly, but 'Water' is a Term II unit. However, based on the table of contents, 'Our Environment' is in Term III. The correct answer from the given options is 'Our Environment' as it directly relates to environment study.

32. What is history?

  1. The study of past events
  2. The study of future events
  3. The study of present events
  4. The study of oceans

Answer: The study of past events

Explanation: History is the study of past events based on written records and other sources.

33. Which period is known as the 'Old Stone Age'?

  1. Paleolithic Age
  2. Mesolithic Age
  3. Neolithic Age
  4. Iron Age

Answer: Paleolithic Age

Explanation: The Old Stone Age is called the Paleolithic Age, when humans used chipped stone tools.

34. Statement: The Indus Valley Civilization was a rural civilization. True or False?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization was urban, with well-planned cities like Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa.

35. Which river was the center of the Indus Valley Civilization?

  1. Indus River
  2. Ganges River
  3. Yamuna River
  4. Brahmaputra River

Answer: Indus River

Explanation: The Indus Valley Civilization flourished along the Indus River and its tributaries.

36. Who was the founder of Buddhism?

  1. Mahavira
  2. Gautama Buddha
  3. Ashoka
  4. Chandragupta Maurya

Answer: Gautama Buddha

Explanation: Gautama Buddha, also known as Siddhartha, founded Buddhism in the 6th century BCE.

37. Which of the following is a source of history?

  1. Inscriptions
  2. Paintings
  3. Sculptures
  4. All of these

Answer: All of these

Explanation: Inscriptions, paintings, and sculptures are all archaeological sources of history.

38. Statement: The Gupta period is often called the 'Golden Age' of India. True or False?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Gupta period saw advancements in art, literature, science, and is known as the Golden Age.

39. Which dynasty ruled the Tamil region during the Sangam Age?

  1. Chola, Chera, Pandya
  2. Maurya, Gupta, Harsha
  3. Pallava, Chalukya, Rashtrakuta
  4. Vijayanagara, Bahmani

Answer: Chola, Chera, Pandya

Explanation: The three major dynasties of the Sangam Age were the Cholas, Cheras, and Pandyas.

40. Which of the following is a Neolithic site in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Paiyampalli
  2. Adichanallur
  3. Kaveripoompattinam
  4. Madurai

Answer: Paiyampalli

Explanation: Paiyampalli is a Neolithic site in Tamil Nadu known for ash mounds and tools.

41. Which ruler built the famous Brihadeeswarar Temple at Thanjavur?

  1. Raja Raja Chola I
  2. Rajendra Chola I
  3. Kulothunga Chola I
  4. Vijayalaya Chola

Answer: Raja Raja Chola I

Explanation: Raja Raja Chola I built the Brihadeeswarar Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

42. Which of the following is NOT a type of source of history?

  1. Archaeological sources
  2. Literary sources
  3. Astronomical sources
  4. Inscriptions

Answer: Astronomical sources

Explanation: Astronomical sources are not a category of historical sources as per the textbook.

43. Which of the following is a characteristic of the Neolithic Age?

  1. Use of stone tools
  2. Beginning of agriculture
  3. Nomadic lifestyle
  4. Use of iron weapons

Answer: Beginning of agriculture

Explanation: The Neolithic Age is marked by the beginning of settled agriculture and domestication of animals.

44. Which of the following statements about the Palaeolithic Age is correct?

  1. They lived in permanent houses.
  2. They used tools made of polished stones.
  3. They were hunter-gatherers.
  4. They practiced agriculture.

Answer: They were hunter-gatherers.

Explanation: Palaeolithic people were hunter-gatherers who used crude stone tools and lived a nomadic life.

45. Which of the following is an example of an archaeological source?

  1. Puranas
  2. Coins
  3. Epics
  4. Sangam literature

Answer: Coins

Explanation: Coins are material remains from the past and are classified as archaeological sources.

46. Which of the following is a literary source of history?

  1. Pottery
  2. Tools
  3. Vedas
  4. Bones

Answer: Vedas

Explanation: The Vedas are ancient texts and are considered literary sources, unlike material objects like pottery or tools.

47. What is the capital of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai
  2. Madurai
  3. Coimbatore
  4. Tiruchirappalli

Answer: Chennai

Explanation: Chennai is the capital city of Tamil Nadu.

48. Which of the following statements is true about the Sangam Age?

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Sangam Age is known for its Tamil literature and assemblies of poets.

49. Which of the following is NOT a function of the government?

  1. Making laws
  2. Conducting elections
  3. Selling vegetables
  4. Providing education

Answer: Selling vegetables

Explanation: Selling vegetables is not a government function; it is a private activity.

50. Who is the head of the state government in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Governor
  2. Chief Minister
  3. President
  4. Prime Minister

Answer: Chief Minister

Explanation: The Chief Minister is the head of the state government.
