26. Which of the following is NOT a major factor influencing the climate of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Proximity to the equator
  2. Western Ghats
  3. Himalayan Mountains
  4. Northeast Monsoon

Answer: Himalayan Mountains

Explanation: The Himalayan Mountains are located far north of Tamil Nadu and do not influence its climate directly.

27. Which of the following best describes a globe?

  1. A flat representation of the Earth
  2. A spherical model of the Earth
  3. A map of a specific continent
  4. A diagram of the Earth's layers

Answer: A spherical model of the Earth

Explanation: A globe is a spherical model that accurately represents the Earth's shape and its features.

28. The imaginary line that divides the Earth into the Northern Hemisphere and the Southern Hemisphere is called:

  1. Prime Meridian
  2. Tropic of Cancer
  3. Equator
  4. Arctic Circle

Answer: Equator

Explanation: The Equator is an imaginary line at 0 degrees latitude that divides the Earth into two equal halves.

29. Assertion (A): Longitudes are semicircles that run from the North Pole to the South Pole. Reason (R): All longitudes are of equal length.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

Explanation: Longitudes are indeed semicircles connecting the poles, and all of them have the same length.

30. Assertion (A): Latitudes are full circles that run parallel to the Equator. Reason (R): The distance between any two consecutive latitudes is constant.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  3. A is true but R is false.
  4. A is false but R is true.

Answer: A is true but R is false.

Explanation: While latitudes are full circles parallel to the Equator, the distance between consecutive latitudes is not constant; it decreases as you move towards the poles.

31. Statement 1: The Prime Meridian passes through Greenwich, London. Statement 2: The Prime Meridian is at 0 degrees longitude.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.

Explanation: Both statements accurately describe the Prime Meridian.

32. Statement 1: The Tropic of Cancer is located at approximately 23.5 degrees North latitude. Statement 2: The Tropic of Capricorn is located at approximately 23.5 degrees South latitude.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.

Explanation: Both statements correctly identify the latitudes of the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn.

33. Statement 1: The Earth completes one rotation on its axis in 24 hours. Statement 2: This rotation causes day and night.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.

Explanation: The Earth's rotation on its axis in 24 hours is responsible for the cycle of day and night.

34. Statement 1: The Arctic Circle is located at 66.5 degrees North latitude. Statement 2: The Antarctic Circle is located at 66.5 degrees South latitude.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.

Explanation: Both statements correctly define the latitudes of the Arctic and Antarctic Circles.

35. Statement 1: The International Date Line generally follows the 180-degree longitude. Statement 2: Crossing the International Date Line from east to west means you gain a day.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.

Explanation: Statement 1 is true. Statement 2 is false; crossing from east to west means you lose a day.

36. The Earth is tilted on its axis at an angle of:

  1. 18.5 degrees
  2. 23.5 degrees
  3. 66.5 degrees
  4. 90 degrees

Answer: 23.5 degrees

Explanation: The Earth's axis is tilted at approximately 23.5 degrees relative to its orbital plane.

37. Match the following terms with their definitions:

  1. Northernmost latitude of the Torrid Zone
  2. Angular distance north or south of the Equator
  3. Southernmost latitude of the Frigid Zone
  4. Angular distance east or west of the Prime Meridian

Answer: Northernmost latitude of the Torrid Zone

Explanation: Correct answer is Northernmost latitude of the Torrid Zone

38. Match List I with List II: List I (Line) A. Equator B. North Pole C. Prime Meridian D. South Pole List II (Value) 1. 90° south latitude 2. 90° north latitude 3. 0° longitude 4. 0° latitude

  1. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  2. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
  3. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1

Explanation: The Equator is 0° latitude, the poles are 90° N and 90° S, and the Prime Meridian is 0° longitude.

39. Match List I with List II: List I (Parallel) A. Tropic of Cancer B. Antarctic Circle C. Tropic of Capricorn D. Arctic Circle List II (Latitude) 1. 23.5° S 2. 23.5° N 3. 66.5° S 4. 66.5° N

  1. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  2. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  3. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  4. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4

Explanation: The Tropic of Cancer is at 23.5° N, the Tropic of Capricorn at 23.5° S, the Arctic Circle at 66.5° N and the Antarctic Circle at 66.5° S.

40. The Earth is a perfect sphere. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Earth is an oblate spheroid, slightly flattened at the poles and bulging at the equator.

41. All lines of longitude are of equal length. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: All lines of longitude are semicircles and have the same length.

42. The distance between lines of latitude increases as you move towards the poles. (True/False)

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The distance between lines of latitude is approximately constant, but the distance between lines of longitude decreases towards the poles.

43. Which of the following is the innermost layer of the Earth?

  1. Crust
  2. Mantle
  3. Outer Core
  4. Inner Core

Answer: Inner Core

Explanation: The Earth's interior is composed of three main layers: crust, mantle, and core. The core is further divided into the outer core and the inner core, with the inner core being the innermost layer.

44. The semi-fluid layer beneath the crust is known as the:

  1. Core
  2. Mantle
  3. Lithosphere
  4. Asthenosphere

Answer: Mantle

Explanation: The mantle is the layer located between the crust and the core. It is a thick, semi-fluid layer of rock.

45. The outermost solid shell of a rocky planet, dwarf planet, or natural satellite is called the:

  1. Mantle
  2. Core
  3. Crust
  4. Lithosphere

Answer: Crust

Explanation: The crust is the thin, brittle, outermost layer of the Earth.

46. Which layer of the Earth is primarily composed of iron and nickel?

  1. Crust
  2. Mantle
  3. Outer Core
  4. Inner Core

Answer: Inner Core

Explanation: The Earth's core, both outer and inner, is predominantly made up of iron and nickel.

47. Statement 1: The Earth's crust is thicker under the continents than under the oceans. Statement 2: The mantle is the thickest layer of the Earth.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.

Explanation: Continental crust is generally thicker than oceanic crust. The mantle is indeed the thickest layer of the Earth.

48. Statement 1: The outer core is solid, while the inner core is liquid. Statement 2: The mantle is a solid layer.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Explanation: The outer core is liquid, and the inner core is solid. The mantle is a semi-fluid (plastic) layer, not entirely solid.

49. Statement 1: The lithosphere includes the crust and the upper part of the mantle. Statement 2: The asthenosphere is a rigid layer beneath the lithosphere.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.

Explanation: The lithosphere comprises the crust and the uppermost rigid part of the mantle. The asthenosphere is a weaker, more ductile layer beneath the lithosphere.

50. Statement 1: The temperature increases with depth inside the Earth. Statement 2: The pressure also increases with depth inside the Earth.

  1. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.
  2. Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is false.
  3. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is true.
  4. Statement 1 is false and Statement 2 is false.

Answer: Statement 1 is true and Statement 2 is true.

Explanation: Both temperature and pressure increase significantly as one goes deeper into the Earth's interior.
