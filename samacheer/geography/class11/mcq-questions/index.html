26. Match List I with List II: List I (Layer) A. Exosphere B. Troposphere C. Stratosphere D. Thermosphere List II (Characteristic) 1. Outer boundary of atmosphere 2. Auroras occur here 3. Contains ozone layer 4. Weather occurs here

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2
  3. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  4. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1

Answer: A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2

Explanation: The exosphere is the outer boundary, weather happens in the troposphere, the stratosphere holds the ozone layer and auroras occur in the thermosphere.

27. The atmospheric layer that has the lowest density of air molecules is the:

  1. Troposphere
  2. Exosphere
  3. Stratosphere
  4. Mesosphere

Answer: Exosphere

Explanation: Exosphere has very few particles, with molecules escaping into space.

28. Which of the following is the primary source of energy that drives the temperature and pressure systems of the Earth?

  1. Geothermal heat
  2. Solar radiation
  3. Tidal energy
  4. Atmospheric friction

Answer: Solar radiation

Explanation: Solar radiation is the primary driver of all atmospheric processes.

29. The normal lapse rate of temperature in the troposphere is approximately:

  1. 6.5°C per 1000m
  2. 1°C per 100m
  3. 4.5°C per 1000m
  4. 10°C per 1000m

Answer: 6.5°C per 1000m

Explanation: Temperature decreases by about 6.5°C for every 1000 meters of ascent.

30. Which instrument is used to measure atmospheric pressure?

  1. Anemometer
  2. Hygrometer
  3. Barometer
  4. Thermometer

Answer: Barometer

Explanation: A barometer is the standard instrument for measuring air pressure.

31. The process by which heat is transferred through the movement of air or water is called:

  1. Conduction
  2. Radiation
  3. Convection
  4. Advection

Answer: Convection

Explanation: Convection is the transfer of heat by the actual movement of the heated fluid.

32. Isobars are lines on a map connecting places having the same:

  1. Temperature
  2. Pressure
  3. Rainfall
  4. Humidity

Answer: Pressure

Explanation: Isobars connect points of equal atmospheric pressure.

33. Which of the following factors most significantly affects the distribution of temperature globally?

  1. Latitude
  2. Soil type
  3. Wind speed
  4. Cloud cover

Answer: Latitude

Explanation: Latitude determines the angle of incidence of solar rays, affecting temperature.

34. What happens to air pressure as altitude increases?

  1. Increases
  2. Decreases
  3. Remains constant
  4. Fluctuates randomly

Answer: Decreases

Explanation: Air pressure decreases with altitude because the air becomes thinner.

35. The 'Inversion of Temperature' typically occurs when:

  1. Air temperature increases with height
  2. Air temperature decreases with height
  3. Temperature remains constant
  4. Pressure increases with height

Answer: Air temperature increases with height

Explanation: Temperature inversion is the reversal of the normal lapse rate.

36. Which of the following is a characteristic of a Low Pressure system?

  1. Descending air
  2. Clear skies
  3. Rising air
  4. High density

Answer: Rising air

Explanation: Low pressure areas are characterized by ascending air, often leading to clouds.

37. Consider the following statements: 1. Albedo is the percentage of solar radiation reflected back. 2. Fresh snow has a higher albedo than dark soil.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Albedo is reflectivity; snow reflects more light than dark soil.

38. Consider these statements: 1. Land heats up faster than water. 2. This difference creates land and sea breezes.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Differential heating of land and water causes local wind systems.

39. Regarding atmospheric pressure: 1. It is highest at sea level. 2. It is measured in millibars (mb).

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Pressure is highest at sea level and measured in mb or hPa.

40. Statements on Heat Transfer: 1. Conduction occurs in vacuums. 2. Radiation does not require a medium.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Only 2 is correct

Explanation: Conduction requires a medium; radiation travels through a vacuum.

41. Statements on Temperature: 1. Equator receives vertical rays. 2. Poles receive slanted rays.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: The curvature of the Earth causes rays to be vertical at the equator and slanted at poles.

42. Statements on Pressure Belts: 1. Equatorial Low is a thermal belt. 2. Subtropical High is a dynamic belt.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Equatorial low is caused by heat (thermal), Subtropical high by air sinking (dynamic).

43. Statements on Advection: 1. It is the horizontal movement of air. 2. It helps in transferring heat across regions.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Advection is the horizontal transfer of heat/moisture by wind.

44. Statements on the Atmosphere: 1. Air is compressible. 2. Air pressure is the weight of the air column.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Air is a gas (compressible) and its weight creates atmospheric pressure.

45. Statements on Temperature Inversion: 1. It occurs mostly in winter. 2. It traps pollutants near the ground.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: Inversions are common in winter and act as a lid, trapping smog.

46. Statements on Pressure Gradients: 1. Steep gradient means rapid pressure change. 2. Steep gradients produce strong winds.

  1. Only 1 is correct
  2. Only 2 is correct
  3. Both 1 and 2 are correct
  4. Neither 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer: Both 1 and 2 are correct

Explanation: The pressure gradient force drives wind; steeper gradients cause faster winds.

47. Assertion: The poles are colder than the equator. Reason: The sun's rays hit the poles at an oblique angle.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: Oblique rays spread energy over a larger area, reducing intensity.

48. Assertion: Air flows from high pressure to low pressure. Reason: Nature seeks to maintain equilibrium.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: Pressure gradient force moves air to balance the pressure difference.

49. Assertion: Temperature decreases with height in the troposphere. Reason: The atmosphere is heated from above by the sun.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: A is true, R is false

Explanation: The atmosphere is primarily heated from below (the ground) via conduction and convection.

50. The normal lapse rate is constant everywhere on Earth.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Lapse rate varies based on humidity, location, and weather conditions.
