26. Assertion: The stratosphere is a stable layer with little vertical mixing. Reason: The temperature increases with altitude in the stratosphere.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but the reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but the reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.

Explanation: The temperature inversion in the stratosphere (increasing temperature with altitude) prevents vertical mixing, making it a stable layer.

27. The layer of the atmosphere where satellites orbit is the exosphere.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Many satellites orbit in the exosphere and the upper thermosphere, but the statement is too general as some orbit lower.

28. The ozone layer is crucial for life on Earth because it absorbs most of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The ozone layer's primary function is to shield the Earth's surface from damaging UV radiation, which can cause skin cancer and other health problems.

29. Which of the following is NOT a component of the Earth's atmosphere?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Water
  4. Iron

Answer: Iron

Explanation: Iron is a metal and is not a significant component of the Earth's gaseous atmosphere.

30. Which of the following is the narrow zone where lithosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere interact to support life?

  1. Stratosphere
  2. Biosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Troposphere

Answer: Biosphere

Explanation: The biosphere is the life-supporting zone where land, water, and air meet.

31. Which ecosystem is characterized by permafrost and very low biodiversity?

  1. Tropical Rainforest
  2. Desert
  3. Tundra
  4. Grassland

Answer: Tundra

Explanation: Tundra regions have permanently frozen soil called permafrost.

32. The primary source of energy for almost all ecosystems in the biosphere is:

  1. Geothermal heat
  2. Chemical energy
  3. Solar energy
  4. Wind energy

Answer: Solar energy

Explanation: Sunlight is the primary energy source for photosynthesis.

33. Which of these is a biotic component of the biosphere?

  1. Temperature
  2. Soil
  3. Bacteria
  4. Humidity

Answer: Bacteria

Explanation: Biotic components are living organisms, such as bacteria.

34. In a food chain, which organism occupies the first trophic level?

  1. Herbivores
  2. Carnivores
  3. Decomposers
  4. Producers

Answer: Producers

Explanation: Producers (plants) are the first trophic level as they produce food.

35. Which forest type is known as the 'Lungs of the World'?

  1. Deciduous forests
  2. Coniferous forests
  3. Tropical Rainforests
  4. Mangroves

Answer: Tropical Rainforests

Explanation: Tropical rainforests produce a huge amount of oxygen.

36. Consider the following statements: 1. Biosphere consists of only land and water. 2. Energy flows unidirectionally in a food chain. 3. Decomposers recycle nutrients back into the soil. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: 2 and 3

Explanation: Statement 1 is wrong because biosphere also includes the atmosphere.

37. Consider the following statements about Tropical Rainforests: 1. They experience heavy rainfall throughout the year. 2. They have a distinct dry season. 3. They exhibit high biodiversity. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 3

Explanation: Tropical rainforests do not have a distinct dry season.

38. Regarding the Tundra biome, consider these statements: 1. It is found in polar regions. 2. Mosses and lichens are common plants here. 3. It has the highest species diversity among all biomes. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2

Explanation: Tundra has very low species diversity due to extreme cold.

39. Consider these statements about the Food Web: 1. It is a single linear path of energy flow. 2. It consists of multiple interconnected food chains. 3. It provides stability to the ecosystem. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: 2 and 3

Explanation: A food web is a complex network, not a single linear path.

40. Consider these statements about Desert ecosystems: 1. Plants have thick cuticles to prevent water loss. 2. Nocturnal animals are common. 3. They have high annual precipitation. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 2

Explanation: Deserts have very low annual precipitation.

41. Consider these statements about the Biosphere: 1. It is the zone of interaction between lithosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere. 2. It is a closed system for matter but open for energy. 3. Life cannot exist outside this zone. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2
  2. 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 3
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All statements accurately describe the biosphere.

42. Assertion: Tropical rainforests have a multi-layered structure of vegetation. Reason: Competition for sunlight is intense among plants in these forests.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: Layering occurs because plants grow at different heights to capture sunlight.

43. Assertion: Decomposers are essential for the survival of the biosphere. Reason: They convert organic matter into inorganic nutrients.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation: Without decomposers, nutrients would not be recycled for producers.

44. Lichens are symbiotic associations between algae and fungi.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Lichens are a classic example of mutualism between algae and fungi.

45. Primary consumers in a food chain are always carnivores.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Primary consumers are herbivores that eat producers.

46. Match List I with List II: List I (Biome) A. Tropical rainforest B. Tundra C. Tropical grassland D. Desert List II (Feature) 1. Xerophytes 2. Savanna 3. Canopy 4. Permafrost

  1. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  2. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  3. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Explanation: Rainforests have a dense canopy, the tundra permafrost, tropical grasslands are called savannas and deserts have xerophytes.

47. Match List I with List II: List I (Organism (plant → grasshopper → frog → eagle)) A. Primary consumer B. Tertiary consumer C. Producer D. Secondary consumer List II (Trophic level) 1. Eagle 2. Green plant 3. Grasshopper 4. Frog

  1. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  4. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Answer: A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4

Explanation: The green plant is the producer, the grasshopper the primary consumer, the frog the secondary consumer and the eagle the tertiary consumer.

48. Match List I with List II: List I (Abiotic factor) A. Water B. Soil C. Temperature D. Light List II (Impact) 1. Metabolism 2. Hydration 3. Anchorage 4. Photosynthesis

  1. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  2. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4

Explanation: Water hydrates, soil anchors plants, temperature controls metabolism and light drives photosynthesis.

49. Match List I with List II: List I (Forest type) A. Tropical evergreen forest B. Mangrove forest C. Coniferous forest D. Temperate deciduous forest List II (Location) 1. Coastal areas 2. Amazon 3. Temperate zones 4. Taiga

  1. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  2. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
  3. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  4. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Answer: A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

Explanation: Tropical evergreen forest covers the Amazon, mangroves line coasts, coniferous forest forms the taiga and deciduous forests grow in temperate zones.

50. What is the role of producers in an ecosystem?

  1. Consume other plants
  2. Convert solar energy to chemical energy
  3. Break down dead matter
  4. Hunt prey

Answer: Convert solar energy to chemical energy

Explanation: Producers create the base of the energy pyramid via photosynthesis.
