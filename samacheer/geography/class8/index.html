Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the Samacheer Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 255 questions.

1. Which is the longest river in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Kaveri
  2. Palar
  3. Vaigai
  4. Thamirabarani

Answer: Kaveri

Explanation: The Kaveri is the longest river in Tamil Nadu, spanning approximately 800 km.

2. Which district in Tamil Nadu is known for its silk production?

  1. Coimbatore
  2. Kanchipuram
  3. Madurai
  4. Tiruchirappalli

Answer: Kanchipuram

Explanation: Kanchipuram is famous for its silk sarees and is a major silk production center.

3. True or False: Tamil Nadu has a tropical climate.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Tamil Nadu experiences a tropical climate with hot summers and moderate winters.

4. True or False: The Tropic of Cancer passes through India.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Tropic of Cancer passes through the middle of India.

5. Which of the following is the largest river in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Kaveri
  2. Palar
  3. Vaigai
  4. Thamirabarani

Answer: Kaveri

Explanation: The Kaveri is the largest river in Tamil Nadu, flowing through the state and providing water for irrigation and other purposes.

6. Which of the following is NOT a coastal district of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Kanyakumari
  2. Chennai
  3. Coimbatore
  4. Cuddalore

Answer: Coimbatore

Explanation: Coimbatore is an inland district and does not have a coastline, unlike Kanyakumari, Chennai, and Cuddalore.

7. True or False: The Western Ghats are located in the eastern part of Tamil Nadu.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Western Ghats are located in the western part of Tamil Nadu, not the eastern part.

8. Which of the following is a major port in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai Port
  2. Tuticorin Port
  3. Nagapattinam Port
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Chennai Port, Tuticorin Port, and Nagapattinam Port are all major ports in Tamil Nadu, playing crucial roles in trade and commerce.

9. What is the primary source of rainfall in Tamil Nadu during the winter season?

  1. Southwest Monsoon
  2. Northeast Monsoon
  3. Cyclonic Rainfall
  4. Convectional Rainfall

Answer: Northeast Monsoon

Explanation: The Northeast Monsoon is the primary source of rainfall in Tamil Nadu during the winter season, contributing significantly to the state's water resources.

10. Which of the following is a major factor influencing the climate of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Proximity to the equator
  2. Presence of the Himalayas
  3. Distance from the sea
  4. High altitude

Answer: Proximity to the equator

Explanation: Tamil Nadu's climate is primarily influenced by its proximity to the equator, which results in a tropical climate.

11. Which layer of the atmosphere is closest to the Earth's surface?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Troposphere

Explanation: The troposphere is the lowest layer of Earth's atmosphere, where most weather phenomena occur.

12. The ozone layer, which protects us from harmful ultraviolet radiation, is primarily found in which atmospheric layer?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Exosphere

Answer: Stratosphere

Explanation: The ozone layer is concentrated in the stratosphere, absorbing most of the Sun's harmful UV radiation.

13. Which gas is the most abundant in the Earth's atmosphere?

  1. Oxygen
  2. Carbon Dioxide
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Argon

Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen makes up about 78% of the Earth's atmosphere, followed by oxygen at about 21%.

14. What phenomenon causes the sky to appear blue?

  1. Reflection of sunlight by clouds
  2. Scattering of sunlight by atmospheric gases
  3. Absorption of sunlight by water vapor
  4. Refraction of sunlight through the atmosphere

Answer: Scattering of sunlight by atmospheric gases

Explanation: Rayleigh scattering causes shorter wavelengths of light (blue and violet) to be scattered more than longer wavelengths, making the sky appear blue.

15. Which of the following is a greenhouse gas?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Methane
  4. Argon

Answer: Methane

Explanation: Methane is a potent greenhouse gas that traps heat in the atmosphere, contributing to global warming.

16. Assertion: The temperature generally decreases as we move higher in the troposphere. Reason: The troposphere is heated from below by the Earth's surface.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but the reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but the reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.

Explanation: The troposphere is heated by the Earth's surface, leading to a decrease in temperature with altitude.

17. Assertion: The mesosphere is the coldest layer of the atmosphere. Reason: Meteors burn up in the mesosphere.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but the reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but the reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.

Explanation: The mesosphere is indeed the coldest layer, and meteors burn up due to friction in this layer, but the reason for its coldness is not directly due to meteors burning.

18. The thermosphere is characterized by extremely high temperatures due to the absorption of high-energy solar radiation.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The thermosphere absorbs X-rays and UV radiation, leading to very high temperatures, although the air is very thin.

19. The exosphere is the outermost layer of the Earth's atmosphere, gradually merging with outer space.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The exosphere is the final frontier of the atmosphere, where gases are so sparse that they can escape into space.

20. Weather phenomena like rain, snow, and clouds primarily occur in the stratosphere.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Weather phenomena occur in the troposphere, the lowest layer of the atmosphere.

21. Match List I with List II: List I (Layer) A. Stratosphere B. Thermosphere C. Mesosphere D. Troposphere List II (Characteristic) 1. Ozone layer is found here 2. Meteors burn up here 3. Aurora occurs here 4. Weather occurs here

  1. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  3. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  4. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2

Answer: A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4

Explanation: The ozone layer is in the stratosphere, auroras occur in the thermosphere, meteors burn up in the mesosphere and weather happens in the troposphere.

22. Match the following atmospheric gases with their approximate percentage in the atmosphere:

  1. About 0.9%
  2. About 78%
  3. About 21%
  4. About 0.04%

Answer: About 0.9%

Explanation: Correct answer is About 0.9%

23. Which atmospheric layer is also known as the 'weather layer'?

  1. Stratosphere
  2. Mesosphere
  3. Troposphere
  4. Ionosphere

Answer: Troposphere

Explanation: The troposphere is where all weather phenomena occur because it contains most of the atmosphere's water vapor.

24. What is the role of the ionosphere in radio communication?

  1. It absorbs radio waves.
  2. It reflects radio waves back to Earth.
  3. It generates radio waves.
  4. It cools radio waves.

Answer: It reflects radio waves back to Earth.

Explanation: The ionosphere, a region within the thermosphere and exosphere, contains charged particles that can reflect radio waves, enabling long-distance communication.

25. Assertion: The temperature in the thermosphere is very high. Reason: The thermosphere absorbs high-energy solar radiation.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but the reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but the reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.

Explanation: The absorption of high-energy solar radiation, such as X-rays and UV rays, leads to the extremely high temperatures in the thermosphere.
