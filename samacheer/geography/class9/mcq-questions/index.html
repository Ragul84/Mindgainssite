26. Assertion: Radio waves are reflected back to Earth by the ionosphere. Reason: The ionosphere contains electrically charged particles.

  1. Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true, R is NOT correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation

Explanation: Ionized particles in the ionosphere reflect radio waves.

27. The temperature increases as we go higher in the stratosphere.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Ozone absorption of UV radiation causes temperature to rise in the stratosphere.

28. The mesosphere is the warmest layer of the atmosphere.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The mesosphere is the coldest layer; the thermosphere is the warmest.

29. Air pressure is highest at the top of a mountain.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Air pressure decreases as altitude increases.

30. Match List I with List II: List I (Atmospheric layer) A. Stratosphere B. Troposphere C. Thermosphere D. Mesosphere List II (Feature) 1. Weather phenomena 2. Ionosphere 3. Meteors burn 4. Ozone layer

  1. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  2. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  3. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Explanation: The ozone layer lies in the stratosphere; weather happens in the troposphere; the ionosphere is part of the thermosphere; meteors burn up in the mesosphere.

31. Match List I with List II: List I (Gas) A. Carbon dioxide B. Oxygen C. Nitrogen D. Argon List II (Share of dry air) 1. 0.9% 2. 78% 3. 0.03% 4. 21%

  1. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  2. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  3. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  4. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3

Answer: A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Explanation: Dry air is about 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 0.93% argon and 0.03-0.04% carbon dioxide.

32. Match List I with List II: List I (Instrument) A. Hygrometer B. Thermometer C. Barometer D. Anemometer List II (Measures) 1. Pressure 2. Temperature 3. Humidity 4. Wind Speed

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  3. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  4. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Answer: A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Explanation: A hygrometer measures humidity, a thermometer temperature, a barometer air pressure and an anemometer wind speed.

33. Match List I with List II: List I (Boundary) A. Stratopause B. Tropopause C. Thermopause D. Mesopause List II (Layers it separates) 1. Troposphere-Stratosphere 2. Stratosphere-Mesosphere 3. Mesosphere-Thermosphere 4. Ionosphere-Exosphere

  1. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  2. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  3. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  4. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3

Answer: A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

Explanation: The tropopause separates troposphere and stratosphere, the stratopause stratosphere and mesosphere, the mesopause mesosphere and thermosphere, and the thermopause the thermosphere (ionosphere) and exosphere.

34. Which layer is the coldest and why?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Mesosphere

Explanation: The mesosphere is the coldest because it lacks gases that absorb heat.

35. What is the role of the Ionosphere in communication?

  1. Absorbs radio waves
  2. Reflects radio waves
  3. Blocks radio waves
  4. No role

Answer: Reflects radio waves

Explanation: The ionosphere reflects radio waves back to Earth, enabling long-distance communication.

36. What happens to air pressure as we go higher in the atmosphere?

  1. Increases
  2. Decreases
  3. Remains constant
  4. Fluctuates randomly

Answer: Decreases

Explanation: Air becomes thinner and less dense as altitude increases, leading to lower pressure.

37. Which gas is essential for photosynthesis?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Carbon Dioxide
  4. Argon

Answer: Carbon Dioxide

Explanation: Plants use carbon dioxide to produce food during photosynthesis.

38. Which layer is known as the 'comfort zone' for aviation?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Exosphere

Answer: Stratosphere

Explanation: The stratosphere is stable and free from weather disturbances, making it ideal for jets.

39. What is the percentage of Oxygen in the atmosphere?

  1. 78%
  2. 21%
  3. 0.9%
  4. 0.03%

Answer: 21%

Explanation: Oxygen makes up approximately 21% of the air.

40. Which of the following is the narrow zone of contact between lithosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere where life exists?

  1. Stratosphere
  2. Biosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Troposphere

Answer: Biosphere

Explanation: The biosphere is the global sum of all ecosystems, integrating all living beings and their relationships.

41. Which biome is characterized by permafrost and very low temperatures?

  1. Tropical Rainforest
  2. Savanna
  3. Tundra
  4. Desert

Answer: Tundra

Explanation: Tundra regions have a permanently frozen layer of soil called permafrost.

42. The 'Lungs of the World' refers to which of the following?

  1. Taiga Forests
  2. Amazon Rainforests
  3. Mangroves
  4. Deciduous Forests

Answer: Amazon Rainforests

Explanation: Amazon rainforests produce a significant portion of the world's oxygen.

43. Which of these is a primary consumer in a food chain?

  1. Tiger
  2. Green Plant
  3. Grasshopper
  4. Eagle

Answer: Grasshopper

Explanation: Primary consumers are herbivores that eat producers (plants).

44. Which of the following is an example of an abiotic component of an ecosystem?

  1. Bacteria
  2. Fungi
  3. Temperature
  4. Plants

Answer: Temperature

Explanation: Abiotic components are non-living chemical and physical parts of the environment.

45. Which forest type is known for having conical shaped trees with needle-like leaves?

  1. Tropical Evergreen
  2. Coniferous/Taiga
  3. Tropical Deciduous
  4. Mangroves

Answer: Coniferous/Taiga

Explanation: Coniferous trees have needle-like leaves to shed snow and reduce transpiration.

46. Consider the following statements: 1. Tropical rainforests have high biodiversity. 2. They experience distinct dry seasons. 3. They are located near the equator. Which of the statements are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1 and 3 only

Explanation: Tropical rainforests have high rainfall throughout the year, so they lack a distinct dry season.

47. Regarding the Savanna biome, identify the correct statements: 1. It is a tropical grassland. 2. It is characterized by scattered trees. 3. It is found primarily in Africa. 4. It has extreme freezing temperatures.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 2, 3, and 4
  3. 1, 3, and 4
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: Savannas are warm tropical grasslands, not freezing.

48. Which of the following are characteristics of Desert biomes? 1. Low precipitation 2. High evaporation 3. Xerophytic vegetation 4. High humidity

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1, 2, and 3
  3. 2, 3, and 4
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: Deserts are characterized by low humidity, not high humidity.

49. Which statements about the Food Web are true? 1. It consists of multiple interconnected food chains. 2. It provides stability to the ecosystem. 3. Energy flow is unidirectional. 4. It is a simple linear sequence.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 2, 3, and 4
  3. 1 and 4 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: A food web is a complex network, not a simple linear sequence.

50. Consider the following about Deciduous forests: 1. Trees shed leaves annually. 2. They are found in regions with moderate rainfall. 3. Teak and Sal are common species. 4. They are found in the Arctic circle.

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 2, 3, and 4
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: 1, 2, and 3

Explanation: Deciduous forests are not found in the Arctic circle.
