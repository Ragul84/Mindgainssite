Class12 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class12 questions to revise the Samacheer Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 459 questions.

1. What is the statistical study of human population called?

  1. Demography
  2. Geography
  3. Sociology
  4. Anthropology

Answer: Demography

Explanation: Demography is the statistical study of human population, including size, structure, and distribution.

2. Which of the following is NOT a factor influencing population growth?

  1. Birth rate
  2. Migration
  3. Climate change
  4. Asteroid strikes

Answer: Asteroid strikes

Explanation: While asteroid strikes can impact the planet, they are not a direct factor influencing population growth.

3. What is the primary focus of Population Geography?

  1. Study of natural resources
  2. Distribution and density of population
  3. Cultural practices
  4. Economic activities

Answer: Distribution and density of population

Explanation: Population Geography primarily focuses on the distribution and density of population.

4. Which of the following is NOT a factor influencing population distribution?

  1. Climate
  2. Soil fertility
  3. Language
  4. Availability of water

Answer: Language

Explanation: Language does not directly influence population distribution, unlike climate, soil fertility, and water availability.

5. What is the primary cause of 'population explosion'?

  1. High birth rates
  2. Low death rates
  3. Migration
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Population explosion is caused by high birth rates, low death rates, and migration.

6. Which unit in the Class 12 Geography syllabus covers 'Human Settlements'?

  1. Unit 1
  2. Unit 2
  3. Unit 3
  4. Unit 4

Answer: Unit 2

Explanation: Unit 2 in the Class 12 Geography syllabus covers 'Human Settlements'.

7. What is the main focus of 'Sustainable Development' in the Class 12 Geography syllabus?

  1. Economic growth
  2. Environmental protection
  3. Social equity
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Sustainable Development focuses on economic growth, environmental protection, and social equity.

8. What is one of the greatest challenges related to population?

  1. Population explosion
  2. Urbanization
  3. Economic inequality
  4. Cultural diversity

Answer: Population explosion

Explanation: The text highlights 'population explosion' as one of the greatest challenges we face today.

9. Which of the following is the statistical study of human population?

  1. Demography
  2. Geography
  3. Sociology
  4. Anthropology

Answer: Demography

Explanation: Demography is the statistical study of human population, including size, structure, and distribution.

10. What is the term used for the statistical study of human population?

  1. Demography
  2. Geography
  3. Anthropology
  4. Sociology

Answer: Demography

Explanation: Demography is the statistical study of human population.

11. Which factor greatly influences the distribution and growth of human population?

  1. Economic factors
  2. Political factors
  3. Physical environment
  4. Cultural factors

Answer: Physical environment

Explanation: The physical environment greatly influences the distribution and growth of human population.

12. How many persons are born every day in the world?

  1. 360,000
  2. 3,600,000
  3. 36,000
  4. 360

Answer: 360,000

Explanation: Approximately 360,000 persons are born every day in the world.

13. What is the main challenge posed by population growth?

  1. Overpopulation
  2. Underpopulation
  3. Population stability
  4. Population decline

Answer: Overpopulation

Explanation: Overpopulation is one of the greatest challenges we are facing today.

14. Which of the following is NOT a component of demography?

  1. Size
  2. Structure
  3. Distribution
  4. Economics

Answer: Economics

Explanation: Economics is not a component of demography.

15. Assertion: The human species may need to populate a new planet within 100 years. Reason: Climate change and population growth make Earth precarious.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true
  2. Assertion is true, reason is false
  3. Assertion is false, reason is true
  4. Both assertion and reason are false

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true

Explanation: Both the assertion and reason are true.

16. Assertion: Population is spread evenly across continents. Reason: Only a few areas support large populations.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true
  2. Assertion is true, reason is false
  3. Assertion is false, reason is true
  4. Both assertion and reason are false

Answer: Assertion is false, reason is true

Explanation: The assertion is false, while the reason is true.

17. Which of the following is a pull factor of migration?

  1. War
  2. Natural disasters
  3. Job opportunities
  4. Political instability

Answer: Job opportunities

Explanation: Job opportunities are considered a pull factor of migration.

18. True or False: The human population has increased at an alarming rate only in the last few hundred years.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: True, the human population has increased at an alarming rate only in the last few hundred years.

19. True or False: Migration only occurs due to economic reasons.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: False, migration can occur due to various reasons including social, political, and environmental factors.

20. Match List I with List II: List I (Term) A. Overpopulation B. Demography C. Migration D. Population density List II (Definition) 1. Statistical study of populations 2. Movement of people from one place to another 3. Number of people living per unit area 4. Condition where the number of people exceeds the resources available

  1. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  3. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  4. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Answer: A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Explanation: Overpopulation is more people than resources can support, demography the statistical study of populations, migration the movement of people and density the number of people per unit area.

21. True or False: The human species has inhabited Earth for less than two million years.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: True, the human species has inhabited Earth for less than two million years.

22. Which of the following is a push factor of migration?

  1. Job opportunities
  2. Natural disasters
  3. Political stability
  4. Better living conditions

Answer: Natural disasters

Explanation: Natural disasters are considered a push factor of migration.

23. Match List I with List II: List I (Aspect of population) A. Population structure B. Population growth C. Population distribution D. Population size List II (Description) 1. Spatial arrangement of individuals 2. Change in population over time 3. Total number of individuals in a population 4. Composition of a population by age and sex

  1. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  2. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  3. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  4. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3

Answer: A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3

Explanation: Structure is the age-sex make-up, growth the change over time, distribution the spatial arrangement and size the total number.

24. True or False: Population growth has no impact on the environment.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: False, population growth significantly impacts the environment.

25. Which of the following statements is true regarding the distribution of world population?

  1. It is evenly distributed.
  2. It is concentrated in certain areas.
  3. It is decreasing globally.
  4. It is only in urban areas.

Answer: It is concentrated in certain areas.

Explanation: The world population is concentrated in certain areas.
