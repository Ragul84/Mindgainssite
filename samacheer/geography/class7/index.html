Class7 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class7 questions to revise the Samacheer Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 218 questions.

1. True or False: The Equator is an imaginary line that divides the Earth into Northern and Southern Hemispheres.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Equator is indeed an imaginary line that divides the Earth into two hemispheres.

2. Which of the following is a major river in India?

  1. Ganges
  2. Amazon
  3. Nile
  4. Mississippi

Answer: Ganges

Explanation: The Ganges is one of the major rivers in India.

3. True or False: The Equator is the longest line of latitude.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Equator is the longest line of latitude, dividing the Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.

4. True or False: The Sahara Desert is located in South America.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Sahara Desert is located in Africa, not South America.

5. True or False: The Sahara Desert is the largest hot desert in the world.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The Sahara Desert is indeed the largest hot desert, covering much of North Africa.

6. True or False: The Equator passes through India.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Equator does not pass through India; it passes through countries like Brazil and Kenya.

7. Which of the following is the largest river in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Kaveri
  2. Palar
  3. Vaigai
  4. Thamirabarani

Answer: Kaveri

Explanation: The Kaveri is the largest river in Tamil Nadu, originating in Karnataka and flowing through Tamil Nadu.

8. Which of the following is NOT a major port in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Chennai Port
  2. Tuticorin Port
  3. Ennore Port
  4. Mumbai Port

Answer: Mumbai Port

Explanation: Mumbai Port is located in Maharashtra, not Tamil Nadu.

9. Which of the following is NOT a major factor influencing the climate of Tamil Nadu?

  1. Proximity to the equator
  2. Western Ghats
  3. Himalayan Mountains
  4. Bay of Bengal

Answer: Himalayan Mountains

Explanation: The Himalayan Mountains are located far north of Tamil Nadu and do not influence its climate, unlike the other options.

10. Which of the following is NOT a major soil type found in Tamil Nadu?

  1. Alluvial Soil
  2. Black Soil
  3. Red Soil
  4. Laterite Soil

Answer: Black Soil

Explanation: Black Soil is predominantly found in regions like Maharashtra and Gujarat, not Tamil Nadu.

11. Which gas is the most abundant in the Earth's atmosphere?

  1. Oxygen
  2. Nitrogen
  3. Carbon dioxide
  4. Argon

Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen makes up approximately 78% of the Earth's atmosphere.

12. In which layer of the atmosphere do all weather phenomena occur?

  1. Stratosphere
  2. Mesosphere
  3. Troposphere
  4. Exosphere

Answer: Troposphere

Explanation: The troposphere is the lowest layer where clouds, rain, and storms occur.

13. Which layer contains the ozone layer that protects us from UV rays?

  1. Troposphere
  2. Stratosphere
  3. Mesosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Stratosphere

Explanation: The ozone layer is located in the stratosphere.

14. What is the instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure?

  1. Thermometer
  2. Anemometer
  3. Barometer
  4. Hygrometer

Answer: Barometer

Explanation: A barometer is used to measure air pressure.

15. Which of the following is the coldest layer of the atmosphere?

  1. Stratosphere
  2. Mesosphere
  3. Troposphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Mesosphere

Explanation: Temperatures decrease to the lowest levels in the mesosphere.

16. Consider the following statements: 1. Nitrogen is essential for plant growth. 2. Carbon dioxide helps in maintaining Earth's temperature. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Nitrogen is vital for proteins and CO2 is a greenhouse gas that regulates temperature.

17. Consider these statements: 1. Temperature increases with height in the Troposphere. 2. Temperature increases with height in the Stratosphere. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Only 2

Explanation: Temperature decreases in the troposphere but increases in the stratosphere due to ozone.

18. Regarding the Ionosphere: 1. It is part of the Thermosphere. 2. It reflects radio waves back to Earth. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: The ionosphere is the ionized part of the thermosphere that enables radio communication.

19. Consider the following: 1. Humidity is the amount of water vapor in the air. 2. High humidity makes us feel cooler. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Only 1

Explanation: High humidity prevents sweat from evaporating, making us feel warmer, not cooler.

20. About the atmosphere: 1. It acts as a blanket. 2. It protects Earth from meteorites. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: The atmosphere regulates temperature and burns up most meteorites in the mesosphere.

21. Regarding wind: 1. Wind moves from high pressure to low pressure. 2. Local winds are caused by local temperature differences. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Air always flows from high to low pressure; local winds like land/sea breezes follow this.

22. Consider these: 1. Condensation is the process of gas turning to liquid. 2. Evaporation is the process of liquid turning to gas. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both definitions of phase changes are correct.

23. Assertion: The stratosphere is ideal for flying jet aircraft. Reason: It is free from clouds and weather disturbances.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the explanation
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: The lack of weather disturbances makes the stratosphere stable for jet flight.

24. Assertion: Air pressure decreases as we go higher. Reason: The air becomes denser at higher altitudes.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the explanation
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: A is true but R is false

Explanation: Air pressure decreases because air becomes thinner (less dense), not denser.

25. The atmosphere is composed of only oxygen and nitrogen.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: It also contains argon, carbon dioxide, and other trace gases.
