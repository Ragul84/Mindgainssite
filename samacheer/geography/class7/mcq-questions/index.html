26. The Mesosphere is the outermost layer of the atmosphere.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: The Exosphere is the outermost layer.

27. The greenhouse effect is necessary for maintaining life on Earth.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Without the greenhouse effect, Earth would be too cold for most life forms.

28. Match List I with List II: List I (Layer) A. Stratosphere B. Troposphere C. Thermosphere List II (Characteristic) 1. Ionization 2. Ozone layer 3. Weather changes

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: The stratosphere holds the ozone layer, weather happens in the troposphere and the thermosphere contains the ionised ionosphere.

29. Match List I with List II: List I (Gas) A. Nitrogen B. Carbon dioxide C. Oxygen List II (Role) 1. Respiration 2. Plant growth 3. Photosynthesis

  1. A-2, B-3, C-1
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1
  3. A-2, B-1, C-3
  4. A-1, B-3, C-2

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: Nitrogen helps plant growth, plants take in carbon dioxide for photosynthesis and living things use oxygen in respiration.

30. Match List I with List II: List I (Instrument) A. Barometer B. Anemometer C. Thermometer List II (Measures) 1. Temperature 2. Pressure 3. Wind speed

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3
  2. A-2, B-3, C-1
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1
  4. A-1, B-3, C-2

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: A barometer measures pressure, an anemometer wind speed and a thermometer temperature.

31. Which of the following is NOT a component of the biosphere?

  1. Lithosphere
  2. Hydrosphere
  3. Atmosphere
  4. Asthenosphere

Answer: Asthenosphere

Explanation: Asthenosphere is part of the Earth's mantle, not the biosphere.

32. The biosphere includes all living organisms and their interactions with the environment.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Biosphere encompasses all living beings and their interactions with the environment.

33. Which zone is considered the most significant for supporting life on Earth?

  1. Tundra
  2. Taiga
  3. Littoral zone
  4. Deep ocean

Answer: Littoral zone

Explanation: The littoral zone is rich in life and supports diverse aquatic life.

34. Assertion: The biosphere is limited to land areas only.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true and related
  2. Both are true but not related
  3. Assertion is true, reason is false
  4. Assertion is false, reason is true

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true and related

Explanation: Biosphere includes land, water, and air; not limited to land only.

35. Match List I with List II: List I (Sphere) A. Atmosphere B. Lithosphere C. Hydrosphere List II (Description) 1. Gaseous envelope of Earth 2. All water bodies 3. Earth's solid outer layer

  1. A-3, B-1, C-2
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-2, B-3, C-1

Answer: A-1, B-3, C-2

Explanation: The atmosphere is the envelope of gases, the lithosphere the solid outer layer and the hydrosphere all water bodies.

36. Which of the following is a terrestrial ecosystem?

  1. Coral reef
  2. Savanna
  3. Open ocean
  4. Mangrove

Answer: Savanna

Explanation: Savanna is a land-based ecosystem.

37. Match List I with List II: List I (Organism) A. Mangrove trees B. Teak trees C. Blue whale List II (Habitat) 1. Coastal areas 2. Open ocean 3. Forests

  1. A-1, B-3, C-2
  2. A-2, B-3, C-1
  3. A-1, B-2, C-3
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2

Answer: A-1, B-3, C-2

Explanation: Mangroves grow on tidal coasts, teak grows in forests and the blue whale lives in the open ocean.

38. The biosphere is essential for maintaining the balance of which of the following?

  1. Climate
  2. Ecosystems
  3. Soil fertility
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: Biosphere influences climate, ecosystems, and soil health.

39. Which of the following best describes the role of decomposers in the biosphere?

  1. Produce energy
  2. Break down organic matter
  3. Photosynthesize
  4. Consume sunlight

Answer: Break down organic matter

Explanation: Decomposers break down organic matter, recycling nutrients.

40. Assertion: The destruction of forests leads to a decrease in biodiversity.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true and related
  2. Both are true but not related
  3. Assertion is true, reason is false
  4. Assertion is false, reason is true

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true and related

Explanation: Deforestation reduces habitats, decreasing biodiversity.

41. True or False: The ocean is the largest component of the biosphere.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Oceans cover about 71% of Earth's surface, making them the largest biosphere component.

42. Match List I with List II: List I (Ecosystem) A. Hot desert B. Tundra C. Tropical rainforest List II (Climate) 1. Hot and humid 2. Arid and dry 3. Cold and dry

  1. A-2, B-1, C-3
  2. A-2, B-3, C-1
  3. A-1, B-3, C-2
  4. A-3, B-2, C-1

Answer: A-2, B-3, C-1

Explanation: Hot deserts are arid, the tundra is cold and dry and tropical rainforests are hot and humid.

43. Which layer of the Earth is known as the 'lithosphere'?

  1. Crust and Upper Mantle
  2. Mantle
  3. Core
  4. Outer Core

Answer: Crust and Upper Mantle

Explanation: The lithosphere is the rigid outer part of the Earth, consisting of the crust and the upper mantle.

44. The semi-molten layer beneath the lithosphere is called the:

  1. Asthenosphere
  2. Mesosphere
  3. Exosphere
  4. Thermosphere

Answer: Asthenosphere

Explanation: The asthenosphere is a highly viscous, mechanically weak and ductile region of the upper mantle of Earth.

45. What is the primary source of heat within the Earth's interior?

  1. Radioactive decay
  2. Solar radiation
  3. Frictional heat from tectonic plates
  4. Gravitational compression

Answer: Radioactive decay

Explanation: Radioactive decay of elements like uranium, thorium, and potassium generates significant heat within the Earth's core and mantle.

46. Which of the following is NOT a major landform?

  1. Mountain
  2. Plateau
  3. Ocean
  4. Plain

Answer: Ocean

Explanation: Oceans are large bodies of saltwater, not considered major landforms which are features of the Earth's surface.

47. The process by which rocks are broken down into smaller pieces is called:

  1. Weathering
  2. Erosion
  3. Deposition
  4. Volcanism

Answer: Weathering

Explanation: Weathering is the disintegration or decomposition of rocks at or near the Earth's surface.

48. Assertion: The Earth's core is primarily composed of iron and nickel. Reason: These elements are dense and were concentrated towards the center during Earth's formation.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but the reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but the reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.

Explanation: Iron and nickel are dense metals that sank to the Earth's core during its formation, making both the assertion and reason true and the reason explanatory.

49. Assertion: Fold mountains are formed by the compressional forces of tectonic plates. Reason: When tectonic plates collide, the crust buckles and folds.

  1. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
  2. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
  3. Assertion is true, but the reason is false.
  4. Assertion is false, but the reason is true.

Answer: Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.

Explanation: Fold mountains are indeed formed by compressional forces during plate collisions, causing the crust to buckle and fold.

50. The innermost layer of the Earth is the:

  1. Core
  2. Mantle
  3. Crust
  4. Lithosphere

Answer: Core

Explanation: The core is the innermost layer of the Earth, divided into the inner core and outer core.
