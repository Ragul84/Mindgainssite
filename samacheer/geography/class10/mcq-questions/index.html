26. Consider these statements: 1. Tamil Nadu is a leader in automobile production. 2. Chennai is called the 'Detroit of Asia'. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Chennai's massive auto industry earns it the title 'Detroit of Asia'.

27. Regarding agriculture: 1. Millets are grown in dry areas. 2. Ragi is a common millet in TN. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Millets like Ragi are drought-resistant and grown in dry regions.

28. Regarding minerals: 1. Garnet is found in the beach sands. 2. Ilmenite is also found in beach sands. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Beach sands of TN are rich in minerals like Garnet and Ilmenite.

29. Regarding the economy: 1. Agriculture provides employment to many. 2. The service sector is growing rapidly. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Both agriculture and the service sector are pillars of the state economy.

30. Assertion: Chennai is called the Detroit of Asia. Reason: It has the highest concentration of automobile industries in India.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: The high density of car manufacturers justifies the nickname.

31. Assertion: Thanjavur is the rice bowl of Tamil Nadu. Reason: It has fertile alluvial soil and abundant water from the Cauvery.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: Alluvial soil and water availability make it ideal for paddy.

32. Assertion: Lignite is called brown coal. Reason: It is a low-grade coal with high moisture content.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
  3. A is true, R is false
  4. A is false, R is true

Answer: Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation

Explanation: Lignite's color and composition define it as brown coal.

33. The city of Tiruppur is the leading center for the knitwear industry.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Tiruppur is globally recognized for knitwear exports.

34. Tamil Nadu has no significant deposits of Lignite.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Tamil Nadu has huge lignite deposits in Neyveli.

35. The Cauvery delta is the most fertile region of the state.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: The delta's alluvial soil makes it the most fertile region.

36. Tuticorin is a major port for the export of salt.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: True

Explanation: Tuticorin is a key port for salt and other minerals.

37. Match List I with List II: List I (City) A. Coimbatore B. Chennai C. Tiruppur D. Erode E. Sivakasi List II (Known for) 1. Knitwear 2. Automobiles 3. Fireworks 4. Turmeric 5. Textiles

Answer: A-5, B-2, C-1, D-4, E-3

Explanation: Coimbatore is a textile hub, Chennai makes automobiles, Tiruppur knitwear, Erode turmeric and Sivakasi fireworks.

38. Match the mineral to its location.

  1. Neyveli
  2. Salem
  3. Shevaroy Hills
  4. Beach Sands
  5. Nilgiris

Explanation: These are the primary locations for these minerals in TN.

39. Match the crop to its leading region.

  1. Thanjavur
  2. Pollachi
  3. Cauvery Delta
  4. Nilgiris
  5. Kanyakumari

Explanation: These regions are the primary producers of these crops.

40. Match the port to its characteristic.

  1. Artificial Port
  2. Deep Water Port
  3. Corporate Port
  4. Small Port
  5. Fishing Port

Explanation: Each port serves a distinct functional role.

41. Match List I with List II: List I (City) A. Coimbatore B. Chennai C. Thanjavur D. Tiruppur E. Sivakasi List II (Nickname) 1. Detroit of Asia 2. Knitwear Capital 3. Firecracker Hub 4. Manchester of South India 5. Rice Bowl of TN

  1. A-4, B-1, C-5, D-2, E-3
  2. A-5, B-1, C-4, D-2, E-3

Explanation: Coimbatore is the Manchester of South India, Chennai the Detroit of Asia, Thanjavur the rice bowl of Tamil Nadu, Tiruppur the knitwear capital and Sivakasi the firecracker hub.

42. Which of the following is the highest peak in South India?

  1. Doddabetta
  2. Anamudi
  3. Mahendragiri
  4. Kalsubai

Answer: Anamudi

Explanation: Anamudi is the highest peak in the Western Ghats and South India.

43. The standard meridian of India passes through which city?

  1. Mirzapur
  2. Patna
  3. Lucknow
  4. Jaipur

Answer: Mirzapur

Explanation: The 82°30' E longitude passes through Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh.

44. Which river is known as the 'Dakshin Ganga'?

  1. Krishna
  2. Cauvery
  3. Godavari
  4. Mahanadi

Answer: Godavari

Explanation: Godavari is the longest river in Peninsular India and is called Dakshin Ganga.

45. The 'Doab' region refers to the land between:

  1. Two mountains
  2. Two rivers
  3. Two seas
  4. Two forests

Answer: Two rivers

Explanation: Doab is a Persian word meaning 'two waters', referring to the land between two converging rivers.

46. Which soil is most suitable for the cultivation of cotton?

  1. Alluvial Soil
  2. Red Soil
  3. Black Soil
  4. Laterite Soil

Answer: Black Soil

Explanation: Black soil, also known as Regur soil, is ideal for cotton cultivation.

47. The Palk Strait separates India from which country?

  1. Maldives
  2. Sri Lanka
  3. Myanmar
  4. Bangladesh

Answer: Sri Lanka

Explanation: The Palk Strait and Gulf of Mannar separate India and Sri Lanka.

48. Which of the following is a tributary of the Indus river?

  1. Yamuna
  2. Son
  3. Chenab
  4. Damodar

Answer: Chenab

Explanation: Chenab, Jhelum, Ravi, Beas, and Sutlej are the main tributaries of the Indus.

49. The 'Western Disturbances' originate from which sea?

  1. Arabian Sea
  2. Bay of Bengal
  3. Mediterranean Sea
  4. Caspian Sea

Answer: Mediterranean Sea

Explanation: Western disturbances are extratropical storms originating from the Mediterranean region.

50. Consider the following statements: 1. The Himalayas are young fold mountains. 2. The Aravallis are the oldest fold mountains in India. Which is correct?

  1. Only 1
  2. Only 2
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: Both 1 and 2

Explanation: Himalayas are young fold mountains; Aravallis are among the oldest.
