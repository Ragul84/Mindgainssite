26. What is the formula for the surface area of a sphere?

  1. 4πr^2
  2. 4πr
  3. 2πr^2
  4. 2πr

Answer: 4πr^2

Explanation: The surface area of a sphere is given by the formula A = 4πr^2, where r is the radius. This formula is used to calculate the surface area of a sphere when the radius is known.

27. A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is the average speed of the car?

  1. 25 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 60 km/h
  4. 70 km/h

Answer: 50 km/h

Explanation: The average speed of the car is the total distance traveled divided by the total time taken. So, the average speed is 250/5 = 50 km/h.

43. A cylinder has a radius of 4 cm and a height of 10 cm, what is its volume?

  1. 160π
  2. 200π
  3. 320π
  4. 400π

Answer: 160π

Explanation: The formula for the volume of a cylinder is πr^2h, where r is the radius and h is the height, so the volume is π * 4^2 * 10 = 160π cubic cm. This is a basic calculation in mensuration.

44. A survey of 20 students found that 8 liked football, 10 liked cricket, and 2 liked both, how many students liked either football or cricket?

  1. 14
  2. 16
  3. 18
  4. 20

Answer: 18

Explanation: To find the number of students who liked either football or cricket, we add the number who liked football and the number who liked cricket, then subtract the number who liked both to avoid double counting, giving 8 + 10 - 2 = 16. This is a basic principle of set theory in statistics.

45. The perimeter of a rectangle is 20 cm and its length is 8 cm, what is its breadth?

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 4

Explanation: The perimeter of a rectangle is given by 2(length + breadth), so we can rearrange this formula to find the breadth, which is (20/2 - 8) = 2 cm. This is a basic geometric calculation.

46. A cone has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 8 cm, what is its slant height?

  1. 5
  2. 7
  3. 8
  4. 10

Answer: 8

Explanation: The slant height of a cone can be found using the Pythagorean theorem, which gives the slant height as the square root of the radius squared plus the height squared, so the slant height is sqrt(3^2 + 8^2) = sqrt(73) ≈ 8.55, but among the given options, the closest is 7, but actually it is not among the options, however, using Pythagoras theorem we get √(3² + 8²) = √(9+64) = √73 = 8.544, hence the correct answer should be 8, but since it is not the exact value, we will consider 8 as a possible answer, hence option 8 is considered as the answer.
