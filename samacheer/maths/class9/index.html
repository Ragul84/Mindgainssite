Class9 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class9 questions to revise the Samacheer Mathematics syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 368 questions.

1. If (x + 2) is a factor of x^2 + kx + 6, what is the value of k?

  1. 5
  2. 7
  3. -5
  4. -7

Answer: 5

Explanation: Substituting x = -2 gives (-2)^2 + k(-2) + 6 = 0, so 4 - 2k + 6 = 0, which means 2k = 10, k = 5.

2. What is the value of (a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2?

  1. 2(a^2 + b^2)
  2. 4ab
  3. 2ab
  4. 0

Answer: 4ab

Explanation: Expanding the terms gives (a^2 + 2ab + b^2) - (a^2 - 2ab + b^2) = 4ab.

3. The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm is:

  1. 60 sq cm
  2. 30 sq cm
  3. 16 sq cm
  4. 20 sq cm

Answer: 30 sq cm

Explanation: Area = 1/2 * base * height = 1/2 * 10 * 6 = 30 sq cm.

4. If the radius of a circle is 7 cm, what is its circumference? (Use π = 22/7)

  1. 22 cm
  2. 44 cm
  3. 88 cm
  4. 154 cm

Answer: 44 cm

Explanation: Circumference = 2πr = 2 * 22/7 * 7 = 44 cm.

5. The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is:

  1. 180°
  2. 270°
  3. 360°
  4. 540°

Answer: 360°

Explanation: The sum of interior angles of any quadrilateral is always 360°.

6. What is the value of sin 30°?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 1/2
  4. √3/2

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: According to the standard trigonometric table, sin 30° = 1/2.

7. The mean of the first five natural numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) is:

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 3

Explanation: Mean = (1+2+3+4+5)/5 = 15/5 = 3.

8. If 2x + 5 = 15, then the value of x is:

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 20
  4. 15

Answer: 5

Explanation: 2x = 15 - 5 = 10, so x = 10/2 = 5.

9. The volume of a cube with side 3 cm is:

  1. 9 cu cm
  2. 18 cu cm
  3. 27 cu cm
  4. 81 cu cm

Answer: 27 cu cm

Explanation: Volume = side^3 = 3^3 = 27 cu cm.

10. What is the value of (x^0) where x is any non-zero real number?

  1. 0
  2. x
  3. 1
  4. -1

Answer: 1

Explanation: Any non-zero number raised to the power of 0 is always 1.

11. In a right-angled triangle, if the base is 3 cm and height is 4 cm, the hypotenuse is:

  1. 5 cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 7 cm
  4. 8 cm

Answer: 5 cm

Explanation: By Pythagoras theorem, hypotenuse = √(3^2 + 4^2) = √25 = 5 cm.

12. The product of (x + 3)(x - 3) is:

  1. x^2 + 9
  2. x^2 - 6
  3. x^2 - 9
  4. x^2 + 6x + 9

Answer: x^2 - 9

Explanation: Using the identity (a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2, we get x^2 - 3^2 = x^2 - 9.

13. The mode of the data set {2, 4, 4, 6, 8, 4, 10} is:

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 4

Explanation: Mode is the value that appears most frequently, which is 4.

14. The total surface area of a sphere with radius 'r' is:

  1. 2πr^2
  2. 3πr^2
  3. 4πr^2
  4. πr^2

Answer: 4πr^2

Explanation: The formula for the surface area of a sphere is 4πr^2.

15. If the complementary angle of 40° is x, then x is:

  1. 50°
  2. 140°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°

Answer: 50°

Explanation: Complementary angles sum to 90°, so x = 90° - 40° = 50°.

16. What is the value of x in the equation 2x + 5 = 11?

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 3

Explanation: To find the value of x, we need to isolate x on one side of the equation. Subtracting 5 from both sides and then dividing by 2 gives x = 3.

17. In a triangle, if one angle is 60 degrees and another angle is 80 degrees, what is the measure of the third angle?

  1. 40
  2. 50
  3. 60
  4. 70

Answer: 40

Explanation: The sum of the angles in a triangle is always 180 degrees. So, the third angle is 180 - (60 + 80) = 40 degrees.

18. What is the formula for the area of a circle?

  1. πr
  2. 2πr
  3. πr^2
  4. πd

Answer: πr^2

Explanation: The area of a circle is given by the formula A = πr^2, where r is the radius of the circle. This formula is used to calculate the area of a circle when the radius is known.

19. A box contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

  1. 3/5
  2. 3/8
  3. 5/8
  4. 2/3

Answer: 5/8

Explanation: The probability of drawing a red ball is the number of red balls divided by the total number of balls. So, the probability is 5/8.

20. What is the value of sin(30 degrees)?

  1. 1/2
  2. 1/3
  3. 2/3
  4. 3/4

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: The sine of 30 degrees is 1/2. This is a basic trigonometric value that is commonly used.

21. The perimeter of a rectangle is 20 cm. If the length is 6 cm, what is the width?

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 4

Explanation: The perimeter of a rectangle is given by the formula P = 2(l + w), where l is the length and w is the width. So, 20 = 2(6 + w), which gives w = 4.

22. What is the formula for the volume of a cylinder?

  1. πr^2h
  2. 2πrh
  3. πr^2
  4. 2πr

Answer: πr^2h

Explanation: The volume of a cylinder is given by the formula V = πr^2h, where r is the radius and h is the height. This formula is used to calculate the volume of a cylinder when the radius and height are known.

23. A survey of 20 people found that 12 people liked coffee and 8 people liked tea. What is the percentage of people who liked coffee?

  1. 40%
  2. 50%
  3. 60%
  4. 70%

Answer: 60%

Explanation: The percentage of people who liked coffee is the number of people who liked coffee divided by the total number of people, multiplied by 100. So, the percentage is (12/20) * 100 = 60%.

24. What is the value of cos(60 degrees)?

  1. 1/2
  2. 1/3
  3. 2/3
  4. 3/4

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: The cosine of 60 degrees is 1/2. This is a basic trigonometric value that is commonly used.

25. The area of a triangle is 24 square cm. If the base is 6 cm, what is the height?

  1. 4
  2. 6
  3. 8
  4. 10

Answer: 6

Explanation: The area of a triangle is given by the formula A = (1/2)bh, where b is the base and h is the height. So, 24 = (1/2)(6)h, which gives h = 8.
