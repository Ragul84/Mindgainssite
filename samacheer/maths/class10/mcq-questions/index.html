26. In a right-angled triangle, if the base is 3 cm and height is 4 cm, the hypotenuse is:

  1. 5 cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 7 cm
  4. 8 cm

Answer: 5 cm

Explanation: By Pythagoras theorem: √(3² + 4²) = 5.

27. The mode of the data set 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 3, 2 is:

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 3

Explanation: The number 3 appears most frequently.

28. The value of cos 60° is:

  1. 0
  2. 1/2
  3. √3/2
  4. 1

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: Based on the standard trigonometric table, cos 60° = 1/2.

29. The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 5 cm is:

  1. 15 sq cm
  2. 25 sq cm
  3. 50 sq cm
  4. 100 sq cm

Answer: 25 sq cm

Explanation: Area = 1/2 * base * height = 1/2 * 10 * 5 = 25.

30. What is the discriminant (Δ) of the equation x² - 4x + 4 = 0?

  1. 0
  2. 4
  3. 8
  4. 16

Answer: 0

Explanation: Δ = b² - 4ac = (-4)² - 4(1)(4) = 16 - 16 = 0.

31. If x + 2y = 10 and x + y = 6, find x

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 4

Explanation: Subtract the second equation from the first to get y = 4, then substitute y into the second equation to get x = 2.

32. What is the formula for the area of a triangle?

  1. 1/2 bh
  2. 1/2 bh^2
  3. 2bh
  4. bh

Answer: 1/2 bh

Explanation: The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and height.

33. A box contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

  1. 1/2
  2. 3/5
  3. 2/3
  4. 5/8

Answer: 3/5

Explanation: There are 5 red balls out of a total of 8 balls.

34. What is the value of sin(30°)?

  1. 1/2
  2. 1/3
  3. 2/3
  4. 3/4

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: The sine of 30° is half.

35. The diameter of a circle is 14 cm. What is its radius?

  1. 6 cm
  2. 7 cm
  3. 6.5 cm
  4. 7.5 cm

Answer: 7 cm

Explanation: The radius is half the diameter.

36. A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?

  1. 45 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 55 km/h
  4. 60 km/h

Answer: 50 km/h

Explanation: Average speed is total distance divided by total time.

37. What is the formula for the volume of a cylinder?

  1. πr^2 h
  2. 2πrh
  3. πr^2
  4. 2πr

Answer: πr^2 h

Explanation: The volume of a cylinder is the product of the area of its base and its height.

38. A shop sells 250 units of a product per day. If the product is sold for ₹50 per unit, what is the daily revenue?

  1. ₹10000
  2. ₹12000
  3. ₹12500
  4. ₹15000

Answer: ₹10000

Explanation: Daily revenue is the product of the number of units sold and the price per unit.

39. What is the value of cos(60°)?

  1. 1/2
  2. 1/3
  3. 2/3
  4. 3/4

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: The cosine of 60° is half.

40. The length of a rectangle is 8 cm and its width is 5 cm. What is its area?

  1. 35 cm^2
  2. 40 cm^2
  3. 30 cm^2
  4. 45 cm^2

Answer: 40 cm^2

Explanation: The area of a rectangle is the product of its length and width.

41. A water tank can hold 1200 litres of water. If 3/4 of the tank is filled, how much water is in the tank?

  1. 800 litres
  2. 900 litres
  3. 1000 litres
  4. 1100 litres

Answer: 900 litres

Explanation: 3/4 of 1200 litres is 900 litres.

42. What is the formula for the perimeter of a triangle?

  1. a + b + c
  2. a + b - c
  3. a - b + c
  4. a - b - c

Answer: a + b + c

Explanation: The perimeter of a triangle is the sum of its side lengths.

43. The area of a triangle is 24 cm^2 and its base is 8 cm. What is its height?

  1. 4 cm
  2. 5 cm
  3. 6 cm
  4. 8 cm

Answer: 6 cm

Explanation: The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and height.

44. A man invests ₹1000 at an interest rate of 5% per annum. What is the interest earned after 1 year?

  1. ₹45
  2. ₹50
  3. ₹55
  4. ₹60

Answer: ₹50

Explanation: Interest earned is the product of the principal, interest rate, and time.

45. If x + 2y = 10 and x + y = 6, find the value of x.

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 4

Explanation: Subtract the second equation from the first to get y = 4, then substitute y into the second equation to find x = 2.

46. In a triangle, if one angle is 60 degrees and another angle is 80 degrees, find the third angle.

  1. 20
  2. 30
  3. 40
  4. 50

Answer: 40

Explanation: The sum of angles in a triangle is 180 degrees, so the third angle is 180 - 60 - 80 = 40 degrees.

47. The area of a rectangle is 48 square cm and its length is 8 cm. Find its breadth.

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 6

Explanation: Area of a rectangle is length times breadth, so breadth = area / length = 48 / 8 = 6 cm.

48. The mean of 5 numbers is 12. Find their sum.

  1. 50
  2. 55
  3. 60
  4. 65

Answer: 60

Explanation: Mean is the sum divided by the count, so sum = mean * count = 12 * 5 = 60.

49. In a right triangle, if the hypotenuse is 10 cm and one side is 6 cm, find the other side using Pythagoras theorem.

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 10
  4. 12

Answer: 8

Explanation: Pythagoras theorem states that the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides, so the other side is sqrt(10^2 - 6^2) = sqrt(100 - 36) = sqrt(64) = 8 cm.

50. The volume of a cube is 64 cubic cm. Find the length of its side.

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 4

Explanation: Volume of a cube is the cube of its side length, so side length = cube root of volume = cube root of 64 = 4 cm.
