Class8 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class8 questions to revise the Samacheer Mathematics syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 504 questions.

1. What is the value of (x + 3)(x - 3)?

  1. x^2 + 9
  2. x^2 - 9
  3. x^2 - 6
  4. x^2 + 6x + 9

Answer: x^2 - 9

Explanation: Using the identity (a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2.

2. Find the value of 2^0 + 3^0 + 4^0.

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 3
  4. 9

Answer: 3

Explanation: Any non-zero number raised to the power 0 is 1. So, 1+1+1 = 3.

3. If 3x - 5 = 10, what is the value of x?

  1. 3
  2. 5
  3. 15
  4. 2

Answer: 5

Explanation: 3x = 15, so x = 15/3 = 5.

4. What is the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?

  1. 180 degrees
  2. 270 degrees
  3. 360 degrees
  4. 540 degrees

Answer: 360 degrees

Explanation: The sum of interior angles of any quadrilateral is always 360 degrees.

5. Find the area of a square with side 6 cm.

  1. 12 sq cm
  2. 24 sq cm
  3. 36 sq cm
  4. 48 sq cm

Answer: 36 sq cm

Explanation: Area of square = side x side = 6 x 6 = 36.

6. What is the formula for the area of a triangle?

  1. Length x Breadth
  2. 1/2 x Base x Height
  3. Side x Side
  4. 2(L+B)

Answer: 1/2 x Base x Height

Explanation: The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and height.

7. What is the additive inverse of -7?

  1. -7
  2. 0
  3. 1/7
  4. 7

Answer: 7

Explanation: The additive inverse of a number 'a' is '-a'.

8. If the radius of a circle is 7 cm, what is its circumference? (Use pi = 22/7)

  1. 22 cm
  2. 44 cm
  3. 88 cm
  4. 154 cm

Answer: 44 cm

Explanation: Circumference = 2 * pi * r = 2 * 22/7 * 7 = 44.

9. What is the value of (-1)^10?

  1. -1
  2. 1
  3. 10
  4. -10

Answer: 1

Explanation: A negative number raised to an even power is positive.

10. Find the mean of the numbers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 7

Answer: 6

Explanation: Mean = (2+4+6+8+10)/5 = 30/5 = 6.

11. What is the product of (2x)(3x)?

  1. 5x
  2. 6x
  3. 5x^2
  4. 6x^2

Answer: 6x^2

Explanation: 2 * 3 = 6 and x * x = x^2.

12. The perimeter of a rectangle is 20 cm. If length is 6 cm, find the breadth.

  1. 4 cm
  2. 14 cm
  3. 7 cm
  4. 8 cm

Answer: 4 cm

Explanation: 2(6 + b) = 20; 6 + b = 10; b = 4.

13. What is the square root of 144?

  1. 10
  2. 11
  3. 12
  4. 14

Answer: 12

Explanation: 12 * 12 = 144.

14. What is the value of x in the equation x/2 = 5?

  1. 2.5
  2. 7
  3. 10
  4. 15

Answer: 10

Explanation: x = 5 * 2 = 10.

15. Which of the following is a perfect square?

  1. 20
  2. 25
  3. 30
  4. 35

Answer: 25

Explanation: 5 * 5 = 25, which is a perfect square.

16. The sum of three consecutive even numbers is 48. What is the first number?

  1. 12
  2. 14
  3. 16
  4. 18

Answer: 14

Explanation: Let the numbers be x, x + 2 and x + 4. Then 3x + 6 = 48, giving x = 14.

17. The parallel sides of a trapezium are 10 cm and 16 cm. If the distance between them is 8 cm, its area is

  1. 104 cm²
  2. 208 cm²
  3. 80 cm²
  4. 160 cm²

Answer: 104 cm²

Explanation: Area of trapezium = 1/2 × height × (sum of parallel sides) = 1/2 × 8 × 26 = 104 cm².

18. Find the volume of a cube whose edge measures 5 cm.

  1. 25 cm³
  2. 100 cm³
  3. 125 cm³
  4. 150 cm³

Answer: 125 cm³

Explanation: Volume of a cube = side³ = 5³ = 125 cm³.

19. The heights (in cm) of 5 students are 140, 145, 150, 142 and 148. What is their mean height?

  1. 142 cm
  2. 145 cm
  3. 148 cm
  4. 150 cm

Answer: 145 cm

Explanation: Mean = sum of observations / number of observations = 725 / 5 = 145 cm.

20. The marks scored in 5 tests are 78, 82, 78, 85 and 78. What is the mode?

  1. 82
  2. 85
  3. 78
  4. 80

Answer: 78

Explanation: Mode is the value that appears most frequently in the data.

21. In ΔABC and ΔDEF, AB = DE, BC = EF and ∠B = ∠E. Which congruency rule applies?

  1. SSS
  2. SAS
  3. ASA
  4. RHS

Answer: SAS

Explanation: Two sides and the included angle are equal, so the SAS rule applies.

22. The value of (102)² using identity is

  1. 10404
  2. 10204
  3. 10004
  4. 12404

Answer: 10404

Explanation: (100 + 2)² = 10000 + 400 + 4 = 10404.

23. The value of (3⁴ × 3²) ÷ 3³ is

  1. 9
  2. 27
  3. 81
  4. 243

Answer: 27

Explanation: 3⁴ × 3² = 3⁶ and 3⁶ ÷ 3³ = 3³ = 27.

24. The diagonals of a rhombus are 12 cm and 16 cm. Its area is

  1. 96 cm²
  2. 192 cm²
  3. 56 cm²
  4. 84 cm²

Answer: 96 cm²

Explanation: Area of rhombus = 1/2 × d1 × d2 = 1/2 × 12 × 16 = 96 cm².

25. The factors of x² - 25 are

  1. (x-5)(x-5)
  2. (x+5)(x+5)
  3. (x-5)(x+5)
  4. (x-25)(x+1)

Answer: (x-5)(x+5)

Explanation: Using a² - b² = (a-b)(a+b), we get (x-5)(x+5).
