26. The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is

  1. 180°
  2. 270°
  3. 360°
  4. 540°

Answer: 360°

Explanation: Sum of interior angles = (n - 2) × 180° = (4 - 2) × 180° = 360°.

27. A shopkeeper buys a pen for ₹15 and sells it for ₹18. Find the profit percentage.

  1. 15%
  2. 18%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Profit % = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100 = (3 / 15) × 100 = 20%.

28. In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite to angle θ is 6 cm and the hypotenuse is 10 cm. Then sin θ is

  1. 3/5
  2. 4/5
  3. 3/4
  4. 5/3

Answer: 3/5

Explanation: sin θ = opposite side / hypotenuse = 6 / 10 = 3 / 5.

29. A cuboid has length 6 cm, breadth 4 cm and height 3 cm. Its total surface area is

  1. 72 cm²
  2. 108 cm²
  3. 144 cm²
  4. 84 cm²

Answer: 108 cm²

Explanation: TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(24 + 12 + 18) = 108 cm².

30. The simple interest on ₹5000 at 8% per annum for 2 years is

  1. ₹400
  2. ₹600
  3. ₹800
  4. ₹1000

Answer: ₹800

Explanation: SI = (P × R × T) / 100 = (5000 × 8 × 2) / 100 = ₹800.

31. What is the value of x if 2x + 5 = 15?

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 20
  4. 15

Answer: 5

Explanation: 2x = 15 - 5, so 2x = 10, x = 5.

32. The sum of the interior angles of a triangle is:

  1. 90°
  2. 180°
  3. 270°
  4. 360°

Answer: 180°

Explanation: The sum of angles in any triangle is always 180 degrees.

33. What is the area of a rectangle with length 6 cm and width 4 cm?

  1. 10 sq cm
  2. 20 sq cm
  3. 24 sq cm
  4. 12 sq cm

Answer: 24 sq cm

Explanation: Area = length × width = 6 × 4 = 24.

34. Simplify the expression: 3a + 4a - 2a

  1. 5a
  2. 7a
  3. 9a
  4. 3a

Answer: 5a

Explanation: 3 + 4 - 2 = 5, so the result is 5a.

35. What is the square of 12?

  1. 24
  2. 122
  3. 144
  4. 169

Answer: 144

Explanation: 12 × 12 = 144.

36. The perimeter of a square with side 5 cm is:

  1. 10 cm
  2. 15 cm
  3. 20 cm
  4. 25 cm

Answer: 20 cm

Explanation: Perimeter = 4 × side = 4 × 5 = 20.

37. What is the value of (-3) × (-4)?

  1. -12
  2. 12
  3. -7
  4. 7

Answer: 12

Explanation: Product of two negative numbers is positive.

38. If the radius of a circle is 7 cm, what is its diameter?

  1. 3.5 cm
  2. 7 cm
  3. 14 cm
  4. 21 cm

Answer: 14 cm

Explanation: Diameter = 2 × radius = 2 × 7 = 14.

39. What is the mean of the numbers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 7

Answer: 6

Explanation: Sum is 30, divided by 5 is 6.

40. The value of x in the equation x/3 = 4 is:

  1. 1
  2. 7
  3. 12
  4. 1.33

Answer: 12

Explanation: x = 4 × 3 = 12.

41. What is the cube of 3?

  1. 9
  2. 18
  3. 27
  4. 81

Answer: 27

Explanation: 3 × 3 × 3 = 27.

42. An angle measuring 90° is called a:

  1. Acute angle
  2. Obtuse angle
  3. Right angle
  4. Reflex angle

Answer: Right angle

Explanation: A 90-degree angle is defined as a right angle.

43. The formula for the area of a triangle is:

  1. l × w
  2. 1/2 × b × h
  3. 2(l + w)
  4. s²

Answer: 1/2 × b × h

Explanation: Area of a triangle is half the base times height.

44. What is the value of 5⁰?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 5
  4. 10

Answer: 1

Explanation: Any non-zero number raised to the power of 0 is 1.

45. If x + 2 = 7, then the value of x is

  1. 3
  2. 5
  3. 7
  4. 9

Answer: 5

Explanation: Subtract 2 from both sides of the equation to find x.

46. The perimeter of a rectangle with length 8 cm and breadth 5 cm is

  1. 18 cm
  2. 20 cm
  3. 26 cm
  4. 30 cm

Answer: 26 cm

Explanation: Perimeter = 2(length + breadth)

47. What is the value of x in the equation 2x + 5 = 11?

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 3

Explanation: Subtract 5 from both sides and divide by 2 to find x.

48. The area of a triangle with base 6 cm and height 8 cm is

  1. 12 sq cm
  2. 16 sq cm
  3. 20 sq cm
  4. 24 sq cm

Answer: 24 sq cm

Explanation: Area = 0.5 * base * height

49. If the cost of 5 pens is Rs. 15, then the cost of 1 pen is

  1. Rs. 1
  2. Rs. 2
  3. Rs. 3
  4. Rs. 5

Answer: Rs. 2

Explanation: Divide the total cost by the number of pens.

50. The volume of a cube with side 6 cm is

  1. 120 cu cm
  2. 180 cu cm
  3. 216 cu cm
  4. 240 cu cm

Answer: 216 cu cm

Explanation: Volume = side^3
