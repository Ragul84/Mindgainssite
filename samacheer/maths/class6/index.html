Class6 MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Class6 questions to revise the Samacheer Mathematics syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 494 questions.

1. A line segment has a length of 5 cm. If it is extended by 2 cm, what is the new length?

  1. 5 cm
  2. 7 cm
  3. 3 cm
  4. 10 cm

Answer: 7 cm

Explanation: Add the extension to the original length to find the new length.

2. What is the sum of the interior angles of a triangle?

  1. 180 degrees
  2. 360 degrees
  3. 90 degrees
  4. 270 degrees

Answer: 180 degrees

Explanation: The sum of interior angles in a triangle is always 180 degrees.

3. A bookshelf has 5 shelves, and each shelf can hold 8 books. How many books can the bookshelf hold in total?

  1. 20 books
  2. 30 books
  3. 40 books
  4. 50 books

Answer: 40 books

Explanation: Multiply the number of shelves by the number of books each shelf can hold.

4. What is the formula for the area of a rectangle?

  1. length + width
  2. length - width
  3. length * width
  4. length / width

Answer: length * width

Explanation: The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length and width.

5. A pencil is 15 cm long. If it is divided into 5 parts, how long is each part?

  1. 1 cm
  2. 2 cm
  3. 3 cm
  4. 5 cm

Answer: 3 cm

Explanation: Divide the total length by the number of parts to find the length of each part.

6. What is the perimeter of a square with a side length of 4 cm?

  1. 8 cm
  2. 12 cm
  3. 16 cm
  4. 20 cm

Answer: 16 cm

Explanation: The perimeter of a square is found by multiplying the side length by 4.

7. A water tank can hold 240 liters of water. If 120 liters of water are already in the tank, what percentage of the tank is filled?

  1. 25%
  2. 50%
  3. 75%
  4. 100%

Answer: 50%

Explanation: Calculate the percentage by dividing the amount of water in the tank by the total capacity and multiplying by 100.

8. What is the formula for the volume of a cube?

  1. side^2
  2. side^3
  3. side * 2
  4. side / 2

Answer: side^3

Explanation: The volume of a cube is calculated by cubing the length of its side.

9. A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. How many kilometers does it travel per hour?

  1. 25 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 75 km/h
  4. 100 km/h

Answer: 50 km/h

Explanation: Divide the total distance by the total time to find the speed.

10. What is the sum of the exterior angles of any polygon?

  1. 180 degrees
  2. 360 degrees
  3. 90 degrees
  4. 270 degrees

Answer: 360 degrees

Explanation: The sum of exterior angles of any polygon is always 360 degrees.

11. A bakery sells 250 loaves of bread per day. If each loaf costs $2, how much money does the bakery make in a day?

  1. $200
  2. $400
  3. $500
  4. $1000

Answer: $500

Explanation: Multiply the number of loaves sold by the cost per loaf to find the total amount of money made.

12. What is the formula for the area of a triangle?

  1. base + height
  2. base - height
  3. 0.5 * base * height
  4. base / height

Answer: 0.5 * base * height

Explanation: The area of a triangle is calculated by multiplying the base and height and dividing by 2.

13. A group of friends want to share some candy equally. If they have 48 pieces of candy and there are 8 friends, how many pieces of candy will each friend get?

  1. 4 pieces
  2. 6 pieces
  3. 8 pieces
  4. 12 pieces

Answer: 6 pieces

Explanation: Divide the total number of candies by the number of friends to find out how many each friend gets.

14. A bicycle travels 10 km in 2 hours. How many kilometers does it travel per hour?

  1. 2 km/h
  2. 5 km/h
  3. 8 km/h
  4. 10 km/h

Answer: 5 km/h

Explanation: Divide the total distance by the total time to find the speed.

15. What is the formula for the perimeter of a rectangle?

  1. 2 * length + 2 * width
  2. 2 * length - 2 * width
  3. length + width
  4. length - width

Answer: 2 * length + 2 * width

Explanation: The perimeter of a rectangle is calculated by adding the lengths of all sides, which is the same as 2 times the length plus 2 times the width.

16. A line segment has a length of 5 cm. If it is extended by 2 cm on one side, what is the new length?

  1. 5 cm
  2. 7 cm
  3. 3 cm
  4. 10 cm

Answer: 7 cm

Explanation: Add 2 cm to the original length of 5 cm to get 7 cm.

17. A cube has an edge length of 4 cm. What is its surface area?

  1. 48 cm^2
  2. 64 cm^2
  3. 16 cm^2
  4. 32 cm^2

Answer: 48 cm^2

Explanation: Surface area of a cube is 6 times the square of edge length.

18. A water tank can hold 1200 litres of water. If 300 litres of water are already in it, what percentage is filled?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 40%

Answer: 25%

Explanation: 300 litres is one-fourth or 25% of 1200 litres.

19. What is the perimeter of a square with side length 6 cm?

  1. 12 cm
  2. 18 cm
  3. 24 cm
  4. 36 cm

Answer: 24 cm

Explanation: Perimeter of a square is 4 times its side length.

20. If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 toys, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 toys?

  1. 5 minutes
  2. 50 minutes
  3. 100 minutes
  4. 500 minutes

Answer: 5 minutes

Explanation: The time remains constant as the ratio of machines to toys is the same.

21. The area of a parallelogram with base 5 cm and height 6 cm is?

  1. 25 cm^2
  2. 30 cm^2
  3. 35 cm^2
  4. 40 cm^2

Answer: 30 cm^2

Explanation: Area of a parallelogram is base times height.

22. A car travels 250 km in 5 hours. What is its average speed?

  1. 25 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 60 km/h
  4. 75 km/h

Answer: 50 km/h

Explanation: Average speed is total distance divided by total time.

23. What is the volume of a cube with edge length 6 cm?

  1. 180 cm^3
  2. 216 cm^3
  3. 240 cm^3
  4. 300 cm^3

Answer: 216 cm^3

Explanation: Volume of a cube is the cube of its edge length.

24. A cylinder has a height of 10 cm and a radius of 4 cm. What is its volume?

  1. 160 pi cm^3
  2. 200 pi cm^3
  3. 400 pi cm^3
  4. 512 pi cm^3

Answer: 400 pi cm^3

Explanation: Volume of a cylinder is pi times radius squared times height.

25. The sum of two numbers is 50. If one number is 30, what is the other number?

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 15
  4. 25

Answer: 25

Explanation: Subtract the known number from the sum to find the other number.
