26. Match List I with List II: List I (Method) A. Auto-reduction of the sulphide ore B. Roasting, then reduction with coke C. Reduction in a blast furnace D. Electrolysis of fused alumina List II (Metal) 1. Copper 2. Aluminum 3. Iron 4. Zinc

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2
  3. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  4. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1

Answer: A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2

Explanation: Copper is obtained by auto-reduction, zinc by roasting and carbon reduction, iron in a blast furnace and aluminium by electrolysis (Hall-Héroult).

38. Which of the following statements distinguishes between absorption and adsorption?

  1. Absorption involves the entire volume, adsorption occurs at the surface
  2. Both occur at the surface
  3. Absorption is a physical process, adsorption is chemical

Answer: Absorption involves the entire volume, adsorption occurs at the surface

Explanation: Absorption involves the uptake of substances throughout the volume, while adsorption occurs only at the surface.
