26. Which rule states that electrons occupy degenerate orbitals singly before pairing?

  1. Aufbau principle
  2. Hund's rule
  3. Pauli's exclusion principle
  4. Heisenberg's uncertainty principle

Answer: Hund's rule

Explanation: Hund's rule states electrons fill degenerate orbitals singly first.

27. True or False: The quantum mechanical model of atom provides the exact location of electrons.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Quantum mechanics describes probabilities, not exact locations.

28. Match List I with List II: List I (Principle) A. Pauli exclusion principle B. Hund's rule C. Aufbau principle D. Heisenberg uncertainty principle List II (Statement) 1. No two electrons have identical quantum numbers 2. Electrons occupy degenerate orbitals singly first 3. Uncertainty in position and momentum of electrons 4. Electrons fill orbitals starting from lowest energy

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  3. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  4. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3

Answer: A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3

Explanation: Pauli: no two electrons share all four quantum numbers; Hund: degenerate orbitals fill singly first; Aufbau: lowest energy first; Heisenberg: position and momentum cannot both be known exactly.

29. Which of the following best describes the shape of s orbitals?

  1. Dumbbell-shaped
  2. Spherical
  3. Double dumbbell
  4. Cloverleaf

Answer: Spherical

Explanation: s orbitals are spherical in shape.

30. Which rule states that electrons fill degenerate orbitals singly before pairing?

  1. Pauli's exclusion principle
  2. Hund's rule
  3. Aufbau principle
  4. Heisenberg's uncertainty principle

Answer: Hund's rule

Explanation: Hund's rule states electrons occupy degenerate orbitals singly first.

31. True or False: Schrödinger's wave equation applies only to electrons in hydrogen atoms.

  1. True
  2. False

Answer: False

Explanation: Schrödinger's wave equation is applicable to multi-electron atoms as well.

32. Match List I with List II: List I (Orbital) A. s orbital B. p orbital C. d orbital D. f orbital List II (Shape) 1. Cloverleaf shape 2. Dumbbell shape 3. Spherical shape 4. Complex shape

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  3. A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
  4. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Answer: A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Explanation: s orbitals are spherical, p dumbbell-shaped, most d orbitals cloverleaf-shaped and f orbitals more complex.
