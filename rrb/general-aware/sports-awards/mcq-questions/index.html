1. Which country won the FIFA World Cup 2022?

  1. Brazil
  2. France
  3. Argentina
  4. Germany

Answer: Argentina

Explanation: Argentina won the FIFA World Cup 2022 held in Qatar, defeating France in the final on 18 December 2022. Lionel Messi won the Golden Ball award.

2. The 2024 Paris Olympics will be the ___ edition of the Summer Olympics:

  1. 30th
  2. 31st
  3. 32nd
  4. 33rd

Answer: 33rd

Explanation: The 2024 Paris Olympics (26 July – 11 August 2024) is the 33rd Summer Olympic Games. Paris has hosted the Olympics three times: 1900, 1924, and 2024.

3. India achieved its first T20 World Cup title in 2024 under which captain?

  1. Virat Kohli
  2. Rohit Sharma
  3. KL Rahul
  4. Hardik Pandya

Answer: Rohit Sharma

Explanation: India won the ICC T20 World Cup 2024, defeating South Africa in the final. The team was captained by Rohit Sharma. Virat Kohli won the Player of the Tournament award.
