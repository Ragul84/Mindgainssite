RRB · Maths · Time, Speed & DistanceTry one

Two trains 120 m and 130 m long run towards each other at 40 km/h and 50 km/h. How long do they take to cross?

Relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total length = 120 + 130 = 250 m. Time = 250 ÷ 25 = 10 seconds.
