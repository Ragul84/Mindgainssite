1. What is the chemical symbol for water?

  1. WA
  2. H2O
  3. HO2
  4. OH

Answer: H2O

Explanation: Water is H2O — two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

2. The chemical formula of sodium chloride (common salt) is:

  1. NaCl
  2. Na2Cl
  3. NaCl2
  4. Na2Cl2

Answer: NaCl

Explanation: Sodium chloride (common salt) has the formula NaCl — one sodium (Na) and one chlorine (Cl) atom.

3. The chemical name of 'washing soda' is:

  1. Sodium bicarbonate
  2. Sodium carbonate (Na2CO3·10H2O)
  3. Calcium carbonate
  4. Potassium carbonate

Answer: Sodium carbonate (Na2CO3·10H2O)

Explanation: Washing soda is hydrated sodium carbonate (Na2CO3·10H2O). Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3).

4. Rusting of iron is a chemical process called:

  1. Sublimation
  2. Corrosion (oxidation)
  3. Reduction
  4. Decomposition

Answer: Corrosion (oxidation)

Explanation: Rusting is the corrosion of iron. It is an oxidation reaction: 4Fe + 3O2 + 6H2O → 4Fe(OH)3 (hydrated iron oxide). Moisture and oxygen are needed.

5. Which enzyme in the saliva begins the digestion of starch?

  1. Pepsin
  2. Trypsin
  3. Amylase (Ptyalin)
  4. Lipase

Answer: Amylase (Ptyalin)

Explanation: Salivary amylase (ptyalin) begins the chemical digestion of starch in the mouth, breaking it into maltose.

6. Which organelle is responsible for protein synthesis?

  1. Golgi apparatus
  2. Mitochondria
  3. Ribosome
  4. Endoplasmic reticulum

Answer: Ribosome

Explanation: Ribosomes are the sites of protein synthesis (translation). They read mRNA sequences and assemble amino acids into proteins.
