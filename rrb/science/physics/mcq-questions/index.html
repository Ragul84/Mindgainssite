1. What is the speed of light?

  1. 3 × 10^6 m/s
  2. 3 × 10^8 m/s
  3. 3 × 10^10 m/s
  4. 3 × 10^4 m/s

Answer: 3 × 10^8 m/s

Explanation: The speed of light in vacuum is approximately 3 × 10^8 m/s (300,000 km/s).

2. Which is the hardest natural substance on Earth?

  1. Gold
  2. Iron
  3. Diamond
  4. Granite

Answer: Diamond

Explanation: Diamond is the hardest natural substance with a Mohs hardness of 10. It is a form of carbon with atoms arranged in a crystal lattice.

3. How many bones does an adult human body have?

  1. 104
  2. 186
  3. 206
  4. 312

Answer: 206

Explanation: An adult human skeleton has 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270-300 bones; many fuse together as we grow.

4. Which vitamin is produced in the body by exposure to sunlight?

  1. Vitamin A
  2. Vitamin B12
  3. Vitamin C
  4. Vitamin D

Answer: Vitamin D

Explanation: Vitamin D is synthesized in the skin when exposed to ultraviolet B (UVB) radiation from sunlight. It is essential for bone health.

5. Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?

  1. Oxygen
  2. Carbon dioxide
  3. Argon
  4. Nitrogen

Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen (N2) makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere, followed by oxygen (21%), and argon (0.9%).

6. Malaria is caused by:

  1. Bacteria
  2. Virus
  3. Protozoan (Plasmodium)
  4. Fungus

Answer: Protozoan (Plasmodium)

Explanation: Malaria is caused by Plasmodium parasites, which are protozoa. The parasite is transmitted to humans through the bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.

7. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?

  1. Gold
  2. Lead
  3. Mercury
  4. Iron

Answer: Mercury

Explanation: Mercury (Hg) is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature (melting point: -39°C). It is used in thermometers and barometers.

8. Sound cannot travel through:

  1. Water
  2. Air
  3. Steel
  4. Vacuum

Answer: Vacuum

Explanation: Sound requires a medium (solid, liquid, or gas) to travel. It cannot travel through a vacuum because there are no particles to transmit the vibrations.

9. Newton's Second Law of Motion states that Force = :

  1. Mass × Velocity
  2. Mass × Acceleration
  3. Velocity / Time
  4. Mass × Distance

Answer: Mass × Acceleration

Explanation: Newton's Second Law: F = ma (Force = Mass × Acceleration). Greater force = greater acceleration for the same mass.

10. The phenomenon of light bending when it passes from one medium to another is:

  1. Reflection
  2. Diffraction
  3. Refraction
  4. Dispersion

Answer: Refraction

Explanation: Refraction is the bending of light when it passes from one transparent medium to another with different optical density (e.g., from air to glass).

11. The 'Ohm's Law' states that:

  1. V = IR (Voltage = Current × Resistance)
  2. P = VI (Power = Voltage × Current)
  3. F = ma (Force = mass × acceleration)
  4. E = mc² (Energy = mass × speed of light squared)

Answer: V = IR (Voltage = Current × Resistance)

Explanation: Ohm's Law: V = IR. Voltage (V) across a conductor is directly proportional to the Current (I) through it, with Resistance (R) as the proportionality constant.

12. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination?

  1. Cerebrum
  2. Medulla Oblongata
  3. Cerebellum
  4. Thalamus

Answer: Cerebellum

Explanation: The cerebellum is responsible for coordination of movement, balance, and fine motor skills.

13. The law of conservation of energy states:

  1. Energy can be created from nothing
  2. Energy can be destroyed under special conditions
  3. Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted
  4. Energy increases over time

Answer: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted

Explanation: The Law of Conservation of Energy (First Law of Thermodynamics) states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed from one form to another.

14. The SI unit of electric current is:

  1. Volt
  2. Ohm
  3. Ampere
  4. Watt

Answer: Ampere

Explanation: The SI unit of electric current is the Ampere (A), named after French physicist André-Marie Ampère.

15. The 'Law of Gravitation' was proposed by:

  1. Albert Einstein
  2. Galileo Galilei
  3. Isaac Newton
  4. Nicolaus Copernicus

Answer: Isaac Newton

Explanation: Sir Isaac Newton formulated the Universal Law of Gravitation (1687): F = Gm1m2/r², stating that every object attracts every other object with a force proportional to their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

16. The SI unit of pressure is:

  1. Newton
  2. Pascal
  3. Joule
  4. Bar

Answer: Pascal

Explanation: The SI unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa), named after Blaise Pascal. 1 Pa = 1 Newton per square metre (N/m²).

17. Which is the lightest element in the periodic table?

  1. Helium
  2. Hydrogen
  3. Lithium
  4. Carbon

Answer: Hydrogen

Explanation: Hydrogen (H) is the lightest element with atomic number 1 and atomic mass ~1. It is also the most abundant element in the universe.

18. The splitting of an atom is called:

  1. Nuclear Fusion
  2. Nuclear Fission
  3. Radioactive Decay
  4. Ionization

Answer: Nuclear Fission

Explanation: Nuclear Fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus (like U-235) into smaller nuclei, releasing large amounts of energy. This is the principle used in nuclear reactors and atomic bombs.

19. The 'Bohr Model' of the atom proposed that electrons:

  1. Move randomly around the nucleus
  2. Orbit the nucleus in specific fixed energy levels (shells)
  3. Are embedded in a positive sphere
  4. Exist as probability clouds

Answer: Orbit the nucleus in specific fixed energy levels (shells)

Explanation: Niels Bohr (1913) proposed that electrons orbit the nucleus in specific discrete energy levels (shells). Electrons absorb or emit energy when jumping between levels.
