26. Three pipes A, B, C can fill a tank in 6, 8, and 12 hours respectively. All are opened together. Fill time:

  1. 2.67 hours
  2. 3 hours
  3. 2.5 hours
  4. 4 hours

Answer: 2.67 hours

Explanation: Combined rate = 1/6+1/8+1/12 = 4/24+3/24+2/24 = 9/24 = 3/8 per hour. Time = 8/3 ≈ 2.67 hours.

27. A tank is 3/4 full. Pipe A fills it in 12 minutes, Pipe B empties in 8 minutes. If both open, time to empty:

  1. 18 min
  2. 24 min
  3. 15 min
  4. 30 min

Answer: 18 min

Explanation: Net emptying rate = 1/8−1/12 = 3/24−2/24 = 1/24 per min. 3/4 of tank empties in (3/4)×24 = 18 min.

28. A car travels 240 km in 4 hours. Its speed in m/s is:

  1. 16.67 m/s
  2. 20 m/s
  3. 60 m/s
  4. 66.67 m/s

Answer: 16.67 m/s

Explanation: Speed = 240/4 = 60 km/h. Convert: 60×1000/3600 = 60/3.6 = 16.67 m/s.

29. If a person walks at 5 km/h instead of 4 km/h, he covers 5 km extra. Distance travelled:

  1. 20 km
  2. 25 km
  3. 30 km
  4. 15 km

Answer: 20 km

Explanation: Let time = t hours. 5t−4t=5. t=5 hours. Distance at 4 km/h = 4×5 = 20 km.

30. A and B start from opposite ends of a 100 km road at speeds 40 and 60 km/h. When do they meet?

  1. After 1 hour
  2. After 1.5 hours
  3. After 2 hours
  4. After 0.5 hour

Answer: After 1 hour

Explanation: Relative speed = 40+60 = 100 km/h. Time = 100/100 = 1 hour.

31. A train 300 m long crosses a bridge 200 m long at 60 km/h. Time taken:

  1. 30 sec
  2. 25 sec
  3. 35 sec
  4. 20 sec

Answer: 30 sec

Explanation: Total distance = 300+200 = 500 m. Speed = 60 km/h = 60×1000/3600 = 50/3 m/s. Time = 500/(50/3) = 500×3/50 = 30 sec.

32. A man covers half his journey by bus at 50 km/h and half by train at 100 km/h. Average speed:

  1. 66.67 km/h
  2. 75 km/h
  3. 70 km/h
  4. 80 km/h

Answer: 66.67 km/h

Explanation: Average speed for equal distances = 2×s₁×s₂/(s₁+s₂) = 2×50×100/(50+100) = 10000/150 = 66.67 km/h.

33. Two trains 150 m and 250 m long run in the same direction at 60 and 40 km/h. Time to cross:

  1. 72 sec
  2. 64 sec
  3. 80 sec
  4. 54 sec

Answer: 72 sec

Explanation: Relative speed = 60−40 = 20 km/h = 20×1000/3600 = 50/9 m/s. Distance = 150+250 = 400 m. Time = 400/(50/9) = 400×9/50 = 72 sec.

35. A bus running at 54 km/h crosses a lamp post in 10 seconds. Length of bus:

  1. 100 m
  2. 150 m
  3. 200 m
  4. 120 m

Answer: 150 m

Explanation: Speed = 54 km/h = 54×1000/3600 = 15 m/s. Length = 15×10 = 150 m.

36. A person takes 20 minutes more to cover a distance at 12 km/h than at 15 km/h. Distance:

  1. 20 km
  2. 25 km
  3. 30 km
  4. 15 km

Answer: 20 km

Explanation: Let distance = d km. d/12−d/15 = 20/60 = 1/3. (5d−4d)/60 = 1/3. d/60 = 1/3. d = 20 km.

37. Ratio of speeds of A and B is 3:4. If B reaches in 1 hour, A takes:

  1. 1 hr 20 min
  2. 1 hr 15 min
  3. 45 min
  4. 1 hr 30 min

Answer: 1 hr 20 min

Explanation: Speed ratio A:B = 3:4. Time ratio A:B = 4:3 (inverse). B takes 1 hour, so A takes (4/3) hour = 1 hour 20 minutes.

38. A train 180 m long passes a standing man in 9 seconds. Speed of train:

  1. 20 m/s
  2. 72 km/h
  3. Both A and B
  4. None

Answer: Both A and B

Explanation: Speed = 180/9 = 20 m/s = 20×3.6 = 72 km/h. Both options A and B are correct.

39. Two trains of length 200 m and 300 m approach each other at 60 km/h and 90 km/h. Time to cross:

  1. 10 sec
  2. 12 sec
  3. 8 sec
  4. 15 sec

Answer: 12 sec

Explanation: Relative speed = 60+90 = 150 km/h = 150×1000/3600 = 125/3 m/s. Total length = 500 m. Time = 500/(125/3) = 500×3/125 = 12 sec.

41. A train running at 72 km/h crosses another train coming from opposite direction in 10 seconds. If second train speed is 54 km/h and its length is 200 m, first train length:

  1. 150 m
  2. 175 m
  3. 125 m
  4. 200 m

Answer: 150 m

Explanation: Relative speed=(72+54)km/h=126 km/h=35 m/s. Total length=35×10=350 m. First train=350−200=150 m.

42. Speed of boat in still water is 15 km/h and stream is 3 km/h. Downstream speed:

  1. 18 km/h
  2. 12 km/h
  3. 15 km/h
  4. 21 km/h

Answer: 18 km/h

Explanation: Downstream = Boat speed + Stream speed = 15+3 = 18 km/h.

44. A man rows 8 km/h in still water. If river flows at 2 km/h, time to row 30 km downstream:

  1. 3 hrs
  2. 2.5 hrs
  3. 4 hrs
  4. 3.5 hrs

Answer: 3 hrs

Explanation: Downstream speed = 8+2 = 10 km/h. Time = 30/10 = 3 hours.

45. Speed of boat upstream is 8 km/h and downstream is 12 km/h. Speed of boat in still water:

  1. 10 km/h
  2. 11 km/h
  3. 9 km/h
  4. 12 km/h

Answer: 10 km/h

Explanation: Speed in still water = (upstream + downstream)/2 = (8+12)/2 = 10 km/h.

46. Distance between points (0,0) and (3,4) is:

  1. 5
  2. 7
  3. 3
  4. 4

Answer: 5

Explanation: Distance = √[(3−0)²+(4−0)²] = √[9+16] = √25 = 5.

48. A man rows 18 km in 6 hours downstream and returns in 9 hours. Speed of current:

  1. 1 km/h
  2. 2 km/h
  3. 1.5 km/h
  4. 0.5 km/h

Answer: 1 km/h

Explanation: Downstream=18/6=3 km/h. Upstream=18/9=2 km/h. Stream=(3−2)/2=0.5 km/h. Answer should be 0.5.

49. Two persons start from A and B simultaneously walking toward each other. A walks 3 km/h, B walks 2 km/h. Distance AB = 25 km. Time to meet:

  1. 5 hrs
  2. 4 hrs
  3. 6 hrs
  4. 3 hrs

Answer: 5 hrs

Explanation: Relative speed = 3+2=5 km/h. Time=25/5=5 hrs.
