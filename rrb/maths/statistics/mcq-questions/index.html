1. In a pie chart, a sector represents 25% of the data. Its central angle is:

  1. 90°
  2. 45°
  3. 180°
  4. 120°

Answer: 90°

Explanation: 25% of 360° = 0.25×360° = 90°.

2. A bar chart shows production: 2019=500, 2020=600, 2021=750. % increase 2019 to 2021:

  1. 50%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 40%

Answer: 50%

Explanation: % increase = (750−500)/500×100 = 250/500×100 = 50%.

3. A table shows: Product A contributes 30% of total revenue. If total revenue is ₹5,00,000, revenue from A:

  1. ₹1,50,000
  2. ₹1,00,000
  3. ₹2,00,000
  4. ₹1,25,000

Answer: ₹1,50,000

Explanation: Revenue from A = 30% of 5,00,000 = 0.30×500000 = ₹1,50,000.
