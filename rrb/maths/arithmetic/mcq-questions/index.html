26. HCF of 36 and 48 is:

  1. 6
  2. 12
  3. 18
  4. 24

Answer: 12

Explanation: 36=2²×3², 48=2⁴×3. HCF = 2²×3 = 12.

27. LCM of 12, 18, and 24 is:

  1. 72
  2. 144
  3. 36
  4. 48

Answer: 72

Explanation: 12=2²×3, 18=2×3², 24=2³×3. LCM = 2³×3² = 8×9 = 72.

28. The product of HCF and LCM of two numbers is 960. If one number is 48, the other is:

  1. 20
  2. 15
  3. 24
  4. 30

Answer: 20

Explanation: HCF × LCM = Product of two numbers. So other number = 960/48 = 20.

29. Three bells ring at intervals of 6, 8, and 12 minutes. If they ring together at 12 noon, when will they next ring together?

  1. 12:24 PM
  2. 12:30 PM
  3. 12:48 PM
  4. 1:00 PM

Answer: 12:24 PM

Explanation: LCM(6,8,12): 6=2×3, 8=2³, 12=2²×3. LCM=2³×3=24 minutes. They ring together at 12:24 PM.

30. The greatest number that divides 35, 56, and 91 leaving no remainder is:

  1. 7
  2. 14
  3. 21
  4. 28

Answer: 7

Explanation: HCF(35,56,91): 35=5×7, 56=8×7, 91=13×7. HCF=7.

31. The least number divisible by 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 is:

  1. 60
  2. 120
  3. 180
  4. 30

Answer: 60

Explanation: LCM(2,3,4,5,6): LCM(4,6)=12, LCM(12,5)=60, LCM(60,2)=60, LCM(60,3)=60. LCM=60.

32. What is the least number which when divided by 5, 6, 7, and 8 leaves remainder 3?

  1. 843
  2. 843
  3. 840
  4. 838

Answer: 843

Explanation: LCM(5,6,7,8)=840. Least number = 840+3=843.

33. The LCM of two numbers is 60 and their HCF is 5. If one number is 15, the other is:

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 30

Answer: 20

Explanation: Other number = (LCM × HCF) / first = (60 × 5) / 15 = 300/15 = 20.

34. 20% of 350 + 35% of 200 = ?

  1. 140
  2. 135
  3. 130
  4. 145

Answer: 140

Explanation: 20% of 350 = 70. 35% of 200 = 70. Total = 70+70 = 140.

35. A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. The net change is:

  1. 0%
  2. 5% increase
  3. 5% decrease
  4. 10% increase

Answer: 0%

Explanation: Let number = 100. After 25% increase = 125. After 20% decrease = 125×0.8 = 100. Net change = 0%.

36. If the price of sugar increases by 25%, by what percent should a household reduce its consumption to maintain the same expenditure?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 15%
  4. 10%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Reduction % = (25/(100+25)) × 100 = (25/125) × 100 = 20%.

37. 60% of a number is 96. What is 85% of that number?

  1. 136
  2. 128
  3. 140
  4. 120

Answer: 136

Explanation: Number = 96/0.6 = 160. 85% of 160 = 0.85×160 = 136.

38. If A is 20% more than B, then B is what percent less than A?

  1. 16.67%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 15%

Answer: 16.67%

Explanation: A = 1.2B. B = A/1.2. B is less than A by (A−B)/A×100 = (A−A/1.2)/A×100 = (1−1/1.2)×100 = (0.2/1.2)×100 = 16.67%.

39. In an election, candidate A gets 55% of total votes. If total votes are 80,000, how many votes did candidate B get?

  1. 36,000
  2. 44,000
  3. 40,000
  4. 38,000

Answer: 36,000

Explanation: A gets 55% = 44,000. B gets 45% = 80,000×0.45 = 36,000.

40. A student scores 450 out of 600. What is the percentage score?

  1. 70%
  2. 72%
  3. 75%
  4. 80%

Answer: 75%

Explanation: Percentage = (450/600) × 100 = 75%.

41. Population of a city is 5,00,000. If it increases at 4% annually, population after 2 years is:

  1. 5,40,800
  2. 5,20,000
  3. 5,41,200
  4. 5,40,000

Answer: 5,40,800

Explanation: P(1+r/100)² = 500000×(1.04)² = 500000×1.0816 = 540800.

42. What percent is 75 paise of ₹3?

  1. 25%
  2. 75%
  3. 33%
  4. 50%

Answer: 25%

Explanation: 75 paise = ₹0.75. (0.75/3)×100 = 25%.

43. If 15% of a number is 45, what is 30% of that number?

  1. 90
  2. 75
  3. 60
  4. 120

Answer: 90

Explanation: Number = 45/0.15 = 300. 30% of 300 = 90.

44. An article is bought for ₹400 and sold for ₹480. Profit percent is:

  1. 16%
  2. 18%
  3. 20%
  4. 15%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Profit = 480−400 = ₹80. Profit% = (80/400)×100 = 20%.

45. A TV is sold at ₹8,400 with a loss of 16%. Its cost price is:

  1. ₹9,500
  2. ₹10,000
  3. ₹9,800
  4. ₹10,200

Answer: ₹10,000

Explanation: SP = CP×(1−16/100) = CP×0.84. 8400 = CP×0.84. CP = 8400/0.84 = ₹10,000.

46. A vendor buys eggs at 20 for ₹5 and sells at 16 for ₹5. His gain/loss percent is:

  1. 25% gain
  2. 20% gain
  3. 25% loss
  4. 20% loss

Answer: 25% gain

Explanation: CP of 1 egg = 5/20 = ₹0.25. SP of 1 egg = 5/16 = ₹0.3125. Profit% = (0.3125−0.25)/0.25×100 = 25%.

47. The MP of an article is ₹600. After a discount of 15%, profit is 2%. Cost price is:

  1. ₹490
  2. ₹500
  3. ₹510
  4. ₹480

Answer: ₹500

Explanation: SP = 600×0.85 = ₹510. SP = CP×1.02. CP = 510/1.02 = ₹500.

48. By selling 12 pens for ₹120, a person loses 20%. To gain 20%, he must sell 12 pens for:

  1. ₹160
  2. ₹168
  3. ₹180
  4. ₹144

Answer: ₹180

Explanation: SP = ₹120, Loss=20%. CP = 120/0.8 = ₹150. To gain 20%, SP = 150×1.2 = ₹180.

49. If cost price of 15 items equals selling price of 12 items, the profit percent is:

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 15%
  4. 12%

Answer: 25%

Explanation: Let CP of each = ₹1. CP of 15 = ₹15 = SP of 12 items. SP of each = 15/12 = ₹1.25. Profit% = (1.25−1)/1×100 = 25%.

50. A dishonest dealer sells goods at cost price but uses 900g weight instead of 1 kg. His profit percent is:

  1. 9%
  2. 10%
  3. 11.11%
  4. 12%

Answer: 11.11%

Explanation: He gives 900g but charges for 1000g. Profit per kg = 100g. Profit% = (100/900)×100 = 11.11%.
