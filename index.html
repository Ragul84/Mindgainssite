NationLap

A kart racing game that quizzes you on the exam syllabus.

Pick a topic on the journey map, read a short lesson at the Study Pit Stop, and then race. Every item box on the track asks a real exam question, and a correct answer earns you a power up and XP. Topics you master come back for review after 3, 7, 21 and 50 days.

  • JourneyPolity, History, Geography, Economy, Science, Quant and Reasoning are laid out as a path of topics that unlock as you master them.
  • Today's AffairsEach morning's current affairs become a race where you steer through the gate with the correct fact.
  • Quick Race and CupsRace rival aspirants over several heats, or chase a ghost of your own best lap.
  • India BoardXP is graded on the server, and the day's top ten appear on the Hall of Fame wall at the starting grid.
