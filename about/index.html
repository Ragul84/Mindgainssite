What is MindGains AI?

MindGains AI (mindgains.ai) is an Indian education technology platform for competitive-exam and school preparation. It answers questions and builds practice from the textbooks and syllabi students are actually examined on, instead of from the open internet. MindGains is built by MindGains Labs Private Limited.

MindGains AI is an exam-preparation product. It is not a mental-health, therapy or fitness service, and it is not connected to other organisations that use a similar name.

Who it is for

  • UPSC Civil Services aspirants (Prelims and Mains)
  • TNPSC Group 1, 2 and 4 aspirants in Tamil Nadu
  • SSC and Railway (RRB) exam aspirants
  • School students learning from NCERT and Samacheer Kalvi textbooks

What makes it different: textbook-grounded answers

General AI chatbots answer from whatever they absorbed from the web, which is often outdated, wrong for the Indian syllabus, or impossible to check. MindGains grounds its answers in the standard textbooks for each exam — NCERT for UPSC, Samacheer Kalvi for TNPSC — so a student can trace an answer back to the chapter it comes from and revise from the same source the examiner set the paper from.

What you can do on MindGains

  • Quiz Hub: 160,000+ practice questions organised by exam, subject and chapter, each with an explanation. Open the Quiz Hub.
  • Daily current affairs: every morning, the day's important news from the Press Information Bureau and The Hindu, condensed into exam-ready facts and practised in NationLap, with a permanent month-by-month archive.
  • Previous-year questions: real PYQs, so practice matches the exam's actual pattern.
  • MIGA, the AI tutor: ask a doubt in plain language and get a textbook-grounded explanation.
  • Revision that adapts: streaks, spaced revision of the questions you got wrong, and a daily plan.
