Class10 MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Class10 questions to revise the NCERT Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 300 questions.

1. Which of the following statements accurately describes the process of photosynthesis?

  1. It is an anabolic process where light energy is converted into chemical energy.
  2. It is a catabolic process where chemical energy is converted into light energy.
  3. It is an anabolic process where chemical energy is converted into mechanical energy.
  4. It is a catabolic process where mechanical energy is converted into chemical energy.

Answer: It is an anabolic process where light energy is converted into chemical energy.

Explanation: Photosynthesis is an anabolic process because it involves the synthesis of complex organic molecules (glucose) from simpler inorganic molecules (carbon dioxide and water). This process utilizes light energy, converting it into chemical energy stored in the bonds of glucose.

2. A student observes that a plant kept in a dark room for several days has yellowed leaves. Which of the following pigments is most likely deficient in this plant?

  1. Chlorophyll
  2. Carotenoids
  3. Anthocyanins
  4. Xanthophylls

Answer: Chlorophyll

Explanation: Chlorophyll is the primary green pigment responsible for absorbing light energy during photosynthesis. In the absence of light, plants cannot synthesize chlorophyll, leading to its degradation and the unmasking of other pigments like carotenoids, which give a yellowish appearance to the leaves. This phenomenon is known as etiolation.

3. Which of the following is the primary site for the absorption of water and minerals in most terrestrial plants?

  1. Root hairs
  2. Stomata
  3. Lenticels
  4. Guard cells

Answer: Root hairs

Explanation: Root hairs are microscopic extensions of epidermal cells of the root. They significantly increase the surface area of the root, allowing for efficient absorption of water and dissolved mineral salts from the soil. Stomata are involved in gas exchange, lenticels in gas exchange in woody stems, and guard cells regulate stomatal opening.

4. During respiration, glucose is broken down to release energy. Which of the following is the correct sequence of events in aerobic respiration?

  1. Glycolysis → Krebs cycle → Electron transport chain
  2. Krebs cycle → Glycolysis → Electron transport chain
  3. Electron transport chain → Glycolysis → Krebs cycle
  4. Glycolysis → Electron transport chain → Krebs cycle

Answer: Glycolysis → Krebs cycle → Electron transport chain

Explanation: Aerobic respiration begins with glycolysis, which occurs in the cytoplasm and breaks down glucose into pyruvate. Pyruvate then enters the mitochondria, where it is converted to acetyl-CoA, which enters the Krebs cycle. Finally, the electron transport chain, also in the mitochondria, produces the majority of ATP.

5. Which of the following statements best describes the function of xylem in plants?

  1. Transport of water and dissolved minerals from roots to leaves.
  2. Transport of prepared food from leaves to other parts of the plant.
  3. Storage of food and water in the stem.
  4. Exchange of gases between the plant and the atmosphere.

Answer: Transport of water and dissolved minerals from roots to leaves.

Explanation: Xylem is a complex tissue responsible for the unidirectional transport of water and dissolved mineral nutrients from the roots upwards to all aerial parts of the plant. Phloem, on the other hand, transports prepared food. Storage and gas exchange are functions of other tissues.
