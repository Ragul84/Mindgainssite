1. Who is the current President of India (as of 2024)?

  1. Ram Nath Kovind
  2. Droupadi Murmu
  3. Pranab Mukherjee
  4. APJ Abdul Kalam

Answer: Droupadi Murmu

Explanation: Droupadi Murmu became the 15th President of India on 25 July 2022. She is the first tribal woman and the second woman to hold this position.

2. Who is the current Prime Minister of India (as of 2024)?

  1. Manmohan Singh
  2. Narendra Modi
  3. Amit Shah
  4. Rajnath Singh

Answer: Narendra Modi

Explanation: Narendra Modi has been the Prime Minister of India since May 2014. He was sworn in for a third consecutive term in June 2024.

3. The Chandrayaan-3 mission successfully landed on the Moon's south pole in:

  1. 2021
  2. 2022
  3. 2023
  4. 2024

Answer: 2023

Explanation: Chandrayaan-3 successfully landed near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023, making India the first country to achieve a soft landing near the lunar south pole and the fourth country overall.

4. The 2023 ICC Cricket World Cup was won by:

  1. Pakistan
  2. India
  3. Australia
  4. England

Answer: Australia

Explanation: Australia won the ICC Cricket World Cup 2023, defeating India in the final at Ahmedabad on 19 November 2023. It was Australia's sixth World Cup title.

5. UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is operated by:

  1. RBI
  2. SBI
  3. NPCI
  4. SEBI

Answer: NPCI

Explanation: UPI is operated by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). In 2023, India processed over 100 billion UPI transactions in a year for the first time.
