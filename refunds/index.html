Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers MindGains Pro and Teacher plans bought on mindgains.ai and paid through Razorpay. Purchases made inside the Android or iOS app are handled by Google Play or Apple under their refund rules.

1. No automatic renewal

All plans are one-time payments for a fixed period (30 days, 6 months or 1 year, as shown at checkout). Nothing renews or is charged automatically, so there is nothing to cancel to stop future payments. Your plan simply ends on the date shown in your account.

2. 7-day refund

  • If you are not happy with your plan, write to us within 7 days of payment and we will refund the full amount.
  • This applies to your first purchase of each plan. Repeat purchases of the same plan, and requests after 7 days, are not refundable except as in section 3.
  • When a refund is issued, the plan ends immediately.

3. Payment problems

If you were charged twice, charged but did not receive Pro or Teacher access, or the payment failed but money left your account, write to us with your payment ID and we will fix it or refund the extra amount, whatever the date of purchase.

4. How to ask

Email contact@mindgains.ai from the email you signed in with, and include the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt. We reply within 2 working days.

5. When you get the money

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account) through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the amount.
