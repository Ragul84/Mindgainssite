# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v2
**Last updated:** 2026-11-18

## Product Overview
**Web focus (decision 2026-11-18):** The website markets ONLY the Live Theatre (major hook) and NationLap. Textbook-grounded AI, the question bank, PYQs and current affairs belong to the Android app; mention them only as "also in the app", never as the web headline.
**One-liner:** Study with all of India, live: watch classes in a 3D theatre with aspirants across India, then race tonight's NationLap.
**What it does:** MindGains answers exam questions from the actual textbooks (NCERT, Samacheer Kalvi), gives 160,000+ practice questions with real PYQs and daily current affairs, and turns study into a live, social experience: NationLap (a live quiz race against aspirants across India) and the Live Theatre (a 3D hall where students watch classes together, chat and answer live quizzes). Pro adds Batch rooms, private theatres for friends, classmates or a tuition batch with their own YouTube videos and a teacher who can pause and quiz.
**Product category:** Government exam preparation app (UPSC / TNPSC / SSC / RRB), "online study room", "study with me".
**Product type:** B2C web app + Android app (freemium).
**Business model:** Free core (classes, quizzes, NationLap; Live Theatre 30 min/day after sign-in). MindGains Pro, one-time payments, no auto-debit: ₹99/month, ₹399 Exam Pass (6 months, ₹66/month), ₹599/year (₹50/month). ₹49 launch offer on the first month. Teacher plan ₹299/month or ₹2,999/year (rooms up to 60 students, about ₹5 per student). Payments via Razorpay (UPI/cards).

## Target Audience
**Target users:** Indian government-exam aspirants, 18–30, mostly on mobile (~90%), many in Tamil Nadu (TNPSC, Samacheer Kalvi) plus national UPSC/SSC/RRB aspirants; many study alone at home or in a hostel, on a tight budget.
**Decision-makers:** The aspirant (sometimes parents pay). For Teacher plans: independent tutors and small coaching centres.
**Primary use case:** Daily, disciplined study with others so they don't drop off.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Keep me studying every day, even when I'm alone and unmotivated."
- "Show me where I'm weak and test me on what will actually come in the exam."
- "Let my friends / my batch / my students study together online for free-ish."
**Use cases:**
- Joining the evening live class in the theatre and racing the quiz
- A hostel group making a Batch room to watch one YouTube lecture together
- A tuition teacher running a class: pause the video, ask a quiz, see who answered

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Preparation is lonely and long; aspirants lose discipline, and generic AI or YouTube answers aren't exam-accurate.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big ed-tech apps are expensive (thousands of rupees) and push sales calls
- YouTube is free but passive and distracting; no testing, no accountability
- Generic chatbots hallucinate facts that aren't in the syllabus textbooks
**What it costs them:** Missed attempts, wasted years, money on courses they don't finish.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of falling behind others, guilt about wasted days, isolation.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Testbook, Adda247, Unacademy, PW (Physics Wallah) — mock tests and courses, but paid, sales-heavy and not social in real time.
**Secondary:** YouTube channels and Telegram groups — free, but passive and chaotic.
**Indirect:** "Study with me" streams, library/reading-room memberships, ChatGPT.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Live 3D theatre: real people studying in the same hall at the same time
- NationLap: a live race with every aspirant in India, ranked nightly
- Answers grounded in NCERT / Samacheer Kalvi textbooks
- Batch rooms: your own videos, synced, with teacher pause-and-quiz and chat lock
- One-time payments, no auto-debit, from ₹99
**Why customers choose us:** It feels like studying in a full hall instead of alone, at a price of a cup of tea a week.

**Anti-persona:** People wanting a full recorded video-course library or one-on-one mentorship.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Loneliness, broken streaks, expensive courses, unreliable AI answers.
**Pull:** Live hall, national race, rank, friends in Batch rooms.
**Habit:** Telegram groups, YouTube playlists, existing paid courses.
**Anxiety:** Payment safety, auto-debit, "is anyone actually there?"
