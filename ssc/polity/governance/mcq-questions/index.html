26. Who is the head of the council of ministers in a state?

  1. Governor
  2. Chief Minister
  3. Prime Minister
  4. President

Answer: Chief Minister

Explanation: The Chief Minister is the head of the council of ministers in a state.

27. What is the term for the process of transferring power from a higher authority to a lower authority?

  1. Decentralization
  2. Centralization
  3. Federalization
  4. Delegation

Answer: Decentralization

Explanation: Decentralization is the process of transferring power from a higher authority to a lower authority.

  1. Article 368
  2. Article 370
  3. Article 371
  4. Article 372

Answer: Article 368

Explanation: Article 368 of the Indian Constitution deals with the provisions for the amendment of the Constitution.

29. What is the term for the system of governance where there is no central authority and power is held by local units?

  1. Confederalism
  2. Federalism
  3. Unitary
  4. Anarchy

Answer: Confederalism

Explanation: Confederalism is a system of governance where there is no central authority and power is held by local units.

  1. President
  2. Prime Minister

  4. Governor

Answer: President

  2. Legislative Review

Answer: Judicial Review

Explanation: Judicial review is the power of the judicial branch to review and interpret laws.

  2. Part IV

33. What is the term for the system of governance where power is held by a single person or group?

Answer: Autocracy

Explanation: Autocracy is a system of governance where power is held by a single person or group.

34. Who is the head of the government in India?

  1. Prime Minister
  2. President
  3. Vice President
  4. Governor

Answer: Prime Minister

Explanation: The Prime Minister is the head of the government in India.

35. What is the term for the system of government where power is divided between a central authority and smaller regional authorities?

  1. Federalism
  2. Confederalism
  3. Unitary
  4. Autocracy

Answer: Federalism

Explanation: Federalism is a system of government where power is divided between a central authority and smaller regional authorities. This system is used in countries like the United States and India.

36. Which of the following is a key principle of good governance?

  1. Transparency
  2. Accountability
  3. Decentralization
  4. Bureaucracy

Answer: Transparency

Explanation: Transparency is a key principle of good governance, as it allows citizens to access information about government activities. This promotes trust and accountability in government.

37. What is the name of the document that outlines the fundamental rights and duties of citizens in India?

  1. Constitution
  2. Bill of Rights
  3. Charter of Rights
  4. Declaration of Independence

Answer: Constitution

Explanation: The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the land, outlining the fundamental rights and duties of citizens. It was adopted on November 26, 1949.

38. Which of the following institutions is responsible for ensuring that the government is accountable to the people?

  1. Legislature
  2. Executive
  3. Judiciary
  4. Media

Answer: Legislature

Explanation: The Legislature is responsible for ensuring that the government is accountable to the people, through mechanisms like questioning and oversight. This helps to prevent abuse of power and promote good governance.

39. What is the term for the process of transferring power and decision-making authority from a central government to smaller regional authorities?

  1. Decentralization
  2. Centralization
  3. Devolution
  4. Delegation

Answer: Decentralization

Explanation: Decentralization is the process of transferring power and decision-making authority from a central government to smaller regional authorities. This can help to promote local autonomy and more effective governance.

40. Which of the following is a benefit of e-governance?

  1. Increased bureaucracy
  2. Improved transparency
  3. Reduced accountability
  4. Higher costs

Answer: Improved transparency

Explanation: E-governance can help to improve transparency, by making government information and services more accessible to citizens. This can help to promote trust and accountability in government.

41. What is the name of the concept that refers to the use of technology to improve government services and operations?

  1. E-governance
  2. Digital governance
  3. Good governance
  4. Public administration

Answer: E-governance

Explanation: E-governance refers to the use of technology to improve government services and operations, making them more efficient and accessible to citizens. This can help to promote good governance and improve public services.

42. Which of the following is a key characteristic of a democratic system of government?

  1. Absolute power
  2. Free and fair elections
  3. Limited representation
  4. No accountability

Answer: Free and fair elections

Explanation: Free and fair elections are a key characteristic of a democratic system of government, as they allow citizens to choose their representatives and hold them accountable. This helps to promote accountability and good governance.

43. What is the term for the system of government where power is held by a small group of individuals?

  1. Oligarchy
  2. Democracy
  3. Monarchy
  4. Autocracy

Answer: Oligarchy

Explanation: Oligarchy is a system of government where power is held by a small group of individuals, often to the exclusion of others. This can lead to abuse of power and undermine good governance.

44. Which of the following institutions plays a crucial role in promoting good governance and accountability in government?
