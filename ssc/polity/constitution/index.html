Constitution MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Constitution questions to revise the SSC Polity syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 701 questions.

1. What is the supreme law of India?

  1. Indian Penal Code
  2. The Constitution of India
  3. Civil Procedure Code
  4. The Indian Evidence Act

Answer: The Constitution of India

Explanation: The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the land. All other laws must be consistent with it.

2. How many fundamental rights are guaranteed by the Indian Constitution?

  1. 5
  2. 6
  3. 7
  4. 9

Answer: 6

Explanation: The Indian Constitution guarantees 6 Fundamental Rights under Part III (Articles 12-35).

3. Who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution?

  1. Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
  4. Sardar Patel

Answer: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constitution and is called its Father.

4. On which date did the Indian Constitution come into force?

  1. 15 August 1947
  2. 26 November 1949
  3. 26 January 1950
  4. 2 October 1950

Answer: 26 January 1950

Explanation: The Indian Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, celebrated as Republic Day.

5. Who is the head of the State Government in India?

  1. Chief Minister
  2. Governor
  3. President
  4. Prime Minister

Answer: Governor

Explanation: The Governor is the constitutional head of each State in India, appointed by the President.

6. What is the minimum age to vote in Indian elections?

  1. 16 years
  2. 18 years
  3. 21 years
  4. 25 years

Answer: 18 years

Explanation: The 61st Constitutional Amendment (1988) reduced the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

7. Which body conducts elections in India?

  1. Parliament
  2. Supreme Court
  3. Election Commission of India
  4. UPSC

Answer: Election Commission of India

Explanation: The Election Commission of India (ECI) is an autonomous constitutional body that administers elections at the national and state level.

8. Who appoints the Chief Minister of a State?

  1. President of India
  2. Governor of the State
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Speaker of Lok Sabha

Answer: Governor of the State

Explanation: The Governor appoints the Chief Minister, who must enjoy the confidence of the Legislative Assembly.

9. The Indian Parliament consists of how many Houses?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4

Answer: 2

Explanation: Indian Parliament has two Houses: Lok Sabha (Lower House) and Rajya Sabha (Upper House), plus the President.

10. Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?

  1. President of India
  2. Prime Minister
  3. Vice President of India
  4. Speaker of Lok Sabha

Answer: Vice President of India

Explanation: The Vice President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

11. How many languages are listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution?

  1. 18
  2. 20
  3. 22
  4. 24

Answer: 22

Explanation: The Eighth Schedule lists 22 official languages of India.

12. The Rajya Sabha is also called the:

  1. House of the People
  2. Council of States
  3. Legislative Assembly
  4. Council of Ministers

Answer: Council of States

Explanation: Rajya Sabha is officially called the 'Council of States'. Lok Sabha is called the 'House of the People'.

13. Which article of the Constitution abolishes untouchability?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 15
  3. Article 17
  4. Article 21

Answer: Article 17

Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability in any form and makes its practice a punishable offence.

14. What is the term of a Rajya Sabha member?

  1. 5 years
  2. 6 years
  3. 4 years
  4. Lifetime

Answer: 6 years

Explanation: Rajya Sabha members serve 6-year terms. One-third retire every 2 years. Rajya Sabha is a permanent body.

15. Which is the highest court in India?

  1. High Court
  2. District Court
  3. Supreme Court
  4. Lok Adalat

Answer: Supreme Court

Explanation: The Supreme Court of India is the apex court and the final interpreter of the Constitution.

16. Right to Education is guaranteed under which article?

  1. Article 19
  2. Article 21
  3. Article 21A
  4. Article 45

Answer: Article 21A

Explanation: Article 21A (added by 86th Amendment, 2002) provides free and compulsory education to children aged 6-14 years.

17. Which amendment gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 73rd Amendment
  4. 86th Amendment

Answer: 73rd Amendment

Explanation: The 73rd Constitutional Amendment (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions (local self-government in rural areas).

18. How long is the term of the President of India?

  1. 4 years
  2. 5 years
  3. 6 years
  4. Till retirement

Answer: 5 years

Explanation: The President of India serves a 5-year term and can be re-elected.

19. What is the national animal of India?

  1. Lion
  2. Elephant
  3. Tiger
  4. Peacock

Answer: Tiger

Explanation: Bengal Tiger (Panthera tigris tigris) is the national animal of India.

20. Which article empowers the Parliament to create new States or alter the boundaries of existing States?

  1. Article 1
  2. Article 2
  3. Article 3
  4. Article 4

Answer: Article 3

Explanation: Article 3 empowers Parliament to form new States by separation, union, or merger of existing States, or by altering boundaries, names, or areas.

21. Under which article can the President proclaim a National Emergency?

  1. Article 352
  2. Article 356
  3. Article 360
  4. Article 370

Answer: Article 352

Explanation: Article 352 empowers the President to proclaim a National Emergency if the security of India is threatened by war, external aggression, or armed rebellion.

22. Which writ is issued by a court to compel a public authority to perform its legal duty?

  1. Habeas Corpus
  2. Mandamus
  3. Certiorari
  4. Quo Warranto

Answer: Mandamus

Explanation: Mandamus (meaning 'We command') is issued to a public authority, lower court, or corporation to perform a public or statutory duty.

23. Which writ is used to produce a detained person before a court?

  1. Mandamus
  2. Certiorari
  3. Habeas Corpus
  4. Prohibition

Answer: Habeas Corpus

Explanation: Habeas Corpus (meaning 'You may have the body') is issued to free a person from illegal detention.

24. The Directive Principles of State Policy are contained in which Part of the Constitution?

  1. Part II
  2. Part III
  3. Part IV
  4. Part V

Answer: Part IV

Explanation: Directive Principles of State Policy are in Part IV (Articles 36-51). They are non-justiciable but fundamental in governance.

25. How many schedules does the Indian Constitution have?

  1. 8
  2. 10
  3. 12
  4. 14

Answer: 12

Explanation: The Indian Constitution originally had 8 schedules; it now has 12 schedules after various amendments.
