Series MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Series questions to revise the SSC Reasoning syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 540 questions.

1. Find the next number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

  1. 40
  2. 42
  3. 44
  4. 46

Answer: 42

Explanation: Pattern: differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Next = 30+12 = 42. Or n(n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42.

2. Find the missing number in the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, ___

  1. 24
  2. 28
  3. 32
  4. 36

Answer: 32

Explanation: Each number is multiplied by 2: 2×2=4, 4×2=8, 8×2=16, 16×2=32. This is a geometric progression with common ratio 2.

3. Find the missing number: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ___

  1. 30
  2. 35
  3. 36
  4. 49

Answer: 36

Explanation: These are perfect squares: 1²=1, 2²=4, 3²=9, 4²=16, 5²=25, 6²=36. The series consists of squares of consecutive natural numbers.

4. Find the missing number: 144, 121, 100, 81, 64, ___

  1. 46
  2. 48
  3. 49
  4. 50

Answer: 49

Explanation: Decreasing squares: 12²=144, 11²=121, 10²=100, 9²=81, 8²=64, 7²=49. Series of perfect squares in descending order.

5. Find the missing letter: A, C, E, G, ___

  1. H
  2. I
  3. J
  4. K

Answer: I

Explanation: The series skips one letter each time: A(1), C(3), E(5), G(7), I(9). Every alternate letter of the alphabet. +2 positions each step.

6. Find the odd one out: 4, 8, 10, 14, 16, 18

  1. 10
  2. 14
  3. 16
  4. 18

Answer: 10

Explanation: All numbers are even, but checking factors: 4=2², 8=2³, 14=2×7, 16=2⁴, 18=2×9. Another pattern: 8,14,16,18 are not multiples of 4 (well 8,16 are). Wait: 4,8,16 are powers of 2. 10,14,18 are not. But 10 is the outlier if looking at multiples: 4,8,12,16,20 (multiples of 4) — but 10,14,18 are not. The simplest: these should be even numbers divisible by 2 but not 4 or vice versa. Looking at it differently: 8,10,14,16,18 differ by 2,4,2,2... actually 4,8,10,14,16,18: differences are 4,2,4,2,2. 10 is not part of a consistent pattern. Or: remove 10 as it's not a product of 2 consecutive even numbers or... 10=2×5; others: 4=2×2, 8=2×4, 14=2×7, 16=2×8, 18=2×9. 5 is the only prime that gives even result without a '2'. Alternatively 10 is out because the series could be: 4,8,14,16,18 where 10 is not fitting. Best answer: 10.

7. Look at the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ___

  1. 40
  2. 42
  3. 44
  4. 46

Answer: 42

Explanation: Pattern: 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42. Each term = n×(n+1).

8. Look at the following: 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 12, 16, ___

  1. 15
  2. 18
  3. 20
  4. 21

Answer: 15

Explanation: Two interleaved series: 3,6,9,12,15 (add 3) and 4,8,12,16,20 (add 4). Or: odd positions: 3,6,9,12,15; even positions: 4,8,12,16. Next odd position term: 12+3=15.

9. Find the odd one out from the series: ACE, BDF, CEG, DFH, EGJ

  1. ACE
  2. CEG
  3. EGJ
  4. DFH

Answer: EGJ

Explanation: Pattern: each group has letters with gaps of 1 position (every alternate letter). ACE: A+2=C+2=E ✓, BDF: B+2=D+2=F ✓, CEG: C+2=E+2=G ✓, DFH: D+2=F+2=H ✓, EGJ: E+2=G but G+3=J (should be I not J). EGJ is the odd one out (should be EGI).

10. What comes next? 31, 29, 24, 22, 17, 15, ___

  1. 13
  2. 12
  3. 10
  4. 8

Answer: 10

Explanation: Two alternating series: 31,24,17,10 (subtract 7) and 29,22,15,? (subtract 7). Next in second series: 15-7=8. Actually: 31-2=29, 29-5=24, 24-2=22, 22-5=17, 17-2=15, 15-5=10. Pattern: subtract 2, then 5, alternating.

11. Find the missing number: 8, 24, 12, 36, 18, 54, ___

  1. 27
  2. 36
  3. 24
  4. 30

Answer: 27

Explanation: Two alternating series: 8,12,18,27 (multiply by 1.5 or ×3/2) and 24,36,54,81. Let me check: 8→24(×3), 24→12(÷2), 12→36(×3), 36→18(÷2), 18→54(×3), 54→27(÷2). Pattern: ×3, ÷2, alternating. Next: 54÷2=27.

12. Find the missing number in the series: 10, 100, 200, 310, ___

  1. 420
  2. 430
  3. 440
  4. 450

Answer: 430

Explanation: Differences: 90, 100, 110, 120 (increasing by 10 each time). 310+120=430.

13. Which letter should come in place of ? in the series: B, E, H, K, ?

  1. M
  2. N
  3. O
  4. L

Answer: N

Explanation: Each letter skips 2: B(2)+3=E(5)+3=H(8)+3=K(11)+3=N(14). The difference between consecutive letters is 3.

14. Identify the next in the series: AZ, CX, EV, GT, ___

  1. HR
  2. IR
  3. HS
  4. IS

Answer: IR

Explanation: First letters: A,C,E,G,I (increasing by 2). Second letters: Z,X,V,T,R (decreasing by 2). Next: I,R = IR.

15. Which letter replaces the '?': P, Q, O, R, N, S, ?, T

  1. L
  2. M
  3. N
  4. O

Answer: M

Explanation: Two alternating series: P,O,N,M (decreasing by 1) and Q,R,S,T (increasing by 1). Missing term is M (from the decreasing series).

16. Find the next letter in the series: A, D, G, J, M, ___

  1. N
  2. O
  3. P
  4. Q

Answer: P

Explanation: Each letter is 3 positions ahead: A(1)+3=D(4)+3=G(7)+3=J(10)+3=M(13)+3=P(16). Pattern: skip 2 letters each time (+3).

17. Find the next letter in the series: Y, W, U, S, Q, ___

  1. M
  2. N
  3. O
  4. P

Answer: O

Explanation: Each letter decreases by 2: Y(25), W(23), U(21), S(19), Q(17), O(15).

18. A series: 1, 8, 27, 64, 125, ___

  1. 196
  2. 216
  3. 225
  4. 256

Answer: 216

Explanation: Perfect cubes: 1³=1, 2³=8, 3³=27, 4³=64, 5³=125, 6³=216. The series consists of cubes of consecutive natural numbers.

19. Which number is wrong in the series: 6, 12, 21, 33, 46, 60?

  1. 21
  2. 33
  3. 46
  4. 60

Answer: 46

Explanation: Differences: 6,9,12,15,18 (should increase by 3 each time). 6+6=12, 12+9=21, 21+12=33, 33+15=48 (not 46!), 48+18=66 (not 60). So 46 should be 48, and 60 should be 66. First wrong number is 46 (should be 48).

20. Complete the series: CMM, EOO, GQQ, ___

  1. ISS
  2. IRS
  3. HSS
  4. IST

Answer: ISS

Explanation: Pattern: First letter increases by 2: C,E,G,I. Last two letters are the same and increase by 2 each time: MM,OO,QQ,SS. So next term: I+SS = ISS.

21. Find the wrong number in the series: 7, 28, 63, 124, 215, 342

  1. 28
  2. 63
  3. 124
  4. 215

Answer: 28

Explanation: Pattern: n³-1 for n=2,3,4,5,6,7: 2³-1=7, 3³-1=26 (not 28!), 4³-1=63, 5³-1=124, 6³-1=215, 7³-1=342. 28 should be 26. 28 is the wrong number.

22. What comes next in the series: 1, 11, 21, 1211, 111221, ___

  1. 312211
  2. 312221
  3. 313221
  4. 321211

Answer: 312211

Explanation: Look-and-say sequence: 1 (one 1=11), 11 (two 1s=21), 21 (one 2, one 1=1211), 1211 (one 1, one 2, two 1s=111221), 111221 (three 1s, two 2s, one 1=312211).

23. Find the missing number in the series: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, ?

  1. 9
  2. 11
  3. 13
  4. 15

Answer: 11

Explanation: The series is an Arithmetic Progression (AP) with a common difference of +2:
1 + 2 = 3
3 + 2 = 5
5 + 2 = 7
7 + 2 = 9
Therefore, the missing number is 9 + 2 = 11.

24. Find the missing number in the series: 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, ?

  1. 13
  2. 16
  3. 19
  4. 22

Answer: 16

Explanation: The series is an Arithmetic Progression (AP) with a common difference of +3:
1 + 3 = 4
4 + 3 = 7
7 + 3 = 10
10 + 3 = 13
Therefore, the missing number is 13 + 3 = 16.

25. Find the missing number in the series: 1, 6, 11, 16, 21, ?

  1. 21
  2. 26
  3. 31
  4. 36

Answer: 26

Explanation: The series is an Arithmetic Progression (AP) with a common difference of +5:
1 + 5 = 6
6 + 5 = 11
11 + 5 = 16
16 + 5 = 21
Therefore, the missing number is 21 + 5 = 26.
