26. Find the missing number in the series: 1, 8, 15, 22, 29, ?

  1. 29
  2. 36
  3. 43
  4. 50

Answer: 36

Explanation: The series is an Arithmetic Progression (AP) with a common difference of +7:
1 + 7 = 8
8 + 7 = 15
15 + 7 = 22
22 + 7 = 29
Therefore, the missing number is 29 + 7 = 36.
