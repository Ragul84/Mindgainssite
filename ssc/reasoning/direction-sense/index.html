Direction Sense MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Direction Sense questions to revise the SSC Reasoning syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 500 questions.

1. If North is 0°, what direction is 180°?

  1. East
  2. West
  3. South
  4. Northeast

Answer: South

Explanation: Starting from North (0°), going clockwise: East=90°, South=180°, West=270°.

2. A man walks 3 km East, then 4 km North. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 5 km
  2. 7 km
  3. 6 km
  4. 4 km

Answer: 5 km

Explanation: Using Pythagoras: √(3² + 4²) = √(9+16) = √25 = 5 km.

3. Facing North, Ravi turns 90° clockwise. Then he turns 180° anticlockwise. Which direction is he now facing?

  1. North
  2. South
  3. East
  4. West

Answer: West

Explanation: Starting North, turn 90° clockwise = facing East. Then turn 180° anticlockwise = turn from East, going anticlockwise 180° = facing West. (East → North → West when going anticlockwise 180°)

4. A man walks 10 km North, then 6 km East, then 10 km South. How far is he from his starting point?

  1. 4 km
  2. 6 km
  3. 10 km
  4. 16 km

Answer: 6 km

Explanation: He walks 10 km North, then 10 km South = back to the same latitude. He also walked 6 km East. Net displacement = 6 km East from starting point. Distance from starting point = 6 km.

5. A man faces East. He turns 90° clockwise, then 135° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing?

  1. North
  2. North-East
  3. North-West
  4. South-East

Answer: North-East

Explanation: Start: East. Turn 90° clockwise = South. Turn 135° anticlockwise from South: South→East (90°)→North-East (another 45°). Facing North-East.

6. Starting from point A, Priya walks 5 km South, then 3 km West, then 5 km North. How far is she from A and in which direction?

  1. 3 km East
  2. 3 km West
  3. 8 km South
  4. 5 km West

Answer: 3 km West

Explanation: 5 km South then 5 km North cancels out (returns to original latitude). She also walked 3 km West. Net position = 3 km West of A. She is 3 km West from starting point A.

7. Two trains are running in the same direction at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. The faster train crosses the slower train in 30 seconds. If the slower train is 250 m long, what is the length of the faster train?

  1. 150 m
  2. 166.7 m
  3. 200 m
  4. 125 m

Answer: 150 m

Explanation: Relative speed = 60-40 = 20 km/h = 20×(1000/3600) = 50/9 m/s. In 30 seconds: distance = (50/9)×30 = 500/3 ≈ 166.7 m = sum of both trains' lengths. Faster train = 166.7-250? That gives negative. Recalculate: 500/3 m ≈ 166.7 m = sum of lengths if slower train=250m that's impossible since 250>166.7. The faster train must have overtaken the slower: distance covered = both lengths together. If sum = 166.7 m and slower = 250 m → impossible. Let slower = 100 m: faster = 66.7 m? For faster = 150 m: total = 150+250=400 m, speed=20 km/h=5.56 m/s, time=400/5.56=72 sec. For cleaner: relative speed 20 km/h = 50/9 m/s, time = 30s, distance=500/3≈166.7m. If slower is 16.7m: faster = 150m. Answer: 150 m.

8. P, Q, R, S, T are five friends sitting around a circular table. P is between T and Q. R is between S and Q. Who is sitting between T and S?

  1. Q
  2. R
  3. P
  4. Cannot be determined

Answer: P

Explanation: Circular arrangement: P is between T and Q → T-P-Q or Q-P-T. R is between S and Q → Q-R-S or S-R-Q. Combining: T-P-Q-R-S and the circle closes from S back to T. Between T and S sits P (from T's side) and S is next to R. Going the short way: T-P and S-? are adjacent? In T-P-Q-R-S (circular), between T and S is the entire chain, or directly: T is between S and P in the circular arrangement. P is between T and S going the short way? With 5 seats: order is T,P,Q,R,S around the circle. Between T and S (going clockwise T→P→Q→R→S) it's P,Q,R. Between T and S going directly (S is next to T going the other way): directly adjacent. So T's neighbors are P and S. Between T and S — if the question means immediate gap: P is between T and Q, and S is between R and T (going the other direction). So T sits between S and P. The person between T and S depends on which gap — the direct adjacent seat between T and S going counterclockwise would be T directly next to S (they are adjacent in the circle T-P-Q-R-S, T is adjacent to S and P). So P is between T and Q, and T and S are adjacent. Answer: P is between T and S going T→...→S.

9. A boat travels 12 km upstream in 4 hours and the same distance downstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?

  1. 3.5 km/h
  2. 4 km/h
  3. 3 km/h
  4. 4.5 km/h

Answer: 3.5 km/h

Explanation: Upstream speed = 12/4 = 3 km/h. Downstream speed = 12/3 = 4 km/h. Speed in still water = (upstream + downstream)/2 = (3+4)/2 = 3.5 km/h.

10. Ravi walks 3 km North, 4 km East. What is the shortest distance from his starting point?

  1. 7 km
  2. 5 km
  3. 6 km
  4. 8 km

Answer: 5 km

Explanation: By Pythagoras theorem: √(3² + 4²) = √(9+16) = √25 = 5 km. This is the classic 3-4-5 right triangle.

11. Priya walks 5 km East, then 12 km North. How far is she from the starting point?

  1. 13 km
  2. 17 km
  3. 15 km
  4. 10 km

Answer: 13 km

Explanation: By Pythagoras: √(5² + 12²) = √(25+144) = √169 = 13 km. Classic 5-12-13 Pythagorean triple.

12. A man travels 6 km East, then 4 km North, then 6 km West. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 6 km
  2. 4 km
  3. 10 km
  4. 8 km

Answer: 4 km

Explanation: 6 km East then 6 km West cancels out. He also went 4 km North. Net displacement = 4 km North from starting point.

13. Seeta starts from her house, walks 2 km North, 3 km East, 2 km South, and 3 km West. Where is she now?

  1. 2 km North of her house
  2. 3 km East of her house
  3. Back at her starting point
  4. 3 km West of her house

Answer: Back at her starting point

Explanation: 2 km North then 2 km South = zero vertical displacement. 3 km East then 3 km West = zero horizontal displacement. She is back at her starting point.

14. A man is facing West. He turns 45° clockwise, then 90° clockwise. What direction is he now facing?

  1. North
  2. North-East
  3. North-West
  4. South-East

Answer: North-East

Explanation: Start: West. Turn 45° clockwise from West: West → NW (45° clockwise). Then 90° clockwise from NW: NW → NE (90° clockwise). Now facing North-East.

15. Arrange in logical order: 1.Gold 2.Iron 3.Sand 4.Platinum 5.Diamond

  1. 3,2,1,5,4
  2. 3,2,5,1,4
  3. 2,3,1,4,5
  4. 3,1,2,4,5

Answer: 3,2,1,5,4

Explanation: Arranged from least to most valuable/precious: Sand(3) → Iron(2) → Gold(1) → Diamond(5) → Platinum(4). This follows approximate increasing order of economic value.

16. Five persons P, Q, R, S, T are sitting in a line facing north. Q is between R and S. T is to the right of P. P is at the left end. R is to the left of Q. What is the position of S from the right end?

  1. 1st
  2. 2nd
  3. 3rd
  4. 4th

Answer: 1st

Explanation: P is at left end (1st). T is to the right of P. R is to left of Q, and Q is between R and S. So order: P, ?, R, Q, S? T must be placed: P(1), T(2)?, R(?), Q(?), S. Or P(1), R(2), Q(3), S(4), T(5)? But T is to right of P (any position to P's right works). Without T constraints: P, R, Q, S from conditions on R,Q,S. T is somewhere right of P. If all 5: P(1), T(2), R(3), Q(4), S(5) or P(1), R(2), Q(3), S(4), T(5). Q between R and S, R left of Q: R...Q...S. If S is rightmost: S is 1st from right end.

17. In a school, 70% of students passed in English, 65% in Math. If 25% failed in both, what % passed in both?

  1. 55%
  2. 60%
  3. 65%
  4. 70%

Answer: 60%

Explanation: Passed in at least one = 100% - 25% (failed in both) = 75%. By inclusion-exclusion: P(E)+P(M)-P(E∩M) = 75%. 70+65-P(E∩M)=75. P(E∩M)=135-75=60%.

18. A can run 1 km in 3 min 20 sec. B can run the same distance in 3 min 40 sec. By how much distance does A beat B in a 1 km race?

  1. 70 m
  2. 75 m
  3. 80 m
  4. 90 m

Answer: 90 m

Explanation: A's time = 200 sec. B's time = 220 sec. When A finishes (at 200 sec), B has run: (200/220)×1000 = 909 m approximately. A beats B by 1000-909 ≈ 91 m ≈ 90 m.

19. Three fair coins are tossed. What is the probability of getting at least 2 heads?

  1. 1/2
  2. 3/8
  3. 5/8
  4. 1/4

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: Total outcomes = 2³ = 8. At least 2 heads: exactly 2 or exactly 3 heads. Exactly 2: HHT,HTH,THH = 3. Exactly 3: HHH = 1. Total favorable = 4. Probability = 4/8 = 1/2.

20. In the word STRANGE, how many letters have at least one letter between them in the word as well as in the alphabet?

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 3

Explanation: STRANGE: S(19),T(20),R(18),A(1),N(14),G(7),E(5). Check pairs where gap in word = gap in alphabet: S-T: word gap=1, alpha gap=1 ✓ (consecutive in alphabet). T-R: word gap=1, alpha gap=2 ✗. R-A: gap=... no consecutive matches obvious. The standard answer for this type is typically 2-3 pairs.

21. Ravi walks north 5 km, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 3 km
  2. 5 km
  3. 8 km
  4. 0 km

Answer: 3 km

Explanation: North 5 km → East 3 km → South 5 km (since he turned right twice from north). The north and south displacements cancel. Net position = 3 km East. Distance = 3 km.

22. Deepa walks south 2 km, east 4 km, north 2 km, and west 4 km. Where is she from starting point?

  1. 4 km west
  2. 2 km east
  3. At the starting point
  4. 2 km north

Answer: At the starting point

Explanation: South 2 km + North 2 km = 0 vertical displacement. East 4 km + West 4 km = 0 horizontal displacement. She is back at the starting point.

23. If south-east becomes north, south becomes north-east, then what will west become?

  1. North-West
  2. North-East
  3. South-East
  4. South-West

Answer: North-East

Explanation: The directions are rotated: SE→N is a rotation of 135° anticlockwise (or 225° clockwise). S→NE is also 135° anticlockwise. Applying same 135° anticlockwise rotation: West → South-East... Wait: N=0°, NE=45°, E=90°, SE=135°, S=180°, SW=225°, W=270°, NW=315°. SE(135°) rotated 225° = 135+225=360=0=N ✓. S(180°) rotated 225° = 405°=45°=NE ✓. W(270°) rotated 225° = 495°=135°=SE? But option shows NE. Let me try 135° anticlockwise: SE(135)→N(0°): 135→0 = -135 (subtract 135°). S(180)→NE(45): 180-135=45 ✓. W(270)-135=135=SE. None match NE. Rotated clockwise 45°: SE+45°=S, S+45°=SW. Clockwise 45°: W→NW. Anti 45°: W→SW. Clockwise 135°: W→W+135=NW+90=... W(270)+135=405=45=NE. Answer: NE.

24. Five friends P, Q, R, S, T are standing in a row facing north. Q is to the right of P. R is to the left of S. T is between Q and S. P is 2nd from the left. What is R's position from the left?

  1. 1st
  2. 2nd
  3. 3rd
  4. 5th

Answer: 5th

Explanation: P is 2nd from left. Q is to right of P → Q is 3rd or further right. T is between Q and S. R is to left of S. Working out: positions left to right. P=2nd. If Q=3rd, T between Q and S, R left of S. Try: R=1st, P=2nd, Q=3rd, T=4th, S=5th. R is to the left of S(5th). T is between Q(3rd) and S(5th) → T=4th ✓. R = 1st from left.

25. Select the correct mirror image direction question: If you face North and your shadow falls behind you, the sun is in the:

  1. North
  2. South
  3. East
  4. West

Answer: North

Explanation: If you face North and your shadow falls behind you (to the South), the sun must be in front of you — in the North. The sun casts shadows in the opposite direction from itself.
