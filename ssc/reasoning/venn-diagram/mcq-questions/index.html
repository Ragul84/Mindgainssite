26. Which Venn diagram best illustrates the relationship between 'Teachers', 'Scholars', and 'Students'?

  1. Three separate circles.
  2. 'Teachers' and 'Scholars' partially overlapping, and 'Students' completely separate.
  3. 'Teachers' and 'Scholars' partially overlapping, and 'Students' partially overlapping 'Teachers' but not 'Scholars'.
  4. All three circles partially overlapping each other.

Answer: All three circles partially overlapping each other.

Explanation: Some teachers can be scholars, and some scholars can be teachers, so 'Teachers' and 'Scholars' overlap. Some teachers can also be students (e.g., pursuing higher education), and some students can be scholars. Also, some scholars can be students. Therefore, all three categories can have some common members, leading to partial overlap among all three circles.

27. In the given diagram, the rectangle represents 'Men', the circle represents 'Educated', and the triangle represents 'Urban'. The numbers indicate the population in thousands. How many educated men are not urban?

  1. A. 10
  2. B. 15
  3. C. 5
  4. D. 20

Answer: A. 10

Explanation: We need to find the number of 'Educated Men' (intersection of circle and rectangle) who are NOT 'Urban' (outside the triangle). The region common to the circle and rectangle, but not part of the triangle, is represented by the number 10.

39. In a class of 50 students, 30 like Math and 25 like Science. If 10 students like both Math and Science, how many students like neither Math nor Science?

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 15
  4. 20

Answer: 5

Explanation: Total students liking at least one subject = (30 + 25) - 10 = 45. Students liking neither = 50 - 45 = 5.

  3. Three concentric circles.

41. In a class of 120 students, 60 students passed in Mathematics, 50 students passed in English, and 30 students passed in both Mathematics and English. How many students failed in both subjects?

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 30
  4. 40

Answer: 40

Explanation: Total passed = (Math + English - Both) = 60 + 50 - 30 = 80. Failed in both = Total - Total passed = 120 - 80 = 40.

42. Among a group of 80 people, 45 like coffee, 35 like tea, and 20 like both coffee and tea. How many people like neither coffee nor tea?

  1. 15
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 30

Answer: 20

Explanation: Total who like at least one drink = (Coffee + Tea - Both) = 45 + 35 - 20 = 60. People who like neither = Total - At least one = 80 - 60 = 20.

44. Out of 150 employees, 70 drink coffee, 80 drink tea, and 30 drink neither. How many employees drink both coffee and tea?

  1. 20
  2. 30
  3. 40
  4. 50

Answer: 40

Explanation: Total who drink at least one = Total - Neither = 150 - 30 = 120. Both = (Coffee + Tea) - At least one = 70 + 80 - 120 = 30.

45. A class has 90 students. 50 students play Cricket, 40 play Football, and 20 play both. How many students play exactly one game?

  1. 50
  2. 60
  3. 70
  4. 80

Answer: 60

Explanation: Only Cricket = 50 - 20 = 30. Only Football = 40 - 20 = 20. Exactly one game = Only Cricket + Only Football = 30 + 20 = 50.

46. In a city, 70% of the population reads Newspaper A, 60% reads Newspaper B, and 50% reads Newspaper C. 40% read A and B, 30% read B and C, and 20% read A and C. If 10% read none of the newspapers, what percentage of the population reads exactly two newspapers?

  1. 50%
  2. 40%
  3. 30%
  4. 25%

Answer: 30%

Explanation: Use the principle of inclusion-exclusion to find the percentage reading all three. Then, calculate those reading exactly two by subtracting the 'all three' from the 'two newspaper' overlaps.
