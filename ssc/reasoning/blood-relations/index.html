Blood Relations MCQs with answers

1. Choose the word that is most similar to: 'ENORMOUS' is to 'TINY' as 'FAST' is to:

  1. Quick
  2. Slow
  3. Rapid
  4. Speed

Answer: Slow

Explanation: Enormous and Tiny are antonyms (opposites). Similarly, Fast and Slow are antonyms. The relationship is: word : its antonym. Quick/Rapid are synonyms of Fast; Speed is a noun form. Slow is the antonym of Fast.

2. Statement: Some apples are oranges. Some oranges are grapes. Conclusion I: Some apples are grapes. Conclusion II: No apple is a grape.

  1. Only I follows
  2. Only II follows
  3. Either I or II follows
  4. Neither follows

Answer: Either I or II follows

Explanation: From 'some apples are oranges' and 'some oranges are grapes', we cannot determine the relationship between apples and grapes definitively. It's possible some apples are grapes (if the overlapping oranges are those both). It's equally possible no apples are grapes. Since both are equally possible and one must be true, 'Either I or II follows'.

3. 'Biography' is to 'Person' as 'Autobiography' is to:

  1. Another person
  2. The author's own life
  3. A fictional character
  4. History

Answer: The author's own life

Explanation: Biography: a book about a person's life written by someone else. Autobiography: a book about a person's own life, written by themselves. The relationship: type of life story : whose life it is about.

4. 'France : Paris :: Germany : ___'

  1. Munich
  2. Berlin
  3. Frankfurt
  4. Hamburg

Answer: Berlin

Explanation: Paris is the capital of France. Berlin is the capital of Germany. Capital city relationship.

5. Complete: 'Window is to Glass as Door is to ___'

  1. Open
  2. Frame
  3. Wood
  4. Close

Answer: Wood

Explanation: A Window is primarily made of Glass (functional material). A Door is primarily made of Wood. The relationship is: architectural feature : its primary material.

6. 'Marathon : Long :: Sprint : ___'

  1. Fast
  2. Short
  3. Track
  4. Race

Answer: Short

Explanation: A Marathon is a Long distance race. A Sprint is a Short distance race. The relationship is: type of race : its distance characteristic.

7. 'Tailor : Cloth :: Mason : ___'

  1. Hammer
  2. Tools
  3. Cement/Stone
  4. House

Answer: Cement/Stone

Explanation: A Tailor works with Cloth (craftsperson : material they work with). A Mason works with Cement/Stone (brick and mortar). The relationship is: craftsperson : their primary material.

8. Choose the pair that has a similar relationship as 'Sculptor : Statue':

  1. Reader : Book
  2. Author : Novel
  3. Singer : Song
  4. Teacher : School

Answer: Author : Novel

Explanation: A Sculptor creates a Statue (creator : what they create). Similarly, an Author creates a Novel. A Reader reads a book (doesn't create). A Singer performs a song. A Teacher works at a school (not creates it).

9. 'Arid : Dry :: Fertile : ___'

  1. Sandy
  2. Humid
  3. Productive
  4. Rocky

Answer: Productive

Explanation: Arid means extremely Dry. Similarly, Fertile means very Productive (especially of land — fertile land is highly productive for growing crops). The relationship is synonym pairs.

10. 'Hard work : Success :: Laziness : ___'

  1. Failure
  2. Happiness
  3. Work
  4. Effort

Answer: Failure

Explanation: Hard work leads to Success. Laziness leads to Failure. The relationship is: cause : effect.

11. Pointing to a man, a woman said, 'His mother is the only daughter of my mother.' How is the woman related to the man?

  1. Mother
  2. Sister
  3. Aunt
  4. Grandmother

Answer: Mother

Explanation: The woman's mother's only daughter is the woman herself. Therefore, the man's mother is the woman.

12. If A is the brother of B, C is the father of A, and D is the mother of C, then how is B related to D?

  1. Grandfather
  2. Grandson
  3. Granddaughter
  4. Grandchild

Answer: Grandchild

Explanation: C is the father of A and B, so D is the grandmother of A and B. Thus, B is D's grandchild.

13. If A is the brother of B, C is the father of A, and D is the brother of E, who is the daughter of B, then who is the uncle of D?

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. E

Answer: A

Explanation: D's mother is B. A is B's brother, making A the uncle of D.

14. Pointing to a man in a photograph, a woman says, 'He is the only son of my father's mother-in-law.' How is the man related to the woman?

  1. Maternal Uncle
  2. Brother
  3. Father
  4. Paternal Uncle

Answer: Maternal Uncle

Explanation: The woman's father's mother-in-law is her paternal grandmother. The only son of her paternal grandmother is her maternal uncle.

15. A is the brother of B. C is the daughter of A. D is the sister of E. E is the son of B. How is C related to D?

  1. Cousin
  2. Niece
  3. Aunt
  4. Sister

Answer: Cousin

Explanation: A and B are siblings. C is A's daughter. E is B's son, and D is E's sister, meaning D is also B's daughter. Therefore, C and D are cousins.

16. Introducing a girl, a boy said, 'She is the daughter of the father of my uncle's sister.' How is the girl related to the boy?

  1. Cousin
  2. Sister
  3. Aunt
  4. Niece

Answer: Sister

Explanation: The boy's uncle's sister is either his mother or his aunt. The father of his mother/aunt is his grandfather. The daughter of his grandfather can be his mother or his aunt. Since it's a girl, she is his sister.

17. P is the husband of Q. R is the mother of S and Q. T is the only daughter of R. How is T related to P?

  1. Mother-in-law
  2. Sister-in-law
  3. Aunt
  4. Mother

Answer: Sister-in-law

Explanation: Q is P's wife. R is Q's mother. T is R's only daughter. Since Q is also R's daughter, T and Q are the same person. Therefore, T is P's wife, making her P's sister-in-law if there was another sibling, but as it's 'only daughter', T is Q.

18. If 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A - B' means 'A is the mother of B', 'A x B' means 'A is the brother of B', and 'A ÷ B' means 'A is the sister of B', then which of the following means 'P is the maternal uncle of Q'?

  1. P x M - Q
  2. P x M + Q
  3. P - M x Q
  4. Q - M x P

Answer: P x M - Q

Explanation: P is the maternal uncle of Q means P is the brother of Q's mother. In option (A), M is the mother of Q (M - Q) and P is the brother of M (P x M).

19. Pointing to a photograph, a man says, 'I have no brother or sister, but that man's father is my father's son.' Whose photograph is it?

  1. His own son
  2. His father
  3. His nephew
  4. His uncle

Answer: His own son

Explanation: The man has no siblings, so his father's son must be himself. Therefore, the man in the photograph's father is the speaker himself, making the man in the photograph his son.

20. Introducing a girl, a boy said, 'She is the daughter of the mother of my mother's only son.' How is the boy related to the girl?

  1. Brother
  2. Father
  3. Cousin
  4. Uncle

Answer: Brother

Explanation: The boy's mother's only son is the boy himself. The mother of the boy's mother's only son is the boy's mother. So, the girl is the daughter of the boy's mother, making her his sister.

21. A is the father of B. C is the daughter of A. D is the brother of B. E is the mother of D. F is the brother of E. G is the daughter of F. How is G related to C?

  1. Niece
  2. Cousin
  3. Sister
  4. Aunt

Answer: Cousin

Explanation: E is the mother of D and B, and A is their father. So, A and E are married. F is E's brother, making him C's maternal uncle. G is F's daughter, so G is C's cousin.

22. Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the man related to the woman?

  1. Son
  2. Nephew
  3. Brother
  4. Cousin

Answer: Son

Explanation: The woman's mother's only daughter is the woman herself. So, the man's mother is the woman. Therefore, the man is her son.

23. A is the father of B. C is the daughter of A. D is the sister of B. E is the son of C. How is D related to E?

  1. Aunt
  2. Mother
  3. Niece
  4. Sister

Answer: Aunt

Explanation: A is the father of B and C. D is B's sister, so D is also A's daughter. E is C's son. Since D and C are sisters, D is E's aunt.

24. Pointing to a man in a photograph, Rani said, 'His mother is the only daughter of my mother-in-law.' How is the man related to Rani?

  1. Son
  2. Brother
  3. Cousin
  4. Nephew

Answer: Son

Explanation: Rani's mother-in-law's only daughter is Rani's husband's sister. This sister is the man's mother, making the man Rani's son.

25. Introducing a girl, Rahul said, 'She is the daughter of the only son of my father's wife.' How is the girl related to Rahul?

  1. Daughter
  2. Sister
  3. Niece
  4. Cousin

Answer: Daughter

Explanation: Rahul's father's wife is Rahul's mother. Her only son is Rahul himself. Therefore, the girl is Rahul's daughter.
