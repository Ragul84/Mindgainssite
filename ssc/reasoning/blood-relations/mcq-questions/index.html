26. A is the brother of B. C is the father of D. E is the mother of C. B is the son of C. How is A related to D?

  1. Brother
  2. Uncle
  3. Cousin
  4. Nephew

Answer: Brother

Explanation: B is the son of C, and A is the brother of B, so A is also the son of C. C is the father of D, so A and D are siblings.

27. If 'P @ Q' means 'P is the father of Q', 'P # Q' means 'P is the sister of Q', 'P $ Q' means 'P is the wife of Q', and 'P % Q' means 'P is the mother of Q', then which of the following expressions means 'M is the granddaughter of T'?

  1. T @ K % M
  2. T @ K # M
  3. T % K @ M
  4. T % K % M

Answer: T % K @ M

Explanation: T % K means T is the mother of K. K @ M means K is the father of M. So, T is the grandmother of M, and M is the granddaughter of T.

28. Pointing to a lady, a man said, 'The son of her only brother is the husband of my wife's sister.' How is the lady related to the man?

  1. Mother-in-law
  2. Sister-in-law
  3. Aunt
  4. Sister

Answer: Sister-in-law

Explanation: The man's wife's sister's husband is his brother-in-law. This brother-in-law is the son of the lady's only brother. This means the lady's brother is the father of the man's brother-in-law. Therefore, the lady is the sister of the man's father-in-law, making her his sister-in-law (as in wife's aunt/husband's aunt).
