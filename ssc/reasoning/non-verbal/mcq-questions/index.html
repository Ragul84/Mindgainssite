26. Which of the given options best represents the mirror image of the following figure if the mirror is placed to the right?

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: B

Explanation: The figure is reflected horizontally. The shaded portion and the arrow's head both flip their horizontal orientation.

27. From the given options, select the figure that can be formed by folding the transparent sheet along the dotted line.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: D

Explanation: When folded, the circle will overlap the square, and the triangle will remain separate. The positions and overlaps are crucial.

28. Which of the following figures completes the given series? Series: ▲ △ ▲ △ ?

  1. ▲
  2. ▼
  3. ◆
  4. ■

Answer: ▲

Explanation: The series alternates between an upward-pointing triangle and a downward-pointing triangle. The next figure should be an upward-pointing triangle.

  1. ●
  2. ◎
  3. ◉

30. Which of the following figures completes the given series? [Image 1] [Image 2] [Image 3] [?] Image 1: A square with a small circle in the top-left corner. Image 2: A square with a small circle in the top-right corner. Image 3: A square with a small circle in the bottom-right corner.

  1. A square with a small circle in the bottom-left corner.
  2. A square with a small circle in the top-left corner (same as Image 1).
  3. A square with a small circle in the center.
  4. A square with a small circle in the top-right corner (same as Image 2).

Answer: A square with a small circle in the bottom-left corner.

Explanation: The circle moves clockwise along the corners of the square. Following this pattern, the next position is the bottom-left corner.

31. Select the figure that is different from the rest. A) A cube with all faces shaded. B) A cylinder with both circular bases shaded. C) A sphere with the top half shaded. D) A pyramid with all triangular faces shaded.

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: C

Explanation: Figures A, B, and D are 3D shapes where all faces/surfaces are depicted as being shaded. Figure C shows only a part of the sphere shaded, not its entire surface.

32. Choose the alternative which closely resembles the mirror image of the given combination if the mirror is placed vertically to the right. COMBINE

  1. ƎNIBMOƆ
  2. ENIBMOQ
  3. ƎNIMBOC
  4. ENIMBOC

Answer: ƎNIBMOƆ

Explanation: In a vertical mirror image, the order of letters reverses, and each letter is individually mirrored. The correct mirror image is ƎNIBMOƆ.

33. Which of the following options represents the correct water image of the given figure? [A capital letter 'P']

  1. P (upside down)
  2. d
  3. q
  4. b

Answer: P (upside down)

Explanation: A water image is an inversion of the original figure along a horizontal axis. The capital letter 'P' inverted across a horizontal axis appears as 'P' upside down.

34. Find the missing figure in the following series: [Figure 1] [Figure 2] [?] [Figure 4] Figure 1: A square divided into four smaller squares, with the top-left and bottom-right squares shaded. Figure 2: A square divided into four smaller squares, with the top-right and bottom-left squares shaded. Figure 4: A square divided into four smaller squares, with the top-left and bottom-right squares shaded.

  1. A square divided into four smaller squares, with the top-right and bottom-left squares shaded.
  2. A square divided into four smaller squares, with only the top-left square shaded.
  3. A square divided into four smaller squares, with all four squares shaded.
  4. A square divided into four smaller squares, with only the bottom-right square shaded.

Answer: A square divided into four smaller squares, with the top-right and bottom-left squares shaded.

Explanation: The pattern alternates between shading diagonally opposite pairs of squares. Figure 1 and Figure 4 are identical, suggesting an A-B-A-B pattern. Thus, the missing figure should be identical to Figure 2.

35. Identify the figure that completes the second pair in the same way the first pair is related. Figure 1: A square with a small black circle in its top-left corner, and a white triangle in its bottom-right corner. The circle moves clockwise 90 degrees and the triangle rotates 90 degrees clockwise and inverts its color. Figure 2: A square with a small white square in its top-right corner, and a black star in its bottom-left corner.

  1. A square with a black square in its bottom-right corner and a white star in its top-right corner.
  2. A square with a white square in its bottom-left corner and a black star in its top-left corner.
  3. A square with a black square in its bottom-right corner and a white star in its top-left corner.
  4. A square with a white square in its bottom-right corner and a black star in its top-right corner.

Answer: A square with a black square in its bottom-right corner and a white star in its top-left corner.

Explanation: The first element moves clockwise 90 degrees and inverts color. The second element rotates 90 degrees clockwise, moves to the opposite corner, and inverts color.

36. Which of the following cubes can be formed by folding the given sheet of paper? Sheet of paper shows a cross-shaped net for a cube. The faces are marked as follows: Top-left: 'X', Top-right: 'O', Middle-left: 'Y', Middle-center: 'Z', Middle-right: 'W', Bottom-center: 'V'. Options A, B, C, D show different folded cubes with various combinations of visible faces.

  1. Cube showing 'X', 'Z', 'W' as visible faces, where 'X' is top, 'Z' is front, 'W' is right.
  2. Cube showing 'Y', 'Z', 'V' as visible faces, where 'Y' is top, 'Z' is front, 'V' is right.
  3. Cube showing 'X', 'Y', 'V' as visible faces, where 'X' is top, 'Y' is front, 'V' is right.
  4. Cube showing 'O', 'W', 'V' as visible faces, where 'O' is top, 'W' is front, 'V' is right.

Answer: Cube showing 'X', 'Z', 'W' as visible faces, where 'X' is top, 'Z' is front, 'W' is right.

Explanation: 'X' is opposite 'W', 'Y' is opposite 'O', and 'Z' is opposite 'V'. Option A correctly shows three non-opposite faces adjacent to each other.

37. Find the missing figure in the series: Series: 1. A large black square with a small white circle in its top-left corner. 2. A large white square with a small black triangle in its top-right corner. 3. A large black square with a small white square in its bottom-right corner. 4. ?

  1. A large white square with a small black circle in its bottom-left corner.
  2. A large black square with a small white triangle in its bottom-left corner.
  3. A large white square with a small black triangle in its top-left corner.
  4. A large black square with a small white circle in its top-right corner.

Answer: A large white square with a small black circle in its bottom-left corner.

Explanation: The large square alternates color and the small shape alternates between circle, triangle, square, then repeats. The small shape moves clockwise to the next corner, and its color inverts.
