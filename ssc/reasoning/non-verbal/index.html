Non Verbal MCQs with answers

1. In a mirror image, when you raise your right hand, your image raises:

  1. Left hand
  2. Right hand
  3. Both hands
  4. Neither hand

Answer: Left hand

Explanation: A mirror reverses left and right (lateral inversion). When you raise your right hand, your image appears to raise the left hand.

2. A mirror image of the word 'MANGO' would appear as:

  1. OGNAM
  2. OΓNAM
  3. MANGO reversed
  4. The letters appear reversed left-to-right

Answer: The letters appear reversed left-to-right

Explanation: In a mirror image (vertical mirror), the word appears reversed horizontally — each letter appears flipped left-to-right, and the order of letters is also reversed. The mirror image of 'MANGO' = 'OGNAM' with each letter mirrored individually. The letters become their mirror images.

3. Count the number of triangles in a figure where a large triangle is divided by drawing a line from each vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side (creating 6 small triangles inside). Total triangles = ?

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 12
  4. None of these

Answer: 12

Explanation: When a triangle has lines drawn from each vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side (medians), it creates 6 small triangles. Counting all triangles (small + larger combinations): 6 small + 6 larger = 12 total triangles. This is a standard figure-counting problem.

4. Find the next figure in the pattern where the number of sides increases by 1 each time: Triangle, Square, Pentagon, Hexagon, ___

  1. Heptagon
  2. Octagon
  3. Decagon
  4. Nonagon

Answer: Heptagon

Explanation: Triangle(3), Square(4), Pentagon(5), Hexagon(6), Heptagon(7). Each shape has one more side than the previous. A heptagon has 7 sides.

5. Which of the following is the correct 'embedded figure' concept?

  1. A figure hidden within a complex design
  2. A figure that is a mirror image of another
  3. Two identical figures placed side by side
  4. A figure with missing sides

Answer: A figure hidden within a complex design

Explanation: Embedded figures (hidden figures): a simple figure that is hidden or embedded within a larger, more complex design or pattern. In exams, you are given a simple shape and asked to identify which complex figure it is hidden in. Tests spatial visualization ability.

6. The following figures form a pattern. What would come next? △ □ △△ □□ △△△ ___

  1. □□□
  2. □□□□
  3. △△△△
  4. △△△△△

Answer: □□□

Explanation: Pattern: 1 triangle, 1 square, 2 triangles, 2 squares, 3 triangles, then 3 squares (□□□). The count increases by 1 for each pair, alternating between triangles and squares.

7. How many triangles are in a figure with a large triangle divided by a horizontal line into two rows, giving 4 small triangles total?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 8

Answer: 6

Explanation: A triangle divided into 4 smaller triangles (one horizontal midline): small triangles=4, larger triangles spanning 2-3 small = additional count. In a triangle divided into 4 equal smaller triangles: small(4) + the original large triangle(1) + medium triangles? Standard: 4+3 medium combinations? Usually the answer for 4-small-triangle figure = 4+3+2+1? Actually: a triangle with one horizontal midline creates 3 small triangles (2 bottom, 1 top) + 1 large = 4. The answer varies with exact figure. Standard triangle divided into 4: answer is usually 5 or 6.

8. Find the odd one out: Triangle, Circle, Rectangle, Cube, Square

  1. Circle
  2. Cube
  3. Triangle
  4. Rectangle

Answer: Cube

Explanation: Triangle, Circle, Rectangle, and Square are all 2-dimensional (flat) shapes. Cube is a 3-dimensional solid figure. Cube is the odd one out.

9. A watch shows 3:45. What is the reflection of this time in a clock face mirror?

  1. 8:15
  2. 9:15
  3. 8:45
  4. 9:45

Answer: 8:15

Explanation: Mirror image of clock time: subtract the time from 11:60. 11:60 - 3:45 = 8:15. Alternatively: in a mirror, the hands appear at 12-3:45 = 12 - 3.75 hours from 12 = hands pointing to mirror position. Standard method: mirror time = 12:00 - actual time (if <12) or adjust. 11:60 - 3:45 = 8:15.

10. Which is the next figure if circles, squares, and triangles are placed in order: ○, □, △, ○, □, △, ○, ___

  1. △
  2. ○
  3. □
  4. ◇

Answer: □

Explanation: The pattern repeats: ○, □, △, ○, □, △, ○, □ (square). After the 7th figure (○), the 8th is □ (square).

11. Which of the following figures is different from the others?

  1. [Circle with a horizontal line]
  2. [Square with a vertical line]
  3. [Triangle with a diagonal line]
  4. [Pentagon with a horizontal line]

Answer: [Triangle with a diagonal line]

Explanation: All figures except C have an internal line that is either horizontal or vertical. Figure C has a diagonal line.

12. Find the missing figure in the sequence: [Square] -> [Triangle] -> [Circle] -> ?

  1. [Pentagon]
  2. [Hexagon]
  3. [Rectangle]
  4. [Star]

Answer: [Pentagon]

Explanation: The sequence shows polygons with an increasing number of sides: 4 (Square), 3 (Triangle), 0 (Circle). A pentagon has 5 sides, following a pattern of changing side counts.

19. Which of the following figures best represents the relationship between 'Birds', 'Parrots', and 'Animals'?

  2. [B: Two overlapping circles, with a third circle completely separate.]
  3. [C: Two circles, one inside the other, and a third circle partially overlapping the outer circle.]
  4. [D: Three separate circles.]

Answer: [A: Three concentric circles, with the smallest inside the middle, and the middle inside the largest.]

Explanation: All parrots are birds, and all birds are animals. This forms a hierarchy represented by concentric circles.
