Non Verbal MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Non Verbal questions to revise the SSC Reasoning syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 500 questions.

1. In a mirror image, when you raise your right hand, your image raises:

  1. Left hand
  2. Right hand
  3. Both hands
  4. Neither hand

Answer: Left hand

Explanation: A mirror reverses left and right (lateral inversion). When you raise your right hand, your image appears to raise the left hand.

2. A mirror image of the word 'MANGO' would appear as:

  1. OGNAM
  2. OΓNAM
  3. MANGO reversed
  4. The letters appear reversed left-to-right

Answer: The letters appear reversed left-to-right

Explanation: In a mirror image (vertical mirror), the word appears reversed horizontally — each letter appears flipped left-to-right, and the order of letters is also reversed. The mirror image of 'MANGO' = 'OGNAM' with each letter mirrored individually. The letters become their mirror images.

3. Count the number of triangles in a figure where a large triangle is divided by drawing a line from each vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side (creating 6 small triangles inside). Total triangles = ?

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 12
  4. None of these

Answer: 12

Explanation: When a triangle has lines drawn from each vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side (medians), it creates 6 small triangles. Counting all triangles (small + larger combinations): 6 small + 6 larger = 12 total triangles. This is a standard figure-counting problem.

4. Find the next figure in the pattern where the number of sides increases by 1 each time: Triangle, Square, Pentagon, Hexagon, ___

  1. Heptagon
  2. Octagon
  3. Decagon
  4. Nonagon

Answer: Heptagon

Explanation: Triangle(3), Square(4), Pentagon(5), Hexagon(6), Heptagon(7). Each shape has one more side than the previous. A heptagon has 7 sides.

5. Which of the following is the correct 'embedded figure' concept?

  1. A figure hidden within a complex design
  2. A figure that is a mirror image of another
  3. Two identical figures placed side by side
  4. A figure with missing sides

Answer: A figure hidden within a complex design

Explanation: Embedded figures (hidden figures): a simple figure that is hidden or embedded within a larger, more complex design or pattern. In exams, you are given a simple shape and asked to identify which complex figure it is hidden in. Tests spatial visualization ability.
