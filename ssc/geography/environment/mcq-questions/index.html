26. Which of the following is a type of natural hazard?

  1. Drought
  2. Flood
  3. Earthquake
  4. All of the above

Answer: All of the above

Explanation: All of the above are types of natural hazards, posing threats to human populations and the environment.

27. What is the term for the movement of people from rural to urban areas?

  1. Urbanization
  2. Ruralization
  3. Suburbanization
  4. Migration

Answer: Urbanization

Explanation: Urbanization refers to the movement of people from rural to urban areas in search of better economic opportunities.

28. Which of the following is a renewable source of energy?

  1. Coal
  2. Oil
  3. Natural gas
  4. Solar energy

Answer: Solar energy

Explanation: Solar energy is a renewable source of energy, generated from the sun's rays.

29. What is the term for the movement of water from the ground to the atmosphere through plants?

  1. Evapotranspiration
  2. Condensation
  3. Infiltration
  4. Percolation

Answer: Evapotranspiration

Explanation: Evapotranspiration is the movement of water from the ground to the atmosphere through plants.

30. What is the term for the movement of animals from one region to another at certain times of the year?

  1. Migration
  2. Hibernation
  3. Camouflage
  4. Adaptation

Answer: Migration

Explanation: Migration refers to the seasonal movement of animals between regions in search of food, shelter, or breeding grounds.

31. Which international agreement aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions?

  1. Paris Agreement
  2. Kyoto Protocol
  3. Montreal Protocol
  4. Stockholm Convention

Answer: Paris Agreement

Explanation: The Paris Agreement is an international agreement to mitigate climate change by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

32. What is the term for the variety of different species that live in an ecosystem?

  1. Biodiversity
  2. Ecosystem
  3. Habitat
  4. Population

Answer: Biodiversity

Explanation: Biodiversity refers to the variety of different species that coexist in an ecosystem.

33. What is the process by which the Earth's climate is warmed by greenhouse gases?

  1. Global warming
  2. Climate change
  3. Ozone depletion
  4. Acid rain

Answer: Global warming

Explanation: Global warming refers to the warming of the Earth's climate due to increased levels of greenhouse gases.

34. Which organization is responsible for monitoring and protecting the environment globally?

  1. UNEP
  2. WHO
  3. UNESCO
  4. IUCN

Answer: UNEP

Explanation: The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is responsible for coordinating global environmental efforts.

35. What is the primary cause of global warming?

  1. Deforestation
  2. Pollution
  3. Greenhouse gases
  4. Overpopulation

Answer: Greenhouse gases

Explanation: Global warming is primarily caused by greenhouse gases, which trap heat in the atmosphere.

36. Which of the following is a renewable resource?

  1. Coal
  2. Petroleum
  3. Solar energy
  4. Natural gas

Answer: Solar energy

Explanation: Solar energy is a renewable resource as it is replenished naturally.

37. What is the process by which water moves through a plant?

  1. Respiration
  2. Photosynthesis
  3. Transpiration
  4. Evaporation

Answer: Transpiration

Explanation: Transpiration is the process by which water moves through a plant.

38. Which organization is responsible for environmental protection at the international level?

  1. UNEP
  2. WHO
  3. UNICEF
  4. ILO

Answer: UNEP

Explanation: UNEP is the United Nations Environment Programme, responsible for environmental protection.

39. Which of the following is a consequence of acid rain?

  1. Soil erosion
  2. Water pollution
  3. Deforestation
  4. Acidification of lakes

Answer: Acidification of lakes

Explanation: Acid rain can cause acidification of lakes, harming aquatic life.

40. What is the term for the conservation of water and reduction of waste?

  1. Water harvesting
  2. Water conservation
  3. Watershed management
  4. Sustainable development

Answer: Water conservation

Explanation: Water conservation refers to the conservation of water and reduction of waste.

41. Which of the following is a benefit of afforestation?

  1. Soil erosion
  2. Deforestation
  3. Increased biodiversity
  4. Climate change

Answer: Increased biodiversity

Explanation: Afforestation can increase biodiversity by providing habitat for various species.

42. Which of the following types of pollution is caused by the release of hot water into water bodies?

  1. Thermal pollution
  2. Chemical pollution
  3. Noise pollution
  4. Light pollution

Answer: Thermal pollution

Explanation: Thermal pollution occurs when hot water is released into water bodies, affecting the temperature and ecosystem.

43. Which of the following international agreements aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions?

  1. Kyoto Protocol
  2. Paris Agreement
  3. Montreal Protocol
  4. Stockholm Convention

Answer: Paris Agreement

Explanation: The Paris Agreement is an international agreement that aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and mitigate climate change.

44. Which of the following types of energy is generated from the movement of the ocean tides?

  1. Solar energy
  2. Wind energy
  3. Hydro energy
  4. Tidal energy

Answer: Tidal energy

Explanation: Tidal energy is generated from the movement of the ocean tides, which can be harnessed to produce electricity.

45. What is the term for the area of land that drains water into a particular river or sea?

  1. Watershed
  2. Drainage basin
  3. Catchment area
  4. River basin

Answer: Drainage basin

Explanation: A drainage basin is the area of land that drains water into a particular river or sea, also known as a catchment area or watershed.

46. Which of the following is a benefit of using renewable energy sources?

  1. Reduced air pollution
  2. Increased greenhouse gas emissions
  3. Higher energy costs
  4. Increased dependence on fossil fuels

Answer: Reduced air pollution

Explanation: Using renewable energy sources can reduce air pollution by decreasing the amount of harmful emissions released into the atmosphere.

47. What is the primary cause of ocean acidification?

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Methane
  3. Nitrous oxide
  4. Ozone

Answer: Carbon dioxide

Explanation: Ocean acidification is primarily caused by the absorption of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

48. Which pollutant is responsible for the formation of ground-level ozone?

  1. Nitrogen oxides
  2. Volatile organic compounds
  3. Particulate matter
  4. Carbon monoxide

Answer: Volatile organic compounds

Explanation: Volatile organic compounds react with nitrogen oxides to form ground-level ozone.

49. What is the name of the international agreement aimed at reducing greenhouse gas emissions?

  1. Paris Agreement
  2. Kyoto Protocol
  3. Montreal Protocol
  4. Stockholm Convention

Answer: Paris Agreement

Explanation: The Paris Agreement is an international agreement aimed at mitigating climate change.

50. Which type of rock is formed from the cooling and solidification of magma?

  1. Igneous
  2. Sedimentary
  3. Metamorphic
  4. Foliated

Answer: Igneous

Explanation: Igneous rocks are formed from the cooling and solidification of magma or lava.
