World Geo MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked World Geo questions to revise the SSC Geography syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 149 questions.

26. Dispur (part of Guwahati) is the capital of:

  1. Assam
  2. Meghalaya
  3. Nagaland
  4. Mizoram

Answer: Assam

Explanation: Dispur (a locality within Guwahati) is the capital of Assam. Guwahati itself is the largest city in Assam and Northeast India. Assam's state legislative assembly is in Dispur.

27. The Equator passes through which continents?

  1. South America, Africa, and Asia (through the islands of Indonesia)
  2. Only Africa and South America
  3. Only Africa
  4. All continents

Answer: South America, Africa, and Asia (through the islands of Indonesia)

Explanation: The Equator (0° latitude) passes through 3 continents: South America (Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil), Africa (Gabon, Congo, DR Congo, Uganda, Kenya, Somalia), and Asia (Indonesian islands — Sumatra, Borneo, Sulawesi).

28. The Khyber Pass connects:

  1. Pakistan (Peshawar) and Afghanistan (Kabul) — historic invasion route into India
  2. India and Pakistan
  3. India and China
  4. Afghanistan and Iran

Answer: Pakistan (Peshawar) and Afghanistan (Kabul) — historic invasion route into India

Explanation: Khyber Pass (3.5 km wide at narrowest) connects Peshawar (Pakistan) with Kabul (Afghanistan). Historically the most important invasion route into the Indian subcontinent — used by Persians, Greeks, Mughals.

29. Which ocean surrounds India on three sides?

  1. Indian Ocean (with Bay of Bengal on east and Arabian Sea on west)
  2. Pacific Ocean
  3. Atlantic Ocean
  4. Southern Ocean

Answer: Indian Ocean (with Bay of Bengal on east and Arabian Sea on west)

Explanation: India is a peninsula surrounded by water on three sides: Bay of Bengal (east), Arabian Sea (west), and Indian Ocean (south). This has made India a maritime trading nation since ancient times.

30. Pune (Maharashtra) is called:

  1. 'Oxford of the East' for its numerous educational institutions
  2. 'Silicon Valley of India'
  3. 'IT capital of India'
  4. 'Gateway of India'

Answer: 'Oxford of the East' for its numerous educational institutions

Explanation: Pune is called 'Oxford of the East' for its many universities and colleges (Pune University, Symbiosis, Fergusson College, etc.). It is also a major IT and automobile hub. Peshwas made it their capital.

31. The capital of India is:

  1. Mumbai
  2. Kolkata
  3. New Delhi
  4. Hyderabad

Answer: New Delhi

Explanation: New Delhi is the capital of India and houses the Parliament, President's House, and Supreme Court.

32. Which of these cities is at the highest altitude?

  1. Mumbai
  2. Chennai
  3. Kolkata
  4. Shimla

Answer: Shimla

Explanation: Shimla, the capital of Himachal Pradesh, is at an altitude of about 2,206 meters (7,238 feet), the highest among these cities.

33. The largest continent in the world is:

  1. Africa
  2. North America
  3. Asia
  4. Australia

Answer: Asia

Explanation: Asia is the world's largest continent (44.6 million km², ~29.5% of Earth's land area). It has the largest population (4.7 billion, ~60% of world). Asia has the highest peak (Everest), lowest point (Dead Sea), and largest lake (Caspian Sea).

34. The International Date Line roughly follows:

  1. The Prime Meridian (0°)
  2. The Equator
  3. The 180° meridian (with deviations for island nations)
  4. The Tropic of Cancer

Answer: The 180° meridian (with deviations for island nations)

Explanation: The International Date Line (IDL) roughly follows the 180° meridian in the Pacific Ocean, but deviates to avoid splitting countries. Going west across the IDL, the date advances by one day. Going east, the date goes back by one day.

35. The deepest ocean trench in the world is:

  1. Java Trench
  2. Puerto Rico Trench
  3. Mariana Trench (Pacific Ocean, ~11,034m deep)
  4. Tonga Trench

Answer: Mariana Trench (Pacific Ocean, ~11,034m deep)

Explanation: The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific Ocean is the world's deepest point at ~11,034m (Challenger Deep). James Cameron reached the bottom in 2012 in a submersible.

36. The 'Ring of Fire' refers to:

  1. A volcanic ring around Africa
  2. The zone of earthquakes and volcanoes around the Pacific Ocean basin
  3. A meteorological phenomenon
  4. Desert fires in Australia

Answer: The zone of earthquakes and volcanoes around the Pacific Ocean basin

Explanation: The Ring of Fire is a 40,000 km horseshoe-shaped zone around the Pacific Ocean, containing 75% of the world's volcanoes and ~90% of its earthquakes. It follows tectonic plate boundaries — Pacific Plate, Philippine Plate, Juan de Fuca Plate, etc.

37. The International Date Line runs approximately along:

  1. 180° Meridian (with deviations)
  2. Prime Meridian (0°)
  3. Equator
  4. 23.5°N

Answer: 180° Meridian (with deviations)

Explanation: The International Date Line runs approximately along the 180° meridian in the Pacific Ocean, deviating to avoid splitting countries. Crossing it westward gains a day; eastward loses a day.

38. The smallest country in the world (by area) is:

  1. Vatican City
  2. Monaco
  3. San Marino
  4. Nauru

Answer: Vatican City

Explanation: Vatican City (0.44 sq km) is the world's smallest country by both area and population. It is an independent city-state within Rome, Italy, and is the headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church.

39. The country with the largest area in the world is:

  1. Russia
  2. Canada
  3. USA
  4. China

Answer: Russia

Explanation: Russia (17.1 million sq km) is the world's largest country by area, followed by Canada (10 million sq km) and USA (9.8 million sq km). Russia spans 11 time zones.

40. The most populous country in the world (as of 2024) is:

  1. India
  2. China
  3. USA
  4. Indonesia

Answer: India

Explanation: India surpassed China in population in 2023, becoming the world's most populous country with about 1.44 billion people. China's population is declining due to its one-child policy effects.

41. The Dead Sea (Israel/Jordan border) is famous for:

  1. Being the lowest point on Earth's land surface and extreme salinity
  2. Extreme cold
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Being the largest saltwater lake

Answer: Being the lowest point on Earth's land surface and extreme salinity

Explanation: The Dead Sea (430 m below sea level) is the lowest point on Earth's land surface. Its salinity is about 34% (10 times the ocean), making it impossible for most aquatic life to survive — hence 'Dead' Sea.

42. The Ring of Fire is a zone of:

  1. Volcanoes and earthquakes surrounding the Pacific Ocean
  2. Volcanic activity in the Indian Ocean
  3. Typhoon activity
  4. Seismic activity in the Atlantic

Answer: Volcanoes and earthquakes surrounding the Pacific Ocean

Explanation: The Ring of Fire is a horseshoe-shaped zone of volcanic activity and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean rim. It has 75% of the world's active volcanoes and experiences 90% of the world's earthquakes.

43. The Mariana Trench (deepest point on Earth, ~11,034 m) is in:

  1. Pacific Ocean (west of Mariana Islands)
  2. Atlantic Ocean
  3. Indian Ocean
  4. Arctic Ocean

Answer: Pacific Ocean (west of Mariana Islands)

Explanation: The Mariana Trench is in the western Pacific Ocean, east of the Mariana Islands. Challenger Deep (10,929 m in the Mariana Trench) is the deepest known point on Earth.

44. Which continent is known as the 'Dark Continent' due to its largely unexplored interior in the past?

  1. Asia
  2. Africa
  3. South America
  4. Australia

Answer: Africa

Explanation: Africa was historically referred to as the 'Dark Continent' because much of its interior remained unexplored by Europeans for a long time.

45. The world's largest desert, the Sahara Desert, is primarily located on which continent?

  1. Asia
  2. North America
  3. Africa
  4. South America

Answer: Africa

Explanation: The Sahara Desert, the largest hot desert in the world, covers a vast area across North Africa.

46. Which of the following mountain ranges is primarily located in South America and is the longest continental mountain range in the world?

  1. Andes
  2. Himalayas
  3. Rocky Mountains
  4. Alps

Answer: Andes

Explanation: The Andes Mountains are the longest continental mountain range, stretching along the western coast of South America. The others are located on different continents.

47. The Strait of Gibraltar connects which two major bodies of water and separates which two continents?

  1. Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea; Europe and Africa
  2. Indian Ocean and Pacific Ocean; Asia and Australia
  3. Red Sea and Arabian Sea; Africa and Asia
  4. Black Sea and Aegean Sea; Europe and Asia

Answer: Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea; Europe and Africa

Explanation: The Strait of Gibraltar is a narrow strait that connects the Atlantic Ocean to the Mediterranean Sea and separates Gibraltar and Peninsular Spain in Europe from Morocco and Ceuta in Africa.

48. Which desert is known for being the largest hot desert in the world and covers a significant portion of North Africa?

  1. Sahara Desert
  2. Arabian Desert
  3. Gobi Desert
  4. Kalahari Desert

Answer: Sahara Desert

Explanation: The Sahara Desert is the largest hot desert globally, spanning across much of North Africa. The others are also deserts but not the largest hot one or located in North Africa.

49. The Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, is located off the coast of which country?

  1. Australia
  2. Philippines
  3. Indonesia
  4. Brazil

Answer: Australia

Explanation: The Great Barrier Reef is a natural wonder located off the coast of Queensland, Australia. It is the world's largest coral reef system.

50. Which of the following rivers flows through the most number of countries in Europe?

  1. Danube River
  2. Rhine River
  3. Volga River
  4. Elbe River

Answer: Danube River

Explanation: The Danube River flows through a remarkable ten countries in Central and Eastern Europe. The other rivers flow through fewer countries.
