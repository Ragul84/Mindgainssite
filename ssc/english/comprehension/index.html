Comprehension MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Comprehension questions to revise the SSC English syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 400 questions.

1. The recent surge in global food prices, exacerbated by supply chain disruptions and geopolitical tensions, has raised concerns about food security in vulnerable nations. Experts warn that without concerted international efforts to stabilize markets and boost agricultural productivity, millions could face severe hunger. This situation underscores the urgent need for resilient food systems and diversified sourcing strategies.

  1. The passage primarily discusses the immediate causes and potential consequences of rising food prices.
  2. The passage highlights the success of current international efforts in addressing food security.
  3. The passage focuses on the agricultural productivity of developed nations.
  4. The passage suggests that geopolitical tensions are the sole cause of food price inflation.

Answer: The passage primarily discusses the immediate causes and potential consequences of rising food prices.

Explanation: The passage details the reasons for the price surge and its negative impact, which aligns with the first option. It does not focus on success, specific nations, or a single cause.

2. Despite significant advancements in artificial intelligence, the ethical implications of its widespread deployment remain a subject of intense debate. Concerns range from job displacement and algorithmic bias to issues of privacy and accountability. Striking a balance between innovation and responsible development is crucial for harnessing AI's potential without compromising societal values.

  1. What is the central theme of the provided passage?
  2. The passage suggests that AI's ethical implications are easily resolvable.
  3. The passage primarily focuses on the technical challenges of AI development.
  4. The passage advocates for unrestricted AI development to accelerate innovation.

Answer: What is the central theme of the provided passage?

Explanation: The passage revolves around the ethical challenges of AI and the need for balance. This directly corresponds to the central theme of the passage.

3. The recent policy shifts concerning renewable energy tariffs have ignited a fervent debate among stakeholders. While proponents laud the move as a necessary step towards fiscal prudence and market rationalization, critics argue it could stifle nascent industries and deter foreign investment. The long-term implications for India's ambitious climate targets remain uncertain, contingent on how these adjustments are absorbed by the energy sector and the subsequent investment climate they foster.

  1. The primary purpose of the passage is to explain the technical aspects of renewable energy tariffs.
  2. The passage suggests that the new policy will unequivocally boost foreign investment in renewable energy.
  3. The author presents a balanced view on the recent policy changes, highlighting both potential benefits and drawbacks.
  4. The passage implies that the government's main goal is to reduce India's climate targets.

Answer: The author presents a balanced view on the recent policy changes, highlighting both potential benefits and drawbacks.

Explanation: The passage discusses both positive and negative perspectives on the policy shifts, indicating a balanced viewpoint. It does not lean definitively towards one outcome.

4. Despite impressive economic growth figures, a closer examination reveals a persistent disparity in wealth distribution across various demographics. This chasm, often exacerbated by technological advancements and automation, poses a significant challenge to long-term social cohesion and sustainable development. Addressing this requires not just economic policy adjustments but also a renewed focus on inclusive education and skill development programs to ensure a broader participation in the economic mainstream.

  1. The passage primarily discusses the benefits of technological advancements on wealth distribution.
  2. The author believes that economic growth alone is sufficient to resolve wealth disparity.
  3. The passage suggests that wealth disparity is a complex issue requiring multi-faceted solutions beyond just economic policies.
  4. Technological advancements are presented as the sole cause of wealth disparity.

Answer: The passage suggests that wealth disparity is a complex issue requiring multi-faceted solutions beyond just economic policies.

Explanation: The passage highlights that wealth disparity is a complex issue requiring economic policy, inclusive education, and skill development. It does not attribute disparity solely to technology or suggest economic growth is a standalone solution.

5. The burgeoning gig economy, while offering unprecedented flexibility to workers and businesses, also presents a quagmire of regulatory challenges. Traditional labor laws, designed for a more conventional employer-employee relationship, often struggle to encompass the fluid nature of gig work, leaving many workers vulnerable without adequate social security or benefits. Policymakers are grappling with how to foster innovation without compromising worker protections in this evolving landscape.

  1. The passage primarily praises the gig economy for its flexibility and benefits to workers.
  2. The term 'quagmire' in this context most closely means a simple, easily solvable problem.
  3. The author suggests that existing labor laws are perfectly adequate for the gig economy.
  4. The passage highlights the regulatory difficulties in balancing innovation with worker protection in the gig economy.

Answer: The passage highlights the regulatory difficulties in balancing innovation with worker protection in the gig economy.

Explanation: The passage explicitly states that policymakers are grappling with balancing innovation and worker protection due to the regulatory challenges. 'Quagmire' implies a difficult and complex situation.
