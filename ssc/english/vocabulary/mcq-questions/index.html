26. What does the phrase 'beat around the bush' mean?

  1. To be direct
  2. To avoid talking
  3. To talk about something indirectly
  4. To give up

Answer: To talk about something indirectly

Explanation: It means to talk about something indirectly or avoid talking about it altogether.

27. What is the meaning of the word 'nuance'?

  1. A subtle difference
  2. A big change
  3. A loud noise
  4. A bright color

Answer: A subtle difference

Explanation: It refers to a subtle difference or shade of meaning.

28. What does the phrase 'call it a day' mean?

  1. To start work
  2. To stop work
  3. To take a break
  4. To go home

Answer: To stop work

Explanation: It means to stop what you are doing and go home for the day.

29. What is the meaning of the word 'verbose'?

Explanation: It means using many words, often unnecessarily.
