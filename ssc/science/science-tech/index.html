Science Tech MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Science Tech questions to revise the SSC Science syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 417 questions.

1. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

  1. Venus
  2. Mars
  3. Jupiter
  4. Saturn

Answer: Mars

Explanation: Mars is called the Red Planet because its surface is covered with iron oxide (rust), giving it a reddish appearance.

2. Which is the hardest natural substance on Earth?

  1. Gold
  2. Iron
  3. Diamond
  4. Granite

Answer: Diamond

Explanation: Diamond is the hardest natural substance with a Mohs hardness of 10. It is a form of carbon with atoms arranged in a crystal lattice.

3. How many bones does an adult human body have?

  1. 104
  2. 186
  3. 206
  4. 312

Answer: 206

Explanation: An adult human skeleton has 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270-300 bones; many fuse together as we grow.

4. The process by which water turns into vapour is called:

  1. Condensation
  2. Precipitation
  3. Evaporation
  4. Transpiration

Answer: Evaporation

Explanation: Evaporation is the process by which water changes from liquid state to vapour (gaseous) state when heated.

5. Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?

  1. Oxygen
  2. Carbon dioxide
  3. Argon
  4. Nitrogen

Answer: Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen (N2) makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere, followed by oxygen (21%), and argon (0.9%).

6. Malaria is caused by:

  1. Bacteria
  2. Virus
  3. Protozoan (Plasmodium)
  4. Fungus

Answer: Protozoan (Plasmodium)

Explanation: Malaria is caused by Plasmodium parasites, which are protozoa. The parasite is transmitted to humans through the bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.

7. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?

  1. Gold
  2. Lead
  3. Mercury
  4. Iron

Answer: Mercury

Explanation: Mercury (Hg) is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature (melting point: -39°C). It is used in thermometers and barometers.

8. The 'Ohm's Law' states that:

  1. V = IR (Voltage = Current × Resistance)
  2. P = VI (Power = Voltage × Current)
  3. F = ma (Force = mass × acceleration)
  4. E = mc² (Energy = mass × speed of light squared)

Answer: V = IR (Voltage = Current × Resistance)

Explanation: Ohm's Law: V = IR. Voltage (V) across a conductor is directly proportional to the Current (I) through it, with Resistance (R) as the proportionality constant.

9. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination?

  1. Cerebrum
  2. Medulla Oblongata
  3. Cerebellum
  4. Thalamus

Answer: Cerebellum

Explanation: The cerebellum is responsible for coordination of movement, balance, and fine motor skills.

10. The law of conservation of energy states:

  1. Energy can be created from nothing
  2. Energy can be destroyed under special conditions
  3. Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted
  4. Energy increases over time

Answer: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted

Explanation: The Law of Conservation of Energy (First Law of Thermodynamics) states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed from one form to another.

11. The SI unit of electric current is:

  1. Volt
  2. Ohm
  3. Ampere
  4. Watt

Answer: Ampere

Explanation: The SI unit of electric current is the Ampere (A), named after French physicist André-Marie Ampère.

12. The SI unit of pressure is:

  1. Newton
  2. Pascal
  3. Joule
  4. Bar

Answer: Pascal

Explanation: The SI unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa), named after Blaise Pascal. 1 Pa = 1 Newton per square metre (N/m²).

13. The splitting of an atom is called:

  1. Nuclear Fusion
  2. Nuclear Fission
  3. Radioactive Decay
  4. Ionization

Answer: Nuclear Fission

Explanation: Nuclear Fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus (like U-235) into smaller nuclei, releasing large amounts of energy. This is the principle used in nuclear reactors and atomic bombs.

14. Which scientist discovered the structure of DNA (Double Helix)?

  1. Louis Pasteur and Robert Koch
  2. Watson and Crick (with Franklin's X-ray data)
  3. Mendel and Darwin
  4. Hershey and Chase

Answer: Watson and Crick (with Franklin's X-ray data)

Explanation: James Watson and Francis Crick discovered the double helix structure of DNA in 1953, using X-ray crystallography data from Rosalind Franklin. They received the Nobel Prize in 1962.

15. The unit of pressure is:

  1. Pascal (Pa)
  2. Newton (N)
  3. Joule (J)
  4. Watt (W)

Answer: Pascal (Pa)

Explanation: The SI unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa). 1 Pa = 1 N/m². Atmospheric pressure at sea level is about 101,325 Pa (1 atm). Named after Blaise Pascal.

16. Bernoulli's Principle states that:

  1. In a fluid, increased velocity causes decreased pressure
  2. Pressure increases with depth
  3. Buoyancy equals weight of displaced fluid
  4. Gases expand when heated

Answer: In a fluid, increased velocity causes decreased pressure

Explanation: Bernoulli's Principle: In a streamlined fluid flow, as velocity increases, pressure decreases. This explains lift in airplane wings, the Venturi effect, and why shower curtains are pulled inward.

17. Ohm's Law states that:

  1. Current (I) = Voltage (V) / Resistance (R)
  2. Voltage = Current × Power
  3. Resistance = Current × Voltage
  4. Power = Resistance × Current

Answer: Current (I) = Voltage (V) / Resistance (R)

Explanation: Ohm's Law: V = I × R (Voltage = Current × Resistance). At constant temperature, current is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance. Named after Georg Simon Ohm.

18. Electric power is given by:

  1. P = V × I (Voltage × Current)
  2. P = V / I
  3. P = I / V
  4. P = R × I

Answer: P = V × I (Voltage × Current)

Explanation: Electric Power (P) = Voltage (V) × Current (I) = I²R = V²/R. The SI unit of power is Watt (W). 1 Watt = 1 Joule per second. A 60W bulb consumes 60 Joules per second.

19. A fuse wire in an electrical circuit is made of:

  1. Alloy with low melting point (tin-lead alloy)
  2. Copper
  3. Aluminium
  4. Iron

Answer: Alloy with low melting point (tin-lead alloy)

Explanation: Fuse wire is made of an alloy with a low melting point (typically tin-lead alloy). When current exceeds safe limits, the fuse wire melts (fuses), breaking the circuit and preventing damage. It is the weakest link in the circuit.

20. Electromagnets are used in:

  1. Electric bells, cranes, MRI machines, electric motors
  2. Only loudspeakers
  3. Only motors
  4. Only generators

Answer: Electric bells, cranes, MRI machines, electric motors

Explanation: Electromagnets (coil of wire wound on iron core carrying current) are used in: electric bells, cranes for lifting scrap metal, MRI machines, electric motors, generators, relays, loudspeakers, and maglev trains.

21. Ionic bonds form between:

  1. Metals and non-metals (transfer of electrons)
  2. Two non-metals
  3. Two metals
  4. Non-metals and noble gases

Answer: Metals and non-metals (transfer of electrons)

Explanation: Ionic bonds form when one atom (usually metal) transfers electrons to another (usually non-metal), forming positive (cation) and negative (anion) ions that attract each other. Example: NaCl (table salt) — Na⁺ + Cl⁻.

22. The most abundant gas in the Earth's atmosphere is:

  1. Nitrogen (about 78%)
  2. Oxygen (21%)
  3. Carbon dioxide
  4. Argon

Answer: Nitrogen (about 78%)

Explanation: Earth's atmosphere composition: Nitrogen (N₂) ~78%, Oxygen (O₂) ~21%, Argon ~0.9%, Carbon dioxide ~0.04%, and trace amounts of other gases. The 1% (besides N₂ and O₂) is mainly Argon.

23. Which gas is responsible for global warming?

  1. Carbon dioxide (CO₂) — main greenhouse gas, also methane, N₂O, ozone
  2. Nitrogen
  3. Oxygen
  4. Argon

Answer: Carbon dioxide (CO₂) — main greenhouse gas, also methane, N₂O, ozone

Explanation: Carbon dioxide (CO₂) is the primary greenhouse gas causing global warming/climate change. Methane (CH₄), Nitrous oxide (N₂O), and CFCs are other significant greenhouse gases. CO₂ traps outgoing infrared radiation.

24. The hardest natural substance is:

  1. Diamond (pure carbon in cubic structure)
  2. Corundum (Sapphire/Ruby)
  3. Topaz
  4. Quartz

Answer: Diamond (pure carbon in cubic structure)

Explanation: Diamond (10 on Mohs scale) is the hardest natural substance — carbon atoms arranged in a strong 3D covalent lattice (tetrahedral arrangement). Graphite is also pure carbon but arranged in layers (soft, used as lubricant).

25. Rusting of iron is an example of:

  1. Oxidation (iron reacts with oxygen and water to form iron oxide)
  2. Reduction
  3. Neutralization
  4. Fermentation

Answer: Oxidation (iron reacts with oxygen and water to form iron oxide)

Explanation: Rusting is oxidation: 4Fe + 3O₂ + 6H₂O → 4Fe(OH)₃ → 2Fe₂O₃·3H₂O (hydrated iron oxide). Prevention: painting, galvanizing (zinc coating), oiling, stainless steel (iron + chromium alloy).
