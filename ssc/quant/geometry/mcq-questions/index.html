26. A point P(x, y) is equidistant from the points A(1, 4) and B(-1, 2). Which of the following equations represents the locus of P?

  1. x + y = 3
  2. x - y = 3
  3. 2x + y = 5
  4. x + 2y = 7

Answer: x + y = 3

Explanation: Since P(x, y) is equidistant from A(1, 4) and B(-1, 2), PA = PB.
PA^2 = PB^2
(x - 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = (x - (-1))^2 + (y - 2)^2
(x - 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = (x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2
x^2 - 2x + 1 + y^2 - 8y + 16 = x^2 + 2x + 1 + y^2 - 4y + 4
-2x - 8y + 17 = 2x - 4y + 5
Rearrange the terms:
4x + 4y = 12
Divide by 4:
x + y = 3

27. A right circular cylinder has a radius of 7 cm and a height of 10 cm. A cone of the same radius and height is removed from the cylinder. What is the volume of the remaining solid?

  1. 1540 cubic cm
  2. 1026.67 cubic cm
  3. 770 cubic cm
  4. 513.33 cubic cm

Answer: 1026.67 cubic cm

Explanation: Volume of the cylinder = pi * r^2 * h = (22/7) * (7)^2 * 10 = (22/7) * 49 * 10 = 22 * 7 * 10 = 1540 cubic cm.
Volume of the cone = (1/3) * pi * r^2 * h = (1/3) * 1540 = 1540 / 3 cubic cm.
Volume of the remaining solid = Volume of cylinder - Volume of cone = 1540 - (1540/3) = 1540 * (1 - 1/3) = 1540 * (2/3).
Volume of remaining solid = 3080 / 3 = 1026.666... cubic cm, which is approximately 1026.67 cubic cm.

  3. 60 degrees
  4. 75 degrees

Answer: 60 degrees

Explanation: In triangle AOB, OA = OB (radii of the same circle).

31. In a triangle ABC, D and E are points on AB and AC respectively such that DE is parallel to BC. If AD = x, DB = x - 2, AE = x + 2, and EC = x - 1, find the value of x.

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 4

Explanation: Since DE is parallel to BC, by Thales' theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem), we have:
AD / DB = AE / EC
x / (x - 2) = (x + 2) / (x - 1)
Cross-multiply:
x * (x - 1) = (x - 2) * (x + 2)
x^2 - x = x^2 - 4 (using (a-b)(a+b) = a^2 - b^2)
-x = -4
x = 4

32. The equation of a circle is x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 3 = 0. What are the coordinates of its center and its radius?

  1. Center (3, -2), Radius = 4
  2. Center (-3, 2), Radius = 4
  3. Center (3, -2), Radius = sqrt(10)
  4. Center (-3, 2), Radius = sqrt(10)

Answer: Center (3, -2), Radius = 4

Explanation: The general equation of a circle is x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0.
The center of the circle is (-g, -f) and the radius is sqrt(g^2 + f^2 - c).
Comparing the given equation x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 3 = 0 with the general equation:
2g = -6 => g = -3
2f = 4 => f = 2
c = -3
Center = (-g, -f) = (-(-3), -(2)) = (3, -2).
Radius = sqrt(g^2 + f^2 - c) = sqrt((-3)^2 + (2)^2 - (-3))
Radius = sqrt(9 + 4 + 3) = sqrt(16) = 4.
So, the center is (3, -2) and the radius is 4.

34. In a circle with center O, AB is a chord. C is a point on the minor arc AB. If angle AOB = 100 degrees, what is the measure of angle ACB?

  1. 80 degrees
  2. 100 degrees
  3. 130 degrees
  4. 160 degrees

Answer: 160 degrees

Explanation: The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle. Here, angle AOB is the angle at the center subtended by the major arc AB. However, C is a point on the *minor* arc AB. This means ACB is an angle in the major segment.
Angle AOB = 100 degrees. This is the angle subtended by the minor arc AB at the center.
Therefore, the angle subtended by the minor arc AB at any point on the major arc is (1/2) * 100 = 50 degrees. Let's call this point D. So, angle ADB = 50 degrees.
Now, ACBD forms a cyclic quadrilateral. The sum of opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral is 180 degrees.
So, angle ACB + angle ADB = 180 degrees.
Angle ACB + 50 degrees = 180 degrees.
Angle ACB = 180 - 50 = 130 degrees.

35. A point P(x, y) is equidistant from the points A(5, 1) and B(1, 5). Which of the following statements is true about the coordinates of P?

  1. x + y = 6
  2. x - y = 0
  3. x + y = 0
  4. x - y = 6

Answer: x - y = 0

Explanation: If point P(x, y) is equidistant from A(5, 1) and B(1, 5), then PA^2 = PB^2.
Using the distance formula, (x - 5)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = (x - 1)^2 + (y - 5)^2.
Expand both sides:
x^2 - 10x + 25 + y^2 - 2y + 1 = x^2 - 2x + 1 + y^2 - 10y + 25.
Cancel out x^2, y^2, 1, and 25 from both sides:
-10x - 2y = -2x - 10y.
Rearrange the terms to one side:
-10x + 2x = -10y + 2y.
-8x = -8y.
Divide by -8:
x = y.
This can be written as x - y = 0.

41. The perimeter of a rhombus is 40 cm. If one of its diagonals is 16 cm, what is the area of the rhombus?
