2. In a circle with center O, AB is a chord. A tangent is drawn at point A such that it makes an angle of 50° with the chord AB. What is the measure of ∠AOB?

  1. 80°
  2. 90°
  3. 100°
  4. 110°

Answer: 90°

Explanation: Let the tangent be AT. Given ∠TAB = 50°. The angle between the tangent and a chord through the point of contact is equal to the angle in the alternate segment. So, if C is a point on the major arc, ∠ACB = ∠TAB = 50°. The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle. Therefore, ∠AOB = 2 * ∠ACB = 2 * 50° = 100°. My options are 80, 90, 100, 110. So 100 is the correct answer. The answer index is 2.

3. Two similar triangles ABC and PQR have areas 64 cm² and 100 cm² respectively. If the perimeter of triangle ABC is 24 cm, what is the perimeter of triangle PQR?

  1. 28 cm
  2. 30 cm
  3. 32 cm
  4. 36 cm

Answer: 32 cm

Explanation: For similar triangles, the ratio of their areas is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides (or perimeters). Area(ABC) / Area(PQR) = (Perimeter(ABC) / Perimeter(PQR))². 64 / 100 = (24 / Perimeter(PQR))². Taking the square root of both sides: 8 / 10 = 24 / Perimeter(PQR). Perimeter(PQR) = (24 * 10) / 8 = 3 * 10 = 30 cm. The answer index is 1.

5. The coordinates of the vertices of a triangle are A(1, 2), B(4, 6), and C(7, 2). What is the area of the triangle?

  1. 9 sq units
  2. 12 sq units
  3. 15 sq units
  4. 18 sq units

Answer: 12 sq units

Explanation: We can use the determinant formula for the area of a triangle: Area = 0.5 * |x1(y2 - y3) + x2(y3 - y1) + x3(y1 - y2)|. Area = 0.5 * |1(6 - 2) + 4(2 - 2) + 7(2 - 6)| = 0.5 * |1(4) + 4(0) + 7(-4)| = 0.5 * |4 + 0 - 28| = 0.5 * |-24| = 12 sq units. The answer index is 1.

7. A cone and a hemisphere have the same base radius 'r' and the same volume. What is the ratio of the height of the cone to its radius?

  1. 1:1
  2. 2:1
  3. 3:1
  4. 4:1

Answer: 2:1

Explanation: Volume of cone = (1/3)πr²h. Volume of hemisphere = (2/3)πr³. Given that their volumes are equal: (1/3)πr²h = (2/3)πr³. Divide both sides by (1/3)πr²: h = 2r. So, the ratio of the height of the cone to its radius (h:r) is 2r:r, which simplifies to 2:1. The answer index is 1.

10. In a circle with center O, AB is a chord. The tangent at A and B intersect at point P. If ∠AOB = 120°, what is the measure of ∠APB?

  1. 60°
  2. 90°
  3. 120°
  4. 30°

Answer: 60°

Explanation: In quadrilateral OAPB, OA ⊥ AP and OB ⊥ BP (radius is perpendicular to tangent at the point of contact). So, ∠OAP = 90° and ∠OBP = 90°. The sum of angles in a quadrilateral is 360°. Therefore, ∠AOB + ∠OAP + ∠APB + ∠OBP = 360°. Given ∠AOB = 120°, we have 120° + 90° + ∠APB + 90° = 360°. 300° + ∠APB = 360°. ∠APB = 360° - 300° = 60°.

11. The coordinates of the vertices of a triangle are A(2, 3), B(4, -1), and C(6, 5). What is the area of the triangle?

  1. 10 sq units
  2. 12 sq units
  3. 14 sq units
  4. 16 sq units

Answer: 10 sq units

Explanation: The area of a triangle with vertices (x1, y1), (x2, y2), and (x3, y3) is given by the formula: Area = 1/2 |x1(y2 - y3) + x2(y3 - y1) + x3(y1 - y2)|. Substituting the coordinates: Area = 1/2 |2(-1 - 5) + 4(5 - 3) + 6(3 - (-1))|. Area = 1/2 |2(-6) + 4(2) + 6(4)|. Area = 1/2 |-12 + 8 + 24|. Area = 1/2 |20| = 10 sq units.

12. The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. What is the perimeter of the rhombus?

  1. 40 cm
  2. 32 cm
  3. 48 cm
  4. 20 cm

Answer: 40 cm

Explanation: In a rhombus, the diagonals bisect each other at right angles. Let the diagonals be d1 = 16 cm and d2 = 12 cm. The half-diagonals will be d1/2 = 8 cm and d2/2 = 6 cm. These half-diagonals form the legs of a right-angled triangle, and the side of the rhombus is the hypotenuse. Using the Pythagorean theorem, side² = (d1/2)² + (d2/2)² = 8² + 6² = 64 + 36 = 100. So, side = √100 = 10 cm. The perimeter of a rhombus is 4 * side = 4 * 10 = 40 cm.

14. The ratio of the radii of two cylinders is 2:3 and the ratio of their heights is 5:3. What is the ratio of their volumes?

  1. 20:27
  2. 10:9
  3. 4:9
  4. 2:3

Answer: 20:27

Explanation: Let the radii of the two cylinders be r1 and r2, and their heights be h1 and h2. Given r1:r2 = 2:3 and h1:h2 = 5:3. The volume of a cylinder is given by V = πr²h. So, V1 = πr1²h1 and V2 = πr2²h2. The ratio of their volumes V1:V2 = (πr1²h1) : (πr2²h2) = (r1/r2)² * (h1/h2). Substituting the given ratios: V1:V2 = (2/3)² * (5/3) = (4/9) * (5/3) = 20/27. So, the ratio of their volumes is 20:27.

16. A solid metallic sphere of radius 10 cm is melted and recast into a number of smaller identical cones. Each cone has a radius of 2 cm and a height of 8 cm. How many such cones can be formed?

  1. 125
  2. 150
  3. 175
  4. 200

Answer: 150

Explanation: Volume of sphere = (4/3) * pi * R^3 = (4/3) * pi * (10)^3 = (4000/3) * pi cubic cm. Volume of one cone = (1/3) * pi * r^2 * h = (1/3) * pi * (2)^2 * 8 = (32/3) * pi cubic cm. Number of cones = (Volume of sphere) / (Volume of one cone) = ((4000/3) * pi) / ((32/3) * pi) = 4000 / 32 = 125.

18. Two triangles ABC and PQR are similar. The area of triangle ABC is 64 sq cm and the area of triangle PQR is 100 sq cm. If the perimeter of triangle PQR is 30 cm, what is the perimeter of triangle ABC?

  1. 20 cm
  2. 24 cm
  3. 28 cm
  4. 32 cm

Answer: 24 cm

Explanation: For similar triangles, the ratio of their areas is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides (or perimeters). Area(ABC) / Area(PQR) = (Perimeter(ABC) / Perimeter(PQR))^2. 64 / 100 = (Perimeter(ABC) / 30)^2. Taking square root on both sides: 8 / 10 = Perimeter(ABC) / 30. Perimeter(ABC) = (8/10) * 30 = 8 * 3 = 24 cm.

20. A right circular cone has a height of 24 cm and a base radius of 7 cm. It is cut parallel to its base, forming a smaller cone and a frustum. If the height of the smaller cone is 12 cm, what is the ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to the volume of the frustum?

  1. 1:7
  2. 1:8
  3. 1:16
  4. 1:17

Answer: 1:7

Explanation: Let the original cone be C1 and the smaller cone be C2. Height of C1 (H) = 24 cm, radius of C1 (R) = 7 cm. Height of C2 (h) = 12 cm. Since C2 is formed by cutting C1 parallel to its base, C1 and C2 are similar cones. The ratio of their heights is H/h = 24/12 = 2/1. The ratio of their radii will also be 2/1. So, radius of C2 (r) = R/2 = 7/2 cm. The ratio of their volumes is (Volume of C1) / (Volume of C2) = (H/h)^3 = (2/1)^3 = 8/1. This means Volume of C1 = 8 * Volume of C2. Volume of frustum = Volume of C1 - Volume of C2 = 8 * Volume of C2 - Volume of C2 = 7 * Volume of C2. The ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to the volume of the frustum = Volume of C2 / (7 * Volume of C2) = 1/7. So the ratio is 1:7.

21. A circle passes through the points (0,0), (6,0), and (0,8). What is the radius of the circle?

  1. 5 units
  2. 6 units
  3. 7 units
  4. 10 units

Answer: 5 units

Explanation: The points (0,0), (6,0), and (0,8) form a right-angled triangle with the right angle at the origin (0,0). The hypotenuse of this triangle connects (6,0) and (0,8). The length of the hypotenuse is sqrt((6-0)^2 + (0-8)^2) = sqrt(36 + 64) = sqrt(100) = 10 units. For a right-angled triangle inscribed in a circle, the hypotenuse is the diameter of the circle.
