2. In a circle with center O, AB is a chord. A tangent is drawn at point A such that it makes an angle of 50° with the chord AB. What is the measure of ∠AOB?

  1. 80°
  2. 90°
  3. 100°
  4. 110°

Answer: 90°

Explanation: Let the tangent be AT. Given ∠TAB = 50°. The angle between the tangent and a chord through the point of contact is equal to the angle in the alternate segment. So, if C is a point on the major arc, ∠ACB = ∠TAB = 50°. The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle. Therefore, ∠AOB = 2 * ∠ACB = 2 * 50° = 100°. My options are 80, 90, 100, 110. So 100 is the correct answer. The answer index is 2.

3. Two similar triangles ABC and PQR have areas 64 cm² and 100 cm² respectively. If the perimeter of triangle ABC is 24 cm, what is the perimeter of triangle PQR?

  1. 28 cm
  2. 30 cm
  3. 32 cm
  4. 36 cm

Answer: 32 cm

Explanation: For similar triangles, the ratio of their areas is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides (or perimeters). Area(ABC) / Area(PQR) = (Perimeter(ABC) / Perimeter(PQR))². 64 / 100 = (24 / Perimeter(PQR))². Taking the square root of both sides: 8 / 10 = 24 / Perimeter(PQR). Perimeter(PQR) = (24 * 10) / 8 = 3 * 10 = 30 cm. The answer index is 1.

5. The coordinates of the vertices of a triangle are A(1, 2), B(4, 6), and C(7, 2). What is the area of the triangle?

  1. 9 sq units
  2. 12 sq units
  3. 15 sq units
  4. 18 sq units

Answer: 12 sq units

Explanation: We can use the determinant formula for the area of a triangle: Area = 0.5 * |x1(y2 - y3) + x2(y3 - y1) + x3(y1 - y2)|. Area = 0.5 * |1(6 - 2) + 4(2 - 2) + 7(2 - 6)| = 0.5 * |1(4) + 4(0) + 7(-4)| = 0.5 * |4 + 0 - 28| = 0.5 * |-24| = 12 sq units. The answer index is 1.
