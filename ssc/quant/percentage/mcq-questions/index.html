26. In an election, Candidate A received 60% of the total votes. If 20% of the voters did not cast their votes, and the total number of registered voters was 15,000, how many votes did Candidate A receive?

  1. 7200
  2. 9000
  3. 10800
  4. 12000

Answer: 7200

Explanation: Valid votes = 15000 * (1 - 0.20) = 12000. Candidate A received 60% of these valid votes: 12000 * 0.60 = 7200.

27. A shopkeeper marks up his goods by 40% and then offers a discount of 20%. If an item costs the shopkeeper ₹500, what is its selling price?

  1. ₹560
  2. ₹580
  3. ₹600
  4. ₹620

Answer: ₹560

Explanation: Marked price = 500 * (1 + 0.40) = ₹700. Selling price = 700 * (1 - 0.20) = ₹560.

28. If the price of an article is increased by 25%, by what percentage must its consumption be reduced so as to keep the expenditure on the article the same?

  1. 15%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 30%

Answer: 20%

Explanation: Let original price be P and consumption be C. New price is 1.25P. To keep expenditure PC same, new consumption C' must be C/1.25 = 0.8C. Reduction is (C - 0.8C)/C = 0.2 = 20%.

29. A's salary is 20% more than B's salary. By what percentage is B's salary less than A's salary?

  1. 16.67%
  2. 20%
  3. 25%
  4. 33.33%

Answer: 16.67%

Explanation: If B's salary is S, A's salary is 1.2S. B's salary is (1.2S - S) = 0.2S less than A's. Percentage less = (0.2S / 1.2S) * 100 = (1/6) * 100 = 16.67%.

30. A shopkeeper marks up an item by 40% and then offers a discount of 20%. If he still makes a profit of Rs. 480, and the cost price of the item is increased by 10%, what should be the new marked price (to the nearest rupee) to maintain the same profit percentage after offering the same 20% discount?

  1. Rs. 3240
  2. Rs. 3360
  3. Rs. 3480
  4. Rs. 3600

Answer: Rs. 3360

Explanation: Calculate the initial CP and SP from the given profit. Then find the new CP and determine the new MP required for the same profit percentage after the 20% discount.

If the total number of registered voters was 25000, and 80% of the registered voters cast their votes, what was the difference between the number of valid votes received by Candidate A and Candidate B?

  1. 5100
  2. 5200

  4. 5400

Answer: 5100

Explanation: First calculate the total votes polled, then invalid votes, and finally valid votes. From valid votes, find votes for A and B and their difference.

34. A student scored 75% marks in an exam. If he had scored 20 more marks, his percentage would have been 80%. What is the maximum marks of the exam?

  1. 300
  2. 400
  3. 500
  4. 600

Answer: 400

Explanation: Let the maximum marks be M. 75% of M + 20 = 80% of M. This implies 5% of M = 20, so M = 400.

35. The price of an item increases by 20% and then decreases by 20%. What is the net percentage change in the price of the item?

  1. 0%
  2. 4% increase
  3. 4% decrease
  4. 2% decrease

Answer: 4% decrease

Explanation: Let initial price be 100. After 20% increase, price is 120. After 20% decrease on 120, price is 120 * 0.8 = 96. Net change is 100 - 96 = 4% decrease.

36. In an election, candidate A received 60% of the total votes. If 15% of the total votes were declared invalid, and the total number of votes polled was 20,000, how many valid votes did candidate A receive?

  1. 10,200
  2. 12,000
  3. 10,000
  4. 9,000

Answer: 12,000

Explanation: Total valid votes are 20000 * (100-15)% = 20000 * 0.85 = 17000. Candidate A received 60% of the total votes, which is 60% of 20000 = 12000. The invalid votes affect the total votes, not the percentage received by A from the total.

37. If 80% of a number is added to 80, the result is the number itself. What is the number?

  1. 300
  2. 400
  3. 200
  4. 500

Answer: 400

Explanation: Let the number be N. 0.8N + 80 = N. This means 0.2N = 80, so N = 400.

38. A man spends 75% of his income. If his income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%, what is the percentage increase in his savings?

  1. 50%
  2. 40%
  3. 30%
  4. 60%

Answer: 50%

Explanation: Let income be 100. Expenditure is 75, savings is 25. New income is 120. New expenditure is 75 * 1.1 = 82.5. New savings is 120 - 82.5 = 37.5. Percentage increase in savings = ((37.5-25)/25) * 100 = 50%.

45. The price of an item increases by 10% and then decreases by 10%. What is the net percentage change in the price of the item?

  1. 0% increase
  2. 1% decrease
  3. 0.1% decrease
  4. 1% increase

Answer: 1% decrease

Explanation: Let the original price be P. After a 10% increase, the price is 1.10P. After a 10% decrease, the price is 0.90 * (1.10P) = 0.99P. The net change is (0.99P - P)/P = -0.01 or a 1% decrease.

46. The price of a stock increases by 25% and then decreases by 25%. What is the net percentage change in the price of the stock?

  1. 0% change
  2. 6.25% increase
  3. 6.25% decrease
  4. 5% decrease

Answer: 6.25% decrease

Explanation: Let the original price be P. After a 25% increase, the price is 1.25P. After a 25% decrease, the price is 0.75 * (1.25P) = 0.9375P. The net change is (0.9375P - P)/P = -0.0625 or a 6.25% decrease.

47. A product's cost increases by 50% and then decreases by 50%. What is the net percentage change in the product's cost?

  1. 0% change
  2. 25% increase
  3. 25% decrease
  4. 50% decrease

Answer: 25% decrease

Explanation: Let the original cost be C. After a 50% increase, the cost is 1.50C. After a 50% decrease, the cost is 0.50 * (1.50C) = 0.75C. The net change is (0.75C - C)/C = -0.25 or a 25% decrease.
