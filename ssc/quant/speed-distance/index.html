Speed Distance MCQs with answers

Use these 5 worked Speed Distance questions to revise the SSC Quantitative Aptitude syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 1,523 questions.

1. A train 150 meters long is running at a speed of 54 km/hr. How long will it take to cross a platform 250 meters long?

  1. 20 seconds
  2. 25 seconds
  3. 30 seconds
  4. 35 seconds

Answer: 20 seconds

Explanation: Convert speed to m/s. The total distance to cover is the sum of the train's length and the platform's length. Time = Total Distance / Speed.

2. Two trains, one 100 meters long and the other 120 meters long, are running in opposite directions on parallel tracks. Their speeds are 40 km/hr and 50 km/hr respectively. In what time will they cross each other?

  1. 8 seconds
  2. 8.8 seconds
  3. 9.2 seconds
  4. 10 seconds

Answer: 8.8 seconds

Explanation: Calculate the relative speed by adding their individual speeds. The total distance to cover is the sum of their lengths. Time = Total Distance / Relative Speed.

3. Two trains, A and B, start simultaneously from stations P and Q respectively, which are 500 km apart. Train A travels towards Q at 60 km/hr and Train B travels towards P at 40 km/hr. How far from station P will they cross each other?

  1. 300 km
  2. 250 km
  3. 200 km
  4. 350 km

Answer: 300 km

Explanation: Their relative speed is 60+40 = 100 km/hr. Time to meet = 500/100 = 5 hours. Distance from P = 60 * 5 = 300 km.

4. A boat can travel at 15 km/hr in still water. If the speed of the stream is 3 km/hr, how much time will the boat take to travel 72 km downstream and return 36 km upstream?

  1. 8 hours
  2. 9 hours
  3. 7 hours
  4. 10 hours

Answer: 9 hours

Explanation: Downstream speed = 15+3 = 18 km/hr. Upstream speed = 15-3 = 12 km/hr. Total time = (72/18) + (36/12) = 4 + 3 = 7 hours.

5. A train 120 meters long crosses a platform 180 meters long in 10 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/hr?

  1. 108 km/hr
  2. 72 km/hr
  3. 36 km/hr
  4. 90 km/hr

Answer: 108 km/hr

Explanation: Total distance covered = 120 + 180 = 300 meters. Speed = 300/10 = 30 m/s. Converting to km/hr = 30 * (18/5) = 108 km/hr.
