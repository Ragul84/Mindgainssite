26. A man rows a boat 40 km downstream in 4 hours and 15 km upstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?

  1. 7.5 km/h
  2. 8 km/h
  3. 8.5 km/h
  4. 9 km/h

Answer: 7.5 km/h

Explanation: Downstream speed = 40/4 = 10 km/h. Upstream speed = 15/3 = 5 km/h. Still water speed = (10+5)/2 = 7.5 km/h.

28. Two trains of lengths 160 m and 190 m are running on parallel tracks in the same direction. The faster train is running at 100 km/hr and the slower train is running at 70 km/hr. How long will it take for the faster train to completely cross the slower train?

  1. 42 seconds
  2. 38 seconds
  3. 46 seconds
  4. 32 seconds

Answer: 38 seconds

Explanation: Relative speed = 100 - 70 = 30 km/hr = 30 * (5/18) m/s. Total distance = 160 + 190 = 350 m. Time = Distance / Speed = 350 / (30 * 5/18) = 38 seconds.

34. A boat can travel 40 km downstream and 30 km upstream in 7 hours. It can also travel 20 km downstream and 25 km upstream in 5 hours. What is the speed of the current?

  1. 2 km/h
  2. 3 km/h
  3. 4 km/h
  4. 5 km/h

Answer: 2 km/h

Explanation: Let 'd' be downstream speed and 'u' be upstream speed. Solve the system of equations: 40/d + 30/u = 7 and 20/d + 25/u = 5. Current speed = (d-u)/2.
