26. What is the remainder when 2^10 is divided by 7?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4

Answer: 3

Explanation: 2¹=2, 2²=4, 2³=8≡1 (mod 7). Pattern repeats every 3. 10=3×3+1, so 2^10 ≡ 2^1 = 2... Actually: 2^3=8≡1, 2^9=(2^3)^3≡1, 2^10=2^9×2≡1×2=2 (mod 7). Answer is 2.

27. A number when divided by 6 leaves remainder 5. What is the remainder when the same number is divided by 3?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. 5

Answer: 2

Explanation: Number = 6k+5 = 3(2k+1)+2. So remainder when divided by 3 is 2.

28. The unit digit of 7^34 is:

  1. 1
  2. 3
  3. 7
  4. 9

Answer: 9

Explanation: Powers of 7: 7¹=7, 7²=49(unit:9), 7³=343(unit:3), 7⁴=2401(unit:1), cycle of 4. 34=4×8+2, unit digit of 7^34 = unit digit of 7² = 9.

29. √(0.0064) = ?

  1. 0.08
  2. 0.8
  3. 0.008
  4. 8

Answer: 0.08

Explanation: √(0.0064) = √(64/10000) = 8/100 = 0.08.

30. The value of (−3)³ is:

  1. 9
  2. −9
  3. 27
  4. −27

Answer: −27

Explanation: (−3)³ = (−3)×(−3)×(−3) = 9×(−3) = −27. Odd power of negative number is negative.

31. HCF of 36 and 48 is:

  1. 6
  2. 12
  3. 18
  4. 24

Answer: 12

Explanation: 36=2²×3², 48=2⁴×3. HCF = 2²×3 = 12.

32. LCM of 12, 18, and 24 is:

  1. 72
  2. 144
  3. 36
  4. 48

Answer: 72

Explanation: 12=2²×3, 18=2×3², 24=2³×3. LCM = 2³×3² = 8×9 = 72.

33. The product of HCF and LCM of two numbers is 960. If one number is 48, the other is:

  1. 20
  2. 15
  3. 24
  4. 30

Answer: 20

Explanation: HCF × LCM = Product of two numbers. So other number = 960/48 = 20.

34. Three bells ring at intervals of 6, 8, and 12 minutes. If they ring together at 12 noon, when will they next ring together?

  1. 12:24 PM
  2. 12:30 PM
  3. 12:48 PM
  4. 1:00 PM

Answer: 12:24 PM

Explanation: LCM(6,8,12): 6=2×3, 8=2³, 12=2²×3. LCM=2³×3=24 minutes. They ring together at 12:24 PM.

36. The greatest number that divides 35, 56, and 91 leaving no remainder is:

  1. 7
  2. 14
  3. 21
  4. 28

Answer: 7

Explanation: HCF(35,56,91): 35=5×7, 56=8×7, 91=13×7. HCF=7.

37. The least number divisible by 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 is:

  1. 60
  2. 120
  3. 180
  4. 30

Answer: 60

Explanation: LCM(2,3,4,5,6): LCM(4,6)=12, LCM(12,5)=60, LCM(60,2)=60, LCM(60,3)=60. LCM=60.

38. The HCF of 2/3, 4/9, and 8/27 is:

  1. 2/27
  2. 8/3
  3. 2/3
  4. 4/27

Answer: 2/27

Explanation: HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators = HCF(2,4,8)/LCM(3,9,27) = 2/27.

39. What is the least number which when divided by 5, 6, 7, and 8 leaves remainder 3?

  1. 843
  2. 843
  3. 840
  4. 838

Explanation: LCM(5,6,7,8)=840. Least number = 840+3=843.

40. The LCM of two numbers is 60 and their HCF is 5. If one number is 15, the other is:

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 30

Answer: 20

Explanation: Other number = (LCM × HCF) / first = (60 × 5) / 15 = 300/15 = 20.

41. 20% of 350 + 35% of 200 = ?

  1. 140
  2. 135
  3. 130
  4. 145

Answer: 140

Explanation: 20% of 350 = 70. 35% of 200 = 70. Total = 70+70 = 140.

42. A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. The net change is:

  1. 0%
  2. 5% increase
  3. 5% decrease
  4. 10% increase

Answer: 0%

Explanation: Let number = 100. After 25% increase = 125. After 20% decrease = 125×0.8 = 100. Net change = 0%.

43. 60% of a number is 96. What is 85% of that number?

  1. 136
  2. 128
  3. 140
  4. 120

Answer: 136

Explanation: Number = 96/0.6 = 160. 85% of 160 = 0.85×160 = 136.

44. In an election, candidate A gets 55% of total votes. If total votes are 80,000, how many votes did candidate B get?

  1. 36,000
  2. 44,000
  3. 40,000
  4. 38,000

Answer: 36,000

Explanation: A gets 55% = 44,000. B gets 45% = 80,000×0.45 = 36,000.

47. SI on ₹2,000 at 5% per annum for 3 years is:

  2. ₹300

48. A vessel has 40 litres of milk. 10 litres is replaced by water. This is done again. Milk remaining:

  1. 22.5 L
  2. 25 L
  3. 20 L
  4. 27.5 L

Answer: 22.5 L

Explanation: After 1st: milk = 40×(30/40) = 30 L. After 2nd: milk = 30×(30/40) = 22.5 L.

49. A grocer mixes 30 kg of sugar at ₹12/kg with 20 kg at ₹15/kg. Average price:

  1. ₹13.20/kg
  2. ₹13.50/kg
  3. ₹13/kg
  4. ₹14/kg

Answer: ₹13.20/kg

Explanation: Total cost=(30×12)+(20×15)=360+300=660. Total weight=50kg. Average=660/50=₹13.20/kg.

50. A's age 4 years ago was twice B's age. After 4 years, A will be 1.5 times B's age. Present ages:

  1. A=20, B=12
  2. A=24, B=14
  3. A=16, B=10
  4. A=28, B=16

Answer: A=20, B=12

Explanation: Let present ages: A and B. A−4=2(B−4)→A=2B−4. A+4=1.5(B+4)→A=1.5B+2. 2B−4=1.5B+2→0.5B=6→B=12. A=2(12)−4=20.
