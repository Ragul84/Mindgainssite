SSC Quantitative Aptitude practice

Simplification MCQs with answers

Use these 25 worked Simplification questions to revise the SSC Quantitative Aptitude syllabus. Each answer includes an explanation; the full practice set contains 267 questions.

1. What is 15% of 200?

  1. 20
  2. 25
  3. 30
  4. 35

Answer: 30

Explanation: 15% of 200 = (15/100) × 200 = 30.

2. The LCM of 4 and 6 is:

  1. 2
  2. 12
  3. 24
  4. 8

Answer: 12

Explanation: LCM(4,6): 4=2², 6=2×3. LCM = 2²×3 = 12.

3. The HCF of 12 and 18 is:

  1. 3
  2. 6
  3. 9
  4. 12

Answer: 6

Explanation: HCF(12,18): 12=2²×3, 18=2×3². HCF = 2×3 = 6.

4. What is the square root of 144?

  1. 11
  2. 12
  3. 13
  4. 14

Answer: 12

Explanation: √144 = 12, because 12 × 12 = 144.

5. If 5x = 30, then x = ?

  1. 5
  2. 6
  3. 7
  4. 8

Answer: 6

Explanation: 5x = 30 → x = 30/5 = 6.

6. A number divisible by both 2 and 3 is divisible by:

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 9

Answer: 6

Explanation: A number divisible by both 2 and 3 is divisible by LCM(2,3) = 6.

7. What is 7² (7 squared)?

  1. 14
  2. 21
  3. 42
  4. 49

Answer: 49

Explanation: 7² = 7 × 7 = 49.

8. The perimeter of a square with side 6 cm is:

  1. 12 cm
  2. 24 cm
  3. 36 cm
  4. 48 cm

Answer: 24 cm

Explanation: Perimeter of square = 4 × side = 4 × 6 = 24 cm.

9. The average of 5 numbers is 20. If one number is removed and the new average is 18, what was the removed number?

  1. 24
  2. 26
  3. 28
  4. 30

Answer: 28

Explanation: Sum of 5 numbers = 5×20=100. Sum of remaining 4 = 4×18=72. Removed number = 100-72 = 28.

10. If a:b = 3:4 and b:c = 2:5, then a:c = ?

  1. 3:10
  2. 6:20
  3. 3:5
  4. 6:5

Answer: 3:10

Explanation: a:b = 3:4, b:c = 2:5. Make b equal: a:b = 6:8, b:c = 8:20. So a:c = 6:20 = 3:10.

11. If sinθ = 3/5, then cosθ = ?

  1. 4/5
  2. 5/3
  3. 3/4
  4. 5/4

Answer: 4/5

Explanation: Using sin²θ + cos²θ = 1: cos²θ = 1 - (3/5)² = 1 - 9/25 = 16/25, so cosθ = 4/5.

12. The probability of getting a head when a fair coin is tossed is:

  1. 1
  2. 1/4
  3. 1/2
  4. 1/3

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: A fair coin has 2 equally likely outcomes (H or T). Probability of Head = 1/2.

13. In how many ways can 5 people be arranged in a row?

  1. 60
  2. 100
  3. 120
  4. 150

Answer: 120

Explanation: Number of arrangements of n people in a row = n! = 5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120.

14. The sum of first n natural numbers is:

  1. n(n+1)
  2. n(n+1)/2
  3. n²
  4. n(n-1)/2

Answer: n(n+1)/2

Explanation: Sum of first n natural numbers = 1+2+3+...+n = n(n+1)/2. For n=10: 10×11/2 = 55.

15. If log 2 = 0.3010, then log 8 = ?

  1. 0.6020
  2. 0.9030
  3. 2.4080
  4. 1.2040

Answer: 0.9030

Explanation: log 8 = log 2³ = 3 log 2 = 3 × 0.3010 = 0.9030.

16. In an AP, if first term = 2, common difference = 3, then sum of first 10 terms is:

  1. 125
  2. 155
  3. 175
  4. 185

Answer: 155

Explanation: S = n/2 × [2a + (n-1)d] = 10/2 × [2×2 + 9×3] = 5 × [4 + 27] = 5 × 31 = 155.

17. If the sum of a number and its reciprocal is 5/2, the number is:

  1. 1/2 or 2
  2. 2 or 3
  3. 1/3 or 3
  4. 1/4 or 4

Answer: 1/2 or 2

Explanation: x + 1/x = 5/2 → 2x² - 5x + 2 = 0 → (2x-1)(x-2) = 0 → x = 1/2 or x = 2.

18. The number of diagonals in a polygon with n sides is:

  1. n(n-1)/2
  2. n(n-3)/2
  3. n(n+1)/2
  4. n(n-2)

Answer: n(n-3)/2

Explanation: Number of diagonals in n-sided polygon = n(n-3)/2. For hexagon (n=6): 6×3/2 = 9 diagonals.

19. Sin30° + cos60° + tan45° = ?

  1. 1
  2. 1.5
  3. 2
  4. 2.5

Answer: 2

Explanation: sin30° = 1/2, cos60° = 1/2, tan45° = 1. Sum = 1/2 + 1/2 + 1 = 2.

20. If nC2 = 10, then n = ?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 7

Answer: 5

Explanation: nC2 = n(n-1)/2 = 10 → n(n-1) = 20 → n=5 (since 5×4=20). So n=5.

21. The smallest 4-digit number divisible by 8 is:

  1. 1000
  2. 1008
  3. 1016
  4. 1024

Answer: 1008

Explanation: 1000 ÷ 8 = 125. So 1000 is divisible by 8. Wait — 1000/8 = 125 exactly. So 1000 is divisible by 8. But 1000 is the smallest 4-digit number, so answer is 1000. Actually let me reconsider — 1000÷8=125, so 1000 IS divisible by 8.

22. The number 987654 is divisible by:

  1. 2 only
  2. 3 only
  3. Both 2 and 3
  4. Neither 2 nor 3

Answer: Both 2 and 3

Explanation: Last digit 4 → divisible by 2. Digit sum: 9+8+7+6+5+4=39, 39÷3=13 → divisible by 3. So divisible by both 2 and 3.

23. The face value of digit 6 in 3621 is:

  1. 600
  2. 6
  3. 60
  4. 6000

Answer: 6

Explanation: Face value is the digit itself regardless of position. Face value of 6 = 6. Place value would be 600 (6 × 100).

24. If a number is divisible by 9, it is also divisible by:

  1. 3
  2. 6
  3. 27
  4. 18

Answer: 3

Explanation: 9 = 3×3. Any number divisible by 9 is also divisible by 3 (a factor of 9). It need not be divisible by 6, 27, or 18.

25. The product of two numbers is 120 and their sum is 22. The numbers are:

  1. 10 and 12
  2. 8 and 15
  3. 6 and 20
  4. 9 and 13

Answer: 10 and 12

Explanation: 10+12=22 ✓, 10×12=120 ✓. Using quadratic: x²−22x+120=0, (x−10)(x−12)=0, x=10 or 12.
