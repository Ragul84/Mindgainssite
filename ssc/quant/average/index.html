Average MCQs with answers

1. The average of 15 numbers is 42. The average of the first 8 of them is 45 and the average of the last 8 is 40. What is the 8th number?

  1. 48
  2. 50
  3. 52
  4. 46

Answer: 50

Explanation: Sum of all 15 = 15 × 42 = 630. Sum of first 8 = 8 × 45 = 360; sum of last 8 = 8 × 40 = 320. The 8th number falls in both groups, so (360 + 320) − 630 = 680 − 630 = 50.

2. The average of five consecutive even numbers is 36. What is the largest of these five numbers?

  1. 36
  2. 38
  3. 40
  4. 42

Answer: 40

Explanation: For consecutive even numbers the average equals the middle term, so the third number is 36. The five numbers are 32, 34, 36, 38, 40, and the largest is 40.

3. The average age of 30 students in a class is 14 years. When the class teacher's age is included, the average rises by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?

  1. 30
  2. 40
  3. 44
  4. 45

Answer: 45

Explanation: Sum of students' ages = 30 × 14 = 420. New average = 15 for 31 people, so new total = 31 × 15 = 465. Teacher's age = 465 − 420 = 45 years.

4. A batsman had an average of 40 runs in 12 innings. In his 13th innings he scored 92 runs. By how many runs does his batting average increase?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 3

Answer: 4

Explanation: Runs in 12 innings = 12 × 40 = 480. Total after 13 innings = 480 + 92 = 572, so the new average = 572 ÷ 13 = 44. Increase = 44 − 40 = 4.

5. What is the average of the first 40 natural numbers?

  1. 20
  2. 20.5
  3. 21
  4. 19.5

Answer: 20.5

Explanation: The average of the first n natural numbers is (n + 1) ÷ 2. Here (40 + 1) ÷ 2 = 41 ÷ 2 = 20.5.
