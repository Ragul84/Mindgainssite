Average MCQs with answers

1. The average of 15 numbers is 42. The average of the first 8 of them is 45 and the average of the last 8 is 40. What is the 8th number?

  1. 48
  2. 50
  3. 52
  4. 46

Answer: 50

Explanation: Sum of all 15 = 15 × 42 = 630. Sum of first 8 = 8 × 45 = 360; sum of last 8 = 8 × 40 = 320. The 8th number falls in both groups, so (360 + 320) − 630 = 680 − 630 = 50.

2. The average of five consecutive even numbers is 36. What is the largest of these five numbers?

  1. 36
  2. 38
  3. 40
  4. 42

Answer: 40

Explanation: For consecutive even numbers the average equals the middle term, so the third number is 36. The five numbers are 32, 34, 36, 38, 40, and the largest is 40.

3. The average age of 30 students in a class is 14 years. When the class teacher's age is included, the average rises by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?

  1. 30
  2. 40
  3. 44
  4. 45

Answer: 45

Explanation: Sum of students' ages = 30 × 14 = 420. New average = 15 for 31 people, so new total = 31 × 15 = 465. Teacher's age = 465 − 420 = 45 years.

4. A batsman had an average of 40 runs in 12 innings. In his 13th innings he scored 92 runs. By how many runs does his batting average increase?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 3

Answer: 4

Explanation: Runs in 12 innings = 12 × 40 = 480. Total after 13 innings = 480 + 92 = 572, so the new average = 572 ÷ 13 = 44. Increase = 44 − 40 = 4.

5. What is the average of the first 40 natural numbers?

  1. 20
  2. 20.5
  3. 21
  4. 19.5

Answer: 20.5

Explanation: The average of the first n natural numbers is (n + 1) ÷ 2. Here (40 + 1) ÷ 2 = 41 ÷ 2 = 20.5.

6. The average weight of 8 members of a club increases by 2.5 kg when a new member replaces one who weighs 65 kg. What is the weight of the new member?

  1. 80 kg
  2. 82.5 kg
  3. 85 kg
  4. 87.5 kg

Answer: 85 kg

Explanation: Total weight rises by 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg. That entire rise comes from the swap, so the new member weighs 65 + 20 = 85 kg.

7. The average of three numbers a, b and c is 20. The average of a and b is 15. What is the value of c?

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 30

Answer: 30

Explanation: a + b + c = 3 × 20 = 60. a + b = 2 × 15 = 30. So c = 60 − 30 = 30.

8. A car travels from town P to town Q at 40 km/h and returns along the same road at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 48 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 45 km/h
  4. 52 km/h

Answer: 48 km/h

Explanation: For equal distances, average speed = 2xy ÷ (x + y) = (2 × 40 × 60) ÷ (40 + 60) = 4800 ÷ 100 = 48 km/h. The plain average 50 km/h is the trap.

9. The mean of 25 observations was found to be 36. Later it was discovered that one observation was recorded as 23 instead of the correct value 48. What is the corrected mean?

  1. 36.5
  2. 37
  3. 37.5
  4. 38

Answer: 37

Explanation: The recorded total is short by 48 − 23 = 25. Corrected total = 25 × 36 + 25 = 900 + 25 = 925, so the corrected mean = 925 ÷ 25 = 37.

10. The average of 11 results is 50. The average of the first 6 results is 49 and that of the last 6 results is 52. What is the 6th result?

  1. 52
  2. 54
  3. 56
  4. 58

Answer: 56

Explanation: Sum of first 6 = 6 × 49 = 294; sum of last 6 = 6 × 52 = 312. These overlap at the 6th result, so 294 + 312 = (11 × 50) + 6th result. 606 = 550 + 6th result, giving 56.

11. The average of 6 numbers is 30. If every number is multiplied by 4, what is the average of the new set of numbers?

  1. 30
  2. 34
  3. 90
  4. 120

Answer: 120

Explanation: Multiplying every value by 4 multiplies the average by 4 as well: 30 × 4 = 120.

12. In a group of 40 students, 25 boys scored an average of 44 marks and 15 girls scored an average of 36 marks. What is the average mark of the whole group?

  1. 41
  2. 40
  3. 42
  4. 39

Answer: 41

Explanation: Total marks = (25 × 44) + (15 × 36) = 1100 + 540 = 1640. Group average = 1640 ÷ 40 = 41.

13. The average age of 8 men increases by 2 years when two women replace two men aged 20 years and 24 years. What is the average age of the two women?

  1. 28 years
  2. 30 years
  3. 32 years
  4. 34 years

Answer: 30 years

Explanation: Total age rises by 8 × 2 = 16 years. Combined age of the two women = (20 + 24) + 16 = 60 years, so their average age = 60 ÷ 2 = 30 years.

14. The average of 20 numbers is zero. At most, how many of these numbers can be greater than zero?

  1. 17
  2. 18
  3. 19
  4. 20

Answer: 19

Explanation: If the average is zero the sum is zero. Up to 19 numbers can be positive provided the 20th is negative enough to cancel them; all 20 cannot be positive.

15. A student's average score in 4 subjects is 75. If she scored 60, 78 and 82 in three of the subjects, what did she score in the fourth?

  1. 70
  2. 75
  3. 78
  4. 80

Answer: 80

Explanation: Total required = 4 × 75 = 300. Marks in three subjects = 60 + 78 + 82 = 220. Fourth subject = 300 − 220 = 80.

16. The average of 7 consecutive whole numbers is 20. What is the largest of these numbers?

  1. 23
  2. 22
  3. 24
  4. 21

Answer: 23

Explanation: For consecutive numbers the average is the middle (4th) term, so the numbers are 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23. The largest is 23.

17. A cricketer's average in 10 innings was a certain figure. In the 11th innings he scored 108 runs, which raised his average by 6 runs. What is his new average?

  1. 45
  2. 48
  3. 50
  4. 54

Answer: 48

Explanation: Let the old average be x. Then 10x + 108 = 11(x + 6) → 10x + 108 = 11x + 66 → x = 42. New average = 42 + 6 = 48.

18. The average of 50 numbers is 38. If two of the numbers, namely 45 and 55, are discarded, what is the average of the remaining numbers?

  1. 36.5
  2. 37
  3. 37.5
  4. 38

Answer: 37.5

Explanation: Total of 50 numbers = 50 × 38 = 1900. After removing 45 + 55 = 100, the total is 1800 over 48 numbers: 1800 ÷ 48 = 37.5.

19. The average temperature on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday was 40°C. The average on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday was 41°C. If Thursday's temperature was 42°C, what was Monday's temperature?

  1. 42°C
  2. 41°C
  3. 40°C
  4. 39°C

Answer: 39°C

Explanation: Mon + Tue + Wed = 3 × 40 = 120. Tue + Wed + Thu = 3 × 41 = 123, so Tue + Wed = 123 − 42 = 81. Monday = 120 − 81 = 39°C.

20. The average of the first three of five numbers is 20 and the average of the last three is 24. If the average of all five numbers is 22, what is the third number?

  1. 22
  2. 20
  3. 24
  4. 21

Answer: 22

Explanation: Sum of first three = 60; sum of last three = 72. The third number lies in both groups, so 60 + 72 = (5 × 22) + third number → 132 = 110 + third number → third number = 22.
