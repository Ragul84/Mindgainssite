1. The value of π (pi) is approximately:

  1. 3.14
  2. 3.41
  3. 3.24
  4. 2.71

Answer: 3.14

Explanation: π ≈ 3.14159... It is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.

2. How much water must be added to 60 L of milk at ₹3.50/L so that cost price is ₹2.80/L?

  1. 15 L
  2. 12 L
  3. 10 L
  4. 18 L

Answer: 15 L

Explanation: By alligation: (3.50−2.80):(2.80−0) = 0.70:2.80 = 1:4. Milk:Water = 4:1. Water = 60/4 = 15 L.

3. In what ratio should sugar at ₹20/kg be mixed with sugar at ₹30/kg to get mixture worth ₹24/kg?

  1. 3:2
  2. 2:3
  3. 3:1
  4. 1:3

Answer: 3:2

Explanation: By alligation: |24−20|:|30−24| = 4:6 = 2:3. Cheaper:Costlier = 3:2. Wait: alligation: (30−24):(24−20)=6:4=3:2.

4. A container has 50 litres of alcohol. 10 litres is taken out and water added. Process repeated twice. Ratio of alcohol to water finally:

  1. 32:18
  2. 32:50
  3. 18:32
  4. None

Answer: 32:18

Explanation: After 1st: Alcohol = 50×(40/50)=40L. After 2nd: Alcohol=40×(40/50)=32L. Water=50−32=18L. Ratio=32:18.

5. The ratio of ages of father and son is 7:3. After 4 years, ratio is 2:1. Father's present age:

  1. 28 years
  2. 35 years
  3. 42 years
  4. 21 years

Answer: 28 years

Explanation: 7x+4/3x+4=2/1. 7x+4=6x+8. x=4. Father=7×4=28 years.

7. Rationalize denominator of 1/√5:

  1. √5/5
  2. 1/5
  3. √5
  4. 5

Answer: √5/5

Explanation: 1/√5 × √5/√5 = √5/5.

8. If the 5th term of GP is 162 and common ratio is 3, first term is:

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 6
  4. 9

Answer: 2

Explanation: aₙ = a×r^(n−1). 162 = a×3^4 = 81a. a = 162/81 = 2.

9. A sum of ₹8,000 is to be divided among A, B, C in ratio 1/2:1/3:1/4. A's share:

  1. ₹3,692
  2. ₹3,200
  3. ₹3,000
  4. ₹3,840

Answer: ₹3,692

Explanation: Ratio=1/2:1/3:1/4=6:4:3. A's share=(6/13)×8000=₹3,692.31≈₹3,692.

11. Ratio of surface areas of two spheres is 4:9. Ratio of their volumes:

  1. 8:27
  2. 4:9
  3. 2:3
  4. 16:81

Answer: 8:27

Explanation: SA ratio=4:9=(r₁/r₂)²=4:9→r₁:r₂=2:3. Volume ratio=(r₁/r₂)³=8:27.

12. The centroid divides each median in ratio:

  1. 2:1
  2. 1:2
  3. 3:1
  4. 1:3

Answer: 2:1

Explanation: The centroid divides each median in ratio 2:1 from vertex to midpoint of opposite side.

13. In what ratio is water added to alcohol to get a solution which is 40% alcohol?

  1. 3:2
  2. 2:3
  3. 1:1
  4. 5:3

Answer: 3:2

Explanation: Alcohol:Water=40:60=2:3. Water:Alcohol=3:2. So water is added in ratio 3:2 to alcohol.

14. The ratio 27 minutes : 3 hours in simplest form:

  1. 3:20
  2. 9:60
  3. 1:20
  4. 27:180

Answer: 3:20

Explanation: 3 hours=180 min. 27:180=3:20 (divide by 9).

15. The ratio of the volumes of two cubes is 8:27. Ratio of their surface areas:

  1. 4:9
  2. 2:3
  3. 8:27
  4. 16:81

Answer: 4:9

Explanation: Volume ratio=8:27=(a/b)³=8:27→a:b=2:3. Surface area ratio=(a/b)²=4:9.

16. Two numbers are in the ratio 5:7. If 4 is added to each, the ratio becomes 3:4. The numbers are:

  1. 20 and 28
  2. 25 and 35
  3. 30 and 42
  4. 15 and 21

Answer: 20 and 28

Explanation: Let 5x and 7x. (5x+4)/(7x+4)=3/4. 20x+16=21x+12. x=4. Numbers: 20 and 28.

18. ₹6,300 is divided among A, B, C in ratio 3:4:5. B's share is:

  1. ₹1,575
  2. ₹2,100
  3. ₹2,625
  4. ₹1,890

Answer: ₹2,100

Explanation: Total parts = 3+4+5=12. B's share = (4/12)×6300 = ₹2,100.

19. A mixture of 40 litres contains milk and water in 3:1. How much water must be added to make ratio 3:2?

  1. 10 litres
  2. 8 litres
  3. 12 litres
  4. 5 litres

Answer: 10 litres

Explanation: Milk = 30L, Water = 10L. Let x litres water added. 30/(10+x) = 3/2. 60 = 30+3x. 3x=30. x=10 litres.

20. Ages of two persons are in ratio 5:3. After 4 years the ratio becomes 7:5. Their present ages are:

  1. 10 and 6
  2. 20 and 12
  3. 15 and 9
  4. 25 and 15

Answer: 10 and 6

Explanation: 5x+4/3x+4=7/5. 25x+20=21x+28. 4x=8. x=2. Ages: 10 and 6.

21. The third proportional to 12 and 18 is:

  1. 27
  2. 24
  3. 36
  4. 30

Answer: 27

Explanation: If a:b = b:c, then c=b²/a. Third proportional = 18²/12 = 324/12 = 27.

22. Mean proportional between 9 and 25 is:

  1. 15
  2. 17
  3. 16
  4. 13

Answer: 15

Explanation: Mean proportional = √(9×25) = √225 = 15.

23. Ratio of speeds of A and B is 3:4. If B reaches in 1 hour, A takes:

  1. 1 hr 20 min
  2. 1 hr 15 min
  3. 45 min
  4. 1 hr 30 min

Answer: 1 hr 20 min

Explanation: Speed ratio A:B = 3:4. Time ratio A:B = 4:3 (inverse). B takes 1 hour, so A takes (4/3) hour = 1 hour 20 minutes.

24. Speeds of two trains are in ratio 3:4. They take 40 and x minutes respectively to cover same distance. x = ?

  1. 30 min
  2. 45 min
  3. 60 min
  4. 20 min

Answer: 30 min

Explanation: Time is inversely proportional to speed. T₁/T₂=S₂/S₁. 40/x=3/4. x=40×4/3=... Hmm: 40/x=4/3. 3×40=4x. 120=4x. x=30.

25. If ΔABC ~ ΔPQR with BC:QR = 3:4, ratio of their areas:

  1. 9:16
  2. 3:4
  3. √3:√4
  4. 6:8

Answer: 9:16

Explanation: Ratio of areas of similar triangles = square of ratio of corresponding sides = (3/4)² = 9:16.
