26. Two numbers are in ratio 4:5. Their LCM is 120. HCF is:

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 4
  4. 10

Answer: 6

Explanation: Numbers=4k and 5k. LCM=4k×5k/HCF=20k²/k=20k=120→k=6. Numbers=24 and 30. HCF=6.

27. Ratio of volume of cube of side a to cylinder of radius a and height a (π=22/7):

  1. 7:22
  2. 14:11
  3. 22:7
  4. 7:11

Answer: 7:22

Explanation: Cube=a³. Cylinder=πa²×a=πa³=(22/7)a³. Ratio=a³:(22/7)a³=7:22.

28. The mean proportional between 16 and 25 is:

  1. 20
  2. 22
  3. 18
  4. 21

Answer: 20

Explanation: Mean proportional = √(16×25) = √400 = 20.

29. 4 boys can complete a task in 10 days. 5 girls can complete same task in 8 days. In what ratio is 1 boy's work to 1 girl's work?

  1. 1:1
  2. 5:4
  3. 4:5
  4. 2:1

Answer: 1:1

Explanation: Boys total work=4×10=40 boy-days. Girls total work=5×8=40 girl-days. Both equal, so 1 boy-day = 1 girl-day. Ratio=1:1.

30. If two numbers are in ratio 2:5 and their HCF is 8, their LCM is:

  1. 80
  2. 40
  3. 160
  4. 120

Answer: 80

Explanation: Numbers = 2×8=16 and 5×8=40. LCM(16,40)=80.

31. Two supplementary angles are in ratio 2:3. The angles are:

  1. 72° and 108°
  2. 60° and 120°
  3. 80° and 100°
  4. 90° and 90°

Answer: 72° and 108°

Explanation: 2x+3x=180°. 5x=180°. x=36°. Angles: 72° and 108°.

32. If A : B = 3 : 5 and B : C = 2 : 7, what is A : B : C?

  1. 6 : 10 : 35
  2. 3 : 5 : 7
  3. 6 : 5 : 35
  4. 3 : 10 : 7

Answer: 6 : 10 : 35

Explanation: To combine ratios, make the common term (B) equal. Multiply the first ratio by 2 and the second by 5.

33. Two numbers are in the ratio 4 : 7. If the sum of the numbers is 132, what is the larger number?

  1. 48
  2. 84
  3. 12
  4. 72

Answer: 84

Explanation: Let the numbers be 4x and 7x. Their sum is 11x, so 11x = 132. The larger number is 7x.

34. If A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 6:7, then find A:B:C.

  1. 9:12:14
  2. 3:4:7
  3. 18:24:28
  4. 6:8:14

Answer: 9:12:14

Explanation: To combine ratios, make the common term (B) equal. Multiply the first ratio by 3 and the second by 2 to get B as 12, then combine.

35. The ratio of two numbers is 5:8. If 9 is added to each number, the ratio becomes 8:11. Find the sum of the original numbers.

  1. 39
  2. 26
  3. 52
  4. 65

Answer: 26

Explanation: Let the numbers be 5x and 8x. Set up the equation (5x+9)/(8x+9) = 8/11 to find x, then calculate 5x+8x.

36. A sum of money is to be distributed among P, Q, and R in the ratio 3:5:7. If Q's share is ₹1200, what is the total sum of money?

  1. ₹3600
  2. ₹2400
  3. ₹4800
  4. ₹6000

Answer: ₹4800

Explanation: If 5 parts correspond to ₹1200, then one part is ₹240. The total sum is 3+5+7 = 15 parts.

37. If 4A = 5B = 6C, then find A:B:C.

  1. 15:12:10
  2. 4:5:6
  3. 6:5:4
  4. 30:24:20

Answer: 15:12:10

Explanation: Let 4A = 5B = 6C = k. Then A=k/4, B=k/5, C=k/6. Find the ratio by multiplying by the LCM of denominators (60).

38. The present ages of A and B are in the ratio 4:5. Eight years ago, their ages were in the ratio 3:4. Find the sum of their present ages.

  1. 72 years
  2. 64 years
  3. 80 years
  4. 90 years

Answer: 64 years

Explanation: Let present ages be 4x and 5x. Set up the equation (4x-8)/(5x-8) = 3/4 to find x, then calculate 4x+5x.

39. A sum of money is divided among P, Q, and R such that P's share is to Q's share as 3:4, and Q's share is to R's share as 5:6. If P receives ₹1200 less than R, what is the total sum of money?

  1. ₹7200
  2. ₹8400
  3. ₹9600
  4. ₹10800

Answer: ₹8400

Explanation: Combine ratios P:Q:R as 15:20:24. The difference (R-P) is 9 units, corresponding to ₹1200. Total sum (15+20+24) is 59 units, which equals ₹8400.

40. A container has a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 7:5. If 9 liters of mixture are drawn out and the container is filled with 9 liters of milk, the ratio of milk to water becomes 7:4. Find the initial quantity of milk in the container.

  1. 35 liters
  2. 42 liters
  3. 49 liters
  4. 56 liters

Answer: 49 liters

Explanation: Let initial quantities be 7x and 5x. After drawing and adding milk, new ratio 7:4. Solving for x, we get x=7. Initial milk is 7x = 49 liters.

41. The ratio of the ages of A and B is 5:7. Four years ago, the ratio of their ages was 3:5. If C's present age is 6 years more than A's present age, what will be the ratio of B's age to C's age after 8 years?

  1. 11:12
  2. 13:14
  3. 15:16
  4. 17:18

Answer: 11:12

Explanation: Set up equations for A's and B's ages using the given ratios. Solve for their current ages. Calculate C's current age and then their ages after 8 years to find the final ratio.

42. If A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 8:9, find A:C.

  1. 2:3
  2. 3:2
  3. 1:2
  4. 2:1

Answer: 2:3

Explanation: To find A:C, multiply the ratios (A/B) * (B/C) = A/C. (3/4) * (8/9) = 24/36 = 2/3.

43. The ages of two brothers are in the ratio 5:7. If the sum of their ages is 48 years, what is the age of the younger brother?

  1. 20 years
  2. 24 years
  3. 28 years
  4. 30 years

Answer: 20 years

Explanation: Let the ages be 5x and 7x. 5x + 7x = 48, so 12x = 48, which means x = 4. The younger brother's age is 5x = 5 * 4 = 20 years.

44. Two numbers are in the ratio 5:8. If 12 is added to each number, the ratio becomes 11:17. Find the smaller number.

  1. 40
  2. 64
  3. 50
  4. 30

Answer: 30

Explanation: Let the numbers be 5x and 8x. Set up the equation (5x+12)/(8x+12) = 11/17 and solve for x. Then find 5x.

45. If 3A = 4B = 5C, then A:B:C is equal to:

  1. 20:15:12
  2. 3:4:5
  3. 5:4:3
  4. 12:15:20

Answer: 20:15:12

Explanation: Find the LCM of 3, 4, 5 which is 60. Divide 60 by each coefficient to get the ratio.

46. A sum of money is to be distributed among P, Q, and R in the ratio 3:5:7. If R gets ₹1200 more than Q, what is P's share?

  1. ₹1800
  2. ₹3600
  3. ₹2400
  4. ₹4800

Answer: ₹2400

Explanation: The difference in shares between R and Q is (7-5) = 2 parts, which equals ₹1200. One part is ₹600, so P's share (3 parts) is 3 * ₹600.

47. The ratio of students in three classes is 2:3:5. If 20 students are increased in each class, the ratio changes to 4:5:7. What was the total number of students initially?

  1. 100
  2. 150
  3. 200
  4. 250

Answer: 150

Explanation: Let initial students be 2x, 3x, 5x. After adding 20, (2x+20)/(3x+20) = 4/5. Solve for x and then find 2x+3x+5x.

48. If A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 6:7, find A:C.

  1. 4:7
  2. 2:7
  3. 1:7
  4. 3:7

Answer: 4:7

Explanation: Multiply the ratios to find A:C. A:C = (A:B) * (B:C) = (2/3) * (6/7) = 12/21 = 4:7.

49. If A:B = 5:2 and B:C = 4:3, find A:C.

  1. 10:3
  2. 5:3
  3. 2:3
  4. 10:9

Answer: 10:3

Explanation: Multiply the ratios to find A:C. A:C = (A:B) * (B:C) = (5/2) * (4/3) = 20/6 = 10:3.

Answer: 2:3
