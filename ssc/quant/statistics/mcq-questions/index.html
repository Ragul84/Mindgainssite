26. In a class, if 5 students each scored 5 more on retesting, the mean:

  1. Increases by 5
  2. Stays same
  3. Increases by 25
  4. Depends on original

Answer: Increases by 5

Explanation: If every score increases by 5, mean also increases by 5. Mean = (sum of all)/(n). Sum increases by 5×n, so mean increases by 5.

27. Coefficient of Variation (CV) = ?

  1. (SD/Mean)×100
  2. (Mean/SD)×100
  3. SD×Mean
  4. SD+Mean

Answer: (SD/Mean)×100

Explanation: Coefficient of Variation = (Standard Deviation / Mean) × 100. It measures relative variability.

28. Geometric mean of 4 and 16 is:

  1. 8
  2. 10
  3. 6
  4. 12

Answer: 8

Explanation: GM = √(4×16) = √64 = 8.

29. In a pie chart, a sector represents 25% of the data. Its central angle is:

  1. 90°
  2. 45°
  3. 180°
  4. 120°

Answer: 90°

Explanation: 25% of 360° = 0.25×360° = 90°.

30. A bar chart shows production: 2019=500, 2020=600, 2021=750. % increase 2019 to 2021:

  1. 50%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 40%

Answer: 50%

Explanation: % increase = (750−500)/500×100 = 250/500×100 = 50%.

31. A table shows: Product A contributes 30% of total revenue. If total revenue is ₹5,00,000, revenue from A:

  1. ₹1,50,000
  2. ₹1,00,000
  3. ₹2,00,000
  4. ₹1,25,000

Answer: ₹1,50,000

Explanation: Revenue from A = 30% of 5,00,000 = 0.30×500000 = ₹1,50,000.

32. What is the mode of the following data set: 5, 7, 8, 5, 9, 5, 10, 7?

  1. 7
  2. 8
  3. 5
  4. 9

Answer: 5

Explanation: The mode is the value that appears most frequently in a data set. In this set, 5 appears three times, which is more than any other number.

33. If the mean of 5 numbers is 12, what is the sum of these 5 numbers?

  1. 60
  2. 24
  3. 17
  4. 7

Answer: 60

Explanation: The mean is calculated by dividing the sum of the numbers by the count of the numbers. Therefore, Sum = Mean × Count = 12 × 5 = 60.

34. The mean of 10 observations is 15. If each observation is multiplied by 2 and then 5 is subtracted from each result, what is the new mean?

  1. 25
  2. 30
  3. 35
  4. 40

Answer: 35

Explanation: If each observation is transformed by (ax + b), the new mean will be (a * old_mean + b). So, (2 * 15 - 5) = 25.

35. A data set has a median of 25. If two new data points, 20 and 30, are added to the set, how will the median be affected?

  1. It will decrease
  2. It will increase
  3. It will remain the same
  4. Cannot be determined without the full data set

Answer: It will remain the same

Explanation: Adding values symmetrically around the median in a balanced way (one smaller, one larger) often keeps the median unchanged, especially if the original median was not an endpoint.

36. For a symmetrical distribution, which of the following is always true?

  1. Mean = Mode
  2. Mean = Median
  3. Mode = Median
  4. Range = Standard Deviation

Answer: Mean = Median

Explanation: In a perfectly symmetrical distribution, the mean, median, and mode coincide. However, 'Mean = Median' is the most robust general statement for symmetry.

37. The variance of a set of 5 observations is 16. If each observation is increased by 3, what is the new variance?

  1. 16
  2. 19
  3. 25
  4. 64

Answer: 16

Explanation: Adding a constant to each observation does not change the variance, as variance measures spread from the mean, and the entire distribution shifts.

38. If the standard deviation of a dataset is 5, what is its coefficient of variation (CV) if the mean is 20?

  1. 0.25
  2. 0.20
  3. 4
  4. 5

Answer: 0.25

Explanation: Coefficient of Variation (CV) = (Standard Deviation / Mean) * 100%. So, (5/20) = 0.25 (or 25%).

39. If the mean of 5 observations (10, 12, x, 15, 18) is 14, what is the value of x?

  1. 13
  2. 14
  3. 15
  4. 16

Answer: 13

Explanation: The sum of observations divided by the number of observations equals the mean. (10 + 12 + x + 15 + 18) / 5 = 14. Solving for x gives 15.

40. A dataset consists of five observations: 10, 12, 15, x, 20. If the mean of these observations is 14, what is the median of the dataset?

  1. 12
  2. 13
  3. 14
  4. 15

Answer: 13

Explanation: First, find x using the mean formula. Then, arrange the dataset in ascending order to find the median.

41. The scores of a student in 5 subjects are 60, 75, 80, 90, and 95. If the score in one more subject is added, and the new mean becomes 80, what was the score in the sixth subject?

  1. 85
  2. 90
  3. 95
  4. 100

Answer: 95

Explanation: Calculate the sum of the first 5 scores. Use the new mean and total number of subjects to find the new total sum, then subtract to find the sixth score.

42. For a given set of data, if the median is 25 and the mode is 20, what is the approximate value of the mean, assuming the distribution is moderately skewed (using the empirical relationship: Mean - Mode ≈ 3 (Mean - Median))?

  1. 26.5
  2. 27.5
  3. 28.5
  4. 29.5

Answer: 28.5

Explanation: Apply the empirical formula: (Mean - Mode) = 3 * (Mean - Median). Substitute the given values to solve for the Mean.

43. A frequency distribution table shows the following data: Classes (0-10, 10-20, 20-30, 30-40) with frequencies (5, 8, 12, 5). What is the modal class of this distribution?

  1. 0-10
  2. 10-20
  3. 20-30
  4. 30-40

Answer: 20-30

Explanation: The modal class is the class interval with the highest frequency. In this case, 20-30 has the frequency of 12.

44. If the standard deviation of a dataset is 4, what is its variance?

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 8
  4. 16

Answer: 16

Explanation: Variance is the square of the standard deviation. So, variance = (standard deviation)^2.

45. A dataset of 10 observations has a mean of 15 and a variance of 9. If two new observations, 12 and 18, are added to the dataset, what will be the new standard deviation of the combined 12 observations? (Assume population variance calculation for both cases).

  1. 3.25
  2. 3.08
  3. 3.16
  4. 3.31

Answer: 3.16

Explanation: Calculate the original sum of squares, then the new sum of squares and new mean. Finally, compute the new variance and its square root for the standard deviation.

46. For a positively skewed distribution, which of the following relationships between mean, median, and mode is generally true, and if the mode is 25 and the median is 30, what is the approximate mean?

  1. Mean > Median > Mode; Approx. Mean = 32.5
  2. Mean < Median < Mode; Approx. Mean = 27.5
  3. Mean > Median > Mode; Approx. Mean = 35
  4. Mean = Median = Mode; Approx. Mean = 30

Answer: Mean > Median > Mode; Approx. Mean = 32.5

Explanation: In a positively skewed distribution, Mean > Median > Mode. Use the empirical formula: Mean - Mode ≈ 3 * (Mean - Median) to approximate the mean.

47. A frequency distribution has class intervals 0-10, 10-20, 20-30, 30-40, and 40-50 with corresponding frequencies 5, 8, 12, 10, and 5. What is the value of the 3rd quartile (Q3) for this distribution?

  1. 35.83
  2. 36.25
  3. 37.50
  4. 38.33

Answer: 35.83

Explanation: First, find the position of Q3 (3N/4). Then, use the formula for calculating quartiles for grouped data: Qk = L + [(kN/4 - cf) / f] * h.

48. If the mean of 5 observations (8, 10, x, 12, 14) is 11, what is the value of x?

  1. 11
  2. 9
  3. 13
  4. 10

Answer: 9

Explanation: The sum of the observations is 5 * 11 = 55. So, 8 + 10 + x + 12 + 14 = 55, which means 44 + x = 55, so x = 11.

49. If the mean of 5 observations (15, 17, x, 20, 23) is 19, what is the value of x?

  1. 18
  2. 16
  3. 19
  4. 21

Answer: 18

Explanation: The sum of the observations is 5 * 19 = 95. So, 15 + 17 + x + 20 + 23 = 95, which means 75 + x = 95, so x = 20.

50. If the mean of 5 observations (20, 22, x, 25, 28) is 24, what is the value of x?

  1. 21
  2. 23
  3. 25
  4. 24

Answer: 25

Explanation: The sum of the observations is 5 * 24 = 120. So, 20 + 22 + x + 25 + 28 = 120, which means 95 + x = 120, so x = 25.
