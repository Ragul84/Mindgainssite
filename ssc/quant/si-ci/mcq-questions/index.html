26. A sum of ₹10,000 is invested at a compound interest rate of 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually. What is the amount received at the end of 2 years?

  1. ₹12,100
  2. ₹12,000
  3. ₹11,000
  4. ₹11,500

Answer: ₹12,100

Explanation: The formula for amount with compound interest is A = P(1 + R/100)^T. So, A = 10000(1 + 10/100)^2 = 10000(1.1)^2 = 10000 * 1.21 = 12100.

27. A sum of money at simple interest doubles itself in 8 years. In how many years will it become four times itself at the same rate of simple interest?

  1. 24 years
  2. 16 years
  3. 32 years
  4. 20 years

Answer: 24 years

Explanation: If a sum doubles in 8 years, the interest earned is equal to the principal. To become four times, the interest must be three times the principal, which will take 3 times the original time.
