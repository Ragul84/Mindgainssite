26. A contractor undertook to complete a road in 100 days and employed 110 men. After 40 days, he found that only 1/4 of the work was completed. How many additional men must he employ to finish the work on time?

  1. 100 men
  2. 110 men
  3. 120 men
  4. 130 men

Answer: 130 men

Explanation: Work done = (110 men * 40 days) / (1/4 work). Remaining work = 3/4. Remaining days = 60. Let additional men be X. (110+X) * 60 / (3/4 work) = (110 * 40) / (1/4 work). Solving for X gives X = 130.

27. Ram can do a work in 20 days. If Sita is twice as efficient as Ram, in how many days can Sita complete the same work?

  1. 10 days
  2. 15 days
  3. 20 days
  4. 40 days

Answer: 10 days

Explanation: If Sita is twice as efficient, she will take half the time. Ram takes 20 days, so Sita takes 20/2 = 10 days.

28. A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C together can complete the same work in 15 days. C and A together can complete the work in 20 days. In how many days can A, B, and C together complete the work?

  1. 10 days
  2. 8 days
  3. 12 days
  4. 15 days

Answer: 10 days

Explanation: Add the individual rates: 2(A+B+C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 12/60 = 1/5. So, A+B+C = 1/10, meaning they take 10 days.

29. P can do a piece of work in 20 days. Q is 25% more efficient than P. The number of days taken by Q to do the same piece of work is:

  1. 15 days
  2. 16 days
  3. 25 days
  4. 18 days

Answer: 16 days

Explanation: If P takes 20 days, Q's efficiency is 125% of P's. So Q takes 20 / 1.25 = 16 days.

30. A, B, and C can complete a work in 10, 12, and 15 days respectively. They started the work together. A left after 2 days, and B left 3 days before the completion of the work. For how many days did C work alone?

  1. 2 days
  2. 3 days
  3. 1 day
  4. 4 days

Answer: 2 days

Explanation: Work done in first 2 days by A+B+C is 2*(1/10+1/12+1/15) = 2*(15/60) = 1/2. Let total days be T. B+C work for T-5 days, and C works alone for 3 days. Total work = 1.

31. 12 men can do a piece of work in 10 days. How many men are needed to complete the work in 8 days?

  1. 15 men
  2. 10 men
  3. 12 men
  4. 18 men

Answer: 15 men

Explanation: Using M1*D1 = M2*D2, we have 12 * 10 = M2 * 8. Solving for M2 gives 15 men.

  2. 15 days
  3. 18 days
  4. 10 days

36. P can do a piece of work in 30 days. Q is 20% more efficient than P. The number of days taken by Q to do the same piece of work is:

  1. 24 days
  2. 25 days
  3. 28 days
  4. 36 days

Answer: 25 days

Explanation: If Q is 20% more efficient, Q's efficiency is 1.2 times P's. Time taken by Q = Time taken by P / 1.2 = 30 / 1.2 = 25 days.
