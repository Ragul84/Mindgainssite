26. A and B entered into a partnership investing ₹20000 and ₹15000 respectively. After 4 months, A withdrew ₹6000 while B invested ₹4000 more. Find the share of B in the annual profit of ₹35000.

  1. ₹17000
  2. ₹18500
  3. ₹19000
  4. ₹20500

Answer: ₹19000

Explanation: Calculate the effective investment for A and B over 12 months. The profit is distributed in the ratio of their total investments.

  1. A
  2. B

  4. D

Explanation: Let CP be $100. The new CP is $90. The profit is 30%, so SP is $117. The original profit was 20%, so SP is $120. This is consistent.

38. A man sold 20% of his stock at a profit of 20% and the remaining stock at a loss of 20%. If the total profit was $400, what was the total cost of the stock?

  1. A
  2. B
  3. C
  4. D

Answer: D

Explanation: Let the total cost be C. The profit on the first part is 0.2 * 0.2 * 0.8C = 0.032C. The loss on the second part is 0.2 * 0.8 * 0.8C = 0.128C. Net loss is $400, so C is $4000.
