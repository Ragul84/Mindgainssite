1. A shopkeeper buys an article for ₹800 and sells it for ₹1000. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 20%
  2. 25%
  3. 30%
  4. 15%

Answer: 25%

Explanation: Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price = 1000 - 800 = 200. Profit percentage = (Profit / Cost Price) * 100 = (200 / 800) * 100 = 25%.

2. If an article is sold for ₹480 after a discount of 20%, what was its marked price?

  1. ₹576
  2. ₹600
  3. ₹500
  4. ₹520

Answer: ₹600

Explanation: Let the marked price be M. Selling Price = Marked Price - Discount. So, 480 = M - 0.20M = 0.80M. Therefore, M = 480 / 0.80 = ₹600.

3. A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 25%. If the cost price of an item is ₹800, what is the selling price and the profit percentage?

  1. ₹840, 5%
  2. ₹840, 10%
  3. ₹960, 10%
  4. ₹960, 20%

Answer: ₹840, 10%

Explanation: Marked Price = 800 * 1.40 = ₹1120. Selling Price = 1120 * 0.75 = ₹840. Profit = 840 - 800 = ₹40. Profit % = (40/800)*100 = 5%.

4. By selling an article for ₹720, a man loses 10%. At what price should he sell it to gain 20%?

  1. ₹864
  2. ₹960
  3. ₹900
  4. ₹1080

Answer: ₹1080

Explanation: If 90% of CP = ₹720, then CP = ₹800. To gain 20%, SP = 800 * 1.20 = ₹960.

5. A dishonest dealer professes to sell his goods at cost price, but he uses a weight of 900 grams for a kilogram. Find his gain percentage.

  1. 10%
  2. 11.11%
  3. 9%
  4. 12.5%

Answer: 11.11%

Explanation: The dealer gains 100 grams for every 900 grams he sells. Gain percentage = (100/900) * 100 = 11.11%.

6. If the selling price of 15 articles is equal to the cost price of 20 articles, then what is the profit percentage?

  1. 25%
  2. 33.33%
  3. 20%
  4. 15%

Answer: 33.33%

Explanation: Let CP of 1 article be ₹1. Then CP of 20 articles = ₹20. SP of 15 articles = ₹20. SP of 1 article = ₹20/15. Profit % = ((20/15 - 1)/1) * 100 = (5/15)*100 = 33.33%.

7. A and B entered into a partnership investing ₹16000 and ₹12000 respectively. After 3 months, A withdrew ₹5000 while B invested ₹5000 more. Find the share of B in the annual profit of ₹28000.

  1. ₹12000
  2. ₹14000
  3. ₹16000
  4. ₹18000

Answer: ₹16000

Explanation: A's investment = (16000*3) + (11000*9) = 48000 + 99000 = 147000. B's investment = (12000*3) + (17000*9) = 36000 + 153000 = 189000. Ratio = 147:189 = 7:9. B's share = (9/16)*28000 = ₹15750.

14. A shopkeeper marks his goods 50% above the cost price and allows a discount of 30%. If the cost price of an item is ₹600, what is the selling price and the profit percentage?

  2. ₹600, 0% profit
  3. ₹660, 10% profit

23. If the selling price of 12 articles is equal to the cost price of 15 articles, then what is the profit percentage?

Answer: 25%

Explanation: Let SP be the selling price and CP be the cost price. 12 * SP = 15 * CP, so SP/CP = 15/12 = 5/4. Profit = SP - CP = 1/4 CP. Profit percentage = (1/4) * 100% = 25%.
