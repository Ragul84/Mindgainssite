SSC · Quant · Number System

What is the remainder when 7¹⁰³ is divided by 5?

The last digit of powers of 7 cycles every 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. 103 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 3, so the last digit is 3, and 3 mod 5 = 3.
